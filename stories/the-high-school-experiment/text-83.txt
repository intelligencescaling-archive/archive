If Nakamura loses the basketball match, Satou will frame him for stealing the student records and Nakamura will be expelled. (Yoshida was sent by Aikawa who was sent by Satou)

This was bad because if you got last place in 4 games then 70,000RP will be subtracted from the least person participating and that was Nakamura.

-Nakamura led his class to victory as Ito got removed from the exam by Yui (though he passed because Nakamura bribed the exam body with 1 million RP)

Mizumi’s plan was for the students to weaken Nakamura so he could beat Nakamura to the point of unconsciousness and (with the help of the testimony of the student council president—Satou Gotou and his, the testimony of the vice president, Mizumi Kazuto) report him for beating the students which is an act of bullying, will lead in his expulsion.
-This is the first fight scene, after Nakamura makes quick work of the students sent by Mizumi, he fights Mizumi and almost loses.
He realized that leaving Mitas’ Womb has made him weaker and sloppier.
Volume 7—Winter Break Arc
-This is actually a short story; It starts with Nakamura spending his morning doing his hobby then by 4 pm, he goes to hang out with his friends though he is being stalked by a girl that he has never seen before.
It ends with him sleeping in his room—until he receives a text from an unknown number saying give me 100 thousand RP or He’ll reveal Nakamura as Entity 001(that is what other classes call the mastermind of Nakamura’s class as they have no idea who the mastermind is.)

Main Character:
Protagonist: Nakamura Eiya is a protagonist who constantly changes his goals.
He is very capable but dislikes attention, he is a genius but prefers average thinking, he is good at most things but fakes being bad at them.
This contrasting behavior is a product of his stay in Mitas’ Womb—an institution made to create the perfect human.
Because of that institution, Nakamura lost his personality and the general ability to express his true emotions.
He fakes his emotions to make himself look normal as all he wants is just to be normal. Take for example, for the entirety of Chapter 0-3, Nakamura had been faking his emotions and even in Chapter 4 where he is accused of rape, he still keeps those emotions in check.
Nakamura is intelligent and cunning but at his core, he is a Machiavellian. He’s manipulative and uses people without guilt—or rather he feels guilt but doesn’t really express it.
Nakamura above all this still thrives to be normal, Mitas’ Womb might have influenced his thinking, but he still just wants to have a normal life.
He has been like a lab experiment his entire life—he has had to kill—both literally and symbolically. His father was the one who led this project, and his mom was one of the scientists in the facility, so Nakamura had nowhere to run to.
Supporting Characters: While this book has only one main character (at least in Year 1: 2nd Term), it has a huge supporting cast but the important ones for this book are Yui, Satou and Mizumi.
They are the primary antagonists, the reason why they want to expel Nakamura is simple—it’s because they come form The Nakamura Eiya Project.
A project to create replacements—rather replicas of Nakamura in case he dies.
They are inferior to Nakamura, so they are unable to expel him—though expelling him is their main goal they still have other goals.
Yui is a unique character as she always contradicts herself, she loves Nakamura yet she wants to expel him. She idolizes him yet hates him, even though she can control her emotions (she behaves like a gyaru, like a Hiyakadere, like she loves every boy that comes her way.
In fact, she tells Nakamura she loves him the first time they meet but despite that, she is actually chronically shy—rather she’s afraid of showing her real self) she sometimes falters unlike Nakamura.
Mizumi is unique in the way he doesn’t actually want to expel Nakamura all he cares about about is if he behaves in a way that he looks glamorous like an elite student. So, keep the façade he just wants Nakamura to be kept occupied with not getting expelled so he can continue being the most elegant student on campus.
Satou has an inferiority complex so he hates Nakamura but he’s a wonderful actor so he acts playful despite being the most hardworking student (he sleeps for 30 minutes every 3 hours—it’s his special sleep cycle).
Book’s Wow Factor:
The book has a unique premise, setting, and protagonist. It follows a story that will feel similar to those who are lovers of mystery and yet still unfamiliar to them.
It has enough twists and turns that make it so complex to the point that if you skip just a chapter, you will get so confused you might need to restart the book.
But it’s so simple that once explained it would be unforgettable.
To put it simply, it’s a book that most young adults—male or female will come to enjoy and might even re-read.
It will indeed be a good read for young adults even warranting a large cult following which could lead to more readers—of course, this could warrant a webtoon adaptation.
One of such mysteries is the institution called Mitas’ Womb, how did such an unethical institution go unnoticed by the Japanese Government, despite Japan being one of the safest places in the world?
Another is why the government started a high school to make biological weapons, investing over a billion into such an endeavor.
The main reasons why this book is unique is because:
The moral ambiguity of Nakamura as a protagonist who is manipulative yet craves normalcy.
The realism of psychological and social strategies in a high-stakes school setting.
The themes of identity, freedom, and the cost of perfection.
These and more mysteries will be of course revealed slowly and with time.
However, I firmly believe if I make the book up until Year 3: 3rd Term then it might even warrant an anime adaptation.

Story Outline
Year 1: 2nd Term
Volume 1—Beginning/School Reveal Arc (Chapter 0-4)Nakamura Eiya enrolls in Tokyo Metropolitan Elysian High School, Japan’s most prestigious institution. He enjoys his newfound freedom and starts dating Suzuki Aiya. However, the story takes a dramatic turn when Nakamura uncovers a chilling secret about the school.
Volume 2—Stolen Records Arc (Chapter 5-7)The student records of Nakamura’s class—confidential data tied to the government—are stolen. Nakamura deduces the culprit through observation and interrogation, ultimately uncovering Yoshida as the thief. Yoshida reveals she was coerced by Aikawa, who was following orders from Satou Gotou. Nakamura strikes a deal with Satou: they’ll face off in a basketball match during the sports festival. If Nakamura loses, Satou will frame him for the theft, leading to his expulsion.
Volume 3—Second Term Midterm Arc (Chapter 8-11)Nakamura’s survival hinges on passing the midterms, where failure means expulsion. To secure victory, Nakamura and his friends—Hiro, Kagura, Ito, and Yui—resort to strategic cheating. Nakamura memorizes the test papers within two minutes and successfully returns them undetected. Simultaneously, he uncovers the identity of a girl involved in leaking information about him, utilizing cross-hacking to trace her location.
Volume 4—Sports Festival Arc (Chapter 12-15)Nakamura’s class faces elimination after poor performances in multiple events. In a critical basketball match against Satou, Nakamura plays a pivotal role. Despite two overtimes, Satou forfeits, preserving Nakamura’s reputation. Nakamura intentionally loses a relay race to avoid drawing attention to himself, balancing his survival with his desire to remain under the radar.
Volume 5—Second Term Finals Arc (Chapter 16-20)Tensions rise as Yui attempts to expel Ito, the class president. Nakamura secures his class’s victory by bribing the exam committee with 1 million RP, despite Ito’s removal. The stakes are high, as losing would allow the school to dictate the class’s RP allocation without question.
Volume 6—Mizumi’s Attack Arc (Chapter 20-22)Mizumi orchestrates a scheme to weaken and expel Nakamura. Students are sent to attack Nakamura, leading to a heated confrontation. Nakamura’s training from Mitas’ Womb helps him prevail, but he barely defeats Mizumi in a brutal fight, revealing his diminished skills since leaving the institution.
Volume 7—Winter Break ArcThis short story arc explores Nakamura’s personal life. While enjoying a rare moment of peace, Nakamura is stalked by a mysterious girl. The arc ends with him receiving a threatening text demanding 100,000 RP or the revelation of his identity as “Entity 001,” the mastermind behind his class.

Main Character
Protagonist: Nakamura EiyaNakamura is a complex protagonist whose goals constantly evolve. A genius who craves normalcy, he deliberately underperforms to avoid attention. His upbringing in Mitas’ Womb, an experimental institution designed to create the perfect human, stripped him of his true personality and emotional authenticity.
Nakamura is manipulative and highly strategic, often using others to achieve his objectives without expressing guilt. Despite his calculated demeanor, he harbors a deep desire to lead a normal life. His past as a lab experiment, coupled with his parents’ involvement in Mitas’ Womb, defines his internal struggle. Nakamura’s core conflict lies in balancing his manipulative tendencies with his yearning for normalcy.
Supporting Characters
Yui: Yui’s contradictions define her character. She idolizes Nakamura but seeks to expel him due to her ties to the Nakamura Eiya Project—a program aimed at creating replicas of Nakamura. Outwardly, she behaves like a gyaru and acts flirtatious, but internally, she’s shy and struggles to reveal her true self.
Mizumi: Mizumi values appearances and elite status above all else. While he participates in efforts to expel Nakamura, his true goal is to maintain his image as the epitome of an elite student. His obsession with appearances often puts him at odds with Nakamura.
Satou: Satou harbors an inferiority complex toward Nakamura but conceals it with a playful demeanor. Behind the facade, he’s a hardworking perfectionist, operating on a polyphasic sleep schedule. His hatred for Nakamura drives many of the conflicts in the story.

Book’s Wow Factor
The book’s uniqueness lies in its morally ambiguous protagonist, intricate strategies, and exploration of identity, freedom, and the cost of perfection. Its realistic depiction of psychological and social tactics in a high-stakes school setting sets it apart.
The mysteries surrounding Mitas’ Womb and the government’s involvement in creating biological weapons add layers of intrigue. The book’s complex yet engaging narrative ensures that readers are hooked, with enough twists to warrant rereads. With its rich characters and compelling plot, the series has the potential to attract a cult following and even inspire a webtoon or anime adaptation.
