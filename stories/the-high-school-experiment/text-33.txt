Chapter 9.1: The Unavoidable Questioning
“Well…” Kagura was disoriented by my words but tried to get herself back. “I was just guessing. I guessed so because it was probably by that time, considering how it troubled you, and since you hadn’t told Aiya, it had to be within a short period of time.”
Kagura was quick on her feet—a needed skill in social interactions; however, she was a bit too short.
Perhaps it would have been enough if I were actually sad and not thinking too deeply; unfortunately for her, this was a planned act.
“But then why aren’t you shocked that Suzuki and I are dating?”
“...?!”
“I just can’t trust you,” I pushed her away, escaping the hug.
“Huh? N-No, I’m trustworthy. I wasn’t the one who called you, I’m—”
“So will you still try to deny?” I asked.
“...” She lowered her gaze, biting her lip.
“Why’d you ask me to break up with Suzuki?”
“...”
“At least tell me why.”
I still couldn’t figure out why Kagura did this. It obviously wasn’t out of attraction, so was it out of concern?
But what is she concerned about?
I had to wait till she told me because theories would be almost useless when I had little to no information.
“...Why won’t I?” she said barely above a whisper. “We have an ‘asset,’ and you think I’ll let Aiya throw that away. Moreover, you’re my classmate. Am I to let you go?”
She narrowed her eyes and then spoke again. “No, I won’t."
An ‘asset?’ Is she referring to me? What does she mean by “throw away”?
“Ka—”
The bell rang before I could continue, and she ran back to class.
“Hey, Kagura!” I screamed, but she didn’t look back.
“Just let me go… You’ve made your choice,” her voice was cold and completely distant—it had no semblance to her usual bubbly personality.
It was like I rejected an angel’s hand when it tried to save me, but then it suddenly turned into a demon’s hand and tried to kill me, or rather, let me fall.
I’ve made my choice?
“Wait, let’s talk about—”
“I said let go,” she forcefully pushed me away, but never raised her voice.
She just pushed me away and continued walking, not even turning back at me.
“K-Kagura?”
“We’re having class. Don’t follow me, and just head over there.”
“...”
Despite what she said, when I arrived in class, she was already there.
So she came before me, huh?
Classes went by quickly, and it was soon time for lunch break. When I left the class during lunch break, I was about to go to the lavatory when a person yanked my clothes—it was Suzuki’s friend, Yoshida.
She was accompanied by another one of Suzuki’s friends, the same one from yesterday, and also Hayashi. They led me to their table in the cafeteria and sat me down.
It must have looked like I was being fawned over by beauties. And while yes, they were, they also kidnapped me.
It made me focus a bit on their features—Yoshida was dressed just like she was during the calligraphy club, so I paid no mind to her features and looked at the other people. Suzuki’s other friend had a more sporty look.
She had short hair that was dyed green and wore a short-sleeved shirt and also wore a very short skirt, even shorter than that of Suzuki and Yoshida.
It was so short that any gust of wind would raise it; luckily, she wore cycling shorts under her overly short skirt.
Why wear the short skirt in the first place?
She also had a sporty look, having lots of band-aids—though those seemed to be for cosmetic choices because she didn’t look athletic in the slightest.
Whereas Hayashi, in contrast to them, dressed like she was in a private academy—she wore everything down to the blazer with her elegant white hair that reached down to her waist.
As I focused on her features, she turned towards me, but I quickly turned away, and it seemed she still caught me.
Luckily, Yoshida spoke up, so Hayashi didn’t have the chance to say anything.
“Nakamura-kun, Aiya-chan told us you and her were dating. We’re here to confirm.”
“Wait, you didn’t notice yesterday?”
Ah, I made a mistake because yesterday I said we were friends.
Though I couldn’t act like I knew that they knew Suzuki and I were dating because I eavesdropped, so I had to just deceive them, just like Kagura.
“I’m the one asking the questions here.”
Seems like it went over Yoshida’s head.
“You are, but can I at least know your names?"
“You don’t even know our names; that’s pathetic,” she tried to hold back laughter by using her left hand to cover her mouth.
“You do know that you didn’t introduce yourselves yesterday, right?” I replied in a matter-of-fact tone.
I knew Yoshida’s and Hayashi’s names, but not the name of the last friend, so it was going to get weird eventually, so it was best to know it through this roundabout method.
Also, how’s that pathetic?
“Oh, right, introductions. The name’s Yoshida Aiko,” she grinned.
Well, I knew your name since you’re dating Innō, but you don’t know that.
“Mine’s Kato Yua, and don’t you forget it. Hmph,” she looked away from me abruptly as if I had wronged her.
Why is she trying to seem tough?
I actually heard her first name yesterday, but I had an inkling it was her first name, and I didn’t want to be impolite, so I didn’t use it.
“My name is Hayashi Nanase,” she said politely.
“Now, back to the questions. How did you and Aiya-chan start dating?” Yoshida asked, her eyes sparkling with anticipation.
“Uh, I just accepted her confession, and that's about it.”
“Nakamura-kun, I think you made a grammatical error. Don’t you mean when you asked Aiya out, she accepted your confession?”
“Yoshida, I made no mistake. She’s the one who asked me out.”
“That makes no sen—Oh, I see. Aiya-chan asked you to fake being her boyfriend to prank us.”
What kind of logic is that? So, she chooses to deny the truth.
Well, even if she doesn’t believe it, it doesn't matter; I mean, I neither gain nor lose anything.
“Aiko, why don’t we call Aiya over and ask her?” Kato proposed as Hayashi watched us intensely—more specifically, she watched me.
I wanted to ask Hayashi a few things, but couldn’t because people were around me. After a while, Suzuki came over to the table and sat beside me.
“Aiya-chan, sorry to ask weird questions, but did you ask Nakamura-kun out?” Yoshida asked politely.
You had no problem dragging me here, but suddenly, when it's her turn, you add ‘sorry’.
“Yeah,” Suzuki replied.
“So, he wasn’t lying, huh?” Kato mumbled in shock.
“Oh, right, I forgot to tell you guys that,” Suzuki continued.
“How would you forget that major detail?!” Yoshida shouted in surprise.
“But you didn’t tell me how you and Innō-kun started dating.”
“That’s… It’s completely different!!!” Yoshida blushed.
The people beside us narrowed their eyes at us, but of course, Yoshida barely noticed. In an attempt to save myself from their wrath, I looked back remorsefully, as if saying, “I’m sorry they dragged me here.”
“Aiya-chan,” Kato said, a bit hesitantly—her hands gripping the table slightly.
“Yeah, what’s up?”
“Why’d you ask out someone like him?”
“...”
“He’s frankly not even close to being good enough for you.”
“There are better people, like Ito-kun, for example. He would have been better op—”
“Don’t talk about him like that!” Suzuki screamed. “You did it yesterday, and I overlooked it because I thought it was teasing from a friendly heart, but now, it’s just plain insults. Nakamura Eiya is the one I love, and you should just respect that.”
“But he’s—”
“Don’t ever bring up this topic or else… I won’t ever talk to you again,” Suzuki furrowed her brows and arranged her stuff—as if about to leave, about to leave with me, apparently.
Kato was clearly shocked, her eyes quickly moving between Suzuki and me, slightly narrowing when she faced me with her grip on the table growing harder.
Before she took in a deep breath and bowed.
“...I apologise,” Kato seemed remorseful, to which Suzuki’s expression also lightened.
“Nakamura-kun, do you accept the apology?”
I nodded instinctively. Although I didn’t particularly like the teasing, it wasn’t so bad that I would want to potentially ruin Suzuki’s friendship with Kato.
“I suppose I don’t get to be mad if Nakamura isn’t…but just don’t do it again.”
All I could do was watch in awe as I was defended, and funnily, no one gave us weird looks despite the emotional rollercoaster.
After that, they went straight into girl talk like nothing had happened—and, of course, I tuned out their words, just ate my lunch (I went back to class to get it), and thought about Kagura and what she could be hiding.
I mean, on one hand, I don’t want to force her to tell me; on the other hand, I wanna know why she doesn’t want me dating Suzuki.
Suzuki didn’t display anything particularly bad; she seemed to genuinely love and cherish me. In fact, she even defended me.
So I was sceptical of what Kagura was trying to pull. However, I didn’t completely disregard her words.
I’ll wait till Kagura opens up because that’s what a good friend will do.
