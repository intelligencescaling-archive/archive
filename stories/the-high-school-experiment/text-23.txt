Chapter 6: Why Can’t I Find Someone Suitable?
Choom.
After I heard the sound of the door close, I lost all my composure—I didn’t wanna make noise and make him come back, so instead, I silently clenched the table with all my strength.
Nakamura Eiya was nothing like he was described. He was so utterly disappointing and lacklustre that I couldn’t help but be frustrated.
Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why?!!!
I thought I found the person who would spearhead Class Epsilon to pass even Class Alpha, but he was just another moron.
While he seemed to have some semblance of intelligence and courage—considering he did remind me of ‘that’—he didn’t seem to have the courage to do ‘it.’
I was sure he would freeze up midway; in fact, ‘that’ was the reason I was actually shaken by him.
Anyway, he wasn’t what I needed; his head was so far up his ass that it was honestly amazing that he had lived this long.
It wasn’t some silent superiority; it was the loud, arrogant, and pompous confidence that suffocated those around him.
It was a lot like those money-grubbing politicians who always surrounded Father; of course, Father was nothing like them, though.
At any rate, now that I was left with nothing, what would I tell Father? I had failed him again.
I’m a disappointment.
That was right; I was nothing but a disappointment to him—I kept doing this over and over again. I had tried my best in the first term, and yet… I had nothing to show for it.
I can’t find anyone suitable; the only ones left are Ito, who is definitely NOT suitable; Kagurazaka, but she has…issues; Suzuki, but it’s best to leave her be; and lastly, A—but should I have been more energetic?
I had been intentionally lackadaisical and insulting towards my students in an attempt to motivate them. In a way, it worked; they began to pay attention to me well after they realised they couldn’t survive in this school by not paying attention to authority.
However, they were still not up to par compared to the other classes. I even constantly re-read the student records of everyone in the class in an attempt to find more hidden gems than the present five that I was aware of.
Yet, nothing worked.
But this shouldn’t even have been happening; Father said that Nakamura Eiya would be enough.
He told me about him, yet he was nothing as he said; now what do I do? He said he was greater than anyone here, that he was a strategic gen—
Wait a minute, Ito told me yesterday that Nakamura was shy when talking with him, but when they started discussing his plan, Nakamura’s voice grew cold.
In fact, it was for that very reason that I suddenly applied pressure today; I didn’t want Nakamura to plan against me or be hostile towards me, especially with him still being in the dark; however, that plan turned counterproductive.
That being said, what if even when he entered my faculty room, he was just faking it? That is that pompous attitude. Yesterday, he was shy, assertive, and cold. Today, he was prideful.
What’s with the switches? Which one is real? What did Nakamura plan to gain when he talked to me the way he did?
That last part was very scary because he was quick to disregard me as a teacher and act as he liked.
So does that imply that he was prideful? I couldn’t tell. Perhaps his entire character was a ruse, or maybe there was some truth to it.
This means I couldn’t just completely disregard Nakamura. I was sure he was at least hiding something because Father never lied, so that was the only way—it was the only way for all the puzzles to fit in.
It looked like I would have to find out more about this guy.
That being said, what Nakamura Eiya has done should suffice for now, that is, if he continues in that manner.
I just needed him to solve problems when they appeared, so Class Epsilon wouldn’t get destroyed. As for his abilities, I would wait for the special tests to test his skills, though in a way, he was already in one.
At any rate, I would have to stay on the sidelines for a while.
That is, of course, only until I find something that can be used against him; then he’ll have no choice but to follow my instructions.
Then at last, I will be the first teacher to lead a class to victory, of course, only if the present third-years and second-years fail.
And even if I’m not the first, I’ll be the first to do it from Class Epsilon, then I’ll… Stop being a disappointment.
About this special case study, Nakamura Eiya, I couldn’t just shake the feeling that perhaps the inconsistency in behaviour was caused by a personality disorder.
Wait, does Nakamura have dissociative identity disorder?
