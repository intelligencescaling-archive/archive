Chapter 1.2: Swim Class
I spent some time thinking at the vending machine before heading to class. The bell for the break rang just as I entered class.
Lucky me.
On entering class, I saw everyone in a frenzy. They were talking to Innō and his friend, who I eventually heard to be Inoue Akira.
My classmates scolded them and the other boys from the cafeteria, but they didn’t just scold them for fighting; they scolded them for dealing with Class Beta.
“Why did you guys get involved?”
“T-They cut the line,” one of the boys voiced.
I was in my seat as class was soon starting, though they were arguing so loudly that I could clearly hear them.
“W-We’re sorry, okay?” Inoue voiced.
“Sorry isn’t going to cut it. Where’s Ito anyway?” One of the girls who followed Ito during lunch said.
Aren’t they overdoing it? Though that’s just my opinion. I mean, they made a mistake, but how does everyone already know?
I spent about 6 minutes at the vending machine, so there's no way people were already done eating. Also, most people are in class because of the social divide, so someone must have told them.
Did someone just rush over in class just to tell everyone?  I mean, I doubt the boys would just rat themselves out.
…Maybe I’m just looking too much into it. Someone probably just came to class and told everyone.
“What are you thinking about?” Suzuki broke my reverie—she just entered class, so she had no idea what happened, and the cafeteria fight wasn’t seen by everyone.
“Oh, nothing.”
“Then why’d you look all serious?”
“It’s just the situation.”
“Oh, right, that fight.”
So she did hear about the fight.
“But like, where’s Ito?” she asked.
“Somebody already asked that. You guys really trust Ito, huh?”
I know settling for this low-level probing is deplorable, but this information might be important.
“Why wouldn’t we? I mean, he’s our—”
Ito entered the class, cutting Suzuki off mid-sentence.
“Ito, where have you been?” a student asked.
“…”
“Ito, what’s going on?”
“…”
“…Ito?”
“…Guys, we’re in trouble.”
His words stabbed through the room, a knife to my heart. I was just as surprised as the rest—though I couldn’t, no, I wouldn’t show it.
However, there was a storm brewing.
“This is going to be a hassle,” I mused.
It will be a hassle? How so?
Well, I instinctively knew I would probably have to do something, or rather, it would be a situation where something would lead me to do something.
But I can just choose to do nothing, right?
“Like that’s possible,” I whispered silently to myself.
“Ito, what do you mean?” a girl asked.
“Yeah, what’s going on?” another student asked.
“We are in—”
“Change and meet me in the indoor pool!”
Ito was cut short by a teacher—supposedly our P.E. teacher. He came down to our classroom to call us out for class.
The man was hairy, quite short, and rather chubby. His appearance was unnerving, especially for a physical education teacher.
Also, who has P.E. on the first day of school? Anyway, I’m sure Ito was about to tell us about the fight.
Talk about horrible timing.
We all followed a straight line walking to our locker rooms, which were divided by gender.
“What do you think Ito wanted to say?”
“I don’t know; maybe it’s about the fight.”
“I hope it isn’t.”
The boys from my class were talking about the fight; they were probably anxious since they didn’t know the outcome of the matter.
“Yeah, me too. But why did they even do it? I understand Inoue; like, that guy is always something. But Innō? Isn’t he on the volleyball team?”
“Yeah, I hope he doesn’t think of doing anything reckless before he gets removed from the volleyball team.”
“You’re just scared that when he leaves, there won’t be any more bonuses from him being on the official volleyball team.”
Bonuses, huh?
From the way it sounds, I’m sure that a bonus is more significant than just a benefit, since it sounds like it also helps people who aren’t from the club activity.
Maybe you get it if you become important in a club, since he mentioned Innō being on the official volleyball team.
“You make me sound like a bad guy.”
“But if he tries something like this, things might…”
“Nah, don’t worry. He doesn’t have the gall; the girls will get angry, and we all know that he definitely doesn’t want that,” Another spoke up; he emphasised the word ‘definitely’.
“Yeah, you know how he is; he’s like a hungry lion except he hunts girls.”
“Yeah, an enemy of the female gender.”
Their words—it’s probably to ease their anxiety.
The way Class Beta treated us showed that they treated our class differently.
Well, not really, since they cut the line in front of some other classes too, but their insults somewhat reflected their feelings towards us.
Moreover, considering the fact that we were divided based on how useful we are to society—ignoring the fact that it was just a rumour—they probably thought of us as trash, perhaps even worse than that.
There was also the fact that no one from any other class spoke up for us except Class Alpha—but that wasn’t even a class action.
Even Class Delta and Class Gamma were silent; it was clear that we weren’t particularly valued by the other classes.
It’s quite sad, isn’t it?
“Yeah, like what a simp!”
The boys laughed merrily, discussing Innō behind his back. I felt kinda bad for him.
Though I didn’t say a word because I was sure they would target me next, and I was sure of two other things.
One was that they wouldn’t hold back, and the other was that they wouldn’t even hide it as they did for Innō.
Don’t get hurt, Eiya!
I’m sure when I get friends, they’ll protect me!
Nah, I highly doubt it.
I carried a grim expression till we finally arrived at the locker room to change into our swimwear; the school had already prepared one for us.
I went to my locker, and on seeing my swimwear, I sighed.
“Heugh.”
It wasn’t particularly bad, though it could have been better. It was black with gold on its sides; it also had the school emblem at the front, just like the blazer.
Though it wasn’t the best, that wasn’t why I sighed; it was because in this school, you can’t choose to avoid doing swimming classes.
Talk about student autonomy. What a joke!
The entire thought of swimming was jarring, to say the least, not because I couldn’t; in fact, I could swim quite well. It was because of Ayato.
I really wish I could just hide in the locker room.
As we were changing, some of the guys were still hesitant to change despite being in the same class for a term; well, I’m sure most didn’t want to show too much skin.
I guess in a way, guys are like girls.
After everyone put on their swimwear, we all came out to the pool.
The pool was divided into two, one with swimming lanes and the other without them, both being about 15 metres long.
“Line up.”
We scrambled, trying to line up at the stern call of our P.E. teacher; we lined up horizontally, and I ended up beside Suzuki.
What should I do? I’m already here, so it seems I must swim, huh?
“Nakamura-kun,” Suzuki whispered.
“Huh? What?” I whispered back.
“You’re really ripped, you know that.”
“Oh, you think so? Thanks,” I grinned nervously.
“So, spill it. Are you secretly an athlete or what?”
“Oh, no. My family really likes exercise, so…”
“Oh, I see. Well, I guess you’re lucky since you were forced to, right?”
Lucky? Well, I suppose you could say that.
My diet was controlled by my parents, but so was my training routine—I had a strict regimen, so I wouldn’t exactly call myself lucky.
In fact, I came to this school just to run away from that restrictive lifestyle.
“Hey, you two shut up.”
The teacher noticed us talking and shut it down immediately.
“Now, as we all know, this is the first swimming class since that incident happened last term.”
“Sorry to cut you short, but what happened?” a student asked.
“Don’t interrupt my class again. Also, did you forget? I’m talking about the one where a student almost drowned.”
Oh, so this is their first swimming class. I guess it explains the behaviour of some of the boys.
I bet the ones who aren’t shy have already gone to a water park or something.
“Anyway, let’s begin. I’m sure you all know how to swim, so we’ll start with drills.”
“Um, sir, I don’t know how to,” A lone girl raised her hand.
“Don’t worry, I’ll teach you the basics. If you can’t swim, go to the shallow side of the pool so I can teach you.”
“C-Can’t I just go back to class instead?”
The girl was probably shy about showing skin and was self-conscious about her looks, as she was on the slender side.
However, our teacher didn’t relent and convinced her to swim—he did this to everyone who asked if they could skip class.
“Now, let’s get started.”
After doing specific drills, we were free to do whatever we wanted, so I sat on a bench.
It so happened that I later found out that if you didn’t want to participate, you could stay in class, though if you came to the pool and put on swimwear, you couldn’t leave.
Meaning I had wasted my time coming here for no reason. The girls with smaller chest sizes didn’t come, almost like it was planned, except for that girl from earlier; maybe she didn’t have many friends.
Anyway, the realisation that I could have avoided this just left me exasperated.
“What a drag.”
