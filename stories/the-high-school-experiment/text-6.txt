Chapter 0.5: Freedom At Last
After Kisaragi left, I ran straight back to class just before the bell rang, although there was no need to rush, as there was no class.
However, something I noticed on my way back to class that really surprised me was that no one was to be found in the hallway, not even the new students. Moreover, no class was noisy.
Huh? But isn’t it normal for high schoolers to be in the hallway before they ring the bells? I suppose that’s what you’d expect from an elite high school.
Anyway, after entering class and reaching my seat, I checked my phone to see my account balance—92,000 Rp.
Since Rp was equivalent to yen, that means they gave me 100,000 yen at the start of the term. I wasn’t sure if that was a lot or not, but I assumed it was.
“Hey, Suzuki.”
“Yeah?”
“Weird question, but… is 100,000 yen large or small?”
“Obviously large.”
I’m sure that must have sounded weird to an average high schooler.
“O-Oh, I see,” I blushed slightly as I turned to see her, and her top button was open.
“Why’d you as—”
“Aiya-chan!” A friend came to her, and they began to talk.
Since my presence wasn’t needed, I dozed off again and was later awakened by Kagurazaka.
“Nakamura, wake up! It’s already 3:20!”
“Huh?! Kagurazaka?”
“Wake up, sleepyhead.”
“Huh? Oh. Thanks for waking me up.”
“I can’t believe you slept through the whole day. You’re really something else.”
I laughed nervously, unsure if she was joking or serious.
“Also, it’s Kagura, KA-GU-RA. It’s just three syllables; it’s not that hard.”
I feel a bit weird calling her by that name. It’s like as if we’re close when we’re not.
“...Sure, thanks again.”
“You’re welcome. Try not to sleep like this during class.”
“No promises,” I arranged my stuff into my bag.
“I’ll try to think of that as a joke.”
It was a joke. I thought it was funny; was my execution wrong? Let’s try another joke.
I looked up to try another joke, but Kagura had already left. I sighed and left for the dorms; I used the map on my phone.
Today has been really good, I guess, even though I’m still friendless.
But at least I got someone’s account name, right?
As I was approaching the dorms, I walked past a convenience store but quickly turned back to check it out and buy some important stuff.
When I walked in, I saw Kagura and Suzuki talking. I went over to talk to them.
Wow, she got here fast. Did she run, or is there some kinda secret pathway?
“Hey, Kagura,” I waved at her.
“Oh, hey, Nakamura-kun,” she waved back with a smile.
“You used the ‘kun’ honorific?”
“Or would you rather I call you sleepyhead?” Kagura grinned.
I laughed nervously—it seemed she was behaving in a friendly manner towards me. At this rate, I could get another account name.
No, we aren’t that close.
“Hey, what about me?” Suzuki pouted.
“Oh, hey, Suzuki.”
“Nakamura-kun, you know Suzuki?”
“Yeah, I do.”
“Mhm,” Suzuki nodded. “He’s my neighbour.”
After Suzuki said that, Kagura’s expression changed for a second.
…What was that?
“Well, Kagura and I are going to the mall. So, see ya,” Suzuki waved goodbye.
"Bye. See you two tomorrow."
Why was she… I guess it doesn’t matter.
I grabbed a basket and headed for the food section. I had packed toiletries, but didn’t pack food.
It didn’t take much to find it, so in less than 3 minutes I found the food section and got some noodles.
There’s ramen? Nice. I’m gonna buy a bunch!
Throughout my life, I’d only really eaten healthy food and snacks because my parents were pro-health.
So my palate was practically bland except for that one time that I tasted noodles when I went to Hiro’s house—when I told my parents, they were quite angry and made sure to tell Hiro’s family to never allow me to eat such stuff.
I had a really good memory, so the savoury flavour never really left my mouth, making me crave it.
“Though I suppose the meal I ate today might taste better than the noodles.”
With that in mind, I bought a lot, ranging from cup noodles to instant noodles. I bought some chicken, fish, oil, spices, rice, and other food items.
I had a decent idea of what I needed to get despite never cooking for myself.
That was because although I ate healthy stuff, I occasionally ate some lavish meals when I went abroad.
Anyway, I also bought some cleaning equipment; my total was 20,000 RP.
I went to the checkout and paid. On leaving the store, I held my receipt in anger.
Damn. Seems like I spent a lot. So, my balance now is 72,000 Rp. I should be careful from now on about how I spend these points.
“I need some points, please,” an orange-haired girl begged on the floor.
I watched silently from afar, trying to understand what was going on.
Huh? What’s going on? Is she getting bullied… No, she’s begging?
“How can you already not have enough Rp? The term just started,” the girl towering over her replied.
“It’s because of what happened last year.”
I left because being caught as an eavesdropper wasn't on my bucket list.
What do they mean? From their conversation, it seems there's some correlation between RP and what she did last term.
Did she get a punishment from school?
Hmm…
Not my problem, though; I mean, all that matters is my freedom.
I grinned from ear to ear at the thought as I walked to the dorms.
On the walk there, I noticed quite a large mall, but didn’t bother approaching it due to my extreme desire to see my dorm room.
In about 5 minutes, I reached the dormitory area by using the map. I headed to the front desk office, but on my way there, I saw some girls entering an elevator.
Hmm? Am I at the girls’ dorm?
I quickly hid behind a pillar before they could notice me and checked the map, but it said I was at the right place—on the map, there were only three dorms, and I was at the first-year dorm.
Don’t tell me the dorms are co-ed. Does that mean I might share rooms with a girl?
Does that mean that there could be…
“...Dirty scenes?” I completed the thought with my own words.
I grinned slightly at the thought, but quickly pushed it away and continued heading to the front desk. The front desk was behind the lobby, so I had to knock before entering.
Clank, Clank, Clank.
“Come in,” the front desk officer said.
I headed in with a nervous sweat dripping down my face because if I were wrong and this was the girls’ dorm, then I would be screwed.
“Good afternoon, ma’am. I would like to know where my room is,” I entered the front desk office, unable to look at the front desk officer’s eyes; instead, I looked at the miscellaneous objects in the office.
It was just your standard office with a desktop, a bunch of papers, a stapler, and other stationery.
Whoa! It’s so arranged and clean, I can’t even notice a speck of dust or a mismanaged
“New student, right?” The front desk officer looked engrossed in her computer work but glanced at me before continuing.
I then glanced properly at her: she wore a crimson blouse and pencil skirt. Her black hair was also tied in a bun as she wore blue ray glasses.
She didn’t react to me being a boy? Woo~
I internally let out a sigh of relief.
“Yes, ma’am.”
“Name?”
“Nakamura Eiya.”
“Is that right? So you're Nakamura Eiya,” she said with a faint smirk, scanning me like I was some kind of exhibit. “You're in room 234; it’s on the second floor.”
Did someone tell her about me? … Does someone know my past? No, it’s too unlikely.
“Ma’am, did someone tell you about me? Particularly anything bad?”
Hayashi might have said something about me, but I couldn’t think of a thing I did to that girl—I wasn’t exactly the kind of guy who just causes trouble; I actually hated trouble.
“Oh, you don’t know. Let me not spoil the fun; I just happened to have heard about you from some colleagues. By the way, your phone is your keycard.”
She heard about me from some colleagues, huh?
“Oh, thanks.”
“You're welcome.”
“Sorry, quick question: do I have roommates?”
“No,” she answered.
Well, that’s disappointing, but I suppose that should be expected. Such a system would just cause issues.
“Heugh,” I bowed and let off a little sigh as I walked off, because although it was expected, I was still disappointed.
“Also, before I forget, here’s the rule book. Could you help me give one of the rule books to a student I forgot to give?” The front desk officer smiled warmly at me, baiting me.
That’s low, very underhanded. Well, it’s not like I have anything better to do.
I agreed; I was supposed to give it to a student in room 233, my neighbour. I re-entered the dorms and went up the staircase because the elevator was crowded.
As I was going up the flight of stairs, I glanced outside a window.
I noticed a purple-haired girl talking to Suzuki, though I didn’t hear what they said due to the distance. The purple-haired girl was the person who sat in front of me.
I wonder what they’re talking about. Maybe they’re discussing their new classmate—me—and how handsome I am.
I wish!
I came to room 233 and knocked on the door.
“Coming!”
It was the voice of a female student, Hayashi.
This will be interesting, won’t it? I wonder how she’ll react when she sees me.
After 2 minutes, she opened the door.
“Sorry I took so long—Eiya?!" Her cheerful tone cracked mid-sentence as her smile faded.
Her brows furrowed, and she stepped back slightly, her eyes narrowing as if I’d just tracked mud into her room. When she spoke again, her voice was curt.
“What are you doing here?” She brought her head out of the doorway, checking if people were close by before speaking.
She doesn’t want people to know, huh? Well, considering she wants to join the student council, maybe being a person who screams in the dorm halls isn’t really good for her resume.
“I was asked to give you the rule book.”
“Ok,” she replied plainly, snatching the book out of my hand.
“Uh, you’re welcome.”
“I never thanked you. Leave,” she slammed her door.
“Bitchy much?” I asked sarcastically, expecting her not to hear me.
“I heard that!”
I hurriedly went to my room after she said that and unlocked the door by scanning a QR code on the door handle.
The door clicked open, and I stepped inside, taking a moment to absorb my new living space.
It wasn’t just a typical dorm room—it was more like a compact one-bedroom apartment, or rather, a studio apartment.
To my left was a small kitchen area with sleek, stainless-steel countertops, a two-burner electric stove, a microwave, and a compact refrigerator.
A cabinet above the sink offered just enough storage for basic cookware and dishes, which it already had.
Opposite the kitchen was the living room, furnished with a minimalistic grey sofa, a coffee table, and a wall-mounted TV.
The TV was already on, playing the school’s dedicated news channel, hosted by a poised student in her complete uniform with chestnut-brown hair and a yellow hairpin.
Her voice was playful yet professional as she reported on the day’s events within the school grounds.
I grabbed the remote and flipped through the options, but the TV only had that one channel.
A small message at the bottom of the screen read, “To access additional channels, subscribe to a cable package via your phone.” I clicked on it to see what the price was.
A message was sent to my phone; the cable package cost 5,000 RP.
It’s already linked to my phone, huh?
I was unsure when I would get more Rp, especially with what I saw at the convenience store, so it was best to guard them jealously.
Beyond the living room, a door led to the bedroom. Inside, a neatly made single bed sat against one wall, accompanied by a small study desk and chair under the window.
The bathroom was compact but functional, with pristine white tiles and a shower stall.
As I darted my eyes around, I noticed my travelling box (suitcase) neatly put beside my bed, blending subtly with the room’s aesthetic.
I tossed my bag onto the bed and lay down, staring at the ceiling. Finally, I was free from him.
I exhaled deeply, sinking into the bed. “Ahh!” I moaned out of satisfaction.
Ok, that was a bit too high-pitched.
Now fully relaxed, I turned on the A.C. and began reading the dorm rulebook.
It mentioned how we don’t pay for gas, water, or electricity but should use them in moderation.
There were 3 student living quarters: one for first years, another for second years, and the last for third years—the staff had special quarters.
Immoral relationships were banned, and those involved would be expelled.
However, as long as there was no proof, students could still be dating without consequence.
“Then what’s the point of saying we can’t date? For appearances? Well, this is still an elite school after all.”
Also, just like the school, this place is under constant surveillance, and you shouldn’t be in another person’s room after 10 PM, especially if they are the opposite gender.
After reading the entire book, I brought out my phone for a bit of unwinding.
“Now what do I do with you?”
I didn’t really have any apps besides Koda and my banking app—of course, the essentials remained, like the app store, calendar, calculator, and Google, as well as some others like YouTube, Maps, and Chrome.
“So do I download a game or… Nah, it’ll just reduce the amount of downtime I have; now let’s arrange my stuff.”
I put all the cleaning stuff in the kitchen area and stored the foodstuff in the cabinet.
After I was done with that, I put my clothes in the right cabinets and whipped up a light dinner—just a cup noodle.
“It looks bland even though I followed the entire step-by-step guide.”
It looked nothing like what Hiro’s parents made for me, but I finished the entire meal despite that.
“Well, it’s not bad, per se, but it’s nothing compared to what Hiro gave me or what I had in the cafeteria—it’s not bad, just disappointing,” I shook my head because it was so disappointing I actually considered crying. “Anyway, it’s bath time.”
I washed my hair with the shampoo the school provided.
Smells like flowers.
Before I lathered myself with soap and got straight to scrubbing. After I was done, I used the shower to wash myself clean and set up a hot bath in the tub.
When it was hot enough, I took a dip in it.
“Eughh,” I let out a satisfied sigh. “This is the life.”
This time of peace made me instinctively self-reflect on all the events of the day.
I made it; I really made it.
Still can’t believe I’m here, but sure enough, I am.
Anyway, now that I’m here, I don’t think I’ll just be satisfied with making friends.
Maybe Kaneko fired me up, but now I have a bigger goal, and that is to have many friends, at least 3, and to get a girlfriend.
But despite that, no matter what, one thing that cannot happen is me standing out.
It was a tough decision, but a required one because, after all, that was the only way someone like me could be normal.

There was a slight flicker of the street lamp by a bench just outside the first-year dorm.
It was a rendezvous area for two students hoping to make a deal; well, at least one of them was invested in said deal.
A girl grinned as she approached a figure in a brown hoodie, a black cap, and a pair of glasses.
“Heyo!” The girl waved, sitting beside the figure.
“Hey,” the figure replied curtly. “I want to make a proposition.”
“Of course, you do. Why else would I be here?”
“...I need you to create an atmosphere where people in your class will clash with students in Class Beta,” the figure explained as he looked down.
“Mhm, mhm,” she nodded. “So what do I get?”
“450,000 Rp.”
“That all?” the girl pouted. “I’m putting my ass on the line by betraying my class over here.”
“I’m aware—however, if you do this, I’ll give you crucial information about any class of your choosing.”
“Is that right? Well, now I’m interested,” the girl sarcastically replied to the figure’s words.
“If that’s not enough, then what do you want?”
“Now, you’ve got my attention.”
“Have I now?”
“I want you to call in all your favours,” the girl’s voice turned serious.
“Go on.”
“I need you to make sure that other classes help with Kaneko-senpai’s command.”
“...Why does that matter to you?”
“Do we have a deal?”
“Well, as long as my side of the bargain is upheld, I don’t mind, so sure, we have a deal.”
The two came to an understanding quite quickly and shook hands; they each had a hidden objective that would eventually spiral into something way worse.
