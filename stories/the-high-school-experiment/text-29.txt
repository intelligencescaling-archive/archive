Chapter 8.2: First Date
I spent the entire walk back to class thinking about Kobayashi-sensei’s words. I was trying to see if there was some sort of hidden meaning, but I couldn’t find anything special.
Anyway, as I arrived in class, I expected an empty class; instead, I found Suzuki asleep at my desk.
Huh? What’s she still doing here?
I tapped her shoulder lightly, and unlike Kobayashi-sensei, she grinned as she woke up.
“Suzuki.”
“Yes?”
“Why are you smiling?”
“I’m just happy to see my boyfriend waking me up.”
I blushed slightly, quickly grabbing my stuff and heading for the door.
“Uh, where are you going?” she asked.
“To my dorm room.”
“What about our date?”
“What date?”
“The one we’re supposed to have.”
“The one you haven’t told me about?” I asked.
“Yup,” she grinned.
A date. I’d never been on one, but I knew the general idea: two people, potentially interested in each other, going out to have fun and deepen their bond.
And yeah, I was in a relationship now, so it was to be ideal and to be expected, but an unexpected date was still, as the name implied, unexpected.
“So, are we going now?”
“Yeah, let’s.”
She stood up—her stuff already prepared—and we walked side by side as we left the first-year building and school gate. Whenever I looked toward her, she would grin.
From the direction we were heading, it seemed she was taking me to the mall.
As we walked toward the mall, I couldn’t shake a certain thought—why was she so eager to spend time with me?
I wasn’t exactly boyfriend material, and yet, here we were.
It’s…weird.
“So, Nakamura-kun, what are your hobbies?”
“Oh, you know, stuff like...existing.”
Hey, wait! I wasn’t being disingenuous; I didn’t actually have a real hobby, though what I said wasn’t exactly a lie. I generally enjoyed just being left alone and doing nothing.
“I… see,” Suzuki answered, a bit confused. “Well, I enjoy baking.”
“Oh, ok.”
We both went quiet after I replied to her. She probably didn’t know what to talk about, which was a shock because she was good at conversations.
Maybe she was shy?
Wait, for real?!!!
“Nakamura, do you have any…friends?” Suzuki continued abruptly, breaking the silence.
She said the last part a bit slowly and quietly; it seemed like she knew it was somewhat of a touchy subject, though I had gotten past that already.
“Yeah, I have a few.”
“Are they in our class?”
“No.”
“Are they female?” she asked, her brows slightly furrowed. There was a hint of malice in her tone.
“The only female one is Kagura,” I answered plainly.
“Okay, good.”
“Why did you—”
Before I could finish, the platinum-haired beast—uh, I mean, the senpai I met today—appeared in front of us.
Her platinum hair moved with elegance that was heightened by her platinum eyelashes, which further emphasised that the platinum hair wasn’t hair dye.
This was in contrast to her red eyes and very casual attire—the first three buttons of her maroon shirt were open, and she wore a miniskirt that was so short that, if not, she wore pantyhose underneath; a single gust of wind would have raised her entire skirt.
If not for the fact that her blazer had the school emblem, I wouldn’t have known she was a student, let alone an upperclassman.
It made her look like she wanted to be looked at by boys. Well, that would have been my train of thought if I hadn’t had this conversation that ensued, though the conversation before already showed her apparent misandry.
What are the odds I'd run into her twice in one day?
“What an unappealing encounter,” she glared at me.
That’s my line!!! Why am I in this unfortunate situation?
Did I do something wrong... Oh, right, I happened to bump into her; well, let’s just see what she’ll do.
“Suzuki, why are you with this man?”
“He’s my boyfriend, and also he has a name; it’s Nakamura Eiya,” Suzuki glared back at her with an even larger amount of malice.
As Suzuki said those words, the platinum-haired girl's eyes lit up, and then she looked at me. At that moment, a grin started to form at the corners of her mouth.
“Oh ho, it seems I’ve met a very important man. No matter, because in the end, a man is still a man.”
Uh huh, I’m super sure now; this girl is a misandrist, but she seems to be more tame than before, maybe because Suzuki is here?
At any rate, I wonder what the cause of her hatred could be.
“Well, we would like to continue our date, so are we done here?” Suzuki rolled her eyes.
So, this is the power of a gyaru—the power of a delinquent. She’s almost like my personal hero.
Go, Suzuki, go! Send the big bad beast away!
“Now don’t talk to your senpai like that, Aiya-chan; I’ll be sure to take my leave. See you two later, especially you, Mr Important Man,” The platinum-haired girl left, grinning at Suzuki and me.
She reminds me of Arakawa; dunno why, though. Perhaps cuz they both rub me the wrong way, though it’s for different reasons. Hmm…
Oh, it’s cuz they are both brimming with confidence! Nah, I’d prefer pride, or something like that.
“Let’s continue our date. Oh, right before that, give me your hand," Suzuki grinned cheerfully.
My hand? What will she want with that?
I gave her my hand then—
She hugged my hand, and though I could feel her breas—I mean, her big heart—I managed to keep my cool without freaking out.
We continued walking to the mall without a hitch, except for the fact that almost everyone on the way there commented on us.
“Wait a minute. Isn’t that…Suzuki Aiya?”
“Yeah, it is.”
“Is that her boyfriend?”
“Nah, she’s probably just being nice. I mean, look at him.”
“Yeah, you’re right.”
Hey, I might not be handsome, but I’m at least cute.
I wasn’t shocked that bystanders made comments about her, as she was quite the people magnet in class.
She was even on par with Kagura, who was friends with everyone.
You could say it was bad luck that she was my seatmate. Most of the time, when I came back from break, my seat was quite crowded.
At any rate, I had gotten used to it; I mean, it was not like I had many friends that would come to visit—in fact, all of them were my classmates.
Enough with the negative talk; I have four friends, and I even have a girlfriend, so that is at least good enough.
We got to the mall, and just from outside, it looked glamorous, so big that there was no way it didn’t have everything.
Yeah, I definitely should have gone here before now.
“Hey Nakmaura-kun, what’s with the surprised look?” Suzuki looked at my face with a sly grin. “Don’t tell me you haven’t come here before.”
“I-I have; it’s just still shocking, y’know,” I lied.
“Is that so? Well, if you’re sure, then let’s go in.”
“Y-Yeah.”
I gulped at the sight I saw outside, but when we stepped inside, it was something else.
The air conditioning was neither too cold nor too warm; it was basically perfect. The place was large with a faint sweet scent alongside some soft music playing in the background.
I expected Suzuki to say something, but she was just quiet, like it was just normal.
Must be because she comes here often. Damn, I look like a freaking novice, don’t I? She knows I was lying when I said this isn’t my first time.
Meh, at least she isn’t teasing me about it.
Anyway, even after we entered the mall, Suzuki didn’t say where we were going, and unfortunately, the school map didn’t explain the inside. It had left me, the reader, hanging.
Talk about useless!
Though I wasn't angry, I mean, the mall was five stories, and I was happy to discover it myself.
From boutiques to general-purpose stores, I was in awe, as I’d never been to the mall.
I was regretting my decisions because even though I had memorised the map, I had only gone to the dorms, the school, and the convenience store.
I was free, but actually going out to express that freedom felt completely alien to me.
At any rate, we kept walking as I silently admired the place until the plant behind us ruffled slightly—most people wouldn’t hear it, and I honestly wouldn’t either, but when there was silence, it was pretty noticeable.
I turned back instantly, but no one was there. I became suspicious after that.
Is someone following me? It seems Suzuki hasn’t noticed. I guess I’ll humour them then. Let’s see what they want.
Wait, what if it’s a stalker? I mean, Suzuki’s like super popular, so that’s possible. Though I kinda doubt that, especially with all the school’s cameras; there’s no way they wouldn’t notice; after all, they boasted about safety.
That means it isn’t a stalker… or maybe the school is testing our autonomy?
Nah, that’s dumb, but it’s best to keep my guard up.
After some more walking around, Suzuki took me to a café on the third floor named Palate.
She took me to a seat by the window. Her friends were there, one of whom was Yoshida, and for the other, I didn’t know her name.
Well, that’s ok, I guess.
After we sat down, the girls went to discuss make-up and other feminine subjects.
I kept quiet, following one of the best principles of life: do not speak until spoken to.
I may or may not have adopted that principle because of a certain girl who endorsed me to drink bleach, basically telling me to kill myself.
I’m not a coward, okay?! I’m just not a dumb ass. At any rate, my silence didn’t last long.
“You've been really quiet; why don't you join in? Don't tell me you're scared?”
What should I do? How bout’ I try to be like all those flirtatious guys?
“Why won’t I be nervous when there are so many beautiful girls in front of me?”
This was the best I could come up with that wouldn’t blatantly show that I was flirting.
As I said that, Suzuki's two friends blushed momentarily. It was a cheesy line but was more than enough for a Japanese adolescent female, at least one that hadn’t dated yet, though those who had just started dating could also be affected.
As I said those words, Suzuki looked at me, giving me an expression that said, 'Don’t even think of it.'
Scary!
I didn’t try another stunt like that because I was afraid of what she would do.
“Anyway, what’s your name?” Yoshida asked.
“Uh, we met yesterday?”
“We did? Don’t remember, so try and remind me of your name.”
You literally said my name yesterday.
“It’s a very common surname.”
“Uh… Miyamoto?”
“No.”
“Satou?”
“No. The name means middle village.”
“Uh…”
“It’s Nakamura.”
“Oh, right, you’re the guy who did that weird introduction; I completely forgot,” Yoshida snickered. “Anyway, Nakamura-kun, what’s your favourite show?”
First you forget my name, then you—Just calm down, Eiya; this is one of your girlfriend’s friends.
I sighed at her words before promptly answering.
“I prefer books to TV shows.”
“Is that so?” She stared intensely at me.
“Yeah.”
“I find it hard to believe that someone my age doesn’t watch TV shows.”
“Yeah, for real. Are you sure you’re a high schooler?” Suzuki’s second friend agreed with Yoshida’s statement.
“Trust me, I am.”
“I highly doubt it,” Suzuki’s two friends said in unison.
“I’m just more into novels than shows.”
“Boring, like there’s no way you’re normal. Come on, tell me the truth,” Yoshida stated blatantly.
“I promise.”
“Ok, then tell me a novel you’ve read,” she put her hand under her chin and grinned, thinking I was lying.
“A Certain Magi—”
“Oh, I know that one.”
“Really?!” I feigned interest, trying to suppress the feeling to roll my eyes.
Of course you would; it’s quite a popular light novel. Why'd you think I mentioned it?
“Well, I’ve watched the anime,” she laughed nervously, scratching her head.
“I see. What about you, Suzuki?” I asked.
“Oh, me? I’ve never been into that kind of stuff; I read books but not novels—that is, I prefer nonfiction.”
“Yup, Aiya-chan’s super mature, the way a high schooler should be,” Yoshida said, her eyes lighting up.
Suzuki’s second friend nodded in affirmation.
The way a high schooler should be my ass. So she’s allowed to read books, but I’m not.
Damn hypocrites.
“Heugh,” I sighed in exasperation. This would really make for a very long day.
