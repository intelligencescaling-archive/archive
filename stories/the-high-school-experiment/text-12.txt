Chapter 1.5: Whoa! So Fast!
With all my books packed up, I headed straight to the dorms since there was nothing more to do in class. On my way there, my mind began to wander.
This case is really building up into something, isn’t it? How are we going to handle the other class when they already have undeniable evidence?
Another thing is how they guaranteed this outcome. I mean, this video basically proves that they planned everything that happened.
The plan only worked because of the instigation, which was a strong implication that the instigation was part of the plan; it also showed how much they understood students of Class Epsilon.
It really was unbecoming of us, but it wasn’t really that hard to analyse my classmates (that is, if the rumour about usefulness to society was true), especially if given an entire term.
The problem was the elusive nature of Class Beta's objective.
Sure, they said they wanted to suspend us indefinitely, but what would that gain? I was sure that after a certain amount of time, the school would have to intervene.
However, we couldn’t rely on that because that would affect our attendance, and while this school was lenient, I was sure you couldn’t graduate if you missed most of your classes, and we couldn’t just report the school because it might make them investigate the case, leading to the student council’s involvement.
But even besides that, what would Class Beta even gain? It was so jarring that I had to ask the question twice. This honestly didn’t make sense at all, but…
“Maybe it’s related to R—oww!” I was so focused on my thoughts that I bumped into a tree. “I guess that’s what I get for thinking too deeply into something that’s being handled by someone capable.”
Well, he probably was capable since everyone seemed to trust him blindly—of course, I was talking about Ito. I didn’t have the chance to probe Suzuki, but I was going to just take the chance and trust him.
Especially since I had more important things to worry about, like who I would ask for notes since literally all my notes were incomplete and would become more incomplete as the days went on.
“Also, I think it’s best to sit down and think rather than to keep bumping into stuff.”
I happened to find a bench nearby, so I went to gather my thoughts there.
It really sucks that all my notes are incomplete, but what makes it worse is that I don’t have anyone to ask.
I hadn’t made a single friend since coming to Tokyo Metropolitan Elysian High School, and honestly, that didn’t seem to be changing anytime soon.
Of course, I made a few acquaintances in just a day, but it wasn’t out of volition—Suzuki was my neighbour, and as for Ito and Kagura, it was because of the Class Beta case, so no one was a viable option.
I could try Hiro, but different classes might have had different notes—wait, what class was Hiro even in?
Well, that’s not important for now.
Anyway, I don’t have anyone to ask, but out of all the options, I might have to go with Suzuki, by process of elimination.
Now, I just had to ask her.
“I’ll just do that tomorrow. But I must say we have some really nice weather. Not the best since it’s quite warm, but at least there’s no… rain.”
Before I could even finish my sentence, I could feel a downpour. “Oh, come on, don’t tell me I jinxed it. Now I have to get an umbrella. Heugh!!!”
I was very, very exasperated, but I couldn’t just sulk around because more important things were at stake—I needed to get shelter fast, and I also needed to get an umbrella because I apparently didn’t pack one.
“Arghhh!!!” I ran to the convenience store with my bag above my head, barely shielding me from the downpour that suddenly increased in pressure. “It didn’t rain yesterday or the day before that, so why today?!”
I’m sure I don’t have to say this, but I was quite pissed. Luckily for me, it didn’t take a lot of time for me to arrive at the convenience store.
Lots of people were there; they were waiting for the rain to end, and I became a part of them.
“When’s this going to end?”
“This is so damn annoying.”
“This is what I get for not checking the weather report in the morning.”
It was a very tense environment, but also a very boring one. So in an attempt to alleviate my boredom, I walked around the entire convenience store.
I mean, I also needed to buy an umbrella, didn’t I?
The convenience store was very compact but practical. It had basically all the things one would need on campus—well, at least all the things needed to make life easier. These things ranged from household needs to miscellaneous ones.
It was also probably quite affordable. I mean, I couldn’t really tell because, as I said before, I had very sheltered parents, but it seemed somewhat affordable.
And I don’t think I can use dollars, dirhams, or perhaps even euros to equally price this stuff since value is different in different countries.
“Also, I should probably be focusing on getting that umbrella, shouldn’t I?”
I promptly focused on finding one, so I quickly picked a basket, headed to the household items section, and spotted it down the aisle.
“So there are different types, huh? Well, an umbrella is just an umbrella, so I don’t think I have to get the one we always got, especially since it’s on the pricey side. Like, why’s a piece of washi worth 16,000 Rp?” I picked up the umbrella to survey it, analysing the bamboo frame.
It looked quite sleek and pristine; its design invited my nose to take a quick whiff, which blessed me with a faint, sweet, resinous smell from urushi lacquer.
“Fine, I’ll admit it’s pretty good, so maybe buying one for someone as a gift is quite nice, but if not, I’ll say it’s a complete and utter waste of funds. You know, this almost makes me feel bad that we all got one. Anyway, I’d rather just get a plastic one for 900 Rp.”
I put a black one into my basket and was about to head to the checkout point when I saw a familiar face run into the store with her bag on her head.
“Suzuki?”
I walked a bit closer to her and saw that she was with a friend. She was trying to calm said friend; both of them were very wet from the rain.
Whoa! Wet shirts!
I grinned at the sight.
Ahh, focus! I need to meet her for the notes.
“Urgh! I can’t believe he cancelled the date! Because of him, we got so wet. This is what I get for not bringing an umbrella just so we could get snug under one! I hate him so damn much!!!”
Uh… Maybe I should wait till her friend’s calmer.
“Hey, hey, hey, calm down. You don’t have to shout; I mean, I’m here with you, too, right? And I also don’t have an umbrella.”
“Yeah, I guess. But why aren’t you angry? You were there with me as we waited in the rain.”
Hmm?
“I mean, there’s no use crying over spilt milk; also, we can just dry our clothes when we reach the dorms.”
“Huh? Well, I guess you’re right; also, maybe Hiroto was just held up or something, y’know.”
“I mean, he is a Class Beta student, right? They probably have something busy to worry about.”
“Yeah, you’re right!”
Suzuki’s friend quickly brightened up and began to converse with her about her boyfriend eagerly.
I had my opinions about “Hiroto”, but I obviously didn’t get the chance to voice them; instead, I silently watched Suzuki before turning my attention away.
The conversation had mostly ended, and the main issue had been resolved, so my curiosity was mostly quenched. Due to said fact, I looked over at the checkpoint area and headed there.
“I’ll just buy this, then go meet—”
“Hey, Nakamura-kun,” a voice greeted me.
“...?!” I instantly tensed up and turned to see who was behind me. It was none other than my wonderful neighbour, Suzuki.
…What?!! Just how the hell did she sneak up on me?
“Did I scare you?” she asked, a bit concerned but sporting a faint smile.
“...Why do I get the feeling you did that intentionally?”
“Me? Why would I do that?” Her voice dripped with a bit of sarcasm, but I overlooked it for now.
“So, what’s up?”
“I should be asking you that. I mean, you were looking at me and—”
“You saw me?!”
Shit, shit, shit! She saw me. Now I look like a weirdo who was eavesdropping. This is literally what I was trying to avoid yesterday. The embarrassment’s so much that I just wanna crawl into a hole!
“I apologise!" I deeply bowed my head, suddenly with a solemn expression. “I wasn’t trying to eavesdrop; it’s just that your conversation was so interesting that I couldn’t turn away.”
I’m going to be frank—I do not think admitting that you eavesdropped because of how interesting the other person’s conversation was is smart, but I was disoriented, and honestly, I couldn’t think of a good excuse.
“I see. Well, I hope you’re ready to accept your punishment,” Suzuki’s voice turned serious as I still stared at the floor. I had never heard her voice so serious before, so I was rightly scared.
She walked closer and closer to me until—
She patted my head?
“Huh?”
“Pfffhaha,” she laughed hard, very hard. “You expected something very different, right? Relax Nakamura-kun.  Why would I actually punish you for eavesdropping? It’s not even that serious, I mean, you make it sound like you did a heinous crime or something.”
“Uh, well… I, um…”
“And he’s at a loss for words,” she giggled. “Well, with that done, I’ll be heading out.”
“Hmm? What about the rain? Also, where’s your friend?”
“What are you talking about? The rain ended some moments ago, and as for Yuya-chan, right after it ended, she also left cuz her boyfriend called her.”
“Oh.”
I didn’t notice that it stopped raining because Suzuki came right behind me, and I didn’t notice her friend had left either for the same reason.
“I’ll see you around,” Suzuki waved as she walked away, and I promptly went to buy myself an umbrella.
Wait a minute, I need her notes!
I rushed outside the convenience store and noticed her at a distance.
“Suzuki!”
“Huh? What’s up?” She walked back to me with a faint smile. “You can’t tell me you missed me already.”
“Uh, that’s not why I called you. I kinda need your notes, so could you get them for me?”
“Oh, so that’s why you called me,” the smile she had diminished.
“Uh, yeah. Is it any trouble?”
“Nah. Do you know when you’ll be done?”
“I can be done by…” I looked at the time on my phone, and it was 3:15. “Perhaps by 6 PM or before then.”
“You’ll be done today?”
“Yes.”
“You’ll be done with an entire term’s work in 3 hours or less?”
“Yeah.”
“I’m sure you know that my scepticism is warranted, right?”
“I mean, I guess, but I really can do it.”
“Uh, sure. Just wait for me at the lobby; I’ll bring all my notes.”
She headed straight to the dorms, and I silently followed. I didn’t know what to say, so I just tried to walk about 3 steps back to avoid a conversation.
“So, what did you think of your first day?” she asked, breaking the silence.
“It was okay, I guess. Everything’s still new, so I can’t help but be amazed at how things work here.”
“I hear you. I got lost in my days at this school.”
“Really?”
“Yeah, it happened like 5 times. You see, I suck at directions. Luckily, I met a guardian angel who helped me.”
“...Who?”
Before Suzuki could answer, we arrived at the dormitory. She headed straight inside before suddenly turning back to look at me.
“I really love talking with you, Nakamura-kun; just know that,” she said before leaving, her cheeks having a slight pink tint.
“...Whoa. Wait, what’s that supposed to even mean? She didn’t even answer my question,” I said this to no one in particular, and let out a quick sigh before heading inside too.
I went over to a bench and sat down comfortably, patiently waiting for Suzuki to return. It didn’t take a while, and in about 2 minutes, she was back.
That was quick.
“Here you go, she handed all her notes to me. “I try to be as detailed as possible and write clearly, too, so I doubt you’ll have trouble copying them. Anyway, I’ll wait for you in the lobby till you’re done.”
“Really? I’ll try to finish up quickly then.”
 ***
Nakamura-kun gave a quick bow before leaving to copy the notes I gave him. His claim of finishing the notes in 3 hours was frankly outlandish, and I couldn’t take it as anything more than a joke.
“Well, that was if it wasn’t you.”
I gave a slight smirk at the thought before I felt a faint buzz.
“It’s probably a message,” I pulled my phone out.
I was correct because it was a message, and it was from Yumemiya-san. I silently wondered why she texted me instead of just talking to me. It was safer and left no traces.
I didn’t want her to have any sort of leverage after this had been handled. Well, I doubted she would do that to me, but I needed to be careful.
Anyway, the message she sent read, “Good enough?”
What does she mean by that?
“I’ll just leave it unanswered,” I sighed. “Also, about this case… I really hope Ito can handle it.”
He met with Honochi after going to the faculty room, and she hadn’t said anything about it yet, which was shocking because that was definitely going to be the first thing she asked him.
Meaning, he probably didn’t tell her? And if he didn’t, why? Did something bad happen? Something that affected him, or perhaps the entire class?
I was probably just overestimating this case; it probably didn’t reach that far. It was probably something that Ito could handle.
“So, that’s it, huh?” I nodded. “Now, time to play some games~.”
I entered a short gaming session—in between matches, I bought some snacks. It lasted 2 hours, with me winning most of them.
“Good game!” I exclaimed after winning the last match.
“Well, you look fulfilled,” Nakamura-kun said, calmly arriving behind me.
He’s trying to sneak up on me, isn’t he?
“Back so soon? Did you give up?”
“Nah, I just finished,” he looked slightly disappointed that I didn’t jerk up like him when he snuck up on me.
“For real?!”
“Yes,” Nakamura-kun said in a matter-of-fact tone. “You’re acting like that’s something impressive.”
“That’s cuz it is.”
“It is?”
“It is!”
“It is?!!!”
“Yes.”
“Shit,” he cursed under his breath. “Well, I’ll see you tomorrow.”
How did he finish it so quickly?
It was a term’s worth of notes, yet he finished it in two hours, and frankly, he didn’t even look tired.
Seems like I was right; it really is you.

It seemed I messed up; I really messed up. I didn’t realize how unusual what I did w—
Good.
I had a faint smile as I turned to look back at Suzuki Aiya. The evening sundown kissed her face; it really showed her deep brown hair that clearly reflected. Suzuki noticed me from afar, gave a wink, and blew a kiss.
…I’m sure I don’t have to say this, but that girl really is beautiful.
