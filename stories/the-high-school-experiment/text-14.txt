Chapter 2.1: My Inevitable Intervention
“Can I join you guys?” I asked.
“Yeah, sure.”
Ito agreed instantly; well, it wasn’t like I was going to cause any problems, so he probably had the idea of ‘The more the merrier.’
We basically marched down to Class Beta. Though we happened to make small talk on the way there.
“So Nakamura-kun, why’d you come along?” One of the girls asked.
“Honestly, I was just curious.”
“Was that also the reason you went to the faculty room the other day?”
“Yeah, I thought it would be fun.”
“You thought the faculty room would be fun?”
The girls started to crack up, laughing at my expense. I couldn’t blame them because, in their defence, who would go to a teacher’s office for fun?
Why the hell did I go and tell them the real reason? I should have just lied.
“You’re way too funny, Nakamura-kun.”
“Yeah, for real, and the way you said it, you make it sound  like you meant it; you should become a comedian.”
I did mean it; also, it isn’t that funny, right?
I looked at Ito for confirmation, just to find out that even he was laughing at me; albeit he giggled a bit, but in the end, everyone laughed at me.
You’ve got to be on a whole new level of low for the nice guy to laugh at you.
“Such is my life,” I muttered under my breath.
“Are you okay? You’re really delicate, aren’t you? Well, you do look the part,” One of the girls asked in a lower tone; she seemed to notice that I didn’t take the joke well.
“I look delicate?”
“Well, just a little bit; it’s just that your face and stature are, uh…”
“Uh, what?”
“How tall are you, anyway?”
I’m just going to ignore that.
“I’m 5’4.”
“Wow, you’re that short.”
“Hey. You do know I’m taller than you, right?”
“Uh-huh,” she grinned. “It’s just that for a boy, you just have a certain persona; you’re not masculine, like, at all, but you’re really pleasant though.”
“…I’ll choose to take that as a compliment.”
“It is; you’re like super approachable and fun to talk to.”
…Is that so?
“Well, thanks. Also, what’s your name?”
“It’s Matsumoto Yuno.”
“Mine’s—”
“Oh, I know your name; I can’t forget your introduction,” she giggled.
“Kinda hoped that you did,” I smiled bitterly.
“Don’t worry, I’ve seen worse.”
“Really?”
“Nope.”
“…” I kept a grim expression.
“Sorry, I shouldn’t have done that; it’s just that I wanted to see how delicate you are.”
“You do know I’m not a lab rat, right?”
“Yeah, sorry again,” she smiled warmly, extending her hand for a handshake. “Do you forgive me?”
“S-Sure,” I blushed a bit as I shook her hand.
She’s really pretty, and she’s not as erratic or energetic as Suzuki or Kagura. I kinda like that peaceful aura.
We arrived shortly at Class Beta; Ito opened the door for us. As we entered the class, we saw some unexpected behaviour.
Rather than the people being loud and rowdy, it was calm and organised, quite a weird occurrence for high schoolers. Especially after school hours.
“That’s Class Beta for you, calm and collected,” Ito said out loud to no one specifically.
“What are you doing here? Looking for someone?” A girl asked with a grin—she had sky-blue eyes, and her hair was blonde with blue at its edges. She wore her complete uniform just like Shimizu and everyone else in Class Beta.
“Yeah, we’re looking for Shimizu-san,” Ito replied.
“Oh, then you must be from Class Epsilon, right?” she smiled as she bowed. “Please come this way.”
She pointed us to his seat and went back to her seat, though I noticed something: she winced as she sat.
Weird, she’s like an attendant.
We went to Shimizu’s seat; he was talking with friends and didn’t notice our presence till Ito spoke.
“Shimizu-san,” Ito said.
“Huh? Oh, it’s you guys,” Shimizu said.
“Yeah, about the—”
“You came with quite some girls, trying to change my opinion, huh? If Suzuki-chan was here, then maybe…”
“Maybe?” One of his friends asked, visibly shaken.
You’re beside him, huh?
“Relax, Yamada, I can’t even if I want to,” he reaffirmed to them.
While I was momentarily shocked at the mention of Suzuki, it wasn’t all that surprising.
I had already noticed Suzuki’s popularity. During break, there were two students always surrounded by people—people not from our class.
Those two were Suzuki and Kagura. This was because, despite being cute, they laughed easily with anyone, integrated into conversations easily, and most importantly, made people feel comfortable.
However, there were differences between them—Kagura was kind and down for everything, while Suzuki was more exclusive to her circle of friends but tried to make time for everyone.
Well, besides that, it was obvious that Shimizu had somewhat of an attraction to Suzuki, especially after what he said last time we met up with our teachers.
“Can’t you reconsider?” Ito asked.
“No, now leave my class unless you have something else you wanna do.”
“Please reconsider,” Kagura voiced.
“Still a no.”
“Please, if you’re so smart, then you’ll understand our reason,” one of the girls voiced.
“Yeah, aren’t they supposed to be like geniuses?” teased another girl.
“Yeah, talk about intelligence—”
“Shut up and leave the class before I change my mind!” Shimizu glared—he didn’t exactly scream, nor did he outright look angry, but one could tell how angry he was just from his eyes.
Scary.
You see, what was scary wasn’t his eyes, though they were also scary in their own right; what was scary was the utter and complete control of the emotions that were on his face.
Huh.
Despite his calm expression, I moved towards him and his friends because I wasn’t sure what would happen if he tried to attack the girls.
Yeah, despite my small frame, I was trying to be a hero.
“Hey, Ito, let’s just leave,” one of the girls, visibly shaken, said, holding Ito.
“Yeah. Well, thank you for your time, Shimizu-san,” Ito bowed.
We left almost as soon as we entered; however, when leaving, the blonde girl ran to escort us, and as she was running, she was holding her waist.
Hmm…
“See you guys later,” she said with a wide grin as she bowed again.
“Yeah, bye,” Ito answered.
We left the class, and then, after being a considerable distance from the class, we started to talk.
“Seems like there’s no getting past him,” Ito voiced with a slight sigh. “Time for plan B.”
“Yeah, but isn’t it funny that we need a plan B for Class Beta?”
“Uh, Ito, what are you guys talking about?” I asked due to confusion.
“Oh, right, we didn’t tell you. We expected this plan not to work, or at least we had a backup plan.”
Oh, that explains why we just accepted defeat so easily.
“I see.”
“Plan B is to try to convince students in Class Beta to try to convince Shimizu to choose another condition.”
“That’s a good plan,” I said plainly.
It’s a bit overly ambitious, but I suppose that’s what we need right now.
“Thanks. Well, now we have to divide ourselves into groups.”
“I wanna go with Ito-kun,” one of the girls said.
“Me too.”
“Yeah, me too.”
All the other girls said they wanted to go with Ito, except Kagura and Matsumoto, though I was sure they were just being polite to me.
Because neither of them blatantly said they wanted to go with me.
“Well, everyone can’t go with me; how about we divide into twos? I’ll go with Nakamura-san.”
“Aww, I wanna go with you, but Kagura-chan is fine.”
We subsequently divided ourselves and went around school, meeting Class Beta students and asking them for help; some were Ito’s friends, and most were at least acquainted with him.
It was an attestation to his popularity because of the girls we met; they were thrilled to talk to him. Much to my envy. Anyway, we asked for help around school, then eventually left and went out onto the streets.
Of course, I remained a spectator through the entire experience.
However, there was nothing I could even do, as their answers were always the same.
“Oh, it’s about the case? Sorry, but you have to ask him yourself, dude. See ya.”
This is the 12th person we’re asking. This might be impossible now.
No one even entertained talking to Shimizu; it was honestly so sad and disheartening to the point that I got bored and stared at the moon—ah, it was already night, wasn’t it?
I’m sure it’s already 7 pm; I mean, it’s quite dark, and we have school in the morning.
“Ito, how’s it going on the girls’ side?”
“I’ll ask them,” Ito brought out his phone to call one of the girls.
Ring, ring, ring.
“How’s it going on your end? Did anyone agree? … No one, huh? This is turning out to be a bit of a problem.”
“Or perhaps it’s just plain impossible?”
“...Huh?”
“Oh my, it seems I butted into a conversation I shouldn’t have; where are my manners? Wonderful weather we’re having today, isn’t it? So, how’s this little plan of yours going, Ito-kyun~?” A voice said, in quite a condescending tone. “No one agreed, huh? This really does seem more impossible, doesn’t it? Especially with the looming threat of the student council.”
Shimizu emerged from the darkness of an alleyway with a smirk.
He kinda looks like a villain.
“Shimizu-san… Can you—”
“Nah,” Shimizu cut him midsentence. “I’m just here to tell you how hopeless you are. You see, I’m a humble messenger, so I really should be on my way.”
“Can’t you just reconsider?”
“In turn, how about you just give up instead? Like, let’s be honest, Class Epsilon is finished already.”
“N-No, not—”
“Not yet? What do you have to even offer in return? The only things you have are Suzuki-chan and Kagura-chan as bargaining chips. Unless you have other options. Like, perhaps you are going to use this girl cosplaying as a guy?”
…?
Who’s he talking about?
…
..
.
…Is he referring to me?
“Hey femboy, are you male or genderfluid? I won’t judge you either way,” Shimizu grinned.
“…”
“Huh? Why aren’t you talking? Don’t tell me you’re afraid of me. Are you perhaps frightened by advances? Maybe you really are a maiden. Well, you look the part.”
Shimuzu’s trying to provoke me, huh?
I thought of simply ignoring him, but a certain thought came to mind.
Yeah, this will work.
“Hey—”
“Ito…” I whispered so only he could hear. I filled him in on my plan and what I needed him to do.
“Are you sure?” Ito asked.
“Yes,” I said with no hesitation.
“...This isn’t the best method, but fine, I’ll do what I can.”
“Hey, big oaf,” I turned my attention to Shimizu with a faint smile that was growing to a large grin.
“Huh?”
“You didn’t hear me? Not really surprised. I mean, your hearing’s gotta be impaired if your brain is smaller than a walnut.”
“What did you say?” Shimizu was fuming—his calm demeanour shattered like a wall of glass.
Frankly, he looked scary, but that didn’t matter; for now, all that mattered was my victory.
“Did I stutter? You talked about me being a femboy, right?”
“What if I did?”
“Well, at least I’m not a raging monkey.”
“…” He was really angry, so angry that his eyes were practically already killing me.
He’s falling for this amount of provocation; does he have any self-control?
Even elementary school students can shake this off quite easily.
But I should be happy that this is even working.
I thought that perhaps his anger was possibly an act, but the more I observed, the more I realised I was wrong.
He could probably allow insults from those on his level, but he couldn’t handle someone inferior insulting him.
I realised this because he got several insults since I had met him (well, since I saw him, not met him), and only the ones from people in my class pushed him over the edge.
“I mean, only monkeys will follow orders without any mind of their own,” I continued my verbal assault; my wide smile was on full display now.
“What did you say?”
“Oh, you think I didn’t notice that your class has a mastermind.”
“Huh?” Both Ito and Shimizu were surprised.
“W-what are you talking about?” Shimizu asked, his voice shaky.
“It’s so obvious. I mean, after all, when idiots like you are his lackeys, you can’t really hide anything.”
“How do you know that we have a mastermind?”
Well, you’re confirming it now, aren’t you?
“It’s quite obvious?” I framed it as a question, making a fool of him.
“How do you know?!”
“A little birdie told me?”
“Stop screwing with me, dammit!”
“Why do I know?”
“I don’t know. Tell me.”
“Maybe it’s because of you?”
“Huh? Just tell me, or I’m really dead now! How do you know, dammit?!” That calm demeanour was all but gone; he looked really ready to kill me.
This is part of the plan, but damn, does this guy wanna end my life or what?
I instinctively gulped at the sight, but I had to continue regardless.
“Haven’t I already said it? It’s because of you.”
“Huh?”
“It’s because of—”
“You’ve already said it; it’s my fault, but how is it my fault?”
“I know you’re an oaf that can’t think, but let me finish. All of this is simply because of your idiocy.”
“…” Shimizu said nothing, just glared at me like I committed an unforgivable crime against him—in a way, I did—and that was repeatedly reminding him of his stupidity.
I wasn’t sure if it was desperation or anger, but Shimizu ran to me and started hitting me.
He pushed me down to the ground and started wailing on me with hits, nothing fatal though, just some jabs on my face.
Even in anger, he showed some restraint.
“Shut up! Shut up! Shut up!”
“Hey, stop!” Ito ran to help me, getting Shimizu off me.
“You really are an idiot, aren’t you? You hit me,” I smirked.
“Huh?” Shimizu was surprised I could still give snarky comebacks, though he was intrigued by why I said that.
“You just lost the case, idiot,” I gave a condescending smile as I pointed at my head.
I said so because he just lost the leverage his class had; he sacrificed it for revenge over my petty insults.
He attacked me, nullifying the fact that we attacked them—I made sure it was for the same reason: petty insults.
Since the case began, I had a certain scientific law in my mind; it seemed like the solution to this case.
The law was none other than Newton’s Third Law of Motion.
Newton’s Third Law of Motion states, “For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.”
While the actions don’t cancel out, they can balance each other. The same could be said in this case.
I managed to create a way that the actions balanced each other, meaning they weren’t above us anymore—that is, Class Beta—but now we both had the same amount of leverage.
Corny, right? While I could admit that the idea of that was so corny that it could fill a whole barn with corn, it was still the plan that helped solve this case.
“Huh?” Shimizu was confused.
“To be honest, I didn’t know about a mastermind,” I said.
“What are you talking about? You said—”
“What I said doesn’t matter. I wasn’t sure, at least not until you confirmed it.”
“Shit, shit, shit,” he rambled.
“My advice is that you leave, like, right now. You don’t want to lose something else, do you?” I tapped his shoulder.
“…Fuck you,” he swore silently as he left.
As he left, Kagura emerged from the shadows wearing a perplexed look.
Though Ito didn’t focus on her and called the others, telling them about what happened, leaving out my provocation and instead telling them that he called him unintelligent, and I laughed, so that’s why he hit me.
“…So, since Shimizu-san hit Nakamura-kun, I think we’re in the clear.”
“What a coward,” a girl replied from the phone. “He probably didn’t hit you since he thought he’d lose.”
“Y-yeah.”
After ending the call, he changed his attention to me. Well, it was given, considering the weird request I asked of him.
“Nakamura-san, why did you give me that request?”
I requested that Ito do three things. Not to intervene, to call Kagura to watch and video what was to happen, and also to remove my provocation when explaining the situation to others.
I would have preferred that she didn’t have to be involved at all, but if wishes were horses, then even beggars would ride.
“Well, I just had an idea and went with it.”
“So that’s why you also said that I shouldn’t intervene. Anyway, thank goodness your plan worked.”
Kagura left silently as we were conversing. It was honestly creepy—for her, that is.
“Yeah.”
“Anyway, since you requested it, I also won’t tell anyone that you were involved. But may I ask why?”
“Well, I’d prefer not to answer.”
“Fine, understood.”
“Anyway, I’m kinda tired, so see ya, dude.”
“Yeah, see you tomorrow.”
With my plan being complete, I headed back to the dorms since it was already late.
Or so I wanted.
I saw Kagura on my way to the dorms; she was on a call, presumably with a friend.
“What? We need an extra person?!” She screamed. “Give me a sec; let me look for someone.”
She cut the call and started surveying the area, looking for someone to join her.
“Now, who to call? People from Class Beta know how to party, but with the case now, hmm…”
I kept walking, ignoring her until—
“Nakamura-kun?”
“Huh? Yeah?”
“Wanna go to a party?”
“Uh, not really, I’m actually pretty tired—”
“Don’t be a bore, just come along. At least this once.”
“Umm…”
“Pretty please,” she looked at me with upturned eyes, pushing her cleavage at me.
No one can blame me at this point; I really can’t resist this.
“…S-Sure.”
“Alright, then let’s go!” She held my hand and dragged me.
What did I just agree to?
