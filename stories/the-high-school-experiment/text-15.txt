Chapter 3: The Treacherous Train Ride
In way less time than expected, Kagura dragged me down to the train station. Her actions were confusing because, unfortunately, the map I memorised only covered the school area, which was about 99% of the map, leaving the rest unknown.
I assumed everything beyond that was uncharted and restricted; maybe it was the living quarters of the staff.
Though on closer inspection I recognised the place; it was the train station we used to enter school.
“Uh, Kagura, what are we doing here?”
Is she leading me outside of school?
“Oh, you didn’t know? Well, I suppose you’re still new to the school.”
“What do you mean?”
“I’ll explain later; let’s just get on the train first.”
I was still quite confused, but my curiosity made me comply. We got tickets and then got on the train. We were lucky because only 2 tickets were left for the train we wanted to get on.
{The train leaves in 1 minute. The next station is the west side of Elysia.}
The words from the announcer just further fuelled my confusion, but I stayed quiet until I got on the train.
On the train, I noticed a large number of adults. No, they were probably employees who worked at the stores in the school.
“So, Kagura, as you were saying.”
“Oh, right. So you see, the school is quite large.”
“Yeah, I know that much.”
“No, it’s larger than you’d expect—it’s ridiculously huge.”
“Uh huh.”
I have no idea what this girl is saying.
“Wait, give me a sec to think. I know Ito-kun said the size before. Umm… Aha, I remember. It’s 58.01 square kilometres,” she grinned as she remembered it.
“…Kagura, are you sure of what you’re saying?”
“Yup,” she nodded in affirmation.
58.01 square kilometres? Bruges in Belgium is only 0.3 square kilometres bigger.
The school was supposed to be 10 square kilometres in size, but this was more than double that number.
Why did the school hide that information?
What else are they hiding?
Does this relate to the point system?
How much money did the government put into this?
…Huh.
“I see.”
“You don’t seem surprised.”
Of course, I was shaken, but I didn’t show it because it didn’t really matter, at least not to me.
It doesn’t really matter to me if it’s big or not.
“I am. Actually, I’m so surprised that I can’t react.”
“That makes no sense.”
“So, when is the party going to end?”
“Don’t change the subject.”
“Why is the place we’re going called the West Side of Elysia?” I changed the subject.
“It’s because the campus is divided into four: north, south, east, and west. They are 0.01, 1, 40, and 7 square kilometres, respectively. The place at the centre is 10 square kilometres—that place is the main schooling area.”
“Ok.”
So then, the campus was divided into 4 regions; however, there was a pseudo-region that was used for school activities as well as the dorms, a mall, and a convenience store.
Ahh, that explained why it took 10 minutes to arrive at the school’s pseudo-region, as the train was quite fast.
I mean, if the train were as fast as advertised, it shouldn’t have taken 10 minutes to enter the school unless we were heading to the centre of a school bigger than 10 square kilometres.
“The thing about the four regions is that only the west side can be used by students.”
“Why is that?”
“I don’t know.”
Hmm. If I were to guess, those other regions were used as recreation and as living quarters for staff members only, but why were the sizes so different?
What are they—
“Anyway, it’s only now I noticed how childlike you are,” Kagura broke my reverie.
I winced at her comment; however, I was astonished at how she said such a comment without any remorse.
“I mean, you’re not that short, but your face looks so cute!”
As if on command, every head in the train carriage snapped toward me the moment Kagura spoke.
The women in the carriage came and started pulling my cheeks; I was being assaulted.
“Pwey wet gwo.” (Hey, let go.)
Some women went after my arms, and others started feeling me up.
“You look average, but you’re actually hiding some muscle, huh?”
“He looks just like my grandson who is 7 years old.”
Heugh.
Double standards: if a guy were to do this to a girl, it would be social suicide.
As the women gathered around me, I noticed something unusual: Kagura was looking at me.
Not in the expected way; it wasn’t a stare of satisfaction. It felt like she was observing me.
Is she probing me?
Strange.
I thought she was on the dumber side. Maybe it was just imagination, or because of what I did today.
Needless to say, I was exasperated. Let’s just say it was a long train ride.
On leaving the train, I felt a very warranted freedom.
“That was the most fun I’ve had in weeks!” One of the women on the older side grinned.
“Yeah, I hope we see him again soon.”
“Bye, cute boy!” A middle-aged lady blew me a kiss.
“Heugh,” I sighed.
“Where are we going now?” I said, directed at Kagura.
“Just follow me~.”
“I hope you don’t lead me to a place where my face will be assaulted again.”
“No promises,” she grinned.
“Huh? You’re joking, right? Right?”
“Who knows?”
Scary.
