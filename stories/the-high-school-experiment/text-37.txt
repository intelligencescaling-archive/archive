Chapter 9.5: Double Dates Are Battlegrounds
Ring, Ring, Ring!
My phone rang loudly while I was in my shower, getting ready for a special occasion.
“Who could that be?”
I wanted to ignore it, but the person kept calling and calling. I eventually grew exasperated by hearing the same ringtone—the standard phone ringtone.
After getting the phone on the first day of school, I didn’t attempt to customise anything.
In fact, my account name was still Nakamura Eiya—unchanged from when I got it.
After hearing it for the sixth time, I rushed out of the bath and picked it up without looking at the caller ID.
“Hello.”
“Eiya! Are you awake?”
It was a Saturday, so most students woke up later than usual, but I still woke up by 7 AM, as I did on other days. Well, most days; the exceptions were the few times I woke up late.
Moreover, today was a special day, so I was inclined to wake up early. I mean, I had a date with Aiya.
Yeah, I had gone on dates with her every day since we began dating on Thursday (this was going to be our third date), and today was one of such dates.
Thankfully, this was planned before, unlike most of our other dates, so I could prepare. Well, planned is a stretch, though. After I went to bed yesterday, Aiya called me to tell me that we would have a date today.
“Yeah, I’ll even be on my way soon.”
“Okay, I was just making sure. I mean, this is the first time we’re having a planned date,” she said.
“Mhm. So, the time is still by 12, right?”
“I thought you said you’d be done soon.”
“Yes, I will. I’ll even be on my way like I said.”
“Then why’d you ask for the time to meet up?”
“I was just making sure of when you’ll come.”
“I’m already on my way.”
“Oh.”
“Yeah, anyway, see you later.”
With our call done, I continued my shower and finished shortly after. I looked at my phone and then got a sudden message from my account—not Koda, but MUFG banking.
Tokyo Metropolitan Elysian High School, or EHS for short, supposedly had a partnership with that bank, so it was used for transactions.
On my banking app, I found out that I had received an extra whopping 100,000 Rp.
It wasn’t from a friend or anything—it was from the school. While the how and why were important, I couldn’t help but be ecstatic at my new balance.
“Yes!!! A whole hundred thousand, really?!” I screamed. “At least now I’m not on a tightrope.”
Since Aiya and I started dating, my balance—which was already low because of Innō—suffered greatly. It wasn’t that we dated for a while or that Aiya ran me dry of my wallet; it was simply because of Innō.
I had only 6,000 Rp left, and this was only my second week at EHS. I didn’t tell Aiya how broke I was, but luckily, I didn’t have to because we paid differently.
“But now things have turned around; I have 106,000 Rp.”
I eagerly put on the clothes that I had set out the day before and went to the dormitory lobby. I saw Aiya waiting for me with a smile.
“Hey, Eiya,” she waved.
“Hey,” I grinned. “Ready to go?”
“Yeah, let me just check if I have everything,” she rummaged through her bag, then stopped abruptly.
“Did you forget something?”
“Yeah, my mirror. Give me a second; let me go get it,” she went to the elevator, then turned to look at me. “Sorry for this,” she then left before I could say anything.
Since we weren’t late on our schedule, there wasn’t a real reason for her to apologise. However, I wondered why she didn’t just check before I came.
Maybe she was in a rush.
Satisfied with that answer, I sat down and went on my phone—there wasn’t anyone around in the lobby, maybe because it was Saturday.
I aimlessly scrolled through YouTube shorts till I heard a voice.
“Nakamura-kun?”
I looked up and saw Innō—he was in casual clothing and was rather surprised to see me, as was I.
“Huh? What are you doing here?” I asked.
Then again, it isn’t weird to see him here. I mean, this is the lobby.
“I’m waiting for Yoshida-chan; we’re going on a date. What about you?”
“I’m going on a date with Aiya.”
Wait a minute.
I could feel the gears turn in my head; I had a feeling that something was wrong, but as I was about to find out what, I was disturbed by a loud noise.
“Heyo!” Yoshida greeted us loudly. Luckily, no one was in the lobby.
She and Aiya came from the elevator and walked together to meet Innō and me.
“Hey Innō-kun~,” Yoshida said in a cutesy tone, then turned over to me.
“What a surprise,” she said, despite her face being devoid of anything remotely similar to that word. “You’re here too, Nakamura-kun. What’s the occasion?”
“Aiya and I have a date to go to,” I replied.
“Really? Innō and I are also going on a date.”
“I see, where to?”
“The mall.”
“Oh, funny, us too.”
“Talk about a coincidence, right?”
“Since we’re all going to the same place, why don’t we just go together?” Aiya asked.
“Wait, like a double date, right?” Innō asked.
“Exactly,” Aiya answered.
“Well, I’m down if Yoshida’s down.”
“Sure, I’m down. It could be fun.”
“And I’m down too. What about you, Eiya?” Aiya asked.
Considering the fact that everyone agreed, I can’t just say no. Moreover, I also want to experience a double date—especially since I only know the general idea.
“Sure, let’s go.”
With that out of the way, we headed to the mall; however, something weird happened. As we were walking, I saw Innō stare deeply at me—no, rather, he glared.
I didn’t do anything wrong, so I threw a perplexed look, and then he realised that he was misunderstood, so he sent me a message.
[Innō-shin: Dude, when people go on double dates, they duke it out to see who’s the better boyfriend. Just know I don’t plan on losing; also, the girls must absolutely never know. If they do, then we both lose.]
So that’s why he looked at me like that—at any rate, this boyfriend battle or whatever is stupid, especially since the girls don’t know about it.
Though I have read something like this before in some novels, so it’s probably best to act accordingly.
After preparing my mind, I glared back at Innō, signalling that we could begin our challenge. I planned to give it my all—I happened to learn tea ceremony and Omotenashi at a young age, so offering lip service would prove to be easy.
