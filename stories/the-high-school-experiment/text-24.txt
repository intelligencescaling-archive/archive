Chapter 7: Dude, Help Me Get a Girlfriend!
I was walking to the dormitory, deep in a reverie, still thinking about what happened in Jackson-sensei’s faculty room.
I made an absolute resolution and planned on following it to a tee without exception. Well, at least that was what I thought as I used a pathway behind the school.
I didn’t wanna run into someone, so I could spend more time thinking—that was the idea, at least.
I happened to conveniently forget that people who didn’t want to be seen by others would also use this pathway. I remembered this when I suddenly heard someone shout something.
“Uh… um… I like you. Please be my girlfriend!”
Huh? What’s going on? A confession?
I walked closer; rather, I sneakily walked there, as I didn’t want to be found.
‘Eavesdropping is bad.’
I knew that, but unfortunately, my curiosity got the best of me.
“Umm… This is really sweet, but Innō, I can only think of you as a friend.”
Innō?
I thought someone like him would focus solely on the sport he played; at least that was my impression of Innō.
Well, he was kinda friendly with Suzuki and normally came to my desk during break just to talk to her, but I thought he was just being friendly because she was pretty cute; besides, she wasn’t the one he was confessing to right now.
“I see…” His mood became visibly sour.
“I’m sorry, it’s just…”
“N-Nagumo-chan… It’s fine.”
“I’m really sorry, I just… I’m sorry!” Nagumo ran away.
She was unable to say anything and ran away in an attempt not to complicate matters. In that way, she was a better person than I; I would have probably just been silent, unable to do anything.
I couldn’t imagine—
“Damn, so that’s it, huh?” Innō fell on his knees, watching as Nagumo left.
Anyway, with the confession finished and my curiosity satisfied, I was about to leave through the forest, but I stepped on a branch.
Shit, shit, shit—wait, what if he didn’t hear me?
“Come out, I heard you.”
Yeah, what was I thinking?
I felt too nervous to move, so I just stayed put for a while, but after realising that staying here would achieve nothing, I reluctantly stepped out with a nervous grin.
“Y-Yo—”
“Don’t ‘yo’ me. What are you doing here?”
“I was uh…just lost. I’m still new, y’know, so I sometimes get lost.”
“Yeah, sure. That makes perfect sense,” he seemed to believe my lie. “Like I’d believe that!”
Of course.
I sighed internally.
“I’m really sorry, okay? I really didn’t mean to eavesdrop. As repayment, w-w-why don’t I buy you a meal?”
His face darkened for a sec, and I was admittedly a little scared, but then he grinned.
Normally, it would have calmed me down, but it wasn’t a normal grin. It wasn’t innocent, like at all; it was the one that was menacing, very menacing.
He looks like Light Yagami.
I had a really bad feeling about what was about to happen next.
“Fine, but only if I can order as much as I want.”
“Sure.”
It seemed I was worried over nothing. Note that the keyword is “seemed”.
“…and also, the lobster special.”
“Sir, that’s the whole menu.”
This guy ordered the whole goddamn menu!
“Don’t worry, I’m not the one paying; he is,” Innō pointed at me with a confident smile.
“Sir, are you sure?” The waiter was very concerned, looking at me as if this were some sort of extortion scheme—he slid a small paper to me.
It read, “Is he forcing you to pay for all this?”
“Don’t…worry…I…can…handle…it,” I barely managed to say.
“But sir, you have to pay 50,000 Rp! If this boy is extorting lemme call the—”
“Relax, it’s my decision,” I managed to squeeze out.
“A-are you sure?”
“It’s… Fine,” I squeezed out.
“I see; then let me bring out the QR code,” he bowed at both of us and subsequently brought it out.
“I apologise for falsely accusing you of extortion,” the waiter bowed deeply at Innō. He wasn’t completely sure Innō wasn’t extorting me (which he technically was), but he chose to believe him for now.
“Relax, it’s honestly not that serious,” Innō answered without missing a beat.
At any rate, I reluctantly paid for the food without a hitch.
So… now, I only have 10,000 Rp left.
The thought made me silently rub at my temple.
After ordering the food, I just watched Innō eat through everything. Honestly, it was amazing how he devoured everything without a care in the world.
No, what was amazing was how fast he finished my Rp. I didn’t know how I would survive like this till they gave me more Rp.
“50 thousand,” I whispered.
“Did you say something?” Innō asked.
“Dude, I know I eavesdropped, but aren’t you pushing it?”
“You should have said something while ordering cuz this place doesn’t accept refunds.”
I suppose I should have said something before he finished everything, but here we were.
Maybe I should have said that Innō was extorting me.
“Plus, you’re still new, so I bet you have a lot of money to spare,” he added.
“That doesn’t mean I have infinite money.”
“Hey, you’re the one who said I can order as much as I want.”
“If you had good etiquette, then you’d know that you should politely decline or only order one meal, a cheap one at that.”
“Etiquette? That’s rich coming from you. Who eavesdrops when someone is confessing?”
“That’s…”
He got me there.
“That’s what?”
“I’m sorry.”
“It’s ok…”
…
..
.
“Uh, aren’t you going to apologise too?” I asked.
“Damn, I’m sorry for ordering the whole menu, ok? I just got my heart torn apart, so I’m still recovering.”
And you’re taking that out on me?
“…It’s fine; I can survive with what I have.”
“Oh, that’s good; everything’s fixed then,” he continued, happily sipping away at one of his drinks.
…Is this guy serious?!
“...Won’t you at least try to pay some back?”
“Why would I?”
“Didn’t your mom teach manners?”
“…Dude, I need a girlfriend,” he said, staring at the window.
He changed the subject? Why though…?
“You wanna try and confess again to Nagumo?”
“Nope, won’t work. I need to find a new girl.”
I was surprised at what he said and made sure he could see it on my face.
“What?” he asked.
“How did you move on so fast? Didn’t you love Nagumo?”
“I did, but she rejected me, so I can’t do much about that, can I?”
“Well, I suppose you’re technically right, but...”
“So I need to find a new girl, and you’re going to help me with that.”
“Uh, when did I agree to that?”
“Come on, it won’t be that hard. Just find a cute girl.”
“Oh, so that’s all you care about.”
“Hey, don’t you dare make me look bad. I mean, you’re a guy, so you should understand where I’m coming from.”
“Yeah, but looks aren’t everything.”
“Yeah, sure. That’s what everyone says, yet they never date the ugly girl.”
“That’s cuz…”
Damn, I don’t have an answer to that, because I want a cute girlfriend too… Maybe he’s right; no, girls shouldn’t be objectified.
Yeah, but I can’t argue that I want a cute girlfriend, but that doesn’t mean I want to date a crazy person, so personality does matter.
But what type of personality do I even like?
…
Doesn’t matter. That type of question can be answered later.
What I should be focusing on now is whether I should help Innō. I mean, so he doesn’t end up dating someone crazy, and maybe this could be a learning experience to find out what type of girl I like.
Regarding his focus on a girl’s appearance, perhaps something can be done to fix that.
…So it seems, despite his wrongdoing, I’m still helping him, huh?
“Heugh,” I sighed. “Fine, I’ll help you.”
“Thank you, thank you, thank you,” he shook my hands vigorously.
“You’re welcome,” I grinned nervously.
“Well, thanks for the food and for listening. Here’s my acc. It’s Innō-shin.”
Innō-shin…? What does that even mean? Oh, right, it’s probably his nickname.
“...You’re giving me your account name?”
“Yeah, also, thanks again. You’re a real good friend. Anyway, see ya,” he stood and left.
For some seconds, I was actually shocked at his words, so shocked that I practically couldn’t move. He called me a friend. I expected him to say I was a good guy, but a friend? Well, that definitely went past my expectations.
I suppose I should go too.
At any rate, I stretched a bit and promptly walked back to the dormitory, thinking about what Innō said.
He’s really interested in getting a girlfriend. I wonder why; maybe it’s related to his mom.
But what’s so special about girlfriends anyway? I know lovers are different from just normal friends.
They go on dates, do things together, hold hands, kiss—and even—
I instinctively blushed; the thought of something like that had suddenly made me embarrassed. It was the curse of being a teenager, I guess.
Anyway, I started to think about who could be good for Innō. I couldn’t just recommend a cute girl for him.
Despite my plan to stay bland, I had to find someone who could fit someone like him. Someone who would be suitable for an impulsive, funny, annoying athlete. It would prove to be a heavy task.
But that’s what you do for your friends, right?
