Chapter 3.2: The Kind of Person I Am
After I fell asleep, I could still hear noises from the drinking game, and it went on for quite a while. I wasn’t exactly sure for how long, though, because I entered REM sleep.
I didn’t have a dream, though; it was just a quiet, peaceful slumber, and after some time, I just woke up, feeling the urge to go back to my room.
“Where am I?” I woke up abruptly with some of my dishevelled hair covering my left eye. “Oh, right.”
After waking up, I stood up to stretch a bit and to check if I was still with my phone.
Nice.
I was still with my valuables, so I calmed down a bit. As I checked my surroundings, I found that all the girls were slumped on the floor and were fast asleep.
“Probably because of the alcohol.”
I pulled my phone out of my pocket and used it to check the time, and it was 10 PM.
“Heugh,” I sighed. “Just my luck; we’re already past curfew.”
I dropped my phone on a table close to me as I thought about what to do.
The right thing to do was to wake everyone up, but I considered not waking up Kagura’s friends.
Especially the one who wanted to force me to drink. I never wanted to drink alcohol, like, ever. Like, why would I take poison that not only tastes bad but also affects my liver and brain?
That being said, I abandoned such a thought and lightly shook Kagura’s friends to wake them up, and then I carried Kagura out of the karaoke room and went to a store nearby so I could get rid of the smell of alcohol.
It was more than likely that we would get punished for drinking alcohol, so I had to purchase perfume.
Though finding a store was easier said than done, the closest one was at least a 7-minute walk from the karaoke place. Anyway, after entering the store and finding the cheapest perfume, I went to the counter.
“That will be 5,000 Rp.”
“Alright, let me bring out my phone,” I checked my pocket and found I had left my phone back at the karaoke room.
“Sorry, could I pay you tomorrow? I left my phone.”
“That won’t be possible, sir.”
I could try to hurry back to the karaoke room and get it, but that could possibly take more time.
“Please, I promise I’ll be back as fast as possible.”
“Unfortunately, it’s not up to me.”
“I see,” I accepted my fate and was about to leave when—
Someone paid for me, handed the perfume to me, and left.
“Uh, thank you.”
Before I could finish appreciating the person, they left, and I didn’t see their face, though their retreating back looked a bit familiar.
Who’s that? Well, I suppose it’s none of my business if they want to stay anonymous.
After heavily spraying the perfume on Kagura and me, I went to get my phone from the karaoke room and headed to the train station.
When I arrived at the train station, I saw a sign that said I couldn’t use the trains, as they had stopped operating by 9 PM.
It seemed I had to go to the dorms on foot. I checked the time and saw it was already 10:10 PM.
I needed to hurry since we had to be in our rooms by 10 PM; the distance was over 4 square kilometres, and to top it all off, it was raining.
It was truly a detestable situation. Still, I carried her back to the dorms.
The ground was muddy, so I had to stay balanced while keeping my movements nimble.
I kept running as the rain poured into my eyes—because that was the kind of person I was.
…I guess.
When I reached the dorms, I checked my phone—it was already 10:25 PM.
Damn, I should have run faster.
I tried to wake Kagura up by shaking her, but she didn’t wake up.
Hmm.
Since I couldn’t waste any more time, I headed to the front desk to get some information.
“Good evening, ma’am,” I said.
“Huh? Why are you still out, and why are you soaked?” she asked, clearly concerned.
“It was raining, and we were coming from the west side.”
“The west side? How did you get here so fast, especially when carrying someone?”
“Oh, we left a while ago. We walked together until a teacher brought us to the dorm gate, saying he wasn’t allowed to escort us inside. As for why I’m carrying her, she said she was tired, so I carried her,” I lied.
“Which teacher brought you here?”
“I don’t know his name.”
“Ok, but why come to the front desk?”
“I don’t know her room.”
“I see. Give me a second to check. What’s her name?”
“Kagurazaka Akane.”
“Alright,” she checked her records to find the name. “It says here that she’s in room 312.”
“Thank you,” I bowed and left; however, as I reached the door, a few words from the miss stopped me in my tracks.
“Nakamura-kun, I’m not saying I don’t trust you, but I do hope you’re not doing anything bad or illegal.”
I didn’t stop because her question shocked me; rather, it would be very weird and unnatural if I walked away as I talked—it would appear as if I were hiding something.
I suppose underage drinking is illegal, though I doubt she would notice since I sprayed perfume, and the rain also washed away the smell.
“Don’t worry, I’m not, and thanks again,” I left the front desk office and went to the lobby.
I walked into the elevator and then pressed the button for the third floor.
“Hey, Kagura, you can stop faking.”
“…How’d you know?”
“Your breathing changed and became uneven when we entered the front desk office.”
“Creepy. Also, why’d you lie to her?”
“We came late; that’s why.”
“Ok then, but why did you say it was a male teacher?"
“Who is more liable to stay out long and pick kids?”
“…Uh, girls?”
“No, it’s more practical to say males. Typically, a female teacher could be partying, trying to ‘re-enact her youth’, while a man just sobering up from drinking wouldn’t want to do anything more than go home—especially middle-aged men. Well, nothing is exactly set in stone though.”
“But why a teacher?”
“I doubt administrative staff will still be out and about. Also, most employees working a 9-5 can’t afford a car.”
“Wow, you really think things through. You’re really smart.”
“I think being smart is a stretch; I just like to be cautious. Also, get down.”
“No, can’t I just stay a little longer?” she pouted.
“Fine,” I sighed. “It’s ok, I guess.”
“Thanks. You’re a wonderful friend, you know that, right? I’m really sorry, though. Damn, this is why I hate drinking,” she said before falling asleep again.
Hate drinking? How can you hate drinking when you chugged down so much? Perhaps she hates drinking when people are around, but who would take care of her when she’s drunk?
I suppose it’s not my business.
On reaching her floor, I used her phone to enter her room. I had seen her unlock her phone before when we walked to the karaoke place, so unlocking it was quite easy.
I sneakily entered her room so no one would see me; it would be weird seeing someone use a sleeping person’s phone to enter their room.
Her room had a red theme and looked cute overall, yet it had an air of maturity.
I shouldn’t stay here too long.
After tucking her in, I left for my room.
You called me a wonderful friend, huh? I suppose that makes you my friend number one.
I can’t believe I made one; I’m so happy!
I grinned as I headed to my room. I was thinking about today and how it was overall a great day, with minor problems, especially the one with Hayashi.
On reaching my room, I noticed how all the burn marks weren't there anymore and saw a note saying they didn’t subtract Rp since it was their mistake, but would subtract if it happened again.
So it wasn’t free. Then again, if it were, it would encourage students to vandalise their rooms.
After keeping the fire extinguisher close to my kitchen, I used the room's landline to order dinner. After eating, I went to take a bath.
After all that, I lay on my bed thinking about the day, reviewing what I did and how it turned out—then I turned my attention back to the matter at hand, the case with Class Beta.
I always suspected Class Beta had a mastermind due to the way their plan occurred, but I had no proof, not until I stepped into their classroom.
It was way too perfect. It wasn’t disciplined but controlled. It felt more like tyranny than order; well, at least that’s what I could deduce from being there for less than 10 minutes.
Then there was the girl. The way she ran to greet us, and also the way she winced when she sat down.
She had been hurt by someone.
But not from bullying—girls don’t turn on each other this fast; it would at least take a few weeks, maybe 3-4.
And even if they did, they won’t do something like that. That kind of injury comes from resisting someone with real power—perhaps they didn’t even have to do it themselves.
A mastermind that wasn’t respected but feared. She had been punished for something, and now she was playing her role as the one who greets guests.
That’s why I pushed Shimizu.
I didn’t know for sure there was a mastermind.
But now, thanks to his reaction, I do.
However…
