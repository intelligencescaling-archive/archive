# Premise

There is a man in society who, despite his wit and cunning, never exploits or deceives people for his own benefit. Any swindler would wish to have his skills, because with his talent they could become the richest and most powerful person, controlling everyone with an iron fist.

But unlike them, this man has strong values that cannot be shaken by money or power. In a society filled with con artists, where lies are told so often that people begin to believe them, he stands for truth and trust.

That man is Akiyama Shinichi-the empathetic liar, the breaker of exploitation, the chaotic strategist, and the boogeyman of con men and swindlers. No amount of power or money could make him abandon his principles. He believes in empathy backed by strength, critical thinking over blind faith, and helping victims of scams, MLMs, and fraud, even if it means dismantling the entire system.

Currently, Akiyama has infiltrated ANHS, an institution built upon a white lie that lures talented youth only to break them. But that is not all.

There is a conspiracy.

ANHS is not only a playground where students prey upon their peers, but also a battleground for adults, where they test and introduce their ideologies to these young minds, shaping the next generation through the students themselves.

Only Akiyama can uncover whether this conspiracy is real or not. If it is, he intends to dismantle it before it can do any further harm. To infiltrate the school, Akiyama assumes a false identity, using another person's credentials to secure a position as the homeroom teacher of Class C, allowing him to directly influence the students while uncovering the secrets hidden within ANHS's administration and board.
