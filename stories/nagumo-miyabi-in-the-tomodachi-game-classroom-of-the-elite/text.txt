# Premise

One day, five of the brightest students found themselves forced to take part in a strange underground game called the Tomodachi Game ("Friends Game"), where friendships, trust, and love are tested through debt and betrayal. Although many people have heard of it, no one has ever been able to prove that the Tomodachi Game really exists.

For four of them, calling each other friends would be a stretch. They were simply classmates who knew each other, not people connected by trust or shared interests. Only Nagumo Miyabi managed to calm himself almost immediately after arriving.

All five participants were students of Advanced Nurturing High School, a prestigious school known for producing some of Japan's brightest and most talented students.

According to the robot host, Friend-san, people usually join the Tomodachi Game for one of two reasons: to betray someone, or to challenge the urban legend for the thrill of it.

It seemed this group belonged to the first category.

**Rules:**

* During each round, every participant submits **"Backbiting Cards"** exposing another participant's secrets. Players can backbite multiple people at once, or simply hand in a blank card.
* To write and submit their cards, participants enter the **Kageguchi Box** one by one. The door locks behind them, and they are not allowed to leave until three minutes have passed.
* After everyone finishes, the contents of the cards are announced to the audience. The audience then votes on which secret is the most unforgivable.
* The participant whose secret ranks **1st** moves **5 steps** forward, **2nd** moves **4 steps**, **3rd** moves **3 steps**, **4th** moves **2 steps**, and **5th** moves **1 step**. After everyone advances, the round ends.
* The spaces on the board contain either **penalties**, **rewards**, or **Special Chance** events. Participants continue accumulating debt until the first person reaches the end, ending the game.
* If everyone submits a blank card, the audience vote is skipped, and all participants simply move forward **1 space**.
* If a lie is written on a Backbiting Card and conclusive proof is presented that the accusation is false, the liar is immediately moved in front of the participant closest to the finish line.
