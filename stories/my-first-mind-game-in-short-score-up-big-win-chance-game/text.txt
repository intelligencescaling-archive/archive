My first mindgame in short

Over a year ago I started my mind game novel, and now I revisited my first one and found and error or two in it - so now I fixed it and while Im at it I thought I could also share it with you guys and see what you think about it!

The name of my novel is Big Win Chance Game - I tried to describe and explain the whole game in detail while not to write too much either

**Game name**: Score Up!

**Place**: A lobby with a few rooms for everyone to live until the game lasts - every player has their own room

**Rules**: 4 player 2 teams - Team A and Team B No one knows which team are they in or who are their teammates are 1 deadline everyday until the game ends at 4PM The players can choose between 2 options (cards) every deadline: +1 or +3 These cards can be submitted to the referee any time of the day The cards contains a QR code (to identify whose card it is), the name of the game on it (Score Up!), Which round does it belong to? (A card that is belonging to round 3 can only be used in round 3) and whether it is a +1 or a +3 card The game either ends after 12 rounds or when one of the teams ends up in an unwinnable situation If you play: +1 - The team you are in is guaranteed to gain 1 point +3 - The team you are in gains 3 points, but only if you teammate did not use a +3 card as well (then its 0 points) Whichever team ends up with the more points is the winners

**Players**:
Greg (Protagonist)
Lucas
Tomi
Samuel

**Round 1:**
Tomis trick: Tomi meets with all the players separately so no one knows Tomi is planning to try this with everyone -> "I will play +3, you will play +1" -> If they are in the same team, they gain +4 points, if not, they gain information, giving an advantage .

If they agree, his team will gain 4 points, but the opponent team will gain nothing, also he will find out which team he is in, though this would not be enough information to figure out who his teammate is

**result - Round 1**:
Team A ---> 4
Team B ---> 4

**Explanation of round 1**:
Greg recognized Tomi is onto something. He said he will play +3 to Tomi, but actually, he played a +1

With this (the results), he realised his theory might be true (of asking everyone to use a +3), as well as he cannot be in Tomi's team because then one of the teams would have gained 2 points and the other should have nothing

Tomi is also visibly frustrated at the results, because his plan got betrayed by someone and now he dont know who was it

**Round 2:**
Tomi meet up with Lucas and Samuel (separatedly) - to find out who is the betrayer he asked for a meeting at the referee at a late (and diffenet) appointment
He also asked Greg, but he DENIED
Tomi suspects that Greg is most probably the betrayer, but he goas along this anyway to make sure

At that point of time, Tomi already knows, that the cards can be asked back from the referee after they are given to them if the round still lasts, and then play either cards any time of the day again

Just like Tomi, Greg also looked for someone to meet up - while making sure Tomi is not around. He found Lucas, and got his theory proven about Tomis trick

Greg then asked Lucas, to switch their cards that belongs to round 2.
Since both of them have the same information (Lucas dont know that Greg knows that he is not with Tomi and that he is the betrayer) + this distorts information for the other players while they maintain the clear picture - He agrees
Then Greg meets with Samuel as well, and he makes the same offer, and he agrees as well - but he dont know, that Greg already switched with Lucas

**Greg has Samuels cards**
**Samuel has Lucas cards**
**Lucas has Gregs cards**

**What Tomi saw, at the meetings:**
Lucas and Samuel both played +3
Tomi played +1

**Results of round 2:**
Team A ---> 8
Team B ---> 8

Tomi knows for certainty that Greg is the betrayer now + They are (Greg and Tomi) is not in the same team

**What Greg sees from this:**
Tomi played +1
Lucas played +3
Greg played +3
Samuel played +1

Since both teams once again gained 4 points, and Greg already knows he cannot be in the same team as Tomi, the only other player who used a +1 is Samuel, so gaining 4 points is only possible if Samuel is his teammate

**Round 3:**
Greg is Confident already, he found out his teammate earlier than Tomi, but then something independent happens.
Lucas gave up on Tomi's plan - he told his his tired of his "master plan" while the game still stands tie without much information gained at all - then he goes for Greg, wants to team with him, to find out what Greg figured out from the last round.
Greg gives him his +1 card and tells his to play it when Tomi is around - and also dont use any of his own cards.

The rules are not stopping you from doing that - Simply nothing should happen

Greg told Samuel he knows they are in the same team - but as Greg explains how he figured it out, he starts doubting Greg - because what if he already switched cards with Lucas or will do something he didnt know about?
But the Greg says - "Oh, I dont want to switch cards now"
Greg says Samuel should play his +3 when meeting with Tomi the next time

Early in the morning then, Tomi sees Lucas walk away from the referee, where they can use their cards - just like how Greg told him to.

**Results of Round 3:**
Team A ---> 12
Team B ---> 9

Tomi notices someone had to not use a single card since one team gained 4, the other only 1, which is not possible if both players in a team played one of their cards.
Since Tomi knows he is not with Greg, and he saw Samuel play +3, saw Lucas play something, but not Greg -

**Tomis resasoning:**
He already knows he is not with Greg. Someone did not play a card. Tomi and Samuel also played their cards, saw Lucas at the referee. It has to be Greg who skipped this round. 1 team gained 4 points and the other gained only 1, so both team gained at least 1 points. If Lucas would be his teammate, then he no team could gain only 1, but either 2 or 4.
The only case that satisfies everything from Tomis perspective if his teammate is Samuel.

**While in reality:**
Tomi and lucas is in the same team.
Tomi played a +1 and Lucas played nothing.

Greg and Samuel is in the same team.

From that moment on, Greg and Samuel switches cards for every round, keeping Tomi decieved for the whole game, keep him thinking he is winning while he actually is losing.
Greg kept playing +1 cards with Samuels cards and Samuel kept Playing +3 with Gregs cards.

So in short, Greg made Tomi believe he is winning in order to make his stop try outsmarting him - when someone this they are winning, they let their guards down.
