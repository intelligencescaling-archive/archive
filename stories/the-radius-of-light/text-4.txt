Fanfic Of the Sub, PT 3: Radius Of Light

# THE RADIUS OF LIGHT

# EPISODE 3; 1973.SEE,WORLD

~~————————————————————————————————————————~~

"Mr. Disintegrator," a man without sterness says. He is not particular. He is made of particulars but conceals them. He is, in truth, a concealed man.

Concealing his nature is not intented. He is merely acting as demanded by himself. To conceal is, in a style of maxim, a form of living. The concealed man is neither person or thing; he is neither the usual acts of being a person: creator, created, torturer, tortured.

"I'm just passing through for the day. John, is, regrettably, sick. He is out. He moves like any good officer, too good. Competence is not my strong suit," the man says.

"Mr. LightingBench," Mr. Disintegrator speaks. "Did I ever bother you enough that you decided to visit me?"

"No. I've been bothered by Blicero,"

"TrueGamer."

"No. AndyTeasey. He isn't a Blicero member, but he is an officer. I'm willfully being ignored and ignorant here, Disintegrator. Cutting any possible chase, I'm saying that the only reason I'm here is because I am here. Otherwise, any other feasible logic to my visit is up to you." LightningBench is not sitting comfortably. He does not sit often. He sits only when alone, so this is a courtesy. He sits not when he is lonely. Over the window's edge, some light strains itself to only be dimmed by the muteness of the room. It is an after effect of the incessant experimenting. Lightningmaster7, ContinentalHater, and ConversationSea are busy. They work to fix any application to the weapon's launch. They have find nothing, and haven't been found by anything yet.

...

Finding anything, it makes someone a dedication stone. Everything is going to be found with the consideration of the first discovery: the first discovery is immortalized with it's finder. It hasn't been 'made'. These days, everything is found, made, and done so to be understood by anything and anyone who doesn't have a degree in bogusness. Yet, less finding, more discovering: fear only comes with discovery, because with it, joy is also found. To find joy and fear in one, we find the unnamable and give them names, even if they can't fit: as said, we're creator and created: torturer and tortured, with names, with clothes. No one is naked until the degrading names come. Again, it's not pleasant to work about without a name.

Only violence can go nameless and still be understood, because it is pain. And the most potent emotions and feelings usually are nameless. Suffering is only the product of our streamlined knowledge of ourselves and everything, which in itself amounts to only a fraction of infinity.

...

1930, June, or July, or August. Some summer. The weather didn't last. It starts pouring. Someone gets married and has some kid. The kid, his name NoMarionBerry, is quick. He speaks before he is a few months old. His words articulte. He can't speak until he is spoken to. If not, he babbles like any baby. Only when interacted does he become an anomaly. So everyone around him, more or less, is in silence. He is normal in silence. He is a map. He remains a map. To close this map, he is issued the usual degrees of education: if his map remains open he'll sink as a ship does in storm. Now everyone wants his map open. Not that they say it. It is unsaid. It is made of particulars, and is concealed.

Maps! Cartography is the study of not only geographic information but the synthesis with information based on our knowledge of both the former and a latter thing, described as the attempt to determine the place and position of a person in regards to a vast multitude of different needs, moving, still parts and situations. Mostly, map reading will be in decline, but now not so. Maps are a visual marker of what we know, and older ones, made of some guesses passed off as information. Whenever information is passed up as truth, usually someone wants to debunk it. To defend it. Or to do nothing with it. Not a whole lot is known, it's only remembered, mixed up, inaccurate, unless some time is devoted to knowing. Even knowing isn't enough. Understanding and knowing are different.

Knowing the map will lead only to two trunks of stone in sand: understanding, that will leave the visage too.

Either way the form is still broken, unknown as it used to be.

Even if it has been, it's going and gone: if it's still with us, someone can't let go. If the departed are not linked to people, they are linked to something else: and again, they are linked to themselves mostly...self contained memories, keeping themselves alive, while history moves on indiscriminately.

It's the same nonetheless: everything that is so contained by itself is everywhere...some inherent ancientness that predates us now making us the chief apparatus of itself: if cruelty is hailed as some special gift, we're sorely wrong in denying nature the distinction; consciousness, so it seems, has separated us and somehow kept us binded to wildness. Now transporting these kinds of things, complete with their usual taxes, to the enemy, is not known, but it is there: into the void we make fear, and out of it here comes the industry to destroy the void and leave us with, again, void, simply pummeled and somehow won over.

Someone is going to be responsible for this in the next decade or so: not now. Then again, maybe not ever. Maybe literally three days after launch too. Who knows. What functions and doesn't function flip flops around, drying fish to the sun.

...

Anyway winning this isn't going to win itself: work is being done.

Mobilizing, drifting armies made singular; AncientEnd takes herself no more day offs. Entirely so, that the others takes themselves to business. No\_Strawberry, being himself, took a dayoff or two, yet no reprieve is felt. CommissionJumpy is, under some miracle of self awareness, taking awareness tests. Dark\_Synapse, consistently, is tired at being tired. He will get his mind together eventually.

The dinner is short; under an earlier sun, an influence of heat beating down, and the bumping, overhill rise in a beat up jeep, CommissionJumpy takes Dark\_Synapse somewhere where the two can smoke in peace. The place is compromised when AncientEnd and Old\_Buy come strolling through the stereotypical, really a simulation of the Nevada desert: moreof they are inspecting it for launch grounds.

Smoke rises indiscriminately in plumes small. Dark\_Synapse, watching AncientEnd stepping on ants, her eyes focusing somewhere, says, like the smoke:

"...The ants, Commission. They aren't aware of their predicament. They rely on pheromones, antennas, and the entire system they got," he lights his cigarette. His suit is dusty. Grey affairs. Grey hairs. Young man with grey everywhere.

"The ants, for all I know, operate with their own sense of justice. This, to them, is natural order," he points. CommissionJumpy idly looks. The sun is going to kill him. "If we drop another ant, of another colony, into the cohort we see, then that ant will be torn apart. That is justice. Mindless justice. But, conscious justice resides entirely on the premise of being aware of what is dropping, whether you are dropping, and what can you drop. He who drops his awareness of dropping is losing. Anyone who knows that awareness is primarily the key to justice are utterly blinded by it. The only thing that matters, is dropping. And being aware of it. Not awareness conquering reason. It is just as is: justice exists because something has fallen, is falling, and we notice. What isn't noticed operates outside of justice. Vigilante justice is borne out of being aware through a singular kind. However, the truest form of justice, Commission, that is hard to find: I believe that the truest form of justice is done not only by the loss of all personal allegiances, but also all social allegiances. It's forgetting, forgetting to be a person. The person who isn't a person, committing acts of justice because they nothing of their surroundings and of themselves...that would be something. Now, this person, the hypothetical person, completely asserts their unconscious justice. If two such individuals meet, the justice may as well be so utterly different it can only be described as the ultimate awareness. Justice, for these two people, would be done so in a blind manner. The embody justice so perfectly because they cannot see nor hear anything. They are singular persons, with their own awareness."

"...Alright man," CommissionJumpy remarks. He is leaving now. The sun is baking him. The usual hat isn't helping.

...

AncientEnd is at her wit's end (no pun being intended around here).

...

AndyTeasey lives in New York because: he can afford it, and two, because of an old hospital visit. He, all the way then, can't stop talking about his hospital dream. He isn't in the hospital bed: no, it's TrueGamer in the bed. He's got no heart after falling out of the sky from a broken monoplane, no limbs. TrueGamer doesn't say anything.

But Andy...he dreams of flying. He feels as if he's a rocket. Not a bird.

He knows that flying is freedom: he is, in a way, free. Violently free.

So when he comes out of the hospital, he stares at the sky. He wants to fly. He wants to see what TrueGamer saw.

Flying.

He neglects falling, for now. It'll bite him later.

...

JohnKonami is driving off: little to his own knowledge, he's already being described as missing.

Precisely as he is being known as missing, TheLightmaster7 is working at his station: a Gravity Refraction Imaging device used to project the route of the weapon. The moment JohnKonami vanishes, he sees that the way the light is being bent inward by the unseen forces, established non-material and gravity theory right there: however, he is seeing as well that although light is bending, the non-materials, and theoretical gravity itself, is being bent by, and influenced by, a constant motion of speeding streams: the streams are without a doubt entirely invisible without the manipulation it's causing...the streams, seemingly endless, stretch, and are stomping on the theoretical gravity in a way that is violent. Non-material is being remade into material through holes in this mysterious stream, and through the streams, it is possible that they are directly piercing the borders of ConsistentEar's described, "circle". The streams, maybe, originate from these borders, and are shared by both circles interlinked.

Meanwhile, something starts to drop in Altruistic5's office all the way in Denver. It's concealed by a curtain and an accompanying shroud of dying light from the setting sun.

Not that anyone's aware of it.

But really, the map is starting to unfurl, revealing itself. Someone can read it...someone can't. Either way, the map is there. Someone has to fear enough to realize fear is keeping everything alive, and that fear is the only lens needed to feasibly read this map.
