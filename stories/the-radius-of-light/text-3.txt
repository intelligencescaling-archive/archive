An entire gathering of such people means that although the night is around, they are sleepless. They are walking, in circles, without knowing where to go.

The Ballistic Officers are: "AndyTeasey,", and "Creepy-Ebb-3026."

The Medical Wing, comprised of the leading doctor of conscious surgery Altruistic5, and assisted by Dark\_Synapse, of Awarenwss Treatment specialization.

Strange things. Always being afoot; LightningMaster7 can't get his limbs working on Sundays. An example.

Again, there, and they, are sleepwalkers. Sleepless sleepwalkers. Where the night has gone they won't be coming, certainly.

"You see that," Consistent\_Ear deftly draws on a blackboard. His hand moves in an arc. "Although, so far, for Theoretical non-material, everything is sourced from the material, we are trying to destroy non-material, and pierce a theoretical source which has its own material and non-materials."

He draws a semi-perfect circle, and a diagram of N, piercing through it. "This circle is representative of the world, our world. For all we know, it is on a plateau of parallel dimensions. Hypothetically, piercing through our circle will give us access to an infinite amount of circles. However, the bounds of our circle are unknown completely. Which is why, we have our damn theoretics. The border is non-materials bound by theoretical gravity, and the light that refracts off of it isn't \*light\*, it most likely is theoretical light equivalent to the sheer force of combination between these two forces. Unproven, of course."

A pause, stretching. The others wait, Consistent\_Ear deftly moves his hand: "however, from a Heischel theory standpoint, these circles are constantly bound to each other; there is no boundary between this circle, our circle, and the next, as they are constantly being bent inward by theoretical gravity, yet they cannot reach and affect the cores, as each gravity is being bounced off of each other...we're, like some damn yo-yos or some shit, flying about, wildly. Crazy, no? the theory is that every paralle dimension is in orbit of each other, and we're, right now, trying to pierce through the orbit of our circle to penetrate the orbit of another."

"So this weapon will operate more efficiently if designed as if it is going into deep space travel," he finishes, putting now down the chalk.

It is settled now. The Light technicians job are cut out for them clearly. All is defined as possible, but fear resides. Someone won't trust another...they can see they do not see eye to eye. There is fear. There is a war between them. A war of theirs, stressed.

Dark\_Synapse lets in Altruistic5 into his office. Drab affair. Time on the clockhand delayed. A light shining through: it's 8:01 am.

Consistent\_Ear does not speak much outside of the meetings....Back in the older days, 1965, 1943, during an undisclosed location in upper Virginia, there is a drought going on, a deep drought, not for water, but for sanity, safety. He is a friend to the times, and is sane nonetheless. This is translated to today. By understanding what he's seen, he's already too far ahead. He is prevented by something. He can't name it, and he knows it is there. If, by AncientEnds definitions on Wednesday, he has already been seen, and has seen what can be done. Only by acting in complete certainty can he negate some deficiencies in being of this circle. He however can't forget. Usually, in days of difficulty, sometimes there needs to be something to fall back on in rage, or merely the raw hole in any person who equates justice with hate so blindly that the two are indistinguishable and are utterly tautology, and with that they equate it with the appropriate face they see fit to have this form of justice be the necessary vehicle for a specific peace: that is merely a passing form of peace, which lives only when they knew not hate alone. And maybe, the dash of fear: fear days. As with anything passing and passed, it's all the same: memories and regret, fading together. Now, what else but the idea of the enemy to force perhaps a great weapon that in twenty years with the right shareholders will be common place in any arsenal? Consistent\_Ear sees that if the enemy is what it is, for all he cares he is that invisible face, dooming some unseen world, with unseen failures, victories, and simply the usual dullness of a life that will be accompanied by the usual accessories: all in a vacuum which is filled with roaring laughter and the deafening shrills from the partygoers and sorrows. He takes a cigarette from Old\_Buy and retreats into a portrait of smoke and sleepless work.

Not that No\_Strawberry is deterred. As everyone is in their finest suits, they all stand in the forms of business.

For him, when asked during any interaction involving his field of work, he says, "My offers are whatever you want it to be. And that amounts to nothing. If the market demands it, nothing means everything, everytime. The businessman stands as the man who holds all his time as a market commodity."

When in time he finds himself alone, he finds company with Mr. Disintegrator.

"You believe the mission?" No\_Strawberry asks.

"If I believe in it, it will most likely become something I'll fixate a degree of hope upon. I can't hope yet, because what's there to hope about?" The answer No\_Strawberry receives.

"Seems dark to me, buddy." No\_Strawberry lights his cigarette. "Let's change the subject. What do you think about, well, all this. How this all feels like some, ah, some crappy jigsaw puzzle?"

"No comment. Pass," Mr.Disintegrator says. Clearly he is preoccupied.

"Each jigsaw piece is without meaning without another jigsaw piece. If each jigsaw piece existed solely by itself, without the knowledge of another jigsaw piece, what would it be? A jigsaw piece that knows of the existence of other jigsaw pieces, but actively cannot interact with them, simply acknowledging them, is doomed." Says No\_Strawberry.

"Doomed."

"Yes. Doomed. It's doomed to becoming efficient at refusing it's placements. It refuses that it needs to have a puzzle to solve itself, believing it can solve itself while acting solely for itself, only for itself, because it only knows itself as such. It attempts, and it realizes, but it never knows. If it knows anything, it knows that jigsaw pieces exist, and that it must be a jigsaw piece. The jigsaw piece, however free, mustn't be too free, otherwise it will fall from it's own freedom, itself, into the domination of the failures that come with flying as an entirely free individual. It will neglect it's own freedoms because it has forgotten it has free without the presence of the puzzle. Without the puzzle, the piece is denied itself by itself because of its refusal to participate. It is in constant critique of the other puzzle pieces while never seeing them. Like a person who demands that their ownness is without a doubt wholly original, while knowing that they've lived in specific cultural, social, and political environments, shaping them, they are in truth a form of ignorant people. Nothing is original. It is yours, yes, but nothing is entirely original without the input of other pieces, conscious or subconscious," the Intelligence Officer says.

"Mhm."

"It's like this whole mission. We think we're at war. But we're at war with ourselves. The winds of war are taking the jigsaw pieces apart, and revealing how everything has been together. And we aren't comfortable with the idea of being people, simply being people. It's bad for science," No\_Strawberry blows smoke, and like a ship departs.

Mr. Disintegrator shakes his head. "I don't smoke, now I smell of this shit." A puritan from 1600s, huh.
