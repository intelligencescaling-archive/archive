Pilot of the Fanfic Of The Sub: .See, Quince

**THE RADIUS OF LIGHT**

***PREMIER EPISODE; 1973*****.*****SEE, QUINCE***
~~————————————————————————————————————————~~

A summertime reminder: heat struck straight roads leading nowhere. Given to seasonal sentimentality, not the usual profanity of the season. A journey. Not really: a dream of flight, everyday, everynight. Arching landscapes of a midwestern stereotype, no name, no doubt, doubtless of the origin of placement of operation, only a government agency withstanding...a plain plain. **Department Of The Blicero Mission.** Blicero is a special name; suggested by some kid,  a Lincoln's appreciation. Again, there's a some worship going on, and it's not to a man in the sky, or a strip club dancer: did not say an empire of decadence, that would be obsolete. Rather worship to their work. It's all unknowns; in invisible rooms, hunkered and proud-standing, sipping, supping, or sitting, they mingled

Almost the Archbishop of the commission, dedicated to their task, unofficial head...prominent. The sign board on their house; an Amsterdam affair, larger, squatting, will tell anyone who sees, in big words:

# SMC—STATE MATERIAL COMMISSION.

**And then some; three cheers for SMC!**

There's a caravan. It's going to halt, but not now. An evacuation of intelligence, to the rescue of our time; a hero of our time, many. They needed more. They can share their invisible rooms with each other. Now, onlookers of feathers or plague bringer would think the caravan is of derelict, hustlers, clothes. No: it's of a bunch of women and men, and above them is their makers watching to unmake their troubles. They have moved from secluded to isolated to securely, strictly classified: an evolution of importance, as this nation's shadow grew longer, and a wind of war aroused an alarm and a paranoid estimation. No one there in that caravan, in the invisible automobile, would say that there's going to be a screaming stop to the quiet corners of all-day everyday life: them and their cigarettes, puffing machines, desires, and a little more of an aged, more a veteran dream of a Prize (Nobel). They, the caravan, are the faces lit up by a stretching light in an elegant machine; behemoth, (one would think Blame). They, bulletproof windows, goggles: scientists, with degrees of caution, precision, and stress.

They come in and come from the

# BLICERO MISSION. AGAIN.

**And a Laboratory.**
**The Laboratary: called it like Der Prozess's prosecuted. The last name.**

K.

K's Laboratory.

Blicero and K...they are in ways planets. In a globular cluster, really. The two are rotating. They are two spinning objects. In tandem. In an orbit, one neither the larger force nor the smaller, influenced. They are twins: not a Hail Mary, not much of miracle makers. But they are going to work, going to make their employees feel some labor. There, and the caravan, coming to a full halt now. When they've arrived they'll share and furnish their invisible rooms. For they aren't crunching the old dirts, from Sodom or Vienna: the long-running fantasy of paradise being preserved by a natural penchant for paranormal physics. Warwork, machine madness and a meaning, like a dream: feverish, pitched in the sky, arching like a baseball. Wherever it is held and lands...where it has landed, and has been caught, will be where the rest of everyone follows. Pathetically, to think, half of theoretical materials, theoretical gravities, are running after, under, leprechauns to the ball, waiting for it to arch, waiting for that pot to be found, by them. Of course the theme of the day is against all of day, all of odds and rights.

Though the caravan stretched far. Some have not even gotten themselves into marching. Some will not march. There is going to be a great dust bowl, across the world: Blicero'e relatives are coming together for it, the ill-relative of intelligence, missions, now getting some love. The interest has grown: whatever is being told, is being asked about...percolating the market of fears and federal uncertainty. Mostly a government of sorts; the preview of time is going to be a long one. Ack, everyone thinks we've reached the end of that preview. Whatever they say, they say, lie in their rooms, unseen investigations...a particle of an acceleration towards truth is enough to endure...leaving behind some crumb or golden nugget in a pile of rubble, endless, for us to pick through: we'll make war out of rubble and make war into rubble. The marker of the Levant of the universe...between us, them, and that march, so soon to be influential in proliferation...it was marriage: yup. For whatever they did at K, they were on trial, and they were going to lose, because their attorney's didn't understand that they and their fields weren't tokens to be put in the big slot machines at the arcades to throw pin-ball or waste time: SMC is big, biggest partner of partying science, distinctly it's a big sin....Overall, not really being Europe's sin, or America's (that would go to that special brand of disillusion, either that or modern English). Men, women: equal madness...we're touting our insanities.

In a house, in NYC's madness, in a tiny apartment: there is an officer. Ballistic field. Of the Theoretical family, solely married to it, probably because it gave him (the man the officer) some sense of Clarice's passions: it was a god, or moreof, and I quote, 'the nothing is living and moist". His apartment hadn't got a maid: he cleaned up by himself, and now he is on his bed, fallen, a Lucifer to a kingdom all his own, self-betraying, self/owning, wounded by a job. He's splayed magnificently. Now he will tumble if he doesn't right his left, and his weight will bring him down...like ballistics. (Poor joke). He won't know about the caravan yet; but they will pick him up, make him walk. His legs won't work, they'll perform a Son on him, make them work, some minor miracle work; he's tethered to theory, an actively trying field without the merit of madmen running amok...few madmen, his field. Theoretical Essential Energies. TEE. Tea for breakfast: toast. A sad plate, he was eating on it, a phone rang phoenix, it was broken a day, two, a week ago: spam call, damp moods, and a thing or two about the Blicero Mission. And K. And also P....Uh, D.U.P.

A card told his name. True, it did. The unexamined name is of mystery; bigger than Noah's Ark and whats left, stranger than anything H.P. Presented as AndyTeasey, he was informed as the less fortunate man to be of use in Blicero. A few tests: achtung, a blitzkrieg of quality...yesterday he fell out of bed, today he got yanked, tumbling, heaven articulate to him, as he was dressed up to march, or long-walk. He took his ostentatious marking, a service token, in his under appreciated field: TEE. Tea, took it more than coffee, a personal tender, shaman healer and also shampoo to his wandering spirit, and his scalp.

And what miniscule, leftover, boomtown decay field did he pursue, and found that now a new respect had grown overnight?

*Theoretical Ballistic Officer in Theoretical Essential Energies.*

AndyTeasey was not alone in his batch of the taken, the marched  V.I.Ps to the unspoken duty: a twin student body of D.U.P, one graduate, one not...prominent faces of theoretics, Noniscists...think of physicist, but without any doubt, they are different, just as difficult. They aren't Wittgenstein's, or got his temperament, but we must appreciate that they aren't capable of marching: off to take their trades to the cutting board, where given enough time after this war is won (hopefully: they don't even know who they're fighting, only that shots are being fired across a black night, and the bullets are as nothing and void-like as the expanse they fly into, penetrate, extricate themselves from, and into again daylight as peaceful reminders in a way on a French field's patchwork artillery scars) they'll make a market for theoretical materials, and the sisters to it. But now; nowhere, advancing in disguise, blandness, formality, fedora and suit, they come, have arrived. Taken up post: here, in this stereotype of a landscape, utterly flat, pointed like a spear in a heavenly body, they're going to make the next step...

**Of a nuke.**

When you must pierce through the depths of the event horizon, see to eye to eye your enemy lies beyond that point...you must tear apart everything to scratch him before he scratches you; so there, the reason of labor revealed, they're going to get to work. Hard work.

Give three cheers for the K. Laboratory. An excellent bachelor group to the hardship of piercing space with a conventional mega-weapon; as one does, pity the engineers.
