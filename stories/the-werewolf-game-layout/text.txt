6. There would be sufficient space so that no one can guess through airflow.
7. Anyone who dies gets shot. The player who is a werewolf if in case get voted out he shall remain inside the room where werewolves are escorted. 8. The votes are verbal. The command is simple - “I vote for” followed by the person you vote for.
9. Each person is provided with a list of names of the people playing with them.
