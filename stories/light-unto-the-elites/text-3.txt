Chapter 3: The Best of The Best

As I stepped off the bus, I began to feel an excitement rising in my chest. I had made so many promises of what I would do here, and now look: My dreams were facing me head-on. I hastily walked through the entrance gate and made my way towards the auditorium, where the introductory ceremony would begin. As I reached the auditorium, I saw four lines of students, as well as a sign hovering above the entrance. It read: “Please line up. You will be assigned to your classes at the desk at the front of the line.” I walked to the line closest to me and situated myself at the back. Not one minute of waiting later did I observe a student causing a fuss up ahead, and, although I couldn’t fully make out what he was saying, it was clear that he held an antagonistic and brutish attitude. It was because of this that I felt obliged to leave my spot in line and try to figure out what was going on.

As I approached the boy, I saw him gripping the collar of another student and sneering. All the others around the scene either averted their eyes or discreetly watched the scene unfold, as if ashamed by their lack of courage. That was honestly the worst part of it all: How could someone just sit by and watch this? This brute was clearly someone of terrible character. Someone selfish and scheming, and bold; Nothing more than a shameless dog.	My angry thoughts were cut off by my own actions, as I grabbed the perpetrator’s wrist as firmly as possible. His hand slowly released the boy’s collar, and he glared at me with sharp eyes. His hair was dyed magenta, and his appearance was that of a thug. This was the type of person who didn't belong in society…

“What’s going on here?” I inquired innocently. The question, though, was just a formality. I doubted that he would respond with anything of substance.

“Tch. Mind your own damn business…” The thug, while saying this, flicked off my hand from his. Typical.

“I had to stop you. It wouldn’t have been a good look on you, attacking someone on the first day.”

He then put his hands in his pockets and walked away, but not before subtly pushing me to the side and muttering a few insults.

I decided to turn my attention to the victim. Turning around, I locked eyes with him.

“Hello. Sorry for that… Did he give you much trouble?”
“N-No…” The bespectacled boy responded. He was nervous. I could understand. This was the first day of the rest of his life at this school, and he had to endure that kind of treatment? It was quite honestly a terrible situation he was in.

“My name’s Yagami. What’s yours?” Trying to offer some friendship, I figured introductions were in place.

“Kaneda…” He said awkwardly.

“Kaneda, if anyone tries to do that, try and get some help, okay? Most of the time, those types are just ignorant bullies; they scare easily.”
With some vigor, and a certain sparkle in his eyes, Kaneda responded: “Mhm! Y-Yeah. I will.”

“Glad to hear it!” I affirmed. “Well, I should be on my way. See you around!”
“See you around, Yagami!” He said as I walked towards the back of the line.

I felt happy at that moment. I felt as if I had fulfilled an obligation of mine and had helped someone get on with their day. As I joyfully walked up the line towards the back, I noticed a petite girl waving to me. Many times before, I had made the mistake of waving back to someone when really they were waving to a person behind me, and to avoid this awkward mistake, I looked over my shoulder. No one was there. So, turning around, I waved back. Although her voice was soft, I could still hear it clearly from a decent distance.
“I saw what you did back there. It was quite heroic.”
“Thanks. Although I would like to think that someone else would have done the same thing.”

The girl made an “I can’t hear you” gesture with her hand as she put it over her ear. I walked forth and repeated myself.

Almost mockingly, she replied: “How chivalrous of you. I know I could never have pulled that off.”

My eyes drifted to a cane at her side, supporting her right leg. “I see.”

The girl wore a serene smile, coupled with light, lilac hair. Despite all appearances, she had an imposing aura about her, one which made me oddly nervous.

“Sakayanagi Arisu. That is my name.” She calmly said.

Her courtesy obliged me to return the favor, and I responded with my full name in turn: “My name’s Yagami Light. A pleasure to meet you, Sakayanagi.”	The student ahead of her looked at us expectantly, and she waved him away with a flick of her hand. Obediently, he put his head down and walked to the back of the line. Sakayanagi then gestured to me to take the other student’s spot ahead of her.

“A reward for your valiant services”, she smiled.
