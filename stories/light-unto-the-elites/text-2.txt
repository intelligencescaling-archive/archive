Light Unto The Elites (COTE What-If Scenario) Chapter 2

Chapter 2: The Filipant Boy and Obstinate Girl

Firstly was the boy. Of good build, I would likely have mistaken him for a fully-grown man had he not been wearing the school uniform. His shoulders were quite broad, and, although seated, it was obvious his height was around six feet. Yet his physique was not the only thing which made him noticeable, as two other things stood out to me. The first was his blonde hair, which had obviously been dyed, and the second was the way he seemed to carry himself. He looked to be a haughty person with little care for others and a penchant for confrontation. As if in response to my thoughts, his eyes opened and met with mine.

In such an awkward situation, most would quickly avert their gaze and proceed like nothing happened. Yet this boy refused to do so, instead only letting out an indignant “Hmph!” and dawning a cocky smile. With such an oddity, I found it best to turn my attention to the girl I had noticed earlier. As my gaze trailed to the side of the bus parallel to the eccentric boy, I saw her also staring at me. Quickly I corrected this thought. She wasn’t staring at me, she was glaring at me. Observing how she wore the school uniform, I thought it might be good to try and acquaint myself with another fellow student.	Taking a step or two towards her, I tried to initiate a conversation, only to find that she had buried her head inside a book. I could only make out fragments of the title, but I eventually pieced it together. Crime and Punishment: A book about justice which posed questions regarding the ethics of killing for the right reasons, and if those reasons were right to begin with. Although I had already come to answer that question, I thought that could make for good conversation.

“Crime and Punishment, huh?”

Irritated, the girl shut the book with quite a bit of force, and looked up at me. It was then that I was able to see her face in full for the first time. She had raven-black hair, and was quite pretty. Yet even with all that beauty, her scowl seemed to cover most of it up. 	“Yes, indeed. You’ve read it before?” She said curtly. It seemed she thought that I hadn’t read the book, and so the conversation would stop there.

“Yep. But I remember struggling with the first half. Honestly, I was pretty bored up until the murder scene. I’m really not one for reading.” I let out a small, self-deprecating chuckle.

The girl let a long, awkward silence ensue, which forced me to speak.	“Anyways, how far along are you?”	“Far enough.”

How rude… It seemed that this wasn’t even a conversation worth having, and so I decided to end it myself, and spare the both of us.

“Well let me know when you’ve finished! I’d love to talk to someone about it!”	“Sure.”

“Oh, and by the way, my name’s Yagami. Nice to meet you.”

Shooting me one last glare, she opened the book, and continued reading. In turn I decided to move away from her and simply await our arrival at the high school.
