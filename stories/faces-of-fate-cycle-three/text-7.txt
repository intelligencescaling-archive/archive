FANFIC OF THE SUB; PART 7, SCHOLARS CYCLE: PRINCE OF DREAMS

#"CHAPTER SOMETHING": MOMENTS LOST IN DENIAL; PT 1 THE PRINCE OF DREAMS, PT. 3

Useful awoke to the sound of a multitude of madness; but then, the sound would quickly replace, placing itself no as firmament for instability to rise and take hold, and then once it had fallen the madness quieted to beauty; beauty of the dream world, middway between death and rebirth, awakening and consciousness, the silent interaction of both dreaming and waking, waking and then sleeping to dream again, perhaps never escape the dream, but always reinventing the dream, like a Prince, with infinite clothes, riches, gems, a person with everything; but really nothing, because what worth is anything when everything is all equalized to the null and void from one own's sight?

No. This was no dream; it was a falsity, but a welcome falsity; an eternal moment, like all other moments, since time itself is eternal, and we are not afraid of time's passing but only shaken by the events that are natural of our attempts to partake in the interval between an ending and a beginning; and once one decides to transcend the world or try to, to become beyond it, to either accept it or detach oneself through and cleanly until you become a conceptual black hole, that is when time is not eternal, and when the past (which one may regret and fear, or deny so clearly), and the future, and the present, all now combined together, the eternal flow of time, a river, now reversed and forwarded, intimidating, leaving the self to feel the infinite moments time destroyed and destined to transcendance flawed.

The sound of the humming had descended all around Useful; he was captured by instinct, now, and he felt compelled to at leasf forgo his lack of presence this once, to admit to Night every detail, to somehow clean his name, to make himself more presentable, to make himself less acceptable, to make himself true, to reveal what is true, to become something entirely different from his aims; to give into content, to become enraptured in this eternal moment and feel Night's flowing nature until that to vanished before his eyes; betraying him and thus returning the wretched self to the spirit of non-innocence and the escapist view; the escapist view is long and large, wide, and contains all things.

Useful had been struck so harshly that the only thing that had taken him from such madness at that moment, that confusion, that clash of ambitions, (which he now doubted were his own true ambitions; what is wealth? What is material? Why did he pursue it? To become acceptable to any person?).

He had not noticed how the birds flocked around Night; how they adored her in silence; how he hated her; how they all felt but in feeling realized that their own feelings were flawed, because they were only feeling the half-side, the side revealed, and the other sides, all kaleidoscopic in being, hidden beneath layers of fear, confusion, sadness, and regrets, despairs, perhaps even the manners of circumstances; but the birds! They represented something quite pure, purer than the holiest of men, higher and ascending quicker than the learned ones, because birds, like a truly free soul, so impossible, are so free they must be careful not to plummet to their deaths, to instinctively flap their wings, to fly, because flying is all they know to do; they are so free they are unable to do anything else but fight for that very freedom; to fly is torture, when flying is what becomes of your entire existence. Freedom is inherently a paradox, an enigma with no answer; the bird is freest but must fight to survive with it's freedom, while everywhere one looks mankind is imprisoned by themselves or by others, or by nothing but the opposing parties that nature is completely indifferent to our experiences.

But Night! Night! How could one laugh! Useful thought. He had snapped with difficulty from the fog of the mind, and was brought to his senses by the brilliant sound of laughter; the birds were upon her shoulders, on her head, all around; they had transitioned from one person to another; to give that person the comfort of freedom impossibly manifested in beings that would surely not realize the gifts of their very existence. She laughed! Alas, Useful thought again; how much of what I could do, could say, would equate to nothing, because I do nothing at all, because the world is nothing but a folly to me, that of which I refuse to partake within the vices, and now I am realizing! Realizing that in my greatest times of denial, in times of misery, Night, a person who has never known freedom at all, still chooses to open her soul wide, and I question why? Why?

So quietly, slowly, Useful asked her as the birds perched upon her; "Do you feel the soul of the birds?"

Night paused; "Maybe?"

Useful; "Maybe?"

Night; "Yes. Maybe. I can't be sure, it's all so new,"

Useful, whilst thinking of how strange this entire dream is; "But do you...feel anything at all?"

Night; "Oh. Yes! I do. I feel a little bit of something I haven't, and it is gorgeous,"

Useful; "But why? Why do you allow such things to be felt? Why feel when feeling turns to hurting, and even then hurting will transform to perfect indifference?"

Night looked at Useful, and saw his question wrapped in his eyes; "Why do I need reason to feel the world? I'll see it in all it is,"

Useful looked at her; "Even me? Do you believe me capable of such meaningful aspects when I have...taken it from another?"

Night; "I admit, I'm not even sure if you have. You are as unsure as me, correct?"

Useful; "No. No."

Night; "Oh? So you...do know?"

Useful; "Yes. I simply denied it...I denied it; I denied it so well I forgot how to realize reality is sometimes the better, no, not sometimes, always, the better of a dreamworld and a realworld. The man I murdered? I never murdered him physically; I only killed his spirit,"

Useful; "This murder isn't much of a murder but more of an honor slaughter."

Useful; "I am a raven of misfortune; I am a footless, wingless bird; why? Because that is what I am, not by nature; I have been removed from the world by my own choices, and I regretted those choices, because I wanted others to know me; to realize I to had a soul worth observing; but I would not stand by while another soul was forgotten thoroughly."

Useful; "The man I killed was my friend. Xamot."

Night stirred but said nothing, the birds on her now stayed.

Useful turned to her and said, without a sense of sadness, but it was still present; "My eyes can see it clearly; Xamot died by my being. He died because he knew too much of false freedom, and when realizing it was all illusory he took me with him down an illusory world to."

Useful; "I killed him; why? Why? Because he asked me to."

Night, shocked; "He, he asked you?" Her voice was neither faint nor shaky; it was firm; it was the voice of a person who knew not of his predicament but innately knew to try and see; and see if in seeing it was honorable and reasonable.

Useful; "Yes. He asked me because he thought of me indifferent to the world."

Useful; "I was and still am,"

Useful; "I killed him because in killing him he could excuse himself from not only being; but he had the greatest faith in life; he had much more faith than I, and the reason he is dead at all is because he is; I reveal to you that he was ill, weakened; he would die regardless, a dilapidated death, and in dying to such a disease that marred his body, slowly, painfully, I only stood by and watched for a good while, as all his worldly connections fled him due to selfish fear; he could no longer provide for them. Xamot was a wealthy man; and now, as he could not give, they took what they had and went off to take from another; they abandoned him."

Useful; "I see Xamot so little in my mind because I will admit; he became almost nothing; an absence in reality. The longer one is forced to love in isolation, the more they become an absence; a necessary aspect denied of trueness. Then, I decided myself free enough to decide his freedom should be dictated by me. I wanted to give my freedom of life to him in death because he deserved it more than me; my freedom is constructed from my sight; my sight is constructed by my opinions; my world is flawed while his was not."

Useful; "And I, I, rather twistedly; I made myself the judge of this ill, wounding, plague-like condition, and he permitted it, because they had destroyed his faith. I killed him to give him eternal life; because those who gave lived in misfortune, and died in misfortune, will now be the best scapegoats for those who claim themselves to be best, and also will be the target of the most genuine of all humans; his friends returned once they heard of his death, his "murder"; they returned with guilt. With shame. With realizations. And I became the greatest villain, to save another man from the ultimate death; the death in abandonment and sheer destruction of faith in others; to die without faith is terrible; to live a life with faith and in death have none of it repaid is worse! so I ask of you, why do you have so much faith? Why do you believe you can feel others when in reality, when you have nothing to give or to give in words, people will not abandon you, will not leave you disrespected, will not harm you by disregarding your existence because you have no material wealth at all?"

Night finally said; "I feel because that is not what I can only do; I am trapped; by circumstances I do not even know; I am cursed!" Here she laughed so light heartedly it wouldve seemed her curse was as equally light.

Night; "I want to feel everything because I never felt them at all; my soul is my world; it has always been, and always will be; when you have nothing the clothes on your back, the (expensive) roof on your head, and the curse of a non-existent witch, all you have is your soul; they can keep me locked well, but they'll never stop my heart from dancing, and from my soul searching for what is true! Truth! I value it more than anything, but I am a bad person in discerning it."

Useful nodded. He felt foolish. But he wasn't foolish.

Useful; "I can't say much."

Night; "I know. Me neither,"

Useful; "I'm quite selfish,"

Useful; "When I saw you for the first time, it was strange. I wanted to know you; then it turned to wanting you; then finally it turned to repulsion. I wanted your words, your truth-seeing eyes, your cursed hands, all of the things that have bewitched me, to be mine. I reasoned you have cursed me like yourself; we are both cursed, aren't we? But you added only one more curse for me, and I probably gave you another curse as well."

Useful; "At first, I saw you as you were and are; surface skin; surface faces, surface being, features fully rendered and hiding such things beneath. You were not the Noble's ward Night, you were not the Night you believe yourself to be, you were not even that Steward's Night, doomed with the nature of a fairy tale folly; no, you were my imagined Night; all wrong, but for me all correct, you were all I wanted; and now you disappointed me by being yourself, and I still wanted to mold you into what I wanted; but you are not what I want because you are what you are, and even then you are not what you truly are in being; by means of freedom you are not at all Night, only a piece of her; and in that piece another face, melted, across the hot sands, pecked at by birds, that face the face of a matyr, a matyr of my own heart, because if you are to ever suffer a fate ordered cruelly by it, to end you, I would find that I unwillingly re-attach myself to an aspect of this world to make you an aspect I can't forget; I can't remember fully either."

Useful; "I decided it was enough; I slept, I sleep still, and I conjure this world; I give myself my titles, my own thoughts manifested clearly in reality that is constructed to appease me; and appease me by all means satisfy my desires to remain blindsided by myself; and I willingly allow myself to be blind, because by being blind I have no means of accountability, only reinforcing my own philosophy; my philosophy of consequences, all of that, and none else. I only see this way because I cannot see myself being wrong; I have no accountability, I fear it; I fear my actions, I fear my future, I fear my present and am similarly comforted by it all the same. I am what I am, demonic, humane, angelic, angry, sad, nothing, detached; that is all I am, a great paradox, of contradictions."

Useful; "Imagine a person, who, as to what you cannot understand because it is just too difficult to; this person has too many contradictory thoughts, ideals, that he becomes a person without any thoughts at all, only experiencing and seeing, but never inputting himself in anything."

Useful; "Unlike you, who strives to feel and input the self into all things, and are forced back by mysterious forces, this man has been thrust unto the world, and, selfishly, has decided to flee it, because it is all too much for him to see clearly."

Useful then finally said to Night, as the birds all around her stayed and adored her and remained stilled and would not move; such birds were like him, but more of a person than he was; "Night, I'd like to take you somewhere, someday. Not because of my wishes, but because of yours. I'd like you to see the stars, and I'd like you to see the moon to."

Night tilted her head; "The stars? Ah? They are real?"

Useful almost shook his head, then reminded himself of her state; "Yes."

Night; "I always thought of them as metaphors or symbolism."

Useful; "Perhaps they are symbolisms, because everyone has their own views on a star; a wish, a dream, a memory."

Night; "Hm?"

Useful; "I'm talking strange."

Night; "I can tell. You are strange, Useful. I will admit that."

Useful; "You are very punctual...no, very blunt."

Night; "I am aware? Hah, not really."

Useful; "Read a book on manners,"

Night; "For what purpose? I'm not going to use it anytime soon." She laughed.

Useful; "I don't know. Read a book...? No. Take a stroll."

Night; "You know I can't...really, well."

Useful; "I know."

Night; "Then why suggest?..."

Useful; "Because I feel mean-spirited."

Night; "Oh."

Useful; "I see."

Night; "What?"

Useful; "Nothing."

Night; "Considering your confessions...hm."

Useful; "Judge me as you may."

Night; "I will try to be as fair as I can," she said this with a small smile that made Useful sigh and perish within himself a thousand times; only to be reborn beneath that smile.

Night; "I'm far too...lazy; I'm afflicted with problems, and I have no way of reasoning them,"

Useful; "You're entire existence is a problem; you are living in a locked room, wandering a locked house, with a possibly manic man and an odd steward about."

Night; "Hah. Ouch," she said, shifting her head to the left to stare at the sky in the dream.

Useful; "Pitiful sky."

Night; "What?"

Useful; "Nothing."

Night; "What?"

Useful; "I won't answer. I feel conflicted on many things right now and you are such a light-hearted fool."

Night; "Oh. Sorry for being...light hearted?"

Useful; "Let me brood."

Night; "Look, if you are hurt...I'll see what I can do."

Useful paused.

Useful; "I see. I will admit to you; my confession isn't even the realest part; only thw parts I want to see. I distort the truth because I am too...too...well, simply just *me* to even try to see the genuineness. My confession, however compelling, however confused, is but an aspect of my mind's guilt and desire to remain what I believe it to be. I am a person who will willingly destroy oneself to reinforce their own beliefs; to appear right. I am confounded, Night. Hopefully you are not."

He smiled.

...

The Steward looked at the Noble.

"She's here? Really? You kept me away  from this cursed...child! You forced me from doing justice." The Steward said, angrily. He slammed his fist against the table.

"You fooled me; with all your Sorceress bullshit; maybe that Sorceress is real, huh? Whatever it is, she's here, and why? I thought she was dead! She's scarred my name, Dar—" the Steward started.

"Leopard, I'm tired." The Noble answered.

"You will not harm her; she has done no wrong. She has been kept here, and has not spread any bad words of your name!" The Noble added.

"Oh great. Fuck off. She's a scar on my name by existing!" The Steward said.

"You **will** not harm her," the Noble said, forcefully.

"I won't." The Steward relented after a long, tense silence.

"And the boy? The murderer? What's he still doing here?" The Steward asked.

"He'll go." The Noble said, turning to look at Useful's slouched form over the desk.

"What?" The Steward said.

"I said, he will go! The boy! He will begone! And then you'll go? Forgetting her? Will you?" the Noble said, rubbing his temples.

"I promise you," the Steward said. The Noble only stared.

"You disappoint me," both men muttered under their breath.

...

Useful noticed that a man stood beside him in silence; Xamot. Night had suddenly vanished beneath a haze; the dream was ending, but not yet, for one last visitation was necessary.

Xamot; "You killed me?" He seemed to be questioning this.

Useful; "I did."

Xamot; "Heh. I had died long before you ever did,"

Xamot; "Are you trying to prove yourself to her? That you are too wrong, too much of an absence? You aren't nothing, Useful; neither am I. Humans are inherently meaningless; the universe isn't. Why? Because the universe has found itself, and its meanings. The universe is its own meaning, while humans, well..."

Now Xamot laughed; it was an utterly gutted laugh, a bitter laugh, a laugh that told Useful of regrets consuming and hopes rising.

Xamot; "Oh. Humanity, with all your imperfections. How I love you,"

Useful; "What?"

Xamot; "I died alone, yes. They all left me; my 'friends'. My 'family'."

Xamot; "In that moment, when I realized I was being left to rot, I felt fear. I felt vindicated by my own thoughts. I felt that every man afterwards was the evil, the demon. No. Not at all. Mankind is no clever 'genie' on your shoulder, Useful. Mankind is no demon; mankind is something else, because we are what we are, and we are wanting what we will never be."

Xamot; "I didn't ask you to kill me, Useful. You did that on your own accord."

Xamot; "But then, I had realized; in death I was revealed that the most detached of us was indeed the most attached after all."

Xamot; "And that is why, I fell back to hope. I hope well."

Xamot; "This isn't me at all; this is your vision of me. But perhaps it is correct. Perhaps it is woefully wrong. You'll never know, Useful. You will never see. And that is what you may realize soon; what you see, what you believe first, may change, because change is always the key to our lives. You may fear change because change will ruin you, but in those ruins you can decide to grow or fall."

Xamot; "The townsfolk abandoned you; for reasons true. They are vindictive and they will not change. You are a monster to them."

Xamot; "And now I see you have not wept in earnest; and until you weep in earnest will you realize that you have felt at least the most powerful aspect of mankind; the aspect of sorrow, because sorrow is what befalls the mind before more sorrow or salvation."

Xamot; "Change. How I see it. I died and changed. A birth without a death is natural; a birth and a life lived without ever feeling anything, without ever growing, without ever realizing that life is an unfortunate river that has so much gold sprinkled in its dirty underbelly, waiting to be found, washed, treasured. An existence that never wills itself to sift through the river of life will only exist and then die; I wasn't living until I died, because that is only when I wept in earnest, and that is when I laughed in earnest, because I found so much of that gold, underneath my ruined fingernails."

Xamot's face had begun to morph into Useful's, a perfect copy. A happier copy; a bloodied copy, a murdered copy. He said; "I saw and never saw; I changed but never changed, I morphed into thousands of illusory forms until my own form was nothing but a coreless form with hundreds of different forms allowing it to exist; every opinion, every dream, was just someone else's form; my own form had died long ago, because I let it die, because that form was sacrificed to morph to other forms."

Xamot, or, well, the dream Useful, the copy, the Prince of Dreams, said to sleeping Useful; "Every form is dead when neglected; it is like war, and war is godlike because it can affect us in such ways, and by ways I mean the millions of agonies and sufferings that are well remembered by generations until they are forgotten, and then we repeat our own mistakes."

The Prince Of Dreams; "You will change. It is not you must, because I have faith you will. My faith is strong, because the river I have sifted through has taken me to many other rivers, and those rivers have led me to others; I observed in silence, and in the silence of death I became the master of my own river at last."

The Prince Of Dreams; "You may deny, and you will always deny, Useful; I am you and you are not me. Your denial is inherent."

The Prince Of Dreams; "I searched hard for myself in death; you killed me and that was the catalyst of my soul's rebirth."

The Prince Of Dreams; "So, love, Useful. Now you will not know why; you will hate such advice, and I do not care if you do, because in time, and I have faith, you will eventually try to see the world wider than ever; you will try to perceive every layer in every situation; in every situation's infinity.

THe Prince Of Dreams; "The beauty of how infinite we all are! Every moment, eternal, everything, so infinite! So much and so little time to see, but we can see as much as we can by not denying, but by denying our own prejudices and allowing such villany to become the goodness that we all must strive towards for the betterment of our rivers, and all other rivers."

The Prince Of Dreams; "You may continue to sleep, perhaps you always will; you are what you make yourself to be, no one else can do such things; you are what you are; the greatest enemy, your greatest friend, only you. So continue your ways; time will tell them to you, of their effects, and if you still do not care for matters of morals and conscience then that is that."

The Prince Of Dreams; "None of what you have seen is real."

The Prince Of Dreams; "So wake up now; your dream is large, but your waking world, reality, is infinite! Infinite!"

And then Useful woke, to find himself with Night near; he could hear her heart; why? What had happened that he had denied to save himself from the ruinuous ability reality had to destroying his philosophy, his ideology?

He thought he could hear he heart and he thought he could feel her heary but that was strange; he felt that his thoughts were untrue, that those who claim, "I think, therefore I am", are not thinking; they don't exist, fully, only the mind exists, and the mind itself is a nebula, and that nebula is contained inside of a supposed body that the nebula creates to support itself from madness.

The heart is steady, Useful thought. He dared not open his eyes; he dared not see. He feared what it may be, what this new event was, what had transpired, what had changed, what it meant to his supposed "ambitions", what it meant to his murderous hands, what it meant to everything, what it meant to Night's doom.

He feared change, and thus welcomed the sound of a steady heart.

For now.
