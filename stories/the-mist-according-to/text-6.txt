**CHAPTER 6: HONESTLY. (Sacrificing readability and accessibility a little)**

Whatever is said of Night's is a remnant of the internal ascendance of her structure which in itself presides no matters nor doubts: to frankly confess, the writer named Vergilius, named Faded, the family, may as well be Night, and borne from the un-borne affections and affairs of the in-state individual with the Hamlet court and courtesy, there be the specific Host awaiting, there be the below Host, the annulment of the fears of un-living, the connection between the states of reading and reader, entering and passing the constancy and perhaps karmaic cycle of an internal life which solely excludes the external! And for, having been of the state without states and of the birth without a mother, daughter without feminine appendages, and the innert ever-movement, Night is indeed left to the vices of their own and her own creations and consciousness, fitting only in the folds of the domains with which she may define the modums of her narration.

And upon the occasion of the un-eventful tumultuous instant-actions that preside the person to the constant struggle between the assumption of their mind, the assumption and consummation of all things dear, all things interesting, all things that remain, and all things that may become far more, there in combatting it is the states of privation that deprives us and Night not only of the necessities but of the knowledge of the beautiful necessities; for being born and again reliving of the un-urban and un-country, of the rejections and the recognitions of the innumerable inhabiting truths and falsities that so imitate the inebriated, and thus so rid her and herself (separated by means of Byzantine Ships) from the pains and soothing of a mirror-world to peer and perhaps place oneself and ones own betrayals, ones own laboring and lazing; the meaning of the mirror world, like many of the chess pieces that so infinitely stretched and copied themselves, moving without touch, the perception of hers and the alien perceptions of these mirror-world and chess coupling, the realities, had become an illusory force, forcing negated of "potency", thus depriving again her of the last vestige of pre-action?

The opinion of ours upon that which we may defend is only for a moment: the acquisition of knowledge new may annihilate such former defensible position: nothing lasts but it remains in such a way we believe they are eternal.

For, what can be deigned, I, Faded, confess, of this immemory? Everyone remembers what they can but are defeated by the circumstances of time and the place no longer: the people we know are people enough until we begin to idolize our memories, and then that occurrence reduces them from an actual presence to a silent comforting tragedy.

Night slept alone in the train: the bed was flatter than usual and the sheets of white snug.

Her suitcase packed stood stoutly by the bedside: one lone suitcase, lone and against, filled with momentarily stolen things.

Night wandered off in sleep.

For what difference was she from the strange pilgrims that descended upon the towns? For what reason was she in constant abyssal light?

Retreating back from uncertain places she found a meadow again and stooped over to pluck a crown: her crown thrown aside now bearing once more. Playing King, consoling.

In the world of strange pilgrims she thought and said (in a method of self-dialogue), "...And because Night saw that the ever-present dis-spirit and dispiriting nature were friends to I eternal, Night wondered if the fervent un-passions drove the soul to the action of in-passion, complete and whole? There was no doubt an Essential Being that withtold us inside the eternal truths which we manipulate to further support the divine un-divine us, but the Essential Being may be the conjuring of consolation? Is it the only move on my board to be without being, to feel without feeling? The truth and the deception; all things we may know of the laughing, silent world is that it is surrounded by veils, veils which hide the real forms and drape them and their concepts sensually and tempting, leaving us with only the path of image, the path of being, the path of presence and of the constant service towards the eternalness of Why."

"Why?"

"Truth is becoming, and becoming is truth; why is the necessary mechanism which we use to question...and yet, the questions we ask are insufficient to salvage anything."

"The world is without us and the world is with us; the world is of us and of us is the world, and within the within there is the deepest recession of the ever-regressing and progressing states of selfhood which in having we are incapable of withholding any semblance of constancy. I have attempted to hem in not only death, the fears of others, the fears of my own, the truth and lies, the comforts and pains, the all encompassing nature of thought and un-thought, understanding only little but grasping enough to speak of anything, I have hemmed it in by the island-solitude of my cursed spirit, dividing and conquering. But, the person who hems in these things is in the end doomed to the sour victory of the encaged."

"Thoughts and words, feelings and this, and the autonomy of our being and Essential Being, the autonomy of the inanimate animations, the bodies that devote different from the mind; these things are moving, moving far more than what can be perceived; and when we perceive we see the slightest deception and the hint of actuality, and in the loneliness between ourselves, our eyes, we fill up our concepts with a concentrated dose of our assumptions of our own..."

"Ultimately, despite those who have lived more intensely, more in sorrow, more in joy, those who have lived with internal consideration, despite the classification of the twin races in one species, the solitudes and the persons, we are strange, strange pilgrims, always and evermore, with our baggage always being lost."

She disembarked in the morning; destination was Mid Subredia, and it had far changed from past years. A low voice that Odysseus fellow possesses.

Cutting a corner: find a couple walking. Walk alongside, not too close. Peter The Great mimicry, on a minor scale. Feeling tired after walking on new roads: always tired no matter where one goes. A Dubliner in London if blindfolded will still know he is not in Dublin: the couple walking know where Dublin is?

My hat: big and useless. Feathered and happy. I got my hat and my shoes and my suitcase and along the way the long way I do not know where I shall go. Turn right or left or keep following? The city feels quaint. Biting her lip is that lady by my side, walking briskly, a dagger facemask of features, tired people, wakeful people, and the prophetic process of the middway dash to the somewheres begins, I usurp my place among them and follow: ate my breakfast at disembarking and went ahead to find Vergilius's place.

She tore away from the pathfinding and instead in pathhood wandered as if born on a road: a length a mile a few, a second a path to find a far-away hue, focus on it, sink into it, forget color of home, forget that forgetting. Aye, aye.

Suitcase now heavy: hand maybe aching. Maybe? Maybe not but I don't know.
