CHAPTER 8: THE GAME, THE FACE, THE REASONS. (Finale Part one maybe? Sacrificing readability)

Night sat in her apartment: chair of oak, wood floor un-acknowledged, smell of old food, smoke accompaniment, the comfort of light, warmth from it, she pondered here.

A feeling of loneliness is where the absence of company becomes unbearable; whereof one may as well realize that by submitting oneself to the company of words and of their thoughts (these thoughts inbeing a containment of a sense), they must also acknowledge they may also become deprived of this company, and whereof one is isolated from their own-ness by the subconscious emotions, they may as well attach themselves to aromas, places, peoples: the path of brick, the sky blue, the wall ascending, looming over: of this proposition, we can become accustomed to the temporary lapses of our thoughts: Night becomes an un-Night to Night: where there may be Night's opinion there may as well be no opinion at all.

Night of faceless traits; never grown, nevertheless Peter The Great, nevertheless Grendel. The inability of the ability to audience and review the work whole: Tristan und Isolde, Tristan Und Isolde landmark, don't stain this and that; she wondered as if a baby to the world, and anew was again undone by the quickness of realization: all things centrifuge to her, and her centrifuged by the centripetal force and the logics; "When you see red and white they are in company well together: colors on their own are disservice to themselves, and colors with other colors are dissident to eyes. For eyes alone may serve us not, a welcome procession instead of the humors and manicness of the infinitely colored world do we regain and renew any chance of re-feeling wonder." (Metanote rlq holy hell what a paragraph .\_.)

Night stopped thinking when she had tried to think; conversely thinking came to her better, the soundness of her reasoning resumed, once in thinking abandoned.

Dusk walks served her well.

They eased the disquiet silence that enfolded the city.

Gravity bent, bow-shaped, under the pressure of unknown forces: if gravity could be easily disregarded by the nameless things than what prevented the man who frequented anything from being disregarded by other such similar phenomena?

Subredia was built on the many before and the many coming: Night lived in the transition, never past, present, nor future, only her time and her awareness, and somehow still felt unsure of it all by the unease and unwieldy, acute points of it.

A person may be nothing until perceived by another: same to the former with the latter.

A trade of perceptions.

The longer a work is written, Night reasoned, the more the work must insist it must continue: for, all works of length necessarily complete the criteria for its size: thus, if a game of chess, or if a moody twilight in the sands infinite of some unnamed desert, they insist upon themselves, for what in this world asks the right questions? The matter is one must ask questions without any doubts nor expected explanations, and thus explain why they insist upon themselves and why they insist upon the act of seeking.

Naturally all entities seek: Night sought for what she could; if born in solitude, is it by that means of having lived in illusory methods now validate the continued pursuit of the un-presence?

*——: If the Night who played King still was of today, most likely she would accept Mephistopheles as he is; if Night of the present remained then the Night of "now" would be annihilated: all reasons of NIght's continued absence-presence was that in insisting she had lived as others, she has failed to comprehend any deal, feeling only through the words and arts: expressing was a falsehood. For Night, having envisioned as she pleased, had traveled along vestigial paths, nowhere leading nowhere finding; upon an old place a bridge a field, a house a road, no names now, she had completed the cyclical recollections: for what is, for the yellow leaves in autumn, for the oak trees, there was their "nothing", an unacknowledgement of their place.*

*—: Despite these reservations and self imposed restrictions, she had realized that in the midst of the continual digressions from an observant, innert choosing of action-type, the world came anyway to the person who refused to act within it: the world will always come to fetch everyone, leaving none behind, leaving the little Subredia's broken, filled with new members again and once more lost with the moving: Night was no different; with every ambition, every remark, every hemming and freeing of decision, declarations of this and that, with the silent loyalties to the community's, they were eventually betrayed, forgotten, uncared for, and after sometime rekindled: of what character did Night possess that which she was sure would define her certainly? There in little letters across a screen or page she is, our friend my friend, no one's friend, her only friend; one mind existing in a space of many, unknown till known, under six feet of dirt of awareness. A curse of this awareness, betrayer and Mephisto of us, whom we are indebted, even the race condemned.—*

She stepped from the chair, grabbed her coat, and went out for a walk beneath the moon.

I was walking home when I noticed the familiar head through the city crowd: cut a path to meet.

Me—"Good evening." (Formal. Formality is worth the bygones equally with the here).

She—"Same as one is, Faded." (Not Vergilius, yet).

We discussed and walked through the city of Subredia. The buildings and their lights seemed more pyres and burning alive, and the path winded down to the Crusades. Subredia, built on the edges of a vague community, of takes and debates, with difficulties varying, with documents, and I the writer despising and enjoying it; Subredia, despite it being a mess, was a good mess, is a good mess, maybe worth writing for, maybe worth writing about.

I temporarily thought she would start flying on some broomstick: I wondered if I could.

Witch, Wizard, matter not, same in treachery to faith.

One can tell when I became not only Faded but  Vergilius to her, when I now had the possibility of being two people, now three with her acknowledgment binding to the twins.

We were twins and were what we were. Becoming was losing and losing was becoming equivalent (to the supposed lover or family I will apologize; you love an illusion, as with the faces of Subredia and it's cosmology, you are deceived and hope not to be: Night in herself the name was an illusory one-ness that split apart to message an infinitely fractured set of relationships, waves and journeys, for beneath Odysseus's ship traveling and Nausicä imprisonment she was the misfortunes and good fortunes: so yes, you are aware and unaware of the trickery, as the trickery is award and unaware of it's own deceitfulness, as the world is).

Night thought and spoke to I, Vergilius Faded, spoke to me and transferred through time and through logic to I, and I accepting of the unnatural notion of dead to living felt as though this distinction as with my identity fused with hers was thinning: we were one and the same, and I was her, and she had become me. Or had it been I had splintered off and found myself unsatisfactory, thus returning to the progenitor.

—"I have hemmed in the world into metaphors, words, and descriptions, but the unknown is far more clever and will refuse these constraints. If I can hem in my own character, what matter is my character to the un-spoken questions? For what instance has continued me in truth, when I have attempted to affirm anything but substance? What feelings are? And what isn't?"—

—"Is this very biography of remembrance possible? Or is it the gross misrepresentation of the obvious?"—

—"I only wish to know the idea of contentment and of serenity: however, I seem to be born for the purpose of being against them. There and remaining is the kingdom we play pretend, a corpse, a living, but neither fully; we are full of uncertainty, certainty, and everything else inbetween. Involved in assuming and feeling the faiths and beliefs of gods, some care others do not: for some there is the dual-nature of bright and shadow, and for others there is only none but the abyss, abyss of awareness, abyss of knowledge, abyss of chess, abyss of abysses and under it more...man, so it seems, is doomed to knowing all abysses and only one heaven."

—"...Where one can speak one may remain silent, and where one must speak they will say deception: for words through the messenger are fallen from inception to consciousness."

The soul from birth had chosen loneliness and in experience wished to correct itself, but was unable to: or instead it had been through experience forced into itself; Night, in that moment, or all moments but now recognizing, felt as if she should not have been or be, had never been: for the remainder of her character questioned the remains, until the remains had to sorrow for itself.
