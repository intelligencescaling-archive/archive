FANFIC OF THE SUB, THE MIST ACCORDING TO: Pt 9, Finale Pt. One.

***CHAPTER 9: FINALE PART ONE, REMEMBER WHAT YOU ARE (The extra painful chapter to read, I am sorry)***

What is clear is unclear: such is words, such is spaces, spaces of lifes, the manifold garden, if you follow the Red Dragon, you see the Red Welsh Dragon mistake, perhaps become it: no one understands anything anymore of anything they don't want to understand: they underestimate and overestimate themselves, they may look at Night and make an assumption of her character: her family is interchangeable: father now Ashes brother now Verstappen, mother Odysseus, fox's Ice-faced. The document that can be written of a character and contain the character's selfhood with the proven facts of them is still a disservice to the character: experience and remember and learn them.

If Subredia collapsed it will be remade: and so would Night's childhood, her meadow, I myself, I her, no changes? The youth-side of time is that it is experienced solely through the direct connection with our own opinion of it, our perceived loyalty to this normalcy: Subredia's collapse would be unknown, only theorized: Night would have only one childhood and many hypothetical others. See to her and see nothing: no member of Subredia whether active or inactive will remain in its fold forever, as it the place itself will remain as long as one willfully tries to apprehend it.

One can argue 1+1 equals 3: if one is adamant on it, it shall be to thee. No less of others: so of this nature Night is completely relevant in. She found and finds herself as the continual bending of gravity of her own system, her own conflict to her peace: where one may experience through themselves one must also acquaint themselves with the inability to encapture fully anything through oneself, only remain comfortable with the unease of it: the unease of this is clear in Subredia: Subredia is vowed to this, as if it is an absolute certainty that ill-understood facts are facts, Night is herself only when acknowledged by herself not by the friends and I, I myself a betrayar of her personage: what is felt by myself as Faded may be reckoned and announced towards myself as Night.

\- Entrance of course to welcome the host: of house, of the dusk dimension, dimension and place, place the name, or arrest them to clarity!

Nevertheless most people, such as Night's family, were spoken to have come by dusk, as the embers fantastically claimed remained, little orange spots, but they no more, unfindable, that was that.

\- If the deception-affections and false-truths continue we won't know what is and what isn't, and merely of how things are they aren't.

\- Say, she was an unusual child.

\- Say, she was a normal child.

\- Say, there is no child?

\- Again, falsity.

\- A moment: 1999: splintering unions, satellite mini-countries, sensitive histories;  Night and Ashes went to the riverrside near the house: dip the toes and leapt into the water, Ashes first as he was much braver and more "active"; the chess game she had unwillingly walked away from—the chess game to her was not only a play but a took to map the reality of things, such as grass and water—yet it is not water nor grass: a child can fall (not children alone) into the model that the map must derive its language, rules, and "forms" from, but the model does not care for the map, nor can the map be fallen through; Night in mastering the game before she had experienced the model, or instead acknowledging the model was of more certainty, had failed to now add to her already too-certain-too-uncertain-being.

\- So when Ashes leapt into the water she was struck with the feelings dual: one to jump, from curiosity irremovable from anyone, and two from certain desires, platonic, play: the need to experience the model over the construction of the perfect map is latent but potent, thus she leaped after him into the water after considering the choices, and an interesting hypothesis to her as well: if I follow, not knowing how to swim, most likely I will drown, and since I have not experienced drowning I have mapped it. Logically I mustn't, but I admit I wish to see if my assertion of common sense is correct: because it is the natural law, it must be: however, according to the rules of the map, the mind comes before the model, as the model itself derives from the mind, vice versa: image to awareness and awareness to image: will I die if I refuse to acknowledge it?

\- In the water she felt a rush of emotion and sensation: no longer considering the maps or the water math or the flow itself, merely shifting in it: a deep pool and sinking in it she was; she was drowning!

\- With this in mind, she pulled herself out of the water, and halfway was tugged by Ashes.

—: Conversations :—

\[Takes place in a spectral place without name: Night is speaking with whoever she can imagine. Passed into death now? Departed, dear friend, departed, but clinging to speech: because of the way she has described of herself and others they exist solely to her reasons and capacities: they mustn't but they do, and they speak because they can\].

\- "Why must you play the game? It has tortured you," Ashes said.

\- "...I play the game because the board is the only thing before me: think of it as this, brother. My view is a wide range, and yet it can only conceive the checkered design. Why? Because I have decided it. The mind always trumps sight, and emotions trumps logic, as does reason to belief, faith, alone," Night said after a pause.

\- "So you live in your mind entirely? Huh. The synchronization of mind and image, feeling, whether you decide the world as real or false, you have to choose what to think of it. It is important: the world before the man," Ashes mused.

\- "Perhaps. You have a point."

\- Silence.

\- "What I'm trying to say-"

\- "Make your point already," Night frowned.

\- "...How do I explain to you?..." Ashes rubbed his forehead.

\- "Explain what?"

\- "That you are departed, Night. Gone. Departed."

\- "...I knew that."

\- "You did not."

\- "...So what? If I did know I have departed I haven't to myself: if I must welcome darkness I will say loudly, 'now come, darkness!' For in the beginning of this, there was it, and now it has come again. I do not fear the end nor beginning, I do not believe darkness alone was there for them both. Instead I believe I was there, and that is torture in itself. In fact, we do not begin, nor end, just pass into some other place that we don't understand. The more we understand the more we name: the name a garb that once imbued with us departs us readily. It betrays us. The names, the places, the beginnings and ends, time and space: a disservice and service we give them. It is eternity, and we are born seemingly finite: make the most of the endless, perhaps fill it laughter, or fill it with sorrow: nevertheless you were and are there, in eternity," Night said. And there was silence for what else could be said?

—: end :—

The original crime is not of her nature: secondary race, criminal already. The firsts no past to her, no ancestry.

If the Dragon is the defendant, I expect that I am the accuser. For many a year I had gone to sleep knowing myself as Night and I said I was: but why I needed her memory of confirmation haunted me and haunts me still.

Simply, as Night thought, there is no secondary species or many deformity's to man: nothing is meaningless: merely, because of it, because of the continued insistence of our consciousness, the only deformity in man is that he is man.

However, if as claimed the absurdity of the universe is truth, are we apart of that cosmos or separated? The absurdity of man then would be the reasonable form in that place.

Similarly, if Night, she feeling and knowing, is of the supposed secondary race, she is no exile to any race, because in straddling the joys of company of concept, and of the absence selfimposed, she is neither of anything. —"I've overstayed my welcome"—, yes: indeed of this welcome, first seemingly transfixed to many, now none, and if ever something? Reduced.

With or without anyone to mourn her, or me, or no one, Night must remember herself through the ineptitude of words. In this way, it is of admittance that whatever has been said may have been substituted with silence, but nevertheless sounded itself: and of the recollections and recognitions, thoughts and past, they are but part of a selfsame prose that mocks and praises itself in mourning.
