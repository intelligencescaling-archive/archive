The Final Fanfic Of The Subreddit. (The long running series comes to an end)

**CHAPTER 10: GRAND FINALE OF THE MIST ACCORDING TO,**

The mist according to Night began with a dream: thus in dream beginning it must end, refraining from itself to conquer all conclusions: if it must assert reason and logic, as it does assert mankind within itself the 1+1, we are free to deny of itself the answer-truth, give it 3. Life is not a dream but merely the space between the "awareness" of the essential essences that feed and sustain our interment in the present, and the constant state of trying to balance oneself between the past and future: seemingly the one thing that restrains us from the current unknown is our ability to find the reason compassion is the mere tie of sense: a compassion for loneliness, a compassionate soul.

From these thoughts alone and what has been observed, what has been seen, thought itself from the dis-attachment from the clinging image and the perception alien, foreign, yet immutable: frankly Night may as well never have existed: she feels her own mind because of her acknowledgment of the entire world, and yet of such infinity it is comprehended solely by the awareness allowing.

She loved the world but despised all things within it including herself for the sole reason it had come to her grasp from her own mind: possession of the shape and name, to hem them, to free them, and for what was her own perception and her own mind and love that was equal to a nameless shroud of umbral, a shaking veil worn upon the nakedness of the realizations and un-realizations: from this shroud does spring all disformity and beauty that captured by incomplete and tainted egos and consciousness: yet far beneath it, far beneath any Subredian or Redditarian imagination, far from them all, was the self-hood attempt to carve from the endless and nightless-dayless shroud a form fully perceptable and understandable, and thus mistaking the shroud itself with the unfolded entity as the flesh and body of truth and deceit, as the light and dark, inbetweens? For man, and as for those condemned, and perhaps all things which attempts to truth, it was boundless, shapeless and without boundary: it was without anything, without end nor beginning, the game useless, useful, the many-awaited promisies kept and unkept, sprouting from this the all and nothing, the disfigured the whole, and dissolved and arisen same, was Night, herself not from this same stream of all, stream of none, as vanished and as come-here as it? Had she since birth been building and undone, un-here and somewhere so distant, living in the mind alone to console her spirit that demanded the truth instinctively, and was then joined to the stream where no creature must go, and yet many enter: must she relinquish? To strip away veil and soul, external and internal afflictions and affections, to allow the flow, the game-superior's form, to let man and to let world and distance grow from far to none and again unreachable, to let them flow, slip into a nameless state free of the burden of the demands of selfhood, springing out of the shapes and out of the illusion of them...

The game was a shadow of the dead: a shadow of the truth, a shadow of reason, a shadow...

From twilight does the glorious moments arise: from darkness does birth begin, virtually independent from light: for what must be born in the darkness is forgiven of its birthright and expectedly experiences light: oh, to think, to relive, to dream, and to the moment declare oneself in it: in the masks of time there is no wearing or departing of them, only in company following them as they follow one; the lament of experience and the lament of being: being and non-being, together and un-together, formed by thought primordial, decided and lived in, died within, reborn, and it shall be said that whereof there is the spoken and the seen thereof counterpart of it is the silence and the unseen, visible and invisible worlds denying and accepting our assertions and associations of impermanence: from where it had all began one must not return to, for dust and the annulment of material is not demise; of death, successive generations of self-growth proved oneself immune to it, only in body failure appears: if Night imagined this as she felt, it would be better to be of witness to the game, for the game had removed itsslf from notations, rules, and designs: the game had transcended itself as the player transcended it, preventing, presuming, assuming, never living, always living, debating and discussing.

Form is sprung equally from the mind and experience, noted Night. Her form as she knew it was because it was assumed to be that singular shape by others: the shape insisted upon itself. If by this measure of nature true, undeniable, what else?

Night had frankly never been as she had decided to be: as Subredia had been, she would be unable to recognize the future-shapes, as well as the past-shapes: only the present was completely considered as a whole. Altogether they came and hung sharply along walls and little kingdoms: such places existed merely to digest thought: Subredia existed to digest thought, not to think; the game similar was now used by Night as an instrument of model-check and map-exploration, for the self that had long feared and isolated itself was no more.

In the beginning, there was first the sense of an act to be performed: beneath it and after was the withdrawal of the masks of time, the masks of companionship.

Silent was the hill in which personal Rome is laid by she-wolves: silent and immaculate, rays of this eternal sunshine from it, descending to those who deign it, to the witnesses.

She who recognizes herself through the dream, as gaining perception—recognizing from ultimate, infinite, finite, and un-sung words, essential qualities, and yet born and raised as the detachment from this wholeness, is doomed to fail: yet Night did not fail from such thoughts. For where was her own face within this little kingdom cosmos, this vast universe? Where was she when tortured or pained? Where was the reception of dreams, where upon this place? Had she, in sleep or in wake, in walk or idleness, had she ascended from others selfishly to vanish entirely?

Ay, for she who looks and looked, entertaining herself and seeking, waiting for the enraptured senses, caught and entwined between strings unnamable, staring upon the hill, staring deeper still, staring past all else that remained, eyes floating now from the body disassembled, evaporated: staring and hearing all the words and silence conjoin to the endless sound that no game may define, no piece within: the sound carried Night because she let it, she saw that in her dream she had entered it and endured the physical: it was as if there, the word written, was the words she had imagined to console herself from the in-qualities of her character; was it as itself a consciousness grown from her, grown from the sound, same and changed, altering and immovable.

Far within this insight there growing was a face massive, sharp and soft and eyeless seeing: this face was hers and not, it was perception and perceived: the chaos and the Subredian kingdoms subsided before, the sound rising, rumbling serene, the absorption of the disquiet lives, lifted and thrown beneath, away and under abyss and heaven: running along the awakened frescoes depicting their chosen arts, flocked to the sides of the now lone and crowded Night as she slipped from reason to reason, darkness to darkness, Subredia to SUBredia, perception and no more...

Having existed solely for oneself and isolated space, the spirit rebelled.

For in speaking there was none said of truth. The transition of word to thought-clarity was failed, always failed.

Thus in despair of all these natures Night abandoned her meadow and her playing-king, attempting to return to the place where truth stood upon none but itself.

Thus drawn forth the stream, the question, the yearning of the heart, and thus from them and others ascending and staying the indescribable honesty of this beyond-flow: the game cannot be stated here anymore, as it would pervert this place: and our singular view, our character, this Night, having sorrowed and having felt sorrow, having been subjected to what was expected of the external, now realized that from this despair only existed the bastian of hope, as the mind itself, as beyond it: growing her garden, growing and shifting, unknown and drifting, shifting back; for what nature of man and truth that made them incompatible? Night did not know: the flow grew stronger, the stream digesting the seas blue and black, brooks and streams chasing, frescoes and chapels along coming...

The dream was ending as in its place was the sole sufficiency: the unnamable treasure, that to some was freedom, others dream, others physical, some mental...

It was all: it was a word and a flow, a sense without knowing it was sense, a feeling and a knowing, a beautiful and nervous doubt: it was flowing now along with this stream, with Night: she shook, a mighty shake that seeming primordial entrenched its voices and sung out the words beyond, taking beyond: consoling, enduring, being of man's ultimate nature, and opposite of them.

...

The stream continued, and emitted from its vast depths was mingling of light inside darkness, and darkness birthing light: roused and spoken to, spontaneous in their apprehensions of the expression, their detainment and marriage to the feelings, escaping and returning: exalted game, exalted player, exalted imagined things, they found no command here, as this stream borne without doubt the things transcending, beyond our understanding, beyond any comprehension: it was waking, slipping from the sleep of man, slipping from the world and joining with the comforting and harsh glow of the spiritual and the heart: and so, let it roar! Let it roar, this stream, let it surpass, let it assist, let it change, let it become and learn, as it moves from Subredia, as it moves from all: the dissolved and acquitted appearances of the flashing universe, now so small as Night was sped along within the flow-invincible, everything destroyed and remade: it was waking! Now allow slumber to pass, as the end becomes beginning and reverses, as floating inexpressible her thoughts and hopes, and she caught so deep, so deep as to sleep still, awaiting her body the natural shock of awakening from this moment, this darkness, this never-living and true-living, this and its companions, swept along by the stream that roared onward, from tonight to tomorrow...

...She snuggled in the stream floated in the pillows and non-pillows of it: for there, to its destiny, destination approaching, never-knowing, still roaring! Yes, it has moved and moves, taking the dreamer and actor, taking Night: and now it roars once more, becoming more elusive, more gray and red and all, a levitation of concepts, a floating of fire and water, of calm, of man, forthcoming from the stream all words, all wakes, all games: forthcoming  from it she might not grasp it tightly: and yet she has, and yet she conjoined the stream, incomprehensible, unutterable, becoming one with this stream: and from her sleep, from the sublime slumber, she awakens, from the bed rising to the dawns and dusks: to it she awakens to the indescribable world, capturing not one moment but all: for there the moment has passed, the stream has taken her to here: the moment uncapturable, and to the dreamer now, now awoken: it was the moment, the moment ascendant of stream or dreams, evaporating her from that of which knew her as Night: it was pure and true, and she stood to greet it: it was life beyond words.
