#**A QUICK SUMMARY OF THE WORLDBUILDING**

**PRINCIPLES OF EXISTENCE**

The True Almighty, an ancient god who existed before everything, gave the world it's existence.

He died and split apart into two sons, both who have gone through thousands of names and faces.

His shattering caused a schism within existence itself, causing everything to sink deep into a primordial abyss that which it existed upon.

The two sons can be described as the Yin and the Yang.

Fate is the Yin. It is the elusive force, the darkness, cold and silence might of a controlling power. It is an unknown thing, and is ever changing like water. It is murky indeed.

However, Fate's brother is none other than Destiny. And something else entirely: not just Destiny, but the Self.

The Self is Yang. It is a warm, yet contorted and ugly thing. It is the sound of the universe and the absolute force of clarity, authority, and clear order.

The Self, however, can be a muddled mess all at once, bleeding into a third unwanted son.

The third son who does not fall into any of the Yin and Yang is none other than Destiny. While Fate manifests itself as a being, and the Self manifests itself inside every living thing, Destiny is the unlucky one. Always controlled by Fate, and defined by the Self.

As Such, all of existence exists on these simple yet complex maxims of yadda yadda (i dunno how else to describe it)

The Two Sons and the unwanted Son further split apart into the first Old Others, which further split apart upon death, leading to more and more.

Godhood in this world is, in a sense, a constant flux of nature. God's rise and fall with their respective concepts.

There is also two types of godhood: true Godhood, and false Godhood.

An Old Others who has risen from the level of a human is not a true god, merely a human existing at the highest levels of primal power.

An Ascendant Old Other is a true God, having been born with the highest level of existence. Formed by stars, wishes, and dreams, these are nearly incomparable beings to different Old Others

Then, there are Mindless Old Others: creatures born from nature without falling into any of the Three Axioms of the world. They are neither fated to live purposefully, nor are they capable of Self.

Comprehension is the catalyst of madness, kindness is the foundation of self destruction, selfishness is the root of all consuming paranoia, and ignorance is the foundation of an ignominious death.

True knowledge is death, basically. The unknown can actively seek you out, it can find you and stare at you from some point in existence.

The fear of the unknown is key. Knowing when to fear something is survival.

**THE WORLD AT LARGE**

The world is divided into several seas, all of which are infinite in size, containing it's own unique concepts, characteristics, archetypes, and multiverses.

Each Sea is basically a reality of it's own. Divided by an ancient Barrier formed by the Orthodox Gods.

The Sea in which the Subredian Investigation Association comes from is none other than the Western Sea, a new and small Sea.

However, there are other Seas, such as the Scarlet Sea, a place nearest to the abyss itself, or the East Sea, a place of worship and refuge.

Beneath the Seas are the infinite Abyss. The Abyss is the emptiness of existence itself.

And all of existence is slowly sinking inside of the Abyss.

There are several kingdoms we know of: East Subredia, Empire Subredia, Kingdom Sunhoset, Conclave Of Laminel, and the West Subredia

(Sieben is from Kingdom Sunhoset, hailing from the Endrica Region. A place bathed in endless sunshine and harsh conditions.)

(ReverseFlash is from East Subredia.)

Each Kingdom has fought each other throughout history, smashing through small holes in the Barrier to fight and win land in entirely different realities.

————————————————————————————————————————

**Existences**: A various amount of information that withholds powerful deities that are not Old Others. These things are actually living things, but are incapable of reaching the might of an Old Others.

Existences are truly a fraudulent weapon, as they cannot be used by humans, but are never strong enough for Old Others. They are only strong enough for Divined.

**Divined**: A human blessed by an Old Other. They can give their bodies to the ir respective Old Other as Vessels, ruining their minds for the sake of bringing their God into reality without any necessary chants, rituals, or needed actions.

**Facets**: A primordial concept that has been captured and given to a being. Minimum's Facet is related to the concepts of Plagues and Degradation.

A Facet is, in itself, a true concept. It is the essence of the universe itself, placsd into a form that can be wielded and understood.

**Grimoire**: A simple thing which can grow to extreme power. A person can use a Grimoire to contain chants, ritualistic magic, sigils, or artifacts of power. Grimoires are Swiss Army knives for almost everything.

One can use more and more powerful Grimoires by adding onto it with one's Self, upgrading it and giving it a 'character'. What does this mean? A Grimoire basically can become nearly a concept, but it is an illusion. It is only a fake concept, something that shouldn't even exist.

**Sigils**: ancient history: times frozen in a moment. I can have a Sigil about someone stabbing something, and then use said Sigil to instantly cause said action to materialize into the current moment.

Higher Character Sigils can contain even the moments when Gods die.

The more powerful a Sigil, the longer it takes to use it. The most powerful of them takes over 100 years to even have an effect. Such, a Grimoire can be used to lower this time.
