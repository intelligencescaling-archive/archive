(For Own_Presentation! This is the "version" of him in the extended scd fanfic verse! Also, I had to use Sieben as some introductory guy)

...However, Sieben had not noticed the young man appear behind him. Dressed in an imperial regalia, complete with a red cravat and black flower pin, he stood out in a rather grand way.

"...You...?" Sieben said, after a pause. His brows furrowed. He had to stay here and await the others to arrive.

The other young man's lips turned downwards slightly, his clouded eyes becoming dangerously dim.

"You're a Divined Of The Old Other Of Fire Sea, yes?

"You will try to stop me using several half-Facet's, correct? They are the Facets Of Verging Death, Temporal Space, and Bubonic Healing, yes?"

"How...did you know about that?" Sieben asked, as he warily lifted his revolver.

"...All according to my plans. This is reasonable."

The young man observed the gun, before he suddenly pressed his forehead against the barrel, smiling broadly. His clouded eyes cleared completely, revealing a pale glare of morbid fascination.

"You're crazy!" yelled Sieben.

Although the young man did not answer, he seemed almost disappointed that the formed had refused to act upon his intentions. He looked at the barrel, before chuckling slowly.

"It's not loaded, isn't it?" he laughed. Then, with a laborious sigh, he smacked the revolver out of Sieben's hand.

It was with this action that he resumed his monologue. Or, Sieben thought. For the young man's countenance suddenly became icy.

"Tell me: where are they?"

He asked.

"Who?" was the former's answer.

A small laugh.

"Don't play stupid with me. I've already planned this out, and it's meaningless to act astray from my predictions."

Sieben stiffened, as he reached for his Grimoire.

"Are you...some kind of philosopher?" he blurted at the young man.

"No. Not really. I ask questions, you all interpret them however you like. I can be an idiot, a genius, a hypocrite, a lover, or a loser depending on what you think I mean." The young man responded, as he calmly kicked the table's leg, causing it to fall, thus removing the Grimoire from Sieben's reach. Sieben's bandages began to hurt at this.

"If I ask a philosophical question, such as: why am I here? Would it matter? All that I know is that once you speak, you're going to be put on trial."

The young man paused, checking his stopwatch and adjusting his cravat.

"You're born on trial and you die on trial, it's all the same. But it doesn't matter to me. I'm living like I won't be alive tomorrow."

With a shrug, he tossed a lighter onto the center of the room, calmly lifting Sieben's Grimoire from the floor.

Sieben could only entertain his interest for so long. He carefully answered this...other young man.

"...Bit much?..."

The young man chuckled.

"Much? No. Not at all. The trial doesn't give you much. Even if it does, you're bound to lose it all."

*Is he referring to life as a trial? that's certainly unique. From a philosophical standpoint, anyway. Then again, maybe he is on trial. Maybe he's a madman.* reasoned Sieben. He felt the bandages on his face throb furiously.

At that moment in time, the room began to shift, turning and bending in black, yellow, and white fire. The clothing on the young man's body changed color entirely, going from black and gold to red and copper.

However, in the middle of his chanting, the room burst into a thousand small crystals, revealing the host of the Subredian Investigation Association!

Sieben gasped as he almost slipped into some unknowable abyss, but he felt the strong grasp of Gabs's hand on his forearm, harshly dragging him out. He was passed along to Proof-Jello, who promptly tossed him to Zesty Low.

"...hm. The Subredian Investigation Association enter, breaking my spell. However, they unknowingly..." the young man looked at the gathered members, suddenly grinning.

"They unknowingly walk into an alternate Sea."

The world surged forward, obeying the thoughts of the young man.

Although faced with such uncertainty and extreme danger, Minimum_Ad stepped forward and, with a flick of his hand, summoned a horde of Grotesque Birds to consume the Sea.

"Own_Presentation...so it really was you? Couldn't bear to see how your little plans went awry?..." Minimum said, sighing. A bead of sweat formed on his head.

They all knew, that the young man before them was a walking terror in his own.

In their minds, they all remembered the warnings given to them by the Mime and the Marquis.

*He is The Catalyst Of Apocalypse.*

*One of the Four Harbingers Of The End.*

*The Hand That Rejects Fate.*

*The Winged Son Of Inferno.*
