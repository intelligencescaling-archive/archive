#"CHAPTER SOMETHING":  The Father, The Apple

(THE ART IS NOT BY ME, art is by "renshengruxi310" on danbooru. Yes I reused the same image.)

The Old Other Of Fire had been forcefully ejected from his vessel.

Then, after that, Sieben's husk began to slowly plummet.

Minimum chased after the falling husk of a body, and reached forward to grab it from the abyss. However, it was too late. The body of "Sieben" was consumed and grabbed by all sorts of mysterious hands coming from the sky above, dragging him up and up.

...

Sieben felt a hand on his shoulder, and looked up to the man.

GreenToastStone smiled calmly, before turning around and placing the cadaver down on the marble floor.

The youth had barely come to comprehend everything, and only understand that he was staring at the manifestation of Fate.

*Fate. Fate can talk. It can walk. It can be seen. It can see me.*

There was something utterly terrifying about this. Not that Sieben could properly think at all, for he was too afflicted by fear and desperation to even coherently grasp this.

GreenToastStone calmly produced an apple from thin air, and, as he spun it in his lanky hand, seemed to notice something unseen by the youth.

"This apple," the man said, "represents Destiny. If I bite it so, I can change the very course and shape of anyone's life. Such is Fate."

Then, after announcing this, he handed the apple to Sieben, his limpid eyes focusing on the youth's confused features. He grasped onto Sieben's shoulder with a fatherly expression, and yet he seemed detached all at once. His face was placid, his eyes were placid. He was ultimately a mystery, a mystery who could not be understood.

GreenToastStone, in a gentle tone that still sent shivers, said.

"Eat the apple. Eat it, and you can gain the ability to change your friend's fates and destinies. Once this is done, I will inhabit you, and you will be 'Fate' itself, wielding the power of infinity."

Sieben almost ate the apple upon hearing the first part of the man's words. However, he stopped himself as he attempted to listen and understand the second half.

On one hand, he could eat the apple, change the course of the past, present, and future, saving all he loved. Fate, as he had seen, was the ultimate power in all existence.

On the other, if he did eat the apple, he would become 'Fate,' letting himself be used as a vessel for this deity. If he was to become 'Fate', would he even be able to care for them at all? Fate, as he had seen, was always cruel and mysterious.

He had viewed himself as a person who truly tried to care, and someone who tried to help everyone. And yet, right now, he didn't want to even accept the notion of "helping." All he wanted was to be himself. Not 'Fate'. He wanted to be that boy from the lower half of Subredia, that youth who everyone turned to for help. The distinguished young gentleman who delighted the souls of many.

Yet, he wanted to be selfish, to be helpful, to be too many things all at once.

He wanted to be a human. He realized he had sacrificed his own body to the Old Other Of Fire. What did he have left to give?

"If I choose both?" He asked suddenly, the words having slipped from his mind.

"Can I choose both and leave a few of their effects behind...?"

"You cannot bite the apple twice all at once, yes?" said the man in response.

Sieben tossed the apple across the marble floor, letting it smash into the cadaver.

"I...I can't. I cannot bite it." The youth admitted, weakly.

"Hm. And why is that?" the man asked,  walking towards the apple and taking it in his hand once more. He turned, focusing his gaze heavily. Although he seemed calm as always, his eyes were more colorful than before.

"I...why cannot I eat it? Because..." Sieben looked down, at his hands.

"I cannot eat it because I wish to stay alive,"

GreenToastStone gently moved to place the apple back into Sieben's hand.

His robes shown brightly underneath the sickly light, and his hair seemed to be both the deepest shade of brown and the most delicate of gold. However, Sieben seemed to realize he wasn't staring at another man's face. Little by little, he could barely make out small details of his own visage, and his own physique.

"How interesting. To be frank, even if you wish to stay alive, you are most likely dead. And so is everyone else. If the other parties weren't so adamant on resisting my desire to revive, I can assure none of this would have occurred."

"With that being said," he said, looking at the apple in Sieben's hand.

"The apple. You willingly let yourself become a vessel, thus invalidating your argument. You used your body as a shield for others. Every action you have taken is a contradiction to that statement."

Sieben stiffened. His head hurt again. The apple seemed ever more tantalizing, with the promise of saving everyone. His mind wandered...perhaps he could even turn back time, and prevent the apocalypse! Fate was indeed infinite power.

The words echoed over and over in his head, until, slowly and hesitantly, he raised the apple to his lips.

However, before he took a bite, he paused.

*How do I know if I am not being controlled by fate to eat this? Is it my destiny? No. If every action is planned out by fate, what can I even do but obey?*

He realized how hopeless this all was. Whether 'Fate' did indeed control him, he had no choice. And if 'Fate' did not, what could he do? He wished to save everyone and everything so dearly, and yet he wished to remain human all together.

"Will I have a heart still? Would I be able to still care, to protect?" The youth blurted again.

No answer, only a quiet, detached gaze that told him everything.

...

In his consciousness, he heard a single voice rumble a powerful and mysterious chant.

Turning his head, his eyes fell onto Zz_Zz9he, who seemed horrified.
Then, he noticed the Lonely Crow.

...Where was he? He had vanished entirely, leaving behind only a single feather.

He heard Zz_Zz9he mutter something underneath his breath.

Whatever had just occurred had completely overwhelmed the Apocalypse.

And ReverseFlash had an idea on what it was and who it was.

...

Morgan also felt such a sensation, as if something was pulling at her dress.

IfTeaz had stopped moving entirely, and seemed transfixed on an entirely new and enigmatic phenomenon: something bright and beautiful, yet sinister hands came forth. Black hands.

Morgan could not even utter a word. To her, the Apocalypse had stopped entirely.

Indeed, the Apocalypse, having begun so violently, seemed to slow and eventually halt, grinding into something else.

"IfTeaz, do not let your guard down, not yet anyway...something is off." Morgan cautioned.

However, IfTeaz did not answer. She had now noticed something coming from inside the horde of majestic and black hands. A body. The body in itself was unremarkable, being a mix between a burnt husk and a corpse. A painted sun had appeared, scorched and tainted, blasphemous to the eyes.

And yet, she had seen this body. From records, or from ancient statues.

IfTeaz spoke, her words whispered and qouted.

"And thus, the Third Harbinger descends onto the world..."

She was correct. The Third Harbinger, the Hand That Fate Judges, the Crawling Mind Of Illogic, and the Loophole In Sense...

MathemticianOne had come. In the grasp of his endless black hands, almost biblically, they held Sieben's lifeless husk.
