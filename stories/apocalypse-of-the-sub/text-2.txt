#"CHAPTER SOMETHING": Ignition

...Sieben opened his eyes, wearily sitting up.

"You've recovered." A voice said to him. The voice was not stern, but still commanding, and it had a sense of greater age.

"...I...I suppose?" Sieben answered, absentmindedly rubbing his forehead.

"Good. Good. One of Minimum's companions, yes?" the voice continued, curious. Sieben was no stranger to anyone, but this voice rang no bell in his head. In fact, he would have never guessed it was the librarian from down the street had he not turned to look.

"...Oh?...Morgan, right?" He said, sitting up slowly. He had never had a look at her, as she always seemed to be hidden away from his eyes. She was beautiful, yes, but Sieben felt a somewhat unearthly thing about her. Was it the limpid eyes, which so resembled Minimum's and the other Divined? No. Her eyes were cloudy...or were they clear? He could not tell.

"Correct." She replied, flipping through a rather thick book aimlessly.

He observed the room around him: an odd collection of knickknacks, books, and Grimoires with unreadable text. However, thanks to his history studies, he was able to locate an object that stimulated a memory.

A pipe. He recalled a small conversation he and Minimum had, not too long ago. It was during the waxing stage of the moon.

...{the memory ensues}

*"You're from the Third Barrier War?" Sieben had asked. Minimum, who was staring outside of a window, seemed to freeze up. He slowly turned his head, raising his crooked eyebrow, putting his pipe away.*

*"Yes...how did you learn that?" He said. Sieben rubbed his temples, pursing his lips. Although Minimum never put on any airs, neither noble or criminal or powerful, his words resonated in Sieben's head. Over and over. How did he learn it?*

*"I...I'm not sure. It came to me in a dream." Sieben laughed nervously, awkwardly. "It's nothing, Mr. Ad. I'm sure it's just a matter to be forgotten." He gave him a small, warm smile.*

*"...No. no. It's not a matter to be forgotten." Minimum started, with a heavy sigh. His dark, limpid eyes of godhood showed clearly under the cosmic and strange moonlight. "That war was long ago...I barely remember it. I was a child then. It's best not too forget the past, not because we cannot, but because we must try to stop it from happening again." He calmly rested on his longsword.*

*He would turn fully to Sieben, his face a monolith. "Sieben. Although we try to remember, we always forget. This place will not be remembered, by **anything**. The barrier will fall again, and we will all play our part in it. We humans, we gods, we never learn. We're in an endless cycle, repeating the same tragedies with different tastes and peoples, but never, ever stopping."*

*"...I...I'm sorry for what you saw," was all Sieben could say. Minimum looked at him, both with pity and respect.*

*"you're much to kind to me. You're much to kind to the world, Sieben." The latter said to the former.*

*"Even now you only seem to care for others wellbeing. Maybe it is fate you came to the Investigation Association..." he trailed off, looking at the golden eyed teen.*

*There was a pause. Sieben looked down, before timidly asking, "Is it my fate to fight?"*

*Minimum looked back at the moon. His answer was simple. "Though you can know a little about your fate, you are still helpless against it. Fate, and fighting it, is like drowning. But do not worry, I will protect you." he gave Sieben a reproachful glance.*

"Are you well?" Morgan suddenly asked, snapping Sieben out of his thoughts and memories. He had forgotten entirely where he was! With a timid but warm smile, he quickly reassured her he was well. He didn't want anyone to worry of his health, he told her. He should be the one to worry about theirs.

"In such a short amount of time, you have completely opened your soul to me. How...unusual. Nevermind, nevermind." she murmured, watching him. Staring deep into him, perhaps.

"Oh? Ah, sorry Miss. I do that. It's rather odd, yes." Sieben quickly spoke, rubbing the back of his head.

"How sweet of you," was all she said, before sitting up. She smoothed the small wrinkles in her grand dress, before snapping her fingers, changing the room's construction entirely.

Sieben gasped audibly, and he almost fell over had not another hand propped him back up.

*Witches are truly remarkable!* he thought, smiling like a child and looking with wonder.

 *I should tell Minimum and the others about this!*

Then he paused. Where were the others?...what had happened?

However, before he could ask Morgan any questions, he felt hands rise up from an abyss unforeseen, clasping at his arms, his hair, his face, his legs. Sieben yelled in anguish as they dug into him, tearing off his bandages.

Morgan, on the other hand, seemed to be focused on the ever expanding streaks of cursed red stars and sanguine water. The immense and horrid gazes of "something" had completely been forced to focus on her.

"Ah. So it begins," she idly lifted her hands, attempting to help release Sieben from his entanglement. Unfortunately, although she tried mightily, Sieben was dragged deep underneath the floor before she could use her Facet.

And, upon this problem, "they" had begun to materialize infront of her, in large and small sizes, abstract and mysterious, glowing like sigils and stars whilst consuming them.

...

Own_Presentation smashed through the Scarlet Sea, then through the abyss beneath it.

The red haired scourage yelled in pain as he felt the longsword of Minimum pierce his shoulder. With a gruff motion, he pulled his own shoulder off, fleeing farther away from what seemed to be a force of nature.

Minimum's eyes burned through the Sea, causing the cosmic and strange water to evaporate from the sheer weight of his focused gaze.

"The Barrier itself is not an object, nor a tangible "thing". Instead, it is an essential layer of existence itself. To pierce it would break apart the seperation between Seas: between history and present, and between the possible and the impossible. Folly."

"Oh, do tell me your foolish oaths!" Said Own_Presentation. "Do not be bothersome with all of your oaths. Sooner or later, the Barrier shall fall."

Minimum paid no attention to him. "Hence the reason why the barrier has to be maintained."

At that moment, Own_Presentation tore off his outer disguise, revealing his red wings and long, majestic crimson hair. He pulled the Spear Of GreenToaststone out from his Grimoire. He was beautiful, yet, like a fallen star, destructive.

Gabs and the other Divined of the Subredian Investigation Association, namely Mediocre_Buy, and Perdido, had only just arrived through the fissure, and they witnessed Own_Presentation raise his Spear. For a moment, his sheer presence caused them to falter in thought. He was the blasphemous prince of the Seas, yes, but never had one expected him to rise so gracefully.

Minimum continued his speech, speaking his words as a teacher facing an impudent student would. It was quite clear he tired from pursuing Own_Presentation so fervently.

"To break it would cause not only catastrophic effects, it would most likely ensue in a war between the various nations."

"The Eastern Subredia, Western Subredia, the Guilds. Just to name the more earthbound parties."

"One must also consider the possibilities of the Four Harbingers appearing: and also the Heralds, the winged descendants of the Four Harbingers. Even then, Divined would also appear."

With this, he finished his speech. Minimum raised his longsword. His clipped wings burst violently from his back, revealing the stumps of what once was. He sighed,
but, with an air of duty, said,

"Thus, I, the Second Harbinger Of The End, pronounce you no longer worthy of your birth right."

However, as the two would move to converge at a sluggish pace, a single black feather fell from above them.

A crow's feather.

 ...

The hooded young man busied himself with tasks, watching the stars and the endless moons without interest.

Although he was attempting to put a calm face, it kept breaking into worry, into utter despair.

"What shall I do? What must I do? I promised my assistance to the Iron Witch and her lady," said Far_Transition.

"And yet...if I do, I will permit him to die."

*Even if I can see fate, I cannot change it. Fate, in it's terrible design and nature, only obeys his word. Even she cannot help me. If I were to destroy fate, it would not be...*

However, Far_Transition did not realize the man appear behind him. He had no time to even rationlize when he **appeared** at all.

The Omniscient Scriptures Gatherer had come.

Zz_Zz9he's limpid eyes shown like a child's. He opened his mouth, and, with the cadence of practiced authority, calmly said,

 "Own_Presentation dies in the Scarlet Sea. With his death, the nation of East Subredia cannot stand idle, and sends a preemptive push into the Barrier, led by SprinklesWarm and EnviromentalNo. This leads to the breakdown of stability of our reality, and escalates this 'lower' war into an apocalyptic event."

He paused, scrutinizing Far_Transition's horrified expression. his apprentice's eyes were darting back and forth, and he could easily discern the uncertainty and confusion in them.

"Can you stand idly and let my writing unfold, letting me teach you the ways of godhood? Or will you plunge in those depths, participating in this event?"

However, no response.

Zz_Zz9he continued, finally stating with a simple set of instructions.

"Will you watch the world burn, or will you help burn it?"

Far_Transition took a deep breath. He slowly removed the blindfold from his eyes. He felt it in his hands, wringing it. He could follow his promises, yes. But, he also wished to save his friend from fated death as well. Thus, slowly, almost painfully, he uttered.

"...I...I will stand idly by."

Zz_Zz9he nodded, without smiling nor frowning.

"With this final action..."

He paused, almost theatrically. His eyes were spinning, and around him thousands of mirrors materialized, revealing realities and futures to him.

"So it seems, the apocalypse begins."
