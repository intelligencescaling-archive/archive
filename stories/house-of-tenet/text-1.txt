CHAPTER ???: LOOP 200

One can say that in life there are certain things that resemble those of the past, those rhat have happened: it would seem that in life, however short or long, there is always a sense of time. For if one is to sleep they become the masters of a world without reason, without time: such a shame that to wake is to end timelessness.

...
...

"...Oi." A familiar voice without reason: a gruff voice. A man's voice, yes, that is who it belongs to, but unnamed for now it remains, until the eyes open to see.

"...Он умер." A familiar voice without reason: a stolid voice: a rarely humorous sentence from that voice. A woman's voice this time. A groggy lifting of his eyes reveals two figures: a man, wearing a bascinet—Section. The woman, whos almost pallid hair that painfully glints in the dull, strobing light, skin equally pale to it, shifts weight from leg to another—so it be now known this woman is Night.

Now the waves felt below the weak boat's bottom lessen: Verstappen uses his damn eyes and sees around him figures shaded by the dim-light above, no good light, a carnival entertaining light which flickers and attempts to salvage itself every time the strange boat lifts up to cut a wave: it's rigging is haphazardly designed, with azure and gold standards all over, muskets sticking out of the sides. To think the commander is that boy-aye not boy, Verstappen slaps his face to wake himself up: he fails.

The thin youngster with brown messes of curls before their forehead and slipping to the edges of the eyes, by the badly edged cut now comes walking, presently nudging the man Verstappen, and oh! Hear the poor man, he gasps by the serious kicking-nay, no savagary but unabated youthful strength. How he pines for energy, Verstappen...

"Aye, stop kickin' the the old dog," another voice, well how obvious, yes, how oddly funny it is!

 "Manager," Ice directs this to Night, "how long before Corporation responds?"

"...Give or take a few more hours," Night says, seemingly pre-occupied.

"Also Ashes, stop Lobo from killing Verstappen," mumbles Ice, hugging her sides with the arms that seemingly stretch out, a hawk-branch-resting in nature, she stiffens with the ocean spray and digs her big heels with the standard boots.

"No, no. It's fun watching it—," Ashes says, now new again. This be Ashes yes, those limbs belonging to the stouter man: he who has his arms stuffed into his pockets, the standard black greatcoat weathered on his form it resembles not of leather and more of the barnacle-metal of the ship the group stay upon: unto it seemingly sutured a kind of safe ossuary and passage of a devil-less heart and spirit that allows no command to dominate it for long. Eventually Ashes nudges Lobo's leg from Verstappen's side.

Section discusses plans with Doggo, while Night watches on, her face extremely melancholic. Tired even. More tired than usual, as she's a certified caffeine addict who can fight sleep off for three days doing paperwork for the Head.

Verstappen looks to speak, "Thanks, uh, Ashes."

"Thanks? To that man?" Doggo says: his leaves bristle. His entire body is an amalgamation of nature, branches, bushes and poking sticks, with flowers. A dapper mess of flowers stuffed into a suit.

"You hurt me." Ashes grunts.

"I don't mean to." Doggo says.

"But ya' keep doin' it," Ashes pinches his nose bridge.

"Maybe you're damn sensitive," Doggo says, softly. Ice gives him a gentle knock on the back of his head.

Section taps his helmet-head: he says, "I think maybe you two should focus. It's just me though. Crazy thought. I know. Calming down? Really crazy..."

Lobo adjusts his sailor's cap: no shrill voice from he but an announcement tinged by a tickling-pouring of vitality; hear him speak, "...Landfall in three minutes! We expect the seas to be strong around these parts, more of I expect: and uh, there's some horde on the shore."

"They look a little to the west now..." Night mumbles, leaning her head back. She seems to already **know** about that detail.

"...Where are we?..." Verstappen mumbles. He feels awfully hung-over. Ice puts a hand on his shoulder; "...I do not know myself. Hopefully they have good beaches over there, right?"

"Oi, they' doon' got any beaches thatta' be nice over there." Ashes says, with a laugh.

"...Where's Night?" a young, far more rough skinned man says, appearing from a hatch in the deck. He carries with him an obscenely long rifle, with golden inlays, poking Night's head with it. "Odysseus," she says, slowly, flicking a cigarette at his emerging body, "Please refrain from...wasteful time management."

Odysseus sighs with a nod of his head. He says to her, "I will! I will. Though, you seem pretty stressed. It's painted on your face."

"...Offhand remarks rewards you with extra paperwork," Night mutters, searching the coat's pockets for more cigarettes. Odysseus groans as Lobo, grinning like an clown, takes a look at his map (and receives a light smack on the back of his head from the former).

"Don't hit each other now," Ice chides, interjecting her arm between Lobo and Odysseus.

"The lady's right. Hitting each other will only make you feel worse when we go through Layers," Doggo adds.

"...Speaking of Limbo, where are we? Right now?" Verstappen says. "I forgot the plans..."

"We're going to Nagumo." Lobo says, nursing the back of his head.

"...Well I'll be damned." Ashes says.

"We are damned," Odysseus says. Lobo laughs boredly. Overly serious that youngster is. "Take a look: the shorelines a mess of Other Flesh!"

"Just don't fall overboard. I'm smashing through." Lobo explains, without hesitation.

The hordes of rotting flesh gather as the group nears. Odysseus opens fire on them, picking out a few of the taller things; they are a gross mess of tangled limbs, tangled heads, eyes and teeth. Other Flesh, all you need to know about them is that A•they are bad. B•they'll probably kill you. And C•or do something far worse. They are corruptions of the mind.

"Everything should be goin' alright," Ashes says, taunting a few with his middle finger.

Night sighs: she looks at her watch.

"...Goddamnit." She says, quietly breathing in smoke from her cigarette.

"What?" Lobo asks, frowning. He's focusing on steering his "ship" (the term is Ether Dreadnought, Verstappen recalls. A personal item from Lobo's peoples, it manifests itself according to the owners perceptions...and Other Flesh? Corrupted? They are, what again? Obviously they are dangerous right, right—

His thoughts are cut short by the sight of Night leaping off of the ship.

Lobo yells out a string of colorful curses, while Ashes almost leaps after her, stopped by Doggo, who is lifting a net with one arm already. Ice reaches out too late, watching their "manager" hurtle headfirst towards those things. Section starts to manifest his ability, but is stopped by a man slamming into his side. The mysterious figure leaps after Night.

Lobo recklessly turns his ship **down**, chasing after the falling night (and that mysterious figure. Even if he doesn't know who it is he's still saving them).

...
...

*I've been chasing her through each loop she tries.*

*Night.*

*It always starts like this.*

*She jumps off of the ship into a horde of those creatures.*

*They follow.*

*Usually they die. Sometimes they get far.*

*...I've forgotten my name through each loop.*

*The only thing that is mine are the clothes on my back: they belong to a guy named. Nagumo. Maybe that's me. I think it's me. That's wishful thinking.*

*...It doesn't matter.*

*This is loop two...? Two hundred.*
