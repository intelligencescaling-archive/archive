Fanfic Of The Sub Pt. 2, House Of Tenet + an edit of Nobody

CHAPTER ???: LOOP TEN.

**MY NAME IS NAGUMO**

THREE DAYS.

**終末の前日**.

—————————

...I tumble through time after Night, colliding with a floating, ethereal object. It fuses into me, causing the loop to shrink, to contract, bursting and collapsing without reason.

Something's wrong. A variable I didn't know, didn't notice. Whatever it is, it's agony.

Sanctity low, Mental almost broken. Sinner Salt almost out: such things are necessary for a Stranger to use their abilities. Take it like this: Sinner Salt is the unique power inherent, and to use it one must use their minds, their Mental, and their Sanctity, the essential life force.

As I crash through time, loops begin to fade away.

Night grabs my arm and tugs me through a closing window.

Without a word, I take out my gun.

So does she.

The place where we are is turning a shade of red: clouds, I think they are, are splitting apart, becoming fleshy.

Then it reveals itself: a sprawling gray scape of buildings. Strange. Foreign.

I expect Loop 201 to start. But it doesn't.

Somehow we're all alive: a crash registers me to the realization that the ship, and, inherentlt, the crew, live. Still falling and chasing me. Chasing Night.

**How are they alive? Did something change? Come to think of it: what did I absorb earlier?**

**A presence, probably.**

**I don't know anymore. I'm just mindlessly chasing after a phantom. My head is in outer space, my body is elsewhere, only my determination is here at all.**

**I am nothing except determination. I am not chosen by any means of fate; fate does not choose, it happens.**

...

Lobo, through the twisted flow of this indescribable chaos, maneuvering his ship with great care, pierced into a calm sky that quickly plunged again to hellish conditions. He felt fear, yes, but despite such harsh conditions his heart had grown accustomed to that agonies: one can say that, to live on the sea, to flow along, is to become acquainted with the numerous and varied conditions which torment our spirits: of the encumbrances of physical and soulful nature we soon come to know as our most constant companion.

Ice gently kept Odysseus's head down, her hand pressed against the back of it. He didn't object, really, but only grumbled slightly.

Ashes was beside Lobo as he helped steady the boy, hands on the shoulders. A pair of two eyes were needed for the excursion after their falling commander (and the mysterious shadow).

Doggo stood on the bow of the ship, arms crossed, staring the maelstrom in the eye (if his vegetable, leaf body had eyes). He casually looked at his map, before saying, "wherever we are, Night better be alive."

Section stepped beside him, calmly. The storm mattered nill to him.

And Section spoke...

...

—————————

**MY NAME IS SECTION**

—If we are to perish amongst this strife let it be.—

—And so I say to Doggo on the deck of the 'ship', "Are you scared of the uncertain?".—

—He nods. Pensively. Sternly.—

—And so I say to him, "there is nothing to fear: for to have faith in our good brothers and sisters we triumph over tribulations and evils; and if evil remains in our hearts we can see that although darkness is forever present, there is light as well."—

—Ashes says to me, "The fuck does that mean?" As he cowers, takes cover from the danger of the intensified storm.—

—Thus I say to him, "I decide to have faith. I ask of you the same. It's better to believe in something than nothing at all. There is always meaning, and it is only the mind that decides whether that is true or false."—

—Ashes shrugs. "Fair 'nough."—

—The 'ship' of light lurches. I will describe it now to you. The ship of Lobo's mind is a creation that is idyllic. Strong bowed and good wood upon it possibly with the way of past-ing Adam and Eve's earliest conceiving present in the form-hull of the vessel.—

—We press onward, passing through nameless voids and lines, shifting into a station; train place of return, instead we return no more to anywhere.—

—Onward, onward! Have faith! Press on!—

—And so we do!—

—One can say even though we've passed through this storm, there will always be another. Time is not a flat plate that spins backwards and forwards: it spins in whatever direction it chooses.—

—Although I extol faith I confess I have none of it.—

—I feel stretched: thin, and with holes. I feel as if we've done this chase hundreds of times before. I'm going insane, aren't I? How fearful.—

—The body is three conjunctions of energies: Mental, Sanctity, and Heretic Salt. We are all born with abilities: to use and to increase the power of them we must sacrifice Mental, our stability, our understanding, our independence from time and space, and with Sanctity we sacrifice vitality. Heretic Salt is the channeling power: without it we cannot use our abilities. However that Salt destroys our place in the here and now. We become something of a past thing. Sometimes, Heretic Salt keeps memories alive, illusions of a person, as long as that person's memory exists in our minds. So, we don't know if half our group is **here**, or they've long since **died** and their memory is just here with us, corporeal.—

...

—————————

**MY NAME IS LOBO**.

Did I tell you how much the sea tastes of salt? Salt of sea ever present yes, tasting in my mouth since I can recall, regrettably I have no liking to salty foods, a common courtesy of my graced mouth, aye, authority of the authentic culinary skills of Doggo who sprinkles heaps of SALT.

The falling pumice substance from the torrent whirlpool of torrential potency descending unto us creates small holes in the air, difficult to breathe conditions and no longer safe.

Follow, following without heed our captain falling, a beg of pardon to my poor english, an appartus of tongue like salt I see everywhere and I taste terribly of it unable to digest, aye, alas, one merely shifts through the substance that resembles sand.

I be young, youngest in the group and half-a-height of most of em', learned my skills of talk from Section (goodman be praised), a medium difficulty of being intelligent in a group of halfwits and forlorn intellects, confess't methinks they have a problem.

Now we are chasing Night, I feel degrees of guilty as she leapt off my ship. Peril to the crew I see and I must focus to press on.

We land between a fake city and a reminiscent village-folk gathering (midsummer festivities) that I know not of, no map marking, so her we chase vanishes and we are left somewhere we ought NOT to be.

They'll turn US into ABOMINATIONS.

Must keep going, wherever she is.

Not one man left behind, I cannot let that happen.

...

—————————

**MY NAME IS NIGHT**

I'm known by many names: I don't recognize most of them. I've forgotten a few.

If I've long since lost any interest in anything I can tell you this: I do not know why I chase loop after loop. I'm doing it from a recess of altruism that refuses to die. There's this vague recollection of pain, somewhere. In my chest. In my neck and stomach, too. But, maybe, from old habits, I continue to chase through time.

I go by Night, mostly.

I chainsmoke regrettably: those things are like coffee for me, to be frank.

Everywhere there is dark, except my crew.

Perhaps there isn't any tomorrow: or, more accurately, the tomorrow is not promised to any of us. Not them, especially.

...

So I hold a gun to Nagumo's head: he's been chasing me since loop ten. Somehow splintered off from the others.

He's traveled through time without any pretenses. Impressive, almost. If it weren't for the obvious decay on his body.

How is he still alive?

I do know our freefall through this shattering of space will end soon.

"What do you want?" I say, over the din.

He struggles to register words. Nagumo blinks. He says to me, "I...I don't know."

He doesn't know? Heh? He seems confused. Out of place, even.

I manage to shake his grip off of my greatcoat, tossing him down into the abyss of time, watching his body get torn. However, I notice that the entire time, his eyes are locked onto me: enraged?...huh. I feel sharp points in my head and spine.

I land somewhere inbetween the "here" and "now,". Stage two commences...whatever I've written in my notes.

I'm out of cigarettes. I search my pockets, nothing. I confess, I look frantic for those damn things.

They keep me sane.

...

—————————

**MY NAME IS FADED.**

Faded lost his head to his stomach, as one say that the secret universe sutured unto his entity as a cave imitation that of which flows the way of time and creation, coursing inside and out. His eyes were meant to liberate his spirit of fate, of the unnamable...however, it was folly, alas. His head turned inward, replaced with a crude, gigantic eye, into a place where it was never meant to be.

...the Director awaits his action.

All he can say is, "I see." That is all.

Reducing the eyes to mere apparatus.

Doomed Director. Journeying to find the fallen bird: white and hazy. He set off like Dante and would not return to his own time until the Company was restored.

Time is relatively short. It is, in hindsight, a tenet-mechanism. There is a constant past and present and future which can all collide.
