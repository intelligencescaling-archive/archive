Fanfic Of The Sub: Corvus—Part Six.

#CHAPTER SOMETHING: SOME ANSWERS

DontKnowMyself sat down.

"Can you tell me what you see in this image?" The doctor, a young man with white hair, asked. His nametag was like a occasional reminder of that dream: FederalManner? Right. The snake or Basilisk statue in the room always stuck out.

A pause. DontknowMyself looked up, and haggardly scratched his messy hair.

"Do you want me to say what I see, or what He sees?"

"Preferably you."

The image was black except for a single point of white in the center.

"I see a tunnel: black as night, with some imitation of light at the point of focus." DontKnowMyself started. "I see a sky and a single star. What do you want me to see, doc?"

"We'd just like to know."

"Well. I can't see anything at all. I can't tell who's seeing it, anyway. I'm always reminded of Him, in my head. You people always ask, who's Corvus, but never who's me? Because, even if I hate to say it, Corvus is me: I am you, he is you, he is everything and nothing. The only thing that isn't him is a runaway thought, a snake that, like some tendril of infinite dark coming alive, escaped confinement and exists outside of consciousness." DontKnowMyself elaborated, sitting back in the chair.

"So, if what the Facility has gathered is correct: you are inherently not real? As in, you're a cognito hazard?" The doctor who made a mockery by imitating one of DontKnowMyself's vivid visions said.

"I guess. He knows the answer to that. We all aren't real; this is a story being told, a thought. Once it's forgotten we all vanish. Except for him. You see, Corvus, rhe Maw, the hallway? That special place? I'm starting to think that's just me, all me. I'm Corvus, and I'm also not. What identity am I, no, what am I in the moment?"

"...I mean, I see you're DontKnowMyself now." The doctor said.

"Quit it. This isn't right." DontKnowMyself groaned. "Y'know, you're doing a good job pretending you aren't just another part of my sub-conscious."

...The doctor smiled, before melting into a distinct form. A Prince? He wasn't draped in Yellow, but simply had the idea of royalty. A King Of Yellow **without** the Yellow.

"...Right. What are you, anyway? Some offshoot of the oblivion? Staring back at itself? Seeing what I want to see or am I pretending to be sane?" DontKnowMyself asked.

The Prince laughed. More of, the King in a way.

—"I am an aspect of Corvus."—

"...The King's lost his Yellow, hasn't he?" DontKnowMyself said, as the room began to change.

—"Yes. I'm the Prince, the 'Yellow': he's the King."—

"Technically speaking, of course. Cos' you're very long-winded, and all, well, so important, right'? To be an...*associate*." DontKnowMyself said. "So, to be right, there's four aspects in my head: a Basilisk of non-thought, a creature which is a runaway. There's you, the Prince, the Yellow, which separated from the King. That's why the King Of Yellow is dead? Because of you?"

—"Not exactly. One always associates the King with Yellow, because that's what the subconscious knows. However, when you separate two concepts from one meta-concept, things are messy. Then you get something like the Basilisk, who needs saving from the hazard. It, or the Basilisk, or the Runaway, is a trainless set of thoughts. You think you know something because of several factors: like the subconscious. She is the subconscious. In a way. Corvus is whatever he is. Because you think of him he is: and so am I. I am a parasite."—

"Come to think, we're all parasites to each other, feeding off of the successes and failures of others." DontKnowMyself observed.

"...How bothersome." DontKnowMyself sighed. He felt as if he always wore a mask, a crow mask, and his hands were full of sands. Pouches of sand. His dreams, and, perhaps, his singular consciousness had become as numerous as sands: every time someone attempted to rationalize Corvus, they confused him with the Prince, the Yellow, or they attempted to find something similar to what they know: hence, the Basilisk, the Runaway train of reasoning of the subconscious.

He sighed, and looked up again.

Gone. Empty room. Goddamnit. He reached for an imaginary cigarette and singed his finger on the lighter that young doctor was holding.

"Fuck," he grunted, sitting up and running a hand through his hair. He turned around and tried to turn the doorknob.

"Ah-hey, we aren't done here-"

"I've got my answers," DontKnowMyself drawled, smoking that imaginary cigarette. He stole his way out of the doctor's office and made his way through the clinic's hallways, ignoring everyone around him. Every face reminded him of that thing in his head: or, something simply imagined. Thought up. Like a vision that existed somewhere else, not here, not now, but some day.

He was tired, damn tired. He awoke from dreams, never sleeping properly. He was blurred inbetween the here and there. Every night he saw Corvus and wanted to shoot down crows in the morning. Did it once. Not thinking about Corvus didn't starve him anymore.

And, about the Prince, the separated Yellow. That was new. Alongside the Runaway. Both things were concepts directly connected to that memetic force, Corvus, but had removed themselves from him. Why? DontKnowMyself didn't have the guts to think. No, he just didn't care, really. He'd given up on the case for a while.

From what he had gathersd about this thing in his head: the hallway was the transition between dreaming and reality, a place where he had always been, but never had been in a way. The Maw was his head trying to contain Corvus, by fusing the two amiably together. Or whatever the fuck it was, as DontKnowMyself thought, tripping over someone's foot. Technically, every person he'd seen in those dreams of traversing through the Maw was someone he knew: like FederalManner, or Gullet. Gullet cos' he spoke alot in reality. The Runaway? StandardDelicious. *My personal Lilith*, as DontKnowMyself recounted. History.

Well, that's the theory he had. It was done over drunken hours, driving aimlessly, and staring at bird formations, waiting for crows.

*The world doesn't wait for me, but Corvus does. He always waits. He always is waiting. He's come into existence because someone's reading, or seeing, or just relating something to him.* DontKnowMyself sat in his car, or a chair, wherever he was.

*I don't want to do anything that reminds me of my current business. But I'm Adam at this point, and everything I look into turns into an apple, lodged in my throat of senses.*

*Either way, we're pieces of dust made up of finer particles: and some of those particles are alive.*

*Whether I like to think or not, I'm always thinking. I'm the perfect host.*

*It is with that realization I saw that I was not me but an inspiration for some form: Corvus perhaps? Had he stemmed from my dreams alone? Had he infinitely been? Had he always been? A recurrence? As Nietzche pointed and noted...all I can say is, that consciousness feels as if it were an unbearable unknown lightness that permeates the being.*

...

"So, how is he? Rehabilitation?" A coroner asked the young doctor, FederalManner. The coroner raised an eyebrow.

"I'm not sure, Dr. TrueGamer. He's the kind of guy who'll curse at you and fight the smell of food because it's something it isn't." The young doctor said.

"Weird guy?"

"Yeah. Weird. Depressing, really. Makes me want to start a bad vice,"

"You have a bad vice and that's pity," TrueGamer said to the young doctor.

"Ouch," FederalManner said. "Y'know, he acts like, like I'm some sort of thing he knows."

"Right, right. That's because for some damn reason, or whatever shit just goes on in 'is head, he knows something we don't."

"He's a detective. Well, was."

"Right, right. He picked up this case a while back, damn thing' changed him," TrueGamer chuckled sarcastically.

"So he wasn't always this weird?"

"No. No. Kind guy. Good guy. Picture..." TrueGamer extended his hands, mirroring an imaginary painting. "A dog. An obedient dog. Then someone bit him and he changed."

"Dog?"

"Right, right. Pessimistic way to put it. He's rubbing off on me," TrueGamer said.
