Fanfic of the sub: The Living City (name change) Part Three

#CHAPTER SOMETHING: CONTACT

...

Corvus staggered across an uneasily shaped and steadied bar, with Gullet carefully following him.

The Maw groaned: Corvus was unsure if the entirety of the homogenous form was sentient, or simply alive; over some ruggedly stacked shelves upon entering a derelict apartment, there and passing through to another entrance; ever closer to the station; Gullet held up their map, guiding the two.

Silence; periodically rain would fall, sticky black rain.

However, the two trudged onwards, hoisting themselves up small or massive furnitures, warped with flesh and eye; dilapidated essences prevalent in the city, extending to every feature.

But, during their journey, Corvus had started to believe something was following them; maybe two things, maybe more. Regardless, they were not alone; simply, whatever was with them was biding its time.

Corvus didn't sleep; unlike Gullet, who lifted themselves up into tiny crooks and cranny, he could not. His pouch of sands was still damp. Unusable, a majority of it. He had to keep his sands dry as to properly conjure his wishes into reality. The preservation of the name was equally important: for, without one's name, there can be no usage of the sands; thus, once a day at least, or whatever time it was in the ever-night atmosphere of the Maw, he said his name, to remind himself clearly; "Dontknowmyself. Yes, I believe that is me." A mirror would suffice as well: there were no mirrors, only broken ones.

He made Gullet do the same: FederalManner wasn't exactly something they liked to say, but they said it anyway.

Sleep evaded him; he felt fatigue by simply being in the Maw, and he was sure Gullet was affected.

He didn't want to rest for, if he did, he was sure that he would be unable to wake; fear mostly. But maybe some truth was there as well.

...

The two eventually entered a wide, completely separate building; far too large to comprehend, a ceiling shrouded in fog; lockers on either sides, towering, and of course, an eye in the floor; an active eye, blinking and following their movements.

Corvus threw a brick at the eye. It screeched and snapped shut.

Before the two was a large hole.

"Don't touch them," Gullet said, using their stick-magic oar thing----yet another display of Corvus's naming skills----to pole-jump over an expanse. They extended their hand upon reaching the other side, and Corvus prepared himself to jump. But, as he leapt over the gorge, he felt like he was being dragged down by that invisible force.

Gullet grabbed his arm forcefully, yet the two were dragged down into that abyss...

Gullet shoved their stick into the wall, slowing down their descent, yet fall they still did.

Corvus held tight to Gullet's hand, his head craned to see where they were falling, but the filth and the mass of disentangling flesh and concrete was far too dense.

A forest of things: unnatural and natural things.

The two slammed into a potruding rock and fell down another shaft, another hole, the crumbling echoes around them far too loud, causing Corvus's ears to slightly bleed.

His mask chipped slightly, but he kept it on with one hand, the other still tightly wrapped on Gullet's. His sands leaked from the pouch, engulfing the two in a haze of half-dream
And half-reality, disorienting them.

There was no control in their fall! Their continued descent through what seemed to be a living sewage system...a city beneath the Maw, a Maw within the Maw.

...

Corvus awoke to a pair of eyes staring directly at his face----oh right, his **mask**----he scrambled away like an animal and reached for some of his sands.

However, the pair of eyes ignored him entirely, idly turning away to stare at Gullet's oar-stick-lamp-thing----*this needs a name*----mesmerized by the form.

"..."

Silence.

Corvus sat up now.

"..."

"...Who are you?" He asked, guardedly, as he raised his hands.

No answer.

Gullet sat up quickly, and suddenly too, grappling with the mysterious person's body and slamming them into the floor. The two scuffled, until Corvus was able to pry them apart.

"...What the hell-" Gullet groaned, before composing themselves.

The person was bedraggled: Corvus was reminded of a runaway from the old picturebooks he read back at the orphanage.

"..." silence.
