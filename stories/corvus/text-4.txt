#CHAPTER SOMETHING: MY EYES

"Please, I hope we all get together. I'll name you Ve—StandardDelicious." Corvus said to the newcomer, crowning them with their own name. The Runaway, as the two had come to know them as, just stuck around them, enamored with the light. Gullet, who was somewhat shaken, didn't say anything in objection.

Please, be careful—Corvus reminded himself, for he was given to bursts of recklessness, no, more of Gullet, actually! Anyway, the three continued on their way through the Maw—Through the Maw—Through The Maw—

Save for the Runaway and her somewhat confused pace, they passed beneath each other's shadows, haunted light from Gullet's oar.

Me and them, me and them! Corvus thought, as he was trying his best to make their newcomer more comfortable...

From there and here they passed, again and again: in circles, in straight ways, through the living city.

The three of them didn't seem to be getting any more tired, only entering this haze of half-sleep, half-dream, and in dream was Corvus reminded of his own vivid visions: sight of that hallway familiar still shook him, for he imparted an essence of himself certainly in there for it to be so homely.

King of dreaming! Grandiose fantasy, yes, but Corvus had to comfort himself somehow: he could ise dreams in sand, but he was still a mere pawn for whatever lived in them.

In dreams one asks? What lives in them, Corvus did not know, neither did Gullet, and the Runaway, well, she didn't even know or seemed to care that Corvus had this sands of dreams.

Yellow or blue or red dreams? Hm. Why am I thinking of dreams so much? Am I dreaming or is this a dream in a dream?

It was strange••• but also incredibly still, the Maw: trying to find whatever they needed to find was difficult•- But still they prevailed, of course•••- Into this dream land •

Sometimes, from such vivid visions, Corvus wondered if he was looking for a place or a person: he had been compelled by a dream to find this Maw, and once near it had been compelled again by that dream-•-- All very confusing--- He assured himself it wasn't ••- Gullet was there•-• As they should••• what else would they do • Still, he felt a fear they would vanish•-•• Or, perhaps, he was scared he wluld vanish in this macabre labyrinth of a city••-•

••-• --- •-•   - •••• •• •••   •• •••   •-   -•• •-• • •- --

...

Right—right—stay awake! Corvus thought, smacking his crow mask against some pillar. Gullet pulled him away with their hand.

For once the Runaway seemed amused at this.

The three continued walking, anyway, in silence. The Runaway was an easy accommodation, though she rarely spoke, and seldom seemed to even acknowledge anything, her presence was clear.

Gullet did not like her.

*DontKnowMyself, you aren't supposed to be here*

*Why?*

*Leave, now.*

*I won't.*

*Leave!*

*You know I can't. I can't ever leave. Never.*

*You can't? What exactly are you, anyway? Draped in Yellow and black, colors don't seem to fit you. Besides, the Maw isn't even a place! It's a place of some sort of mirrored and fractal essences inconveniently assorted: it's memory. It's what you think of what you think, it's a denial and an answer.*

*Somewhat correct. Maybe? I don't even know what I am doing in there, Federal. I'm tired. So, so tired.*

*Tired? Stop that, stop it. I'm more tired than you! I want out! Quit fucking with me, quit it!*

*I've never been **"fucking"** around with you. Not once. Not ever.*

*Stop it! I want out!*

*I can't do that. I can never leave.*

*Color me shocked.*

*What is the color of shock?*

*What? I want out and you—*

*I think the color of shock is the same as sickness.*

*What?*

*Forget it.*

*Forget it? Yeah, forget it. I want to be freed!*

*...You know I can't do that. **I** can't do that. I cannot do that thing, or such a thing. Such a thing, such a thing. A thing isn't a thing until it is thought to be a thing••   And I am not a thing, but still, I am that kind of thing•- --   Did I leave anything behind-• --- -   I did, didn't I•-- •••• •- -    I'm talking nonsense, and I know it-•-- --- ••-   What sort of thing and thought is the Maw- •••• •• -• -•-   The Maw is that kind of thing-••• ••- -    That Maw is a Maw because I, or more of we, yes we, see it as such•-- •••• •- -     You thought you could fool me, didn't you-•-- --- ••-     I never thought anything, I am not fooling anyone, not anyone, not even myself, or that's what I think, that's what I believe- •••• •• -• -•-*

*Stop! Stop it!*

*Shall we go back to the Maw? We haven't finished anything. We haven't done anything. We haven't learned anything.*

*No. I can't. I can't hold that light anymore, no more! I've done it thousands of times, and for what—!*

*It's the same. It will always be the same. The Maw isn't and is a place in-between my thoughts and your thoughts: our thoughts, or, inherently, my thoughts. They do say, crows like shiny things. But why? That thing isn't shiny for any reason beyond the crow. Or, perhaps, it is shiny. We don't know what is and what isn't, or what should be: we simply think of what they are and the thoughts reinforce what they should appear as.*

*Enough. I'm tired, damnit. Tired, tired!*

*So am I. Not really. Maybe. Anyway. The Hallway? We'll get there, eventually.*

*Eventually? We never have seen that hallway.*

*That's what you think.*

*It's true-*

*Don't trust your eyes. So, please. This time it will be different.*

*In what way?*

*...*

Gullet sat up. They looked at Corvus. They looked at the newcomer.

They saw something they weren't supposed to see. Or did they imagine it?

They shook their head, and calmly looked again at Corvus sleeping. They adjusted his cloak.

*I shouldn't be awake.* Gullet thought.

/Again/

*I shouldn't **be** awake.* Gullet thought.

/Again, if you will/

*I shouldn't be awake.* Gullet was compelled to think.

They thought of the Maw and the Maw happened to grow larger, more fervent, in its growth.

The Runaway seemed to be disconnected from everything once she had achieved her goal in some semblance, as if she had forgotten some sort of details of her character. What to do?

/She hasn't forgotten, but simply is/

\Will you stop interrupting me?\

/If you insist/

/../

/...if I insist./

\Nevermind\

The Runaway sat up, staring at Gullet for an uncomfortably long period of time.

"...Is that light?" She asked, pointing at the Oar's head.

"...Yeah."

"..."

"That will get him interested," the Runaway said. She tilted her head left.

"What."
