(Notes: Souichi is in Perfect Mode and will remain as such throughout the entire match. Likewise, this is Akane Kurashiki after the events of Zero Escape 999. Leap Second will not be a factor)

The Obelisk stood tall against the morning sun, shrouding all those that stood beneath it in its shadow. Of all the people gathered there on this day, none of them had gotten any amount of sleep for quite some time yet they could not be more alert. They were invigorated, energised by the sheer excitement of the match that came before, mere hours ago. They were even more excited for the match that was to come.

Emerging from the horizon, the referees spotted her. The woman who had won her claim to this match through sweat and tears, the young lady who had come to threaten the existence of Kakerou itself.  Yet still, the referees waited with an exterior of perfect politeness, and an interior of feverious elation.

The referees began murmuring amongst themselves.
“There she is, The God Slayer herself”
“To think that there was somebody out there who could defeat Usogui…”
“Air Poker…that was a gamble I will never forget…”

“Leader.” Yakou spoke up as he turned to Souichi, the leader of Kakerou. “She’ll be arriving soon. I simply wanted to ask, are you sure about me being the referee for this match?”
Kiruma Souichi stood in front of all the referees, a symbol of his power. He turned back to address Yakou. “Of course, Yakou-san. You, above all others, are the most suited to preside over this Surpassing The Leader. After all, this is the woman who took Baku Madarame, who you seemed to treasure a lot, away from you.”
With that, Souichi turned back to face his enemy.
Quietly, Yakou said to himself.
“You seemed to treasure Baku Madarame more than I ever had, Leader…”

The young lady lay only a few steps away. With that, Souichi began to address her.
“The owner of Crash Keys, Akane Kurashiki, you have met all the conditions required, even having taken down the legendary gambler Usogui, indeed, you are remarkable.”
Akane was taken aback. “O-oh! Thank you…”
Yakou whispered into Souichi’s ear. “Leader, the agreed upon time has come.”
Souichi once more focused on Akane. “It is time. Come, let’s begin.”
Akane raised her hand. “Um, before we start. Is there any reason for there to be so many referees? I thought only one was required…”
Souichi found himself…shocked. Akane Kurashiki, the woman who had defied all odds and had beaten Baku in a gamble, the woman who saw through Usogui’s threats as if they were nothing, the woman who didn’t so much as flinch at the possibility of drowning during her match, yet she seemed to be intimidated by the presence of the referees.
“Akane Kurashiki…just what kind of person are you?”

Yakou spoke up. “No need to fear, Kurashiki-san. I will be the only referee presiding over the match, the rest are here to observe and watch the match for themselves. They will be in a separate monitoring room, only the 3 of us will be entering the Obelisk.”
 She relaxed. “Oh, that’s a relief.”

Silently, the trio walked up the stairs of the Obelisk, reaching the top floor of the arena that will decide their fates. Yakou gave an explanation for how the game works, they were about to decide who Drops first until Akane raised her hand again.
“Um, just to make sure I’m not misunderstanding something. All I have to do is win this game and I get your organization and all its capital, correct?”
Yakou let out a slight chuckle. “Of course, naturally…” his expression turned cold, emphasizing the point he wanted to make. “...Should you lose your life in this gamble, Leader would take over your organization as well.”
Akane, unbothered, replied bluntly. “That’s fine. It’s only fair, after all.”
Souichi stared intently at Akane, gauging her every reaction.

With everything out of the way, the match could finally begin. They started with playing rock paper scissors in order to determine who would Drop first. Akane wins and chooses to Drop first.

Both players readied themselves, moving into their respective spots. Yakou stood to the side of both of them, staring at his watch. “The game will begin at 8:12:00, on the speaker clock’s signal…”

Both players fell into deep contemplation.

Souichi: “You…you’re a walking inconsistency. I could see it in your eyes, during the match against Baku, you stopped being nervous the moment you felt the water envelop you. So what about now? Now that this match has started, what kind of eyes are staring at my back right now?”
Akane: “Junpei… You remember this game, right? We used to play something similar when we were in grade school. Oh how I miss your voice right now… But now’s not the time for nostalgia, I must push forward.”
Souichi: “What kind of person are you? You, who emerges from nowhere and threatens the very foundations of Kakerou…You, who killed my only friend…”
Akane: “This is more than just a mere game, the rewards from taking over Kakerou will be the stepping stone towards a brighter future!”
Souichi: “For his sake…and for the sake of Kakerou-”
Akane: “For the sake of the entire world-”

Both: “I will win!”

Yakou reset his watch. “The check was successful, the next round will start at 8:14:00”
Akane was still breathing quite heavily, shaking off the unsatisfying results of the first drop. “Oh jeez…”
“Kurashiki-san.” Souichi interjected, his gaze focused and intense. “My instincts were correct, you really weren’t faking your nervousness. You are a person who wears their heart on their sleeve. To think that someone like that could’ve beaten Usogui in a gamble, it’s hard to believe.”
Akane gulped. “You mean Baku Madarame-san? You were his ally during that Air Poker game, weren’t you? You guys must’ve been pretty close, I’m…”
“It was our destiny to stand before each other in this moment, until you came along.” Souichi interrupted.
“I know, I’m so…” Akane hung her head low, looking at her feet. “I’m so sorry…”
“So she feels remorse, how pitiful. The winner of a gamble never needs to feel sorry for the loser.” Souichi thought to himself.
He spoke up once more. “No need to apologise, you earned the right to be here today. You’ve taken Usoguis place as the final tribulation I must defeat.” His gaze toughened once more, the aura that emanated from Souichi had only one name, killing intent. “I will kill you myself.”

Akane didn’t so much as flinch. Souichi gauged her reaction to his provocation and didn’t find so much as a nervous blink from her. “It’s unmistakable, you are  timid and you are shy. However…when it truly counts… You’re as immovable as a mountain, Akane Kurashiki.”
Yakou raised his arm and held his finger over his watch’s start button. “The 2nd half of the 1st Round will begin soon, please return to your positions.”

Yakou’s voice was calm and docile, his signature as a referee. “Kurashiki-san, please lay down and roll up your sleeve.”

Souichi stood over Akane, looking down on her as Yakou injected the Near Death drug. “You disappoint me, I hope you can make up for it next time. Only if there’s a next time.” He leaned in closer. “‘We are at peace now’. Please convey these words to Baku Madarame if you meet him.”
Akane’s eyes went hollow, marking the effects of the Near Death drug kicking into her system.
Yakou starts the 1 minute timer on his watch. “That was harsh, Leader.” he remarked.
Souichi remained utterly silent, his expression cold and emotionless. He had turned away from Akane, almost disgusted to even look at her.
