Pokemon Mystery Dungeon: The Past Pursuits - CHAPTER 2: Cat‘s Paw

                                                14 years earlier

After a good 30 minutes of nerve wracking waiting in the same spot, a local dungeons entrance on the outskirts of their hometowns forest, both Cathy‘s mother and father still haven‘t uttered a single word to each other. At best they only occasionally shared a few short-lived glances at each other.

The mother didn‘t even bother to look whether they were back or not. Her and the father just kept fidgeting with the item bags harnesses slung around her shoulders. A few neighbours curiously watched them for a few seconds here and there from their nearby huts.

Finally, a gray-purplish umbreon, covered with red rings instead of the usual yellow, began to emerge from the lush covered, rocky and cave-like dungeon entrance, carrying a smaller 9-year old Cathy in his mouth by the scruff of her neck.

„You-you did it! You actually saved our kitten! We truly don‘t know how to thank you, sir!“

Cathy‘s mother, a sleek liepard in similar black fur, gave her thanks in a relieved tone that Cathy had rarely ever heard from her. Both her and Cathy‘s father, a disheveled looking white mightyena with cyan markings, rushed towards them.

The smell of iron hit their noses first as they slowed to look at what instantly caught their attention: Cathy‘s tail wasn‘t kept safe properly, sickeningly bright red flesh exposed that was still faintly oozing blood, leaving behind a red trail in the grass wherever Cathy's shadow darkened the ground. Instead of idly swaying like it usually did, it hung limp like a wet noodle.

Cathy herself wasn‘t in an ideal state either; shaking, wide eyed, fur fully soaked from multiple water type attacks she likely fell victim of, nervously looking around and refusing to talk.

„Oh my...“ Cathy‘s father took a step back while the mother fell completely silent.

„Yeah, I did manage to get her out of there mostly unscathed. But her tail was already injured when I found her on the last floor. Part of the tip seems to be completely gone. I couldn‘t make out where the missing piece was either. Sorry...“

Silence fell over the meadow as the umbreon put Cathy back on the ground and looked away at absolutely nothing, just not into the eyes of the purrloin‘s parents. The only sound was the padding of Cathy‘s feet on grass as she carefully stumbled her way towards her mother, hugging her left front leg.

„Yeah, not a pretty sight to look at. Maybe it‘ll grow back, maybe not.“ The umbreon tried to introduce a more optimistic outcome, but he really wasn‘t sure of it either.

The umbreon's voice snapped both parents out of their shock. The liepard and mightyena exchanged a quick look before nodding. Cathy was swiftly taken by her mother by her scruff again as she dashed back to their house to treat the still mostly fresh wound.

Cathy‘s father turned back towards the umbreon after his spouse's steps grew inaudible in a matter of seconds.

„I still want to sincerely thank you for your service.“

The umbreon was taken aback and scratched the back of his head with a paw as the other kept him in his sitting position.
„Oh, it‘s really no big deal, Mister Fisher. I‘m just doing my job at the end of the day.“

Cathy‘s father was in thought for a moment before reaching into the bag hanging off his right side and taking out a small grey pouch, coins clinking as he put it down in front of the umbreon's feet.

„Then we please ask of you to accept our gratitude this way!“ He said, his tone leaving no room for decline.

The umbreon looked down at the pouch and back at the mightyena, seemingly puzzled.

„2000 Poke.“ The mightyena answered what the umbreon was unsure of asking.

„I-i don‘t know if I-“

The umbreon's protests were interrupted.

„we insist on it, okay?“

The umbreon took a few seconds to actually take the pouch.

„I hope that isn‘t a lot for you.“ He accepted, his own bag slung over his shoulder rustling with many various dungeon items.

„You are a truly noble Man. May I have the honor of knowing your name?“

„uhhh, my name? You can just call me Ron.“ Ron hesitantly stored the pouch in his own bag.

To fill the silence, he steered the topic away to the more pressing matter.

„Do you have an idea on why she might have marched in there, or was it really just childish curiosity?“

The mightyena shook his head, genuinely trying to think of a legitimate reason but failing horribly.

„We really have no idea what could have driven her to just march into a local dungeon when we explicitly told her not to. We will most certainly try talking to her about this.“

„i was gonna suggest that too. Lets hope i won‘t have to pull her out of another dungeon in the future. We don‘t want that, do we?“

The advice seemed to have reached Cathy‘s father as he nodded softly.

„Have a splendid day, Ron. We wish you great luck on your next mission.“

Ron nodded and turned to walk away, raising an eyebrow for just a second before his expression turned neutral once more.

 „yeah, you too...“

Only a few minutes passed between their interaction and the arrival of Cathy‘s father at their residence, a considerably large brick hut built for them years ago by some zealous excadrill, many of them lightly spalled from weather cycles and age but still holding up strong.

He pushed the sliding door back behind him, the faint smell of dust and fresh wood hitting him first and the sight of his spouse waiting for him second. Cathy stood across from her mother as they waited for him, tail already treated with healing items and bandaged.

He and his spouse exchanged another look once again, her dark grey eyes meeting his sky blue ones.

Cathy‘s father turned his head towards her again and said nothing for what felt like an eternity, his look not being met by Cathy.

„...“

The young Cathy directed her focus to the clock's loud ticking, whatever put her mind at ease, fidgeting with nothing in her paws as her injured tail flicked weakly behind her.

„...“

What came out sounded completely monotone.

„Go to your room, Catherine Fisher. Now.“

No anger, no sadness, not even disappointment or fear. Just neutral.

Cathy's ears drooped and eyes softened, feeling her heart sink in her chest yet simultaneously beating harder too.

She remained silent and just lowered her head, doing her walk of shame to her rooms door as she felt both her mother‘s and fathers gazes pierce into the back of her head. She didn‘t dare meet her parents eyes as she slid the door shut without looking back, only entertaining the thought of slamming it for a split second.

Cathy just sat in the middle of her bed and stared at the wilted petals from the white hydrangeas in front of her on the windowsill for what felt like hours, like they would hold the answer.

She wished her parents had just yelled at her that night instead.

Connor looked back at umbreon after analyzing his surroundings thoroughly. Around the office, crude paw paintings were plastered on several closets, small plushies of a whimsicott, swirlix and buneary on the windowsill and shelves as well as a ton of framed pictures of Guildmaster Umbreon alongside an eevee. He couldn‘t make out anything else on the pictures, but Connor already knew where the guildmaster‘s soft spot lied.

„I do hope that my origin isn‘t looked down upon here and that this Guild, like all the other ones, is accepting enough to look past it.“ Connor set up the expectations Guildmaster Umbreon will want to meet.

Cathy‘s eyes widened as they darted to Connor. The way he hyped it up made her believe for a second that he was gonna drop the bombshell on them.

„I know it might be an odd habitat for my species, but I have lived nearly my entire life in the mountains. My mother...She was this graceful and powerful luxray.“ Connor sniffed and shifted in the chair in a way so Guildmaster Umbreon and Poppy couldn‘t see his tail.

Connor kept holding only occasional eye contact as he continued to paint his narrative.

„So I want to apologize in advance for my lack of knowledge about the culture here. My intent is to learn extensively about it in the future.“

Guildmaster Umbreon nodded. „Mhm, i suppose that does explain a lot.“ He replied coldly, focused on keeping things professional, but his voice was now laced with a certain weight that wasn‘t there before.

The office was silent for multiple seconds, save for the scratching of the quill on paper as he wrote down Connor‘s answers he already gave for two questions at once.

„Good. Now I‘ll ask you a little something to gauge your resourcefulness.“

Guildmaster Umbreon laid his quill pen on the desk, signaling Connor to take time with his answer.

„So imagine you are stuck on a deserted island or in a dungeon. You have no way to get any kind of help sent to you, no communication orbs, no nothing. But you can choose 3 objects or items to take with you.“

Connor started to hold eye contact again. After all that explaining and lying about things he barely understood, he didn‘t expect a question so ordinary all of a sudden.

„Uhhhh...“

„A raft...To get off the island? I don‘t think I need anything else actually.“

Guildmaster Umbreon looked at Connor for a moment before turning to Poppy, who just met his gaze and shrugged. Guildmaster Umbreon shrugged back before writing it down

„I-I suppose that makes sense.“
 he huffed like he saw Connor‘s answer as a bad joke that fell flat.

CLUNK

Guildmaster Umbreons talking was interrupted by the sound of an inkwell he knocked over while reaching for a form for Connor to fill out, staining only some semi-important documents and one of the four basic figurines on the desk that were designed as him and the other three guildmasters.

„We got time here...“

Guildmaster Umbreon and Poppy aggressively wiped away the ink as he and Connor awkwardly mumbled over each other, Poppy moving like this was a daily occurrence.

A deafeningly silent 20 seconds later, he held the blue smudged towel towards Poppy.

„Bring this to Pania please, will you?“

„Now you just have to take your time to fill this out and I will have a short talk with my right-hand afterwards to decide on the matter.“

He tried and failed again as it struck him that since being a pokemon, he hasn‘t even spent a minute trying to learn how to pick up things with these stupid, clunky paws.

„Sorry, sorry. I‘m just walking off a recent injury I caught in the mountains. That's why I was walking all funny too while coming in here.“

„No no, I don‘t think that‘s necessary!" Connor forgot to let Guildmaster Umbreon finish speaking.

„It‘s been getting much better the past few days anyway. I‘m used to danger but not immune to it, you know?“

Connor tried thrice more to get a proper grip on the quill pen, each attempt ending in his cheeks heating up and the pen falling onto the form and spilling tiny drops of ink on it. The silence other than Cathy‘s occasional chuckle she couldn‘t hold back that filled the office was one of the worst kinds. It felt like way more than just two pairs of eyes were on him.

„Uhhhmmm, Could you?“ he looked between the form and Cathy a few times before quickly looking down at his paws again with a sigh.

She barely stifled another laugh as she took the quill pen herself. „With pleasure, gotta help my new teammate, after all.“ She leaned in and wrote down all the basics of what Connor shamefully whispered into her ear.

„Rookie explorer - no experience“

„December 21...“

„Ooooh, winter solstice. Makes you real special, huh?“

„to apply my skill set properly and follow family tradition...“

Cathy stopped her writing for a moment to glance at the next question.

„It says here why this guild specifically.“

Connor looked closer at the question, his tone becoming even quieter. „God, no idea...uhhh, It‘s style of leadership aligns closely with my expectations and ideology.“

Guildmaster Umbreon's eyes narrowed as he could clearly hear what they were saying, even in hushed whispers.

Connor cleared his throat while Cathy handed over the filled out paper. A lot of it was left empty, such as education, specializations and confirmations for soft and hard skills. Thinking about it, Connor realized his narrative didn‘t give him much to work with, but it was better than nothing.

Cathy‘s handwriting was also incredibly refined for...Someone like her.

 Just at taking the first look at the form, Guildmaster Umbreon‘s eyes widened back up

„I see here you didn‘t fill out any details about your bank account. I‘m assuming you likely don‘t have one? Do you know about bank accounts?“

„Nope, don‘t have one yet. And yeah, I do have a good grasp on currencies.“

„Would you mind if your earnings were transferred to your partner's account for the time being until you‘ve opened your account? I‘m trusting you two to be upstanding enough to split it fairly.“

„...“

Connor‘s brow furrowed and he barely held back his scowl as he looked to Cathy, who met his look with a smirk, knowing he had no choice but to accept.

Connor turned back at Umbreon and exhaled.

Just before Connor got his word out, the office door opened and Poppy stepped back in again. His, Umbreon‘s and Cathy‘s heads shot towards her.

„Alright, are you three finished or did I put a jam on the brakes of this romantic moment?“

Connor leaned forward again to take a look, but Poppy‘s voice continued.

„Oh, and uhhh, well. Now it‘s obviously pricey to finance the saving of countless lives, inspiration for many and enable others to explore the world. So we‘re gonna have to call dibs on about four fifths of the Poke that you make just from missions.“

Connor raised his head towards her and the Guildmaster again, his expression remaining neutral.

„And for that you get your own dorms, breakfast along with dinner in the mess hall and access to training grounds, the guild library and medical care. The amount of guild tax also decreases with every rank you climb, so that there‘s real motivation and potential for growth! Doesn‘t that sound wonderful?“

Cathy quickly straightened up in her chair, her paws dampening as she looked back at Connor to see how he would react, who nearly rolled his eyes at Poppy‘s attempts to make this sound appealing.

„Okay, these terms seem acceptable. Do you mind if I ask an unrelated question?“ Connor broke the minute long silence with that.

„No, not yet?“

Connor grumbles ever so slightly as Cathy ticks the upper box for him and tops it off with a signature for him.

„By the way, they took questions at any time when I applied here“ Cathy hurriedly whispered into Connor‘s ear, earning her a dismissive look from Guildmaster Umbreon that made her slowly sink back in the chair, avoiding eye contact while her ears drooped.

„I was just curious to know why exactly the rule of two explorers having to join at once was introduced. I mean yeah, it‘s dangerous. But wasn‘t it always like that?“

„Ah yes, that. We had to implement it for additional safety because of a recent increase of explorer casualties regarding rookies. From the data we gathered it seems like an unidentified outlaw seems responsible for that rise.“ He explained away while beginning to fidget again, this time with some fur on the back of his paw.

Poppy chimed in to take the pressure off of Umbreon.

„We are also making an effort to send new explorers into dungeons where this Outlaw hasn‘t stuck their snout into. We found it odd that they've only been locking horns with rookies yet so we‘re still kinda investigating...Or maybe we won‘t have to! The last confirmed attack was around 3 weeks ago, so we can assure you it‘s no biggie.“ Poppy shut down any further potential worries.

„...They both got 50/50 custody, am I right?“

„Uhhhhh, I dunno? Not like it matters much honestly, but I think 50/50 does ring a bell in my head. Howd‘ya know?“

 „The gaps of the dates on the pictures after his wife vanished in them are all multiples of two weeks, so Umbreon likely gets to see his daughter for 2 weeks, then his ex gets the kid and so on. He also looks like he‘s aged 15 years since then from all the stress, so I‘m guessing that‘s where the mood swings could come from.“

„...Fine...I think we‘ll give them a chance.“
