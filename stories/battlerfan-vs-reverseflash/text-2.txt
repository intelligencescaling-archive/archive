#"CHAPTER SOMETHING": The Greatest Performance Of Our Time, Pt. 1: The Mime.

(ART NOT BY ME. ART IS BY "biezhifeng" on Danbooru)

Tell me. What do you think of...
...ReverseFlash.

A person who isn't very sentimental, but pertains certain aspects of someone who has never Dived. A person who has been thoroughly reconstructed by his experiences with the world around him.

A masterful actor, who fakes his actions for the betterment of others.

A mime, who doesn't belong anywhere, whose stage is the whole world. You could say, we're all in his play, and he's just playing his part till' curtain call.

...

ReverseFlash looked out of the arched windows, still bathed under the gentle light. His eyes were clear, and the diamond monocle on his left eye illuminated his face with bright blue. He had still not removed the paint from his silk suit, and frankly, he looked somewhat ridiculous had it not been covered by his inverness cape.

He looked back, to all those behind him, to the faint visages of his companions. He politely raised his silk tophat, while giving one last smile. A foolish smile. Yet a smile nonetheless.

*To pierce through the Veil, I'll need to use various Facets. Luckily for me, I have the tacet approval of the Old Others. I wonder how BattlerFan figured that out.* He thought, as he carefully prepared to 'Dive' through the Veil and surface through the Scarlet Sea.

*This is quite risky. I can't be taken into the depths, or anger the Old Others Of The Light.*

*However, it's only natural I do something as audacious as this. I need to catch him truly off guard. Besides, I have nothing to truly feat, as I have ascended to Old Other status.*

With a deep breath, he stepped unto the arched window's edge, staring down at the nearly endless heights of the Knave Palace. He then took out his pocketwatch, staring at the emblazoned images of his companions.

*Even though they may not remember me...I will be back. I promised myself...heh, no dying idiotically.*

Then, with a faint laugh, he let himself drop.

Falling down a nearly endless building was certainly jarring. However, he was unfazed, and calmly took out a grimoire, whilst carefully holding his tophat. Multi-colored water bubbles began to flow around him, and his diamond monocle shined with every color of the rainbow.

He had begun the process of 'Diving.'

At that critical junction, before he hit water dissipation due to his outer form eroding from physical damage, he tossed up his grimoire, seeing it fly into hundreds of pages.

"Mid. Diffeas." He chanted, and his body became 'streamlined' to the flow around the Knave Tower.

*Yes!* he thought. *Next, the Veil. Hopefully I don't die.*

With an elegant hand flick, he swung his cane, creating a break point upon the Veil.

Yellow and blue light erupted from the crack, and it began to tear and shred his outer form apart completely.

Thus, he began to chant, to himself. Quite egotistical, yes, but he knew that *he* was technically the only Old Other who could pierce the Veil.

"The face unchanged by time," he started. His diamond monocle began to crack under the incessant light beams.

"The hand that pleases the audience." His arm became almost invisible, a light beams of it's own. Assimilation was near.

"The smile to hide beneath." He could feel his torso becoming one with the light.

"Help me now, Mime That Does Not Know Fear!" He finished.

There was a brief second of pure silence, before thundering crack could be heard.

[The Mime That Does Not Know Fear has successfully dived through the Veil]...his Spiritual Presence told him.

At that moment, he felt as if he were levitating above everything. He saw hundreds of things: wonderful things, terrible things, but, to him, all he could focus on was the faint sight of a white butterfly. Fleeting beyond his reach.

Before he could even think, he pierced through the Veil, his body protected by his own Facet.

Then, he fell deep into water. Red, perhaps even sanguine water. The Scarlet Sea wasn't particularly warm either.

But nevertheless, in the distance, he could hear BattlerFan's rather loud chanting. Was he already trying to pierce the Barrier? How audacious.

The corners of ReverseFlash's lips turned up.

*Found you!*
