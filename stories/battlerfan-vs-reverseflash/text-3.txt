#"CHAPTER SOMETHING": The Greatest Performance Of Our Time, Pt. 2: Memories Of The One Winged Crow.

...The chanting came to a sudden halt, and, like a phantom, BattlerFan's body phased through the air, becoming a haze of black crows and bubbles.

Without a word, ReverseFlash followed in suit, precariously entering another Sea to chase after the fleeing party. However, he had not properly calculated the dimensions of the Sea, nor the general thickness of the 'Water'.

Simply put, a fatal mistake.

...

In other words, BattlerFan knew that ReverseFlash would do something so, *so* audacious.

And, natural to his character, he had already prepared everything necessary for such an event to occur. Existences, Facet Objects...just to name a few.

ReverseFlash, unfortunately, as he suddenly felt a tugging sensation upon his outer form, realized his mistake. Thus, he quickly attempted to right it, using yet another bold tactic: summoning his Old Other self once more.

However, this was yet another mistake.

As he had begun to speak, the Sea he was 'Diving' through became dark. Foggy, almost.

It was with this, the fog, ReverseFlash's eyes flashed with something foreign to his inner boldness.

Fear. Primal and something that should stay innert.

*No.* ReverseFlash thought. *This is a mental attack...surely. He can't be strong enough to completely overwhelm an entire Sea with his own-*

*"Are you sure?"* A voice spoke through his thoughts.

Before he could even speak, a blackened and burnt hand fell upon his mouth, it's long fingers grasping his cheek quite roughly.

His diamond monocle cracked completely, and his mind became muddled with thoughts...memories...everything. ReverseFlash was being subject to *everything*. Infinite information passing through his eyes.

A single voice pierced through the storm. A condescending one at that.

"You should have become my vessel."

The hand around his mouth tightened, and he was forcefully yanked out of the Sea completely. He felt as if he were flying.

A single, enormous black wing flashed before his eyes.

"..."

In his muddled mind, ReverseFlash fought back for control. If he failed to gain back his conscious senses, he would be decimated entirely. Not only would his outer form become a puppet for him, but his internal form, his 'Submerged'ness would be completely left to the whims of BattlerFan.

But, at that moment, how could he think? How could he, when the chanting had started once more? When memories of warnings swam freely through his mind?

*"You see."* He suddenly remembered one of his companions saying.

*"Imagine the world is a clock. Everyone follows the two hands, correct? But not all. You...you see. There is a hand that tampers with the clock, with it's internal functions.*"

*"I suppose you're right...this..."*

The black hand began to loosen, and he felt it go beneath his chin.

*"Whoever has been doing such actions..."* his memories continued, as his head was tilted upwards painfully.

His gaze made contact with those  limpid eyes.

*"Whoever has been...they have made a mockery of existence.* the memory continued.

BattlerFan finished chanting, and the world around them began to shatter. And yet, ReverseFlash could only hear these words, whispered into his befuddled mind.

*"They have been scamming everyone, controlling that clock since it could even work. And now, he's bored. He wants to break that clock.*

*"Tell me, who could stop him if he tries?"*

Then, as these memories ran through over and over, ReverseFlash felt himself beginning to fall. The blackened hand let loose of him.

Down, down, through the shattered mess of infinite reality.

*Is this what despair is?* he thought, looking up. A single, black feather fell through the water, touching his cheek before sinking deeper.
