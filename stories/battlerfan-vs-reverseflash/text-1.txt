*Chapter something: Good Evening, Goodbye, Hello!*

...ReverseFlash turned his head, holding the silk tophat's rim with one hand, the other holding his elegant walking stick.
He also had taken the flower bouquet which he had purchased, leaving it nestled under his arm.

*Tap*, *tap*, *tap*.

At that moment, having fixed the monocle on his left eye, ReverseFlash recognized who stood before him. The young man with the bright, limpid eyes from earlier! Or, more correctly, Mr. BattlerFan, as the residents of Subredia called him.

Before ReverseFlash could speak, the corners of BattlerFan's lips turned upwards conspiratorially, yet also somewhat with a gentle air.

Stopping himself from his usual habits, ReverseFlash tipped his silk tophat with a quick, perfected movement. The flower bouquet underneath his arm slowly crumpled.

*Deep breaths, Reverse. Deep Breaths* He thought to himself.

Every small movement BattlerFan made almost made ReverseFlash twitch out of human instinct.

"Are you, perhaps, fearful, my 'friend'?" BattlerFan spoke.

At that, ReverseFlash could not answer. It was almost futile.

"I admit, yes. I am."

"Hmm."

ReverseFlash chuckled nervously, as he gently placed the flower bouquet down on the most quaint object in the room: the small ledge of one of the grand windows. He took one last glance at it. He took one last glance of humanity. Then, with a deep breath, he turned to look upon something...beyond.

By then, the soft light of the painted sun slowly rose through the arched windows, covering everything with an almost fairy-dust touch. The long curtains slowly swayed, the veils to prevent Judicians from entering following in suit.

The world was gentle at that moment.

"Won't you miss them?" BattlerFan asked, curiously. He cocked an eyebrow, tilting his head to a side. "This is a rather audacious bet."

"...I daresay, I might." ReverseFlash lied. He already did miss them. All of them and their antics, their stories, their company. However, he had decided already, and that was that. He could not turn back, even if he wished to do so. "I believe you know me as a very audacious person, though."

With a nod, BattlerFan gently clapped his gloved hands together. He fully focused his limpid eyes upon ReverseFlash, giving him an almost insidious glance.

"Ah. However, sacrifices must be made, yes? You're bold, I'll give you that. Why don't you stop this crusade of nonsense, and simply become one of my vessels? It's easier for the both of us. And, you'll see your fellows once more." BattlerFan said.

ReverseFan nodded, as he tapped his elegant cane once more.

"Possibly. But, you've planned all of this, haven't you? Even if I accept, the chance of the world being locked is too high."

"So, you'll try to kill me, yourself, and possibly all the Salt Of Existence?" laughed BattlerFan.

"Like you said. Sacrifices must be made."

The two began to calculate, to think, and to prepare. They were evenly footed, as ReverseFlash had the strength of the Old Others, and BattlerFan had ascended himself to a higher Existence. However, such things are merely things to parlay. One must use these things correctly.

Then, BattlerFan began to chuckle.

"So. It's been decided? You hope to defeat me, and, inherently, become the next Fate Spinner?"

"How utterly, utterly foolish of you!" he said.

"Tell me, will it be worth it? Will it be worth chasing me through time? Perhaps, I'm not saying it is likely, but perhaps, you'll find yourself chasing me through the Gnostic World. And then, what will you do? You'll regret your choice, you'll-"

ReverseFlash held up his hand.

"No. I may regret it. I may suffer. But..."

He paused, staring out the arched windows, to the world beyond. The beautiful world beyond.

"I will persevere. It's not a choice. Though the tribulations will be, certainly, extreme, do I really have any regrets? I will cry, I will feel the pain, but do I have a choice to stop? No. Do we ever get a choice to stop? No."

ReverseFlash turned to look at BattlerFan, whose limpid eyes were filled with visible curiosity, and spoke..

"This world, so beautiful beyond compare, fleetingly flying by us like butterflies, does not allow most to have choices. All we can do is fight the hand of fate till' it claims us all."

BattlerFan chuckled softly, "You wish to fight fate then? To fight *me*? We are one and the same, you and I. Bound by the false illusion of choices, bound by a selfish desire, bound by a sense of our own justice. So, let me ask again: do you wish to fight me?"

A pause.

ReverseFlash sighed, as he finally abandoned those flowers, letting his memories become past. He turned to BattlerFan, giving a small smile. A gentle smile, if you will.

He spoke

"If necessary."
