Synopsis:

After getting away with actions he couldn't undo for too long, the 29 year-old Elias Kaiser was forced to deliver his own consequence himself and woke up in an outdated world only inhabited by pokemon. His first impression of another pokemon in this world? A purrloin trying to trick him into helping her join a guild she was declined from because of having no partner. Now, having minimal knowledge of his surroundings, a partner that feels almost like a liability to him, trying to come to terms with what he's done in his past life, a new body he can hardly control and trying to hide the fact that he used to be something most pokemon only see as a mythical creature, he only had one advantage left to navigate this mess: his genius intellect.
