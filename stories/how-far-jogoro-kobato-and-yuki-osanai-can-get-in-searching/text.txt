"Someone in Class D must have the same calibre as me," Ryuen declared. "Someone who can manipulate people from behind the scenes without anyone realizing who's really in control."

Ryuen discussed the mystery with his goons while the rest of Class C stayed silent or occupied themselves with other things, hoping to avoid his attention.
