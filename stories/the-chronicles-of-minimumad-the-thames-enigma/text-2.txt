Chronicles of MinimumAd: The Thames Enigma

# Chapter Two: Smoke, Silk, and Strange Men

The moonlight washed over Piccadilly with a pale gleam, bouncing off the rain-drenched windows of the second-story apartment Dr. SiebenGuts now called home. The air smelled of old cobblestones and a lingering storm. He sat by the window, sipping the remnants of a lukewarm tea, his mind not on his drink, but on the man behind the locked door across the hall.

*"Who is MinimumAd, really?"*

Sieben had met his share of eccentric men in the medical field—surgeons who mumbled Latin under their breath, pathologists who found joy in decay—but this man had an intensity that disturbed and intrigued him in equal measure. He had never met a man who looked at blood like it was paint on canvas, or who dismissed the entire discipline of forensics with such effortless scorn.

*"Not a chemist,"* Sieben thought aloud, fingers tapping against the ceramic cup. *"But something close. An experimentalist? A rogue scientist? No... he reads people too well. The way he knew I worked at Charing Cross—that wasn’t deduction, it was observation stacked upon inference stacked upon—"*

Suddenly, a sharp knock shattered his thoughts.

He opened the door to find MinimumAd standing there, coat thrown over his shoulder, eyes lit with a kind of manic glee. "Put on your boots, doctor. You’re coming with me."

Sieben blinked. "To where?"

"A crime scene."

"I'm...not a detective."

"And I," Minimum said with a wry smile, "am not really asking."

Ten minutes later, they were darting through misty alleys behind a constable who looked more puzzled by their presence than the actual crime.
"Where're we going Minimum?" asked Sieben, who was equally as confused as the guiding Constable.
"Victoria's Embankment" Minimum replied plainly, smirking "Hope you're not too bad with corpses doctor?"

"You're surely jesting, aren't---?" Sieben was interrupted as he saw a violent scene unfold before him. A human hand, cleanly cut from the torso was lying in front of his leg. He was about to jump but got more confused when Minimum ducked, holding the hand in his own.

"Huh, cleanly cut, as if he went to a sawmill to do the job" Minimum said, analyzing the cut hand.

From the shadows, a silhouette approached them. Sieben got scared for a moment from the approaching figure, but Minimum shrugged.

"Gosh, Ifteaz, you've never changed, did you?" Minimum chuckled "Scaring our doctor out of nowhere"

Ifteaz smirked, offering her hand to Sieben, "Sorry Doctor, it's kind of a hobby of mine. Inspector Ifteaz, Scotland Yard" she introduced herself.

Sieben smiled at her "Nice to meet you, I'm Dr. Sieben Guts, ex-surgeon"

Ifteaz's eyes gleamed, "Surgeon? You've finally decided to question your capabilities, did you Minimum?" she smirked teasingly, looking back at him as they walked furthur towards the bank of Thames.

"Oh, not at all" Minimum replied, "I just wanted him to taste the thrill of this adventures. So, who died?"

"Persistent, aren't we?" Ifteaz smiled, "The victim's name is TrueLuck, according to his Driving License. We're still trying to find more from wallet" She replied as they reached the body.

Almost immediately, Minimum sat down beside the body, examining every nook and cranny of it. He put his hand inside victim's suit, finding a buch of business card, with the name "Smith inc." printed on all of them.

"Looks like our friend here was an Advocate. Also should have a work visa, so that's a lead" Minimum murmured, "Jittery, had spectacles, but aren't on his face, can I see his wallet?"

Ifteaz passed Minimum the wallet, "Hmm, he's either been robbed or doesn't carry any money with him, highly unlikely. But this wasn't any mugging-homicide. The killer had enough time to separate both of his hands from the torso"

"Oh, so the genius is here" A silhouette approached from behind, standing behind Sieben.
"There you are Morgan" Ifteaz smiled, walking towards her "Where have you been?"

"Xamot called me" Morgan exclaimed "Had one of those 'Official cases' I had to handle, so I was late. Now who do we have here?" She smiled at Sieben.

Sieben smiled back, offering his hand for a handshake "Dr. SiebenGuts. Nice to meet you, miss...?"

Morgan chuckled softly, shaking his hand "Morgan, Detective Inspector"

"Now, can I get what you've got before coming here Morgan" Minimum said, his focus on the corpse.
"Nice to meet you too, Mr. Ad" Morgan smiled, holding out a paper "Victim had some things upon him from a long time. Bribery, Unlawful behaviour, Penalties, all went unpunished from some unknown reasons. Great background I supposed" Morgan replied.

"I see" replied Minimum "Sieben can you keep the paper in your bag and come here with me, if you insist?"

"Absolutely, no problems" Sieben sat beside him and started examining the body.
