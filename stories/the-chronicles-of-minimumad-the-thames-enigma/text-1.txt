The Chronicles of MinimumAd: The Thames Enigma

# Chapter-One: The Neighbour

The rain slowly fizzles down, as SiebenGuts steps out of the carriage in the middle of Picadilly. After giving the driver a generous amount of tip for taking his luggage out, he slowly walks towards the two-storied building he's just rented for living.

Dr. SiebenGuts, former surgeon, working in the Charing Cross hospital for the last two years was not a note-worthy person. The life of a surgeon was a tiring job and he used to spend his mellow off days, drowning in Alcohol, which slowly pushed himself towards clinical-depression. The Medical Board ruled him to be unable of performing surgery and suggested for a break from it, rather working as a house Physician. So, he exchanged his place from Downing Street to Picadilly for a change.

SiebenGuts reaches the main door, knocking softly. A little pause, before a beautiful young lady opened the door. It was miss CreationCawthon, the landlady. "Good afternoon miss Cawthon"- Sieben said, bowing a little. Creation smiled, greeting him back as she led him upstairs to his new rented room. Sieben followed her lead, dragging his stuff with him inside the house.

As Sieben walked upstairs, he found another door in the opposite of him, with a locked door separating the neighbours. He looked at Miss Cawthon with a intrigued gaze. Creation, sensing his curiosity, replied-"It's Mr. MinimumAd's room. He's more of a solitary person, so we normally don't disturb him. He's doing....all sorts of experiments and stuff all day". Sieben's curiosity about his neighbour increased-"Chemist?". "I don't know what's his profession, but he's pretty bizarre. And he's got a lot of visitors, even though he likes Isolation."-Creation replied. "Interesting"-thought Sieben, as he bid miss Cawthon goodbye for the day and started unpacking his stuff.

Once he's done with unpacking, he decided for a small visit to his new intriguing Neighbour. Slowly walking, he knocked on MinimumAd's door thrice, waiting for the response. As he knocked, he found the door creak open slightly. After a little while, a voice called out from inside-"Come in, it's open".

Out of Curiosity, Sieben opened the door and entered the room, finding a Young Gentleman, wearing a dressing gown, pouring a chemical using a dropper inside a beaker. After some time, the liquid inside the Beaker suddenly turned orange, making the man look eager for more. But suddenly his eyes fell on Sieben, and he slowly walked towards him. Slightly intimidated, Sieben stepped back a little, but stood more firmly as he approached. "MinimumAd"-the man offered his hand for a shake to Sieben. "You're too young to be a Surgeon, no less in Charing Cross"-Minimum smirked, shaking Sieben's hand firmly.

Sieben looked shocked. How did this man, who he's never met before, knew about his profession and origin? He was about to ask the question, but Minimum interrupted him, walking back to the chemical. "See this doctor? This just proved my theory that the blood, appearing on victims or potential suspects, can be idenfied via a simple chemical test"-Minimum said, as if he just discovered the cure of Cancer. Sieben smirked slightly, intrigued by his neighbours discovery. "Interesting mr. Minimum, this is a brilliant solution for many police cases I suppose?"-Sieben replied-"You really should take this to te forensics, they'll be interested in this discovery". "Forensics? They're a bunch of timewaste, not a noteworthy job"-Minimum replied, annoyed. Sieben smiled at his remark. "I think I'll find you compelling. So until we meet again Neighbour"-Sieben smiled at him, walking out of the room, after a fascinating encounter with a rather peculiar Neighbour.
