New Oc scaling! Shopping mall escape feat(also can be named as "Fake reporter framing" feat)

"Man continues to fight and kill for victory, only to prove his uniqueness."

Hello again! This is the second OC scaling post. Now, I will discuss one of my female characters, who is again a villain🤣 (sometimes she is neutral, but overall she is very evil). Let's go! (Also, the first picture is not really what she looks like; I just found that picture cool, so I added it.)

Name: Mimizuku (real name Takashima Hinazumi). Her age varies from 16 to 19, depending on the story.

Keywords: Washinaga Ryo, reporter Tsukiuma, Ebara Kimiko, dimethyl sulfate, teicoplanin.

A little background: At the age of 5, Hinazumi's dad and elder sister died in a car accident (at least she thought that way). Because of that, her mother slightly went insane and decided to join the Scientology cult, moving to a village near Tokyo, where the leader of the cult, Washinaga Ryo, became interested in Hinazumi because of her rare form of synesthesia, which was progressing very slowly and could reach its full potential only at 40. So, Washinaga decided to speed up the process by experimenting on her (of course, it was painful, and there were side effects such as periodic bleeding from her eyes, nose, mouth, and ears). After all the experiments were done, Hinazumi's five senses were boosted very high (for example, she could hear a nearby person's heartbeat, and by tasting blood, she could easily understand its type, etc.), and now she became a secret recruiting weapon in the Scientology cult, telling newbies that they could gain the same abilities. You can understand that such a life cannot help but leave a mark on her psyche, and yes, she slowly began to go insane, starting to believe that she was the only one blessed and that others could never be as unique as she was (there was one situation that made her think like this, but I decided not to retell it). So one day, she just shoots her own mother, sets the entire church on fire with all the followers, and starts her campaign to take down Washinaga Ryo and the entire Scientology cult in Japan.

So now let's get to the feat. After Hinazumi ran away from the cult and managed to hide from them, Washinaga finally started seeing her as a real threat to him because she may have information that can destroy his cult. So he decided to lure her into a trap. Using his influence, he created a fake identity of a reporter named Tsukiuma on social media, who was rumored to be exposing the cult. To make things seem more convincing, Washinaga allowed several exposing posts to be published on Tsukiuma's Facebook page. He wanted Hinazumi to contact Tsukiuma to find out where she was hiding and capture her. After one month, Hinazumi finally wrote to Tsukiuma and asked him to meet her at Aeon LakeTown mall because she had very important information about the Scientology cult. Washinaga understood that it was his chance, so he sent one of his men to meet Hinazumi and pretend to be Tsukiuma while some others waited outside to capture her when she left. When the fake Tsukiuma entered the mall, Hinazumi sent him a message to go up to the third floor and take a small bag containing all the necessary information from a woman named Ebara Kimiko.

After the fake Tsukiuma took the bag, he wanted to leave but suddenly faced Hinazumi. After a small talk, she left some clues, making Tsukiuma think that she was totally aware of their trap. After a quick handshake with the still-shocked fake reporter, she left him. Then he quickly walked down to the exit of the mall, but something caught his attention. A large group of people had gathered around the woman who gave him the bag. She had an oxygen mask on her face, and from time to time, they defibrillated her. Suddenly, one of the nearby people started coughing, then another. The fake Tsukiuma himself sensed a strange smell. The situation got worse when one of the people fainted. This began to escalate, leading to panic among the crowd as many of them rushed to the exit (the fake reporter did the same).When he finally was outside, he contacted Washinaga, saying that he got what they wanted. But suddenly, he was surrounded by police, and they accused him of killing Ebara Kimiko.

Okay, now let's see what Takashima did. When Washinaga created the fake identity of reporter Tsukiuma Hinazumi, she understood right away that it was a trap for her, but she could also take advantage of the situation. She decided to contact the fake Tsukiuma after one month, not wanting to let Washinaga suspect anything, and during that time, she prepared for the future meeting. For her plan on social media, she found necessary information about Ebara Kimiko (the most important being her late-stage cancer diagnosis) and, using a newly bought SIM card, wrote her a message the day before the meeting with the fake Tsukiuma, offering to help her and suggesting she try treating her cancer with medicine that was used in the 1980s. It may seem illogical that she agreed, but if we think about it, people can agree to anything if they are in despair. So, she told her to go to the Aeon LakeTown mall and take a small bag from the restroom, where she would find needles and two medicines that she needed to inject into her veins. After making the injection, she must wait for a man named Tsukiuma, and only then could she leave. When the day of the meeting came, Ebara followed all the instructions, but when she was on the first floor, her vision started getting blurry, and her breathing became heavier until she fell to the floor, causing other people to gather around her and even bring an oxygen mask to help her breathe.

Now let's talk about the medicine that she injected: dimethyl sulfate (DMSO) and teicoplanin. The first one was actually used as a cancer treatment in the '80s but was soon prohibited because of its side effects. The second one is used for treating certain infections. At first, nothing seems wrong, but in reality, the dose that Hinazumi made Ebara take was very high, and the second medicine has very bad side effects; after using a high dose, it can lead to kidney failure (which is what has now happened to Ebara). All of this led to unexpected results. As Ebara's kidneys could no longer remove toxins, the DMSO in her blood started to connect with the oxygen that was entering her body from the mask, leading to the development of DMSO2. But then, after Ebara fell into shock (in medical terms), the workers at the mall started using defibrillators on her. The electricity converted DMSO2 into DMSO4 (an extremely toxic gas), and because of that, many people around Ebara began to feel unwell.

The chaos created by Hinazumi's actions allowed her to exit without getting caught by Washinaga's people. When she made her way to a telephone booth, she called the police and said that Ebara had given a small bag to a strange man (the fake Tsukiuma), which prompted the police to search for him. After they caught him, they found in the bag not only empty bottles of DMSO and teicoplanin with needles but also a SIM card (which was used by Hinazumi when she was chatting with Ebara). To make things worse, traces of DMSO were found on his right hand (remember the handshake that Hinazumi gave him?). So now he was arrested for murder, and that damaged the reputation of the Scientology cult because of the fake Tsukiuma's connection to it.
