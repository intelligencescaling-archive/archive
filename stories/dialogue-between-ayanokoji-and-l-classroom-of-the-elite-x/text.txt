Dialogue between Ayanokoji and L

Context/narrative for the two talking:

L was notified of the whiterooms existence shortly after Kiyotaka had managed to make it into ANHS, this version of L had reached out to Ayanokoji after his escape and offered him a chance at guaranteed freedom under certain conditions and if a specific set of events were to occur, Ayanokoji after hearing was skeptical but had of L due to him being an example and framework of logical reasoning in the whiteroom due to his reputation in elite and well known spaces in the world as the greatest detective so the two share a mutual respect and insight into each other based off of reputation.

\*Ayanokoji would be called up during the lesson. He was initially slightly wary and confused, after all. Nothing eventful had happened so far he couldn't imagine any reason why he'd be summoned.\*

\*He'd walk up to the principles office and immediately hear a familiar voice and raise an eyebrow. As he saw a man hunched over a table eating cake, and elderly man stood beside him rather calm.\*

"If you'd close the door, please."

\*He'd lock the door and sit down.\*

"So it's happened. Either you suspect Kira will kill you soon, or you want to engage with him. What's the catch here, then L?"

\*L would turn around and set down his cake and ruffle his hair, Ayanokoji was almost unimpressed that the man the instructors used a guide for efficient deductive had such terrible posture and unusual habits, which was a bit ironic.\*

"Yes.. I suspect that at soon, possibly at any moment, Kira will kill me. In this case, I've planned for Watari to download all files on the Kira case and send them to you, and have you join the Kira task force.. But. Before you do, under any circumstances, do not reveal your real name. At all."

"That's a very specific set of instructions, I'm guessing his method to kill is directly tied to this? It's hard to believe, but for now. I'll take what you're saying to be the truth."

\*L would nod.\*

"I'll explain the terms and conditions of you becoming L after I die. You will immediately be removed from this school. And placed either in a safe house I protected or introduced to the task force as a new investigator via email after I die in case Kira kills Watari. Or by Watari himself, in the case we're both dead, do mention your authority or motivations unless it's seen as strategically beneficial. We've planned to introduce you as Naoya Keijo, a young detective who's helped me in cases. Stick to this story. I've even prepared made-up cases and files for you to fall back on to assist you in catching Kira. Direct approaches are dangerous. Kira is as charming as he is deceptive. Be warned."

\*Ayanokoji would note not only the amount of instructions and information he'd have to catalogue, but L's tone and serious manner of speaking, he spoke with finality. It's almost as if he had planned for this in case it happened for months already. He was impressed that L was so accepting of death and that Watari was just as ready to follow him into the after the afterlife. With this in mind, he decided to place faith into L.\*

"Understood, and I'm guessing if I succeed in capturing or being able to arrest kira with solid evidence, you've already set up the manner in which I'd be free?"

\*L would smile at the boy's intelligence reminded of someone. Although younger, he had hope in that boy seeing Ayanokoji's brain in action.\*

"Yes, but if I told you, you'd use that method and ensure it yourself. Although I am sorry for essentially risking your life to settle this petty dispute."

"No worries. I'll promise 100% effort."

\*The two would shake hands, although both had an uneasy feeling it'd be the last time they'd see each other — and they were right.\*

\*Ayanokoji would receive an anonymous email as he left campus and boarded a black limousine, and arrived at a tall yet complex building. As he'd enter. A good-looking boy with brown hair would stand up, greet him, offering a hand.\*

\*Ayanokoji was skeptical immediately but politely shook his hand.\*

"I'm Light, and you're Naoya, correct? We had an email.. recently about you. I'm currently the head of the kira case."

\*Ayanokoji felt almost disgusted hearing that, but remain poker faced as he returned pleasentries.\*

"Yes, I'm Naoya. So you've taken over?"

\*Light would nod.\*

\*Ayanokoji would think: Judging by the files and profiles L laid out for me. This guy is almost certainly kira. How he managed to corner L and kill him is one thing I'll have to be careful of and figure that out.\*
