Chapter 1: Boredom

The Shinigami World

A gray, stagnant atmosphere hung over the wasteland of bones. Ryuk, a grotesque-looking creature, yawned as he watched his companions play a game with dice made of skulls.

"Same old thing... day after day," Ryuk murmured, letting a rotten apple slip from his hand. "What a bore..."

Earth: Japan, 8:16 AM

Amidst the crowd flowing like a river through the streets of Tokyo, a young man came to a halt. The giant screens covering the buildings reflected the chaos of the world. One commentator spoke of a 3% increase in the homicide rate, while another voice bluntly recounted the discovery of a young woman buried in concrete.

The young man frowned. The sound of the news mingled with the city's bustle, creating an unbearable cacophony. He took a bite of the apple he was holding but spat it out instantly. It was rotten to the core.

“Just like this world”, he thought, wiping his mouth with disgust. Murders, rapes, impunity... everything is rotten.

The Shinigami World

"Haha! I won again!" one of the creatures bellowed.

"Hey, Ryuk, why don't you join in?" the other insisted. "It's boring losing to this idiot, but you could give it a try."

Ryuk didn't even look at them. His yellow eyes were fixed on the void.

"No thanks. I have to go find something."

"What did you lose this time? What a drag... the Old Man is going to be furious if you lost another notebook."

"I dropped it," Ryuk replied with a twisted smile. "In the human world."

Before his companions could react, Ryuk opened a portal and plunged into the void, tracking the energy of his lost notebook.

Japan

The object fell from the sky with a dull thud in the university courtyard. Light Yagami, a 17-year-old student with an analytical gaze, observed the notebook from the classroom window. Upon walking down and picking it up, he read the title in English: \*Death Note\*.

“Death Note?,” he thought, skimming through the rules with skepticism. “Sounds like an internet hoax. "Write the name and the person dies." How stupid.

However, the weight of the notebook and the quality of the paper felt... real. He tucked it into his backpack, a growing unease settling in his stomach.

Light turns on his TV and flips through the channels until he finds a live broadcast.

That night, in the solitude of his room, Light tested the notebook in front of his television. A kidnapping case dominated the live broadcast.

Otoharada Kurou, he wrote. Cause of death: Suicide by gunshot to the head.

He waited forty seconds. Nothing. He sighed, feeling ridiculous.

"I should have known..." he began to say, just as a gunshot resonated from the television. The kidnapper had died.

Light stood up, his heart pounding hard. The television showed the hostages running to safety. It wasn't a coincidence. It couldn't be.

The kidsnapper…dead.

Light reads all the other rules:

Death Note Rules: First 4 Rules

Rule 1: "If the cause of death is not specified, the person will die of a heart attack."

Rule 2: "If a cause of death is written within 40 seconds, that will be the chosen cause of death."

 Rule 3: "This notebook will have no effect if the writer does not have in mind the name, surname, and face of the person they are writing about.”

Rule 4: “The cause of death can be detailed in the next 6 minutes and 40 seconds, even if it is detailed but letters are missing or if it is not fully detailed, or the death is not physical and logically realistic, the person will only die from myocardial infarction”.

He went out to the street with the excuse of buying drinks, his mind clouded by adrenaline. He needed definitive proof. He saw a group of bikers harassing a girl.

In the entry, he writes the name seven times with different pronunciations according to the Japanese kanji.

"Shibumaru Takuo," he whispered, writing the name in the notebook under the store's exterior light. "Traffic accident."

“…”

 Forty seconds pass, and the scene exactly as described occurs: the harassing motorcyclist dies in the crash. Amidst the shock and horror, the girl takes advantage of the situation and escapes from the thugs, while Light watches in astonishment, confirming, in turn, the veracity of the Death Note…

"I'm not a hero," he told himself, staring at his hands. "But... if I eliminate those who corrupt this world, aren't I doing justice?"

"This world needs to be fixed. And I will be the one to do it.”

 .

Five days pass, and Light continues writing names incessantly, seemingly obsessed. One stormy night, as he continues writing, a strange voice speaks: "Looks like you're enjoying this." Light turns and sees a dark being about eight feet tall staring at him. Reflexively, he falls out of his chair. The monster reveals himself as Ryuk, a Shinigami, or god of death. He says that the notebook Light is using now belongs to him, but since it fell into the human world, it now belongs to Light. Light gets up, tenaciously looking at Ryuk, says:

"Ah... so you came. I was startled by your appearance and by taking me for granted, but to be honest, I knew you'd come, Ryuk”.

 When Ryuk appears, Light is not scared but mentally prepared. He deduces that a powerful object like the Death Note must imply the existence of a supernatural entity (an original property) accompanying it.

Ryuk claims he didn't choose Light and just dropped the notebook. Light deduces this is a lie because the instructions written in the book are clearly intended for a human user, not just Shinigamis, But later think:

“The book is written in English, which means it's intended for anyone on Earth to pick up and use, since English is a universal language.

  Following this reasoning, it means the users could be anyone, which proves Ryuk right, since if it were meant for me, it would be written in Japanese…”

 —Is correct for this part. While the Death Note is obviously also for humans, the truth is that it actually fell to Earth by chance, not by fate..- light responded.

Light asks the shinigami why something so dangerous fell into the world. The shinigami, with the same unsettling gaze and without blinking, replies:

Ryuk:

 Because I was bored. I dropped it on purpose so a human would get it and I could see what they'd do with the notebook.

Light can't believe that's the reason he possesses a divine weapon—the boredom of a god of death... Without hesitation, Light confronts the shinigami, demanding more answers and asking what will happen to him now that he owns the notebook. The shinigami replies coldly and decisively:

 Ryuk:

 The human user of the Death Note, once they use the notebook, will go neither to heaven nor hell."

Even after hearing that  won't go to heaven or hell and when he dies Ryuk will be the one to write his name. Light is still capable of laughing like it was nothing…

Ryuk sees the staggering number of victims, acknowledging Light as the human who wrote the most names in the notebook. He asks Light sarcastically if he harbors so much hatred for people, or if he keeps a personal enemies list. Light simply replies,

Light:

 Because, I'm bored too. I want to put this notebook to good use. I've decided it's not a weapon, but a tool—a tool to create the world I've always wanted, a world without evil, where society rises to peace and prosperity. To create a new world..

 Ryuk bursts into laughter.

Ryuk:

And when you're the only scum left, what will you do?

Light, with an expressive and kind look, replies:

Light:

 -I'm not scum. I'm just a university student with a bit more brainpower than the rest, but also... I'm the one who will become the God of the new world.

Ryuk looks impressed and thinks to himself:

"Humans are so interesting…”

2 days ago, somewhere else…

 In Russia, at a maximum-security prison, guards reported a total of 36 victims, most of them prisoners convicted of murder and rape, many of them recently incarcerated. This phenomenon isn't limited to just one prison; it's occurring in several prisons across the country. For no apparent reason, all the prisoners began dying of heart attacks. A director of the main prison in Moscow raised the alarm and presented the case to the Russian government, reporting the mysterious deaths of 348 confirmed heart attacks: 100 in St. Petersburg, 73 in Omsk, and the alarmingly growing number in the capital, Moscow.

 "It's not illness or poisoning," the director informed the Russian Ministry of Security.

 "It could be something more sinister.."
