A while ago, a long while ago, the news of the urban legend of the “Monster of Europe” spread like wildfire around the world when popular detective Lunge, an ordinary surgeon Tenma, MI6, and various EU and German agencies found themselves entangled in an unsolved and untraceable case, now known as the John Doe Case, or, as a placeholder name, the Placeholder Crimes Cases.

A lot of details in the cases were kept in the dark because of the sheer absurdity and the entanglement of its scope in various scenes of society, adding to the silence of the aliases, and of both Tenma and Lunge about what they saw and their original operations. Its mysterious status became an urban mystery in the zeitgeist of the public around the world, raising questions about and exalting the role of investigation and justice.

Despite the case still being unsolved, it exposed the underworld of society and its various hidden facets, which changed the whole world for the better.

Detective Lunge was the teacher of Quillsh Wammy, the founder of the esteemed detective training house in the world, the Wammy House in Winchester, England.

In Quillsh's words, he was never able to match his master's calibre, but his master gave him a mission: to raise a generation of detectives who could achieve what he had failed to do.

Detective Lunge carried the secret of what his mistake was to his grave. Only those close to him were able to understand what his master meant by the word “mistake,” mostly through intuition.

Both man behind the crime and the case itself were monsters who changed the world.

The unknown man, who was never found, somehow became a perverted goal for detectives, a figure they continued trying to understand even to this day. His existence became a challenge that continued to improve the methods of today's detectives, criminals, conmen, and others who studied the art of deception.

And the case itself provided various secrets that were kept hidden from other agencies. Rumours of extreme theories involving the pedagogy of children, psychopathy, and brainwashing techniques became a guide to the elite strata of society, a popular controversy for many, and a conspiracy for the few who still had a naive view of the world.

However, the inception of the case was a sign to the world that there was a new breed of killers in this world, especially in Europe, where uncaught killers who had ties into the cases existed all over the world, and the rise of a kind of killer in America who prescribed to the unknown man's doctrine of leaving no trace and integrating well into society and the law, such as the Hannibal case of America, the Bay Harbor Butcher of Miami, and Red John, who still roams free.

And this is considering the known world.

What happens to the political world is another beast to deal with, with sanity not to be lost.

According to popular belief, the case gave the individual side of the world the doctrine and the permission of exploitation, and the collective side the guide and ruleset of maintaining domination.

Doctor Tenma's involvement caused the involvement of Japan in the case. It's said that Japan and other Asian countries' involvement was under the guise of justice. Their main reason for involvement was the intrigue.

The underground crime and government scene thrive all around the world. In the case of Japan, it became the rot of the world in underground crime.

Rumours of Wahizu's mafia, underground gambling games for the elite where the poor die, rumours relating to the disappearance of siblings Sora and Shiro, and the legendary deceased swindler Shiba Taizen, known as “Sensei” for the conmen, who claimed to have encountered the Nameless Monster personally and claimed to be able to create a “monster” as his successor, also began spreading through the underground.

A secret government body that controls everything from behind the scenes, with its titular leader trained using the Kinderheim 511 brainwashing techniques. The accounts of Wolfgang Grimmer being brainwashed, while still showing abnormal cognitive functions and extraordinary physical abilities, became evidence that such techniques could produce abnormal results.

The political scene is an entangled mess, where politicians scout their future candidates from private schools and academies they created solely for the purpose of creating the elite and the best of the best. Politics are heavily entangled with this part of society, much like the chaebols in Korea, where private schools are literally created by companies.

Who knows if they are involved with the shadow government, or if it's simply the dream of politicians to be involved in that world, where power is immeasurable.

Two popular schools/academies in Japan are ANHS and Hyakkaou Institute. Both have various political and government sponsors and affiliations, with each school having a separate network within society. For other academies and institutes, it is a dysfunctional system.

Here are two people who are going to change the current trajectory of the world.

Light Yagami, the son of the police head of the Kyoto district, believes that this world is rotten to the core, and only God could fix a world where monsters are running it.

The ex-con, nightmare of every swindler, Akiyama Shinichi, is at the same time shocked to know that the founder of the Liar Game Tournament was his own teacher. It is revealed that he was a teacher at ANHS, one of the first-generation teachers. What he saw in ANHS was horrific. He saw how ANHS encouraged exploitation and manipulation, and how the school wanted to create an “elite” for society rather than focus on education. His experience there became one of the reasons behind the creation of the Liar Game, a system where people were tested through deception, psychological manipulation, strategy, betrayal, trust, and extreme social pressure.

The Liar Game was not simply a game. It was based on what he saw in ANHS, a system designed to show what people become when they are placed in a world where everyone is forced to deceive, manipulate, and exploit others to survive. In some ways, the Liar Game became a warning about ANHS itself, and about the kind of society that could be created when the elite are raised through manipulation rather than education.

Meanwhile, at the same time, two new students arrive. At ANHS, the boy Ayanokoji, who claims to be a follower of the ostrich policy, and at Hyakkaou, the girl Yumeko, who got the name “Compulsive Gambler,” turn on the TV to see the performance of what people dubbed the “Monster” of baseball and the mindreader.
