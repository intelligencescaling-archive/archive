**CHAPTER - 2**

The girl looked surprised. After scanning me for a few minutes, once the shock in her eyes faded, I naturally expected her to speak and pulled my left hand back.

"Who the hell are you, you poor bastard... HELP!!!"

However, she was still screaming...

I covered her mouth with my left hand again, but this time really hard; not a single molecule of oxygen could escape now.

"Look, ma'am, I'm just here out of curiosity, and I'm serious. What kind of connection do you have with that man?"

The woman stared at me with angry eyes, as if she were about to burst into curses. Up close, she was so terrifying—it felt as if the eyes of a giant blue whale beneath deep waters were about to swallow me whole.

I was scared, but right after my moment of fear, the girl began to relax significantly, and I pulled my hand away from her mouth once more.

"I don't know that new prisoner up there. And no, if your boss sent you here, there is no way in hell I'm getting that man out of there."

After looking at the woman for a few minutes, I was puzzled. I stood up and scratched my lip.

"Thanks for the answer," I said, and continued walking along the mountain path toward my own tent. But there was a question lingering in my mind: if he knew this woman, but she didn't know that scumbag, why did he send me to her in the first place?

As I kept walking, I brought my hand to my mouth in realization. I realized I couldn't go see that man tomorrow because I had run off yesterday without turning in my report to the boss.

And I couldn't go to the boss either, because I was genuinely curious about what the man had to say.

"Honestly, instead of facing severe violence from the boss, I could just go to that man as a fugitive," I said to myself, entering my tent through the back section. If you're wondering why I entered through the back, in my clan, trespassing on someone's personal space is a very serious issue, but the rule itself is enforced just as strictly. So, I was sure there were a few guys waiting outside to demand an explanation.

Despite these thoughts, I didn't even have time to change my clothes. I had gone outside around 2 AM, and it was probably 3 AM by now. That's why I threw myself straight onto the bed.

"I seriously have a weird clan..." I groaned as I drifted off to sleep.

In the early hours of the next morning, I woke up to the sound of the first leg-whip cracks. My tent was closest to the chain of command, which automatically meant violence.

After splashing some ice-cold water on my face and shoving a few snacks into my mouth, I looked out the window at the view.

Overall, it seemed surprisingly normal, except for the increased military discipline patrolling the area.

That didn't affect me, though. I went outside and headed toward the right alley of the grocery store up ahead. Normally, cinchona apples could often be found here, but since they depleted so fast, this place emptied out on the 3rd of every month.

By chance, today happened to be the 3rd. Actually, I usually take this path when sneaking out, whether there are apples or not.

Then, with great excitement and curiosity, without greeting anyone, I reached the rear exit of the floating safe zone. And he was still there. Eating an apple, looking somewhere for reasons unknown, gazing toward the glowing barrier as I approached.

"You broke your promise."

He was still looking down.

"I don't get it. Who didn't I bring to you? If you mean that girl, she didn't want to come."

He slowly stood up and stepped behind the apple tree. A few minutes later, he pulled out a map he had carved into the tree trunk.

"Do you know what this is, you ignorant fool? Look, I depicted that *exaaact* mountain right here!"

I looked at the man in disbelief and took the map he handed me.

"So? What am I supposed to do with this information?"

The bastard was acting like an egotistical jerk, carrying a constant smirk and putting on airs of authority toward me.

He did the same thing again: sat on the bench, crossed his legs, and started laughing in a cocky, arrogant way.

"When I first arrived here, I thought I understood this place. But within a few hours, I realized something: you guys are just normal humans."

I narrowed my eyes and let out a breath. "What?"

He continued right where he left off:

"The clothes you wear, the way you speak, your food... especially your words. They are eerily similar to the real world. Look at that wooden board—that Y symbol over there? Over time, it mutated, taking the shape of a T with four thin horns sprouting from the top. That's actually the guttural 'Y' sound."

Without breaking my stride, I continued:

"Actually, now I understand why that angel looked so bored when she kicked me out. The time flow where the angel is vs. the time flow here are oceans apart, and that's why you guys have changed so much."

I looked back at the man's face in shock. Honestly, the only thing I gathered from his sentences was that he was an exceptionally intelligent human being.

"Still, I don't get it, sir. What does this piece of wood actually mean?"

The man stood up, swayed slightly as he walked over to me, and began explaining step-by-step from behind the barrier:

"I realized that, for no apparent reason, I could see very far. While you were trying to bring that girl to me, I decided to inspect the surroundings—and I did. Especially during this time frame, I managed to learn the slightly deformed language of this place, which is a mix of English + Arabic + Latin. It was a strange dialect."

My eyes widened in disbelief. Did he just say *"I learned a language in 1 day"*?

"Ah, the leader of that woman I sent you to... he was doing some accounting this morning. Deep stuff, honestly. But they will pass through that steep mountain slope... and what great tragedy: during this beautiful autumn, man-eating wolves roam the edges of those regions... thanks to the reporter."

"So, if I understand correctly, you want to lure the wolves to the edges of the path that leader will take, and use that girl to incite a rebellion?"

My mind suddenly cleared up. So *that* was why that girl's ear was cut off—she had a rebellious mindset.

"No, no, no, don't misunderstand me. I'm not *trying* to do it; I am doing it right now. And by listening to my words up until now, you have officially become my soldier."

I was both shocked and confused. Why was I even holding this map, and why did I go to that girl in the first place?

"By the way, you're probably asking yourself why right now. The truth is, you feel guilty... but aggh, I really don't feel like talking anymore. Now go and execute my plan."

He took a deep breath and posed slightly, emphasizing his ribs.

"You never keep your word anyway; at least do this for us."

In that moment, I just found myself nodding. It felt as if all my muscles and nervous system were moving independently of my thoughts.

Lastly, as I was walking down the stairs, the man asked me for "rare, bloody lamb meat." But where on earth was I supposed to find lamb meat here?

I looked up and let out a deep sigh, but immediately collapsed onto my knees right after. The scale of the plan had just registered in my mind: MEAT! THIS MEAT IS EXPENSIVE!

"What can I do? I can't afford this. Even if I took out a loan, neither I, nor the guy up there, nor the gods could stand against the shopkeeper's 'Ultimate Debt Collector Sako' mode."

Then it hit me. One of the reasons for the sudden spike in military presence and strict discipline in the clan might be that the clan leader's youngest son was returning from a grand hunting trip to prove his manhood and claim clan leadership.

"HUNTING = MEAT. Therefore, MEAT = WOLVES."

Am I really going to commit theft now? What have I degraded into?

Taking the same route back, I reached my house. In front of my tent, I saw a guard waiting in a dazed, sluggish state due to something he ate for lunch.

Since the guard was out of it and the weather was a bit hot, he probably wouldn't put up much of a fight. Plus, I had a knife.

"If I stab that guy, enter the kitchen room, and then... robbery\~"

I was biting my nails. After all these years, was I really about to use a knife... on a guard? I can do this. Yes, yes, I can do this.

I reached my back yard and took my knife, treading with stealthy steps toward the entrance of my tent.

Visualizing it might be tough, but what would happen when the body was found... Clearing these thoughts from my mind, I froze one step away from the entrance of my tent.

He was sleeping, and I could see his shadow behind the curtain. Throwing myself forward, I dragged the man inside my tent and immediately aimed for his windpipe so he couldn't make a sound.

"Don't struggle. It won't be painless, but don't make it even more painful."

He was thrashing around more, but since my main targets were the uniform and the envelope, I flipped him over and ruptured his eardrum.

He screamed even harder and finally began to cry.

I could feel the membrane inside his ear. I needed more force, so I slapped the hilt of the knife embedded in him, completely shattering his brain structure.

"I talk to my inner voice during every murder because that way my guilt fades, and I don't notice how exhausted I am getting."

I slowly changed my clothes and threw the corpse into the cauldron. For some reason, I felt a sense of lightness inside. I was relieved, but...

"I have to go."
