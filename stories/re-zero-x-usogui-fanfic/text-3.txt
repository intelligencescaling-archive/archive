Chapter 4 -

Subaru’s team all stood in a circle. They sat in silence for a while until one of them decided to speak up.

 “What is the verdict?” Otto narrows his eyes.

 “I am not sure I trust Baku. He is very deceptive from what I am getting. His intentions aren’t clear. All I am sure of is that he is waiting for us. He expects us to show up sometime sooner or later,” Anastasia sighs while rubbing her temples.

 Baku sure is a pain to deal with, Anastasia thought to herself. She was used to dealing with cunning people. After all she was a merchant. Baku on the other hand was a whole different story.

 “My divine protection never once activated during his speech. Yet he still was very suspicious. Whatever he is planning it is reliant on him,” Crusch points out.

 “Captain, what do you think of this?” Garfiel looks towards the black haired knight.

 Subaru looked down at the floor intently, chin rested on a tight fist. His eyes were scrunched up as if deep in thought. When Subaru heard Garfiel’s voice he flinched, his iron gaze breaking from the floor.

 “Oh, yeah, sorry about that. I was just thinking… maybe Baku may be holding information from us to deceive us. Technically he wouldn’t necessarily be lying and he still would be telling the truth. Most of the information we needed was left out and we were given partial answers.”

 Baku said that Kaji was partially involved with whatever he was doing. How Kaji was involved is up to debate. We should have pressed him for more knowledge— damn it! Baku’s behaviour was very inconsistent. His behavior didn't match with this outward appearance, Subaru clenched his fist in annoyance.

 “How about this,” Otto coughed into his fist getting everyone’s attention. “How about we have Crusch be more involved with the negotiations with Baku. Since he is the least trustworthy we should take the most precautions while talking with him.”

 “I object,” Subaru raises his hand. The others look at him in confusion. Crusch especially.

 “And why is that Subaru?” Julius questions Subaru.

 “I think Baku may know about Crusch’s divine protection,” Subaru’s statement is met by shock. The only one who wasn’t was Anastasia. She stood there arms crossed with a deadpan stare.

Baku POV
 Kaji, Baku, and Marco all sat in their room. Baku was looking around the room in the cabinets and drawers, since there hadn't been anything else to do. Staring at the abstract bright orange striations on the walls wasn’t a very riveting activity.

 “Hey Baku, did you find anything of use?” Kaji looks back to see the lie eater rummaging through a filing cabinet.

 “Nah not yet,” Baku’s voice muffled.

 As they had been eating Marco had opened a drawer and found a document. They had looked through it but couldn’t read it. It was very similar to Japanese but wasn’t exactly the same. Kaji opened the final drawer. As expected he didn’t find anything. As Kaji went to shut the drawer, the bottom of the wooden drawer moved. Kaji opened it back up and pressed down on the bottom.

 Inside revealed a leather notebook. It was worn out, the pages were yellow. Kaji wiped his hand on the surface of the book. The soft worn out leather was separated by a thin layer of fine gray dust. The leather on the front had scratches. Kaji took a closer look and realized there were words. Words that he could read. Although barely legible he could still make out the words written in gold, property of flugel.

 Baku looked over Kaji’s shoulder, “What do you have here?”

 “It looks like some old notebook.” Kaji flipped through the musty old pages, the rough and brittle pages pressing on his skin.

 Kaji tilted his head slightly back, “It is written in Kanji unlike the other papers.”

 Baku and Kaji flipped through the pages. The pages were chalk full of writing. The handwriting was very messy, similar to the front page. Kaji noticed some names in the notebook. Most often recurring ones being Satella and Alec Hoshin. Another word that had stood out was the word elf. Unlike the other notable ones this one was more relevant, only slightly more relevant.

 That girl with the silver hair. Her description nearly exactly fits the one of Satella in this notebook. If this were her or someone related that would make her an elf. This would also mean they most likely aren't from earth, Kaji deduced.

 There was suddenly a knock at their door. The loud noise snapped Kaji out of his deep thoughts. He scrambled with the notebook, quickly closing it and putting it back in the drawer. Baku stumbled towards the door. He looked back at Kaji, first pointing at the drawer with the notebook then putting a finger to his lips. Kaji quickly nodded. He got up and dusted his pants off. Baku slowly opened the door.

Subaru POV
 They didn’t know how to respond to this revelation. Felix’s face had gotten pale. His eyes widened. He opened his mouth but no words came out. He was the most affected out of all of them. Anastasia spoke up.

 “This shouldn’t come too much as a shock. If you had noticed, Baku paid very close attention to Crusch. He was the one he was most wary of. Now this could just be his intuition telling him that Crusch isn’t one to be easily deceived,” Anastasia took a breath, “But we can’t take any chances.”

 “Even if he knows about Crusch’s ability, how would that affect her? She still has her ability. Don’t underestimate Crusch. She is Crusch after all,” Felix gave Anastasia a frustrated look.

 Anastasia rubbed her temples, “Felix, it isn’t just Crusch that is at a heavy disadvantage here. We all are, we have no idea what this Baku guy is capable of. We don’t even know what Kaji and Marco are capable of. Be cautious, let’s still team up with him. If we can team up with him we could secure ourselves a more safe spot in this death game.”

 Felix attempts to retort but is then cut off as Crusch starts to speak, “I must agree with Anastasia about this. As confident as I am with my ability I am not sure if I am up for the current task.”

 Felix looks towards Crusch with his mouth wide with shock and dismay, “But Crusch.”

 “Don't worry Felix.” Crusch stops and her expression changes to confidence, “I may not be of use right now but I will still help as much as possible. Even if my divine protection has been rendered useless.”

 Felix quickly nodded. He slowly started to smile again. A loud almost childish voice cut through the room.

“Myself thinks Subaru’s got this. I mean he was able to figure out a lot about him. No one is undefeatable after all!” Garfiel exclaims while giving a wild toothy grin.

“I- i mean I guess but like we don’t know much about him. Even I couldn’t—” Subaru paused for a second.

Should I tell them that I don’t really know much about him? I am just as confused if not more confused than them. I am not nearly as great as they make me out to be. I’ll just be gambling their lives away. If I tell them about how much they don’t know it may collapse their morale. I might just need to use return by death. I don’t want to but it feels really necessary. I’ll hold off until later. It is the best way to counter most people. I just can’t let Baku know about it.

“I couldn’t really do much to help out,” Subaru responds sheepishly, scratching the back of his head.

“Even if you can’t directly help with negotiations you can still help decipher the man’s intentions. That is in and of itself a very herculean task. This foe we are facing is not a joke after all,” Julius chides his fellow knight.

 “First of all we don’t know if he is a foe or friend. Second of all, what the hell is up with you right now. This nice act is very weird,” Subaru huffed angrily, snapping Subaru out of his thoughts. Julius chuckled at Subaru's outburst.

 “Please stop fighting with Julius. So, back on topic, who are we sending to talk to Baku and his team?” Otto fidgeted with his cloak.

 “I say we send Otto, Anastasia, and Subaru. After all you guys have the most experience dealing with him,” Emilia smiled. Everyone nodded in agreement.

 “Don’t worry Emilia. I’ll make sure everything goes smoothly,” Subaru exclaims while hitting his signature pose. Subaru felt a pang of regret as Emilia met his gaze.

 Making large promises may be a bit reckless. I must do it for them, Subaru steels his determination.

Baku POV
 Baku gripped the shiny silver door knob. It was cool to the touch. He slowly opened the door. On the other side stood three other people. The merchant in pink; Anastasia . The merchant in green; Otto. Finally there was the knight; Subaru. Seeing the familiar faces at the door, Baku gave them a big smile. Baku had anticipated them coming. He knew it would be them. Since they had been the ones he originally talked with he knew that they would be the ambassadors for the group. Their expressions were slightly different from when they had previously met. They were more cautious. It was barely noticeable but was definitely there.

Since they have sent out the same people that can tell me one thing. They have figured out my schemes to some extent. They have some level of competence unlike the other groups. Seeing such potential is rare, Baku notices.

“What are you guys up to? How are you doing?” Baku gave a friendly smile.

“We are doing pretty good. How about you?” Anastasia responds with a smile reaching out her hand to greet the lie eater.

“Couldn’t be doing better. How about you guys come inside,” Baku shakes Anastasia’s hand.

 As they walk inside, Subaru sees Kaji. Kaji approaches Subaru, “Hey Subaru, how are you doing?”

 “Good, all things considered.”

 The guests entered and took a seat on a chair. Baku’s room was a much smaller version of the other team’s room. It didn’t have the fancy cathedral that looked out to the sky but it had its other advantages. There was much more furniture. There was an oval table in the middle. Surrounding it were chairs. They were a dark brown and had a shine to them. They looked just as expensive as the rest of the tower.

 “So have you considered the alliance?” Baku asks while maintaining his cheery voice.

 “Yes, we have decided to agree to the alliance. However you must at least share some information on the next challenge,” Anastasia presses unwaveringly.

 They are much more reserved with their expressions. They don’t seem to have the same level of trust as earlier. This further confirms my theory. Things are running very smoothly so far. Seems like my predictions were correct. They seem like suitable partners in the long run. Still have a lot to confirm, Baku thinks to himself.

“So what I am aware of this will be an escape room type game. You shouldn’t have to worry about having the death of other people on your hand,” Baku drops innocently.

Anastaisa’s looked quickly towards Subaru. Her eyes widened with surprise for a split second. Then her facial expression returned to normal. Baku had noticed the expression.

“Surprised? Come on we are allies are we not. I am offended by how low you think of me,” Baku gives a mock offended expression.

“No, no it isn’t that. Alliances have their standards don’t they. It was only natural to give us such info,” Otto speaks up.

“I am glad we are on the same page,” Baku looks up at the clock. “Well look at what time it is. It is almost time for the game to start. This next game will be very instructive.”

“What did you say? I didn’t catch that last part?” Otto gives Baku a confused look.

“Oh no it is nothing.”

“We should probably start heading back now,” Subaru tells Baku. With that they get up and head towards the door. As soon as they walk out Kaji takes out the journal from the cabinet. Kaji knew there was more to this place. He knew there was a history to this place; he just had to uncover it.

Subaru POV
The group anticipated anxiously for the teleportation to happen. The idea of being sent to a completely random place at the will of a somewhat sinister being was gnawing on their nerves. Soon enough the floor beneath them disappeared momentarily. Under them was a completely different door in a completely different room.
