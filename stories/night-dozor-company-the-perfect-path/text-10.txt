Chapter 10-Hospital | Night Dozor Company: The Perfect Path

Everyone was shocked when Patrick looked at them, announcing his last mission. They all go near Patrick. Accomplished Ice asked Patrick.
>
“If you knew that we were going after you, why did you let us follow?”
>
Patrick looked at them with an indifferent expression before slowly going towards the entrance of the hospital. With a soft voice, he said.
>
“This is the place I will solve my final case, this is the hospital that was flourishing quite well before the war. Suddenly, this place was abandoned….Why? That's what I have to figure out here.”
>
Patrick observed the gate’s lock and called out.
>
“Dog-”
>
He shut his mouth for a moment before opening his mouth.
>
“Oh yeah…..he's gone.”
>
He muttered under his breath and took a knife out of his pocket. The knife was rusted and the handle looked like it could fall any moment. With a deep breath, Patrick raised the knife in his hand.
>
*clank”
>
Patrick struck the lock on the old dirty entry gate to the hospital with the knife. The lock broke down, the knife broke into smaller pieces that went flying against the direction of the gate. One of the pieces hit Verstappen on his arm, right below the gunshot wound.
>
“Aarghhh”
>
Verstappen fell down in pain. Section kneeled beside him, helping him to stand. The new wound had triggered his old “supposed numb” gunshot wound. His mind went blank for a second. After a few seconds of groaning, he grabbed Section’s hand with his other hand and stood up.
>
“Still hurts, huh? Why pretend that it doesn't?”
>
Verstappen looked at Section for a moment and showed a painful smile before saying.
>
“It wouldn't help if I complain, we have more important matters to attend to right now.”
>
Patrick looked at them with a cold expression as if he didn't care. He tried opening the entrance door to the hospital, applying all his strength but the door didn't budge. Accomplished Ice and Odysseus helped him push the door with all their might. Suddenly, a creaking noise was heard. The door was pushed open. A little bit of blood flowed from Verstappen's wound but it stopped after a few drops.
>
“...”
>
The hospital looked uncanny and dirty, spider webs, algae in water bodies and moss could be found almost everywhere. The whole place was green with a pathway between the trees that let to the entrance of the building.
>
“...”
>
A noise from a tree was heard. They all looked at the trees at the side of the footpath. A swarm of pigeons flew from the tree. Everyone looked at the swarm with envious eyes.
>
“I wonder when we will regain that freedom….like those pigeons have.”
>
Odysseus said in a disheartened tone. He slowly muttered.
>
“It's been feeling like it's been years since Night betrayed us. But shockingly, it's been just 15 days. I wonder when this hell will be over.”
>
Section looked at Odysseus. He tapped Odysseus’s back lightly.
>
“We soon will, Ody. We soon will. We just have to wait a little longer. We have to just wait….till Patrick’s final mission is resolved. We'll be living like we were before, just need to wait.”
>
“Just like before, you say? But Doggo won't be there anymore.”
>
Section’s mouth was shut after hearing this. Accomplished Ice shrugged at the conversation and quietly followed them beside Verstappen. They all arrive at the entrance. Patrick looked at the glass door that led to the entry of the hospital.
>
“..!!”
>
Patrick looked at the ground around the door. What he saw shocked him. The ground had marks of someone opening the door recently. The dust was significantly less than the other places on the ground. Patrick grabbed his broken knife as he carefully opened the door. The floor was dusty and filled with broken tiles.
>
*cough cough*
>
The dusty environment made Accomplished Ice cough. Odysseus got spooked out at the sudden coughs. Patrick continued his careful approach inside. He found a few drawers and locker and started searching through them. He found some records of history of the hospital but it was written in another language
>
‘Kirchten Hospital had 77 patients. After the ⬛⬛⬛⬛. The hospital is in chaos. The war has started affecting the hospitals now. Patients A45 to C23 missing, other deported…’
>
“Why do the papers degrade in quality right where the important parts lie?”
>
He kept searching and searching but he couldn't find anything useful. He zoned out for a moment, trying to sort out his findings. After a few seconds, Odysseus tapped his shoulder with a medium force, snapping Patrick back to reality.
>
Suddenly the lights around the building turned on in an instant. The doors they came from shut off immediately. A cover of aluminium wrapped the windows and glass doors from inside. It hurt their eyes as the lights started twitching.
>
“Goddamn, what the heck is this?”
>
Suddenly a loud sound started playing in the building. They all covered their ears in an instant. Blood came from their nose and eyes. Section gathered courage and assessed the room.
>
“Bingo!”
>
He removed one hand covering his ear to grab a big rock from his pocket and threw it at a switchboard looking thing. The rock broke through the switchboard and damaged the wires. Everyone started gasping as they fell to the dusty floor.
>
“Good thinking.”
>
Patrick complimented Section before getting up after a few minutes. He went towards the switchboard to observe it. The switchboard had sparks coming out of it. Patrick tried to move his face closer to the switchboard only for it to explode. Patrick felt a burning sensation in his eyes for a moment. He put his hands in his pocket and broke the tiny bottle he grabbed into his eyes.
>
“Ahh! Man it hurts!”
>
He gasped for air, but the dust had other plans. He started coughing violently before spitting out blood.
>
“And that's what should happen to kids like you. Always trying to know more than necessary. Oh how good it feels, knowing you all will die in this place!”
>
A voice of a female was heard out of nowhere.
