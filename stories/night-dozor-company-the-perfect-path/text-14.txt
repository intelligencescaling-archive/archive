Chapter 14-Odysseus's Answer | Night Dozor Company: The Perfect Path

“The lever has access to a poison that's right underneath the ground…right under you. The lever will determine who you want to kill. Simply use the lever to navigate through your options to kill someone…and you will be spared. But if you take too long, the poison will be shot at a person randomly.”

Environment told Odysseus in a bold tone before going back and leaning his back towards a wall.

“Kill…”

Odysseus gazed at the screen for a few minutes with a straight face as he heard the instructions from Environment. He started tapping the floor with his legs. The screen showed everyone lying unconscious. Odysseus smiled bitterly before saying.

“I am happy that they are alive…I thought they all died when they…”

Odysseus started sobbing and faced downwards, as if not wanting anyone to see him like this.

“Would you like to strike a deal with me?”

Night Dozor scoffed at Odysseus and looked at him with an arrogant look. Environment, leaning with his back towards the wall, pulled out a cigarette. Lobo just stood there as if he was contemplating something.

“What do you think is your state right now?”

Night Dozor walked around slowly with small steps as she said.

“What could you possibly trade right now? You are infront of me…”

Night Dozor paused and came near the window, standing with her hand on the window. Odysseus could not clearly see Night Dozor so far away, he squinted his eyes and saw a yellow light coming from the window.

“...helpless, tied to that stupid chair. Your friends are out there in that place, unconscious at that. You have nothing to offer.”

Odysseus stood up slowly. He spread his arms wide open. He shouted loudly when he revealed.

“I still have evidence against you.”

Night Dozor was shook at the mention of evidence against her. She had been thinking that the evidence was destroyed along with ‘Dozor’s info dump’. She clenched her fists tightly as she asked.

“Where is it?”

She didn't want any evidence to be left behind. She thought in her head.

“I don't want that evidence to remain. If even a bit of the evidence is leaked, then I'm screwed.”

“Come with me, then. I'll show you the way…”

Odysseus said with a painful expression. He chains bit his hands here and there.

“Argh…”

Odysseus fell back on the chair. Night Dozor contemplated the decision in her mind. She kept glancing repeatedly at Lobo and Environment. Environment looked back as he threw the ciggerate.

“I won't help you in making your decision. Boss has only given me the task of protecting you. Not to help with your mental crisis.”

Night Dozor had an anxious expression on her face. She feared that Odysseus might try something smart if she went down there. She thought for a moment before nodding to Environment as if telling him to follow her. She walked towards the exit of the room and took the stairs.

“Heh, even if he tries something…i doubt Envi wouldn't be able to tackle that. After all…”

She glanced back at Environment following her.

“...he is a trained professional.”.

As Night Dozor opened the door to the white room Odysseus was sitting in, his legs taps became faster and faster. Night Dozor scoffed at Odysseus and thought.

“He must be quite anxious…quite pitiful for Ody.”

Night Dozor approached Odysseus closer with a wide smile, along with Environment following her slowly. Odysseus had fully laid his body on the chair, with his face facing upwards. Night Dozor’s smile froze as she saw Odysseus smiling ear to ear. Odysseus straightened his back as he said.

“Made a mistake again?”

*boom*

Suddenly, loud explosions were heard.

“Wha- what's going on?”

Night Dozor panicked. She looked at Odysseus with a scared face. Environment pulled out the revolver from his holster. Then, the wall to the left side of Odysseus blew up, with debris flying everywhere.

“Miss me?....Dozor, or should I say… Fox?”

“You!”

Night Dozor and Environment gritted their teeth tightly as a man emerged from the black smoke of the explosions.

“Sorry Ody, it took me long.”

Faded appeared out of the smoke, throwing his RPG away.

*bang bang*

Environment didn't waste a second as he fired at Faded. Faded blocked the bullet with a big barrier that looked like a shield but it was the size of a human. Night Dozor yelled at Faded.

“Why…HOW ARE YOU HERE! YOU BASTARD!”

Faded looked at Night Dozor with a frown. The former pulled out a Desert Eagle from his coat and fired at Night Dozor while approaching Odysseus.

*bang*

“Quick!”

Environment pushed Night Dozor aside. Environment carried Night Dozor to cover and shot back at Faded. Faded placed down the barrier on the ground and tried to cut Odysseus’s chain.

“..!”

Faded smelled a familiar scent coming from the chains. He quickly stopped cutting the chains quickly and fired at Environment to make sure he doesn't leave the cover.

“These chains reekl of nitromethane…if I cut the chains wrong, you will be blown into pieces. Same will happen if you put any force on them…after I cut them a little. Do you know where the control room is?”

Faded told Odysseus with a bitter expression. He knew that there wasn't much time. Odysseus looked at the screen the entire time and said.

“The control room must be through that door Night and Environment walked in…howev-”

*bang*

The door opened. Lobo stood behind the door with a gun in his hand facing towards Faded. The bullet missed Faded and hit the barrier on the side.

“Didn't expect this from you, pal.”

*bang bang….bang*

“Shi-!”

Faded, without hesitation shot around the panicking Lobo. Lobo ran to dodge the bullets. Faded kept shooting around him, guiding his way to Environment and Night Dozor. Lobo sat beside Environment as he panted heavily.

“Lobo! Take Ms. Night Dozor out of here. I'll find a way to rendezvous at the headquarters.”

Environment told Lobo in a serious tone. Lobo simply nodded in answer. He held Night Dozor’s hand tightly and ran to the exit of the building. Environment shot at Faded to keep the cover.

“Anyway…”

Odysseus continued.

“I can't leave this chair, I suspect that if I leave it forcefully, the poison will be shot.”

Faded gave Odysseus a sad look when he heard that. Faded started to think of a way to get to the control room without leaving Odysseus at Environment’s hands.

“Aha! Got it!”

An idea flashed through his mind. Faded fired a whole magazine of seven bullets at Environment. Environment kept his cover to protect him from the bullets. Suddenly, he felt something drop around him.

“Maybe, it's just debris.” Environment thought to himself as he looked around. Suddenly he saw a grenade at a foot’s distance from him.

“Fuck!”

*boom*

Environment got away by the grenade. Faded threw another grenade to make sure Environment was dead.

*boom*

Faded quickly ran to the control room Odysseus mentioned before.

“Yeah! Things were already bad.”

He saw a lot of buttons on the wall beside the window Night Dozor looked at Odysseus. He took two-three minutes to analyse the buttons.

“Ye, got it.”

Faded pushed a button. The button was blue in colour and was hard to press.

“...”

Odysseus looked at Faded waving happily with a smile from the window. He saw a red light coming from the control room above. Although he was not sure if the red light really was there.

*beep beep beep*

Suddenly he heard a sound of a beeps coming from underneath. He quickly looked around. He looked at the huge hole in the wall Faded blew up.

“Ahh, this wall had the connection to the explosion *and* the poison. The time limit Environment mentioned before must be near its limit.”

“The poison must be contained beneath me too.”

Odysseus heart sank as he realised this. He looked around in a panic until his eyes were laid on the screen.

“...”

Odysseus looked at Patrick standing up from the screen. The sight filled Odysseus’s eyes with tears. He felt a strange sense of relief.

“Looks like I'll have to do it…”

Odysseus looked at Patrick waking Section up.

“For your sakes…”

Section’s instincts were slow as he wake up.

“If I have the chance to pick between ‘my death that'll confirm my life with you all’ and ‘a long life without you all’, I'd pick my death again and again…”

Odysseus spread his arms open like a magician. He looked at everyone with a bittersweet smile on the screen twitching as he said with tearful eyes.

“...even after a million times.”

With this said, Odysseus pulled the chains tightly and fell on the ground. The fluid inside the chains blew up Odysseus along with the poison beneath him.
