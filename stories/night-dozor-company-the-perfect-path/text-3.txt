Chapter 3-Wounded Use || Night Dozor Company: The Perfect Path

“Here, right here. In this place we created this group, we will be renaming our group to Night Dozor Company.”

“To Night Dozor Company.”

“To Night Dozor Company.”(everyone)

Patrick heard these voices faintly.

“Hey you, get up.”

Patrick felt a sudden force smack him on his cheek.

“You are to go inside and meet the boss right now.”

Saying this, a young man shot a glare at him before leaving the room. Patrick realised that he was not at the Night Dozor Company. He was in a familiar place.

“The IBI Headquarter, huh? This is nostalgic.”

Patrick felt numb, he was betrayed, he was alone, he was probably at Death's Door. He looked around but couldn't find anything, until he looked at his clothes, there was blood. That was not his blood, he was sure. He tried harder and harder to remember.

“Ashes….”

He was reminded of how AndyTeasey had sacrificed himself for the survival of the remaining Night Dozor Company.

“Night….”

He clenched his fists tightly, but the power he once had was not there anymore. He felt powerless, he could not do anything. He tried to stand. But his legs won't support him. He tried holding onto the chair for support but his hands gave up. He closed his eyes.

“...”

He felt himself being carried like how people carry an unconscious body.

“Is it my end?”

*thud*

As he thought he was dying and ascending somewhere, he was dropped onto the floor. He opened his eyes to see a familiar face.

“Boss, here you go. The person you wanted to interrogate has come.”

Patrick heard a familiar voice behind his back. It was Environment, he had been fed up with how long Patrick took to enter the room and threw him inside by himself.

“Good job, Envi. You may leave.”

The ‘boss’ said in a bold tone. Environment left the room at once.

“Now, for you Nagumo, how does it feel to be so powerless? You couldn't save your friends. Your friend betrayed the company and sold you out. Your friend sacrificed his life to save the other members. Everyone else fled the scene apart from you.”

‘Nagumo’ was a code name for Patrick which the higher ups used. He had not heard this name in a long time. However, he was still out of energy to say something.

“Hahaha…” The boss laughed lightly. “Do you wish to save your friends?”

Patrick felt a surge of energy generated within him.

“I… can save my friends?”

“Indeed you can. But of course, there's a price to pay.”

“I don't care about the price. Name it and I'll pay it.”

Patrick felt energetic once again. He now had the energy to hold himself together once again. He had found a light of hope.

*Tch* “Have your senses gotten dull after you left the IBI. I don't have any shortage of money. It's your intellect that I want.”

“....Do you want me to rejoin the IBI?”

“No, that would be pleasant but I will trap myself if I make you rejoin the IBI, because as per the rules. No one that left the IBI can rejoin it.”

“Then what do you want from me?”

The boss took some papers from his desk and threw them at Patrick.

“Here are some cases I want you to solve. These cases haven't been solved for years now, of course you will not be getting paid for this.”

“I don't care about the payment, if I can help my friends survive, if I can rebuild the Night Dozor Company again.” He closed his eyes before slowly reopening them. “If I can confront Night again, then I'll take on the job.”

“Good… very good Nagumo.” He came closer to whisper in Nagumo's ear. “Keep in mind that your friends are still on the hunt right now. The hunt won't be stopped until you complete every case I provided you.”

Adrenaline rushed through Patrick's body. He quickly grabbed the papers thrown at him and started running outside. His memory helped him to navigate through the building. He had finally found a way to save his friends.
As the boss came out of the room and stood at the terrace of the building, he heard a voice.

“Boss, why didn't you tell him about how his friends will be killed on sight if they are found.”

It was Environment.

“Now tell me Envi, what's the point of rushing him. Let him take his time, we will have enough time to hunt everyone like we did with Faded. But why hasn't ⬛⬛⬛ come back?”
