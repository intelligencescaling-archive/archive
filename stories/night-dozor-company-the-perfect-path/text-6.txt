Chapter 6-Chaos | Night Dozor Company: The Perfect Path

“This arm sucks.”

Verstappen thought as he felt numb in the arm he had been shot. Although he couldn't sense anything in the arm, he could still feel the phantom pain. Doggo was driving swiftly on a rough road at night. Section was asleep in the back seat of the car. Suddenly the car stopped, causing Section to hit his heat on the front seat.

“Ouch! What kind of driving did you learn, goddamn it.”

He held his head for a moment as he said.

“We are at the destination.”

Doggo said in a calm tone in response. Section looked at Verstappen who was looking through the window at a bus stop. The bus stop didn't seem to have any buses here for a long time, maybe years. Section felt bittersweet as he came back to the place he once called home but it was during a crisis, especially when he had to betray a person that shared this same ‘home’.

“W-”

As Doggo was about to say something, Section stopped him as he covered his mouth with his hands. Doggo looked at Section's eyes that said “look quietly”. He looked in the direction Section was looking at, only to see another car parked at a distance. It seemed to be the only car that ever came in the past few years.

“Vers, do you have any guns in the car?”

Section asked Verstappen in a low, cautious tone.

“Why would I carry guns now?”

“Tch, we have to change the approach.”

He got out of his car, looking for some things. Doggo and Verstappen looked at him and wondered what he was doing.

“Any idea?” Doggo asked Verstappen.

“None.” Verstappen said after wondering for a few seconds.

Section grabbed two medium sized sticks, some rocks and pebbles. He carried the sticks in his arm and stuffed the pebbles in his pockets and returned to the car window.

“Let's go. These would be at least better than nothing.”

“What did you pick these for? Are we fighting dogs now?”

Doggo asked him in a frustrated tone.

“Suppose you face someone there, are you planning to fight them empty handed? You might be able to face them as you know Muay Thai and Judo, but what about me and Vers? I don't have fighting experience and Vers is carrying a wounded arm.”

Doggo thought for a moment and nodded quietly. Doggo and Verstappen jumped out of the car and followed Section. Section looked back to see them following him.

“I am feeling a bit nervous.”

He thought to himself.

“It's the same feeling when I accepted the position of CEO. Everyone following my back. Just the thought of it is enough to make me nervous like the first time.”

He smiled internally.

“Good old days.”

He whispered to himself unconsciously.

“You said something?”

Verstappen asked him in a soft tone.

“No… nothing.”

Section shrugged as he felt the same sensation he felt when he was the CEO. It didn't take him long to quickly focus on the path he was walking on.

*thud*

*thud*

*thud*

He walked and walked till he reached the place he once called home. He walked towards the abandoned place. Once he was a few steps away the from the entrance, his eyes were opened widely in surprise.

*gasp*

The entrance that shouldn't have been opened for years was as open as it once used to.

“Someone was here? Or maybe he is here right now.”

He thought to himself in disbelief.

“Guys-” *bam*

As he looked back to tell the others about what he saw, something hit him on the face hard enough to knock him unconscious. There was a person behind him, masked, with a rusted metal pan in hand.

“No!”

Doggo screamed and ran off to the person who knocked Section unconscious and found two other masked people with sticks in their hands.

“Son of a bitch. Now that's dangerous.”

He thought. One of the people ran towards Doggo with the stick to strike him down. Doggo quickly dodged the attack and punched the person in the gut. The person held onto his stomach and laid down in pain. As the person with the metal pan ran towards Doggo from his back and went to strike, Verstappen shouted.

“Doggo!”

“Wait? Doggo?”

The person thought to themselves as he forgot they were running towards Doggo and struck on his head. Surprisingly, Doggo parried the pan with his elbow and kicked the person with a roundhouse kick to the stomach. Doggo looked at the fallen person, about to kick once again as he heard the person talk.

“Wait, wait, wait, Doggo, it's us.”

The person removed the mask from his face and looked at Doggo. Verstappen flashed his phone's flashlight on the face.

“Ice?!!!! What are you doing here? Why are you attacking us?”

Doggo asked AccomplishedIce as he helped her stand.

“Wait… if you are Ice, then…”

He quickly went to the person he punched in the guts and removed his mask.

“Ody!!? Sorry bro, are you alright?”

Odysseus pointing his thumb upward while smiling in pain, still clenching his stomach.

“We're all back here now?”

Lobo removed his mask as well, revealing his face.

“We thought you were some officers or Night's men. We couldn't take any chances.”

AccomplishedIce looked at Doggo as she held the side of her stomach where she was kicked.

"Your hand...." AccomplishedIce looked at Verstappen's wounded hand and said.

"Oh don't worry about it. It doesn't hurt anymore."

Verstappen looked away slowly while holding his arm.

“Understandable, I suppose.” Doggo said.

“We thought Night's men had already gotten rid of the evidence stored here. Glad to know that you were the ones that opened the door here instead of them.” Verstappen said in a relieved tone.

“Huh? What do you mean? We just got here.” Lobo said in a horrified tone. “Why do I feel like I'm forgetting someone?.... Oh shit, Section!!!”

Lobo kneeled beside Section's unconscious body and sprinkled water on his face. After a few minutes of sprinkling, Section finally came conscious.

“Section has awoken!” Lobo notified everyone as he helped him get up.

Section looked around to see Lobo, Odysseus and AccomplishIce sitting with Verstappen and Doggo.

“Huh? You guys? But before that where is that goddamn bastard that knocked me out. We need to deal with them first.”

AccomplishedIce raised her hand in an embarrassed way.

“Huh??? Why???”

“Sit down and eat. We'll explain everything to you ....”

Odysseus said in a calming yet anxious voice. Hearing everything, Section argued with them and ate his pack of fingles before saying.

“Let's go inside. Night's downfall starts now.”

As they went inside the facility, squeezing between the walls, they got a nostalgic feeling of the place they once called home. They started roaming around, reminiscing the memories they shared here.

“Ahhhhh….”

A scream was heard from the way to the storage room. They quickly rushed there to find Odysseus screaming in horror.

“What happened Ody?”

Section asked in a concerning manner. Odysseus pointed at the door of the storage room in a horrified way. Odysseus said tremblingly.

“Blood….”
