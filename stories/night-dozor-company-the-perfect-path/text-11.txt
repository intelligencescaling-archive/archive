Chapter 11-Meeting Again | Night Dozor Company: The Perfect Path

Everyone felt shivers down their spine when they heard the sound. The sound of the girl was loud and direct. Patrick looked around the room in a hurry to find the source of the sound.

“You guys won't be finding anything useful. If anything, you brats should try to leave this room. Hehe.”

The girl giggled as she ended her speech. Accomplished Ice tried to leave the room swiftly but she noticed something. A metal tripwire was attached to each door. She didn't know what the tripwire did but she was sure that it didn't bring any good.

“Hey, I don't think we can leave this room.”

She said in a disheartened tone.

“Don’t stop. Keep looking around. You are bound to find something.”

Patrick said while searching the drawers. Hoping to find anything useful. He moved his eyes here and there until his eyes laid upon the place where the switchboard was. He grabbed his broken knife and hit the wall with it.

*thud*

The wall didn't budge. Odysseus walked beside Patrick and said.

“Give it here, you already suffered the explosion.”

Odysseus snatched the knife and hit the wall with all his force.

*thud* *thud* *thud*

Accomplished Ice kept roaming around the room, grabbing random stuff here and there. Verstappen observed the surroundings carefully while grabbing his wounded arm. Section looked at the ceiling as if he suspected something.

*bam*

The wall Odysseus was smashing broke down.

“It appears that the wall’s entire balance was that point. When the point crumbled, the entire wall did.”

Patrick said with a soft smile and went inside the room the wall was hiding. It contained a ladder, a rope and some gunpowder.

“...”

Suddenly, a sound of a saw spinning came from the darkness in the revealed room. Then a sound of metal dragging on the floor came. Everyone looked in the darkness, trying to see what was the source of the sound.

*buzz*

A robot came from the darkness, saw on its head. The saw was spinning at an incredibly fast speed. It ran in a straight line towards the hall in which everyone was. Verstappen barely dodged the saw on the robot. The robot crashed into the tripwire Accomplished Ice noticed earlier.

*boom*

A door in the way of the tripwire blew up, letting the robot go even further. The robot seemed to go in a path which was already determined. Everyone followed the robot.

*step*

*step*

*step*

*boom*

They found the robot crashed on the wall. Beside the wall, were stairs that led to a dead end. Section looked around the room before coming back at the robot. He disassembled the saw of the robot and put it on a stick. He looked at the place the robot crashed. A hole had formed on the wall. He tried to peep through the hole but before he could do so, acid started flowing from the hole.

“Holy, it almost caught my eye.”

Patrick pulled him back. The flow of the acid was increasing rapidly. They tried to run back but a huge metal door blocked the way. The acid was now overflowing from the hole. Accomplished Ice went to the stairs, she put something on the wall and took a container out of her pocket. She took the acid overflowing into the container and threw it on the implanted thing on the wall.

*boom*

An explosion occurred in almost no time. She tried to look the other way, but she was already knocked away by the explosion. Her eyes closed by the flash of the explosion. She lost her balance, almost falling into the acid.

“Not time to die yet, Ice.”

Odysseus grabbed her and saved her from falling into acid.

“Thanks”

Said Accomplished Ice while her legs trembled. She tried to open her eyes.

“...”

Accomplished Ice opened her eyes.

“...”

“Did the explosion blow up the bulb?”

“What are you talking about?”

Odysseus said in a ridiculous way.

“What? Why is there no light then?”

“...”

As they realised what happened. The female voice they heard previously could be heard again.

“Hah, haha, hahahaha.”

The female voice started laughing as if it was crazy.

“How does it feel? I knew the ‘explosives’ your friend used. The acid that was spilling all over in the floor…is highly reactive to the ‘explosives' that were used. As a result, the explosion took place earlier than anticipated. Thus, rendering your friend…”

Everyone heard the voice in an expression of horror. It was as if they saw a ghost. They looked at Accomplished Ice’s open eyes. All they could see were two greyish pupils in her eyes.

“...blind.”

Verstappen cried in horror as he yelled.

“WHAT DO YOU MEAN YOU KNOW THAT? HOW *COULD* YOU KNOW THAT?”

“Why do you think I can't know that?”

Asked the female voice in an amusing tone.

“That's not important. Tell us how you know that.”

Odysseus said in a trembling voice.

“You guys are no fun. I know the exact nature of the explosives because…”

As the voice completed its sentence, they felt a chill down their spine.

“...I was the one who taught the person who made these explosives.”

Accomplished Ice listened to the voice carefully. A question rose in her mind. She spoke aloud.

“If you taught this person how to make these explosives, would you know his name?”

The female voice hummed for a second.

“Ah, it's been so many years…his name was probably 5A03D…”

Accomplished Ice was initially confused at the combination of alphabets and numbers but she tried to decode it.

“Hmm? Why are you confused? Was it not 5A03D-”

Suddenly a sound of glass breaking came. Other female voices was heard from out of nowhere.

“You fucked up. You shouldn't have indulged in the conversation with Ice so deeply you forgot that there are more people here.”

“Umm, I think I'm more shocked to see that you are a man.”

Accomplished Ice was sent into a state of pain and confusion. Her eyes were aching and she couldn't process what was going on.

Suddenly Section’s voice was heard in an echo.

“Guys, me and Patrick have neutralised this guy. You can come from the room where the explosion blew up.”

A heavy, aged male voice came.

“Hahahaha, neutralised? It will take more than that to neutralise me.”

Sounds of Section and Patrick groaning in pain were heard. They quickly rushed to the place mentioned by Section earlier. Odysseus supported Accomplished Ice to run. They were surprised to see a ladder heading upwards. Verstappen climbed it quickly using his functional arm, Accomplished Ice and Odysseus took their time climbing it. Soon they found an open door. They rushed towards it, only to find an old man sitting in a chair facing a wall. The wall had Section and Patrick pinned on it by something.

“What the…”

The old man saw everyone come and spoke aloud.

“How does it feel to meet your doctor once again?”

Verstappen squinted his eyes on Section and screamed in confusion.

“Pigeons???!!!”
