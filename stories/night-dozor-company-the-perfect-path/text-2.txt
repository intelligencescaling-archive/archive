Ch 2-The Manhunt Begin | Night Dozor Company: The Perfect Path

Patrick was shaking in his boots when he heard Environment say that.

"What? Faded was hunted? No, that can't happen. No..."

Patrick knew in his heart that Faded was probably killed when he heard the gunshots and explosions from the call, yet he didn't want to believe. The guy, that the Executives would go to for unbiased judgement, was killed by IBI men? Patrick was numb. Environment hit Patrick on his head.

"Start walking, Mr. Honakyio. Ms. Night, I'd like you to follow us to complete the paperwork."

"Sure thing, Detective Environment. I'll go alongside you." Night said while teasing Patrick, giggling softly.

"Night... Why did you do this?"

Patrick murmured softly to himself, he had too much to process to be able to speak clearly. Patrick walked along Environment, still dozed off.

Environment took Patrick and Night Dozor to the car he came from. He looked at the road, a few cars were chasing a red VMW.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Section, Verstappen and Doggo went out the adjacent windows. They all tried to run to their cars. Unfortunately, Section's and Doggo's cars' tires didn't have any air. It was like someone had popped their tyres. They all look around, they find Verstappen's car and start running towards it.

*bang*

Not all of the shots that Environment had fired missed, one bullet struck Verstappen in his right arm.

"Ahhhh....." Verstappen groaned but kept moving towards the car along with the others.

They all reached his car, it was a blue Cerrari 420. Doggo took the driver seat and started to accelerate the car. He had experience in driving but not as much as Verstappen. He could only go 50 kmph on a curved road while Verstappen could go 80.

"It's not the time to be discouraged by this, Doggo. If you mess up here, everyone will die."

Doggo reminded himself and started speeding. Meanwhile, Section and Verstappen took the back passenger seat. Section started putting pressure on the wound Verstappen had suffered. He tore a part of fabric in Verstappen's car and binded his arm with it.

Verstappen was sub conscious. Section made him drink water and tried to keep him awake. Verstappen, thriving through the pain, was now feeling better than before. Seeing this, Section pulled out a map on his phone and started to look for a place to go. He started to quickly swipe out through the map.

"Come on, there must be a place where we can go..." He started to pray in his mind. He swiped... and swiped... until his eyes fell on a place he had marked a long time ago.

"That's it!”

“Doggo! Left!"

He started to narrate the path to Doggo. Doggo was confused to hear the path Section said about. "Looks like our CEO has yet another plan in mind." Doggo said jokingly and started following the path speedily.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Odysseus, AccomplishedIce and Lobo took the other side of adjacent windows. They suffered some cuts but nothing too harsh. They run to Ice’s car to escape. It was a red VMW. AccomplishedIce started the car, going about 45 kmph. Odysseus looked outside the car window, only to find a trap ahead of their way, designed to pop their tires.

“Ice! Go back! There's a trap ahead!”

Ice heard Odysseus’s warning and took a reverse turn. The man in charge of the trap was crouching beside the trap, grunting that it didn't work. He fired a few bullets at AccomplishedIce’s car in frustration.

“Watch out!”

Lobo screamed out in a panic. Ice started taking sharp turns to avoid the bullets. After all it was her new car, she didn't want it to get scratched just yet.

The guy in charge of the trap then got into his own car to follow the former's car. He was very fast, driving at 50 kmph.

*thud thud*

AccomplishedIce kept speeding and speeding, hoping to lose the guy. But the guy was just too persistent in chasing her car. He followed through her sharp turns. AccomplishedIce began looking for things she could use to lose him. She looked at the rear view mirror, however what she saw horrified her. Lobo was lying on the back seat unconscious. Odysseus was still looking for usable things left and right.

“Lobo!”

AccomplishedIce groaned and looked back in a panic. Subconsciously, she forgot to remove her hands from the steering wheel. She turned the steering wheel all the way to the left. There was a left turn, however the sudden impulse caused the car to flip over to the side, blocking the entire road. The car quickly caught on fire.

The guy chasing them stopped at the scene of the accident and started panicking.

“Oh shit, Sir ordered us to bring the criminals alive, if he gets to know they all died, I will lose my job. Probably even my life and my family. No I can't let that happen, I'll have to protect them, my family.”

He took a deep breath and then called Environment.

“Sir, unfortunately due to circumstances, we have lost the car you ordered us to follow.”

Environment was enraged to hear this. He started shouting at the guy.

“You goddamn dumbass, I told you before that you can't lose them, it was a mission we could not afford to fail.”

The guy heard Environment in fear as he stood there, speechless.

“...”

“What are you waiting for?!!! Go and find out where they went off to.”

“....yes sir.”

The guy looked at the sky as he talked to himself,

“Dispersed, you are screwed.”
