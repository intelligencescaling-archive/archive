Chapter 22-Revenge | Night Dozor Company: The Perfect Path

After the death of Night Dozor, the company wasted no time. They went back to their place by repairing the shuttle in a matter of two days. The pyramid had enough supplies for the time period.

“I won't forgive him…”

Lobo murmured in his sleep. His voice was filled with frustration. His face was swollen from all the crying. Patrick looked at Lobo with a worried expression.

…

After they had arrived back to their place, Faded went and hid the shuttle underground. The place looked like a recreation of Chernobyl. It was an exact replica of it. Lobo exited the shuttle with an angered expression, seemingly hiding something in his jacket.

…

“Hmm…”

A man wearing a black suit and tie was sitting on a chair, legs on top of his desk. His hands were supporting his head.

“I didn't expect you to come here. Weren't you working with Night Dozor?”

Lobo had entered the headquarters of IBI. His expression was static with his teeth gritted in frustration. He didn't waste a single minute and pulled out his assault rifle.

“Woah…you have big guns. Did you know I have some too?”

With this said, the IBI boss quickly slammed a button on his desk with his foot and hid under the desk.

“...”

“Why is nothing happening? Wher-”

The IBI boss groaned in frustration. He couldn't understand why no one had come.

“Where are the guards? You mean?”

Lobo finished the boss's sentence in a tired voice.

“..!”

The IBI boss quickly pulled out his tablet from the drawer to watch the cameras placed.

[Camera 1-No input]

[Camera 2-No input]

…

“Goddamn what happened to these cameras now?”

The boss started sweating in the chill room. He stood up slowly in disbelief.

[Camera 19-No input]

As he reached camera 20, a static flowed through the screen as the IBI boss felt a relief.

“Finally…”

But that relief was short-lived.

“What the…”

All he could see were corpses. Not a single person was alive. There were however very few traces of blood.

“No…”

The IBI boss felt hopeless as he stood up. He slowly turned to Lobo who had already aimed at him. Lobo fired a shot at his left leg.

“This one is for the experiments.”

He fired another shot at his right leg. The IBI boss couldn't help but scream in agony.

“This one for killing your own son.”

He then fired a shot at his shoulders.

“These for Doggo and Odysseus.”

“Stop!!!”

The IBI boss had fallen to his knees, clenching his wounds. Blood started coming out of his nose and mouth. But Lobo didn't stop. He fired another two shots at his stomach.

“These for Night and Ashes.”

Lobo fired a shot at his chest. A handful of blood was coughed by the boss.

“This one is for the Night Dozor Company.”

He then fired a last shot at the boss's head

“And this one…is for me.”

The IBI boss was lying on the floor, blood coming out of his lifeless body as Lobo put on a hat and slowly walked away.

….

{A few months later}

“Hey Vers.”

Section shook Verstappen's arm as he pointed towards a man walking in a busy street.

“Doesn't that man look familiar?”

Verstappen looked at a man who was wearing a beanie. Section then continued.

“That man killed Doggo. Looks like all of Night's men were not present in the pyramid.”

Verstappen didn't know what to say as he could only look in disbelief. He clenched his fists but released them in a moment.

“Wait a second. I'll do something.”

Section grabbed a metal wire and put it in his pocket. He went and followed the man quietly for a while before dragging him into an alley. The drag felt like a friend’s surprise hug.

“...”

A few minutes passed. Section came out of the alley unscathed. He quickly went back to Verstappen and sat in a car.

“Your hand good now?”

Verstappen smiled as he said with confidence.

“Better than ever.”

…

They then arrived at the new Chernobyl where Accomplished Ice was wearing contact lenses.

“It seems like your eyes are cured?”

Accomplished Ice said in a light voice.

“Not cured, I'm still 70% blind in my right eye.”

As she said this, Faded came out of his room, wearing a black leather jacket and a black hat.

“Ah…You all came back huh?”

Faded said in a gentle tone.

“Well, since Dozor is dead, what do you want to call this place?”

Everyone wondered with hands on their chins. Suddenly, Section exclaimed.

“How about we call this, The Imperium of Eutopia!”

….

“Haha, what a weird name. Did he want to create an empire of some sort?”
