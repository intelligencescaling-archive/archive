Chapter 8-"Goodbye, Old Friend" | Night Dozor Company: The Perfect Path

The fallen Section on the group felt pressure on his back as the bullets came into contact with Doggo's body, which was lying on his back. Night Dozor looked around and sensed the place was about to collapse.

“Quick Dozoriques! Head out, the place is going to fall.”

Hearing this, ‘Dozoriques’, the people working under Night ran outside. One of them carried Night on his shoulder and ran with the others.

Section got up from the group, only to see a Doggo who was groaning in pain. Verstappen’s eyes were filled with tears as he tried to go in to carry them. But seeing his wounded arm, everyone else stopped him. Patrick had already reunited with the group, meanwhile Lobo tried to pick up Doggo and run with Section. Once they all arrived at the main hall, Doggo fell from Section and Lobo’s hands and started vomiting out blood in huge quantities.

“Go! Leave me behind.”

Doggo said with a heavy breath.

“You all know….i won't survive these.”

Lobo, who was still trying to pick Doggo up, was trying to hold back his tears.

“We can still save you. We can't leave you here.”

“Trying to save me…will….be nothing more…….than a waste of effort.”

Lobo knew that what Doggo was saying was true, but he did not want to believe it. Lobo shut his mouth closed and left Doggo’s hand. He went to the corner of the hall, holding the ledge of the door as support.

Section put Lobo in a sitting position and he knelt down along with others.

“You all…it's been nice all these years….”

*cough* *cough*

Doggo vomited blood over and over again, trying not to lose his consciousness.

“Hey Doggo, this is the document we found. We can finally execute our plan.”

Doggo faintly smiled, his torso dripping with blood, knowing his sacrifice wasn't in vain. He accepted his death, knowing his death helped his friends to live as they did before. He let out some final breaths as he saw the document. Suddenly, the facility started shaking violently, more than before, as if it could be falling down any time now. Section took a deep breath and stood up.

“Let's go…..guys.”

They all started running towards the exit, Doggo still lying on the wall against his back. They knew exactly what was going to happen when the facility would collapse.

“Hey Faded, what would happen if everything goes south, and this place collapsed.”

A Section, in his teenage years, asked Faded with a concerned look.

“A Nuclear Meltdown. Do not worry, we'll be alright if we manage to escape from here. Because I have covered the entire Nuclear reactor with a substance that prevents Nuclear waste or radiation to bypass it.”

Section remembered it, along with the others.

“Hey Vers….you wanna drive?”

Doggo took his final breaths as he said this. He knew that Verstappen might not even hear his words, still he wanted to say it.

“...”

Verstappen looked back, however Doggo didn't lay on the place where Doggo lay moments ago. Instead, there were chunks of debris blocking his view. He ran along with the others, eventually going past the entrance they came from.

“...”

They watched as the place, no, their home crumbled down to mere debris in a matter of seconds. Everyone was trying to hold back tears, but alas they couldn't. Verstappen fell to his knees. He started having flashbacks of memories when he, Doggo and everyone else was spending time in their home when they were younger. He looked down, tears falling from his eyes. He wiped them clear and looked forward.

“Yes Doggo, I'll drive.”

He yelled towards the fallen home he once lived in. He then muttered under his breath.

“Goodbye…. Old Friend.”

Everyone saw Verstappen and tried to regain their composure. Verstappen looked at Section, grabbing him by the collar with his left hand and asked.

“Why did you lie to Doggo? Why did you tell him that we can execute our plan.”

Section replied with a sad expression.
“He's done a lot already…wouldn't it be better to rest in peace, even if it's a false delusion.”

Hearing him made Verstappen drop his hand from Section’s collar and stare at the sky. The sky was starry, but dusk was arriving. Slowly by slowly, he looked at the sun, eventually overshadowing the stars’ brightness. Only a single star, the Sun, remained.

Accomplished Ice suddenly asked in a concerning voice.

“Hey, where did Lobo go?”

Night Dozor had successfully evacuated from the facility.

“These wave detection gadgets are too good. Man they helped ease the trouble of finding them. But still, I can't wrap my head around how Faded survived. He shouldn't have survived. Envi confirmed it too.”

Suddenly a Dozorique came up to Night Dozor and said.

“Madamé, one of us found this man while he was late to evacuate. You might have something to do with him, we suppose.”

Saying this, the man presented a body to Night Dozor. The man was wearing a grey suit and pants. Night Dozor inspected the unconscious body of the man.

“....Lobo? Yes, this is definitely Lobo.”

Night Dozor beamed with joy as she realised that Lobo was captured.

A few minutes later, Lobo regained consciousness. He looked here and there, until he heard a voice.

“How does it feel to be captured, Lobo?”
Night Dozor said with a smug tone.

“...”

“What? Too shocked to speak?”

Lobo looked at Night Dozor with a confused look before finally opening his mouth.

“Who….are you? Who am I?”
