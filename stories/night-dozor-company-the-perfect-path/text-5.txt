After a few hours, Lobo regained his consciousness, he felt his wounds aching very badly. He felt a strong burning sensation on his burns.

“Who are you?”

“Ody, I'm Odysseus. Did you forget me?”

“Ice, what did you do?”

“Trust me, it'll make him remember.”

“Ice… Ody…”

“Lobo regained his consciousness! And he remembers us as well!”

“What do you mean by me remembering you? I'm so confused and my wounds are aching. What happened to us? Why are we wounded and weren't we in the car?”

Lobo enquired AccomplishedIce and Odysseus and tried to complain but after looking at their state, he held off his complaints.

“It's a long story, Lobo. I'll tell you on our way to ⬛⬛.”

“What? We are going there?”

“Yes, Section told us to regroup there.”

“Section? That must mean things are really bad.”

Hearing AccomplishedIce’s words, Lobo thought as he tried to remember but his memories were too blurry. It seemed like the accident had caused his recent memories to distort.

“So are you going to stand there or join us?”

Ody said in a low tone as he offered a can to Lobo.As they finished their meals, a question popped in Odysseus's mind.

“Hey Ice, wasn't your car blown in the accident? How are we supposed to go?”

AccomplishedIce looked at Odysseus in a mixed expression before saying.

“We'll steal one, duh. I hope you are not scared of it. We were going to commit illegal things worse than this in the plan Patrick mentioned and you agreed to it.”

Odysseus, wanting to complain, held them off as he remembered that he himself agreed to them. He looked at AccomplishedIce, who was dozed off, looking at the sky.

“What state would Patrick be in?” Was her thought as she shrugged it off and stood up.

“Let's go.”

To his surprise, AccomplishedIce had already started running away, he ran after her but the space between them neither grew nor got reduced. “Wait. What the hell?”

Odysseus and Lobo were already sitting in the police officer's car and Ice got to the back seat, waving goodbye to him. He looked as Odysseus started the car and speeded very fast.

“Why does this kind of stuff keep happening to me? Why in the world are you so unlucky, Dispersed”

“To ⬛⬛! Ody!”

A few days had passed since Section, Verstappen and Doggo went to the place Section had mentioned before. AccomplishedIce had also begun her journey to the place with Lobo and Odysseus. Meanwhile Patrick had solved 8 out of the 10 cases in the same span. His next case caused him to go to a place he knew very well. He got into his car and drove off to the place. It was raining heavily.

“So I had to come here in the end, huh? Guess the past never dies.”

He got out of his car and looked upon the abandoned place he once called home. The place was barren with no signs of life till eyes could see. There was a hidden button on the wall, a bit deep inside it, that was made by Faded years ago.

“Will this button really work after all these years?”

He pressed the button and a loud noise came out. Slowly, the building walls were pushed aside and a very thin pathway appeared between the walls.

“Guess we weren't this big back then.”

He chuckled, thriving his way through the tiny gap until he found a light coming out.

“So this actually still works, heh. Guess Faded’s gadgets were not only big talk.”
He came out of the tiny gap to be greeted by a big entrance, with a sign saying, ‘Dozor’s Info Dump’. He looked at the sign, although looking damaged, was still beaming with light like it once did.

“This place… is one that I'll never forget. This is the place where we created the Night Dozor Company. This…”

He paused for a moment before speaking again.

“...was also during the time we said goodbye to this place 12 years ago.”

Patrick went inside the entrance and found the old living room with a T.V, doubting it even works anymore. He went deeper into the facility, stealing glances across the place. The kitchen, the bedrooms, the research grounds. His mind was flooded with memories of the past, he looked at everything until he finally went to the main storage room. It required 2 passcodes.

“Passcodes, huh? I doubt I remember it tho.”

He brainstormed the things they used to do, especially Night. His mind flashed with an idea. He quickly typed a passcode on the tab.

‘D E R A N G E D C O M M E N T S’

He remembered how Night Dozor used to keep a record of anything unhinged they used to say. ‘Deranged Comments’ was the name Night Dozor gave to the record. As he thought of the second passcode, he felt a cold sensation down his spine. There was something poking his back. He froze in place.

“Quite interesting…. An intruder? I didn't think Dozor would send her men here to destroy the evidence. Well intruder, here's a piece of advice for your next life. Don't try to know too much.”

Patrick heard a voice behind his back. He found the voice quite familiar but he couldn't turn back. After countless efforts, he looked back.

*bang*

A gunshot was fired.
