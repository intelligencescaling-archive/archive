Chapter 13-Trolley of Death | Night Dozor Company: The Perfect Path

Odysseus saw Patrick fall into the collapsing floor. Luckily, Odysseus had been farther to the old doctor than Patrick. He looked at Environment.

“Now Mr. Odysseus, this is nothing personal but as I said I have to eliminate you.”

Environment approached Odysseus like a point forming an arc.

“...”

Odysseus had his mouth shut as he thought to himself.

“I do not think negotiating with him would be very fruitful.”

Odysseus walked away from Environment, completing the circular path.  Suddenly he felt a sense of danger like a snake had wrapped itself around him. He looked back.

“Hey, Ody.”

Odysseus could not believe his eyes. He looked at the face of his friend. Lobo stood behind him, smiling nostalgically. His smile felt warm and comforting to Odysseus. Odysseus grabbed Lobo by his arms as he said.

“Lobo? Is it really you?...”

Lobo didn't answer but continued to smile.

“Oh you don't know how everyone was worried about you. Oh and sad news..”

Odysseus paused for a moment and turned his head at Environment. He gritted his teeth as he mentally cursed Environment in a rage. He finally looked back at Lobo. He was shook to his bones when he saw another person walking through the door from which Environment previously entered.

“What?! Why are you here?”

He then looked at Lobo as if he was asking him.

“Lobo!!! Why is *she* here?”

Lobo stood there like a statue, still smiling as before. However, his smile now didn't feel warm, but it felt treacherous. He started taking small and slow steps backwards.

“No… It can't be! LOBO, YOU WERE THERE WHEN SHE SOLD US OUT!”

His eyes felt watery as he continued.

“Didn't you see how Ashes was killed! Patrick…Section…ICE AND VERS! THEY ALL FELL INTO THAT SINKHOLE RIGHT NOW! HAVE YOU LOST YOUR MIND!”

Odysseus lowered his head in disappointment. His eyes wouldn't open. He didn't want to believe that Lobo was working with Night Dozor. He felt powerless in his legs when he realised he wasn't moving back anymore. He slowly opened his eyes.

*clank*

---------------------------------------------------------------------------

“...”

“I'm back here again?”

Lobo gazed at the white sky as he opened his eyes. He was lying on the pitch black ground. The place didn't seem to change one bit.

“I've been coming here quite often. But I can't remember anything before waking up here.”

He then stood up on his feet. Lobo felt light on his feet as well as his entire body. His body felt like its weight was halved. But this was nothing new to him. He looked around for a while before black smoke filled the area.

“Hehe…”

He heard a soft giggle as the black smoke enveloped the entire place.

“Hey Lobo, how are you?”

Lobo signed as he was fed up but had gotten used to ‘his’ giggles.

“Thanks to you I've no idea how to answer that question. Anyway, you have provided me with no knowledge that would help me determine what ‘this place’ is, who are ‘you’?”

‘His’ giggles were now filling Lobo’s ears.

“Arghhh…”

Lobo was annoyed at the giggles. He stomped the ground in anger.

“...”

Suddenly the giggles stopped and ‘he’ came in front of Lobo. Lobo couldn't see ‘him’ because of the black smoke.

“Finally…”

Lobo felt a sense of relief as the place was now quiet. The density of the black smoke lowered. The smoke reduced to the point Lobo could see everything except ‘his’ face. The one that was giggling at Lobo this entire time was a kid. ‘He’ stood in front of him with his hands behind his back. ‘His’ posture felt bent towards him.

“Come with me.”

‘He’ said in a soft cheerful voice. ‘He’ had fair skin and was wearing white clothes like a priest. Lobo thought in his head.

“This kid looks no older than 8 years old. Why am I stuck in this place with him… I will lose my mind here.”

‘He’ started humming as he hopped cheerfully while walking. They walked for almost half an hour before Lobo heard something.

“Hey Lobo! Lobo!”

Lobo heard a voice of a girl waking him up.

“Just how much are you going to sleep?”

“I guess Night is here…I'll have to go now…”

‘He’ turned around to look at Lobo. Lobo couldn't see his face but sensed a sad expression from ‘him’. He sighed lightly as he woke up.

“...”

Lobo saw Night Dozor yelling at him, trying to wake him up.

“Lobo! Did you forget we have to take revenge on our ‘friends’ that betrayed you?”

---------------------------------------------------------------------------

“Fuck…My head hurts…”

Odysseus woke up from a deep sleep. He was lying on a cold, tiled floor. He tried to stand up.

*clank*

“Whoa!”

His hands were tied to a switch with a chain. When he tried to stand up, he lost balance and ended up sitting on a chair.

“What the heck?!!!”

He wasn't in the abandoned hospital he entered. Where he found himself was a large illuminated room. The room’s walls were whitewashed without any paint applied.

“Where am I…?”

“Boss, he woke up.”

Odysseus heard a young male voice through a speaker.

“What the hell? Did I go blind or something? I can't see any speakers. This voice doesn't feel like I heard it directly either.”

Suddenly a voice of a mic being tapped was heard by Odysseus. He put his hand on his head, as if lessening the pain.

“Odyyyy.”

Odysseus heard Night Dozor's voice through the speakers. He felt his blood boil as he heard that voice.

“Night…”

He said while gritting his teeth tightly.

“Now I remember what happened…Lobo…we won't forgive you…you have no idea how worried we were when we saw you were missing…”

He paused for a moment before yelling out. He stood for a second before falling back onto the chair.

“JUST RIGHT AFTER DOGGO DIED!”

He didn't hear a response from Lobo, but instead one from Night Dozor.

“Blah blah blah! I have no time for your complaints.”

Night Dozor said in a high voice while laughing at him. His eyes widened, he clenched his fists and tried to stand up again.

*clank*

Yet he was pulled back onto the chair.

“Hahaha…Look up.”

Night Dozor laughed. Suddenly Odysseus looked up, he saw Night Dozor, Lobo and Environment behind a glass window around 10 meters above him.

“Ody, there is a lever under the chair you are sitting on…a lever that'll kill someone.”

Odysseus quickly looked for the mentioned lever. He found the lever under his chair. The lever was coated with a lubricant. He quickly looked forward where a screen appeared out of nowhere.

“Odysseus, look. Your friends…”

Environment said in a cold and commanding tone. The screen displayed the people who fell into the sinkhole earlier. Everyone was lying on the ground, unconscious.

“PATRICK! SECTION! VERS! ICE!”

Odysseus tried to break open his chains but the chains didn't break snd instead bruised his hands.

“Odysseus! Tell me…”

Odysseus looked at the screen hopelessly as he heard.

“...Who do you want to kill?"
