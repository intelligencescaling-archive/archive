Chapter 16-Questions | Night Dozor Company: The Perfect Path

It was a few days after Odysseus died. The surviving Night Dozor Company had found an apartment they rented for some days. Accomplished Ice was taken to a doctor for her eyes.

“Hard to say if she'll regain her sigh completely."

Was the answer by the doctor. They stayed in that apartment for a few days, contemplating their decisions till now.

“If we continue further, we would have no option other than to go all or nothing.”

Section said in a weak tone. His body laid against the carpet on the tiled floor. The floor looked clean and the walls had a brick design to them.

“Do we not take revenge for what she did? To Odysseus and Doggo?”

Verstappen said in a sullen yet frustrated voice. He gritted his teeth as he clenched his fists while closing his eyes.

“But who will we be going against? Night is one of us, after all. I can't bring myself to kill her…”

Patrick held his face as he looked upwards.

“...even after what she did.”

Everyone looked at Patrick in a daze. They didn't know what to say to the answer to this statement Patrick just said.

“Even if we win…against the IBI…the Dozoriques…Night Dozor…”

Section closed his eyes as he tried to go to sleep.

“...will we get the satisfaction of victory?”

Section slowly lost track of what everyone else was saying as he opened his eyes, just to stare at the ceiling fan.

“Will we ever regain the friends we lost? Is there any meaning?”

Section continued to stare at the ceiling fan as everything he heard was now distorted to him.

“If we manage to defeat Night, will anything change?”

As he was slowly about to close his eyes and fall into slumber, his eyes were laid on Verstappen. He remembered what Verstappen said a couple days ago.

“...I don't want to lose any of you…”

The memory of the latter’s tear filled face after the destruction of Chernobyl flashed through his mind. Soon enough, a person with eyes, clear as a crystal appeared infront of him as ‘he’ said.

“You can't lose. Mr CEO.”

As Section was about to fall asleep, he felt a surge of shock in his spine, causing him to stand straight in a second. Everyone's thoughts were replaced by Section jumping into a standing position.

“What are you doing?”

Verstappen looked at Section, his eyes wide open and his hair mostly covering his eyes. Section looked around in a caution manner before opening his mouth.

“Guys, we can't lose hope here. Even if Night killed some of us, we have to get going.”

Section spread his arms out wide open.

“Don’t forget all our promises, remember what we promised?”

Section raised his hand forward at waist height.

“We don't leave each other hanging, we teach them a lesson when they mess.”

Everyone looked at Section in a daze as if they thought,

“Has this guy lost his mind?”

Verstappen and Patrick smiled as Section’s expression went from a motivating one to a dumbfounded one. Their smiles were bitter as they remembered how their friends died. Meanwhile, Accomplished Ice looked into darkness as she felt the tight bandage wrapped around her head to cover her eyes.

Lobo looked around as he found himself in the same place. He was again greeted by the white fog and black ground. His eyes were somehow, a bit watery this time. ‘He’ looked at Lobo and and signalled to follow.

“How much will you make me walk? We've been going for days, if not weeks at this point. Why don't you just tell me what we are searching for?”

Lobo said in an exhausted manner while sitting down. ‘He’ looked at Lobo with an indifferent smile as the fog surrounding the place reduced.

“Interesting, you have a face.”

Lobo looked at the child wearing a thin white robe, staring into his eyes. ‘His’ hair was golden and eyes were blue as a sapphire.

“Follow me, I trust you the end would be worth the walk.”

‘He’ then looked away and started walking. Lobo felt his body grow lighter as the fog around dissipated. He felt a new energy surge within his body.

“...”

Lobo walked, and walked slowly but steadily.

“...”
