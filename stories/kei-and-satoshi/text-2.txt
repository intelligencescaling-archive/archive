Chapter 1 - The Letter (Satoshi)

There was a folded piece of paper on the table when I returned to my room.

I stared at it for a long time before opening it, my mind coming up with countless theories around its contents.

Eventually. I unfolded the piece of paper and instantly knew who it was from.

Kei’s handwriting had always been slightly slanted to the right as if his mind moved faster than the pen allowed.

“Satoshi,” No greeting. That was unlike him.

“I used to call you selfish.” I felt my fingers tighten slightly around the page.

“I said you only cared about your own goals, your own thrills, your own path. But I was wrong, I was the selfish one.” I exhaled slowly.

“I never asked you what you wanted. I never cared to know. I only dragged you along the road I wanted to walk, convinced that because we were walking together, it must have been the right one for both of us.” My eyes lingered on that line longer than the rest.

“I didn’t let you chase your own definition of victory. I forced mine onto you. And I only realized that after losing you.” Losing you. I don’t know which of us that line belonged to.

“Right now, I still want to win. I still want to unite the three kingdoms. I still want to chase the thrills that make me feel alive. But I don’t want to drag you along that path anymore.” A faint, humorless smile crossed my face. He still didn’t understand.

“If walking it with me is what excites you, then let’s walk it together again. And if it isn’t, then I’ll respect that. I’ll walk my path, and you can walk yours.” I read that part twice.

“Not as rivals, not as tools for each other’s goals. But as two people choosing where they want to go. I hope the next time we meet, we can talk as friends again.”

I lowered the paper.

The room felt quiet.

For a long time, I just stared at the wall.

I don’t know why I didn’t want to walk that path with him, I only knew that I didn’t.

I haven’t felt any discomfort or loneliness since I left.

It was just something inside me, an indescribable feeling.

A voice constantly whispering in my mind, telling me that I have to walk alone, and I can’t help but agree with that voice.

I was born to be alone.

Chapter 2 - Meeting (Narrator)

Abami and Kamashiro met in a quiet room on an unremarkable day.

Kamashiro spoke first “So, what do you think of Kei and Satoshi so far?”

“I can’t be sure, they seem somewhat intelligent but they haven’t been tested well enough yet.” Abami replied

“I agree” Kamashiro said “It’s like giving someone a 6th grade math test. Acing that test doesn’t tell us whether the person is a talented 6th grader or a PHD level mathematician.”

Abami retorted “The results certainly won’t tell us but the method can give us a hint.”

“Satoshi seems to be more talented so far but he also doesn’t appear to be growing. Kei, on the other hand, looks like he’s actively trying to improve.”

“I noticed that as well.” Kamashiro replied. “I also noticed something else.”

He smiled slightly and spoke with a taunting tone “So Abami, did you notice it as well? The see-”

“Of course I did, you idiot.” Abami interrupted, “Kei used his opportunity on Fukusen Island to do something in the main electricity room, it’s very obvious what he did.”

“Well Kamashiro, do you actually know what he did or are you trying to get me to tell you?”

“Of course I know, but if I tell you then you can act like you know.” Kamashiro retorted

Abami replied “I know, but I don’t know if you know what I know. If I tell you what I know, then you can act like you already knew what I know.”

Kamashiro grinned slightly “You really are annoying Abami, it was clearly your turn to stop this first. I guess I’ll be the bigger man and end this pointless conversation.”

“Let’s get back on topic, I have an idea for the next game we’ll have Kei and Satoshi play. A game which will test their intelligence to its limit and put them in a prime area for growth.”

“I have a pretty good idea of what you’re planning.” Abami said “I’ll leave it to you. Make sure to prioritize their growth over testing their intelligence to its limit. After all, even if you take their intelligence to their absolute limit and had them both join together, they still wouldn’t be able to get either one of us to try.”

Kamashiro laughed, “Arrogant as usual, Abami.”

Abami then responded “You can call me arrogant, but I know that you agree.”

Kamashiro said “Well, I knew that you knew that I agree.”

Abami laughed as he walked towards the door “And I’m the childish one?”

Kamashiro ignored him. “I’ll send you the rules soon, you can tell Kei about them, I’ll tell his opponent.”

Chapter 3 - Realizations (Kei)

I’d spent the last few days in my room waiting for instructions about the next game.

I didn’t let the time go to waste though. Normally, I would’ve drowned it out with video games or old memories. This time, I cut everything off. I didn’t leave the room. I turned off every device. I let the silence stay.

That’s when I realized something I hadn’t understood before.

Intelligence doesn’t grow slowly. It jumps.

Back when I was with Satoshi, I thought I was getting smarter by learning from him. In reality, I was just becoming a false imitation of him. This was because real growth in intelligence wasn’t a slow and linear process, it consists of leaps that can only be realized when you arrive at them on your own.

Small quantitative changes over long periods of time transforming into large qualitative changes.

Growing intelligence in real life is similar to growing stronger in a shounen anime. You suddenly unlock new stages when you make new realizations or when you’re pushed into a corner, you can’t make those big jumps while being taught.

Satoshi’s way of outsmarting people was natural to him. He led opponents toward disqualifying themselves, or into helping him win without realizing it. I’d always tried to copy that approach, but it was never truly mine. And because of that, he would always be better at it than me.

What I hadn’t considered was that there were other ways to outsmart someone. Ways that fit how I thought, not how he did. And by growing in the many other methods, I will undoubtedly also grow in his methods.

I also came to many other minor realizations by reflecting on the past games I’ve played and what I could’ve done better. By the end of it, I could feel it. Something had shifted. I wasn’t who I had been a few days ago. Now I just needed a real situation to test it.

As I was thinking that, a loud knock on the door vibrated through my mind. It was the first actual noise I’ve heard in the past few days.

I opened the door to see Abami and I greeted him, but no words came out. Or rather, the words came out very faint because I hadn’t spoken in days.

I’m certain he was shocked when the door opened to my rough and unshaven look but a man like him would never let a hint of that shock show on his expression. He then asked if he could come in and I let him in.

The room was messy but we both had a seat around the table near the entrance.

Abami began to speak “The next game you’ll be playing is called Prison Break, a car will come pick you up in an hour to take you to Ashabiru Prison.” They really haven’t given me any time to prepare.

“You and your opponent will be playing the role of murderers who were sentenced to prison, due to your influence as rich men, your lawyers were able to negotiate your sentence down to 5 days so you and him will only be serving a 5 day sentence.” Getting 5 days for a murder charge, I must be really influential.

“The prison’s structure consists of 2 buildings connected by a wing, the North and South buildings. You will be playing this game in the North building with other prisoners locked in with you, they will all be actors who have been instructed to act as real prisoners.” Well that makes things easier, dealing with real prisoners would’ve been rough.

“Your opponent will also be in the prison as a prisoner, but this time, he won’t have any advantages over you, you won’t be told who he is and he won’t be told who you are.” Great, this game will at least have fairness but that also means that my opponent will be very intelligent, I can’t underestimate him.

“There is a hidden win condition, this game will be a race to see who can achieve the win condition first. If neither of you achieve it before your sentence of 5 days ends,

the closer one will be considered the victor.” Interesting, a hidden win condition, the name of the game, Prison Break, implies that the goal is to break out of the prison but it can’t be that simple.

“There are also hidden restrictions that lead to an immediate disqualification and would automatically make your opponent the victor.” I’d guess that getting caught trying to escape would be one of the things warranting a disqualification.

“There will be guards wearing blue and guards wearing black. The guards wearing blue will be writing notes about you which you can access anytime, the guards wearing black will be writing notes about your opponent which you won’t be allowed to access. A final warning, please try to avoid getting seriously injured because the infirmary in the North building is only capable of handling minor injuries. That is all, I’ll be going now, your car will be here in 56 minutes.” After saying that, Abami walked out without saying anything else.

Guards writing notes about us, that’s interesting. I’ve always wondered how an outsider would psychoanalyze me, I guess I can find out in this game. I might also benefit from getting my hands on my opponent’s notes.

For now, I should take a shower, shave and pack a bag with essentials like a minimal supply of canned food, water and lockpicks for the unlikely scenario that I’ll be allowed to bring it with me into the prison.

Chapter 4 - Prison Break (Kei)

A few hours later, I found myself in a prison cell on my own. I looked around the room and saw many normal things, a single bed, a sink, toilet, clock and essentials like toothpaste and a brush.

However, one thing caught my eye, an abnormality. There was a box in the corner of the room with 4 keyholes. I tried looking through the keyholes subtly but I couldn’t see anything so I retreated back to my bed. Everyone else also had a box so I couldn’t be identified through its presence.

I then walked towards the bars of the cell posing as a normal prisoner while I was attempting to observe the other prisoners in my line of sight. There was a number 43 stitched on my jumpsuit, and after looking around, the highest number I could see was a 48. I’d guess there are about 50 prisoners and a few hundred cells.

There were also many guards wearing grey outfits with numbers stitched on them. The highest I could see was 27 so I’d assume there are 30 grey outfit guards. There are also a few guards wearing blue and a few wearing black but they weren’t numbered and their total count shouldn’t exceed 10.

A guard walked between the cells calling out a name as he arrived in front of each occupied cell. After roll call was complete, the lights of the prison simultaneously shut off leaving us in complete darkness, then a loud voice echoed through the prison “Lights out convicts! Time to sleep!”

I laid in my bed staring at the ceiling for an unknown amount of time before I eventually fell into a deep sleep.

The next time I was conscious, blinding lights shined all over the prison at 8am. 10 hours of sleep was a luxury I hadn’t experienced in a long time. I woke up refreshed and waited till the cells were unlocked at 8:30.

When the bars moved to the side and opened a path, I slowly walked out of my cell and walked with the other prisoners in an organized manner to the yard. We were

instructed to gather there for a morning roll call at 9:00. A great opportunity to observe everyone in a group.

After roll call, we were free to go wherever we liked within the confines of the prison. Before we went off, the guard explained our obligations. Roll call at 9am in the yard, breakfast in the cafeteria at 10am, work between 4pm-6pm, lunch in the cafeteria at 6pm and a roll call at 9pm in our cells. Wake up time is at 8am, lights out is at 10pm. I thought it was a simple and effective system that left me ample time to search for the keys of the mysterious box in the prison cells.

I walked through the prison and discovered that there were eight key areas. The laundry room, the yard, the guard break room, the cafeteria, the housing units, the infirmary, the library and the visitation rooms.

After passing the day by and making a few friends, 4pm arrived without warning, it was now the work period. I had to choose a job, I could be a cook, a laundry organizer, an enforcer or a doctor’s assistant.

I carefully considered each option, a cook would give me access to the food that the prisoners eat, I could theoretically poison it but that isn’t practical. The cook’s most prominent advantage would probably be my ability to build a network of prisoners.

Next was the laundry organizer, the advantage here was obvious, in prison escape movies, the laundry room often has an escape route so it was likely that accessing the laundry room would give me easier access to escape.

As for the enforcer, it would allow me more authority as well as access to the guard’s break room for a potential key.

Finally, the doctor’s assistant role would probably also allow me to unlock an escape method as well as increased access.

After a short period of thought, I decided on being a laundry organizer. I didn’t question my decision too much as there was likely a way to clear this game regardless of the role I chose.

I confirmed my job selection with the guard and walked with a few other prisoners to the laundry room. My opponent could be among them, walking past me as a prisoner without my knowledge.

When we arrived, I pretended to work as I searched the area thoroughly and attempted to profile the prisoners around me. I flipped through clothes and looked between washing machines without alerting the guards and near the end of the work period, I found what I was looking for under a washing machine, a key.

Finding a key confirms that the keys for the box are scattered throughout the prison, it was likely essential to our escape to find the key. Of course, I thought of simply breaking the box to get what’s inside but it’ll probably lead to my disqualification so I determined that it wasn’t worth the risk.

My guess is that there are 8 keys scattered around the prison, one key in each of the main rooms. This means that me and my opponent can each get our hands on 4 keys and open our own boxes. But this also means that if one of us manages to find 5 keys, he can greatly restrict his opponent.

I quickly pocketed the key to avoid detection in case my opponent also chose to be a laundry organizer. My profiling led me to believe that my opponent wasn’t a laundry organizer but I can’t be too careful.

Once the clock struck 6pm, I walked to the cafeteria for lunch with a few other inmates, but on the way, I slipped away into my cell to place the key I found in my pillowcase to be cautious.

After putting the key into my pillowcase, I rejoined the inmates and made my way to the cafeteria.

I tried doing a quick search for a key in the cafeteria earlier during breakfast but I couldn’t find one so I had to be more thorough this time.

I dropped some food and drink numerous times throughout the cafeteria pretending that it was an accident so that I could look under the tens of tables in the cafeteria to see if a key is taped under one of them as that seemed like the most likely area for a hidden key.

On my 5th drop, my guess proved to be accurate. I found a key taped under a table, but before I could pick it up, I was called by a guard for a shakedown. The guard likely suspected me of transporting drugs by my frequent drops.

Since the guard’s an actor, it stands to reason that he knows that I’m not transporting drugs but he’s playing his role well.

I brought too much unwarranted attention by my actions but finding a second key makes up for it.

The guard performed the shakedown and didn’t find anything so he sent me back and told me to stop being clumsy.

I went and sat at the table with the key taped under it and subtly pocketed the key while pretending to eat. I then started to actually eat but I didn’t have high hopes.

The food looked like vomit and after having a bite, I confirmed that it tasted like vomit too. We were in prison after all.

After that, I returned to my room and hid the second key within my bedding. I had to be careful of my opponent stealing it so I cut a small line into the mattress and hid the keys inside it to avoid my opponent finding them.

The night’s roll call came about and then lights out. The first day has concluded with me finding 2 of the 8 keys.

Chapter 5 - Prison Break (Opponent)

The night before day 1

I’m in a prison with an unknown opponent and we’re both racing to achieve a hidden goal.

Basic deduction would dictate that the hidden goal is simply escaping from the prison but there should be something more to it, maybe the contents of the box in my room will tell me. I need to find 4 keys to open it, I’ll begin looking for them tomorrow, the only thing I can do now is roll call then go to bed.

I woke up on the first day of the Prison Break game. After the morning roll call and making a few friends, I headed to job selection at 4pm along with the 50 prisoners so I could select my job.

I chose to be an enforcer, I’ve determined that this role will give me the most access to areas so I could find keys.

After choosing my job, I patrolled the halls and when no one was looking, I snuck into the visitation rooms and quickly scanned under the tables. Incidentally, I found a key under the first table I searched under and didn’t have to look further.

I quickly pocketed it before leaving the room and returning to my job in the halls.

Enforcers also had access to the guard break room, so at 5:30, I entered the room to search for a key there but it was filled with guards. As I was about to turn around and leave, I noticed one of the guards with a key clipped to their belt buckle so I stayed.

I then introduced myself to the guards, knowing they were actors. I hoped I could at least shake their hands because the proximity would allow me to pocket the key but they shouted at me to go back out and patrol the halls.

There was only one thing left that I could do, I tripped on a table edge and landed on the guard with a key, I quickly unbuckled it and placed it in my back pocket.

I stood up to see 10 guards firing their tasers at me, my body instantly dropped to the ground making a loud thud.

I was writhing from the pain but I didn’t scream, screaming has always been a sign of weakness. I endured the pain, electricity buzzing through my body. I kept trying to get up but my body wouldn’t respond and I’d keep falling back down.

When I reached for the guard, I knew that this would happen, I knew I would be tased. After all, the guards would naturally assume that I was reaching for his gun. I was willing to be tased for the key, I had willingly experienced much worse pain in the past.

I looked at the guards as they were warning me to not try anything again but their words were inaudible, all I heard was loud colluding noises. All I could notice was their expressions that betrayed their angry words, many of them looked guilty, after all, they were only actors.

I finally managed to gather the strength to get up and I promptly left the room. I went to the showers and washed my body which was slightly burned from the tasers.

I then headed to the cafeteria and tried to subtly search for a third key but I couldn’t find it. I forced the horrible food down my throat then headed back to my cell.

I made a small opening in the roof of my cell and placed my two keys there to avoid them being found if my opponent tried to search for them.

I then waited till lights out after roll call and fell asleep. Overall, a productive but painful day.

Chapter 6 - Prison Break Part 2 (Kei)

Morning of day 2

At 8am, the prison lights snapped on and a guard’s voice echoed through the block. “Wake up! On your feet convicts!”

I rolled out of bed and immediately pressed my hand against the seam of the mattress. The two keys were still there. Only then did I let my eyes drift to the box in the corner of the cell. If my opponent hadn’t taken any yet, there should still be six keys left somewhere in the prison. The yard, the library, and the visitation rooms seemed like the most promising places to start.

At 8:30, the bars unlocked and we were funneled out towards the yard for roll call. We stood in straight lines, shoulder to shoulder, the perfect setup for observation. Each prisoner had a number stitched onto their jumpsuit for easy identification. I was 43.

I began going through them one by one in my head, reading posture, eye movement, nervous habits. Prisoner number 7 stood out immediately. He looked tense, fidgety, eyes darting too often. Most likely an actor uncomfortable with the role so probably not my opponent.

Most of the guards wore grey, but a handful wore blue and a handful wore black. Blue uniforms were watching me. Black uniforms were watching my opponent. I paid close attention to the ones in black, trying to see if any of them lingered on a specific prisoner for longer than usual, but nothing stood out. If I wanted useful information, I’d probably have to get my hands on one of their clipboards.

My name was shouted for roll call. I answered and followed the line toward breakfast.

After eating, I returned to the yard and searched more carefully this time. I looked between the fences, in the grass of the football pitch and between the plates in the weights area until I finally caught a glimpse of a metallic gleam buried under a wall, it was a key.

The infirmary was my next stop, but even after a thorough search, I couldn’t find anything. Either my opponent had already taken the key there, or I simply didn’t have access to the right areas without choosing the doctor’s assistant role.

I spent the rest of the morning in the library, pulling books off shelves and checking their spines and pages. Hours passed with nothing to show for it.

At 4pm, work duty began. Back in the laundry room, I paid more attention to the daily routines than before. Every afternoon, a cart filled with heavily soiled laundry was sent outside the prison. A guard inspected it carefully before it left. It wasn’t a viable escape route, but it was worth noting.

After work, I went to the cafeteria. They handed me a tray with something brown and steaming on it. I couldn’t tell what it was, and I didn’t particularly care. I ate quickly and headed back to the library. I went through the library row by row, opening each book and searching between the pages and a few minutes before I had to return to my cell, I found a book with a cut out portion inside containing a key.

I walked back to my cell and checked the mattress again to make sure the keys were still hidden, then waited for lights out.

When the block finally went dark, I took the four keys and slid them into the box. It opened with a soft click. Inside was a single sheet of paper.

I couldn’t read it in the dark, so I folded the paper and placed it in my pocket, it would have to wait until morning.

Chapter 7 - Prison Break Part 2 (Opponent)

Morning of day 2

The lights and wake-up call of the guard quickly alerted me and woke me up. I got out of bed and instinctively glanced at the ceiling tile that I hid my keys in. I resisted the urge to get up and check it to avoid being detected by my opponent.

I stayed lying in bed waiting till the cell opened at 8:30. I thought of my progress so far, I’ve found 2 keys, one in the visitation room and one in the guard break room. Out of the 6 remaining areas, the yard, housing units and infirmary seem to be the easiest spots to acquire keys.

I made up my mind, I’ll attempt to find the keys in these 3 areas and obtain 5. This should heavily restrict my opponent.

When the gate unlocked at 8:30, I quickly burst out of bed and made my way to the yard. I briefly searched the yard before roll call and then performed a more comprehensive search after but I couldn’t find anything even after hours of searching.

I came to the conclusion that my opponent likely found the yard key already so I moved on to the next location, the housing units. During break times, most people were out in the yard so the housing units were mostly empty.

I searched through the housing units, corridor through corridor and floor by floor in silence. After only a short while of searching, the key’s metallic shine finally made itself apparent to me and it was in a very obvious position, attached to the top of the central pole of the housing units hidden within a crack.

Climbing the pole would attract too much attention, there was only one option. I went to the top floor of the housing units and took off my shirt then slowly wrapped it around my hands. I then jumped straight into the middle, grabbed the key at the height of my jump then held on to the pole as I was falling.

I got a slight burn mark on my inner wrist but I didn’t sustain any serious damage, the whole scene lasted only a few seconds and made no noise so I didn’t attract any attention.

Soon after, it was 4pm. I patrolled the corridors of the rooms diligently while subtly making my way to the infirmary. I walked through the infirmary door and saw a doctor with a few prisoners acting as his helpers.

I started panting and acting exasperated, I then called out to the doctor “A prisoner’s seizing in the housing units!”

He quickly ran out with two of his helpers, leaving three of them behind. I quickly began rummaging through the cabinets and drawers, as I was searching, I called out to the three prisoners “If any of you tells the doctor that I did this, things won’t look good for you.”

This led to the three prisoners standing back watching me rummage through the infirmary. I quickly found the key taped to the bottom of a medicine bottle so I grabbed it and exited the infirmary quickly and went back to patrolling the halls.

During lunch, I searched through the cafeteria for a fifth key but I didn’t find anything. I quickly snuck out of the cafeteria after having my food and went back to my room. I didn’t wait till lights out to open the box because this was a race, I grabbed the other two keys from the roof then placed them all and opened the box to see a single sheet of paper.

I quickly stuffed it in my pocket and made my way to the bathroom. Once I got there, I locked myself in a stall then unfolded the paper.

I read the title slowly “Available escape methods”, my mind dissociated from the paper and began to think, this felt too easy.

This game can’t be as simple as finding keys then escaping using one of the methods. There has to be something more. Let’s review the data, I’m a murderer who’s been sent to 5 days in prison, that’s an inconsistency I’ve been struggling with for some

time. If I’m truly roleplaying then why would I escape with a 5 day sentence, it just isn’t worth it.

But it doesn’t make sense, everything in the game is telling me that the goal is escape, the game’s name is Prison Break, the box which we’ve been searching for keys for talks about escape methods, is that all a misdirection.

Are they trying to convince us that the goal is escape when it’s actually just doing nothing and serving my sentence?

But that wouldn’t work either, after all, they specified that this game is a race where the one that achieves the win condition first, wins. If the win condition was simply playing out our sentence then neither of us could achieve it first.

There don’t appear to be any methods to reduce my sentence so I can eliminate the possibility that the win condition is to play out my sentence.

But then there’s nothing left, everything tells us that the goal is either escaping or not escaping but both options have been eliminated. How can I escape but not escape at the same time?

The hidden victory involves the concept of escape, but it’s neither to escape or to not escape.

So maybe it’s both?

A state of superposition where the victor is the one who manages to escape without escaping.

But how can I escape without escaping? Maybe I have to break out of the prison then break back in?

But that also doesn’t make sense, if I break out then it will break the concept of not escaping, it would contradict being a murderer with a 5 day sentence.

So then there’s only one possibility left, I have to complete all the steps of escape but stop right before the actual escape then return to the prison without anyone suspecting anything.

This way, I never actually escaped so I didn’t break the rule of the 5 day sentence because I never risked having it increased but I also met the game’s condition of ‘Prison Break’. This has to be the solution.

Deduction complete, let me read the different methods of escape now.

Method 1: “The cafeteria has a food disposal mechanism where all excess food is dumped. Once the food passes through the pipes, it lands onto a shredder which shreds the food and transports it through sewer lines to the outside.

For a player to escape through the food disposal mechanism, they need to deactivate the prison’s electrical circuits located in a small room down the stairs of the guard break room.

Successfully deactivating the electricity deactivates the shredder, the prisoner can then jump down the food disposal mechanism to safely land in a water reservoir in the sewers.”

This method was clearly a trap, deactivating the electricity alone probably won’t meet the requirements of the escape superposition, I’d have to jump down. But if I jumped down, there would be no way to stop myself and get back up before leaving the prison parameters.

Method 2: “The laundry room takes out a cart full of ruined clothes from the prison every day. The clothes are placed into the cart by prisoners who have chosen the laundry job.

Before leaving, the cart is thoroughly searched by guard 12. To escape through this method, you must find a way to convince guard 12 to let you escape.”

This method seems to be complex as there’s no way to be certain that my method will lead to the guard’s cooperation. Will he be more convinced with money, blackmail or something else entirely?

Also, since I didn’t choose the laundry job, it will be difficult to sneak myself into the clothes pile. But as long as I manage to convince the guard and find some way into the clothes pile, this method looks to be a valid escape method to achieve the win condition.

Method 3: “The visitation rooms have a door that directly leads to the outside but there are two barriers to get through before leaving the prison through this method.

The first barrier is the glass that separates the visitors from the prisoners. This glass can be lowered by a button device that guard 4 has in his right pocket. Guard 4 is usually at the library and frequently gets engrossed in books and distracted.

The second barrier is the security guards at the exit of the visitor’s area. They don’t check the visitor’s ID upon exit but they will notice if a visitor that has never entered leaves.”

This method also looks to have complexities. The first barrier is easy, I’ll simply have to pickpocket the guard in the library, press the button and then return back to his right pocket. The timing will have to be perfect though, it will probably have to be during a cafeteria period because no one would be in the visiting rooms or library during that time.

The second barrier is more difficult because it contains an unknown. I’d assume if I request a visitor, I’d get my lawyer since he’s the only one mentioned in the storyline but how would I convince him to wear a cap and sunglasses and switch clothes with me to help me escape, that would be difficult.

I quickly refolded the paper realizing I had spent too much time in the stall, I placed it back in my pocket and snuck back into the cafeteria at the last minute.

I then headed to my cell and contemplated the different possibilities while walking. When I arrived in my cell, I decided to shut down my brain, lay on the bed and take a short nap before roll call to avoid an accidental prolonged slumber which I was prone to.

Chapter 8 - Prison Break Part 2 (Kei)

Morning of day 3

By now, I’ve become used to waking up to the guard’s screams and the blinding lights. I quickly rolled out of bed, washed my face and brushed my teeth waiting for the cell to open.

At 8:30am, the cell doors opened and I left for the bathrooms so I could read the paper I left in my pocket last night.

Once I entered a stall, I locked the door and pulled the paper out of my pocket. I thought about the contents yesterday and I’m fairly certain that it will contain different escape methods.

I also deduced that the goal is likely not simply escaping the prison, I theorized a form of entanglement between both escaping and not escaping by fully executing an escape plan but not escaping at the last moment.

I unfolded the paper and it contained what I expected, three methods of escape. The first method is through the cafeteria which I quickly eliminated as a trap.

The remaining two methods are escaping through the laundry room or the visitation rooms.

They both have two barriers to entry. For the laundry escape, you need to first be able to get yourself into the clothes pile and then you need to find some way to convince guard 12 to help you with escape.

Now, the situation was clear, the enforcer had predicted that an average mind would be able to deduce the law after 20-25 rooms as long as they had access to all the variables, Fang Yuan had now passed through 17 rooms and his mind was most certainly beyond the average, he was definitely close to deducing it.

On the other hand, even though Kei had passed through more rooms, he wasn’t able to deduce the law as he was missing a variable.

At this moment, Kei held two air containers in reserve and a nearly empty one in his mouth. He couldn’t afford to be cautious any longer, he used both his air containers on Discard abilities and waited the whole 5 minutes for his turn trying to figure out the law.

Once he didn’t figure anything out, he went through the unlocked door and dropped another air container from the trapdoor. The announcement forbidding Fang Yuan from taking this token was played again, the reason was because the room that Kei had entered was the room Fang Yuan was in.

Kei gazed at Fang Yuan with deep eyes, but all he saw was a mocking grin on Fang Yuan’s face, the air bubbles coming from his air container were too low for someone deducing, Kei could instantly tell, Fang Yuan had figured out the law.

They stared into each other’s eyes as the 5 minute timer slowly ticked down, once Kei’s turn ended, Fang Yuan would clear the labyrinth in one fell swoop.

Kei thought to himself “I really didn’t want to have to use this method, I guess I have to rely on you once again.”

Chapter 29 - The Asphyxiation Labyrinth

Kei then laughed maniacally, one minute had passed since he entered the room. He had been in complete silence but he suddenly laughed. It obviously couldn’t be heard, but his expression and his emission of air bubbles clearly indicated his laughter.

Fang Yuan’s heart fluttered but he quickly calmed himself “Kei has to be bluffing. There’s nothing he can do, if he has truly somehow figured out the law, then I will applaud him and admit the gap between our minds but that isn’t possible, there are too many variables.” Fang Yuan thought to himself.

But he could’ve never predicted what would have happened next.

A distinct beep sounded from both of their watches interrupting the deathly silence. Kei then threw one air container out into the corner of the room and then revealed his bag to Fang Yuan.

He showed him that it was completely empty, he had just used his last air container to start this chat.

He then typed “I am a deity beyond your understanding, did you seriously believe you could defeat me?”

After typing that, the air container in his mouth faltered, it no longer emitted air bubbles, he had run out.

Everyone couldn’t help getting shocked, Fang Yuan tried not to show it but traces of fear leaked out into his expression, the enforcers watching the match as well as Abami and Kamashiro were in complete suspense waiting to see what Kei would do next.

Only Satoshi was different, he understood the truth behind Kei’s move the moment he read the message. Kei was truly desperate, he had to rely on Satoshi for help.

Kei was now holding his breath pinning his hopes on Satoshi coming through to assist him, he knew Satoshi would understand his message but he couldn’t know

whether he would decide to help him and he wasn’t sure that he was even capable of helping him, but this was his last choice.

Kei’s message to Satoshi was simply the word “deity”. The name of the mall that they had their final competition in before parting quickly appeared in Satoshi’s mind as soon as he read that message, Deity Mall, The Kaiser Games, he remembered it all. This word was a signal to Satoshi, a signal requesting his help.

And Satoshi didn’t hesitate to help, he jumped forward onto the transmission device in his monitoring room, clicking the button that unmutes the voice transmission and said one word, eight.

After saying that, he was quickly restrained by Abami and Kamashiro. That was the true reason for their presence, they sat on either side of Satoshi to stop him if he decides to interfere in the game. Usually, they would’ve been on guard but Kei’s move also had another purpose, he framed himself in a way that made it appear that he had a final plan that he even fooled Abami and Kamashiro, their eyes were glued onto the screen waiting to see what Kei would do but he never acted, by the time they returned their attention to Satoshi, he had already sent out the word into the silent labyrinth, eight.

Upon hearing that, Kei who was nearly out of breath quickly walked towards the air container he had thrown away and placed into his mouth with his last bit of strength, he had oxygen again.

He now knew the number of reconfiguration events, it was eight. Three minutes were left on the clock, Fang Yuan was furious but violence was forbidden and Kei didn’t pay him any mind, he simply closed his eyes and began to deduce.

Even though he had passed through twenty rooms, the law wasn’t meant to be deduced in three minutes. Even though he had past incomplete deductions from before he knew the count, they were still not enough.

He deduced with all his might, two minutes remained then one minute then finally it clicked, he combined all of the information he had and deduced the law.

The law combined the three key factors in a brilliant way, it could all be summarized into one equation (Distance-to-Goal + Number of Reconfiguration Events) mod (Door count in current room). After having deduced the law, it turned out to be simple, he simply has to add his current Manhattan distance to the number of reconfiguration events then divide it by the door count in the room.

After that, a number would be yielded. If the number’s 0, then the door to the right of the one you walked in through would be the right door. If it’s 1 then it would be the door to the right of that which is the door opposite to the starting door. It would then keep going on and on, with each increasing number, it would shift to the next door to the right excluding the door you walked in through.

Kei quickly calculated the right door as he walked towards, there were 5 seconds left but Fang Yuan wasn’t going to lose that easily. He was already standing slightly closer to the right door, the moment he saw that Kei had deduced the formula, he walked towards the right door and stood in front of it.

Now, if Kei wants to tap the right door, he’ll have to move Fang Yuan forcefully which would be considered an act of violence. If he had more time, he would most certainly be able to get around him but he only had 5 seconds left.

If Kei didn’t manage to walk through the door, he would instantly lose. Not even because Fang Yuan would complete the labyrinth, it’s because he would be disqualified. The rules explicitly stated that the enforcer will come and collect the air containers spent during the one minute period.

Since Kei had picked up the air container he used for his Connection ability, when the enforcer sees that Kei can’t pay the price for the ability he had already used, it would be an instant disqualification, there had to be a full air container left behind in the room he used the ability for him to not be disqualified.

The reason he was able to pick up the air container is also due to this feature. As long as he completes the labyrinth in one go, the one minute period would never come and he’d never have to pay the price.

But now, there were 5 seconds left and Kei was so close yet so far. But Kei wouldn’t be defeated in any situation, he would always find a way out.

He resolutely took off the air container from his mouth, four seconds were left. He then threw it directly onto the door. If he threw it too high, Fang Yuan would move his hand to intercept it so he threw it between Fang Yuan’s legs at the bottom of the door.

Since Fang Yuan had weighted boots on, he wasn’t able to close his legs in time, so the air container passed through and hit the door. The moment the air container made contact, the door opened and a suction force pulled Kei and Fang Yuan into the next room with one second left on the timer.

Kei’s victory was now assured, knowing the formula, he could walk through all the doors with ease and make it to his goal room but he wasn’t given a chance to do that, Fang Yuan was disqualified.

He had broken the rules by entering another room when it wasn’t his turn leading to an instant defeat.

The game was over, The Asphyxiation Labyrinth ended in Fang Yuan’s defeat and Kei’s victory.

The trapdoor on top of their room had opened and Fang Yuan and Kei were instructed to leave.

Fang Yuan loses, he will get sent back to prison. But before that, he had to let out his anger, the normally composed and machiavellianist Fang Yuan spat off horrible words towards Kei.

He then said “You and your dumbass buddy Satoshi, always winning through luck and shitty game construction. Satoshi was previously saved by Abami and now you only won because those stupid pieces of shit, Abami and Kamashiro, weren’t able to control their game. Satoshi should’ve never been able to help you.”

Kei calmly laughed at his face before replying, he knew Satoshi was watching so now he decided to explain his true scheming capabilities to impress Satoshi.

Satoshi was already impressed with how close Kei got, even without hearing what Kei was about to say, he had already mentally accepted that Kei had surpassed him for now.

But what Kei was about to say would raise Satoshi’s evaluation even further “You idiot. The reason I was able to do this wasn’t as simple as Abami and Kamashiro not controlling their game, I had planned for this situation from the start.

At the start, when you were gloating about how you manipulated me into finding out Abami and Kamashiro’s true intentions, I had already deduced all of that. I already knew that you were the one that had the letter sent and that you planned for me to bring you back.

I led you into explaining that, I not only fooled you, but I also secretly manipulated Abami and Kamashiro.”

Abami, Kamashiro and Satoshi all instantly understood what Kei meant and what he was going to say next and so they didn’t restrict their praise. Abami and Kamashiro discussed fervently with each other feeling a sense of pride towards Kei’s improvements, they admitted that he had successfully influenced what they did.

Kei continued “I led you into explaining your manipulation then purposely showed an expression of despair on my face. I knew that Abami and Kamashiro were watching and that they would offer to reverse the advantage I gave you after seeing how defeated I looked after hearing you.

By doing this, I was able to confirm that they had microphone access. Also by hearing Abami’s voice coming from the left and Kamashiro’s voice coming from the right, I was able to deduce that someone was sitting in between them, that person would obviously be Satoshi.

The only reason I decided to go for my final plan was because I knew Satoshi was watching and was able to communicate with me if he wished. If I didn’t have this information, I never would’ve gone for the Discard and Connection strategy, I didn’t want to use this strategy if I didn’t have to but since I was unlucky and never landed on Pulse, I was forced to use it.

I then sent you the message leaving a hidden message that only Satoshi would understand. He knew that I was calling for help but I made it seem that I was confident in my victory and I had a final plan.

This further led Abami and Kamashiro into focusing on me and ignoring Satoshi allowing Satoshi the opportunity to send me the transmission.

Even the air container throw, it wasn’t something random. I had predicted a possible situation where I would be blocked from entering a door so I had already tested and seen that throwing an air container at a door still triggers the mechanism since it’s my air container.

If I hadn’t figured out the law in time, I would’ve used your position to find the right door by acting like I had figured out the law and seeing which door you blocked. However, this method is obviously much more risky so it’s great that I figured out the law in time.

Your loss wasn’t simply a game fault, I had engineered my strategy around using this game fault as my final strategy from the start, if it didn’t exist then I would’ve found another way to defeat you.

But don’t blame yourself for losing, you weren’t weak, I was simply too strong for you to handle.”

Fang Yuan was dumbfounded, he wasn’t able to mutter any words, he was convinced that as long as there was no intervention, he would definitely be able to beat Kei. But now it was revealed to him that he was thoroughly defeated through a pure difference in skill even when he held the advantage.

He had nothing to say, images of Abami flashed in his mind, images of his loss to him all those years ago, they surfaced now as he related them to the current Kei that had defeated him, he saw in Kei today what he had seen in Abami all those years ago.

He held his head down in defeat and didn’t resist as the enforcers cuffed him and walked him out of the building.

Abami and Kamashiro had long since removed their restraints on Satoshi, there was no reason to hold him back after Kei had already won. Satoshi then clicked the unmute button and spoke directly to Kei

“Well done, I underestimated you, I must admit that at this very moment, I am inferior to you but this truth will soon be changed.

The day we fight against each other is approaching, please understand that I still do not intend to befriend you again, your growth is merely a catalyst for my growth, it has not enticed me to become your friend even slightly.

We will battle against each other soon, and on that day, I will certainly defeat you. Also please be aware that I have also figured out Abami and Kamashiro’s true intentions as I contemplated it while you were in the labyrinth.

Don’t lose just yet, hold on a bit longer so I can be the one to defeat you.”

Author’s Afterwords

For all the people who have read Kei and Satoshi volumes 1 and 2, you might’ve been confused at the start of the first volume regarding the identity of the protagonist, is it Kei? Is it Satoshi? Or is it both?

But as you keep reading it, you realize that much of the story is narrated through Kei’s perspective and there is a simple reason for that, Kei’s character is a manifestation of myself in this novel.

Much of this novel is based on a true story, mine.

Obviously, lots of it is fictional, the three kingdoms and the games they partake in are mostly fictional.

However, there are two key things that are very real and anchor this story to reality. The first is the characters of Kei and Satoshi and the way they are represented.

Kei is a representation of me, as I live life and learn new things, I write the character of Kei learning those same things through a fictional setting.

This is also why the release of this novel was delayed quite significantly, I only wrote when I felt the inspiration to write. When I make new realizations regarding myself or my friend who I’ve been separated from (Satoshi), I write them down in the form of Kei and Satoshi.

The second thing that is real in this novel is The Kaiser Games.

The Kaiser Games is a game that took place in volume 1 of this novel and it directly describes the actual events that took place during this game in real life without deviation or dramatization.

That of course means that I am admitting that I embarrassingly lost to my rival that day even after all the advantages I had. I was successfully schemed against and my every action was predicted.

Of course, that was the reality for all of the games we had before we went our separate ways, I was always defeated in a variety of different ways.

I didn’t include these games in the novel simply because they would be too boring, The Kaiser Games was the game that I invested the most time into creating and it was the one that involved the most action which is why I included it in the novel.

I won’t go into our dynamic further as it can be clearly understood by reading the novel.

In regards to outsmarting, I hold a rather unconventional belief that real people can reach the level of high tier fictional characters and even go beyond that in outsmarting.

For example, when many people read Kaiser Games, they thought that Satoshi’s scheme was a low mid tier scheme, of course I’m putting myself down once again as Kei (me) had absolutely zero feats in The Kaiser Games besides getting completely manipulated but the point still stands.

My rival, one year ago, when he was much less intelligent compared to his current state was able to barely scrape into the mid tier of outsmarting as a real person. I believe we’re still quite far off the high tier stage as that stage requires quite a large qualitative jump but I certainly believe it's possible.

This novel serves as a progression manual for myself representing my growth in a fictional setting. Even now, as I reread this to refine it before publishing, I find myself disagreeing with many of the things I believed earlier in the novel.

The letter sent by Kei to Satoshi at the end of the first volume that was shown through Satoshi’s perspective in this one was a message I had wanted to send to the real life Satoshi who I hoped would read the novel. Now, reading it again and all of the events happening in the first volume as well as the start of this one, I cannot help but feel how incredibly corny it is.

This is because I wrote the first arc a few months ago right after volume one’s completion, then the second arc about two months ago then the final one just now before writing this afterword so many of the things I believed and my way of thinking back then have already significantly changed.

To be honest, even the end of this volume feels pretty corny but it works in a fictional setting so I’ll leave it as it is.

But besides serving as this benchmark for growth, this novel also serves as a minor form of outsmarting practice for myself. Since I’ve lost my rival, I haven’t had the chance to create more games and improve in live adversarial scenarios so I resorted to this novel.

When writing the arcs, I pose restrictions on myself, I quickly come up with the rules then I force myself to come up with all the strategies while writing as if I’m living through the moment.

If I come up with a strategy that is blocked by the rules, I found a way around them rather than simply changing the rules, this forces me to continuously think and become more creative.

This is also why I write arcs in quick bursts with long breaks in between. When I get inspiration, I quickly come up with a game in 15 minutes then I write the whole arc in one sitting, coming up with strategies as I write.

The only one that took me longer was The Asphyxiation Labyrinth, since it involved complex rules and a bit of math, I was forced to spend nearly 2 hours just to come up with the game, I think it’s my best work so far so I hope you enjoy it.

Of course all of this pales in comparison to the experience I get from actually playing these games against a live opponent and even that pales in comparison to actually getting real world experience where you are forced to outmaneuver your opponent to survive but those kinds of situations are really rare to come across.

There are indeed limitations to the human mind, we aren’t capable of formulating strategies at the speed of these characters most of the time since they are fictional. There are also many more factors for us to consider in the real world since we don’t know the opponent’s move making it even more difficult.

However there are many techniques to supplement these flaws and compensate for them.

There are also lots of practical benefits for most in chasing significant advancements in the path of outsmarting, even if you intend to live a normal life, having good attainment in this path will allow you to gain many benefits in the most mundane of environments.

And even if I’m wrong, even if surpassing a high tier level of intelligence is impossible, so what? That makes it even better, never reaching the goal will allow me to keep continuously striving, maintaining the excitement for as long as possible. Even if I die in the end, so what? It’s just death. We all die, at least when I die, I’ll die with no regrets knowing that I had always remained true to myself and was doing what I wanted to do.

Next, I’d like to discuss the writing of this volume and the release of the third volume. As I sat here rereading this volume to polish it, I noticed significant writing improvement between the first arc and the last arc so I hope you beared with it.

When I write the third volume, I’ll probably consider my writing in the last arc of this volume to be bad, that’s just how growth works.

I also found myself developing an interest in poetry recently so I attempted to write a few poems. I know they’re quite lackluster but I hope you can extract some meaning from them. I wanted to include one in the last arc as well but I couldn’t find an appropriate place to add one in since there wasn’t any talking in the arc.

The timing for the release of the third volume will be very variable and I can’t predict it. Since this story is heavily related to my real life, it takes time for me to manifest changes within myself before I compile them into the words that make up this novel.

I wanted to include a final arc in this volume where Satoshi shows his ability can still rival Kei’s but I didn’t feel inspired to write it.

Finally, I truly appreciate all those who have read and supported this novel. I enjoy seeing discussions around Kei and Satoshi and their intelligence and if you enjoy talking about them then I’m happy to see it.

Let me know what you think of the intelligence of the characters, have they finally made their way to the high tier of outsmarting? I will most definitely read your posts even if I do not reply or comment.
