# **📜 LIE TYPES REEXPLAINED (as requested earlier)**

**Common Sense Lie**
A lie that contradicts basic logical or physical reality (like giving an impossible time on a clock).

**Emotional Lie**
A lie someone says because their emotions are manipulated—anger, panic, fear, shame.

**Relational Lie**
A lie triggered because of social relationships — someone protects a friend, partner, or group.

He forces Li Annan to admit he is a shepherd card by using clever psychological traps and by referencing the announcement rules that prohibit revealing one’s role. Chen Ran then demonstrates how to break a Weird Speaker: by denying part of their misleading rule logic so the entire structure collapses. By using the contradictions in the announcement rules, Chen Ran forces the game to settle infinitely, winning automatically. He exposes Li Annan’s manipulation, reveals the Future Lies he used against him, and explains how he cornered him step by step. Chen Ran finally kills Li Annan with his own hands, giving the man a chance to taste despair before dying. Even at the end, Li Annan tries to trick him, but Chen Ran outplays him completely. Li Annan dies giving him the middle finger.

Chapter 30 Summary
The instance ends with all other players and the BOSS dead. Chen Ran vomits from mental exhaustion but also feels relieved seeing the families he avenged smiling at him. When he picks up the Liar Killer, it transforms into a white weapon and reveals its Skill, Eternal Kingdom. The skill can erase the effects of others’ Liar Killer Skills and prevent anyone from using theirs for one hour. Chen Ran realizes this skill is terrifying and must be hidden because it could make him the enemy of the entire Eighteenth Floor. He reviews his mistakes from the novice instance, learning not to be reckless, not to reveal intelligence too early, and not to talk too much before acting. He decides he will likely join the Liar Killer Alliance when he reaches the Eighteenth Floor. Hungry and tired, he finally steps into the blinding white light leading out of the novice instance, ready to enter hell.
