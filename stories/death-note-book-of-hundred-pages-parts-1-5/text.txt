Death Note: Book of Hundred Pages Part 1-5

This is a story where L gets trained by Fang Yuan to become the greatest detective in the world and most importantly **to not hold back against his opponents.**

**Whenever L becomes highly suspicious that Light is Kira, he sends an assassin after him.**

Light Yagami, possessing his unimaginable luck, obtains a book called The Book of Hundred Pages where he can return from death to an earlier point 100 times! Let's see how their battle unfolds.

**Part 1:**

L has realized that Kira is most likely in Japan based on Light's first murder (Kurou Otoharada), as well as that Kira is a student based on the timings of his kills.

However, rather than implementing the Lind L. Taylor trap, he first decides to work together with interpol to track down Kira via browsing metadata. Why does L think of doing this? Because all the criminals killed have data that is publicly accessible in Japan, so L thinks Kira might have used sources other than broadcasts in order to research the criminals he kills.

L together with the Japanese police force use telecom providers to identify anonymous devices that repeatedly access criminal profile pages, especially within the timing of their deaths. Using pressure from the other countries that are part of interpol, as well as his global authority, he successfully gains access and begins working with the Japanese police.

Based on the browsing timings and history, the suspects are limited further and further until Light Yagami becomes the sole suspect. Since Light never set the timings of the deaths, his browsing history almost perfectly matches which the criminals that died. Oftentimes, the criminals die minutes or even a single minute after Light accesses their criminal profiles.

Thus L sends an assassin after Light Yagami to solve the problem. If Light dies and the killings stop, problem solved. If Light dies and the killings continue, then whoops, too bad, it would just mean that Kira is more sophisticated and cautious than expected.

(L was trained by Fang Yuan by the way, and we all know how Fang Yuan operates)

...

Light Yagami woke up in the middle of the night, a chill running down his spine (intuition feat).

Opening his eyes, he noticed a shadowy figure standing in the doorway.

Instinct propelled Light to quickly push himself to the left, using his high bodily kinesthetic intelligence to dodge the first knife sent by the assassin. The second pierced his back. The third and fourth ended his life.

System: You have died. First page of the book of hundred pages has been consumed. Next life advantage: the NPA will no longer be able to access public browsing metadata.

**Part 2:**

Light is angry, but happy at the same time.

*What a wonderful system! 100 revives... even if L is a million times smarter than me, I will no-diff him with this system I stole from Lu Yang (some random Chinese SCD character).*

A few days later, Light Yagami sees the Lind L. Taylor broadcast. Ecstasy fills his mind as he kills "L".

For several days straight he continues to kill criminals joyfully, now that L is dead.

Meanwhile L is gradually using false broadcasts to triangulate Kira's area of operation. After Lind L. Tailor died, L remained cautious. He broadcast the clip to all areas of the world in order to prevent Light from finding out that it was a trap broadcasted only to Kanto. The broadcast caused a lot of social instability all over the world, but L didn't care. Finding Kira was the priority. Once he releases the documentary on how he caught Kira, the social instability would be quelled somewhat, problem solved.

Anyways, Light continues to kill criminals that are broadcast, overconfident after having killed the fake L, and L continues to broadcast to exclusive areas, limiting to particular regions, using broadcast delays to prevent Light from getting suspicious. First Kanto, then the Tokyo Metropolitan Area, then the region containing To-Oh university, and eventually it reaches a point where Light's family is broadcast to exclusively, from which the criminal dies. Light's other family members (sister, mum, dad) are slowly filtered out as suspects (because their conduct is not reminiscent of Kira's personality) and the evidence all begins to point at Light. L, having been trained by Fang Yuan, decides not to waste time on trying to gather definitive proof for an official court to judge Light. He simply can't be bothered. So he sends in the assassin.

...

Light stands by the window of his room, gazing out at the bright night sky, the depths of his eyes unfathomable. "This world... shall see a new order."

The bright stars flickered on and off within the night sky, so distant, but so close at the same time. Light's heart was welling up with a newfound feeling he had never experienced before. "I shall be the harbringer of this new era! With L dead, who else dares to stand in my way?"

Suddenly, Light felt a chill run down his spine (intuition feat).

"It... feels a little cold tonight."

As soon as he turned around, several knives pierced his body.

System: You have died again. Second page of the book of hundred pages has been consumed. Next life advantage: L can no longer broadcast criminals exclusively to your house and he must also reveal that he's actually alive after the Lind L Tailor trap. Bonus: the FBI agent following you will carry his real ID card rather than a fake one.

**Part 3:**

Light wakes up again and realizes he respawned AFTER the Lind L. Taylor trap already occurred. What a pity. "So that was a trap?.. I really thought L was that goofy and decided to publicly challenge me."

Light continues doing what he did in the manga, using a bus hijacking in order to get the name of the FBI agent following him. Since the system confirmed Raye would carry his real identity, Light is certain that it isn't fake.

However, Light overlooks something.

After Raye compromised his identity, he decided to follow the instructions he was trained to follow, which is reporting the incident to his superiors.

L now knows about the bus hijacking incident and finds it awfully suspicious, so he decides to keep an eye on the agents, especially Raye Penber. Raye is even wiretapped as an additional contingency measure.

Raye sticks to following Light's family, due to L's orders given because of the suspiciousness of the hijacking incident. Only Light's family is implicated when Raye dies.

When Raye's underling asks for the names and faces of the criminals, the FBI director finds it suspicious, given that Kira's kill condition is name and face, so he defers it to L, and L decides to give fake names and faces.

Thus, only Raye Penber dies, while the other agents are still alive.

Raye also does not suffer from memory loss this time, so he recognizes Light's voice and leaves a message on himself that Light Yagami is Kira. Since he was wiretapped, the audio recorder on him gives additional evidence when Light's voice appears.

Light is now the sole suspect for being Kira. L has enough evidence. He sends out the assassin.

...

On the train headed towards home, Light sighs, his gaze unfathomable, deep like the depths of the ocean, unyielding, unstoppable.

*With all the agents dead, who can stop me now? All I need to do now is to take care of L...*

He rested his head, pondering on whether he left behind any evidence that could be used against him.

Thinking back to the moment he said "Sayonara, Raye Penber," a smile creeps onto Light's face. *"That was some insane aurafarming haha,"* Light smiles to himself.

Looking at the seat across from him, Light saw a hooded man. The man had a familiar silhouette... he was reading a newspaper. His face hidden by a wide-brimmed hat. This shape...

"Stop 84F!" came a voice from the train's speaker.

The train came to a halt, and the doors opened.

This man... It can't be...

Suddenly Light felt a chill run down his spine.

The man across dropped his newspaper and in less than a fraction of a second, sent out several knives, ninja stars, and a nunchuck.

After confirming Light's death and swiftly retrieving his weapons, he left before the doors of the train could close.

System: You have died again. Third page of the book of hundred pages has been consumed. Next life advantage: Raye will hide what happened with the bus hijacking from his superiors. Bonus lucky drop: you will soon coincidentally encounter Raye's fiance who was told about the incident, by going to the police station.

Light is starting to get a little pissed at the assassin that keeps disposing of him.

Everything goes the way the manga did, once again.

When Light meets up with Naomi, he first manipulates her into giving the fake identity.

After Ryuk starts laughing and Light realizes it was a fake identity, he uses his impeccable social intelligence and acting skills to manipulate Naomi into giving her driver's license to him.

Naomi's thoughts: *Raye got killed after he showed his license to a random guy on the bus. Not to mention, something suspicious is going on here... why is Light so adamant in following me? Is he hitting on me?!.. Why did he even bring his father's jacket to the police station rather than the task force where his father resides? If he was part of the task force, he would know that he should bring the jacket there... is he an idiot? Or is he lying? Fortunately after I heard how Raye died, I have been carrying fake ID cards on me. Let's see what he does with it.*

Naomi's eyes darken. The terror is clear on her face.

Light stands smiling, waiting for her to go off and suicide or something.

But...

Naomi pauses for a few seconds to process what happened, then rapidly sends a kick to Light's sensitive spot. Light dodges the kick by jumping backwards, but Naomi is already running off.

"Dammit. She double bluffed me?! Ryuk! I'll do the trade!"

It's too late. With Naomi's back turned, Light cannot see her name. She refuses to take chances by stopping. Aizawa who was also headed towards the police station sees her running past and follows.

*Raye Penber dying... controlling actions before death... minor criminal... tests... distraction... bus hijacking... security footage... Naomi Misora and Light talking... Light admitting he is Kira... Fake ID... it all fits. Light is Kira. Time to send out the assassin.*

Light Yagami's thoughts:

*Dammit. Why did I reveal that I'm Kira...? I should have been more cautious.*

*I should... pretend I'm being framed. Yes, she's just a crazy woman who was disturbed by her fiance's death and suicided due to stress. Actually... even better, Kira intentionally controlled her actions before death to frame ME as Kira! Perfect! This is exactly what they think Kira has the ability to do. L definitely cannot convinct me without evidence... because it's illegal... it's... illegal...haha.. wait.*

Suddenly he felt a chill run down his spine.

"N-not again!"

Light quickly jumped to the left in order to avoid the knife.

Thud. Thud. THwshhh

System: You have died. Fourth page of the book of hundred pages has been consumed. Next life advantage: you will now know the full name of Raye Penber's fiance.

**Part 5:**

Light wakes up, realizes he respawned on the morning of the same day, and instantly writes down Naomi's name, as well as the causes of her death.

Light is no longer on the cameras with Naomi which keeps him safe from being suspected.

Everything continues like the manga did.

L surveils the 2 families that were being watched by Raye Penber.

While L is surveilling Light Yagami, he notices several suspicious points.

First of all, Light's words about what Kira would think attracted L's attention.

Next, there was a massive THWOCK sound when Light threw the potato chip bag in the bin. It was also quite weird how the potato chip bag did not even move from that spot, as if weighed down unnaturally, the entire night Light spent with it. Giving L's high sensory capability, this much is very easy to notice.

L thus hires Wedy to bypass Light's home security in silence and fish out the potato chip bag. After she finds the mini TV inside, with the names taped, L confirms that Light Yagami is indeed Kira.

...

"Aah... that must've gotten L" thought Light. "A television inside of a potato chip bag. Who could've thought? Hehe. Fortunately tomorrow is the day they dispose of the trash..."

As soon as he was about to doze off to sleep, he suddenly felt a chill running down his spine (intuition feat).

He instantly sits up to find his door slowly turning on its hinges.

Framed by the doorway is the silhouette of the person he saw on the train. It was the same assassin that killed him the previous four times.

"L really enjoys sending this basta-"

THWOCK!

System: You have died again. Fifth page of the book of hundred pages has been consumed. Next life advantage: L's sensory capability will be downgraded to the level of a normal human.
