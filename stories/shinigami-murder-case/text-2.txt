Deathnote || Shinigami Murder Case ||

I called Watari and he quickly entered the room,

"Contact the Japanese task force. Tell them I accept the case and that our culprit is most likely sociopathic."

"As you wish."

Watari left the room to carry out my order, however, I felt somewhat queasy as I pressed harder against my lip. This isn't like me. Not at all.

It might be due to Light's sudden death, but...I just can't shake this awful feeling.

I'm certain Light's demise isn't the cause of it because although I am saddened; evidently, it hasn't diminished my deductive capabilities in the slightest. If anything they've been enhanced a fair bit.

Still, it's been only half a day since the last murder so assuming Kira was satisfied with the attention from the news there's a chance he won't feel the urge to kill again.

Then again...with the death of Light Yagami and Misa Amane gaining this much traction on various platforms, and social media forums, his emotions are sure to run high.

People with a disposition like that naturally tend to push buttons harder the next time they're given an opportunity and seeing as the police haven't got any leads yet...

His impulsiveness guarantees a 95% chance of—dammit. How he kills isn't relevant at all, the fact is he can!

Don't tell me he's—

I instinctively looked to the door Watari had gone through as I hurried out of my seat, tumbling and grumbling along the way.

"Watari!" I shouted, scampering to my feet and bursting through the door.

"Yes?" He turned to me, confused at my approach. Without warning, I snatched the iphone from his hand and began speaking.

"Chief Iwauzumi?"

"Oh, L, it's a pleasure to finally me—"

"Let's skip the formalities," I interrupted, "Have 5 of your teams search within a 20 block radius of the Yagami household inside the Kanto region for people who've had trouble controlling their emotions, been on edge or have become noticeably bold as of late. Have them split up and look through yesterday's video footage with each person in charge of an hour out of the 4.8 assigned to a team. Do it now."

"A-alright, teams 1 - 5 do as he said!"

Soothing the bridge of my nose, I groaned. Where would he go at a time like this?

"Also send me logs from all hospitals, clinics, dental facilities or any place within the vicinity that has provided a large number of opiods to their costumers illegal or not. Ignore anyone below the age of 19 and above 45. I'll parse through the rest."

I waddled over to the bench and crouched on top of it, while holding the phone to my ear with a miffed expression. It's still not enough, I need to be more precise.

"The suspect is most likely on his way to his next victims right now which is why I'm certain he will do something drastic within the hour so please, dispatch crews to each of the most populated areas as of this moment. I recommend manning train stations and the places closest to the center of Tokyo."

"In the even you see a male acting suspicious or overly zealous, do not let him know you're with the police or else he will flee. Lastly, there's a 55% chance he's hacked the comms between now and 3 days ago so ensure the operators check for unauthorized access before distributing intel. That is all."

There was no reply from chief's Iwaizumi's end, so I waited a few seconds before speaking.

"Should I repeat—"

"Gwark-Ack! Urck!" A cacophonous, and disturbing sound like that violated my ears for half a minute with a device clattering to the ground as the noise began to die out.

What's happening? I scratched at my knees, "Chief Iwauzumi, are you there?"

No reply.

"Chief Iwauzumi?"

Still no reply. Damn, you Kira.

Unless he's a member of the task force...this means he doesn't have to be there in person to commit a crime at scale. Logically, that's impossible unless he has someone do the job for him or had set traps to ensure such a scenario. However, considering his untraceable killing methods, the latter is still a possibility.

If I were to discard those theories, the only thing I'm left with is delving into mysticism. But, if that were the case, this would likely be the work of a god of some sort or atleast someone possessing the ability to command death like one.

A Shinigami, perhaps?

Watari looked at me, perplexed, "Did something happen, L?"

"...Yes." I hung up the call and immediately broke the phone along with the chip while wincing as a chill ran up my crooked spine.

"The Japanese task force has been compromised."

"It was Kira, no...the Shinigami. He wiped them out." I looked Watari in the eyes. Despite him retaining his usual pokerface, I could see his pupils shake just slightly at the absurdity of it all, "Destroy any line of contact we have with Japan."

"I see." Watari composed himself, "Should I alert the other countries?"

"Yes. Tell them to cut off any communications as well. As of today the Japanese borders are a danger to everyone. I don't know how the Shinigami kills, however, I do know he won't hesitate."

I went over to the door and gripped the handle, "Best case scenario is casualties are confined to Japan until I come up with a solution."

"Worse case..." My grip tightened, "This might spell the end for humanity."

This is not an arbitrary claim. I believe with 100% certainty the individual commiting these crimes has no qualms with starting a war.

It's only a matter of time for the more well known politicians to be eliminated seeing as he's already dispatched the task force. An action that's sure to make news headlines outside the country if not contained.

Should something like that ever reach to international media, mass hysteria won't just be a possibility anymore. It would become our new livelihood.

"I can't let that happen." I muttered, walking out the door.
