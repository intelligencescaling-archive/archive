Deathnote || Shinigami Murder Case ||

-1 hour Later-

I stood by the whiteboard where varying images with dates and time stamps had been aligned.

Some way or another they all connected with the Shinigami Murder Case. Most of it were in regards to the deaths of the victims while the others had to do with the personality and a sparse background of each.

Seeing as the Shinigami has forced me to withdraw from Japan, my data pool is only limited to these 7 victims and a map of Tokyo.

It's annoying, but I'll have to make it work. Constructing an effective plan of action doesn't necessarily require full understanding of the culprit beyond motives or personality. Both of which can be derived from studying the victims.

"Alright, let's start again from the beginning." I muttered, pressing a thumb to my lip as I slowly backpedalled into a random seat. "Ushikawa Waka. Found dead inside a cardboard box in the Osaki district at April 15, 8:40 pm. Was an ordinary salary man prior to the death of his wife and child 3 years ago..."

"Kanzaki Ryodo. Suddenly died in the lab after arriving an hour late to work on the 19th in the Ebisu district. He has no family to speak of nor is he an avid party goer, however, to those who saw him the day before, he seemed tired. Hm..."

What connects the first and second killings?

Firstly, both are males who have virtually no familial connections. Next they both read as rather depressed individuals for obvious reasons.

Ushikawa lost his purpose his after the death of his wife and child, Kanzaki was orphaned and lacking in social connections outside of work...meaning they were easy targets to manipulate.

I suspect the Shinigami didn't simply want to "kill" these two men. It's likely he was testing a theory seeing as his killings were 4, 5, 6 and then 2 and 3 days apart.

Of course, this conclusion extends upon my earlier thesis wherein I stated these dates were irregular. It seems to me that the killer had convinced these two to willingly take opioids that would result in anaphylaxis. But why?

Hmm... Did he want to see what level of dosage would result in a person's death?

I suppose.

He refrained from searching the internet to avoid leaving a digital footprint, but if we factor in his presumed hacking skills he'd be able to easily wipe that away.

Also, this doesn't answer how he managed to kill Kanzaki in the middle of a lab full of surveillance cameras. The only explanations are mysticism—which I'm inclined to believe and setting up pre-emptive measures like poking him with a needle that was coated in pethidine strong enough to induce anaphylaxis. Seeing as it takes 20 minutes to act after ingestion and 2 - 4 hours to wear off, it is still a viable option in my eyes.

Anyhow, the final victim, Ryoutaro Beppo, must've been somewhere in the Osaki area prior to his kidnapping.

I stared at the various photo's belonging to him.

Even though he was reported missing within 24 hours of the first crime, he was never found until 20 days later right back in Osaki.

His clothes were tattered, likely from attempting and failing to escape from his captor and being forced into anaphylactic shock. However, the question now becomes how did he manage to get so close to his house just moments before death?

1. The killer dumped him there.

2. He was somewhere nearby when he was killed.

3. He was brought there by the killer and then killed.

The first and third options have a 45% and 35% probability of happening respectively. The second option only has a paltry 20% because it's unlikely for the child to have escaped by himself.

Nothing suggests he received any help either, which means the killer might've dumped him there after deciding he wasn't worth the trouble of keeping alive. It could also be his way of saying "I'm still here." and "What are you gonna do about it?" to the police

If I were to go with the third option, however, I'd be doubling back to my earlier thesis of the killer discarding whatever shred of empathy he had left along with Ryoutaro's body. I'd also add that the killer might've seen it as him doing the boy a favor as he was the only child of a broken home.

His father was an irresponsible drunk and his mother was forced into illicit activities to provide for them. The question of why the killer didn't simply eliminate the family instead...possibly ties into how he kills.

Both parents have illegally migrated from Russia and Malaysia before taking up residence under fake names.

Not much is provided about them, however, I can conclude that they most likely lacked anything tying them to their original identities and nationality.

My head tilted as I analyzed Ryoutaro's physical traits.

High cheek bones, meaty broad face, flat chin, black hair, tan skin.

Hmm...His parents might've been of...taiwanese, central and south american or European descent.

It doesn't narrow down the options nearly enough to profile them, and I doubt he'd bother to hack the databases of all those countries just to kill a single couple which would explain why the killer didn't bother going after the victim's parents in the first place.

But then again, with mysticism involved, this shouldn't have limited the Shinigami's options. In fact, evidence like this points to him only being able to kill those whose information he's aware of.

I drew my left toes over the arch of my right foot. It's slowly begining to make sense.

Even though he has control over who dies (supposedly) he can't simply say "Kill the task force" and it happens. No. He has to specify the members.

This adds motive to why he hacked the database. It wasn't just a way to gain traction or limit my intervention, it was his way of tying the noose.

Each of these victims have a single, overarching commonality that I'd previously dismissed as insignificant because, normally, that would be the case. However, this is clearly abnormal.

"He needs a name and face to kill." I concluded. "That's the only logical explanation."

"Are you suggesting supernatural ability?" Watering asked, placing a buffet of sweets on the portable table he'd set up next to me.

Due to being so focused, I hadn't noticed when he entered. This is the first time it's happened, however.

"Yes. I believe that is the case." My reply was as smooth as the scoop of cake I'd placed in my mouth.

"It seems rather simplistic or even stupid to consider, however, when I cross-referenced the deaths for each individual and arrived at Beppo, I realized he was the only outlier among these people."

I pointed the spoon at his picture.

"He was forced to die, unlike everyone else, because he was at the wrong place at the wrong time. Not because the killer initially wanted to."

I took a few more swift scoops around the strawberry to sate my starving brain. Within moments, the fruit was the only thing left.

"With that said, I believe I've come to a greater understanding of who our Shinigami killer is." Picking up the red harvest, I dropped if into my mouth while staring up at the ceiling.

"This killer of ours, despite his seemingly chaotic nature, is rather meticulous. He's got what I'd refer to as a mix of narcissism, sociopathy and saviour complex. This incompatibility is why his killings feel so random yet so scripted all the same. Everyone he's killed were either begging for it or had been at the center of his envy some way or another."

"Prior to their deaths, he took the time to contact Misa and confine Ryoutaro for multiple days which suggests he felt he was doing the two a favor by eliminating what bothered them. Misa was likely given a choice to either die with Light or to live. We both know the outcome of that."

"For Ryoutaro though, it was his family. His parents are still alive so the Shinigami failed him in that regard but because he didn't want to admit it he killed Ryoutaro under the justification that it was for his own good after thinking over it when he was about to send him home. Then, as a means to cope with it, he shifted the blame to the police. He was probably thinking something along the lines of, 'It's your fault! If it wasn't for you not doing your job I wouldn't have had to kill him!' or something like that."

I reached for the vanilla ice-cream cup and scratched my thigh.

"Anyhow, I've come up with something that might allow me to isolate this mystery killer within a few days. But due to his unique skillset I have to be in the field." I said, "I want you to contact Near and relay the the information I've just given you. I'll need eyes monitoring any other cases in my absence. Additionally, tell Aiber and Erina I will be needing their assistance."

Taking a scoop, I swallowed the colored cream.

"Finally, have the world powers be on standby. If I die, please have them nuke Japan. It's the only way to minimize casualties." I didn't sound particular bothered at the prospect of dying, but Watari knew better than anyone my fear of death.

Going up against someone who can bend that to their will with only a name and face...is quite frightening, honestly.

"Noted. I'll get right to it."
