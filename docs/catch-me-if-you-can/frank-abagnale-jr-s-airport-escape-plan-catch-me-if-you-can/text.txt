An analysis of Frank Abagnale jr's Airport Escape plan from 'Catch Me If You Can'

Hello! I had just recently watched Catch Me If You Can (goated movie btw, would recommend) and I wanted to do an analysis on one of Frank Abagnale's feats that personally stood out to me as being quite clever. Essentially, this feat was a plan created by Frank to sneak past the FBI who were monitoring an airport 24/7 in an attempt to catch him (who had also been evading the FBI for years as well).

# What was the context beforehand that led to Frank making this plan?

Basically, when Frank was impersonating a doctor he ends up falling in love with a woman named Brenda and after a while, they plan to get married. Carl (the main FBI agent guy played by Tom Hanks) manages to track down Frank at his engagement party. Still, Frank finds out about this and escapes through a bedroom window where he tells Brenda to meet him at Miami International Airport two days later beforehand.

At the airport, Frank spots Brenda but realizes she has given him up when he also identifies a large group of FBI agents disguised in plain clothing who have set up 24/7 surveillance across the entire airport ready to arrest him to which he then drives away. Now keep in mind Frank has no other way to get out of Miami other than through that airport which is why the plan is being made in the first place.

# What was the plan and how did he do it?

Frank takes on the identity of a pilot using the uniform he already acquired beforehand (which he's already used way earlier in the movie), then he makes a phone call in a booth to a local college and convinces them that he's a pilot looking for stewardesses to recruit.

This lets him stage a recruiting drive at the college where he says he'll be selecting 8 stewardesses for a "flight crew program" to which he then afterward interviews each individual person from an extremely large crowd full of women to make his selections for the ideal candidates for him. Under his pilot identity, he arrives at the airport with the 8 stewardesses and camouflages himself in the middle of the entourage allowing him to avoid all of the FBI Agents & surveillance inside the airport as well as also keep their attention focused only on the stewardesses with no one even suspecting him the entire time.

For extra measure to ensure that he doesn't get caught, he pays $100 to a guy to dress up in the same pilot uniform (since Frank's already aware that the FBI knows he's disguised himself as a pilot before which he used to his advantage) and stay in a car with his face hidden as a way to deceive the whole FBI at the airport by diverting all their attention to that specific person which also gives him more time to get on a flight to Madrid to escape and to just simply taunt the FBI too.

Now you might be thinking why would Frank dress up as a pilot again if the FBI's already aware that he's disguised himself as a pilot before? Well, that's simply because while it does have its risk of being identified due to the FBI's prior knowledge, it's also the easiest and fastest way for him to gather people to blend into a crowd (which counteracts that risk) to even be at an airport in the first place as well as board the same flight as him. After all, regardless of the disguise or not, the FBI already knows what he looks like so he may as well as use an identity that can give him influence to execute a plan.
