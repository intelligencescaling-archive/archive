So, after Igor Samarin ("You won't catch me" or shortly YWCM) learned that Meglin was alive and had been released from a mental hospital to help the police with difficult cases, he first decided to remind Yesenia of his existence and then decided to begin his plan to restore Meglin's memories. To do this, he first sent a package to Meglin's house containing his old clothes and a folding knife. Over the phone, he told Meglin that he was his "old friend" and that he would help him remember everything.

Alphabet killer case:

From episodes 3 to 4, Yesenia leads the case of a killer who breaks into women's homes, cuts out their hearts after killing them, and then draws strange pictures on the wall in the room (pic 1 example). Yesenia realized the killer was targeting women whose first and last names began with the same letter, as well as street names. With Meglin's help, they realized the drawings depicted the killer himself as a child (+ his childhood trauma)and found other clues, leading them to postman Sergei Vereshchagin (pic 2). Once they were completely convinced that Vereshchagin was the killer, they set out to apprehend him. At this point, Samarin decided to act. He called Vereshchagin and warned him that the police were coming for him. Samarin, knowing the killer's psychology, convinced Vereshchagin to follow his instructions so he could "finish the painting." In exchange, Vereshchagin would have to do whatever Samarin asked. Having escaped just in time, Vereshchagin followed the coordinates Samarin had sent him. There, he found a buried phone. Vereshchagin then went to a café, which quickly alerted the police, who again set out to arrest him. However, Samarin warned Vereshchagin again, and he escaped just in time. While Yesenia was interviewing witnesses, Meglin escaped, stealing her car. Meglin knew Vereshchagin would kill according to the list he had, so he decided to ambush the killer before the police arrived. Hiding in the apartment of the woman Vereshchagin was planning to visit, Meglin engaged him in combat, but nearly died. However, the killer was forced to flee before the other residents arrived. After this, Meglin was returned to Bergich's psychiatric hospital, and Yesenia went home. While at home with her daughter, Yesenia remembered a strange detail. Earlier in the episode, Meglin, having escaped from his base, came to Yesenia's house, but she never told him her address. She called Bergich and asked Meglin who had given him her address. Meglin replied that it was a "friend." Sensing something was wrong, Yesenia began locking all the doors in the house, but it was too late. Vereshchagin was already there and, armed with a hammer, told Yesenia that he had been ordered to kill her (implying YWCM). Just as he was about to kill her, Yesenia was saved by her husband, Yevgeny Osmyslovsky (pic 3), who had returned home after forgetting his phone and shot Vereshchagin. This moment in the series actually plays a more important role than it might initially seem. Samarin used Vereshchagin to make Yesenia realize that YWCM was much closer than she thought (which he later told her over the phone). After all, YWCM knew and warned Vereshchagin twice that the police were coming for him, and he also knew Yesenia's address. Why he needed this will become clear later, and I'll discuss it in the next post. For now, let's move on.

Dollmaker killer case:

After solving the Alphabet Killer case, Yesenia and Meglin traveled to a small town where young girls aged 13 to 16 had been disappearing. Their suspicion of a serial killer in the town became clear when they found one of the victims on the road. Her mouth and eyes were sewn shut, and cuts on her arms and legs were stitched up, revealing pink ribbons, creating the appearance of a doll. While Meglin and Yesenia, along with the local police, were searching for clues, an interesting detail emerged. Meglin had previously worked on the Dollmaker case. The man turned out to be a certain Ilya Admayev (pic 4), whom Meglin had also killed when he tried to kidnap his next victim. This has created a situation in which the local police chief believes Meglin made a mistake in the past and killed the wrong person, and that the real Dollmaker is still alive. He particularly suspects a man named Ivan Ivashov, who was a suspect back then. However, Meglin is certain that the current Dollmaker is merely an imitator of the old one, as the styles they used to kill their victims are vastly different. I'll cut to the chase: the killer turned out to be Vyacheslav Malyavin (pic 5). He was the one Meglin saved from the first Dollmaker in the past and was the only surviving witness in the case. In reality, things were different. Back then, young Malyavin was being bullied, and one day in the forest, two bullies started beating him up again. At that moment, The Dollmaker appeared and killed both bullies. Instead of killing Malyavin, The Dollmaker offered to help him, saying that he, like him, could change the world by cutting out all the dirt from it. However, Meglin, who had recently arrived, killed him, believing he wanted to kill another person. Later, Malyavin, inspired by his "savior," decides to continue his work. After some time, Samarin secretly contacts him, offering Malyavin to follow his instructions to take revenge on Rodion Meglin for the murder of the first Dollmaker (Samarin chose Malyavin for the plan precisely because it would force Meglin to gradually remember his past and, in particular, how he had conducted investigations in the past). Following his plan, Malyavin deliberately allowed one of the victims to escape, knowing that she would not survive anyway, but in this way he would be able to lure Meglin and Yesenia to the city. Samarin then directed Malyavin to kidnap a new victim, the daughter of the local police chief. This was to frame Ivan Ivashov, a suspect from his past. Malyavin, finding a moment when Ivashov would be asleep after drinking alcohol (he had deliberately left a bottle for him near the door, knowing he would drink it), planted the body of a murdered girl in his guardhouse (Ivashov works at a cemetery). Yesenia and Meglin arrived at Ivashov's before the police. When the police arrived with the chief, Meglin, realizing that the chief might even kill Ivashov out of sheer emotion, and certain that Ivashov had been framed, lets him go, giving him a chance to escape (according to Samarin's plan, this led to the local police ceasing to be allies for Meglin and Yesenia in their investigation). At the same time, Malyavin kidnapped his own daughter (not exactly to create an alibi. He's just a crazy guy with delusional ideas. But it really did help divert suspicion from himself and further antagonize the police with Meglin). Now alone, Meglin and Yesenia question Ivashov and learn that a strange man has been arriving at the cemetery in a car every day (Dollmaker's victims are also buried there). Realizing this was their chance, they followed the car the next day, but unfortunately, at that moment, the police arrived and detained Ivashov. Trying to find the true killer, Yesenia searches the archives, finding the first victim of the new Dollmaker, as well as discovering a connection between the victims (all the girls had recently entered into relationships at the time of their deaths). They found the first victim's mother and interrogated her. Meglin also learned about her childhood friend, with whom she had played together, and after comparing him with the maniac's profile, he realized that this boy was the grown-up Dollmaker. However, the woman doesn't remember his name and only has his photo (this photo caused Meglin to further activate his memories). After the interrogation, they continued on, but at that moment, on Samarin's orders, Malyavin kidnapped Yesenia to complete his revenge. Meglin, meanwhile, realizes that Malyavin is the Dollmaker, so he hurries and arrives at Malyavin's lair just as he's about to sew Yesenia's eyes shut. Malyavin begins to explain to Meglin why he's doing this and that he's pursuing his goal of purifying the world. Meglin, meanwhile, experiences flashbacks of how he killed the first Dollmaker and, at one point, picking up a knife, began stabbing Malyavin over and over again until Yesenia knocked him out. After this, Meglin regained some of his memories of his detective career and that he used to kill, and a lot of them (not the most pleasant memories), and because of this, he was sent back to Bergich's mental hospital.

After this, Samarin decides to move on to the next part of his plan, now planning to frame Meglin for the murder and make Yesenia and the police think Meglin is "YWCM," but I'll write about that in the next post (I think there will be two more posts about Samarin). And for now, bye everyone. See you later.
