New interesting SCD character (imo). "You won't catch me" case

Recently I finished the first season of quite interesting TV show "Method". The story follows the adventures of Russia's most effective detective, Rodion Meglin, who was granted the broadest powers and freedom to conduct his investigations using his unusual method to catch serial killers (and most of the time killing them like Dexter), and Yesenia Steklova, whom he took on as an intern (the story itself is told from Yeseniya's perspective, who is being interrogated by the high-ranking police officers). Meglin and Yesenia spend most of their time solving various serial murder cases across the country so that Yesenia can gain experience and eventually replace Meglin (an interesting fact is that many episodes are based on real events and the characters have their own prototypes), but periodically the series mentions an unknown criminal who always remains in the shadows and commits murders by the hands of others and manipulating other killers. He calls himself "You won't catch me" YWCM (Yea kinda long name, but that's what we have😅). He constantly challenges Meglin by attacking his allies or close people and putting psychological pressure on him. So now I want to analyze this guy actions in first season cause I find them really impressive (especially his scheaming and manipulation). Firstly I will give some information about main characters, then retell episodes in which he appeared or they're some information needed for understanding his further plans and finally I will try my best to analyze his entire first season scheme. Warning spoilers ahead! So let's go.

Khm so let's start with the main detective of the story. Major Rodion Meglin. From his backstory, we learn that when he was about 10, a criminal broke into his house, killed Meglin's father, and wounded his mother, forcing Rodion to finish off his mother with his own hands. This left a deep trauma on the boy's psyche and he promised that one day he will take revenge on this man. After 10 years he found the murder of his parents and killed him along with everyone who was there. He later said to police that he intuitively sensed that this man was the killer of his parents and other people there were no better than him, so they also deserved to die. Of course, no matter how bad those people were,  police couldn't let that slide, so Meglin was sentenced to death. While sitting in prison and patiently waiting for his death, Meglin suddenly was visited by high-ranking man from Ministry of Internal Affairs. He introduced himself as Grigoriev. He said that he was interested in Meglin and his strange abilities that helps him find criminals. Grigoriev released Meglin from prison and brought him to the military base where Meglin trained for some time to develop his skills in shooting and hand to hand combat. Later Grigoriev took him to Psychiatric Hospital No. 13 where he met Vadim Bergich the head physician. Bergich concluded that after traumatizing experience in his past Meglin started to feel pathologies in other people (in simple terms, to sense serial killers). Bergich taught him how to use this ability. Let me explain a little how exactly his ability works. I can compare this with how Will Graham identify and understand the emotions and motivation of killers. Meglin even can see the past of the killer and exact events that made the killer who is he now. Also I need to mention that the more Meglin uses his unique method for investigations, the more his psychological state worsens (important detail)

Then a little about Yesenia. She is the daughter of the senior justice adviser, Colonel Andrei Steklov. When she was little, her mother Vera Steklova was shot by an unknown assailant. After that, she wanted to become an investigator in order to try to find out who killed her mother. After graduating from university and events of the first episode (I will write about them later) she became interested in working for Meglin hoping that this would help her uncover the mystery of her mother's death. Her father was against it and he had reasons for that, which we learn at the end of the season. In the last episode, she finally learns the truth. Her mother, Vera Steklova, formerly Olga Beristova, was a patient of Bergich's. She suffered from severe psychosis following a traumatic experience and violence, making her a danger to society. However, during a period of stable health, she gave birth to a daughter, Yesenia. Then, Yesenia's father decided to get his wife out of the clinic, and with Bergich's help, he managed to do so by swapping her names with another patient, thus becoming Vera Steklova. However, his wife's illness never left her, and she began killing again. She herself already wanted to die and begged Andrey to do so, but he couldn't bring himself to do so, so Meglin did it himself. This was the main reason why Yesenia's father didn't want her to work for Meglin: he didn't want to traumatize her with the truth about her mother. Well, now let's move on to the other part with a retelling of the episodes.

The first episode: "Holiday killer" (pic 1).

So in the first episode Yeseniya Steklova with her classmates throw a graduation party. During that time Yeseniya's friend Anya went out for a smoke. Near her was a man in his 40s holding a small box in his hand. He introduced himself as a wealthy man and that recently he bought a gift (a necklace with living diamonds) for his girlfriend, but she broke up with him and left for another young man. At first he wanted to throw it into trash can, but he felt sorry to throw away such a beautiful necklace. So he offered Anya to take the necklace. She hesitated, but in the end accepted it. She went to the restroom and put on the necklace admiring its beauty. However something strange started to happen. The necklace made mechanical sounds and began to tighten around her neck, choking her and diamonds began to dig into her flesh. Bleeding and choking she left the restroom with difficulty reaching the hall where all her classmates were. At the same time it turn out that the police monitored everything that was happening in the building through cameras. After they saw what was happening, they immediately gave the command to begin storming the building, and catch the killer. By that time, Anya had already died. Then Meglin appeared for the first time, managing to identify the killer before anyone else, in just 20 seconds, by eliciting his emotions. Meglin quickly realized an important detail. This man could never have committed this crime on his own. Meglin later explained to Yesena that Vladimir (the real name of the Holiday Killer) had very expensive glasses and clothes despite not living a rich life. Furthermore, he was socially awkward and unlikely to have been able to convince girls to accept his jewelry on his own. This meant that the words he used were pre-written by someone else, and Vladimir had simply memorized them. When Meglin directly asks him for the name of his accomplice, we hear his name for the first time. "You Won't Catch Me". Yesenia was impressed by Meglin's quick identification of the criminal and, despite her father's protests, was able to secure an internship with him.

Episode 7: The Subbotnik killer case (pic 2). His nickname "Subbotnik" in Russian means voluntary collective free work to complete some socially useful work task.

I won't write much about this because the only important information is about the main killer of this episode. Yesenia and Meglin took on the case of a vigilante killer who kills corrupt officials, prostitutes, etc and and conducted surveys in a closed social media group popular among young people and neo-Nazis where new targets for assassination were selected. The killer turn out to be 18 years old Fyodor Yashin son of the police officer who survived after being assaulted by Subbotnik killer, but was left completely paralyzed and could only move his fingers slightly (Fyodor used this to trick the police when they were drawing up a portrait of Subbotnik killer). Before Meglin guessed that it was him Fyodor uploaded a video to his group called "Manifesto" where he showed all the videos of his executions, calling on people to join him and help him cleanse the world. He kidnapped Yesenia, wanting to kill her, but Meglin prevented him by making him believe that people did not admire him but considered him a psychopath, and he committed suicide.

Episode 11: School No. 23 massacre.

In this episode, high school student Nikita Pavlov (pic 3) first kills his parents at home and then comes to school hiding a gun in a guitar case and commits mass murder in his classroom, killing six people. During his arrest, Nikita demanded a meeting with Meglin, stating that he had a message for him. It turned out that Nikita had subscribed to Subbotnik's group and had read his Manifesto. Someone in the comments under the post and convinced him to commit this crime. When Meglin arrived, Nikita gave him a message: "Meglin, you think you know everything, but you know nothing. You think you control everything, but I'm the only one in control here. And instead of looking within, you've made the wrong moves again and again. You've betrayed yourself. I want to show you how pathetic and helpless the world you created is. Tomorrow I'll strike even closer, and you won't be able to do anything. You won't catch me." While Meglin was thinking about what YWCM meant, Meglin's former boss Grigoriev and his bodyguard were killed by unknown man in military uniform (pic 4). While Meglin was inspecting the crime scene, YWCM called Yesenia. He mocked Meglin a bit and said his next attack would be in three hours. Meglin assumed the criminal was targeting his superiors, so he stayed with Yesenia's father and sent her to the Bergic Psychiatric Hospital to search the archives for former soldiers who had previously been treated there. At this time, Nikita was undergoing examination at the clinic itself, and while Yesenia was checking the archives, Rubel (that was the name of this former soldier) after three hours had passed, he himself went out to Meglin and Yesenia’s father. He relayed a new message from the YWCM: "You think you're doing well? You're surviving on the deaths of your brothers. You're a lion working for jackals. It's not your fault. You were taught to live by their rules. It's their fault. They made you their pet, but I'll free you and execute them. Welcome to my world. A world without rules." At that moment, Meglin realized that YWCM wasn't hunting his bosses, but those who taught him what he knows now, which meant Bergic should be the next target. At the clinic itself, Nikita was taken to the restroom where he retrieved a gun hidden in a zip-lock bag from the toilet tank (it had been left there a month earlier, when Rubel was still at the clinic). After killing the guard, Nikita tried to reach Bergich's office. Esenia arrived just in time, stopping him and nearly killing him, but Bergich managed to stop her.

Episode 14: Nikolay "Pinochet" (pic 5)

In this episode, an unknown criminal kills people by first tying them up and then burning them alive. Meglin realized that while his victims were burning, the criminal himself was standing elsewhere, watching them burn in agony. Finding the spot from which the criminal had likely been observing, Meglin discovered burnt matches on the ground. This immediately made him suspect his old acquaintance, Nikolai, nicknamed "Pinochet." Pinochet himself suffered from severe psychosis and pyromania. As children, he and his younger brother were kidnapped by a notorious serial killer, who hid them in his country house, where he tortured and abused them. Finding the moment, when the criminal fell asleep drunk, Pinochet managed to escape and set the house on fire. He thought his younger brother was following him, but it turned out he had returned to the house to get a toy that had been left there and didn't manage to get put. After this, Pinochet experienced hallucinations in which he heard his brother screaming and calling his name. To drown out the voice, Nikolai began finding abandoned houses and setting them on fire. At one point, he accidentally burned an old man alive in a house and was nearly killed by an enraged mob, but Meglin saved him in time. He ended up in Bergic Hospital, where he was admitted regularly once a month, undergoing all the procedures again and again. Although Meglin suspected Pinochet, he was still undergoing treatment at Bergic and it was unclear how he could have committed these crimes. While researching the victims' information, Meglin discovered that one of the murdered men was Viktor Korsikov a former patient of Bergich's, whom Meglin himself had captured and brought to the clinic. Moreover, the manner in which he was hung from a tree and later burned was similar to the way he had nearly killed his wife. Meglin realized that the second victim was also connected to past cases he had once handled. As Meglin put it, "He started killing our own people" (implying that he, like them, was mentally ill and little different from the murdered men). Arriving at the scene of the first murder, Yesenya found a carved smiley face (red john reference 🌚) on the tree where the victim was hanged. She found a flash drive inside a tree. It was locked, and Yesenia took it to "Deaf" (Deaf was Meglin's partner who assisted him in gathering information, hacking, and tracking criminals. It was stated in the show that he joined Meglin after his wife and daughter were murdered, and Meglin found and dealt with the killer). "Deaf" hacked the flash drive, and sent it to Meglin. It contained a video of the first victim being burned, along with a message from YWCM. In his message, he expressed disappointment with Meglin's actions, which force people like Viktor Korsikov to stop being who they are. He stated that, unlike Meglin, he doesn't lie to them about pretending to be normal and gives them the opportunity to be themselves. In his eyes, Meglin is not a hero, but a murderer who is tolerated as long as he is useful. Finally, he set a condition for Meglin: he would have to kill one of his own (mentally ill criminals he sent for treatment to Bergicu), no matter who. Otherwise, he would continue to kill Meglin's people. After this, Yesenia reported that "Deaf" had found a program on a flash drive that could track the computer on which it was viewed. Meglin realized Deaf was in danger, and they quickly went to him, but it was too late; firefighters were already working at Deaf's house, and he was already dead. Meglin and Yesenia went to the home of Pinochet, who had recently been released from a mental hospital. When Meglin asked him directly why he was killing these people, Pinochet replied that Meglin had lied to him, claiming that Bergich's treatment would help him silence his dead brother's voice and that the murders had indeed been helpful. Furthermore, Pinochet believed he was helping Meglin rid the world of evil, even though these men hadn't committed any crimes since leaving the hospital. However, Meglin explained to Pinochet that the YWCM had deceived him because "Deaf" wasn't a murderer or a criminal and YWCM was only using Pinochet to kill someone close to Meglin. Realizing he had killed an innocent man, Pinochet asked Meglin to burn him to atone for his sins. Meglin, realizing Pinochet couldn't bear such guilt, agreed, but not before asking Pinochet for information about the YWCM. Pinochet explained that the YWCM called him constantly, chatting and sharing information about a new victim and where he could find them. Pinochet also said that YWCM was a mental hospital employee because he opened the door to Pinochet's room at night, and when Pinochet returned in the morning, the same man closed it. After killing Pinochet, YWCM called Meglin again, declaring that he would keep his promise and stop killing. Meanwhile, using information obtained from Pinochet, they quickly checked the staff at Bergich's clinic. The one who was on duty at the same time the murders were taking place was Leonid Skuteev (pic 6). The police, along with Meglin and Yesenia, immediately arrived at his house, but when they entered, Skuteev was already dead, sitting in a chair in front of the TV. Next to the television was a video camera on which Skuteyev recorded a message to Meglin: "Well, that's it. I could never admit I was wrong. And I won't now. I spent so many years serving monsters considered human, and so many years tormenting poor souls who weren't allowed to live a normal life. They were called psychos, madmen, moral monsters. Although they are a thousand times sincere, smarter, and more humane than their tormentors. I couldn't give them freedom. I'm guilty, but only before them. You won't catch me." With these last words, he took poison and died. After that, Meglin and Yesenia officially closed the YWCM case.

Episodes 15 and 16. Alexei Anufriev (pic 7).

I will also try to tell these episodes as quickly as possible. In this episode, Meglin, on Yesenia's advice, goes to Bergic's clinic for treatment, because despite the deterioration of Meglin's psychological state after all the investigations, he had a chance to recover (mainly due to the fact that Yesenia was his intern). However, at this time Meglin learned some terrible news. Meglin learned that one of the former criminals he caught eight years ago, named Alexei Anufriev (he killed 9 people in less than a week), had escaped while being transferred from prison to a psychiatric clinic. In the previous episode, we saw that Yesenia's father, realizing she was getting too close to solving her mother's death, told his superiors that Meglin had to be stopped because he had become uncontrollable. They agreed, but they knew that openly trying to eliminate Meglin would outrage many people, so they came up with a more cunning solution: allowing that psychopath, Anufriev, to escape so he could go after Meglin. Anufriev, having met with Meglin, told him that he was going to kill another 40 people, and despite the fact that Meglin and Yesenia managed to catch him during their first meeting, he escaped again and continued to commit mass murders. Anufriev also deliberately committed mass murder in the orphanage where Meglin grew up, killing over 15 children. However, after this, Anufriev himself surrendered to the police since his goal of killing 40 people was completed and before this he promised Meglin that he would return in a year and kill 365 people this time. Despite the fact that Anufriev had already been detained by the police, Meglin was not going to let him simply get away with it. Meglin attacks the convoy transporting Anufriev and kidnaps him, then takes him to the cemetery and buries him alive. Yesenia tried to persuade Meglin not to do it, as she knew if he killed Anufriev, Meglin's psyche could completely break down. And that's exactly what happened. After Anufriev's murder, Meglin practically became disabled, unable to react to anything and unable to even think clearly. He was admitted to Bergic Hospital, and Yesenia made a difficult decision. Coming to his room, she stabbed him in the heart to stop him from living like that. After that, she returned to Meglin's house when she received a call on her phone and the already familiar mechanical voice mockingly asked how she felt after being left completely alone. With his final "You won't catch me," he hung up, leaving Yesenia to prepare for a new investigation, as she is now Meglin's full-fledged successor to his post.

Phew, okay, now let's move on to analyzing the actions of this mysterious YWCM. So I think we should start with the fact that this YWCM had access to the criminal archive, as well as to information about Bergic's patients (this is clear from the fact that he knew about Viktor Korsikov, Nikolai Pinochet, "Rubel", etc). This means that he could well have known about the case of Olga Berestova (Vera Steklova). Despite the fact that Bergich and Colonel Andrei Steklov got rid of information about her and switched her case with another patient, the YWCM itself, who even knew about Meglin's former boss, Grigoriev, figured out that the mysteriously deceased Vera Steklova was the same Olga Beristova and, most importantly, she had a daughter, Yesenia. The main goal of the YWCM itself was the moral and psychological destruction of Rodion Meglin, and Yesenia herself could greatly contribute to this. So, first, he needed to get Yesenia to intern with Meglin, and he already had a plan for that. He found Vladimir (the Holiday Killer), who had recently been abandoned by his wife and killed her in revenge by crafting poison-laced earrings for her. YWCM quickly gained his trust by manipulating him and convincing him to continue killing "mercantile women." As I stated in the analysis of the first episode, to get the girls to accept Vladimir's jewelry, YWCM bought him expensive clothes and prepared a script that Vladimir used to gain the girls' trust. By killing two more girls in this manner, YWCM managed to draw Meglin's attention to the case, and then, when the police were already tracking Vladimir, YWCM gave him another target in the form of Yesenia's close friend Anya. Thus, YWCM was able to arrange for Yesenia to get an internship with Meglin. After the first part of the plan was completed and Yesenia and Meglin began working as a pair, YWCM now moved on to the next part: increasing the psychological pressure on Meglin. To do this, he contacted Rubel, a former soldier and Bergic's patient, who was still undergoing treatment at the time. During my analysis of Episode 11, I wrote down the full message that YWCM transmitted to Meglin through Rubel. This message, addressed to Meglin, also reveals how YWCM manipulated Rubel, convincing him that he wasn't to blame for his incarceration in a psychiatric hospital, and that the real culprits were those who used him. Having completely subjugated Rubel, YWCM used him to hide a pistol in a toilet cistern for future use. YWCM himself later chose a new pawn to exploit. Finding high school student Nikita Pavlov in the comments under Subbotnik's manifesto, YWCM convinced him to first kill his parents and then carry out a massacre at his school. He also told him to convey a message to Rodion Meglin that he would strike much closer in a day. After this, YWCM used Rubel to kill Meglin's former boss, Grigoriev, leaving a new message stating that the next attack would occur in three hours. Mistakenly assuming that Yesenia's father would be the next target, Meglin stays with him, sending Yesenia to Bergich's clinic, where Nikita is undergoing an examination. However, as we already know, Bergich was the real target, and Nikita, while in the restroom, pulled out a hidden pistol left by Rubel and began shooting, attempting to kill Bergich, but Yesenia stopped him. The fact that Bergich's assassination failed was not a big problem, since it was only the beginning of the plan, and further actions would lead to Meglin's psyche being on the edge. This time, YWCM decided to use Meglin's close friend, Pinochet. Through Leonid Skuteev (Just like with Rubel, it can be said that his message to Meglin also showed how YWCM managed to manipulate him), he brainwashed Pinochet, convincing him that only murder would help him silence the cries of his dead brother. Knowing Pinochet's gratitude to Meglin, he also convinced him that his actions could help Meglin cleanse the world of evil. Thus, he convinced Pinochet to kill his first victim, Viktor Korsikov, and hide a flash drive with a message for Meglin in a tree, urging Meglin to kill one of his own. YWCM knew that to bypass the lock on the flash drive, he would take it to his partner, "Deaf," and that the program inside the flash drive would help him track "Deaf" and send Pinochet after him, to whom he lied that "Deaf" was the same criminal as the previous victims. He knew that when the truth comes out, Pinochet himself will want Meglin to kill him, which will force him to unwittingly play by his terms and will put his psyche on edge. This was also a signal for the fake YWCM (Leonid Skuteev) to record a video of himself committing suicide in order to make Meglin and Yesenia believe that he was dead. This fake suicide plan was crucial to the scheme's continued success. As I've already said, Yesenia's becoming Meglin's intern wasn't a coincidence; it was carefully planned by the YWCM. All of his actions were aimed at more than just exerting psychological pressure on Meglin. Since Yesenia's work with Meglin could have allowed her to learn the cause of her mother's death, her father, Andrei Steklov, being a caring father, didn't want to traumatize his daughter with the truth about her mother. Therefore, as the YWCM predicted, Colonel Steklov resorted to desperate measures to hide the truth from his daughter. Yesenia's father appealed to his superiors, who decided to release this psychopath, Anufriev, from prison. The fake death of the YWCM was one of the reasons for this decision, because if the case was closed, Meglin, who had at that time decided to undergo treatment in a psychiatric hospital, would no longer be needed, meaning he could be disposed of. This was the final blow to Meglin's psyche, and it doesn't even matter whether he guessed then that Yesenia would kill Meglin or not, because in any case, Rodion Meglin was finished.
