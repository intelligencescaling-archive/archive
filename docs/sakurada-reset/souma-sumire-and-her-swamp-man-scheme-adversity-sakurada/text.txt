Souma Sumire  and her swamp man  scheme adversity [Sakurada Reset]

Souma Sumire is an ability user from *Sakurada Reset*. She planned and orchestrated basically everything that happened throughout the two years of activity, both before and after her death in the story, with good methodology and traceable logic.

Her ability is future sight, or, in other words, future-sight\*\* with a small f and an asterisk on the sight. Her ability activates as long as she talks or has a conversation with anyone, allowing her to see the future. However, it comes with several caveats, as we see with another similar ability user.

In intelligence scaling systems with such methodology and proven ability (funny enough, both systems were created by Dax and his friends), despite popular belief, hax/powers don't nerf a character's outsmarting feats when those feats are good and above average.

Take this analogy:

*A different character having Six Eyes and Limitless doesn't mean they would be like Gojo Satoru, who showcased the heights of Six Eyes and Limitless, compared to Yujo or Go/jo.*

Taking general outsmarting situations into account makes the comparison even more skewed in her favour.

**Explaining Souma Sumire's feats and schemes would take a lot of time, so I've opted to explain the adversity instead, which will help people digest the degree of Souma Sumire's outsmarting and scheming.**

Souma Sumire's ability causes a weird dissociation in her perception of the world. For Souma, the boundaries between the world and herself are vastly different from what anyone else could experience, as she can see the present, future, and various possible outcomes just from talking.

Souma has high emotional management, allowing her to never be swayed by everything she sees, including her own end , the deaths of others, and the future state of Sakurada. This also causes Souma to become isolated from the world, herself, and others in every sense.and souma cannot make true bonds as it would interfere with her swamp man scheme

Souma Sumire not only continually dodges Kei's PHM worm vigilance but also leads Kei towards the correct path for the Swamp Man scheme without him knowing, all while recalling every past detail. Every move was calculated to ensure that the Swamp Man scheme would work in both the present and the future.

Souma has to put everything in place at the right time, from the MacGuffins, photographs, her own end, resets, and various people like Murase and Sakagami, while accounting for their personalities and character traits. This also includes every timeline manipulation, event, and other necessary detail.

Souma has to go against the Bureau, the Nameless Witch system, and the Witch, who can also see the future, while constantly hiding that she possesses an ability similar to the Witch's.

Due to how the timeline works, Souma also has to plan and orchestrate everything in such a way that she won't be betrayed by her own future self.

The whole Swamp Man scheme consists of careful decision-making, methodology (onscreen btw), and long-term indirect manipulation. Souma has to account for how various abilities would interact with one another to ensure that her Swamp Man scheme succeeds. This also requires her to understand the different ability users and their abilities better than they understand themselves.

tldr: swamp man slams memory loss plan by leagues in both normal and methodology  and outcome and magnitude

This is only what I've gathered and briefly analyzed from V1 to V4, btw. Who knows how much the last volumes buff her scheming and outsmarting?
