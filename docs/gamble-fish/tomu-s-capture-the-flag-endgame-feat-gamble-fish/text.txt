Talking about an impressive feat from Tomu from Gamble Fish that I feel is worth mentioning, cause why not?

If Gamble Fish didn't have female characters constantly stripping for the most blatant fan service that makes Tomodachi Game look G Rated, I think Gamble Fish would genuinely be a top contender Manga recommendation. (I think the fan service is literally the only thing holding back an Anime Adaptation.)

That being said, I Do genuinely think a lot of the feats are genuinely impressive. At Least on Yuichi's level (who is underrated as Hell).

I think a feat worth discussing is the end of the Capture the Flag Game.

Spoilers Ahead-

The feat itself isn't that complicated and it's also literally the End of the Game. It's not like this is it- This is just the End Game.

Tomu has the flag and just needs to plant it. However, the planting station is at the top of a very tall mountain and is in the literal arctic and up against a character who literally has the magical ability to look someone in the eye and hypnotize then into doing whatever she wants.

The End Game involves Tomu sending up a greedy character up to the mountain to take her own.

Naturally, he fails. Even though he tried closing his eyes, the flash of her hypnosis is apparently bright enough to simply bypass the closing of his eyes and he's effected anyway.

This alone allows Tomu to realize that- indeed- closing your eyes of attempting to simply blindfold yourself is not going to work against her power.

As the greedy guy falls down the mountain, this gives the pulley system Tomu is on enough force to catapult him right to the top of the mountain, where he hides behind a rock to keep from being effected by the hypnosis girl.

He has a brief chat with her about how he's probably screwed, and then does something grotesque off screen. He then declares: 'Let's finish this,' and emerges from behind the rock, blind folded, but with blood all over his facial area.

It appears that Tomu literally cut his own eyes out in order to render himself immune to her effects.

As he approaches her though, she fires her gun at his blindfold, causing it to fall off and reveal that Tomu did Not in fact cut out his own eyes. Tomu looks caught off guard.

The girl figures this was just a trap to throw her off guard while Tomu goes and plants the flag. She flashes her eye at him, causing Tomu to fall under her Spell.

Despite this, Tomu falls onto her (and yes, I think that is supposed to be erotic because literally everything in this Manga outside of the Mind Games is), and plants the flag, declaring victory.

It turns out that while Tomu did not in fact cut out his own eyes, he Did cut out his own ear lobes. Thus, while he was still technically from vulnerable to falling under the girl's Spell, the Spell was effectively useless against him, as he wasn't actually able to Hear any orders.

I dunno. I think it's Cool.
