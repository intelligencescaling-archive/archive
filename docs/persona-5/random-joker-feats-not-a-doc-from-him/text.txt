Feats of Joker from Persona 5 Royal, assuming the correct decisions of the player
Crystallized and General Intelligence:
Joker has knowledge that transcends the encyclopedic, possessing erudite knowledge according to his social stats. He has knowledge of ancient Rome, the four-color theorem, linguistics, Edo period history, the brain's function responsible for the illusion of a figure (Cognition), the placebo effect, the historical fact that Katsushika moved over a hundred times, the halo effect, and can solve crosswords with ease. Additionally, his application of knowledge is evident when he can apply battle strategies learned in Hifumi's social link. He applies his craftsmanship knowledge when creating smoke bombs, gomo-h for infiltrating palaces, martial arts, and negotiating skills, being an exceptional negotiator when needed to recruit people, as well as theft techniques. He is comparable to Akechi, who has the highest scores in Japanese national exams. Moreover, if the player makes the right decisions, Joker always has perfect grades, which is even more impressive considering how difficult the Japanese educational system is. There are also no signs of excessive effort or cognitive fatigue during this. He also has knowledge of the Metaverse and people, with allies like Futaba Sakura, who can "read" through people's information as if they were books through the browser, and the social link with Igor, who is semi-omniscient. Finally, he has the personas of Satanael, who could defeat a god like Yaldabaoth, which was semi-omniscient, meaning he is dimensionally more intelligent. Additionally, in the Royal version, we see that he learns Olympic gymnastics at a professional level with Kasumi/Sumire Yoshizawa's social link. Also, Mona/Morgana declared that Joker can do complicated math exercises, which is evident when he studies at Leblanc. Remember that Morgana is a skeptical and highly intelligent entity.
Joker also has access to knowledge that surpasses common education and exceeds even the most prestigious elite schools. For example, Joker has access to the Velvet Room, where he has Igor as a confidant, a semi-omniscient being. Additionally, Joker has access to Futaba from the Metaverse, who can access almost anyone's data as if it were a book.
In Royal, Joker was able to fuse Justine and Caroline to create Lavenza without Igor’s help, an act only Igor can perform. This is interpreted as a cosmological feat, as only Igor can carry out such fusions.

Joker had to employ tactics to resist attacks from a being manipulating reality itself. • Emotional Manipulation of Yaldabaoth: Joker emotionally frustrated Yaldabaoth when he summoned Satanael, indicating that even an entity of that level was psychologically affected by him.
Calling Card Feat: A key aspect of Joker is his ability to deeply influence the emotions of others, evident in how he handles the Calling Cards.
Effect of Calling Cards: • They not only reveal the true form of the Palace but force the owner to experience an extreme psychological crisis. • This results in the highest level of security in the Palace, making infiltration much harder, but also emotionally weakening the target. • Simply receiving a Calling Card makes the bearer emotionally collapse, showing Joker’s psychological manipulation power.
Supreme Example: • The Calling Card to Maruki (Persona 5 Royal) is the most impactful, as his Palace represents his ideal world and deepest emotions. Joker not only forces him to recognize his true nature but confronts him with a reality he wanted to ignore.
Conclusion: Joker not only understands human emotions but uses them strategically to manipulate even the most powerful individuals in his world. From criminals like Kaneshiro to a god like Yaldabaoth, Joker proves that emotional intelligence isn’t just about understanding emotions, but using them as a weapon.
Feats of Mass and Indirect Manipulation: Joker's Chain Reaction
One of Joker's most underrated feats is his ability to manipulate events without direct intervention, creating a domino effect of psychological influences that culminate in his victory.
Mishima Case and the Creation of the Phantom Site • Mishima creates the Phantom Site under Joker's indirect influence, driven by his obsession with the Phantom Thieves. • Joker never directly commands him to do this, but his mere presence and actions generate Mishima's devotion. • Later, Mishima censors the Phantom Site when the reputation of the Phantom Thieves is attacked after Okumura's murder, maintaining control over the narrative.
Manipulation of Shido and Akechi • Joker indirectly manipulates Shido by becoming the focal point of his plan. • Shido wants to eliminate the Phantom Thieves, so he manipulates Akechi, his own son, to do it. • Akechi, with his inferiority complex, wants to capture Joker to gain his father's approval, falling into Joker's trap. • Akechi murders Okumura, destroying the Phantom Thieves' reputation and causing an internal crisis. • Mishima, obsessed with the Phantom Thieves, protects Joker's image by censoring the Phantom Site. • Subsequently, Joker massively manipulates the Japanese media with: Shido's influence as prime minister, Akechi's motivations narrated as a celebrity, Mishima censoring one of the Phantom Thieves' fan sources, and then using social engineering with Futaba's help across all of Tokyo to reveal to the public that Joker was never dead.
Conclusion: Joker creates a psychological collapse in Shido, Akechi, Mishima, and the entire Japanese society without directly intervening in each instance.
Other Achievements: Massively manipulating Shujin Academy with the calling card sent to Professor Kamoshida. When Kamoshida confesses his crimes, he does so in front of around 100 to 300 students simultaneously. Furthermore, Joker had already spread rumors when he sent that calling card.
Emotional Engagement and Realization:
Arsène Feat: Joker's awakening in Kamoshida's Palace occurred when, finding himself in a life-or-death situation, seeing Ryuji about to be killed by Kamoshida's Shadows and unable to do anything to help, his mind replays the events that led him to that point. He recalls how his life was unjustly ruined after being accused of a crime he didn’t commit by Shido, and how, while defending a woman who was about to be raped by Shido, his parents neglected him by sending him to Tokyo to live with Sojiro Sakura in the attic of Leblanc. He remembers his isolation at Shujin Academy and the discrimination he suffered due to the rumors spread by Mishima. All this accumulation of injustices, combined with the imminent threat of death in the Metaverse, triggers an emotional explosion that sparks his rebellion.
His frustration and desire to change his fate awaken Arsène, his Persona, but the process is not easy. Summoning him causes extreme pain, reflected in massive blood loss when he tears off his mask, symbolizing the price of his transformation. This is the only moment in the entire game where Joker completely loses his composure, showing the magnitude of the sacrifice it takes to accept his true self. Unlike other characters who awaken their Persona with less resistance, Joker fully surrenders to his transformation, demonstrating absolute resolve.
After this scene, Joker is no longer simply "Ren Amamiya" or a student with a criminal record. • On a symbolic level, he stops being a victim and becomes a born leader. • His personality changes from someone merely surviving to someone who shapes his destiny with his own hands. • From here, his decision-making, strategic coldness, and leadership ability become much more pronounced.
Cognitively: • Joker challenges and surpasses the limits of cognition, physiology, and even human metaphysics/ontology. • This is not an intellectual feat but a metaphysical one that transcends ontology between the Metaverse and the human world. • Joker endured extreme stress, which could be post-traumatic, due to his ongoing trauma with Shido. However, this does not significantly alter his composure throughout the game.
Emotional Management: Joker perfectly manages his emotions when he is about to be killed by Akechi in Sae Nijima's Palace, which was a planned and anticipated action by the Phantom Thieves to uncover the real traitor. When Akechi decides to deceive Joker into falsely rescuing him, he points the gun to his head and then fires it. The key here is that his only reaction was dilated pupils, but beyond that, there are no screams, no signs of feeling any other emotions. Joker maintains his composure and, most importantly, remains calm until he reappears in the Velvet Room, later deceiving Akechi into having the Japanese media announce the death or suicide of Joker (the leader of the Phantom Thieves), while Akechi left the weapon in Joker's hands, also using gloves to eliminate evidence.
He never lost his composure when summoning his Supreme Persona, Satanael, nor did he lose his composure when reality was about to be altered by Maruki in Royal.
Joker also managed his emotions decently when his body was fading due to Yaldabaoth, as he was never manipulated by this semi-omniscient being, unlike the collective unconscious.
