After finishing both series Johan Liebert vs Patrick is a very close Cat and mouse

I recently finished monster and after watching it Johan vs Jane is a very close battle. Here is my breakdown. Also will be using a EOS Jane.

Johan is a master at manipulation, both in situational and psychological. Johan manipulated people with feats like getting everyone to kill each other at Kinderheim, getting Richard to kill himself, manipulating killers to kill for him and then kill themselves, being able to remove dementia from an old women for a time. In terms of situational manipulation he was able lead Tenma to get close to him and brought him down a whole path throughout the series, Johan at age 15 started an underworld bank, killed his foster parents and got away multiple times, killed all the doctors thst fired Tenma and made it so all the evidence pointed at Tenma, he got close to Hansa Schuwald and created a situation where someone posed as his son then had that man killed once the son appeared, the whole situation where he revealed he is a monster to Hansa with the whole thing on fire, manipulating a town into killing each other, etc. He is one of the best manipulators in fiction and is really good at covering his tracks. What is so hard about his case is all the moving parts in it, leads going dead, Johan changing his plans halfway through, Johan not necessarily getting his hands dirty, etc. But it is not an impossible case to solve, we see Richard figure out that Johan is the killer without clues intentionally left by Johan. Lunge also figured it out after getting tunnel visioned by Johan and actually figured out where Franz Bonaparta was before Johan and figured out Johans plan before it even started without the clues left for Tenma.

Now for Patrick Jane, Jane is a very good manipulator in his own right. He manipulates people psychologically by getting them usually tell hin things without saying it, weather thats getting them to tell hin where someone is by a map, getting close to a killer who killed their husband to solve a case, ego baiting into getting Panzer to kill himself, when he gets captured by killers he gets them to either turn themselves in or stall them from killing him long enough to get saved, getring his captures to kill each other (unintentionally), manipute people into thinking he can see the afterlife, etc. He does this by reading people, he is able to read micro expressions thst tell him everything like if you are lying, if you are telling the truth, if you are worried about something, if you have a secret and what that secret is, etc. He can read practically everything on an individual and can know their whole story. The other part is his situational manipulation, he catches around 140 individual killers in different ways and got each one to confess, he got a gambler to give a coin only the killer could have, he manipulated a Red John associated into thinking he got into a fight with Lisbon and was on his own, was able to get someone who was faking autism to confess, etc. He has other feats like being to hypnotize someone into remembering memories where she was blackout drunk and knocked out, convincing everyone he is insane including Red John to get close to him to try and recruit him. In terms of memory and attention to detail he remember all +1400 people he had shook hands with in 10 years and deduced where they were and if they could be Red John getting into down to 7 names. He was abke to see through Red Johns play in the cabin and deduced that Red John was sheriff McAllister even tho he should have been dead, he solved a case immediately by watching an individual based on how they were acting, figured out a car crash was a murder based on the way the guy looked in the car, figured out what type of man and job someone had based on a hand an no body, etc. His main weakness is he doesn't know forensics and sometimes cant figure out the bigger picture till later like with the Blake Association. His traps also dont work all the time like vs Panzer where he had to get him to trash talk Red John to get himself killed because he couldn't prove it. He also went mentally insane for a bit after his wife's murder.

To make the battle fair we will replace Lunge with Jane placing him in Johans time period where there were no cameras. With Jane on it we will just use him and the local police, so he wont have Lisbon with him which allows him to work more freely. We also will give him other cases like they did with Lunge but only cases that involve Johan meaning it's just the monster case. Looking at the monster case as a whole it is very similar to the Red John case but easier in some ways and harder in others. Johan doesn't have the same level of organizational that Red John had, Johan has killers and insane people on his side with the occasional corrupt cop. He also does have a neo nazi organization that adores him but he doesn't care for them at all. Red John had FBI agents, CBI agents, the CBI director, cops, forensic scientist, killers, lawyers, judges etc. He had a better organization backing him. However Johan is a bigger threat than Red John and is the better manipulator and doesn't really see the cat and mouse as a game like Red John. Johan is also constantly on the move and covering up his tracks, having people wonder if he exists. I feel Patrick Jane would struggle to find evidence but would eventually find out that Johan does in fact exists through interviewing cases and killers through the monster. I don't think he will get tunnel visioned the same way as Jane doesn't really care about evidence and goes off reading people. I think it will eventually come to where Jane and Johan have a conversation, people will say that Johan would get Jane to kill himself, I doubt that heavily. While he did go insane and Johan will be attacking that but Jane would see through it and might get angry but would never kill himself, but with Johan trying to manipulate him Jane would know for sure that Johan is the mastermind behind everything and is manipulating the events. It then comes down to Johan's assassins vs Janes traps, I personally dont see any trap of Jane's working on Johan, he would see through every single on of his plans. I also think Jane could get himself killed by the assassins if he isnt careful but could potentially manipulate the non Roberto assassins to not kill him. If he has the FBI helping him with security this could also help man power wise to keep him going. I do think eventually Johan would either escape or turn himself in / perfect suicide himself to an L but Jane i think could prevent everyone from going crazy by convincing them that its not one of them doing it. I do think he could figure out where Franz Bonaparta is before Johan and maybe prepare a trap there. I think in that town Johan could fall for a trap as he will he too focused on the perfect suicide with Franz Bonaparta. Jane could also pull a Red John and kill Johan if he thinks hes too great of a monster.

In the end I do think Jane would figure out that Johan is a monster, Johan would struggle to get him out of the picture but evade Jane for the most part. Jane is going to struggle gathering evidence against Johan and is going to need to get him in an trap closer to the end of the series to have a chance in it working. Johan will have assassins going after Jane but Jane will find ways at avoiding or talking his way out of it. Its a very close battle with both sides struggling. But I do have Jane eventually winning in the long term because of the perfect suicide plan which I both think he can prevent and catch Johan. I know monster fans are going to be annoyed and just say Johan manipulates him to kill himself but I doubt that. Jane is too secure now to want to kill himself and is too aware of manipulation. Johan is a great manipulator but he made a career on manipulating insane people and off screen feats. I doubt Johan can do what he did to Richard to Patrick Jane.

Winner: Patrick Jane extreme diff
