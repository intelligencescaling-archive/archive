PART 1
Johan knew of the German situation (East Germany being controlled by communism and West being controlled by democracy) because he had multiple elderly parents, plus all the experiences in the red rose mansion Anna had. So, he came up with a plan to go to east Germany where he and Anna could escape.
“I just killed all of our former, almost adoptive parents, so we need to escape. Not just from the law but from Franz Bonaparta and the whole experiment. The Eastern German and Czech border is where we will go. Since it’s a border and they are part of the Warsaw Pact, there is likely a border patrol due to Cold War tensions. Since me and Anna would be in the middle of nowhere, they will likely not take us back to Czechoslovakia, and instead take us back to east Germany out of curiosity.”
Johan has knowledge on the ongoing Cold War and the relationship between Czechoslovakia and East Germany. He knows about how the Grenztruppen are always on border watch as it was a very popular job at the time plus there was rising tensions in the Cold War, so it should be extra tight, minimizing their chances of dying.
Because of these increasing tensions, he knew that the soviets would also be eager to get as much help as possible in preparation for a Great War. With Johan being physically almost perfect, and having high survivability, they’d take interest in him and take the twins to East Germany instead of back to Czechoslovakia.
“Since we are children, they will take us to an orphanage. Thing is, me and Anna will be separated. I know this because of the history the kinderheims, they usually separate boys and girls. They also further separate good and rich sons from sons of criminals or corrupted politicians.”
Johan knew about the kinderheims in East Germany as they were fairly notorious for the mistreatment, 511 kinderheim specifically being the worst and most notorious of all. He also knew about sex segregation as there were certain values and preconceived “lives” for boys and girls. He wouldn’t have to worry about Anna, but he himself would likely get sent to one of the bad ones.
“Now, I need to learn which type of orphanage they will send me to. Since we are clearly run aways, and because they are militaristic, they will likely see potential in me, potential to be a good soldier. With this information, I can deduce that I’ll be sent to a militaristic kinderheim for training, kinderheim 511. I will find a way to break out accordingly once I’m there, so I can go find Anna and we can escape.”
Johan was able to put himself in their shoes, and see himself how they see him, a kid who’s on the run in the middle of a restricted border. He was on the run for a reason, from their point of view he was a kid with a dark past seeing as they saw the evil within him.
He took their perspective and the only designation he saw himself going to was likely not just any kinderheim, but kinderheim 511, because their goals were also well known. They wanted to build super soldiers for the union, and Johan also took this into account.
So before they even went to the border, he knew where he would wind up being sent to, and after destroying it, he could find Anna fairly easily through getting good, powerful adoptive parents, and insisting that she must go with him for him to be with the parents.
PART 2
(I will call General wolf, wolf.)
Johan woke up, and saw outside his window that he was at a military hospital. His plan succeeded, he’s in East Germany. Now, he meets Helmut Wolf. He is able to deduce that he’s a general through seeing how different soldiers interact with him, and how they call him “General Wolf.”
“Now that I’m in east Germany, I see a high ranking soldier, one that can affect my treatment. So now is my chance to affect how I’m treated in 511 kinderheim.  Also, because he alone can affect all of this, it’s safe to assume there is a social structure in the orphanage. Where there is a structure, it can be destroyed. I can make the structure in this orphanage unstable by manipulating the low ranking people into hating the higher ups like this wolf guy.”
Johan was able to deduce that since wolf had the power to transport him to 511 kinderheim, he must be an official person within, ergo being able to affect his treatment in kinderheim.
Now that he also knows there’s a hierarchy, he can make and draw a line between the high and low ranking people, he can make hatred and chaos. Through this, there opens a window of opportunity to escape while covering up his tracks.
“I already know wolf is fascinated and almost scared of me, so he has high expectations. I will meet and exceed these expectations so I can get complete control over my stay. First, I will threaten him and make his fear of me solid. If I do this, he will make sure I’m treated in a way where I’m surrounded and watched by low ranking soldiers, sedated. He sees me for who I am, so he’d want to make sure I’m not a danger to him. It may take a while, but I will grow resistant to these drugs eventually. While they do study me, they’ll see my capabilities, and they’d also be scared of me. However, they’ll also know what I’m capable of, so I can use that to entice their greed. Once I’m resistant to the drugs, I will wait until the right moment to talk to the guards. I will then promise power, so they’ll let me escape their custody. I’ll tell the guards to do me a favor while I’m there, and promise that it’ll help them get a higher position. I can also ask them for Anna’s whereabouts, they should be able to get access to her location. After destroying kinderheim, I’ll go to Anna.”
Johan could tell Wolf was afraid of him because he had guards, was at a military hospital, and observed the mannerisms. He also observed that Wolf had an interest in him as he separated Johan and Anna but was only staying with Johan.
To get Wolf even more terrified, he threatened wolf in a roundabout way, saying “you’ll know how I feel here shortly.” This was to push Wolf over the limit, as Johan knew wolf had the power to control Johan’s situation. He knew that if he pushed Wolf over the edge, he’d want him locked away and sedated while being studied due to the interest.
Johan also knew due to the nature of a watch guard duty, it would be low ranking people watching over him, low paid individuals who’d want money and power. He also knew that through these experiments, they’d observe just how unbelievably intelligent and capable Johan is. So if Johan said or promised power, they’d take it, which he would after gaining resistance to drugs.
“I would need to cover my tracks though, and to do that I’ll need to kill the guards. It sounds like a place I’d hate anyway, so I’ll just destroy it, the adults and the fellow children. After being let go, I could blend into the crowd of children. Then, I could fan the flames of fear and hatred, making them also help In the chaos I’ll make, all the while eliminating each other.”
Johan knew that the environment of kinderheim 511 is specifically bad, and he heard the stories of the abuse. He took this into account while being able to plan out how to make the kids, guards, and higher ups have war with each other.
“First, I’ll spread fear by making up a story of some sort, then I’ll tell the kids a way to escape the fear and hatred, I’ll tell them to make the guards and higher ups kill each other, and to use the knowledge we’ve learned to help it. While we do that, I’ll order the guards I promised power to, to kill one of the higher ups, causing chaos among the lower ranks trying to succeed the position. They will fight with their weapons and the children will get caught in the fighting as well. I can then watch and make sure no unexpected variables escape.”
Johan knew of the fear the children will have, and he’d know how easy it is to influence these kids seeing as how they were raised to be loyal to the strong-the union. However, Johan knew he could take advantage of that and he could change the kids views into escaping the institution rather than bending their knee to it.
He also knew to spread a rumor, seeing as how the kids were likely gullible, he’d raise their fear by telling them of himself, a monster who was under drugs but grew resistance, and is now among the children.
He deduced the kids will be taught things related to intelligence and strength seeing as that was the goal of 511 kinderheim.
While this Chaos peaked, at the right moment, Johan would’ve order one of the guards he promised power to kill one of the higher ups. This would cause chaos among all the people in kinderheim, specifically the guards and higher ups at first, but he knew the chaos would drag the children in.
After this, everyone would be dead and Johan would have no past to speak of. Due to his apparent good looks, intelligence, and charming personality, he’d be the ideal kid for a rich person.
Johan essentially took control of his entire stay in kinderheim through manipulating wolf. He also even foresaw the guards that would be watching him and what they’d want given the nature of how underpaid and mistreated watch guards were.
He also planned as far as the destruction of 511 kinderheim here based off observations he gained in the hospital and former knowledge.
