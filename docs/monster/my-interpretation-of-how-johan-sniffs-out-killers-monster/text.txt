Anyway, my perspective on how Johan "sniffs out murderers":

Well, obviously I don't believe this is a literal statement. Johan does not have a superhuman abilty to locate killers on instinct. Whether or not he does it through literally sniffing them because they have a distinct odour, or he was bitten by a radioactive spider and developed Spider-sense to detect dangerous criminals and learned that with great power comes great manipulation. No. There's nothing in the story suggesting he possesses inexplicably abilities like this.

So how does Johan do it?

Extensive research, profiling, psychological analysis, and acute emotional perception.

Is this still pretty far fetched? Sure, but it's nothing too crazy for SCD and it's much more digestible than super senses.

Across the story Johan is shown capable of identifying potential killers, even as a child, and provoking them towards killing people. Additionally as an adult, he is able to understand a killer's psychosis and recreate their traumas as seen with him decorating a basement to appear as it did in Jurgen's childhood.

Firstly, detecting killers by just looking at them isn't impossible for SCD characters. Patrick Jane does exactly this at least twice from what I can remember, and Hannibal Lecter is also implied capable of it in both the series and novels. Johan could very well have noticed their innate psychopathy in the same way they do: through remarkable emotional perception and psychological knowledge.

But how does he know such extensive details about the psychologies of the likes of Jurgen? Jurgen states he never spoke a word to Johan himself so how did he know?

Well, even without Jurgen's own word, it's still not completely unfeasible that he couldn't find this information through research, profiling and investigation. Johan very likely could have simply identified Jurgens as the killer first, then performed a background check to further diagnose his psychosis. It wouldn't be difficult to find out Jurgens lived with his grandmother, and correlate that he likely suffered at the hands of a female abuser with his crimes against women.

How did he recreate the basement?

Perhaps he simply broke in and explored the house. Maybe the basement was left completely untouched after all those years and he simply put everything in position.

Alternatively. Maybe Jurgens did tell Johan everything and Johan just used his sigma male dark psychology powers to make him forget he ever did. Jurgens does kill himself in prison after being shown a picture of Johan and he does straight mind control people at points. It's not impossible Johan just made Jurgens forget about their conversations to make himself look scarier lmao.

(This is mostly a joke if you can't tell but if you want to take it as a valid interpretation, I won't stop you).

Okay now for the actual panel on the post. This panel is apparently evidence that Johan is not using impressive profiling and investigative techniques but is instead just inexplicably immediately understanding the murderous intent and complex psychologies of killers through nothing but ToM. So mind reading, pretty much.

Why do I think this isn't valid?

Firstly, Gillen knows next to nothing about Johan himself. He only knows what Jurgens has told him and what he's inferred through his own investigation. As gifted a psychologist as Gillen may be, he'd need to have ToM capabilities equally as supernatural as Johan's for this to be accurate.

Secondly, none of that matters because Gillen is very obviously speaking metaphorically. I once found a TV remote that got lost and my parents said I was able to sniff it out. Is this an insane sensory upscale for me because I was able to innately sense the remote's location?

It's just an expression, a figure if speech.

But let's go beyond this. If you look even deeper into it, you can see that Gillen himself does in fact say "I don't know how he does it" and "somehow". The use if these very interesting words and phrases gives the very subtle and mind blowing implication that maybe, juuuuust maybe, Gillen doesn't know how Johan does it. And that he's just acknowledging that he's very good at it.

Okay, but you may he asking, how do I know for certain that Johan is using these techniques to identify and manipulate killers instead of supernatural ToM?

Well, I guess I don't. But this is just my philosophy, I suppose you could call it, when it comes to scaling:

If a character is not explicitly and irrefutably shown to be a supernatural, then I'm going with the most logically likely explanation for the feats. Johan has always been a character that requires some suspension of disbelief but it can only go so far for me.

I would even argue that my interpretation is more impressive for SCD as it requires him to show proficiency in various fields instead of just emptying all his points into magical ToM.
