Herr Doktor Tenma (1-8)
* In The Revelation of Saint John the Divine, John symbolically explains his visions of the battle between good and evil. The evil that opposes everything the kingdom of God stands for is represented by a beast; this beast has the authority of the dragon (Satan), and as a result of Satan endlessly creating new ones to compose it, it's incapable of being stopped no matter how much you get rid of it. The beast uses these attributes to make people he's capable of exercising control over worship him (anyone who isn't a part of God.) The reason this is important to know is because this verse (particularly the beast) is used by Urasawa to represent Johan. Johan is the beast, he figuratively has the authority of the dragon, he is inevitable. Which is to say that as long as someone is evil/not a part of God, Johan can potentially control them. (Chapter 1, Page 8) (Manipulation)
   * I don't think this means that Johan can control literally anyone in any way he wants but instead that the scope of his manipulation (and subsequently his EQ) are ridiculously vast.

* After Johan's foster parents fled to West Germany, he utilized their televised appearance to attempt his first perfect suicide. Johan knew that his parents' wealth would serve as a plausible motive, so destruction of property and a carefully orchestrated pattern of deaths that would leave his sister—Anna—as the sole survivor were prepared to manipulate the numerous investigators working on the high-profile case into believing a burglary occurred. (Chapter 1-3 & 118) (Manipulation: Mass/Indirect/Logical, Deception: Concealment/Fabrication, Planning, Thinking: Creative/Application, Foresight)
   * Johan instructed Anna to shoot him in the head, subsequently inflicting psychological trauma that would cause her memories to dissipate, to ensure that his perfect suicide would be completed. (Chapter 4, Page 3—Chapter 14, Page 7—Chapter 118, Page 17-18) (EQ: Understanding/Management, Manipulation: Direct/Emotional, Foresight)
   * When conducting this horrible act, Johan permitted only evidence that supported his desired story. He wiped the bullets fired clean, leaving no fingerprints, but allowed footprints to be found near a window to bolster the burglary theory. (Chapter 4, Page 4) (Deception: Concealment/Fabrication)

* Johan killed the people Tenma wished death upon to repay him for saving his life. He accomplished this by poisoning candy after anticipating which gift the director would choose to unpack and share among his colleagues. This feat is particularly impressive because, according to what we know, he had almost no information about his targets, yet he was able to predict their actions perfectly while still healing from his brain surgery. (Chapter 4-5 & 8) (EQ: Understanding, Strategy, Thinking: Creative/Application, Foresight)
   * Outside of the candies, Johan did this without leaving a single piece of evidence. (Chapter 6, Page 20) (Deception: Concealment)

* Johan used a poisoned candy to kill the guard watching over Junkers' hospital room, knowing that Junkers would come out to confess and see the body. Given that Johan anticipated every move Junkers would make—including the location he would run to in fear of his death—he was able to execute Junkers and cut off any link between him and the crimes he had ordered. (Chapter 7) (EQ: Understanding/Management, Manipulation: Indirect/Emotional, Planning, Thinking: Creative/Application, Foresight)

* Johan's profound EQ allows him to render nearly insurmountable limitations meaningless. This is exemplified through his strategies utilized to kill the Doctors and Junkers, they are both almost entirely contingent on his anticipations of their actions which are derived from his understanding of them.

Take in mind, Johan is still a child when he performs these feats. When he fully develops and becomes an adult, he should scale much higher.

After the Carnival (33-41)
* Similarly to the Springer's strategy, Johan used his victims' past to generate an emotion that drove them to murder. This time, it was Jürgens and his foster mother, Frau Kempf. Johan knew the sight of this doll—along with other decorations—would cause Jürgens to recall his childhood and lose control, inciting him to murder Frau Kempf. If he had told someone (say, a social worker), Jürgens would almost definitely have been able to see it as a feasible solution, but he did not. Meaning that, as before, Johan most likely used his emotional understanding to overcome a limitation (in this case knowledge) and guide his plans, giving the impression that he possesses supernatural abilities when, in fact, his EQ just scales ridiculously high.
