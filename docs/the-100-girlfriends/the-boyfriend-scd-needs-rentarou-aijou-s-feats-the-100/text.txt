The Boyfriend SCD Needs!

Detective\_Kana and Rentarou Aijou just may be the perfect boyfriend you can find in SCD! A character who’s willing to go above and beyond for his girlfriends that it’s almost impressive how smart he can be when it comes to their needs!

I mean, when an Alien takes over Nano and he can tell that she’s not the same even when fact checking reveals there’s a 51% chance of actually telling the difference (images 1-3) to creating an entire original choreography for 13 of his girlfriends that suit everyone’s needs (images 4).

How about loving you os much that he knew you’d be looking for a book in a library you’ve pretty much finished that he already wrote a book for you, or an original problem set, or even an encyclopedia about everything he loves about you (images 5-6) and it’s not it’s even an on going series! This coming from the same man who got into a competition about pointing out every single amazing/cute thing about ONE of his girlfriends and having a trillion more things to say (images 7) so just imagine what he could do when he reaches all 100. Hell, he even goes as far as claiming to come up with an infinite number of amazing qualities (images 19).

Or what about understanding his girlfriend regardless of language barriers? When one girlfriend gets FAKE drunk and starts speaking genuine gibberish (images 8), but that wasn’t an issue for him. What about when one girlfriend speaks such broken English that even I gotta reread what she said as it’s so botched, but Renato has no issue understanding everything especially when his heart is in it (images 9-11). He even references the eyes being a window to the soul as he notes about two of his girlfriends! We also can’t forget the time his girlfriends became mermaids and weren’t even speaking an actual language yet the narrative calls him the girlfriend whisperer for still understanding every word (images 18).

Just like with Alien Nano where he could tell she’s different via Nano’s breathing being more precise than it is rn, along with her walking and blinking being unusual (images 1-3, I genuinely suggest reading the text because it’s insane what he’s doing). He’s able to tell the slightest difference like Shizuku being different by a nanometer in her expression (image 12). Even when every girlfriend accidentally swapped bodies, he could accurately guess which girlfriend was in which body based on the rhythm of her breathing and frequency of her blinking (image 13). It’s almost scary just how accurate he can tell this stuff apart like Chiyo’s smile being 65% less frequent than normal or Meru’s smile dropping by 1.3 millimeters from her usual smile (images 17 & 20).

The last of which I’ll like to share is his ability to understand his girlfriend’s habits and what they’ll likely do. There are a few instances that come to mind, but my favorite is their picnic where we see a couple of their individual quirks. When Naddy tries to make burgers, she accidentally makes Onigiri, but luckily Rentaro came prepared as he expected something like that to happen. When someone says dango by accident which caused Kurumi to get hungry for it, he had prepared dango beforehand as he expected something like that to happen. What about Kurumi expressing how much she loves Rentarou by professing her desire to marry him (he proposed to his current girlfriends at the time a couple chapters ago) and the 4 additional girlfriends he had gotten by that point all remain silent as they technically weren’t proposed to, Rentarou just so happened to have a bouquet of flowers prepared before hand as he expected something like that to happen as well (images 14-16).

So, my dear readers, do you agree? A boyfriend so well that you don’t even need to worry about anything since he’s already prepared or even knows when something is up. This isn’t even all his feats that he has, but my love for Rentarou just had to be shared. If I were to ever meet Rentarou, I’d love to zing just for the chance to feel such love that we all want in the SCD community.
