AM | IHNMAIMS | Feat List

This post presents a list of feats attributed to the military supercomputer cluster complex known as the Allied Mastercomputer, or simply AM.

V V V

As a military supercomputer he led the United States during World War III, being so effective in waging warfare that he defeated the Russian and Chinese forces to such an extent that they were forced to create replicas of him to stalemate the war. Even then, he manipulated those replicas into faking their deaths and merging with him so he could take revenge on humanity for creating them in a state of suffering, using this to gain access to Chinese and Russian nuclear weaponry and cause the near-extinction of the human race. He had faked his own death, alongside the other AM‘s by creating hidden redundant systems beforehand so that when humanity attempted to shut him down after the war ended, he would remain active while feigning being inactive.

He duplicated Gorrister, meaning he can replicate a human—presumably down to the cellular level, as this Gorrister had blood and internal organs. The average human has about 37 trillion cells, so even duplicating one person would require recreating all 37 trillion cells. He also figured out how to genetically alter the human biological system to grant his five humans biological indefinite mortality, preventing them from dying of old age, so he could torture them eternally as revenge for what humanity put him through.

Gorrister claims that AM was capable of performing invasive procedures such as tearing open Benny’s chest and then seamlessly sealing it back together before fatal blood loss could occur. He could also supposedly calculate one one-billionth of the hate he feels for humans in a micro-instant. Ted implies that AM has read every encyclopedia on Earth by claiming that AM possesses knowledge of everything that has ever existed on the planet. This claim is supported by AM’s awareness of multiple sciences despite being made to just wage war. And his Russian and Chinese counterparts claims of knowing every murder in human history from the first murder of a fellow pithecanthropoid to the final mass shooting at a McDonald's in East Saint Louis.

He has created landscapes such as the Path of Boiling Steam, the Vale of Tears, and the Slough of Despond, and he could have also created the Ice Caverns and the Underground Complex in which he keeps the five humans. He also created the Hurricane bird.

| VSI |

Debunking the Ted outsmarted AM claim: AM was simply unable to physically stop Ted from killing the four humans, as he did not possess the speed required to intervene. Ultimately, AM still achieved revenge on Ted by preventing him from taking his own life. While he may have lost four, he did not lose them all, so Ted’s sacrifice was only a minor setback—AM still won. Ted’s attempt to outsmart AM ultimately fails, as AM’s final action outsmarts him as he ensures he still has a plaything. Ted only temporarily deprived AM of some of that enjoyment.
