Anastasia's first appearance is in the central fortress, where she is introduced as the main antagonist. The scene depicts a dark and oppressive atmosphere, with Anastasia standing before an ancient altar, surrounded by mystical symbols and advanced technology. Her posture is serene, but her gaze conveys a latent threat. "The power that lies in the shadows is not for the faint of heart. Only those willing to embrace their destiny can claim it."

The phrase is laden with symbolism and double meaning, suggesting that the power he wields is both physical and metaphysical. By using terms like "shadows" and "destiny," he appeals to universal concepts that generate a sense of inevitability and fatalism. This not only establishes his authority but also psychologically manipulates the protagonist and the other characters, sowing doubt and fear.

The scene is designed so that her presence dominates the space. The characters surrounding her show deference, and the lighting highlights her figure as a focal point, reflecting her ability to control the environment and people without resorting to violence.

Although there is no direct interaction, her composure in a hostile environment indicates absolute emotional control, suggesting that she will not be an impulsive adversary but a calculating one.

Before the final battle, Anastasia confronts the protagonist in a room filled with ancient relics and ruined technology. The tension is palpable, and the dialogue becomes a verbal duel where she attempts to break Luke's spirit.

"Do you think your efforts will change the course of the inevitable? Every step you take only brings you closer to your downfall. Don't you feel the weight of fate crushing you?"

This line is a clear example of gaslighting. Anastasia not only challenges the protagonist's abilities but also makes him question the validity of his own actions and hopes. By suggesting that fate is immutable, she undermines Luke's confidence, weakening his will before the physical fight.

The precision with which she attacks the protagonist's morale indicates that Anastasia has read his emotions and weaknesses. She doesn't hurl generic insults, but rather targets hopelessness and fear, emotions she knows can paralyze.

On a strategic level, this verbal confrontation is part of a broader method: to wear down the mind first in order to reduce resistance during actual combat. Anastasia doesn't waste energy; she optimizes it. She knows that an enemy who enters the fray psychologically weakened is less capable of reacting, planning, and executing. That's why she manipulates Luke's narrative about the situation, not to convince him, but to disrupt his mental clarity and diminish his ability to make decisions under pressure.

Thus, his intervention is not only an act of intimidation, but a tactical move that reveals his mastery of emotional analysis, control of the environment, and discursive manipulation as tools to tip the scales in his favor before the fight begins.

In the midst of the battle, just as the protagonist manages to withstand her initial attacks, Anastasia transforms into a gigantic dragon. The scene shows her changing form, her wings unfurling, and her power dramatically increasing, altering the dynamics of the fight.

The transformation is not merely a change, but a tactical response to the opponent's resistance. Anastasia demonstrates mental flexibility to shift her strategy and form, adapting to the situation to maintain her advantage.

In its dragon form, it dominates the airspace, attacking from multiple angles and anticipating movements. This requires complex and rapid mental visualization, as well as holistic thinking to coordinate attacks in three dimensions.

The controlled fury he displays in this form enhances his attacks, reflecting a conscious management of his emotions to maximize his performance.

"That's right, just like you, I am the second creation after Brad, and when they created you they decided that Brad was no longer relevant, just like me, where I was just a system error for not being completely perfect."

This reflection demonstrates a high degree of self-awareness and a complex view of reality. Anastasia understands the moral and existential implications of her actions and projects them onto the protagonist.

Anastasia has left subtle narrative clues that suggest she has been manipulating events from the shadows long before the direct confrontation.

It is speculated that Anastasia fomented conflicts between rival factions to weaken her enemies without exposing herself, demonstrating a mastery of mass manipulation and indirect strategy, which helped Dr. Eric to get the government to contribute to the DNA mutation experiments and thus carry out his plan (which is explained below).

He also keeps secrets and controls the information that reaches his subordinates and enemies, ensuring that no one has a complete view of his plans, giving him a significant strategic advantage.

Ana, being Dr. Eric's second creation, was created to absorb other beasts (remember this for later). 1-Ana knows that Luke can transform into various sentient mint beasts, but he needs the chips to do so. Ana studies this and uses it as a lure for Brad to escape from the lab with the genetic chips. Luke finds out and is sent to retrieve the chips, where he has a confrontation with Brad and, needless to say, loses. With this, Ana gets rid of Brad and avoids creating conflict between the two brothers. With Brad out of the equation, she begins her plan.

2- When Eric was summoned to discuss his experiments, Ana was present and was the Doctor's "assistant," making it difficult to suspect she was up to something. Ana managed to convince the executives and special forces to fund the Doctor's experiments under the pretext that "they would support the military." Half agreed and began funding the experiments, which involved testing different gases on animals to alter their behavior in order to create biological weapons that could support the special forces. The other half remained "out" because the experiments were unethical and against the laws of nature. Ana understood the executives' point and decided to tell the Doctor to consider the following points.

* 1-The experiments will be for the government only and no one else

* 2- Any inconvenience or issue outside of what was agreed upon will result in a refund of the money that was contributed.

* 3- Everything was for research purposes and to help the government; there was no intention to cause harm to human beings…

With all this, the executives came to accept the terms that the doctor had set (clearly the idea was Ana's).

3- Ana spent little time studying how the Gas Mist worked and how living beings mutated upon contact with it. Ana studied each animal body and how they changed biologically. She had limited time, and these were the few times she participated in the experiments and the creation of each chip. Speaking of these chips! Ana perfectly understood how they worked and how the chips revealed noticeable physiological changes in animals and how these altered their behavior to a more hostile state. In any case, it was only a few times, vaguely, which shows her incredible memory at this point. Ana also took on the task of studying the key entry and exit points of the gas mist. She also took chips and strategically scattered them where Luke could find them after arriving on the Island. Once Luke had them, he could absorb them and become the ultimate beast, thus being recognized by Dr. Eric.
