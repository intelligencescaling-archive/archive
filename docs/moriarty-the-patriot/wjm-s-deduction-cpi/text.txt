WJM's Deduction CPI
Creator : AwaKen
________________

In this doc, I will explain a CPI feat performed by William James Moriarty in the first chapter of Moriarty The Patriot: The Remains.

This feat will be separated into two sections:

1. His deductive speed.
2. The data he gathered.

His deduction speed:

Let's start with the first point, the time it took him to deduce that information, therefore I'll show you some panels from MTP Remains chapter 1.

The Panels :

He opens the door and enters Moriarty's room, WJM here asks him what was the reason for coming to him so late at night.

Moran tells him about how he met a girl named Marie who was in need of money since she recently got engaged to someone of a higher status. She needed 100 euros for dowry, Moran then asks Moriarty if he can borrow it from him.

Moriarty says that's a big sum, then immediately asks him when he got caught cheating were his cards open?

This was the deduction.

Now even before Moran said about the money he needed WJM already knew he was lying because of his quick deduction skills but how quick was that? If we go back to Noah's ship Arc or Train arc we can see him deducing everything in detail with a single glance, and he did the same thing in this scene. It took him mere seconds ( 1-3 seconds ) to deduce that, so that's about his speed.

The Information he deduced

1. Moriarty was able to deduce that Moran was lying about everything he said about Marie except that he actually needed 100 euros.

2. Moriarty was able to deduce that Moran cheated in the pub, he then makes an assumption that if his opponent saw his hand, to which Moran said yes. ( Moran is a retard )

These two deductions were shown on screen but this is not the entire thing, Moriarty also gives his own reasoning of his Deduction.

Moriarty's Reasoning -

Firstly, from how he called upon Moriarty, this late at night, one might think it's an emergency but from how he acted earlier, as if he didn't want Louis to hear what he was about to say, Moriarty could see that as far as possible he wanted to keep it a secret from others. However the way he spoke about the 100 euros was genuinely urgent, so your need for it was probably real. In other words he got in some trouble earlier tonight and needed money from Moriarty now the only place Moran could be in trouble was the west end pub where he goes to place cards, now considering Moran's personality it has to do something with Women or Gamblings ( It was about Gambling ). Moriarty considered the first option but then discarded it because he found it difficult to believe. Now Moran knows how to get along with women, even if he did shoulder a lady's wedding expenses he wouldn't have any reason to run around raising money so late at night which meant everything Moran said about Marie was a lie. The only possibility now was that it was regarding cards, but if he had merely lost then he would've just forfeited the money he had with him and there would be no need for 100 euros, Therefore something must have happened in that pub that made him oblige to prepare that sum, Hence the most plausible explanation was that his tricks were found by his opposition

Conclusion : Moriarty was able to deduce all this within 1-3 seconds at best which makes it a very good PSI feat and decent-good WMI feat. Overall it is a good CPI feat.
