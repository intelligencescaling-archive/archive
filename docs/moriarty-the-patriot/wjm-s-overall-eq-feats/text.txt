Note: This document does not go into detail about his EQ abilities; it is intended to provide you a quick overview of his Emotional Intelligence feats.

Emotional Engagement - William James Moriarty is a person with great intellectual capabilities; from birth, he had a strong motivation to make London a crime-free city; he was willing to go to any length to make London a crime-free city, even killing his own adopted family as a child, which included his mother, father, and 2nd brother without hesitation; even as an adult, he didn't think twice before killing corrupt nobles; Duke Blitz Enders is an example, where he smiled at him as he was ready to commit suicide Even in the Final Problem arc, we see him slaughtering Nobles in front of their own children . Even after all of this, he never believed he had the authority to punish others for their misdeeds (as he stated in the Final Problem arc). His determination and desire to eliminate classism in London was so intense that it drove him to do something against his own morality, become a criminal, and die as a villain in the eyes of everyone, despite the fact that he had good intentions to the end.

Emotional Understanding - The best way William James Moriarty influenced the emotions of the people of London was in The London Mass Scale Manipulation, where he influenced the commoners and the high ranking people (nobles) to act together and end the classism in London, the effect was visible in 1/4th of the entire world. The Nobles used to treat commoners like animals, looking down on them; even the family who adopted WJM treated him and his brother Louis terribly; the distance between them (Nobles and Commoners) was enormous To end it, WJM became the Lord of Crime and began killing all the corrupt nobles, instilling fear in the hearts of all nobles, good or bad. The commoners, on the other hand, liked the fact that someone was assisting them in living a normal life in London, but WJM decided to become the common enemy to both sides in the Final Problem arc, where he begins to burn every house in London with the help of his team, this time he was not targeting the Corrupted Nobles But everyone in London, including the Commoners, felt betrayed and enraged by his actions and the people of London resolved to unite together to get rid of WJM.

Emotional Management - Emotional Management is about regulating emotions and oneself and others to achieve goals. In both the Jack the Ripper arc and Final Problem arc, Moriarty was able to regulate the emotions of him and the people to achieve his goals and he was successful both the times. Moriarty portrayed himself as the devil of London, also known as Lord of Crime, a person who murders all corrupt lords and helps commoners live peacefully. In Final Problem, he posed as the villain, he burnt the houses of London, the Tower of London, he began slaughtering Nobles, the entire London joined to battle him, he was able to affect their emotions and had them fight him together, thus achieving his aim by utilising the emotions of others. For the Intrapersonal EQM, Moriarty displayed great composure and acting skills throughout the Manga, which are essential in terms of Intrapersonal EQM. In The Two Detective Arc, Sherlock spotted Moriarty in one of the seats and sat near him and told him that someone named Hope revealed the Identity of the Lord of Crime, and to make it even more crazy, he actually bluffed saying that Hope revealed that Moriarty is the Lord of Crime, however Moriarty was still able to keep his composure and saw through Sherlock's bluff. Another great Acting skills/composure feat was seen in the Durham arc where he was kidnapped.
