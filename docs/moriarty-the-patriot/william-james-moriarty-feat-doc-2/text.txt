The golden ratio or golden mean, also known as the golden ratio of geometry, is an expression that denotes some properties of shapes, especially rectangles, that are perceived as aesthetically pleasing. This ratio can be expressed in mathematical terms as (a + b) / a equals c, which in the case of rectangles is known as the aspect ratio.
The golden ratio is equal to approximately 1.618, and it is found throughout the natural world, particularly in the realm of art, architecture, and photography. It is often found in the form of the spiral, which is seen in various different forms in shapes and designs.
It is used to determine the ratio of the different sides of the block, which can then be scaled to create a better layout of the shapes and patterns. The golden ratio is also used extensively in the design of logos, websites, and other visual elements, as it creates proportions and balance that are pleasing to the eye.
The golden ratio is considered to be a great design feat because it creates a sense of balance and harmony within the design elements. When using the golden ratio, it helps create an aesthetic balance between the various shapes and elements, such as the background, foreground and objects, by creating an even flow throughout the design.
