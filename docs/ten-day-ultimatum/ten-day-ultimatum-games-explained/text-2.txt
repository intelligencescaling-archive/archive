# First game: Liar identity card game

10 participants wake up in a completely dimly lit, sealed room. A goat-mask-wearing figure introduces himself as {Mortal Goat}, then kills the smiling participant. He then refers to the other {nine} participants and tells them that they will participate in the game for the creation of a god, Nuwa (the creationist god of Chinese mythology).

{Mortal Goat} commences the game and informs them of the rules:

"The instructions are as follows: I want each of you to recount the events leading up to your arrival here," the goat-headed figure instructed. "However, please be aware that among all the storytellers, one person will not be telling the truth. After each of you has finished narrating your story, we will proceed with a vote. If all eight of you correctly identify the {Liar}, then the liar will be eliminated and the rest will survive. But if even one person selects incorrectly, the liar will survive and the rest will be eliminated."

Someone interrupts {Mortal Goat} during his introduction, so {Mortal Goat} allows one minute for the {nine} participants to strategize.

After one minute, {Mortal Goat} makes everyone draw identity cards (Game of Nuwa). The game officially commences.

Our MC, Qi Xia (Neat/Nice Summer), has the liar identity card.

{Mortal Goat} adds one thing before leaving:

"I shall no longer intervene in the proceedings. Each of you shall write down the names according to your own judgment. Remember, {the rules are absolute}. And in the end, I shall personally administer the {punishment} to the loser."

A common pattern can be noted: participants were vague about what they were doing and how they got into the situation that resulted in them losing consciousness and waking up in this place.

One participant was using a pseudonym, and all the stories were really close to each other province-wise.

This catches the attention of our MC.

He also notices two people arguing fiercely, and he couldn't shake off the peculiarity of this situation. It wasn't the dubious nature of their stories that troubled him, but rather the fact that he too had experienced an earthquake.

He wasn't in Shaanxi or Guangdong, but in Shandong Province. Could there truly be earthquakes of such magnitude in this world?

The earthquake spanned half the country and affected three provinces.

If they were all telling the truth, wouldn't that have been an unprecedented disaster?

He notices the weird coincidence of other participants telling the wrong details about their provinces and mentioning that an earthquake happened.

Considering there might be multiple liars, Qi Xia remembers that the rules were absolute, as {Mortal Goat} said.

An intermission of 20 minutes is announced.

Qi Xia notices a desk clock at the center of the table, and the participants can't move from their positions, as if they are glued to them.

He also questions the intermission.

However, the game had only been in progress for thirty minutes, yet they were given a twenty-minute break?

After the intermission, the discussion continues.

The discussion continues with the same themes of the earthquake, losing consciousness, participants being in nearby provinces, and the mention of a swindler, which our MC is.

Finally, the MC's turn comes.

Qi Xia gives out his story of being a swindler, the earthquake, and losing consciousness.

After that, an intermission is allowed.

Qi Xia quickly begins solving the mystery, refraining from participating in the discussion and reasoning through the logic of the game where, if the liar wins, everyone is doomed.

Qi Xia reasons that the goat-headed figure said:

{among all the storytellers, one person will not be telling the truth}.

{the rules are absolute}.

He also remembers:

{You've all been asleep in my presence for twelve hours}.

Qi Xia finds the key piece.

The criss-crossing lines on the wall and floor spark an awakening in his mind. He glances at the desk clock—it is nearly 1 o'clock.

Qi Xia accepts the paper and begins his calculations. He tallies the large squares on the wall, which total nine. Next, he determines there are a combined sixteen squares on the floor and ceiling.

Qi Xia continues scribbling at a rapid pace.

‘If I'm correct…’ he pauses briefly, then resumes, ‘these squares represent square meters, implying that we're currently in a room measuring four meters in length and width, with a height of three meters…’

‘Four by four by three... that's forty-eight cubic meters.’

Qi Xia's hands tremble slightly. "It's insufficient... completely insufficient..."

Confusion fills the room as everyone stares at Qi Xia.

Qi Xia arrives at the answers—{54.6} and {49.14}.

Upon seeing these two numbers, Qi Xia's complexion pales, as if he is grappling with an unsettling realization. If his assumption proves entirely accurate, then the present circumstances are undeniably ominous.

His pupils dilate incessantly, and his emotional state seems to have transcended into realms of unimaginable distress.

The clamor of everyone's arguments gradually subsides as they notice the man who remained aloof from the discussions, deeply engrossed in his calculations.

\[Could it be that he had indeed unearthed the {solution} to this perplexing question?\]

After a moment of silence, Qi Xia speaks, addressing everyone and revealing that initially he had no intention of saving anyone. But if they make the wrong choice, it will result in his demise as well, which he can't afford because someone is waiting for him outside.

He urges everyone to listen to what he has to say.

Showing his card—Liar—to everyone, Qi Xia continues by explaining that everyone has inserted a lie into their story without disrupting its logical coherence.

Tian Tian lied by omitting whether the billboard struck the vehicle and knocked her unconscious while she was working within the car.

Qiao Jiajin lied by stating that he had no recollection of what happened afterward when he fell onto the billboard.

Xiao Ran lied by not specifying whether she and the child truly evaded the incoming car.

Doctor Zhao lied by not stating whether he was really rendered unconscious by the collapsed ceiling, along with the fact that operating rooms were constructed for stability.

Han Yimo lied by claiming he had no knowledge of what fully occurred. However, when one is focused on writing, even the smallest distraction can be noticeable.

Lawyer Zhang lied by not specifying how deep the crevice was.

Li Shangwu lied by not specifying the brand of car he was driving. This leads to the deduction that he should have reclined the seat and freed himself from the restraints of the person behind him.

Lin Qin lied by stating that her studio had collapsed, but she mentioned that it was situated on a high floor. If a high floor were to collapse, the entire building would cease to exist.

Upon receiving opposition to the idea that everyone had lied, Qi Xia explains the nature of the game. The game encourages overconfidence among the non-liars, and discerning the truth from the stories is difficult because the stories overlap, making it hard to figure out the logical inconsistencies.

Qi Xia explains that the organizer deliberately selected nine of them for a reason. It was to foster a sense of absolute confidence in themselves and to encourage them to uncover the "inconsistencies" in the seemingly intertwined narratives.

He finishes by saying that there is only one person who set up all these circumstances:

{Mortal Goat}.

He picks up the pen and inscribes the words {Mortal Goat} on his paper.

"I always found it peculiar why he introduced himself with such an unusual moniker as {Mortal Goat} initially. It seemed rather superfluous. Now, upon reflection, it's clear that this was a component of the game."

A question arises: when {Mortal Goat} told a storyline?

Qi Xia replies, "When he said he assembled us here to create a {God}, isn't that an outrageous story?"

Another question arises: how can you tell that everyone has lied?

Qi Xia replies, "It's impossible that all of you spoke the truth. I've dedicated some time to debating this matter. Not only am I aware that you've all lied, but I've also pinpointed the exact instances."

He slides his scrap paper forward, then turns to Tian Tian beside him.

"Tian Tian, while you were {working} in the car, with your head protruding outside, did the billboard truly strike the vehicle and render you unconscious?"

He continues this process until he gains the trust of the group.

Then he finishes by saying:

"Everyone, let's face it. We all have already died, myself included."

One question arises: how did he figure that out?

Qi Xia replies that he noticed the sealed nature of the room, with no gaps or windows, the lines on the wall and floor, the clock, and why {Mortal Goat} forced the intermission.

He adds:

If one human consumes 0.42 cubic meters of air per hour, multiplying that by nine people would amount to 3.78 cubic meters per hour.

Multiplying that by the amount of hours nine people could have been in the room, which was 13 hours, they would have consumed 49.14 cubic meters of air.

Using the clues that the organizers left, he finds out the room volume and the amount of air needed.

Both answers of 49.14 and 54.6 cubic meters of air consumption contradict the maximum amount of air that could be stored within the room.

This meant that, at some point or another, they should have been struggling for air. However, they weren't.

Qi Xia then considers the assumption that {Mortal Goat} isn't human, considering he crushed someone 's skull with ease.

With time running out, he writes {Mortal Goat}'s name in the judgment and urges everyone to do the same if they want to survive.

{Mortal Goat} follows the judgment, takes out the gun, and shoots himself in the heart, screaming in agony for a few minutes before dying.
