# Third game  : NO & YES

The {nine} hanging participants are greeted by {Mortal Snake}, who is on the ground near the lever.

"Long time no see, everyone. I am Mortal Snake," he begins in a deliberate tone.

"Please, let's not get too excited," Mortal Snake's voice remains calm. He glances up at everyone before continuing, "You are in the final stage of the trial. I have a lever by my hand here. Once I pull it, you will descend safely, and no one will be hurt."

"Listen attentively, everyone. This game is titled {Yes or No}," Mortal Snake declares with a slight step forward, addressing the individuals suspended from the ceiling.

"You, as a collective, have three opportunities to question me; my responses will be confined to {yes} or {no}.

I assure you, I will uphold truthfulness. Following these queries, if I choose to spare you all, I will activate the lever. Otherwise, I will seal the door, and your destiny will unfold accordingly."

One of the participants, Xian Ran Sei, seizes the moment and speaks out without hesitation.

"Hey, can you lower us down?!"

Qi Xia tries to stop her, but it is too late.

Mortal Snake sneers and replies, "No."

Qi Xia ponders for a moment, then figures out a method to beat the logic of this game.

Qi Xia asks, "Mortal Snake, if my next question were {Will you pull the lever?}, would your answer be the same as this question?"

"Hahahaha!"

Mortal Snake suddenly bursts into laughter, so hard that he almost loses his balance. After a while, he stops laughing and says, "You are truly interesting."

"Interesting?" Qi Xia coldly regards Mortal Snake. "What's so interesting? Didn't I already win?"

Mortal Snake slowly approaches the wall and reaches out to pull the lever.

# Logic

First query:

Hey, can you lower us down? → Answer: NO

Second query:

Mortal Snake, if my next question were {Will you pull the lever?}, would your answer be the same as this question?

{Mortal Snake} can't lie, so when asked if his answer to the next question would be the same, the answer would have to be YES.

Then, for the third query:

Will you pull the lever?

{Mortal Snake} would be forced to give the answer YES.

Using {Mortal Snake}'s own statement, {I assure you, I will uphold truthfulness}, against him, Qi Xia forces him to answer YES and therefore forces him to pull the lever.
