Joe Goldberg VSI Feat

 The feat is from:
YOU (Season 3 Episode 10)

Created by: umogrimes (dc & tt) & sido75 (dc) & yildizya/cheatspeed (dc/yt)

WARNING: Contains spoilers.

INTRODUCTION
In the last episode of the season, Joe escaped Madre Linda. He killed Love, faked his death, and framed Love for it; then, he escaped to Paris. In order for him to do this, he needs to evade security cameras, because if the cameras see him, it would show that he is not actually dead.

After killing Love inside his house, Joe cut off two toes and put them inside a newly baked food to make it seem like Love killed him and made food out of his body. He then made his way to the airport (this part is offscreen) to escape to Paris. Joe had to visualize every security camera in Madre Linda (the town he lives in) and their point of view. By looking at some footage, we can count the number of     cameras in the town.

We can count 59 cameras from these photos. These photos come from Matthew’s computer who had access to every security camera in Madre Linda.

In this photo, we can see the names of 14 places in Madre Linda (all of these are exterior cameras). If we say that there are a minimum of 2 cameras in every place, we get 28 cameras from this photo.

Additionally, in this photo it shows 2 more place names, “A Fresh Tart” and the library. Again, if we say there are a minimum of 2 cameras in every place, we get 4 cameras.

In this photo, we can see dozens of people that live in Madre Linda. If we exclude the people from work, and count families as one person, we get 17. This is the number of houses in Madre Linda. Although Matthew had access to 4 cameras per house, 3 of those look directly inside the house, and only one looks outside. So we get 17 cameras from this picture.

CONCLUSION
Adding up all the numbers we got from these pictures, we get 108 cameras in total. As I said earlier, Joe had to visualize all these cameras and their point of views in order to not get seen by them. This feat buffs Joe’s VSI.
