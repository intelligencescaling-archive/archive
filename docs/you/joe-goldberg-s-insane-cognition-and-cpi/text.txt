Multi Tasking

Human multitasking - Wikipedia
The human brain is capable of multitasking up to a certain level. For example: you can write with both hands or eat while watching a movie. However, as the complexity of the tasks and the number of errors increases, the performance and efficiency of our brain starts to decline. The main reason for this is that the human brain is quite restrictive when it comes to really complex processes. For example, imagine a situation where a person is trying to listen to two different conversations at the same time. As a result, he or she can only understand parts of these conversations, and this means that our brain has a hard time organizing its resources for each of its operations

Working Memory Aspect
One of the main criticisms that can be made for this feat is that this feat is only within the scope of Processing speed. This is not entirely true.
To put it simply, processing 2 different personalities in your mind means transferring the information from 2 different personalities through working memory into long-term memory, a process that requires a very complex and intensive cognitive processing. Each personality processes its own experiences, thoughts and emotions separately. This information is stored primarily in working memory, i.e. short-term memory. Working memory is used to process and temporarily store immediate information, which is then transferred to long-term memory. Long-term memory is where information is permanently stored. Carrying out this process for two different personalities at the same time puts an extraordinary strain on the brain's processing capacity.
