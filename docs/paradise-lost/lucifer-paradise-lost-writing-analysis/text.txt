Paradise lost is often known as Milton's magnum opus  in which he wrote Lucifer through depicting him as a tragic figure and his psychological state in defying God,. Often when we recall the name of Satan or Lucifer we think of absolute evil that caused the fall of Adam and Eve and brought forth death and sin to the world when he was cast for disobedience in heaven. The original sin who as one of the first creations of God disobeyed his order and did not respect His hierarchy.

Miltons intent in writing paradise lost was to justify the ways of god to men, and this conflict which tries to depict Lucifers internal struggle and frustration towards his eternal punishment, in book 4 mentioning his mind being the source of those raging flames that fuel his unrelentless pursuit of executing a futile attack against heaven,and conflicting natures between satan's perspective of the ways of god and the divine which he cannot actually deconstruct for its the creation of God, he mentions later in the Analysis of hypocrisy and it being invisible to All but God.

"What though the field be lost?
All is not lost—the unconquerable will,
And study of revenge, immortal hate,
And courage never to submit or yield:
And what is else not to be overcome?
That Glory never shall his wrath or might
Extort from me."

Here, Satan emphasizes his own will and determination against what he sees as the despotic rule of God. He likens his situation to a fight against oppression, suggesting that God's demand for obedience is self-serving rather than truly just.
Later, Satan articulates his struggles with the idea of divine justice and the actions imposed by God, reflecting his belief that God’s actions do not align with the principles of just governance. This perspective aligns with the notion of a hypocritical deity in Satan’s eyes, as he argues that God’s omnipotence comes with an expectation of unquestioned loyalty, blurring the lines between true justice and authoritarianism.

Fueling his anger more Satan finds it intolerable that God can define what is what, in the eyes of Satan his actions would be deemed disloyalty to God and in that he finds it hypocritical that God cannot be judged for such, only himself.
