Vampire massacre at 31st october plan analysis. (New mid tier character)

This is Anko Uguisu, a character from Call of the night (some might recognize) her. I'm going to analyze her best feat, which I call "Vampire massacre at 31st october" or just "Halloween massacre"

First of all, we need to understand the world building, Anko's past, relationships and motivations. Call of the night is centered around a world with two races: humans and vampires. Humans live normally without knowing the existence of vampires, who hunt at night and blend perfectly with humans, creatures that evolved to being highly popular, often in important roles in society. Anko, whose original name is Kyouko Mejiro, used to be a normal high school girl, a loner, who discovered the existence of vampires because of her best friend and love interest: Nazuna Nanakusa.

At first, Anko was amazed and wanted to be a vampire, not knowing the risks, and Nazuna promised she'd turn Anko in one. One day, Anko's 18th birthday came in 31st october, her parents who were usually distanced from her prepared a birthday cake. Anko talked to her dad about the zippo lighter she gave him in his birthday, and he says he didn't use it since he considered it special, since it was Anko's gift (him considering the zippo special is VERY important.), then, he picks up the lighter and decides to smoke a bit, but, as soon as he picks it up, he seems disturbed and distressed, dropping the lighter.

His form as a vampire is revealed, fangs visible and paler skin. His instincts kicked in, and he attacked his own wife, drinking her blood and killing her.

Then, he went after Anko, who looked at the lighter and then at him. He lunges at Anko, and in a quick reflex, Anko associates the strange behavior of her dad towards the lighter with some sort of weakness. She grabs the lighter and brutally spanks her dad with it, ending his life. (This demonstrates quick thinking, adversity capacity and pattern recognition)

After that event, Anko got rid of her name Kyouko Mejiro, and assumed her name Anko Uguisu, a mere pseudonym. The Mejiro family death case was investigated, but later got discontinued: 2 deaths, 1 disappeared (in that case, Kyouko aka anko.)

After a decade, Anko got her job as a detective, which allowed her to get info easily of people without it being considered a crime, and would help her to investigate vampires.

Using her own past as experience, she quickly tested a theory on other vampires. Her theory was using an object vampires considered special in their human life against them. She went after Kou and questioned him about the disappearing of his friend, Akkun, who was turned into a vampire by Seri.

Kou lied about not knowing anything, but Anko saw through his lie and tracked Kou down, linking him to Akkun. Now, with better knowledge of Akkun, she investigated his life deeper and got his old glasses that belonged to him when he was human.

Later in a bathhouse, Anko stalked Kou and his group (consisting of Kou, Mahiru and three vampires including akkun)

She gave back the glasses to Akkun, and Akkun instantly felt disturbed just like Anko's dad in the past. Anko uses that opportunity to stab Akkun viciously, noticing that his regeneration was considerably slowed down, and his vampire immunity to common weapons, along with the hardness of vampire skin to piercing things was reset back to human for a brief moment.

Later in the series, Anko stalks the vampires to collect their weaknesses, but they're faster to burn down their human memories because of Kou giving away info to them about Anko.

Due to that, Anko had to reformulate her plans. She wanted to kill all vampires, but, doing that one by one would be difficult if they kept multiplying. How vampires multiply? Biting someone who loves them. But how would Anko prevent Vampires from drinking blood? The 10 years rule. Another weakness of vampires. If they stop drinking blood for a decade, they die of starvation.

That's where Anko madness reaches its peak. In 31st october, where people would be out in the night to celebrate, Anko shot down vampires knowing full well it wouldn't kill them, but it would catch the attention of the person who was essential to her plan: Nazuna.

First, she lured Nazuna to the school they used to go to together a decade ago. Anko threatens to kill who Nazuna loves the most, Kou, with the intention to pressure Nazuna and put her into an abnormal state of mind, forcing her to fight to kill Anko, or else Kou would die.

But thag was a bluff. Anko wouldn't kill Kou, she didn't kill humans, only vampires. Her plan was: be killed by a vampire and leave clues behind about her death. The police would find out about her death and eventually find out about her real identity: Kyouko Mejiro, who supposedly disappeared 10 years ago, and the mejiro family death case would be investigated again, eventually leading to the police finding out about the existence of vampires, which would eventually be reported by journalists, telling the population to be careful, and eventually a lockdown at night would happen, making vampires starve to death slowly.

But, of course, her plan failed. Because Kou arrived at the location she was fighting with Nazuna and exposed her plan. Anko gave up of her plan and decided to kill herself, since 10 years of her life were wasted, but Kou doesn't let her kill herself.
