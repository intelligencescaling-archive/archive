Beating Abyss is Impossible (All in Abyss: Judge the Fake)

Detective\_Kana and as for my first proper post, I wanted to talk about a game I’m very interested in. I’ve already made a post (TikTok & YouTube if you’re curious) about Light Yagami going against the Witches (With & Without prep-time) in their respective matches if you’d like a reference, but I wanted to paint a picture of what to expect against one of the two most impossible matches in this game and why I believe it to be realistically impossible for any SCD character to overcome (assuming they’re not some higher being/character I forgot with insane hax).

For context, all matches play Texas Hold’em. There’s an additional rule, but it’s irrelevant for this specific match. The character we’ll be looking at is Abyss (the woman on the right of the first image). Her match is split into 3 phases: her initial introduction (meant to demonstrate her opposing power), rematch, and full power. I’ll also use Asuha’s skills as canon feats she can preform as they neither contradict anything and are arguably referenced in lore (specifically her passive skills, but I digress).

With all that set, allow me to paint the narrative that would be followed:

You just became the number one gambler, and Abyss suddenly appears before you. No breaking and entering, she just appeared. She congratulates you before accepting your challenge.

If you’re lucky like Asuha, you’d probably get a Straight Flush on your first round, but you watch as Abyss gets a Royal Flush. Maybe she’s lucky? So you start the second round and it ends the exact same way. You do one last round as she bets it all with another Royal Flush.

At this point, you’re familiar with the way Witches play and you call out Abyss’s blatant cheating. It’s impossible for someone to realistically get multiple Royal Flushes in a row without a means of cheating, but weirdly enough, Abyss explains that she did not cheat. At least, not in the traditional sense.

You see, Abyss is the most advanced computer alive. A perfect AI system as she explains, becoming one with the very city and posses the ability to control everything within. As she puts it, everything she plans will come to pass (second image). So Asuha had no means of actually winning especially when both she and the city they’re in are part of her system.

Plot happens and Asuha challenges Abyss once more on a space shuttle (just assume the character has some sort of means to go to space). The threat of the world on the line (it goes from zero to hundred in seconds honestly).

But Abyss is so confident in her abilities that she withholds her “cheating” and allows an honest one-on-one. As honest of a one-on-one as a perfect AI system can be with her innate ability to calculate the results as accurately as possible.

What does this mean exactly as AI can very well be applied differently in different media, ESPECIALLY with sentient AI?

As the third image implies, the results especially in a card game can be predicted. It won’t be 100%, but it can be calculated as accurately as possible.

Sadly, that doesn’t exactly answer the question, but I do believe I have an answer. First off, while the image isn’t here as it’s not the most important of sources needed, Asuha does imply that her calculations could be 99% accurate. The reason for this is because Asuha talks about how her calculations can be 99% repeatedly, but with gambling, luck will always be a factor and she’s willing to be on that 1% if it’s that impossible.

Actual evidence can be seen on images 4 and 7 (with 5 and 6 as possible help). We know that when Abyss access her full power, her calculations remain constant even then. That tells us that besides her additional power she can access, her analytical abilities allow her to calculate every possible gambling outcome. This is evident when she refers to her technology specifically for allowing her to predict every possible gambling outcome. If you’re questioning the validity of that sentence, I’ll get to it when we reach her full power, but just trust me for the sake of entertainment.

This means that Abyss in her base form without the ability to will whatever she desires into existence can calculate every possible outcome. The best comparison I can think of is a game of chess. Imagine going against a computer that knows every possible move. That’s what Asuha is pretty much doing except there’s at least a 1% chance of luck being involved.

If you’re wondering, you can’t get lucky during this match, Asuha is just an exception, as you’ll understand later.

But let’s say you’re hypothetically able to overcome that challenge… somehow. Abyss will begin to rage (understandably) and absorb all the data in the world. With one final play, she’ll begin cheating once more and will a Royal Flush. You could try and try again, hoping for a different outcome in the game, but it’s physically impossible for you to win.

Now here comes the insane part (as if everything before wasn’t insane): you, THE PLAYER, will need to go to the Home Screen which is now in ruins since you’ve lost. You’ll have to enter a secret menu and will need to turn off every one of her godlike abilities while actively turning her human (you could argue this is Mimi’s involvement and you don’t actually do anything, but there’s some issues with that). You’ll then begin the match right before you lose and suddenly her ability to will the cards doesn’t exist.

What’s the in-lore reason for this happening? You see, Asuha gambles more than anyone. She believed in that 1% and lived life thinking that everything in life is ultimately a gamble. Imagine Baku if Baku was actually insane. Asuha simply believed so much, always praying at a shrine before a match that she willed THE F\*CKING UNIVERSE or something to not only result in Abyss turning human, but everything Abyss willed to turn into a matter of luck.

Abyss calling out how that makes no sense as she’s a AI that needs logic (in which Asuha would counter by saying gambling is all about luck), would actually reveal that she’s able to access her full power thanks to her being human.

What does this grant her? The ability to see the future. Look at image 5 and 6 for reference, but we’re not talking about a hax that allows her to see the future, it’s just her calculations on a whole other level!

Image 5 and 6 happen right after one another so when Asuha thinks, Abyss responds even commenting that her thoughts are also accounted for in the calculation. As Abyss explains, Asuha’s very thoughts and actions cannot alter anything as every possible gambling outcome (now with Asuha’s exact thoughts and actions added) has been foreseen.

Ignoring how Asuha’s will just overcame even that and allowed her to actually gamble with Abyss (Abyss is able to recognize when Asuha has a change in her behavior even when she knows everything about her technically), it just paints the picture of what’s required to beat her.

Also, if it wasn’t obvious, Asuha is the second person I reference in being impossible to beat, but not because of a lack of SCD feats and instead because she just wills literal change in everything(?).

Just as a cherry on top, Asuha has sets of skills (both passive and non-passive) that can be deactivated when a character is on guard. If you haven’t thrown your opponent off then both your passive and non-passive skills won’t activate until you can freely use them.

Asuha’s skills include: Asuha’s perception (a passive skill that allows her to know what two cards someone has that vary from specific numbers for one card, whether they have a pair, if they have a higher card than her own, roughly if they have a pair or three of a kind, if they likely have a flush. In lore, we see that this also allows her to just know weaker opponents’ cards and is even described as her looking through someone’s soul), Asuha’s senses that tell her what cards will be dealt for any hand, her will to make the next card of her choosing, Asuha’s luck, Asuha’s sharp tongue that forces an opponent to be unable to fold, Asuha’s ability to amplify her luck, her will to lose less damage, any hand boost, Asuha’s morale/damage amp, likely her will to be dealt with a suit of her choosing, Asuha’s luck to improve her hand with the next card, and Asuha’s will or luck to be dealt a card with the same rank in her hand.

Remember that this is said to mean that Abyss can technically prevent any of these from happening, the most important being her unreadability unless caught off guard (which is unlikely to happen unless you preform everything needed and/or you have a reading ability that goes beyond Asuha’s).

TL;DR: Beating Abyss (and Asuha) is Impossible
