\[CHAPTER 5 PLAN\]

He made Monocrow write a false rule in the school rules: no entering the twin buildings at night time. He then made Iroha paint the door to the storage room with white paint to cover it up, and he made Iroha draw trick art on the connecting bridge to make it seem it disappeared. He then had Iroha kidnap Teruya and poison him to death through his rice, hiding the corpse inside the storage room, which just looked like a wall because of Iroha’s prior painting, causing the investigation to go cold and causing the investigators to leave the twin buildings. He also convinced the investigators beforehand that the bridge was bugged during the investigation, which they believed because of what happened on the Tower of Babel.

He then made Iroha make a premonition video, having her move to the east broadcasting room to create a stab wound on Teruya’s corpse, which was a deliberate red herring planted in relation to the broadcast she would make. He then had her erase the paint hiding the door to the storage room and he moved to the west broadcasting room. His plan was to make it a locked-room mystery. He locked the door automatically using the projector film, picked out a creepy film and slotted it into the projector, and covered it with yellow phosphorus.

He then hang the film through the sprinklers and stuck it to the door’s lock with tape. He then turned the projector on, allowing him to lock the door without being inside the room, as the film would pull the door’s lock. He then had Iroha draw Teruya on the wall, imitating Teruya’s appearance of lying dead in the east storage room, messing with the investigators’ depth perception.

He then had her use the computers to broadcast the footage to the whole city, and had her film herself holding a knife approaching the painting of Teruya. He then had her break one of the windows to make the investigators think the culprit escaped and to gain a glass shard. He then had her crush the shards to create glass powder, and he had her spread it on a piece of paper. He then had her tie the paper with glass powder on it onto the sprinklers, arranging it so that the side of the paper with glass powder on it faced the side of the film with yellow phosphorus on it. He did this to make it so that once the door was forced open, the film would roll back in, causing fire by friction between the two materials and activating the sprinklers.

He then had her turn on the projector and leave the room. Then he used the bridge to move her and him over to the east building. He and her then went down the elevator and out to the east area, meeting with Yoruko and creating an alibi for themselves. He used the bridge because the investigators thought it was out of order and thus impossible for the culprit to get to the east area. He and her then regrouped with everyone and helped break down the door to the west broadcasting room.

He anticipated the fake Teruya paint washing off from the sprinklers and thus placed a prop cloth near the wall to soak up all the paint, not leaving a trace. And thus he fooled everyone into believing it was a locked-room mystery. And even if it was solved, he wouldn’t be executed since he didn’t murder Teruya.

However it is revealed Iroha didn’t actually poison Teruya to death by poisoning his food because the poison Iroha was given was just salt it was actually him who poisoned Teruya to death through putting cyanide in his rice in advance then telling Iroha to put “poison” on the already poisoned food.

It is revealed in Chapter 6 that he carried out the poisoning in Chapter 5 because he knew Teruya was acting as a firewall. He had modified Teruya’s digital avatar’s source code into a firewall that would alert the Kisaragi Foundation to the Neo World Program upon Teruya’s death. He also trapped Teruya because, even if Teruya refused to eat the rice, he would have eventually starved to death. He then made everyone believe that Iroha was the culprit—including Iroha herself—so they would all be executed, preventing the Kisaragi Foundation from rescuing them. He also violated the rules to ensure that Yuki would not be executed among them. He also chose to kill Teruya in Chapter 5 instead of Rei because Teruya was less intelligent, making the plan easier to execute.

\[CHAPTER 6 PLAN\]

However, his plan A failed because the Neo World Program was rebooted by outside interference from the Kisaragi Foundation, so he initiated plan B. He had already predicted that this might happen, so he modified Yuki’s digital avatar source code to keep him trapped in the Neo World Program. He also pre-programmed a new building—the Final Judgement Tower—to appear only after the reboot began, serving as a failsafe.

He then planned for Sora to have Yuki returned to her through the graduation exam, but the exam was actually a secret personality override. Even if they chose to repeat the Neo World Program, it would simply loop, trapping them indefinitely until they eventually chose to graduate. However, this was a lie, as he had secretly modified the graduation exam system so that if they chose to graduate, their memories would be altered and they would be placed into their own ideal worlds instead of returning to the real world. He later revealed to Yuki—forcing him to remain in the virtual world—that if he ever left it, he would exist only as a brain, which he had managed to keep alive through a constant supply of nutrients. Since Yuki’s avatar was also brain-based, designed to simulate false sensations, he never suspected that he lacked a physical body within the Neo World Program. He revealed that he was actually planning to use Akane Taira’s body for the personality override, allowing him to escape into the real world after overriding Yuki’s personality to turn him into Utsuro and stealing his divine luck. For the final nail in the coffin, he programmed Sora—who was later revealed to be an algorithm that believed they were Akane—to safeguard him during the graduation exam by pressing *Graduate* unconditionally. He then drove the point home by showing Yuki a glimpse of Utopia through a revived fake Shinji, ensuring that Yuki would press *Graduate*. However, Sora ultimately defeated him by defying fate through her divine luck, as it was revealed that Utsuro no longer possessed divine luck—he had been revived from the moment of his death, after his divine luck had already passed on to its new host, Akane. As a result, Sora was able to save everyone and help them escape the Neo World Program—by, and I’m not even kidding, having Shobai guess the password to the Neo World Program Administration. It worked purely due to his own luck, not even her divine luck, allowing him to overwrite Yuki’s personality with Akane Taira’s body and activate the shutdown sequence.

He also forced the Kisaragi Foundation to enter the Neo World Program by locking the facility doors and telling them they would only retain the advantage of the forced shutdown if they entered. Additionally, he tampered with their radios so they could not call for help, leaving them no choice but to enter the Neo World Program. This was necessary to do the personality override.

He had priorly countered Kinjo’s plan to use Shobai as a spy for Void through physical currency by turning Shobai into a double agent using digital currency, allowing him to stay ahead of Kinjo’s strategy. He had also priorly used Shobai to kidnap the other Children of Utsuro and the Symbols of Hope, placing them into the Neo World Program after accessing networks worldwide to gather information about them. He then altered their memories using the synesthesia and assigned their old high school appearances as digital avatars, making them believe they were Hope’s Peak freshmen. Finally, he had priorly hired Shobai to murder his real-world self, since he was too incompetent, as demonstrated by the failure of his plan.
