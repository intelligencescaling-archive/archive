Kanade Otonokoji (SDRA2 Docs) VS The Protagonist (Tenet) in Strategy

The Protagonist Doc: [https://docs.google.com/document/d/1uuIf41aczG69PHssCZ0lNoxvGFc0I-cqrkbcRxryx7w/edit?usp=sharing](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1uuIf41aczG69PHssCZ0lNoxvGFc0I-cqrkbcRxryx7w/edit?usp=sharing)

**\[ Strategy Building \]: The Protagonist Mid-High Diff**
Reasons: If timeframe is important to you for this subcategory then you could definitely argue that Kanade takes it just by being able to formulate allat in a FAR shorter timeline (minutes/hours VS decades). But the overall building of the Bootstrap Strategy involving the creation of the Tenet organization, stealing the 241 before Sator decades prior to the Opera Siege to build plausible deniability and then deliberately causing the Opera Siege to happen all in order to set up the events needed for TP's past to take place sets up far more and far better objectives/goals and ultimately TP achieved a lot more in his building of his final plan than Kanade did with hers.

**\[ Strategy Efficiency \]: The Protagonist Mid Diff**
Reasons: The Opera Siege strategy alone was literally like 5 steps total and it managed to setup an inescapable timeloop and shielded TP's involvement via a complete logical contradiction that could never be uncovered (TP being simultaneously dead and the leader of Tenet at the same time). This is honestly TP's best strategy category and I regret saying that Strat Complexity is his best because him being able to achieve so much in so little moves is actually crazy

**\[ Strategy Logistics \]: The Protagonist Low Diff**
Reasons: The Tenet organization alone had it's own private paramilitary, SIS/MI6 agents, an entire shipping boat with it's own unique turnstile on it and several CIA agents within it's ranks all for the sole purpose of carrying out TP's mission. Yeah his strategy involved a lot more elements/variables and moving parts than Kanade's (which isn't necessarily a nerf for Kanade btw)

**\[ Strategy Shielding \]: The Protagonist No Diff**
Reasons: Not close at all. Kanade's strategy started to unravel just by pointing out that a pool sanitation didn't actually take place that day (I know it was an instance of Divine Luck but still, that's really all it takes). Meanwhile as previously mentioned, TP's strategy was shielded with a literal temporal paradox. I know people say this all the time with their favourite characters plans/strategies (ESPECIALLY Kanade fans) but it's serious this time: TP's strategy LITERALLY CANNOT be uncovered by people alive during the mission and even by people alive 200 years after the mission already succeeded (Posterity).

**\[ Strategy Forecasting \]: Kanade Otonokoji Mid-High Diff**
Reasons: Kanade gets this category by virtue of TP's strategy not exactly involving *true* forecasting since he already knew where his strategy would lead to by virtue of having gone through it himself in the past. Meanwhile Kanade was operating in a far more novel situation where she couldn't guarantee what would happen next thanks to a bootstrap paradox lol. The whole Iroha thing is definitely enough for her to take this.

**\[ Strategy Complexity \]: The Protagonist High Diffs**
Reasons: This one is tricky because as I said before, TP's strategy is better in terms of it's efficiency than it's complexity. But needless to say, it is still VERY complex and intricate. Creating the Tenet organization and all it's quirks, recruiting and involving Neil just so he can die at the end, the entire Sator baiting strategy by making the Opera Siege and Stalsk-12 happen on the same day, the Tallinn Sequence baiting and then all culminating in creating a Bootstrap Paradox so the plan can stay truly invincible forever should be enough for TP to take this. IMO, Kanade's strategy is also better for it's efficiency than for it's complexity but then again, I'm only getting this from docs.

**\[ Strategy Fortitude \]: The Protagonist Low Diff**
Reasons: Basically the same reason as Strategy Shielding. The fact that Divine Luck could effect her strategy AT ALL is enough for TP to take this here. Yes I know it took divine intervention to stop Kanade's strategy but I'm telling you now, no amount of Divine Luck could EVER get anyone close to uncovering Tenet, let alone TP and his plan. CTW L with a trillion instances of Divine Luck gets hardstuck at uncovering the Tenet org (he may be able to deduce that such an organization exists, but cannot go further than that because any and all members he locates and captures have been trained and tested to literally die before giving up any amount of information, even when faced with torture). That's just how fortified TP's strategy really is.

**\[ OVERALL: The Protagonist Mid Diffs \]**
