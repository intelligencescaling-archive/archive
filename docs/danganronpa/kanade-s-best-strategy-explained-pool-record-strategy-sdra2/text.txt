Kanade's Best Strategy Explained (Pool Record Strategy)

***Kanade’s Pool Record Strategy***

**A bit of Context:**

In SDRA2 Chapter 3, the map of the Island consisted of multiple structures in which one could enter and leave using a tablet. When entering, there would be a display of the structure creating a one-time-usage ID, which would stay coherent only for the entering “score” and the outgoing “score”.

**Objective Explanation:**

Anticipating the fact that Syobai would reach the following conclusion: the dismemberment of Setsuka’s body into the Ghost House was non-sense.

This was due to:

1- the fact that the dismemberment should have taken multiple hours even for professionals, but the time the culprit had was at most an hour and 45 minutes in total (from 17.57 up to 19.42) which would have made it impossible

2- no pieces of flesh were found in the Ghost House

3- They needed to turn the lights off, but with lights off it would have been impossible to dismember a body. And they couldn’t just drop them off the moment someone arrived, since it would have taken too much time.

Instead he obviously suggests a place more fit for a dismemberment, being the Pool, since “you could get rid of the flesh and blood just by drowning them in the drain”.

Kanade needed to find a way to dismiss this conclusion.

**Kanade’s Dismissal:**

Kanade used the Pool Records to demonstrate that it would have been impossible.

Pool Records:

8:44 DJ6SX7 IN

8:45 IS3JZ2 IN

9:22 DJ6SX7 OUT

9:54 IS3JZ2 OUT

12:54 CN9FU3 IN

17:24 NU3OD2 IN

17:25 CN9FU3 OUT

Now:

Step 1- the 8.44 and 8.45’s IDs both had “IN”s and “OUT”s records, meaning they both couldn’t have been victims.

Step 2- the only ID without an “OUT” record was the 17.24’s one, meaning she would have had to be Setsuka’s ID.

Step 3- but that would mean that the culprit needed to be 12.54’s ID, considering it was the only ID present at the Pool at such time.

Step 4- this is a problem because that 12.54’s ID left the Pool at 17.25, meaning the “culprit” would have had only a mere minute (from 17.24 to 17.25) to murder and dismember Setsuka’s Body, which is even more nonsense than the hour and 45 minutes Syobai was claiming to be impossible.

**Strategy Explanation:**

Kanade noticed that the rule about entering and outgoing with a tablet, didn’t specify whether the tablet used should have been the owner’s one or someone else’s.

She used this fact, alongside the control she had on her sister, in order to enact her strategy.

Kanade used Hibiki’s tablet to invite Setsuka to the Pool.

And so here we describe what initially happened:

8:44 DJ6SX7 IN Kanade with her own handbook

8:45 IS3JZ2 IN Setsuka with her own handbook

Once Setsuka entered, Kanade drugged her into being unconscious.

She then passed her tablet to Hibiki who was waiting outside of the structure, by throwing it above the fence of the Pool, so that it would appear as though the 8.44’s ID got out, while in reality it was another person joining the Pool.

Before Hibiki entered, she placed a warning sign of Pool Sanitation, so that people wouldn’t enter the Pool.

9:22 DJ6SX7 OUT Hibiki with Kanade's handbook

Once they performed the Double Culprit Strategy, Kanade gets out (to take the saw, used to dismember Setsuka’s body) using Setsuka’s handbook, making it seem as though no-one was left in the pool, while in reality Hibiki (her sister) and Setsuka’s dead body were still there.

9:54 IS3JZ2 OUT Kanade with Setsuka's handbook

Kanade then comes back with the saw, and they start dismembering the body from 12.54 up till 17.24 when they both start to leave (meaning 5 hours of time).

12:54 CN9FU3 IN Kanade enters with her own handbook

Finally, by having Hibiki leave with her Tablet and Kanade leaving with her own tablet, the end result is someone coming in at 17.24 without ever leaving.

17:24 NU3OD2 IN Hibiki goes out with her own handbook

17:25 CN9FU3 OUT Kanade goes out with her own handbook.

**This Strategy is the best of Kanade’s genius, but it still has lots of problems:**

1- the dismemberment would have made Kanade and Hibiki smell really bad, which would have compromised their concealment

2- what if someone joined the Pool earlier?

3- what if Syobai walked to the Pool? (he would most probably ask Monocrow about it, or at least doubt it if he had insights about it)

4- what if someone were to see them while joining and getting out the pool (they had 4+ occasions of being seen), especially considering the fact they brought a whole saw and a medikit to the Pool

5- what if someone interacted with Hibiki before this happened? (they would have noticed she lost control, that she didn’t act normally and that she was in a hurry)

6- the dismemberment would have done lots of noise

7- what if Setsuka woke up later?

But most importantly: what if someone were to see the Pool records?Because yes, the Pool records are observable from the OUTSIDE of the structure, meaning someone could have noticed “Oh, there is Pool Sanitation time, but people are still joining (and currently doing so) the Pool?”.

And Narrative POV wise, Kanade has the biggest anti-feat ever, since Kanade HERSELF told the others (with literally no strategic motivation at all) that Hibiki could lose control.

If she didn’t say this, Syobai wouldn’t have been capable of discovering this trick, since 2 people were needed.
