\[CHAPTER 3 PLAN AND CLASS TRIAL\]

She first wanted to remove Iroha Nijue’s potential alibi to make everyone believe she could have been the culprit. She did this by analyzing Iroha’s schedule in advance and exploiting it to her advantage, lacing her Coffee with sleeping pills from the infirmary so that once Iroha returned to her room, she would sleep all day.

After this, she began her main plan. She first entered the pool using Hibiki’s handbook and lured Setsuka Chiebukuro there using Hibiki’s handbook. She then electrocuted Setsuka, with a taser knocking her out, and poured blood on her using blood bags she had taken in advance from the infirmary to make it appear as though Setsuka was dead. Next, she had Hibiki enter the pool area using Setsuka’s handbook, making it look like Setsuka had exited alive.

Hibiki fell into a trance, and she took advantage of this to perform a synchronized double stabbing of Setsuka’s abdomen with Hibiki. Her strategy was that if they both stabbed at the same time, they would both be considered the killers, making the class trial easier for her to win. She would only need to convince everyone that there was a single killer; no matter who they voted for, she would win. She had, of course, anticipated who to involve by testing Hibiki’s synchronicity with her in advance, discovering an error margin of only 0.000001 seconds. She knew Hibiki would be perfect for the double-culprit strategy and she brought a first aid kit she had taken in advance from the infirmary just in case they were out of sync. Her next step came after realizing that the parade on the island took place at approximately 6:00 PM, allowing her to create an alibi. She retrieved her own handbook through Hibiki and exited through it, which was marked as an entry since it was her first time using her handbook.

She then set up a pool maintenance sign to prevent anyone else from entering the crime scene and left to collect dismemberment tools and returned, marking her entry as an exit. She dismembered Setsuka’s hands, placed them in a bag and gave them to Hibiki as padding by pinning it to her chest, and switched hair clips with her, sending Hibiki to the parade as herself, thereby giving herself an alibi in advance. Additionally, she tricked the others into believing that Setsuka was alive and still planning to attend the parade, though she would be arriving late, using Setsuka’s handbook. Also removing the pool maintenance sign.

This entire setup took place between 8:30 AM and 12:00 PM, completely concealing the actual time of the murder. She dismembered the body into ten parts, placing them into seven different bags and thoroughly cleaning the scene. She then transported the seven bags onto a monorail after leaving the pool through Setsuka’s handbook, which was marked as an entry, and hid the remaining two parts inside an animatronic at the storage facility.

She returned, re-entering through Hibiki’s handbook, which was marked as an exit, and then left again using Setsuka’s handbook, which was marked as an entry. She handed Hibiki Setsuka’s handbook, effectively removing herself from suspicion, and began transporting the body from the pool to the ghost house.

That prior Coffee plan, however, failed because Iroha didn’t drink the Coffee that day, so she quickly improvised another ploy. Using Setsuka’s handbook, she texted Iroha while posing as Setsuka, arranging to meet at exactly 6:00 PM in the Mirror Mansion. This ensured that Iroha would be stuck there due to the parade’s numerous animatronics blocking her path, once again removing her possible alibi.

After that She quickly moved on to the next phase. Using the monorail that operated between 5:00 PM and 6:00 PM, traveling in both directions with a five-minute stop at each station, she used her above average accuracy to throw the body bags into the ghost house chimney as the monorail passed overhead. Since she could only throw one bag per pass, she calculated, based on the monorail’s speed and stops, that she would have seven opportunities before 6:00 PM. That’s why she cut the body into precisely sized parts in advance to ensure they could fit through the chimney. By 6:00 PM, she had successfully gotten all the bags into the chimney.

She now had to conceal the timing of the Ghost House. She did this by assigning Hibiki to enter the castle shortly before 6:00 PM using her handbook, then had her leave using Kanade’s own handbook. This created a record showing that two different people had entered. She then tricked Iroha into coming to the haunted castle by posing as Setsuka once more through Setsuka’s handbook. She planned this so that Iroha would see Hibiki entering, linking the crime scene to a potential killer and witness. Finally, she retrieved Hibiki’s handbook and entered through it, marking her entry as her exit. In this way, she used her sister as a scapegoat while framing Iroha as a witness.

Upon entering the Ghost House, She cut the fuse box wires and killed the fire in the fireplace creating a blackout from within to clear the chimney passageway and make others not notice the discrepancies inside until the designated time she had planned she then retrieved the two parts inside the animatronic under the guise of searching for Setsuka with the others and entered the ghost house through Setsuka’s Handbook making it seem as though she entered and was killed retrieved the body parts from the chimney and hid them behind the curtains. She then used blood packets to create a trail leading to the Devil’s Room, making it appear as though Setsuka’s dismembered body had been dragged there placed the parts in the Ghost House, arranging each on a separate altar, reinforcing the illusion that the murder had taken place there and used blood packets to create a trail leading to the Devil’s Room, making it appear as though Setsuka’s dismembered body had been dragged there.

And finally, she not only framed her sister as the culprit but also removed yet another potential alibi. First, she left her sister’s hairpins in the Devil’s Room to increase suspicion when the evidence was found. Then, she priorly convinced Iroha that she would personally investigate a completely remote location while she herself went to investigate near the crime scene, closer to the pool. This way, if suspicion arose, she could claim she was far from the crime scene, while Iroha appeared to be alone near the actual crime scene—making it look as though Iroha was attempting to erase evidence.

During the Class trial she used all the fake evidence she had planted on her sister to frame her as the sole murderer. This included Hibiki’s hairpin being found in the Devil’s Room, the fact that Hibiki couldn’t remember anything, Hibiki having her hair down during the investigation, no one having seen Hibiki all day except Iroha, who saw her enter the Ghost House, before the body was found in there and the fact that Setsuka was placed on the altar, indicating she was precious to the culprit—a trait Hibiki did consider true. She manipulated everyone into believing Hibiki was the murderer, except Syobai, who tried to argue that Kanade was framing Hibiki due to Setsuka’s missing abdominal area, but Kanade shut him down with refutations.

She also used all the fake evidence she had planted on Iroha to accuse her of being the sole murderer. She pointed out Iroha’s lack of an alibi, that no one had seen her that day, that she had been at the crime scene before the investigation began, and that she was left-handed—matching Syobai’s theory that the culprit was left-handed. She manipulated everyone, including Syobai, into believing Iroha was the murderer, except for Mikado, who revealed that Iroha had been painting at the time of the murder. Syobai again tried to accuse Kanade after hearing Teruya’s and Sora’s accounts, but Kanade shut him down again by pointing out the flaws in his theory, forcing Syobai back into silence.

After Hibiki confirmed that Kanade had another ultimate talent, which would explain the body part overhead monorail tosses and her match against Hibiki in darts where she hit every target with perfect precision getting a perfect score and according to Hibiki she’s always gotten a perfect score in darts everyone was able to back her into a corner—but this was exactly what she wanted. If they only voted for her, they would all die, and she would win. She deliberately acted irritated and scared, pretending to be on the verge of exposure and even confessing, convincing them that she was the sole murderer.

At the end of the Class Trial before her and Hibiki’s execution she deliberately revealed Setsuka’s will and piqued everyone’s curiosity about the will without revealing its contents, then burned it with a lighter to sow psychological turmoil.

\[CHAPTER 1 CASE\]

Before the case started after Mikado had set up a dinner for everyone Kanade chose to eat Mikado’s food knowing he wouldn’t willingly poison himself while he would possibly poison the others if he was the mastermind.

During the investigation she was able to deduce that anesthetic had been used on Yuri based on the blood splatters on the ground, distinguishing that the specific anesthetic used would typically be for general anesthesia.

After reasoning this out, she went to the hospital and noticed a missing syringe.

She then concluded that the culprit had used this missing syringe because they would have been able to calculate the sedation time using the scale on the bottle compared to the other syringes.

During the class trial, she also reasoned that the culprit didn’t pull Yuri up directly. Instead, they used a hook attached to the life jacket, which was connected to them by a rope, utilizing the bell tower to pull the body up. She reasoned this because the culprit wouldn’t have been able to jump off the bell tower themselves due to it’s height—as the kinetic energy from the fall would have dislocated their shoulders upon landing.

She also pieced together the timeline of events: Yuri’s death occurred before the body discovery announcement, giving the culprit extra time to move around while Sora and Yuki were climbing the stairs. The culprit used the hook to descend instead of the stairs, eliminating the possibility that they wouldn’t have had enough time to commit the crime.

She reasoned that the culprit yanked the hook out of Yuri’s right calf while carrying Yoruko to the top, then reinserted it afterward, explaining the double wound.

She reasoned that the broken wires were set up as an alibi and that the culprit had jumped off the bell tower twice.

Finally, she concluded that the culprit used Yuri’s E-Handbook to prompt anyone who witnessed the murder scene to run up the bell tower to catch them.

She also reasoned and manipulated nearly everyone into believing Yoruko was the culprit for the first half of the trial due to her lack of an alibi.

She also set a trap for the culprit after the crime by having someone guard the actual crime scene, knowing the culprit would volunteer for such position. This led her to already suspect that Hajime was the culprit, since he volunteered first.

\[CHAPTER 2 CASE\]

She immediately realised the pair of icicles in the refrigerator was Kokoro’s dying message. Even though icicles could form normally if the fridge door wasn’t closed properly or if it was set to instant-freeze mode—which it was—She knew the output could be tampered with because Monocrow said the fridge could switch between freezing and defrosting depending on the power level.

This led her to conclude Kokoro had actually been frozen inside the refrigerator, since the dying message was there even though Kokoro’s body was found on the bed.

The liquid nitrogen in the room didn’t fool her either. She immediately reasoned it was a decoy because the tag angle was slightly off and there were no traces of liquid nitrogen anywhere. From this, she concluded: the liquid nitrogen was never used, and the refrigerator divider had been removed and put back.

She deciphered that Kokoro’s dying message formed a pattern representing each icicle as a girl, with the pierced icicle symbolizing Kokoro. Since the pierced icicle was the largest, the culprit had to be the tallest girl, Emma Magorobi.

\[HER PAST\]

She would repeatedly murder Hibiki’s acquaintances to psychologically break her, reducing her to an obedient puppet. This is known as the *Hibiki Puppet Project*. She began by killing Hibiki’s dog, Shiro—taking a kitchen knife, butchering him into pieces, and washing them down the drain to dispose of the evidence. She then pushed Hibiki’s best friend, Ayumi, down a flight of stairs, leaving her paralysed for the rest of her life. After that, she gave the middle-school teacher Hibiki had a crush on an extra dose of muscle relaxant and buried him outside the school to hide the evidence. She continued by killing the recording engineer, makeup coordinator, and manager for Hibiki’s band, turning the makeup coordinator into sushi to dispose of his remains and covering up the rest. Finally, she murdered her own parents and successfully hid the evidence, completely breaking Hibiki.

Despite being interrogated by Tsurugi Kinjo, the Ultimate Police Officer for these murders she managed to stay composed and act innocent convincing him that she wasn’t involved.

At the age of 3–4, she realized she was superior to Hibiki in everything and that was why Hibiki would pick on her.
