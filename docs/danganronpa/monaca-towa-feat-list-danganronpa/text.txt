*Monaca Towa IQ Feat List:*

She [faked](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CJ1j54cFdO8&t=420s) being disabled for years, to gain sympathy fooling not just her parents but also the Warriors of Hope [engineered](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QSMt1GJoIC8&t=1209s) the Monokuma Units, brainwashing helmets, obedience bracelets, and the Excalibur Airship. She replaced Miaya Gekkogahara with a perfect robot duplicate she created, exploited [Kotoko](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86w0lVSizuk&t=2073s) and [Nagisa’s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=djxiYJkiUQY&t=58m3s) trauma, [manipulated](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QSMt1GJoIC8&t=1580s) the group into killing adults, saw [through](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QSMt1GJoIC8&t=1109s) Junko’s manipulation and [understood ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QSMt1GJoIC8&t=19m53s)that Junko was only interested in her because Junko was only finding a way to mass produce killing machines, but played along out of [boredom](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QSMt1GJoIC8&t=1088s), drove the Future Foundation out of Towa City using Hit List Targets and the Monokuma Kids as hostages. [incited](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QSMt1GJoIC8&t=36m25s) the adults in Towa City to turn against children to the[ point](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QSMt1GJoIC8&t=41m39s) that they'd want children killed. Originally [lied](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QSMt1GJoIC8&t=1209s) to her father and brother stating that the Monokuma Units were for domestic helping and emergency aid helping. She [created ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LM3jBjrWclg&t=59m7s)a robust security system inside the Monokuma wristbands that even Taichi Fujisaki couldn't bypass with his programs. She [modified](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LM3jBjrWclg&t=1h4m25s) the security system programs Taichi created for the elevators, which even he struggled to hack into. She [orchestrated](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QSMt1GJoIC8&t=17m26s) and staged a fake group suicide with the Warriors of Hope as a prank, deceiving even Kotoko Utsugi into believing it was real. After the entire Towa Conglomerate became aware of her schemes, she [manipulated](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QSMt1GJoIC8&t=22m5s) and blackmailed the group, including her father, into continuously supporting Junko's cause by threatening to expose their actions should they go against her plans, [enticing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QSMt1GJoIC8&t=24m45s) them with promises of financial gain and prestige by orchestrating problems and then solving the problems themselves. She [planned](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QSMt1GJoIC8&t=36m22s) a war scenario between the Future Foundation and Towa City, should Komaru choose to destroy the controller and kill the kids. She also [prepared](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QSMt1GJoIC8&t=2786s) cameras so the footage of Komaru destroying the controller would be sent to the future foundation creating a rift between them \[47:21\]. [Surpassed](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CJ1j54cFdO8&t=6m45s) her father and her brother. She[ serves](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QSMt1GJoIC8&t=18m40s) as the chief executive of the robotics branch of Towa Conglomerate in Towa City A technology conglomerate that owns and controls Towa City, [leading ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LM3jBjrWclg&t=1h5m1s)the global technology industry in complexity and scale, [attracting ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LM3jBjrWclg&t=1h5m8s)engineers from around the world to test their skills in the city. her [Li’l Ultimate Homeroom](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q6gnVCRUozs&t=3143s) talent allows her to get assistance from anyone. She also hardly breaks composure even when her dad realises what she’s doing. She faked her crying at Masaru's Shrine. Predicted that Nagisa would be defeated by Komaru and Toko. Psychologically broke Komaru by showing her a video of her hanged parents trying to turn her into Junko Enoshima II. Predicted Kyoko’s imminent fate since her robot Miaya contained a PDA that showed every player's NG Code. And she faked Aoi's death as a prank.
