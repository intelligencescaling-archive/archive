An Analysis of Danganronpa Mauve Chapter 2 Blackened’s Scheme

Before i get into the plan itself. i have 2 primary reasons for analyzing this plan specifically. 1, this case was just really good and deserves some attention. 2, i already talked abt Satoshi who is from the same fangan. i want to go over his reasoning for this chapter as its his best reasoning feat and rlly good.

Impulsive Murder and Before
it’s important to note that this killing was an impulsive murder. the killer Tomoe was helping the victim Rikka in the library with investigating the books in there. Rikka prior would usually use a step ladder to get books do to her short height, this time the stepladder was taken by someone else. this resulted in Rikka getting hit in the nose by a falling book causing a nose bleed. and her glasses to fall off. when Rikka has her nose bleed he was wiping the blood on the ground with her sleeves bcuz of her coat size(her sleeves covered her hands) this nose bleed caused Tomoe to become a freak and kill Rikka bcuz he is a very disturbed individual. basically its mentioned that he had some deep urges that he had kept repressed for awhile. the motive in this case was a machine that gave advice suited for the individual. his very first advice was smth that went along the lines of “be true to your heart”. so when he saw Rikka’s nose bleed and saw it as ‘beautiful’ he killed her with a book. pretty big EM antifeat but this whole murder is basically crazy quick thinking after a silly murder.

before the murder happens a series of events transpire. Rikka finds a book abt crime scene forensics and teaches everyone finger printing for when a murder occurs. the day the murder does occur, the protagonist Miyuki and another character Erika who have been investigating the library alongside Rikka didnt sleep the whole note. the leader of the group recommended both to sleep and they slept through the entire murder, giving them no alibi. Last important note, Rikka had been keeping a roll of toilet paper with her due to a condition she had she dealt with allergies and excessive sneezing. with her allergies, being in the dusty library required the roll of toilet paper.

Crime Scene Set Up.
everything after this is where the impressive part comes in. after Tomoe kills Rikka, he uses the toilet paper Rikka had been keeping with her to wipe his finger prints off the book. Tomoe grabbed the toilet paper and made his way into the locker room and threw the toilet paper into the pool where the door just so happens to he broken. During 4:20 Tomoe sees Mikawa with the stepladder and uses this fact as another trap in this murder. around this time he hears Shoji in the hallway talking abt returning a specific book to the library. Shoji’s whole deal is that he is a streamer and uses a camera everywhere he goes. Tomoe gets the idea to go into the library whenever Shoji returns the book at 4:30 and replace the cover. Tomoe predicts the camera will he used for evidence so he uses this to fabricate a fake time of death so that he could have an alibi during this time. Tomoe roams around the school to meet up with anyone and he runs into Satoshi(the worst possible person 😭). this alibi would have him with Satoshi from 4:30 to 4:52. in the precaution his traps get figured out, he uses the remaining 8 minutes to accuse Satoshi. All of this set up done, his biggest goal in his plan is to specifically frame the Miyuki and his secondary framing being Erika. going into the trial, his biggest crutch in his plan is his “alibi trick” which was ofc the fabrication of an alibi and a false time of death, as well as the the logical manipulation involved. the trick itself is layered with 4 levels of contingency, those being the framing of Miyuki, Satoshi, Erika, and Mikawa. he might’ve been planning another with Shoji but based on the recording there was no way of framing him. simply put, the misdirection, fabrication, and concealment of the crime scene set up is already pretty impressive especially when taking account that the murder was from impulse.

During the Class Trial
Tomoe’s strategy going into the trial is to subtly direct the group into the framings he made. As i mentioned before there are 4 ppl being framed and within Tomoe’s plan there is a linear order. first to be accused is Erika which he anticipates will fail. then Mikawa framing, which works as another layer in the Miyuki framing. then the next being Miyuki which was his ultimate goal. lastly, Satoshi as his final concrete framing. this will only ‘work’ if the alibi trick gets discovered.

Starting with Erika framing. Tomoe makes several tactics to keep up his act. when the topic of touching the floor first makes ‘incorrect deductions’, such as the killer not having a reason to touch the floor. his plan hinges on the investigation of the floor so why does he do this? Tomoe is fully aware of how good his classmates are at reasoning and is fully aware that they can deduce the floor was definitely tampered with. eventually after this some typical dangan nonsense happens and they mention a beast being the killer bcuz they had to have been in all fours. using this opportunity Tomoe cleverly uses this to direct the class by mentioning that the ppl saying it was a beast has a point and then corrects ppl’s perception by bringing up the killers posture.   he then lets other ppl make deductions to lead to the ‘truth’. after the group deduces that the killer must have dropped smth then Tomoe gives the answer that it was glasses, mentioning Shinano pointing him to this conclusion. this officially putting Erika in the hot seat as the prime suspect. now some might be wondering “why are you yapping abt all this your just repeating events”, well these events are important to consider bcuz Tomoe anticipates the group finding out the culprit was leading them to a trap. without mentioning things specific moments this strategy becomes a nothing burger. this particular act gives clarity on Tomoe’s foresight, strat shielding, etc. its also important to note how this connects to Miyuki framing which i will get to. Tomoe anticipating the the counterargument of the straps, he mentions if the strap was cut. Tomoe knowing this argument sucks since the strap was metal, predicts someone else mentioning Rikka pulling the strap as she was being murdered, but the argument of students only getting 1 accessary proves her innocence.

Now let’s discuss Mikawa framing. the topic of the step latter being introduced by Miyaka. Tomoe at first defends Mikawa when he took the latter for the act mentioning the possibility of another despite knowing he’ll get debunked immediately when someone brings up Mikawa being the only one of two with finger prints on the latter the other being the victim. as Tomoe predicts Erika, Ego, and Miyuki deduce that it was an impulsive murder which eliminates the possibility of the latters finger prints being removed due to the straining time which incriminates Mikawa further. Then Tomoe accuses Mikawa with the information given however Ego deduces it cant be Mikawa bcuz he only has 1 glove and cleaning that one glove would take too much time.

Now, for the biggest part. Miyuki Framing. Tomoe anticipates both of the framing attempts being debunked and makes this trap. Tomoe predicts Miyuki and Ego will deduce the male locker room being used to dispose of the toilet paper. this will prove that the killer must be a male. during this point every other male would be accounted for instead of Miyuki. all other framings would be pinned perfectly on Miyuki framing as well, since Miyuki has been making a lot of the deductions(since he’s the protag duh). the framing itself is almost perfect, Miyuki has no evidence of his innocence and he himself couldnt think of counter arguments for awhile. however, what Tomoe didnt know was that Miyuki has severe hydrophobia. it was so bad he had seizures at a glance of deep water. there was also 2 witness accounts. Tomoe forms several counter arguments with were pretty good. he brings up the possibility of the door being at a glance yet, Satoshi brings up he had his seizure at a glance(after Satoshi was the reason he had that seizure).

Lastly, for Satoshi framing. this part of his plan isnt rlly much to note, it was basically just a desperate attempt. basically Tomoe used short time frame Satoshi was gone to accuse him of hiding evidence for a murder or commit the murder itself but this got debunked rather quickly by Satoshi.

After the plan itself gets dismantled Tomoe rlly has nothing left. he makes a few good counter arguments but nothing worth noting. he basically just stubbornly denied killing Rikka.

This plan, and the strategies involved shows a great level of deception, foresight, planning, strategy, and thinking. it is one of the smartest plans ive seen from a fangan so far and makes me more hyped for the series.
