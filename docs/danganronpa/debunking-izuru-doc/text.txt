Izuru's situation at birth shares similarities with individuals affected by mental disorders like dementia and Alzheimer's. Despite the prominent memory deficits, motor skills tend to remain relatively intact. Individuals may retain learned skills, even without conscious recollection of the learning process. This suggests that Izuru may not have needed to acquire all his skills immediately upon awakening but could have already possessed them, similar to the retention of procedural memories in individuals with cognitive impairments.
