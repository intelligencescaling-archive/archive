Friend case negs

Reasoning for why it’s not top tier material:

First of all, the choice of conviction was really bad. Kanade chose two of *the dumbest* people to frame, while she herself acted like a complete maniac during the trial. 60% of the reason why Sora and Syobai were able to discombobulate the case was because bubblehead girl could lit never in a million years do something like that. Hell, even if she was the ultimate actor, I still doubt she could pull off any of the things she would have needed to do given her physical stature and limitations.

Second of all, Kanade revealed way too much into, and she didn’t keep Hibiki in check. Them being synchronized down to the 0.000001 second already made me suspicious of the possibility of a double murder the second they revealed it. Second of all, Kanade being good at all games she played would definitely alert me. The latter isn’t a huge anti feat as Hibiki is a moron but the former is something I would never reveal to anyone if I was in such a game and academy. And if I did, I probably wouldn’t go for that murder style.

Which brings me to my biggest problem of this case. That is, that you don’t actually have to solve it or resolve all the contradictions to get it right. Even if you didn’t find all the evidence needed or somehow couldn’t deduce all the traps and methods, it still wouldnt matter. Iroha was already proven to not be the culprit without a shadow of a doubt, and if it’s not her the  it **has** to be Kanade due to her being the only one to know of Hibiki’s state. Via just unveiling the Iroha framing, you already reduce the number of possibilities down to three: Kanade, Hibiki, Kanade/Hibiki.

From there, you can easily remember the talent both had and guess it from there fairly easily. Case closed.

But even if u don’t want to simply guess (btw guessing is totally valid, it’s not as if u *need* to resolve everything, as long as the theory mostly matches up with reality, the guess would still be very good), you can still deduce that its most likely Kanade or Hibiki/Kanade due to how stupid Hibiki is (this is ofc without knowing that Kanade can control Hibiki). From their pov, Kanade would never let Hibiki murder by herself, very unlikely at least. And the chances of Hibiki doing it by herself are obviously 0 due to all the previous points (body moving for example) as well as her general profile.

Just from these simple notions, you can confidently say that either Kanade was creative and used her ability to double murder or she murdered herself.

It’s not as if Crow won’t share the info that a double murder can’t happen so the notion that Kanade’s method is fullproof also collapses. Crow literally told everyone that a double murder is possible. Kanade should have known that Crow would tell them based on his previous actions, but I digress.

So at worst its a 50/50 between a double murder and it being Kanade, and at best its just Kanade/Hibiki double murder which you can deduce from their ability and the other evidence (although, having to hide the torso is already a pretty good indicator that it’s probably a double murder once you look past the Iraho framing. Hell…Kanade lit messed up herself by talking about not using the bottle lol. Beyond birthday level anti feat).

The best cases shouldn’t have the murder be a suspect in the first place.

This case is too prone to simply guessing the right answer based on some fairly reasonable guesswork.

If I was Kanade, I would make Hibiki murder Setsuko and still do everything else she did. The case becomes harder even from that little change.
