Monaca Towa Intelligence feats:

**“When Monaca talks, everyone freezes... When Monaca smiles, everyone stops smiling... For Monaca, the outsider, the eyesore, to survive in that house, she did what she had to do.” – Monaca Towa**

She faked being disabled, using only an electric wheelchair, and acting after suffering abuse from her brother and father, gradually convincing them she was truly disabled and deliberately gaining their pity—something she couldn’t achieve before because her father and brother had been jealous and abusive—turning her past abuse into emotional leverage, not just over her family, but over everyone else around her as well.

\[PS (Problem Solving) PP (Planning Practicability) IDM (Indirect Manipulation) ST (Strategic Thinking) LGT (Logical Thinking) CT (Creative Thinking) LT (Linear Thinking) EU (Emotional Understanding) AS (Acting Skills) and PM (Psychological Manipulation)\].

She gave Komaru a controller that, if broken, would free the adults but kill the children, or if she did nothing, would result in the killing game continuing. Either option was deliberately disastrous, as she designed this trap to instill despair in Komaru and turn her into the second Junko Enoshima. Additionally, either outcome would be broadcasted to the Future Foundation, creating a rift between Komaru and them, which would further amplify the despair.

\[TS (Trap Setting) PP (Planning Practicability) PC (Planning Convergence) ST (Strategic Thinking) CT (Creative Thinking) IDM (Indirect Manipulation) IM (Individual Manipulation) and PM (Psychological Manipulation)\].

She was the mastermind behind the Demon Hunting game, a city-wide genocide game where the Warriors of Hope, manipulated by her, used Monokuma units to murder the adults they referred to as Demons in Towa City as retaliation for Junko Enoshima’s murder. The manipulation was mostly carried out using Junko’s likeness; she knew the Warriors of Hope liked her, so she made them believe the adults had killed her, knowing they would accept it due to their past trauma with adults.

\[DIM (Direct Manipulation) MM (Mass Manipulation) EU (Emotional Understanding) EM (Emotional Manipulation) ST (Strategic Thinking) LGT (Logical Thinking) CT (Creative Thinking) and LT (Linear Thinking)\].

She made the Towa Group’s robotics branch mass-produce Monokuma units under the lie that they were for domestic help and disaster-relief efforts. When her father discovered the truth and tried to stop her, she coerced him into letting it continue by telling him that if he said a word, everyone would learn about his involvement with the Ultimate Despair. She argued that it was better to keep going and make it appear as though they were fighting against the Ultimate Despair while actually helping her, simultaneously inciting the branch with promises of financial gain and prestige by creating problems and then providing the solutions, making them look like heroes while secretly playing both sides.

\[DIM (Direct Manipulation) IDM (Indirect Manipulation) MM (Mass Manipulation) PM (Psychological Manipulation) PS (Problem Solving) PP (Planning Practicability) PC (Planning Convergence) ST (Strategic Thinking) LGT (Logical Thinking) CT (Creative Thinking) and LT (Linear Thinking)\].

She designed and engineered an animatronic replica of Miaya Gekkogahara, then murdered the real Miaya Gekkogahara and swapped him with the animatronic to infiltrate the Future Foundation while controlling the robot.

\[PS (Problem Solving) VSI (Visual Spatial Index) IDM (Indirect Manipulation) PM (Psychological Manipulation) ST (Strategic Thinking) LGT (Logical Thinking) CT (Creative Thinking) and LT (Linear Thinking)\].

She saw through Junko Enoshima’s manipulation, reasoning that Junko was only interested in her because she was the head of the robotics branch and Junko was looking for a way to mass-produce machines, so that role worked perfectly she also knew she merely went along with Junko because of her own boredom.

\[EU (Emotional Understanding) LR (Logical Reasoning) LGT (Logical Thinking) LT (Linear Thinking) ER (Emotional Realisation) and SA (Self Awareness)\].

She predicted Kyoko Kirigiri's death since her animatronic Miaya contained a PDA that had every player's Never Again Code and showed she had violated hers.

\[PER (Perceptual Reasoning) LGT (Logical Thinking) LT (Linear Thinking) and Foresight\].
