Celeste feat list

Celestia Ludenberg intelligence feats:

**“A lack of adaptability...is a lack of survivability. Survival is not based on who is the strongest or the smartest. It comes down to who can adapt.” – Taeko Yasuhiro**

\[CHAPTER 3 PLAN\]

Her plan began after hearing the new motive: 10 million in cash for whoever graduated from Hope's Peak Academy. Wishing to achieve her dream of owning a large European castle with many servants, she saw the perfect opportunity to win the money and escape the school.

After seeing Hifumi and Kiyotaka fight over Alter Ego, she used this to her advantage, luring Hifumi Yamada to her dormitory in the night after hiding away Alter Ego in a locker room and lying that Kiyotaka sexually assaulted her and stole Alter Ego through her by the usage of blackmail with the photos he had taken during the SA and was planning a murder against him so he could keep Alter Ego for himself and escape the school. This caused Hifumi to become her accomplice.

She then convinced Yasuhiro Hagakure into coming to the Rec Room at 1 AM and had Hifumi force him unconscious with chloroform, and then helped stuff him into the Robo Justice suit she had Hifumi make in order to cover a person's face and body.

She then had Hifumi position himself to appear attacked by the Robo Justice suit while she took pictures on a digital camera of the assault, as well as faked being attacked alongside Hifumi with Justice Hammer one. This was done to create a first fake sequence and later evidence that puts suspicion on Yasuhiro. When they were done with him, they shoved him, still in the Robo Justice suit, into the pool room locker.

Then at 6 AM, she lured Kiyotaka to the Equipment Room and had Hifumi kill Kiyotaka with Justice Hammer four. This was done to create a fake last sequence committed by the Robo Justice suit.

Then, using the Library, she created the fake second sequence by having Hifumi deliberately injure himself with Justice Hammer 2 and scream. This resulted in him being moved to the Nurse’s Office. While they left him alone to continue the investigation, she then had Hifumi fake being killed using a blood packet from the refrigerator and Justice Hammer three, luring them back with another scream. Then, when they left him alone again, she made him escape, luring them back to the Nurse’s Office while he made it to the Equipment Room.

She made him wrap Kiyotaka’s body in tarp and use the dolly to move it to the Repository. And then killed Hifumi using a regular hammer in the Repository to get rid of the one person who could betray her.

This plan showcases \[NCPS (Novel Complex Problem Solving) STP (Short Term Planning) PM (Psychological Manipulation) EM (Emotional Manipulation) MM (Mass Manipulation) AT (Analytical Thinking) IM (Individual Manipulation) DIM (Direct Manipulation) IDM (Indirect Manipulation) PC (Planning Convergence) DP (Detailed Planning) PP (Planning Practicability) LT (Linear Thinking) PS (Problem Solving) FRI (Fluid Reasoning Index) LGT (Logical Thinking) CT (Creative Thinking) ST (Strategic Thinking) Foresight and Deception\].

\[CLASS TRIALS\]

During Hifumi’s and Kiyotaka’s class trial, she uses all the evidence she and Hifumi had planted to convince her fellow classmates that Yasuhiro Hagakure was the culprit and she was innocent through her acting she also temporarily managed her composure while being accused.

This feat showcases \[LM (Logical Manipulation) LR (Logical Reasoning) LGT (Logical Thinking) IDM (Indirect Manipulation) AS (Acting Skills) LT (Linear Thinking) PS (Problem Solving) FRI (Fluid Reasoning Index) and EM (Emotional Management)\].

Before her execution, having foreseen the possibility of losing, she kept the key to the locker room hiding Alter Ego on her she then gave it away to Kyoko so she could use Alter Ego to try and escape.

This feat showcases \[Foresight\].

She recalled her exact last meeting with Chihiro Fujisaki which was a day ago in detail, even noting the location, time, and the tracksuit Chihiro had attempted to hide.

This feat showcases \[Memory and Observation\].

\[OTHER INFORMATION\]

She created the fake persona, Celestia Ludenberg, temporarily deceiving everyone into believing she was this character. Obsessed with European royalty, she crafted a false backstory for the persona, claiming her father was French nobility and her mother was a German family of musicians. She even altered her clothes, body language, and accent, to embody a noblewoman. It’s also possible she faked her Ultimate Talent, though that remains speculation. As for why she crafted the persona of Celestia Ludenberg, it was her way of escaping the reality of her loser life, creating a sense of superiority and control.

This feat showcases \[PM (Psychological Manipulation) MM (Mass Manipulation) DIM (Direct Manipulation) AS (Acting Skills) IDM (Indirect Manipulation) and CT (Creative Thinking)\].

She reasoned that the mastermind must be someone with immense power to have created Monokuma and taken over Hope's Peak Academy.

This feat showcases \[LR (Logical Reasoning) LGT (Logical Thinking) and LT (Linear Thinking)\].

She quickly adapted to the killing game environment.

This feat showcases \[Adversity Capacity\].

She supposedly won a shogi game against an unusual maid without knowing how to play—a reference to 8divers. It is unknown whether she actually did or is just lying through the reference.

This feat showcases \[LA (Learning Ability) Intuition and LC (Learning Capability)\].
