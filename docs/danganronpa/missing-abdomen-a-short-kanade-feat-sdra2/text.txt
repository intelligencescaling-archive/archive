Missing Abdomen - A Short Kanade Feat

This post will cover a short tactic that Kanade used in chapter 3. Specifically, her removal of the abdomen from Setsuka's corpse.

In case anyone is unfamiliar with this case, the surface level reason that she did this was to hide the stab wounds. Kanade and Hibiki performed a double murder by stabbing Setsuka simultaneously in the abdomen. The main reason that this was necessary was that Kanade and Hibiki are left and right handed respectively, and this would allow a skilled detective to determine that the two stab wounds came from two different killers. Her removal and discarding of the abdomen conceals this entirely. However, after closer analysis she also had several other reasons and intertwining tactics to do this.

This tactic ties directly into the Iroha framing; from the detective's position, it is clear that the culprit had a reason to remove the abdomen. Kanade correctly predicts that Shobai will postulate the reason as the killer hiding their dominant hand. Why would a killer need to hide their dominant hand? The obvious reason is that there are far fewer left handed people than right handed people. In the SDRA2 cast, only Iroha and Kanade are left handed, immediately making them potential culprits, assuming the previous reasoning is correct.

https://i.redd.it/1p9j1fhhm2yh1.png

As we know, Kanade goes on to framing Iroha later in the trial after Shobai makes this deduction, using this piece of evidence as a very strong and convincing premise to pile onto what was already a good plan.

https://i.redd.it/yz6huzoqm2yh1.png

Now, what about the fact that she made herself a suspect? This was also entirely planned. Kanade even leaks other details that put her on Shobai's radar, namely mentioning how she is aware of Hibiki's trauma induced memory loss. Kanade had purposely set things up so that it is extremely difficult to line up the events that would result in her involvement to the murder. In other words, if she was accused it would be almost impossible to pinpoint how she was able to do it. As predicted, Shobai's reasoning falls short. Shortly after, she is able to use basically the exact same premises that Shobai outlined to successfully incriminate the other suspect created by the abdomen's removal: Iroha.

https://i.redd.it/94l3g933n2yh1.png

Probably the funniest part about this feat is the fact that while it does arouse suspicious towards certain people effectively, it isn't even close to definitive evidence. There are countless other reasons for the abdomen's removal. This is important because it means that if Kanade's plan to frame Iroha fails, the removal doesn't necessarily incriminate her either.

Even if Shobai never accused Kanade for some reason, this still leaves no evidence pointing towards her.

https://i.redd.it/gj1j4bern2yh1.png

Just for the extra slight wank, we can also detail how this ties into the logistics of the murder plan. Deep into the plan, Hibiki is required to sprint a medium distance while carrying the torso in as little time as possible.

Doing some quick google searches we can attain some useful info (I hope I don't get put on a registry for this). If Hibiki were to carry her entire torso (thorax+abdomen) then that would be around 29% of Setsuka's bodyweight, which comes out to around 16kgs or 35lbs. Without the torso, this is reduced to 17% of Setsuka's bodyweight which is approximately 9kgs or 21lbs. In other words, Hibiki would likely be able to sprint much faster than with the entire torso intact. The reasons for her speed needing to be that high are a bit beyond the scope of this post, in short Kanade needed this time interval to be as short as possible in order to clear herself of suspicion.

https://i.redd.it/cwfdlxsjo2yh1.png

In SCD terms, this fulfills a decent amount of deception categories on its own. Uses EU/EP/ToM/logical manipulation to utilize Shobai's knowledge and background against him. Strategy logistics and foresight for the last part.

Even knowing to remove the abdomen proves she knew the effects of this action on Shobai's inference, alongside purposely choosing Iroha as her framing target. If she didn't consider this, she could've picked Nikei or Teruya to frame because of their hat/baggy clothes.
