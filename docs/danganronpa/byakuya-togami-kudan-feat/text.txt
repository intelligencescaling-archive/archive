Context - contains some spoilers

The Danganronpa: Togami recounts the story of the World Domination Proclamation, a global event in which Byakuya Togami, the Ultimate Affluent Progeny, and his older sister, Shinobu Togami, otherwise known as Blue Ink, face the Ultimate Despair before the eventual Tragedy.
Shinobu, the Ultimate Secretary and student of one of the 77th class batches, serves as the “Blue Ink” who writes “Journey Under the Midnight Sun”, a biography of Byakuya Togami, and is the protagonist of the series.
The World Domination Proclamation kicks off with a speech made by the Ultimate Imposter acting as Byakuya in which he announces that he will take over the world if neither one of these two conditions are met: either kill Byakuya or find the “pitiful cattle” hidden in the world. This “pitiful cattle”, known as the Kudan, is the secret to the Togami family’s prosperity. Thanks to its high analytical prowess which allows it to predict future events with certainty akin to what seemed to be prophesying, it was considered as a major obstacle in bringing forth the Ultimate Despair’s plans.
Being the big secret the Togami family held, many organisations and conglomerates tried to locate it through Byakuya, who knew its location, as only the top of the family knew about it, Byakuya being the new heir.
Thus, one of the main objectives of the World Domination Proclamation included getting rid of the Kudan.

As the event carried on, Shinobu, with a fake eye serving as a “library” of information directly affecting her field of vision with augmented reality named Borges, started to notice discrepancies between what she was seeing and her memories, which led her to learn the truth that Borges had been feeding her false information overlaying with reality itself, causing her to live in between both fiction and reality.
We get to learn that Borges is in fact carrying the K2K system, another name for the Bible Plan or the Story AI, one of the two experiments performed by Hope’s Peak to propagate hope in the world (the other being the Sage Plan, otherwise known as the Izuru Kamukura Project). The K2K system is a sort of artificial intelligence whose role is to write stories to make the reader feel hope just by consuming said stories.
However, after several complications notably involving inducing one of the researchers to go wild and commit murders, K, a Hope’s Peak alumnus and participant of the research project, took the Story AI and ultimately hid it within the Togami Corporation, collaborating with them to keep it in secrecy.
Hence, it was inserted into an artificial eye, later known to be the Borges in Shinobu.

During the finale of the event, in which Byakuya and the Imposter directly meet and confront each other, the latter, representing the Ultimate Despair under Junko Enoshima’s planning, drops the truth that the Kudan was Shinobu all along, that she was the secret to the Togami family’s prosperity. This in turn causes her to learn of all the hidden revelations: that her entire life was a lie, that all of her memories of being Shinobu Togami were just fabrications by the K2K system. These memories include the falsified rendition of the Legacy Crowned Championship, her memories of her brothers Kazuya and Suzuhiko, and so on. Essentially, everything she ever remembered, such as her meeting with Byakuya Togami and her deep affection towards him were nothing but machinations and lies of the K2K system, feeding her piles of unreality into her brain. This caused her to feel immense despair, to have both an existential and identity crisis, which is quite logical after learning that she in fact never existed.
But at the brink of despair, one voice managed to keep her going on. Byakuya Togami told her to confront this truth and to create her own meaning, to follow him, and to trust him as he will guide her.
Subsequently, Blue Ink started to regain focus of herself, and even after the near perfect gaslighting of the Imposter, she continued to stand up against these circumstances to finally achieve authenticity, to create her own identity.

Byakuya, given the choice of either conquering the world (which would lead to Blue Ink’s death) or not conquering the world (which would lead to both Blue Ink and Byakuya losing specific parts of their memories including them knowing each other and some of Byakuya’s talents), chooses the latter option, deciding not to conquer the world, and to let Blue Ink live on.

Abstract - short summary

Thus what is the Kudan feat?

The Kudan feat is Byakuya’s feat to create Shinobu’s personality  with the K2K system. This includes selecting a specific personality, involving memories, likings, and such, which ultimately would lead to Shinobu’s resistance to high levels of gaslighting or emotional pain upon the revelation of the truth. It is also a method of sealing the Kudan, whose abilities, according to Byakuya himself, are very destructive.
It specifically entails emotional understanding and facilitation (interpersonal management), as it required Byakuya to have a deep grasp of the human mind to create a persona able to defy such truths which render the individual’s existence into nothing, non-existence and worthlessness, and to be able to himself be able to trigger the individual’s motivation to stand up against the harsh reality.
Also supposing the fact that Blue Ink’s persona is based on the actual Shinobu’s, we can assert that it required the persona to highly resemble that of Shinobu herself, misdirecting those who knew the original one as to not create confusion nor suspicion.
Ultimately, it is implied that Byakuya always planned on preparing for the revelation of the truth to Blue Ink, which is why he shaped her in such a specific way.

Therefore this feat also contains some planning aspects, albeit extremely minor, and some positive individual manipulation.

The Kudan feat - explanation

The feat entails Byakuya creating the Blue Ink persona, which mainly involves her memories and personality, with the help of the K2K system, in order to both seal and “free” the Kudan, someone he knew would serve as a target for the interested, as he believes that its abilities go beyond the physical level, being more dangerous than even nuclear weapons, which might bring great adversity to him or any other.

There are quite a bit of layers to this.
First of all, he chose to erase her memories of being the Kudan, perhaps requiring some knowledge in neurology. This obviously laid the groundwork for introducing a new persona. This persona is filled with specific memories and personality to fabricate a false history, that of a loving older sister to Byakuya and the Ultimate Secretary at Hope’s Peak, writing his biography of “Journey Under the Midnight Sun”. This admiration, one of her viewing him as a god, serves as the underlying method and potential contingency that Byakuya will use to keep Blue Ink sane after the revelation, which will be expanded on later on. He also erased the memories so that there was no risk of her leaking her identity as the Kudan. Having her write  his biography, Byakuya gives her a reason to stay close to him, so that he can keep tabs on her state. Due to the fact that the Story AI went rogue and caused a researcher to commit murder, it was possible that it could malfunction within Blue Ink, and it would certainly bring havoc.
He equally needed to shape the persona consistently with the original Shinobu who existed, so as to not create suspicion amongst those who knew her.

And on a more existential note, if Shinobu knew she was the Kudan, it could develop major problems, again, as someone who would be the target of interest. This is another reason as to why Byakuya erased the memories.

Knowing that the K2K system would malfunction, the personality he inserted was perfect to fend off the complications that would arise. This is seen with the finale of the World Domination Proclamation where the Ultimate Imposter, following the plans of Junko Enoshima, attempted to gaslight Shinobu by revealing the truth. This caused her to undergo deep psychological distress.

Do keep in mind that the Imposter is acting according to the script of Junko Enoshima’s plans, and with the Imposter’s near perfect acting (obviously, he wouldn’t be able to replicate Junko’s style identically), it’s almost as if Junko herself is the one doing it. As such, the method does stem from Junko herself, someone whose manipulation tactics are undoubtedly monstrous.

Hence, the reason why this feat demonstrates extensive emotional intelligence is because Byakuya was able to figure out a way for someone, even after sinking in great despair, even after learning that they ultimately don’t exist, even after individuals like Junko Enoshima try to bring them down, to still trust hope, to still believe in something greater, to still live on and seize the day of one’s own life, against absurd situations where loss is inevitable.

One question may remain in your mind, why would Byakuya ever come up with this initially?
We know that the original Byakuya was quite virtuous deep down. He’s someone willing to guide others towards hope even if he is humiliated. He’s unwilling to fall into despair. Yet he hides his intentions so that people can also act by themselves, stay true to themselves. He’s merely a guide, and wants others to be responsible in creating their own purposes.
This is why he would want to “free” the Kudan. It is his objective to turn someone who people see as a monster into someone who people see as a human being.
