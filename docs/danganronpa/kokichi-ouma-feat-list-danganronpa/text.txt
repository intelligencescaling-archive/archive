Kokichi Ouma feat list

*“Why do you guys hate lies so much? There's only one truth, but endless possibilities for lies, y'know? And some of them are only white lies, or lies to be kind to people... If you deny all of that just because it's a lie... Then that means you guys are just terrible at being lied to! Seriously, the worst!”* — *Kokichi Ouma*

(Say hello to my final Goat 🐐)

Kokichi Ouma Intelligence feats:

Reasoning: Helped prove Kaede was guilty for Rantaro’s murder. After seeing Maki desperately shoo people away from her research lab and choke him after he called out how she may have a true identity she’s been hiding he knew she was the Ultimate Assassin. Correctly deduced that the school was specifically made for all of its participants due to Angie's lab having all her favorite sculpting and art supplies.

Prediction: He had predicted Miu's plan to use the virtual world simulator in order to kill him.

Manipulation: Manipulated Gonta into kidnapping all the students due to a lie. Manipulated Gonta into believing that being dead without knowing anything is the best option for everyone and that he should Mercy Kill Miu. Manipulated Shuichi into giving him the Exisal hangar's alarm remote back using Kaito's own safety.

Deception: During his Free Time Event claims he became the supreme leader by murdering his parents and usurping their role. Then claims he lied, and that the one he killed was his older brother. He then claims he lied again and that the real reason is a secret. Lied about being the mastermind of the Killing School Semester and that he was the leader of the cult bent on stopping the Gofer Project. He also lied about being the one who let Monokuma enter the spaceship. Created a kind lie in order to make the group consolidate once again after failing to escape the killing game through the manhole cover. He lied to Gonta that everyone in the academy hated bugs. Made up a lie at how a curse could've killed Tenko. Pretended to not mind the prospect of a Killing Game. Shedded fake tears at Miu’s belittling. Faked his own death so convincingly that even Shuichi and Maki believed in it. Lied about being the culprit that killed Angie to draw out the real culprit. Withheld his knowledge that the earth had been destroyed by meteorites from everyone by lying that he couldn't find where the card key went to. Rejoined the group, after executing his Miu murder plan pretending to know nothing. Lied about his meeting with Miu on the roof. Claimed that he was faking his own tears and mocked Gonta post-mortem, calling him an idiot and saying that he died a meaningless death along with Miu he also claimed that he himself inflicted pain and suffering on the group for no other reason than the thrill of it all of which were lies. Pretended to be the Mastermind by controlling the Exisals through Miu's universal remote control. Pretended to drink the antidote after being poisoned.

Strategy: All of his lies were done in order to get everyone to fall into so much despair that they would not continue the Killing Game. Made Gonta kidnap everyone by lying about them hating bugs so he could go snatch their motive videos.

Planning: After the first trial he would begin to devise of a plan to get everyone to believe he was the mastermind. To begin this plan, he would leave a subtle message in the courtyard Horse A, which he would later fill in with more letters as the killing game continued. Planned the murder of Miu it went as follows Kokichi meets up with Miu on the Roof Gonta ambushes from behind with a roll of toilet paper strangling her avatar causing her real body to die from shock had Gonta place Miu’s dead avatar on a lattice from the storage room and slid her off the roof, sending her through the wall and crashing into the chapel due to the world’s looping effect. The loud crash alerted everyone. He then told Gonta to climb down using toilet paper and return it to the bathroom he then rejoined the group, pretending to know nothing. Finally after his encounter with Kaito he finished the message in the courtyard “This world is mine, Kokichi Oma" Created yet another final plan with Kaito faking his death as Kaito they did this by recording a false “time of death” then swapped places so Kaito could crush Kokichi disguised as Kaito under a press, leaving Kaito’s own jacket sleeve as a fake clue. Kaito then hid in a red Exisal and impersonated Kokichi during the trial using a voice changer and a guidebook Kokichi wrote for him to finally put an end to the killing game due to Breaking the Game's Premise by making the Mastermind think Kokichi was the killer.

Other: Saw through Kaede's lie, due to being a liar himself but left it up to everyone else to see if they would choose to believe in her. He found Maki suspicious for not allowing anyone into her Research Lab and while he wouldn’t investigate he did correctly theorise that Maki was hiding her true identity from the rest of the group because of it. He correctly hypothesised that Kirumi had seen her own motive video. He saw through Kirumi's charade. He Didn’t buy into Angie's nonsense. He noticed Kaito’s punch was slower than usual and theorised that he was hiding something from everyone because of it. In Kokichi's last Free Time Event with Shuichi, he explains he knows psychologically Shuichi is afraid of him. He seems to be skilled in different languages, as during his Free Time Event with Kaede he casually lists Japanese, French and Spanish words for common sense. He always found Tsumugi Shirogane suspicious as shown on his white board in his room. He also believes Santa to be a lie as shown from Kazutaka Kodaka’s 2017 Christmas tweet.
