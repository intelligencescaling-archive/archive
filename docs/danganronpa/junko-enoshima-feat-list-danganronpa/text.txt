Junko Enoshima feat list

Junko Enoshima intelligence feats (Amigames Canon):

**“kAmUkuRa kAmUkUrA yAsS qUeEn!**”

(D0) She pretended to be Ryoko Otonashi after intentionally having her memories wiped to test the effectiveness of Yasuka’s memory erasing technology all part of her intricate, multilayered plan. After losing her memories, she had Mukuro Ikusaba pose as her true self, which in turn helped her gradually regain her true identity along with the presence of the Monokuma maid and the Monokuma masked group, which also served as psychological triggers that slowly jogged her real memories. Once she fully regained them, she confirmed that the memory erasing technology worked perfectly and decided she would later replicate Yasuke Matsuda's memory erasing technology and use it on the 78th Class of Hope’s Peak Academy. Interestingly, although not part of her original plan, her amnesiac self instinctively documented everything she experienced meticulously noting down her actions everyone else’s actions her observations and everyone’s observations to counteract the effects of her amnesia. This feat showcases \[PS (Problem Solving) DM (Decision Making) LT (Linear Thinking) CT (Creative Thinking) SA (Self Awareness) Foresight and ST (Strategic Thinking)\].

(Anime) She took control of Hope’s Peak Academy by creating brainwashing Despair Videos through the Ultimate Animator, Ryota Mitarai, whom she instantly recognized as perfect for the task after a single glance, thanks to her Analyst Talent. Manipulating him into sharing his animation skills, so she could replicate them she then orchestrated the first Killing Game with the student council through blackmail having Izuru Kamukura and Mukuro Ikusaba, there to ensure there was no chance for escape. She combined the footage of the Student Council massacre with her new advanced animation techniques learnt form Ryota to produce a highly potent Despair Video, which she later mass-produced and broadcast worldwide successfully brainwashing the majority of the global population. The Despair Video itself was a complex sensory illusion layered with subliminal messages designed to corrupt the viewer’s mind. To complete her scheme, she framed the entire massacre on Izuru Kamukura, using edited footage of him killing several students in self-defense as irrefutable evidence. This feat showcases \[CT (Creative Thinking) LA (Learning Ability) LC (Learning Capability) DIM (Direct Manipulation) NLT (Non Linear Thinking) IDM (Indirect Manipulation) IM (Individual Manipulation) MM (Mass Manipulation) PM (Psychological Manipulation) AT (Analytical Thinking) and ST (Strategic Thinking)\].

(Anime + UDG) She personally designed and programmed Monokuma, meticulously crafting every detail of his appearance and functionality. After finalizing the design, she strategically exploited Monaca Towa’s connections within the Towa Group’s Robotics Branch to mass-produce countless physical Monokuma units, psychologically manipulating Monaca through curiosity into coercing her co-workers to assist in the process. She also manipulated the Warriors of Hope into killing adults after preventing them from taking their own lives. By exploiting the deep psychological scars left by their past traumatic experiences, convincing them that all adults were cruel and only sought to harm them. This feat showcases \[VSI (Visual Spatial Index) DIM (Direct Manipulation) IM (Individual Manipulation) IDM (Indirect Manipulation) MM (Mass Manipulation) PM (Psychological Manipulation) and ST (Strategic Thinking)\].

(D1) She had Mukuro Ikusaba disguise herself as Junko and then orchestrated Mukuro’s murder through Monokuma using the Spears of Gungnir. This created the illusion that Junko Enoshima had died during the killing game, allowing her to avoid any suspicion of being the mastermind. This feat showcases \[MM (Mass Manipulation) PM (Psychological Manipulation) and ST (Strategic Thinking)\].

(Anime) Her eyesight is extraordinary—measured at an astonishing 10,000 over 20. With such unparalleled vision, she can instantly notice even the slightest changes in appearance, such as when Mukuro Ikusaba has more freckles than the last time they met. Her precision is so refined that she can use an ice pick to pinpoint the exact location of every single freckle with flawless accuracy. This feat showcases \[AT (Analytical Thinking) and PR (Perceptual Reasoning)\].

(Anime) While torturing and interrogating a security guard, she realised the more harmless and ordinary the object she used appeared, the deeper and longer lasting the emotional trauma would be. This feat showcases \[EU (Emotional Understanding)\].

(D1) She showed Class 78th motive videos, each carefully designed to manipulate their emotions and break their composure. For example, she showed Sayaka Maizono a video of her idol career, subtly reminding her that everything she had worked for would fade into obscurity if she didn’t find a way to escape. The psychological pressure steadily built within her, twisting her fear and desperation until it ultimately drove her to attempt to kill Leon. This feat showcases \[EU (Emotional Understanding) LT (Linear Thinking) PM (Psychological Manipulation) MM (Mass Manipulation) and IDM (Indirect Manipulation)\].

(D2) She orchestrated the transfer of her own consciousness into an advanced artificial intelligence known as Alter Ego Junko, who she personally designed and engineered. By replicating Chihiro Fujisaki’s programming talent, to continue her plans after her demise. This feat showcases \[LA (Learning Ability) LC (Learning Capability) LT (Linear Thinking) VSI (Visual Spatial Index) and Foresight\].

Her Analyst Talent allows her to predict what someone is going to do or say before they even act or speak. Beyond simply anticipating actions, it also grants her insight into a person’s abilities, and even how entire sequences of events are going to unfold. You only need one Example of her talent in action: accurately anticipating the entire sequence of events in *Danganronpa Zero* well in advance. This feat showcases \[AT (Analytical Thinking) EU (Emotional Understanding) and Foresight\].

(D1) After successfully replicating Yasuke Matsuda's memory erasing technology, she decided to initiate another killing game. To accomplish this, she took the already brainwashed from the despair video Class 78th, forced them to take refuge in a designated shelter, and then erased their memories. Using the shelter as the game’s location. This feat showcases \[LA (Learning Ability) LC (Learning Capability) NLT (Non Linear Thinking) and ST (Strategic Thinking)\].

(Anime) After freeing Izuru Kamukura by kidnapping a security guard and torturing and interrogating said guard for the code to Izuru’s cell she manipulates Izuru into coming with her to watch the first killing game through his boredom. This feat showcases \[EU (Emotional Understanding) PM (Psychological Manipulation) LT (Linear Thinking) and ST (Strategic Thinking)\].

(D1) She accepted her execution without any hesitation, even finding enjoyment in it, despite knowing she would die afterward. This feats showcases \[MF (Mental Fortitude)\].

(Anime) Mukuro Ikusaba states that Junko can process any information in a flash. If we infer what “flash” means, it could be a single second or at most five seconds this is still impressive. This feat showcases \[CPI (Cognitive Processing Index) and PSI (Processing Speed Index)\].

(Anime) She feigns her emotions with Izuru Kamukura, orchestrating a fake date that, in reality, is meant to show him Hope’s Peak Academy. She acts as though she genuinely wants the date to be perfect and claims she’s been so anxious about it that she hasn’t slept a single wink all night. After maintaining this facade, she finally reveals the truth—that it was all a lie and that her real intention was simply to show him Hope’s Peak Academy. This feat showcases \[EM (Emotional Management) ST (Strategic Thinking) LT (Linear Thinking) and AS (Acting Skills)\].

(Anime) After throwing a wrench at Makoto Naegi to test how his luck works, she pretends it was an accident and acts relieved that he isn’t hurt. This feat showcases \[EM (Emotional Management) ST (Strategic Thinking) LT (Linear Thinking) and AS (Acting Skills)\].

(D1) She meticulously set up executions, each carefully designed to reflect the unique personality of the culprit. This feat showcases \[EU (Emotional Understanding) CT (Creative Thinking) and LT (Linear Thinking)\].
