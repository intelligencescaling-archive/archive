Makoto Naegi feat list

Makoto Naegi Intelligence feats:

**"I refuse to give up. I refuse to get bored. I refuse to throw it all away. I refuse to despair. Because all I have going for me is the desire to keep moving forward!" - Makoto Naegi**

FSIQ (Full-Scale-Intelligence): He [deduced](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mkuaGnwcubs) that Leon killed Sayaka after she attempted to kill him in order to frame him, and Leon tried to cover it up. His conclusion was based on several pieces of evidence: Sayaka’s dying message “11037” written on the wall; the precision required to hit the incinerator button with a crystal ball from a distance; a blood-stained shirt that was partially burnt in the incinerator; the dorm nameplates; the kitchen knife set; the bathroom knob and doorframe; evidence of a struggle; the replica sword and sheath; the shattered crystal ball; Aoi’s account; the missing hair; the fact that the tap wasn’t running; the crime occurring in his room; the switched room keys; and the condition of Sayaka’s left index finger, palms, wrist and Leon’s toolkit. Anime Version: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UClhMstwj0M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UClhMstwj0M). He [deduced](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bv81kyg0Kco) that Mondo killed Chihiro and that Byakuya tried to frame the murder on Genocide Jack. He also deduced who Genocide Jack really was. His conclusion was based on several pieces of evidence: the locker room dumbbell, the Genocide Jack case file, the fact that Genocide Jack reportedly had Dissociative Identity Disorder matching Toko’s personality—proving she was Genocide Jack—the positioning of the old Genocide Jack victims, the differences between Chihiro’s fatal injury and those of the previous victims, as well as how Chihiro was suspended compared to them. He also noted that Byakuya had prior knowledge of Genocide Jack’s methods, the library desk lamp, the two locker room posters, and the boys’ locker room carpet, which together proved the locker rooms had been swapped. Finally, the broken e-handbook and Mondo’s knowledge that Chihiro’s tracksuit was blue served as decisive evidence. Byakuya being surprised to learn that Chihiro was a boy proved he wasn’t the culprit. In the end, he made Mondo confess by having everyone show their e-handbooks, knowing that Mondo had swapped his. Anime Version: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PQkCyv-TDJA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PQkCyv-TDJA). He [deduced](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6OqjfRSoiwk) that Hifumi was manipulated by Celeste into killing Kiyotaka, and that Celeste later murdered Hifumi and framed Yasuhiro as the killer. His conclusion was based on several pieces of evidence: Yasuhiro's message, the blue tarp, the repository dolly, the bloodstain in the equipment room, the Robo Justice costume, the numbering on the Justice Hammers, a wristwatch, Hifumi's glasses, the glasses cleaning cloth, the note Hifumi had, the broken wristwatch, Kiyotaka's scrap of paper, the spotless Justice Hammer, Celeste’s past wording slip-up, and that Celeste’s e-handbook would reveal her real name which matched Hifumi’s last words. Anime Version: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6OqjfRSoiwk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6OqjfRSoiwk). He [deduced](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g3eJ5eJ_HjM) that Sakura poisoned herself after locking herself in a room, and that Mina deliberately made herself look like the culprit. His conclusion was based on several pieces of evidence: Mina’s account, the pocketed trashed candy wrapper, Kyoko’s examination summary, a chess piece, a shattered bottle, the Monokuma bottle experiment, the locker handprint, the status of Sakura's body, the magazine shelf bloodstain, yellow powder, footsteps in the yellow powder, the barricading, the Monokuma File #4, the glass orientation, and an empty protein drink. Anime Version:[ https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yFkbmJ3-2po&t=1178s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yFkbmJ3-2po&t=1178s). He [deduced](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=re70g0fI4qA) that the Mastermind used Mukuro Ikusaba’s corpse and disguised it as Kyoko Kirigiri also [deduced](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0MIbKiX4m0A) that the Junko Enoshima they saw die prior was actually Mukuro Ikusaba in disguise and the true mastermind was the real Junko Enoshima. His deduction was based on several pieces of evidence: Kyoko's gloves, the fake nails, the tattoo on the right hand, Mukuro Ikusaba's profile, the sprinklers, the exploded body analysis, the tarp, the body before the explosion, the chicken coop and missing chickens, the disguised dead body, Monokuma File #5, the fragments near the dead body, the victim's fatal injury, the titanium arrows, the bloody duct tape, the woodblock key, the group photos—which proved the existence of amnesia and the mastermind’s intent to make everyone fight one another—the interview DVD, the Monokuma control room, the Bio Lab, and the Bio Lab lights. Anime Version: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TebM3EjcWw0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TebM3EjcWw0) [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EAiHW2lEqkU&t=893s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EAiHW2lEqkU&t=893s).

EQ (Emotional-Intelligence): He repeatedly overcomes Despair showing immense Mental Fortitude. Knew Mina was acting unusually defensive in Sakura’s class trial also knew how sincere she sounded in the past talking about Sakura compared to how unsincere she sounded in her fake confession. After Leon’s trial he correctly psychoanalysed Sayaka figuring out her motive. He doesn’t back down from counterarguments disproving them even in tense moments showing immense Stress Tolerance.
