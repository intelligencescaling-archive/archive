Now going into the trial. Takiko saw through Fu’s plan of talking to people, recording their interactions, and using that information to gain an advantage. Knowing that, Takiko went through her own reasoning process:
        Premise A: Fu was recording everyone who was on guard duty with him.
        Premise B: Some prisoners must have shared some really sensitive information with him.
        Premise C: Molly would have shared some deep secrets with Fu, why? Because of this:
1. Premise C/A: Molly was in a mentally unstable state.
2. Premise C/B: Molly has been actively showing her emotions, which Takiko would have easily caught on to.
3. Premise C/C: Molly must be someone willing to open up for some company, being somebody who would reveal information or crucial secrets to everybody else.
Conclusion: Molly Yuzuki’s tape must hold an interesting amount of information that has been recorded in this tape.
Luckily for Takiko, the things Molly said during these tapes could, if taken out of context, sound incredibly suicidal. They at first took that tape out of sheer curiosity, but after Fu told them (before they heard the tape) that Molly was the one behind the suicide pact, she started formulating her murder plan. We can also make the assumption that she was acting like Fu’s daughter in that moment in order for Fu to give her easier access to the tapes.
Not only that, but Takiko already had a reason to kill, with the motive targeting people like her specifically, but she was also in a very mentally unstable state due to the events that followed her. As such, she had decided to formulate a plan to kill Molly at that very moment.
And so, early in the morning, the killer, Takiko gathered the necessary resources in order to solve the case herself. Some weights, a rope, a box, a broom, and a tape player. We can assume she already heard the tape at that point.
Takiko carried multiple smaller weights instead of a larger one, making multiple trips for two reasons:
1. They were easier to carry for her.
2. They were easier to hide in her backpack.
And so, with that in mind, the preparation for the murder plan was concluded.
Takiko reasoned out that nobody would be around that night. Why? This:
        Premise A: Most people, if not everyone, was already asleep at that point.
        Premise B: Everyone was exhausted from their dares and needed to rest.
        Premise C: Everyone was exhausted from being woken up at midnight.
Conclusion: It is highly unlikely that anyone would see her, which is abductive reasoning.

So it is likely that Takiko would have an easier time killing her, which we can assume she foresaw. Then, Takiko took one of the pillows, and smothered her.

All she had to do now was wait for Shinku to wake up. They were waiting for hours.

They waited until Shinku would start banging at the door to start playing the tape recorder.
While the tape recorder was playing, she used the curtain to move up to the door leading to the maintenance room at the top of the lecture hall, and exited through it.
