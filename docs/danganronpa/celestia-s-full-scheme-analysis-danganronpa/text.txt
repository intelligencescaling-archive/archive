CELESTIAL'S FULL SCHEME - ANALYSIS  (She's great + underrated)

The huge cash reward offered to anyone who kills and remains undetected convinces Celeste to go along with the entire scheme.

Her strategy is not merely to commit a murder, but to craft a narrative that everyone will ultimately believe.

Celeste selects Hifumi as the perfect pawn for her scheme, as she is able to deeply understand and manipulate him. She knows he is strongly attached to Alter Ego and is competing with Taka for its attention.

Celeste’s strategy is not merely to convince Hifumi that killing Taka is a good idea, but to make him believe it is the only option and that Taka is an enemy.

She manipulates and deceives Hifumi, claiming that Taka has harmed her, harbors malicious intentions toward both Alter Ego and Hifumi himself, and has taken Alter Ego away.

Hifumi comes to believe he is acting of his own volition meaning Celeste’s strategy succeeds as she leads him to think that neutralizing Taka is the only way out.

She has manufactured a motive for him.

Of course, it turns out that Celeste was the one who took the alter ego, not Taka.

Thus, her goal of manipulating Hifumi and essentially Taka as well is realized.

Celeste knows that if Hifumi were simply to kill Taka, it wouldn't be enough people would link her to Hifumi and investigate his motives. She needs a grander, more elaborate narrative.

That is why she plans the creation of Robo Justice a fake culprit and a key part of the scheme she later explains.

Celeste persuades Hifumi to build the robot and the hammers.

Through the robot, Celeste aims to conceal Hifumi's identity and create a "fictional culprit" to blame for various events, thereby distracting people from crucial questions and sowing confusion.

    shes goal is to make people wonder where the robot is, rather than who is inside it; she deliberately wants to focus their attention on the robot.

Celeste chooses Yasuhiro as the scapegoat because there is evidence linking him to the crime: materials related to the robot are found in his room, and they are of a similar build.

This is the specific narrative Celeste constructs.

Celeste stages Hifumi's "kidnapping" she drugs Yasuhiro with chloroform and uses him while he is inside the robot. She takes a photo of Hifumi being abducted by the robot to serve as evidence, build the narrative, confuse everyone, and divert attention from certain questions and suspicions.

Instead, she leaves him in the locker because she needs his identity to serve as the suspect. As planned and orchestrated by Celeste, Hifumi kills Taka with a hammer.

Her goal is achieved: Taka is dead, and Hifumi is the one who actually committed the murder.

The next step in Celeste's plan involves the fake "Robo Justice" attacks.

She deliberately orchestrates an attack on herself and, subsequently, on Hifumi.

The attack on Hifumi is crucial because it drives a key part of the plan forward.
