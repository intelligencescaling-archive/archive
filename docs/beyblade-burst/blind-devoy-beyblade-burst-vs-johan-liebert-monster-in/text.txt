Johan Liebert's EU/EP can best be summarized through his Beast of Revelations narrative, which is maintained throughout the entire manga.

![img](0f26s2yj050i1)

To quote directly from Fall's document on Johan regarding the significance of this symbolism:

>"In The Revelation of Saint John the Divine, John symbolically explains his visions of the battle between good and evil. The evil that opposes everything the kingdom of God stands for is represented by a beast; this beast has the authority of the dragon (Satan), and as a result of Satan endlessly creating new ones to compose it, it's incapable of being stopped no matter how much you get rid of it. The beast uses these attributes to make people he's capable of exercising control over worship him (anyone who isn't a part of God.) The reason this is important to know is because this verse (particularly the beast) is used by Urasawa to represent Johan. Johan is the beast, he figuratively has the authority of the dragon, he is inevitable. Which is to say that as long as someone is evil/not a part of God, Johan can potentially control them."

This goes to show that Johan stands above humanity in terms of Emotional and Social Intelligence, to the point where he is practically portrayed as an otherworldly being.

![img](91yx53dr050i1 "\"An exact recreation...\"")

As far as direct feats are concerned, a good example of how far this narrative is pushed is the Jürgen feat, where Johan is able to accurately profile a criminal's entire backstory and recreate the scene of his trauma with literally no information—relying purely on his raw Emotional Understanding.

However, none of this compares to Blind's EU/EP feat, where he essentially mind-reads and paints what lies deep within Delta's heart despite never having met him beforehand.

To understand just how broken this feat is, you have to consider something.

![img](p3d1skx7150i1)

As stated by the author, all of the main characters are embodiments of the concepts they're imbued with.

![img](sr5euwua150i1 "\"The Devil's Bey, Diabolos!\"")

Delta's narrative actively compares him to the Devil, with the story directly telling us this, as shown through Episode 16's title: "Bey of the Devil: Diabolos." A Bey is made from the user's soul, meaning that Delta himself is the embodiment of the Devil.

![img](7tgsk9df150i1 "\"Darkness? Delta doesn't have any darkness!\"")

![img](6tstny3w250i1 "\"The embodiment of friendship.\"")

The narrative embodiment of friendship, Dante, is explicitly stated to be unable to see the darkness inside Delta. Blind, however, was—and is—able to perceive it, stating:

>"I know that you don't truly desire the light. I want to see the true you. The one dyed in black."

![img](ng9bbwwk150i1 "\"You don't desire light.\"")

![img](1mocrywk150i1 "\"Show me your true self.\"")

This directly ties into 2 Corinthians 11:14–15:

>"And no wonder, for Satan himself masquerades as an angel of light."

With that being said, this would give Delta a logically higher plane of existence in terms of his Emotional Plane.

Argos, Psalm 8:4–5:

>"What is mankind that you are mindful of them, human beings that you care for them? You have made them a little lower than the angels and crowned them with glory and honor."

Not only was Blind able to accomplish this despite never meeting Delta beforehand, but he was outright able to narrow down the image he received specifically to Delta.

![img](lyawtgbw150i1 "\"And the wait has filled me with inspiration!\"")

He also knew when Delta was coming and that he would be coming, implying that he had a practically perfect understanding of Delta based solely on that single image.

![img](vbgt7ng5250i1 "\"Your true self...\"")

On top of all of this, Blind was outright able to perceive a future emotional state of Delta and Diabolos, using that representation to understand everything described above.

![img](ddkksq3f250i1 "\"My art will be revolutionary!\"")

Blind was able to perceive the deepest aspects of Delta's emotional identity, identify the person represented by that image, understand the darkness within him despite even the narrative embodiment of friendship being unable to perceive it, anticipate his arrival, and even perceive a future emotional state of both Delta and Diabolos.

* In other words, Blind's EU/EP feat is an instance of him directly perceiving and understanding the emotional essence of a character whose narrative places him on a fundamentally higher Emotional Plane than humanity itself.

Which absolutely bullies Johan's feat—no matter how highly you interpret his EU/EP.
