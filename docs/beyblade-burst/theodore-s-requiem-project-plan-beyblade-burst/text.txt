\## \*\*Theodore’s Requiem Project Plan\*\*

\*\*• Step 1: Influence Free to Leave BC Sol\*\*
  – Influences Free into leaving BC Sol by telling him the team would remain at the same level if he stayed, carrying them through all their victories.
  – Free thinks about it and leaves because he trusted that Chris and Valt would be able to make the team better without him.

\*\*• Step 2: Secure Free for His Team\*\*
  – Not only does this get him Free but it also allows him to get Joshua.
  – He waits until he gets Free before contacting Joshua.
  – He knew Joshua wouldn’t resist being able to be on a team with his idol and lead the team.
  – With Free and Joshua, the deal was sealed.

\*\*• Step 3: Use Free to Break Down Shu Mentally\*\*
  – With Free on his team, he has what he needs.
  – Free thoroughly destroys Shu in battle.
  – Theodore takes advantage of this and uses it to take Shu to the Snake Pit under the deal of making him stronger.

\*\*• Step 4: Predict Wakiya’s Reaction to Shu’s Disappearance\*\*
  – He predicts in advance every action Wakiya would make.
  – He knew Wakiya would search the entire world for Shu.
  – He leaves little hints for Wakiya through informants.
  – These hints lead to the conclusion that Shu is in Mexico due to rumors of the Snake Pit.
  – He knew Wakiya would bring Valt, Rantaro, and Daigo.

\*\*• Step 5: Anticipate Cuza’s Involvement\*\*
  – He knew Cuza would also be there.
  – After hearing the explanation, Cuza would obviously be interested in the Snake Pit and helping search for Shu.

\*\*• Step 6: Pre-Plan Their Arrival at the Snake Pit\*\*
  – Upon their arrival, he already planned for them to face replicas of their Beys.
  – This was to test them and the Snake Pit bladers using the replicas.

\*\*• Step 7: Predict Their Post-Defeat Behavior\*\*
  – After they were all defeated by Red Eye, he knew they would search for the Snake Pit again.
  – He was right—they searched the entire forest.
  – They couldn’t find it because it was gone.

\*\*• Step 8: Use Boa to Pressure Red Eye\*\*
  – Whenever Red Eye left the Snake Pit, he had Boa chase him.
  – Boa defeated all bladers in the Snake Pit.
  – When Red Eye was defeated by Lui, this became the final piece needed to convince Shu to get rid of his current Bey and join the Requiem Project willingly.

\*\*• Step 9: Creation of Spryzen Requiem\*\*
  – His observations of top bladers like Lui, Free, etc, led to a synthesis of their strengths.
  – This created Spryzen Requiem, specifically designed to perfectly counter them all.
  – Built for his vision of the God Blader’s Cup.

\*\*• Step 10: The God Blader’s Cup as His Final Testing Grounds\*\*
  – The God Cup was him using everyone to test Red Eye.
  – He manipulated Snake Pit members like Boa, saying he wanted them to kill Red Eye.
  – In the end, it doesn’t matter who wins—Theodore still does.
