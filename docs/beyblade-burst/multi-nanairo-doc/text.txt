Chapter 02 - Rainbow:

The first time we see Multi is in Chapter Two when Bird and X have a Pro qualifier scheduled match.

However, the problem was that in the Chapter before, Bird had his Bey crushed, so he couldn't battle because he didn’t have a Bey, which is obviously logical.

After all, they would lose by default.

That's when Multi comes in, saying as stated in the panel above, “Take a Bey from me”.

Naturally, however, Bird says that she shouldn't just carelessly hand out her Bey as it's supposed to be her partner

However, Multi had a bunch of Beys, it is stated that she made all of them later on down the line.

Anyway, continuing on, she egged him on to pick a Bey; however, he was still being stubborn.

She noticed how he took a liking to Hell's Scythe.

Despite him denying the fact that he took a liking to it she gave it to him anyway as she would then tell him almost as though she was reading his mind that he should rely on his first impression and that he shouldn't calculate which bey should be his partner as she then said it's instinct that forges that bond.

And with this, the first part of her plan was completed as she left after talking to them for a bit more , telling them that she would see them later.

After this, a few panels later, it is shown that she would be Bird and X's opponent for the Pro Qualifier match.

it's stated that the reason she gave Bird a bey was another part of her plan to gain more and more popularity and to make sure that the battle wasn't an absolute curbstomp she made it in a way that the battle would boost her popularity because there was no illusion that she would ever lose to someone like Bird.
