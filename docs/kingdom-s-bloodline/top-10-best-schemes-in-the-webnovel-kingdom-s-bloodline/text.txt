TOP 10 BEST SCHEMES IN THE ENTIRE WEBNOVEL KINGDOM'S BLOODLINE

After rereading Kingdom's Bloodline several times and analyzing the very complex chain of schemes within the story, I came to make this post which took me a long time.

**Very small context on the worldbuilding** : This is a low-fantasy medieval world in the aftermath of a lost empire. About 700 years ago the Final Empire collapsed during the War of Ending, a catastrophic war against the Mystics, near-immortal beings with world-bending powers. Two human kingdoms rose from its ruins on the Western Peninsula:

* **Constellation (the Star Kingdom).** Founded by Tormond I, the King of Restoration, an illegitimate son of the last emperor. It’s ruled by the Jadestar dynasty from Eternal Star City and Renaissance Palace.
* **Eckstedt.** Founded by Nairu, the Dragon King, with the Sky Queen dragon. It’s a looser northern kingdom of powerful archdukes.

Beyond them lie desert tribes and orcs, the Comas trading alliance, the Eastern Continent, the Night Kingdom of vampires, elves and other races.

In both kingdoms a king struggles against hereditary great houses: Constellation’s six Guardian Dukes, Eckstedt’s archdukes. Much of the plot is the crown trying to centralise power, through standing armies, taxes and bureaucracy, against nobles who defend their old privileges. An influential church, the Sunset Temple, and the Secret Intelligence Department, the crown’s spy service, operate in the shadows. So do criminal underworlds like the Black Street Brotherhood and the Blood Bottle Gang.

The power system goes like this :

**Power of Eradication (or Termination):** martial energy that warriors cultivate. It comes in many styles taught by schools like the Tower of Eradication, and its highest levels are “supra” and “ultimate” class.

* **Psionic abilities:** rare innate powers, such as Morris controlling the air around him.
* **Mystics:** individuals who cross a metaphysical “threshold” and become living calamities, each tied to a domain such as air, blood or senses. The world calls them “Calamities” and fears them.
* **Magic:** largely erased from history by the Cleansing of the World Plan. Remnants survive in towers, seals, recording spells and curses**.**

The Two Magic Empresses won the War of Ending. They made Legendary Anti-Magic weapons that can seal Mystics, gave them to kings and churches, and vanished. They wiped Mystics from history and still hunt any who appear, while secretly fearing each other.

This is where our protagonist appears. Thales was a nameless beggar suddenly declared a Jadestar prince. He also carries Mystic power (and seems to have memories of another world). He’s caught between three things: the crown’s ruthless politics, the noble houses resisting it, and the hidden war between Mystics and the Empresses. Underneath it all lies the unsolved **Bloody Year**, a massacre of the royal family 18 years ago that set everything in motion.

With all that context, this is my ranking :

# 1. The Two Empress Rule

That's a very strange scheme that cannot be accurately scaled. Every power in the story have been orchestrated by them (Kessel to the Secret Intelligence Department to Eckstedt). It is implied that the wars between Kingdom's Bloodline and the entire Bloody New Year (though the cause of it is unknown) has been part of their plan.

# 2. The Founder's scheme : Jadestar Long Game (Tormond → Leinster → the Virtuous King → Midier → Kessel)

Arguably one of the best schemes I've ever seen. What was planned is this :

\- Tormond’s founding legend and the Secret Intelligence Department.

\- The “chessboard laid by the Virtuous King,” on which Dragon’s Blood placed its key piece (confirmed).

\- Dragon’s Blood, with several secret allies, Lampard among them, and Thales as one of its “backup pieces” (confirmed). That makes Lampard’s rise a sub-scheme here: he thinks he’s the winner, but he’s one of the department’s planned partners.

\- Sand King, drawn up by Midier and carried on by Kessel (confirmed).

\- Kessel’s covenant: Thales as the fake rival who draws out disloyal lords (confirmed).

\- Val Arunde’s fall (likely). His crime let the crown jail him and leave the North in limbo, which is exactly the result Sand King-style centralisation needs. Whether the crown or Lampard’s side actually pushed him into it isn’t shown, but either way the crown is who benefited.

\- Miranda’s offer to hand the North to the crown (confirmed result, not a planned one).

\- Jann’s downfall and Federico’s revenge in Jade City (likely). Kessel sent Thales to “depose Jann,” and Federico is likely Kessel’s tool.

\- Zayen’s submission and petition (confirmed outcome).

This is a scheme that spans over generations and generations where so many characters who seems to be facing against it are actually feeding it

# 3. Bloody New Year mastermind

Biggest mystery of the story that is yet to have been resolved. This scheme can be part of the two schemes as we see above but this is unknown so I ranked it as alone. I have it on the same level as Ang Xiao's Underworld scheme, which is top tier.

* Teng’s assassinations, the guards’ complicity and Horace’s coup.
* The Nine Powerhouses and Black Sword’s role, possibly as revenge for Constance.
* Mallos’s informing on his father, the riot it set off and the opening it gave the assassins.
* John Jadestar’s “heroic” death, and the betrayal by the Duke of Nanchester.
* Possibly the exposure of Freylan.

# 4. The Alliance of Three Calamities: Freylan, Ashida and Giza

A scheme that pushed characters from scratch to nearly reach the literal throne. Though it was countered, this was insane (Xun Yu-Guo Jia level in scheming):

\- Freylan’s two-hundred-year infiltration as Queen Fiosa.

\- The Blood Bottle Gang as cover (confirmed). That puts the gang’s whole history, the “One Night War” and the gang’s later decline under it.

\- Ashida’s research into the Empresses’ secret, which is how Thales now knows about it.

# 5. Fakenhaz’s anti-Kessel play

Herman’s private meetings with the Shadow Shield, which Fakenhaz arranged. The sword Sentinel, the Amos evidence and Anker’s banquet attack. The collective apology and new obedience of the Western Desert lords after Thales’s letter. He completely orchestrated Thales moves to keep Kessel in check, though this is included as part of the Founder's scheme (this one too is a weird rank, too much weird ranks to be honest).

# 6. Zayen and the Cavendish “Nova” plan

Though it was a complete failure, the execution is very great (unsure of his rank too):

* The vassals’ attack on the Eckstedt diplomatic group (confirmed) and Thales being made the scapegoat.
* Hiring Serena and lending her Vine Manor, plus the vampire assassination attempt (confirmed).
* Arming Anker at the banquet (confirmed).

# 7. Lampard's counter-scheme against Mindis The Third

Madarame Baku if he was a politician. Bro literally bet his entire country's ressources and so many of his allies, without hope that the conflict he was about to start was going to be successful. Countering a 150 years scheme by using Gu as the go-between with the Shadow Shield, the Mystic Gun attack at Broken Dragon Fortress, and Nuven’s murder. Rebuilding Black Sand Territory under his own rule. Planning Val Arunde's scheme. This is definitely the most successful scheme in this list besides number 2 and 1 (Above Baku level, and probably Ayanokoji Kiyone's equal in it)

# 8. Nuven's scheme for Ecksdat

Though he got cooked by Founder's scheme and Lampard himself, his plan was pretty good (unsure of his rank) :

* The fake archduchess, and Moriah’s escape into the Quick Rope identity .
* His pressure on Thales as a hostage.

# 9. Serena's takedown scheme

She planned her entire awakening across the first hundreds of chapters, set contingencies across so many situations even up to current chapters now. One of the most successful villains in the story for sure and her scheme is not even properly completed yet. We can probably see far more of her in the future. I got her at CTW Light level in scheming (Memory Loss + LUTNW post-mortem plan)
