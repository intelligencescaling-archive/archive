Neuro: Supernatural Detective is a series by Yusei Matsui, the same author as Assasination Classroom. The premise of this series is a demon from the Underworld coming to Earth in search of the “Ultimate Puzzle” to solve. Now, the questions you may be asking are “What kind of puzzles is he searching for?” and "Why is he searching for puzzles, or the ‘Ultimate Puzzle’?” and the answers to those are quite simple. The reason why he’s searching for puzzles in the first place is because they’re his source of food, and naturally, seeking the “Ultimate Puzzle” would grant him the “Ultimate Meal”. Now, even though the Earth has significantly less Miasma, which is comparable to Oxygen for a demon, Neuro still leaves the Underworld to Earth in search of these puzzles, even though it reduces his overall ability to a mere 1/10000 of its true potential. “Why would he risk his life, even giving up his source of breathing, just for food? Couldn’t he just keep devouring puzzles in the Underworld?” one may ask, and indeed, a great idea, if not for one small issue: Neuro has already devoured all the puzzles from the Underworld (More on this later).
