- Hwang In-ho, while acting as FrontMan, seemed more reactive and passive than actually scheming or deceiving. In season 1, we predominantly saw his detective skills and his intimidating aura, but the next seasons will explore situations of greater risk and the need for cunning or planning, including exploring his past as Player 132 (in the 2015 games). The script for this first season will have many allusions to the next ones (such as good acting skills, self-control, moral duality, empathy, emotional understanding, FSIQ, Reasoning, etc), with me picking up details from the beginning that will be explored soon, but for now I have FrontMan (S1) already comparable to the most intelligent characters in this universe (Like Sang-woo, Il-nam, MG Coin), but it is in the next seasons that it becomes noticeable how absurd the community is for generally not seeing that In-ho is ARGUABLY the most intelligent character in Squid Game and probably even a mid tier in SCD as a whole.
