S3 Ep 1

- The next game is a game called Keys and Knives. Players with the keys try to hide from the players with the knives and escape before they get killed by them. Seekers have to kill at least one person to pass the game. Myung Gi ends up in the blue team (Hiders) while Jun Hee ends up in the red team (Seekers). Before the game starts, players are allowed to switch teams. Myung Gi goes up to Jun Hee and says that they should switch teams. He knows that Jun Hee won’t be able to kill anybody so she wouldn’t pass the game if she plays as a seeker. So he suggests that he should be a seeker, this way he could kill someone and then come to protect her and the baby. He then says “I'd do anything for you and the reward, even if it meant killing everyone.” Jun Hee agrees to switch teams. Myung Gi has ensured his and his girlfriend’s safety.
(EE, Critical Thinking, Convergent Thinking, Tactics)

- Just before the game starts, Nam Gyu goes to talk with Myung Gi. Nam Gyu says that he is thankful that he has killed Thanos and they should work together to pass the game easier.
(Charisma)

S3 Ep 2

S3 Ep 5

- Players advance to the final game with 9 people remaining. The game is named Sky Squid Game. It’s split into 3 sections, and at least one person must be thrown off in each one. Each round starts when a button is pressed by the players. The game ends when 3 people get thrown off and all the remaining others advance to the final section. Player 100’s team’s plan is to kill 456 (Gi Hun), 222 (Jun Hee’s baby) and 125. Keep in mind that Gi Hun is holding the baby. The group says that they should kill the baby in the first section. But Myung Gi says that Gi Hun won’t let go of the baby no matter what, even though his group thinks otherwise. So killing them would be hard for now. He convinces his group to kill 125 first. Myung Gi takes a look at the environment and he sees that the pole in the center of the platform can be taken off. None of the players wanted to throw off Player 125 by theirself because they were afraid of falling too, but with Myung Gi’s idea of using the pole it has become very easy to throw off Player 125 without getting in contact. Myung Gi slams the pole into Player 125 and he dies. Player 100 says that Myung Gi is a genius. They advance to the next section. Just as Player 100 was about to press the button to start the round, Myung Gi stops him by saying that they shouldn’t press the button yet until Gi Hun and the baby get separated to not waste time. But they press the button anyway and so Myung Gi comes up with a plan to separate the baby from Gi Hun. He orders all of the members of the group to hold onto each other in a straight line and the person in the front tries to take the baby while Myung Gi waits in the side to protect himself from anything that could go wrong. Gi Hun starts to fight back with the person in the front so in that moment Myung Gi pushes off the person from his own team and he sides with Gi Hun. Player 100 tries to convince them by saying that they should be a group again but Myung Gi sees through his bluff and says “They are 4 people, we are 2 people. They could betray us at anytime” so he chooses to not side with them. Player 100 then calls out Myung Gi by saying that he was the one to come up with that plan to break his relationship between him and Myung Gi. Myung Gi however, uses the fact that he is the baby’s father to convince Gi Hun into thinking that he wasn’t trying to hurt him. Myung Gi then says to Gi Hun that they both have weapons so the other team wouldn’t be able to hurt them and they are in control of the game, he manages to convince Gi Hun into thinking that he is good. Because he said that they are in control now, a fight started in Player 100’s group that led them to choosing a victim. Another fight starts between the groups when Gi Hun lowers his guard and after the fight only Myung Gi, Gi Hun, Player 100, the baby and another guy remain. Player 100 says that they should push off the other guy and split the money in four, where everyone gets 11,4 billion won. Myung Gi in that moment calculates that if he pushes Player 100 off too each player would get 15,2 billion won (Total prize is 45,6 billion won, the money would be split between him, the baby and Gi Hun). He pushes Player 100 off. Then, the other guy jumps off himself too. So now there is only Gi Hun, Myung Gi and the baby remaining. Gi Hun tells Myung Gi that he could kill him in the next section but Myung Gi doesn’t believe this. He advances to the other section while he tells Gi Hun to in the previous one. He makes Gi Hun place the baby to the ground and walk away, then he takes the baby with him to the next section. The reason for doing this is that he wanted to kill Gi Hun but Gi Hun was holding the baby so he made him let go. But Gi Hun jumped to the other section at the last second so they got into a fight. During the fight, Gi Hun’s knife dropped to the ground. Knowing that Gi Hun would do anything for the baby, Myung Gi threatened to throw the baby off and using this he made Gi Hun walk away from the knife so that he could pick it up. Even though Myung Gi grabs the knife, he still loses his fight to Gi Hun and dies there.
