Short analysis of Netora Reiko's manipulation of Shirase Yuri

(The manga is Reincarnated Beauty Netora Reiko and this feat happens in chapter 2)

So while Netora Reiko and her childhood friend Yuu are seeing which clubs they should join, they open the library to check out the literature club and Yuri Shirase, one of its members, is surprised by their sudden entrance and slips, with a ton of books crashing onto her

After they help her out, Reiko (who knew from earlier that Yuri is a lesbian due to the way she stared at Reiko in class) gets up close and stares at her for a moment, making Yuri feel awkward. Reiko then asks if she is Yuri Shirase from her class, which Yuri confirms and says that she's surprised that Reiko knows her name to which Reiko replies saying that it's only natural since they're classmates

Reiko then asks Yuri about the literature club and bons with her over their shared love of books. Reiko mentions how she's wanted to talk to Yuri more, to which Yuri responds by saying that she also wanted to talk with Reiko more. Reiko then "remembers" that the books that fell on Yuri were quite heavy and shows a more kind and caring side, checking Yuri's head for injuries which was actually just so Yuri would see Reiko's chest up close which is meant to serve as direct form of seduction

After confirming that Yuri's head is fine, she asks her if they can join the literature club (referring to her as "Yuri-chan" instead of "Shirase-san" like earlier, which is a more intimate way to refer to someone)

After Yuri explains how to get the form submitted so they can join the literature club, Reiko once again calls her Yuri-chan and hugs her a thankful gesture for helping them out, once again expanding the intimate bond she created. When Yuu-kun points out how Reiko's being a bit too touchy with Yuri, Reiko says that this is perfectly normal for girls and then whispers to Yuri that since it's perfectly normal, she doesn't have to hold back. This creates the idea in Yuri's mind that this quickly-created and intimate bond between Reiko and Yuri is completely normal

Reiko then leaves and says they'll see each other tomorrow

So in summary, Reiko within minutes creates and then gradually builds an intimate bond with Yuri. First starting by telling Yuri that she knows her from her class which is an effective way to seduce her as Yuri's shyness would make it so she'd feel really happy about her crush recognizing her, then Reiko starts talking about their common interest, then showing a more caring side towards Yuri by checking for head injuries, at the same time she's making Yuri feel aroused by being so close to Reiko's chest, then starting to call her Yuri-chan as a way to clearly show a more intimate bond between them, then thanking her and hugging her which once again builds a more intimate bond and then finally she tops it off by convincing Yuri that their intimate bond is completely normal
