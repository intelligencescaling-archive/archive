From the start, Kenjaku’s main plans were two simple things: ushering the Heian era into modern times and optimizing cursed energy beyond the threshold reached in the Heian era.

The Culling Game is Kenjaku’s long-term plan, which he formulated long before the story of Jujutsu Kaisen, or even before the Heian era, for one simple goal, which Kenjaku refers to as “advancing to the other side.”

The Culling Game is both a ritual and a game where various types of jujutsu sorcerers and cursed spirits from different time periods participate to cull the weaker and unfit participants who cannot adapt in this highly volatile environment with ever-changing complexity. It also serves the purpose of  generating raw cursed energy in the environment for Kenjaku’s plan of merging the whole population of non-sorcerers into the next stage of cursed energy evolution.

The nature of the Culling Game, from a macro viewpoint, is a microcosm of the Heian era, where jujutsu was at its peak and the strongest ruled the system. Only those with high potency and high adaptability could survive and thrive, while others who could not properly flow within these dangerous waters of changing difficulty were annihilated. The law of dark forest rule the era

“Sink or swim” at its purest.

In the storyline, we know Kenjaku is far wiser than anyone else in the series albeit in a twisted way. Kenjaku understands that one cannot achieve their true potential, the metaphorical “120%,” within a safe and comfortable environment, weak mentality, or without hunger for learning.

True evolution requires chaos, pressure, competition, adaptability, and the ability to think on the fly.

The Culling Game’s structure provides an environment that forces evolution and encourages quick thinking, adaptability, and hunger for growth among the participants

The participants in the Culling Game would unknowingly emulate the principles of the Heian era and gradually mold their mentality to become similar to Heian era sorcerers 's mentality.

And from a micro viewpoint, the Culling Game is a massive petri dish and Kodoku where different sorcerers with the potential to rival each other are placed in a sealed ecosystem and isolated with other similarly powerful participants. They are left to fight and consume each other, with the goal of actualising their potential and increasing their potency through absorbing the influence and outcomes of other participants. # Designing

After the Shibuya Incident, Kenjaku started hijacking Tengen’s special barriers and repurposing them. He forced Tengen into two choices.

Either he allowed Kenjaku to hijack these domains, or he destroyed these special domains, which would cause an increase in cursed spirits.

This was an illusion of choice created by Kenjaku. Either choice would serve Kenjaku regardless.

Also, Tengen is possibly the ancestor of “potential man” by sheer bum energy they share within this series.

Kenjaku repurposed these special barriers into colony barriers and deployed the Kogane spirits as interfaces for participants.

Kenjaku also designed five different participant classes.

1. Normal humans who became cursed.
2. Awakened sorcerers whose innate cursed techniques activated through changes within their body systems, such as Junpei Yoshino, and awakened sorcerers whose cursed techniques activated through cursed object ingestion, such as Yuji Itadori.
3. Special grade cursed spirits.
4. Established jujutsu society sorcerers.
5. Reincarnated ancient sorcerers.

Kenjaku also made a binding vow with himself for the Culling Game to work , that he would not become the Game Master nor a participant, while also allowing newly cursed humans to leave the colonies if they wished.

However, this also served Kenjaku because killing him would no longer stop the Culling Game, while the cursed humans would still remain altered and continue playing a role within the merger.

# System

Kenjaku created the rules in a way that reflected both his personality and his desire to preserve the game’s structure.

1. Players must declare their participation at a colony within 19 days of awakening their cursed technique. Failure to do so results in cursed technique removal.
2. Any player who breaks the first rule shall be subject to cursed technique removal. basically death sentence
3. Non players who enter a colony become players at the moment of entry and are considered participants in the Culling Game.
4. Players score points by ending the lives of other players.
5. Point values are determined by the Game Master according to the strength and danger level of each participant.
6. Excluding the point value of one’s own life, a player may expend 100 points to negotiate with the Game Master and create a new rule for the Culling Game.
7. In accordance with the previous rule, the Game Master must accept any proposed rule unless it has a long lasting effect that severely disrupts the Culling Game itself.
8. If a player’s score remains unchanged for 19 days, that player shall be subject to cursed technique removal.

Each of these rules reinforces the others while still providing a certain degree of freedom to the players.

The players are allowed to add rules, but they cannot directly remove existing ones. Because of this, they are forced to find indirect ways to deal with the structure of the Culling Game itself.

The rules are intentionally designed to make players feel like cogs within a larger machine, reducing individuality while increasing pressure, isolation, and desperation. Participants are constantly pushed to improve and survive because stagnation within the system leads to death.

At the same time, the rules gradually force the players to think within Kenjaku’s own logic and worldview..
