Kenjaku's Gojo sealing categories rating

Logistics >= Fortitude > Building > Complexity> Adaptability

# Logistics - 10/10

The strongest part of the strategy. Kenjaku demonstrates an exceptional understanding and utilization of Shibuya's geography, infrastructure, timing, resources, and most importantly, Gojo's own psychology.

From choosing Shibuya as the location to exploiting the structure of Shibuya Station, Kenjaku turns the environment itself into part of the strategy. The metro system, civilians, barriers, cursed spirits, transfigured humans, and positioning of the participants all serve a specific purpose.

The timing of the metro and the conditions inside the station are particularly important because Gojo cannot simply use overwhelming force without considering the civilians around him.

TL;DR: Kenjaku makes exceptional use of territory, infrastructure, resources, timing, information, and Gojo's psychology, making Logistics the clearest 10/10 aspect of the strategy.

# Fortitude - 9.8/10

The strategy has extremely high fortitude because Gojo's individual response does not fundamentally change the final outcome.

Almost every major option available to Gojo ultimately leads toward the same result:

* Domain Expansion = CT burnout + the circumstances surrounding Gojo eventually allow the Prison Realm to seal him.
* 0.2 second Domain Expansion = Gojo still ends up sealed.
* No Domain Expansion = Gojo remains trapped in the situation and is still sealed.
* Use CT = the surrounding circumstances prevent this from simply solving the problem.
* Attempt to blitz = Kenjaku's setup and the surrounding conditions still lead toward the sealing scenario.

The impressive part is not simply that Kenjaku predicted one specific action from Gojo. He constructed the situation so that multiple possible responses still converge toward the same end result.

Even Gojo's overwhelming strength is not enough to invalidate the strategy once the necessary conditions have been established.

# Building - 8/10

The Building aspect is somewhat overlooked, but it is one of the strongest parts of the plan.

The timing of the metro, Shibuya Station's infrastructure, civilians, barriers, and positioning of curses and transfigured humans all helped create the conditions needed to trap Gojo.

And the construction goes beyond just sealing Gojo. The Shibuya Incident was part of Kenjaku's larger plan, where he had already prepared sorcerers, curses, and jujutsu society to take advantage of the situation after Gojo was removed.

# Complexity - 7.9/10

The strategy is complex, but not necessarily because it contains an absurd number of simple XYZ layers operating simultaneously.

Its complexity comes from the scope of what Kenjaku is coordinating before, during, and after Gojo's sealing.

The immediate objective is to seal Gojo, but the Shibuya Incident also incorporates numerous other moving pieces such as curses, transfigured humans, sorcerers, civilians, barriers, the Prison Realm, and the broader collapse of jujutsu society.

Therefore, the complexity comes less from unnecessary layers and more from the number of interconnected components serving a larger objective.

# Adaptability - 7/10

This is probably the weakest category, although 7/10 is still solid.

The main reason it is lower is that Kenjaku's preparation reduces the need for active adaptation. Once the Shibuya conditions have been established, the plan is designed to play out largely according to the framework he constructed beforehand.

Gojo's sealing itself does not require Kenjaku to constantly improvise against an unpredictable opponent because the environment and circumstances have already been manipulated to heavily restrict Gojo's options.

The same applies to the post sealing portion of the operation. Kenjaku had already accounted for much of what would happen after Gojo was removed.

This also fits Kenjaku's general approach. Rather than constantly interfering and reacting to every development, he prefers to prepare the conditions and allow events to unfold.

# Overall - 8.5/10(imo 9/10)
