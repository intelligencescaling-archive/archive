Additional info

Disaster curse spirits are special-grade cursed spirits that desire to rid the Earth of humanity for various personal reasons:
* Mahito – A cursed spirit born from human hatred and indifference. He can transfigure anything he touches into any form he desires and reshape himself to escape situations. Mahito believes humans have no value and supports the eradication of humanity.
* Jogo – A cursed spirit born from the fear of volcanoes. He possesses fire- and volcano-based cursed techniques. Jogo believes cursed spirits are the true humans and desires a world where they dominate the Earth.
* Dagon – A cursed spirit born from the fear of the ocean. Initially underdeveloped, he evolves significantly during the Shibuya Incident.
* Hanami – A cursed spirit born from the fear of land-based natural disasters. Hanami can control plants and nature and seeks to eliminate humanity due to their destruction of the environment.

All of these cursed spirits possess Domain Expansion, which creates a territory reflecting their inner nature (e.g., Dagon’s ocean-like domain).A Domain Expansion guarantees a sure-hit attack on targets inside it and acts as a barrier that traps opponents with little to no chance of escape.

Domain amplification- A barrier that wraps the user in a thin domain layer, neutralizing cursed techniques on contact but preventing the user from using their own

Commission curtain/veil - A large-scale barrier with custom conditions (entry/exit rules, visibility control, targeting specific people)
