Short kenjaku feat of weakening jujustu society

**Context: Before the Tengen merger was going to happen, Kenjaku made his move against Jujutsu society as a whole and destroyed its structure after a long period of dormancy.**

**BLOOD CLAN**

In the 19th century (Meiji Period, late 19th century), Kenjaku possessed Noritoshi Kamo (Ancestor), using the body's Blood Manipulation and his own Cursed Object Creation to create the Death Womb Paintings.

This led to the Blood Clan becoming a taboo subject itself due to their ancestor's actions, leading to a fracture within the clan.

From Noritoshi Kamo, Kenjaku came to know about the warehouse path (a small gap in Tengen's barrier) where cursed tools and weapons are stored. This is where Kenjaku learned that the Prison Realm and the 16 Sukuna fingers were stored.

At the same time, Kenjaku formed a secret alliance with one of the higher-ups as a spy within Jujutsu society.

**KAORI ITADORI**

According to the timeline, Kenjaku then possessed Kaori Itadori, who had presumably died in some accident or something.

Kenjaku chose Kaori Itadori because she was the wife of Jin Itadori, who was related to Sukuna's twin soul family.

Kenjaku manipulated Jin Itadori, presumably because he desperately wanted a son.

Due to Kenjaku's CT, he can inherit the previous body's CT. Kenjaku inherited Noritoshi Kamo's Blood Manipulation CT, which was presumably used in the creation of the Death Womb Paintings.

Kenjaku possessed Kaori Itadori, used Noritoshi Kamo's CT and his method of fusing cursed spirits with humans, along with Sukuna's twin's reincarnated soul, to create Yuji Itadori — a perfect vessel with a perfect cursed body.

After creating Yuji Itadori, Kenjaku left the Itadori family (presumably killing Jin to cover up his tracks) and began his operations to stop the Tengen merger.

Kenjaku succeeded in stopping the merger. However, that wasn't even the best part for Kenjaku. His plans had caused a rift between Gojo and Geto, which led to further schisms within Jujutsu society.

Kenjaku made a vow with Geto to exchange cursed spirits for his body. Presumably manipulating him, he also pushed for an age of sorcerers (he wanted an era like the Heian period).

Geto caused the Night of 1000 Curses, which reduced Jujutsu society's power considerably. The presumed death of Geto pushed Gojo even further away from Jujutsu society and deepened his isolation.

Kenjaku then targeted Megumi's sister as a reincarnated vessel, rendering her hospitalised. This pushed Megumi further away from Jujutsu society.

First, both Megumi and Gojo don't like Jujutsu society's edicts overall.

Second, Megumi became more invested in sorcery, developing his weird saviour complex.

Third, this caused the rift within the Zenin clan to remain permanent.

Kenjaku also gained the Anti-Gravity System due to the fact that Kaori Itadori was presumably a passive sorcerer.

Kenjaku then gathered the Disaster Curse group and Uraume for his plans.

He also gave a gift to his son Yuji through his friends to complete his scheme.
