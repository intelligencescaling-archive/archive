Kenjaku's intelligence story-wise

*why i think kenjaku ranks and scales high despite , some saying he's low-mid tier by xyz or methodlogy ??*

Simply put, I believe they can neither scale and rank characters properly nor read and discern their intelligence, while claiming to know how smart a character is. I've found that most stories clearly show how intelligent a character is, both in SCD and non-SCD terms (note: the term SCD is used arbitrarily here).

# JJK Powersystem

JJK's powersystem tells us enough about Kenjaku's intelligence. In JJK, powers are dependent on the body.

Kenjaku's cursed technique reflects his scientific mindset and his genuine interest in exploring the system, something his CT inherently allowed him to do and further improve upon.

His CT basically demonstrates this exploration and scientific approach through body-swapping, accessing inherent memories and CTs, which help him further explore and research CTs — something he considers important.

"Optimizing the cursed energy."

Kenjaku has optimized the fun of exploring and experimenting with the system, judging by the main story and backstory in JJK as a whole.

One thing people forget is that Kenjaku was able to use Domain Expansion in Geto's body. And not just any Domain Expansion, but an OPEN DOMAIN EXPANSION, a rare type that signifies his exceptional understanding of barrier techniques and his confidence in utilizing his domain without a conventional closed barrier.

Considering the fact that most sorcerers in JJK don't fully understand how or why their cursed techniques work, Kenjaku's understanding of how his techniques work is significant. That's why he can utilize his cursed techniques in his operations and master the techniques available through the bodies he inhabits.

You know who else understands how their CTs work? Sukuna.

# HIS ACHIEVEMENTS

His biggest achievement, IMO, in his experiments involving the body in JJK is the reincarnation loop.

Basically, the story establishes a complex relationship between the body and the soul, and Kenjaku's experiments with cursed objects and contracts allowed him to exploit this relationship. However, Kenjaku also believed that the body and soul were interconnected in a way that influenced how his own CT manifested.

This loophole was so extraordinary that it literally took Angel, who possesses a technique capable of extinguishing cursed techniques, to oppose Kenjaku's plans. And that's how he gathered reincarnated sorcerers for his New Heian Era plan.

Then there's the Cursed Womb: Death Paintings, created through experiments involving cursed spirits and human bodies.

And, of course, the birth of Yuji Itadori - the perfection of the cursed body.

# Social Espionage

Kenjaku has demonstrated social espionage throughout JJK on multiple occasions ; during the creation of the Death Paintings, his attempts to eliminate the Six Eyes user, and his ability to hide his existence from Tengen and other sorcerers for centuries while still influencing jujutsu society as a whole.

His influence over the disaster curses also shows that he can navigate and maneuver through social settings.

On top of that, the Culling Game itself confirms his social intelligence overall.

# Planning

Kenjaku's failure to eliminate the Six Eyes user doesn't make him less intelligent. If anything, it highlights the scale of his planning.

He waited centuries while gathering the right information and acquiring the necessary tools, such as the Prison Realm, to deal with the Six Eyes user.

And he was doing all of this while operating against Tengen, who had control over the vast majority of jujutsu society's resources.

The fact that Kenjaku couldn't immediately overcome these obstacles doesn't make him less intelligent. Rather, it highlights the scale of the challenge he faced and the extent of his long-term planning.
