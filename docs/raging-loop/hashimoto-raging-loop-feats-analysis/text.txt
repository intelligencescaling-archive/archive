This document will cover Hashimoto’s feats within the Darkness route of Raging Loop, as he is either dead in the other loops, or barely does anything SCD note-worthy. Hashimoto is a photographer who works alongside Hisako Mamiya (a journalist)- the pair alongside Haruaki are the only “outsiders” to the village and this is especially important to note as during the feasts, the village are very much a tight-knit group who struggle to work with outsiders, making Hashimoto more impressive in his maneuvers.

I will cover the Feast of Yomi-Purge, its rules and its participants here.

Feast of Yomi Purge (Darkness Route) Day 1:

Hashimoto is able to survive the first night and thus is able to participate in the Feasts of Yomi-Purge to determine who are the wolves thanks to Haruaki’s efforts to prevent his early death and create a new timeline so he can explore alternate fates.

In this loop, Haruaki himself, Haru Makishima and Kaori Oribe are the wolves. This will be more relevant later. Due to Haruaki’s efforts in procuring places for everyone and ensuring no rules of the feast are broken on the first night- there are no casualties, and as such Hashimoto finally survives his first night (previously he’d be killed for rule-breaking as he was unaware of the feast’s processes).

As a result of there being no rule-breaking participants on the first night, a large group of wild-beasts are killed and dismembered violently to show what would happen to rule-breakers. Furthermore, despite waking up to this and being told that he and Hisako (his journalist partner) have no way to escape, remains completely composed and accepts to have things explained to him later, all the while calming down Hisako. (composure, mental fortitude, stress tolerance, self-management, emotional management, empathy)

While having the feast explained, Hashimoto remains completely calm and inquires as to how you know if you have a guardian. As the feast progresses, participants (Nosato voices this) decide that someone has to hang and Haruaki is a good choice due to his reasoning for coming to Yasumisu (having “given up on suicide and looking for a fresh start”).

Here Hashimoto interjects, laying out his reasoning for why they shouldn’t hang anyone today- he starts by saying since no-one has died yet, killing someone at the feast may push the wolves to start killing (NOTE that since this is the “first” feast for many participants here- due to branching loops- some people still believe the wolves may not kill at all, although several participants insist they will) and would justify them doing so.

Furthermore, he explains that it’s not worth killing someone on the first day regardless, as there’s no reason to do it beyond allowing the crow to reveal themselves- however, even considering that it's possible that this crow is  a wolf and is lying, unless another crow spoke up against them. (probabilistic reasoning, abductive reasoning)

His final reasoning is to keep the group unified- if they kill someone and they turn out to be innocent, it’s sure to sow discord amongst each other, however if they wait one day and the wolves actually do kill- the humans can then unite over avenging the murder. (social skills + awareness + integration, inductive reasoning, emotional awareness, persuasion, negotiation, influence building, concrete + fast + linear + convergent thinking)

He continues to build onto Hisako’s point, noting that today they’ll learn from the snake about the identity of one person. Having laid out a lot of valid reasoning and just before the vote on whether to hang someone begins, Hashimoto finally reveals that he’s the monkey alongside Chicamocchi. (strategy building, influence building, social integration, anticipation)

The feast ends with no-one being hung and the mood is stated to be far more cheerful, with Hashimoto “boosting morale”. He’s even stated to be able to get Haru Makishima to faintly smile (typically very antagonistic towards outsiders) and gets Chiemi to open up about her studies. (social skills + integration + strategy building, charisma, relationship management, empathy)

While discussing the legend of the feast of Yomi Purge with Hisako and the group, Hashimoto notices and picks up on the existence of the “badger” in the song, but finds it odd how no-one mentioned it as a guardian.

Despite any conclusive evidence, Hashimoto reasons (correctly, but he’s not got proof yet) of the existence of the badger. Hisako goes further into the legend and begins to look into the origins of the name of the supposed God - “Shin’nai-san” - which causes minor outbursts from the more religious of the group, however, he swiftly diffuses the situation by noting that he and Hisako were just ensuring that they don’t miss anything important as the legends were passed down via word of mouth. (abductive reasoning, social skills + charisma)

Hashimoto chooses to talk to Yasunga, pointing out that they’d agreed to never lie - so that way when someone does (and they’d be the most likely to be a wolf) they slip up and hang them - raising the issue that if a badger exists (human siding with the wolves), they’d lie or do something drastic for the wolves sake. Hashimoto does this to prompt a decision to hang any compulsive liar, even if they’re human (this is important as it sets up a trap to counter the badger whether or not it exists AND prevents humans from lying for no reason)  Yoshitsugu takes the bait and immediately says this outloud, without Hashimoto needing to do so himself. (emotional understanding + awareness, social + indirect + individual manipulation, strategy building + execution, setting traps, abductive reasoning)

As the day continues, Hisako tries to talk to Kanzo about the previous feasts he was in, however, Hashimoto stops her, seemingly to prevent Haru from potentially freaking out once more. (emotional perception + awareness)

By the end of the day, Hashimoto is heavily suspicious of Haruaki for a few reasons: notably, Hashimoto notes that Haruaki was the reason they were able to acquire rooms: they’d been trapped in the village due to their tyres mysteriously bursting (Haruaki burst them) and thus appears to have known about the feast despite seemingly being an outsider just moving there- this explains his later suspicions and tactics to either confirm Haruaki as an ally or root him out. (convergent thinking, abductive reasoning)

Feast of Yomi Purge (Darkness Route) Day 2:

Night arrives, and the wolves are unable to kill tonight as Chiemi kills herself with a shotgun- OUTSIDE. Due to the nature of her death, her corpse is badly ravaged and the shotgun disappears to make it appear to be the “corruption” (punishment for rulebreaking).

There’s little to note regarding the day up until the feast here.

During the feast, Yasunaga begins, listing possible causes of Chiemi’s death- accidentally wandering outside, not returning home, or killed by wolves in a way to frame it as the corruption. The group quickly note that it’s impossible to stay awake even if you wanted to and several participants vouch for this, as a result they conclude she couldn’t have gone outside had she been at home (ruling out accidental).

Yasunaga then rules out the “framed as corruption” theory, noting that Chiemi’s futon wasn’t laid out and thus she never intended to sleep to begin with- suggested that perhaps she’d stayed outside all along. As a result, Yasunaga concludes that maybe she truly was killed by the corruption, and that the wolves hadn’t killed yet.

Haruaki steps in and suggests Chiemi killed herself, which explains the lack of futon and argues the corpse may have been destroyed due to wild beasts.

Hashimoto joins in, noting that as a former warzone photographer, the way her head was scattered about - with “pieces reaching high up the wall” that it was probably a large gun wound from point blank range. (abductive reasoning, linear thinking)

This leads to suspicion on Kanzo Makishima, the hunter of the group who always carries a firearm, however Hashimoto quickly shuts this down- he explains that typically the only time blood/pieces splatter so highly are due to placing the gun in someone’s mouth and firing- which would be difficult, noting that if it were a murder, the angle would’ve been more likely to be at an even or downward angle. (abductive reasoning, application thinking, VSI)

Hashimoto’s reasoning supports Haruaki’s claim. However another issue arises with the lack of weapon/ammo. Hashimoto reasons that since Chiemi’s room was open, it’s possible they stole the gun.

Due to her death presumably being a suicide and Rikako suggesting Chiemi had killed herself to protect another, Hashimoto concludes that she didn’t have a guardian. (probabilistic + deductive reasoning)

The vote is held, and the group decide that someone has to be lynched today- (9-6 split in favour of hanging). Hashimoto brings up the snake before the lynching vote is held, saying if they have found a wolf they should come out- after not getting any response, he asks if anyone else wants to be investigated before anyone else - this trap is especially impressive as it specifically attacks Haruaki- who due to his nature and typically vocal involvement in the feast, would want to be cleared of suspicion naturally, in the event that he doesn’t raise his hand this happens: Haruaki is quickly pointed out for his suspicious behaviour alongside Takumi who also didn’t raise his hand, Takumi here goes to reveal he is the snake, and Haruaki being at the end of his rope does the same, this leads to the pair lashing out at each other, violently claiming the other to be a liar, Hashimoto steps in prevent the pair from getting physical and after a brief discussion, Haruaki is voted to be hung - otherwise, Haruaki raises his hand asking to be investigated. (trap setting, strategy control + execution + fortitude, tactics, connective thinking, social awareness)

This leads to the group deciding who the snake should investigate first, and Hashimoto immediately suggests Haruaki, framing it as since Haruaki is decently clever, it’d be good to know for certain that he’s human. Furthermore, since Haruaki has been selected to be investigated, it saves him from being voted for, which looks like a kindness and diffuses potential suspicion. (strategy control + trap setting, tactics, logical reasoning, social awareness)

Haruaki counters by suggesting that the snake should investigate Takumi first as he’s incredibly useful to the feast, going so far as to say that if they suspect him, they can hang him right now. As a result, Hashimoto is unable to push back and has to readily accept the outcome, however his lack of ready acceptance may suggest he’s figured out that Takumi is the snake and thus agreeing to "investigate Takumi” would be pointless, however, the more likely case is just that he was still very suspicious of Haruaki.

The feast ends with The Old Man who Cried Wolf being hung.

Just before the day ends, Hashimoto makes note that no-one should hold any private conversations during the night as it’s far too easy for people to eavesdrop- it’s possible he does this to protect Takumi and has also deduced his role as the true snake, but lacks anything concrete/doesn’t want to compromise his role.

Yomi-Purge Feast Rules:

The Feast of Yomi-Purge is a tradition that occurs whenever the “mists” arrive in the town of Yasumizu. During which, up to 3 people become “wolves” and are tasked with murdering the rest of the village.

The villagers are supposed to uncover who the wolves are and lynch them to counter this.

___________________
There are a total of 6 roles within the feasts:

Human Side:
1- The Snake; is able to investigate one person each night to discover if they are a human or wolf, however they are not able to discern the role of the human they investigate (if they had one, so in other words, if they investigate the crow- they’ll only know that the person is human, not that they’re the crow).

2- The Crow; is able to investigate whether the person who was lynched during the feast is a human or a wolf- this allows the group to confirm the total number of remaining wolves- however, it is unable to investigate corruption related deaths.

3- The Spider; is able to protect one person each night- if the wolves attempt to kill this person, they will be unable to and thus won’t be able to kill anyone that night.

4- The Monkey; there are two monkeys within each feast, they know who the other monkey is, otherwise they have no power (this can be used to instantly rule out two humans, and since the Monkeys don’t have other powers, it’s unlikely for them to be killed early).

Wolf Side:
1- The Wolves; there are up to three wolves in each feast, on the first day of the feast this is revealed by bloodied symbols to the group (the number of symbols = the number of wolves) they are able to kill one person each night, however are unable to kill more than that. By killing more than one person, they will die from corruption.

2- The Badger; the badger is the only guardian to join later in the game. They are tasked with aiding the wolves, however, are considered human regardless (thus if the crow investigates them assuming they’re lynched/the snake investigates them, they’ll be found to be human).

______________________
The Corruption:

The corruption is the seemingly supernatural aspect of the feast. Corruption will kill anyone who breaks any rules.

The rules of the feasts are as follows: as a human you must shelter, cleanse and dream. If you break one of these rules, you are at risk of being killed that night. Furthermore if you break the rule of no more than one lynch per day (i.e. you as a human kill someone else for whatever reason, you’re at risk of corruption).

The wolves must also shelter, cleanse and dream, however, are woken up at roughly 10pm so that they may leave and decide on who to kill. When wandering around in the mist, they must spray water behind them while wearing a special costume to protect themselves from the mist. The wolves also cannot break the rule of one kill per day for them, or else they’re also at risk of corruption.

There is much more to the feasts that I’ve not covered, but this is due to it being a MAJOR spoiler and also fairly irrelevant to the scaling of Hashimoto.
