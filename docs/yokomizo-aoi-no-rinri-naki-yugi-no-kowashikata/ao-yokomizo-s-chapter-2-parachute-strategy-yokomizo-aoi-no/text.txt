Ao Yokomizo chapter 2 strategy

This is a character from LN Yokomizo Aoi No Rinri Naki Yugi No Kowashi Kata, and I think he's a new solid mid-high tier, so I decided to analyze his first strategy.

Context
Ao is running out of money, so he decides to take part in a game organized by a large corporation where he can earn money. In short it's something like a battle royale with 60 people in Tokyo. All players wear collars that explode if they break the rules or run out of points. To win you need to earn 55 points, which can be earned by killing other players. All players are also given a tablet where they can assign a hunt for a specific player and know the locations of other players. There are a few other small details, but they are not very important. The very beginning takes place on an airplane, where the first group of players are located.

At the very beginning, when Ao gets on the plane, the first game is announced. It consists of jumping out of the plane with a parachute. If you don't jump out within 30 minutes, you die. There are 19 players on the plane, but only 10 parachutes are provided. When plane's hatch opens, everyone rushes to the parachutes and starts fighting over them. He recognizes this brand of parachutes, they are single-seaters with a maximum load of 260 lbs. He immediately realizes that 260 lbs is quite a lot of weight, and that everyone might be able to survive if they jump in pairs. He starts screaming to attract attention, but no one notices him, so he takes out the hammer he brought with him and starts hitting the wall of the plane so that everyone would notice him. When it got too noisy, everyone stopped for a second and looked at him, and then he immediately began to explain his plan to them. When he explained that they could jump in pairs, everyone became interested and continued to listen to him. He continued and asked everyone to tell him their weight and give him any jackets, belts, or towels they had. When everyone agreed with his idea, he began asking everyone their weight, giving parachutes to the heavier ones and tying the lighter ones together with jackets and belts they gave him (this is because if you tie a heavier person to a lighter one, the heavier one will tip the lighter one over and tangle the slings, and the suspension system will better withstand the load if the main weight is pressed against the backpack). The further he went, the more difficult it became to find a combination that would fit within 260 lbs, but he managed it, and only he and another man weighing 285 lbs remained. He decided to give the last parachute to him and hope that the parachute would hold, and then only he was left without a parachute.

Then host approached him and congratulated him on saving all the players except himself, and that he was the only one she had ever seen who thought about jumping in pairs and managed to do it. Then she warned him that there were only 10 minutes left and gave him a choice: either she would shoot him, or he would jump without a parachute. Ao knew that the approximate height was 2 miles, because he had been looking out the window from the moment the plane took off until the game began, so he knew that if he jumped without a parachute, he would definitely not survive. So he just smiled and threatened to knock her out. Host aimed her golden gun at him and repeated that he would either jump or she would shoot him anyway. She also decided to ask his name, to which he confidently replied that his name was Genoside Edogawa (yes that's a Conan reference). Host ofc didn't believe that was his name and said she could check his real name at any time and that there was no point in lying. Ao confidently insisted that it was his real name (it's not his real name btw), but she decided to check his real name anyway and typed something into her phone and was surprised, as the database actually registered it as his real name. (most likely, Ao simply entered a fake name during some kind of registration before the game started, so his fake name is displayed in the database). After that, host looked at the armed guard on the plane and asked him to go to the cockpit and tell headquarters to check his name again. Guard nodded and walked away through the metal door. When they were left alone, he smirked and said that as a criminal, she's ass because she looked away when she was left alone with the great detective (a little more context: he's son of a famous detective). She asked what he meant, and he said he would destroy their game, and then said that there was a fire behind her. She looked back and noticed white smoke. In fact, Ao had thrown a smoke bomb he brought, wrapped in another player's towel when she turned away. Due to the towel and the loud hum of the aircraft engine, no one heard the smoke bomb rolling across the floor. Because of the smoke, she began to panic and look for a fire extinguisher and while she was doing so, he approached her and knocked her out with a stun baton that he had also brought onto the plane. When she passed out, she fell into his arms and dropped her golden gun, which he picked up. Now Ao decides that it might be possible to hijack the plane, since he cannot escape without a parachute but if he hijacks plane, the other hosts might remotely detonate his collar for breaking the rules. However, Ao then realizes that host is a very valuable person, since she has such expensive weapons compared to the guard, and besides, even before the game started, the security guard behaved as if he would die himself if the host was injured. But while he was thinking, guard approached him, pointed a gun at his head, and asked him to drop the host and throw away the gun. Then it becomes clear to Ao that   host is a very important person. Because if the guard wanted to shoot him, he would have done so already, and in fact he was afraid of hitting host with a bullet, so Ao quickly put on the handcuffs he had also brought with him. He showed guard that he had strapped her to himself, and while the guard was confused, he hit him with his baton and knocked him out. Then Ao went into the cockpit and found another spare parachute there. He put it on, strapped the hostess to himself with a belt, and jumped out of the plane. Now real hosts cannot kill him with the collar for breaking the rules, because this could harm an important person whom he has tied to himself with handcuffs.

I think that's good strategy that requires crazy AC, EQ, PSI, PRI and VSI
