Before we get started, a clarification between a debunk and a hypothetical must be established: a debunk despite seeming broad and intuitive is often misunderstood. A debunk is an argument that aims to undermine the validity of a particular claim in a verse using already established propositions.  Which is to elucidate that the implementation  of a hypothetical “X doing Y would have resulted in X’s victory against S therefore X not being good at z category” is fundamentally flawed as for any proposition P, not P would exist which would be outside the established narrative of a particular character or verse (insofar as not P isn’t actually present or elucidated in the verse, that would then result in the feat getting contradicted and thereby debunked)
