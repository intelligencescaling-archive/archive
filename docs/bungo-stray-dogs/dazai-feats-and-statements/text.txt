★What happened in Prison Game Arc:-

Fyodor and Dazai are given lethal injections which will lead to their death in 30 minutes. The lethal injections are to be injected by Fyodor and Dazai to themselves which are described by Fyodor as a piece of art.

Fyodor then smilingly injects the lethal poison to himself, as well as Dazai. They are then given information about the facility's structure and security which will be obstacles and will hinder them from escaping the place. The two are soon presented with four items on which each one of them is allowed to bring an aid to their escape.

 After explanations of each of the items, Nikolai lets Fyodor choose his weapon first. Fyodor proceeds to choose a key card as his weapon.
Five minutes into their duel,Fyodor has not yet moved.

He instead assumes that one of his pawns has started moving, to which Fyodor smirks in response. As an intruder wipes out an entire floor's battalion, Fyodor gets surrounded by Meursault's forces, while Gogol teleports away. Fyodor is instantly rescued by the undead Chūya Nakahara, who turns out to be his next pawn against Dazai and Sigma.

As they successfully make their escape, Fyodor and Chūya soon come across a wrong passcode, which turns out to have been changed by Dazai. Containment shutdown initiates, and Fyodor has Chūya break through the walls, but to no avail. They eventually get flooded in heavy water while Fyodor continues to fail inputting the passcode.

Dazai then communicates with Fyodor and Chūya from the control room, who goads Fyodor into being defeated. Though Fyodor is confident that Dazai cannot kill him, the detective feels likewise that Chūya would not be able to break through the walls and use his gravity manipulation that easily, nor would Fyodor resort to use his ability, regardless what it is, much to Fyodor's visible ire.
