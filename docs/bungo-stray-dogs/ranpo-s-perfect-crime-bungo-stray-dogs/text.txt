Ranpo's Perfect Crime

Title. I'm not sure if this is ranpo's best, but its an example I've seen brought up. After reading the manga, I've decided to write something about it.

Ranpo's goals: Find the 'cleaner' that erased the evidence to prove Kunikida's innocence. Chapters are 54-56 for people that don't know, since I'm too lazy to type out everything that happens. I'll also be looking at the situation from Ranpo's pov.

When the body fell: The red envelope containing the novel was placed next to a sign telling them to look up, before the 'cleaner's' body fell in front of their eyes. From this, it could be deduced that the person that put the envelope there was either related to the seller or was the seller themselves.

Upon investigation of the fake body: The body was fake since the shoes didn't fit. Ranpo confirmed that the shoes were that of the cleaner, but since the shoes didn't fit, the body was likely not the cleaner.

Erasure of the crime: This is something I want to call out. What evidence exactly was erased? The shoes were still there, but we have no idea what evidence was erased.

After these events, Ranpo's next steps were only shown after he confronted the criminal.

Reading the novel: Ranpo said that he read through the last chaoter of the novel. From there, he realised that the last page was photocopied, and a hastily made one at that. Why would the seller only photocopy the last page if he wanted to keep the original? Since in was an hastily made photocopy, it would be hard to change anything on the last page. There were water marks on the last page of the photocopy, which means that the original had to have been wet somehow. Since the seller would likely not want to touch it due to its value, the drops had to have come right as it was finished, as ranpo deducted.

Going to the gift shop: Tbh I felt this was unecessary. Its impossible to tell what the water came from without lab analysis, so the original really didn't do much. Also there was really no justification why the seller didn't just throw away the original copy due to that. Maybe I'm missing something but idk.

That just leaves with a few remainding questions that I haven't covered yet.

1. How are the seller and the cleaner connected? Ranpo came to find the cleaner, so it wouldn't be surprising that the cleaner knew beforehand. (A common bs trope in BSD) So the cleaner obviously wanted to throw ranpo of his trail. And the fact that the red envelope lead to the murder scene means that the seller and killer were either a) in cahoots, b) cleaner stole it from seller who knew that Ranpo would be there, or c) one and the same. As far as I know, I can't draw any reasonable deductions that lead ranpo to figuring out that the answer was c, maybe I missed something.

2. The identity of the cleaner. The cleaner used a fake name + fake image, and somehow ranpo still figured out who tf they were. There was no evidence at all pointing to the guy but somehow ranpo figured it out. So far what happened and how it happened was clear, and by some deduction (that imo is a bit of a stretch) why it happened. But not who.

If I'm missing something please tell me. Also if any more information was given in the ln it would help too.
