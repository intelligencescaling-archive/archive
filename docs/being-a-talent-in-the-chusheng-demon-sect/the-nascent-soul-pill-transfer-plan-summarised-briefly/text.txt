The Nascent Pill Transfer Plan, Summarised Briefly - Being a Talent in Chusheng Demon Sect (Batics)

So actually Nascent Soul Pill plan is part of multiple schemes. Im summarising this crudely cuz its dispersed among 100s of chapters

1. Originally, Dan Ting wanted First Saint to not ascend further on the Other Shore, so he created a recipe to make this Nascent Soul Pill in the Underworld.
He named it Nascent Soul Pill to make people fight iver it and used his reputation as  the Lord of Pill refinement to make various faction believe in the feasability of this plan.

He specifically contacted Mana Path Dao Lords, among whom some had recently reached Underworld to get resources for its refinement. The Dao Lords agreed to this and have been sending materials for it.

He made it so that his Junior Brother Dao Tianqi can deduce his plan to create this pill without alerting the First Saint (I forgot the exact methods).

He then proceeds to decisively kill himself because he anticipated that First Saint might control him using names, he also leaving a method for someone else to temporarily assume his identity to refine this pill, while also ensuring that the First Saint's killing move with "Fixed Number"\[which prefixes the destiny of a target\] can be evaded by shedding this identity.

He also made clues in the overworld to point toward Nascent Soul Pills, but have Life and Death Reincarnation pill there, which can help one enter underworld and also acts as an id card which could make the brothers recognise that the holder of the pill is part of his plan

2. Dao Tianqi's original plan wih the Underworld was to make it like another Other Shore like First Saint has done, so that more Dao Lords can be supported. This was detected by First Saint and the Dao Lords of Other Shore, who made Dao Tianqi undergo an eternal slumber.

Dao Tianqi knowing he might be targeted, prepares a contingency in \[Remnant Conciousness of Celestial Beings\] to get back to his underowrld position when he could. Also made  it diagrams of the Underworld so whomever visits this place and triggers his consciousness can also help him in the Underworld, due to the valuable stuff there (Void stones)

After awakening, he proceeds to obfuscate Underworld's operations to the First Saint, trying to make him beleive that he was elevating his position but instead refining this pill.

He chooses a candidate to use Dan Ting's identity to refine the pill, while also making arrangements to share his wisdom light, which Dan Ting had foreseen he could due to the Names.

3. World Honoured One always was vary of First Saint actually turning hostile (He was their Master who was v friendly and supportive w them).

He had already planned on proving the Heterdox Dao of Karma through the use of City Wall Earth Fruit Position, which essentially lets him travel back in time to tranfer information or items, but he can't modify much at all.

He also does this as a way to curry favor with First Saint, who holds the Dao of Time by stabilising the River of Time with Karma, but secretely make it so that First Saint would take time to detect what had changed in the Karma/ Past

He had always anticipated betraying First Saint, so he had acted out specific scenes in the past, which can instead be used to exchange information and items. This was also known to Dan Ting, Dao Tianqi and Heaven Mending Defect (His 3 Junior Brothers). Thus they would leave contingencies too, deducing each others plans because they had characterised the other so well.

He also only ascended to 2nd layer of the Other Shore and reegularly makes contact with Mortals to descend his consciousness thru a special technique. This prolonged contact makes others think that since he can't ascend, he is just playing with the mortals, but the World Honoured One has been doing this to conspire in the Mortal World in secret and exert larger influence in the world cuz he is closer (lower in status)

He also made sure to get the role of Gatekeeper of the Underworld after Dao Tainqi slept, so that he can one day allow Dan Ting or his conspirator to enter the Underworld without alerting First Saint

4. Heaven Mending Peak was currently hidden in the seal of Ancestor Dragon, hollowing out the base of The Other Shore upon which all Dao Masters/ Nascent Souls derive their status from.

He had made sure to hide from First Saint only by using the Dao of Yin which he loots at Si Sui's legacy.

Also anticipates that Dan Ting might need someone to transfer the Nascent Soul Pill to the Other Shore for it to take its effect. But he cannot interact with anyone, or else he will be discovered.

So he leaves a smokescreen legacy in the Chusheng Sect, which appears as a inheritance ground on the outside, but can be used as a method to contact his true body at the base of the Other Shore.

He also, before saying his final goodbyes to the World Honoured One in the past, gets an item of protection from the World Honoured One, but this scene can also be used to alter past and get the Nascent Soul Pill instead, which he can transfer to the Other Shore in present timeline.
