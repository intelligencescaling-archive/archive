**Background:**

In this world, there used to be only one Soul Transformation Realm existence and that is the Absolute End of everything which later fused with First Saint.

That was until one certain genius found a way to ascend to Soul Transformation.

The goal of the Absolute End is as the name suggests, to end the entire existence which results to a brand new existence.

The second Soul Transformation Realm seems to oppose that. So the two existence has a war which they fight by using pawns.

From then on an infinite cycle was created.

Whenever The Soul Transformation Cultivator succeeds in the chess game, he will successfully cultivate another Soul Transformation Cultivator which will replace him and the former Soul Transformation Cultivator will ascend to a higher unknown realm. The cycle continues...

Whenever a Soul Transformation Cultivator loses, he will serve as nourishment for the next world.

**Jun Daoist**

Jun Daoist is one of the Cultivators stuck in this endless cycle, his goal is simply to win the game against the Absolute End.

Thus he foresaw and planned everything in the story, he gave everyone equal chances probably due to his morals and his entire plan probably has the highest efficiency in fiction.

His entire plan which spanned for 1400+ chapters and crazy timescale inside the story was done through one single move.

Give the Book of Hundred Lives to Lü Yang. Later on, Lü Yang tells us that when he looked back and processed everything he realized that if even one single event were to slightly change then Jun Daoist' plan wouldn't have worked.

The goal of his plan was to turn First Saint into a gate so that everyone else can ascend to the higher realm.

So why is this Top Tier? Without context this only looks like it would've been Very High\~Extreme High Tier only.

Aside from Crazy time scale, so much events happen in a single year in this world.

Karma is also something that connects everything in this world, meaning one small action can affect various variables in ways that doesn't exist in our world.

Quality of cognition: A Qi Refining Realm Cultivator would get disintegrated out of existence if they comprehend things that only Foundation Establishment Cultivators should know. This is because cognition literally transcends one another, that is: cognition of higher realm transcends the former. It's just like how a 8d being will beat a 3d being in powerscaling, but intelligence wise.

Unlike other stories, the narrative of this story goes crazy as a Qi Refining prodigy would've been equivalent to how we perceive people with 180+ iq, now imagine that there are apparently 5 Major Realms that transcends one another and each of those Major Realm have the minor realms with significant gaps.

And there's so many such people with their own sets of schemes.

According to Lü Yang's statements and authorial intent, Jun Daoist should've foreseen and planned everything since Lü Yang who became the Venerable Lord said his plan would've gone wrong if the small events where to deviate. As such Jun Daoist even hinted it himself that the plan was risky.

So yeh, that should give enough explanation why he is among my Top 10 in Normal/Narrative Scaling.
