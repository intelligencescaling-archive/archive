PRE CHAPTER 1 LORE:

Fang Yuan did a 30-year plan resulting in the deaths of thousands of people to refine the spring-autumn cicada, a gu insect that can make its user teleport his will and soul to the former self of his past. It’s the same kind of feat as creating a low-tier time machine, but it’s more complex because of the conditions RI has for one to create the gu (Long term Planning, Knowledge , Thinking)

1. Creating a rank 6 gu insect is an impossible task for a gu immortal as it requires a high amount of knowledge in other paths, luck, other skills, and decision-making abilities. (Knowledge, Intelligence)
2. This particular insect has a success rate of 1-10% even though it got used perfectly because of the limitations heavens gave it. (Risk assessment, knowledge, intelligence)
3. In the RI verse, time is regulated by a spiritual concept called the river of time, which holds the infinite dao of time, and one must move against the current of the river of time to go to the past. (“DEPENDS ON THE SCALING IT SCALES TO 6D TO OUTERVERSAL , ANOTHER IMPORTANT THING TO UNDERSTAND IS THIS SENTENCE DEFINES NOTHING ABOUT FANG YUAN’S COSMOLOGY AS I ONLY INCLUDED THE COSMOLOGY OF THE GREAT RIVER OF TIME HERE “)

4. An immortal gu casually has at least 1000+ steps, which the gu master has to do in perfect harmony to create the gu with no errors. (CPI, faster thinking, multitasking)

Fang Yuan has to perfect his rank 6 gu insect to beat all these conditions to move his present self to the past.

Debunk attempt: Fang Yuan replicated the methods of Red Lotus Demon Venerable, so it’s not acceptable.
Truth: Red Lotus is a rank 9 gu master, and most importantly, he’s the person who held the dao of time, while Fang Yuan at that time was a rank 6 who held the grandmaster attainment in blood path, so it’s the same as a 5-year-old doing research about university-level quantum physics.
Also, Fang Yuan has no one to help him directly to make this gu successfully.

DEMON JUDGEMENT BOARD: Since fang yuan became a gu immortal of blood path, an organisation named demon judgement board is dedicated to neutralising him, and they keep sending gu immortals (rank 6) and mortal gu masters (rank 4.5) to chase after fang yuan and to make him perish, which fang yuan anticipated their moves most of the time and escaped from, and his goal of creating a spring autumn cicada is also linked to him escaping from this organisation (foresight, planning, AQ))

Fang yuan was once an unknown named Chinese scholar in modern earth (estimated age: 20), where he got sent off to the gu world by mysterious means and got transmigrated inside baby Fang yuan from gu yue village.

And after Fang Yuan got reborn in his first life, he learnt how to make poetry in the Gu world’s language, and he’s also skilled in Tang and Song Dynasty’s poems in his earth self. (VCI, Intelligence)

He himself recreated many poems of the earth in the world’s language and got himself a name in his childhood itself to avoid the bullying of his stepparents, aunts, and uncles.
(Minor scale trap evasion)

His 15 yr old self got forcefully injected with the memories of 535+ year old fang yuan and his 15 yr old self still had the ability to hold up most of the information about his future self in his 15 yr old self (Long term memory , Overall memory , Mental cognition)
