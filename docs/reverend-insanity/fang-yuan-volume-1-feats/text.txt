1 to 30 odds
This means that out of 31 possible outcomes, odds are that there will be 1 of one kind of outcome and 30 of another kind of outcome.

For every 31, odds are that 1 will be a particular event and 30 will be another event.

1 to 30 probability
There is a 3.23 percent probability of a particular outcome and 96.77 percent probability of another outcome.

1 to 30 odds payout
If you bet 1 on a game with 1 to 30 odds and you win, your total payout will be 1.03 which is your bet plus 0.03 profit.

Odds vs. Probability
Note that odds and probability are not the same. Odds compare one event to another event, whereas probability compares one event to both possible events. Thus, when the odds are 1 to 30, the fraction for the Odds is 1/30, but the fraction to calculate the probability is 1/31.
