Reverend Insanity fan, How high do you think Fang Zheng (End Of Series) scaled in SCD, is he a fodder or nah?

Fang Zheng is the little brother of Fang Yuan, he has A-grade talent and was the disciple of Gu Yue Bo the clan leader of the Gu Yue Clan. In the Great Era, he rose to become Demon Judgment Board Lord as well as member of Heavenly Court, participating in their key battles as support.

Small Feats Listing (Helped by the wiki since I haven't reach volume 2) :

-He have way too many anti feat in volume 1

-In Volume 2, his name becoming renowned in the lower echelons of Immortal Crane Sect for claiming first place in the competition and becoming an elite disciple after defeating his competitor Sun Yuan Hua in the finals.

[ Influnce Building?, Influnce ]

-At the point of volume 4, Fang Zheng was now fully grown, he had been training for 8 years inside Fu Hu Blessed Land, with this training and the use of Relic Gu that Immortal Crane Sect provided he quickly became a rank 5 Gu Master with quasi-master attainment in enslavement path. Later on, he became sect elder of Immortal Crane Sect and started to experience the political competition of the (mortal) upper echelons.

[ AQ ]

-it was revealed that he was a fate escapee that should have died back at Qing Mao Mountain. And due to that, Heavenly Court ordered Immortal Crane Sect to execute him which was carried out by He Feng Yang. Unbeknownst to the other parties, Fang Yuan requested Tai Bai Yun Sheng to use Man as Before to resurrect Fang Zheng from an arm, he previously severed from Fang Zheng. At this point, he became a secret captive to Fang Yuan who started to indoctrinate and mentally break Fang Zheng into becoming his willing servant. Although he finally started to realize that his sect betrayed and unrighteously sentenced him to death, his hatred for his elder brother prevented him from being turned and instead started longing for the sweet release of death from this cycle of misfortune. His days were henceforth spent in captivity with indoctrination sessions by Fang Yuan.

[ Perseverance, Mental Fortitude, EM, Self Awareness ]

-After Fang Yuan moved out all of his possession from Hu Immortal Blessed Land into Lang Ya Blessed Land, Fang Zheng became a prisoner of the mortal hairy men living in the Lang Ya Blessed Land. After being tortured by them and sold as a slave to the female Steel Thread City Lord who took a liking to him and used him as a sex slave for some time. At one point the Steel Thread City Lord was forced to send Fang Zheng into the hairy men competition to compete with others in Gu refinement, at that time it was revealed that Fang Yuan left a fake will inside him.

[ EQ, AQ ]

-This will took over the role of indoctrinating Fang Zheng who slowly but surely started to develop feelings of understanding for Fang Yuan's action on Qing Mao Mountain and himself actions.

[ EU, ER ]

-Despite putting up a front he soon started to follow the instructions from his brother's fake will that helped him win the first match. After that Fang Zheng refined River Swallowing Toad under Fang Yuan's instructions. After his Rescue by Feng Jiu Ge from Lang Ya Blessed Land he is forced by Heavenly Court to cultivate Blood Path to best use him against Fang Yuan.

[ AQ ]

After he's captured by FY, got left in Lang Ya Blessed Land with his fake will, and participate in their hairyman continental war, Fang Zheng had since changed many of his trait. He was no longer hot-headed and could in fact understand to some degree Fang Yuan's actions, though he hasn't forgiven him for it. He's also no longer feel any pride over being chosen, nurtured, or even being a Gu Immortal. As he's gaining experience he also began to understand the cold nature of the world around him, knowing full well that he was always used even back in Gu Yue Clan. Through his experience he also possess enough humility to accept this as truth, in stark contrast to his youth's constant denial.
