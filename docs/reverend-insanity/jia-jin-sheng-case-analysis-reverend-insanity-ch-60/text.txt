Jia Jin Sheng Case Analysis and bit before that.

Reverend Insanity Chapter 60, the Jia Jin Sheng case comes to an end with an astonishing victory by Fang Yuan.

So I decided to naturally write an analysis on it explaining different parts and how it eventually fell apart.

Also I would like to ask some great questions regarding this.

Background: Fang Yuan was dominating the whole class by his ruthless nature. There are different foes here:

1. Mo Bei - He truly is a character for not giving up like the rest, bro should be respected for that.

2. Academy Elder - He was trying to suppress Fang Yuan's image

3. The Fighting Master - He was with Academy Elder

4. Jia Fu - Brother of Jia Jin Sheng who brought the whole case

5. Other clan members

First I would like to go with the whole story. So Fang Yuan buys 5 gambling rocks from gabling den. He purposefully, bought one with a mudskin toad. His entire intention was to bring the attention of Jia Jin Sheng. He bought a sixth gambling rock to reveal the Liquor Worm, without any attention.

Jia Jin Sheng falls into the trap. However Fang Yuan doesn't sell it, since he knows Mudskin Toad's value.

Jia Jin Sheng, is later on forced to pay 500 stones to Fang Yuan and they both walk away.

Fang Yuan then feeds monkey wine to Liquor Worm. This establishes two things, that liquor worm was hungry, so naturally it could've come from the sixth gambling rock. So Fang Yuan has now two people - the owner of the Gambling Rock Den and the waiter at the Wine Shop. The waiter tries persuading Fang Yuan but fails to do so.

Later on Jia Jin Sheng reputation is at stake because of his brother Jia Fu.

Fang Yuan uses this as an advantage. He is surely to be seen, but that's what he wants.

Jia Jin Sheng follows Fang Yuan to the Cave. When the image changed, Fang Yuan basically cold read Jia Jin Sheng at that time. Evidence linking is that Jia Jin Sheng thinks because of his reputation he cannot be killed, so he could trick Fang Yuan. But Fang Yuan kills him first.

Evidence is that, the image change mentioned about Flower Wine Monk's inheritance, knowing Jia Jin Sheng's character Fang Yuan kills him.

Then the next scene shift to Academy Elder failing again. He wanted to set Fang Yuan on consecutives fighting rounds so he can get tired and get defeated eventually. Fang Yuan gives up 20 stones, and refuses to fall under this scheme

Firstly Fang Yuan wants to stabilize his image, this establishes that no one would challenge Fang Yuan in future, and he cannot be disturbed in the future. Secondly he wouldn't have to waste too much of his energy on the academy gates during looting. The moment his image falls, he has to show his talent.

Then next stage is during Middle age advancement. Low tier feat but still.

Fang Yuan cultivates through night and eventually becomes dawn. He is late for the academy classes. The Academy Elder sent people after him. Then next second Fang Yuan walks in with the guard brutally injured.

First Why? Fang Yuan says that they were disturbing his cultivation. Elder is responsible now. Fang Yuan cultivation is checked, it is middle stage, so what  Fang Yuan wanted to establish as a doubt becomes evident.

He wanted the academy elder to think as to why his cultivation is fast.

Also at the same time Academy Elder is forced to accept his mistake on the behalf of guard.

Now this forced the academy elder to investigate and put detective on Fang Yuan.

Next the scene shifts towards the Class Chairmen. Essentially position matters more. Normal student is not equal to Class Chairmen, this in people's heart create a relief. Everyone would wanna be around a chairmen. However Fang Yuan refuses this position and later on beats the shit out of the chairmen, breaking the illusion of this superiority. This ultimately makes Academy Elder fail again.

Now the stage really begins, when Fang Yuan is brough in for questioning.

So the mediator of the gambling den rocks, blames Fang Yuan. However those blames are only to confirm whether if Fang Yuan met Jia Jin Sheng. Fang Yuan portrays himself as a weak and scared child, lying for the first half and then admitting.

Then the liquor worm, thing is brought up by the waiter and how he saw Jia Jin Sheng with Fang Yuan.

The scheme here is that Fang Yuan wanted that, so he turns Jia Fu's witness into his by saying that he bought the sixth gambling rock from the den. The girl says the truth. Then he explains how he went to Jia Jin Sheng, for price consultation. He exploits Jia Jin Sheng's nature and creates a reasoning which fits perfectly. Then he brought up Jia Fu's internal resentment and affairs, which immediately puts Gu Yue Bo into protective mode. Basically Jia Fu is attacking Gu Yue Clansmember, not just Fang Yuan, so Gu Yue Bo has to protect the image to prevent a war, if Fang Yuan folds under pressure. He doesn't.

Then he asks for a witness, which is academy elder, and the elder confesses about how he sent people to watch Fang Yuan and note down his daily routine. He explains how he could never had time even if he wanted to.

Jia Fu is at immediate pressure, but the internal affair thing by Fang Yuan puts his suspicion from Fang Yuan to Jia Gui.

Also displaying a coward behavior creates a fake image, inside Gu Yue Bo's mind that Fang Yuan was suppressed in the start, so now he is acting like that.

Jia Fu leave, Fang Yuan leaves.

Only Gu Yu Bo remains. He tells about Rules and Regulations to Academy Elder. He says how, Fang Yuan is just blinded by his rage, but will eventually succumbed to the clan and academy elder stops plotting against Fang Yuan which was a part of FY's plan, just mentioned above.

So overall I would say Political Knowledge, Emotional Manipulation, Deceiving, Verbal Trapping, Long Term planning and Facial Control came into play.

One can say that Fang Yuan has 500+ experience, however, most of it is in secluded cultivation. In terms of knowledge, he is a ahead of them, but only has a much more deep understanding than his other clansmate. He is much more experienced in fighting, gu worms etc.. Also he did established his own sect, but I don't think so it was truly around for more than 100 years, because 200 years then he broke through Rank three that's when he found a Rank Raising Gu, then going from Rank three to six with B grade aptitude as a demonic cultivator.

**Feat Analysis**

**1. Cold Reading** \- Fang Yuan cold read and saw through numerous schemes, including those by his peers.

**2. Long term planning** \- He successfully merged two planning. One was Liquor Worm revelation, which was initial but after Jia Jin Sheng he had to change course while maintaining the whole thing about Liquor Worm which was already revealed. So by humiliating the clan elder, he was able to make the clan elder as his witness, if Jia Fu further pushes him.

**3. Emotional Intelligence** \- It is evident that Fang Yuan did portray a lot of emotional manip. It shows the insight he has in human emotions, such as the acadmey elder, Jia Fu, Gu Yue Bo, the clansmate etc.

**4. Verbal Trapping -**  He tricks the Academy Elder into admitting he was spying on Fang Yuan. By doing this, Fang Yuan forces his enemy to legally provide him with a perfect alibi.

**5. Foresight -** He was basically letting everything fall according to his own, ways.

**Using my own structure now:**

**1. Time Complexity -** The whole thing was done under a month of planning (estimating).

**2. Variables -** The amount of variables are low for this to be a high tier feat

**3. Advantage -**  I would say Fang Yuan did have advantage but that it also a by a bit, like I said, he spent a lot of his previous life is secluded cultivation.

**4. Environment -** Both Fixed Situation (Clan's rules) and Non Fixed Situation (Murder case)

This feat is a Mid to High Mid tier feat at best. Not a high tier feat, there is lack of variables and cats like SQ, Information Processing, Working Memory etc.
