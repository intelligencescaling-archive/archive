Fang Yuan feat: Jia Jin Sheng Murder Misdirection Strategy

This is one of my favorite feats from Fang Yuan.

1: Fang Yuan chose to sell blackmail material against his own clan to a merchant named Jia Jin Sheng (due to Jia Jin Sheng's weakness and manipulability). This was also a planned setup for his future scheme of infiltrating the Jia family's arranged competition.

2: However, he got unlucky that at the exact moment he took Jia Jin Sheng to the cave where the blackmail material was present, it was revealed to be a high quality inheritance.

3: The inheritance was of high enough quality that Jia Jin Sheng would murder Fang Yuan for it, so Fang Yuan chose to decisively kill him first. This renders the pawn Jia Jin Sheng useless as a means of gaining access to the family's competition, which is why Fang Yuan thought it pitiful that Jia Jin Sheng had to die, but the inheritance was more valuable, so killing him was worth it.

4: After cleaning up the murder scene, Fang Yuan returned to the village and refrained from revisiting the cave, ignoring the inheritance temporarily to prevent suspicion (this would end up being around 1 month of cautiously refraining).

5: There were valuable gu worms present in Jia Jin Sheng's aperture, but he destroyed them to prevent any tracking links to the body.

6: Fortunately a heavy downpour swept away the smell of blood and helped clean up traces. This also delayed the return of the merchant caravan that Jia Jin Sheng was a part of. However, this lucky event was not actually completely necessary for the misdirection to succeed.

7: It was inevitable that they would return to investigate, since the leader of the caravan (Jia Fu) was entrusted with the safety of his brother Jia Jin Sheng as a test of his character, which would determine the share of inheritance that he receives.

8: To prepare for this investigation, Fang Yuan needed to set up a witness for himself. The witness he chose as most suitable was the academy elder, since he had the resources to investigate Fang Yuan, the factional neutrality to not refrain from speaking up as a witness for Fang Yuan (for some of the elders, speaking up may risk compromising political benefits relative to the other factions), and also the ideal conditions needed for Fang Yuan to practically put his setup into effect.

9: During the interim period of preparing for the upcoming investigation, Fang Yuan undergoes 33 consecutive battles against his classmates and forcefully control his breathing, expression, and posture, to intimidate his classmates into submitting to his weekly extortions. He even acts rebellious and angry against the martial arts instructor, which has the purpose of reinforcing his image to the students, while also attracting the attention of the academy elder he wants to set up as a witness.

10: In order for the elder to actively defend Fang Yuan, he needed to show his value to the clan and not just come off a harmful weed that needs to be cut off at the roots, so he showed his emotional state by acting rebellious against the martial instructor, and also by blowing up an upcoming issue which I'll explain later. This showcasing of vulnerability implies that he isn't detached or emotionless, which allows the academy elder to manipulate him into the clan structure through benefits, and also incentivises the clan leader and faction elders to prevent the academy elder's suppression of him, while supporting his defense in the upcoming investigation. However, Fang Yuan would need a far bigger setup to bring the attention of the elder onto himself, so he chose to advance to middle stage rank which would become a setup for his misdirection while also giving the elder incentive to investigate him (because it makes no sense for Fang Yuan to have reached middle rank the fastest among his classmates with his low talent unless he had external help).

11: However, when he reached middle stage, he chose to patiently wait in his room so that the elder sends guards to his house to question why he was absent from school. This is needed for 3 purposes. Fear inducing the students, forcing the elder to send spies after himself to save his own reputation, blowing the issue out of the water to have the factions suppress the academy elder's interference in the issues of the students, and setting up the misdirection in the upcoming investigation, by using this basis to bring up the liquor worm as an excuse for hiding information, the purpose of which was to make the elders believe he isn't competent at acting. This will be explained.

12: Fang Yuan does the above by cutting off a leg from one guard and the arm of the remaining guard. However, he chose to leave one guard behind and dragged the other one to the classroom, which made the students believe he had killed the guards. (inducing PTSD)

13: The act was justified for multiple reasons: Reason 1: The clan (and the entire gu world for that matter) values strength above all else, especially given the 3-way war between Fang Yuan's village and the other 2 villiages on the mountain. The elder punishing the student who rises in rank the fastest would be seen as suppression and discourage students from seeing hard work as supreme which contradicts the entire paradigm of the gu world. This would be neglecting his role as the academy elder who should be raising the students to become powerhouses for the clan. Reason 2: Mortals (who are weakest) have lives that are of no value relative to gu practitioners like Fang Yuan. Reason 3: Fang Yuan did not actually kill either of the guards. Thus it was not a crime for Fang Yuan. The legal structure of the mortal-level clan in a world focused on strength and cutting off weaklings is obviously not as sophisticated as law in real life.

14: Fang Yuan had also made up lies about the guards slandering the clan and barging in right when he was advancing to middle stage, which forced the elder to either accept his explanation in that moment or investigate into the event. He could not look into the event because the truth of whether they had truly slandered the clan would be revealed (they didn't) which would destroy his reputation if he continued punishing them. If he did not want to become known as an elder that suppresses students with potential in the clan, he had no choice but to refrain from investigating the matter, instead pretending that Fang Yuan's obviously slanderous words were true.

15: The frustration he made the elder feel sets him up as a witness during the interim period after he murdered Jia Jin Sheng, as the elder sends investigators after him to determine how he advanced to middle stage. Fang Yuan did not receive any punishment. On the contrary, he was showered with compliments, which made the other students very afraid. With the issue blown up like this, it will also force either the clan leader himself, or the 2 main elders to interfere and prevent the academy elder from suppressing Fang Yuan (they want to protect the clan rules because the clan rules prevent the interference of the other factions with their own descendants). This frustration in the elder was further strengthened when Fang Yuan continued to extort them after they became student leaders, but could not interfere since the student leaders were from the main factions, and the faction heads wanted to see them grow in the face of setbacks (a student who needs interference from the adults because he's too weak to be able to go up against another fellow student does not deserve to be the leader of the faction). Fang Yuan's extortion (“rashness”, “arrogance”, and “displeasure” was done for the higher-ups to interpret as “showing weakness”.

16: This is what the spies investigated: Fang Yuan's daily life was very simple, and he had a rigid schedule. During the day he would always be attending lessons in the academy, and at night he would always sleep in the dormitories. He worked extremely hard on his cultivation, nurturing his aperture every single night. On some days, he would go out to the village’s only inn to have better meals and to buy wine. He had a special affection towards wine, especially green bamboo wine. He kept a dozen jars of it under his bed. The last point sets up his misdirection to the liquor worm, while the first part makes it clear he has no time for killing Jia Jin Sheng. Also the elder investigated his background and Fang Yuan's experience of being at such a high point and then suddenly falling when his low talent is revealed deceives the elder into thinking Fang Yuan's rebelliousness and arrogance against the clan was understandable.

17: The merchant caravan finally arrives and Fang Yuan who was last seen with him is brought to interrogation. Fang Yuan is brought into the room with all the elders including the Merchant Caravan Leader Jia Fu who needs to find an explanation for his brother's death if he doesn't want to make it seem like he killed his own brother for a higher share of the inheritance. Even the clan leader thought that if Fang Zheng was actually the murder, he would scapegoat Fang Yuan instead since Fang Zheng had higher innate talent.

19: When Fang Yuan was first asked if he knows who Jia Jin Sheng is, he asks "who is Jia Jin Sheng?" which forces the female witness who saw him at the caravan to scream "he's lying!" and explain how Fang Yuan gambled in Jia Jin Sheng's caravan. Fang Yuan puts on an expression of nervousness before pretending that he suddenly realized who it is, lying that he never saw him again.

20: When another witness (a male employee at the tavern he met with Jia Jin Sheng again in) calls him out for lying again, Fang Yuan looks at him as if he only just recognized him, reveals a shocked face, then tries to suppress the shock once more. The elders saw these changes in expression, but this was intentionally done to make them believe he isn't great at acting, while also setting up a further misdirection. This is also seen through by the tavern witness which gave him the confidence to reveal the truth about how Fang Yuan had met with Jia Jin Sheng at the tavern.

21: Fang Yuan puts on a frantic expression and shouts “How can that be!” He hurriedly said, “The wine shop had so many people, how can you be sure it was me? You might be mistaken!” This supposedly clear attempt at an excuse makes it obvious Fang Yuan is lying and truly did meet him at the tavern, and that he is also not that competent at acting. However, Fang Yuan needs an object of misdirection, and he chose to use the tavern worker because of a prior setup that accidentally became useful (the tavern worker also saw him feeding a rare liquor worm). This becomes the excuse for what he was trying to hide with his lie.

22: When the witness smugly mentions how Fang Yuan had the rare liquor worm, Fang Yuan laughed coldly in his heart, but showed an expression of shock on his face and pretended to subconsciously take a step backwards.

23: The elders asked him where he got the liquor worm from, but he clenched his fists, gritted his teeth, and refused to answer, acting stubborn! This forces them all into a frenzy as they ask him where he obtained the liquor worm, and when finally an elder asks him if he killed Jia Jin Sheng to obtain the liquor worm, Fang Yuan uses the opportunity for his acting by raising his head and indignantly shouting loudly as if he had been slandered, "How can it be his? I obtained it myself! I opened it!"

24: When he is question, he lies, “That’s right. At this point, I shall not hide it any further!” Fang Yuan acted as if he was risking everything, speaking urgently. (he chose to tell a half-truth that conveniently leaves out him going to the cave with Jia Jin Sheng)

25: However, there needs to be a source for the liquor worm. Fang Yuan actually set up the source in a previous tactic that did not succeed, so he reused the same tactic here to convincingly excuse the existence of the liquor worm as a product of luck, with a proper witness (the female worker) and justification. The trick was that when he bought 6 rocks at the gambling tavern, he did not actually open the sixth one. He only opened it at home, and it turned out to be empty, but he lied that the liquor worm was obtained from it! At this point he simply stayed silent and let the elders justify it themselves! Probability being low? Anything can be justified with luck, and it was already proven Fang Yuan supposedly got lucky when he opened the other gu worm with multiple witnesses in public. Refinement difficulty? Easy, since gu worms are weak after hibernation, etc.

26: Finally, Fang Yuan continues once more, making up the story about Jia Jin Sheng, without contradicting the logical facts revealed by the witnesses. However, Fang Yuan also intentionally revealed how Jia Jing Sheng had tried to scam someone only for Jia Fu to appear and save the day, revealing their internal conflict. This "accidental" mention sets up his upcoming accusation which indirectly misdirects the murder to Jia Fu's other brother, while also compelling Jia Fu to change the topic out of embarrassment. Jia Fu asks if JJS chased after Fang Yuan when he left the tavern, and Fang Yuan speaks the half truth that he did, but that the purpose was to buy the liquor worm and they never saw each other again.

27: “Speak, did you kill Jia Jin Sheng?!” Jia Fu demanded, attempting to pressure Fang Yuan with his aura. Fang Yuan denied it once again, claiming that he did not see Jia Jin Sheng after that. Jia Fu had no other evidence. At this point, it had become a stalemate. As Gu Yue Bo listened, he grew displeased. Jia Jin Sheng actually dared to threaten the Gu Yue Clan? This was not giving them any respect! Now Jia Fu dared to interrogate a Gu Yue clansman so intensely in front of all the clan elders. It was one thing if he had evidence, but he clearly did not.

28: Their family also had a method to trace the gu worms of the dead member, but Fang Yuan choosing to destroy the gu worms cut off this method, so what exactly is the motive? The murderer stole nothing, and it can't even be a crime of passion (such as both being in love with the same person) because that would be longer-term which would make it even less likely for this exact village to be the culprit since it would open up possibilities of prior planning before the caravan had even entered the village.

29: When the hall falls into silence and a stalemate is reached, Fang Yuan finally opens up the core method of his entire scheme! He says to Jia Fu “Maybe you killed him. I heard that your father is splitting his inheritance. Now that one brother is dead, wouldn’t you get more?” Everyone is already fairly certain it isn't Jia Fu so the elders tell Fang Yuan to shut up. There is no way Jia Fu is so dumb that he's going to kill his own brother who was entrusted to him, since it'll only guilt him in the eyes of the father sharing the inheritance. This makes Fang Yuan look politically "average" in intelligence in the eyes of the elders since he was only able to think a few layers deep and not see the big picture. But he did this intentionally!! The second setup is that, while Jia Fu himself would not want to kill Jia Jin Sheng, the OTHER BROTHER who wants to frame Jia Fu as the murderer for a higher share of the inheritance has the motive!!

30: “The Jia family’s internal dispute!” One of the elders had a flash of inspiration and lightly muttered. He did not speak loudly, but in the silent hall, it could be heard clearly. All at once, the elders’ eyes lit up. *Finally, they’ve realized it.* Fang Yuan’s mouth twitched, his eyelids drooping to hide the cold light in his eyes.

31: *To make someone believe in something wholeheartedly, you cannot convince them, you must direct them.* Fang Yuan’s keen eyes allowed him to see these glances, and he laughed coldly in his heart, but his face still showed indignation and stubbornness. At this point, even Jia Fu was deceived into believing his own brother had killed Jia Jin Sheng. However, he had no choice but to "frame" Fang Yuan as a scapegoat.

32: Jia Fu kept questioning Fang Yuan, and asks if he has a witness. Eventually, Fang Yuan shows an indignant expression after being berated, but finally grits his teeth and "helplessly" says “No.” Right when he is about to be accused as the murderer, the academy elder who Fang Yuan set up as the witness speaks up to defend him, and mentions how he sent spies to investigate Fang Yuan with no time available for Fang Yuan to harm Jia Jin Sheng.

33: The main reason the clan Leader approves of the academy elder speaking up is political and geographical (the mountainous region makes travel and trade difficult so the merchant caravans need to be relied on but if the clan gains the reputation of murdering an important caravan member, caravans will avoid them due to lack of trust). Fang Yuan was a mere C grade, so if he really had harmed Jia Jin Sheng, handing him over was nothing. But now that he had been cleared of all suspicion, if he was handed over, the Gu Yue Clan would suffer a great injustice and suffer a terrible loss. *Yes, this is it…* Fang Yuan was hidden behind the academy elder’s back, and no one could see his lips curl into a slight smile.

35: Jia Fu also uses a footprint gu to see Fang Yuan's 30000 footprints, but since he chose to stay away from the cave in the past few days, all his footprints show him circling the village, corroborating the academy elder's investigation.

36: The final method was a truth detector gu, but rather than panicking, Fang Yuan confidently and unhesitatingly does as instructed since he realizes that he has a rare gu worm that can suppress its effect.

37: There is one final investigator remaining (Tie Xue Leng), but he is terribly busy and likely to come only in 2-3 years, by which Fang Yuan plans to have already escaped the mountain after reaching Rank 3.

I didn't explain a lot of the background of the story, but it helps to make it less confusing when you know how the gu world is structured.
