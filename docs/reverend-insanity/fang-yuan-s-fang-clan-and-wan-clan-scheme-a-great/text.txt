Great Underrated feat (reverend insanity)

This feat lasted only eight chapters, making it very short compared to other major feats in the novel, such as the 88 yang and the Fate War, which lasted for hundreds of chapters.

A few notes for those who haven't read the novel:

1. Fang Clan has no blood relation to Fang Yuan. They simply share the same surname.

2. The Righteous Path is made up mostly of clans that exploit morality, rules, and reputation for their own benefit. Their philosophy is essentially: "A crime isn't a crime unless it's exposed." They usually operate as organized clans.

3. The Demonic Path follows the same pursuit of profit, but unlike the Righteous Path, its members make no attempt to hide behind morality. They are typically lone cultivators rather than clans.

4. The world consists of five regions, each hostile toward the others and they doesn't like someone from another continent interfering in their continent's affairs. All the clans mentioned here belong to the Western Desert, while Fang Yuan is based in the Eastern Sea.

\---

At the beginning of this arc, the Fang Clan, a Righteous Path clan, secretly cooperated with Fang Yuan, the greatest demonic cultivator of his era and one of the strongest beings alive. Like many Righteous Path clans, they had no issue working with someone from the Demonic Path—as long as the cooperation remained hidden and profitable.

Through a chain of events, Fang Yuan deliberately exposed this secret alliance.

Seeing the opportunity, the Wan Clan, an old rival of the Fang Clan, began rallying the other Righteous Path clans to launch a joint campaign against them under the pretext of punishing their collaboration with Fang Yuan.

The other clans were eager to join because destroying the Fang Clan promised enormous benefits. However, they hesitated because they didn't know whether Fang Yuan himself would intervene to protect the Fang Clan.

To intimidate them, the Fang Clan launched a raid on one of the Wan Clan's resource points, intending it as nothing more than a warning.

Then something unexpected happened.

The Wan Clan Leader died.

Why?

Unknown to everyone, Fang Yuan secretly used a technique that distracted the Clan Leader at the critical moment. The attack was something he could have defended against under normal circumstances, but because of Fang Yuan's interference, he failed to react and was killed.

The Fang Clan had no idea Fang Yuan was responsible. They genuinely believed they had accidentally killed him.

Now they were trapped.

Attacking a resource point could still be justified according to the hypocritical standards of the Righteous Path. They could fabricate historical claims, argue over ownership, or invent some ancient dispute dating back thousands of years.

But killing a Clan Leader was an unmistakably demonic act.

There was no easy way to back down anymore.

The Fang Clan had no choice but to completely annex the Wan Clan.

And that's exactly what they did.

The surviving members of the Wan Clan escaped and began gathering the other clans, promising generous rewards to anyone willing to join the campaign against the Fang Clan.

Now, virtually every clan in the Western Desert coveted the profits that could be gained from this war.

At the same time, their fear of Fang Yuan disappeared after a supreme expert from Central Continent guaranteed that he would not interfere in Western Desert affairs. Furthermore, if Fang Yuan—who belonged to the Eastern Sea—attempted to intervene, this expert promised to stop him.

With that assurance, nothing remained to prevent the alliance from attacking the Fang Clan, now branded as a "demonic clan" for killing another clan's leader.

At this point, Fang Yuan contacted the Fang Clan and proposed a deal.

He offered to purchase most of the resource points they had seized from the Wan Clan in exchange for powerful offensive techniques and combat methods that would strengthen their ability to defend themselves.

The Fang Clan had no better option, so they accepted.

This immediately alarmed the alliance.

The very resource points they had hoped to seize as spoils of war had now been sold off.

While they were busy renegotiating the terms of their coalition...

The Fang Clan suddenly launched a surprise attack.

Armed with the offensive methods Fang Yuan had sold them, they actually gained the upper hand.

But just as victory seemed within reach...

Everything changed.

The legendary ancestor of the Wan Clan suddenly appeared.

It was revealed that he had been resurrected by the Central Continent expert supporting the alliance.

The tide instantly turned.

The Fang Clan suffered heavy casualties and was forced to retreat.

Now both sides had paid a heavy price.

The allied clans had already invested enormous resources into the campaign and couldn't afford to leave empty-handed.

Since the Wan Clan's resource points had already been sold, the only way to recover their losses was to destroy the Fang Clan itself and seize its territory instead.

As a result, their determination only grew stronger.

Meanwhile, the Fang Clan found itself in the worst possible situation.

They had suffered severe losses, the coalition had become even more determined to eliminate them, and now the resurrected Wan ancestor stood against them.

Then Fang Yuan arrived like a "kind-hearted savior" with another irresistible proposal.

"How about making another deal with me?"

"Sell me all your remaining resource points and all your territory. Become wanderers, and in return, I'll provide you with the offensive methods you need to survive."

At that moment, the Fang Clan finally understood Fang Yuan's grand strategy.

The inexplicable death of the Wan Clan Leader.

His encouragement to sell off the Wan Clan's resource points.

His knowledge that the Wan ancestor would be resurrected—without ever warning them.

Everything suddenly made sense.

Once the Fang Clan sold all its land and resource points to Fang Yuan, it would no longer have any territory to defend.

Its only path to survival would be to roam the Western Desert, attacking one clan after another, seizing their resource points, selling them to Fang Yuan in exchange for even stronger offensive methods, and then repeating the cycle indefinitely.

In the end, every single faction lost this war...

Except one.

Fang Yuan.

He had effectively transformed the Fang Clan into his own private raiding force, looting the Western Desert on his behalf without lifting a finger.

An endless stream of resource points would flow into his hands, while the Fang Clan would bear all the blame as the "demonic clan."

And Fang Yuan?

His reputation would remain spotless.

Officially, he had never interfered in the affairs of another region.

Meanwhile, resources would continue flowing to him endlessly while he relaxed comfortably in the Eastern Sea.
