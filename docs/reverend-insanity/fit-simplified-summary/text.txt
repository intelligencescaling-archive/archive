Historical Inertia reasoning:

Historical Inertia is a metaphysical law in the Gu World. It shows that while the Butterfly Effect can change many small details, major historical events are the result of long term accumulated conflicts, and has a trigger point that makes the event happen. Therefore, history resists change. Even if a specific trigger is removed, the world will generate a new one to make sure the event occurs.
I’ll explain the lowest scaling interpretation of this feat, i.e, assuming that FY deduced Historical Inertia in chapter 404.

Chapter 404:

This was already the third time Fang Yuan had used Spring Autumn Cicada and by accumulating his experience, he had an even deeper understanding of rebirth.
There was indeed a butterfly effect to the aftermath of rebirth that could cause some things to change completely. However, there was also historical inertia; many great events that occurred were conflicts that accumulated over time, they only lacked a trigger point. Even without the original trigger, a new one would always appear.
This was not something a person alone could influence and change easily.

What he deduced: Even without the original trigger, a new one would always appear.

Explanation:

Firstly, the deduction operates at micro level. Meaning, you can’t just simply observe the phenomenon. You have to know/deduce the causal chains of events at a very deeper level. Or in simpler words, it needs higher order causal reasoning to deduce this.

The feat is done in three steps.

Step 1:

FY had to reconstruct causal chains over the past few years, since the first rebirth. The text stated: by accumulating his experience. Which is why I said the timeframe of the data is from the first rebirth to the 3KI, as that’s the timeframe the experience referred to.

Step 2:

Now with the reconstructed data, he has to recognize that the events are following a specific pattern. We know that the data he has to process is impossibly huge, that is, the past few years since the first rebirth, and also the same timeframe of the few years of the first 500 years of his life.

After comparing the past few years of both timelines, and how his changes had fared, he had to recognize a pattern like this: First he needs to identify there are main points in the events. Then he has to notice that there are trigger points. Then he has to notice the trigger point mechanism(the process of replacement). The complexity of pattern recognition becomes exceptionally harder when the quantity of the data where the pattern is recognized increases.

Step 3:

Then he has to generalize the pattern into a statement “Even without the original trigger, a new one would always appear.” which comes under inductive reasoning feat.

Simply put, FY deduced a metaphysical law of the gu world in a mere seconds.

FAQs about Historical Inertia Reasoning:

Q1. Can the premises and conclusions of the reasoning be inferred?

A1. Yes. Throughout Volumes 1 and 2, the author provides the necessary data. By tracking the changes FY makes and the resulting outcomes, the reader can reconstruct the same causal chains FY used to reach his conclusion.

Q2. What if FY already knows about this from his previous 500 years of life?

A2. This is very retarded because it can only be deduced by someone with future knowledge. It requires a "control group" (the first life) and a "test group" (the second life) to see how the timeline has inertia.

Q3. Anyone whose natural observation operates at higher order causal inferences can do this too.

A3. No. Even if a character has the advantage of higher order causal inference, they might not need to manually reconstruct the causal chains, but they still need to:
1. Recognize three complex causal chain patterns across a massive amount of data.
2. Have the inductive reasoning ability to generalize those three complex patterns into a single rule.

Q4. What do you mean by saying FY deduced this in chapter 400?

A4. We know this specific inductive reasoning and pattern recognition feat needs higher order causal chains. Chapter 400 is the only place where we know FY was doing that.
