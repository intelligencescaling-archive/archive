First let me describe the events.

So after Jia Jin Shen, Fang Yuan used the recurring curriculum to prepare himself for the Mid Year Examination by killing boars. After that he purposefully lets Fang Zheng, Mo Bei and Chi Cheng go from his extortion. Next up in the examination he finds Wang Da cave's. He instantly deduces the fact that Wang Er's brother Wang Da was alive and was looking for him. Hot reads his entire set up and deduces his rank, cultivation, etc. Then he uses the memory of the map to avoid Wang Da and eventually walks away. Then he shows the amount of tusks he collected, redirecting Gu Yue Bo's thoughts towards a secret backer. Next up during the end term examination he was able to successfully cold read Gu Yue Bo which could've exposed the backer, by joining Sickley Snake's group.
