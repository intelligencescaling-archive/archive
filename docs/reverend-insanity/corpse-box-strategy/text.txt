FIRST THING TO REMEMBER:

Black Coffin Luck
Since Fang Yuan used the Spring Autumn Cicada in Chapter 1, he has been cursed by a calamity known as Black Coffin Luck which is a form of bad luck associated with the luck path.
The effect of this curse is to lower Fang Yuan’s success rate to a minimum, requiring him to work harder to achieve his goals. It acts as a contextual adversity, constantly reducing his luck even below natural level.
For example, if Fang Yuan’s natural success rate in an action is 7/10, Black Coffin Luck reduces it to 5/10. Thus, Fang Yuan has to be extra careful in every move he makes.
Note that, from Chapter 1 itself, Fang Yuan has had Black Coffin Luck 24/7, making every challenge harder to overcome and attracting more problems. To navigate this, he must strategize carefully to avoid his bad luck and even turn these challenges to his advantage.

Fang Yuan leverages the gruesome delivery of Gao Wan's remains to exert psychological and social pressure on the Mo family. Initial Intimidation and Option to Negotiate:

Consequences of Non-Compliance:
Fang Yuan's plan for a public reveal by placing the remaining part of Gao Wan's corpse at the Mo family's front entrance highlights the stakes of the situation. The risk of being seen as powerless or overreaching would severely weaken the Mo family's political standing, potentially shifting power dynamics within the clan. Something that Mo Chen wants to avoid at all cost.

Mo Chen's Response and Compliance:

Through this scheme, Fang Yuan's intricate planning and social awareness force Mo family into a submissive position without further escalation. By presenting clear, calculated choices, Fang Yuan ensures the outcome aligns with his goals and effectively manipulates them to secure a lasting advantage.
