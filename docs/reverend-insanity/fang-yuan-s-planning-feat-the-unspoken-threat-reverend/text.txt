Fang Yuan planning feats "The Unspoken Threat"

Hello there.

I've been on this sub for a decent amount of time, despite this I've actually seen no posts or actual Google docs talking about the genuine feats of Fang Yuan. So i decided to change that because I've actually been reading Reverend Insanity recently, so without further a do. Let's begin.

(Spoiler Alert for Chapter 31-39 of Reverend Insanity of course)

Context: Fang Yuan has died but got reborn as his teenage self in a mountain villain called "The Gu Yue Village", there Fang Yuan got into the Gu Yue Academy dorms and has been living there for awhile, the Elder of the Academy tasked the students to gain a bunch of Primeval Stones (which is a form of currency that can be transformed into Primeval Essence, spiritual energy that is used to feed/upgrade/cultivate Gu. So Fang Yuan has been blocking the gates of his school and has been blackmailing his classmates by telling them to either give up their own Primeval Stones or get beaten up and robbed anyway. Now let's start.

Fang Yuan was in an Inn (a small hotel) eating his food and drinking, until suddenly, Mo Yan (the sister of one of Fang Yuan's beaten up and robbed classmates) showed up with a bunch of servants by her side. Mo Yan threatened Fang Yuan to beat him up, Fang Yuan realised his had no way to fight her (as he is currently a Rank 1 Gu Master, Mo Yan is a Rank 2 Gu Master, so she is far superior to him in combat). So to improvise, Fang Yuan acted clueless and decided to act as his own brother (Fang Zhen), this is possible because Fang Yuan and Fang Zhen are actually really similar in terms of physical appearance. So Fang Yuan acted as his brother said "I'll lead you to Fang Yuan".

Afterwards, he and Mo Yan with her followers left the Inn and went to the Academy, there Fang Yuan revealed himself. This is clever because the Academy rules actually forbid an elder (anyone above 18 years old) to fight against a junior (anyone below 18). So Fang Yuan has tricked Mo Yan into a safe space where she cannot attack him at all. He ended up locking himself inside his dorm room until Mo Yan got up and left due to boredom and that Fang Yuan was ignoring her threats and attempts at intimidation. Mo Yan left however she did leave one follower behind, this follower was called "Gao Wan" and he had been tasked of staying outside the dorm until Fang Yuan left, which he then would've kidnapped him and brought him to Mo Yan.

Fang Yuan however improvised by leaving his room and just suddenly started attacking Gao Wan by using his moon blade from his moonlight Gu (which is smart because Gao Wan outmatches big Yuan physically). Eventually Fang Yuan won that fight and purposely took the dead body and cut into pieces to dispose of evidence.

Fang Yuan also lied to the Academy Elder and said that he heard rumours about Gao Wan working with other mountain villages that were enemies towards the village.

So yeah, correct me if i did anything wrong here. I think this feat is decent, although not a crazy feat like memory loss plan or anything like that.
