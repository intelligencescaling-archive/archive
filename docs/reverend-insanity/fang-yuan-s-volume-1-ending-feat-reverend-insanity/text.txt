Fang Yuan Vol 1 Ending Feat Established

"The stake were high still manageable"

This time it was Fang Yuan who got schemed and plotted against somehow this mf still won.

And I know people will complain about the use of SAC being a hax, but really what was to be done at that point? With that being said, let's continue.

**Backstory:**

After killing Ye Yao which was a mid tier feat, Fang Yuan returns as a clan elder. He discovers the Treasure heavenly earth flower Gu and wants to obtain it for himself. But he couldn't do that now, so he goes to elaborate with a scheme. There were also some high mid tiers schemes with his match with BNB and working together too.

Next the Divine Investigator comes along Tie Xue Leng who gives JJS case to his daughter Tie Ruo Nan. Tie Xue Leng is genuinely a good schemer. He was able to put a tracking gu worm and was able to find Fang yuan. Tie Ruo Nan was a good investigator however her evaluation is a bit lower due to Intution Gu.

Anyways after that, the Three Clans Battle engaged. Meanwhile below, First Gen Gu Yue engaged in a battle with Tie Xue Leng and Fang Yuan barely made it alive. Needed to leave the entire place fast, he hurried and nearly succeeded but met BNB. Went head on head and then survived. Next he survived the explosion of BNB aperture and got a rebirth again. He schemed against First Gen Gu Yue, and Sky Crane Lord and succeeded escaping with BNB.

#

VCI - Yes VCI in this feat was high tier. Comprehending people's words and understanding the in depth message behind it.

VSI - Fang Yuan was digging through tunnels, walking across mountains. Yes he was inside an unknown space with a Rank 5 battle and many gu worms which could've killed, still he managed to identify the known spaces such as man made caves and found Thousand Li EarthWolf Spider.

PSI - Insane, he was taking in new information, and was able to process it faster than normal human speed. Such as absorbing the effects of First Gen Gu Yue gu worms etc.

WMI - He was able to identify things from his 500 years of experience requires great WMI and was able to still do it.

FRI - Fluidly reasoned out the cause of Flower Wine Monk's death and outmaneuver First Gen Gu Yue.

Direct Manipulation - Directly encaptivated First Gen Gu Yue with his words taking advantage of the pressure from the situation. Also captured BNB with his own words.

Indirect Manipulation - Not a lot but there was some decent moments where we could see the influence from previous strats.

Adaptability - Arguably his strongest cat in this feat. He was adapting here and there while processing complex information. (Very High)

SQ - Again was able to use his social connection with Mo Chen's family + Tie Xue Leng + First Gen Gu Yue + BNB.

BIQ - Very High, up with Adaptability

Adversity Capacity - Again Very High. This was one of the moments where he took a gamble for himself and still did it.

Theory of Mind - Very High again. Up there at his very best.

Foresight - Truly high, predicting people's behavior, measuring the outcomes of the battle, while multitasking with Gu Worms.

Hindsight - Also very high.

EC - Very High, this feat is alot dependent on FY's EC. Maintaining his composure while trying to fake emotions in front of Rank 5's is not an easy task just by their fame and aura.

EI - Very high again.

Knowledge - Extensive in Human's nature and psychology.

Planning - Even though it failed I say his plan was decent. Mid tier.

Scheming - Yes this is a very high. Under such short time, he built a scheme, merging three layers with it. First manipulate First Gen Gu Yue and subdue his Gu Worms. Second Manipulate Bai Ning Bing and convince him to join him. Third allow Sky Crane Lord to kill First Gen Gu Yue and convincing Bai Ning Bing to commit suicide, while surrounded by Clan Elders + Two Rank 5 + Many Rank Three's + Many Rank two's etc.

Intuition - High with his navigation in the terrain and his detection of faint aura of Thousand Earth Li Wolf spider + Sky Crane Lord and First Gen Gu Yue's relationship + TXL vs First Gen Battle.

There could be other cats too, but my overall evaluation is that Fang Yuan Vol 1 ending feat was High to Very High Tier considering the risks overall.

**Some things I wanna clarify:**

Using SAC doesn't really make this feat fall of grid. It is only boosting Adaptability + AC. SAC is a hax yes but it doesn't boosts someone's intelligence unlike the intution gu. So saying SAC + 500 years of knowledge is a huge advantage to him, I want to remind the butterfly effect, so while the experience may come in handy, it was a new situation to FY. Rebirth feats are what makes this feat incredible under such terrain + time complexity.
