Introduction

Reverend Insanity takes place in a cruel world, a world where people don't hesitate to betray and kill, a world where people crave for wealth and power. Schemes run deep everywhere, from between Rank 1 students of a small sect to the divine Rank 9 Venerables. People scheme for wealth, for love, for lifespan and for their dreams.

Fang Yuan, originally a scholar from Earth, started in this Gu world as a naive good hearted person. He had experienced many things, and had gotten bored of what the world had to offer him after living for 300 yrs… He then decided after meeting the love of his life, to drop his mask and live as he wanted, to chase after a grand goal… What goal can be grander than obtaining immortality? Thus he chooses to do anything, to himself or others, to become truly immortal.

Due to a series of events for the next 200 yrs, he had refined a Rank 6 Spring Autumn Cicada, a Gu that can allow him to travel to the past and change his future. He had promised that he will be a demon in his next life, and had exploded himself when surrounded by enemies to travel 500 yrs to the past, to be reborn again as the kid from Gu Yue Mountain.

This Doc covers what Fang Yuan did after Volume 2, his schemes, deception and manipulation throughout the volume to move toward his ultimate goal. Fang Yuan after vol 2 has the ownership of the Hu Immortal blessed land, and Fixed Immortal Travel Gu (Rank 6), along with the anger of the entire southern plains.

Note : There are various mentions of Wolf kings. Wolf kings are larger wolves that command an army of wolves. They can be ranked based on the number of wolves under them as hundred, thousand or myriad wolf king…. The character Chang Shan Yin is also called The Wolf King as he is able to command a large number of wolves in a battle after enslaving them. Enslaving these wolves involves fighting them till they are weakened enough.

The Power System

                The knowledge of the power system is required for scalers to understand the Adversity Fang Yuan faces in the entire volume. Lemme break it down for ya

Firstly there are mortals. Mortals don't have any power.
Then there are people amongst mortals who can awaken their aperture with hope gu at a young age. These are cultivators.

These cultivators progress from Rank 1 to Rank 9. Rank 1 to Rank 5 are mortal cultivators, while Rank 6 to Rank 8 are immortal cultivators. Rank 9 beings are called Venerables.

These cultivators have apertures from rank 1. Apertures are like a small sphere filled with Primeval Essence (Think of em as fuel). The cultivators can use these primeval essence to control creatures spread out among the Gu world called “Gu” (Think of em as pokemons but insects)

The Gu s can also vary from Rank 1 to Rank 9. A Rank 3 Gu can be used effectively by a Rank 3 and above cultivators (If lower rank cultivators use it, It will consume a very high amount of essence, among other side effects).

Also lower ranked gus can be fused with other lower ranked gus through a specific process called Refinement along with other raw materials and sometimes with specific rituals to make higher ranked gus’ with specific recipes. But Refinement always has a chance of failure, the chance of failure increasing the higher the target gu rank is.

After refinement, catching it in the wild, or taking it from someone else ( usually difficult cuz once under control, the cultivator can kill the gu in a second through a mental command ), the cultivator’s own essence from the aperture is inserted into the gu to take control of it. Once under control, the gu lives in the cultivator’s aperture, requiring regular feeding of food [depends on a case by case basis] and the essence to nourish itself.

The higher the Gu’s rank, the larger the amount of food the Gu worm needs, but the interval it takes to feed another time gets longer too.

Whenever the cultivator uses essence, it is refilled automatically in the aperture. Another way to refill lost essence is the primeval essence stones, which are also used as the preferred currency in the mortal gu world. Essence can be found in essence springs, natural structures that naturally produce essence stones over time.

Rank 6 and above are Immortals cuz their apertures is a mini world which produces Immortal essence over time that can activate immortal gu. The measurement unit of immoral essence is in beads, and 1 bead of immortal essence can produce an infinite amount of primeval essence. Thus Immortals from rank 6 onwards use immortal essence stones and barter methods in their economy.
They can also infinitely use Gus’ under Rank 5, but in an immortal lvl fight these Gus’ get damaged from overuse and may even die.

The Gus’ can have various powers, for example, Rank 1 moon blade gu can shoot moon blades that can inflict cuts. The higher the rank, the better tha power until rank 5. From Rank 6 onwards, only one Gu shall exist in the entire world for that unique power. Ex: Rank 6 Spring Autumn Cicada is only one in number in the whole world and is with Fang Yuan, while Rank 1 moon blade gus’ can be found in hundreds. When an immortal ( >Rank 6  Gu) is present with someone, no one else can refine even if they have all the raw materials.

Instead of using random Gu worms, ppl use a similar set of gu worms to follow a path. For eg: Strength path Gu Masters (another name for the cultivators) use strength increasing gus’, Skin hardening gu, some type of movement gu for faster movemnt while fighting, etc.. Also, various gu worms can be used at the same time to produce a synergistic effect called Killer moves. Ppl usually take many years to find the right combination and a very good killer move

Progression : From Rank 1 to 5, ppl had to use their essence as a strong wave against the wall of their Aperture to break it down, first eroding it slowly and then breaking the walls in one fell swoop to progress to the next level

From Rank 5 to 6 after breaking the aperture wall, both heaven and earth start pouring their energies towards the aperture. This energy can also carry enlightenment regarding anything, but mostly on a recipe to refine immortal gu. The heave, earth and life energy needs to be balanced perfectly for the advancement to work, and it is followed by tribulations.

From Rank 6 onwards a Gu Immortal faces tribulations ( Natural Calamities ) of varying scales in the Aperture which has become a small world by now. The Gu Immortals can place their Aperture Outside the body for entering inside, and tribulations take place in this world on a regular basis. The Aperture needs to withstand a certain amount of tribulations, which occur every 10, 50 and 100 yrs to advance. It is very difficult to enter the Immortal Aperture of someone without their permission

The Rate of flow of time in the aperture is different from that of the world. When Gu Immortals die, their Apertures are left behind in the world where it is managed by their will ( An AI with the goals of the person ), which can choose someone else to take ownership of the land.

The Venerables are the most powerful in the verse, capable of living thousands to millions of years and have complete mastery over their path. Historically, there were 9 venerables, but currently in this arc, there aren't any alive or significant to this arc.
