Fang Yuan Volume 2 PSI Feat Debunked (MostAncientVenerable Document)

First of all, unlike many of the other people yapping duck duck, MostAncientVenerable was actually polite, so don't go spamming him with hate just because of the debunk.

However, I will still debunk it since he said he wants to see more perspectives and I think the feat overrates way too hard.

Here is the scans that the PSI feat of 87 billion calculations is based off:

***\[Fang Yuan pondered, all sorts of people, incidents, cause and effect flickered in his mind.***

***Wang Xiao, Chou Jiu, Wu Shen Tong, Zhang San San, Long Qing Tian, Xiao Mang, Tie Ruo Nan, Bai Ning Bing, Mo Wu Tian, Yan Jun, Feng Tian Yu…***

***Slavery Gu, jade sky Gu poison, land spirit, bronze hall, immortal essence, fixed star Gu, second aperture Gu, undefeated hundred battles Gu…***

***Numerous choices give birth to unlimited possibilities. Infinite possibilities affect each other.***

***Fang Yuan's mind worked rapidly, all sorts of inspiration and scenarios happened in his head like lighting a flint in the darkness.\]***

You might be wondering, where did 87 billion calculations per second come from? We will get there.

First of all, the document claims ***\[This feat, described as happening in the time it takes to "light a flint" is a massive demonstration of active, parallel cognitive processing.\]***

Most people don't seem to understand what parallel processing actually is. A trillion calculations could happen in 0.0000001 seconds and it still wouldn't be enough proof that it's parallel processing, moreso it would indicate insanely high serial processing + taskswitching speed.

I have a friend who's a really good scaler and he confirmed Fang Yuan has parallel processing, but that is in another section of the volume, not here.

**Here is where the document gets the 87 billion calculations per second claim from:**

11 people + 8 things were specifically mentioned. The document adds 5 other situational factors, mentioning "his own weakness, the collapsing land, etc." making it **24 core variables**.

He then multiplies all 24 core variables by 20 because it's supposedly **\[a fair estimate of the number of distinct actions Fang Yuan could consider for each variable\]**. Thus we are left with **480** distinct actions over the 24 core variables.

He then multiplies it by 5 important agents (even though those agents are already part of the core variables), then multiplies that by 30 possible actions because that's an estimate of the actions the agents could take. This leaves **72000** secondary possibility branches that Fang Yuan must calculate.

A strategy is more than 1 move and you gotta think about the future, etc., so he multiplies it by 5 again, leaving him with **360000** simulated steps.

Of course, Fang Yuan needs to scan all the data and connect each of them individually to his vast internal knowledge, so he then multiplies it by 1000 high-level concepts. And now we're left with **360 million**.

Objection: "Woah woah Mr Disintegrator calm down, how did we go from 24 explicitly mentioned variables to 360 million calculations processed already?"

Response: "Only 360 million? You think we're done? This is Fang Yuan we're talking about bro, we ain't finished."

Let's think about the connection/integration that's obviously gotta happen. Fang Yuan would probably come up with like 10000 strategies, with 1000 steps per strategy for comprehensiveness sake. So 10 million calculations. You gotta compare all the strategies with each other, so multiply it by 9999 again. Obviously you gotta compare them under at least 5 criteria, so multiply it by 5. Now we're left with **499.95 million** calculations.

Then you obviously gotta add them all up: 480 + 72,000 + 360,000 + 360,000,000 + 10,000,000 + 499,950,000 = 870,382,480 active calculations. 870 million!!! Sheeesh.

Objection: "M-Mr Disintegrator. Don't you think you're overestimating how much thinking Fang Yuan really did with those 24 core variables the novel explicitly mentioned?"

Response: "We're almost done, almost. This is Fang Yuan we're talking about. I gotta factor in the timescale."

So the scan says ***\[Fang Yuan's mind worked rapidly, all sorts of inspiration and scenarios happened in his head like lighting a flint in the darkness\]***.

A flint lighting up the darkness probably takes like 0.01 seconds, meaning Fang Yuan did **87 billion** calculations per second. Ontology no diff. Cosmology no diff. Anthropology no diff. Sheesh!

Ok guys so what actually happened here? I don't think it's necessary for me to debunk this at all because the calculations are speculative, but don't hate on MAV either, he even commented "Why even bother with my dumb ahh narrative feat 🥀" which means he wasn't scaling too seriously.

However, I'll still point out the main flaws.

The multiplicative nature of the calculation assumes every single agent reacts with every single variable and step and possibility and concept and so on and so forth. So something like undefeated hundred battles gu has 20 uses calculated, and every single one of these uses is simulated to interact with every single one of the 5 core agents, and their 30 reactions, and every single one of these is simulated 5 steps forward, and every single one of these further simulated 15000 resultant possibilities are multiplied with 1000 distinct high level concepts. And this apparently applies to all 24 of the variables explicated.

It's an oversimplification of how humans think and strategize. A more appropriate documentation of the feat would be to look at constraints, tools, and outcomes, without throwing them all into 1 big calculation to high ball near the top and call it a day.

This is one of the main reasons FSIQ feats tend to be very horrendous in the first place.

There's also the flaw in assuming Fang Yuan came up with 10000 strategies, each with 1000 steps, then comparing all the strategies with each other under 5 different criteria, which is even more absurd. Humans usually discard strategies that reach a dead end, there's no point in comparing it with every single other strategy multiplicatively, much less in 5 different criteria.

And finally there's the act of multiplying the final calculation by 100 which is also invalid, because if you look at the original Chinese text, it says "各种灵光 (flashes of inspiration) 如 (like) 黑暗中 (within darkness) 火石 (flintstones) 碰撞 (colliding)". This is a metaphor to describe that he had flashes of inspiration, like flintstones colliding which causes flashes to appear, not that he produced the whole plan in the time it takes for a flintstone to cause a spark.
