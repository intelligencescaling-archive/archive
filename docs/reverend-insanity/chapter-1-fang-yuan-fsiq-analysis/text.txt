This feat analysis of Fang Yuan's mindblowing FSIQ shockingly only pertains to the very first chapter of the novel. If you haven't heard of Fang Yuan, he is a person who has lived over 500 years and uses his experience to outwit his enemies.

Life Recalling (CPI):
As Fang Yuan stands on the top of the Mountain, cornered by the righteous faction and losing vast amounts of blood from standing with his wounds open for six hours, he gets sentimental and as characters often do in this kind of situation, recalls all the memories of their life they’ve lived through.

This is a feat that often gets overlooked due to it being one of the very first things ever happening in Reverend Insanity story, while also being so inconspicuous.

A blink of an eye is approximately 0.1 seconds and within this span of time Fang Yuan had processed a total of 500 years worth of memory. The sheer insanity one needs to reach this kind of level of Processing should be self explanatory. These 500 years also consist of lots of complex memories, where he needs to strategize and manage an environment where millions of dangers lurk in every corner while outsmarting the most powerful enemies with incredibly complicated schemes. And the fact that he is doing this while having lost severe amounts of blood (for six hours), while also being in need to keep the righteous faction, who managed to corner a genius like him, in check by constantly keeping a close eye on them only serves to make the CPI that much more difficult to perform and therefore that much greater. The point is that Fang processes all of his 500 years of memories, flashing by in 0.1 seconds, he actually performs this with his attention divided onto the dangerous enemies standing before him. It was also stated that these memories emerged before his eyes, which implies that he also visualized said memories.

Each and every memory which Fang Yuan had experienced during his lifetime(s) are included here. All the smallest details, all the experiences he has shared, his time before transmigrating into the Gu World, etc. This is actually shown multiple times in later chapters how he remembers most, if not all his experiences in said 500 years, including Gu Recipes, how Aperture Rankings worked, his old Battle IQ (He had been stated to have been better than multiple trained Gu Masters in combat from his 500 years experience in later chapters.), the plans which others were going to execute on him and much, much more. The list goes on and on. All this and much more, flashed before Fang Yuan's eyes in the amount of time which had been mentioned before. The fact that he also visualized said memories says a lot about his Full-Scale IQ, and we also have to take into account the situation he was in, as he was also inducing fear to the Righteous Faction although he had been bleeding out everywhere around his body for six whole hours. And yet, in such a situation, he had visualized and processed each and every single memory of the 500 years he had lived in the Gu World.
(CPI, WMI, PSI, VSI, Multitasking, Composure.)

Fang's History (FRI):
During the 500 years, Fang Yuan's Learning Ability is shown by him being able to refine Spring Autumn Cicada. Spring Autumn Cicada had been stated to have never been refined prior to Fang Yuan's refining of the Gu(other than by Red Lotus whom had Rank 9 cultivation, transcending the Blessed Lands and transcending everybody in the ranks below. Red Lotus also had to use 3000 years of Knowledge to refine SAC). Fang Yuan, with his OWN Knowledge, learnt by himself how to refine SAC and ultimately execute his plan of going back 500 years. This Knowledge, along with the 500 years of memories(about his family, the events which took place, etc). all flashed by in a blink of an eye. It also shows that at 500 years, he still has full memory of incidents which happened when he was 15. (FRI, Learning Ability, Abstract Reasoning, Knowledge.)

Rebirth (GAI)
To highlight Fang's FRI further, consider the divine level of CPI he performed, which is a compartment of FRI. Also, Fang Yuan, having lived over 500 years knows immense amounts of languages and dialects which he can speak perfectly. This fact in addition to his supreme knowledge that he acquired over all those years puts him at the top of VCI as well. The experience, verbal skills, negotiations he has all done throughout his life to survive should speak for this absurd level of VCI.

Also, after FY got reborn, he learnt how to speak when he was 3 months old and recite poetry when he was 5 years old. If you do use the argument that he was reborn when he did this, it would be false as Fang Yuan had gained his 500 year old consciousness around the time he got his Grade. As well as the fact that this had actually already happened *before* Fang Yuan lives the 500 years should prove that his early intelligence has nothing to do with the rebirth and 500 years of memory. (VCI, Vocabulary, Linguistic Intel.)

Conclusion
To sum up, Chapter 1 Fang Yuan has the knowledge obtained throughout 500 years, making him unrivaled in that department. His CPI no diffs the likes of Ayanokoji, Souichi Kiruma and Hannibal Lecter. His FRI and Learning Ability is also overpowered to the extreme, especially when factoring in the processing of his life's memories. This, combined with Fang's knowledge and language skills is one of, if not THE best in VCI. When it comes to FSIQ only the high tiers in NGNL and BSD stand to surpass a single chapter of Fang Yuan's story.
