Talking about Tie Ruo Nan in Vol 1

Tie Ruo Nan, the daughter of the late Tie Xue Leng was shown to be more impressive than the entire clan system. However there were flaws in her ways and I wanna discuss cause she had potential.

**Backstory:**

In Vol1, Tie Ruo Nan, is the daughter of the late Tie Xue Leng. They arrive in Gu Yue Village by Lord Sky Crane request who tracked down his elder brother First Gen Gu Yue by Blood path methods. During that time, Tie Xue Leng tells Tie Ruo Nan to take Jia Jin Sheng's case, while he himself was investigating, First Gen Gu Yue. Tie Ruo Nan, who has intution Gu, would later on systematically find out logical flaws and inconsistencies.

**Feats:**

1. VCI - She had a good VCI. She was able to see through Fang Yuan's words and was naturally able to make guesses. Those higher ups were blinded by their righteousness and illusion but Tie Ruo Nan wasn't. She was able to identify Fang Yuan's ability to speak perfectly such that there are logical inconsistencies in his verbal traps.
2. Hot reading. She was able to figure out and trace back the origin of the corpse inside the box, which later made several deductions. Tie Ruo Nan displayed mid tier hot reading under such small time complexity.
3. Intuition is where I take points off, because she is still using intuition Gu as stated. However her natural intuition is good, paired with the Gu itself.
4. Cold reading. Yes that part includes with her ability to see through Gu Yue Bo, Gu Yue Fang Yuan, etc. she has a solid low-mid mid tier cold reading.
5. VSI - There is not much to say, however walking through unfamiliarized terrain, this was one of her VSI feats there. I say sold low tier.
6. PSI - This is a solid high mid tier. She was able to process complexity of Fang Yuan's lies structure.
7. FRI - This is a solid high mid tier too. Using Jiang He, and was able to reason out Wang Er's ambush while constantly breaking Fang Yuan's lies. This actually forced Fang Yuan to leave the mountain beforehand.
8. Deductions - Again a solid mid mid tier. Mostly meth based, but yes her feats are good.

Where does she lack though

1. ToM - There was no consideration what other might think of her deductions, which later on restricted her investigation
2. EI control - Her emotions, are abrupt, disturbing meetings, exposing Fang Yuan which could've been fatal if not executed properly.
3. WMI - There wasn't much display of WMI.
4. Scheming - She was a pure investigator no schemes, but abrupt focusing on exposing Fang Yuan which turned to be fatal.
5. Planning

Overall I would say she is a solid Mid Tier, specifically Mid Mid tier and it's mainly due to lack of feats in Vol 1.

I know a lot of people wouldn't like this evaluation, but that's just the truth is, she did more work in days than the Gu Yue Village Gu Masters did in an year.

Yes her deduction regarding aperture was wrong, however that was the most sensible approach. Saying Fang Yuan has SAC it is an extreme statement, and with no physical evidence that is a foolish gamble as FY can just make you disappear.

Overall she did great according to her own accord.
