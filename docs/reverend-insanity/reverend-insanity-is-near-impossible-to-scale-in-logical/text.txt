Reverend insanity is near to impossible to scale in logical scaling.

Ri is one of the best stories to have nigh omniscient characters and still explain them logically. It has 2334 chs. But what makes it harder to scale? I will state some reasons to simplify why it is absolutely necessary to give whole essays to explain why fang yuan farted in ch x. I will also try to keep it simple. (Keep in mind, this is about logical scaling. As normal/narrative/Other scalings are easier to track).

1. The mechanics/rules of the universe:- Ri is very uniquely crafted. It has its own laws of nature and physics. There are dao marks, accumulations, fate gu, destiny gu, River of time, Secluded domains of heaven, etc. What makes it hard to track is its complexity and web format of story. Each component isn't independent. It is all interconnected. Thus making it hard to make you understand the feat without a whole ass doc.

2. Interconnection of characters and the world - one theme of ri is the insignificant character of us all. In ri there is no mc or side character. Fy will fail. He might even lose if that makes the story interesting(author stated). But that also makes the story incredibly complex. Like RoT, Ri is inherently complex, not bc x character outsmarted y character. But bc a charcter schemed someone and left behind a inheritance that Z character found using his/her wits while he was outwitting E character. Later on the inheritance and outsmarting he/she did before comes again as A character was impacted due to butterfly effect.

3. The schemes - Author does mention alot and explains alot. But there is still lot of things subtle in the story. If you reread again and again you will gain and understand it differently. Why fy didn't do that or did this. Why some random character was mentioned for a ch and then didn't appear, after like few hundred chs. It is very complex and hard to notice as you need to track thousands of chs.

4. Immortals/immortal gu - till volume 3, it is still some pain in the ass. But beyond it, it becomes very hard to scale. Why? The reason is "immortals". As I mentioned the rules and physics are unique, and immortals use(abuse) these rules. Wisdom gu gives infinite inspirations, Vajra thought gu uses thoughts as weapons, star thought gu, and what not. Like the entire rule path is just maths as path. How can you scale thinking/fsiq categories when the character uses those fucking thoughts as weapons?? And has like infinite inspirations and a faster thinking due to hax??? This makes it very annoying as we count the hax from gu worms, then it would be a little unfair. But in the end it depends. As doctor who himself has absurd tech and machines. (I will upload a post on him too...)

5.symbolism and stuff :- There is Supreme grandmaster attainment in any path. Which means you have Omniscience of that path. Like in refinement path, there is a margin of error no matter how much skilled you are. But if you are refinement path supreme grandmaster and use refinement path materials to refine a refinement path gu, you have a 100% chance of refinement as you not only understand each aspect of refinement path, you use the freaking Dao marks on the materials and play with it so they work according to you. Hence 100% success. This is crazy broken and near reality manipulation skill. Now mind you, by omniscience I don't mean knowledge but understanding. You don't know everything but you understand easily and can gain 100% knowledge of all things. (In the novel to reach supreme grandmaster, you either have to know everything and go beyond it by creating new things or use a floating ice rock to increase your attainment).
