Giant Sun negs your favourite Venerable (RI)

**I’m currently at ch 1936**, and im piecing things together as i go. I might make more posts on the Venerables, like Limitless (when i see more about his plan), and Red Lotus (since this is supposed to be his arc, so im expecting some major top tier feats from him) in the near future.

This is just me currently trying to get the full picture.

Now, onwards with Giant Sun, the goat.

With a bit of inference and interpretation, his feats get so much better. He did so many things all in order to eventually infiltrate Heavenly Court and seize Fate Gu. Also assuming he didn’t use any luck path shenanigans to see the future or something.

Im basically trying to rebuild the entire plan.

So first, Giant Sun had a tour in Heavenly Court because they invited him to join Heavenly Court. While he was there, he observed Heavenly Court, their defenses and some of their important Gu Houses. He also secretly placed an arrangement inside Heavenly Court.

This arrangement went undetected for around 300k years. It was basically a backdoor that would later allow Calamity Luck Altar to directly enter Heavenly Court, letting Longevity Heaven bypass a huge part of Heavenly Court’s defenses.

So, during what was supposed to just be a normal visit, Giant Sun was studying Heavenly Court and preparing for a war that would happen hundreds of thousands of years later.

Then there is the reason why Giant Sun made basically every woman he could his concubine and impregnated everyone who could breathe.

This also served multiple purposes.

1: To make his Huang Jin bloodline as large and powerful as possible, even after his death. His descendants eventually dominated Northern Plains, and the Imperial Court contest kept making the Huang Jin tribes stronger. Weaker tribes would get swallowed up, resources would go to the winners and talented Gu Masters would keep appearing through all the fighting.

Giant Sun built a system that kept strengthening his bloodline over generations. During the great era, this would also make it much easier for Longevity Heaven to unite Northern Plains.

2: This also connects to Call of the Ancient. Giant Sun already knew that Call of the Ancient would be used during the future invasion of Heavenly Court.

It lets Longevity Heaven summon Northern Plains Gu Immortals from the past in their peak states to fight in the present. This was basically their answer to Heavenly Court’s immortal graveyard.

Heavenly Court had been storing rank eight experts for countless generations inside the immortal graveyard. Meanwhile Giant Sun spent his life spreading his bloodline across basically all of Northern Plains, and his descendants kept producing more experts over hundreds of thousands of years.

So by fucking half of Northern Plains (like the goat he is) and spreading his bloodline everywhere, Giant Sun also created a massive amount of connections to past Northern Plains experts that could later be summoned with Call of the Ancient.

3: This also connects to later arrangements like 88 True Yang Building, which specifically benefited his descendants and constantly gave them resources, inheritances and opportunities to become stronger.

After his visit to Heavenly Court, he also built 88 True Yang Building.

That building alone has a shit ton of purposes.

88 True Yang Building is extremely beneficial to the Huang Jin tribes, like i said before. It gives them resources, Gu worms, inheritances and basically helps keep Giant Sun’s descendants above everyone else in Northern Plains. It was also connected to Imperial Court blessed land.

The blessed land would face extremely powerful calamities and tribulations, so Giant Sun used the building to redirect them into the outside world. This is where the ten year blizzard comes from.

Giant Sun then used the blizzard itself to create the Imperial Court contest. So even the calamities threatening his blessed land became part of a system that forced Northern Plains into constant competition and made his descendants stronger.

Giant Sun also planned around 88TYB eventually being destroyed. He literally built it already knowing that it would collapse one day. In fact, the thing he was most anticipating was its destruction.

He predicted the coming great era and knew that 88TYB collapsing would mark the changing of the times. He told Bing Sai Chuan that once 88 True Yang Building collapsed, it would mean that the opportunity to attack Heavenly Court had arrived.

He didn’t necessarily predict Fang Yuan specifically, at least I don’t think that has been outright confirmed. Instead, Giant Sun said that whoever destroyed 88TYB would be the “crucial person”.

But it does make sense that he could have known it was Fang Yuan. In the original timeline, Hei Lou Lan eventually led Central Continent Gu Immortals into Northern Plains and destroyed 88TYB.

If we assume Giant Sun somehow predicted Fang Yuan specifically, then he must have also at the very least loosely deduced some of the events around Yi Tian Mountain. It would be a bit weird if Fang Yuan just died at Yi Tian Mountain and the entire thing Giant Sun prepared for became useless.

So under this interpretation, he must have expected Fang Yuan to survive long enough to become relevant during the current Fate Gu conflict.

Again though, this part is more inference than something I think has been outright stated. But the evidence is there.

Giant Sun even told Bing Sai Chuan that if Longevity Heaven failed to seize Fate Gu and eventually lost hope, they should seek help from this crucial person.

Then you have the actual fight between Longevity Heaven and Heavenly Court.

Bing Sai Chuan eventually awakened, and Calamity Luck Altar used Giant Sun’s old arrangement to enter Heavenly Court. Then you have Call of the Ancient actually being used, bringing Northern Plains experts from the past into the present and letting Longevity Heaven fight against Heavenly Court’s immortal graveyard.

He also has an absurd amount of side planning, and we have probably only seen some of it.

For example, Five Elements Grandmaster.

Five Elements Grandmaster found a formation path true inheritance that changed his entire life and made him one of the best formation path Gu Immortals in Northern Plains. Turns out that inheritance was arranged by Giant Sun.

Five Elements Grandmaster was specifically not from the Huang Jin tribes either, which was part of the deception. If Giant Sun gave those methods directly to Longevity Heaven or one of the Huang Jin tribes, Heavenly Court could maybe connect it back to him. So instead, he left the inheritance completely separate from Longevity Heaven and let some future lone cultivator obtain it.

That person ended up being Five Elements Grandmaster.

Giant Sun also predicted that the person who inherited it would eventually enter Longevity Heaven and get involved with Bing Sai Chuan. When Five Elements Grandmaster attacked Longevity Heaven, Bing Sai Chuan literally asked him why he thought he managed to enter in the first place.

And inside the inheritance there was even a hidden message that said:

*“Listen to Bing Sai Chuan and attack Heavenly Court.”*

The inheritance also had methods specifically made to counter Five Deity Hall, a Gu House from Heavenly Court that Giant Sun had already seen during his visit there.

So Giant Sun saw this Gu House hundreds of thousands of years ago, realized it could be a problem in the future, created a counter for it, hid that counter inside an inheritance completely separate from himself, predicted that someone would inherit it and eventually end up in Longevity Heaven, and even left hidden instructions telling him to listen to Bing Sai Chuan and attack Heavenly Court.

Then hundreds of thousands of years later, Five Elements Grandmaster actually reaches Heavenly Court and destroys Five Deity Hall using those exact methods.

And this is just ONE arrangement. Who knows how many other things he did like this.

From that single visit, he gained information on Heavenly Court’s defenses and Gu Houses, secretly planted an arrangement inside their own headquarters, and used what he learned there for preparations that would only become important hundreds of thousands of years later.

Then you add everything else on top of it.

The creation/collapse of 88TYB, using that collapse as a signal for the great era, preparing Bing Sai Chuan for the future, building up the Huang Jin bloodline across Northern Plains, uniting Northern Plains, using Call of the Ancient against Heavenly Court’s immortal graveyard, creating counters for Heavenly Court Gu Houses, creating indirect pawns like Five Elements Grandmaster that Heavenly Court wouldn’t connect back to him, and preparing Longevity Heaven to eventually invade Heavenly Court and seize Fate Gu.

He formulated this entire plan from one visit in Heavenly Court.
