His Plan Against the Southern Border Superforces

Wu Yong had been concealing his true capabilities for years, long before his mother’s death. His goal was to strengthen Wu clan’s position in Southern Border and eventually expand its influence and territories even further.

He deliberately concealed his true strength and capabilities, maintaining the image of an ordinary rank 8 who couldn’t compare to his mother. His deception extended to practically everyone around him, including his own clan, trusted subordinates, the other superforces of Southern Border and even his own mother, Wu Du Xiu. He had also kept a rank 8 Immortal Gu House hidden, without even the Gu Immortals of his own clan knowing about it.

Think about how many people he had to deceive for this. His own family, people who had known him for years, Gu Immortals with hundreds or thousands of years of experience, and practically every superforce in Southern Border. And this wasn’t something he maintained for a few days or months. He had to consistently present himself as someone weaker and less capable than he actually was for years, without ever exposing his true abilities or intentions. Even the people closest to him had no idea what he was actually capable of.

After Wu Du Xiu died (his mother), the other superforces believed Wu clan had become significantly weaker. These clans were led by Gu Immortals with hundreds or thousands of years of experience, yet they still underestimated Wu Yong because of the image he had maintained for so long.

They began taking Wu clan’s territories, believing he lacked the strength to retaliate. Wu Yong deliberately allowed this to happen, despite having the strength to stop them.

His hidden Gu House alone was powerful enough to give Wu clan the equivalent of another rank 8 Gu Immortal. Revealing it would have been enough to deter the other superforces and stop them from taking more territories. He still chose to keep it hidden.

His plan was to let Wu clan lose even more territories, allowing the other superforces to expose their greed and ambitions. Once enough territories had been taken, he would counterattack with proper justification under the righteous path’s rules. He could then seize resources and demand compensation from the clans that had attacked him, potentially making Wu clan even greater than before his mother’s death.

The righteous path is made up of powerful clans that compete over resources, territories and influence. Even if one clan is much stronger than another, it can’t just openly attack it without justification, since doing so would damage its reputation and potentially turn the other clans against it. This is why political justification matters so much.

And the righteous path rules are important here. These clans couldn’t just openly seize Wu clan’s territories without a reason. Hou clan, for example, had one of its Gu Immortals pretend to be a demonic cultivator while another pretended to chase him, using that as an excuse to occupy a resource point. Yang clan also spent a long time preparing its justification by getting involved in conflicts near Wu clan’s mortal villages.

Wu Yong understood how these superforces operated and was planning to use their own actions against them. He had already deceived them into believing he was weak, and now they were making decisions based on that false impression. Their attempts to take advantage of Wu clan would eventually give him the justification to retaliate and take even more from them.

And this wasn’t just about recovering his territories. Wu Yong wanted to establish enough authority that the other superforces wouldn’t dare challenge Wu clan again for decades.

Even his closest aide, Wu Fa, had no idea about the plan. This also meant that the people within Wu clan were genuinely responding to the crisis without knowing Wu Yong’s intentions, making the clan’s apparent weakness even more convincing to outsiders. His own subordinates couldn’t leak a plan they didn’t know existed.

Xu Tuo Scheme

This feat goes hand in hand with the first one, since Wu Yong had already spent years establishing the image of someone who was relatively weak and easy to manipulate.

Xu Tuo was a Ba clan spy who had infiltrated Wu clan and become one of Wu Yong’s most trusted subordinates. He was also the highest ranking Ba clan spy within Wu clan.

Wu Yong had already figured out his true identity long ago, yet deliberately kept him around and pretended to trust him. He probably also used Xu Tuo to feed misinformation to Ba clan, since he knew exactly who Xu Tuo was working for. He could control the information Xu Tuo received while Xu Tuo believed he was secretly gathering information for Ba clan.

When Wu Du Xiu decided to leave her personal inheritance to Wu Yi Hai, Wu Yong brought up the issue himself in front of Xu Tuo. He complained about his brother returning and taking his mother’s rank 8 Immortal Gu, acting furious about the situation.

Wu Yi Hai was Wu Du Xiu’s illegitimate son who had spent most of his life in Eastern Sea, away from Wu clan. His return meant he could claim a portion of his mother’s inheritance, including extremely valuable Immortal Gu, while also potentially becoming a political rival to Wu Yong.

Xu Tuo suggested killing Wu Yi Hai, but Wu Yong acted hesitant and brought up his fear of his mother finding out. Xu Tuo continued persuading him, eventually coming up with the plan to leak Wu Yi Hai’s travel route so that the other superforces would assassinate him.

Its very likely that Wu Yong indirectly manipulated Xu Tuo into proposing the plan himself. He was the one who brought up the problem, showed his frustration and then acted hesitant when Xu Tuo suggested killing his brother. This made Xu Tuo continue looking for a solution until he proposed using the other superforces to carry out the assassination.

And remember, Xu Tuo had spent a very long time trying to gain Wu Yong’s trust. He genuinely believed he had finally managed to manipulate him into doing something that would benefit Ba clan.
Xu Tuo even thought that if his involvement in the assassination was exposed, Wu Yong would protect him. He was so convinced of his influence over Wu Yong that he considered himself safe despite the risks.

But Wu Yong had known his identity all along.
Wu Yong went along with the plan. His intention was to have his brother assassinated without taking responsibility, and then publicly expose Xu Tuo as a traitor during his mother’s funeral.

He had already prepared irrefutable evidence against Xu Tuo beforehand. And the funeral was attended by representatives of the Southern Border righteous path, meaning he could expose the traitor in front of the very people whose opinions of him mattered politically.

Theres also the fact that Xu Tuo’s original objective was to weaken Wu clan and damage Wu Yong’s reputation. He wanted Wu Yi Hai’s assassination to create suspicion and internal chaos, making Wu Yong’s position even more unstable.

Wu Yong turned the entire thing against him. The assassination that Xu Tuo thought would weaken Wu clan was going to be used to strengthen Wu Yong’s position instead.

Through this scheme, he could eliminate his brother, secure his mother’s inheritance, dispose of a high ranking Ba clan spy, clear himself of suspicion and strengthen his reputation as the new leader of Wu clan. He also got to expose Ba clan’s infiltration of Wu clan, giving him another political advantage against one of his greatest rivals.

All of this required him to first discover Xu Tuo’s identity, conceal that knowledge for a long time, maintain Xu Tuo’s trust and manipulate him without revealing his intentions. And he was doing this while maintaining his much larger deception against the other superforces.

Xu Tuo genuinely thought he had manipulated Wu Yong, only realizing the truth after Wu Yong exposed him.

However, the real Wu Yi Hai had actually been killed by Fang Yuan during his journey back to Southern Border. Fang Yuan then impersonated him and unexpectedly appeared at Wu Du Xiu’s funeral, introducing a completely unpredictable variable that Wu Yong couldn’t have accounted for.

Wu Yong had to adapt on the spot, since he couldn’t simply reject his supposedly surviving brother in front of everyone. He managed to maintain his image as the new clan leader without exposing his previous involvement, and even began considering other ways to deal with Wu Yi Hai.

3 Assignments Trap

Later, Wu Yong became suspicious of Wu Yi Hai’s growing relationship with Qiao clan. If Wu Yi Hai married Qiao Si Liu, he could gain considerable political influence within Wu clan and potentially threaten Wu Yong’s authority.

And Qiao clan already had considerable influence within Wu clan. They even had connections to Wu clan’s third supreme elder, so a marriage between Wu Yi Hai and Qiao Si Liu could allow them to interfere even further in Wu clan’s internal politics.
Qiao clan relied heavily on Wu clan politically, and having Wu Yi Hai on their side would give them someone with a legitimate claim to influence within Wu clan’s leadership. This was especially dangerous for Wu Yong since he had only recently succeeded his mother as the clan’s leader.

Wu Yong couldn’t simply get rid of Wu Yi Hai either. He already had evidence of his corruption, but Wu Yi Hai was still his brother, and openly taking action against him could create even more political problems. He also couldn’t just force him to end his relationship with Qiao clan, since that could backfire.

So Wu Yong wanted to figure out where Wu Yi Hai truly stood politically, without directly questioning him or revealing his suspicions.

For this, he had to consider the different tasks and responsibilities currently available within Wu clan and select three that could reveal Wu Yi Hai’s intentions. The assignments had to be genuine clan affairs, and each choice had to tell Wu Yong something about his brother.

The first involved Xuan Ming Mountain, where Wu clan was competing with Yang clan over a wild Immortal Gu. Choosing this would show that Wu Yi Hai was willing to take risks and fight for Wu clan’s interests, making him useful to Wu Yong.

The second involved Red Dragon River, where Wu clan and Qiao clan both had territories. Choosing this would give Wu Yi Hai an opportunity to collaborate with Qiao clan, revealing his interest in strengthening their relationship.

The third involved attending Yi clan’s banquet. Since Yi clan had close connections to Eastern Sea, choosing this would suggest that Wu Yi Hai still had attachments to his previous region.

Wu Yong had to select these three assignments from the actual affairs of his clan, work out what each choice would reveal about Wu Yi Hai, and present them naturally without exposing his intentions.

And each task was something Wu clan genuinely needed to handle anyway. So regardless of which one Wu Yi Hai selected, he would either contribute to the clan or reveal something about his political interests.

It was a nearly foolproof way of gathering information, since every available option would tell Wu Yong something about where his brother stood politically. Wu Yi Hai would reveal his priorities through his own decision, without Wu Yong ever having to ask him directly.

Theres also another complication here. Wu Yong was judging Wu Yi Hai based on what he knew about his background, personality and possible ambitions. The real Wu Yi Hai had spent most of his life in Eastern Sea, so Wu Yong could use that information to work out what his choices might mean.

However, the person he was actually dealing with was Fang Yuan, who had killed and impersonated Wu Yi Hai. Fang Yuan had an entirely different personality, different experiences and completely different objectives. Wu Yong had no way of knowing this, meaning he was trying to analyse the psychology and intentions of someone based on the profile of an entirely different person. This also meant that Fang Yuan could make decisions for reasons Wu Yong couldn’t possibly account for with the information available to him.

Wu Yi Hai chose Red Dragon River, confirming Wu Yong’s suspicions. Wu Yong immediately decided that he needed to separate him from Qiao clan before their relationship developed any further.

He then took advantage of the situation at the super Gu formation, where Wu Bei had been injured and Ba clan was creating problems. This gave him a legitimate reason to send Wu Yi Hai back there.

The super Gu formation was an important strategic location controlled by multiple righteous path clans, so assigning Wu Yi Hai there was a legitimate responsibility. It also kept him far away from Wu clan’s political centre and limited his opportunities to build influence through Qiao clan.

And he didn’t even have to confront him about his relationship with Qiao clan. Wu Yi Hai was simply being reassigned to deal with an important matter involving Wu clan’s interests.

Wu Yong had effectively removed him from the political centre of Wu clan, while keeping him occupied with another responsibility. He had also accomplished this through ordinary clan procedures, without giving Qiao clan an obvious reason to oppose him.
