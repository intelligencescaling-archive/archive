How difficult is it to discover and prove the existence of the Men in Black organization?

A couple of days ago I decided to rewatch the Men in Black trilogy (it's amazing, I recommend it) and I realized that the organization as an *outsmarting* situation **would actually be extremely difficult to overcome.**

Throughout the trilogy, the agency demonstrates absolutely massive feats of deception, manipulation, and influence. Their protocols are flawless, allowing them to operate in the heart of New York without leaving a trace or witnesses, thanks to enormous neuralyzers (devices that erase and reprogram memories), such as the one hidden inside the Statue of Liberty, which can be activated to erase the memory of anyone who has seen them. Furthermore, the neuralyzers they possess allow them to rewrite the memories of months or even years of any person with just the push of a button, thanks to which they carry out extreme acts of concealment and fabrication, as they plant evidence of alien existence as scenarios of completely different, ordinary, and everyday events. In addition to what has already been mentioned, the Men in Black system protects and keeps them secret through other measures, such as massive acts of misdirection by creating films that portray their stories as "pure fiction," thus shielding them from potential breaches—Agent M in International being the only exception—since anyone who still witnessed their existence would be dismissed as insane, as the general population lives under the false impression that these stories belong to old, famous movies and nothing more. They have literally manipulated the world's perception to make people believe that reality is fake and to make them skeptical of these cases. Further examples of fabrication and influence come into play here, as MiB controls tabloid newspapers that report encrypted alien information, which, lacking public credibility, are also rejected as mere sophistry.

Finally, the agents pose no risk whatsoever. To begin with, they completely erase their identities from any terrestrial database (the agents don't exist and never have), but not only that, according to Agent Z, their suits were meticulously designed to leave no lasting impression on anyone they encounter, as their appearance exploits a concept of psychological manipulation: inattentional blindness. For added protection, all their distinguishing features have been eliminated: fingerprints, possibly scars, moles, certainly bite marks, and any other trace of forensic analysis, so no matter what they do, they leave no evidence of their presence. The organization controls where they live and even where they eat to guarantee their anonymity, and assigns them false identities to project an image of authority that grants them access to any location, just like any other official.

I don't want to go on any longer, although I could easily also talk about how even governments aren't aware of their existence and the numerous extraterrestrial patents they own and use to fund themselves through nonexistent people (for example, they own Velcro and the microwave), or other feats in the same and other categories, but I'll end here with a question: **Which fictional detectives do you think would be able to discover and publicly prove the existence of Men in Black?** And, if achieving this were a SCD situation, how difficult do you think it would be?
