We know L's ability to analyse, and his ability to process is extremely high just via statements and implications and his monitor hax just adding onto the quality and strength of this feat for Light.

As Light with 64 cameras constantly monitoring him is able to deceive the kira task force and force L into conceding and retreating in this strategy by maintaining and building up the image of perceived normalcy, which is only one layer of this feat, as if the killings suddenly stop with Whilst he is monitored L has a win-condition in proving his guilt, requiring Light to set up the bag of potato chips to simultaneously kill and act at the same time with near perfect facial expressions maintaining a calm posture and outwardly 'normal' demeanour to avoid scrutiny from L.

Clearing Light and Confusing L, Light's request that the death note goes in the hand of someone greedy and that desires power is due to the fact he understands the philosophy and appeal this person would take on after receiving the death note. They'd be inherently more likely to continue killing criminals or just people that oppose them.

Using her love for misa to force rem into ending herself to save misa and kill L, checking off all of boxes in order to counter and predict L relying on his foresight and understanding of the human mind to defeat him.
