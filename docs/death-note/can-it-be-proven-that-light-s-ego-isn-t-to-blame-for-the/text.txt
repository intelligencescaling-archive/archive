It's not assured at all even if Kira is egotistical or prideful. Somebody calculated like L, who we've seen use intricate and intelligent traps (like in the cafe) is inconsistent with the image of L people have in their minds whose grand plan in the broadcast is for the crux to be provoking Kira.
