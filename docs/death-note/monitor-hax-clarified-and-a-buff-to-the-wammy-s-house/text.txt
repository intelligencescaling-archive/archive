The people who can use it aren't related. They have no biological relation to eachother.

Why am I saying this? Because we should delve deep into what the abilities needed for MH actually are. It's learned, not biological.

So anyway, the people taken into the orphanage are.. orphans, who come from different families and places, yet we see multiple examples of WH children using MH (Mello can, even if he's not good at it. Near and L can too.)

If they're not related, it's not something biological. It's not an ability you're born with

This leaves us with 2 options; through human modification or learning. Human modification is a possibility because of how it genuinely violates a lot of things humans can do.

But WH is way more relaxed after the first generations. The generation Near and Mello went through compared to BB is way different, way more relaxed. Less treacherous.

They would've stopped with the modification, especially since you have children going into different fields. So you have no need to modify their bodies to be able to do MH seeing as it would be a waste considering how there are a myriad of different fields which would never require you to use it.

That means it is a learned ability.
