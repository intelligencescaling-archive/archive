Light Yagami Musical --- feats analysed

# Act 1

Light purposefully led L to the conclusion that Kira may be a student by killing outside of school hours. After Light learns from his father Soichiro that L did reach that conclusion he started to schedule criminals to die each hour, so L would not only confirm his hypothesis that Kira’s a student but also realise Kira has access to police information. Light had exploited that weak trust amongst the investigators. L is suspicious of the police, and the police do not trust a man whose face or name they don’t know. Which would lead to L investigating the police and the police investigating L. Light’s endgame is the police figuring out who L is and getting a chance to kill him.
**(EU, SU, SE, Creative Thinking, MM, LM, EM, IM, Information Control)**

When it has been announced that the FBI will send their agents to Japan to investigate the Kira case, Light uses one of the agent’s photos from the press conference to find a post from his finances that leads to the wedding registry with his name. He couldn’t just look for his name directly because the FBI scrubbed it. Light writes down Raye Penber’s name and makes him go to the train station and makes him write down the names of all of his coworkers in Japan on the Death Note sheets he prepared and commit suicide by jumping onto the tracks.
**(Information Gathering)**

# Act 2 and Act 3

Light enrolls into Tooh university, on the entrance exams he scores 100% on each subject and had been chosen to be a freshmen representative. Light learns that there’s another student who also scored 100% on each subject and had been chosen to be a freshmen representative. Hideki Ryuga who tells him that he’s L. He processes that information and asks himself 4 questions in his head faster than anybody can move.

*“I wonder, is this guy for real?*
*I wonder, is he who he claims to be?*
*And if he is, then what's the deal?*
*Why would he make a point of telling me?”*

A bit later in the same speed he reasons out that L’s strategy is probing him to make a mistake by the obviously fake name of Ryuga the pop star.

*“His name is obviously a fake*
*So he inoculates himself from me*
*He's waiting for my first mistake*
*So I'll wait also, but more patiently”*

**(VCI, PSI, Academic Knowledge, Abductive Reasoning, EU)**

2nd Kira sends a message online from her social media account to Kira to meet at crisscross crowd and that she has shinigami eyes. Light reasons out that the 2nd Kira must mean the biggest one, the one at Shibuya. Light goes there despite being followed, and manipulates Misa Amane to give him his tail’s name.
**(Practical Knowledge, Abductive Reasoning, Indirect Manipulation)**

Light tells Misa to meet him at a tennis court in Tooh University the next day at 2 o'clock and that he wants her to meet a friend of his. Right around the time Light and L will be playing tennis.

During this game of tennis both Light and L sing in sync with each other which symbolises how both understand each other to think like each other. L also mentions that he can’t read Light at all.

L sees through the plan and arrests Misa, or so he thinks. Light expected L to arrest Misa since he knew that L knew that 2nd Kira can kill with just a face (at least that’s how he interpreted it) and as a result he put her in a dangerous situation forcing Rem to write down L’s name, the only thing Light added was the scenario.

Light made L set up a meeting with him in the Yellowbox warehouse and bring a gun in order to fabricate a narrative to make it look like L was Kira and brought the gun to kill him due to the lack of evidence.

Even though this scheme has one big hole that is: Why would L bring a gun or even schedule the meeting if he knows Light’s face and a name?

**(EU, EM, SE, Prediction, Acting Skills, Concealment, Misdirection, Trap setting, LM, IM, Scheming)**
