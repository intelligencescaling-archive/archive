In chapter 18 of Death Note Manga It is revealed that L reapeatedly
reviewed the audio and the video recordings from the past five days on
both families.

Context- When L found that Kira was being investigated by Raye
penber L installed electronic bugs and hidden cameras in chief yagami’s
and deputy-chief kitamura’s homes to catch Kira.

Calculations- The number of  cameras installed were 64 in Light’s
room alone. There are 3 rooms in Light’s house, a dining room, a
bathroom and corridor, that will be 5 places (The number of cameras
installed in the bathroom should be negligible) to install cameras.
Let’s suppose that L installed more cameras in Light’s room compared
to other rooms, Let’s say there were just 40 cameras in a room on
average. Then the total Number of cameras would be 40*5 =200 which
is the minimum number , Let’s say that they installed 200 cameras on
both homes , a total of 400 cameras.

had repeatedly reviewed all the Audio and the Video recordings”.

recordings of the nights as in L’s perspective Kira could try to deceive
him in night by making L believe that he is sleeping and L doesn’t need
to monitor him when he is sleeping.
L had booked two extra rooms just to serve as monitoring stations to
perform this feat.
