Executive Summary
This document extracts and analyzes all feats and strategic plans from chapters 24-26 of the GOTNW novel, covering the post-L arc where Light faces Near and Mello simultaneously. A total of 73 individual feats and 22 strategic plans have been identified across the three chapters, each scored 1-10 with L canon as benchmark. All subcategories from the SCD framework are extracted for each feat, and the aggregate average is compared with the previous analysis of chapters 1-23.
Key findings: chapters 24-26 contain the highest density of complex strategic plans in the entire novel, with 22 strategic plans (vs. 28 in chapters 1-23, but across 3 chapters instead of 23). The average feat score for chapters 24-26 is 8.12/10, higher than the chapters 1-23 average of 7.57/10, reflecting the escalating sophistication of Light's planning as he approaches the endgame. The aggregate SCD score rises from 8.7 (chapters 1-23) to 8.9 (chapters 1-26), confirming Light GOTNW's position in Tier 2.5 (Mid-High, equivalent to L canon).
The standout feats include the Morse Code tactile communication system (H246, 10/10), the double-fake Death Note plan (H250, 10/10), the Plan B torso page reveal (H273, 10/10), and Light's meta-strategy of exploiting Near's need to prove rather than merely discover (H274, 10/10). Mello's feats are also analyzed, with his deduction that Light = Kira scoring 8.5/10 and his Takada kidnapping strategy scoring 8.0/10.
