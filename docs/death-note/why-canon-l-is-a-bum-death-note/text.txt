Why Canon L is a BUM

The primary reason L is so heavily overrated comes down to a specific group of fans who love to push the narrative of *"you all just lack the critical thinking needed to correctly interpret him."* In reality, all they are doing is plain glazing in the name of "analysis." Honestly, I’m not even sure if they are genuinely that delusional or if they’re just desperate to maintain the propaganda.

Fans constantly bring up bogus interpretations of feats like the LABB light novel or the stock market stuff to hyper-buff him. But whether it’s File 15 or LABB, it’s the exact same animanga L in a different timeline. The manga gives us the most accurate and precise display of his thinking ability, how he handles cases, his profiling, and every other outsmarting aspect and it is way more sophisticated than these glazed interpretations. People will literally look at the stock market feat, apply real-world logic to it, and claim L has "superhuman learning ability and perfect reasoning." Interpretations like that on feats purely made for glazing don't just distort the character, they completely contradict his actual outsmarting ability and intelligence in the story.

Let me give you a simple example of where his supposedly god-like reasoning completely fails.

He literally has physical evidence on Misa; rare cat hairs, clothing fibers, and matching DNA. This proves she is at least a complicit accomplice, which is a serious global crime and gives him full legal grounds to hold her. The Task Force themselves literally backed this evidence up during the Second Kira Arc. Yes, he held them under surveillance, and he knew they didn't have their memories. But then one day, Misa starts acting completely different. Later on, when they are in the helicopter, L not only lets Light hold the literal murder weapon right after Higuchi tells them it’s the weapon, but Higuchi suddenly dies on the spot and L does nothing about it.

It was completely unsafe to release them, especially Misa who had seen his face multiple times. At the absolute least, just test the 13-day rule first and then release her. As for the Task Force crying about it? So what? He isn't obligated to listen to them. He could have told them to leave, especially since they were obstructing his investigation anyway. Releasing them was a terrible decision.

And you want to know why Light is actually cooked if Misa isn't released?

Early in the Yotsuba/Confinement arc, Rem agreed to cooperate with Light’s plan under one strict, fatal condition: if Misa was not safely released, Rem would immediately kill Light.

From L’s point of view, there was absolutely zero logical reason to release her. He held overwhelming physical evidence linking her to the Second Kira tapes.

L fully held the power to make the calls, and testing the rule before releasing them would have been completely fine as it’s just 13 more days. The Task Force didn't think they'd die anyway, and L didn't actually believe the rule, so there was literally nothing to lose. It's also wild how in those panels, L doesn't even mention the DNA and hairs on the tapes to defend keeping her. Even if she was being used, accomplice to murder is a serious crime and reasonable grounds for detention.

What makes it crazier is L's theory right before capturing Higuchi. Light had made him confident that the Kira power was swapped via Light's own will rather than some greater force. Light stated verbatim that if he were Kira, he would have planned for the power to return to him so he could kill L and take his position, which is exactly what happens! Yet L still caves.

On top of all that, he instructs Watari to delete all information if something goes wrong. Why the fuck would you do that? Send it to Wammy's House or the police! Instead, the evidence completely disappears, the physical traces are forgotten, and L lets her go.

So yeah, your favorite detective navigating global systems with "superhuman reasoning, unparalleled strategic decision-making, and god-like learning" isn't so god-like after all. On top of that, he lacks basic critical thinking at times too 😹

He also has some serious mentality issues. It doesn't completely demerit his intelligence, but it definitely hurts his outsmarting ability. For instance, poor L went with the less unscrupulous method; he wanted to catch Kira red-handed with tangible evidence and determine their methodology. Like Near later stated, using ex post facto justification just wasn't L's modus operandi. Even if the killings stopped, it wouldn’t "prove" to L that Light was the one doing it the whole time. L wanted a perfect psychological victory. Light, on the other hand, wasn't held back by any ideology. He eliminated everyone by utilizing every single resource available to him, including literal gods of death.

Canon L and Light are both absolute BUMS that’s why one of them canonically extreme diff’ed the other. Even if you have L winning against Light(I have L winning too) the diff is not below very high diff in any universe unless you are a hard core COTE or Deathnote fan🤣
