Orthogonal Crazyhouse Chess Feat Debunk
First of all let’s understand what the phrase “seeing x moves ahead” means. Magnus Carlsen, the current peak of humanity can see 15 to 20 moves ahead allegedly. Most grandmasters claim they usually see 3,4,5, or even 10 moves ahead when they are forced situations. This simply means that they see a few ways the next 3 to 20 moves can go. This is because with only 2 moves there are 400 ways a game can go. I doubt people compute 400 possible combinations before starting a game in seconds. That would be superhuman already and beyond what actual human calculators can do.

I hate to use AI for this, but something is better than nothing. As you can see a 10 move game along is somewhere around 10^30 possible combinations. I doubt any human can do that. Usually when people say they “see x moves ahead” they usually mean they see a few combinations of x moves. That’s all. So at the minimum we can say they thought about x moves. The feat in question however stems from this statement in the novel:

It mentions L looking hundreds of moves ahead. This would only mean that L computed at the minimum 200 moves. It is nowhere near the proposed 9.546887x10^273 computations in the doc. This would only be true if it were stated in the novel that L looked into all possible combinations of the next 100 moves. However nothing like that is stated. So this debuffs L’s CPI from doing 10^273 computations to 200 computations as a conservative estimate. The doc also mentions L visualizing 10^273 chess boards which is again never stated. Note that 200 moves is still a superhuman feat in CPI and VSI.
4D Visualization Debunk

Apparently some people use the statement of L not looking at the chessboard while moving his knight back and forth as an argument for 4D visualization. I’d like to point out a couple of things here:
1. L is moving his Knight back and forth.
2. L is not looking at the chessboard while doing that.
Now I’d like to present another statement from the novel:

L moving the knight back and forth was not only against the rules (no, that does NOT confirm it was crazyhouse), it was just a metaphor for Suruga from L. Which means at the time he wasn’t even playing a real game. He was spacing out. Even if we grant that L was looking at the monitor while simultaneously visualizing the chessboard, that would just be parallel processing and multitasking. Which would be impressive but not comparable at all to visualizing 4 spatial dimensions.

This is a common argument made in favor of 4D visualization. First of all, popular grandmasters often visualize chess boards in their heads. That is nothing special. Secondly, you can visualize a 3D object in a 2D grid. That’s how human visualization and imagination works. The images in our retinas are always 2D. Our imaginations as well are 2D. We might think they are 3D if we rotate them in our heads, or use the different shadows and depth to create a 3D perception, but that doesn’t change the fact that it is all 2D. It’s similar to a drawing or a video of a 3D object.
Thirdly, a weird nitpick but, you can’t really visualize temporal dimensions like that. It would have been one thing if the argument said 4 spatial dimensions with coordinates x, y, z, w, but mentioning a temporal dimension is completely unnecessary. Visualizing an object with 3 spatial dimensions and a temporal dimension would mean someone is visualizing a 3D object with uncountably infinite snapshots simultaneously, while visualizing an object with 4 spatial dimensions would be like visualizing a tesseract. Nothing of that sort is ever stated. L is just visualizing a chessboard. It is very common and probably peak human level with wank.
