Shinigami DEBUNKED

"Light manipulated gods" is a horrendous claim to be making when the so-called gods of the death note world are mere fodders.

The random shinigami are bad enough, but even the shinigami "king" is shown to be deceivable.

1: shinigami feel boredom.

2: shinigami can get pissed from losing gambling matches.

3: shinigami can be shocked such as Ryuk being shocked that Light wrote many names in the notebook.

Quote: “I just didn’t feel like I was really alive… In actual fact, shinigami these days don’t have a lot to do. All they do is nap, or gamble. If they see you scribbling humans’ names into your Death Note, they say ‘what’re you working so hard for?’ and laugh at you.” — Ryuk

4: shinigami can feel like they aren't really alive (lethargy).

5: shinigami are generally lazy. Many of them forget to write names and die off after losing their lifespans, or they wait until their lifespan nears the end before desperately trying to write a name.

6: shinigami are conceited. They even take pride in their laziness by mocking those who aren't lazy.

7: shinigami are against hard work.

8: shinigami are bound to reputation. Another example of this is when someone mocks Ryuk for having no shinigami pride and becoming Light's pet.

9: shinigami are bound to the rules set by the shinigami king. For example if a shinigami writes a name to extend another person's lifespan, the shinigami disintegrates. Another rule is that the shinigami can become bound to a human for the whole lifespan of that human if the human finds the Death Note. There are many rules like this, for example at one point the shinigami Sidoh takes out a long scroll containing many rules he is bound to.

10: "The realm of the shinigami has really gone to the dogs these days. Nobody even knows what we’re here for anymore. Talk about a meaningless existence." — Ryuk

11: even something as important as killing a shinigami (which is by them killing a human using the death note in order to extent another human's lifespan) is not common sense for them. For example Ryuk didn't even know this. Rem who was shown to know this only found out after witnessing it personally.

12: Ryuk felt jittery because he felt like he was being watched, to the point where he interfered and let Light know he's being stalked. (Raye Penber)

13: shinigami can get addicted and go into withdrawal when they stop consuming what they're addicted to. Ryuk was so addicted to apples, he let Light know the positions of all the cameras in his room.

14: shinigami can feel meek when confronted by humans. After Light screamed at Ryuk and got mad at him, Ryuk sweated nervously and said "...uh... sorry..."

15: shinigami are bound to linguistics even among themselves. For example at one point a shinigami said "what the hell's he thinking?" and another shinigami assumed he was talking about Ryuk, after which he got corrected that he's referring to Light by the word "he."

16: shinigami do not hide information well enough. Rem gave out the information on how to kill shinigamis to Misa.

17: shinigami can fall in love and sacrifice themselves to save humans. Two shinigami were explicitly shown to do this.

18: shinigami are also bound to morality. For example Rem saying "humans are such ugly creatures" after hearing Yotsuba talk about killing innocent people.

19: when L asked about humans losing their memories, Rem replied with "who knows..." which is suspicious and makes L less likely to believe in Misa and Light's innocence. Light confirms this in his mind and calls Rem an idiot.

20: shigami are generally not knowledgable about the human world. One of them was surprised about the high quantity after hearing that there are billions of humans.

21: Ryuk is even less intelligent than Rem, to the point where Light wishes Rem was the one alive after asking Ryuk a question about death notes and Ryuk being confused and uncertain.

22: even the shinigami king can be deceived. A shinigami went to the shinigami king asking for his notebook, and the shinigami king replied that Ryuk said it was his and thus took it. This is further confirmed at the start of the story when the random shinigami mock the king behind his back without any consequences.

23: a shinigami even less intelligent than Ryuk was manipulated into standing guard for Mello rather than letting them die to take his notebook back. Both Ryuk and Light mocked him for this, calling him "not that smart".

In conclusion, shinigami are nothing special, most of the ones with a lot of screentime are on the level of a normal human. Actually, most of the common ones might even be below the level of a normal human. So whenever someone says "Light manipulated death gods" or anything stupid like that, you can choose to ignore them unless they make it clear how intellectually challenging it really was.
