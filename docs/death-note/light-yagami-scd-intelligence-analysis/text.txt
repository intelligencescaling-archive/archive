Introduction and Methodology
This document presents a comprehensive, feat-by-feat intelligence analysis of Light Yagami (Kira) as portrayed in the first eighteen chapters of the Death Note fan fiction "God of The New World," written by u/AnnualFrequent7426 and u/Last-Trainer-333. The analysis employs the SCD (Smart Character Discussion) methodology, a community-driven framework developed on Reddit for the rigorous, comparative assessment of fictional character intelligence. The SCD approach draws inspiration from the WAIS (Wechsler Adult Intelligence Scale), adapting its index-based structure for fictional analysis while expanding far beyond standardized testing into the domains of strategic thinking, psychological manipulation, deception, social intelligence, and adversarial reasoning.
Every single intelligence feat performed by Light Yagami across the eighteen chapters has been extracted, categorized, and scored on a 1-10 comparative scale. Complex feats, those involving multi-layered strategies, significant psychological warfare, or operationally sophisticated methodology, receive detailed breakdowns covering context, objective, methodology, reasoning process, psychological warfare elements, and outcome. Simpler feats receive condensed summaries with their relevant SCD category tags and scores. The scoring is comparative, meaning a score of 10 in a given subcategory represents the theoretical ceiling of what a fictional character of this type has demonstrated in SCD discussions, not an absolute standard.
The taxonomy encompasses 16 primary categories with over 100 subcategories: Strategy (11 sub), Planning (10 sub), Manipulation (12 sub), Deception (6 sub), Thinking (11 sub), Reasoning (8 sub), Foresight (3 sub), Emotional Intelligence (6 sub), Social Intelligence (8 sub), Adversity Capacity (6 sub), Insight (9 sub), Field Skills (7 sub), Adaptability (5 sub), Psychology (8 sub), Counteraction (3 sub), and Countering (5 sub). WAIS-inspired composite indices (VCI, PRI, EI) are calculated at the document's conclusion to provide a holistic intelligence profile.

A total of 70 distinct intelligence feats were identified and extracted from the first eighteen chapters. Context
Finds a supernatural notebook that claims to kill anyone whose name is written in it. A rationalist mind requires empirical proof before committing to any course of action.
Objective
Confirm strict causality between writing a name and the target's death, ruling out coincidence through controlled experimentation.
Methodology
Two experiments: (1) Otoharada Kurou, specifying suicide by gunshot, with 40-second observation window. (2) Shibumaru Takuo, written 7 times with different kanji pronunciations, specifying traffic accident. Each test isolates a variable to verify the mechanism.
Reasoning
If the notebook were a coincidence, varying the kanji would produce different results. The fact that all 7 variations work proves the notebook operates on semantic identity, not written form. This suggests supernatural cognition, not mechanical process.
Psychological Warfare
None directed externally. Internally, Light constructs a self-justification framework: 'If I eliminate those who corrupt this world, am I not doing justice?' This rationalization is the first step in the god-complex formation.
Outcome
Causality confirmed. Both targets die exactly as specified. Light's scientific mind is satisfied, and the god-complex begins to crystallize.

Context
After beginning his Kira operations, Light recognizes that death patterns can be analyzed to determine his location. Every intelligence agency in the world is watching.
Objective
Create a complete false geographic profile that diverts all international investigation toward Russia/Eastern Europe, away from Kanto, Japan.
Methodology
Thought like a genuine Russian person: imagined their routine, work hours, and killing schedule. Used geostrategic misdirection targeting Russia as zone zero, then spread outward following realistic propagation delays. Made the pattern so coherent that the profile of a real human being emerged from the data.
Cross-referenced timezone calculations with cultural norms. The statistical distribution of 1,862 deaths in Russia, 477 in USA, 339 in Ukraine, 72 in Kazakhstan, 22 in Turkey, and 42 in Iran created a plausible Russian-centered identity.
Reasoning
Investigators rely on pattern recognition. If you give them a perfect pattern, they will follow it. The human brain seeks narrative coherence; a Russian profile with realistic spread provides that coherence.
By targeting the assumption that Kira operates where the most deaths occur, Light exploits the fundamental cognitive bias of all detectives: proximity equals causation.
Psychological Warfare
This is a masterpiece of psychological manipulation at the civilizational level. Light created a non-existent person with a complete routine, schedule, and geographic profile. Every intelligence agency in the world followed the phantom.
Interpol blamed the KGB. The CIA blamed each other. Even L, the world's greatest detective, admitted: 'All the agents and detectives in the world, with a single line of thought at a time, fell into your elaborate trap.
I almost fell too.' Only L's atypical, non-linear, neurodivergent thinking style could break through, and even he nearly didn't.
Outcome
COMPLETE SUCCESS. Every conventional detective and intelligence agency was fooled. The misdirection held until L's unconventional analysis broke through.
This single feat demonstrates grand-strategic thinking, operational planning at continental scale, and a deep understanding of how intelligence analysis works.

Context
Family dinner. Soichiro is exhausted and carrying classified information about the Kira investigation. Light needs to know what L has deduced without raising suspicion.
Objective
Extract investigation status, L's current hypotheses, and team dynamics from Soichiro through casual conversation.
Methodology
(1) Opens with casual, non-threatening question: 'Kira again, right?' calibrated to show interest without urgency. (2) Displays concern with precisely measured facial expression.

Uses modesty as a weapon: 'Modesty was a weapon. It always worked.' By appearing humbler than he is, Light makes his father feel like the wise mentor sharing knowledge with an eager student, when in reality the student is the predator extracting intelligence from the prey.
Outcome
Successfully extracts: (a) L suspects Kira is a student, (b) the team is confused by changing hypotheses, (c) internal divisions between L's followers and skeptics. Critical intelligence for counter-strategy.
