My interpretation of memory loss as an anime watcher

I could start with a long ahh intro but no need I'll get right into it

INITIAL: Light did the notebook swap trick with Rem and Ryuk, he then explained to both of them his plan and gave Rem some times to choose a third Kira. He also told Rem to tell Misa that Light wants her to give up ownership of the death note.

This tactic accomplishes 3 objectives:

The first is that it creates the first hint towards Light's second Kira misdirection. Light predicted that Misa will act differently after losing her memory and that L would pick up on that. (Rem unintentionally reinforced this tactic by touching Misa's hair)

The second objective this tactic accomplishes is that it will make sure that Misa can do the eye deal again and it itself adds other layers of shielding as when the task force gets the death note, they will talk to Rem and not Ryuk (will be very important for later)

The third tactic this accomplishes is add yet another layer of shielding. Rem clearly hesitated when Light asked her to kill L and Light doesn't want his plan to fail just because midway Rem had a change of earth. This third tactic is a direct result of the second tactic as Rem will spend time near a person who is greedy and power hungry and Light predicts that Rem will naturally not like said person which creates a false dichotomy for Rem where she will subconsciously view Light as the least bad options compared to others. Light will look better in comparison as he is protecting somebody Rem loves and he is doing all of this with a noble goal in mind unlike that other person who will use it for their own benefit.

Secondly:

Light gives up his ownership of the death note when he's jailed. This tactic also accomplishes a few really important objectives.

1: It completes the basis of the misdirection as Light predicted he will act differently

2: It shields his strategy by forcing L's hand into having him in the investigation

This is the initial stage. You may be asking "But how does the second tactic guarantees Light's involvement in the third Kira case"?

Let's look at it from L's perspective:

Light is Kira and Misa is the second Kira.

Miss has been imprisoned for a while and she suddenly out of nowhere started acting differently as if she was kidnapped.

Light also requests to be sent in jail because he suspects he may be Kira. While in jail, the killings stop.

A few weeks pass, and Light suddenly also starts acting differently out of nowhere and the killings starts happening again.

L, reasonably so, is  confused. He doesn't and has no way to know what's happening yet. He doesn't know what light did and therefore the optimal move for L in this instant is to keep Light and Misa near him so he can monitor their actions while he tries to uncover the scheme. And the fact Kira started killing again gives L the opportunity to do so by chaining himself to Light and having Matsuda watch over Misa.  L also doesn't really have any other alternatives. He has no grounds to keep Light and Misa jailed when the killings are happening again and not having Light and Misa near him is straight up reckless strategically speaking.

Second part: YOTSUBA.

The Yotsuba arc does not really have anything new and most of it is planned. Light misdirection with the third Kira however, unlike what some people say, in my opinion wasn't what light was planning. Instead this misdirection was a fake deception to a fake strategy (I will explain earlier) that acted as a double concealment for Light's true trap. Light however know L isn't dumb, having his entire plan rely on a simple misdirection is too fragile so Light anticipated that L would abduct the general concept of memory loss. Notice how I say the general concept, L didn't knew exactly how Memory loss would go but he abducted the concept. This is an important part of Light's third Kira misdirection. Furthermore, we can infer Light anticipated his own reactions months in advance during the Yotsuba arc as the good Light is analyzing Kira and noticing how similar their ideas are which causes Light to question himself on if he was Kira. This anticipation is mostly another layer of shielding. It's meant to reinforce the third Kira misdirection to keep L occupied because L anticipating it too early would be bad.

RETRIEVING THE NOTE BOOK:

This is where most of the stuff that was planned for happens. At this stage of the story, L has reasoned the general concept of memory loss however because of its nature there is nothing else he can infer until they catch the third Kira and learn how his/her powers work.

When the task force catches Kira and Light regains his memory, Light actually does a really good tactic. This was improvised as Light had no way to know when he would regain his memories, but it's killing Higuchi.

Killing Higuchi achieves these objectives.

1: It frames Rem.

Higuchi dying before the task force can question him is highly suspicious which leaves Rem as the only potential suspect. Light is very unlikely to be Kira thanks to his prison tactic and Misa isn't even in the scene. Therefore in the eyes of the task force it will look like Rem killed Higuchi. The best part is that if questioned, Rem has to play along because if she denies responsibility they would look for other suspects which is against Rem's best interests. This is connected to an earlier tactic with the notebook swap earlier. Light knows Rem will maintain this lie because of her attachment to Misa however if it was Ryuk, he could simply say whatever and sabotage Light just for the fun of it.

2: It shields the plan even more.

Light knows that L will find it highly suspicious how Higuchi died which will lead him to the conclusion that Light is highly the one behind it. L knows the concept of memory loss and could suspect Higuchi's death gave L an advantage. Light also knows that L will notice the torn paper in the notebook and from there on he'll know how Light did it which will confirm in his mind that Light HAS to be Kira. This also double act as a double layer of shielding because in a very bad scenario for Light where L failed to abduct the general purpose of memory loss, Higuchi's death will re confirm his suspicions that it's Light which will encourage him to test the 13 day rules.

3: It gives a cover for L's death.

Light knows that Rem will kill L as part of his plan. However he isn't dumb enough to think that just because he killed L means he won. He still has to convince the task force. Once Rem kills L, Light can use the fact Higuchi died to convince the task force it's likely Rem also killed L. And he can also use this line of reasoning:

"Rem killed Higuchi when he got caught and the moment we got distracted and tried to test something with the death note it grabbed it, ran away with it and killed L. It's likely that death notes are very important tools for Shinigamis and she used the first opportunity available to escape with the notebook"

Light could probably come up with something better than this since he's smarter than me but you get the general idea. If Rem killed Higuchi it makes the narrative that Rem killed L for XYZ reasons more plausible.

Anyways to go back onto the main topics, the 13 day rules also has a second layer to it which is deceiving L even more. Let's look at it from his perspective:

-He knows Light and Misa erased their memory to gain an advantage later.

-Now that he has the notebook, he knows the rules and he knows Light did not had the notebook with him yet the killings eventually restarted

-L could abduct from this that Light had help from Shinigami in delivering the notebook to somebody else.

-With this and the fact the 13 day rules is the least logically plausible rule, it's likely Light asked a Shinigami to forge a rule to confirm his and Misa's innocence.

-L can also confirm that this rule also means that he is in severe danger. After all he can't keep Misa in jail so L probably anticipates that Light's strategy was to use this rule to clear their name and then use Misa to kill L scottfree.

This is why in the anime, L asked Light why he stayed in the HQ even when the Kira case was over. It was a trap to make Light sound more suspicious in the eyes of the task force. However Light completely countered this by ordering Misa to start killing again.

From L's perspective it now looks like this actually was Light's strategy all along: Forge a fake rule to clear his name, create another misdirection of another Kira so that the case would be reopened and Light and Misa could stay near L, and then Light uses Misa to kill L.

So now L's options are extremely limited and there's only one option left: Debunking the 13 day rules. This also serves, from L's perspective, as an inescapable trap.

If Light kills him, it will look extremely suspicious on him and Misa. The task force could eventually converge to the fact the rule may be forged since L tried to debunk it and instead decide to put Light and Misa yet again in prison which would be a game over for both of them.

If Light doesn't kill him the same option happens.

This also explains the rain scene in the anime. L, knowing that Light is a petty and egomaniac manchild, will choose option 1. L knows that Light will see through his strategy and that Light will decide that if he goes down, L will go down with him. The scene in the anime isn't L begging for mercy, it's both  as a final goodbye to Light and to see who Light truly is: If Light was just corrupted by the death note or if Light was always Kira within and the death note simply allowed him to unleash his true self on the world.

However, L does not anticipate that Rem will kill him. It doesn't really makes sense for L, after all the relationship between Light and Rem must  be limited because if Light's Shinigami was willing to tell him L's name L would have died a while ago. It makes sense L would think the forgery was maybe a one time arrangement instead of the more wild explanation that what is essentially a demon fell in love with Misa and is willing to kill L to protect L, and therefore Misa by default.
