I've rewritten Light's final plan from the musical

img src= [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pk6\_3UOQ3XE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pk6_3UOQ3XE)

I had an issue with the ending of the musical; don't get me wrong, I liked it, but I've always felt Light's plan wasn't all that smart because of one issue.

Why would L bring a gun or even schedule the meeting if he knows Light's face and name?

I came up with the idea that allows the confrontation in the warehouse and Light's death to happen while making the plan much smarter.

\--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The first thing I would change is removing the gun from the equation.

Light's new scenario for L:

"L Lawliet - schedules a meeting with his top 1 Kira suspect in the Yellowbox Warehouse without any cameras or other people. He confronts the man he invited, and as the meeting comes to an end, he realises Kira's moral and intellectual superiority and submits himself to him as his new God. Over the course of the next 22 days, he does everything in his power to remove any suspicion that his main suspect is Kira. On the 23th day he'll choke on a sweet to death after removing all the data he has on the Kira case."

This new scenario allows for the confrontation in the warehouse to happen and the "Way it Ends" to be sung with a minor change in the "you will die today" line.

\--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Additional element I would add, but it wouldn't have to be in there:

First, I would add a conversation between L and a voice from a computer he would call N. L would tell N about the case while following the scenario from the note (I would throw in a line about L promising before the case to tell N how it was); he would tell N how he mistakenly suspected Light of being Kira and how he cleared him, and then he would tell N that after that he had no leads, and the case went cold.

The next scene would be a criminal who has been trailed for blackmail and won that trial, walking to a prosecution office after a year.

"\[Insert Name\]- after 22 days, he finds at a \[insert place\] a package whose recipient is written on the box to be Prosecutor Mikami working in \[insert a place\], and the date of receipt is a year later; he decides to keep the package and not open it, not knowing that, in his own body, lung cancer began to develop. A year later, the man brings the package to the prosecution office and tells a woman at the reception that a mysterious man had given him the package and told him to give it to Mr Mikami. After that, the package will find itself in the intended hands; he lives for another half a year and dies of lung cancer."

Teru Mikami gets the box and finds inside a letter and a plastic capsule (the one that can be found in machines in Japan), and in the letter he reads that Kira has chosen him to be his successor for the mantle of Kira and that the piece of paper in the plastic capsule allows him to kill anyone whose name he'll write on it.

\--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Thought process behind the successor contingancy:

First of all Light would know that if a task is impossibe the victim dies of a heart attack and that if a victim is written to die of an illness the Death note user can manipulate him for as long as he needs. After Light writes down the victims name and leaves the package at a chosen place the only thing he needs to do in order to cancel the plan is to take away the package before the 22 days will pass, so Light has a way to make sure if he dies or will be unable to be Kira he'll have a successor.

Ryuk at the end kills Light and comes back to the Shinigami realm, so if someone were to have a piece of the note on himself and write down a name that person would become the new owner of the notebook and force Ryuk to bring the Death Note which belongs to the human realm to it's human owner, Teru.
