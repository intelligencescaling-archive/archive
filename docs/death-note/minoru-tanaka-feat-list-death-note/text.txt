Minoru Tanaka feat list

Minoru Tanaka intelligence feats:

**"Yep, I can go with the plan I came up with two years ago."** \- A-Kira

As a strategic move, he asked Ryuk, when he first received the Death Note, to repossess it and return it to him after two years, giving him time to weigh his options instead of acting without a plan. He did this because he knew and reasoned that the advanced technology of the modern world, compared to the era of the original Kira, would make things far more difficult. He also needed a Yotsuba Bank account for his plan, which he was not old enough to have at the time. This feat showcases \[LGT (Logical Thinking) DM (Decision Making) LR (Logical Reasoning) ST (Strategic Thinking) and Foresight\].

He begins his plan after the two year wait by having Ryuk deliver a message to Sakura TV stating: “THE POWER TO KILL REMOTELY, AS USED BY KIRA, IS BEING PUT UP FOR AUCTION. IF YOU WANT TO BUY IT, USE THE #POWEROFKIRA ON TWITTER AND INDICATE YOUR BID AMOUNT IN AMERICAN DOLLARS.” This allows him to identify every bidder online without revealing anything about himself. Next, he has Ryuk deliver another message to Sakura TV: “ONE WEEK LEFT UNTIL THE END OF THE AUCTION. IT MAY END PREMATURELY AS WELL. YOU WILL BE INFORMED IF THAT HAPPENS. THE TRANSACTION WILL BE FINAL. NO COMPLAINTS, NO RETURNS.” This triggers a bidding war between the United States and China. He has Ryuk deliver an additional message to snuff out all the government spies: “DON’T BOTHER WITH TWITTER. HAVE YOUR GOVERNMENT REPRESENTATIVES APPEAR ON MEDIA TO MAKE AN OFFICIAL ANNOUNCEMENT OF HOW MUCH YOU WILL PAY.” He ensures that only monetary bids are accepted by having Ryuk announce: “ANY WORLD LEADER MUST NAME A PRICE ONLY. NO OTHER NONSENSE.” He ultimately sells the Death Note to the American President for one quadrillion yen. His final message, delivered through Ryuk to Sakura TV, reveals the outcome he intended: “THE MONEY FROM THE WINNING BID SHOULD BE DEPOSITED BY MAY 27TH OF THIS YEAR IN EQUAL AMOUNTS TO EVERY PERSON WITH A YOTSUBA BANK OF JAPAN SAVINGS ACCOUNT AND A REGISTERED RESIDENCE, AGED 60 AND UNDER AS OF MAY 24TH, 2019 — WHICH EQUALS ONE BILLION YEN PER PERSON.” In the end, he creates an untraceable one-billion-yen deposit for himself and hides any trace of it leading back to him by having the money distributed to countless other people just like him. He uses Ryuk as a proxy for all messages as A-Kira, never using a Twitter account and instead monitoring the hashtag to identify bidders. He also forces government officials to reveal themselves and publicly fight over the bidding. He also has Ryuk return to his house after delivering the message to Sakura TV through underground routes, ensuring no one who might see Ryuk can identify him as A-Kira. This feat showcases \[LGT (Logical Thinking) CT (Creative Thinking) PP (Planning Practicability) PC (Planning Convergence) PS (Problem Solving) DP (Detailed Planning) ST (Strategic Thinking) and Adaptability\].
