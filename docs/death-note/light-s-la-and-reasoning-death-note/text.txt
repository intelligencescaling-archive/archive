Light's LA and Reasoning

This is a little analysis I made of Light, and it's my first one. I don't really wish to debate anyone right now, but if you disagree, please explain why. Some points will be rehashed and reused from a Light Yagami doc I just read (Light Yagami Reevaluation), and these are points that I would like to bring to "light" (Get it?).

This analysis will be mostly lowballed, and so whatever I say is likely the worst version/interpretation of his feats.

Light's Current Position:
After killing L, the taskforce is left without a leader, without their main weapon. All the data related to the case has been deleted, and they are faced with the major setback of no longer possessing the abilities of the world's greatest detective. After some deliberation, Light is chosen as L's successor, and it is to be hidden that L has died.

Light's LA:

Light has to invent two voice modulators using the limited technology of the mid 2000's, the modulators having the constraints of being unable to be reverse-engineered, as well as the constraint of exactly replicating the voice modulators of both Watari and L. Keep in mind that he likely did this under time pressure, considering it would be quite odd if L were to be radio silent for over even a month. Lowballing this feat to the max, Light singlehandedly invented two voice modulators, which we are just now catching up to within two months.

During this period of time, Light also has to amass the knowledge of L so as to effectively take on his role. Of course, there will be gaps in this knowledge, and yet it's still insanely impressive to do so. Taking into account that there's bound to be gaps in his knowledge, and that he probably already had a solid base, Light (likely during the time of the previous feat) learned enough to effectively operate as L, all whilst maintaining his health (as Light is someone who, as we see early on, rarely eats junk food and values sleep).

Overall:
1.  Light singlehandedly invented two voice modulators, which we are just now catching up to (in terms of tech) within two months.
2. Simultaneously, whilst Light did all of this, he amassed enough knowledge to functionally operate as L while being on top of his health.

Light's Reasoning:

Considering Light had to take up the mantle of L, it is fair to assume he, for the period of time he operated as L, solved cases at L's level (prior to the Kira case). To establish what type of cases are at "L's level", L, it is stated that he singlehandedly solves cases that are considered impossible. It's also highly plausible that L began to solve these "unsolvable cases" not long after he began work as a detective. It is stated that the first case he ever worked on was "an unsolved serial murder case without any clues", therefore, it's not a stretch to say that he began his work on the unsolvable (and solved them), early on into his career. It goes without saying that L's ability to solve cases grew with time, and that would mean that by the age of 24, L's ability had transcended the impossible by miles. Going back to my original point, this means that, having to take on the role of L, Light's ability would have at least had to have been  comparable to L's in terms of detective work and reasoning with much less experience prior to the Kira case.

L is also stated to work on multiple cases at a time, and we all know he gets very little sleep. As I mentioned earlier, Light prioritizes sleep, and so that means that Light has far less time than L to solve cases at L's level.

Light's reasoning, through this narrative, is extremely underrated.

Conclusion:

Light is overly downplayed, and actually possesses some crazy reasoning and LA.
