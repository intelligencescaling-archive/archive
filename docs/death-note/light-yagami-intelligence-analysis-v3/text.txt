   4. Key Insights About Light's Intelligence Profile

Planning Dominance: With 31 detailed planning feats, Light demonstrates the most sophisticated planning capability in the analysis. His plans are characterized by multiple layers of redundancy, pre-positioned countermeasures, and the rare ability to plan for his own cognitive incapacitation (memory wipe).

Strategic Depth: Light's 25 strategy feats reveal a thinker who operates at the game-theoretic level, reframing all actors as pieces with utility functions and move sets. His application of military theory (Napoleon at Austerlitz) and game theory (Nash equilibrium with Rem) demonstrates cross-domain strategic thinking.

Deception as Core Competency: With 25 deception feats, Light's deceptive capabilities are not auxiliary skills but a core cognitive function. His ability to manufacture genuine-seeming emotions, maintain multiple false narratives simultaneously, and instantly conceal information recovery suggests that deception operates at near-automatic processing levels.

Reasoning Triad Mastery: Light's 22 reasoning feats show balanced mastery of all three reasoning modes
- deductive, inductive, and abductive. The Yotsuba deduction in particular demonstrates the integration of all three modes: inductive pattern recognition, abductive hypothesis generation, and deductive elimination.

Psychological Warfare Expertise: Light's 16 psychology feats reveal an intuitive psychologist who reads people with extraordinary speed and accuracy. His cold reading of Naomi Misora and comprehensive profiling of 8 Yotsuba board members demonstrate rapid, accurate personality assessment under time pressure.

Cognitive Dual-Processing: Multiple feats demonstrate Light's ability to maintain two independent cognitive tracks simultaneously - killing with one hand while studying with the other, teaching his sister while strategizing, performing innocence while concealing guilt. This dual-processing capability is a direct manifestation of exceptional WMI and PSI.

Chapter 3 and Chapter 5 as Peak Density: The feat density analysis reveals that Chapters 3 and 5 contain the highest concentration of intelligence feats, corresponding to Light's initial defensive architecture construction and the FBI elimination operation respectively - two of the most cognitively demanding sequences in the narrative.

Adversity Resilience Pattern: Light's adversity capacity feats reveal a consistent pattern: the ability to maintain composure under direct threat, suppress emotional responses within seconds, and transform hostile
conditions into strategic opportunities. This resilience is not passive endurance but active exploitation of adversity.

Conclusion: Light Yagami's intelligence profile as documented across 18 chapters of GOTNW reveals a cognitive profile of extraordinary breadth and depth, with 204 documented feats spanning 15 categories. His planning and strategic capabilities operate at the highest documented level, while his deception and psychological manipulation skills function as core competencies rather than auxiliary abilities. The integration of exceptional IQ (estimated 185-201), emotional intelligence, and adversity resilience creates a cognitive profile that is not merely above average in multiple domains, but demonstrates the rare ability to apply all capabilities simultaneously under extreme pressure. The most revealing finding is not any single feat but the consistency of exceptional performance across all domains, suggesting that Light's intelligence is not domain-specific but represents a general cognitive excellence that manifests differently depending on contextual demands.
