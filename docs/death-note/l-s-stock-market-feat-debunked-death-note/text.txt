L's stock market feat DEBUNKED

Premise 1: L at the age of 8 multiplied Watari's assets from at least £1 million to over £20 billion via stock market investment advice over the course of only 2 years.

Premise 2: L was stated by Mello to have grown exponentially in intelligence over the course of time.

Premise 3: The intelligence L displayed during the Kira case isolated from his childhood self was not at a level that implies he is smarter than his childhood self. There are even explicit flaws in his thinking that indicate a lower level of intelligence compared to what someone who can do the stock market feat possesses, such as overlooking Misa's footage after touching the Death Note, the low-level reasoning he used to justify not observing Light in the helicopter, and also not being able to notice the patterns Yotsuba displayed before Light noticed them, despite being free while Light was confined for the majority of the arc.

There are 2 main ways this discrepancy can be easily resolved.

Possibility 1: Mello was mistaken with the subjective observation, so L did not grow in intelligence exponentially over time. It can even be argued that he deteriorated in intellect since the stock market feat entirely transcends the level of intelligence L displayed during the Kira case.

Possibility 2: All 3 premises are true. By backward abduction, it can be concluded that the stock market was not at such a high level interpretation and non-intellectual factors overcontributed. It must have overrelied on a factor that makes his childhood self exponentially lower in intelligence than his adult self if Mello's statement is true, such as luck or economic simplicity relative to our world, or technological advantage over his own world, or any other factor that justifies the feat as an intellectual manifestation being exponentially lower than his display of intelligence during the Kira case.

Evidence for Possibility 1: Mello stated that Light is not even worth the dirt under L's shoes. Light is obviously worth more than the dirt under L's shoes, especially when accounting for the adversity Light gave L to the point where he schemed him to death. Mello either hates Light, glazes L, or both, which is what made him exaggerate the dirt beneath L's feet as being above Light. In 2 of the 3 cases, Mello is determined as an L glazer. It is thus probable that Mello was glazing L unjustifiably when stating he grew exponentially over time. More proof for this possibility is that humans tend to peak in intelligence at a certain age and then gradually deteriorate, and since L is biologically different from most humans, the growth window may have been narrower due to his precociousness.

Evidence for possibility 2: this possibility maintains that all the premises are true. Since the feat is ambiguous, it cannot be inferred to what extent L's intelligence contributed. Luck is intangible and thus cannot be excluded, economic simplicitly can be reasoned using the other simplifications within the verse (such as political simplicity the US government displayed during the Mello arc), technological advantage is also feasible given Watari is supposed to be an advanced inventor and the technology L showed during the Kira case such as what he used to track Yotsuba's murders, etc. Obviously the correct factor cannot be deduced, but it's feasible for a multitude of these factors to have contributed in making the feat easier, given the ambiguity of the 2 years of investment.

More evidence for possibility 2: L did not do direct short-term trading (like arbitrage) since the manga shows L did not specify to Watari the market for buying the specific stocks, and Watari mentioned specifically that he "kept investing as L said." This reduces the risks significantly and increases the probability that L relied on individual hits (such as a single startup company multiplying in stock price immensely) more than a display of raw IQ / induction.

Possibility 3 is that the third premise is false and L actually did not display a lower level of intelligence during the Kira case than what he did as a child. Similarly to possibility 2, this implies stock market was one of L's worst feats since he did it during a period wherein he was unintelligent relative to what he displayed during the Kira case. With this possibility, the debate still falls down to how intelligent L was during the Kira case (whether the antifeats are antifeats, whether he displayed a high level of reasoning or planning or strategizing etc.) and the stock market feat is still useless since it gets no-diffed by his intellect during the Kira case.

Possibility 2 and 3 both imply the stock market feat is useless, since it implies that the Kira case is a far higher and more accurate display of his intelligence, especially given that we have more detail about it than the stock market feat.

Only possibility 1 implies the stock market feat is not useless, but it implies that child L transcends Adult L in intelligence when analyzing both intellectual periods in isolation, which from what I've seen most Death Note fans disagree with.

This post obviously uses normal/narrative scaling since the feat is not valid in methodology.
