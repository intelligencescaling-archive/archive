A Case for Mello's Monitor Hax

**What's the common consensus on Mello's capability for Monitor Hax?**
I'm moreso speaking to scalers of verses other than Death Note, id like your opinion the most. I'll lay the case for Mello's Monitor Hax but tell me what you think.

### First, to define Monitor Hax — here's the definition from Volume 13:
[Volume 13 screenshot](https://imgur.com/a/GuKql1c)
To sum it up succinctly: Volume 13 describes it as the ability to perceive and process not only multiple monitors at once, but potentially large amounts of varying types of information as well.

Ohba states the top students of Wammy’s House should be able to perform Monitor Hax, and later also says that he can't imagine Mello being able to.
You could very well take this to mean Mello then must not be able to perform Monitor Hax, because it seems Ohba cannot imagine he could — but that isn't necessarily the case.

If one prerequisite to this ability (as Ohba states) is to be a top student of Wammy’s House, I can't find a reason as to why Mello shouldn't be capable of it.
He’s undisputably one of the top students in the fourth generation of Wammy’s House — second only to Near.

Ohba’s remark on Mello could also just mean that he doesn't think Mello is the type of character who is as perceptive and attentive as Near and L, prephering to delegate such work to his Lackey's than doing it himself.

Accepting Monitor Hax for Mello wouldn't mean he'd scale to Near and L in PSI, WMI and VSI, but that we can compare their showings so we can scale Mello's FSIQ more perceicley, especially in-verse And by that I mean:

> • We know Near has used Monitor Hax on a little over **100 monitors** before
> [Near 100+ monitors feat](https://imgur.com/a/6uiWbSI)
> → Roughly 50 of which would have been in his peripheral vision, the other half would be strictly audio and not visual information.

> • Mello has been shown perceiving **12 monitors at once** with both audio and visual information
> [Mello 12-monitor panel](https://imgur.com/a/RrbMdta)
> → This would put him at a little over **24%** of Near and L’s PSI, WMI, and VSI — only in so far as it pertains to their Monitor Hax, of course.
> (L and Near have more impressive feats in those same categories, such as Ryuk Facial Reconstruction for Near, and the LABB's Locked Room for L, as well as narrative upscaling to or above Near’s 100-monitor feat.)

If you haven't dropped the notion, Mello has Monitor Hax, then it's safe to assume he's using the ability in the panel above.

### Another thing to note:
We are careful to not grant Monitor Hax or any similar ability to just any character without an explicit statement for the ability or sufficient reason for such an ability. For instance, this is why we don't assume Light Yagami has the ability, even though he's been shown multiple times to seemingly be observing multiple monitors at once, it isn't precisely stated he does so specifically. Because of the lack of sufficient reason, we assume a character simply observes multiple monitors normally (more or less independently, with focus mainly on a single Monitor).

A good explanation of this standard is Kirari's Monitor Hax Debunk by HelloThere1089:
