L's genius verbal trap against Light Yagami

In chapter 21 of Death Note, L shows Light 3 images, which Kira had given L in this order:

L 知っているか (L, do you know)

死神は (gods of death are)

林檎しか食べない (do not eat other than apples) ((only eat apples))

Putting them together in the same order Kira gave, it becomes "L 知っているか死神は林檎しか食べない"

This is a complete sentence which means "L do you know, gods of death only eat apples."

The original Kira would instantly be able to recognize this ordering and message, because he himself made the message. He would also be too preoccupied with his own message to notice that L added numbers on the back for ordering the message differently.

However, a smart person that isn't Kira would also be able to notice the possible sentence that can be created, and if they get preoccupied with the sentence they discovered, they wouldn't notice the numbers, so L was intently observing the expression Light was making the whole time. It's not as simple as failing to notice the numbers = you are definitely Kira. It's a layered trap.

Anyways if you notice the numbers and order it the way L ordered it rather than the way Kira knows it should be ordered, by switching picture 2 and picture 3, it becomes:

"L知っているか林檎しか食べない死神は..." which is an incomplete sentence because it ends with the particle は.

In English, it would be something like "L do you know, gods of death that only eat apples (are)..."

This automatically implies there is another picture to finish the sentence. Anyone who understands basic Japanese will be able to tell there's a 4th picture needed to complete the sentence, and they'll be wondering "when is Kira gonna release the sequel?" However, Light who was Kira and made the message obviously knew it was misnumbered which occupied his mind, and he was unable to process the information quickly enough to realize this because of all the other information L was giving him to occupy his cognitive processing. L had been bombarding him cognitively the whole time with information and traps, to not give him time to process the grammatical cue which would automatically mean there's a 4th card to finish the sentence.

Finally, the trap led up to the final purpose which was L pointing out that there's a 4th card. It is already suspicious that Light who was a top student in Japan didn't notice the basic grammatical cue, but it would be absolutely horrendous if Light complained about there being a 4th card or got defensive and said it must be only 3, because the average person that isn't Kira would immediately realize they were being tested for their detective skills and thus say something like "oh crap I should have thought of that, obviously I shouldn't assume all the information I am given is all the information available" because that's the kind of skill a detective should have!

Light passed the first point of suspicion which was not becoming occupied with the message he himself had made, which only Kira would know. He failed the second hurdle which was the grammatical cue that any Japanese person with basic Japanese skills would realize if they're not cognitively occupied with processing other information. He almost failed the final definitive trap by L, when Light Yagami said "I would have been right if there were 3!" but after L started to press him a little more, he barely salvaged himself from suspicion and overcame it by "admitting" that "Gee... you're right, I didn't think of that."

Thus Light passed the trap by L, but increased his suspicion by not completely perfectly acting as a normal intelligent Japanese person who isn't Kira would act. He had passed the first layer kind of successfully, failed the second layer, almost failed the third layer, but salvaged himself of his mistake in the third layer with the fourth layer which was the final pressure by L.

In case you're curious, adding in L's fake 4th photo, it becomes "L知っているか林檎しか食べない死神は手が赤い" which means "L do you know, gods of death that only eat apples have red hands." The incomplete sentence by the switched ordering becomes fully complete with L's fake 4th note.

This scene shows L's creativity, being able to come up with such a simple but effective tactic, using the small amount of information that Light himself somewhat randomly came up with, thus increasing the probability in his mind that Light Yagami is Kira because of how barely Light passed the traps that a person who isn't Kira wouldn't overthink. Even though Light didn't fail the trap, he failed to evade some sections of it, which L used to increase the chance in his mind of Light being Kira.
