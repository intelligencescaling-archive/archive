5 Mistakes Light Yagami (Kira) Made That Cost Him Dearly

1. Killing Criminals According to His Schedule

At the beginning of the series, Kira mainly killed criminals during his free time and avoided doing so during school hours. This immediately raised suspicion for L, who noticed that the number of deaths increased during evenings, weekends, and holidays. From this pattern, L concluded that Kira was likely a high school student and probably under the age of 18.

2. Killing Lind L. Tailor

One of Light's biggest early mistakes was killing Lind L. Tailor immediately after being provoked on television, without even considering whether he was actually L.

As the anime later shows, L never publicly revealed his face during investigations, which should have been enough reason for Light to be cautious. Instead, he acted impulsively.

This mistake gave L far more information than Light realized. Not only did L confirm that Kira was located in the Kanto region of Japan, but he also learned that Kira was willing to kill anyone who opposed him, not just criminals. L additionally discovered that Kira required both a face and a name to kill someone.

3. Responding Directly to L's Challenge

After L exposed Kira's killing schedule, Light immediately changed the timing of his killings and began demonstrating that he could control the time and circumstances of death.

In essence, he was telling L:

«"I can kill whenever I want. What are you going to do about it?"»

This response revealed too much about Kira's personality and motives. Instead of remaining unpredictable, Light allowed L to see his pride and desire to challenge anyone who opposed him. This would later work against him.

4. Falling for L's Police Information Trap

At one point, L allowed access to police investigation data and confidential files. Shortly afterward, Kira began killing newly reported criminals.

This helped L narrow down his investigation considerably. He arrived at two possibilities:

- Kira had access to information through someone inside the Japanese police.
- Kira was closely connected to someone involved in the investigation.

Either way, the suspect pool became much smaller. As a result, L sent 12 FBI agents to monitor everyone connected to the task force.

5. Underestimating Near and Failing to Control Mikami

This was arguably Light's biggest mistake and the critical flaw that ultimately led to his defeat.

To understand why, we need to revisit the events leading up to the warehouse confrontation.

Near challenged Light directly, and Light accepted because he wanted to eliminate all opposition in a single night. Although safer alternatives existed, Light chose the riskier option due to his confidence and arrogance.

The plan was straightforward: Near and the SPK would gather at the warehouse, and Mikami, possessing the Shinigami Eyes, would write everyone's names in the Death Note.

Light anticipated Near's move. He knew Near would tamper with pages of the notebook and therefore arranged for the real Death Note to be hidden in a bank while Mikami carried a fake one. Everything appeared flawless.

However, three days before the final meeting, Mello kidnapped Kiyomi Takada.

Without receiving any instructions, Mikami acted on his own and killed Takada using the real Death Note. This independent action became the turning point.

Giovanni, who was monitoring Mikami, noticed something unusual: Mikami visited the bank twice in a short period despite normally following a strict routine.

This inconsistency caught Near's attention. He investigated further, discovered the real notebook, and found Takada's name written inside it. Near then realized that Mikami possessed two notebooks and that the one being observed was likely a fake.

Near successfully replaced the real notebook with an identical forgery, even reproducing Mikami's handwriting.

As a result, during the final confrontation, Mikami believed he was writing names into the real Death Note, when in reality he was writing into Near's fake notebook.

At that moment, both Mikami and Light were exposed, allowing Near to solve the Kira case once and for all.

This entire disaster could have been avoided if Light had simply instructed Mikami not to act independently.

An even safer alternative would have been having Mikami write only the names of SPK members while Light personally handled the Japanese task force members using a piece of the Death Note. That way, even if Near had forged the notebook, suspicion would have been spread more broadly and Light would have retained additional options.

Or, more simply, Light could have controlled his arrogance and avoided making unnecessary moves that attracted suspicion.

Conclusion

This post isn't meant to downplay Light Yagami or claim that he wasn't intelligent. On the contrary, Light remains one of the smartest and most impressive manipulators in anime.

The purpose of this post is simply to highlight a few mistakes that are often overlooked. Despite these errors, Light's achievements and strategic feats throughout Death Note remain extraordinary, and there are plenty more worth discussing in another post.
