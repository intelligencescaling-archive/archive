First Post: Light Yagami "Downtime" Analysis

This is mainly in response to a post I saw, which supposedly "invalidated" the majority of Light's feats in the time between L's death and Near's appearance.

	In the Death Note canon, Light Yagami kills L and is chosen as his successor. This will go over the narrative implications of Light’s actions from the “downtime” in between part one and two of Death Note.

	1st Feat: L and Watari’s Voices

	As shown in the canon, both L and Watari use deepfaking devices which conceal their respective voices. These deepfaking devices rely on AI, and are at a presumably sophisticated level which is reserved only for the world’s greatest detective. L is now dead, and with even the technology behind these devices destroyed, Light (to take L’s place) must re-create them.

	Lowballing this feat he would have had four months to create this device, as any more would be extremely suspect. This means that Light has to essentially (from scratch) learn about and create an AI voice modulator and revolutionize the DN world’s AI singlehandedly within at most 4 months.

	(A highball of this would be around one month)

	Potential Refutations and Responses:

	But voice modulators have been around for decades!

	(Not ones at the level of L’s. It’s only fair to assume that it’s at such an advanced level that it’s nigh-impossible to reverse engineer. It’s stated that even ICPO knows next to nothing about L, and I would bet they would have tried to figure out even what his voice sounds like. What does this mean? Light invented a complex AI voice modulator in less than four months without any prior knowledge of said field, a vm which cannot even be deconstructed by multiple government agencies.)

	Reinvention is nothing special!

	(Yes, it is. It’s still impressive if someone figures out how to perfectly swing a baseball after seeing a professional do it once.)

	2nd Feat: Taking L’s Place

	Now Light Yagami must take L’s place. Don’t worry, I’m not going to necessarily argue that he was solving all of L’s cases, but I will argue that he was solving Eraldo Coil and Deneurve's cases. It’s common knowledge that L took the names and positions of both EC and D, both of them being within the top 3 greatest detectives in the world.

	This means that Light must solve cases complex enough that they were once solved by the 2nd and 3rd greatest detectives. Now what makes this impressive? Light has a normal sleep schedule and acts as kira, opposed to L who manages D, EC, and the L personas with a pretty fucked sleep schedule.

	It’s even possible that Light had to assume the identities of far more than just two detectives, as L has a history of taking those who he has defeated’s identities for his own.

	Potential Refutations and Responses:

	There’s not enough crime for there to be complex cases!

	(Yes, there is. Mello finds and eliminates a mafia boss unpunished by even Kira. There are still plenty of crimes going on around the world to solve.)

	Near and Mello claim the new L to be incompetent!

	(This could very well be a provocation on their part, and is contradicted by the very author who states “Light continues to play both the roles of L and Watari brilliantly.” This point is further supported by the fact that there’s no mention by any external sources other than the two people (other than Watari) closest to L that L has fallen off.)
