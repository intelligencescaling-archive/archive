When thousands of Kira followers were cramming outside his door to rip him apart, not a single hint of worry was on his face (compare this to Light stressing out over nearly every inconvenience), and when the President illegalised his organisation, and Mello wiped out nearly all of his people, Near never once faltered in his determination to complete the case.

Strategy goes to Near for him faking Mogi's death. He easily and instantaneously created a no win situation for Light as only someone on the Task Force knows of Mogi's situation. So if Mogi is dead, Kira must be in the Task Force therefore strengthening their already planted suspicions of Light and encouraging them to feed information to Near. And Light can't stop this by confirming Mogi's alive because how would he know if he's not Kira? It's not a perfect strategy but it's better than all of Light's imo because I think Light's strategies are bad frankly. They're only good in their complexities but just about everything about them sucks.

Knowledge might be a count of confusion because they both have equal knowledge scores in the HtR stats. Here's the thing though; Light's high knowledge level likely consists of his ample knowledge of the supernatural in addition to human academics. I think this makes sense because Light has a higher score in it than Watari, literally the world's most successful inventor. Near on the other hand had very little supernatural knowledge by comparison and with him being L's top student in the most demanding education centre in the world, it makes more sense that he'd be as knowledgeable as Watari than Light imo. Even putting all that aside, Near does have the A-Kira feat of him seemingly singlehandedly building Shinigami recognition technology.

So, yeah, overall I have Near winning. "Oh but if Mikami didn't screw up Light would have won!!!" yes, and Light only would have won because of his massive advantage. idk why people keep overlooking just how badly Near had the odds stacked against him. He had to deal with Kira and Mello simultaneously, the latter of which killed the majority of his allies off the bat, and had no government assistance like L or Light did while still having very little understanding of the Death Note's functions, plus having the entire world against him. Near in my opinion was in by far the worst and most disadvantageous position in the whole series. People never shut up about how bad L's situation was, but L full authority over Task Force and tons of government help and resources. Near had three people and a hideout. And he still pulled it off, especially if we take Matsuda's theory to its fullest extent wherein he just outplayed Light and Mello completely both at once.

So in the end, I do have Near winning.
