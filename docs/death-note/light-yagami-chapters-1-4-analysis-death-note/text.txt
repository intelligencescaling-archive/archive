# “Boredom”

Light Yagami after coming back home goes inside his room and there he meets Ryuk, he expected him to show up after a while, his reasoning being:
**P1: The Death Note exists and works**
**P2: An item of such power has to have an intended holder which can’t be human.**
**C: There exists an owner he will eventually meet.**
Light easily realises that Ryuk dropped the note intentionally and with a purpose, because why else would he write down the instructions in english. (Critical Thinking, Abstract Thinking, Deductive Reasoning)
Through the last 5 days Light couldn’t sleep and lost 10 pounds, yet he continued writing names of criminals to change the world. (Mental Fortitude, Stress Tolerance)
Light kills criminals with the default cause of death - heart attack, he does that so people would start to notice that somebody is passing righteous judgement on the wicked. he did that so people would get scared of being punished and won’t commit crimes anymore, then he would start to kill off people he considers immoral by illnesses and accidents (EU, SU, Information Control)

# “L”

Light’s plan had started to work, people had already realised that somebody is passing righteous judgement and the internet called him “Kira”. (SE, MM, ME, MI)

# “Family”, “Current”

Light purposefully led L to the conclusion that Kira may be a student by killing outside of school hours. After Light learns from his father Soichiro that L did reach that conclusion he started to schedule criminals to die each hour, so L would not only confirm his hypothesis that Kira’s a student but also realise Kira has access to police information. Light had exploited that weak trust amongst the investigators. L is suspicious of the police, and the police do not trust a man whose face or name they don’t know. Which would lead to L investigating the police and the police investigating L. Light’s endgame is the police figuring out who L is and getting a chance to kill him.

(EU, SU, SE, Creative Thinking, MM, LM, EM, IM, Information Control)

Light Learned that whoever touches the Death Note can see its Original Holder, the Shinigami. He has to hide it in a way that would let him quickly access the notebook without leaving the possibility for it to be found if the room was searched.
Light builds a secret compartment in his drawer; it can only be opened with a pen cartridge because it contains an electric circuit that ignites gasoline in a plastic bag if it connects. The cartridge acts as an insulator when open and a rubber piece when it’s closed. Light also leaves a key and a diary in the drawer. If anybody would try to look for something that person would be satisfied with the diary they find in the drawer and wouldn’t suspect that there’s anything more. If somebody would find that compartment and try to force it open it would set the house on fire and destroy the note along with it.
