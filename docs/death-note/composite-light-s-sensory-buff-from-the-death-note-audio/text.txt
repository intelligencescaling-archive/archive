*Ten seconds of gunshot noises.*

**LIGHT:** Matsuda...??

**MATSUDA, speaking laboredly:** I said... I said... I said... don’t move.

**LIGHT:** I’m dying, Ryuk... I’m dying...

**RYUK:** Exactly what I’m doing.

**LIGHT:** But we’re friends... allies... You can help me... Just give me a bit of lifetime....

**LIGHT:** I can see someone. I feel that someone is here.... with you...

**RYUK:** Oh really? You can see him? You really see him?

**LIGHT:** Someone’s here.... a man...

**RYUK:** Ah, there’s a term for this. When everything goes clear at the end... what was that called again?

At the climax of the drama, after being shot by Mogi and with his name written by Ryuk in the Death Note, Light enters a terminal state. In his last seconds of consciousness, as his heart stops, the following exchange takes place:

***LIGHT***\*: I can see someone. I feel that someone is here... with you...\*
***RYUK***\*: Oh really? You can see him? You really see him?\*
***LIGHT***\*: Someone's here... a man...\*
***RYUK***\*: Ah, there's a term for this. When everything goes clear at the end... what was that called again?\*
***L***\*: Mental clarity before imminent death.\*

L answers Ryuk's question from his post-mortem state, confirming that he is physically present as a spiritual entity and that Light does indeed perceive him. Light perceives and discriminates characteristics of a metaphysical entity. Existence ("someone is here") , Humanoid form ("I can see someone") , Male sex ("a man"), Relative spatial location ("here... with you").

This perception occurs about an entity that: It is not linked to any Death Note that Light touched (L died years before; his spiritual presence is not linked to a notebook). It does not emit physical stimuli detectable by ordinary human senses. It is not perceived by any other human present at the scene (Matsuda, paramedics, police).

Light processes non-physical sensory input to the level of consciousness. This categorically transcends any feat of human perception operating within the physical spectrum (extreme distance vision, echolocation, acute hearing). Those feats operate on photons, sound waves, vibrations detectable physical stimuli. The input that Light processes has no physical emission by definition (it is a spiritual entity).
