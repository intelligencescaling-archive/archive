L was destroying Light during the cafe scene (explained)

This will be a slightly deeper analysis compared to my previous one, and include some surrounding context to further exemplify L's intelligence during this chapter.

The photo trap is especially underrated by people who don't understand either Japanese or human psychology. It is very realistic and would work on most humans in real life if they were Kira and didn't have Light's emotional management skills.

Firstly, the premise of the interrogation: L says "Can I test your reasoning abilities?"

After Light accepts, L asks him the question: "Does the fact that I told you I'm L tell you anything?" and urges Light to continue speaking out his deductions for the sake of testing his reasoning ability. This throws Light into thinking mode which is what causes the overthinking that happens later.

Next L gives a clarification of the premise of the interrogation: **"Since this is an interview to see if I can request your help in the investigation..."**

Thus the test is of Light's reasoning abilities to determine whether he is fit for helping the task force in the investigation. If Light is Kira, it also provides him with what he wants, which is to get closer to the task force, and thus makes him more susceptible to showing off his reasoning skills rather than clamming up.

On the other hand, if Light was not Kira, this premise would become useful for manipulating his actions during the photo trap such that he does not make the mistake Kira would have as I shall explain.

L then shows Light both the list of twelve FBI agents who were murdered by Kira, with the time they received the file, the order in which they received it, and the order in which they died, as well as the 3 photos that Kira sent to the task force.

This creates a psychological anchor in Light's mind that makes him assume that is all there is, by revealing both tests at the beginning. L puts the 3 photos aside and asks Light for his opinion on the list of FBI agents, which is a trap, but only a minor trap that Light pretty easily passes by asking about what file he's even talking about.

L then asks about the 3 photos instead, and unlike the files, this is a major layered trap. The files were actually not useless even if they were easily passable, it was a mere single layered trap that caused psychological anchoring in Light that makes him think upcoming traps will be just as simple, meanwhile it is instantly followed up by a multilayered trap (the photo trap). Also, the presentation at the start with the files and the 3 photos at the same time would make a person think that is all there is, and thus further anchors Light into thinking there are only 3 photos. Kira would all the more think there are only that many because it is the number that he himself released.

Here are the hidden lines in each of the photos L gave, which Kira also released:

Photo A: えるしっているか **(L, do you know?)**

Photo B: りんごしかたべない **(do not eat other than apples)**

Photo C: しにがみは **(shinigami do/are)**

The letters are just for your own reference so you know which message I'm referring to.

Kira who is Light released them in this order: A, C, B

If you put them together in Kira's order, it becomes "えるしっているかしにがみはりんごしかたべない" which is a natural Japanese sentence that means **"L, do you know, shinigami only eat apples."**

L numbered them in the order of photos A, B, C, which when put together becomes "えるしっているかりんごしかたべないしにがみは" which means **"L, do you know, shinigami who only eat apples are"**

You'll notice this is an incomplete sentence. Shinigami who only eat apples are... what? What are they? This is where the major trap lies, as I will explain:

**Layer 1:** At the start of the trap, if Light was Kira, he would notice the message that he placed within the three photos. Since Kira would also try to prove his reasoning skills to L in order to get closer to the members of the task force by joining them, Kira would point out the message he noticed, and may overlook the existence of the numbers on the back. However, L was intently observing Light's expression, because even a normal student may have just missed the numbers on the back after noticing a pattern in the images. So don't think it's as simple as failing to the notice the numbers = you are Kira. It's not that simple.

Light Yagami does notice the numbers on the back, but he first shows L the message that he found within the photos in order to prove he has good reasoning skills. This is slightly more suspicious than how a normal human would have acted, since they would have been likelier to check the ordering of the photos before noticing the hidden message, so there was no need to show he noticed the hidden message first other than to pretend he has impressive perceptual reasoning skills, so if Light wanted to act like a normal human, he should not have explained the message before pointing out the numbers, but rather pointed out the numbers first. However, Light still passed the first layer semi-successfully by noticing the numbers on the back after telling L about the message that can be created.

**Layer 2:** After noticing the numbers and ordering the lines the way L numbered them, it forms an incomplete sentence. "えるしっているかりんごしかたべないしにがみは" which means "L, do you know, shinigami who only eat apples are..." The sentence ends with は which is almost always a grammatical cue to finish the sentence in order for it to make more sense. However, Light is preoccupied with his 3 notes being the only available notes, and therefore does not think of it as an incomplete sentence, he only thinks of it as a nonsensical sentence. Light says **"That doesn't make much sense so I find it hard to believe Kira wanted L to read it like that."** Thus he doesn't notice the grammatical cue for what L intended it as, and fails the second trap, which is fortunately for Light still not that major of a layer within the trap.

**Layer 3:** L says "incorrect, in fact there is a fourth note" and shows the fourth note that reads "てがあかい" which means "hands are red." This completes the incomplete sentence and makes it "えるしっているかりんごしかたべないしにがみはてがあかい" which means **"L, do you know, shinigami who only eat apples are red-handed (have red hands)."**

The nonsensical sentence is now a natural and complete sentence.

Going back to the premise of the whole interview at the start, L was trying to test Light's reasoning skills. If Light was just another smart high schooler who isn't Kira, he would think "Oh yeah, the sentence was incomplete, I should have thought of that, whoops." but since Light is Kira and he was preoccupied with 3 photos being all there was, as well as due to some psychological techniques employed by L as I mentioned before, particularly anchoring bias, he instead fails to notice the trap and thinks: **\[Is this guy an idiot? What does he think he's going to find out by showing me this bogus note..? Is he just fooling with me..?\]** and proceeds to tell L **"Okay, but if there were only three notes, my reasoning was flawless."** This is him horrendously failing the third layer of the trap, which is a major layer. It is very suspicious of him to respond that way given that he's supposed to be the one who has his reasoning skills tested, and he's supposed to be a highly intelligent person. However, fortunately for Light, it's still not certain that he is Kira because a prideful upstuck student who is used to being the smartest might have responded in a similar manner.

**Layer 4:** L says **"No it wasn't, because there were in fact four notes. Figuring that out would have made your reasoning flawless. I think I'm correct in deducing that you decided there were only three notes Yagami-kun, and therefore could not infer there might be a fourth one."** which is a very major layer of the trap that places psychological pressure on him. If he was Kira, he would most likely think "shit, he got me!" and start panicking, stuttering, etc., while if he was a normal smart high schooler, he would say something like "I guess I should have thought of that." admitting that he could have done better in the detective test, because no detective should assume all the information available is all the information existent. Forcing information to fit into a picture without considering the possibility of the information being incomplete is something only a shitty detective would do. From here, Light finally notices the nature of the photo trap, and passes the fourth layer extremely successfully, he scratches his head and says "Gee, you're right, I didn't think of that... But still, either way I don't think these notes will help you find Kira. I mean, shinigami don't even exist." which is good acting, and especially good emotional suppression.
