L DEBUNKED working memory + attention capacity + sensory + intuition DEBUNKED

L's cognitive capacity, working memory, sensory perception, and intuition is overrated. He has way too many antifeats that contradict the high scale people give him.

I think I overrated L way too hard with my previous takes. After some recent analyses, he still mid-diffs Light, but they're both around low tier.

People use monitor hax to bring up his working memory and such to superhuman levels, but either the author was using a figure of speech, or people have been misinterpreting his words, because the narrative completely contradicts this. You can read [my post on monitor hax](https://www.reddit.com/r/IntelligenceScaling/comments/1pr7seg/l_monitor_hax_debunked_whammys_house_narrative/) if you want more information, but I will also go into some additional detail within this post.

Point 1: L focused his attention on the prisoners dying, and thus was prevented from noticing the problems going on with the FBI agents.

https://preview.redd.it/3tk23gpblf8g1.png?width=854&format=png&auto=webp&s=e217f9e12599ca3ea9e876eac3915dfe8602be4f

For someone with supernatural working memory capacity who can process 2 streams of thought simultaneously (baseless claim), all Light needed to do was kill a few prisoners and send out some messages to L, and it completely filled up L's maximum attention capacity.

Goes to show how useless his "parallel processing" is. Even if we assume the author wasn't using a figure of speech in his interview, if a character can process 2 streams simultaneously but they don't have the working memory to make good use of this ability they have, then the "parallel processing" they have is almost completely useless.

Important analogy: think of working memory as a lake filled with water. And think of parallel processing as a cup which you can use to scoop up water. If your working memory is low (for example the whole lake is dried up, with only a few patches of water), then your cup of water being bigger doesn't help you at all. It'll just be dead weight. Similarly, L having parallel processing (allegedly) is useless if his working memory capacity and attention span isn't high enough to let him utilize his parallel processing.

Point 3: L's sensory perception, attention capacity, and working memory during the helicopter scene were horrendous.

You can read my [debunk of the memory loss plan](https://www.reddit.com/r/IntelligenceScaling/comments/1pr2gtc/memory_loss_plan_debunked_light_yagami_debunked_l/) for more context about everything that happened, but basically L had average human level olfactory perception and below average human level visual perception.

For example, even though Light cut open a wound and scribbled his blood on a piece of paper, L did not notice any weird smell (blood has a very distinct smell). You could argue that an average human wouldn't be able to notice anything either, which would be valid, but there are humans who have really good sense of smell that would, so L is around the level of a normal human in this aspect of sensory perception.

The worse thing is obviously the visual perception. Light fiddles with his watch, pulls a button 4 times, takes out a compartment, takes out a needle, cuts his finger, scribbles someone's full name onto the paper using his blood, then potentially grabs the paper and EATS IT (proof for this is what he said to Ryuk in the forest, that he would swallow the paper). ALL WHILE L WAS SITTING NEXT TO HIM. And L does not notice anything! This is horrendous visual perception.

You could argue that L had his attention on the shinigami, but this would just prove that L's working memory and attention capacity is so damn low, he can't even focus on the guy acting suspiciously next to him, just because he's looking at a shinigami. This further debunks the claims about monitor hax.

The worst part about this is even choosing to focus on the shinigami and letting Light do what he pleases. Horrendous intuition. Light had so many factors that pointed to him as Kira, to the point where L definitively said "you ARE Kira" and "What are you going to do next Kira?" in his mind prior to the memory loss plan. Light and L both even hypothesized that Light being Kira would most likely imply he intentionally transferred his power (which memory-less Light admitted!) and after all these factors + the ones I mentioned in my Memory Loss Plan Debunk, to have the Death Note return into Light's hands, and for L to sit there oblivious and unaware, unsuspicious, this is horrendous intuition. It's seriously got to be one of the worst antifeats L has committed. Even a normal human would feel that something is wrong.

When L touched the notebook and was finally able to see Rem, he could have chosen to check the surveillance footage and confirmed whether Rem was visible during Misa's confinement. If he did, he would be able to realize that "Misa talking to herself" was actually her talking to the shinigami. This would be an obvious judgement to make. But some Death Note fans defend this by saying L had a lot on his mind. For example talking to the shinigami and testing the 13 day rule. This further proves L's working memory is not that great. The only reason he wanted to test the 13 day rule was because Light and Misa were still under suspicion, but something as obvious as footage which would have been easier for confirming his suspicions didn't come to his mind at all, just because his head was filled with the shinigami and testing the 13-day rule.

Point 5: Letting the new Kira rampage for approximately 35 days.

This is an antifeat for judgement, working memory, attention capacity. When Light was imprisoned, L sat there observing him for 50 days. On the 15th day, Kira started killing again. L could not focus on the new Kira or try to track him at all, because he was too busy observing Light.

This is first a lapse in judgement, because Light is confined and bound, meanwhile the new Kira is free outside, killing people. But it is also lacking attention capacity and working memory, because L cannot even place his attention on the new Kira, due to his attention being on someone that is literally locked up and can't do anything.

This lasts 35 whole days. Kira is outside rampaging for more than a month while L can't do anything, because of his low attention capacity.

**Conclusion**

These are just a few of the evidences from the manga that debunk L's working memory, which people upscale to superhuman just because of their interpretation of what the author said in some interview. If you read the whole manga holistically, you will realize L's working memory is at a level that even average humans in real life have.

On the other hand, his sensory perception is below average humans, especially his auditory and visual perception. It's way too inconsistent though. Sometimes you'll see that he has a good feat in a category, then other times he'll do something that puts him below average humans in it.

Same with intuition. L has good feats as well in this category, but the helicopter scene specifically is such a major antifeat, it would place his intuition far below average humans if you look at it in isolation. Yes, even fodder humans in real life would be likelier to have warning signs blaring at Light regaining the death note if they were aware of all the premises that L was aware of.
