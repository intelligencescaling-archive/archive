Sometimes, life throws you a curveball at a sudden moment and now, it’s your turn to try to adapt to it and not succumb to the ground forever. Some succeeded, some did not. And that is the case of our main protagonist of this made up fanfiction today, Naomi Misora. Intelligent, Courageous, Kind and most importantly: Loyal.

        She was in a loving relationship with her fiancé, Raye Pember… well, until he was unexpectedly killed by this “new savior of Earth”, Kira. To be honest, right from the getgo, she can already tell that ‘Kira'’’s viewpoint is too black and white, too naive… though, not that she should judge someone for justice, she’s not one to uphold one. But with Raye, this is different. Kira killed Raye, someone that is innocent at the end of the day, no matter how flawed he is. No matter how, it’s her Raye. And love always conquers, doesn’t it?

        Well….. unfortunately, Naomi died of suicide- well, more by Kira but he had altered her mind to suicide canonically. But in this alternative and made up timeline, what happened if things went different? What happens when love doesn't fully blindside our former FBI agent?

December 31st, 2003 - January 1st, 2004
        It couldn’t have been a coincidence. No…. Raye didn’t die, he was murdered. He was killed by Kira. There has to be no option. No one would die of a heart attack that instant. And she knows well enough Raye isn’t secretly a junkie nor someone unhealthy, in fact, he’s athletic. So it already crosses her mind that it's Kira that killed her fiancé.

From what she had seen on the news, Raye was momentarily there which gave her an idea that he was likely following someone, which could be Kira in her eyes.

After questioning the bus driver with somewhat enough information, Misora reasons that Kira was likely one of the passengers. Why?

Well, here’s her reasoning process (sorry if this sucks ass, I’m bad at being a detective):
Past knowledge (her conversation with Raye when he caught on with the bus hijacking incident):
She knows that a criminal who had attempted to rob a bank 2 days before December 20th, tried to hijack a bus.
Raye then mentions how he just jumped off and got run over.
She then intuitively said that he’s following someone isn’t he?
He deflected by saying that she’s not an agent anymore (which means her intuition was correct)
Current knowledge (after interviewing with the bus driver)
There were 6 passengers
While the bus driver didn’t remember the other passengers due to fear (which is fine since that’s how humans react), he confirms Raye was one of them.
In addition, the driver had seen the hijacker being scared of something after picking something up (he wasn’t sure), shooting at it as if he had earlier smoked a lot of cocaine.

Using what she got, she can infer that:
Kira was one of the passengers inside.
Kira has supernatural powers
Using the second knowledge + with what is happening with the world (I meant how Kira can just gives out heart attacks?) Kira can control death at any time, at any moment.

In addition, she also saw that there were 4 people that died at the Shinjuku station (she saw it on the TV), right when Raye said he’s going to Shinjuku and died there which solidifies her intuition that Kira can kill anyone with more than just heart attacks. Again, she knows it’s just purely guesswork and one person babbling about something seemingly supernatural like the Kira Case could get her be deemed crazy- but she’s desperate alright?

As she walked to the National Police Agency, she was determined, but also wary and tired from crying a lot last night.
