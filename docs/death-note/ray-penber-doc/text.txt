Brief Introduction: Raye Penber is one of the most memorable characters in Death Note. The fact that he only has around 2-3 episodes worth of screen time and is still remembered by the fans is truly remarkable. This character’s impact was so immense that it prompted the Death Note authors to create a new character named Stephen Gevanni.

Raye Penber and his wife Naomi Misora are both FBI agents. In order for one to become an FBI agent, they have to meet certain requirements and it is safe to assume that Penber met those requirements because he is already an FBI agent. He passed the FBI Phase I test. Phase I test is a three hour long computer based exam that assesses the candidate’s logic, reasoning, personality, and judgement. Penber very clearly scored high on all of these because he’s already an FBI agent. There is also an in person interview that assesses the candidate’s communication skills and professionalism. It is safe to assume that Penber aced this as well as the PFT (Physical Fitness Test) because he is already an FBI agent. He also completed the Phase II test which is a computer based writing assessment (counts as IQ via VCI) and an hour long interview with three special agents. After this, he passed a Pre Quantico PFT and a Basic Field Training Course that covers academics, case exercises, firearms, and tactical skills. He was also trained to hide his identity and to use his communication skills to get what he wants without asking for it (for example: If he wants intel on Light’s routine. He wouldn’t go up to his mom and say “what’s your son’s daily routine?”. He would stir the conversation in that direction and get her to tell him Light’s routine without him directly asking for it).
Raye Penber was also evaluated on the FBI core competencies. There are a total of eight of these. Collaboration, Communication, Flexibility/Adaptibility, Initiative, Interpersonal Ability, Leadership, Organizing, Planning, Problem solving, and Judgement. This means that Penber scored high on collaboration which means that he has the ability to work well and respectfully with diverse groups including other law enforcement agencies, scored high on communication which means that he can speak and write (counts as IQ via VCI) clearly and listen effectively, He scored high on Flexibility and Adaptability which means that he can easily manage change and adapt accordingly. He scored high on initiative which means that he can take charge of projects and solve problems without needing to be directed. He scored high on interpersonal ability meaning that he can establish rapport and resolve conflicts with others. He scored high on organizing and planning meaning that he can prioritize and execute tasks effectively. He scored high on problem solving and judgement, meaning that he can identify problems, make sound decisions, and manage risks.
Our first introduction to Raye Penber is when he’s following Light Yagami. Now, keep in mind that Raye Penber and everybody else besides Light had no idea that Shinigamis existed, especially as early as Penber’s introduction. Now that we have that out of the way, when Penber was following Light without being noticed by him, Ryuk was the one who told Light that he was being followed. Now imagine if Ryuk never told Light that he was being followed. What do you think would’ve happened? Light would get caught talking to himself (to Ryuk but it would look like he was talking to himself), Penber tells L and L deduces that Shinigamis exist and Light gets in trouble all because of Penber. Now why didn’t this happen? Because of Ryuk. If Penber knew about Ryuk, he would have never been caught following Light. He would’ve used different tactics to gain intel.

The second time we see him is when he follows Light to the bus and the bus gets hijacked. Now, you have to understand that at this point in the story, no one besides L has knowledge that Kira can control the actions of his victims. It was impossible for Penber to look at this situation and think “Woah this is odd. This must be Kira! He is the one controlling this guy!” when the guy has a gun is literally hijacking a bus. Penber was able to predict how the bus hijacker would react after hallucinating. He showcased leadership skills as he was able to take charge and tell everyone to get down. He tried to tackle the bus hijacker but the only reason he wasn’t able to do that was because the bus hijacker’s death had already been written. That scene also backed up the fact that Penber did score high on FBI requirements and core competencies because his leadership and field skills were on display here.
The last time we see him is when Light gets him to write down other FBI agents’ names on the death note pages. That scene begs the question, where was Ryuk when it happened? If he was there then Raye Penber would’ve seen him because he had the pages or not because the pages were covered and the only way Penber could’ve seeb Ryuk was if he had touched the exposed part (name area) which would be the Death Note page and that would prompt him to see the Shinigami. It's crazy how Light’s plan worked here because there are literally so many ways it could have gone wrong. I doubt he had any knowledge about Penber’s commanding officer. I doubt that he knew that Penber didn’t know the names of the other FBI agents. Actually, it was probably a precautionary measure of some sort because he probably thought “I’ll have him write his commanding officer’s name at the very top so that the commander can send him the list of names of all the FBI agents in Japan just in case he does not know the names of all of them”. Honestly, it’s pretty brilliant. Being able to plan that far ahead and making contingencies just in case something went wrong. He probably sat down and thought about everything. The only issue I have with Light’s plans is that they are not 100% foolproof. Light and Walter White (crazy comparison) both have this issue with their plans. Take the memory loss plan for example. The odds of the Death Note coming back to Light are low as hell. Rem could’ve given the book to another genius psycho like Light who would’ve used his intellect to avoid getting caught. I doubt Rem would let that happen though because she cares about Misa and I’m pretty sure she would’ve done something to get the book back to Yagami. Light probably thought about this and was like “yeah I’ll have Rem give the book to someone else instead of Ryuk because she’s easier to manipulate than he is”. Quite brilliant honestly and the piece of paper in the watch was crazy too. He knew himself well enough to know that he would be wearing that watch WHEN (not if) the notebook gets back to him. This guy is crazy smart. Honestly, I was very disappointed that he was not able to beat Near and the SPK. Anime Near is total trash. He literally says “I’ll be the god of the new world” in the FIRST episode which is crazy. Now, some of you might say that he was psychotic from the beginning or that he is inherently evil. Clearly, he was a nice person before he got the book so it just does not make sense for him to go from extremely good to extreme evil within a 22 minute FIRST episode. I guess Death Note is not meant to be that kind of show. It’s not meant to be a “corruption arc” or anything. It’s meant to be a cat and mouse kind of show. I do think that it would’ve been interesting if Death Note dealt with more psychological aspects. For example, the aforementioned “corruption arc” and just a more in depth exploration of the characters’ psychology. I’m pretty sure he would’ve tried to kill Naomi Misora if he failed to get her name because he was considering doing that and he can also get impulsive so it would’ve made sense. I feel like it would’ve also impacted his character in a good way and added more depth. Anyways, I did point out Light’s brilliant feats just so you can get the idea who Ray Penber lost to. He didn’t just lose to some high school kid, he lost to a genius with a magic notebook and crucial knowledge about that book that Penber had no access to. His defeat makes sense. He didn’t die because he was dumb, he died because he was up against a very smart, dangerous, and unusual opponent. Any realistic genius would’ve lost. “Oh but a realistic genius wouldn’t show his name and badge number when there's literally a threat that can eliminate you just by knowing your name and face” Shut up. Things like this are easy for us to say as viewers but if you really try to imagine yourself in his situation, it’ll start to make sense. Chief’s son with his girlfriend on a bus who is willing to tackle a bus hijacker would be the last person someone would suspect to be Kira. THAT would be your thought process if you were in his situation and had his amount of knowledge (you won’t know about Shinigamis, the notebook, Kira having more than one way to kill, and Kira having the ability to control his victim’s actions). It is perfectly understandable for him to show his ID instead of verbally saying “I am FBI” and risking alerting the hijacker. The best part about all this is that LIGHT KNEW what Penber’s thought process would be!!!!!!!! He engineered the situation that way ON PURPOSE!!! THIS GUY IS CRAZYY.

Debunking Antifeats:

“The last time we see him, he is basically doing everything Light is telling him too.” His name was already written so there is no way that he could act outside of how he was written to act in the Death Note. We do see him acknowledge the fact that Light thought everything through because he was using an untraceable communication device (reinforces the fact that he is a legitimate FBI agent and not a fraud).

“Bro he literally got caught following Light. He should be good at following people because that's his job lol” Ryuk caught him. It’s impossible to avoid being seen by someone you cannot see and do not know exists. If he knew of Ryuk’s existence, he would’ve used a different strategy.

“Lmao, raye penbigga showed Light his FBI ID with his name and face and everything.”  Touché, but let's not pretend like Penber is the only one with antifeats. Light wrote Lind L. Tailor’s name which made L aware of his existence. Massive antifeat.
