To summarize the Entire Death Note Auction Plan that Minoru came up with when he was AT MOST 15 years old and with only a few hours…. This is it:
1. After Ryuk answers Minoru’s 2 questions, He tells Ryuk that he will forfeit ownership of the notebook and for him to come back in 2 years and he promises apples and to use the notebook. He does this for 2 main reasons. 1st: He anticipated that even without knowledge of his plan or the notebook he would’ve opened a savings account with Yotsuba in those 2 years. 2nd: If he were to open an account right then, and went through with the plan, there would’ve been a bigger chance of the authorities to narrow down suspects. He also was able to lowkey manipulate Ryuk by the promise of using the notebook and apples everyday.
2. After the 2 years pass, Ryuk comes back, touches Minoru with the notebook and the plan Minoru thought of 2 years ago comes back to him. Right away he sends Ryuk to the Sakura TV station via travelling underground to be undetected from any camera’s and previous people who can see him. Once Ryuk gets there, he tells him to use a pen and paper that is already at the station to make sure that Minoru’s Fingerprints or any other possible DNA isn’t left. After 30 seconds Ryuk leaves. He uses this method a few times more in the future.
3. Minoru understood that the starting bids would more or less be nothing special and was waiting for the big countries and nations to get involved. After he realizes they are starting to bid. He makes Ryuk use the same method at the station and makes the nations make official statements and bids. This makes the world understand that the big nations are bidding.
4. Minoru lets the official bidding run a little bit and then in the end says that the United States has won the auction of Kira’s Killing Power (A.K.A the book) for 1 quadrillion Yen, and will reveal the method of transference soon.
5. He later revealed that he wants The United States to deposit the money IN DOLLARS, in equal amounts to every person with a Yotsuba savings account and registered residence in Tokyo and under the age of 60. Which is quite literally 100,000s of people.

This entire plan was quite literally as perfect as you could get without writing in the notebook and leaving NO proof/evidence whatsoever. He did it all not only without having to write in the Notebook. This makes it nigh impossible to actually track it back to him since, at this point he won’t even have memories of the notebook or Ryuk. AND he won’t have any physical evidence that has the death note or the plan or Ryuk mentioned. But also without even doing anything different from his usual lifestyle. Nothing can be traced back to him, and he himself won’t be able to remember anything about his plan or the notebook. And even when he asked the United States money for the deposit, he asked for it in Dollars since it wouldn’t negatively affect the Japanese Yen. This was all as I mentioned before, thought out in just a few hours by a middle schooler.

These are some key planning points I wanted to just point out separately:
1. Delayed Action to Avoid Suspicion
   * He waited two years before executing the plan, ensuring no immediate connection between him and the Death Note.
   * This eliminated any surveillance or behavioral anomalies that could have linked him to the event.
   * And anticipated he would get a Yotsuba savings account on his own accord.
2. Manipulating Ryuk to His Advantage
   * By promising apples and future notebook use, he ensured Ryuk would cooperate without giving away too much information.
   * This allowed him to maintain control over the situation, and not end up in worse hands, even though he was just a kid. (everyone says that light manipulated the shinigami so if you believe that, and use it as a feat for light, then you also have to use it as a feat for Minoru)
3. Zero Physical or Digital Evidence
   * He never directly interacted with the Death Note beyond touching it to regain memories.
   * Every message was delivered through a third party (Sakura TV), eliminating paper trails, fingerprints, and digital traces.
   * The Yotsuba savings account setup was “natural”
4. Using Nations as a Shield
   * Instead of personally benefiting from the notebook, he leveraged global superpowers to create a situation where nobody could trace him. Or making it pointless to go after him rather than the big country’s.
   * The U.S. winning the bid put a target on them now, not him. Shielding him from attention.
5. Mass Money Distribution to Hide the Trail
   * By distributing the payment to hundreds of thousands of people, he ensured that no single individual (including himself) would stand out.
6. Erasing His Own Memories
   * By forfeiting ownership immediately after, he no longer knew his own plan, meaning even if he was caught and interrogated, he couldn’t confess.
   * This also meant he wouldn’t act differently, keeping his behavior normal and unsuspecting.
