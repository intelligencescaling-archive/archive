Light Yagami vs Normal Human after reading that one dude's debate on complexity

**FSIQ:** Light Yagami mid-high diffs.

Example feats: Memorizing cameras (overrated but valid), speaking to Takada while writing something different, exam scores (overrated but valid against his unimpressive oversimplified verse), etc. Example antifeats: taking several minutes after the time for Naomi's death should have elapsed before he finally concluded that Naomi used a fake identity which should not have been difficult given that her fiance got killed after showing his identity to Kira. The level of FSIQ indicated by all his feats and antifeats within the narrative is around "smart human" level, which is above what a normal possesses but nowhere near the peak of humanity.

**Scheming:** Light Yagami extreme diffs.

Example feats: L Isolation Strategy (overrated and with severe flaws but not too bad), Memory Loss Plan (overrated but valid scheme), Fake Notebook (overrated with almost no redeeming qualities). Light's schemes are often atrocious and would cause severe positional disadvantages if the opponents were not forced to contradict their prior traits or abandon basic common sense. Given a more chaotic causality that governs the system of operation such as our own in-verse systemic complexity and universal laws, Light would not be able to operate at levels far beyond normalcy. The complexity thinking of Light within the system he operates in is largely detracted from by the immense luck favouring him when it is required, which reduces the burden of needing to consider a multitude of variables. Without the Death Note, there is not much evidence Light would fare a lot better than a normal human in real life would.

**Reasoning:** CGEW

Excluding the speed at which Light processes information (which is merely his FSIQ), the reasoning itself is often potentially self-destructive given alternate probable realities, or outright mediocre. The narrative often clearly indicates that Light Yagami despite operating within a complex system does not account for a multitude of variables within the system or broadly consider the emergent complexity within the system, opting instead to apply basic linear abduction which he succeeds at due to his innate luck and the simplified absolute complexity of the Death Note verse that negates severe factors like the butterfly effect. An example of this is when Light concludes something along the lines of 'L will suspect the task force now and investigate them, so the task force will suspect him in return and investigate back, then once they find L's name I can kill him.' It is improbable and relies on overly simplistic highly specific assumptions about multiple agents within the complex system, and yet Light overly depends on the conclusions to succeed. You'll notice this same linear non-complex thinking applies to the most of his remaining reasoning feats as well.

**Adversity Capacity:** Normal Human

Light possesses a normal human being's capacity for adversity. For example, when he killed someone for the first time, he ran off, almost vomited, hid under the cover of his bed in fear, and had nightmares for 5 days straight. The majority of the feats people claim him to have such as L calling Kira divine for killing criminals without blinking stem from Light getting used to the act itself (even a below average human in adversity capacity can kill criminals using the Death Note without blinking after having written so many names already over multiple months). More examples include: Lind L Taylor, shouting at Misa, getting enraged by Mello and Near, revealing he is Kira to Naomi before confirmation of the Death Note's effect, getting emotionally destroyed at the end of the novel to the point where he can no longer think straight or reason properly, etc., Light is approximately at the level of a normal human in adversity capacity and it can even be argued that normal humans generally would have handled the first in-person kill (Takuo Shibuimaru) far better than how Light handled it. Another feat which people use (Light being composed after L reveals himself) is not beyond what a normal human can achieve and Light proved it is his limit via the forced attempt at composure and failure to go into more sophisticated emotional control such as by acting surprised and in character with how a normal human who does not expect L to show up would have acted.

**Social Intelligence:** CGEW

While Light's social skills are shown to operate at a high enough level that allows him to maintain complex relationships with multiple people, this is heavily carried by biological factors like height and looks which should not be accounted for when scaling the mental outsmarting faculties of a character. The macrocosmic social faculties are indicated to be lacking and inferior to what normal humans possess by both his inability to understand Lind whose viewpoints on killing = bad (not unexpected of a normal human to have) ragebaited him, and his inability to use the death note in more creative ways to affect the political landscape, instead opting for a simplistic bruteforcing of names including criminals that have already been locked up.

**Creativity:** CGEW

Despite spending so much time getting used to the Death Note, Light fails to make proper use of his informational and physical advantages, never using the Death Note on a more macro scale to change the political and social landscape, focusing instead on bruteforcing criminals including those who have already been locked up, which is a failure in prioritization. There were too many opportunities that even a normal human could have taken advantage of, using information and power asymmetry to accomplish changes, some of which I will mention in the remaining categories.

**Countering & Adaptability:** CGEW

The narrative often indicates that adaptability is Light's weakest category. We begin with the initial antifeat (Lind) from which we expect Light to grow and adapt to the intellectual scale of his opponents, but the author clarifies Light's childishness and his refusal to adapt when the character sacrifices his main advantages using the L isolation plan, in order to even the playing field with his opponent due to a sense of pride, rather than adapting to the intellect he was faced with. Even after L's self-revelation, Light underestimated him by treating his cafe trap as another simplistic puzzle, which resulted in L increasing within his mind the probability of Light being Kira after Light spoke too much. Finally after L died, Light then moved onto showing his inability to adapt to L's successors which resulted in his underestimating and getting outsmarted by them multiple times, due to his either highly simplistic or overly complicated moves that were done due to pride, underestimation, or the inability to properly adapt the way a normal human could, which culminated to result in his eventual death. It is only when the opponents unnecessarily give Light extreme time advantages that he is able to adapt, which is something a normal human would also be able to accomplish.

**Emotional Intelligence:** Normal Human

Light does have some moments of deep emotional comprehension such as when he needed to manipulate Naomi into giving up her identity, however, it never reaches a level that definitively exceeds what normal humans (even your average psychology student) can achieve. When Light failed to understand Misa's feelings of love, instead trying to force an interpretation onto her that she only admires him, or when he failed to understand the betrayal the task force members felt, expecting them to suddenly jump on his side once he gives an inspirational speech, there is a clear separation between the qualia (how a human feels) and the theory (what Light believes they should feel due to logic). This is also why he failed to understand how a normal student would have acted if L suddenly showed up to stalk a university student while touting their personal information. Some feats people bring up such as Light predicting that Rem would sacrifice her life for Misa are invalid, since Rem literally told Light that she would sacrifice her life for Misa. Not much emotional intelligence needed there. Even worse was him trying to force Misa to comply before that, rather than channeling her already present love to make her comply more subtly.

**Acting:** Light Yagami very high diffs

The control Light has over his expressions is above the level of what an average human actor can do, considering Light often needs to adapt his act to the situation on the spot such as when L revealed himself. However, flaws can often be easy to find just as it would be for a normal human actor. The only people who are unable to see through his acting are characters like the task force members who are confirmed to be stupid, which is why the moment L tested his acting skills against someone who is smart (such as at the cafe), the probability of Light being Kira immediately jumped above 90%. Not too great. There is no point in the entire series where Light deceives someone intelligent with his acting other than Naomi who was suffering from depression, gave away all the information about her history (such as admiration for L) to Light, and felt an affinity for Light due to the his image overlapping with L. The actual acting required such as calling her beautiful and telling her their meeting was fated is barely above normal human level acting at best.

**Deception:** CGEW

All of Light Yagami's deceptive feats are overly simplistic, with a severe failure in layering deception well without relying on external supernatural forces like Ryuk who conveniently wrote a fake rule in the Death Note in return for an apple. Even with an immense supernatural advantage (the Death Note) Light failed to use more advanced and subtle deceptive trickery that a normal human may be able to think of such as by having Lind L Tailor gradually go crazy and see visions of Kira before his death (which would even make L look suspiciously like someone who is actually the true Kira and wants to gain public influence for Kira by using the broadcast). Even Near did a similar trick with Teru Mikami, deceiving all the task force members except for Matsuda who came up with the theory for Near. If even national police agency members are this dumb, imagine how easy it would have been for Light to have deceived the world.

**Decision Making:** Normal Human.

Every decision Light made throughout the manga culminated to his eventual death. The suspect list was initially almost boundless since Kira could be a human or some supernatural entity, but the moment he allowed himself to be ragebaited by a TV broadcast, he confirmed that he is a human being who cares enough about the opinion of a mere human on broadcast that disagrees with his ideology, when he could instead have deceived the world by acting aloof and continuing his kills, or using better creativity to do the more terrifyingly convincing trick I mentioned in the previous point, letting people more naturally become disappointed in Lind L Tailor's insignificance compared to Kira who is aloof and does not care about the opinion of an ant. The poor decisions continued to accumulate, such as when Light gave L free information that he is related to the Japanese Police Agency which reduces the suspect list, as well as that he can set the timings of deaths which reduces his information assymetry, and also that he can obtain information from them which severely destroys his massive information advantage even more. Three massive advantages for himself destroyed by a single move from Light, just to accomplish an unlikely tactic that never ended up succeeding too. From the whole universe to only Kanto, then from Kanto to the members of the NPA and related parties, then to the families stalked by Raye Penber, decision making antifeat upon decision making antifeat lead to L closing in on Light. It's even worse when looking at the decisions without benefit such as choosing to show up at the place Penber died just so he can aurafarm, telling Naomi that he is Kira before the death note even takes effect just so he can aurafarm, telling Near "I win!" just so he can aurafarm, etc. Normal humans are generally instilled with a biological instinct for self preservation, caution, and proper risk management which would prevent them from making illogical decisions that have no significant gain, especially on such a consistent scale.

**Bodily Kinaesthetic Intelligence:** Light Yagami low diffs.

This is Light's best category and the only one in which he actually shines. If you had to pick one category to represent a character, bodily kinaesthetic intelligence is definitely the one that represents Light. It is the only domain wherein he isn't severely lacking compared to what an a normal human generally is able to accomplish. Light has both good athleticism (such as Tennis feat) and Dexterity (such as writing criminal names with left hand then immediately switching to picking up a potato chip).

**Conclusion:** CGEW. Light Yagami is approximately on the level of a normal human. Keep in mind that by normal human, I am not necessarily talking about a completely average human, but rather someone who is neither all too bright nor too blunt. For example, among my dozens of close friends, actually competent rivals, and even enemies, Light Yagami is somewhere on the upper end, but nowhere near the top.
