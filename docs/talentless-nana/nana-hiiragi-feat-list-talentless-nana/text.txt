Nana Hiiragi intelligence feats:

**"Humans don't believe the truth. They believe people."**

Who she is: [https://munou-na-nana.fandom.com/wiki/Nana\_Hiiragi](https://munou-na-nana.fandom.com/wiki/Nana_Hiiragi)

\[EP2\] After hearing Seiya’s rumor that Shibusawa has a time-related ability, she decides to confirm it for herself. She first finds Shibusawa eating alone, so she joins him and intentionally spills his water while making it look accidental as she reaches for his pickles. Knowing he would use his ability to prevent the spill, she watches closely. When the water does not spill, she confirms that he indeed possesses a time-related power. However, because he shows visible physical strain, she deduces that he is actually rewinding time rather than stopping it. Presumably, because if he were simply stopping time, he would not feel any strain at all. This leads Shibusawa to believe that she can read minds. Kyouya then interrupts the two to ask questions about Nakajima’s disappearance. Since she has already manipulated Shibusawa into thinking she is psychic, she pretends to perceive an unfamiliar inner voice coming from Kyouya when she mentions that enemies of humanity take human form. This convinces Shibusawa—who already believes she can read minds—that Kyouya is untrustworthy, preventing him from getting involved with someone who might suspect her. However, Shibusawa still wants to learn what happened to Nakajima, so he goes back in time. Fortunately for her, the past Nana sees Shibusawa hiding in the bushes, which forces him back into the present. After he leaves, she formulates a plan. She goes to the shed, grabs a shovel and presumably a bag of soil, and pours the soil over part of the lake that Seiya had previously frozen to disguise the ice. She wakes Shibusawa and tells him she knows where Nakajima went missing, bringing him to the soil-covered section of the lake and instructing him to go back in time so they can find the culprit.

In reality, this traps him beneath the lake and kills him, as he cannot swim—a detail she already knew from watching him get bullied previously. Additionally, since he experienced physical strain while using his ability, he would be unable to fight back against the lake—exactly as she planned.

\[EP5\] She starts out in a horrible situation. Tsunekichi, a student who can see the past or future in his dreams and then bring those events into reality through his camera, threatens to reveal a photo showing her pushing Nakajima off a cliff. This would destroy her class leader reputation and expose her as a murderer. She puts on a show of desperation while deducing that he doesn’t have friends on the island—his comment about the island being boring suggests as much—so no one would believe his claim that she killed Nakajima. Still, she continues to play along, realising the situation is manageable if she can destroy the photos and kill Tsunekichi. Maintaining her act, she asks what she can do to make him trust her. To make him bring her somewhere private—specifically, his room. After he falls asleep, she begins her investigation. She observes the process of Tsunekichi’s camera producing photographs and deduces that his dreams are the true source of the prophecies, while the camera merely manifests them into the real world. The fact that he hadn’t taken any pictures yet, but the camera was still producing photos, confirms this. She also deduces that he can’t choose which future he sees; otherwise, he would already know what happens after the photo where she attacks him. Checking the time, she takes Tsunekichi’s camera and photographs the night sky, confirming that the camera itself is ordinary—his ability comes from him, not the device. She decides she must destroy the camera, knowing it contains the photo of her killing Nakajima. In the supply shed, she eyes the jump rope she would use if the prophecy were inevitable. She wonders if fate can truly be changed but resolves to fight it. She tampers with Tsunekichi’s watch, setting it back ten minutes in an attempt to alter the future. When this trick fails, she turns to her backup plan: poisoning him during the struggle with the toxic needles she had prepared in her sewing kit. She quickly fabricates a cover story: Tsunekichi blackmailed her with premonition photos created by enemies of humanity. She explains that she lured him to a remote location and hid a fake premonition photo to trap him. As Tsunekichi succumbs to the poison, When Kyouya and Michiru arrive, she navigates their questions carefully, ensuring they see only the specific spirit photo so they believe Tsunekichi couldn’t predict the future. She then gathers the remaining premonition photos and the camera before they can find them.

\[EP3\] She was pissed off at Kyouya even previously stopping herself from killing him without hesitation when they hung out, simply because she didn’t know his ability. On top of that, she endured him revealing that he knew Seiya and Shiori were dating, nearly blowing her fake psychic cover: It had been pouring since that morning, yet Seiya’s left side was dry and Shiori’s right side was dry. Walking to class together under a single umbrella, it was logical to conclude that they were in a relationship. Determined to figure out Kyouya’s ability, she made a plan. As he carried out his usual routine of giving the cat warm milk, she trailed him from the shadows. She observed that his bathroom was clean while his room was messy, that his trash bags were doubled as if to avoid odor, and that he kept tomatoes on his desk with expiration dates listed. From this, she deduced that Kyouya’s senses were unusually sharp. Acting on her deduction, she saturated the shed with gasoline, planning that when he lit the camp stove, it would explode. She then watched Kyouya fall out of the shed and collapse, wondering what his ability was—since it seemed he hadn’t used it to escape the flames. She tried to leave to fetch water to put out the fire, to appear innocent but Kyouya stopped her.

\[EP4\] She ran past Kyouya and burst into Michiru’s room. Michiru asked why she was there, and she shouted that an enemy of humanity was at the window. While Michiru wasn’t looking, she stabbed herself in the back with a gimlet and tossed it aside, causing Michiru to rush to her aid. Michiru pulled off her uniform to examine the wound and healed it. She thanked her while warning her not to move too much. A group of girls then arrived to help, reinforcing the story. She had orchestrated all of this to make the rumour about enemies of humanity seem real and to make it appear as though she had been protecting Michiru, positioning herself as a hero. By stabbing herself, she acted the victim, gained sympathy from the Talented, and set herself up to be class leader because Michiru assumed she saved her, voting for her to be the next class leader, which caused everyone else to agree.
