A Note On Kyouya Onodera (Spoilers for Talentless Nana)

As a Talentless Nana scaler, I feel like I should address Kyoya and how weird his scaling is, I see people say that the timeskip nerfed him and I think that is both kinda true and kinda false. I don't think his scaling got weaker but instead it shifted to reveal itself to be a whole other thing (which may actually make him noticably better than just his Pre-timeskip version alone, but it is pretty situational.)

In short I think people have been getting what Kyoya is best at slightly wrong because of how he was in Pre-timeskip. Instead of being a logic type I see him more as kinda like a matchup demon because though he is technically weaker than alot of characters that is actually to his advantage. If his adversary rejects or downplays him as an actual threat then in the long term he will come back to bite them, his adaptability I think is one of his greatest strengths.

Of course when I say this it means that characters that don't downplay him and take him seriously would win fairly easily and at some point he will not be able to out adapt his opponents who vastly outscale him but I find the fact he can punch above his own scaling class like that pretty entertaining.

Now if you are wondering why I am saying that he is actually maybe better than people give him for, is because of how Pre-timeskip and Post-timeskip Kyoya differ in terms of distribution. They are definitely different and I wouldn't say one is notably weaker than the other because their strengths and weaknesses are kind of parallel to eachother, Pre-timeskip is grounded in a more pure logic approach while Post-timeskip takes a more emotion based and understanding people approach.

There is something to be said with Pre-timeskip creating a precaution that allowed him to come back but I am not really going to argue for any kind of upscale with him as this is more like a reframing of his scaling so the validity or how high it scales is as you will make of it.

Anyway Kyoya ends up adapting the emotion and understanding people approach in the timeskip after being saved by Nana and being shown that building connections pays off, a weakness with Pre-timeskip Kyoya is that he isn't very good at EQ and SQ or any sort of the irrational stuff that comes with emotions leading to him having to lean on logic which can only get you so far. Afterall that is what lead to him getting indirectly no diffed by Tsuruoka.

So with that fatal flaw established this time he adapts to look throughvan irrational lense too, this doesn't exactly make him scale better than he did before but it does make him fit his environment much better allowing him to do better than his previous approach. That probably makes the fact that he was a part of Nana's plan to completely counter Tsuruoka more fitting, they both indirectly no diff eachother somehow.

I guess if I were to explain how I think he is as an SCD character I wouls call him a 'smurfer' so I guess in a weirdly roundabout way he is kinda like Tsuruoka just instead of farming teenagers for feats he would be farming low tiers and mentally unstable mid tiers for wins. If I were to compare him to anyone that I have read as an SCD character it would be Mary Saotome considering their win to loss ratio and impressions of getting no diffed quite a bit would make you think they are lower than they actually are. Actually he is kind of like a proof of how T3 of Talentless Nana deserve their spot in the top 3 as all three of them have outsmarted him in one way or another.

How he works as a SCD character is quite fun especially as like a higher low tier or lower mid tier (though the lower mid tier thing is highballish).
