So I'm gonna be debunking the "Jason Voorhees is mentally a child" thing.

A lot of people consider Jason to be just a childish brute when he's a lot more clever than people give him credit for.

if you’ve been in the horror space you’ve seen this theory spread around, mainly to silence shippers, Jason yumes, or just general fans. I’d like to make a thread on why this is misinformation. I’ll also be debunking the statements of Jason being “stupid” or childlike.

Not everyone who spreads this theory around is doing so with malicious intent or out of self inflicted ignorance, a lot of people are newer fans or just straight up didn’t know. I hope to clear some things up to new or confused fans here!

1. “He drowned as a kid, so he’s mentally one!”

Wrong. He never drowned, he survived this. It’s shown throughout the movies that he survived the drowning incident. Pamela BELIEVED he died because they never saw each other again afterwards. He had to grow up in the woods on his own. Why my goat had so much gains.

if he died as a kid, him coming back as an adult makes… zero sense. If it was true that he DID die from drowning, we wouldn’t have a Friday the 13th franchise. Like I said, Pamela believed he died, anyone would.

2. “He’s obsessed with his mother, therefore he must be mentally a child!”

A lot of his mother related issues stem more from psychosis and age regression. Him hearing her voice despite her death and imagining women as her is his brain’s way of coping with a stolen childhood.

if you want examples of this, Friday the 13th part 2 and the remake are good ones. He knows his mother is dead, yet apart of him still clings to memories of her in other women. People forget that this is a very traumatized man who never had the chance to grow up.

3. “Jason is the dumbest of the slasher villains (or any argument insinuating that he is)”

These arguments are not only ableist but also just wrong. Yes, jason may not have certain smarts like other horror characters, but he is far from dumb.I’ll use part 6 as an example.

In part 6, Jason is shown to adapt quickly to his new strength after learning of it, he hides in the trees, and sets up diversions outside of Cort’s RV. Another example is in Jason X where he got out of his restraints and posed a body under a sheet as a fake.

He's posed bodies many times before, unlike Michael who usually does it out of sadism Jason does it because he clearly knows people don't think straight when they are scared.

Mind you, If Jason was stupid or at all as dumb as horror fans make him out to be, he would’ve never survived in the woods alone. He would’ve never been able to use weaponry or hunt counselors as effectively as he did.

Throughout the movies, you can see very clearly him using skills common in hunting to kill counselors (diversions, stealth, camouflage, traps, patience) his adaptability ALONE makes him smart. No idiot would’ve done all of this.

It’s important to remember that a lot of his more “dumb” traits could be chalked up to him being socially isolated throughout his childhood and adulthood and being severely traumatized.

Would someone who’s mentally a child do all this killing? Would they have the wits to outsmart military personnel? Would they have the wits to know a counselor’s every move? The patience? The weaponry and environmental adaptability?

If you watched the f13 movies and came to the conclusion that Jason was a child, you are VERY wrong about his character. I don’t blame newer fans or older fans getting back in, but this theory gets debunked if you’ve seen… any of the movies recently.
