Some Eren Yeager FSIQ (Full-Scale IQ) Feats 🥶🥶🥶🥶

EREN YEAGER’S FSIQ FEATS:

—————————————————————————————————————————————————————————

WMI:

# Outsmarting Reiner and Bertholdt in the Return to Shiganshina (Chapter 77)

* During the fight against Reiner and Bertholdt, Eren **remembers Annie’s fighting techniques** and applies them in battle.
* He uses **Annie’s grappling moves**, countering Reiner’s brute force with technique.
* This shows his **ability to store and recall training-based information under stress**, a key WMI indicator.

# Mastering the War Hammer Titan’s Abilities Instantly (Chapter 117)

* After consuming the War Hammer Titan, Eren quickly understands how to **manifest structures from the ground**, something that its previous user needed **years of training** to master.
* His **ability to analyze and recall Titan abilities instantly** shows high WMI, as he mentally retains and adapts to new information **with minimal effort**.

# Memorising the Battlefield During the Liberio Attack (Chapter 104)

* Eren processes multiple layers of combat: fighting the War Hammer Titan, luring in Porco (Jaw Titan), and monitoring the Marleyan forces.
* He **tracks multiple enemies and allies** at once, directing Armin’s Colossal Titan transformation as a final blow.
* This requires an **advanced working memory**, allowing him to execute a **complex multi-step strategy under extreme pressure**.

# Outsmarting the War Hammer Titan in Seconds (Chapter 101)

* Eren **immediately deduces** that the War Hammer Titan’s user is **not inside the nape but underground**, after mikasa’s attack.
* Instead of wasting time attacking the body, he **digs through the ground** and **rips out the real user**, bypassing a fight entirely.
* This is **exceptionally high PSI**, as he instantly processes new data and reacts **in seconds**.

—————————————————————————————————————————————————————————

PSI:

#  Dodging and Countering Reiner’s Attacks Mid-Fight (Chapter 77)

* While fighting Reiner, Eren **quickly adapts to new situations**, shifting from raw aggression to technical counters.
* Instead of mindlessly punching, he **redirects Reiner’s weight and locks his joints**, proving he can **analyze movement and react faster than his opponent**.
* This shows **high-speed visual and kinesthetic processing**, a strong PSI feat.

# Outsmarting Zeke in the Paths (Chapter 122)

* Eren **immediately understands that Ymir obeys only the royal bloodline** and figures out a way to override Zeke’s control.
* Instead of brute-forcing a solution, he **emotionally manipulates Ymir**, convincing her to break free.
* He processes **Zeke’s royal advantage, Ymir’s history, and an emotional argument** within seconds—**insanely fast logical deduction**.

# F. Analyzing and Exploiting the Jaw Titan’s Weakness in Seconds (Chapter 104-105)

* During the Liberio attack, Eren quickly **notices that the Jaw Titan’s claws and teeth can break the War Hammer Titan’s crystal**.
* Instead of wasting energy, he **grabs Porco and forces him to bite down**, instantly achieving victory.
* This shows **incredible reaction speed**—he **analyzes enemy abilities and counterattacks within seconds**.

QRI:

# Using the Paths Dimension to Manipulate Past and Future Events (Chapter 120-121)

* In *Chapter 120-121*, Eren **instantly understands** the concept of Paths and how memories flow through time.
* He realizes that **his future self can influence the past**, then **manipulates Grisha to carry out his plan**.
* This is **abstract reasoning at an extreme level**, as he **processes nonlinear time manipulation instantly**.

# Analyzing and Exploiting the Jaw Titan’s Claws Against the War Hammer Titan (Chapter 104)

* Eren **immediately figures out** that Porco’s Jaw Titan can break the War Hammer Titan’s crystal.
* Instead of wasting energy, he **catches Porco, forces him to bite the crystal, and cracks it open**.
* This demonstrates **high-level cause-and-effect reasoning** and **extreme quick thinking under pressure**.

# Recognizing Zeke’s True Intentions Before Anyone Else (Chapter 112-113)

* While others believe Zeke is fully loyal to Paradis, Eren **analyzes his behavior and deduces his hidden euthanasia plan**.
* He **tests Zeke’s loyalty by playing along**, while secretly preparing to **betray him at the right moment**.
* This shows **exceptional psychological analysis and long-term reasoning**.

—————————————————————————————————————————————————————————

VCI

# Convincing Ymir to Rebel Against 2,000 Years of Slavery (Chapter 122)

* When Zeke tries to command Ymir using royal blood, Eren **uses psychological manipulation instead**.
* He **analyzes her trauma and uses powerful rhetoric**.
* This **emotionally charged argument** is so strong that **Ymir abandons her loyalty to the royal family**.
* This is **one of the best persuasion feats in the series**, proving **exceptional VCI**.

# Manipulating Grisha into Destroying the Royal Family (Chapter 121)

* In *Chapter 121*, Eren **guides Grisha into killing the Reiss family** using subtle emotional pressure.
* Instead of commanding him outright, he **exploits Grisha’s doubts, past trauma, and parental guilt**.
* His ability to **use words to push someone into committing genocide** shows **high verbal manipulation and persuasion**.

# Deceiving Zeke by Pretending to Agree with Him (Chapter 112-120) (yes this is also a VCI feat)

* When Zeke tries to recruit Eren for the Euthanasia Plan, Eren **pretends to agree**, making Zeke lower his guard.
* He **plays along, matching Zeke’s tone and arguments**, making it seem like they’re on the same side.
* The deception works so well that Zeke is **completely shocked when Eren betrays him in Paths**.

#  Controlling Historia’s Decision-Making (Chapter 130)

* Eren subtly **influences Historia’s emotions**, making her feel that **stopping him would betray Paradis**.
* He recalls key moments from their past, using **selective memory recall** to strengthen his argument. (ALSO WMI Feat)
* Historia, despite her resistance, ultimately **hesitates and doesn’t stop him**, proving **Eren’s verbal manipulation ability**.
