Zhuo Fan is the protagonist of Magic Emperor, a former Demonic Emperor who is reborn after being betrayed and killed. Retaining the knowledge, experience, and memories accumulated during his previous life, he begins his second life as the steward of the declining Luo Clan.

Rather than relying purely on cultivation or overwhelming force, Zhuo Fan frequently approaches conflicts as problems of information, manipulation, preparation, and strategic positioning. His experience as the former Demonic Emperor gives him extensive knowledge of the cultivation world, its factions, techniques, power structures, and human behavior.

Throughout the story, Zhuo Fan repeatedly operates several steps ahead of his opponents, manipulating circumstances so that enemies make decisions that ultimately benefit his objectives. He is particularly notable for his ability to exploit psychological weaknesses, incomplete information, political relationships, incentives, deception, and long-term planning.

For SCD purposes, Zhuo Fan is best examined not simply through his cultivation achievements, but through how effectively he gathers and processes information, predicts opponents, constructs plans, manipulates individuals and organizations, adapts when his plans encounter unexpected variables, and converts seemingly disadvantageous situations into favorable positions.

SCD PROFILE

Zhuo Fan's intelligence profile can therefore be summarized as a combination of:

Experience + Strategic Planning + Manipulation + Deception + Psychological Insight + Foresight + Adaptability + Information Exploitation

His SCD value comes particularly from his ability to turn information and human behavior into strategic leverage. Rather than merely attempting to outthink an opponent in a single exchange, Zhuo Fan often seeks to control the circumstances surrounding the conflict itself.

VOLUME 1

Chapter 1-3: The Demonic Emperor's Possession — Day 1

Context: Demonic Emperor Zhuo Yifan is the strongest demonic cultivator in the Sacred Domain. He obtains the legendary Nine Serenities Secret Records, a cultivation manual of immense power. His disciple, Zhao Chen, betrays him out of jealousy, revealing his location to the Seven Emperors (the strongest righteous cultivators) and a Saint (an even higher existence). They surround him and demand the manual. Zhuo Yifan refuses, destroys the manual, and self-destructs. However, his soul does not perish—it drifts and finds a dying 15-year-old servant named Zhuo Fan, whose obsession to protect the Luo clan creates a "heart demon" contract, binding Zhuo Yifan's soul to the servant's body and forcing him to protect the Luo clan.

Current Situation: Zhuo Yifan awakens in a pile of corpses, inhabiting the body of a 15-year-old servant who has never cultivated. The body is weak, but it is a "pure body," which is required for the Demon Transformation Art in the Nine Serenities Secret Records. He finds an injured guard (2nd layer Qi Condensation) and needs to grow stronger quickly to survive.

Feat 1: Overnight Foundation Establishment and Qi Condensation Synthesis

This is the foundational feat of the entire story. Zhuo Yifan assesses his new body and identifies its most valuable asset: it is a "pure body" that has never cultivated. This is critical because the Nine Serenities Secret Records' Demon Transformation Art requires a pure body to train in it. The art allows the user to steal others' cultivation and eventually "swallow the Heavens and devour the Earth." He finds an injured guard (2nd layer Qi Condensation) and uses the Demon Transformation Art to drain his Yuan Qi, turning him to dust. He refines the energy overnight, a process described as being on the verge of bursting his meridians. By morning, he reaches the 5th layer of Foundation Establishment, but his Yuan Qi quality is equivalent to a Qi Condensation expert. This means his physical body is far stronger than his cultivation level suggests, and his foundation is described as "never before seen." The text notes that even rare geniuses from the Sacred Domain need a year to reach the 5th layer of Foundation Establishment, yet Zhuo Fan did it in one night. This establishes his core advantage: a cultivation method that allows for exponential growth through devouring others, combined with his vast experience as a Demonic Emperor to maximize every gain. The heart demon contract is also established here: Zhuo Fan's resentment fused with the Demonic Emperor's hatred, becoming a shackle that forces him to protect the Luo clan or suffer heart demon attacks. This means he cannot simply abandon the Luo clan; he must ensure their safety to avoid the heart demon, which would cause his cultivation to deviate and potentially kill him.

Key Dialogue: "This kid is 15 yet has never cultivated. His body is untainted." / "I am the Sacred Domain's Eighth Emperor, Demonic Emperor Zhuo Yifan!" / "Man, this Demon Transformation Art is fantastic."

Chapter 4-6: Steward Sun's Ambush — Day 1

Context: The Luo clan was attacked by Blackwind Mountain bandits. Steward Sun, a trusted member of the Luo clan, betrayed them and helped the bandits breach their defenses. The surviving Luo clan members—Luo Yunchang (young miss), Luo Yunhai (young master), and Captain Pang (guard captain)—fled into the forest. Steward Sun and his bandits pursued them. Zhuo Fan, now possessing the body of the dead servant, hears the commotion and is forced by the heart demon to intervene. Steward Sun (6th layer Qi Condensation) and his bandits have surrounded the Luo siblings and Captain Pang. They demand the Returning Dragon Palm, a spirit-ranked martial art passed down through the Luo clan for generations. Luo Yunchang refuses. Steward Sun threatens to kill them all. Zhuo Fan is currently at the 5th layer of Foundation Establishment with Qi Condensation-level Yuan Qi.

Feat 2: Blitz Attack on a Higher Realm Cultivator

Despite being 5th layer Foundation Establishment, Zhuo Fan targets a fat bandit (7th layer Foundation Establishment) holding a blade to his neck. The bandit, underestimating him as a mere servant, is completely off guard. Zhuo Fan uses surprise and Qi Condensation-level Yuan Qi in his left hand, elbowing the bandit's chest with enough force to shatter his ribcage. In one fluid motion, he grabs the fallen blade and decapitates the fat man. The text notes this brutality shook both the bandits and the Luo guards, as it was a swift, lethal execution that even the guard captain could not match. The guards, who knew Zhuo Fan as an honest and submissive servant, are left in shock, wondering if he was scared silly or had been hiding his true nature. Zhuo Fan then immediately grabs the siblings and retreats, prioritizing their safety over fighting the remaining forces. He throws the bloody saber at Steward Sun as a distraction, showcasing his ability to use every moment for tactical advantage. He also demonstrates his ability to adapt to a situation, turning a disadvantage (a blade at his neck) into an opportunity by exploiting the enemy's overconfidence. This feat establishes his combat philosophy: use surprise, speed, and lethal force to eliminate threats before they can react.

Chapter 13-14: Blood Infant Refinement — Days 5-15

Context: Zhuo Fan acquires the Blood Spirit, a rare demonic treasure that can be refined into a Blood Infant. According to the Nine Serenities Secret Records, the Blood Infant is a powerful entity that can absorb blood essence and Yuan Qi, and can grow alongside the cultivator. Feat 5: Refining the Blood Infant and Mastering the Blood Palm

Zhuo Fan feeds the stone his own blood for hours, almost dying from blood loss. The text describes how the Blood Spirit is formed only when bathed in the blood of millions of people and absorbing the essence of the sun and moon for thousands of years. Zhuo Fan's blood is not enough to fully satisfy it, but his determination and the Demon Transformation Art allow him to synchronize with it. The Blood Spirit synchronizes with his heartbeat, forming a palm-sized red infant in his Dantian. This grants him an unorthodox healing factor (it acts as a secondary heart) and an external combat drone. He also masters the Blood Palm, a mid-mortal-ranked martial art that becomes as strong as a high-mortal-ranked art due to the Blood Infant. According to the Nine Serenities Secret Records, there was once an ancient demonic cultivator expert, Patriarch Blood Demon, who trained the Blood Infant to the Saint Stage, making even the ten ancient Sovereigns unwilling to make an enemy out of him. The Nine Serenities Sovereign obtained Patriarch Blood Demon's secret method, improved it, and added it to the Demon Transformation Art. Zhuo Fan notes that if he had the Blood Infant in his past life, a Saint would have been nothing. This process is extremely dangerous, as stopping midway would cause the Blood Infant to reject him forever, but Zhuo Fan's determination and demonic cultivation experience allow him to succeed. He also demonstrates his willingness to endure extreme pain and risk death for long-term gain, a hallmark of his character. The Blood Infant also serves as a secondary heart, meaning that even if his original heart is destroyed, he can survive as long as the Blood Infant exists. This is a crucial advantage that he will exploit in future battles.
