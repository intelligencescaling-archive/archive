REGULAR SHOW
Written by ctrl

Regular Show is an animated cartoon series that covers the adventures of two reckless employees, Mordecai & Rigby, and how they keep their jobs at the not-so-regular park they work at. The story also contains a wide variety of side-characters that become involved in the two’s shenanigans, including other co-workers, Muscleman & Fives, the park manager Benson and the owner’s son, Pops. Together, they delve into the most absurd plots imaginable for the sake of achieving a usually underwhelming objective, one that often gets them in trouble with their boss.

Cosmology: Outerversal

Regular Show’s universe has an infinite number of timelines, as stated by Future Rigby, alongside containing a number of multiverse realms within it, such as the Theoretical Mall, a store in which each floor has every conceivable item you could want; or an infinite amount of dimensions; since an infinite number of objects literally cannot be contained within any other space of 3d, 4d, 5d etc.
The Digital Realm is a dimension that contains every conceivable version of any show, video game etc. Additionally, every single one of the video games are their own universe, meaning the verse contains two different realms with an infinite number of dimensions.
But actually, the full cosmology contains an infinite amount of realms encompassing infinite universes & dimensions based on the fact there are infinite timelines.
