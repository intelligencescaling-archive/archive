The Trojan Vessel (Nightmare's Manipulation of Nim)

![img](em7c3d1jq90i1 "\"The Tree of Feelings...\"")

The tree is established to be the source of everyone's emotions, meaning that if the tree dies, emotions themselves die.

![img](ij1khoa0s90i1 "\"The embodiment of Negativity\"")

Since Nightmare's existence is tied to negativity, the tree's survival is therefore essential to his own existence.

![img](p4r4wcn3s90i1)

Nim was attacked by an individual whose negativity was far beyond what would normally be expected.

![img](t4uf74e1v90i1 "\"The Inner voice of Malice...\"")

This can be attributed to Nightmare, who is the root of negative thoughts and acts as the inner voice that encourages beings to act upon emotions such as hatred, fear, anger, and disgust.

Rather than directly controlling someone, Nightmare can amplify their existing negativity and manipulate their thoughts until his influence feels like their own by acting as their Inner voice.

This is exactly what happened with Nim.

![img](74024udes90i1 "\"A problem...\"")

After the attack, Nim was overwhelmed by fear and disgust, particularly because her life was connected to the tree.

Nightmare exploited these emotions while disguising himself as Nim's own negative thoughts—the voice in the back of her mind—and simultaneously as the voice of the tree.

![img](yau9sl3hs90i1 "\"The former Guardian.\"")

Nim can perceive the auras and intentions of other beings.

![img](81vvcr2ks90i1 "\"Best Friends!\"")

Yet, despite having an ability specifically suited to recognizing another person's intentions, she failed to recognize Nightmare because he concealed his influence as something originating from within her own mind while also deciving her as an outside influence—aka the voice of the tree.

![img](tyoye0nps90i1)

Nightmare had also caused Nim to distance herself from the other Guardians, isolating her from people who could have challenged his influence.

![img](t1pqmo1zs90i1)

He then guided her toward the idea of creating two new Guardians:

* Dream, composed of positive energy

And

* Passive Nightmare, composed of negative energy.

![img](yhbqig7vv90i1)

The human vessels initially failed because they could not contain the enormous amount of energy involved.

This forced Nim to search other universes for suitable vessels, eventually leading her to Swap Sans in Underswap. Using his skeletal body as a template, she created vessels capable of containing Dream and Passive.

Once they existed, Nim could finally fuse with the tree and ensure its continued protection.

So Nightmare's manipulation can be summarized as this:

Isolate Nim → Manipulate the aggressor

Create the crisis → amplify Nim's negative emotions

Disguise himself as her thoughts and the voice of the tree → guide her toward creating Dream and Passive

Indirectly lead her to Swap Sans → ensure the tree remains protected.

And the plan didn't end with Nim.

After her death, Dream and Passive were left with the purpose of avenging her, which was ultimately what Nim wanted.

![img](l23qxlmnt90i1)

They themselves would not know the true circumstances behind their creation or Nightmare's involvement; they would simply exist as normal beings carrying out the purpose Nim had given them. (Protecting the tree.)

This leads into the true purpose behind Passive's creation.

He wanted Nim to create a vessel for himself.

![img](lvrw11aou90i1)

By making Passive entirely from negative energy, Nightmare effectively ensured that his future vessel would already be compatible with the same energy that defines his existence.

Passive therefore provided Nightmare with two major advantages:

A compatible vessel: His body was composed of negative energy, making him far more suitable for containing Nightmare than an ordinary being.

A vulnerable mind: Because Passive himself was made from negativity, negative thoughts and impulses were already inherent to his existence.

Nightmare would therefore not need to introduce negativity into Passive's mind. He could simply exploit what was already there.

Passive could experience a negative thought and believe it was his own, allowing Nightmare to act as the voice behind those thoughts without revealing his presence.

This makes Passive especially vulnerable to Nightmare's manipulation because the distinction between his own negativity and Nightmare's influence would be extremely difficult to recognize.

His role as the Guardian of the negative side of the tree further reinforced this connection to negativity, giving Nightmare another foundation to exploit later.

Ultimately, Nightmare manipulated Nim into unknowingly creating the vessel, the mindset, and the circumstances he would eventually need.
