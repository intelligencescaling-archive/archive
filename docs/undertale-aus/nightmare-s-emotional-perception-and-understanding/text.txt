Nightmare's Emotional Perception and Understanding

Corrupted Nightmare is described as negativity itself, existing for as long as time and functioning as the voice in the back of one's head that sounds like their own thoughts.

![img](1r3477u4ea1i1 "\"Inner Voice.\"")

He guides beings toward negative actions they have already considered and can bring up memories that intensify those emotions.

![img](3cqwllzeea1i1 "\"A true narcissist who likes his job.\"")

However, it' also is stated that he can produce the same effects through his words alone.

He can take someone's existing self-pain and redirect it into hatred toward their family.

Meaning that his manipulation does not fundamentally depend on his aura or conceptual existence.

Even without his supernatural powers, Nightmare would still be able to understand someone's emotions, identify their sources, recognize the memories reinforcing them, and redirect those feelings toward a desired action.

His powers simply amplify a psychological ability he already possesses.

This becomes more insanely wild when considering the cosmology he operates within.

![img](56kd0o96fa1i1 "\"Spacetime continuum.\"")

Timelines are part of the spacetime continuum, each containing their own events, choices, and possibilities.

They remain separate from one another, meaning that altering or destroying one does not necessarily affect the others.

![img](4pcgyun0ga1i1 "\"Miku Killer! Sans?!?!\"")

Killer!Sans demonstrates this concept by existing as an alternate version of Undertale!Sans. (While also having literally any version of himself be canon, he's Miku basically)

![img](6rkq664dga1i1 "\"Timelines...\"")

![img](vtkhf64dga1i1 "\"Can't stop emmmm.\"")

Because choices can create alternate timelines, and those timelines can branch into further timelines through additional choices, the structure contains an effectively infinite number of possible timelines and outcomes.

![img](cd3dnd5gga1i1)

These timelines exist within universes, with an infinite number of possibilities across the overall structure.

![img](dwrrr95iga1i1 "\"Alternate Multiverses.\"")

Beyond them are the various Alternate Multiverses of Undertale AUs, which would follow the same general cosmological structure.

![img](vd603b9mga1i1 "\"This ain't my place gng.\"")

Above these realities are the Creators, who exist outside the universes and perceive the AUs as stories.

The Creators are ultimately the writers and readers who exist outside the fictional realities.

And even the Undertale multiverse isn't the entire cosmology.

Nim is stated to have entered the Undertale multiverse to escape the other trees, implying that—alongside the existence of the other trees—that therefore other realities or multiversal structures—exist beyond it.

![img](por2o88dha1i1 "\"The tree's purpose.\"")

Since the tree is connected to all beings, this shows the existence of life beyond the Undertale multiverse itself.

Therefore, Nightmare's range of emotional understanding extends across an enormous cosmological structure containing countless beings.

Most importantly, this does not rely entirely on his conceptual nature.

Even if Nightmare's aura, supernatural abilities, and conceptual existence were removed, he could still manipulate all of these people through ordinary psychological means by understanding their emotions and using his words to redirect them to induce basically the same exact result he would with his powers.

He would still be capable of recognizing pain, resentment, fear, hatred, guilt, and other negative emotions, understanding what caused them, and determining how to intensify or redirect them.

So with all of this being said—it would go something like this:

* Cosmology → establishes the Infinite number of distinct minds and possible emotional circumstances.
* Manipulation feat → demonstrates Nightmare's ability to perceive, understand, and manipulate those emotions with his raw EP and EU.
