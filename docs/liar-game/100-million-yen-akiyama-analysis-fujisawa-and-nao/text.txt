100 Million Yen Akiyama analysis (Fujisawa and Nao Manipulation)

LIAR GAME TOURNAMENT and there is 100 million yen. She reads the rule of the game which being:

* Players compete for the money. They can use as much money as they want from the 100 million yen they got.

* Since the game is played in mutual agreement, stealing other players' money would not be accused of stealing to the police.

* Once the opponent is chosen the game will last 30 days.

* Whoever manages to steal the money from their opponent and add it to the money they already have wins.

* After the match is over official representatives will come to retrieve the 100 million yen. The rest of the additional money that you stole will be yours to take.

* If you don't have the 100 million at the end to give it back since your opponent probably stole it you will be in debt.

Nao explains her situation. Akiyama, understanding it, asks whether she wants to win or quit the game.
Nao says she wants to quit and get out of the game. Akiyama then simply just asks her to tell him to give her money back and he will tag along with her.

Nao confronts the teacher and  reveals  that  she  overheard  his  conversation  of  scamming  her  but  the  teacher  acts  like he doesn't  know  what  she  is  talking  about and The bank opens limited hours from Monday to Friday 9 to 5 and without attended he wouldn't/can't open the box so he isn't able to give her money back currently.

The psychological stress from the game is already all time high. The fact that they are in this game where they are fighting for 100 million yen is already absurd combined with the fact that this isn't a public systematic game.

The game is held by some secretive private organization so while the card says the game doesn't count as theft or scam or whatever in the name of law it would still affect the players moral perspective behind the very nature of the game residing in theft and scam and legal validity of the organization who is holding this absurd game.

This intern results in lack of trust behind the game. And with Nao revealing she knows he scammed her, Fujisawa is at a breaking point of his emotional composer.

Akiyama, knowing this, steps  in  and asks if he is returning the money or not   threatening   him   by   saying  if   by   tomorrow   he   didn't   return   the  money they will call the police on him.

This makes the teacher burst in panic having his mind go blank following the instinct of someone who is just trying to survive and immediately trying to argue, isn't it just a game? And isn't by the game rules lost or taken money isn't considered a crime?

Falling for Akiyama trap as he accidentally admits to scamming Nao in a heat of panic. Akiyama points out his wording that “It's just a Game” so everything he did was in the belief of the game not helping Nao. In simple words he scammed Nao of 100 million yen.

Which reveals the vulnerable side of Fujisawa as now he has nothing to hide and the teacher reveals his true personality. Mentioning if he hadn't stolen the 100 million Nao could have stolen his 100 million. “People Can't be trusted”.

This is where we learn about the teacher's past where he basically falsely got reported by other teachers with malicious intent which led to him getting fired which led to him getting divorced as he no longer had a source of income. bla bla bla

Points taken from this interaction:

* The bank opens limited hours from Monday to Friday 9 to 5.

* You must be accompanied by the bank attendance to open the box.

* The teacher has a history of getting into false relationships which lead to his lack of trust in people.

After the interaction Akiyama thinks of a plan to steal the money. Since the bank is only open on Monday and Friday means that the teacher would have to take out the money on Friday or just any day since 23 days are left till this game finishes and by the day this game finishes it will be Sunday the day where the bank is close so he needs to take money out on Friday before the game ends. Meaning they still have a chance to steal the money.

Akiyama understands the psychological state of the teacher that despite the teacher already scamming Nao being in a winning position being all happy he is still not fully on board with his actions.

Which is why he shows a sudden moment of panic the moment Akiyama mentions bringing in the police if he doesn't return the money. With the teacher immediately mentioning the rule.

If the teacher had already accepted the game as it is, being all okey he shouldn't have panicked that way hell it would have been a moment to laugh at Akiyama and Nao telling how futile their attempt to get the police involved is but no.

This established the teacher's lack of trust in the organization holding the game and the game rules itself.

Normally this lack of trust wouldn't amount to anything in this type of situation as it's a normal reaction to have but taking in account the history of betrayals that the teacher experienced in the past which hurted him to the point where he gave up on trusting people. Even someone that was his student (Nao) whose characteristics are defined by naivety. Akiyama is able to make a guess that the teacher might have not even kept the money in the bank and had lied about it as the situation he is dealing with isn't some thug level but a high level scam setup.

* One day he will go to the bank and the money might just not be there.

* One day police might just pull up to him questioning where he got all that money.

And he needs that 100 million yen to get his life together otherwise he will basically be homeless after some time. So the best option for him is to keep the money where he can fully trust it to be safe and that trust isn't some bank, isn't some friend, isn't some random place but the one and only person/place he can put his trust too and it's none other than himself/his house. Now this raises another question: Where in the house has he hidden the money? Akiyama solves this question and also confirms his hypothesis about the money being at the teacher house with a simple tactic.

Akiyama makes Noa confront the teacher once again but this time to declare the war that she WILL steal the money back not just 100 but the entire 200 million Yen.

This brings out the teacher's hidden fear that had been lingering in his heart of the game not being over by just scamming Nao. Due to his lack of trust for anything. Which makes the teacher constantly think that the battle is not over yet.

Surveillance Plan:
After that Akiyama and Nao watch over the teacher and his house 24/7 taking turns. Akiyama also confronts the teacher and tells him that he is helping Nao to steal all of 200 million yen and he has a great plan. Putting more pressure on the teacher as now Nao has an ally and that Ally isn't some random person by Akiyama himself the guy who brought down the largest MLM co post war..

The teacher calls the cops on them describing them as suspicious people roaming around his house. But Akiyama had foreseen that so he had rented a house across from him for a month so they can still keep watching over him without having to bother with the police anymore.

Nao asks Akiyama why they are doing this but Akiyama only gives her a vague explanation of the plan explaining what is going on fujisawa's mind right at the moment. Making sure that Nao herself is also feeling the pressure of the situation, unsure whether she will win or not even with Akiyama's help.

This plan has multiple purposes behind it.

1st is to keep fujisawa under constant pressure. Akiyama announced he is helping Nao and has a great plan to steal the money back with full confidence. Nao also announced she will steal the full 200 million Yen declaring war.

This naturally kept Fujisawa in constant guard thinking every possible way Akiyama and Nao could steal the money and ways to counter that. Now why all this pressure? Akiyama had planned to forge a fake mail card, a liar Game card.

The game ends after 30 days but at what exact time does it end is still unknown. So a mail from Liar Game is expected to be sent to the player near the end announcing the exact time the game will end to inform players ahead of time so when they come to take the money the players have it with them.

Now, according to Akiyama's hypothesis, Fujisawa had hidden the money in his house. With Akiyama and Nao constantly monitoring him and putting pressure on him occupying fujisawa mind with nothing but fear and anxiety on how the money will be stolen.

To guarantee 100% safety he will eventually stay alone side the money all the time guarding it 24/7. This will give Akiyama a chance to secretly put the fake mail without fujisawa knowledge.

So when fujisawa reads the mail the time he gets on when the game will end will be earlier than the actual time.

Akiyama then plans on using that fake time by putting up a disguise of the liar game attendee pretending that the liar game has come to take the money as the game had ended. When in reality the game is still on. In the end stealing all 200 million yen.

The 2nd purpose of this strategy is to confirm his hypothesis about the money being in fujisawa and not the bank.

With Fujisawa being constantly under stress and pressure if Fujisawa had truly kept the money in the bank. He wouldn't be much bothered with Akiyama and Noa surveilling his house.

And if it does affect him mentally like just the thought of being looked at 24/7 by people, especially your enemy, is not really a nice feeling to have. With the lack of privacy. So he can just move out to different places for the time of the game or just avoid staying in the house as much as possible.

Akiyama looks for these 2 specific actions. If Fujisawa shows any signs of these actions then he did put the money in bank or at least it's not in the house and even if there is, it's not an amount that Fujisawa cares for even if Akiyama and Nao manage to steal it. If he doesn't then the money is indeed in the house.

A week passes by. At 1st the teacher was going to the supermarket and stuff but recently he hasn't gone anywhere and is just staying in his home. This pretty much confirms that the money is indeed in his house.

As If a person has 200 million yen and they went to bank to store their money in the safety deposit all while their enemy is trying to steal the money so they keep watch on the person for 24/7 the obvious act would be to run away since the money is safe in the safety deposit so there is no stress of it getting stolen and it's very uncomfortable to get watched for 24/7 but what if the person didn't deposit the money in safety box?

* What if he hid the money in some bushes near their home then he would have a reason to stay at his house.

There is still a chance that a person may randomly stumble upon the money so he has to hide it in a place where he can keep watch and protect the money from getting stolen and that place is the house.

That's why Fujisawa is locked up in his house despite claiming that the money is in his safety deposit. At 1st he went outside of his home here and there but after being under the surveillance of Akiyama and Nao for too long he stopped going outside,  scared  that  they  might  actually  have  a way to steal  the  money.  So  Akiyama  and  Nao  continue  to keep the pressure on Fujisawa.

Observing the house from outside, Akiyama builds a map of the house by guessing. To have a sort of idea on where he might keep the safe box. This was done to please Nao concerns, making her think that they are making some progress. This is followed by the next step of his plan.

By day 10 the fear and anxiety has fully gotten to Fujisawa, making him unable to sleep or even get a proper rest as he has slept for 3 days.

This is where the 3rd part of the plan comes into play. Akiyama and Nao team up which further lightens Nao concerns as they send smoke through the vent outside the fujisawa house and use a radio/speakers for the fire alarm to create the effect of the house getting burned down to observe fujisawa action which was.

* He barely tries to locate the source of fire.

* He didn't call the fire department but his friend Tanaka to locate the source of the fire.

* Despite being in a state where he thought the house was burning he didn't leave the room he was sitting in. So Akiyama figures he hid the money in the room they are currently standing in.

This was devastating for Fujisawa as from his perspective, while Akiyama and Nao did announce that they were going to steal the 200 million and that they had a great plan to pull of this theft. The location of 200 million was still hidden, which was one of the only sense of security Fujisawa had in his chaotic mind as there was a lingering possibility that Akiyama and Nao was bluffing about it as logically speaking they doesn't who where the money is so the heist is impossible to pull off.

But Akiyama completely destroys that piece of security providing his deduction to fujisawa that now he knows where fujisawa had kept the money. Saying he knew the money was hidden in the house from the beginning but the problem was where? As fujisawa didn't run off to different rooms when he thought the house was on fire meaning he didn't hide the money separately in different rooms only on the room they are currently standing on.

The lethal deduction breaks fujisawa on his emotions as he once again goes crazy letting out all of the emotions that had been built up till now, getting cocky, shouting out loud revealing the safe box himself thinking even if they know where the money is they can't do nothing as it's locked inside. This was the final piece of security in chaotic mind of Fujisawa.

Akiyama  confidently  replies that he can  easily  take  the money  out  of  that  safe  box.  Fujisawa  thinks  it's  just  a  bluff  but  Akiyama  once  again  replies with confidence  that  he  can  steal  it  pretty  easily. This completely shatters Fujisawa's burst of emotion and sense of security.

As Akiyama fully utilizes the effect of a psychological state called cognitive dissonance, a psychological term defining moments where you do things not because not because you want to do it but because everyone does it. Putting you in an awkward position where if you do different things than others you feel alienated like you don't belong in the group so you just do whatever others do to feel like you belong.

Fujisawa with his burst of emotion and the reveal of safe box expected Akiyama to give up on stealing after he showed them the safe but to his surprise akiyama didn't flinch at the revelation at all which made  him  think  akiyama  actions  isn't  predictable and  is  like  an  alien  to  him  making  him  more anxious  thinking  of  different  way  that  akiyama might just be able to steal the money. Locking himself to the room constantly guarding it detaching himself completely from outside world and thought. Only thinking of ways to stop Akiyama and Nao.

But this doesn't just shatter Fujisawa's final piece of safety and confidence but also Nao's. With the money being locked inside there is no way to steal it.

The little hope Nao had garnered teaming up with Akiyama was fading in front of her eye as yeah now they know the location of the money with Akiyama's clever plan but there is nothing they can do about it. and Akiyama is still keeping her on a blimp not telling the full scope of his plan. Heck for her she doesn't even know whether Akiyama has a plan or not at this point to steal the money from the safe box.

He didn't tell Nao about his plans since if he told about it to her then there would be no tension created so fujisawa might suspect that something is wrong considering how distrustful he is, Akiyama had to break that distrust and Nao was the means to break it. So Nao had to also be under great pressure for Fujisawa to completely buy on him winning the game.

4days left fujisawa receives the message from the Liar game office on when the game will end.

Time closes in nao is losing hope more and more as she has no idea about Akiyama's plan and Fujisawa is gaining  more  and  more  hope  nearing  the  end  of the game  and  finally  the  game  is  over  and  an  officer comes  by  to  take  the  money  but  after  an  hour  3 officers  come  by  to  take  the  money  where  it  is revealed  that  the  1st  officer  who  came  by  was none other than Akiyama in disguise.

Due to Nao's raw emotions of utter despair and hopelessness Fujisawa didn't second think about the situation immediately thinking he won, meeting his demise at the end. Nao is able to play the 100 million back and win the remaining 100 million.
