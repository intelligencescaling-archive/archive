Explaining SCD Characters- Harimoto (Liar Game)

In SCD, Harimoto is often seen as beyond a joke. Which is interesting, because when we first meet Harimoto in the Liar Game manga, he's built - up to possibly be the smartest character in the whole - story.

We are first introduced to Harimoto just after the halfway - point of the entire - manga. After having multiple battles between Akiyama and Yokoya, things are shaken - up once - more with the addition of a third major faction. Fukunaga, one of the allies of Nao and Akiyama (the main - characters), claims she was just beaten by Harimoto (although she insists it was a narrow - margian). Nevertheless, she claims he may have the ability to take - down both Akiyama AND Yokoya.

Harimoto is the leader of a cult, where he has brought 3 of his most - loyal - followers into the Liar Game with him. Makimoto, Abe, and Kimura.

As soon as Musical - Chairs starts, Harimoto prices himself to potentially be ahead of even Akiyama and Yokoya, by using a black - handkerchief to absorb sunlight and see what's in his preview - envelope before anyone is even allowed to open - them.

Kimura, one of his followers, also manages to dupe Akiyama, in a way that hadn't really been seen up until that point. While Akiyama is quick to realize he had been tricked, and swiftly recovers without much - difficulty, the fact that he was tricked - at - all proved Harimoto and his followers were formidable - foes.

Musical - Chairs is a fantastic 3 - way battle between Akiyama, Yokoya, and Harimoto. However, Harimoto turns - out to be the weakest of the few, and ends - up losing, not even to Akiyama, but to Yokoya (although Akiyama calling him a pest and refusing to work - with - him because of his attitude towards other - people didn't exactly do him any favors). Considering Yokoya was actually put in the least advantageous position out of the three main - factions speaks - volumes to how Harimoto never really stood a chance against either Yokoya or Akiyama.

Despite this masterful - arc, Harimoto would only appear in the following - arc, Bid - Poker. While not nearly as good as Musical - Chairs, it is a fun arc with several - laughs... But that's the thing. While Harimoto and his group Do manage to stir - the - pot and cause quite a bit of trouble for Akiyama and Nao... Their plans are a little.... - Odd. And they allow Akiyama to exploit and/or take - advantage of them in such - a - way that they practically humiliate them. Peaking at Akiyama using Harimoto ranting about his literal: "Spirit," intervening with the Game, as a reason to literally lock Harimoto in the bathroom until further - notice.

Now, you may be wondering- Why is Kimura so highly - represented in SCD but not Harimoto?

Well, that's the thing. It's revealed in the middle of this arc that Kimura actually has essentially been pupeteering Harimoto around his cult, in a very - similar fashion to how many people believe a certain political conspiracy is acting up around these days. Essentially, most of Harimoto's previous - feats, were now being handed from Harimoto to Kimura, as Nao manages to deduce that Kimura is the True leader of the cult, having influenced Harimoto to do as she pleases. (Akiyama then reveals he actually deduced this a while - ago and didn't think it was necessary to bring - up until that - point).

Once this reveal occurs, Harimoto becomes more of a Background - Character, with Kimura doing most of the work and preparing to crush Akiyama... Only for the rug to be pulled out from under her feet and- Surprise - Surprise- Akiyama Wins!

Harimoto then abandons his declaration that Nao and Akiyama were demons and says they truly were good - people and he was being a prick.

Harimoto and his cult then voluntarily drop - out of the Game (when honestly they could have been useful to Nao and Akiyama if they stayed to help them take - down Yokoya, but whatever), and leave the manga never to be seen again, only for SCD fandom to mock Harimoto as a fraud until the End - of - Time.

And That was Harimoto. A character introduced to us as having so much potential. Yet, after being defeated by Yokoya (who was in an incredibly disadvantageous - position), and then Akiyama and Nao, Harimoto was destined to always be seen as one of the lesser of the impressive characters in Liar Game. The reveal that he was actually been pupeteered around by Kimura, One of his supposed followers, was the final blow that was needed to completely demolish his reputation as an SCD character. Ah, well.
