Breakdown of Yokoya's Reasoning and EQ/ToM

Contraband Game is probably Yokoya's most analysed feat and is very much the feat people use while trying to scale Yokoya high in scheming, foresight and manipulation.
So instead I am going to analyse the chains of reasoning along with the Theory of Mind/EQ aspect of day 1, enjoy.

**Before the game began**

P1 Contraband is basically a game of smuggling money from the north country into a third country to protect the income

P2 The money not smuggled by a country will be seized by the opposing country

P3 The 2 teams take turns as smugglers and Inspectors to play the game

P4 The total amount to smuggle into the country is 300 million, with 100 million existing in the player’s account by default , the players are required to return the 400 million to the liar game at the end.

P5 the smugglers have to smuggle any amount that is 100 million or below.

P6
\>Inspector has to attempt to guess a higher value equal to or higher than the amount of money which if guessed the amount the smuggler was attempting to smuggle will be transferred to the inspector.
\>If the inspector guesses a value and the inspector hasn't smuggled the inspector must pay an indemnity of half the amount smuggled.
\>If the Inspector guesses a lower value than the amt smuggled the smuggler gets the smuggled amount + the indemnity fee.

P7 The winner of contraband is decided by who is able to smuggle more money from the opposing country into a 3rd country.

P8 The winner of the game will have to pay 50% of their winnings in order to be able to leave the Liar game and also pay off their debts.

C1 (from P5 and P6) The Inspector has a choice to simply attempt to figure out whether the smuggler is carrying money and doubt 100 million to prevent any smuggling attempts.

C2 The smuggler can utilize a low risk strategy of not smuggling at all preventing any loss but opening themselves up to gain indemnities via failed guesses.

C3 (From P2,P7,P8) This shows that the true method to actually make money in the contraband game will be by Losing the game on paper but making money by increasing the money into your inside account.

C4 (from P4 and C3) there is a high chance that the initial rounds would result in no progress being made and a chicken game would ensue.

P9 The ATMs have no withdrawal limits and the cards can also be used to deposit money into an ATM.

C5 (based on P8 and C4) it is possible to envision a scenario where when a player from one country empties their entire ATM and stashes it somewhere so that a traitor can deposit the money into their ATM making the value of their money in the other countries bank 0 and their internal bank value increases allowing them to make money without winning the game.

P10 The smugglers and inspectors are granted a certain amount of time and privacy in the room with each other when it's their turn to smuggle and inspect.

C6 This allows him to secretly negotiate with one member of the opposing team with whom he can collude and can trade information about the money smuggled

P11  Kikuzawa was deeply influenced by Yokoya's authority during their childhood and possesses his own desire to exercise authority over others.

C7 Kikuzawa will almost definitely go along with his plan.

C8 (From P11 and C7) Kikuzawa will get high over his power and will mistreat his teammates due to his position as their saviour.
