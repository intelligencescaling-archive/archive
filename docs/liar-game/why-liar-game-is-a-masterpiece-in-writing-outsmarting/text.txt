Why Liar Game is a masterpiece in Writing Outsmarting.

Most Fictional Stories You read Which are mind Game based Stories or have Some Sort of mind games involved in the Plot Would definitely make these Mistakes.

You need to remember the most important thing when Writing a smart character, The Audience needs to feel Why the character is smart, not because you said so.

1. Conclusive Reliance.

In Most Fictional Stories The character often Relies on Intuitive Guesses, Like baku madarame, Sherlock Holmes, L Lawliet etc.

What we need to understand is that We can't remove Ambiguity from information entirely because then You aren't trying to outsmart someone you are just playing chess, but even So if the information is Ambiguous The conclusion the character comes to shouldn't feel Reckless but the most logical from there perspective.

And The best way to portray this is by Making inductive Reasoning the Characters Strongest point, For Example take the Clairvoyance induction from contraband, Akiyama doesn't just trust one Stream of info but when Several Streams of info Pointed towards the same Conclusion Without Contradicting each other only then he Trusted his Conclusion.

In most Outsmarting Scenarios We often see the main Character rely too much on Intuition and Guesses whereas there opponents often make the most Logical Judgement, This is a fatal Flaw When writing outsmarting scenarios, Why?

Simply because the Audience Can't see the character as smart if they themselves can point out several Leaps Of logic and Multiple logical Errors, The audience feels like The Author is trying to force them into thinking intuition is the smarter way when it's the exact opposite.

Whenever You write An outsmarting matchup Try your best to give your character the most Hints, And make sure those hints aren't predicted by there opponent without making the opponent seem dumb.

2. Info is never Truly Hidden.

If I asked you why a character gets Deceived in any Story And You had to answer in 2 words, the most likely conclusion would be Hidden info.

But what writer's don't understand is that hidden info doesn't make the MC Smarter nor The Opponent dumber, and This is That aspect Where Liar games destroy's other fictional stories.

In Liar game all characters operate under the same constraints, All characters are always aware of the rules as well.

Therefore if information is hidden it isn't physically impossible for the opponent to Deduce it, For example the Tape recorder trap in Contraband, it was always possible for Yokoya to come to the Conclusion that Hasegawa Could have been made to Switch Sides using the tape recorder method because Yokoya is also Aware of them, making the assumption that Akagi Could Have leaked the info regarding the revorders is not am illogical assumption.

This is just one Example but Liar game is Filled with Such Examples, And this is exactly Why a character like Yokoya actually feels Dumber than Akiyama on Screen, and this is why Even When Yokoya's nature and weaknesses were consistent throughout the Story, us as audience often misjudge his Mistakes as nerfs, and this is the Sign of Good Character Writing.

3. When a character gets outsmarted it Makes sense Why, Without giving the Smarter character any other advantages except his intelligence.

Very often in other Stories The character Actually has Advantages over His opponents That the opponent either has no idea about or Couldn't do anything about them.

For example Light being able to write Exact Scenarios To help himself and Miss get Cleared in front of the Task Force, The Death note Aided Light a lot the Same way.

On the other hand in liar game a character only gets outsmarted in 2 ways.

1. The Smarter character manages to Hide a Secret action behind something an opponent Already expected.

Take the Forced Rotation Trap for Example, After Clairvoyance was Exposed they all believed Yokoya had no moves, that's why their Rotation became predictable and Yokoya Took advantage of that opportunity to Crush South.

And this Approach for the Character to Get outsmarted makes the most Sense, because the reader can Always judge why the Character got outsmarted, he doesn't think or believe the character was nerfed just because he didn't doubt info he already expected.

2. The Dumber character Doesn't manage to think of the same Information in a different way, but the Smarter one does.

This is Liar game's main Approach to outsmarting, The smarter Character always sees Something in the same info the Dumber character fails to, and that's actual outsmarting by definition.

Take The ATM card replacement trap for example, Yokoya fails to think that the Card can be replaced Without a Scratch and makes the assumption that the Card wasn't replaced.

Akiyama or Yokoya don't outsmart a character because they have hidden info Resources or advantages, they Outsmart because they can predict how their opponent would think of the same information, then they think of something else Which Counters their opponents original plan while Misguiding The opponent to Continuously Drown themselves in What they thought earlier, You lose because you didn't doubt, and that's the main theme of the manga. People should be Doubted.

4. You don't just outsmart the opponent, You outsmart the Audience themselves.

And this is Where Liar game becomes a masterpiece, You as a reader Always have the necessary info needed to See through the Trap, not just you but the opponent does as well.

And when Both the Opponent and the reader Fail to see through the Trap Despite having the necessary hints to deduce it, you actually feel the Character is smarter, That's how the best Outsmarting Stories are written.
