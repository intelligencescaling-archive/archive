Rules of the match:
1. A match between 4 different kingdoms.   four rooms for 4 kingdoms with each room having a tower with 100 life points.

   2. These life points can be used to attack other kingdoms.

   3. The purpose is simple. Destroy other kingdoms.

   4. 300 million to each player. They have to return that 300 million by the end of the game.

   5. Those whose kingdom falls, all of the money from that kingdom will transfer to the winner. With the game going on until only 1 kingdom is left.

   6. A general will be chosen that represents their kingdom. Who will be the one to execute attacks to bring others down.

   7. A tablet is given to the general where he can input his commands to either attack or defend.

   8. If you attack another kingdom you will lose 1 life point and the kingdom that got attacked will lose 3 life points.

   9. If you defend yourself it will cost 1 life point that way you won't lose 3 life points, 2 life points considering you lose 1 defending.

   10. When you defend yourself you will lose 1 life point. It doesn't matter if you haven't been attacked.

   11. To put it simply the game is about making your enemy perish before yourself.

   12. 6 possible strategic combinations on whether to attack other 3 kingdoms or defend yourself from other 3 kingdoms.

   13. You are limited to using 3 life points each turn.

   14. Each turn you will get 20 minutes to decide and plan out on whether to attack or defend yourself from other kingdoms. Or You can just not attack or defend at all if you wish to.

   15.

      16. Before the 20 minutes planning time runs out the general must enter the tower and put command. If they didn't it would count as the kingdom having to plan.

      17. In a case where 2 kingdoms fall simultaneously. For eg a kingdom has 1 life point another has 3 life points. The kingdom spent that 1 life point attack so they are 0 now will count as falling 1st and the kingdom who got attacked losing 3 life points will count as falling second.

      18. The general who inputs their command 1st will be the 1st one to execute the action.

      19. Each kingdom will have a boss who will choose who will be the General of their kingdom.  The boss will also manage their team overall.

      20. The boss cannot choose his or herself as general. In a case of that he or she will have to give their boss role to others only then they can be general.

      21. For the position of boss itself a preliminary round of the record of the 4 kingdoms will be held among the team itself. The one who comes out on top will be the boss.
