Akiyama's AC is better than Baku's.

Akiyama is criminally underestimated for his adversity capacity. I think this is due to people not knowing what AC really means.

Most people think AC is how calm you seem.

If this is the case then Baku wins. He is well known to have risked his life countless times. Willingly risking Drowning in Air Poker. Risking dying via STL Drug penalty. Deliberately losing first STL (even though it could have killed him). Playing abandoned building game with superhuman weapon trying to kill him. Risking hanging in Hangman. There is no need to explain who Baku is to anyone who has read Usogui.

But AC is not this.

It's how well you can handle stress and not let it affect your decisions.

When judging based on this Akiyama is slightly better than Baku.

Baku only has 1 life.

All of these crazy things he does are putting himself at risk. Drowning. Hanging. Drug penalty. Losing STL. If he gets it wrong he dies. It is crazy.

Akiyama has 56 lives.

For 66 days straight.

Not just wanting to save people. He had 7 different goals he was trying to achieve in EVERY game. If he messed up even 1 goal it would all fall apart.

1. Extracting as much money as possible from the game. Even refusing to give back the money given to him in the beginning of each game. He was continuously playing to drain the Liar Game office.

2. Getting as much debt as possible. He ended up having 1.1 Billion Yen on his head. And was prepared to get to 3.05 Billion in the final game. He deliberately did this. If the debt is all on 1 person it can't be collected. Which is why the whole NPL plan works.

3. Save as many players as possible. Not just Nao. All players. Even those who teamed up to make Nao lose. He saved 56 confirmed players. Many more if you include the other tournaments that were cancelled due to his actions.

4. Nao was constantly risking her life. After saving her she kept entering revival rounds. Bid Poker. Human Auction. Akiyama was trying to get her out of it. Get her debt to 0 so she would leave. But she kept coming back. So he had to save her and everyone else.

5. Lose the main round. In all main games he was deliberately losing so he could enter the revival round. He had to be good enough to control the whole game but still lose at the perfect time.

6. Win the revival round at all costs. If he lost the revival round the whole plan would fail. The revival round was life or death.

7. Make sure the main round winner made 0 profit. So they couldn't quit. He had to make sure whoever won didn't actually make any money. If they quit they would be allow LGT to make money and in effect everyone else would accumulate more debt. He had to make sure everyone was stuck in the system until he could save everyone.

Try keeping 7 goals in your head whilst playing against Yokoya. Harimoto. Someone with superhuman eyes who can literally track cards. And organisers changing the rules mid tournament.

This is what Akiyama did. For 66 days. In every game.

Also Baku chose to take on adversity. He entered Air Poker. Abandoned building. Accepted to play the hangman game. He decided to risk his life.

Akiyama had no choice.

Akiyama had to fulfill all those goals not to escape but to face a fate worse than death. Baku played to win but Akiyama played to die.

In every game Akiyama faced immense adversity just to fulfill his goals. In Round I he had to risk theft after his prison sentence was just finished. In minority rule he had to face fukunaga influencing Every players decision. By Revival I Nao was in last place and everyone had teamed up to make her lose. Yet he still wanted to save them.

In the Contraband game he didn't even know Yokoya and Kikuzawa were working together. He didn't know Yokoya ran his whole country like a dictatorship whilst his team wanted to vote. His own teammate betrayed him. 3 of his players fell into Yokoya's ATM trap. Basically guaranteeing defeat. Yokoya captured his 3 Northern allies. Forced 1 to talk. From this point Yokoya knew Akiyama's plan. Yokoya had control of all ATM cards. Tested if Akiyama was bluffing. Despite this Akiyama managed to come back using Yokoya's own tape recorders against him.

In Revival II he was up against Kikuchi who could track cards mid shuffle. Always getting the Joker. Basically invincible in a 17 card game. Kikuchi was ahead for almost the whole game. Akiyama literally panicked. This was used against him. "see, he panicked, Baku wouldn't panic."

But this is the point. He panicked but his decisions improved. He came back. Worked out where the cards originally were. Shuffled 8 times. Predicted Kikuchi's hand. Pretended his hand was rubbish so Kikuchi would over bet. He felt the panic but it didn't affect him at all. This is AC.

In the Pandemic qualifier Yokoya made it a personal vendetta. He didn't just wanted to win. He wanted Nao to lose as he knew it would hurt Akiyama more than anything. 2 players he just saved refused to contact his team. Yokoya banned his minions from contacting Nao. Despite this Akiyama somehow managed to get everyone qualified by blocking the check rooms so Yokoya couldn't be sure of his team.

In Musical Chairs Harimoto and Yokoya (the 2 smartest players) teamed up to get rid of him. 1 of his own guys betrayed him in the elections. Baldy threatened to expose his chair numbers. Akiyama used this as an opportunity to control the whole game. He deliberately lost. A rubbish medal player won so he could give everyone medals.

In Bid Poker he could have easily won. Harimoto returned 1 deck. 2 players passed. Everyone was happy. But Akiyama said no. He forced Harimoto to betray him. Most players became pigs. But he could still win first place with a straight flush. Pay everyone's debt and get them all out. If he miscalculated by 1 card everyone who lost couldn't pay and would go to the black market. He knew this. But still did it.

In Human Auction Yokoya bought Nao. His most loyal companion was now his enemy in the final game. All other players were trying to save up to buy Akiyama as owning him was valuable. He was in 19th bid. Last place. But he still manipulated everyone to bid on players they didn't want. And forced the 3 richest players to be eliminated so the poor players could survive.

In the final game the organisers introduced a completely new system. They took the money from Human Auction. Losers had to pay 300 million (Something they had never done before). Total debt was 5.5 Billion. Winner only got 4.6 Billion. It was mathematically impossible to win. They did this specifically to stop his plan. But he still forced a draw. By making the last 2 kingdoms end on 1 life point in the same round. The game couldn't decide a winner. No one lost. Tournament was cancelled. Everyone was free.

Baku accepted he "could" die.

Akiyama accepted he "would definitely" be tortured and killed if he succeeded. From Round 1 he knew this. He had the idea even in round 1 that LGT would Sell the Losers unable to pay their debts back. Losers would go to military weapon testing and human experiments. His life would be collected. But he still entered the final game.

And he did it for people he hated.

This is why he is better than Baku. Akiyama's mom committed suicide after getting into debt. Nao reminded him of his mom. So he got involved. But he didn't just save Nao. He used it as motivation. To see the same type of people he hated. Tricksters. Liars. People who wouldn't hesitate to trick someone. As victims of a greater evil. The LGT. The people who betrayed him. Stole decks. Refused to help. Teamed up to make Nao lose. Those were the people he saved. He didn't just save people he loved. He saved people he had every reason to hate. And was willing to face a fate worse than death for them.

Baku's AC was "I'm going to risk my life."

Akiyama's AC was "I want to save people" Accept that I will be tortured and killed if I win. Carry 56 peoples lives and 7 impossible goals for 66 days. Whilst my own allies betray me and organisers change the rules to stop me. Save people I hate as I know they are victims. My decisions will not be affected.

This is why he is underestimated. People look at Baku and see him drowning. Hanging. Drugging. Superhuman trying to kill him. It's obvious.

Akiyama's adversity is hidden. He had debt. Betrayals. 7 conflicting goals. Deliberately losing. Winning revival rounds you have to win. Making sure winners make 0 profit. Whilst knowing if he succeeded he would be tortured.

Baku is the king of individual, obvious adversity.

Akiyama is the king of long term, forced, hidden adversity. Which is harder to cope with without your decisions being affected.

This is why Akiyama is slightly better than Baku.
