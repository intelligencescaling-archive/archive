Explaining SCD Characters- Liar Game

General Spoilers ahead, but any Major Spoilers will be given an Extra Warning-

Akiyama- Akiyama is introduced to the story at the end of Chapter 1. Despite what someone who hasn't read the story might think, Akiyama is actually not the main protagonist, but is instead a deuteragonist. The story is mostly told from Nao's perspective, but Akiyama sticks around her for every single Game. Akiyama is high tier for a reason, as his strategies to win every Game are very out of the box and occasionally very complex as well, with Contraband Game, 17 Poker, Pandemic Game, Bid Poker, and Record of the Four Kingdoms tending to invoke strategies that require insane mental arithmetic, that a normal human would just not be able to pull off on the fly even if they knew exactly what to do.

MAJOR SPOILERS- >!Despite his background as a conartist, Akiyama Is not a villain in disguise. He remains loyal to Nao and her cause for helping people until the end. It is explained to Nao by a Liar Game Agency Member that Akiyama Has a tragic background where his mother was swindled out of her money and offered herself in order to give Akiyama her life insurance money, which he used to go to college and study Psychology, earning most of his Skills. His actions as a: 'conartist,' involve dismantling the pyramid scheme that his mother fell victim to. Akiyama Most likely could have prevented himself from going to prison, but ultimately, he figured his mission was done once the pyramid scheme was dismantled, and he figured the 5-year prison sentence was a short price to pay for avenging his mother. It is heavily implied that he sees Nao as a reincarnation of his mother, which is why he goes out of his way to keep helping.!<

Yokoya- Yokoya is the main antagonist of Liar Game, but doesn't show up until the 4th Game, in the Contraband Game. While appearing to be physically weak, Yokoya is quite wealthy due to his family background, and uses his money and overall ruthless tactics to manipulate people into doing whatever he wants. He is not afraid to create incredibly toxic environments for people, and ultimately has little to no regard for the emotional pain that he causes people. He ultimately sees Winning the Liar Game as the ultimate trophy for himself, and believes he will have made his Father proud if he Wins. He despised Akiyama and Nao with equal passion, believing them to be direct threats to his strategic ability, and his overall ideology respectively. Everything Yokoya does is for his own benefit, with the very rare times he shows empathy for Players being implied to be him simply playing the long game in maintaining their faith in him so that he can continue using them for future Games.

Kimura-

MAJOR SPOILERS- >!Kimura is a follower of Harimoto's cult. When she is first introduced, it is believe that she is simply one of the three main followers we see in the Liar Game. However, we eventually find out that she is in fact The mastermind. While she doesn't technically hold the leadership position of the cult, she had successfully controlled Harimoto through her own manipulative schemes. She still remains loyal to Harimoto And ultimately respects his toxic cause, however, it is implying that most of the strategies that his cult came up with throughout the Games were actually her ideas. This is why she is considered the third Smartest in her Verse.!<

Fukunaga-

MAJOR SPOILERS- >!Fukunaga Is the first real Smart antagonist of Liar Game, appearing in Minority Rule. Many people are confused by her gender identity, and to be fair, it seems that the author didn't really decide on anything until halfway through the story. When she is first introduced, she believes to be just a regular girl, however the big reveal of Minority Rule is that she is actually the person that Akiyama and Nao were looking for, that they believed was a guy. She takes off her wig, revealing that she managed to disguise herself as a guy due to her gender fluid nature. Akiyama however saw through it all, although he didn't see through her disguise. For the next 2 Arcs, Fukunaga two keeps her wig off, And even says directly to Nao in the Contraband Game that she's: 'talking to a guy.' However, in Revival Round 2, she has returned to a full female appearance, declaring that it is simply easier to manipulate male Opponents this way. She maintains this female appearance for the rest of the story, and her gender is rarely brought up. In a random first panel of Chapter 66, Fukunaga is interviewed by the author about her gender identity, where she firmly declares that she is a woman both in body and in mind. Oddly enough however, the rest of the characters continue to refer to her with: 'he/him,' pronouns, or simply: 'That Person.' It's also revealed that Fukunaga has a crush on Akiyama, although this plot thread ultimately goes nowhere. Fukunaga Is the main antagonist for the first two Arcs she appears in, two however, she slowly warms up to Akiyama and Nao's side, especially when threats like Yokoya or Harimoto and his cult prove too much for her to handle by herself. By the end of the story, she has completely adopted Nao's mindset and ultimately redeems herself entirely as a new Hero. Despite her Often not being mentioned in SCD, her strategies are nothing to scoff at, and in early days of SCD, it was often debated if characters like Yuichi or Ayanokoji would easily be able to Pass Arcs where she acted as the Protagonist. In later Arcs, Fukunaga is implied To essentially just be one step down from the big three Players, and she often manages to give insight into what's going on, although it's also implied that Akiyama can easily figure this stuff out by himself, however Fukunaga is just around to bring it up when he's busy. In the J-Drama, Fukunaga is a man Who simply put on a disguise as a girl in his first Arc. J-Drama Fukuanaga remains much more selfish than his manga counterpart, continuing to betray and abandon Akiyama and Nao despite helping them when it is necessary or he is ultimately not at risk. In the Taboo Game against Yokoya, he manages to put up quite the Fight!!<

Leronira- Leronira Is rarely talked about, mainly because he's not an actual Player in the Liar Game. He is simply a moderator, who appears from Contraband Game onward in order to explain to the reader the complex strategies of Akiyama, Yokoya, and Harimoto In more detail. He doesn't break the fourth wall, but he simply explains to his fellow moderators what the characters are thinking. Despite not managing to pull off any genuine feats of his own, his ability to predict and understand the strategies of Akiyama, Yokoya, and Harimoto puts him quite high in SCD Scaling. In fact, seeing a showdown between him and Akiyama has the potential to be even closer than Akiyama VS Yokoya, as Leronira Often shows incredible predictive power, often being able to predict what Akiyama is planning before Yokoya can despite his access to Cameras not giving him much of an Advantage.

Nao- Nao is the main protagonist of Liar Game and the loyal follower, helper, and moral spectator of Akiyama. She starts out in the story of hearing incredibly weak and helpless, however manages to get more confident as the story progresses. She never manages to become Smart enough to fight Harimoto or Yokoya, or stand as an equal to Akiyama, However, she manages to evolve into a character that understands the other Players better than anyone, all while refusing to lose her faith in Trusting Others. In Stationary Roulette, Nao ends up playing a Game all without Akiyama's help and manages to Win through her own, relatively simple strategy.

MAJOR SPOILERS- >!Nao's Strategy is technically with Fukuanaga's help, And it's ultimately her deception that manages to make the strategy work, it is implied that it was entirely Nao that came up with it.!<

Harimoto- Harimoto is introduced as a character that could potentially rival both Akiyama and Yokoya. However, he ultimately ends up losing to both of them. Nevertheless, his strategies are quite impressive. At the beginning of Musical Chairs, he manages to peek into a letter using a black cloth that absorbs most of the light. A strategy neither Akiyama nor Yokoya attempt, nor do they find out about it at any point throughout Liar Game.

MAJOR SPOILERS- >!It Is eventually revealed that while Harimoto while it's the leader of his cult, it was ultimately Kimura that was pulling the strings, manipulating Him, and coming up with the strategies. This is why many people consider him a: 'Fraud,' and barely ever mention him in SCD.!<
