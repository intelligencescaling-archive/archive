Akiyama Shinichi (Liar Game Manga) VS The Protagonist (Tenet) in Strategy

The Protagonist doc: [https://docs.google.com/document/d/1uuIf41aczG69PHssCZ0lNoxvGFc0I-cqrkbcRxryx7w/edit?usp=sharing](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1uuIf41aczG69PHssCZ0lNoxvGFc0I-cqrkbcRxryx7w/edit?usp=sharing)

**\[ Strategy Building \]: The Protagonist Mid Diff**
Reasons: The foundation of the Tenet organization and it's mission showcases better Strategy Building due to the sheer complexity and scope of the organization/mission and the circumstances and limited intel The Protagonist was working with as he did it.

**\[ Strategy Efficiency \]: The Protagonist Low Diff**
Reasons: The Opera Siege strategy was crucial as a foundation on the road to victory for The Protagonist and he was able to execute such a multifaceted, layered scheme with only the bare minimal amount of moves and the most half-baked contingency ever. He really didn't need to go beyond what he did

**\[ Strategy Logistics \]: The Protagonist Low Diff**
Reasons: The Opera Siege strategy alone involved the manipulation of two foreign nations, two different intelligence bureaus and it was up against someone who readily had access to a Turnstile (a device capable of reversing the flow of time for a person or object). The field that The Protagonist operated in is several orders of magnitude more complex than Akiyama's and this is just for the very first step in his plan

**\[ Strategy Shielding \]: The Protagonist No Diff**
Reasons: The Protagonist was able to shield his entire life's mission not only from everyone alive on Earth, but from everyone alive centuries into the future as well. That alone would be enough to No Diff Akiyama in this subcategory in Proven Ability and yet it doesn't get much better in Methodology. The Faked Death ploy, Sator's Manipulation strategy and Priya's Manipulation are all enough to get TP safely above Akiyama in this regard

**\[ Strategy Forecasting \]: The Protagonist Mid-High Diff**
Reasons: I'm not sure if it can be counted as true forecasting if you already had prior knowledge of your strategies path to success. But since there had to be an original Protagonist who started the bootstrap timeloop to begin with, we can reason that the Bootstrap Creation was definitely apart of the plan all along and would certainly No Diff Akiyama in this regard but since this is entirely conjecture, Mid-High diff is the safe option. The Opera Siege stuff would once again is still enough for TP to take this subcategory as well but Akiyama's ROT4K is not *that* far behind

**\[ Strategy Complexity \]: The Protagonist Very Low Diff**
Reasons: Stalsk-12 alone is enough let alone the Opera Siege or Tallinn strategies. I have nothing more to say other than just telling y'all to read the doc as an explanation as to why TP's strategies are vastly more complex than Akiyama's.

**\[ Strategy Fortitude \]: The Protagonist Low Diff**
Reasons: The Protagonist creating a whole ass timeloop that gave each past incarnation of himself a clear road to victory is basically as fortitude as your strategy could get. He not only guaranteed the success of his plan once, but perpetually for all of time as past TP's become future TP's and the cycle begins again, guaranteeing that Posterity will never ever succeed in their plan to destroy the past. This doesn't even mention the various other manipulations and tactics he sprinkled throughout the whole mission that just further guaranteed the success of the plan but I think creating a timeloop after the success of your strategy so it can *keep* succeeding is more than enough

**\[ OVERALL: The Protagonist Low-Mid Diffs (-) \]**
