I suggest you to read my previous [post](https://www.reddit.com/r/IntelligenceScaling/comments/1l5fxep/my_analysis_of_fukunagas_strategy_in_the/) since it includes the introduction to the Downsizing, as well as Fukunaga's Strategy, before Akiyama arrived.

Manipulation is often controlling someone without their full awareness, which makes it really effective. Once they realize they're being manipulated, resistance grows. Which is why It’s harder to manipulate a group, especially if they're together. Akiyama easily manipulated Nao's teacher because he’s alone, but if he had allies, he might’ve resisted.

This is where “Desperation” comes into play and become the key to manipulating someone despite their awareness of the situation. When someone is desperate, they’ll willingly be manipulated to achieve their goal, even if they know it’s happening. Which is exactly what Akiyama did to all the players involved.

So, we all know that the last time, Fukunaga completely deceived Nao, and even won in the card game and that is when Akiyama arrived.

https://preview.redd.it/8gi8kz39tu9f1.png?width=790&format=png&auto=webp&s=6fd97258d284b73ddbe01dc3667e486e1eafed34

Akiyama then, completely saw through Fukunaga's tactic, showcasing his Mathematical Intelligence, as well as the ability to reason from the info he had.

Although the game seems to be 50/50, it is actually a trick because 50% of the time, it will be Joker and the card would be returned.

So, to counter this, Akiyama came up with a cheat, which is making a tiny dent to the side of the card. This seems like a basic cheat, but what impressed me was how Aki told Nao to execute it.

https://preview.redd.it/t2kbp50yyu9f1.png?width=744&format=png&auto=webp&s=32148d92d2b3210846a9505c57b544ceda305aa5

Rather than outright using the cheat to win from the start, Aki talked about keeping 2:1 Ratio, this is especially effective because someone like Fukunaga could see through Nao's cheat if Nao kept winning continuously. There was also the fact that Nao only marks the non-double sided card, so that Fukunaga would have no way to inspect the card to see any cheats, since the marked card would remain in the bag. With this, Nao was able to win the game and got her 10 votes.

Now, these 10 votes will be important for the next strategy of Akiyama to begin.

1. Nao approaches Kakuzawa & the others individually
2. Tries to garner sympathy by telling how Fukunaga rips her off with a cruel method.
(This helps to make Kakuzawa antagonistic towards Fukunaga)
3. Tells how Fukunaga will have an advantage in the next round, so Kakuzawa can have her money for him to win against Fukunaga
4. Talks about how there are multiple revival rounds, and that Nao needs to get his ten votes because the one who loses with highest vote would get revived
5. Talks about allying with Kakuzawa too, which motivates him even further.
6. The deal is closed, allowing Nao to get the ten votes.

This bit required careful planning, and also decent understanding of the human's psychology, their greed, as well as making proper logic behind the promises to convince the other party. Aki understood their desires well, playing on their ambitions, while making Nao frame her words to appear as though she was offering them a rare opportunity they couldn't afford to miss. Through this, Nao disguised her true intentions, making it seem like the alliance benefited both sides equally, when in reality, she was one step ahead due to the fact that she approached others as well.

With this, Nao managed to convince the others and get 80 votes.

Aki managed to convince them that paying him 70 million is the way to go since that would allow everyone involved to benefit. This way, Nao can avoid the Invalid Transaction Penalty.
