**Yokoya’s counter attack/Logical manipulation**

Yokoya after seeing through Akiyama’s trick comes up with a strategy to use it against him

First Yokoya makes the claim That Akiyama is deceiving everybody else and calls on to the fact that he is abandoning Akagi, This makes Nao to go and make Contact with Akagi to debunk Yokoya making her N+1 due to use of vaccine and Akagi Normal, then Yokoya Forces everyone else in the Rotation(Muscles, Akiyama ,Nao and Greese) to contact with Akagi making the overall score
Nao(N+2), Akiyama, Akagi, Muscles and grease(N+4).

Then Yokoya next claims that Akiyama said 2 other lies
The 2nd being that the pendulum reacts to the signals emmited by the wristwatch, (Yokoya can have easily inferred that Akiyama’s bluff involved the term signals since he was holding the pendulum in front of players wristwatch).
He does this by taking the pendulum from Akiyama and holding it in front of muscles(Who should be N+4) wristwatch and making the shorter wire move and when everyone is confused He makes the longer wire move.
After this he proceeds to explain why Akiyama was able to both Muscles and Greese’s identity as Normal Players by purposefully using Akagi as a scapegoat and giving him the cold shoulder to discourage the infected players to Identify themselves.

Then he also proceeds to explain why Akiyama was able to discern why Nao and Akagi were either normal or infected by first bringing to everyone’s attention that Nao and Akagi didn’t volunteer for the test but where forcefully chosen by Akiyama to take the test and then makes the claim that the 3 of them have been working together all along.
This is able to work perfectly in increasing the players distrust towards Akiyama and Nao since Akiyama’s early reveal of Yokoya teaming up with Fatso and Forelock caused the remining players to have a negative bias towards the existence of groups.

Yokoya next makes use of this mistrust by asking muscles and Greese how do they plan on continuing now that the swinging ball test has been debunked Both Greese and Muscles who are currently N+4 make the decision to not take any further part in the game due to them getting the necessary points to clear the game with this Yokoya is able to remove 2 of the 8 players who are confirmed to be Normal.

Using both Muscles and Greese’s lack of willingness to continue as definitive proof that they are N+4 and hence both Akiyama and Akagi must also be N+4 due to the contact sequence.
And given how Muscles and Grease acted after being N+4 Yokoya basically primed the remaining players into believing that N+4 players are unhelpful due to them not having any reason to contact.

Nao attempts to counteract this by claiming that she still is N+2 and hence will definitely want to make contact with other players to clear the game, Yokoya proceeds to debunk this by calling on to the players memory regarding Akagi being infected, and then says that Akagi’s actual status doesn't make any difference to Akiyama’s strategy since even if he was normal he can still behave as an outcast to defer the infected from contacting and If Akagi was never infected then Nao wouldn’t be N+2 but would be N+4 and would have also have the means to pass the game (This is Yokoya attributing Akiyama’s supposed swindling to the rest of the group to destroy both Nao and Akagi’s credibility).

This perfectly connects back to the reason why Yokoya made sure that Akagi would be saved by Nao (due to his existing psychoanalysis of her) and then Forced him to contact with the Rotation of the normal players to make sure that all of them reach N+4 making sure that Akiyama and Akagi had no leverage over muscles and greese so they could safely quit, and that Akiyama and Akagi were N+4 as well with Nao being either N+2 or N+4, with this Yokoya was able to completely shut out every other normal option from the other players via utilizing the concealment of Akiyama’s own strategy to

To increase the players distress Yokoya reminds the other players that in the scenario that Nao is N+4 there are 2 infected among them making it very difficult for them to proceed, after this Yokoya states that him and his crew are all N+2 and are still in need to gather 2 more points each and hence they will willingly accept any player regardless of their status to join their group.

As a final blow Yokoya states that if they have 10 seconds to join his team using the sense of urgency instilled by his statement of their being 2 potential infected causing them to make a run for it towards Yokoya and his group since their seemingly only viable option would shut down in 10 seconds.

TLDR: Yokoya after quickly seeing through Akiyama's bluff with minimal info and only observation and is then able to perfectly utilize the strategies own Nature and hidden concealments to ruin his credibility and manipulate Muscles and Grease into abstaining from participation and then Fabricate a hypothesis that Nao is normal +4 to take control of the narrative leaving his team as the only viable option and generating urgency causing them to rush to join Yokoya's team
