Before we get into the feat theres one thing id like to tackle which is the idea yokoya naturally is emotionally weak and therefore can be easily manipulated however this is simply not true, yokoya does have anti-feats i agree but to simply frame him as weak-minded is inconsiderate of his actual feats [(scan)](https://imgur.com/a/gqVco1R) for example look here in chapter 89, yokoya is currently in a very emotionally conflictive position, akiyama not only completely deduced and reversed his scheme unto him, which caused yokoya to feel a large form of contemptment and disdain towards everyone now under extreme pressure, which only would heighten after being threatened yokoya quite literally does not endow a single reaction, it's a small example but showcases that yokoya's mental fortitude and his ability to resist forms of emotional manipulation are quite good infact (he also preforms similar in bid poker so its not entirely inconsistent).

To get into the contents of the feat its a rather short one so the feat or moreso my analysis starts out with a delving into yokoya's backstory who from child has always looked up and aspired to be exactly like his father infact this ideology engraved within him has been not only his aspiration for years and years but also a result of his own fathers WILL and conditioning, yokoya from a young age has been conditioned by the person he admires the absolute most and his very own loved one to follow this ideology including methodology of training which further induces the depth of this ideology into yokoya's psyche [Scan](https://imgur.com/a/7cNUqhL)

So yokoya not only has a plethora of biases and emotional attachment to this ideology which has been engraved since near birth but he's been engaged with this ideology for even longer his life has literally been centered around it, so when challenged with a ideology of akiyama's which directly contradicts his entire life and notion yokoya is more than disgusted and appalled by such an attempt, yokoya then garners a sort of bias against akiyama he starts to hate him and nao and their ideology to the absolute extremes it becomes even motivation by this point that amongst his greater goals of becoming a leader he further engages with his hatred and conniving distaste for the people who dare follow an ideology his life has contradicted

Which leads to the contents of the feat we are finally here now that we are done with the context and what makes the feat SO good in my opinion, the feat starts out with yokoya approaching akiyama and expressing his outright denial of his victory claiming "you simply forced a tie lets say nothing more than that" this showcases yokoya's clinging to his former ideology he's not even remotely changed and he expresses it openly. He's the same yokoya he was before the only difference now being that theres a tie and that he's still engaging in this sense of otherwordly hatred and despise for akiyama which only actually heightened under the pressure of his plans success ([scan](https://imgur.com/a/CCJr7m5))

Yokoya further emphasizes my claim by continuing "to think you'd use the existence of a rich person among the players  in that way...failing to see it was indeed my oversight" which blame  shifts and showcases that yokoya's true belief is still present it wasnt akiyama and his ideology which earned the victory in his eyes no it was yokoya's oversight and his own inability which further emphasizes lack of change "it's not like your plan worked either, you were simply lucky that the moves the players did coincided with what your scheme required, the people gathered are good for nothings and one misstep could've easily lead to SHU or WU winning, the tie wasn't a result of your plan, it was dumb luck" here we see yokoya self-justifying an emergence of vigour to challenge the new circumstances yokoya's ideology his very will being endowed upon akiyama in a showcase of his sheer hatred towards akiyama and his ideology and a complete and utter lack of change in his words.

Akiyama quickly refutes with a laugh and a remark "you still dont get the fundamentals do you?" which sends yokoya into a state of confusion and inhibition, yokoya is confused and starts visually sweating, akiyama continues "the kanji for wei means entrusted to a demon, while for wo it means entrusted to a human, so i entrusted it to humans" this sends yokoya into a state of utter shock and confusion he's sweating here again and he's sent into a reflective position now wondering how akiyama's ideology of entrusting humans lead to this outcome, akiyama continues "a former teacher of mine, okabe-sensei, was always saying this to me, to believe people is to doubt them" and goes onto to quote his teacher ([scan](https://imgur.com/a/uWLjD6N))

This draws parallels with yokoya who himself is following an ideology and a belief from someone in his youth, though take a close look at what akiyama says next "i forsook his words for a long time but you know, meeting an honest to fault like kanzaki made me remember them" Akiyama isnt just drawing parallels to yokoyas situation, even further he's opening POSSIBILITIES, he's inducing a sense of familiarty and the idea of change and the idea that one can properly adjust their belief even if they formerly contradicted it to a precise level which sends yokoya into a further state of shock

He continues yet again "your right about these players all being good-for-nothing, they are weak, selfish, impatient and generally full of shit, but through her communication with them their heart began to understand" again inducing this idea into yokoya and further emphasizing the idea that people can change subtly letting him know that he himself has the capacity to change which again further induces the sheer shock into yokoya Akiyama then says "this is by no means dumb luck, nor is it a result of my great plan" completely blowing any idea yokoya had of the circumstance "this is the one move of trust that was only possible because of kanzaki's efforts to doubt people around her and under-stand them in their entirety, this is the victory of kanzaki inspite of everything she's been through"

This completely shatters yokoya's ideology and the induced confusion and doubt comes crashing down on him all at once he begans a state of reflective metacognition, thinking about everything that's gone on here and theres a visible change in his demanour he's unsure he's confused he's wondering how this possibility occured and the effects of akiyama's words are already deeply engraving themself into yokoya's mind even further than what his father has conditioned him for years the very person he loves and admires and aspires to be is currently being undone by akiyama [scan](https://imgur.com/a/W8qfDG0)

Then, after some consideration and not even a few seconds later, yokoya concedes he completely gives into the ideology and conceptualization of akiyama's words it IS kanzaki's victory and he will therefore support it and the very fact he did so means akiyama manages to completely overhaul the ideology that has been induced upon yokoya for years and years on end, you can see how yokoya feels relieved all the stress and shock and confusion and bitterness the desperation and hatred the fear and conniving distaste for akiyama and nao completely transformed he's composed now and akiyama has induced upon him a new ideology one which he completely and utterly despised before, the one person he hated managed to completely overhaul his ideology and alleviate ALL the previous biases he had before hand [scan](https://imgur.com/a/gfBNU2e)
