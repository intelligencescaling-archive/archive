A unecessarily full scale of the most weirdest matchup. Akiyama Shinichi vs Tokuchi Toua (both manga only)

**Hella hot take in the categories. I'll only add the explanations for the categories I find to be a hot take in.**

Intelligence: Akiyama extreme diffs

* Cognitive Abilities: Aki >= (Overall memorizations and variables in ROT4K and MC > Toua's microexpressions and adaptation on knuckleball in vs Bugaboos.)
* Knowledge Application: Toua >= (hot take but his three main strats and the replication of the knuckleball beats quality wise. But Aki has more quantity and overall more varied KA in psychology, philosophy, mathematics, disguising, physics and in his KA on the games he participated in.)
* Problem Solving: Aki >= (Another hot take ig. Toua's insanely good at this don't get me wrong on his continuous adaptability during each games against Fingers, New Marines, Bugaboos but Aki's continuous complexity rules in Liar Game makes me give Aki the slight edge)

Emotional Intelligence: Tokuchi very high diffs.

* Emotional Understanding: Toua (3 rookie awakening, dismantle of New Marines and psychology/profiling of Saikawa.)
* Emotional Perception: Toua
* Emotional Management: Aki (Hella hot take I know. But Toua, at least for me, utilizes his Mental Fortitude more than his Emotional Management. We never see him react at all. Aki, meanwhile, while more reactive, actually kept his calm and despite his fears, was able to manage them.)
* Emotional Engagement: Aki
* Emotional Facilitation: Toua
* Emotional Realization: Aki (Fairly a hot take. While Toua talks about power, mindset, control and you could argue he has a fair understanding of himself, I feel like it's easier to say it when you really don't give two shits about what people think about you. Aki for me, shows growth of realizing Yokoya's words, his philosophy on doubt and the different between doubt and apathy, his acceptance and willingness to sacrifice himself for his mother and His acknowledgement and convincing Yokoya at the end.)

Social Intelligence: Tokuchi mid diffs.

* Cognition: Toua (fairly easy.)
* Integration: Toua (Him positioning himself as the leader)
* Engineering: Toua (Aki's MC feats, while impressive is not as good as Toua's manipualtion to make Lycaons winning the championship.)

Adversity Capacity: Akiyama high diffs

* Mental Fortitude: Toua
* Perseverance: Aki
* Stress Tolerance: Aki (Him having to carry dead weights. Toua, while having a useless team, had an easier time considering he just needs to get them to be at a blank state. Aki for me, has to deal with selfish people and would have a harder time budging. Also, remember at the human auction arc where a woman told Nao about Fukunaga's supposed fate? Aki was able to kept calm during this. After hearing about how horrible it was.)
* Impulse Control: Toua (unsure about this one)
* Cognitive Discipline: Aki
