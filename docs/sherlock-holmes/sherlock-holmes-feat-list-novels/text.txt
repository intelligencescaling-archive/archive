Sherlock Holmes feat list

Sherlock Holmes intelligence feats:

**“From a drop of water a logician could infer the possibility of an Atlantic or a Niagara without having seen or heard of one or the other”** \- Sherlock Holmes.

Planning: (The Adventure of the Empty House) Created a Wax Dummy version of himself then used said wax dummy to catch Colonel Moran attempting to assassinate the decoy Holmes. (The Final Problem) Faked his death after tumbling over the edge with Moriarty by clinging onto a rocky ledge beneath the edge of Reichenbach Falls and leaving a letter behind for Watson. (The Adventure of the Six Napoleons) Orchestrates a trap for the criminal using smashed busts.

Reasoning: (A Study in Scarlet) Deduced John Watson was a Former Army Doctor returning from an Injury in Afghanistan from his dark face and left arm. (The Hound of the Baskervilles) Deduced James Mortimer's profession dog breed personality and recent visit from a walking stick. (*A Scandal in Bohemia*) Deduces a man’s recent travel history from his hat’s condition. (The Adventure of the Yellow Face) Deduces a discarded pipe's owner is left handed. (The Red Headed League) Deduces a man’s profession by his hands. (A Scandal in Bohemia) Deduces Watson has recently resumed practicing as a doctor after not seeing him in weeks. (The Adventure of the *Speckled Band)* Deduces hidden compartments in furniture and walls*. (*The Adventure of the *Cardboard Box*) Recognises when someone is lying by behavioral cues. (*The Sign of Four*) Deduces Jonathan Small’s leg injury and history from subtle gait changes. (The Adventure of the Yellow Face) Deduces that the "beggar" is actually Neville St Clair in disguise based on the absence of shaving cuts and his familiarity with greasepaint. (A Study in Scarlet) Deduced the murderer’s height dominant hand smoking habits and use of a poison pill all from footprints cigar ash and the writing on the wall.

Prediction: (*A Scandal in Bohemia*) Predicts a person’s typical reaction to stress from micro-expressions. (The Adventure of the *Blue Carbuncle)* Recovers stolen items by predicting the criminal’s storage habits**.** (*Adventure of the Priory School*) Predicts the criminal’s plan to murder for inheritance.

Calculus: (*The Adventure of Silver Blaze)* Deduced a train’s speed by calculating how fast poles passed through his field of view also uses timing and behavioral modeling to figure out the missing horse’s location. (*The Adventure of the Priory School)* Deduced a rider’s speed weight and frequency of travel by calculating faint bicycle tire marks spacing and tread depth. (*A Study in Scarlet)* Calculates a person’s age and deduces habits from the dust on their shoes*.* (The Adventure of the *Empty House*) *Calculates the exact time and trajectory of an attack.* (The Adventure of the *Musgrave Ritual*) Calculates the timing of letters and shipments to track movements. (The Adventure of the Retired Colourman**)** Calculates measurements and runs chemical tests to deduce the criminal’s identity.

Deception: (The Adventure of the Dying Detective) Pretended to be deathly ill for days making Culverton Smith confess to murder. (A Scandal in Bohemia) Made Irene Adler reveal the location of a hidden photograph by making her think her house was on fire via a smoke rocket, Watson threw after Holmes faked an injury disguised as a clergyman triggering her instinct to protect it however she did anticipate this. (Multiple Examples) Deceived multiple people with disguises including his assistant John Watson who was intelligent enough to nearly solve the Hound of the Baskervilles on his own. (The Adventure of Black Peter) Uses observation and deduction to create a profile for his suspect, and then lures them in under false pretenses. (*The Adventure of the Norwood Builder*) Feigns knowledge and false leads to manipulate a suspect.

Other: (The Adventure of the *Dying Detective)* Uses scientific experiments on site to test hypothesises. (The Adventure of the *Empty House)* Uses psychological misdirection to force the criminal to reveal vital information*. (*The Adventure of Charles Augustus Milverton) *He has a burglary kit. (The Adventure of the Dancing Men*) Decodes an entire cipher from tiny pictograms. (The Final Problem) Led Moriarty on a European chase using disguises false travel routes and coded messages before finally trapping Moriatry at Reichenbach Falls. (A Study in Scarlet) Mentally maps buildings in London. (The Adventure of the Gloria Scott) Memorises his old case files.
