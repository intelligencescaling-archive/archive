Novels Sherlock's FSIQ, EQ and AC feats

“To a great mind, nothing is little.”

\-Sherlock Holmes

I know that a lot of people larp classic novels for some reason, so I've made this post to help people see what the OG detective can do in these three categories.

**FSIQ:**

VCI: Sherlock can speak multiple languages and has great knowledge of English, Latin, French, Greek, Italian & German.

In the Dancing Men case, Sherlock solved a code made of what seemed to be childish scribbles. He then used his knowledge of the english language and applied it to the code, manually replacing each hieroglyphic thingy with a letter. He did this a bunch of times and finally got an answer in plain english

WMI: In A Study In Scarlet, Sherlock has a sort of mind palace he calls his 'brain attic'. And before anyone says he doesn't even know that the earth travelled around the sun, let me tell you, this does not mean he is ignorant of everything around him. Later, in The Adventure of the Lion's Mane, Sherlock says he is an omnivorous reader, so this statement cancels out what he did before. Anyways, what I want to say is that Sherlock has a massive memory palace where he stores information on every case he's solved, tobacco ashes, tire tracks, soil types etc, etc. He also has countless files on various criminals that he can just recall from memory

VSI: In one of the novels, Sherlock visualised the ENTIRE city of London, while travelling in a cab with the windows covered. I think given that he's used to memorising buildings and minute details, it's safe to assume he can visualise the entire city in his mind and that it wasn't luck or an exaggeration.

He also visualised a building's layout perfectly including the stairways, rooms, and downstairs area. He then visualises the room of the said building where an important document was taken perfectly, down to every detail such as random papers, documents, couple desks, a shelf, the two doors and the windows which were thirty feet off the ground.

PSI: In A Study In Scarlet, he was able to deduce that Watson was an army doctor returning from Afghanistan in less than a second by observing his expression, the tan on his skin and so on

FRI: Just read any one of his stories for a reasoning feat (preferably one of the four main novels)

**EQ:**

EP: In The Sign of Four (ch 2), Sherlock was able to perceive from somebody's handwriting (particularly the way they wrote the words) that the writer had self-esteem but was hesitant for some reason

In A Scandal in Bohemia, he perceived that Irene & her lawyer were actually in love despite her having a near perfect act that it was just an ordinary relationship between a lawyer and an actor.

EU: In The Adventure of the Blue Carbuncle, Sherlock analysed the man’s hat he had made the inference that he had recently been divorced. Now he had also bought a goose early that morning (Which Sherlock inferred was for his wife), that implies desperation to stay with his wife but not malicious desperation but rather sad desperation and he trying anything that is possible to stay with his wife and not be alone. Just to note he profiled all this about a man FROM HIS HAT and everything he profiled about him is correct. (this can be counted as EP as well)

EE: For all his disguises (Sherlock is a master of disguises, see specifically the Charles Augustus Milverton case) he formulates completely different personalities for each one for him to act out perfectly, including the emotions (I guess this could be counted as acting skills as well)

**AC:**

Mental Fortitude: In the Devil's Foot story, Sherlock blocked out a drug from his mind that induced insanity and death through sheer mental fortitude

Perseverance: When he was confronting Moriarty, Sherlock never stopped trying to bring him down, even up to the point of sacrificing his life in a confrontation. However, when he realises that he, in fact, did not die (he got that plot armour), he continued to hunt down the remnants of Moriarty's gang, who were actively trying to kill him and nearly did on multiple occasions.

Also, just to let everyone know, these feats don't include the full canon Sherlock, so these are just the ones I can remember from the top of my head
