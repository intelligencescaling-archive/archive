My take on novels Sherlock vs Shinichi Kudo (animanga) with explanations

So a few days ago, some people called Kudo 'mediocre' which kinda pissed me off, so now I'm just going to do a matchup between my two favourite characters to cool off with minor explanations along the way.

**FSIQ: Kudo (VCI>=, WMI), Sherlock (VSI, FRI>=, PSI>=)**

FSIQ is probably the closest category after thinking and reasoning. For VCI, Kudo is stated to know multiple languages and frequently solves difficult cases using his knowledge of Japanese. He also takes WMI due to the hospital files feat and possibly PSI, but I think Sherlock could beat him there (deducing Watson's entire military background in less than a second + calculating the speed of a train instantly). For VSI, Sherlock takes due to memorising the city of London and FRI is also super close.

Overall: Sherlock extreme diffs

**EQ: Kudo (EE, EM>=), Sherlock (EP, EU)**

Kudo takes EE (his relationship to Ran and wanting to protect her from harm multiple times in the story) and arguably EM (12 Million Hostages feat) although Sherlock is no slouch either. IMO, Sherlock takes EP and EU due to him being able to sense out people's emotional state through NON-LIVING mundane objects such as hats and handwriting. His EU is also good (profiling many people with complex motives).

Overall: Sherlock high diffs

**SQ: Kudo (skills>=, charisma, engineering?), Sherlock (integration, understanding, influence>=)**

I'm actually not too sure about this one; Kudo shows great leadership (which I use in skills) in the Clash of Red and Black arc and might take charisma but I'm not sure about engineering. Sherlock, however, has excellent social integration and understanding as he can blend in with any crowd in his disguises. Influence also goes to him via his brother Mycroft, who has the entire government of Britain behind his back (although Kudo also has the help of the FBI and so on).

Overall: Sherlock very high diffs

**AC: Kudo (cognitive discipline), Sherlock (mental fortitude>=, perseverance)**

This is also another super close one, but cognitive discipline goes to Kudo via 12 Million Hostages case, mental fortitude to Sherlock via blocking out the Devil's Foot drug from his mind (though Kudo could potentially take it as well due to being drugged by APTX, (which literally melted him into his child form) but Sherlock takes perseverance due to hunting and being hunted by Moriarty for years and not giving up, even at the verge of death multiple times.

Overall: Sherlock extreme diffs

**Adaptability: (LA=), Kudo (situational adaptability, situational awareness), Sherlock (none)**

Sherlock's just not great at adaptability (at least, onscreen feats), so I gave it to Kudo due to him instantly countering the BO at multiple times (Clash of Red and Black arc, The Jet-Black Mystery Train arc). LA is CGEW, since both characters are known for learning many skills and stuff.

Overall: Kudo mid diffs

**Thinking: Kudo (lateral, convergent?), Sherlock (holistic, linear, divergent?)**

This one is way harder for me to explain, but I can confidently say Sherlock takes linear and holistic thinking due to the Hound of The Baskervilles case.

Overall: Sherlock extreme diffs

**Reasoning: Kudo (deductive), Sherlock (abductive>=, inductive>=)**

Also can't really explain this one as they both have way too many reasoning feats

Overall: Sherlock extreme diffs

**Deception: Kudo (concealment, misdirection, fabrication>=), Sherlock (acting, disguise, bluffing?)**

For deception, Kudo takes most of the important categories with his Akai fake death plan which spans for multiple arcs, faking the death of an FBI member and convincing the entire FBI and BO that he's dead. Sherlock takes acting because he has, on multiple accounts, disguised himself so well even Watson, who's been with him for years, could not tell who he was.

Overall: Kudo high diffs

**Manipulation: Kudo (indirect, mass), Sherlock (direct, emotional)**

For indirect and mass, the reason is the same as deception, although Sherlock takes direct and emotional by manipulating a culprit into confessing through his pride and using his superior acting skills to provoke him into revealing himself.

Overall: Kudo high diffs

**Scheming: Kudo (invincibility>=, complexity, coverage, intricacies>=), Sherlock (efficiency, contingencies?)**

Sherlock basically has very few onscreen feats for this category, so it's safe to say Kudo takes this again via the Akai fake death plan.

Overall: Kudo mid diffs

**ToM: Kudo (none), Sherlock (cognitive, emotional)**

I did a post on Sherlock's ToM feats before (and those aren't even his best), which beat Kudo's ToM feats.

Overall: Sherlock high diffs

**Field Skills: Kudo (trap setting>=, trap evasion, info control), Sherlock (info gathering)**

Sherlock is superior in info gathering because whenever he's on a case, he actively searches and questions suspects carefully while noting down tracks, footprints, etc meticulously, and while Kudo is also good at this kind of stuff, Sherlock takes it, because Kudo is often held back due to being a literal child. For trap setting and trap evasion, he was able to see through and counter Gin's schemes perfectly fine in the Clash of Red and Black arc. Info control also goes to Kudo because was able to manipulate the information that the FBI received (fingerprints of the faked death FBI member) and therefore trick them into thinking that Akai was dead.

Overall: Kudo high diffs

Kudo takes this matchup imo high diff
