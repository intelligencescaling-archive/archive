Anthony Horowitz Moriarty's plan explanation.

Context: Professor James Moriarty reigns over England's criminal underworld as the Napoleon of crime, however over in America, they have their own arch-criminal, a much less methodical, and profoundly more violent criminal named Clarence Deveraux. Nobody knows what he looks like save a few lieutenants, namely Scotchy Lavelle, Edgar Mortlake and his brother Leland Mortlake, however it is known that Deveraux has agoraphobia. Lavelle and his gang have traveled to England and seek to usurp Moriarty's rule as the foremost criminals, and go into it offensively, rooting out Moriarty's agents and offering them positions, violently killing them and their loved ones upon refusal. What's more, they've been leaking info from turncoats to Scotland Yard and by extension Sherlock Holmes himself, who additionally took advantage of this to seriously cripple Moriarty's operations. Before long, Moriarty finds himself with no more than three compatriots whom he can trust, his right hand man and the best sniper in the empire Colonel Sebastian Moran, a young, somewhat psychopathic, yet loyal boy named Perry, and a son of one of Moriarty's old friends, Jonathan Pilgrim. Moriarty sends Pilgrim to infiltrate Deveraux's gang as Leland Mortlake's secretary. Pilgrim is subsequently discovered as an informant and killed. Moriarty thus devises a plan.

–First, he uses makeup to whiten his appearance, concealed heighteners to appear taller, a barely noticeable wig and purposely hunches his shoulders in order to alter his looks for when he meets Sherlock Holmes in person the first time. This meeting goes as Holmes describes in The Final Problem in canon, with Moriarty feigning that he still has all the influence of his prime and threatening Holmes to back off, with the disguise being to warp the way Holmes describes him, as one of the only people to ever see him face to face, to Watson, who would inevitably chronicle it in his memoirs. He then arranges a couple of small "attempts" on Holmes' life to further intimidate him, such as dropping a large stone near his head from a roof and using what little money he has to send a man with a club that he knows Holmes will easily beat. All of this amounts to driving Holmes from England after having sent a list of Moriarty's former associates who were now with Deveraux to Scotland Yard, and to keep him away, Moriarty even has Holmes home of 221b Baker Street burned down. This would allow for Moriarty and Holmes' "final showdown" to occur somewhere removed from Deveraux's gang and Scotland yard interfering. (Disguise, Bluffing, Fabrication, Coercion, Strategic Deception.)

–Next, once Moriarty and his group have followed Holmes to Meiringen in Switzerland, Moriarty anticipates Holmes would visit their central attraction, the Reichenbach Falls, and walks up there before him, using his mathematical skills to calculate the necessary angle and speed necessary to fall and survive. Once Holmes and Watson climb the mountain, Moriarty has Perry pose as a messenger boy claiming that a woman staying at the same hotel as Holmes and Watson needed medical help, drawing Watson away, and allowing Moriarty to confront Holmes one on one. They fight, and Moriarty guides Holmes to the exact place he needs for the angles he needed, and allows Holmes to throw him over. Once in the water, Moran throws boulders at Holmes to distract him so that Moriarty can resurface. (Anticipation, Visual Spatial Index, Crystalized Intelligence, Fabrication, Strategy.)

–Moriarty finds a guy in Meiringen who could feasibly pass as him, being in his forties, a similar height and kinda lanky, and kills him. With his group they dress him as Moriarty, and even sew in a concealed pocket with a note inside, a coded message from Deveraux to Moriarty. They then drop it in the river of the Reichenbach falls to be found and identified as Moriarty, taking advantage of Holmes also faking his own death at the Falls by leaving a note to Watson, meaning it would be known that Moriarty "died" in the Falls, yet no one could positively identify him. (Fabrication.)

–Moriarty himself deigns to insert himself with the police to leverage them against Deveraux. To do so, he decides to take on the persona of a detective from an American agency known as the Pinkertons who have a history of opposing Deveraux's gang, meaning it would be only natural for one to travel to England. To this end he obtains clothing uniquely American, such as a striped waistcoat with four pockets, uncommon in England, he purchases a watch with the Pinkerton's logo and puts a New York Port Authority Stamp on his trunk. He further changed his basic vocabulary even as small as saying phrases such as 'I guess' as opposed to 'I think' and affected a Boston accent. It is worth noting that the accent is good enough to consistently fool actual Americans such as Scotchy Lavelle, the Mortlake brothers and Clarence Deveraux himself. Moriarty, now known as Frederick Chase, meets Athelney Jones, a Scotland Yard official who makes an attempt at a Sherlock-scan picking up on all the fabrications Moriarty used to imply the fake identity Moriarty assumed before he had to explain himself as well as making several false deductions, showcasing himself as clever, but not as much as he thinks himself. Jones had been sent to examine the corpse of "Moriarty" and the real Moriarty asserts that Moriarty and Deveraux were actually beginning co-operations in the lead up to Moriarty's supposed death, hence the Pinkerton involvement. He guides Jones to the coded note (Disguise, Acting Skills, Bluffing, Strategy.)

–For the note, Moriarty copies a page from Watson's first memoir A Study in Scarlet, with the letters alternating between capital and lowercase. The total number of letters on the page was divisible by five (and six but when the code was tried that way it didn't work) so when the letters were divided into groups of five and designated either 'L' for capitals or 's' for lowercase they would form 77 groups of five looking like 'LsLsL' or 'LLsLL'. The solver would then need to designate each combination of letters a number in order of appearance, the first one I noted for example was 1 and the second was 2. Going on like this, you end up with 19 unique combinations repeated over the near 80, with each designating a letter of the alphabet. The 17 first digits of the message was 1 2 3 4 5 6 6 3 2 7 3 2 8 9 2 10 11, and if one were to assume the message opened addressing the intended target, "PROFESSOR MORIARTY" would fit. With this assumption, the rest of the message can be puzzled out through substitution and trial and error. The final message is "PROFESSOR MORIARTY MEET ME AT THE CAFE ROYAL LONDON ONE O CLOCK MAY THE TWELFTH WEAR A RED TULIP." Athelney Jones figures it out, though of course Moriarty would have aided him wherever necessary. (Verbal Comprehension Index, Creative Thinking, Fabrication.)

–Moriarty plans with Jones that he, as Frederick Chase, will wear a red tulip and pretend to be Professor Moriarty meeting with Clarence Deveraux's agent. The agent is to be Perry, and they will deliver a rehearsed performance in which Perry demands a pass-phrase that Moriarty, pretending to be Chase, pretending to be Moriarty, does not know. Perry then runs away, wearing a bright blue coat that will make him easy to tail. He will lead them to Scotchy Lavelle's home, one of the few bits of info Moriarty got from Pilgrim, and them discard with the blue coat and disappear in the crowd as though he had entered the house. (Fabrication, Acting Skills, Into Control, Strategy.)

–Moriarty and Jones visit Scotchy Lavelle's home under the guise of a pair of detectives, now able to enter it with Scotland Yard by his side unlike before. Moriarty informs Jones of Lavelle's role as lieutenant to Deveraux and they both question him of any connection, to which Lavelle naturally denies it. Moriarty takes the opportunity to learn where exactly the lock on his front door is, and slips opium into Lavelle's household's meal while searching the kitchen. That night Moriarty returns with Perry, and they drill through the door where the lock was. They enter the house and, thanks to the opium, everyone was sound asleep. They smother or slit the throats of Lavelle's mistress, cooks and manservant, before dragging him downstairs and tying him to a chair, splashing him with water to wake him. They torture him and manage to learn that Clarence Deveraux is located in the American Legation, and learn about a bank robbery planned and being carried out by some pawn working for Deveraux at a barber's shop named Horner, at 13 Chancery Lane. Perry then slits Lavelle's throat, and Moriarty writes HORNER 13 in Lavelle's diary and marks it, placing a bar of shaving soap in the same drawer. (Tactics, Info Gathering, Info Control.)

–The next day Moriarty and Jones return to the house, now a crime scene. Moriarty is able to convince Jones that this was Deveraux's work, seeing as "Frederick Chase" knows the gang and their brutality better, and this too further serves to impress upon Scotland Yard the ruthlessness of the gang and why they need to take action. He then finds the memo he had written in the diary and Jones takes note. Jones then brings Moriarty to a meeting with several other Scotland Yard inspectors, however one of them, Lestrade, insists on messaging the Pinkertons overseas for further info via telegraph. So, later that day, when Moriarty and Jones are out, Moriarty has Perry deliver a bomb to the Yard positioned at Jones' office, next to the telegraph room. This way the message would be delayed tremendously, as these things take days anyway. What's more, this attack galvanizes the Yard even further, none more than Jones. He believes the bomb was for him and now fears for his life, and lives even were lost anyway, this brings the gang as a threat to the highest level in the Yard's eyes. Further, Moriarty has Moran and Perry meet and get into a brougham right after the bombing where Jones and Moriarty bot see them, recognizing Perry. A brougham is a rarer kind of carriage, and so easier for Jones to trace. He finds that the brougham had gone to the American legation, directing his attention there. (Logical Manipulation, Fabrication, Misdirection, Emotional Manipulation, Mass Manipulation, Strategy.)

–Moriarty takes an invitation to a party at the legation from Lavelle's house. He then capitalizes on Jones fear from being targeted, and his anger from the loss of life in the attack to convince him that they need to do whatever they can to stop Deveraux's gang. So, they enter the legation on the date with the invite, yet are soon found out. They are ushered out by the third secretary of Robert Lincoln, a man named Coleman De Vriess. De Vriess when chastising them, uses Jones' first name, something neither Jones nor Moriarty had mentioned, prompting Jones to pull back the window curtain and, when De Vriess recoils in terror, this proves that he is Deveraux, especially as he had the hubris to use the same initials. For this, naturally Jones' career is all but over, yet his determined to make the remaining week he has left to do all he can to catch the American criminals. Further, Moriarty plays to Jones' deep-seated envy of Sherlock Holmes' skills, his inferiority at being shown up time and time again and need to prove himself as smart, by motivating him with the prospect that if they solve the case, they could even go to business together as private detectives like Holmes and Watson. (Emotional Manipulation, Info Gathering, Psychological Manipulation.)

–Moriarty and Jones search Pilgrim's room in a hotel run by the Mortlakes, the Bostonian, once more. Moriarty plants a card for Horner's barber shop in a book for Jones to find. They then travel down there, and Jones immediately uncovers a bank robbery plan in progress from the owner, a former criminal named John Clay. Clay all but jumps at the opportunity to turn on Deveraux, as he demands half of the profit and threatens violent murder, unlike Moriarty who was civil and never took more than 20%. Jones organizes for the newspapers to report a thwarted burglary with only a few valuables stolen while Clay sends to meet with Edgar Mortlake, with the meeting to be intercepted by a dozen Scotland Yard men. At the meeting Mortlake's own henchmen are killed yet he escapes, but not after naming Deveraux in front of several officers, someone both he and his brother had denied ever knowing. This is enough information for Moriarty and Jones to take Leland Mortlake back at the Bostonian in for questioning, but on the way, Moriarty slips a poisoned dart into Mortlake's neck. This is taking advantage of Jones' first experience with Holmes on a case, chronicled in The Sign of Four, wherein poisoned blow darts are used, to conjure the image of Deveraux once again eliminating his own men once the law closes in, and also to force Deveraux to expose himself further, now having stopped a heist he'd planned and killed two of his chief lieutenants, one being the brother of the only remaining one. (Info Control, Psychological Manipulation, Logical Manipulation, Strategy.)

–Deveraux kidnaps Jones' daughter and sends a message to him for Jones and Moriarty to meet in a graveyard at night. Jones uses skills he learnt from Sherlock Holmes' writings to determine that the note was written on paper taken from a book in a library seldom read, likely close by to Deveraux, and was written by a man of high education pretending to be one without, most likely Deveraux. Moriarty and Jones go to the meeting and see the release of Jones' daughter, but Moriarty has Moran and Perry tail them secretly. Moriarty allows himself and Jones to be knocked out and brought to an underground location where they're kept  in a freezer until they can hardly move, then brought out into a room with Edgar Mortlake and Deveraux, where they're beaten badly and threatened with torture. After Deveraux's exit, with aid from Moran and Jones' own concealed firearm within his cane, they escape, killing Mortlake in the process. Now both being able to positively testify against "Coleman De Vriess" and with testimony from Jones' wife and daughter, in addition to the note Jones was sent which he proves via Holmesian analysis was from a book in the American Legation, Moriarty and Jones have enough grounds to have Coleman De Vriess removed from the Legation for questioning. in transport however, Moran kills several of the Scotland Yard officers and along with Moriarty and Perry, they hijack the cart taking De Vriess/Deveraux. They take him to a wide open park and force him out, abusing his agoraphobia as badly as possible, before confronting him with the fact that his top lieutenants were all dead and his operation in England was all but over. What's more, he was now in Moriarty's hands, to do with as he likes. Having established Deveraux's powerlessness, as well as simply how much smarter than him Moriarty was, they set off for America. After all, several Scotland Yard inspectors have now seen Moriarty's face and will no doubt realize what his game was all along after this. Instead, they set off for America to use Deveraux's contacts, force him to reveal his allies to them, and take over his empire, as he had seen to do to Moriarty. (Emotional Engagement, Emotional Management, Perseverance, Stress Tolerance, Mental Fortitude, Strategy, Psychological Warfare, Long-term Planning.)
