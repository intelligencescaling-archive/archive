Who is the smartest character that Original Sherlock Holmes can beat just with feats from "A Schandal in Bohemia"? (In categories that have these feats. Like who he can beat in planning or analysis with these feats.)

So like i started reading Sherlock Holmes. I started with "A Scandal in Bohemia" short story.

And this story alone have great feats. And story is good too.

Anyway here's feats:

* Looking at Watson, he thought Watson had gained 3.5 kilos (Watson had gained 4 kilos).
* Noticing the six scratches on the side of Watson's leather shoe, he immediately knew that Watson had been out in bad weather and had an incompetent servant. He also understood from Watson's smell of tincture of iodine, a nitrate stain on his right index finger, and a swelling on the brim of his top hat that Watson was a doctor and that there was a stethoscope under the hat.
* Watson finds the word "EgpGt" on an expensive letter. Sherlock finds Gt for Gesellschaft (German for company) and P for papier (German for paper). He looks in the book for Eg and finds Egria. From this and the construction of the sentence, he understands that the man is German.
* He understands from the sounds that the carriage is driven by two horses worth at least 3000 shillings.
* He realizes from the man's appearance that he is the King of Bohemia. When he calmly and impatiently addresses him as "your majesty", the king reveals himself with excitement and despair.
* Here's the case: When this king was young, he had an affair with a woman named Irene Adler, and there's a photo of the two of them together. The king is getting married in a few days. Irene Adler threatens to ruin the king by showing him the photo so that he doesn't marry someone else. The king sent some thieves, but they couldn't find the photo. Sherlock needs to find the photo.
* Sherlock disguises himself to spy on Irene. Then he spies on the mansion and remembers every detail of it. Then he follows her with a man and witnesses their marriage.
* Sherlock then makes a plan. According to him, a woman does not want to share her secret with anyone else. He is sure that Irene would not want her husband to see the photo and that she kept the photo at home because it was heavy. Also, according to him, women's instincts are directed towards saving the most valuable thing in case of danger. Accordingly, he makes a plan: He hires a bunch of men. While these men are fighting like thugs, they turn towards Irene. Sherlock tries to stop them by disguising himself as an innocent protestant priest who has nothing to do with him or resembles him. Then he lies down on the floor and hits his forehead with his hand that has red paint on it, pretending to be injured. Then he is taken to the bedroom and the living room, which he suspects (he is a little suspicious of the bedroom, but completely suspicious of the living room). There, he asks for the window to be opened. When the window is opened, he gives Watson a signal. Watson fires a simple smoke rocket. When this thing creates harmless fire and smoke, Watson shouts "Fire!" and panics everywhere. While people are running away and Watson is going to the end of the street, Sherlock sees that Irene has hidden the photo behind a picture. Sherlock tries to get it from there, but when he sees Coachman staring at him, he gives up. Then he goes to Watson.
* (Anti Feat i guess?) The next day, Sherlock goes to the mansion with the King and Watson and learns that Irene has gone to Europe, never to return. While calmly examining the surroundings, he finds a different photo and a letter behind that picture. In the letter, Irene praises Sherlock for his acting. However, since she is a trained actress and sometimes wears men's clothes, she realizes that it is Sherlock, the person she was warned about before. Then she sends the coachman after them. Then she dresses like a man and follows Sherlock to show him that she is an attractive subject, and even greets him (Sherlock only says that he has heard this voice before). After this incident, he decides to escape because it would be dangerous to stay. He also says that he has given up on blackmail and the King can sleep soundly. Sherlock has not made sarcastic jokes about women's intelligence since that day and respects Irene.
