Moriarty vs Moriarty (BBC vs Elementary).

Iq: Jamie. (Neither really demonstrate a great amount of feats in it tbh which is expected because they're mostly off screen antagonists. But Jamie was able to recall and paint Watson's face with such accuracy that the prison guards were adtonished at how perfect the portrait was when Watson came to visit. I'm quite sure this counts for WMI and maybe VSI too? On the other hand, Jim's only feat I can think of is him perceiving the exact make of the gun in Sherlock's pocket in S1, which I think is also a VSI feat. The problem with this feat though is that it's not impossible that one of his spies simply saw Sherlock pocket the gun and identified the make for him. Plus... he literally got the make wrong lol. He says it's a British Army L9A1 but it's apparently actually an L105A1. But I guess canonically he did get the make correct and he's more likely narratively to have recognised it himself, which would be more impressive).

Eq: Jamie. (Jim demonstrated great EU by seemingly being able to predict when Sherlock's friends and colleagues would turn on him, but I'd honestly still give it to Jamie. She also takes EE and absolutely demolishes in EM. EP might be close).

Sq: Jamie. (I can't explain without spoilers but she's really good in this. I don't think Jim ever shows particularly impressive social skills anyway).

Ac: Jim.

Planning: Jim. (Reichenbach Fall with Final Problem contingency is actually really really good and I'd honestly say Jim is one of the best characters in planning there is because of it).

Strategy: Jamie. (Sherlock and Irene reunion and the Currency Manipulation strategies are better than Jim's Great Game strategy imo. I'm not even sure Great Game is a strategy and not a plan tbh).

Knowledge: Jamie. (Jim knows about the history behind certain musicians and maybe the solar system depending on your interpretation of his fake painting, but Jamie demonstrates more impressive knowledge in physics, art, and seemingly also botany).

Reasoning: Jamie. (Jim has no feats in it).

Manipulation: Jamie. (Jim could take mass from convincing the world Sherlock was a fraud, but Jamie still takes the point due to how she manipulates Sherlock and Moran. Plus, she could arguably take mass as well since her criminal empire is much larger than Jim's from what we see, and even fewer people know who she is).

Overall: Jamie mid-high diffs. Could be mid diff but I decided some points were quite close.

These two are difficult to scale because they're mostly off screen antagonists and their motives aren't always made clear. We mostly just see their plans play out and see the protagonists react to them while the villains themselves are rarely physically present. As such, it's hard to say how much of what happens was actually planned by them from the start, or what was a strategy they had to formulate to counter a particular development in circumstances, or what was just dumb luck in their favour. So do keep in mind that this is just mased on my interpretations of their stories.
