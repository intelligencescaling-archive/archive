Some of Sherlock's ToM feats explained

"It is not really difficult to construct a series of inferences, each dependent upon its predecessor and each simple in itself. If, after doing so, one simply knocks out all the central inferences and presents one's audience with the starting-point and the conclusion, one may produce a startling, though possibly meretricious, effect." - Sherlock Holmes

For some time, I've wanted to do a small post on Sherlock, so here it is:

**The Adventure of the Dancing Men (The Return of Sherlock Holmes)**

One day, Sherlock and Watson were sitting and chilling when Sherlock suddenly claimed that Watson did not want to invest in South African stocks (or securities as it is said in the novel) just by looking at Watson's forefinger and thumb.

P1: Watson had chalk between his left finger and thumb last night, after returning from the club last night

P2: He knows Watson uses the chalk to play billards to steady the cue

P3: Watson doesn't play with anyone except with a guy named Thruston

P4: 4 weeks ago, Thurston had an option on some South African property which would expire in a month and he desired to share it with Watson

P5: Watson's check book is locked in the drawer

P6: Watson has not asked for the key to unlock the drawer

**C1:** Watson does not intend to open his drawer

**C2:** Because he doesn't intend to open the drawer and get his check book, he does not intend on investing in the South African stocks

**The Adventure of the Cardboard Box (His Last Bow)**

Watson has his entire thought chain listed out by Sherlock again, this time, Watson's thinking about how ridiculous it was for international problems to be solved through war and fighting.

P1: Watson sat for half a minute with a vacant expression then stared at his newly framed picture of General Gordon

P2: Watson's facial expression changed slightly

**C1:** Watson has begun to think, possibly about General Gordon or along that line

P3: Watson's eyes then flashed to the unframed portrait of Henry Ward Beecher

P4: He then glanced up at a bare space in the wall

**C2:** Watson is thinking that if the portrait was framed it would cover the bare space and correspond with Gordon's picture as well

P5: Watson's expressions then turned thoughtful

P6: He was still looking at Beecher's portrait but more interestedly now

**C3:** Watson was recalling Beecher's career

P7: Beecher's career included a mission which he undertook on behalf of the North during the Civil War

P8: Watson had earlier stated his passionate indignation on the way Beecher was received by the more turbulent people from Britain

P9: His eyes then turned away but he still had a thoughtful expression + there were a few other micro-expressions

**C4:** He was now thinking about the Civil War, especially the gallantry which was shown by both sides during the struggle

P10: Watson's face then turned sadder and he shook his head

P11: His hand stole towards his old wound and a sad smile quivered on his lips

P12: Watson had experienced a lot of hardships during his time fighting

**C5:** Watson was thinking about his time fighting and the useless waste of life he had to see

**C6:** Through this, Sherlock deduces that Watson thought it was preposterous that war was used to settle international affairs instead of peaceful methods

So yeah, these are just a few of Sherlock's ToM feats. There are some better ones though these are the only ones that came into mind when I was writing this down.
