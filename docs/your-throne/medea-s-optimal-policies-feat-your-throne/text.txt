Medeas's optimal policies  feat

I've started reading and analyzing your throne and I think I came across an insane feat of NCPS and prediction for Medeas.

Basically, 10 year old Medeas is essentially in a short break and she's with her parents in a carriage. Her father, who is a Duke, is complaining about the supply issue and that if it keeps going they'll lose the war. Medeas then offer a solution that he should try to trade with the neighboring kingdom for food in exchange for sending their knights to protect said kingdom. This worked and it stopped the famine (stated directly by the narrative)

This feat alone isn't insane because of how ambiguous it is and the fact we know Medeas wasn't the one who worked the logistics behind the policies and stuff.

However what happens next is insane.

It's basically a complication of 3 scenes which basically shows how from this day all the way to when she would be engaged to be married to the crown prince, her father had Medeas essentially help him with anything related to governing (help is a strong word it's more like begging tbh lol)

Because her father is a Duke, his area of responsibility is a Dukedom which is basically a whole region so his responsibility are varied and complex Including:

Managing the vassals and millitary

The economy of the Dukedom

Handling general administration/law problems

The general politics (Her father took the credit for all of her ideas so we can also infer he used her for ideas regarding the political situation so that he could optimize his standing which is further confirmed by the fact that Medeas read a ridiculous amount of books from a young age which were considered adult level + her statement in earlier chapters saying she's well versed in the history of nobles houses)

Etc etc.

So Medeas, for several years, essentially offered optimal policies related to everything ruling a dukedom.

This is basically confirmed by the narrative. The Duke earned the favor of somebody important after proposing this solution, he was getting praised for thinking several steps ahead and the narrative never explicitly showed an exemple that one of Medeas's policies failed.

To do this, you would need a superhuman level of foresight and planning. A dukedom is insanely complex and policies usually have direct/indirect side effects on other subjects and said impacts will also result in indirect impact onto other subjects so on and so forth. So to do optimal policies that make your father extremely famous she'd need to account for all of these factors and more and where it gets even crazier is real life is unpredictable and she did it consistently over several years meaning her policies are so effective that random BS that could make them less effective (like changes in the culture, new technologies, new laws, new political standing etc) didn't affected their efficiency.

It's also a great feat of crystallized intelligence for obvious reasons and it gets even better as a feat of fluid intelligence considered she did most of these policies as a child at an age where certain components of your brain are still developing and when your fluid intelligence is still increasing.
