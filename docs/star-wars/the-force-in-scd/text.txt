The Force in SCD
Made by Ok_Cardiologist_5925 / Coup
The aim of this doc is to show which categories the Force, some to an unfair level, can boost and give short explanations as to why. This doc is only for those who like to scale characters with their abilities. Depending on how you scale, this can upgrade or downgrade Force users.

VCI/NVI/FRI/WMI/Learning Ability/Information Gathering – Force users can use Translation to translate and speak an unknown language to them. This applies to body languages too. Exar Kun, for example, learned the language of the ancient Sith when he fully embraced the Dark Side. Darth Revan also used another technique called Drain Knowledge to learn a new language.
* Sources: Jedi Academy Sourcebook, Tales of the Jedi – Dark Lords of the Sith Issue #4, Knights of the Old Republic

WMI/Memory – Force users can use Force to easily remember symbols or events and recall them later. Anakin Solo used to remember multiple Massassi symbols, completely unknown and incomprehensible to him, and later write them down despite only seeing them once for about a second.
* Source: Junior Jedi Knights - Lyric’s World

PSI/Faster Thinking – Force Users can move and think faster due to Force Speed. For example, Luke needed a nanosecond to realize Abeloth was alive. Whether the statement is hyperbole or not is up to debate.
* Source: Fate of the Jedi - Vortex

EP/EU/Perception/See-Through People/Social Awareness/Relationship Management/Cold Reading/Mind Reading – Force users can perceive many things outside the scope of what is realistically possible. Force users can feel others emotions without even having to see them. Palpatine, for example, could understand Maul’s emotions and told him that he knew everything about him. He would also use it on Anakin to read his emotions and thoughts. Palpatine that way can be more socially aware of his surroundings and maintain his relations better. Plagueis can also use his Force sense and telepathy to tell the truth from the lie. Jacen Solo also could also tell when Nom Anor was lying about where the escape pods were.
* Sources: Darth Plagueis, Prequel Trilogy, The New Jedi Order - The Unifying Force

EM/EE/Overall AC – Force users have forms of meditation and other means to clear their mind.
* Source: Knights of the Old Republic 2

Foresight – Force users can accurately predict the future or at least have intuition on what will happen. For example, Palpatine foresaw that Obi-Wan will teach Luke and that Luke will destroy Death Star I.
N.B. Foresight is a rare and many times ambiguous power – characters do not have full futures or sometimes are shown in a more metaphorical sense. For example, Cade Skywalker foresaw that he would kill his uncle and master but he did not kill them. They sacrificed themselves so that Cade could live. So, Cade did not kill them but rather they died for him.
* Sources: Galaxy Guide 5, Legacy War Issue #1, 4 & 5

FRI/Fluid Intelligence/Intuition/Problem-Solving Skills/Lateral Thinking/Foresight – Force users can have boosted intuition in different spheres. For instance, Kyle Katarn intuitively could solve many engineering problems, Anakin Skywalker rebuilt a droid, piloted spaceship and Palpatine knew that Anakin is in trouble, referring to Anakin’s fight against Obi-Wan despite the fact that Palpatine logically should not know Anakin is fighting Obi-Wan, unless of course Palpatine’s Force sense told him so.
* Sources: Dark Forces - Soldier for the Empire, The Phantom Menace, Revenge of the Sith

Observation – Force users can heighten their senses, including sight. Palpatine used it to look at Plagueis’s corpse better
* Source: Darth Plagueis

Direct Manipulation/Mind Control/Influence/Persuasion – the Force allows Force users to control the weak minded. Unfair, I know. Obi-Wan used it on stormtroopers to make them believe R2D2 and C-3PIO are not the droids they are looking for. Once  Palpatine was talking with a hostess of gala and she mentioned how the Force users can use their powers to control others and have influence on them
* Sources: A New Hope, Darth Plagueis

Charisma/Aura/Charm – The Force can be used to get people to like you and form very strong bonds with the other, to the point where you can feel each other and become more drawn to each other. Such bonds are the one between Surik and Kreia and the one between Anakin and Obi-Wan.
* Sources: Knights of the Old Republic 2, Revenge of the Sith

Multitasking – Tenebrae split his mind into two. One ruling the Sith Empire while the other was making the Eternal Empire
* Source: The Old Republic
Of course, there could be other categories the Force boosts or the same ones in a different way but those are the first ones which came to mind.
