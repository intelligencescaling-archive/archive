Character’s characterization reflected by feats

this was inspired by the the post from u/IzyaboiConner this gives me the chance to talk abt characters i love.

imma use the characters that ive “introduced” or in thrawn’s case just discussed more. ik lysander technically isnt a full scd character cuz im the only person who brings bro up and thats like in 3 posts, however hes my fav antag and i can talk abt whoever i want.

Thrawn is a pragmatic, utilitarian, imperialistic genius and Zahn rlly does express this through his writings of Thrawn’s feats. most of his feats are inspired by real generals, like the tactics he uses are similar to napoleon. all of his actions are guided by logical reasonings and constantly thinking abt what is going to happen after. his usage of other’s culture also reflect on his characterization. the way he uses these stereotypes are exactly how imperialists would, especially using it to manipulate enemy governments from the inside. it also expresses his love for other species and his desire to keep the galaxy, especially the unknown regions in order. $8: out of the box thinking also reflects how he breaks the norms set by the empire and the ascendancy. the empire has a mostly strict “no alien” policy. yet despite this he joins the empire as he sees it as the best chance to combat the impending threat. he, unlike most of the ascendancy. advocates for warfare against foes which is different from the defensive chiss ascendancy. he also unlike most chiss, has no care for rising higher in the political scene. he also advocates for the freedom to choose your role, instead of how the ascendancy chooses your role for you based on your skills. he’s by no means a good person but all of his actions reflect his ultimately selfless goal to keep the galaxy safe from either the grysks or the yuuzhon vong.

The Mastermind, i’ll put a spoiler tag here. im going into big spoiler territory bcuz i love this character and dont want to make this choppy. >!simeon is made to be a dark reflection from edgeworth. instead of edgeworth fighting corruption with truth and reasoning. simeon fights it with lies and deception. instead of edgeworth’s appearance commanding respect, simeon flies under the radar or appears to others bcuz if his silly appearance. simeon’s design of a clown also reflects his duality which in turn reflects his feats. he hides in the background and right in front of you. he controls everything and everyone just like the animals he trained. he manipulates others to kill one another bcuz of how the victims he killed were either following orders or commanding others to do so. its also the best way he can to fight the system he was forced to be afraid of. instead of being massively influential like most other villains, he’s just a circus worker and was an orphan. just like he himself says “what was i supposed to do”. yet despite this, he was able to manipulate authoritative figures into preforming his show, just like the animals bcuz we are. !<

Lysander is a little fucking weasel. dont get mistaken by my constant praise of this guy. i hate lysander, he is by far my fav “love to hate” type of character. just like the weasel he is, his feats specialize in manipulating the masses, deceiving others, and being incredibly socially intelligent. his idealism and views of inherent superiority reflect his ability to make others follow him. just like mastermind is to edgeworth, lysander is also a dark parallel instead to darrow. darrow is no politician, but lysander perfectly fits what a politician does. he manipulates lower standing individuals to accept his world views, makes them follow his commands. he manipulates other golds into viewing him as a chosen one figure. even when they see who lysander rlly is, he somehow weasel’s his way out and gains their support just so they can be betrayed later on. now for the minds eye. the minds eye is a power which gives someone the ability to view things on slow motion, make’s quick calculations, perfect memory, perfect self cognition, and lastly superior understanding of those around him bcuz of the slow motion and calculations. the minds eye is symbolic for his supposed inherent superiority, and his “destiny” is a constant reaffirmation. even with the minds eye however, his own problems weaken its potential. he is so groomed by the golds and his grandmothers teachings that despite having perfect memory he was somehow able to erase specific memories tied to understanding himself.
