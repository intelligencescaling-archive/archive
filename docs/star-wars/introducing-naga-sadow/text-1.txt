Introducing new Star Wars characters to SCD Part 1 - Naga Sadow(1/2)

Part 1: Naga Sadow's takeover

I decided to make a short post about Naga Sadow because, as dumb as this guy is, I loved reading his story when I was younger and since I postponed the release of my Palpatine doc, I might as well post about a low-tier Star Wars character.

Setting:

* Character and occupation: Naga Sadow, very powerful Sith sorcerer, member of the Sith Council
* Time period: 5000 BBY (Before Battle of Yavin/Before Episode 4)
* Location: Sith Empire(planets Korriban and Ziost)(N.B. Sith does not refer to the religion but to the Sith species; the Republic does not know about the Empire’s existence)

Story:

Marka Ragnos, the Dark Lord of the Sith and ruler of the Sith Empire, is dead, so there is a power vacuum. The two main contenders for the next Dark Lord are the rivals Naga Sadow and Ludo Kressh. Naga Sadow is a progressive Sith Lord who seeks to expand the Sith Empire and conquer the Republic. Ludo Kressh opposes it because waging war on the Republic is too dangerous, and it is best if the Republic does not learn of the Empire’s existence.

Both would often argue. At the funeral of Marka Ragnos, Sadow says that the Sith Empire has done nothing but wallow in its riches and relive their ancient conquests of its worlds. Sadow would bring their history about how their ancestors were Jedi, banished for their beliefs, and “won’t let that flame extinguish.” Then they would start fighting. Then the ghost of Marka Ragnos appears, retells them the origin of their empire and how this is their Golden age. He warns them that they should act with caution, otherwise the entire Sith Empire might perish. (If only that idiot Sadow listened)

Shortly after the fight, a ship appears, carrying siblings Gav and Jori Daragon. They say they come from the Republic and are interested in settling and starting trade. The Sith capture them. Kressh believes they are Republic spies and wants to execute them. Sadow wants to interrogate them first because they could be the key to the expansion of the Sith Empire. (By the way, they aren’t Republic spies, but just some runaways)

They imprison the siblings in Ziost’s Fortress and the Sith Council begins discussing what to do with the prisoners. Most of the Council members agree with Ludo, so they decide to execute them and destroy any evidence of them ever being here. Sadow, however, cannot let it happen.

Sadow sends his men to steal weapons and technology from Daragon’s ship. Sadow and his men then strike Ziost’s Fortress, free the siblings, using weapons they took from the ship. They kill every Sith in the fortress to make sure there are no witnesses left. Sadow even kills his mentor and close friend Simus without hesitation, which shows how determined he is. Then they leave the Republic weapons behind. The weapons look different from the ones the Sith Empire uses; Sadow even called them strange. Therefore, once other Sith come to investigate and find the weapons, they will believe that there are other Republic spies around and that the Republic is planning an invasion. He then escorts each sibling to a different base of his. He reasons that it is better to keep them separated because if one of them gets captured, the other one will be safe, but that is a lie. He has different reasons to keep them seperated, which I will explain in part 2.

The Sith Council sees the weapons and is fully convinced that the Republic is attacking them. Sadow uses Simus's death to his advantage, saying he will avenge him and that the Sith must take immediate action against the Republic, before the Republic takes over, and nominates himself as the next Sith ruler. Every member of the council, except Ludo Kressh, falls for his speech and supports him becoming the new Dark Lord. Ludo Kressh refuses to accept him as his new lord. He takes his supporters and leaves the Council.

So far, everything is going exactly as Sadow planned.
