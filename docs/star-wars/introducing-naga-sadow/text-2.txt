Setting:

* Character and occupation: Naga Sadow, very powerful Sith sorcerer, Dark Lord of the Sith
* Time period: 5000 BBY (Before Battle of Yavin/Before Episode 4)
* Location: Sith Empire (Ziost) (N.B. Sith does not refer to the religion but to the Sith species; the Republic does not know of the Empire’s existence). Republic(

Story:
Naga Sadow has become the Dark Lord of the Sith and is planning an invasion of the Republic. He still keeps Gav and Jori separated. He teaches Gav Sith sorcery because he is interested in Gav’s potential and wants to become his master. Gav isn’t interested in learning the Force, but Sadow lies to him that those skills will help him get home, because Sadow keeps Gav disinformed about what is happening around and that the Sith are not looking for him and his sister.

Sadow finds out that the siblings’ ship is guarded by Ludo Kressh’s men. He needs the ship because, without it, they won’t know the way from the Sith Empire to the Republic and he cannot just ask Kressh to give him the ship because he is still against Sadow and his wish to attack the Republic. So, Sadow makes a plan to get rid of Ludo and find a way to the Republic.

Sadow sends his men to steal the ship and bring it to the Khar Delba citadel, where he keeps Jori in and orders them to purposefully leave the mask of Sadow on the crime scene because once Ludo Kressh sees the mask, Kressh will have figured out that Sadow was behind all the attacks and not the Republic. Kressh gathers councilmen who aren’t loyal to him and plans to attack his base. That is all, of course, part of Sadow’s plan. Sadow knows that among his men there are Kressh’s spies. So, he tips them off that his main base is the Khar Delba citadel. However, that is a lie. It is his decoy base. Once Kressh’s fleet attacks Sadow’s base, he will tell Jori to take her ship and run to the Republic. Jori does not want to leave without her brother. Sadow reasons with her that he promised to keep them safe and cannot allow them to get captured. He also lies about Ludo’s intentions. He tells her that he wants to torture her and get information about the Republic, exploiting her lack of knowledge about Kressh. Sadow successfully persuades Jori. She goes on her ship and begins travelling to the Republic. He put a tracer on her ship so they could follow her trail later. After Jori leaves, Sadow sends a signal to his fleet on the moon of the planet to emerge and attack Kressh’s ships and also to have the spies on his ship take them over and shoot at the remaining ships. Ludo’s forces are weakened and are forced to retreat.

Sadow takes Gav and begins preparing for his invasion. Sadow gives Gav gems and tells him that he can give him a much better life than the Republic would. Sadows justifies his invasion, saying he the Republic is an oppressive system and that he is liberating people like Gav who were struggling there. He even tells him that Gav will be viewed as a hero on his planet once their takeover succeeds.

Sadow sends his entire fleet to the Republic and begins attacking them. His invasion will be remebered in Star Wars history as the Great Hyperspace War. The Sith have no real tactics or strategy. Sadow’s army is just swarming some major planets. Sadow lets Gav lead his army while Sadow remains on his Meditation Sphere. There, he will be able to “see the whole Galaxy in his mind’s eye” and concentrate on making illusions, making his army seem stronger than it actually is and distract the Republic soldiers.

After witnessing all the bloodshed, Gav finally fully sees through Sadow’s manipulations and turns against him. Sadow tells him to reconsider and that they can do great stuff together. Gav refuses, so Sadow makes a plan to lock up Gav and deal with him after the war. Sadow tells Gav that they should at least discuss this face-to-face on the Meditation Sphere. Gav owes him at least that much, Sadow says. Sadow then damages his Meditation Sphere and leaves it; the only thing which worked was the communication system. Sadow leaves the Meditation Sphere and locks it once Gav boards on it, leaving him stranded near the star Primus Goluud.

Sadow’s forces eventually lose and are forced to retreat. He uses one of his ships to make Primus Goluud use a supernova, which kills Gav and uses it as a cover for his escape. Sadow and his fleet successfully run away from the Republic ships.

Sadow has more appearances, but he has no feats in them and it is really him just taking Ls, so I won’t bother writing about them.

Conclusion:

Low-tier character

Best categories: Deception, Planning, Manipulation, Foresight, Reasoning, Strategy
