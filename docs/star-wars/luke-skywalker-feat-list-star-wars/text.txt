Luke Skywalker feat list

Luke Skywalker intelligence feats (Disney Canon):

**I'll never turn to the Dark Side. You failed, Your Highness. I am a Jedi, like my father before me – Luke Skywalker**

(ROTJ) He created a multilayered plan to rescue Han Solo. First, he sent R2-D2 and C-3PO to Jabba’s Palace as gifts, attempting to peacefully persuade Jabba to release Han. He secretly hid his lightsaber inside R2-D2 in case the negotiation failed, allowing him to resort to combat if necessary. Then, he had Leia and Chewbacca arrive—Leia disguised as the bounty hunter Boushh and Chewbacca as her prisoner—attempting to deceive Jabba into believing she was there to collect Han. Even if that failed, Leia would be in position to free Han manually. Knowing Jabba would eventually capture Leia, Luke entered the palace himself and tried diplomacy, appealing to Jabba’s pride and arrogance. When Jabba refused, Luke allowed himself to be captured, having already anticipated this outcome. With R2-D2 now aboard Jabba’s sail barge and in position, Luke signaled him to launch the hidden lightsaber, which he used to defeat Jabba and his crew. He had also learned from the mistakes of his past plans. This feat showcases \[LA (Learning Ability) PS (Problem Solving) CT (Creative Thinking) EU (Emotional Understanding) NLT (Non Linear Thinking) Foresight and ST (Strategic Thinking)\].

(TLJ) He used a Force Projection against Kylo Ren, appearing in physical form across the galaxy to duel him while not actually being there, buying enough time for the Resistance to escape and deliberately fooling Kylo into believing he was physically present by using Kylo’s own rage against him he also knew he would die afterwards but still chose to do this. This feat showcases \[AS (Acting Skills) EU (Emotional Understanding) DM (Decision Making) LT (Linear Thinking) MF (Mental Fortitude) PS (Problem Solving) CT (Creative Thinking) and ST (Strategic Thinking)\].

(ANH) He appealed to Han Solo’s greed, persuading him to attempt the rescue of Princess Leia by emphasizing the substantial wealth and reward he could gain from the mission. This feat showcases \[EU (Emotional Understanding) PM (Psychological Manipulation) DIM (Direct Manipulation) SOS (Social Skills) and IM (Individual Manipulation)\].

(ANH) He was responsible for the first Death Star’s Destruction with a 65B X-wing Starfighter piloting it with expertise despite having little to no training. This feat showcases \[LA (Learning Ability) Adaptability and LC (Learning Capability)\].

(ROTJ) He recognized that his father was deeply conflicted internally and was being subtly manipulated by Palpatine, who was exploiting his father’s doubts and emotions to further his own agenda. This feat showcases \[EU (Emotional Understanding)\].

(TLJ) After isolating himself for six years of reflection and training, he concludes that the old Jedi Order’s arrogance and detachment contributed to their downfall realising that true balance in the Force requires acknowledging and understanding both the light and the dark within oneself, rather than suppressing it. This feat showcases \[ABR (Abstract Reasoning) EU (Emotional Understanding) NLT (Non Linear Thinking) and ABT (Abstract Thinking)\].

He endured immense trauma including the murder of his aunt and uncle the murder of his mentor Obi-Wan Kenobi the death of Yoda, the loss of his hand to his own father, the revelation that his father was the villain Darth Vader, and the failure of his first Jedi academy, which was destroyed by his nephew, who then fell to the dark side and he still carried on. This feat showcases \[MF (Mental Fortitude) ST (Stress Tolerance) and Perseverance\].

He received his Jedi training under the guidance of Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi for a single day, followed by three years of self-directed training. He then trained with Jedi Grandmaster Yoda on the remote planet of Dagobah for about a month before dueling Darth Vader. Although he initially lost, he learned from that defeat, ultimately returning and being able to fight Vader on equal footing after that he spent six years training in isolation. This feat showcases \[LA (Learning Ability) LC (Learning Capability) CI (Crystallised Intelligence) Adaptability and BI (Battle Intelligence)\].

All Force-Sensitives possess the ability to anticipate their opponents’ movements in combat, allowing them to predict attacks with remarkable precision. They can also detect traps through the Force, granting them an almost preternatural awareness of their surroundings. Furthermore, some like Luke are capable of glimpsing the future through Force visions. Additionally, they can manipulate others through mind tricks, secretly communicate, or read their opponents’ thoughts through telepathy. This feat showcases \[Foresight Intuition SOA (Situational Awareness) EU (Emotional Understanding) and DIM (Direct Manipulation)\].
