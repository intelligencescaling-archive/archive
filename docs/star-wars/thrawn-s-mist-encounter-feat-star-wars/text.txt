Star Wars: Thrawn’s Mist Encounter Feat

This post is about how the Empire discovers Thrawn. The events of Mist Encounter are canon to both EU(Adventure Journal#7) and Disney Star Wars(Thrawn Novel/Comic). This post will show the encounter more from Thrawn's POV, opposed to the journal and novel.

CONTEXT:

Booster Terrik is running from an Imperial Star Destroyer. He makes a desperate hyperjump and gets to the planet on which Thrawn is exiled. The Star Destroyer chases after Terrik on the planet. They send TIE Fighters after him, which he manages to run from and even destroy. One of them crashes near Thrawn’s camp and Thrawn recognises this as his opportunity to escape this planet.

THRAWN’S MECHANATIONS:

Thrawn needs to somehow reach the Star Destroyer in orbit. His only way of getting there is to infiltrate the camp that the Imperials made. The problem is that Thrawn is a Chiss. Chiss is a humanoid alien species with blue skin and the Imperial army consists of humans only. So, Thrawn would need Stormtroopers’ armor to cover his racial features. That is why Thrawn will need to make them send Stormtroopers by making himself seem like a threat.

Thrawn examines the TIE Fighter and the corpse of the pilot. Thrawn hides the body, takes the blasters, grenades and the comlink. That way, he can listen to Imperial's conversation. He then stuffs the armor with grass and berries. He does it for X reasons:

1. The grass and berries will confuse the Imperials. They will be focused on why anyone would put them in the suit so that they would not notice the missing comlink.
2. The Imperials will assume that Thrawn is a group of native group of native primitives. Because of that, they will underestimate Thrawn.
3. Because of how strange the suit is. The Imperials will bring it to the base. The smell of the berries will attract animals. Thrawn put the grenades on small animals that would be attracted to the berries and because it was night, they would not be noticed. He timed the grenades, giving the animals enough time to reach the Imperial camp. The Imperials, of course, would not know that animals are behind the attacks and will think intruders snuck in. Thus causing panic.

Thrawn predicts that they will send ships to search for him, so he sets up traps to crash TIE fighters looking for him- monofilament lines on tall trees. One of the TIE fighters falls for Thrawn’s trap and crashes. Thrawn gets to the crash site before the Imperials. He takes the weapons and switches the comlink with the first one he took. Thrawn knows that by now the Imperials should have figured out that they are not attacked by primitive savages because a primitive would not know how to use grenades(well, at least without blowing himself up). He predicts that because of this, the Imperials would check the armor that they took earlier and find that the comlink is missing. They would then deactivate the first comlink, not knowing Thrawn had switched it with the second one.

Thrawn later uses the berries again. He uses a sling to shoot sentries with the berries. The berry juices on the armor will attract animals. Thrawn will use the animals to track the sentries from far and kill them when he has the chance. He kills 3 of them (and 2 panic and shoot each other) to force the Imperials to send heavily armed soldiers. That way, he can get armor that can hide his skin color.

The next morning, the Imperials send 18 stormtroopers. Thrawn uses the second comlink he stole to jam the communications. He then caused an explosion, which killed a stormtrooper who was on his own, and because the coms were jammed, they didn’t know that an explosion had happened or that a Stormtrooper had died. Because of the jammed communications, the stormtroopers were ordered to camp and take everything from Thrawn’s camp to inspect it later.  He studies the armor so that he can find a way to kill a stormtrooper without damaging the armor much. He manages to kill one of the soldiers from inside and steal his armor. He goes to the camp and then to the ship, which will take them to the Star Destroyer. The ship was empty at the time. Thrawn takes the first armor he stole and makes it stand using smaller branches he took outside the doorway of the ship. He puts explosives in it to destroy it. That way, the Imperials won’t realize that there are actually two missing stormtroopers. Thrawn then goes to hide in the second power generator casting they took from his camp since it was mostly empty.

The captain suspects that the ship may have been intruded so he wants it checked and eventually they find Thrawn.

At the end, we learn more about Thrawn’s motivations and how he does everything to protect his people despite them not agreeing with him and exiling him. The Imperial Captain offers Thrawn to join the Empire and Thrawn agrees since he and his people can also benefit. (I won’t go into details but Thrawn and Palpatine have known each other from before. Maybe I will cover it in the future but not on this post, especially since Palpatine and Thrawn knowing each other is a bit of a retcon, anyway).
