Feat:

Palpatine learns about the destruction of the Death Star. Palpatine is beyond furious with its destruction. Exempt from the chapter describing his anger:

"The Emperor closed his eyes and let the rage consume him.

An energy bolt of anger crackled across his body, turning his blood black with venom.

A red mist clouded the darkness behind his lids. The fog of hate would have shrouded the vision of a lesser man. But when the Emperor opened his eyes, the blood-tinged world was sharper than ever."

Palpatine, however, manages to control his anger and uses it to boost his power in the Force. "The Emperor played with his memory of the moment, polishing it in his mind like a precious gem." Palpatine evn thinks it was neccessary for the start of a new path. I would say this a decent mentality feat for Palpatine, taking advanatge of a bad situation and his anger well.

He, also, uses this moment as a way to test some of his subordinates. He invites ten of the most powerful people in the Empire to his throne room on Coruscant to see how they would react. It is said that Palpatine went through a lot trouble to make sure his throne room looked "awesome and intimidating".  A short description from the novel: "Behind his shadowed throne, a wall of permaplas windows looked into the heart of the Coruscant night." I would say it is decent fearmongering, given some people here are like "He has a creepy smile. ToP tIeR fEaRmOnGeRiNg FeAt" smh...Still Palpatine was what grabbed their attention. The old geezer was scarier than his throne room.

Now for Palpatine reading people's reactions...he likely used the Force. This invalidates parts of the his reading of the ten people he summoned but I don't think his readings were **fully** carried by the Force since some of the reactions I think Palpatine would just need basic observation skills.
