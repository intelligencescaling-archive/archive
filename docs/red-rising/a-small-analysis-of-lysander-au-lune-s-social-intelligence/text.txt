a small analysis of Lysander Au Lune’s social intelligence

before i get into the subcats i should probably tell yall abt bro since im sure most dont know him. Lysander is the main antagonist of Red Rising after the trilogy. he is also my favorite antagonist of all time so i wanna talk abt him. his best category is easily social intelligence with maybe fsiq coming close.

this is from a first read knowledge so ofc i wont understand everything perfectly since that always requires at least a 2nd time reading of anything.

Social Awareness, during the battle of mercury(one of my favorite arcs oat.) lysander after only being there for a short time is able to understand how much of the planet dislikes the solar republic and wants the society back. he uses this for his plan to invade the planet from the inside. before this when he was first captured, he knew how to act in order to get darrows attention. he pretended to he a stereotypical gold who enjoyed relishing in slaves and glory.

Social Engineering, the feat prior with using mercury’s population could work here. another feat is that lysander was able to manipulate a large group of the society on one meeting to invade mars starting with one moon. he did this by bringing up the core weakness with gold, its lack of unity and how it caused their loss. he also brought up that despite what they’ve been told for years, they can invade the capital and bring the society back into power. he appealed to their egos and yearning for power in order to gain more favor. mind you, during this time he literally had ppl who wanted to kill him and he convinced them onto his side.

Leadership, he was the one person(at least shown) to have ever united most of the society. the only reason he didnt end up controlling all of the society is bcuz of a secret mastermind who was basically controlling everything. that same mastermind who wants to work with lysander bcuz he sees him as the society’s biggest hope for unity and prosperity. he was able to lead the invasion into mars and took a moon.

Networking, almost everything mentioned would apply here.

Social Skills, he has multiple instances of convincing  ppl who have full intent go kill him to work with him. he knows how to unite the most egotistical ppl ever into working for him. he convinced one of the most machiavellian characters in the series to not kill an entire planets population. he manipulated his aunt who was going to assassinate him into having a romantic relationship with each other(yeah this part was fucked especially when you know more context)

Self Awareness, yeah so this is where things begin to change. before those feats are pretty good right? well he has terrible self awareness. so instead of listing feats i’ll list anti feats. he has a massive identity crisis. he is constantly betraying ppl for a goal he at first didnt know he truly wanted. he changes his perspective on himself and his ideals constantly bcuz of his obsession with his freedom to think. he deludes himself into thinking what he is doing makes lower colors happy, so much so he promises a slave he forced into combat that he gets to talk to him at any time. despite having an ability to give himself perfect memory with perfect detail, he somehow forces a core memory out which was essential for knowing who he is. this guy srsly has an identity crisis as bad as vader and can trade blows with johan.

as of currently i think he is a mid tier character, maybe if this is well received i might do fsiq next, or a small analysis of his scheme. or doing smth like this for another character who has never been mentioned before.
