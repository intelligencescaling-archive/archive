Lysander Au Lune’s Mind Eye(Red Rising)

my second analysis on lysander which is funny this is the second considering his FSIQ is more impressive than his SQ

for those who haven’t read Red Rising, Lysander is the main antagonist and one of the POV characters.

his “Minds Eye” is an ability which allows him to view things in slow motion, while also processing things in superhuman speeds

Im just gonna go over some impressive instances of his usage of Minds Eye, and yes i believe it can be used in SCD bcuz it is a biological hax special to him.

# Processing Being Poisoned

during his siege of Mars, he was doing surprisingly too well for Atlas’s liking. Atlas needed to halt the siege, and his method? poisoning Lysander with an incapacitating drug.

this particular drug is incredibly powerful, not only does it require a strong drug to incapacitate a gold in the first place, but the symptoms absolutely destroyed Lysander.

however, while being drugged, he was able to use his Minds Eye to still process his surroundings, keep composure, locate who could’ve given him the drug, make theories on who had given him the drug(which if i recall i think he was somewhat correct), and process what everyone else around him was doing.

also, using minds eye, he tries to prevent the spread of the poison all while this is going on, but he fails.

this drug is also meant to do temporary brain damage, and put the body in paralysis while doing an excruciating amount of pain.

# Suppressing His Hatred

Lysander during the events of lightbringer is planning to usurp Atalantia from her position after learning that she caused his parents death.

Lysander would obviously feel a lot of powerful emotions however he needs to hide them in order for deceive Atalantia about his true feelings and to hide the fact that he knows she killed his parents. she does find out he is trying to deceive her but only because she has Atlas helping her and bcuz she found a partner of his and tortured him for information. also, she was incredibly paranoid in the first place.

this is a mid feat but i included it to show how he has complete control of his emotions whenever he uses mind’s eye.

# Using It During An Ambush

Atalantia sends golds to kill him so that he can’t use his Lune heritage to usurp her.

Lysander, who is fairly unexperienced in fighting other golds especially during an ambush comes up with a strategy to kill them using his mind’s eye.

Lysander was also incredibly exhausted and wounded.

Lysander uses the minds eye to completely reconstruct the battlefield after blinding everyone including himself to give him an advantage.

He then fights 7 assassins while still blind, these assassins.

He then loots them while having a conversation, still blinded then goes north. once again, still blinded so he is somehow able to process he is going north after everything that transpired.

# Perfect Memory

kinda self explanatory, he remembers every moment of his life. if it happens, then it’s in his head until he dies.

not only that but he experiences every sensation perfectly, including when his grandmother would practically torture him as a child in order to physically train him

in fact his memory was so perfect, he had to get his memory wiped for over 2 weeks to forget about his parents death

# Darrow’s Ship Calculations

now this is gonna be simplified and missing the actual math made, but i will probably do a post abt that at some point.

Lysander wants to deduce what is going on in Darrow’s flag ship. Lysander, all in a very short time observes skiffs are riding in the atmosphere, measures the visual displacement against their known load capacity, and deduces they are overloaded

using his measurements of the skiffs, measures the mass to volume of the cargo.

using his memories of the morning star(darrows ship) to measure it’s dimensions. then determines the exact mass is of a mineral called iridium. then he measures the volume of iridium being hauled in the ship.

he concludes that Darrow is likely making a EMP device based on his calculations of the iridium.

how much of mind’s eye being used is unknown, but he was def using it.
