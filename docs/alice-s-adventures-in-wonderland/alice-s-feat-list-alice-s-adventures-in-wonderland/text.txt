Alice’s feat list

This post presents a list of feats attributed to Alice from *Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland* and its sequel, *Through the Looking-Glass*, both written by Lewis Carroll.

It is not intended to serve as a compilation of every feat demonstrated by the character, but rather as a selection highlighting her most noteworthy feats.

I will also highlight which SCD categories are in each feat.

Feats:

She subconsciously constructed two country-sized dream worlds, Wonderland and the Looking-Glass World, in seconds creating all of the characters and locations from both worlds. She also in seconds subconsciously constructed the inverted system of logic, the mirrored system of logic, the mirrored language, the characters dialogues, and the actions and personalities of all the characters.

At seven years old, she endured size distortion, nearly suffocating to death from a caterpillar’s smoke, nearly drowning to death in a pool of her own tears, total isolation, time dilation, inverted logic, insults, death threats, riddles, an identity crisis, being framed for assault and being framed for theft all in Wonderland. Rather than succumbing to it all. She persisted learning to accept and exploit the illogicality to her advantage, maintaining her resolve until she finally escaped. She also endured the Rabbit Hole. Which is described as being so deep that it passes the Earth’s Core. Despite this, while Alice is falling, she is not screaming. She screamed only once during the initial drop, then quickly composed herself, calmly observing her surroundings as she descended. During the fall, she began calculating the distance she had traveled, trying to determine how many miles she had fallen. Drawing on her past geography lessons, she concluded that the Rabbit Hole must be deep enough to stretch from Victorian England to New Zealand or Australia. More Importantly, Alice did not believe this was a dream. From her perspective, the fall was real—and was going to be fatal. Yet she still calmed herself.
