She gassed Young Sigma on December 25, 2028, making his consciousness jump to 2074 so he could experience the AB Project. After explaining everything at the end of the AB Project, she sent Sigma back to April 13, 2029, the day the Radical-6 outbreak began, so he could work with her past self for 45 years to build the AB Project. After 45 years, she then put Old Sigma’s consciousness back into Young Sigma’s body on December 25, 2028, completing the loop and making the jump from Point B to A possible.

This feat showcases \[NCPS (Novel Complex Problem Solving) LT (Linear Thinking) ST (Strategic Thinking) IDM (Indirect Manipulation) IM (Individual Manipulation) PC (Planning Convergence) CT (Creative Thinking) and SC (Strategy Complexity)\].

She was trapped in an incinerator as a child by Ace and would have died, creating a time paradox that would erase her adult self. To prevent this, she later orchestrated the Second Nonary Game to guide Junpei, via morphogenetic resonance, onto the correct path, allowing him to solve the incinerator sudoku puzzle for her past self via morphogenetic resonance, saving her life, and since she broke out of the paradox, she was then able to kill everyone involved in creating the first Nonary Game in one fell swoop, both saving herself and getting revenge as part of her plan.

This feat showcases \[NCPS (Novel Complex Problem Solving) TE (Trap Evasion) PS (Problem Solving) LM (Logical Manipulation) ST (Stress Tolerance) IDM (Indirect Manipulation) IM (Individual Manipulation) LT (Linear Thinking) LGT (Logical Thinking) ST (Strategic Thinking) PC (Planning Convergence) CT (Creative Thinking) and SC (Strategy Complexity)\].

She created a plan that both ensured the AB Project’s completion and guaranteed her own resurrection. Before the game even began, she was stabbed and killed by Dio; foreseeing this, she arranged beforehand for Sigma and Phi to send their consciousnesses back in time from another timeline to prevent her death. She then went back to the starting area and assumed the armour of a mysterious person in the AB Games named K, effectively turning herself into a Schrödinger’s cat. Because Sigma knows that it is either she or Kyle Klim inside the suit, but both remain in the box until Sigma’s actions reveal the truth.

This feat showcases \[MF (Mental Fortitude) NCPS (Novel Complex Problem Solving) TE (Trap Evasion) PS (Problem Solving) LT (Linear Thinking) LGT (Logical Thinking) ST (Strategic Thinking) PC (Planning Convergence) CT (Creative Thinking) IDM (Indirect Manipulation) PM (Psychological Manipulation) Foresight and SC (Strategy Complexity)\].

She uses Luna, the android, a GAULEM designed by the future Sigma to look like Diana. She uses her to make past Sigma Klim experience love for the first time, and then makes Luna end up self-terminating after being forced to disobey her orders to save humanity, violating the laws of robotics. The end goal of this was to make him later fall in love with Diana by having him experience love with someone like her beforehand. This also shows she understood Sigma's emotions enough to create this whole plan.

This feat showcases \[PM (Psychological Manipulation) NCPS (Novel Complex Problem Solving) PS (Problem Solving) LM (Logical Manipulation) DIM (Direct Manipulation) IDM (Indirect Manipulation) IM (Individual Manipulation) EU (Emotional Understanding) HR (Hot Reading) LT (Linear Thinking) LGT (Logical Thinking) ST (Strategic Thinking) PC (Planning Convergence) CT (Creative Thinking) and SC (Strategy Complexity)\].

She deceived Gentarou Hongou into believing she genuinely cared for him despite actually hating him. And understood and anticipated Gentarou Hongou's personality, in order to create the perfect chain of events that would lead to Junpei uncovering his past and lead Hongou to his own fiery death.

This feat showcases \[PM (Psychological Manipulation) IM (Individual Manipulation) DIM (Direct Manipulation) EM (Emotional Management) AS (Acting Skills) IDM (Indirect Manipulation) NCPS (Novel Complex Problem Solving) PS (Problem Solving) LT (Linear Thinking) ST (Strategic Thinking) PC (Planning Convergence) CT (Creative Thinking) SC (Strategy Complexity) and Deception\].

She withstood the pain of her past self burning alive while experiencing the pain of being stabbed to death and still maintaining enough composure to talk to Junpei and manipulate him further.

This feat showcases \[MF (Mental Fortitude) ST (Stress Tolerance) and Perseverance\].

She calculated the digital roots of combinations of people's numbered bracelets and determined how their roots change when the actual values of her and her brother's are included.

This feat showcases \[WMI (Working Memory Index)\].

After seeing brief visions of future timelines over the course of a year she deciphered her future plan from them.

This feat showcases \[IR (Inductive Reasoning)\].
