Akane Kurashiki EE/EM feat analysis

**(SPOILERS FOR ZERO TIME DILEMMA)**

The feat in question: 16:49-20:00 of this video ([https://youtu.be/BBC4I9Vajrc?si=YnHnm\_1evvuhw\_kg](https://youtu.be/BBC4I9Vajrc?si=YnHnm_1evvuhw_kg))

**Explanation:** Prior to the timestamp I provided, Akane, Junpei and Delta were having a lengthy chat about what the purpose of the Decision Game really is (Delta didn't reveal everything but still). Afterwards, Akane and Junpei with the help of Carlos escape the Decision Game facility but then shortly learn that they're in the timeline where Radical-6 escaped (they had previously shifted timelines so they weren't aware)

Upon learning this, Akane states that she has to start all over again and build the AB Project to prevent this timeline from occuring. Junpei doesn't know what she's talking about but wants to help Akane anyways (Junpei and Akane are lovers btw), Akane says she can't and then stabs him with her Sorporil which will shortly erase Junpei's memories of that exact moment. She simply asks Junpei to forget about her, says that she'll do anything to accomplish her goals to Carlos and then walks off without ever saying goodbye to Junpei.

**Why this feat is impressive:** The impressiveness of this feat becomes apparent when you consider exactly what Junpei means to Akane. I mentioned that they're lovers but it goes so much deeper than that. Akane during the events of 999 was literally inside Junpei's consciousness for the entire ride as a 12 year old, she loves him just as she loves herself or perhaps even more so. Her entire motivation for the VLR/ZTD project is to create a timeline where she can spend the rest of her life with Junpei in peace.

Prior to this scene, in another timeline. There's a moment where Junpei dies under mysterious circumstances and Akane thinks that Carlos is the one who did it. Although she didn't even have proof that it was Carlos who killed her bf, she almost immediately went into a blind frenzy, chasing down Carlos with a chainsaw and crying profusely before eventually catching up to him and bashing his head in until he dies a brutal death. Akane cares about Junpei so much that even her THINKING that someone had killed him caused her to absolutely lose all semblance of sanity, and yet she was able to completely override such immense feelings she has for Junpei all for the sake of her goals.

**What this means:** Akane states it herself, she will spend the next 45 years on the moon in complete isolation away from the person she loves arguably more deeply than herself. Basically, she instantly switches to how she acts in the VLR game and just one look at one scene of her in 999 VS her in VLR will tell you just how sheer of a difference it is in their mindset. Akane goes from being a very emotional person to having basically none at all, she goes from caring about Junpei with all her heart to still caring about him but willing to completely throw him aside for the sake of her plan and she does all of this with practically ZERO emotional turmoil, showcasing her instant transformation (and no, Akane did not go through the AB Project herself already from her POV, it's the first time she had to do this)

Basically, she completely transformed her entire neurology and personality within seconds without any noticeable difficulty as well. She personally erased the memories of her BF and just walked away without saying goodbye to him when previously she cared about him so deeply that she brutally murdered another man just because she thought he had killed Junpei. Which I think is incredibly goated in EE/EM
