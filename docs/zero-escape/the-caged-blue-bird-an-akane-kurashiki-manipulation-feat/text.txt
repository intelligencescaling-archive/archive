Part one: the setup

This is Sigma Klim

Fanart of Sigma (left) and Phi (right)

Sigma Klim is the main protagonist of Zero Escape Virtue’s Last Reward, the second game of the Zero Escape series and is the character that you play as throughout the entire game. He is a compassionate, intelligent and a highly driven person who, despite some of his flaws, always makes sure to do what’s right for everyone around him. He has done nothing evil and is by all accounts, a good person.

This doc is an analysis on how Akane Kurashiki completely and utterly ruined his life

But first, it’s important to understand the overall story of Zero Escape VLR and ZTD as those two games are incredibly interconnected and relevant to the story of Sigma and how Akane manipulated him throughout decades in order to achieve her plans.

Virtue’s Last Reward (VLR) exists in the timeline as a sort of “bad ending” to the entire Zero Escape story, it is an alternate timeline compared to the “good ending” and it’s entirely started within the game of Zero Time Dilemma (ZTD). Specifically, it was caused by the events of D-END 1 in the game but I will explain what that ending is later and why it’s important

The earliest game in the series, Zero Escape 999, takes place in 2027 and is the earliest playable point in the ZE timeline. Zero Time Dilemma, the third game, takes place only one year after 999 in late 2028 to early 2029 and is, of course, the second earliest playable point in the ZE story.

Virtue’s Last Reward, however, takes place in 2074. A full 45 years after the events of ZTD. This is important to keep in mind as I will be talking about the events of VLR first before I talk about ZTD, despite it coming way later in the Zero Escape Timeline. This will make sense later but for now just understand that it’s the correct thing to do to play VLR before ZTD as the events of ZTD don’t make sense without VLR, despite it coming earlier in the timeline.

It is also very important to understand the general concept of SHIFTing in the Zero Escape series. Due to the sci-fi elements of the story, certain characters are capable of “SHIFTing”, which allows them to transfer their consciousness across time and even to alternate timelines but only under certain conditions. Characters that have this ability include Sigma Klim and Akane Kurashiki, which is why I mention that Akane and Sigma’s story goes across many years and timelines. Just note that it’s not Sigma’s body that travels through time, only his consciousness.

Part three: the ab project, reprised

We find ourselves at the beginning, the starting point of the AB Project in 2029. Diana, who had not been a part of the Project at all nor even of any particular help, requested to join the two of them for their mission on the moon. Akane,  despite having no real reason to include her, decides to let her join anyway.

Something immediately stuck out about Diana to Sigma, a certain necklace that he had only seen one other person wear. A caged Blue Bird that also doubled as a musicbox. It was identical to Luna’s own necklace. This is probably what caused Sigma to develop a connection to Diana in the first place. Her looks combined with the necklace, it reminded him so much of Luna, the android he deeply cared for if not loved, but it was otherwise a relationship cut too short due to the tragic events of the Luna Ending.

As days turned into months turned into years on the moon, Sigma and Diana became close and eventually fell in love. Diana tells Sigma about her own life, her past and her experiences which strengthens their bond. Diana helped out whenever she could with Sigma’s research and study on the moon in order to build the AB Project but 3 years after arriving on the moon, tragedy struck. Due to the moon’s low gravity, Diana developed a circulatory illness that, despite having access to the best medical technologies, ended up killing Diana, cutting her life short.

Grief-stricken and overwhelmed, Sigma poured everything he could into the AB Project, including developing a line of androids named GAULEM’s to handle the manual labour aspects of building the AB Project. In honour of his dead lover, Sigma took one of the GAULEM’s and molded it into something new, a special purpose android that took on the resemblance of Diana. Having kept Diana’s Blue Bird necklace this entire time, Sigma gives it to his creation for her to keep.

He named his creation: Luna

Summary:

As is common with a lot of Zero Escape plotlines, the love story between Sigma and Luna/Diana is a Bootstrap Paradox.

Young Sigma (the one who’s going through VLR for the first time) comes to deeply care/love the android called Luna →  Young Sigma gets his consciousness sent back to 2029 to start on the AB Project he already went through and meets Diana → Young Sigma becomes Old Sigma as he works on the AB Project alongside Diana, who he falls in love with → Diana dies and Old Sigma is heartbroken → Old Sigma creates Luna to resemble his old lover Diana → Old Sigma, upon the completion of the AB Project, gets his consciousness sent back to 2029 but this time it’s to take part in the ZTD Decision Game → Young Sigma gets kidnapped by Akane and is forced to take part in the now-completed AB Project → Young Sigma falls in love with Luna.
It’s a loop, crafted and facilitated by Akane Kurashiki.

Also, it’s theorycrafting as it’s never been outright confirmed in game, but there’s strong reason to believe that Akane either directly or indirectly caused Diana to die so that Sigma could create Luna. Isn’t it odd that Diana died from the negative effects of living in low gravity after only 3 years but the same thing didn’t happen to Sigma or Akane even after 45 years? From this I believe it’s safe to conclude that it was Akane’s fault that Diana died as it was aligned with her goals.
