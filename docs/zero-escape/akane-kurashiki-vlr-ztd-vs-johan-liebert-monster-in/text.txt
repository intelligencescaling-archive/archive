Akane Kurashiki (VLR/ZTD) VS Johan Liebert (Monster/Another Monster) in Emotional Intelligence (WITH PROOF)

Inspired by a lovely post made earlier today on [Koji VS Light in EI](https://www.reddit.com/r/IntelligenceScaling/s/N7T69L2Bju) where they provided solid arguments (and more importantly, scans) for every claim. I'd prefer it if every post on the subreddit was like this so I guess it's time to be the change I want to see in the world and use this as an opportunity to show how my Akane scaling has risen since a year ago where I previously claimed that she was even "bad" in this category. The sources for all my claims will be at the bottom of this post.

**Categories:**
For this post I'll be using a basic split of Emotional Understanding (EU) and Emotional Perception (EP) for Interpersonal EI and Emotional Management (EM) and Emotional Engagement (EE) for Intrapersonal EI. I'll go through each subcat one-by-one with explanations for each characters best feat in that particular category.

**Interpersonal EI (EU/EP): Akane Kurashiki**
Johan Liebert's EU/EP can best be summarized with his Beast of Revelations narrative, which is maintained at both the beginning and end of the manga. To quote directly from Fall's doc on Johan for what the significance of this symbolism is: "In *The Revelation of Saint John the Divine*, John symbolically explains his visions of the battle between good and evil. The evil that opposes everything the kingdom of God stands for is represented by a beast; this beast has the authority of the dragon (Satan), and as a result of Satan endlessly creating new ones to compose it, it's incapable of being stopped no matter how much you get rid of it. The beast uses these attributes to make people he's capable of exercising control over worship him (anyone who isn't a part of God.) The reason this is important to know is because this verse (particularly the beast) is used by Urasawa to represent Johan. Johan is the beast, he figuratively has the authority of the dragon, he is inevitable. Which is to say that as long as someone is evil/not a part of God, Johan can potentially control them."

This goes to show that Johan stands above all of humanity in terms of his Emotional and Social Intelligence to where he's practically an other-wordily being. As far as direct feats go, a good example of how far this narrative gets pushed is the Jurgen's feat, where Johan is able to accurately profile a criminals entire backstory and recreate the scene of his trauma with literally no information, just raw EU.

However, this still pales in comparison to Akane's EU. Similarly to Johan, Akane's EU is summarized in the form of an analogy. Zero Sr describes Akane as a "higher order intelligence" that literally looks above humanity like a human looks over a termite mound\[1\]. This is further backed up and exemplified by Akane's own explanation of the Morphogenetic Field. To summarize, the Morphogenetic Field is a 4th dimensional plane that all human consciousness resides on. Akane compares human consciousness to a 2D subsection of tree roots viewed from the bottom, from that POV, it looks like each of the roots are independant, but when viewed from a higher dimension, one can see how all the roots connect to each other and are apart of the same system. Human consciousness, according to Akane, acts similarly as humans appear to be individuals with their own minds in 3D but in the 4th Dimension, one can see how it's all connected\[2\].

These two explanations combined with Tenmyouji's description of how Akane has changed, saying that she's "not even human anymore" and how she's "not anywhere" (in space and time)\[3\] along with the general functionality of her feats (see the Phi Ending of VLR), it can be safely claimed that the "higher order intelligence" that Zero Sr referred to is Akane herself. This means that Akane, despite being a 3D being with a 3D brain, is able to literally peer into a 4th spacial dimension to view the totality of human consciousness in the same way that a human looks over a termite mound, implying a vastly superior understanding/cognition compared to what they the human are observing. And no, this has nothing to do with Akane's abilities as an Esper, there is no other Esper in Zero Escape that has ever come close to performing anything like this and there's nothing about Esper abilities shown that even potentially *could* lead to this, meaning that this feat was achieved from raw Cognition/EI alone.

Basically, unless a character also as 4D levels of cognition and emotional understanding, they lose to Akane in Inter EI by default. Since Johan is also just a 3D human, he cannot ever hope to reach the same level of emotional understanding and perception as Akane.

**Intrapersonal EI (EE/EM): Akane Kurashiki**
From what I can tell, Johan's best EE/EM is the fact that he's able to manipulate his own memories, taking in Nina's story genuinely feeling as though he experienced her memories himself. If any Johan scalers wanna correct me on this and provide better EE/EM for Johan, let me know in the comments.

Either way, I can't see a world where Johan doesn't get completely gapped by Akane in this regard. I made a [brief analysis on Akane's EE/EM](https://www.reddit.com/r/IntelligenceScaling/s/WP1DADJLu6) a while ago and I'll be using the same feat here (source provided in the post). In summary, Akane is able to radically transform her entire persona and perhaps her whole neurology within seconds. Junpei is someone that Akane cared about so deeply to the point where his death drove her LITERALLY insane in two different timelines, yet she was able to coldly toss him aside and ask Junpei to forget about her before leaving to abandon him for 45 years, not even looking back to say goodbye. Even 45 years later when they meet again in VLR, she acts cold and uncaring towards him, barely even exchanging a word towards the man who was the love of her life, a man who's brain she had literally been inside of and got to live as him for several hours, a man she loved like she loved herself.

It gets better. In ZTD, despite being the one to orchestrate the games which meant she had knowledge of all events and possible timelines that would transpire, she still willingly placed herself in C Team and subjugated herself to half a dozen gruesome deaths in many different timelines. Even better, she doesn't even SHIFT her consciousness out of these situations. SHIFTing away from the threat of death is shown to be an involuntary reflex in the end of VLR, yet Akane is able to temper this reflex many, many times despite the cruel and painful nature of her deaths. Despite knowing that they were coming, she still showcased genuine fear over them as well\[4\]\[5\]\[6\].

All of this (among others) comes together to paint a picture of Akane's intra EI. Basically, I believe she has completely control over her neurology. She's able to override the philosophical impossibility of "wanting to want" something (Eg: No matter how hard you try, if you don't already like to be tortured and don't already *want* to like torture, you cannot make yourself genuinely like torture) by both genuinely caring over Junpei's life enough to drive her insane yet discarding that at a moments notice whenever convenient and being able to both genuinely feel fear over her death yet temper the desire to avoid it anyways. She can well and truly become and do anything all for the sake of her goals. She basically as Souichi's Perfect Mode at base.

**Conclusion:**
**Akane Kurashiki No Diffs**
Akane takes all four categories without any difficulty.
