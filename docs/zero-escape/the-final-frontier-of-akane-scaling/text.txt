Just when I thought I was done, just when I thought Akane's scaling couldn't be taken any higher, she still managed to surprise me one last time. To tell the truth, I've known about this aspect of Akane's scaling for quite a while, I've just dismissed it until now for reasons I will get into later.

First, let's go over an overlooked ending of VLR: ["Another Time"](https://youtu.be/vyQaEB87DKE?si=C8KnXVOd8fJvPxOi)

In this ending, Kyle Klim (Sigma's clone/son) wakes up from his pod and begins talking to the participants of the AB Project, learning more about what happened within it. This continues until "Kyle" talks directly to Akane, who explains to "Kyle" (who is labeled as "?" in the dialogue box) that he is not actually the consciousness of Kyle Klim. She also states that "?" is an esper that can move freely through time, and that he is an "uncontrolled variable"

https://preview.redd.it/j1dama3bd2oh1.png?width=1322&format=png&auto=webp&s=e0f055c97fbb16af92f902bd0056159377b6bf8f

https://preview.redd.it/axqinkmed2oh1.png?width=1232&format=png&auto=webp&s=1c69306da38b8abc83388916c8c8f4f25c31229b

Just as "?" starts to get confused, Akane interjects with **"Please don't try to play dumb, I know what you are. Surely you must know by now too."** She then directly states that "?" will go into ZTD and that only he can save the world. To cut to the chase, this is Akane implicitly stating that "?" is the player themselves, and that the player has a role within her plan to save the world.

Now, before I get into the implications of where this scales Akane, I'll explain why I dismissed this aspect of her for so long. The main thing here is that Uchikoshi, the author of Zero Escape, explicitly called this ending ***Non-Canonical*** and therefore, does not apply to Akane at all...But I neglected something else he stated about this ending, twice.

https://preview.redd.it/15866zn6e2oh1.png?width=589&format=png&auto=webp&s=1b8f99f0b4e41770ab1d81e99e1e3032c1e9d132

https://preview.redd.it/t9st396ae2oh1.png?width=801&format=png&auto=webp&s=63c6a8fc4bd869e208177b05749e0f3c42064062

Somehow, a non-canon VLR ending is canon to ZTD? What's up with that? I simply decided to ignore it until I was ready to re-examine ZTD, which I did with the [Force Quit Strategy doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1CWdKH3jZ4wD9oq2haRTIahYZLU9JzNMOTd0vdbQuqzc/edit?usp=sharing). I can say for certain now that Uchikoshi is telling the truth, "?" most certainly was in ZTD and I will prove it later. But for starters, why does Uchikoshi maintain that Another Time is non-canonical yet "?" is canon to ZTD? Well, my answer to that is the actual explanations given in that ending are canon, but the actual events of the ending itself is not canon. That would explain why Uchikoshi has put this aspect of the story into a schrodingers cat of canonicity, he didn't want readers to think that this ending is showing actual events that happened after the Phi Ending.

Ok, now for the proof that "?" really did interfere with the Decision Game. We really need to look no further than Force Quit C.

In C END 1, Akane, Junpei and Delta all have a yap session with each other while Carlos is outside the facility thanks to his (really just Akane's) plan, ready to rescue them. Before this rescue happens, Delta reveals to Akane and Junpei the password for their Force Quit Box: VIVEHODIE.

https://preview.redd.it/qldqvoi3f2oh1.png?width=1322&format=png&auto=webp&s=eea533138f90e57e73c4b19740f7a1191b55658d

As I said and as you can see here, Carlos is nowhere nearby. In fact, he's not in the facility at all, and this ending swiftly moves on without Junpei or Akane revealing this Vive Hodie code to Carlos. So, now that we've established that Carlos could not have possibly known the Vive Hodie passcode...how on Earth did he know it in the Force Quit ending for C Team?

https://preview.redd.it/8hc9bweaf2oh1.png?width=1325&format=png&auto=webp&s=ec12ee828a01f311fb59fe6b751a7fb53fc8a7dc

The Force Quit C ending is literally Carlos conjuring the VIVEHODIE passcode from absolutely nowhere with even Akane being shocked at this

https://preview.redd.it/rm1ec9ief2oh1.png?width=1317&format=png&auto=webp&s=cf59b51a8e45e3c9979c547b4475cd7c5673dc34

The only possible way Carlos could have known about the VIVEHODIE passcode is because we, the players, also heard it. We were there when it was revealed to Akane and Junpei after all. This is also why Uchikoshi stated that "?" (us, the player) has an ability similar to Delta's mind hacking, ***because as the player, we control the decisions of Carlos, Diana and Sean.*** We pass on information that only we know through to those characters in order to influence their decisions, **and Akane's plan rested quite heavily on that.**
