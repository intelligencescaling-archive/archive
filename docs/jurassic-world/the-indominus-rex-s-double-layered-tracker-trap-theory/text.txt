The Indominus Rex’s double-layered tracker trap (THEORY APPLICATION)

Hello!! As some of you may know, I released a post detailing a compelling theory that attempts to frame the I-Rex’s actions throughout Jurassic World as coherent. This was done because, obviously, its actions seem rather disjointed or even downright unclear without deep analysis. If you haven’t seen the post, here —> https://www.reddit.com/r/IntelligenceScaling/s/uiofEVWeeH

To summarize, the theory posits that the I-Rex had reasoned out the rough conceptual existence of the velociraptors far before the events of the films (or at least anticipated the POSSIBLE existence of another species she might be capable of communicating with), with one of her main goals actually being to come into contact with them (which serves the greater purpose of counteracting the humans).

This post will act as a direct extension and application of this theory, applying it to her removal of the tracker in the film and adequately elaborating on why it’s actually a brilliant strategy/double-layered trap according to the theory.

**[ FIRST LAYER — TRANSLOCATION REVERSAL ]**

To preface this layer, it’s worth noting the I-Rex had specifically fabricated a false profile for the park staff to work with. This profile portrays the I-Rex as a primal beast, and not a being of intelligent thought (we know this isn’t true, obviously - Owen states it multiple times over and she shows far beyond average cognitive skills). This profile revolves around several fabricated details and omitted abilities —>

1. The I-Rex had notably killed its sibling at birth, displaying the trait of primal aggression or an overwhelming need for dominance. Now, this serves the dual purpose of concealing the abilities of her genus by ensuring she’s the only subject park staff has to monitor - using the surface-level perspective of her simply acting aggressive as a shield.

2. The I-Rex had concealed its ability to camouflage for years, however it made sure to showcase several other abilities as red-herrings - this includes supposed thermo-vision or an acute sense of smell (which is further supported by when Owen directly plays on this sense of smell to evade the I-Rex, showing her only the abilities she has directly displayed are the ones the humans are capable of countering).

3. Finally, the I-Rex’s paddock escape proved to her that the park staff have no knowledge on her abilities outside of the ones she purposefully showed them. Her escape, in actuality, was a filter-test to observe if the humans had extra information she’d need to account for. If she escapes, it directly indicates they have no knowledge on her concealed abilities (due them failing to foresee her thermo-heat masking), which she can utilize as a confirmation her future endeavors (specifically revolving around the raptors) will work. Not only this, it also ties into the tracker - since her paddock escape worked, it directly indicates the tracker hasn’t been activated just yet (which sets the tracker removal trap up perfectly for the I-Rex). As a branching side-observation, it shows her the park staff usually make hasty decisions when a threat is present - which becomes the premise for her predictions later on. This ensures her next steps are guaranteed to operate smoothly, building the fortification of her strategy.

Fundamentally, the I-Rex had set up a filtering system to ensure her strategy will not be countered - through her paddock escape, she ensured the primary tool she utilized to enact it was her thermo-masking, a trait she notably concealed. With that, the I-Rex also purposefully displayed several abilities to park staff (such as her sense of smell or thermo-detection). Finally, she curated a false profile of pure primal aggression (as well as tying into the limited pool of abilities she gave the humans observation to), which would contradict the idea she could possibly outsmart the humans later on (which is a canonical fact Owen states several times). The end result of these preparations is two main layers of strategic fortification now given to the I-Rex —>

1. A false profile to misdirect park staff, consisting of an intentional framing of her intellect being lower than what it actually is (which Owen states as a possibility - simply not to the level of what was actually happening, due to the fact he was still deceived at several key moments).

2. The I-Rex now has direct confirmation that the park staff don’t have the knowledge on her concealed abilities specifically, ensuring her main goal of raptor contact is fortified (she accomplished this through a complex ability filtering system in the form of her paddock escape, as covered above).

With these factors in place, the I-Rex enacts the main portion of the trap’s first layer. She removes her tracker from her body, having memorized where the staff had put it in when she was smaller - purposefully leaving it in a sizable chunk of her own flesh afterwards. The I-Rex anticipated that upon her escape, the tracker will be activated - thus, she set it out as a trap to reverse this method of capturing her. With this, two outcomes are now in play —>

1. The park staff track the I-Rex and send a team out to recapture her. This is the outcome the I-Rex naturally anticipated, removing the tracker to set a counter-trap. The I-Rex then utilizes her concealed abilities (camouflage) to eliminate the team, with the new confirmation she’s obtained through the paddock escape filtering (being that the park staff doesn’t know of her concealed abilities specifically) to ensure the park staff are unable to foresee this occurrence.

2. If the park staff don’t track the I-Rex (which is pretty unlikely), it gives her free rein to the park. This is obviously detrimental, as even the staff state they need to recapture her (acknowledging the impact of this outcome). This also acts as a secondary smaller filtering system, where if the park staff doesn’t respond it implies they now have knowledge on her concealed abilities (albeit roughly). In this scenario, it deadlocks the park staff from responding to the escape - still progressing the I-Rex’s scheme regardless of information uncovering or counteractive response.

With this trap now enacted, it serves multiple purposes (all of which benefit the I-Rex greatly) —>

1. Notably, it eliminates an entire large squad of the park’s internal defense force - this ensures their resources are depleted, increasing the likelihood they’d turn to alternative means (such as the raptors, as seen later on) to recapture her, which is precisely the entire goal of the strategy.

2. In the same sense, it instills panic within the park staff. This obviously results in hasty decisions (such as the weaponization of the raptors), which the I-Rex has directly observed as a trait of the park staff - her paddock escape showed her that the park staff act hastily in the threat of potential disruption (her escape), utilizing this observation as grounds for her future forecasting.

3. Most crucial to the interpretation, is the fact the I-Rex notably left the tracker in a large piece of her own flesh. This is intriguing, as wouldn’t destroying it entirely afterwards be the more efficient play? Actually no, as this hunk of flesh is later used to track the I-Rex using the raptors. The I-Rex simply had to reason the animal she has roughly profiled as existing in the park conceptually (the raptors) possesses a sense of smell similar to her own, which indicates she simply took traits she knew she had and applied them to potential external agents. As a final note to this reasoning process, the I-Rex anticipated that leaving such a large piece of her genetic material would increase the likelihood of another animal with strong senses being deployed against her to track her - just so happening to be the raptors here. Leaving behind a large, viable biological sample increases the probability that any non-human pursuit assets deployed by park staff would be sensory-guided, thereby granting the I-Rex indirect control over intercepting measures.

**[ SECOND LAYER — VELOCIRAPTOR LURING ]**

As covered in the first layer, the I-Rex engineered the situation to be primed for the release of the raptors as offensive agents to track and recapture her. She achieved this through targeted depletion of park defense resources, panic-installment, and purposeful biological material granting. To continue enforcing this strategy, the I-Rex deploys several consecutive tactics to further lure and eliminates park defense - such as the pterosaur dome tactic, which I won’t cover too much in this post. The main point is that the I-Rex shielded the deployment into occurring through —>

1. Methodical isolation of the park staff - luring and eliminating multiple defense teams (as seen even in the first layer, incorporating this specific shielding in the double-layered trap itself). This ties into panic installment, through which the I-Rex ensured hasty decisions were increasing in probable likelihood as the scheme progress (as she observed in the paddock escape).

2. The I-Rex purposefully aligned the option-narrowing with the placement of her hunk of flesh, granting the park staff a piece of her biological material - proposing the option of utilizing it to track the I-Rex through her scent, mainly using the raptors as the conduit for this strategy. As the options dwindle, this option pressingly becomes stronger.

3. As the I-Rex ensured through her paddock escape filtering (and by proxy the first layer acting as a secondary filter), she has been given direct confirmation that the park staff do not have the knowledge of her concealed abilities - this displays to the I-Rex that her opposition most certainly is not aware of her ability to communicate cleanly, precluding the possibility that the park staff foresee the raptor deployment as a misstep.

4. The I-Rex had concealed this layer of the trap with the first layer - the tracker removal acts as a perfect alibi as to why the hunk of flesh would even exist, misdirecting the park staff into believing the true trap had already been sprung, fundamentally utilizing the trap’s first layer as a “false strategy”.

Through these factors, the I-Rex created a situation where the only option to counteract her was the deployment of the raptors - and with that, multiple outcomes arise —>

1. The park staff deploy the raptors and the I-Rex utilizes her concealed ability to communicate with them to become the pack’s leader, gaining control over the raptors (and thus they’re agents that the I-Rex can utilize to finish off the humans or combat other animals). This is the finalized purpose behind the trap’s second layer, successfully reversing the park’s last resort into a final nail in the coffin for them through immense anticipation and forecasting.

2.  The park staff choose not to deploy the raptors (either out of caution, lack of formulation, new information - it doesn’t really matter). In this scenario, the I-Rex has once again deadlocked the park staff from counteracting her. By narrowing the options surrounding the raptors down so thinly, it converges in a perfect scenario for the I-Rex where all routes of real counteraction have been cut off (the defense teams) and the seemingly only true final resort only results in a worse position being placed upon the park overall.

3. The park staff deploy the raptors, however they refuse to allow her leadership to dominate them (or she simply can’t communicate with them). As Owen canonically stated, the I-Rex had already learned its place in the food chain - thus, she already knows no creature exists that can contend her (especially at this stage). If the raptors refuse, she still has reasoned out that she’s superior enough to simply eliminate them, similar to the ankylosaurus and the apatosauruses (which these kills can be interpreted as the I-Rex testing this contingency in real-time). As such, the raptor lure merely acts as an extension of the tracker removal - the I-Rex purposefully lures a team of offensive agents to her and eliminates them (which she has done multiple times), removing the park’s final effort to halt her scheming.

(NOTE — You can also interpret this as the I-Rex merely anticipating the possible existence of another intelligent species and thus she concealed her communication abilities as a contingency in case she could genuinely communicate with them - otherwise, it’s merely a tactic to lure in possible external agents, specifically animals, to eliminate them - similar to the defense teams)

**[ SUMMARY/CATHARSIS ]**

I know this entire analysis has a lot of moving parts, so a summary may be helpful. To begin, let’s start with with the I-Rex’s goal(s) —>

1. To escape captivity (obviously).

2. Obtain contact with the raptors (theory).

3. Get revenge on the humans/the park.

With these goals in mind, let’s touch on how difficult the situation really was for the I-Rex - the contextual adversity —>

1. Obviously, the I-Rex has little to no resources whatsoever.

2. The park’s internal infrastructure houses hundreds (if not thousands) of agents, given the fact the park has around 10,000-20,000 visitors per day.

3. As some theories have posited, the I-Rex may have suffered from some heavy mental disorders - specifically those similar to schizophrenia, due to the presence of multiple instinctual genuses in her coding.

4. Even if these theories aren’t applied, there’s no doubt she’s still trapped in an enclosure and has no contact with the outside world - both limiting her and mentally damaging her with isolation.

5. The park itself has access to highly advancement technology and equipment - loaded with thermal cameras, trackers, firearms, general cameras, armored vehicles, military intervention, and weaponized biological beings.

6. As stated by Henry Wu, the I-Rex was only three years old by the time the film’s events take place.

If it isn’t clear, this situation is EXTRAORDINARILY difficult for the I-Rex here. Her goals are rather ambitious, yet her situation is quite dire. With that, here’s a summarized roadmap of her strategy —>

I. LAYER #1 (tracker translocation reversal)

1. False profile creation (misdirects park staff with a fabricated profile to go off).

2. Long-term concealment (purposefully conceals certain abilities for years).

3. Paddock escape filtering (ensures future events are shielded).

4. Tracker removal + initial defense team lure/elimination.

5. Double-branching outcome equilibrium (both pathways benefit her greatly and are canonically shown).

II. LAYER #2 (velociraptor luring)

1. Defense team elimination/isolation (ties into first later).

2. Panic installment (ensures hasty decision-making).

3. Option suggestion/narrowing (leaving the chunk of flesh opens the option of utilizing the raptors).

4. First Layer = false strategy (conceals the second layer’s existence with the first).

5. Triple-branching outcome equilibrium (all three outcomes greatly benefit the I-Rex and are contextually supported).

Finally, each layer acts as a contingency - if the second layer never triggers, the first was in play to deal damage regardless. If the first never comes into play, the park staff is deadlocked from countering the I-Rex due to the nature of the scheme (and the tracker). As such, the strategy was incredibly well-fortified and highly effective, acting as a branching strategic mechanism for the I-Rex to maintain leverage through actively dismantling threats through anticipation or forcing stalemates.

The strategy itself spanned roughly three years, overcome all six factors of contextual adversity, accounted for the park staff’s precise counteractive responses years in advance, managed intricately subtle multi-agent complexity through the raptors + defense teams, incorporated profile fabrications + strategic layering, and achieved most (if not all) of the I-Rex’s goals.
