Indominous Rex THEORY (Something of a Correction)

After Fluffy's post on the Indominous Rex, I thought I should probably watch it. I made sure not to read the analyses he made until afterwards cuz I wanted to look at it myself to see what exactly she did and how she went about doing it.

This post in itself is just me patching the theory in real time:

**The Indominous Rex didn't conceal her abilities consciously. She did have a reason for not using them though.**

The indominous using the camouflage and heat cloaking ability surprised the staff because they didnt know what animals she was made of. They just knew that the Indominous had the T-Rex as a base genome with the rest being *"classified"* as Claire herself states when Owen asked her about it.

Furthermore, even if the Indominous did use these skills, they'd never know. Because, given its intelligence, it would figure them out while it was still pretty young and small enough to enter a camera's blind spot or hide in the forest (it's prolly how she sneaked her sibling too).

If the Indominous used it then, only once is enough since we already know from her taking out the tracking device that she has an exceptional memory.

The indominous killing its sibling was only for 1 reason, and that is because she saw them as a threat (the dominance aspect is still there). She's smart enough to know that two of them would never cooperate given their intellect and soon to be/observable differences in nature.

The indominous communicated with the raptors because they were clearly intelligent. She only became aware of the raptors' existence AFTER they tracked her down. But she did believe that another like her must've been around somewhere.

Nothing there indicates that she was privy to the raptors before as that'd require some level of information gathering that was impossible given what she is. She was also isolated for her entire life, same as the raptors, so she couldn't sense them even if she wanted to.

She can sense the heat signatures and only identify the being itself after seeing it, not before.

While it might seem like she could identify them because she ran through the aviary where the pterodactyls were located while being chased by the helicopter—she didn't run through it because she knew that they were pterodactyls.

She did it because she sensed the heat signatures and thought to use the aviary as a cover from gunfire. She used the pterodactyls (via roaring) to distract the gunners but when they took them down instead she immediately left the scene as there was no more reason to stay hidden.

The indominous' actions following her first escape were mostly improvisation.

___

**"Another Like Me" and understanding the Food chain**

She believed another like her must exist because her sibling existed. It's believable to her that there must have been/must be another like her out there at a point in time. They were probably captives too.

Knowing who exactly isn't possible until meeting them face to face.

The indominous learning its place after engaging is ≠ to the indominous reasoning out her place in the food chain before engaging.

The indominous learned its placement from direct experience and not isolated reasoning.

She only reasons that she is competent after successfully escaping. Her being captive is partly why she believes she's strong. It's why she fights the biggest dinosaurs (titanosaurs) and started to hunt for fun when she realized she could easily beat them.

The indominous fighting the T-Rex on sight and communicating with the raptors on sight has to do with context.

The T-Rex was already charging at her with malicious intent while she was injured and agitated from the pain. She'd already abandoned communication by then cuz the raptors, who showed the most intelligence of all the others she's encountered, betrayed her for the humans. Additionally it was a human who led the T-Rex towards her so she saw no sense in reasoning with them.

On the other hand, the velociraptors were tracking her down when she was in a far calmer state of mind. And even though they were closely followed by humans, they were not clearly malicious in their actions and actually seemed willing to communicate after hearing her roar.

**[Conclusion]**

1) The Indominous had no need to CONSCIOUSLY conceal it's abilities (the activated state of the abilities are not it's base state. This means she had to consciously ACTIVATE the abilities, not consciously keep them deactivated. [Think of it like a car where after you turn it off you dont need to keep the key in and hold it in place to let it stop]) the camouflage ability was not known because as I said, it's best to assume she learned it in her infancy. And if she did learn it as an adult then it'd be possible for her to activate it in the woods (iirc she did it during her first appearance with the investor guy)

2) The indominous' primal and instinctive nature being swapped out for a more deliberate and strategic nature was already there during the events of her escape as seen from the claw marks on the wall.

It was further refined after hunting for sport and killing off more humans via luring them in with the tracker she scraped off.

3) The raptor vocalizations weren't concealed, they just weren't used until she met them.

She hadn't heard the sound of a raptor before and after hearing their vibrations (instead of roaring like usual) she mimics them and communicates effectively.

Furthermore, Claire not finding the fact of her communicating with a different species suspicious or worth acknowledging indicates that dinosaurs communicating with other species wasn't anything uncommon.

Even if she made those vibrations before, why would she even think to conceal it when it's just noise in the end? Because she prefers her own.

She likes to have control as seen from her leading the velociraptors to attack the humans. Her not using another language (raptor vibrations) would be because she prefers to use her own sounds.

So no, she couldn't have hid it on purpose.

4) Yes, her killing her sibling is most likely due to figuring out her capabilities as an infant (since their very existence was a threat to her in two different ways [enemy + being used/watched by enemies {since the park didn't kill her after she killed her brother she likely reasoned that killing was allowed and was probably a regular occurrence so her not using her abilities afterwards would be justified then. She only needed to use them ONCE because of her exceptional memory tho so there was no need for consciously hiding her abilities}] ) but no, she can only be sure that they existed at some point in time, and not that they still do exist because she was too isolated from the rest of the facility.

She cannot set a stage for something she doesn't know needs one:

- She knows she was in captivity.

- She knows  another intelligent lifeform MIGHT also be trapped somewhere.

- She doesn't know who that lifeform is or if it even exists currently.

It's possibly partly why she ran through the aviary and roared at the pterodactyls if you think Abt it.
