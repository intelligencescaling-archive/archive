The Indominus Rex always knew about the velociraptors (THEORY)

A while ago, I was rewatching Jurassic World and found the I-Rex’s actions to be quite nuanced and subtle from a behavioral standpoint. So, I started brainstorming - “how would her actions make coherent sense?”, which led me to create this theory - which, in my opinion, is actually quite strong.

The theory posits that the I-Rex had reasoned out the conceptual existence of the velociraptors far before the events of the film, which is obviously pretty impressive. This may sound luscious, but I’ll present the 10 strongest pieces of evidence I found in the film to back this narrative up (as well as a small note at the end explaining why this makes sense from the narrative’s POV).

There is obviously more miscellaneous details sprinkled throughout the film that support this interpretation, however I want to keep this post as short as possible for the time being.

**[ EVIDENCE/PROOF ]**

1. Claire had begun working at the park at 19 years old about a year before the park’s official opening (stated in the novel Jurassic World: Evolution of Claire).

2. The I-Rex was born years before the film’s events, and multiple references were made to the park’s already established rates and earnings being one of the reasons behind the I-Rex being created (which implies the I-Rex had only begun development AFTER the park’s opening).

3. This concludes that Claire had a direct viewing all throughout the I-Rex’s growth, due to being a consistent force within the park’s creation since before day one.

4. Upon the I-Rex utilizing its camouflage AND heat cloaking ability within the film, the staff is notably surprised. This implies the I-Rex hadn’t used this ability in the past whilst under observation (being Claire). This showcases that the I-Rex had concealed this ability from any viewing for all this time, uniquely.

5. It’s stated that the I-Rex had killed its sibling before the film’s events whilst both were rather near-infants (an exact age is not given to my knowledge). The I-Rex clearly did this to establish dominance, a common tactic for many real world animals. However, this is distinctly different from how the I-Rex had claimed dominance over the raptor pack during the film’s occurrence (being more so social and conversational dominance). This implies a direct split in the two instances, showcasing a more strategic route opposed to internally consistent primal behavior. Especially considering the I-Rex is capable of communication with both parties, not just one.

6. Due to the I-Rex’s vocalizations, it can be concluded this would be the method she’d speak to other members of her genus (as the velociraptor is the only dinosaur we see consistently speaking to one another throughout the franchise). Not even the I-Rex’s base genus, the T-Rex, has been shown to have complex or intricate speech patterns; at least not to the level of the I-Rex or velociraptors. However, the I-Rex had never produced such vocalizations to communicate to her brother, and instead chose to kill him. We know the vocalizations weren’t ever made due to the fact none of the park staff nor Claire ever said they heard it (despite picking up on exhibited traits such as thermo detection). If the I-Rex later communicated with the raptors, why didn’t it at least attempt to do so with its own sibling? This implies the vocalizations are not instinctual, but rather conscious efforts. To add onto this, Owen Grady states the main raptor pack in the film only has such a strong social and communication-based bond due to active learning, which again implies the raptor’s communication isn’t directly unconscious, but rather purposeful and completely deliberate.

7. Claire, being involved within the park before it was even opened, strongly implies she’s had viewing over the velociraptors. Not only this, it’s stated she once held a relationship with Owen Grady, the direct tamer for the main pack of raptors we see. This implies Claire had knowledge on some surface-level facts of the raptors, yet still never relayed nor recalled the fact that the I-Rex produces similar vocalizations (implying concealment on the I-Rex’s end), even when presented with the idea of possible contact between the two species. As shown before, these vocalizations are the only form of communication the I-Rex could possibly utilize - which indicates she concealed these traits on purpose.

8. Claire stated the only genus she knew for sure was within the I-Rex was its base genus, the T-Rex. Yet she also states she’s heard fellow park staff say it can perceive thermal radiation, LIKE snakes (the use of “like” implies a non-confirmation from Claire’s perspective, strongly implying this trait was only ever exhibited and never told by Wu himself). This showcases how the I-Rex does have the capacity to showcase her abilities, but as seen with her camouflage, oddly doesn’t in some cases (implying conscious concealment). This displays that the abilities the I-Rex concealed were actively utilized in her future actions, yet the ones she didn’t weren’t (implying a distinct effort to conceal certain abilities and a lack of circumstantial concealment).

9. It’s stated by Owen that the I-Rex was actively learning its place in the food chain, which implies a rough acknowledgment of other species. Owen was not aware of the I-Rex’s presence until moments before its escape, so it’s completely logical he only arrives at this deduction now opposed to beforehand (where it’s likely the I-Rex had already reasoned out how powerful it is).

10. When the I-Rex came into contact with the raptors, it notably didn’t start a physical altercation. This is SUPREMELY contradictory to its previous actions (instantly engaging with an Ankylosaur and T-Rex), and since we’ve covered it likely wasn’t instinctual, why would she explicitly assert dominance in such a way that’s out-of-line with her previous showings?

**[ CONCLUSION ]**

With all 10 reasoning points in place, the conclusions can be made that 1) The I-Rex has CONSCIOUS concealment over its abilities (via the thermo detection being known yet the camouflage is not), 2) Claire had overseen the I-Rex’s development and thus it would’ve needed to consistently conceal its abilities, 3) The I-Rex’s later actions do not fit the typical code for primal or instinctual action, 4) the raptor vocalizations were concealed, and 5) the I-Rex concealed its ability to generate these vocalizations in a conscious effort to deceive the park staff (imploring multiple methods of misdirection such as hand-picking which abilities to reveal as to fabricate a profile for the staff and acts of concealment such as killing her own sibling, which filled the dual purpose of ensuring her sibling doesn’t reveal the abilities AND concealing her raptor vocalizations, all whilst propagating the idea she merely operates on an animalistic basis).

Finally, for the I-Rex to actively conceal the vocalizations - it implies the I-Rex had foreseen the possible usage of and the overall conceptual existence of the raptors, and prepared the stage to be ideal for when this occurred and the two inevitably meet. Naturally, this is a pretty solid feat - reasoning out the existence of a specific species whilst being raised in absolute captivity at only a couple years, if not months, old - is pretty impressive (all things considered).

Narratively, this actually makes a lot of sense. Perhaps she reasoned out the existence of another intelligent animal besides herself, out of desperation for true connection and liberation from the humans - a consistent theme of her characterization is the fact she was raised in isolation and developed a somewhat hatred for the humans. Naturally, she wouldn’t want another animal around just as large and dangerous as herself (her sibling - which could be reversed onto her by the park staff in the event of her escape) - but rather a smaller group of animals she could become the “leader” of, a group of animals that would empathize with her pain (another core detail of the film is that the raptors are being abused for military purposes) - utilizing them as a conduit for her own loneliness brought on by her isolation, as well as a method to get revenge on the ones who hurt her (the humans).
