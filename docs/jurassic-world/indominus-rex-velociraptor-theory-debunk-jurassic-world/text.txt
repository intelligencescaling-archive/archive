Indominus Rex Velociraptor Theory Debunk

Fluffy’s “The Indominus Rex always knew about the velociraptors (THEORY)” post was AMAZING. But I did notice some mistakes so I decided to debunk it. Feel free to correct me in the comments.

Point 4: It‘s not necessary that she’s been concealing her abilities. For camouflage, she could’ve just accidentally happen to know about it deep in the forest in her cage. And the staff wouldn’t have noticed because they weren’t inside to see that she can camouflage. And her heat clocking ability is actually not possible since tree frog’s temperature cannot be controlled, it reacts to the environment’s temperature. But she’s done that so I‘ll say it could’ve been the same thing for her, she accidentally discovered that while the staff wasn’t looking.

Point 5: It is also not necessarily true because she’s ate her sibling. So she could’ve done that to eat her sibling, not assert the dominance. And a lot of birds, who are direct descendants of dinosaurs kill and eat their sibling.

Point 6: It has hindsight bias since I-Rex likely wasn’t that smart when she was young and it’s likely that she didn’t know she could communicate so she wouldn’t have tried to communicate with her brother. And also birds in the same specie have almost the same vocalizing patterns so it is mostly intuitive.

Point 7: It also has a lot of issues since I-Rex just wouldn’t vocalize with no one. And even if I-Rex did, it’s very probable that Claire never heard of I-Rex’s vocalization since Claire’s not always present at the building. It’s even the opposite, it is likely she’s rarely present there because she was a manager. And also if she has, it doesn’t mean she’s heard Velociraptors vocalizing, it is quite the opposite, very unlikely since Owen isn’t always training the velociraptors and she’s probably was speaking to him when he was out of the cage, etc. And even if she did, it’s very unlikely she’s remembered it and noticed patterns of their vocalizations since she was probably speaking with Owen at that time and it would make her forget vocalizations because she didn’t concentrate on them. And even if she was spending time with Velociraptors (which is just very-very unlikely since she’s the manager and she has other stuff to do), it doesn’t mean she would connect her hearing I-rex’s vocalization patterns (very unlikely that I-rex was vocalizing and even if he was it’s very unlikely she was present and even if she was she would’ve been talking to staff and even if she wasn’t it it’s not likely she would’ve remembered it) with vocalization patterns of velociraptors (very unlikely that she’s even remembered them to compare both patterns).

Point 8: Didn’t understand this point since “like” just refers to the analogy with snakes (snakes can perceive heat).

Point 10: It’s really just a plot move, but if we go off by that move — it could be that she’s just seen 4 dinosaurs standing with each other while she was in the forest (she has thermal vision) and she concluded that they are social and it is an instinct. Also, she has spikes that the velociraptors do as well. So she may’ve just remembered her brother’s spikes and connect it to the velociraptors. This would also be supported by her instincts
