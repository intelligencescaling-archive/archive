The Kanau game changed this category for me.

Before The Boy Who Cried Wolf even started, Rin secretly removed three cards from the valid deck:

1 Villager

1 Wolf

1 Boy

That's important because those cards technically stopped being part of the valid set.

He then kept this information hidden throughout the entire game.

When Ibara unexpectedly arrived, Rin used the distraction to make his move.

The final hand appeared impossible, but Rin's trick was based on the distinction between:

"This physical card exists."

and

"This card is still part of the valid deck."

Kanau accused him of lying.

That was exactly what Rin needed.

This is such a good feat because the final trick depended on preparation that happened before the game even started.

For me, this single feat gives Rin major points in:

Planning Intricacies, Planning Coverage, Preparation, Foresight, Misdirection, Trap Setting and Information Control.

───

Rin's Kanau Feat

I think this feat deserves its own section because it seriously changed how I scale current Rin.

Kanau doesn't randomly end up against him.

She specifically chooses Rin because she knows the Obami are swindlers and wants to eliminate him.

So basically:

1. Kanau knows Rin is a con artist.

2. She considers him dangerous enough to target him directly.

3. They play a game specifically centered around detecting lies.

4. Kanau successfully catches several of Rin's lies.

5. Rin drops from 10 points to only 1 point.

6. From everyone else's perspective, Rin is getting destroyed.

7. Rin remains confident anyway.

8. Ibara's arrival creates an opening.

9. We find out Rin altered the deck before the game even started.

10. Rin exploits the difference between a physical card existing and that card being legally part of the game's valid set.

11. Kanau makes exactly the accusation Rin needs her to make.

12. Rin wins.

That's not just Deception.

It's:

Deception + Planning + Foresight + Strategy + Trap Setting + Information Control + Misdirection + Risk Taking + Logical Manipulation.

I think people who only remember Rin from his loss against Ririka seriously underrate him because of that.

Current Rin has better feats.

Rin Obami > Kei Shinomiya — Extreme Diff (+)

This is ridiculously close, and I could see someone giving it to Kei without having a problem with it.

Kei has the advantage in FSIQ, Cognition, Reasoning, Thinking, Adaptability, Counteraction, Resistances and on-the-spot problem solving.

Rin has the advantage in Deception, Manipulation, Social Engineering, EI/SI, Planning, Strategy, Psychological Warfare, Field Crafting and long-term schemes.

The reason I currently lean Rin by the smallest possible margin is that I think current Rin is much more impressive than the version people usually remember from the Ririka match.

Scumcoin + War + Old Maid + The Boy Who Cried Wolf gives him a pretty ridiculous combination of social manipulation, deception, preparation and long-term planning.

The Kanau match is what finally pushed Rin slightly above Kei for me. Kanau knew exactly what kind of person Rin was, specifically targeted him because of it, and then played him in a game where detecting lies was literally the main mechanic.

And Rin still managed to build a deception around the game itself.

That's the kind of feat that makes me give him the matchup, even if it's basically 52/48
