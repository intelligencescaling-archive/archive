My honest take on Yuichi VS Liar Game Arcs -

Criteria-

Yuichi is playing the Liar Game in Akiyama's position. This means he must Play with the same goals as Akiyama. Said goals being-

Winning each Round is ideal, but throwing a victory position, or even a passing position, may be necessary if things would work out better. As long as Yuichi isn't eliminated from the Liar Game entirely, he can initiate any plan he wants.

Protecting Nao is a priority and Yuichi can use her to help Win the Games.

Saving the other Players is what Nao wishes, so Yuichi must do what he can to secure this too.

Passing the Game is fine, but Yuichi wants to humiliate Yokoya and Harimoto's cult while he's at it.

Regardless of whatever happens at the End of Each Game, Yuichi will Start Each Arc in the same position Akiyama did.

Let's go-

#1- Round 1-

Yuichi Passes Mid Diff (I see this playing out similarly to the Friend's Sin Trial Arc from Tomodachi Game. Yuichi will use much more ruthless tactics than Akiyama to manipulate the Teacher, but ultimately this will be a tough - battle to Win.)

He will most - likely see right through Fukunaga, deducing her to be a bad egg, earning her trust, and then backstabbing her. Still, coming out on - top as either the sole Winner, or one of the two Winners will be tough.)

#3- Downsizing Game-

Yuichi Passes High Diff (Yuichi will struggle to come up with a plan that Nao can actually follow through on, as Yuichi's plans usually involve much more malice than Akiyama's. Still, if push comes to shove, Yuichi is likely to just walk right in and start threatening Players on Nao's behalf. Remember, at this stage, violence is an explicitly prohibited, and regardless, Yuichi isn't even technically a Player here, and while Nao may end up being punished, as he's acting on her behalf, Yuichi could ask each Player if they really want to test him like that.)

#4- Contraband Game-

Could Go Either Way (Yuichi's All Bet Calculations proves he's able to do the math necessary for this Arc. The question is- In a confined Team VS Dictatorship environment like this, can Yuichi Truly overcome Yokoya's malice, and vice - versa?)
