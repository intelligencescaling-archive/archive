Tenji : The one who was hiding in the shadows

As game was finally coming to climax tenji joins the battlefield after yuuichi was down but the real question is where was he all this time.

To avoid being detected by the administrators, Tenji hid inside a cave, simultaneously spending days completely visualizing the camera positions across the entire island to avoid getting spotted. He managed to identify hundreds of camera's blind spots. Meanwhile, his friends were in a rough spot — they had been betrayed, were on the verge of losing the game, and some nearly died. Seeing Tenji just sitting in the cave doing nothing might have made you feel frustrated, but don’t worry, my readers — staying in that cave was the very reason they won the game in the first place.

His ultimate master plan was to stay far enough to avoid danger but close enough to stay aware of the events unfolding. While it might seem like Tenji was just waiting and wasting time, he had already completed the preparations to rescue his friends. If he wanted, he could have won the game on his own — it was the perfect hiding spot. The longer he stayed hidden, the better his chances of winning, as the rules states victory will go to those who will be last five survivors. Though our Chad Tenji isn’t selfish about winning, when he went out to observe what was happening in real time, his eyes caught Kei and Satone in danger. Without hesitation, he jumped in to save the genius boy and the tiger girl. After talking to them, Tenji gets to know  about Kokorogi's scheme.

After discovering that Kokorogi was the real threat all along — plotting to pull Yuuichi to her side, give him a black bullet, and frame Sawaragi — Mikasa Tenji didn’t hesitate for a second. He rushed to rescue Shiho, playing a crucial role in taking down Subaru Gaku. Tenji cleverly lured Gaku into Yuuichi and Kaido's trap, exposing him through their carefully crafted plan. From the very start of the game, Yuuichi and Tenji had been secretly exchanging information, working together to defeat the most dangerous player, Subaru Gaku, who dominated everyone with his hitman skills.

Conclusion :

Tenji's ability to map out the entire island’s camera system showcases an extraordinary level of visual spatial index. Spending days visualizing camera positions and identifying hundreds of blind spots demonstrates his capacity for complex mental mapping and pattern recognition. He was in a strategic position which gave him a massive advantage. By staying out of sight, he could observe events without being detected. During the hide-and-seek game, Tenji was trapped in a cave for four days without food or water. In that isolation, he reflected on everything that had shaped him — his father, his old and new friends, and the struggles he had endured. This experience became a turning point in his life. We might even say that Tenji developed a special connection to caves, seeing them as his natural habitat — the place where he was reborn, given a new chance to prove himself to his friends, and where he endured the greatest test of his life.
