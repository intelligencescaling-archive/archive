Yuuichi scaling is weird  but ppl have no idea that yuuichi was highly held back

TG and Yuuichi were pretty good in SCD, both normally and methodologically. I explained why TG was in SCD pinned guide : TG had digestible and diegetic games and mind games, as explained in the pinned guide.

However, I’d seen people underrating Yuuichi. At the same time, I’d also seen people overestimating Yuuichi without providing any concrete explanations for either side.

Since I was scaling TG’s arc, I could explain why I thought Yuuichi’s scaling is weird and why people had no idea how much he was held back throughout the series alone.

In SCD, a character being held back for a myriad of reasons was frowned upon since it blurred their ability and scaling. Nobody liked when someone said, “They were holding back” or “This thing/situation held them back; otherwise, they would stomp.” Personally, I found these arguments weird because, while they made the story happen, they also affected the desire to see them completely unbound and operating optimally. It felt like a cop-out.

# 1. Yuuichi was mentally held back by his past

This shouldn’t have been surprising, since the series repeatedly showed that Yuuichi in the past was a completely different person.

The old Yuuichi would have done certain things in a heartbeat, while the current Yuuichi had a different philosophy regarding money and friends, which severely held him back.

*i would have done in heartbeat*

It mattered because Yuuichi wasn’t trying to use his old ways throughout the series, even though doing so could have given him an upper edge easily.

# 2. Yuuichi’s friends

Yuuichi had to save his friends and maintain his friendship with them while trusting them. Yuuichi valued his bum and fraudulent friends, which was why he was testing and trusting them at the same time. He hoped they were still his friends and would remain so.

In the final arc, he literally proved that he was doing all of this for his friends’ sake.

# 3. Traitor in the group

Yuuichi had to figure out the identity of the traitor while having all of the above self-imposed conditions. At the same time, he had to do it without informing or leaking anything about his actions to the traitor.

The traitor, Yutori, literally had the Tomodachi Game bum head helping her against Yuuichi, along with all the information and resources.

# 4. Tomodachi Game bum head

The Tomodachi Game bum head was literally against Yuuichi. He inflated the game’s difficulty and sent assassins after him.

This actually worked in Yuuichi’s favor since it turned NFS into FS, but that didn’t change the fact that the game administrator was actively working against Yuuichi.

# 5. Tomodachi Game’s destruction

This was the biggest issue.

Yuuichi wanted to destroy Tomodachi Game completely. This led to Yuuichi making decisions to ensure Tomodachi Game’s destruction, but at the same time, it limited Yuuichi again.

With all these points—three self-imposed and two external—it didn’t mean we had no idea about Yuuichi’s outsmarting ability. We absolutely did.

We saw that in the Friendless Game arc, where his limits were a bit looser. However, there were still limits even there.

# VERIDICT

Most people underrate Yuuichi and the difficulty of his situation because they don’t know about these reasons. At the same time, the other side overestimates the adversity he faced while ignoring the feat framework in favor of scaling.

And that’s why his scaling is weird.
