These two have always been my favourite, but for clarification, I'll be explaining what each category I use for myself and my scaling is.

Autonomy in emotional intelligence -

A combination of cold reading and psychological profiling, the understanding of someone's thought process and the actions they'll undertake based off of your preconceived notion or your ability to read their thoughts fluidly and account for them.

Composition in strategy -

The steps, process and logical reasoning, and modules you'd take to execute the process and steps in the layering and efficiency in the execution and formulation, tying into planning as well, whoever takes Composition in strategy will logically take fluid intelligence due to the fact its application of your thinking and modalities or application of your ability to fluently reason and properly understand what intricacies and coverage your strategies will need.

Oppositional adversity-

Self evident, but accounts for the situational understanding and reasoning we take to appreciate how hard X's feats would've been to perform against Y in the story, whether due to onscreen feats or narrative importance or statements.
