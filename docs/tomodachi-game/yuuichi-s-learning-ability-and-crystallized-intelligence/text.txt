YUUICHI'S LEARNING ABILITY AND CRYSTALLIZED INTELIGENCE

# Learning Ability

Shiba Taizen was a first-rate swindler with decades of experience who took Yuuichi in and trained him to become his successor. However, **Yuuichi surpassed him while still a child, within only a few years at most.** Their earnings doubled as Yuuichi developed, and the relationship eventually reversed: Yuuichi became the one directing their operations, while Shiba became subordinate to him and helped carry out his ideas.

This is pretty significant when you consider that Yuuichi wasn't simply learning from some amateur. He was catching up to and surpassing someone who had spent decades practicing his craft, all within an exceptionally short period.

His learning ability is also demonstrated through the gun incident involving Shinji. Yuuichi repeatedly claimed that he knew nothing about guns, yet he managed to learn enough to modify a firearm so that pulling its trigger would cause it to blow up in the user's hand. And he couldn't just make some obvious modification, either. Shinji was highly knowledgeable about guns and could notice changes in a weapon's weight, so Yuuichi had to account for that and modify it to the point where even Shinji wouldn't notice anything unusual. He accomplished this while under constant surveillance, meaning he had to find opportunities to learn and perform the modification privately, within a short period.

**The important part here is how quickly he acquired and applied unfamiliar technical knowledge under serious constraints.** He wasn't simply using something he had already mastered; despite claiming ignorance, **he learned enough to produce a modification that accounted for the scrutiny of someone far more experienced with firearms in several days.**

As for the argument that Yuuichi is bad at academics and therefore has poor learning ability, this has been debunked before, but whatever. He was working alongside his schooling, and he frequently slept during classes that weren't giving him the opportunity to study effectively. Sleeping through classes or performing poorly in an academic environment doesn't automatically mean someone has poor learning ability, especially when their time and attention are occupied elsewhere. **Academic performance, learning ability, and accumulated knowledge are three different things.** His demonstrated ability to acquire specialized knowledge and outperform an extremely experienced swindler is much more relevant to evaluating his learning ability than simply pointing at his classroom behavior.

# Crystallized Intelligence

Now, regarding what Yuuichi actually knows and can apply, his background as Shiba's successor provides plenty of material. His training wasn't limited to learning a few tricks to scam people out of money. Shiba wanted someone who could understand other people's suffering, gain their trust, and still ruthlessly exploit them when necessary. Through their criminal operations, Yuuichi developed practical knowledge across multiple fields.

**Psychology and Social Psychology:** Yuuichi's work required understanding people's motivations, vulnerabilities, behavior, trust, and reactions. He needed to understand how individuals could be influenced and how relationships and social dynamics could be exploited. His ability to manipulate people and construct situations around their psychological weaknesses supports knowledge in both areas.

**Business Management:** Their earnings doubled under Yuuichi's development, and he eventually took control of their operations. This supports practical knowledge of organizing operations and directing activities toward greater effectiveness and profit.

**Swindling Techniques and Operations:** This is arguably the most important area of his knowledge. Yuuichi's expertise extends beyond simply lying or manipulating someone in a single interaction. His background supports knowledge of confidence tricks, deception, exploiting trust, psychological manipulation, and the construction and execution of elaborate swindling operations. This also includes strategic techniques such as his *Hedge-Bet in the Heart*, which reflects an understanding of maintaining opposing options and preparing for different possible outcomes rather than becoming dependent on a single approach.

**Cold reading** also belongs here as a specialized technique: drawing and presenting inferences about someone using available cues to make it appear that you know more about them than you actually should. It can overlap with psychology and observation, but that doesn't mean it cannot count as knowledge of a specific technique. The same principle applies to other specialized swindling methods: **knowing a technique and demonstrating the psychological abilities used to execute it are related, but distinct, things.**

**Criminal Investigation and Forensics:** Yuuichi's criminal operations caused countless deaths and suicides across the country without ultimately resulting in his exposure or accountability. He also personally killed Shiba with a knife and still avoided being held accountable. His ability to evade consequences across such extensive criminal activity supports the inference that he understood relevant aspects of avoiding detection, concealing criminal involvement, and preventing incriminating evidence from leading back to him. This also makes knowledge of criminal investigation and forensic evidence reasonable to consider.

**Firearms and Technical Knowledge:** The gun incident also provides evidence of the knowledge he acquired. Even though he repeatedly claimed not to know guns, he learned enough about a firearm's construction and physical characteristics to modify it while accounting for Shinji's expertise and ability to notice differences in weight. The learning process belongs under Learning Ability; the technical knowledge he acquired and applied belongs here under Crystallized Intelligence.

So, to summarize, the knowledge areas supported by his background and feats are:

**- Psychology**
**- Social Psychology**
**- Business Management**
**-** **Swindling Techniques and Operations:** including confidence tricks, cold reading, deception, manipulation, and strategic swindling methods
**- Criminal Investigation:** particularly knowledge relevant to evading detection and concealing criminal involvement
**- Forensics:** as an inferred area of knowledge concerning incriminating evidence
**- Firearms and Technical Knowledge:** based on the knowledge demonstrated through the incident involving Shinji
