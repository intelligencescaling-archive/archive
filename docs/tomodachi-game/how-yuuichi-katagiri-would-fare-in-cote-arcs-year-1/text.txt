How I think Yuuichi Katagiri would fare in COTE Arcs (Year 1)

__Volume 1 - Midterm Exam:__ We clown on Yuuichi for being dumb and all but his bad grades were due to working too many jobs and sleeping in class. In ANHS he’d have no issue whatsoever so he’d be fine. With his PSI and learning ability he’d have okay grades imo. Helping out the idiot trio would be a different story. He’d have Kushida help out just like Ayanokoji did and mediate any tension between Sudo and Horikita starting up. I believe he’d also try to convince any willing students to score lower so the idiot trio doesn’t get fucked by the average, even using points as compensation. I don’t think he’d get the answers from another student bc that’s too easy but he’d use Kushida as a honey trap if such thing happens. Some other notable issues are Kushida’s true nature, Yuuichi would see through her bsfr and blackmail her using a secret recording. Manabu hurting Horikita or whatever. If he comes across this he’d record the scene and step in before it gets serious. Telling Manabu off at a distance bc he’d get his shit rocked if he stepped in like Ayanokoji.

And he’d likely use the same strategy as Ayanokoji with the fake cameras. He could also just intimidate them and possibly even figure out it was Ryuen’s doing but meh.

Kinda similar to what Katsuragi did but he’d have a better strategy. Even if the holder gets caught they’d be in the clear unless the name gets seen. First off Ryuen, coming across Ibuki injured in the woods, he’d probably still let her in but have students keep a close eye on her so she can’t spy. He’d also get to talking with her and learn about Ryuen. Imo Ryuen made it too obvious he was a leader but he had a good strategy so I’ll give him that. yuuichi would be able to pick up on Ryuen’s behavior patterns and figure out he’d aura farm for the win at the end and deduce that he’s still on the island even though his class had left. For Ichinose, as thanks for helping Sudo I don’t think he’d go after her class. He’d probably figure out Shironami’s the leader after just picking up on actions and whatnot. Now for Katsuragi, this might be the hardest for Yuuichi imo but he’d still come out on top. Yuuichi’s Social Intelligence and Emotional Perception would help him realize just how cautious Katsuragi is, leading him to look elsewhere. He’d pick up on how much Yahiko sucks off Katsuragi and sticks by him most of the time and come to a conclusion. I don’t think he’d come to it as fast as Ayanokoji did but he’d figure it out sooner or later. And once again Class D comes out on top.
