Yuuichi after extending the period for Shibe’s trial, develops a strategy to get the money from his classmates.

Due to Yuuichi’s prediction coming to fruition, Tenji decides that he should take responsibility for the failure of Yuuichi’s plan, keep in mind that Yuuichi fed Tenji the idea that they were completely done for due to Tenji’s failure, and with the ticking clock, Shibe’s time was neigh.

We can see a proof of Yuuichi’s prediction here, as Yuuichi doesn’t even question Tenji’s motive here, as he’s already anticipated his next actions, and they will play exactly as his actual strategy intended.

The next part will be divided into 2 parts:
1- Yuuichi’s anticipation.
2- Yuuichi’s blackmail.

1- Yuuichi’s anticipation:
Yuuichi realizes that Tenji, after having failed Yuuichi’s plan, will go ahead and do anything to convince the class, having been backed into such a corner, to save Shibe.

Yuuichi was able to predict the following:
- Tenji, in a deranged mental state, having busted the only plan to save his friends, will beg the class to save Shibe, and will choose to admit his crime of stealing the trip money, ultimately, exposing himself completely clean for the class to see his sincerity. (abduction)

- The class (or more likely an individual or so) will question Tenji’s motives and reasoning and what he did with the money, Tenji can’t talk about Tomodachi Game, he will not be able to confess to anything, so as a trade-off, Tenji, the most remarkable student of their class, and bluntly a nerd, will choose to abandon his academic future for the sake of working to ensure paying back the money he’s trying to take from the class, as for why this is, it’s because the class will reasonably assume that Tenji stealing the money was because he lacked it. (deduction)

- The class will ultimately turn to Hanamiya for a choice, and as the class leader she is, she will decide to not give Tenji the money, by extension, choosing to not save Shibe; she’ll reason that it’s more responsible to leave the matters to adults. (induction + deduction + legit kind of common sense ngl)

Tenji here, ultimately failed at saving Shibe, but he moved exactly as Yuuichi wanted.

2- Yuuich’s blackmail.

He uses Kokorogi to catfish his class teacher through inappropriate images of Kokorogi, this in result allowed Yuuichi to blackmail his teacher by catching him in the act.

Now we touch the conclusion of Yuuich’s strategy.
Due to Tenji’s act, a huge weight of guilt will surround the students in regard to the money they’re withholding, and with Yuuichi blackmailing the teacher, and forcing him to recollect the money from the students, Yuuichi guarantees that they will have no qualms about returning it, as the associated feeling of “guilt” and “fear” will be gone alongside the money, and Yuuichi successfully saves Shibe by adhering to the conditions of the game.
