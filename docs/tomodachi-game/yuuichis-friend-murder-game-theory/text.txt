Other points:

When did Yuuichi think of an idea for the plan?
Yuuichi only briefly somewhat thought of the plan once he passed the Friend Sin Trial with the confidence of Shibe’s likelihood of innocence, Tenji’s admiration to let Yuuichi use him, with himself excluded, that would mean it’s either Kokorogi or Shiho. However, they do eventually pass by Shiho, so Yuuichi must have begun thinking of why she was there. Additionally, Manabu announces there’s two parts to the Adult Tomodachi Game, with selling people and a talk about an island, so Yuuichi possibly generalized the fact that he could eliminate members to find out the traitor. So in general, once he thought of the idea of an island, is when he started to put his previous ideas together, as that would be the only realistic time he could have done.

Why did Yuuichi formulate the plan?
Yuuichi formulated the plan in order to lure out the traitor. The basis for this is that in the Friend Murder Game, there can only be 5 survivors in the aftermath of the game, and there is already a struggle in Yuuichi’s social group, and for the fact that in All Bet he stated he would destroy Tomodachi Game, which was also evident previously in game 2 when he declared he would stop the game alone and go all out, game 3 with telling Tenji he would stop Tomodachi Game to help Tenji avenge his father and the thought about a traitor, the fake Tomodachi Game with Kokorogi’s supposed kidnapping, and in game 4 with Shibe’s innocence and Kuroki’s confession all point toward that A: he wants to stop the games, and B: Find out the real traitor. Additionally Yuuichi really just wants to get information to expose the traitor, not just by intuition.

If Yuuichi already knew Kokorogi was the traitor or suspicious, then what was the whole point of the plan, and why was he surprised about Kaidou’s testimony of Sawaragi Shiho’s father being in the police task force and his backstory?
If by statements, and what is true about Yuuichi and Satone’s level of intuitions, then it would just be Yuuichi intuitively knowing Kokorogi was suspicious from an earlier point, and he was only surprised about the police infiltration and the general knowledge of Shiho’s past.
