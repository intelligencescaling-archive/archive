Yuichi taking on Kokorogi is just as, if not More impressive, than Ayanokoji taking on Kushida

Hey guys! It's me! One of the last surviving members of the Yuichi outsmarts Ayanaokoji and it's not when Extreme Diff gang!

I've come to talk about stuff again.

So, Kokorogi is basically a version of Kushida on crack.

They're both essentially the same character. They're both incredibly sweet and kind girls on the surface, but it turns out that they're hiding a very dark personality that secretly despises everyone.

Kushida and Kokorogi however Are pretty different when it comes to actual.. You know, Character.

Kushida Is someone who has the ability to socialize with people as a true strength. In Year 2, Volume 10, The social bonds that she has with students around the school sincerely helps the class in winning the special exam. And this is after her identity was exposed, so she truly was doing it to just help the class.

Kushida also has virtually no reason to actually act the way that she does. Her only motive is that she got upset that she was treated as one of the best students in early years, and then when she realized that she no longer academically surpassed her peers, she decided to be the best at socializing, but this was at the cost of her own sanity, as being liked by everybody comes at the cost of not being able to vent out your actual feelings, causing her to essentially have a split personality, and wanting to dispose of literally anyone who finds out about her dark self.

Kokorogi on the other hand, had a pretty rough upbringing, and basically just went insane. I honestly can't remember if she actually had any clear motive. She was just kind of crazy.

Another thing though - Kushida sucked at hiding her dark personality way more than Kokorogi.

Kushida is found out by Ayanaokoji in Volume 1.

Kokorogi on the other hand, is found out by literally no one until the middle of the second to last arc.

And when she is found out, her main goal is to keep her dark personality hidden from Yuichi at all costs, and mess with the minds of anyone who knows about it already.

It's pretty interesting actually, because they're essentially polar opposites.

Kushida Is found out by the main character but is trying to keep her dark personality a secret from literally everyone else.

And Korkorogi Is a character who's dark personality is figured out by literally everyone else, and she's doing everything she can to keep it a secret from the main character.

When Ayanokoji finally decides to expose Kushida's dark personality to the school, he does so by giving a grand speech essentially. He even needs Hirata to get people to shut up. And it takes quite a long back and forth for him to finally get her to reveal everything.

Versus Yuichi, who just doesn't give a crap, and the moment he's given the opportunity, he literally shoves a gun down Kokorogi's throat and calls her a b***h.

Yuichi also managed to stay in hiding and see her dark personality when she thought he wasn't watching, which really embarrasses Kokorogi when she realizes she really never had a chance to keep herself a secret from Yuichi at all.

Also, it's worth noting that Yuichi was literally in hiding when Korkorogi was acting out her true personality, which was just like Ayanaokoji with Kushida in Volume 1. However, Ayanaokoji was caught. Yuichi wasn't. And Ayanaokoji apparently needed a year and a half to actually expose it, versus Yuichi who just goes all out the moment he talks to her next.

I think Yuichi taking on Kokorogi is more impressive than Ayanokoji with Kushida.
