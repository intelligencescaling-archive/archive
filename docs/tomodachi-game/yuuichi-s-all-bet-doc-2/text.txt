Old Maid is a traditional card game for children. Here's a simple explanation of how it works:

1. **Deck Preparation**: The game uses a standard deck of playing cards, but one of the queens (usually the queen of clubs) is removed. This leaves an odd number of cards.

2. **Dealing Cards**: All the cards are dealt out to the players. Some players might get more cards than others, which is fine.

3. **Objective**: The goal is to form and discard pairs of cards from your hand.

4. **Gameplay**:
    - Players look at their hands and remove any pairs (two cards of the same rank, like two 5s or two Kings).
    - Once the pairs are removed, the real game begins. Each player takes turns to draw one card from the hand of the player to their left.
    - If the drawn card forms a pair with a card in their own hand, they discard the pair.
    - The game continues until all cards have been paired and discarded, except for one card – the unpaired queen.

5. **Ending the Game**: The player who is left holding the unpaired queen at the end of the game is the "Old Maid" and loses the game.
