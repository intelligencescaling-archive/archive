KOKKURI-SAN GAME.

Game Rules:
-There are five rounds/questions total. If the players correctly answer at least once, the game is cleared.

-During each round, all players must place their finger on the ten yen coin
-The round's questioner asks a yes/no question, and each player moves the coin to "yes" or "no"
-If all five players answer the same, the coin moves toward the unanimous answer and the game is cleared
-If the answers are split, the coin is moved to the option with the least number of answers
-If the selected answer is correct, the game is still cleared
-The questions are provided in envelopes and must be read in the order provided. The answer is also provided to the questioner on the card
-Participants (aside from the questioner) are prohibited from talking during the game, at a penalty of doubling his/her debt.
-If the game is not cleared, the person with the largest debt will shoulder the debt of the remaining participants
Game Questions
-Cards 1-4 read: "The question will be provided by the questioner. Move the ten yen coin to "yes" to win the round. Move the ten yen coin to "no" to half the questioner's debt."
-Card 5 reads: "Question: We, after all of this, will still be friends forever"

ALL BETS

-Players have until they arrive at the destination (Kanegashima) to gamble and increase their savings. Only those who clear the conditions can proceed to the second half of the game.
-Before the game starts, players are assessed for their monetary value. Each team can choose to "sell" teammates to gain funds to gamble with, and sold teammates cannot participate in gambling. By paying the full amount the teammate is sold for before the end of the game, you can buy them back.
-Each player must also pay 1 million yen in boarding fees after the game ends, which is punishable by 100 million yen in penalty fees if not paid.
-Money can be used for gambling as well as buying things on the ship.
-Having more money is advantageous in the second part of this game (after reaching Kanegashima).
-To win money, players can decide on or create games to gamble on by themselves.
Yuuichi’s strategy begins when he gives the players in the sold room a contract to sign regarding a bet on who can guess what order the teams in the end game will be based on their money, and the “final order” will be when 2 or more of the people who take part in the bet confirm the scores.

In terms of the sold players that were present when the bet was happening, Kaido had 3 players in there, Mishima had 2, Kamishiro had 2, and Yuuichi’s team had 3 including Yuuichi himself.

There are 24 possible outcomes for the end game scores based on pure randomness, but Yuuichi likely assumed that it was obvious that every player would choose a bet of their team being in the top ranks, meaning each player had around 4% chance to be correct (otherwise 30-50% if they guaranteed their own team won), so there would be no overlapping, making up a total of 32% chance that a correct guess is made, this is excluding Yuuichi himself whose team didn’t know about his plan and therefore likely voted in the same manner as the other players.

Yuuichi’s own prediction was:
Kaidou > Kamishiro > Mishima > Katagiri

This is a dangerous position since it means Kaidou’s team has a 50% chance of guessing correctly after Yuuichi carries out his strategy, this will spell a loss-con that Yuuichi didn’t consider later.
