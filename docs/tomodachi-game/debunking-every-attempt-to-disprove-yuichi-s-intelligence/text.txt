Debunking every attempt to disprove Yuichi's intelligence

I always find it annoying seeing Yuichi at the bottom of SCD lists. He should be the one slowly but surely overtaking Akiyama in videos, not Ayanaokoji.

There are three main Points people have to make when it comes to claiming Yuichi is not that smart. Personally, I believe them all to be incorrect anyway, but Even If they were valid, I don't believe you can really use these points, because there are other characters in SCD who scale much higher, and yet also have these problems.

#1- Yuichi's opponents are not smart.

Firstly, this is flat out wrong. Tenji, Kei, Kamishiro, Gaku, And several others all prove themselves to be incredibly intelligent and able to come up with complex strategies. Even if you don't find these strategies as complex as in other franchises, they are still effective. Gaku's ability to use the money he had gotten from All Bet to scare the zombies in the Island arc gave him a shocking advantage against everybody else.

But, even if you were to prove that Yuichi's opponents Really aren't that smart and don't double check anything, and can't come up with anything complex... Guess who also had to deal with opponents like that?

Tokuchi. From One Outs. Name a single opponent he had that actually challenged him. And yet he's often claim to be on, if not above Akiyama's level.

#2- Yuichi His only seen in a fixed situation.

This is another take that I saw that I thought was interesting. It cleans that since Yuichi has only really done anything. Impressive when in a fixed situation, where there are figures of authority watching over everything and making the final decision on what is "allowed" Or not, this gives Yuichi an advantage when doing his feats as compared to other SCD characters Who did not have such members of authority, and had to deal with the practical consequences of what they do (mainly, angering their opponents and getting punched in the face).

Firstly, I do have to ask why this even matters. Whether in a fixed situation or not, coming up with strategies to win is difficult, especially when every Game seems to be all about luck until you can think outside of the box like Yuichi can. Having 3 VS 5 in Hide & Seek was very difficult, as was having to convince everyone to vote for you, or tricking everyone into making a mistake when gambling against you, or setting up dominos whatever I want is trying to vote you out, or proving your friend's innocents out of trial (Which is maybe the most Non-Fixed Situation we see Yuichi in). You could also make an argument that being a fixed situation is actually MORE difficult, because then you're also bound by rules, and you can't just do whatever you want, and you have to make sure what you're doing fits in with the rules set-up. In Tomodachi Game specifically, not all the rules are made apparent, and sometimes the rules flat out change in the middle of a Game like in Backbite Hopscotch or in the Island arc.

But regardless, who's the first person that comes into your head when you think of "Fixed-Situation"? If it's not Akiyama, I dunno what to tell you.

#3- Yuichi's wins often depend entirely on his luck.

I do have to ask what people are even talking about when they say this. Literally the only thing that possibly comes into my head is the Prison Game where he just happened to come across the keys, And even then, you could argue that since he's a smart person who would naturally know where to look first when looking for such a small object, it technically wasn't entirely on luck, especially when Kei was the other person who found the other key. I'm personally wondering more as to how they made the realistic fake keys so quickly and out of what material.

Other than that, I really don't know what people are talking about when they say Yuichi "just got lucky." Especially when we're talking about a manga that has a character like Satone, Where literally her main ability is that she's lucky. Yuichi has an entire monologue before he faces off against her about how she may be his toughest opponent yet, because she, like some people in the world, sometimes just gets lucky. Stack that up to her intuition, perception, and overall intelligence, And you've got a pretty strong character. One that Yuichi Was still able to beat thinking outside the box and not being bound by the fact that he was unlucky here.

But also, who's another character that just gets lucky and kind of has everything fall into his lap whenever he needs it?

I would say Ayanaokoji.

My biggest example of this would be the class poll. We're confronted with Ayanokoji seeming doomed as all of his class is going to gang up against him, and yet he overcame it. How? Did he pull some complex strategy behind the strings?

Well, no, Not really. It just so happened that Sakyanagi Really wanted to face off against him in the future, and had her entire class give him appraise Point so that he won. Him winning gives him a protection point that saves him when he loses in the next Game too Where he must play chess and yet somehow overlooks the fact that one of his moves was inputted incorrectly somehow.

But then you also look at how he "saved Ryueen." Class C students literally come to Ayanaokoji And beg him to come up with some strategy to save him, and it turns out all that he really does is get their Points and use them to pay Ichinose who just so happens to desperately need 4,000 Points, and then she just tells her Class to give Ryueen Praise Points.

I hate to be looking a gift horse in the mouth, but I can't help but feel like all this is a little too... Convenient.
