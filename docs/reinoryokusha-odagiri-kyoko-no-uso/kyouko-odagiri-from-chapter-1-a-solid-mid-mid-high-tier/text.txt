Primeiramente, nós somos apresentados aos dois personagens principais, Odagiri Kyouko e Motohashi Keita (posso estar confundindo o nome do cara, mas isso é irrelevante).

Right off the bat, we see a feat of mass manipulation (and other categories that I'll leave for you to decide), because despite the absurdity of her supposed psychic methods (literally visions), and anyone stopping to think for a few seconds being able to understand that it's more likely that she's solving her cases and catching the criminals with actual investigation than being a psychic, actual 20 million copies of her fortune telling book were sold, which makes it fair to assume that millions of people were convinced enough to even be willing to spend money to read Odagiri's advice; not only that, but apparently she managed to make her ~~self-help~~ fortune telling book satisfactory enough, which was demonstrated by several compliments to the book made by the protagonist (the random guy whose name I'm not even sure of lol), who even started to admire Odagiri as a person after reading it and even held her responsible for him having actively started to seek to improve his own life and stopping being a fricking shut-in, therefore meaning that her advice was actually useful and grounded on actual psychology. Also, the fact that she's still standing with high popularity during five years ~~of being a big fat liar~~, never ever getting debunked, must mean something.

Coming back to the random guy, when he sees him leaving his house for his job interview or something, and guess what? HE TAKES THE DAMN BOOK WITH HIM lmao. Anyway, he ends up arriving too early and having to wait before going to the interview location.... And then, suddenly, he sees something very suspicious (for us readers): A woman with sunglasses and loose hair, who the "protagonist" recognizes as being Odagiri and STARTS FOLLOWING HER, even following her to a forest....

Obviously, the dumbass fell for a trap, and was acused of being a molester, and when he denied it by saying he actually just intended to follow Odagiri, he was also acused of being a stalker; but whe know that's wrong.

Let me explain! The reason for her trap to seemingly have failed into it's purpose is because all the info she had at the moment was:
- Several women were molested at that region.
- The person who called her to solve the case was Professor Takashima Ayako, a female university professor who had recently been promoted and was previously only an assistant professor.
- At the moment, no further details were given, and Odagiri most likely set this trap not with the purpose of actually catching the culprit, as that would be too simple, but rather to gauge what Professor Takashima's reaction would be to seeing her accuse a poor soul without evidence, based on how she later says that she was already suspicious of why Takashima, a renowned researcher who definitely wouldn't believe in supernatural nonsense, decided to call Odagiri, a controversial figure with an unknown background and dubious methods - her 100% success rate was hardly convincing enough, since a skeptic might believe that it was just a set-up for her television show.
From this she came to the conclusion that Professor Takashima didn't call her because he trusted her, but for a different reason. And what's different about her compared to the police? It can't be because she would save the College's reputation if there were any employees or students involved in the case, since Odagiri herself claims that the amount of data they hid would be insufficient for people not to realize that the College was involved.
From this, Odagiri inferred that Takashima might be planning something that had to do with the case becoming mainstream, something that wouldn't happen if she had simply called the police or a private investigator who doesn't have A LITERAL TELEVISION SHOW.

Now, let's see what Takashima's initial reaction was and *the actual given information* of the case:

*Takashima, sem nenhuma cautela, disse "You are mistaken. This person is not the culprit." E quando Odagiri a apresentou ao protagonista como Assistant Professor, Takashima exasperadamente corrigiu com "Just PROFESSOR, without assistant."

*Takashima, então, diz enigmaticamente que ela já explicou antes que o culpado desse caso É 'an insider to this College'. O que não muda muito as coisas, já que ela só mostrou as evidências disso depois da primeira armadilha de Odagiri já ter sido completada, mas ainda serve para melhorar a narrativa de que **Odagiri já desconfiou de Takashima desde o princípio**.

* Information given by Takashima BEFORE she had already started to fall for Odagiri's trap (you'll understand it latter):
- In the last 2 months, 17 instances of molesting with 14 victims, all female students of the College that were walking by the same path from the station to the College.
- In all but two attempts, physical contact occurred between the molester and the victim.

At the exact moment this information was given, Odagiri asked if there where any instances where the molester 'showed his junk', which was answered negatively and treated as a mere joke by Takashima, but which for us already shows that Odagiri was subtly looking for if there was any proof that the molester was really a man, which we can interpret as her already starting to form a theory about why Takashima might be the culprit (and yes, in the end Takashima is the culprit, I don't know if you've noticed....)

- Takashima claimed to have been attacked twice, which is 100% reliable, trust bro.... Odagiri reacted by pretending to believe her, even telling Takashima to 'not underestimate herself' and complimenting her beauty and her *athletic legs*.

- The culprit chose only physically weak women, who had never played sports in their entire lives, which indicates that the molester had access to the student's profiles.

- This College is for women only, wich means that the only males there are staff members.

- On a certain day, when Takashima organized a supplementary exam and several of his students had to go to the College on a day when the College is normally closed (i.e., Takashima got an exception), a person was attacked, meaning that the culprit was aware of this exception.

- Only professor can have acess to the students' past sport experiences, and Takashima says "Not including me there are other 23 professors working at this College. The culprit is probably one of them." Hmmmmmmm

- The discriptions of the criminal obtained from the viictims differ too much.

*Then Odagiri herself went after the students to get their accounts, and she got 25 accounts in total, from both victims and witnesses. Based on the descriptions given, there could have been as many as 10 different culprits, meaning there were 10 variations of the molester's body type, ranging from thin to fat and from ~160cm to ~190cm in height.

There was a brief interruption for a comical moment where she admits out loud the strangeness of Takashima calling her to solve the case, admitting to not being a psychic and after that it's stated that "From a very young age Kyouko-san was a highly gifted prodigy, she graduated from high school in the USA skipping grades. At the age of 16 she entered the Harvard university, at the age of 20 she was handpicked in the course of intense selection for a FBI special team." Interpret this statement however you will lmfao.

Them Odagiri goes "i already know who is the culprit, that is indeed one of the professors working at this College", and then gives some hints/info she gathered off screen (Wich was probably intended to let the reader have a chance of solving the case, but of course my dumbass didn't solve it lol):

- At the end of this year there will be an election for the position of the College president.

- Two testimonies that occurred within the same short period of time — the first said that the culprit was ~190cm tall and thin, and that the witness had screamed and run away when he saw him approaching; the second said that the culprit was ~160cm tall and thin, and that the witness had run to the aid of the woman who screamed (the woman from testimony 1), and after hearing about the molester tried to chase him, but he managed to escape.
