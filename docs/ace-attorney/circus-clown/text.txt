Reason of Revenge
His entire scheme is built to take revenge. So 1st let's understand why he is doing this. 18 years ago, the confectioner Carmelo Gusto and the sculptor Artie Frost, better known by his professional name Paul Halique, entered a dessert contest together. The two were acquainted through their sons, who were best friends at their elementary school. Gusto and Frost agreed to work together to win the confectionery contest, and split the prize, the ultimate cookbook, between themselves. However, on the day before the final, Frost stabbed Gusto in the back, and broke off their partnership. He refused to help Gusto design his creation, and ordered his son to prevent Gusto's son from attending the final. Gusto, having lost his chance at winning the contest, tried to obtain the contents of the ultimate cookbook through other means, but was confronted by Frost. Frost tried to blackmail Gusto about his taste disorder, and Gusto murdered Frost in response. This incident became known as the IS-7 Incident.

The  two boys spent the final day of the contest trapped in Frost's car. At his father's demand, Frost's son (Bronco) had tied up Gusto's son (Simeon), and held him in the car, to prevent Gusto's son from helping his father in the dessert contest. However, the harsh winter weather caused the car's door to freeze shut, trapping the two boys within.

The two boys had almost frozen to death, when the assassin Bodhidharma Kanis happened across the car. The assassin decided to save the two boys, freeing them from the car. The two boys had lost much of their memories as a result of their experience, so Kanis brought them to a nearby orphanage.

Seven years later, Kanis returned to the same orphanage. He had been hired by Excelsius Winner, Fifi Laguarde, and a body double of President Wang of Zheng Fa, to assassinate the real President Wang, which would enable his body double to take his place. The three conspirators then planned to dispose of Kanis, to prevent him from ever revealing the truth of Wang's death. However, this plan was discovered by one of the two boys Kanis had rescued seven years earlier, by now known as Simeon Saint. Simeon decided to repay Kanis for saving his life, by setting off a fire outside the orphanage to distract the would-be killers. Kanis used this time to make his escape. Simeon, meanwhile, collected the horn that was cut off Wang's Taurusaurus doll.

Laguarde  began  harshly  interrogating  Simeon,  in  an  effort  to  discern  exactly  what  the  boy knew  about  Wang's  death.  Simeon  struggled with  the. constant  interrogations,  and  would  cry  out  at night  for  his  father  to  come  and  save  him  -  but  the man  never  came,  having  abandoned  his  son years  earlier  in  his  pursuit  of  the  title  of world's greatest confectioner.

Eventually, Simeon ran away from the orphanage, leaving behind the horn of the Taurusaurus doll. Unable to turn to the police, who would have simply disposed of any evidence under orders from Excelsius Winner, and being followed constantly by Excelsius’ henchmen, Simeon's heart hardened, and he came to lose trust completely in other people, deciding instead to take matters into his own hands. To avoid getting tailed by excelsius henchmans constantly he works in a circus, a job where they would have to move to different places constantly to perform shows. Making it harder for his henchmen to keep following him. At some point over the following 12 years, Simeon regained some of his memories. He did not recollect all of the details, however, and ended up confusing certain critical elements - most significantly, the identity of his father. Simeon believed that his father was the murdered sculptor, Artie Frost, and that the father of his close friend, Bronco Knight, was Carmelo Gusto - Frost's murderer. Due to this mixed-up memory, Simeon developed an urge to avenge his "father", by killing who he believed to be the son of his father's murderer along with Laguarde, Excelsius and Body double Wang who made his life miserable.
