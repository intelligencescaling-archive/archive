A deeper look at The Mastermind’s AQ, EM, and EE

a common trend that might be noticed when looking at my various comparisons, is that mastermind tends to take these categories. that might appear as nothing special to most ppl, but if your an AA fan this might seem pretty interesting. that’s bcuz AA culprits tend to either be featless in these cats, or terrible in them. a major reason why i like mastermind so much is how different they are from every other culprit in the series.

when wanting to take deeper looks at the character i was deciding on prolly reasoning, however i noticed that masterminds AQ is significantly more insane than i previously thought.

this will spoil, A LOT of their backstory. so if you want to play the game or watch a walkthrough then skip this. if you read the doc, played/watched the game, or just don’t care then proceed.

Frozen Car Feat - >!this is undeniably simeon’s best feat in these cats and one that i severely neglected. in order to understand just how insane this feat is, it’s going to take a bit of a biology lesson abt brain damage. for context, simeon was tied down in his father’s car by bronco so that bronco’s dad can betray simeon’s. when bronco tries to leave, he notices the door handle is completely frozen and they cant get out. they remain locked inside of this car until kanus saves them. all the while, the investigation of artie frost’s death is happening. if you are reading into this at surface level, you rlly wont see how this is that impressive. but the part that makes this feat so impressive, is that while bronco has passed out. simeon is somehow STILL AWAKE 😭!<

.>! now for the lesson on brain damage. these kids were suffering from hypothermia. the car door handle being frozen indicates that it was at least 0C. a door handle takes minutes to freeze at this temperature. the door and kids indicated no moisture so if cant be hotter than 0C unless they were there for hours which makes no sense. simeon being able to fight off bronco long enough for the door to freeze, in the freezing cold, when bronco is a bigger kid is already pretty impressive. but it’s everything after that’s important. this event is what caused bronco’s and simeon’s memory loss. the things they forgot are extremely important memories as well, such as their names, where their homes were, and their fathers. simeon’s case he would never learn who his real father was until adulthood. memory loss this severe is usually not caused by hypothermia, but it can be from asphyxiation. it takes around 5-10, much closer to 10 minutes of asphyxiation to lose these important memories. keep in mind, that simeon was wrestling with a bigger kid just before this. another thing to note, is that this is even more stressful bcuz his father will lose his competition if simeon cant try his food. this extra stress likely causes less oxygen to reach the brain. now for the major part, simeon somehow being awake for all this. it usually takes around 2 minutes to pass out from asphyxiation. we know simeon was awake bcuz of the art shown when kanus describes when he saved them. bronco was passed out, while simeon was awake. its also important to remember, that this is during the time where bronco’s father’s death is being investigated which would logically take at least an hour. this means simeon was in the frozen car, suffering from asphyxiation for 10 mins-1 hour at minimum and it doesnt end there. 2 other condition occurs when someone is suffering from asphyxiation, that being anterograde amnesia and retrograde amnesia. the former is the inability to form new memories for this period of time and the latter being the inability to remember the events himself. despite suffering from severe amnesia, he was somehow able to form memories of kanus rescuing him and remembering the events in the car. this means that he was still somehow able to use his working memory while suffering from asphyxiation and hypothermia for a long period of time. this is fucking ridiculous 😭!<

orphanage feat- >! this is his events during the orphanage and what led him to do his revenge. simeon was out one night in an igloo with smth flammable that i forget for wtv reason, he was lowk prolly trying to commit arson for fun. but anyway, he witnesses the murder of the president at the orphanage by kanus. he watches as excelsius and laguarde hide the body and hears their plan to kill kanus. bcuz simeon remembers kanus, he decides to save his life. however after this incident, simeon would be routinely harassed, interrogated, stalked, and threatened for the rest of his life until he finally joined the circus. during all this time he never snitches on kanus or describes what he saw or did. mind you, he’s a child being interrogated by his caretaker, stalked and threatened by an immensely powerful chief prosecutor, and threatened by the president of a fucking country. yet he never gives up that information.!<

revenge ambition- >! this is gonna be rather short, its his entire ambition for revenge against laguarde, the presidents body double, excelsius, and his father. having the ambition and determination to track down and kill the president of a country and the chairmen of the prosecution committee is crazy ambition.!<

lastly, hot air balloon feat- >! ive already went over the things he needed to calculate in this feat in my post surrounding masterminds fsiq so i wont bother repeating it here. the important part is that he’s forming an incredibly complex plan while being shot at and at an extremely small time window, he had to do reasonings, scheming, and general calculations during this short time. again, while being shot at.!<

getting this deeper look i definitely think his AQ scales way higher as well as his FSIQ with frozen car, but let me know your take on this.
