Background

Simeon Saint(which i will be addressing him as onward as its his real name), is the main antagonist on Ace Attorney Investigations 2. He serves the role of a mastermind, controlling everything from the background and planning every case with the exception of 2-3. To understand the character and his feats, we must first know his goal. Simeon’s motive is revenge. He wants to get revenge on the president’s body double, his father, Excelsius, his “best friend” Bronco Knight, and Nina Leguarde.

Childhood

Now, usually I would start with his first scheme or the first episode but Simeon’s childhood is needed to understand his scheme’s. It will also provide context for people who haven’t played the game(what the hell are you doing, get out of this doc and play it), and those you might’ve not played in a while. His childhood also offers his best EE feat, and some of his best AC and EM feats.

Simeon Saint and Bronco Knight are the sons of Artie Frost and Simeon’s father. Both of the fathers were competing in a confectionist competition and were working with one another until Artie Frost would later betray Simeon’s father. Artie Frost would tell his son Bronco to hold Simeon captive because Simeon’s father was suffering from a condition that would cause the loss of taste and he was using Simeon to taste test it. However, when Bronco had tied Simeon’s hands inside of a car, the doors were frozen shut, leaving them both stuck inside. While both were stuck inside, Simeon’s father would murder Artie Frost. Bodharmma Karnus a blind assassin who used a dog, had passed by the car Simeon and Bronco were stuck in; his dog would notice the children and attract Kanus’s attention. Kanus broke open the door saving the two boys' lives which both would remember for the rest of their lives. Bronco and Simeon were sent to an orphanage after Artie had been killed and Simeon’s father would abandon his child. However, when the two boys were stuck in that car both of their memories had jumbled around. Simeon believed his father with Artie and that Simeon’s father had killed him. It's never stated whether Bronco had the same thing happen to him, but he never held any sort of grudge against Simeon.

This onward, is when Simeon starts to offer feats of his. Simeon would witness an event at the orphanage that would change his life. The president was going to the orphanage to give a child he had no idea existed a present, likely more but it doesn’t really matter and it's not stated. Kanus confronts him and kills him. Leguarde, the body double, and Excelsius planned this assassination together, hiring Kanus to kill the president so that the body double can take his place. After the assassination Simeon who is hiding overhears the 3 planning to kill Karnus to cover up the crime completely. He warns Kanus and helps lead him away, he lights a fire in front of the orphanage to distract them, saving the life of the man who has saved his own.

After this Simeon spends the rest of his years at the orphanage being interrogated for what he did and saw which Simeon had never admitted too. Excelsius had his men stalk and interrogate him as he grew up, threatening his life. This constant badgering and threatening fills Simeon with even more hatred than he had before, and as he grows up he begins to plan his revenge against everyone involved in his childhood. being motivated by Kanus bcuz he was the one who had saved him, he had no qualms taking their or others' lives for that revenge.
(EM, EE, Composure, Perseverance, Mental Fortitude)

The “Fake” Assasination Plot

This is the first of his schemes and takes place at the beginning of the game. The “president” arrives in Japan to give a peace speech. Suddenly a shot is fired and sends everyone into a panic, the bodyguards leading the body double into the plane.

One of the president’s bodyguards is Bronco Knight, Simeon’s childhood friend and who he manipulates into helping him orchestrate this plan. Bronco offers the president a plan for getting his approval ratings up. After the assasination of the real president and the body double taking his place, the president’s competence took a fall making him well liked by his own people. Simeon profiles the body double bcuz of this and makes up a fake assasination plot, and gives Bronco the idea and tells him to pitch it to the president which Simeon knows he’ll accept.
(EU, Psychology Profiling, Psychological Insight, Strategy Building, Planning Building)

Simeon would need to visualize this spot and what happens here in order to know its the best place for the plot. After the shot rings out the president will run into the plane with a vest already hit by a bullet to make the plot more convincing. Simple plan, nothing complicated.

What Bronco doesn’t know however is that Simeon’s real plan is to hire a hitman who we learn is our beloved Shelly De’killer to kill the president and give him the fake assasination plan. Prior to the events in the first ep, we know Shelly attacked before meaning Simeon likely gave him the plans to kill him before or at any time possible before the actual fake assassination plot is over. Shelly however messes up in this.

Involvement Concealment

Categories I Think Simeon is Great In and My Explanations(skip this if you dont rlly care for my explanations)

Scheming
This is arguably Simeon’s best category. Simeon’s schemes are some of the intricate and invincible plans in semi-realistic SCD. Legal Loophole is near unbeatable because of the several layers it has.

Planning Intricacies-Legal Loophole has at minimum 6 layers of other plans and strategies involved and that isn't mentioning the various tactics and foresight feats. Including Body Double Murder Plan, it too is very layered in its primary goal of framing Shaun.

Planning Invincibility-Similar reasoning as intricacies, its multi-layeredness makes it hard to beat. Legal Loophole specifically is impossible to beat unless you don’t care for the law and kill him instead. Referring to the Body Double Murder Plan, the framing of Shaun is also very tough to prove as the time of death gets manipulated to fit with “Shaun’s” murder, and Simeon anticipated most of Edgeworth’s arguments.

Planning Building-Again, similar reasoning but with the addition of Simeon’s ability to formulate complex plans quickly. Excelsius Murder Goading and Laguarde Murder Goading despite being short term plans are made complex and untraceable to Simeon. When met with an unforeseen consequence, that being detained for Bronco’s murder. Simeon quickly comes up with a plan to manipulate Edgeworth to solve his case and defend him. Body Double Murder Plan is similar, it was quickly formulated, and it was complex and intricate despite being a short term plan.

Planning Coverage-I don’t think I need to repeat myself again, however the feats of his final confrontation apply here. He anticipates and prepares for arguments to reveal his crime, making efficient counter arguments against Edgeworth.

Strategy Logistics-Simeon’s strategies are all detailed, manageable, and effective. He succeeds in all of his strategies and it’s all logically sound.

Strategy Shielding-With similar reasoning as prior, his strategies are very protected. His Edgeworth Involvement Strategy is untraceable similar to Legal Loophole and Edgeworth(who finds himself constantly fighting against ppl attempting to control him) is incapable of seeing through the strategy.

Strategy Effectiveness-All of Simeon’s strategies work pretty much flawlessly. He gets everything he wants with his strategies. They are also logically sound and applicable without need of resources or influence.

Strategy Versatility-Simeon’s arrest and Excelsius Counter Strategy best shows his versatility. Simeon’s arrest offered him a perfect opportunity to use his Edgeworth Involvement directly, manipulating him to catch Laguarde and saving himself. Excelsius' Counter Strategy is done quickly, he then changes his strategy whenever Excelsius’s men accidentally kidnap Eustace.

Manipulation
Another one of Simeon’s top 3 categories. All of his manipulation feats are applicable and involve a lot of understanding of his victims as well as deep effects to those victims.

Direct Manipulation-probably his 2nd worst subcat in manipulation, not to say it’s necessarily bad. His main showings of direct manipulation is his rage baiting the entire cast during his confrontation and his manipulation of Kay and Edgeworth to get Edgeworth to help solve the Bronco murder. His methods in the latter are actually impressive. He uses Edgeworth’s philosophy against him, then taking advantage of his trauma relating to Gregory to convince him to “do the right thing” all the while pretending to be scared and along, with Edgeworth being a prosecutor “scaring” him even more.

Indirect Manipulation-Easily his best subcat. Simeon is a d1 indirect manipulation spammer 😭.  His Laguarde manipulation involves making her lose her mind and getting extremely paranoid in her “paradise” to the point she commits murder herself. He basically indirectly manipulates Excelsius’s entire plan during 2-4 and causes him to commit a murder out of fear. Most of Edgeworth Involvement is him indirectly manipulating Edgeworth using his philosophy, trauma, and emotions.

Emotional Manipulation-Simeon’s 2nd best subcat. Just as Edgeworth says. Simeon’s main strategy is to target the emotional weaknesses of everyone he manipulates in order to control what they do. All of his manipulations involve this specific method and he accomplishes it well.

Logical Manipulation-Legal Loophole and Shaun Framing is Simeon’s primary usage of logical manipulation. He manipulates the reasoning of Edgeworth to come to several dead ends when investigating Simeon’s crimes. His counter arguments also exhibit great logical manipulation feats.

Mass Manipulation-Simeon’s worst subcat. The only feat in this I can think of is ensuring Laguarde and Excelsius’s guilty verdict. He ensures all parties involved keep up with their roles. Not the best of feats but as I said easily his worst subcat.

Deception
Deception is what I would say is probably Simeon’s best category. Most of his feats, especially his schemes are covered in elaborate deception schemes.

Acting Skills-This is probably Simeon’s best subcat. He might have the best acting skills in semi-realistic scd. Nobody suspected a thing even Laguarde who interrogated him for years didn’t recognize him and when Simeon took the fall for Bronco’s murder, she wanted Edgeworth to save him. Speaking of Edgeworth, his various mind chess feats showcase great STP and EP yet he doesn’t suspect a single thing of Simeon. Simeon tells him a bold face lie and Edgeworth doesn't suspect a thing.

Misdirection-This is incorporated in most of Simeon’s schemes. Simeon misdirects the entire cast to chase an invisible mastermind while no one suspects him. He misdirects Edgeworth to solving these crimes thinking of them as simple coincidences. He would’ve been able to continue misdirecting everyone regarding the mastermind’s identity if he wasn’t cursed with the worst luck ever. That being the only person with info that can trace to Simeon who happens to he blind escaping prison the exact day of the body doubles murder.

Fabrication-This is another subcat involved in a lot of Simeon’s feats. Simeon’s direct manipulation of Edgeworth is him fabricating a false identity, this same false identity he keeps up with everyone else. The “Fake” Assasination Plot also uses deep fabrication in order to associate Bronco with the crime and leading Edgeworth into believing a false reality of what was happening during that particular plan. Lastly, Laguarde’s Murder Goading involves fabricating a fake connection between Bronco and Kanus into manipulating her to do Simeon’s bidding.

Concealment-Simeon erasing any trace of his involvement in the body doubles murder, and any of his murder incitements. As mentioned before, he manipulates and erases any evidence of his involvement. His Edgeworth Involvement however is perfectly concealed. Edgeworth suspects nothing and is never given a reason to suspect anything until Kay’s statements of the red raincoat and Shelly telling them about a mastermind. This wasn’t very critical to his schemes since this was supposed to be the end of Edgeworth Involvement.

Info Control-Simeon constantly controls the information of every scheme he makes. Edgeworth Involvement is basically him leading Edgeworth around with information he is controlling.

Emotional Intelligence

Simeon’s EQ is commonly applied to a lot of his feats, hence him being good at it.

EU-Easily Simeon’s best subcat. Edgeworth manipulation, Laguarde manipulation, Animal Understanding, targeting emotional weaknesses, Excelsius manipulation and action anticipations. All of these feats have Simeon displaying almost if not literally a complete understanding of these characters, those of which he has limited knowledge of excluding knowledge he knows because of the president's murder.

EP-His weakest EQ subcat. Not bad but he only has 2 feats and neither of them I would consider being crazily high. Him perceiving the reporter’s emotions through only listening to her talk then anticipating she would tail Edgeworth for info is pretty good. His other feat is perceiving Edgeworth and Kay’s emotions during his detainment. Kay wears her emotions on her sleeve however, Edgeworth has really good composure and EM so being able to perceive his emotions at the extent he does is pretty impressive.

EM-Simeon getting shot at by the body double and still formulating his plan of murdering the body double.

EE-Simeon’s EE is VERY strong. Simeon as a boy had his life threatened by 3 powerful members of society, one of them being the president of a country. Simeon plans to take out these 3 despite having a drastic advantage in influence and resources, and he succeeded.

Social Intelligence

Social Engineering-Simeon’s strongest subcat, manipulating several people into killing one another despite Simeon having barely to no contact with these(aside from Bronco). Causing all of his victims to feel paranoid and fearful.

Social Awareness-Simeon’s ability to know how everyone will think and what they will do shows his great awareness. He does this with almost every character in the game.

Social Integration-Simeon garnering the sympathy of Kay and Edgeworth is a great feat in integration, as well as having Laguarde’s trust.

Social Skills-Everyone for the most part really likes Simeon when he isn’t being an evil genius clown. He’s seen as funny and nice, as well as trustworthy.

Social Management-Simeon makes everyone feel the exact way he wants them too. Evident in his Laguarde manipulation, he constantly manages her mental state making sure she never feels safe despite being in her “paradise”.

Field Skills

Trap Setting-This is his best field skills subcat if you don’t consider foresight field skills(which I honestly don’t). Edgeworth Involvement is a constant trap setting feat, putting him in situations he’ll need to get out of by doing exactly what Simeon wants. Laguarde manipulation is a trap setting feat. Then there’s Shaun framing where he creates one of the most elaborate murders I’ve seen in AA, the body double murder.

Trap Evasion-Simeon never finds himself in others' intentional traps. However, he is constantly getting screwed over by luck which has him slipping his way out of those inconveniences. Examples of this are the body double on the Bigg Building, Excelsius’s men kidnapping Eustace, Kanus escaping prison and giving Edgeworth some of the best clues he could’ve gotten, and Simeon’s arrest. There’s also him ensuring a lack of proof in inciting the murders.

Improvision-Simeon constantly improvises in his schemes. In the same reason his Trap Setting and Evasion are good, Simeon will find himself in a problematic scenario and worm his way out of it by some elaborate means.

Observation-Simeon’s Shaun framing is a great observation feat. He is observing his surroundings while being shot at and he sees some random kid dropping a large dinosaur head beside the large 51 story skyscraper he is flying a hot air balloon above. That's some crazy observation skills.

Perception-Basically the same thing except this time its his ability to interpret that as the perfect chance to frame the kid and kill the president.

Tactics-Simeon like most of his feats, does elaborate tactics whenever met with an obstacle. I’ve already discussed his loads of tactics so I don’t think I need to go further.

Decision Making-Simeon’s decision to kill the body double is a great feat. I could understand someone disagreeing with this saying “he should’ve waited, it was the only reason he got caught” however there are 2 problems with that. 1. Kay could wake up on the hot air balloon messing up everything and 2. Data Gathering-I also feel like I don’t need to explain this. Just basically, he learns as much as possible(which we see is a lot) about the people he manipulates then exploits said data.

Unpredictability-Edgeworth not suspecting a thing, then being completely stuck when at his final confrontation when reasoning isn’t enough is a really good feat. This is the same Edgeworth who again, can see through most acts and how people will respond because of mind chess.

Creative Thinking-Same argument as divergent with the body double murder, however his contraption in 2-2 also shows good creative thinking.

Critical Thinking-His inductions of Kanis and Laguarde, his manipulation of Edgeworth, the body double murder, and his counter strategy for Excelsius are all great showings of critical thinking. All of them have a logical basis and process behind all of them.

Quick Thinking-Him coming up with body double murder while being shot at. I don’t really think I need to go further here honestly, however I will. That’s because Simeon’s arrest also shows great quick thinking. Now obviously to a lesser degree and granted he could’ve planned ahead for this or had some time on his hands, however it wasn’t that long when Edgeworth first learned of his detainment and met up with him.
