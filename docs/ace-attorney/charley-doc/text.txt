Lore:
Charley is a potted plant that has been at the Wright Anything Agency since it was founded as Fey & Co. Law Offices. It is stated that Mia loved the plant, providing his name and making him the official mascot of the law firm.

[Seduction, charisma, influence, leadership]

Charley’s feats:
During the case in which Mia Fey was killed, Charley was caught in the crossfire. He had been knocked over by Redd White and found in the trashed office. When Phoenix returned, Charley had been returned to his upright position thanks to the police investigation team (Dick Gumshoes presumably).

[indirect manipulation, adversity capacity]

Charley was subjected to Maya Fey's overenthusiastic watering, which resulted in the plant looking a little "swollen" and has led Wright to wonder if Maya was perfecting her water torture technique on the defenseless plant. Then, during her visit at the office, Ema Skye's well-meaning attempts to improve Charley with an unknown additive resulted in the plant quickly reacting negatively to it and temporarily turning a little yellow. Wright eventually perfected the correct watering technique, which resulting in Charley's return to health. However, almost a year later, during the three days when Maya Fey was kidnapped, Charley, who had already been recently watered by Wright, once again came to peril when he was excessively further watered by the combined efforts of the over-eager Pearl Fey (described by Wright as "Typhoon Pearl"), and then later by the temporarily-discharged Gumshoe, with Charley wilting a little as a result.

[adversity capacity]

Trucy Wright, his adopted daughter, taking over as the main provider of Charley's water and referring to him endearingly as "Mr. Charley". When he first began working at the Agency, Apollo Justice was slightly confused by the amount of respect given to the plant, and was later somewhat suspicious that Charley was seemingly the only object in the room without some secret function, but he eventually warmed to Charley.

[charisma]

Phoenix has occasionally been caught staring at Charley and smiling to himself.
