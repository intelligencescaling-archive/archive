Lore:
 Furio “The Tiger” Tigre, is a minor antagonist from the third Ace Attorney game: Trials & Tribulations. He was the owner of a loan shop known as “Tender Lender” alongside his assistant Violette (Viola) the granddaughter of a powerful criminal known as Bruto Cadaverini.

Furio would use his “similar” appearance to Phoenix Wright to pose as a defence attorney (successfully I might add) whilst attempting to frame Maggey Byrde for the murder of Glen Elg (a programmer who racked up a large debt with Furio).

(disguise, cunning, acting skills, threats, extortion, fear inducement, unpredictability, creative + connective thinking, mass deception, non-verbal deception, love bombing (technically, albeit by accident), planning aim, influence, influence building)

Accident:
(Note, Viola is not his assistant yet here) Alright, this next part is important as it explains why Furio is in this position, in the summer of 2018 (in-universe obviously), Furio got into a hit-and-run accident whilst riding his scooter, as a result Viola (the driver of the car he hit) is severely injured. Recall her being the granddaughter of a powerful criminal? Well, he discovers the accident to be Furio’s fault and so forces him to pay the $1 million fine for Viola’s life-saving surgery.

Wanting to please the mob boss (out of fear mainly), Furio quickly called in all loans - most notably Glen Elg’s who had amassed a huge debt thanks to his gambling addictions, Furio would go on to have Glen use his skills instead of paying directly and have him create a powerful computer virus which he planned to sell to earn millions. As a result of Furio paying for Viola’s life saving surgery, she soon fell infatuated with the man (she was unaware the reason he was paying for it) and began to work with him as his assistant.

Elg’s Murder/Furio’s plan:
Furio would meet Glen at “Trés Bien”, a French Restaurant owned by Jean Armstrong who - like Glen - was in deep debt with Furio. However, unfortunately for Furio, Glen would discover midway through their exchange meeting that he had just won the city’s lottery - he’d receive more than enough money to cover his debts without even needing to hand over the CD! Furio in a desperate panic, as without that CD he wouldn’t be able to nearly cover his own debt from Viola’s surgery, instead poisoned Glen and took the CD himself.

Following the poisoning, Furio reenacted the murder, posing as the victim (with Viola posing as the imposter for Maggey Byrde - who they intended to pin the crime on), this plan created a false witness - Victor Kudo to further assist with testifying and pinning the blame on Maggey.

Furio would then take it to the next level, posing as Phoenix Wright himself in court using a CARDBOARD ATTORNEY’S BADGE to defend Maggey. In court he would perform miserably (deliberately, mind you) and such Maggey would be convicted- it should be noted that it’s stated that some people in the court suspected something to be amiss, however, Furio’s imposing self prevented anyone from speaking out about this, furthermore, he explained his appearance as the result of a terrible sunburn.

(improvisation, planning formulation, planning intricacies + coverage + preparation process, threats/extortion/coercion - e.g. Jean Armstrong, planning aim, disguise, acting skills, fabrication, strategic deception, smearing, setting traps, trap evasion, fear inducement, strategic thinking)

NOTE: Following this success, he would sell the virus (but keep the original CD) and pay off his debts from the surgery.

Recipe for Turnabout:
Unfortunately for Furio, one month later Dick Gumshoes would go on to angrily chastise the real Wright for his (Furio’s) performance and getting Maggey convicted. This would lead to Wright officially taking Maggey’s appeal and investigating her case himself this time.

Sadly, Furio doesn’t play all that much of a role in the case, showing up briefly at points to threaten Wright and get him to stop investigating (which of course, he never does), eventually Wright is able to inform Viola of Tigre’s true role in the accident (where he hit her and caused her severe injury) and she gives him her medical records.

Tigre would ambush Wright at Trés Bien and (after forcing Armstrong to assist) rough up Wright and try to burn the medical records, luckily for Wright (not so much for Furio), Gumshoes would intervene and allow Wright to flee with the records while he held off Furio.

The next day, in court, Wright proved how Furio had coerced Armstrong and Godot proceeded to subpoena Furio, Furio would attempt to avoid accusations (somewhat successfully at first), before being tricked by Wright into implicating himself, Wright presented a bottle which he then claimed that contained the poison Furio used to kill Glen with- Furio would then foolishly laugh and mock Wright, correcting him in detail what the bottle looked like, something he obviously shouldn’t be able to do if he wasn’t the killer, as a result of his mistake Furio would breakdown.

(threats, minor trap evasion - before majorly screwing himself over, minor composure/determination/perseverance)
