In Depth Psychological Analysis of Laguarde Murder Goading(Ace Attorney Investigations 2)

before i go into the analysis a few things should be noted first. this is an analysis of The Mastermind’s (who i will for now on be actually addressing as his name) best manipulation feat.

this will not be applying the subcats we use. i will strictly mention real world manipulation methods. the reason for this is i believe this conveys the psychological aspect most. for those who might struggle to understand what this feat will fall into or simply want to know before going in, this feat covers basically every subcat except mass(bro’s worst subcat)

these terms will be important so if you dont know what these are its best to remember now.

Gaslighting-A form of manipulation where the victim is made to distrust others and their own thoughts.

Scapegoating-Pushing blame on a person or group for something bad that was done.

Suggestion-Planting thoughts into someone without outright stating it.

Love Bombing-Using affection to gain the attention and trust in order to manufacture dependency.

Paltering-Misleading someone by telling technically true statements while withholding critical information.

some of these may appear as odd for being here but trust the process. but now lets actually get into the manipulation.

The plan starts off screen, before the events of the story take place. We can reasonably assume this starts after Kanus is transported to the prison this takes place in. This is bcuz Laguarde herself mentions Kanus blackmailing Laguarde whenever he had arrived. This was orchestrated by Simeon. Simeon had begun sending letters to Kanus, correspondence chess letters. Kanus would immediately start using these letters to deceive Laguarde into thinking he had people working under him, Kanus addressing them as his “young pups”. Kanus would deduce that the person sending these letters would be one of the kids he had saved, and had saved him in return. Simeon planting this suggestion in Kanus’s head had affectively begun the manipulation of Laguarde. We know this is suggestion and not Simeon outright telling him because the letters being sent to Kanus isn’t actually Simeon’s. It’s Bronco’s who Simeon is also communicating with in order to manipulate him once Simeon gets Bronco arrested for the murder goading. Also, Laguarde finds nothing from the letters itself and only makes a connection once she sees the corresponding chess moves.

Simeon knows Kanus will do this because of how Laguarde, Excelsius, and the body double tried to kill him. He knows Kanus will see this as an opportunity to revenge and(tho this maybe stretching it) Kanus likely deduced living in the orphanage for the “young pup” after that day was terrible. This would motivate him further as it was the one life he had ever saved making him cherish it.

While this is happening, Simeon had successfully gathered the trust of Laguarde. Simeon and the Berry Big Circus had been visiting the prison on a schedule. Laguarde wanted to transform her prison into a paradise in hope of reforming or making the inmates simply less likely to be dangerous. Now, usually we would assume Regina who actually owns the circus would make these decisions, but on several occasions in the story Regina had let Simeon plan mostly everything, examples being managing the freezers for the animals food and the show itself in the prison before the murder. So, it isn’t hard to believe that Simeon had also planned the schedule at the prison as well. in fact, it would make a lot of sense and wouldn’t make the planning itself appear as luck which is very uncharacteristic of Simeon to rely on luck.

With the Berry Big Circus going to the prison on a monthly basis, Laguarde would be in a constant state of anticipation and comfort every time they arrived. The animals wondering around the prison as well, being a constant reminder of their presence.

Once Kanus begins blackmailing Laguarde. The prison she was so attached to and the image of what it could be begins to fall as Kanus becomes “the fixer” and runs an underground trafficking ring in the prison.

This causes Laguarde to become gradually more angry but also more paranoid as Kanus would continue to threaten her family. This rage and paranoia would boil inside her more and more, but in her mind she would still have the circus and the animals to find comfort in. However, this would cause her to be even more paranoid of losing it all.

Now for when the story actually begins, Simeon convinces Bronco to stage a fake assassination for the president in order to boost popularity. He plants the suggestion off screen and gives him a plan layout. Simeon is also aware that the body double would absolutely agree to this plan. he knows the body double, unlike the real president was a cunning man. he can also infer by the low popularity that he lacked the courage the president had. he also could’ve inferred that bcuz he made Excelsius and Laguarde handle the disposal of the body he could’ve perhaps been unable to handle it bcuz he was a coward.(or simeon saw him being a coward after the murder but i sincerely doubt this would go unstated considering it described clearly what they were feeling at the time). Meanwhile, as this happens Simeon hires Shelly De’Killer to assassinate the president.  Simeon knows Bronco will be exposed for the crime, and he also knows Bronco won’t sell him out for planting this suggestion.

He infers because of how high priority this would be, that the chief prosecutor Edgeworth would get involved. With Edgeworth working the case, there’s no doubt he would deduce that Bronco and President Wang staged the fake assassination.

The plan would fail for Simeon and Shelly would decide not to kill the body double but for such a stupid and petty reason, Bronco would commit his own murder and get caught.

This plan would happen close to the day of the circus show in the prison, which Simeon had planned so that he could manipulate Leguarde into killing the day of the circus show.

In order to prep for how he would direct Leguarde in how she would commit the murder. Simeon had used his visits to gain intel on what Kanus had been demanding in order to establish how he could plant a suggestion into Leguarde in how to frame Kanus for Bronco’s murder. Simeon would use a chisel because that is one of the primary things Kanus had been asking for.

Now, for before the circus show, when Simeon and Regina would be preparing it. Simeon visits Bronco who is currently in holding and hands him a chess board. Simeon would tell Bronco that he has another present inside that being the chisel. Leguarde would notice the correspondence chess game and make the connection that Bronco was Kanus’s “young pup” and would constantly and aggressively interrogate him. Bronco would have no idea which would aggravate Leguarde even more.

During this point and time Leguarde, just as Simeon would later describe, would be seeing enemies and threats wherever she looked. This causing her to increase every day in paranoia as the prison’s image she had wished so much for would be completely dismantled. Both the image of what the prison could be, and the destruction of that image being orchestrated by Simeon via love bombing. Leguarde would he increasingly attached and scared of it going away.

Leguarde had remembered Bronco from the orphanage, and gave a report to Excelsius due to her paranoia likely asking for help. Excelsius had the report on the hidden floor of the Bigg Building meaning he was clearly paranoid as well. Though this next part is never stated, it’s likely that this paranoia was founded on the belief that Bronco was the kid who had rescued Kanus and witnessed the murder. Though it’s possible Excelsius would be concerned about hearing of Kanus having any henchmen. As for why they wouldn’t recognize him as an ordinary orphan, it’s clear names weren’t enough as Leguarde had no idea Simeon was the orphan who witnessed that murder and based on her lines she didn’t recognize him before at all.

Simeon’s intention was to manipulate Leguarde into framing Kanus for the murder by using the chisel, but having Edgeworth deduce the truth behind what had actually happened because Simeon had already figured out how this crime would be deduced logically. He knew the chisel wouldn’t be considered the murder weapon if a genius like Edgeworth had investigated the crime like he was supposed to.

However Euestace Dewinner the SMARTEST prosecutor in the series would catch the mastermind instantly. nah, in reality Eustace would mistake the chisel as the murder weapon bcuz this is before his glow up in AA7(capcom plsss). In fact, this would make Edgeworth trust him further as it would further reinforce his image of the harmless, silly, clumsly, idiot causing Edgeworth himself to state that he couldn’t imagine Simeon committing murder which is one of the funniest lines in AA.

Kanus had successfully gaslit Leguarde into believing the possibility of his henchmen being capable of killing her family at any moment(tho the family part could be referring to the prison) and Simeon had planted this idea into Kanus with the correspondence chess letters knowing full well what he would do, then planted a suggestion into Bronco’s head manipulating him to do the fake assassination. While all this is happening, Simeon would manipulate Edgeworth into involving himself by planting another thought and using Edgeworth’s sense of justice. he had affectively manipulated 5 people for this plan all without the slightest clue of what might really be happening with the exception of Kanus who still had no clue what was going on except that a secret mastermind was behind everything.
