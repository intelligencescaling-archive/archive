Lore:

Redd White is a minor antagonist in the first Phoenix Wright: Ace Attorney game. He was the chief executive of the company: Bluecorp. This company would specialise in gathering information on high-profile individuals (e.g. celebrities, high-ranking prosecutors, finance workers, etc.), White would use this knowledge to blackmail and extort them, leading to a number of them (not specified) committing suicide to escape his grasp.

(influence, influence building, relationship management, social skills, suicide inducement, blackmailing, information gathering, information control, threats, extortion, mass manipulation, emotional manipulation, event manipulation)

________________________________________
DL-6:

Redd White plays a minor role in the events of DL-6, as he was the one who leaked the information to the press regarding the spirit medium used to attempt to determine the killer in the trial by the police, this would lead to Misty Fey leaving the Kurain Village deemed as a fraud.

White would then blackmail Grossberg (Mia’s former boss) in exchange for not revealing to the police that it was him who had humiliated them by revealing the truth about the trial.

(blackmailing, information gathering/control, threats, influence, influence building, mass deception, direct manipulation, strategic deception, misdirection, smearing)

________________________________________
Turnabout Sisters:

Following the events of DL-6, 15 years later, White (and his assistant April May) would continue to keep tabs on Mia Fey- Misty’s eldest daughter- who had been searching for sufficient evidence to convict White for his crimes. Using a wiretap, White was able to discover the location of the evidence (inside a clock statue shaped like The Thinker) and so set out to destroy it himself.

He used the statue and smashed Mia’s skull, killing her almost instantly and stole Mia’s files on him, writing the name “Maya” in blood on a receipt in the room to attempt to pin the blame on her for Mia’s death (using Mia’s finger to do so), he then had his assistant April May, call the police and testify that she had seen Maya murder her sister.

(counter difficulty, fabrication, concealment, formulation speed, strategic deception, improvisation, information gathering/control)

Wright would take up Maya’s case, believing her innocence and attempting to prove it to the court, after his initial investigation (and failing to get Grossberg to take the case for Maya- due to Grossberg not wanting anything to do with the case, knowing that it’s to do with Redd White) he would go into court the next day and following his cross-examination with April May, proved the possibility of a different culprit. This bought him the much needed extra day to investigate.

Wright would continue his investigation, eventually leading him to Redd White at Bluecorp, the man would punch Phoenix unprovoked to prove his superior influence and how it allowed him to do pretty much whatever he wanted, Phoenix left, investigated more and discovered suicide articles with “White” written above them (Mia’s investigation files), he then confronted White again and accused him of causing a politician’s suicide through blackmail, White responds by calling the prosecutor’s office and informing them that he’ll testify against Phoenix in court, promising the young attorney that he’ll get the most incompetent lawyer possible, promptly Dick Gumshoe’s arrives and arrests Phoenix.

(influence, threats, cunning, improvisation, formulation speed, moving the goalposts, setting traps, control over victim, scapegoating)

Wright refuses his state-appointed lawyer (for obvious reasons) and proclaims he has a plan to defend himself, he would push through in trial to the point that White was on the ropes (here Edgeworth would interject and inform White that he should admit to being the one to place the wiretap- this allows him to avoid responsibility for the murder itself, a far worse crime, by using it as an alibi to why he knew where the destroyed lamp was in the office, even though it’s not visible from the hotel room), Mia would appear (through Maya’s spirit channeling) and tell Phoenix to look at the receipt (used to incriminate Maya earlier), he would then point out the dates on the receipt and what had been purchased on it- the lamp, and the dates were only a day before Mia’s murder, proving that White had no possible way of knowing where the lamp was unless he had actually been in the law firm (and thus, the murderer).

Edgeworth attempts to call for another day to investigate whilst White tries to leave court, however, Mia hands Wright a list which he begins to read out, listing off several celebrities and finance workers, White would panic and beg him to stop, confessing to the murder at the cost of not having the list publicly revealed. White was arrested and Wright declared innocent.

(counter difficulty, influence, improvisation, logical reasoning, scapegoating, concealment, information control, strategy)

6- improvisation/ counter difficulty/ planning/ formulation speed/ adaptability (White had a decent plan and formulated it quite quickly, he also displayed a strong ability to adapt such as his quick decision to throw Wright under fire when it was more beneficial to him)

8- Emotional understanding/awareness/facilitation (as a blackmailer, he needs some understanding of his victims emotions otherwise he can’t abuse them)
