Roger Retinz is the main antagonist of the second case of Spirit of Justice. He was a famous TV producer who was formerly an aspiring magician, a part of the Troupe Gramarye, however, after being kicked out of the Troupe and having his dreams dashed, went onto hold a deep-seated grudge against the Gramarye’s and sought to get his revenge. (emotional engagement)

Approximately 2 years before the case, Roger met a fan of his magician alter ego- Manov Mistree- who he took under his wing and taught all his tricks to, allowing him to become the second Mr. Reus. It  is important to highlight that at the start of the case, no-one is aware of the fact that Roger was the original Mr. Reus, and that everyone is under the impression he's the same Mr. Reus (Manov Mistree) taking part in the show. (relationship management, social skills, knowledge, experience, crystalised intelligence, fluid intelligence)

The Magical Turnabout - Scheme:

Years after Retinz was ousted, he saw an opportunity for revenge when Trucy Wright - the only living heir of the Gramarye technique - was to make her television debut. To this end, he offered to produce and broadcast her magic show, having it framed as the great Troupe Gramarye revival with Trucy taking on the mantle as heir and Mr. Reus returning to perform alongside her. This is important to note as it links into Retinz second part of his revenge plot- ensuring this is a famed news story to dash the Gramarye reputation. (influence building, resource management, relationship management, social engineering, strategy building, logistics, vision, emotional engagement)

Having secured his position as the producer for Trucy’s show, he hired (Manov Mistree is also in on this) twin magicians “Bonny” and “Betty” de Famme to “prank” Trucy (side note, Retinz takes advantage of Betty’s incredible disdain for Trucy to convince Bonny to agree to the prank, while also positioning himself in the pairs relationship as a submissive admirer who she was in control of (in reality it was the other way around), also it is important to note, early on in the trial, no-one is aware that Bonny/Betty are two people as they both work as a duo “teleportation trick” which Apollo notes is done by identical siblings, from then on they testify together, moreover, these three are completely unaware of Retinz’s true intentions/scheme), placed a sword in the skywalk of the stage so that when Manov Mistree is pulled up, he is impaled on the sword and killed, and planted blood in the coffin used in the magic show that Trucy was meant to stab with a prop sword to make it appear as though she killed Manov Mistree. Lastly, he gets Betty to ensure the large prop dragon crashes onto the stage, obscuring the view of the audience and causing a panic.

Retinz also ensured that he would be at Take-2 Studio so that he had an airtight alibi for the murder. To prevent the murder being ruled as “accidental”, Retinz used a special notepad to falsify Trucy’s signature over a document that said she was aware of the prank and planted Trucy’s notebook filled with Gramayre tricks in Mistree’s bag (this was done to create a motive as though Mistree had intended on revealing Trucy’s magic tricks and she’d killed to prevent it). Lastly, he used Trucy’s signature again to falsify another document saying she had to payout $3,000,000 to him in the case of the show's cancellation to ensure her complete destruction. (anticipation, tactics, planning intricacies, invincibility, coverage, preparation process, contingencies, creative + application + linear thinking, setting traps, trap evasion, fabrication, concealment, misdirection, acting skills, resource + relationship management, leadership, emotional realisation + understanding, indirect + social + emotional + direct manipulation)

(the note Retinz uses to establish Trucy’s “prior knowledge” of the prank)

After the murder occurred, Retinz used his influence to get back into the studio and the ensuing chaos to swap out the metallic sword that killed Mistree with the prop sword Trucy had used. Here, Retinz runs into his first issue, as Bonny de Famme had set up Mr. Hat over the wrong trapdoor (this led to Trucy stabbing the wrong side of the coffin), which meant Retinz was forced to swap the coffin side with the planted blood on it to keep it consistent with Trucy being the killer.

Following this, Retinz edited the footage of the murder, removing the cards thrown when Trucy performs her sword swap to make it appear as if she never swapped them. In case this was ever revealed (as it eventually is), Retinz is able to deflect this as a problem as all media made for TV is edited and if push comes to shove, can always produce the original himself that he kept a copy of (this is important as naturally having the court’s version revealed to be manipulated with malicious intent, Retinz would be placed under immediate suspicion). (influence, fabrication, scapegoating, critical + creative + linear + holistic thinking, planning contingencies + invincibility + intricacies + coverage, anticipation, setting traps + trap evasion, FRI + VSI)

Also note that Retinz ensured to make contracts of all the magicians involved to prevent them revealing the prank script.

The Magical Turnabout - Court:

Some context as to how come Justice has managed to bring Retinz onto the stand:

1. Having seen through Bonny/Betty’s teleportation trick and revealed they were twins and not one person, he moves to indict her (Betty) for the murder as she was under the stage at the time of the murder and has a motive to want to frame Trucy (despising her).
2. From there, Justice is able to disprove her reason for being under the stage as “preparing for the fire trick” since she didn’t fill the fire bucket, arguing that she didn’t do any of that as she knew the show wouldn’t go on long enough for it to matter.
3. Prosecutor Nahyuta reasons out a potential “camera prank” for the reason Betty was under the stage, and here Betty hands over the prank script, informing the court that she knew the show would end as the prank as Mr. Reus was supposed to pretend to be a corpse.

4. Following this, Betty hands over Mr. Reus video before the prank

   5. Having concluded that Mr. Reus couldn’t have died while under the stage, this exonerates Betty and returns the suspicion to Trucy again since this implies he died at the point she stabbed the coffin. Here Apollo moves to assert that the sword Trucy used must not have been the murder weapon.
   6. This is in spite of the rubber sword she was meant to have been using being found under the stage, as well as the metal sword being found on stage and the blood being in the coffin as well as on the blade- Justice argues that the culprit swapped the blades after the fact.

   7. Nahyuta objects, noting that the blood in the coffin matched the blood on the sword, however Apollo argues this was fabricated after the crime. Nahyuta objects once more asking Apollo to explain how the victim died if he was alive up until he entered the coffin and died after she stabbed it.
   8. Trucy testifies, performing the sword swap trick for the court, here Apollo objects comparing her movements to the show footage and noting the discrepancy with the cards used when the sword is swapped.
   9. Bonny de Famme jumps in and reveals she had her own footage made by the cameraman who was a close fan of hers (outside of Retinz control/knowledge) and gives it to the court to compare.
   10. Apollo is able to reveal that the show footage had been tampered with to remove the moment Trucy swapped the swords- thus hiding that she DID have rubber one and couldn’t have killed Reus- to indict her as the murderer.

At this point Retinz himself objects, asking to take to the stand. He brings out the uncut footage and shows the court, it backs up what Apollo had been saying and he pushes this, however Retinz objects, noting that she could’ve killed him just after the dragon crashed down on the set. (abductive + logical reasoning, linear thinking)

Apollo tries to argue that Retinz is simply forcing his theory onto them to direct their attention away from the edited footage which was clearly made maliciously, however Retinz simply waves this away, explaining that editing is a simple core part of production and the change wasn’t intentional. (sophistry, verbal deception)

Apollo argues that he clearly did do this maliciously as he had forged Trucy’s signature showing the note that implied she knew about the prank and the contract for the show as being precisely the same- this is dismissed by both Nahyuta and Retinz who note that it doesn’t prove any ill intent, but rather immense greed.

Here Apollo seeks to prove a connection to Roger and Trucy by any means, positing the idea that Roger was in fact a magician himself to the court (this is due to having seen him make his camera disappear in the court lobby earlier). Apollo is unable to link this to Roger bearing ill-will however, as him simply being a magician (which is still dubious to the eyes of the court at this point), however has an epiphany that the only magician with such a grudge on Trucy would be the Great Mr. Reus himself who was ousted from the Troupe Gramarye.

Retinz falls silent for a moment and Nahyuta attempts to dismiss this, however Apollo notes Mr. Reus' scar in the 13-year-old poster and showing that the victim “Manov Mistree” did not have this mark.

Retinz reveals his mark and admits to being the true Mr. Reus. He explains that he had met Manov Mistree and trained him up to take over as the new Mr. Reus.

Despite having proven this, Retinz remains calm and cocky, explaining that it meant nothing as Retinz had been at the Take-2 Studio all morning before the murder. (misdirection, tactics, trap evasion)

Apollo is unable to dismantle this alibi, as it is the complete truth and has all Retinz’s staff to back him up. Unfortunately, here Retinz makes a cocky slip of the tongue.

By bragging about his grand illusions and magic, Apollo is able to realise that he’d been operating under the wrong assumptions- the culprit did not have to be at the scene of the crime to kill Manov Mistree. He replays the footage and notes the shadow that they had agreed was the culprit may not have been at all- he asks Trucy and she explains it couldn’t have been Mr. Hat’s shadow as he was kept at the top of the skywalk after being pulled up and Apollo argues it was Manov Mistree being raised.

Apollo reasons that this was done to kill Manov Mistree, and explains that the true culprit had planted a blade in the skywalk to kill Mistree once he was raised up there- thus removing the need for Retinz to be present to stab him at all. Here Nahyuta objects, pointing out that Apollo had only created a theory and lacked sufficient evidence as no blade was found in the skywalk.

Apollo is able to finally pinpoint Retinz as the culprit due to Bonny’s mistake in the show where she placed Mr. Hat on the wrong trapdoor, and that the culprit swapped the coffin sides to keep the crime scene consistent as a result of this.
