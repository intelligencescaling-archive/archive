A mini analysis on Xue Yin's counterattack against Li Guang

**CONTEXT: Xue Yin has just been defeated by Li Guang and had his upgraded body stats taken away by Li Guang via a mysterious obsidian dagger. After his defeat, Xue Yin somehow used his MC cheat code and regressed (respawned). Just before he regressed, the current (defeated) Xue Yin sent a message to the past Xue Yin, who was about to face off against Li Guang. This is the counterattack Xue Yin performed against Li Guang, which is also arguably his best scheming/ToM feat for now.**

**Summary of Li Guang's scheme:** [**https://www.reddit.com/r/IntelligenceScaling/comments/1uf3ms1/li\_guangs\_scheme\_for\_cultivating\_himself\_the/**](https://www.reddit.com/r/IntelligenceScaling/comments/1uf3ms1/li_guangs_scheme_for_cultivating_himself_the/)

Anyways, here it is (it's also my first time analysing stuff so bear with me)

1. To start off, current Xue Yin sends a message to his past self with this information:

* His teammates are innocent
* The recipe of the cultivation body-tempering technique (skim through Li Guang's feat; it's the same recipe)
* Feign knowledge against Li Guang
* The body in the Cultivation world functions the same as the body IRL
* Snow Thorn Roses can detoxify toxins
* Li Guang can share sense with animals
* Li Guang can absorb cultivation with an blade
* Li Guang schemed against us

This message achieves a few goals being: alerting his past self of Li Guang, indirectly putting the idea of manipulating his teammates more efficiently (since they are innocent, so he doesn't have to worry so much), and indirectly putting the idea of pretending that a stronger force was backing him up.

Xue Yin knew his past self was highly intelligent and is highly self aware.

(cognitive ToM, abductive reasoning, prediction)

2. Next, with this information, Xue Yin respawns into the cave with the cultivation recipe scripture, so he pretends he doesn't know how to solve it, because it would be suspicious if he did, since the recipe was hard to solve. He then goes to a guy named Genzou (who is in charge of the newbies) and uses this information to pretend he has a powerful force backing him up. Genzou thinks he has a backer, because Xue Yin purposely reveals he has a lot of information (the info from his past self). Using this intimidation, he pressures Genzou into thinking they're equals and so form an alliance. With this technique, he goes around influencing everyone.

(linear thinking, abductive reasoning, acting skills, concealment, cognitive ToM, social skills, social engineering, direct manipulation)

3. Next, he goes to Kang Seol-hee (to get supplies for the cultivation recipe) and makes an alliance with her too by using lanterns to make it seem that Genzou is the one backing him up. (direct manipulation, social skills) Of course, if she refused, Xue Yin could have just got them from Genzou himself. (contingencies) He keeps on talking to her and finds out that only four other people knew of mysterious ghouls on a mountain, which methods were very similar to a Cultivator called Corpse Collector. The four people were Middle-aged man, Li Guang, Genzou, and Shu Yangyi. (social skills, info gathering)

4. Xue Yin then indirectly manipulates Genzou and Li Guang into fighting each other. Kang Seol-hee said that Corpse Collector had been buying some materials, so if Li Guang was cooperating with Corpse Collector, he could easily deny this fact. Shu Yangyi didn't need materials, since she was rich. Therefore, only Genzou remained who could be allied with the Corpse Collector. Li Guang and Genzou then fight because (I'm assuming) Li Guang sees Corpse Collector as a threat. (indirect manipulation, abductive reasoning, linear thinking)

5. Now that Genzou and Li Guang are fighting, Xue Yin uses this as an opportunity to escape. He gets extra materials and sets up a scene to make it look like a cultivation accident, to distract Genzou and make Li Guang think that someone was backing Xue Yin, which would most likely be Genzou. Xue Yin finally escapes using the water streams, after the monsters on the surface disappear (he knows this because of the message his future self sent him). To test which water stream led to the outside, he first drifted some objects and waited to see if they made it outside. If they did, then he would take that stream to escape from his cave (he was also assisted by Li Yu, who successfully tempered him body). (lateral thinking, intricacies)

6. Finally, Xue Yin fights a weakened Li Guang, who stabs him again with the obsidian dagger, but this time, instead of absorbing a perfect body, he absorbs a terribly weak body. Xue Yin actually did solve the cultivation recipe, but purposely left his state incomplete and very weak. This move is what he had been waiting for since with Li Guang absorbing an incomplete body, he would be unable to use strong powers because of a chaotic Qi flow. So when Li Guang did absorb Xue Yin's weakness, the battle was evened out and Xue Yin just made it out alive. During all this time, Xue Yin, due to the incomplete cultivation, was extremely weak with his heart experiencing very high pressure. (mental fortitude, cognitive discipline, trap setting)

Overall, this counterattack against Li Guang had good manipulation, thinking, reasoning, scheming (not so good efficiency and effectiveness though because of his advantageous position and high risk), foresight, ToM (cognitive), and VERY good AC.

Tell me if anything's missing (also, this is all explained in chapter 34)
