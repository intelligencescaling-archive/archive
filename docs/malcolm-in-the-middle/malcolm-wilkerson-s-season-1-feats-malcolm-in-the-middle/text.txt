Been watching Malcolm in the middle, and decided to also look out for any noticeable feats that he might display, although the show should mostly be watch for its main purpose, but every media Isn’t really made for SCD anyways.

1x1
Malcolm was able to immediately notice every oddity in a photo test which he was brought in to do during school hours by the highest class, also known as the krelboynes. Stuff he managed to notice almost instantly was how he had more fingers (that one might be a little wrong since I’m going from memory), how it was sunset but the man watch in the drawing said 12 0’clock, and that the words should be backwards in the mirror.

1X8
He was able to memorise 32 digits between two credit cards, each one taking him about 1 - 1.5 seconds to memorise, leading him to memorise and recite both credit cards front digits in the matter of 2 - 3 seconds. Malcolm then was able add the individual digits of each card (74 & 66) which he correctly multiple them; which he then correctly squares it to 23,830,456. Malcolm would then correctly get the square root of 4,884 to 69.8856, which he then roots this and etc. Malcolm would then factor it correctly, multiple it by pie, cubed it, etc; showing us his extraordinary mathematical skills and working memory.

This shows to us that Malcolm can seemingly instantaneously do math problems in his head with correct precision, and this is definitely the most likely answer because of the random choice of credit cards that were shown to him, which he started from.

1x15
In this episode, Francis had a note from a college (if I’m remembering right) which he didn’t want his mother (Lois) to see, but since he screwed over his younger brothers, leading to them all getting grounded for 2 months, they ended up messing with him by acting like (mostly Malcolm) they had handed in the letter. Francis sent in his friend, Richie, to get the letter off his brothers, but Malcolm formulated up a quick tactic to get them out of the house while keeping the letter. What he did was get a envelope with a bunch of coupons in it, this being put in the VCR, told Reese where he’d put the fake letter, anticipating that his oldest brother, Francis, would know that Reese would know where it was hidden at, which Richie got after Francis told him to keep looking into his eyes, leading him to the fake letter in the VCR.

After running out of the Wilkerson’s house, Malcolm would tell them his strategy, revealing what he’d did, saying that he “knew what they all would do” which he revealed that he gave to the less suspected person, a place they’ll never think of looking; which was giving it to Dewey.

Not a whole lot, but surprising enough to say. If I had to choose his best stuff as of right now, I’ll say his observation and working memory are definitely his stand out qualities.
