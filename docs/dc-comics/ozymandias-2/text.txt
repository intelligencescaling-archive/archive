Physically gifted enough to shatter/break/dislocate bones at 6 years of age with little martial arts skill.

Adrian was born with Eidetic memory, the ability to remember everything he consumes , from when he was born he taught himself to read.at the age of 2 and started consuming anything he could, his mental aptitude marks were perfect he stated in Watchmen, (this made the teachers believe he was cheating)

Has eidetic memory, the ability to recall every piece of info he experiences. (Perfect memory), Alan Moore has stated he’s able to have 100% control over his brain. This means in his mind he’s able to store everything from his day to day life.

Further proof of this, he was able to perfectly recall the early years of his life during watchmen down to the test scores he received at what date, and his autobiography comic was narrated by him solely, what happened during what day, colours, every piece of dialogue and setting. Granted only the most important events were displayed (due to it being a comic book the purpose isn’t for the feat itself) it still supports the fact he has perfect memory.

Knows how to disable all firearms and extremely proficient in hand to hand and knife combat.

Gave away all of his fortune to charity and became a multi billionaire from scratch while being incredibly young.

Defeated Nite Owl II and Rorscach at the same time without much difficulty.

Kicked Batman off of the ship before he could react.

Master acrobat with extremely impressive dexterity and precision/accuracy able to throw a needle directly through the barrel of a gun from ~5+ meters away during night time.

When faced with 8+ armed thugs in a warehouse at the beginning of his career, within a matter of seconds he was able to calculate every possibility that would happen in the future and remove every unlikely possibility before they could shoot at him.

Brilliant Scientist: Very well versed in genetic engineering and quantum physics. His mastery of genetic engineering is so great, that he was able to create his pet, Bubastis, along with cloning the psychic brain of Robert Deschaines, mutating it into a giant alien-looking squid creature.

He was able to create a machine in his time to teleport a brain-like creature (genetically engineered out of a real human brain) in the middle of New York (all during his time without much revolutionizing technology). This was done in order to manipulate the world into unifying against his made up (brain octopus) aliens.

Master Engineer: Veidt is well versed in mechanical engineering as demonstrated through his creation of a variety of inventions such as spark hydrants, as well as designs that utilized Doctor Manhattan's energy. Veidt even successfully built his own private residence in the frigid climate of Antarctica.

Master Tactician: An excellent schemer and manipulator, with an ambition matching his intelligence, Veidt managed to successfully execute a plan to help Earth towards utopia by ending international hostilities between the United States and the Soviet Union and while maintaining secrecy about it. He is shown to be a ruthless master strategist, swiftly eliminating anybody who dares to get in the way of his plans, while maintaining a façade of a billionaire philanthropist.

Multilingualism: Veidt is fluent in English, Turkish, German, Russian, Vietnamese, and various other unspecified languages.

Master Martial Artist: Veidt is a superb fighter, martial artist, and almost superhuman unarmed combatant, able to subdue Rorschach and Nite Owl II with little effort. With the sole exceptions of Doctor Manhattan, Ozymandias was easily the most dangerous of the crime fighters of the 20th century due to his fighting skills alone. His fighting abilities are such that even Rorschach was wary of facing him in combat.

Rorschach stated he could not think of a more dangerous opponent to go up against when he was faced to fight against Ozy even when it’s a 2v1 with Nite Owl II.

Master Businessman: Veidt is a remarkably brilliant businessman and financial analyst. Veidt deftly built both a legitimate and criminal billion-dollar empire from nothing and it became large enough to become a global threat through his exploitation of advanced technology and genetics. Through Veidt Enterprises, he established several subsidiaries including Pyramid Deliveries and Dimensional Developments to help further his illegal activities.

Able to retain several sources of info at a time as shown with him having multiple computer screens playing at the same time each tuned to a different channel all the while he kept an eye on nite owl and rorschach behind him.

Caught a bullet point blank on 2 separate occasions.

Created a psych profile on Manhattan to manipulate him to leave the planet, an extremely impressive EQ feat as Dr.Manhattan cannot be read by anyone else. Manipulated Reggie (New Rorschach) and called the previous rorschach as a brilliant detective as he was, “predictable”.

A genius physicist able to lead the craft of a tachyon device over years to block Dr Manhattan's future sight in a time period without any revolutionizing technology.
