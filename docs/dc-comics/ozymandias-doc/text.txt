BEFORE WATCHMEN: OZYMANDIAS
Before I start with this I want to clarify, like the bane doc, Ozymandias also has Eidetic Memory. However unlike bane, Ozymandias takes it to the extreme, so extreme to the point where I’m gonna dedicate everything to his knowledge and his plan in Watchmen(just a warning, this will likely be one of the only feats mentioned in watchmen, since he doesn’t have much)

After Ozymandias quickly intimidates a thug so that he can gain information on an underground drug facility, he quickly heads to a ball where he knows the people will be. He doesn’t want himself to be tracked in this room however, so to make sure he doesn’t, he makes it so his eyes seem perfectly still, when in reality he’s scanning the entire room to check even the slightest detail as to where the boss is, as he knows a thug will be given a letter. He knew how they were gonna act, trying to be as unseen as possible, which is why Ozymndias does this in the first place. After he sees the letter and sees the man leave, Ozymandias circled the place 3 times and went after the thug. He didn't want to be seen so he stayed exactly 55 feet away from the thug. After locating where the base is he suits up and gets ready to take them down.
(Abductive Reasoning, Social Skills, Observation, Non-Verbal Deception, Camouflage, Convergent Thinking, Divergent Thinking )

Ozymandias makes a very quick and short plan when he infiltrates the place. In fact we know he does it exactly when he gets in as right then and there he observes EVERY possibility that could happen in this instance. Quickly making a plan that is full proof and removes every single possibility that could lead to even having a chance of lowering his chances of winning. So he takes the full proof route that will lead to his success 100%. This plan consists of him mostly blitzing everyone to the point where they fire where he isn’t, but we do get an interesting part at the end. After throwing
his headband with pinpoint accuracy, he makes sure to perfectly throw people into where it would benefit him. One person he throws causes cocaine to drop on the ground, which Ozymandias knows would create a gas that would not only cause people to go blind, however would also cause people to struggle breathing. Making them susceptible and completely unaware of anything that could happen. It’s revealed here that he has actually trained to a point where he doesn’t need to breathe to keep his body functioning. He uses this exact skill to get the hand over everyone in the facility. He gets a gun pointed at him by the boss, who he directly insults and makes fun of for not shooting the bullet. Just to further push his ego, which leads to a Klieg Light to slam into the back of the bosses head, which he perfectly planned by throwing his headband earlier. The cops come and Ozymandias quickly leaves, knowing this will surely impact everyone and will want them to have more of him.
(CPI, VSI, FRI, EQM, Self Regulation, Fluid Intelligence, Crystallized Intelligence, Deductive Reasoning, Inductive Reasoning, Logical Thinking, Abstract Thinking, Lateral Thinking, Creative Thinking, Linear Thinking, Analytical Thinking, Short Term Planning, Risk Calculation, Planning Scope, Planning Improvisation, Planning Depth, Formulation Speed, Emotional Manipulation, Verbal Deception, Foresight, Setting Traps, Prediction, Anticipation, Observation, Perception, Information Gathering, Pattern Recognition, Decision Making, Data Analysis)

Ozymandias Confronts Comedian. The Comedian tries to off guard Ozy by shooting him, he however hears the machine gun go off before the bullets are fired which he instinctively jumps out of the way and throws a Trash Can top at the machine gun. Keep in mind, he never knew exactly where he was. So he uses the machine guns sounds as a way to perfectly figure out where he is. We see them have a head on confrontation, where it’s revealed Ozymandias actually caught a bullet. He caught it specifically for this moment so that he could reverse the bullet and make it Break the Comedians gun. He does this by looking at his pointer finger and having instinct carry him all the way through. At this moment he knows that The Comedian will be blinded, he sprints into action and puts all of his body weight into his one attack so he can get a grip on The Comedian. Ozymandias demonstrates his perfect memory and knowledge of the human body, and he can put pressure on an exact body part if he wants to, not needing to apply it everywhere. He quickly adapts to being slammed to the ground by taking a bite out of the Comedians Cigar and spitting it into the Comedians Face. After this they have a 1v1 fight where they are evenly matched, but Ozymandias decides to lose. But why? Because he used this fight as a way to get an analysis on The Comedian. As a way to understand how he fights, acts and behaves.
(VSI, CPI, EQU, EQP, Crystallized Intelligence, Fluid Intelligence,  Knowledge, Deductive Reasoning, Abstract Thinking, Critical Thinking, Linear Thinking, Concrete Thinking, Analytical Thinking, Acting Skills, Observation, Intuition, Cold Reading, Information Gathering)

Overall Plan
______________________
This might get a bit confusing, so I'll start with the overall goal and then explain further.

What is Ozymandias trying to achieve? Simple: World peace. To do this, he needs to end the global pandemic and stop constant wars, so you might be wondering about his solution? He blows up most of New York which forces himself to endure the pain and suffering of the millions who die in the incident.

How does he do this? With the help of Dr. Manhattan, whose immense power is well-known; he’s essentially the “Superman” of the Watchmen universe. Ozymandias uses Dr. Manhattan's power to carry out his plan but ensures that Dr. Manhattan isn’t blamed. Instead, he convinces the world that aliens were responsible for the attack.

Here's the shocking part: Remember how I said he eliminated every possible way his plan could fail? That was when he was still a hero. He retired as a hero in 1975, according to the DC Wiki, and Watchmen takes place in 1985. This means Ozymandias spent 10 years—an entire decade—planning and predicting every move, manipulating everyone's actions to make his plan work. And he did all this inspired by science fiction media.

To convince Rorschach and Owl that his ideology was the right way to go, he made it possible, but not too easy, for them to find his hideout. This way, he could confront them and explain his plan. He always knew Rorschach wouldn’t agree and would rather die, while he expected the others to go along with it. In the end, Dr. Manhattan leaves, and everyone else lives a happier life.

So, that's Ozymandias' full plan, explained to a point where its understandable, but not fully explained but a bit, as we'll have doc solely on Otz plan, but It’s still pretty insane when you think about it.
