# BASE PREMISE OF THE FEAT:

During conversation with Saturn Girl in Doomsday Clock #11, Ozy exhibits complete resistance to SG’s telepathic influence, which is made possible by his perfect self-regulation.

To fully evaluate the impressiveness of the feat in question, we need to establish Saturn Girl’s competence:

Saturn Girl is basically a telepath from the 30th century. Based solely on the Doomsday Clock, her telepathic abilities extend to penetrating minds at unconscious lvls. This level of influencing implies the deconstruction of an individual’s mental settings in the deepest, most autonomous part of their psyche, which is hidden from the direct control of consciousness; These include repressed traumas, biological instincts and suppressed desires.
Access to these layers is blocked by psychological barriers, created by the brain defence mechanisms to protect against traumatic experiences. However, as already mentioned, Saturn Girl’s abilities bypass all these limitations.
My words are confirmed by the illustrated DC#4, which displays Saturn Girl’s competence as a telepath at the level I have stated.
