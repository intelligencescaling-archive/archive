                          Anti Feats:
* Now while Oz is very smart, he isn’t perfect with his intelligence, so I think it’s important for me to showcase his anti feats, this is basically for the people that want to know where he lands, whether it’s low tier, mid, high, etc.
* Has clear mommy issues, being pretty obsessed with having his mothers approval and affection, which his enemies could easily exploit since Oz still decided to keep Francis alive despite her turning into a vegetable. Sofia almost won by using this.
* He has a very insecure ego, anytime someone insults him he flies into a murderous rage, as shown with Carmine, Alberto and Johnny, while none of these instances caused him any issues, with Alberto’s murder even starting his campaign to take control of Gotham, it is still something of note since if we start comparing him to other SCD characters, that insecurity could surely be used against him.
* He drives a very vibrant and flashy purple vehicle, and if any of y’all watched Better Call Saul, you would know that having these cars are a huge liability if you don’t want to be arrested. This leads to Sofia being able to find Oz when she was first going after him, stating that the downside of driving a car like that is that it’s very hard to forget.
* He is insanely sadistic and bloodthirsty, this caused him a major problem since him burning Taj and Nadia alive instead of just giving them a quick death, led to nearly all of his product turning dead and useless save for two small buckets, due to the fire activating fire extinguishing sprays. At least Oz’s adaptability made up for this since he was able to make up for this stash pretty quickly.
* Despite attempting to make his hiding location a total secret, he made the mistake of trusting Eve, the leader of the strippers that work at Oz’s club with knowing his location, even though he should have known that Sofia could have found her and got the information out of her someway or another, which is exactly what happened.
* Either way, these anti feats aren’t the end all be all for Oz’s intelligence and for the most part he’s a really good tactician, and a lot of the time he is able to adapt to these mistakes or even use them to his advantage.
