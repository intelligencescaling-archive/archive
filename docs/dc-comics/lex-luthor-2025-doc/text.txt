This document is on the feats of Lex Luthor from the Superman (2025) film.

Prior to the film:

Luthor created a pocket dimension prison to house people who spoke out against him, metahumans and political dissidents. Furthermore, Luthor analysed and collected data on all of Superman’s battles, finding a strand of DNA and using it to create a clone - Ultraman - someone capable of being controlled and fighting on par with Superman. Luthor also conducted an experiment which would lead to the creation of “The Engineer”, who would fight using nanotechnology.

Furthermore Luthor had been the one to orchestrate the war between Boravia and Jarhanpur, predicting how Superman would rush to stop the war, this is what leads to the events that allow Luthor to tarnish and destroy Superman’s reputation.

Within Luthor’s pocket dimension, he keeps a team of trained monkeys that spam propaganda and further tarnish Superman’s reputation.

Superman (2025):

Using Ultraman (disguised as “The Hammer of Boravia”), Lex attacked Superman - Ultraman is allegedly attacking due to Superman having interfered with Boravia’s conflict with Jarhanpur - Lex, controlled Ultraman from a distance, in his “LuthorCorp” Tower, issuing orders and relentlessly attacking Superman. This is done to wound Superman, forcing him to retreat back to his base and thus allowing Luthor to discover his fortress in Antarctica.

After the battle, Luthor’s group travels to Antarctica (to ensure Superman doesn’t return in the meanwhile, Luthor has a creature released into Metropolis to buy time), having gained awareness of Superman’s fortress, breaking in- he anticipates a DNA lock and uses Ultraman to bypass it, destroying the robots and incapacitating Krypto. Luthor has The Engineer hack into the Fortress’ computer and steals the message Superman’s parents sent with him, recovering the damaged second half that Clark had never heard himself.

Luthor has the message translated, and spread to the masses, cleverly twisting Superman’s actions to fit the second half of the message and portraying the Hero to be a tyrant who was planning to rule over Earth.

Soon enough, Superman angrily confronts Luthor (who has kidnapped Krypto) and thanks to his outburst, Luthor is able to further shame and portray Superman as a violent alien who solely wants domination.

Superman turns himself in (hoping to be taken to where Krypto is), however, LuthorCorp’s PlanetWatch is in charge of his incarceration, and Luthor has him imprisoned in his pocket dimension prison, alongside Rex Mason whom he orders to use his power to create Kryptonite to subdue Superman preventing him from escaping by using Rex’s son as leverage over the metahuman.

Luthor soon brings a man who Superman had once saved to use as leverage while interrogating Superman, however he kills the man in a game of Russian Roulette without gaining any new information on Superman’s motives/parents, threatening to find more people to kill in front of him.

Soon enough, Superman escapes, and enraged, Luthor attacks his girlfriend - Eve - she turns on him and sends evidence of Luthor’s wrongdoings to a reporter at the DailyPlanet.

Thanks to the evidence, Luthor’s “real” plan is seemingly revealed… he had been selling weapons to Boravia at an incredibly low price in exchange for half of Jarhanpur for him to rule over and crown himself king of. The reporters begin to craft a takedown article to reveal all of Luthor’s treason to the public.

To ensure Superman returns, so that Luthor can have him killed, he creates a dimensional rift that will easily devastate the world, only planning to stop it once Superman is dead, as a result Superman is forced to head back to Metropolis.

Ultraman and the Engineer attempt to kill Superman by suffocating him with nanites (at Luthor’s command), however, eventually Superman knocks the Engineer unconscious and frees himself, continuing to battle Ultraman. Using cameras, Luthor is able to order Ultraman about, almost killing Superman.

Due to Krypto’s interference however, Luthor loses his cameras and thus is no longer able to issue orders to Ultraman, resulting in Superman overpowering and defeating him. Luthor prepares to escape, however Superman confronts him and soon enough Luthor is arrested, having been exposed of his crimes.
