【 NEW 52 】
Introduction
Bruce Wayne’s parents are gunned down in front of him during a botched robbery, swearing vengeance on all crime Bruce travelled the world honing his body, mind, and skills to perfection. It is stated that during his training Bruce Wayne became the best detective in the world, best martial artist in the world, and has mastered every field and subject. He also has an eidetic memory and has essentially no time between his thoughts and actions (perfect PSI + reaction time/speed). This is something that would take a supergenius their entire life to do 1/1000th of. Returning to Gotham he began his way on crime, however he lacked an edge. Taking on the mantle of the Bat as a symbol of fear he became a boogeyman to criminals in Gotham and beyond. Bruce’s uncle declares Bruce Wayne as dead while he is not in Gotham to hide the possibility of his identity being revealed.
When Bruce returns to Gotham after his training has concluded and is met with the Red Hood Gang. Edward Nygma (the Riddler) is working for Bruce’s uncle at this time, to avoid Bruce taking over and thus lowering Nigma’s standing in WayneTech he hires the Red Hood Gang to assassinate Bruce Wayne. Bruce is attacked by the gang before managing to escape to Wayne Manor. The fear he feels from this gives him the idea to use fear as a weapon becoming “The Batman”.

FSIQ, All Thinking, All Reasoning, Adversity Capacity, Emotional Understanding, Concealment, Intelligence Concepts, Learning Ability, Crystallized Intelligence

【 ZERO YEAR: FALL OF THE RED HOOD 】

Bruce Wayne, now Batman, uses the declaration of his death as a chance to stop as many RHG jobs as possible. He eventually reasons they’re stealing chemicals and committing other crimes to cover their tracks and dilute the directness of their plan. Bruce meets with Philip who is overjoyed to see he is still alive, he uses the information Nygma gave him to get access to the WayneEnterprises system so he could see what had been given to the RHG. He discovers one site the RHG hasn’t touched and deduces their final plan.
        He appears in public as Bruce Wayne and announces that the next RHG job will be a hit on ACE Chemicals. He claims they are making a corrosive cocktail that will be detonated in multiple spots around the city. As the announcement concludes RHG members appear in the ACE Chemicals building and fire RPGs at Bruce and the newscasters. Bruce enters the facility only to be met with RHO’s gun and the closing of the doors before anyone else can enter. The lights go out before RHO can shoot Bruce. The RHG put on their night vision goggles and see Batman swoop in carrying Bruce Wayne away before the lights turn back on blinding them. Batman takes out the RHG members and sees a member shoot RHO throwing him off of a shot aimed at Batman, RHO retaliates shooting him in the stomach before escaping to the roof.
        Batman deduces that the fallen RHG member is his uncle and rushes over to check (RHG blackmails people, Philip Kane had been supplying them weapons). Gordon and police have entered the building and hold Batman at gunpoint, Batman shoots a blank at Gordon knocking him back from where a support beam just fell. Batman escapes to the roof as well and shoots his grapple hook at the rope attached to the helicopter pulling it and RHO down to the crumbling building. They fight on a bridge until a vat splits open, melting the bridge down the middle. RHO clings on to a railing denying Batman’s help and allows himself to fall into a vat of acid. The tech that the RHG had gotten from WayneEnterprises had been tampered with by Bruce to show the image of Batman swooping in giving Bruce time to escape and reappear as Batman.

VSI, Deductive Reasoning, All Thinking, Leadership, Direct Manipulation, Logical Manipulation, Fear Mongering, Concealment, Sabotage, Short-Term Planning, Contingencies, Adversity Capacity,

        RHO’s body is not found but his mask is, RHG members gave up the name “Liam Distal”. Liam Distal however, had been found in a barrel full of lye (a toxic alkalic compound)that made it impossible to tell when he was killed or relocated. The Red Hoog Gang had been defeated and disbanded.

The TVs and digital billboards displayed a man donning a green costume and hat, “Hello, Gotham. Hello you, and you down there. My name, in case you were wondering, is Nygma—Edward Nygma. But you can call me the Riddler and I'm here to make you smarter. It's my mission, my calling, my duty. Riddle me this, Gotham: There are two sisters. Each gives birth to the other. Who are they? No, I'll give you a hint: One sister says, 'I am the day,' and the other one says, 'I am the dark... dark knight.Immediately following the riddle, the entire power grid of Gotham cuts out into darkness as Nygma officially kicks off his citywide takeover.

This riddle is the 2nd riddle of the Sphinx (alluded to earlier by the aviary wing riddle, to which the answer was the sphinx.) The answer to the riddle is Day and Night. In ancient Greek, the words for day (hēmera) and night (nyx) are both feminine nouns, making them "sisters". As day ends it gives birth to night, as night breaks it gives birth to the new day. This riddle is a parallel to Gotham’s transition from light to dark (Gotham also being a parallel to Bruce, as his parent’s death is the marker for the transition of Gotham from light to dark.) It also plays on the thematic connection of Batman and the night (his title, the dark knight, as well as his nocturnal behavior).

【 ENDGAME 】
This part of the doc will cover Batman - Endgame which has some of Batman’s best contingencies and establishes him as t1 in contingencies.

Bruce Wayne is suddenly attacked by Wonder Woman, she carries him off into the sky, he uses a pocketed device to make her lose focus and drop him. He inquires her reason for the incursion. She says that she is simply doing what the JL has wanted to do for a long time, kill him. He escapes and tells Alfred to release “Plan Fenrir”. He gets into a massive suit and returns, punching Diana into a wall. WW leaps onto the suit and rips an arm off stabbing Bruce through the suit.

Batman is bleeding onto the street with WW standing triumphantly above him, this is a lie. Batman snuck an ancient artifact called the ‘Bind of Veils’ to show her what she wants to see. Batman anticipates the next JL member that would come after him being Flash (Alfred was not able to track any of them, Flash is too fast to be tracked with Alfred’s technology). Batman activates the contingency for the Flash and uses the JusticeBuster Suit to track Flash’s movement. He follows this up with a frictionless coating sprayed onto the ground forcing flash into a building. Aquaman is next into the fray and Batman at this point has reasoned that the JL has somehow been mind controlled, he uses a hardening foam that sucks moisture out of its target to incapacitate Aquaman. Alfred informs Batman there are 3 members of the JL left, he stays put listing contingencies for members of the league before Superman crashes him into a nearby building. They fight for a short time before Superman rips the suit off of him prompting Batman to spit concentrated kryptonite dust into Superman’s eye, knocking him out.

        During the fight Batman asks “who did this to you” to which Superman responds “It’s quite a funny story”. This small clue tells Batman that it was the Joker.
