ozymandias: The Keene act

**context: The keene act is a law passed in** ***1977*** **that outlawed costume adventuring (masked heroes) making vigilantism illegal, Prior to that the act of vigilantism was allowed shortly after \[\[Doctor Manhattan\]\] creation allowing him to wipe out criminal organizations and demonstrate his Godlike powers to gain political attention, when the riots began ozy was already retired for two years thus rendering his public image intact**

**----------------------------------------------------------------------------**

**in** ***1975*** **ozy orchestrated an interview using his front company \[\[Nova Express\]\] to announce his retirement, the reason why being that dr manhattan is already a thing and a human like him can't do much and that he would rather focus his attention on helping the world by extending his business and becoming a figure for the poor and weak**

**ozy statement here achieved a few things:**

**1- ozy already anticipated the keene act and retreated from the watchmen beforehand to sell his public image and to avoid detection under dr manhattan and the team surveillance**

**2- bringing up how vigilantism became superfluous due to dr manhattan actions in vietnam hoping to draw attention to dr manhattan endeavors and reframe his image from a masked hero to a demigod and draw distinction**

**3- the timing of the interview as the journalist put it "considering the current social instability, why now?" driving the point of ozy wanting to lessen his involvement with the government since he mentioned \[\[Richard Nixon\]\] by name and stepped aside from his prior involvements with kennedy and the political scene, hoping to get under the radar**

**when the riots began in** ***1977*** **and the whole "who watches the watchmen" movement broke out ozy decided to take three routes to deal with the riots and public instability:**

**~~First route~~****: he participated with the watchmen helping them keep the riots under control in soho section without their knowledge since that area wasn't covered, walking around that section and avoiding detection yet making sure that the criminals knew who he is**

**~~Second route~~****: he used his front companies \[\[Nova Express\]\] and \[\[Triangle inc\]\] to push against the watchmen by covering the event and spreading propaganda about the watchmen brutality and invalidating \[\[New Frontiersman\]\] efforts to color the watchmen in a more appealing way thus forcing the keene act to drop faster by turning up the pressure**

**~~Third route~~****: maintaining the premise of his retirement it would appear to the public that ozy had nothing to do with the riots, \[\[Nova Express\]\] and \[\[Triangle inc\]\] aren't traceable back to him and the allegations of ozy participating would get shut down without concrete proof unless it benefited him**

**---------------------------------------------------------------------------**

**the** **~~First route~~** **took into account the possibility of the keene act being rejected due to the comedian and dr manhattan having ties to the government, it would put ozy in a comfortable position where he could simply confirm the allegations and get praised for his heroic actions**

**the** **~~Second route~~** **guaranteed the keene act to enroll, severely limiting the amount of information dr manhattan could get by foreseeing his future conversations with the watchmen members, and putting a wedge between the watchmen members that resulted in them barely talking with eachother anymore**

**the** **~~Third route~~** **ensured plausible deniability if these two routes failed, nothing can be traced back to him and as far as the public know ozy is working in the entertainment and charity filed after he resigned two years ago, placing him in safe spot**

**----------------------------------------------------------------------------**

**by ensuring the keene act enrollment ozy gained a few advantages:**

**1: hiring \[\[Moloch\]\] without anybody knowing since the original watchmen members moved on with their lives (dr manhattan interview trap preparation)**

**2: getting dr manhattan to work under the government which resulted in the public opinion of him to drop**

**3: making Rorschach theories easily dismissible to owlman since they haven't worked together in ages, and Rorschach reputation going downhill after vigilantism was deemed illegal resulting in the other members to avoid him**

**4: cutting the connection between the watchmen members making the last people that truly knew him forget about the one thing that they had in common, furthermore isolating each member resulted in dr manhattan inability to anticipate the invasion and stop it beforehand due to the watchmen falling off even when Rorschach tried to warn them**

**5: working with dr manhattan on new tech since he was working under the government which allowed ozy to observe his teleportation and replicate it for the invasion.**
