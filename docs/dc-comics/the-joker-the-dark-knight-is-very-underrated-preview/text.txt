The Joker (The Dark Knight) is very underrated + Preview Analysis

I just recently rewatched The Dark Knight and good lord does the SCD heavily scale him low.

His scheming, reasoning, EQ and foresight is insane in retrospect. Already planned everything ahead since the very beginning of the movie. I currently have him above DN and The Mentalist Duo and I'm planning to write an analysis doc about it.

One of the crazy part is I haven't seen anyone mentioned that it is heavily implied that \*he reasoned that Bruce Wayne is Batman early on.\*

Just to do a recap and Analysis:

When Harvey successfully made a leap in his progress to stop the Mob by imprisoning Lao (Joker intentionally helped him). As predicted, Bruce Wayne will be the one hosting the celebration party. This is Joker already reasoning the candidates for who Batman is.

Then Joker then declared to Gotham that he will start killing people until Batman reveal his face in the video recording, he placed the dead Batman impersonator and the joker card in directly in front of the police department.

By doing this:

\- Started the continuous series of making the police and the media question the justification for Batman to exist.

\- The Police will be the first people to closely examined the crime scene and the higher authorities wouldn't just give it to Batman to figure out.

\- The Announcement being televised make sure that Batman is already aware of it and won't need to visit Gordon for the chance of identifying the DNA himself quickly.

= This is to make the Police force discover the 3 DNA inside the Joker card while concealing the info from Batman by already fanning the flames of seperating Gotham and Batman.

This is important for the trap that Joker would set next.

When Joker heard that the Police finally discovered the DNA card, only then did he started his next move. Why?

1) This is to let everyone know and particularly Harvey, that the entire system is compromised for Joker to get that close to be able to get their DNA.

2) To have the crooked cops be the one to kill the targets and for everyone especially Harvey to know about it for a fact.

Joker inserted 3 people inside that card:

\* The Judge who prosecuted the Mob

\* The commissioner

\* Harvey Dent.

= As expected by Joker, the police would assume that these will be the people that he plan to kill them next. They would be correct except for the fact that as revealed in the end, Joker never intended to kill Harvey, at least not this early.

Joker visited Harvey himself personally while bringing his own men and didn't discretely assassinated like the other two- because he really didn't intend to kill Harvey as this is preparation with the goal to confront the Batman.

Why those 3?

The Jury who Prosecuted the Mob

\- Joker made a deal with the mob that he'll help them in exchange for their money and support, so Joker needs to make it look like he's keeping his word of the deal by freeing them from prosecution.

\- Joker had everyone and especially Harvey instantly assume that the mob is connected/responsible for this.

\- Joker put fear in other Judges and lawyers by killing her, effectively shutting down anyone who will attempt to stand up against the mob.

\- Joker made Gotham more scared as The Mob is on power again.

\- Joker made Harvey more frustrated as his life's work has been heavily setback.

The commissioner

\- Joker critically damage the morale and power the police hold, both internally and externally.

\- Joker created a free slot for someone to takeover as Commissioner, which is intended for Gordon, already setting him up to make Gordon hold responsibility for what would happen to Harvey soon.

\- Joker continue to put pressure to Gotham, making its people more scared.

Harvey Dent

\- Joker make Gotham know that Harvey will continually be in danger as it's hero.

\- Joker will frustate Harvey even more that Rachel's life is directly in danger.

\- Joker can personally identify the importance of Rachel to both Harvey and Bruce.

\- \*Most importantly, bait Batman to confirm his identity in this party\*

Due to previous tactic of effectively concealing information, Joker knew that for Batman to be here this quick and specifically Harvey, it likely means that he is one of the people inside this party.

As Joker arrives, Harvey is nowhere to be found but Batman appeared and when Joker throw Rachel out, he quickly understood the underlying emotional attachment/connection that Batman has towards Rachel, meaning that Batman is someone close to Rachel, confirming it to be either Harvey or Bruce.

At first, Joker thought it was Harvey due to him being coincidentally absent while Batman appeared. However, during Harvey's confession that he's Batman, Joker saw through this and realize that this is just a scheme meant to trap Joker. Confirming it by willingly stepping on that trap for the real Batman to appear and have him arrested.

Unbeknownst to them, Joker wanted to be arrested and already prepared a counterattack that further confirm that it's Bruce when Joker predicted that he will pick Rachel instead of Harvey to save.

This is why Joker believed Mr. Reese when he told the news that he knows who Batman is as Joker knew that it's Bruce and he knew that Mr. Reese worked for Wayne. This is also why Joker knew about the trauma that Bruce had when he lost his parents that lead him to become Batman, as he told him that he brought Harvey to their level.
