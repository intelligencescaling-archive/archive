Riddler's Hush Plan explanation

–Riddler is diagnosed with brain cancer. He goes to see one of the best neurosurgeons in America, a childhood friend of Bruce Wayne named Thomas Elliot, about it only to find that it is inoperable. He instead goes to visit a Lazarus pit, a magic pool that can grant lengthened life or even bring the dead back, and uses that to cure himself. Upon using the pit he finds clarity of mind enough to realize that Bruce Wayne is Batman. He then returns to Thomas Elliot to recruit him to terrorize Bruce with the promise of access to the Lazurus pit, only to find that Elliot already despised Bruce, and so the two begin working together. Riddler then devised a new look for Elliot, wearing a trenchcoat and bandages over his face, going by the name Hush. It would be this new villain Hush who, in the eyes of Batman and all his allies, has figured out Bruce’s secret and is organizing the villains, as Hush is the one who will be seen and is carrying out the legwork, allowing Riddler to work behind the scenes. (Fabrication, Concealment.)

–Riddler enlists The Joker through his perceived amusement of what they plan, Clayface, Scarecrow and Poison Ivy with money, and a disabled man named Harold through the promises of having Elliot restore his ability to speak through his surgical skills, which he similarly promises to Harvey Dent, in fixing his face. Lastly, he secretly infects Killer Croc with a virus and appears with a cure which he will provide in exchange for his loyalty. (Influence Building, Leadership.)

–Harold is a technological genius who had worked on even Batman’s technology, and so Riddler has him operate on Batman’s computer, installing a device with camouflage regarded by Barbara Gordon as ‘flawless’ to look like a standard CIA surveillance device, which would flash images of Thomas Elliot into Batman’s eyes randomly and too quick to be picked up upon, subliminally implanting the thought of Elliot in his mind. This came useful, when next Nygma has Killer Croc kidnap a rich boy and, while running, lead Batman to Crime Alley, where Hush would be waiting to shoot Batman’s grappling hook, making him crash and injure his skull. While unconscious, thanks to the subliminal messaging, Bruce would then use morse code to signal for Alfred to call for Thomas Elliot, thinking it to be simply as he’s an amazing surgeon and nothing else, allowing Elliot to implant Bruce with a tracking device and giving him an excuse to rejoin Bruce’s life. (Concealment, Psychological Manipulation, Indirect Manipulation, Strategy and Planning.)

–In the initial chase with Killer Croc, Batman came across Catwoman, who had been brainwashed by Poison Ivy into stealing Killer Croc’s ransom money. The next time Batman attempts to chase Killer Croc, the two come across each other again after the front left tire of the Batmobile’s car was shot. The coma Bruce was put into put him into a contemplative mood, making him malleable, and getting him to think about his life and his loneliness, driving him and Catwoman closer together. They would then grow even closer defeating Poison Ivy together. (Indirect Manipulation, Emotional Manipulation, Psychological Manipulation, Strategy and Planning.)

–Thomas Elliot, Bruce Wayne and Selina Kyle all visit an opera together. While there, it is attacked by Harley Quinn under Riddler’s orders. Thomas fakes running off to get help, and when Batman sees him next, he sees Clayface having transformed into Elliot after being shot by Harvey Dent, with Joker standing over the ‘corpse’ holding a literal smoking gun. This drives Batman into a brutal, animalistic rage. This is after all, the same man who beat Jason Todd to death, who shot and crippled Barbara Gordon, who has killed untold hundreds,  now having killed Bruce’s childhood friend. This is especially impacted as Bruce had recently been considering his life and loved ones, reuniting with an old friend and also growing closer to Selina, yet the Joker just had to ruin things once again. He almost kills Joker, and would have if it hadn’t been for Jim Gordon stopping him. Had he done so though, he would have been confronted by the further realization, upon analysis of Joker’s gun and the bullet in the body, that they didn’t match, and Joker hadn’t even shot him anyway, dealing a likely crippling blow to Bruce psychologically to know that he had broken his golden no-killing rule for a murder that the Joker hadn't even done. This would also have eliminated one of Riddler’s conspirators, the most chaotic and untrustworthy of them all at that. (Fabrication, Logical Manipulation, Emotional Manipulation, Psychological Manipulation, Strategy and Planning, Psychological Warfare.)

–Riddler then performs a simple heist he knows Batman will stop. What’s more, he leaves traces of the Lazarus Pit in the van he used. This causes Batman to seek out Ra’s Al Ghul who had been looking to find and eliminate the user of it, and this way Nygma could send Batman after him and hopefully eliminate him. (Logical Manipulation, Misdirection, Strategy.)

–Riddler finally has Clayface disguise himself as Jason Todd and take Tim Drake hostage, and even removes the real Jason’s corpse from his grave. This is the culmination of various clues set up earlier, such as the front left tire of the Batmobile being shot, as the hubcap of that same wheel was what Jason was trying to steal the night Batman found him, and Bruce injured his skull at the beginning in Crime Alley, where Bruce and Jason first met. What’s more, this would explain the Lazarus Pit connection as Jason must have been brought back with it, and why this mysterious party had wanted Batman to kill the Joker, as those were the two he would hold accountable for his death, further playing on Bruce’s guilt for his hand in said death, his greatest failure as Riddler saw it, and helping to even further destabilize his mentality. (Fabrication, Strategy, Long-Term Planning, Strategic Deception, Logical Manipulation, Psychological Warfare.)
