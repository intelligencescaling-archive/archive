【 BATMAN’S STRATEGY 】

A seemingly very small exchange, but a lot of thought from both Darkseid and Batman was processed during their standoff.

What Batman referred to when he said that Orion’s death “wounded” Darkseid is something that Superman recognized way later, and that is that through assassinating Orion with a time-travelling bullet, it ensured that John would find it, giving it to Batman, who would effectively kill Darkseid.

Batman quickly then follows this statement by telling Darkseid how he’s still restricted to a “mortal body”. A verbal deception on Bruce’s part to remind Darkseid that Bruce hijacked his plan and closed it into a self-consuming loop where the beginning requires the end where he loses, but without making Darkseid think Bruce realized this.

Darkseid, facing his own mortality, realizes that his victory is essentially impossible now, so Bruce basically forces Darkseid’s hand into putting Bruce in a trap of his own making.

The two possibilities at stake here for Bruce were either:
* “Darkseid realizes he can’t win, therefore he kills me with the Omega Beam, but I ensure his death. Mutual destruction and a sacrifice I’m willing to make.”
* Or “Darkseid realizes he can’t win, therefore to ensure another contingency in motion, he won’t kill me and instead will try to circumvent this time-loop by messing with time once more.”

Batman exploited the bullet’s own temporal anomaly and checkmated Darkseid into a corner, all while shielding said strategy with a clever deception, and the entire move is 100% in Bruce’s control. Bruce was already familiar with how Omega Sanction works and how it can send a person to die infinitely, therefore a temporal trap by Darkseid wasn’t impossible to predict.

Batman showcased incredible strategic efficiency (only used the materials available), effectiveness (singlehandedly weakened Darkseid enough to the point where the rest of JL could finish him), resilience (the only two possible options that he gave to Darkseid were beneficial to him), shielding (shielded Darkseid from thinking that Bruce was aware of his situation), and adaptability, given how he figured it out so quickly in such a bleak scenario by using his deductive (Darkseid’s temporal loop with the Radion bullet), abductive (Darkseid’s possible intent to preserve himself through another trap) and inductive (Darkseid’s Omega Sanction could be that trap) reasoning to the best of his abilities, all while perfectly anticipating and understanding Darkseid’s emotions.

When it comes to further details of other contingencies and layers Bruce set up for this strategy off-screen before he confronted Darkseid, it will only be revealed later.

【 DARKSEID’S STRATEGY 】

In order to fully understand the scope of Bruce’s plan, we must first analyze Darkseid’s, and in-turn, Hyper-Adapter’s plan.

As we learn in the end of The Return of Bruce Wayne, Bruce losing his memories wasn’t Darkseid’s idea, and instead, he tried to do the opposite.

As we learn, the Hyper-Adapter isn’t a physical entity, which means it’s hard to track, it’s an entity that operates on a conscious level.

Darkseid’s strategy was to use Batman’s own survival as the engine for his own, and everyone else’s, doom. The parasite was keyed to his consciousness, something we’ve seen Darkseid use plenty of times before in that very same plan, with Turpin, with Kraken, hell with everyone on Earth.

Turpin only succumbed to Darkseid once Darkseid inherently overrode his own consciousness, leading him to experience Ego-death, which means the same method had to be used on Bruce.

Darkseid already proved with his own revival that the JL wasn’t able to track New Gods’ possession of mortal bodies, meaning that this strategy was well shielded.

The idea is, given how Bruce will go throughout the entire history of mankind, if he’s accumulating Omega radiation throughout its entirety, he can effectively nuke the entire timeline.

It is important to remember that this strategy was thought of by Darkseid in a couple of seconds before Batman shot him with the Radion bullet.

* Figure out Darkseid’s temporal loop.

This already shows impressive tactical complexity, as Bruce was playing several layers into the future.

【 BATMAN’S ULTIMATE PLAN 】

To finally examine Batman’s feat, we have to breakdown the timeline into his chronological POV:

BATMAN in the 20th Century

Bruce’s realization that something is wrong stemmed from the 20th century, when we see him reevaluating his purpose as the “detective” for Marsha, and the book which saved his life containing his handwriting and allusions to various talks of the Bat People and some kind of higher deity, a symbol, which is protected in a box.

We’re never shown Bruce going through the entire book, but the heavy implication, and the only thing that makes sense is that he definitely saw talks of Thomas Wayne, a.k.a. Hurt. The reason for why this isn’t a reach, is because Bruce alludes to “Waynes” being mentioned a lot, and he was already familiar with the name of Thomas Wayne, who was apparently killed very recently.

The name being the same isn’t a huge stretch on its own, but the fact that Martha’s grieving mother goes out of her way to describe how terrible Thomas was, in ways that are perfectly aligning with the things he was doing and were mentioned in the book, means it’s highly likely Bruce connected the dots and figured out that this must be related to cultists.

The timeline on this wouldn’t make sense, meaning that this Thomas must’ve had some sort of immortality, which is probably what these cultists were trying to achieve.

Either way, Bruce’s own placement in this doesn’t add up still, because why would he be present throughout those moments, and randomly transported to the time when Marsha would need a lookalike? Maybe he’s being actively targeted temporally.

Bruce, while going over the book, unsure how he ties into all of this, says, and I quote:

“It was hard to avoid the obvious conclusion. The whole thing was a hoax. It was performance from the very beginning.”

This realization has two meanings. The first one, a more obvious one, is that Bruce realized he was being set up by Marsha into this cultist sacrifice. The evidence for this is how, even when the fire burns through the ropes which were tying him down, he still didn’t try to fight it, and allowed himself to almost die, as if he was counting on it.

The second meaning implies Bruce figured out that his own amnesia was a hoax, a purposeful, calculated move, purposely trying to lose his own awareness against whatever is clearly targeting him through time.

He allows himself to be captured and brought to near-death right as he starts getting overtaken by the Hyper-Adapter, who uses the ability provided by Carter to travel to the future.

BATMAN at the Vanishing Point

Due to suddenly overflowing with Omega radiation, Bruce is captured by the Archivists. They try to quarantine the parasite from Bruce, but to no avail, as it’s not a physical or biological entity. At this point, Hyper-Adapter has seemingly won, not completely, because Bruce is still not fully aware of his identity, but has enough idea to the point where the parasite is overtaking him.

At this point, he plans for both the future and the past. This is where things get a little complicated, which is why I’ll segment each one of his individual decisions:

【 MEMORY LOSS 】

Once Archivists take him, but are unable to truly destroy the parasite, Bruce tells them it’d be the most optimal if his memories were erased after Superman arrived in Hunt’s time-sphere.

The reason for this is that it will only deviate Hyper-Adapter’s control on Bruce further, plus, Bruce anticipates that without his memories, he will continue to move forward, ultimately reaching the 21st century and meeting the JL in the Hall of Justice.

【 ARCHIVIST BOND 】

Bruce suggests he should bond with the Archivist's cybernetic body. This is for two reasons:
* It will help him disguise himself in front of the JL, which will make his overtaking of the Time-Sphere easier.
* & He will be a far bigger physical challenge for the Justice League in the 21st century.

The reason for that second factor ultimately ties to his planned aim. By being a physical challenge, JL can’t take him on lightly, but they will still naturally try to be as non-lethal as possible.

After learning from the Archivists of Superman, Wonder Woman etc. Bruce notes that Wonder Woman will be present in the Hall of Justice, as he learns that her Lasso of Truth would be a natural way to disarm Batman in that situation, as it’s not a physical attack that will cause pain, while clearly immensely powerful and should keep him down.

Because the Lasso of Truth will force Bruce to tell the truth, his self-imposed amnesia must be overridden, which will give Hyper-Adapter the chance to manifest as a physical entity.

【 TRAPPING SUPERMAN 】

This was a move that Bruce had to pull, as otherwise, Superman, and the rest of JL members who were riding in Hunt’s Time-Sphere, were presenting themselves too dangerous, as they could potentially intervene with the timeline and make the parasite arrive early.

By ensuring Superman, Hal, Booster Gold and Hunt are occupied for the last couple of minutes at the Vanishing Point, but by then anticipating that Hunt would recognize the looping strategy Bruce ensured and use it to upgrade the Time-Sphere, he made the Hyper-Adapter think it has won only to be trapped in its own time-loop.

【 SKY CART 】

During pre-history, when Bruce just started his travel, a sky cart appeared which held relics of Superman, Wonder Woman and Batman, which Bruce from the future anticipated Bruce from the past would prescribe, especially given how the cave already had strange symbols on them.
During Vanishing Point, this sky cart was specifically used as a contingency to fall into the loop of the universe’s end and beginning. It purposely appeared to not just stir up Bruce’s thought-process, but the thought-process of the Deer People. They mentioned how they were far away from their tribe and were risking being attacked by Vandall Savage’s Blood Mob, and that wouldn’t have happened had the sky cart not appeared.

Because the tribe was naturally overwhelmed, alongside Bruce, he was tied down with the intention of being eaten the following day. He was weakened and in the process of death, which is when he began having illusions of a giant bat creature.

Archivist Bruce anticipated this, because he at that point already understood how Hyper-Adapter worked, as it needed Bruce to either fully remember his identity or to be on the brink of death. He indirectly manipulated Hyper-Adapter into exposing itself early on, which is how he ultimately trapped it.

Despite taking care of the physical manifestation and sending it back to time, that on its own wouldn’t be enough to kill the parasite. Instead, Bruce orchestrated this moment and sent the parasite specifically in the very moment when it would induce an illusion to Bruce, trying to conquer his mind and overtake his body.

Beating the parasite in the same he beaten Darkseid, by manipulating them into committing suicide.

Clarification

With everything out of the way, let’s wrap up this plan in its entire, chronological order:
* Bruce figures out Darkseid’s weakness and sets up a contingency to make sure his mind cannot be tampered with the same way Turpin’s was.
* Bruce deceives and checkmates Darkseid into sending him through time with the trap he had anticipated.
* Said counter makes the parasite attempt a different method, one which Bruce sees through, all while indirectly manipulating it into believing Bruce isn’t aware of it.
* Anticipates the moves of Superman, Hal Jordan, Hunt, Wonder Woman, Hyper-Adapter AND himself, after figuring out a loophole to fully defeat this contingency Darkseid sets out.

It was a multi-layered plan that proved itself incredibly resilient after facing counters to it left and right, not just from the parasite, but from his team unknowingly. The plan is effectively entirely invincible, as Bruce ensured the parasite wouldn’t be aware he had seen through it, because he allowed the parasite to pull off the plan it was trying to pull off.

Important to also note that throughout this entire plan, neither Darkseid nor Hyper-Adapter made any logical mistakes. Darkseid played the optimal strategy and he had no logical premise to expect Bruce would in advance tamper with his own memories.

Hyper-Adapter also made no mistakes, as it effectively already won, and the only reason Bruce came on top was because he formulated another layered plan on top of parasite’s, which it obviously couldn’t have taken into account because it didn’t even start.
