Batman's biggest anti-feat (and why omniscience doesn't work in SCD)

Personally, the biggest problem I've always had with the SCD community Is that deciding? Who wins often doesn't come down to analyzing their character and what they're most likely to do in a situation, but instead simply looking at who did the most impressive stuff. It feels like you could have a character that's really dumb and barely does anything smart, but if there was one random comic that existed where they did the most impressive thing ever, then suddenly they scale higher than Fang Yuan just because you put "Comp" next to their name.

As for Batman, >!he's literally in possession of a "Mobious Chair," that can answer any question he wants, so he asks it, what's the identity of The Joker? And the Mobious Chair apparently tells him that there are actually three different versions of The Joker that have been running around all this time. But instead of doing the obvious and going on to ask the Mobious Chair who those three Jokers are, he instead just gets up and starts going through existential-crisis about the three Jokers. Like... Why aren't you asking the Chair more questions- Even just How to defeat them all?!<

The problem with having the ability to do anything is that then you literally have no excuse for failing, So if you do fail, it puts into question. If you're really as smart as everyone thinks you are. It reminds me of a movie that exists called "Absolutely Anything," And it's actually a pretty funny movie, and not meant to be taken seriously at all. >!The plot literally revolves around a guy who is given a superpower by aliens to literally change reality to whatever he wants by simply stating what he wants to happen, and then waving his hand. Of course, This cues a whole bunch of cases of: "be careful what you wish for." A bunch of people die in a plane crash, so he wishes for: "Everyone who's dead, be alive again." But this results in a zombie apocalypse. He wishes to be President of the United States, But this results in him suddenly being shot at by a bunch of people and being given a massive amount of paperwork.!<

Then there's a scene where >!he sits in front of a TV and tries to solve all the world's problems by solving the housing crisis, and global warming, and making it so that "there is no reason for anyone to go to war." This of course causes global cooling, for there to be a financial and land crisis due to too many houses, and, most comedically, for the entire world to go to war "for literally no reason." But, instead of him trying again, he just wishes everything to go back to normal. There's even a scene early on in the movie where he bothers to wish for a good idea on what to do next, so why doesn't he ever try that again?!<

It reminds me of an episode of the Twilight Zone >!where a guy Is given the power to manipulate reality to his will, and because he apparently finds people annoying, he essentially wishes everyone away, but quickly realizes how boring and lonely it is, so he tries to get some excitement by wishing some random natural disasters and to being, and then when he realizes that stupid, he instead just wishes everything to go back to normal, and they're supposed to be a moral about how maybe this really is the best possible world or something. It's just so stupid.!<
