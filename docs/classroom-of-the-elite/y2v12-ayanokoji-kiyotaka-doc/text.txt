Ayanokojis ideal plan is to have all 4 current leaders graduate, but this special exam made things difficult because of the bet that both arisu and ryuuen made, which consisted on whoever lost they would be expelled from the school
So koji made meetings with the leaders of each class: horikita, ryuuen, ichinose and arisu. And with other persons
He first met with horikita, which was the most important cause he is her classmate, to make koji the general and horikita the middle guard, this to assure victory for class B, with the condition that he wont help class B again, unless horikita managed to win against ichinose without his help, in which horikita agreed, and with hirata, they were the 3 representatives of class B, being hirata the vanguard, horikita the middle guard and koji the general
He then met with ryuuen to see how he was doing, having to see his resolve on how he was going to beat arisu
After the meeting with ryuuen, he met with yamamura to get information, information that ill say later on the doc, he also met matsushita to have that information confirmed
He then met with hashimoto to know how he was doing as arisu knew he was a traitor, while hashimoto wants to go to class C, he also wants koji to go there, but leaving the response in suspense, koji said to hashimoto “dont lie to yourself” so he can express his feelings, but this had an ulterior motive aswell
In Sunday, he met ichinose to remind her about the promise he has with her since last year, he also met with kanzaki after he was done with the gym. Kanzaki asked if the rumours that koji was going to be general were true, koji denying it, he pleaded koji to let class D win since they are going to be more burried in class points if they lost, koji ignored him
He then called arisu at night for the same thing as ryuuen, and concluding that, the exam would start tomorrow
Im going to divide the battles now
* Class B vs class D
After horikita losing all her lives to ichinose, she was met with koji, she had failed to win without needing his help so she was sad because she lost, but koji comforted her, saying that she has allies to rely with, and with that, he faced ichinose.
Ayanokoji had 2 objectives in here: defeating ichinose which was the main objective, and expel maezono
In order to do that, koji told ichinose to reveal her group selection while revealing his own, this to ensure fairness using the fact that ichinose has modals, and let her select her class traitor, this to make her let koji select his class traitor, which he selected maezono as the traitor, fast forward 3 rounds, koji interrogated maezono, she said she wasnt the traitor, and koji outed her as the traitor, this means maezono would be expelled. The reason he was doing this its because she was leaking information to hashimoto, her lover, and was making things difficult for class B, he had proof from yamamura and matsushita that she leaked the information, and therefore, expel maezono from the school. Koji then proceeded with his other objective, win the match, in order to do so, he told ichinose the truth. Koji made ichinose rely on him for over a year so he can use that for his advantage, this is what made her a fearful and fearless opponent, so koji decided to break that relationship by saying to her that he was the one that leaked the rumours about ichinose and the other classes in order for her to rely on koji, and summing the sudden expulsion of maezono, it break ichinose, being unable to interact with anything and be there, sitting, not reacting to anything, including the exam, making class B the winners
