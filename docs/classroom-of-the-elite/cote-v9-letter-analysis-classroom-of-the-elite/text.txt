COTE V9 Letter Analysis

There has been a lot of analysis on the V9 rumor mill. So, I am not going to go over that in this post. Rather, I want to go over the lead up to the rumor mill. The below dialogue would give a better idea of what I am aiming to analyze here -

![img](1x57uu2cm9xh1 "COTE Y2V12 dialogue between Kiyotaka and Ichinose")

In the above dialogue, Kiyotaka clearly mentions that he was the person that spread the "Ichinose Honami is a criminal" rumor.

Based on various context clues given in V9 as well as the fact that even Kiyotaka's monologue in Y2V12, we can comfortably conclude that he is telling the truth.

This raises a few questions. How did Kiyotaka even figure out, that Ichinose had committed a "crime" in the first place? This post is going to tackle that question.

Before, the analysis starts, I want to establish a few concrete points.
A)Sakayanagi's main motivation is to get Kiyotaka to compete/fight her.
B)Ichinose had already underwent a similar situation of being attacked by rumors in V6.

Now, lets get into the actual analysis -

Sakayanagi started her attack on Ichinose by spreading all sorts of malicious rumors about her. However, Ichinose wasn't taking any action at all to prove her innocence or fight back against the rumors that were going on. In fact, it had gotten to the point where Kanzaki was making moves on his own to find the source of rumors and put a stop to them.

Ichinose in V6 instantly shut down the rumors about her illegally accumulating private points by going to the school and proving her innocence. Yet, she hadn't taken any action at all this time around. This can possibly imply that the rumors were actually "true" and that Ichinose didn't want to go to the school to avoid leaking it.

However, that doesn't work as well. Sakayanagi was very confident about her plan succeeding. She had even gone as far dropping clues about all of this to Kiyotaka (in V7.5 & V8). This would mean that Sakayanagi thinks whatever 'weapon' she has can destroy Ichinose. So, if this 'weapon' were the rumors, then it had already failed. Cause, while Ichinose isn't reporting it to the school, she was also relatively unaffected and was going about her regular life.

Sakayanagi had mentioned to Kiyotaka that Ichinose and Kamuro shared a "problem" (V7.5) and that she possessed an "hidden side" that made her unfit of being the class bank (V8). Sakayanagi would have no reason to lie here, since her main motivation is to fight Kiyotaka and she was trying to destroy Ichinose, to get Kiyotaka to acknowledge her ability.

From this Kiyotaka can infer a few things -
Sakayanagi had a weapon, that she was confident can destroy Ichinose. Ichinose knew whatever this weapon was, and that's the reason why she didn't report these rumors to the school.

But, what was the point of these rumors then, if they didn't contain any truth? Rumors can affect people's perception of someone. Especially, in the case of someone who enjoys the trust and attention of others like Ichinose. So, Sakayanagi was using the rumors to damage Ichinose's reputation, since she knows Ichinose wouldn't report to the school.

So, Kiyotaka concludes that the rumors that were spread initially, effectively sets the scene for the final revelation to happen. If we look at the rumors that were spread -

* Ichinose was a delinquent in middle school.
* Ichinose had a lot of anger issues.
* Ichinose engaged in compensated dating.
* Ichinose has an history of drug abuse.

If Sakayanagi was setting the scene with these rumors, then the 'weapon' she possesses is associated with these rumors. The common point between these rumors were that they are all considered as problematic by society or acts committed by people who are viewed as sinners/criminals.

Sakayanagi had already mentioned how Ichinose has a problem and also that she cannot be trusted with the matters of being a class bank. With the above point, Kiyotaka concludes that the 'weapon' Sakayanagi holds, is the knowledge of some problematic action/sin/crime committed by Ichinose.

Sakayanagi had declared war on Ichinose. And also knows her past. Given Sakayanagi's rather ruthless nature, Ichinose should have known that her past mistake getting exposed is an inevitability. So, she should try and clear her name before Sakayanagi exposes everything. Like come out with the truth herself or point out that Sakayanagi is harassing her.

But, Ichinose wasn't doing anything. She was just silently ignoring all of the rumors. From this Kiyotaka concluded, Ichinose didn't want to face her past. She likely felt guilty about whatever mistake/sin she committed.

And that Sakayanagi was using exactly this to push her into a mental corner. Since Sakayanagi's end goal is to impress Kiyotaka and get him to acknowledge her and then fight her, Sakayanagi was most definitely going to use this to get Ichinose to step down as the leader.

In order to that, Sakayanagi was sure to attack Ichinose in the coming days and in order to maximize the use of Ichinose's guilt, this attack would likely occur in the public. Though Kiyotaka figured out this much, he cannot do much to stop it, since he didn't even know what mistake Ichinose made.

The only way to stop the attack in this case, would be to prevent Ichinose from stepping in public. But, not attending/skipping school would result in class point reduction. So, simply asking Ichinose to not attend school or go out in public wouldn't work.

But, Kiyotaka knew Ichinose still felt guilty over her past mistake. So, before Sakayanagi could, he triggered her guilty feelings by spreading the letter that claimed "Ichinose Honami is a criminal". Ichinose would be forced to face the memories of her mistake every time someone brings up that rumor. This would cause Ichinose to hole herself up in her dorm room.

But the rumors also had another objective. Sakayanagi would have immediately know someone else had spread the letter rumor. Since Nagumo had settled on using Sakayanagi, it doesn't make sense to think he would actively involve himself and spread rumors either. The only person who could have done so, is Kiyotaka. Since Sakayanagi gave hints about Ichinose's weakness only to Kiyotaka.

Since the main reason behind Sakayanagi's goal of crushing Ichinose is to prove herself to Kiyotaka, the possibility that Kiyotaka is interested in this and that she can play with him would influence her more so than destroying Ichinose. So, Sakayanagi would opt to use Ichinose as a medium to fight Kiyotaka.

This is why Sakayanagi sent Kamuro to Kiyotaka's place, to reveal details about Ichinose's past mistake AKA shoplifting, to Kiyotaka. In V9, Kamuro met with Kiyotaka right after the rumors accusing Ichinose of being a criminal dropped. The timing is actually very convenient.

Even Kiyotaka directly states that -

![img](ucghqag8x9xh1 "V9 Kiyotaka noting that he predicted Kamuro getting in touch with him and informing him about Ichinose.")

Using this info, Kiyotaka ends up rescuing Ichinose (both from Sakayanagi, Nagumo and herself). This all happens even before the actual rumor mill sequence in V9 and effectively is the set up for it.

Also on how Kiyotaka deduced Nagumo's involvement -

But how did Sakayanagi secure this information from Ichinose? In V7.5, Sakayanagi had simply mentioned that she had cold read Ichinose. But Ichinose has a surprisingly good poker face (as seen in V4 Zodiac exam). While naive, Ichinose isn't incompetent or a type of person that can be easily lead fooled/charmed like Ike or Yamauchi.

So, Kiyotaka reasoned that someone must have given Sakayanagi information. Ichinose's closest friends seem unaware and Hoshinomiya likely wouldn't reveal info to Sakayanagi either. The only culprit left could only be Nagumo. He had went behind Manabu's back to get Ichinose to join the student council. Given that Ichinose used to admire Nagumo (as shown in V4.5), it makes sense that he would have been able to get Ichinose to reveal her past.

By identifying Nagumo as the person who gave info to Sakayanagi, Kiyotaka was also able to identify Nagumo's goal as well - to break and then save Ichinose, so he can use her in the future to expand his own influence.

I thought of making this post, because while the rumor mill gets a lot of attention (deservedly so), I haven't see a lot of people discuss this.

TLDR: Kiyotaka, using the clues that Sakayanagi had given him and Ichinose's actions, was able to identify that not only did Ichinose commit a sin/mistake of some kind in her past, but also felt very guilty about it. He then deduced Nagumo's involvement, figuring out he gave info to Sakayanagi. He was able to anticipate Sakayanagi's actions of aiming to make Ichinose give up her leader position. He countered this by using the letter rumors to make Ichinose hide out in her room and to also get Sakayanagi to basically confess the truth about Ichinose's past. This info is valuable for Kiyotaka to actually rescue Ichinose from herself.

Mini TLDR: Kiyotaka only manipulates high school girls lol.
