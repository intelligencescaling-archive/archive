short summary vol 2 feats of ayanokoji kiyotaka

(by a some other not me)

Year 1, Volume 2 (Part 1): Short-Term Planning / Preparation Process / Working Under Greater Limitations / Strategy (Castling) / Direct Manipulation / Indirect Manipulation

Context: Ryuen orders Ishizaki, Komiya, and Kondo to lure Sudo to a building without cameras or witnesses so they can provoke him into attacking them. Since Sudo was impulsive, the plan works. Ishizaki's group then falsely reports that Sudo assaulted all three of them in a 1v3 fight.

Sudo tells Ayanokoji what happened. Ayanokoji initially decides that saving him would be difficult and has no intention of helping.

The next day, Chabashira informs the class. Sudo asks Horikita for help, but she refuses, believing he should face the consequences of his actions. The class then tries to find a witness.

Sudo mentions hearing someone breathing behind a wall during the fight, suggesting there may have been a witness.

Ayanokoji deduces that Horikita would only help if she had a valid reason. When Kushida asks him to help convince Horikita, they fail because Horikita believes saving Sudo would only repeat the cycle.

Ayanokoji also deduces that Horikita intends to abandon Sudo to teach him a lesson. (Deduction)

Ayanokoji joins Kushida, Ike, and Yamauchi in searching for a witness, mainly to appear cooperative. (Disguise)

Later, Horikita reveals that the witness is Sakura after noticing her nervous body language when witnesses are mentioned.

Kushida and Sudo fail to persuade Sakura to testify. Even if she did, the school could dismiss her testimony because she is from the same class.

Ayanokoji then points out that classrooms contain tiny, hidden surveillance cameras that nobody had noticed in two months. (Preparation Process, Strategy, Observation)

He also realizes Ryuen deliberately chose a location without cameras. (Observation, Deduction)

Ayanokoji takes Horikita to inspect the special building, confirms there are no cameras, and remarks that cameras would have solved everything.

They then meet Ichinose, who offers to help. She proposes an alliance between Classes B and D to deal with situations like this more effectively.

Horikita accepts, and the alliance is formed.

Later, Sakura decides to testify thanks to Ayanokoji. However, the night before the hearing, she becomes frightened and wants to withdraw.

Instead of forcing her, Ayanokoji simply tells her to do what she believes is right and that they will manage if she cannot testify. Encouraged by his words, Sakura gathers the courage to appear at the hearing. (Emotional Understanding, Direct Manipulation, Emotional Manipulation, Persuasion)

On the day of the new disciplinary hearing, Ishizaki's group withdrew their complaint, and both sides agreed to settle the matter. Ayanokoji deliberately did not attend, leaving Horikita to handle everything herself.

Afterward, Chabashira-sensei told Horikita that she had unknowingly fallen into Ayanokoji's trap. She questioned who had truly devised the strategy, why Ayanokoji always acted like an average, incompetent student, why he had intentionally scored exactly 50 on every entrance exam, and why he only involved himself indirectly.

Horikita eventually realized that Ayanokoji had been the true strategist from the beginning. She concluded that he had intentionally pointed out the nearly invisible classroom cameras, then deliberately taken her to the special building so she would notice the lack of cameras there. By doing so, he planted the idea of using fake surveillance cameras as a bluff, making Horikita believe she had thought of the strategy herself while he anticipated her every move. Although Ayanokoji never admitted it, his inner monologue confirms Horikita's conclusion was correct. (Preparation Process, Strategy Castling, Direct Manipulation, Indirect Manipulation, Logical Manipulation, Verbal Deception, Disguise, Acting Skills, Bluffing, Fabrication, Misdirection, Setting Trap, Anticipation)

By subtly guiding Horikita into "creating" the plan herself, Ayanokoji directly manipulated her into asking Ichinose for assistance. Since Ichinose was widely known for her honesty, having her present the bluff alongside Ayanokoji made it far more believable than if Horikita had done it herself, as Ishizaki's group would likely have dismissed Horikita's attempt as a desperate last-minute bluff.

Through Horikita, Ayanokoji indirectly manipulated Kanzaki and Ichinose into acquiring the cameras. He then manipulated Ichinose into carrying out the bluff, while her credibility indirectly manipulated Ishizaki's group into surrendering. During the confrontation, Ayanokoji also directly manipulated them through carefully layered deception. (Preparation Process, Strategy Castling, Direct Manipulation, Indirect Manipulation, Logical Manipulation, Verbal Deception, Disguise, Acting Skills, Bluffing, Fabrication, Misdirection, Setting Trap, Anticipation)

Although Ayanokoji did not yet know Ryuen was the true mastermind behind the incident, he still incorporated a contingency into his strategy by ensuring Ishizaki's group would not have an opportunity to stop and think by contacting someone. If Ichinose had not seized Ishizaki's phone, Ayanokoji intended to do it himself. (Intuition, Contingency Planning)

Ayanokoji successfully misdirected Ichinose, Kanzaki, Ishizaki's group, Tachibana, Sakagami-sensei, Ryuen, and even Horikita into believing Horikita was the mastermind behind the entire operation. Horikita only discovered Ayanokoji's true role after Chabashira pointed it out. (Misdirection, Disguise)

Manipulating Ishizaki was particularly difficult because he was significantly less gullible than Komiya and Kondo, requiring multiple layers of deception and carefully structured arguments before he finally surrendered.

Other feat (Year 1, Volume 2): During a conversation with Hirata, Ayanokoji noticed that Hirata never called Karuizawa by her first name despite them supposedly being a couple, while Karuizawa addressed Hirata by his first name. This subtle inconsistency led Ayanokoji to suspect their relationship was fake. (Observation)
