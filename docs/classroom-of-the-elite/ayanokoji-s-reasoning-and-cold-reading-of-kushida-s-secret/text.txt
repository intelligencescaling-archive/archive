In the class introduction koji notices that kushida weirdly includes the fact that none of her old classmates were at this school.
\[Social/Emotional Perception\]

After a little tussle between horikita and sudou, kushida uses the opportunity to confront koji and feign concern for horikita. Kushida then proceeds to ask koji questions such as whether he knows horikita from before and whether they were dating since she really wanted to be friends with her and wasn't sure how to do it.

This alerts koji as kushida used horikita's name which she would have no way of knowing as there had been no register and hori had only talked to koji thus far and declined giving a class introduction. This allows koji to make a few deductions,

\>kushida knows horikita from before, they likely went to the same school together

\>kushida was a liar as she was acting as if they had never met before whilst trying to ascertain information about hori under a guise of friendship

\>To be talking to him rn meant that kushida had successfully managed to ascertain horikita's only relationship she had made in her time at the school up to this point

\>Since hori had been very antisocial up to this point and they had limited interactions kushida must have been tailing them and observing horikita closely to find her only somewhat positive relationship she had made

\>people would only put this much effort in to gain information on someone if they perceived them as a threat. kushida perceives hori as a threat likely due to a past event. The past event was likely in school and to do with her classmates due to the odd inclusion she made in her class introduction

\>kushida was trying to judge koji's closeness to horikita to ascertain whether he could also be a threat

Due to these deductions koji chooses to downplay his closeness to hori as much as possible to avoid giving kushida information and prevent himself from becoming a target.

Keep in mind this all happens in the seconds that they converse and he responds. He must have managed to make all these deductions in just a few seconds and still manages to respond appropriately to counter her espionage
