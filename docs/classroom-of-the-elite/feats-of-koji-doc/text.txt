But it is precisely this conscious decision, declared of Kinugasa in Y2V11, that makes the final point of transformation of Nagumo’s thinking. Why does Nagumo ultimately make such a “senseless” (from the point of view of the mass of CotE readers and active participants in this subreddit) decision? This is the riddle Kinugasa offers readers to solve so that they understand once again one of the main plot ideas, but refracted through the development of Nagumo.

Kinugasa never wrote and did not intend to write that the purpose of ANHS is to identify and develop people who suppress the desires and capabilities of other people in order to become the “elite”. Kinugasa is exactly against what it is considered as elite in modern society: one people who suppress other people. That is why his protagonist, while remaining psychologically and emotionally neutral, directs all his activities at ANHS to improving the abilities and capabilities of other students.

When Nagumo realized that he could not “defeat”, that is, suppress and subjugate Manabu, he was simply annoyed by this fact. But when in Y2V9 Horikita Suzune revealed to him Kiriyama's entire plan in a short time, which he himself had never been able to notice and realize before, to which Kiriyama stated that he sensed Manabu's return to the student council, Nagumo became very thoughtful. An almost random girl instantly figured out the method by which Kiriyama simply controlled Nagumo as his own pawn, but Nagumo himself did not even notice it. There is definitely a need to reassess your own abilities and capabilities.

When in Y2V7 Kiyo offered Nagumo two scenarios that he was interested in pursuing, but needed Nagumo's resources and authority to execute them, Nagumo didn't even realize that this was not a duel between him and Kiyo, but simply a way for Kiyo to use Nagumo in the dark to get the result he needs. Defeating Nagumo in a duel was simply part of Kiyo's plan, not his goal. It is unlikely that Nagumo understood this, but he was greatly alarmed by the complete secrecy of Kiyo’s actions to eliminate Yagami from ANHS. This made Nagumo want to more fully appreciate Kiyo's abilities and capabilities as an individual whom he wants to fight, but he does not understand what he will face.

Kinugasa gave Nagumo this opportunity in Y2V11. While remaining unnoticed, Nagumo can observe and evaluate Kiyo's abilities and capabilities in a wide variety of activities. He just needs, without arousing suspicion, to interest Kiyo in a fair fight for the result, which is not a problem for Nagumo, since he has a lot of personal points. Kiyo takes Nagumo's bait and shows his real abilities and skills, especially since these his actions aroused in him a genuine interest in new types of human activity.

Kiyo's result in this competition most likely simply amazed Nagumo. After all, he was not only able to verify for himself the enormous variety of Kiyo's abilities, he was able to compare this, while remaining honest with himself, with his own expectation of the result that Nagumo himself could actually get. He would have lost devastatingly to Kiyo in a personal competition. For his hypostasis is not self-improvement and the achievement of some new personal qualities and abilities. His hypostasis is finding weaknesses in the people around him and using these weaknesses for his own benefit (pleasure).
