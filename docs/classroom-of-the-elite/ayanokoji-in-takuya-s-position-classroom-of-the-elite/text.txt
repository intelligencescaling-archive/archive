# SITUATIONS:

# Class Exam: Passed

The best way to stay hidden is to hide in plain sight and be dull. Ayanokoji collaborates with Nanase and Tsubaki, becoming a side member of their class leadership group.

That way, the White Room would be less likely to suspect him. Even if they did, proving it would require the White Room student to expose themselves to public attention, which goes against the key doctrine of the White Room curriculum.

# Partner Exam: Passed

Ayanokoji passes this exam by partnering with Second-Year Class 2-D student Kushida.

While helping Nanase and Tsubaki solve their class's internal issues, he meets Nagumo and Kyo Ishigami. Ayanokoji feels that something is wrong about Ishigami, but for now, he doesn't get involved since it isn't necessary.

# Uninhabited Island Exam: Mid-High Diff

The pressure from the second and third years is overwhelming. At least the first years realize they have to form a temporary year-wide alliance; otherwise, they will be swallowed by the second and third years during this exam.

During this exam, Ayanokoji, along with Tsubaki, meets the leader of Class 2-B, Yagami. A very amicable person with a pleasant personality or so everyone thinks. In reality, almost all of the chaos throughout the exam is being orchestrated by him through other parties.

Ayanokoji realizes that the White Room student is active during this exam, causing the chaos to identify the anonymous White Room student among the first years. The chaos serves as a litmus test.

He is able to dodge suspicion of being the White Room student, but doing so still makes him stand out as unusual from the White Room student's perspective, while avoiding becoming the center of attention.

According to Ayanokoji, there should be four suspicious candidates in the eyes of the White Room student:

* Riku
* Ayanokoji
* Ishigami
* Yanagi

After this exam, Ayanokoji has several new priorities:

* Become more sociable and make more friends to avoid looking suspicious.
* Figure out the main orchestrator behind the island exam.
* Determine whether the orchestrator and the White Room student are the same person or two different people working together for different goals.
* Preserve the year-wide alliance since it is beneficial for maintaining his cover.
* Get rid of Hōsen and other potential enemies of the planned year-wide alliance while building trust with the second and third years for information and future partners.

# Unanimous Vote Special Exam: Low Diff

Unfortunately for me, Hōsen nearly gets me expelled through his connections. He now knows that someone from Class 2-B wants me gone as part of their plan.

It's now confirmed that the White Room student is increasing hostility toward the second years by manipulating the questions in this exam.

The White Room, according to the program, isn't responding or interfering. If this isn't dealt with quickly, the White Room student will do anything to destroy the first years just to reach the anonymous White Room student.

Right now, the orchestrator appears to be someone from Class 2-B. Whether the White Room student is also in Yagami's class is what's troubling me.

Could Yagami be the White Room student?

A class leader being a White Room student would go against the White Room's doctrine of never revealing yourself to the public eye.

Figuring out whether the leader of Class 2-B is truly the White Room student is something I have to do alone. However, doing so also carries the slight risk of exposing myself as a White Room student.

# Festival: Low-Mid Diff

Someone starts a fire in one of the school buildings. Fortunately, students near the location, along with the ANHS staff, manage to stop it from spreading.

Rumors quickly spread that a second-year student started the fire.

There are also rumors of a pickpocket operating among the first years.

Ayanokoji concludes that, at first glance, it looks like the White Room's work, but it isn't. It's the orchestrator's work to divert attention. The White Room student is simply covering for them because it helps identify anyone actively searching for the anonymous White Room student.

The plan is to confront Riku and Yagami to determine whether either of them is the White Room student. Unfortunately, the plan itself triggers their suspicions, exposing the investigation.

Ayanokoji manages to inform Nanase first. She then relays the information to her other allies.

Right now, Ayanokoji's main question is whether he should become Student Council President. It would certainly give him enough authority to counter the White Room's influence.

No.

It would be better to have the Student Council President act as his proxy against the White Room's influence over their year. In the worst-case scenario, becoming Vice Student Council President would be enough.

If only he had entered one year earlier, he could have controlled the first years from behind the curtain, just like the current White Room student is doing against him. Facing an adversary like this was exhausting, even for Ayanokoji.

The state of the second-year classes, as Ayanokoji observes it, is a constant struggle of proxies and influence.

**Class D** is basically a vassal of Class C's whims. It's full of agents acting in Class C's interests. Ryuen rules through fear and has no hesitation in getting his own hands dirty if necessary.

**Class B** appears to be led by Ichinose, but it actually has a dual leadership. In reality, Yagami controls Ichinose and uses her to influence the Student Council President and Nagumo's decisions. Yagami is the shadow leader. If Ichinose is the face of the class, then Yagami handles the PR and OPSEC. It's highly probable that the White Room student is from this class.

**Class A** has a monopoly over private points and academics. It also maintains a network of spies and double agents across the other classes. Compared to every other class, it almost functions as two classes operating as one.

Hashimoto acts as the main handler between the different factions. To everyone else, his connection with Ayanokoji appears nonexistent, and Ayanokoji firmly denies any association with him. Behind the scenes, however, the two are quietly working together.

Ayanokoji now realizes that only he and Ishigami remain as the primary suspects in the White Room student's eyes.

There is no choice but to form a mutual partnership with the leader of Class 1-A, Ishigami, so that both can achieve what they want—expelling Yagami.

For Ayanokoji, Yagami's expulsion would also be a litmus test. If Yagami is expelled and the White Room's activities stop, then Yagami was the White Room student. If the activities continue, then either Yagami was never the White Room student, or the real White Room student was using him as a cover. If someone prevents Yagami's expulsion, then the possibility that Yagami is the White Room student increases.

For Ishigami, it's necessary to prevent Yagami from abusing his Student Council position to extend his influence over the first years. As long as Yagami remains in power, he can manipulate school decisions, interfere with first-year affairs, and strengthen his control through proxies. Removing Yagami would weaken Class 2-B's influence and restore the balance of power between the years.

# Survival and Elimination Special Exam : low mid diff

In the end, their partnership isn't able to expel Yagami during this exam. However, they are now certain that Yagami is the White Room student. Their next objective is to expose the facade of Class 2-B's "angel" and have him expelled in the following year.

At the same time, Yagami is left confused. From his perspective, there are several possibilities: there could be two White Room students, either Ayanokoji or Ishigami could be the White Room student, or both could simply be acting independently. That uncertainty gives Ayanokoji and Ishigami the upper hand. Unlike Yagami, they already know about his involvement in the Uninhabited Island Exam, the Festival, and several other incidents, while Yagami is still forced to guess at their true identities and intentions.

After the exam, the main concern is electing a new Student Council President. Yagami is expected to do everything in his power to prevent anyone independent from being elected, instead ensuring the position goes to one of his puppets, the anonymous pickpocket who has been operating behind the scenes.

For Ayanokoji, the ideal outcome is for Ishigami to become the Student Council President, with Nanase serving as the Vice President. To avoid exposing any direct connection, Ayanokoji communicates with Ishigami indirectly through Nanase and Tsubaki
