Ayanakoji's False Personas

This is an analysis of one of CotE's many hidden plots. This is a very misunderstood one which is bad since imo its vital to properly understand and put into context every single action Koji takes. Once understood False Persona provides the basis and evidence for Koji's insane EQ, SQ, acting skills, deception and complete resistance to all forms of emotional manipulation. Often people read CotE and are left with the impression that Koji is a robotic EQ/SQ fodder or that he often falls into others traps but that's simply untrue you simply fell for his False Persona.

Persona 1: The Broken Boy

Whilst the dual manipulation of head researcher Suzukake and Atsuomi to escape from the white room deserves its own full analysis I'll briefly go over the part pertaining to his first recorded false persona.

*In order to escape from the white room, Ayanokoji prepared a long term strategy where he would use a "false persona" to deceive the owners of the white room and Atsuomi. Ayanokoji acted as if the whiteroom had broken him and had lost all emotions. In this way Ayanokoji manipulated Atsuomi to take him out of the white room to fix this "weakness".*

First the sheer amount of acting skills, emotional management and adversity capacity required to pull of such a large scale deception from the before the age of 10 is insane. In the whiteroom Koji was monitored on tape and by individuals 24/7 meaning that any micro-expression or micro-movement that would conflict the narrative he set up would've been noticed despite this Koji was able to keep up the façade and deliver perfect micro expressions and actions to fool top behavioural and psychological researchers into believing his false narrative.

Using this false persona Koji was also able to evade many traps Atsuomi created to test him to keep selling Atsuomi the lie:

Atsuomi sets Koji up to fight several armed men in order to test his capacity for fear in this fight not only does Koji defeat them he is also able to maintain the emotionless façade by keeping his facial expression blank and displaying total control over his heart rate by keeping it completely steady throughout the whole fight.

Atsuomi brings Koji to a distressed Yuki someone he should have a strong connection with due to them spending years together in the whiteroom. Koji is able to once again keep his cold composure and deliver cruel words that would indicate poor social intelligence to Yuki (whilst secretly helping her trauma) despite having a strong connection to her. Throughout all this Atsuomi was monitoring him in person and still could not see through the façade showing how Koji is able perfectly uphold the masquerade even in high pressure situations

Atsuomi rides with Koji in his car and asks him how it is to be outsider for the first time. Koji answered with nothing and continued playing the role, fooling Atsuomi into thinking he was looking outside with no emotions and that he had lost the ability to distinguish between the virtual world and reality. Koji thinks to himself, this was Atsuomi's big mistake, as he was only letting his father and the instructors think that they have everything under control whilst he was always sharpening his fangs.

Persona 2: The Synthetic High Schooler

This is the more known false persona and the one the readers are first introduced to but after analysing it the repercussions on Ayanakoji's abilities are insane. In order to integrate into the highly social normal environment of ANHS Koji takes on the persona of a normal high school boy. In this persona Koji displays normal human emotions such as laughter, anger and adequate hornyness for a teenage boy its also fully capable of banter and casual talk. This persona also has separate desires and goals from True Koji such as "wanting to live a normal life". These aspects of the persona were not just exterior either Koji managed to rewrite the very thoughts in his mind to match the persona. This persona even had synthetic memories of a past with no white room. All these aspects of the persona leads me to believe that Persona 2 is a fully sentient personality that True Koji steers towards his goals. This provides context for why Koji's schemes have to be put together by readers despite us having access to his thoughts, how Koji lies about things the readers are clearly aware of in his mind and why Koji has complete resistance to emotional manipulation. True Koji is in the shadows of his own being puppeteering the synthetic high schooler towards his own goals. In order to manipulate Koji u would need to bypass Kojis False Persona (which no one has been able to do so far) and also overcome True Koji's insane emotional awareness, management and overall control of his emotions.

Moving on from the mechanisms of this persona Koji was able to use it to integrate into ANHS without alarming anyone he didn't want to of being more than a normal awkward quiet high schooler. Imo this is an insane feat of social intelligence to be able to craft a persona to fool people into believing this narrative despite never properly being in any social environment or society before this. He went from labrat to normal quiet kid meaning he was able hit every micro expression, overcome any linguistic disparity and perfectly match a high schoolers energy. People take for granted how easily neurotypicals can spot others and what Koji was able to do not just for normal people but social butterflies like Ichinose and Kushida is very impressive to me.

Persona 3: The Broken High Schooler

This is an extension and combination of the previous personas that Koji uses to keep manipulating Atsuomi and achieve his goals. This persona is the emotionless genius that we read the thoughts of after Year 1 Volume 3 however it was not created after V3 it was simply under the synthetic highschooler before. It has memories of his whiteroom past however Ayanakoji still supresses some  memories such as the ones about Yuki before Y3. Its thoughts are emotionless and are more robotic than persona 2 yet they still often contradict True Koji's motives. This leads me to believe persona 3 is once again fully sentient yet subtly puppeteered by True Koji to accomplish his goals.

Under this persona Koji was able to undergo a variety of high pressure situations whilst never dropping he mask e.g.:

In Y1V7 Atsuomi comes to visit Koji at ANHS to continue his "manipulation" of Koji and check up on his progress with developing emotions. To do this Atsuomi acts as if he's upset that Koji "escaped" despite Atsuomi setting it up for him to do so and tells Koji of his butlers death another person Koji should have a fairly strong connection to due to the butler being the only person to show Koji genuine kindness before ANHS. Koji once again sees through this and uses the persona to deliver a cold harsh response in order to keep manipulating Atsuomi into thinking he needs to stay at ANHS to keep developing his emotions.

In Y2V5 Yagami psychoanalyses Horikita and uses Kushida to create a situation that would tank Koji's class' class points or lead to the expulsion of Airi someone close to Koji. Koji is able to expel her from the class using persuasive language yet harsh words and cold composure. This allowed Koji to keep up the act of being emotionless to Ishigami who was spying on him on Atsuomi's behalf. Koji then finishes this defence by expelling Takuya in a way that would show him little care or any grudge thus showing to Atsuomi that he was still emotionless so that Atsuomi would further let him stay at ANHS
