Ichika Amasawa feat list

Ichika Amasawa’s intelligence feats:

**"I won't be convinced with just words alone\~ I'll have to make sure of it with my own eyes” – Ichika Amasawa to Suzune Horikita**

Manipulation: Coerced Kikyo Kushida into revealing her true personality and giving up on her plan to get Kiyotaka Ayanokoji expelled. Temporarily [Seduced](https://imgur.com/a/raq5YeG#Qd10uFh) Ken Sudo. Manipulated Hosen into accepting her demands for assisting him in the Uninhabited Island Exam including her taking the entire bounty for Kiyotaka Ayanokoji since he would get the reputation of being the one who expelled him.

Reasoning: Correctly deduced what Kikyo Kushida had planned for her group if they fell to the bottom of the leaderboard during the Uninhabited Island Exam. Correctly deduced that Suzune wanted a low-academic student like Ken to pair with her in order to save him from possible expulsion. Reasons she couldn’t be a intermediary due to how she was not really close friends with any of her classmates whatsoever, saying it has nothing to do with herself trusting either Ayanokoji or Suzune but not being of help to them. Correctly deduced Kei Karuizawa was Ayanokoji’s girlfriend. Deduces she would become suspicious in the Uninhabited Island Exam for breaking her wristwatch intentionally but far from being concluded as a culprit even if her GPS signal happened to be the only one missing back then as there was not enough evidence.

Strategy: It was revealed that Ichika had supported Kazuomi Hosen’s plan to expel Kiyotaka Ayanokoji. When Ayanokoji investigated her actions, he learned from a store clerk that she stopped Hosen from buying a knife — the same expensive fruit knife Ayanokoji had once purchased for her. She had given that knife to Hōsen, helping refine his plan by choosing it for its sheath, which made it easy to conceal. Ayanokoji realised she must have visited the store with Hosen earlier, as she found the knife instantly despite claiming it was her first time there. Later, she visited his room under the pretense of retrieving a lost hair tie, to recover the knife she had planted earlier. Ayanokoji then deduced that she waited for the right moment to take it back, ensuring no fingerprints remained before handing it to Hosen for his scheme. During the Uninhabited Island Exam she destroys her wristwatch intentionally to erase her GPS signal from the radar and prevent her name from getting identified.

Foresight: She was able to see through Kikyo Kushida's plan of recording the battle between Kiyotaka Ayanokoji and Tsubasa Nanase as evidence to get Ayanokoji expelled. During the Volleyball Match of the Year 2 Sports Festival she was able to completely out-predict Suzune and made it clear she [was ](https://imgur.com/a/upXfAQO)memorising the attack patterns of her opponents so she could predict their next moves to the point where her intuition helped her [to](https://imgur.com/a/dLTdvjc) predict the trajectory of the ball even when Mio Ibuki completely changed her attack patterns she managed to hit her every time.

Deception: She purposefully chose not to stand out by deliberately getting average scores in ANHS just like Kiyotaka Ayanokoji. She pretended cooking was something she often did with her mother. She visited Kiyotaka Ayanokoji’s room under the lie of retrieving a lost hair tie.

Other: Kiyotaka Ayanokoji regarded Ichika's pure combat prowess as being levels above that of Ken Sudo and Kakeru Ryueen combined this was shown when Ichika single-handedly defeated both Suzune Horikita and Mio Ibuki simultaneously without difficulty despite being heavily injured. [Practiced](https://imgur.com/a/SVLSBAx) with a bow a day before an exam and had so much accuracy she scored 1 point below Ayanokoji. She noticed the Year 2 Class A students organized by Arisu stalking her every movement despite their best attempts to conceal their presence, labeling their attempts as cute showing high situational awareness. Kiyotaka Ayanokoji admits Ichika has extraordinary acting skills and the uncanny ability to put plans into action without difficulty. During her fight with Suzune and Mio she controlled the flow of the battle by forcing them to distance themselves, allowing herself to get used to the pain and delayed them by engaging in frivolous conversation showing battle intelligence and psychological warfare. She was able to stalk Kei Karuizawa for two weeks without her being aware. She was able to corner Kiyotaka Ayanokoji into allowing her into his room after shooting down all his excuses and having flawless countermeasures to his lies. Outmanoeuvred Tsubasa Nanase in the Uninhabited Island Exam using the terrain showing situational awareness. She ignores Tsubasa Nanase's words refusing to falter or trip over by falling for her strategy that was meant to cloud her ability to move carefully.
