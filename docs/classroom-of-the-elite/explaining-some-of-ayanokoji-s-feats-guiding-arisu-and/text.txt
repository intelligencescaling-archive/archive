Explaining some of kiyo's feats to this sub..

It’s really interesting to look back and see how Ayanokoji’s influence stretched across so many volumes in such a deliberate way. In Year 1 Volume 7.5, right after Volume 6, Arisu was already becoming interested in Ichinose. But that wasn’t just something that happened on its own Ayanokoji actively encouraged it.

You can see it starting in Volume 7, and by Volume 7.5, he basically tells Arisu, *“If you can’t defeat Honami, then you have no hope of matching me.”* He was implying that she needed to prove herself by surpassing Ichinose, or else she wasn’t on his level. Because of her pride, Arisu couldn’t ignore that challenge. She felt she had to go after Honami to validate herself.

What makes this so significant is that in his Volume 7.5 monologue, Ayanokoji actually admits he manipulated her thinking on purpose. He clearly says he planted that idea to motivate her. This wasn’t an isolated move it started a whole chain of events that continued far beyond that volume.

For example, later in Year 1 Volume 9, the rumor about Ichinose being a thief spread around the school. That rumor wasn’t something new. It was recycled from what Ayanokoji had already hinted at in Volume 6, when he made people suspicious about Ichinose’s large amount of points. Arisu noticed Honami was hiding something and used the same method: she approached Nagumo and spread the rumor to undermine Ichinose’s confidence.

But Ayanokoji wasn’t doing this just to damage her. He actually intended for Ichinose to break down so that he could help her rebuild herself later. He even confirms this in Volume 11.5, where he says that the following year would be the right time to bring her back, once she had learned from the experience. That moment sets up their later interaction in Year 2 Volume 12.5, where you can see the results of everything he set in motion.

What’s also important is that he didn’t stop with Ichinose and Arisu as separate pieces. He created a situation where they would inevitably clash. That conflict gave him even more room to maneuver behind the scenes. This also contributed to the tension between Ryuuen and Arisu in Year 2, which later became connected to the betting game and other class rivalries.

All of this fits into his strategy as “X,” the hidden figure guiding events. The continuity is what really stands out he started it all in Volume 6, kept pushing it in Volume 7, set up the challenge in 7.5, let the rumor explode in Volume 9, and continued through 11.5 and into Year 2. There’s a strong case that he was planning for Year 3 as well.

Even the situation where Kei was taken to the rooftop by Ryuuen and threatened *“I’ll tell the whole school you were bullied”* shows how carefully Ayanokoji shaped perceptions. At that point, nobody trusted Ryuuen’s threats because of how Kiyotaka had already influenced the way people viewed him.

When you look at this step by step, you can see nothing about it was random. Ayanokoji was intentionally guiding both Arisu and Ichinose, and setting up situations that he could later resolve or exploit. From Arisu’s need to prove herself, to Honami’s temporary collapse, to the big confrontations later, everything was part of a plan that started with just a few suggestions back in Volume 6.

This is an insane level of multi tasking and long term planning from kiyotaka
