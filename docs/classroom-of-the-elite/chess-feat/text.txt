Light Novel on the Chess Feat:

if one wishes to scale CF solely based on LN, the following are available:
* Arisu's chess skill exceeds that of all Whiteroom instructors Ayanokoji faced.
   *  While some interpret this to mean she has an ELO rating of at least 2500, it's crucial to consider Japan's chess context. Unlike the US, where top players reach 2800 and 2500 places in the top fifty, Japan's highest-rated player is only 2390, with 1875 securing a top 50 position. This disparity is partly due to classical chess's lower popularity in Japan.
   * Given this context, Arisu's ELO can realistically be estimated at least 2300.

* Ayanokoji's move surpassed those of the professionals hired by Tsukishiro, despite their use of a chess engine.
   * This doesn't imply Ayanokoji played at or near the engine's level. In chess, there are numerous instances where humans, who cannot compete with engines like Stockfish, have made superior moves.
   * Such occurrences, however, require exceptional strategic thinking typically seen only in high-level Grandmasters.
   * Given this context,, Ayanokoji's estimated ELO rating would be at least 2700.

* Arisu and Ayanokoji possess similar chess skills. to the point that Arisu suggested that in ten matches, they would go 5-5. Ayanokoji not only agreed, he monologues that things will go exactly as Arisu predicted.

TLDR
* Arisu is at least 2300 by comparison to chess professionals (statement)
* Ayanokoji is likely at least 2700 (feat implications)
* Arisu is equal to Ayanokoji in chess (statement).

Conclusions from the chess analysis (anime & LN):

- Arisu vs. Koji: Arisu and Koji are roughly at a similar level in chess and Arisu could have won if she hadn't tried to bait him into a mistake, which she did because her priority wasn’t winning but drawing his full skill;
- Koji's Potential Mistake: Koji stated that if he makes a mistake, he will be finished off. This is correct, as every move Koji could have made when he said that, except for two, almost guarantees defeat.
- Tsukishiro's Chess Engine: Tsukishiro likely used a top-tier chess engine, as it recognized that a qd8 was excellent, it's a move as good as the ideal at extremely high depth. This indicates that it was a high-depth engine at least.
- Koji's Move: Koji made one of two optimal moves, the one he made was actually the easier one to spot. It was definitely a tricky position.
- Koji vs. Chess Engine: The claim that Koji made a better move than a chess engine is inaccurate, since the chess engine evaluates both moves similarly (and both moves are roughly the same, one only delays an action the other takes).
- Engine (LC0 High Depth) >>> Koji: since he evaluated a position even when a chess engine thought it had a 99.7% chance to win with it.
- Koji/Arisu ELO :Determining if their level is equivalent to what ELO is beyond me, however based LN implications of making better move than an engine and the anime’s portrayal (for the last position at least), at least 2700 is the most reasonable estimate
