Observation Feat:
Adding together the context seen earlier and this simple scan, we can understand the power of Ichika's feat.

Ayanokoji first says that he's understood the mystery of Ichika's coming, so all he's going to say next is what happened.

Ayanokoji states:
“You examined **every** nook and cranny in my room because you were checking to make **sure** no one else was listening in”

Ayanokoji states 2 extremely important things here and gives all the keys to understanding feat without assumption. Firstly, Ayanokoji says that Ichika has seen **EVERY** nook and cranny of his room, which is to say absolutely every corner and place, given that every means all the individual members of a set without exception, also used for emphasis i.e. the utmost, so the greatest or most extreme extent or amount. So Ichika by **statement** analyzed and observed the whole room. Secondly, and even more importantly, Ayanokoji says that Ichika did this to be **SURE** that no one was eavesdropping on their conversation, here sure refers to being completely confident that one is right, it's also used for emphasis. The fact that Ayanokoji shows that by statement Ichika is **sure** that no one is spying on them is already an indicator of the certainty Ichika has in the fact that no one is spying on them, as she is sure there is no way a spy device is in the room because she hass looked at **every** possible place of the room. What's interesting is that with the context part, we could already easily reach the conclusion that Ichika was able to see that there were no spy devices in the room, yet Kinugasa goes further, and gives statements literally to the word that prove by A+B that Ichika was indeed able to be **sure** that there were absolutely no secret spy devices in the room.

Let's summarize the feat and formalize it.

A - Ichika is afraid that Takuya has already set a trap on Ayanokoji. (stated)

B - Ichika is afraid that Takuya has access to her convsersation and what she's going to reveal. (explained before)

C - Ichika and Takuya have potential access to secret spy surveillance devices. (explained before)

D - Ichika searched the room for a long time, examined **every** nook and cranny in the room. (stated)

E - Ichika tells Ayanokoji the secrets she has to tell, despite her initial fear of being watched. (on screen)

F - Ichika did E in contrast to A because thanks to D she was **sure** no one else was listening in. (stated)

G - If we use C and F, we know Ichika is sure the room is not spied on, while Ayanokoji thinks Tsukishiro might have given them something to spy on, and Ichika thinks Takuya can have, so Ichika thinks Takuya used this and that's why she's looking for spy devices, if Takuya ever used any. (explained)

H - Ichika did E and F while she thinks Takuya has been using spy surveillance devices (G), which means that according to Ayanokoji's statement(F) and Ichika's opinion of her abilities(E), her Observation is strong enough to detect any spy device, because she's **sure** that there's nothing listening in on them after a long search.
