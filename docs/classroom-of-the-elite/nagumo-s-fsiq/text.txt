Arisu vs Nagumo in FSIQ Explanations

Why Nagumo takes FRI from Arisu

Nagumo takes FRI due to the many statements of him being a prodigy since childhood confirmed in Y2V9 monologue which includes many applications such as football, academics, physical abilities etc

This shows a superior level of fast learning.

In terms of adaptability I don’t think I need to say much as Nagumo already has many known feats of his adaptability that surpass Arisu such as his Ichinose + Manabu feats etc.

In terms of logical reasoning, Arisu takes the edge due to taking abduction and deduction from Nagumo with him only taking induction.

An example of a great abduction of Arisu was in V9 where she deduced Kamuro had experience in shoplifting based on observations from the fact she didn’t buy anything implying Kamuro must have memorised the camera’s positions + Kamuro’s composed look implying lack of guilt therefore showing the fact she’s done this enough times to steal more expensive items such as beer.

A great example of Nagumo’s Induction is during the meeting with Ayanokoji and Suzune in Y2V2, Nagumo was able to confirm 2 things, first one is that Ayanokoji was indeed the one who countered his Y1V9 strategy, the 2nd was Kiriyama's involvement with Ayanokoji.

Along with Nagumo's deduction in Y1V9 regarding Ayanokoji's interference, Kiriyama's cooperation with him was still only a likely possibility but not a guarantee until now. Making use of one mistake Kiriyama made which alerted Nagumo, he was able to come to the conclusion that Kiriyama is definitely to be involved with the events of Y1V9 as the one who spread the rumors on the bulletin board.
Kiriyama's behavior in the Y2V2 SC scene had all the reasons to seem suspicious to Nagumo, mainly because of Kiriyama's lack of caution which made him stand out specifically when it came to Suzune Horikita's request. Kiriyama never personally attended any council requests where he was not needed in the past, but showed interest in a situation which involved Ayanokoji.

Why Nagumo takes VCI from Arisu

Information/Vocabulary - Nagumo has overall more general knowledge being not only a 3rd year but a genius from the start. So much so as he’s said to be above the rest of his peers in academic ability.

Even if Arisu has better Long Term Memory which contributes to information, Nagumo overall has greater experience as he’s even said to be on par with Manabu Horikita in terms of academic performance.

Comprehension - An example of Nagumo’s comprehension is in Y2V7, When Ayanokoji dropped a subtle hint about him being aware of Nagumo's future actions he planned to implement soon, Nagumo instantly deduced what Ayanokoji meant by his "participation" and realised that Ayanokoji had entirely anticipated that he was planning to make an attack against Ayanokoji.

Why Arisu takes VSI from Nagumo

Although it is only on a tablet, Sakayanagi can see the uninhabited island in three dimensions. She has a realistic simulation in her mind
of who they are and what they are like. And beyond that, it goes and captures the shadow of the person who is painting this whole picture.

Keep in mind the entire island is divided into a total of 100 individual spaces with each space measuring 500 metres from north to south, and 700 metres from east to west. In other words she has a realistic simulation of an entire island of 500 x 100 and 700 x 100 metres.

If this wasn’t broken enough, she also has a realistic simulation in her mind of the exact people in their given locations including the shadows of the many many people.

Not only does this clear Nagumo in entire aspect of VSI but it also beats now every other COTE character in VSI (EXCEPT AYANOKOJI: Ayanokoji >= Arisu)

COTE's ridiculous setting advantage when it comes to Working Memory and Processing Speed, as even someone like Matsushita was able to achieve an impressive feat here.
