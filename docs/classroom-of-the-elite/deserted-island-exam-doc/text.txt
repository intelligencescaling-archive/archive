Additional Rules Explained by Chabashira

There are several designated “spots” on the island. These spots have rights of exclusive possession, and only the class that occupies that spot may exercise those rights.

The class that obtains those rights is entirely free to determine how they wish to exercise them. However, the rights of exclusive possession are only valid for a period of eight hours after being invoked, after which they are automatically revoked.

Another class can acquire those rights at that time. Upon occupying a spot, the class can gain one bonus point. These bonus points are not counted as Special Points and can’t be used in the exam.
