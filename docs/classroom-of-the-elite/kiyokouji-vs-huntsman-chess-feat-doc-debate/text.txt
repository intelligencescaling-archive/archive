Straw Man Fallacy - The Straw Man fallacy occurs when someone distorts or misrepresents their opponent's argument to create a weaker version, making it easier to attack. Instead of addressing the actual points made by the opponent, this fallacy relies on attacking a misrepresented or exaggerated version of their argument. It's a form of argumentative misdirection aimed at making the opponent's position seem easier to refute than it actually is.
