Who she is: [https://you-zitsu.fandom.com/wiki/Arisu\_Sakayanagi](https://you-zitsu.fandom.com/wiki/Arisu_Sakayanagi)

\[S3EP5 / Volume 9\] She orchestrates a public attack on Honami Ichinose that could have led to Ichinose’s expulsion by spreading rumours through her A-Class classmates and then recruiting C-Class student Haruki Yamauchi, seducing him into following her to an undisclosed location, and convincing him to also spread her rumours about Ichinose throughout the school to ruin her reputation. The plan was for some students to question whether there was any truth to the rumours, stirring controversy over whether they were half-true, fully true, fully false, or half-false. She then directly confronts Class B and manipulates Ichinose into confessing that the rumours were true and that she had been a shoplifter—under the glimpse that if she didn’t, irrefutable evidence would be presented anyway. She also attempts to involve Ayanokoji in her scheme by sending Masaki Kamuro to convince him that she wants to help him because Sakayanagi had used her in the past, ostensibly to prove that she isn’t a pawn. In reality, she is still a pawn of Sakayanagi, who is using her ruse to uncover his counter-plan to save Ichinose from expulsion and stop it. To support this deception, Kamuro gives Ayanokoji a stolen can of beer after emerging from a store, giving him leverage over her. The can actually comes from Kamuro’s past shoplifting incident and had been handed to her beforehand by Sakayanagi to trick Ayanokoji into believing she stole it in that moment. In the end, it is revealed that the entire plan was devised solely to attract Kiyotaka Ayanokoji’s interest so she could make a bet with him.

\[S3EP6 / Volume 10\] She talks to Kiyotaka Ayanokoji, reasoning to him that a mastermind has deliberately set up a special exam to expel him by suspending her father from his board position. This, she explains, is why Advanced Nurturing High School suddenly decided to hold an unreasonable special exam that didn’t match the other exams. She asks to postpone their special exam battle until after this special voting exam, promising not to vote to expel Kiyotaka Ayanokoji and instead vote to expel Kohei Katsuragi. However, this mastermind was actually herself, and she was lying—she only did this to convince Kiyotaka Ayanokoji that she wasn’t the mastermind. She then used C-Class student Haruki Yamauchi, promising him Class A Protection, to make him directly manipulate Kikyo Kushida into going around to class students who hate Kiyotaka Ayanokoji, and encouraging them to vote to have him expelled, causing the number of expulsion votes for Ayanokoji to skyrocket until he had to intervene. But in a plot twist, she explains to Ayanokoji that she used all her A-Class positive votes to save him from the expulsion and let Yamauchi get expelled instead because of his rude behavior towards her once during the training camp.

\[S3EP9 / Volume 10\] She deduced that Acting Chairman Tsukishiro was watching her and Kiyotaka Ayanokoji interact on the nearby surveillance camera, and that he intentionally stopped their conversation to introduce himself and ask for directions as a ploy by noticing the inconsistency in Tsukishiro’s words, noting how an Acting Chairman should have familiarised himself with the campus beforehand, so he wouldn’t need to ask for directions. She also noted the oddness of his actions—assaulting her and Ayanokoji as an Acting Chairman on a live surveillance camera—so it’s possible that from this she had already deduced the surveillance camera footage was swapped after he saw their interaction and went to interfere.

\[S3EP9 / Volume 11\] She chose chess as one of her A-Class subjects, knowing that the rules of the paper shuffle exam would allow her to indirectly duel the Captain of the Class she is up against, aka Kiyotaka Ayanokoji. She deliberately hid the information of what she picked for the five other subjects to her class because she knew the information could be tunnelled to Ayanokoji or someone from his class through Kohei Katsuragi. She also acknowledged she, Ayanokoji, and Ichinose made a false assumption that the captain’s would only be protection point holders. She first tries to defeat Ayanokoji in basketball by hiding the fact that one of her chosen participants, Hayato Kito, has played basketball before. Compared to Ayanokoji’s team, she also deliberately makes Kito hold back until Ayanokoji’s class has a big enough score, using his experience to win and psychologically trigger Sudo by realizing Kito has played before, so he is off his game. However, Ayanokoji foils this and wins. After Ayanokoji also wins the typing game, she realizes she needs to win the number-memorizing game. She also reasons Ayanokoji’s strategy of using Chiaki and Koenji, because the rules state the winner isn’t decided by combined scores but instead the student with the highest score takes the win. So even if Koenji doesn’t take it seriously, Chiaki is there to cover him. She stopped Kohei Katsuragi’s potential betrayal and made him pull out his best to win by confronting him priorly and saying an A-Class Student will be expelled if he does betray her class. She then defeats Ayanokoji in the final subject, chess, using Qxf3, a queen sacrifice that set up a quick two-move checkmate. After Ayanokoji captured the queen with gxf3, Sakayanagi played Rg6+ to check the king, forcing it to move to Kh1, which was followed by Bxf3# to deliver checkmate. But she only won because the last move of Kiyotaka Ayanokoji, Kh1, was manipulated by Tsukishiro. She also deliberately didn’t pick a one-on-one chess match to hide Kiyotaka Ayanokoji’s White Room abilities and to be in the same space as him for as long as possible.

(2nd Year Volume 1) She quickly grasped Ryuen’s strategy of using private points to secure first-year students. Instead of engaging in a financial war, she adapted by creating a counterplan: she would match Ryuen’s offers on paper without actually paying the first-year students. This forced Ryuen to keep raising his private point offers while A-Class spent nothing. She achieved this by appearing to give first-year partners private points through later-scheduled private point-giving finalisation, relying on A-Class’s reputation to make them trust the finalisation would be done later and to attract more first-year students naturally.
