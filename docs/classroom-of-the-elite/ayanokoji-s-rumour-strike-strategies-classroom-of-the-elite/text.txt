Ayanakoji's Rumour Strike Strategies

This is an analysis of one of CotE's many hidden plots. This is one of my favourites and provides plenty of decent feats for koji's insane foresight, manipulation, EQ and SQ in how he perfectly engineers social situations and plays on peoples emotions to achieve his goals with flawless execution. This is still not one of his absolute best feats but a very interesting one. People like to act as if koji is just responding to things but no everything that happens is according to his plan.

Kiyo released the rumor that Ichinose is illegally gathering points to the whole of his year and marked it as from ryuenn. This one tactic is a testament to his efficiency as it serves several objectives which will be a running theme in these strategies.

\>He wanted to see what the school will do in case of rumours. Arisu told him that she knows about the White room so what if she spreads rumours about it?

\>He also wanted to distract Arisu's attention from him to Ichinose so she does not bother him or interfere with his X plan whilst he's working on Ryuenn. He accurately predicted that by exposing Arisu to the novelties of using rumours as offense and Ichinose as a target she would feel compelled to play with them

\>He also wanted to see what kind of person Ichinose is. Is she truly good? By releasing this rumour he would also poke at her character so that her weaknesses could be revealed for ayanakoji to help fix and improve her or destroy her for his class transfer strategy

\>By writing Ryueen's name on the paper Kiyo wanted to entice Ryueen to continue to his search for X by toying with him whilst also breaking ryuenns partnership with kanzaki as to weaken Ryuenns position in the overall X arc.

\>Kiyo also wanted to make Kushida distracted so the answers could be more easily planted in her uniform for his paper shuffle strategy.

With one tactic koji progresses 5 different objectives. To do this he used a high level of emotional intelligence and foresight to figure out a creative move that would influence all of these characters to further his own goals.

In the background of kiyo blocking Ichinose from Nagumo he is also blocking kushida from Nagumo. In order to stop the Nagumo and Kushida alliance, Kiyo approached Kushida and offered a very tantalising deal- one-time information in exchange for perpetual payment of private points. Kushida considers this too good to be true so is suspicious at first, but Kiyo manipulates her into taking the deal by allowing her to record the meeting, faking weakness (asking maybe if she could not target him anymore), feigning a strong interest in Ichinose and flattering her and appealing to her ego by going on about how he finds her such a troublesome enemy. The combination of all these factors influences kushida into giving koji the information that he needed to spread his own rumours.
