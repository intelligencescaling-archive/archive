Checkmate Ayanaokoji Analyzed- Chapter 1, Part 1

I decided to add another project to the list of stuff I do and never finish.

Something that kinda bugs me about this subreddit is that people often have a hard time explaining things in ways less knowledgable people would understand. I don't mean to roast anybody, but it was hard enough even getting someone to explain that: "Checkmate Ayanaokoji" is actually a real fan-fiction character with an actual-story, and not just a complete fabrication in someone's head with no actual feats to analyze.

So, since it's hard enough getting anybody to actually send me a link to the story, I figured it's not even worth Trying to get anyone to explain what: "Checkmate Ayanaokoji" has actually done. So, I decided to do it myself, bit by bit.

What I know about Checkmate Ayanaokoji going into the Story-

This story is an alternate-universe telling of the original Classroom of the Elite story. Most of the major events that happened in the original story still happen here, but a lot of them will happen in a new context, or be altered entirely, Because this story is identical to the original in every way except for one specific alteration- Ayanaokoji is feeling a lot less empathetic this time-around.

I also know that at this moment, only 34 chapters have been released, and it's been over a year and a half since the latest chapter was released, so it's honestly a good possibility that it will never continue. This is disappointing, but we work with what we have.

CHAPTER 1 PART 1-

This part is the original story's first bus scene. Kouenji refuses to give-up his seat despite an office lady and Kushida politely asking him if he can do so for an elderly lady struggling to stand.

At first glance, nothing seems that different at all. Between this scene and the original. The wording is a bit different, and if I'm not mistaken, this scene is a lot shorter, which I guess makes sense, because you wouldn't want to literally copy and paste the original scene just for the sake of having it here. This seems like a very trimmed down version of the original scene. For example, Kouenji briefly mentions that he is clearly younger than both the office lady and the elderly woman, but he doesn't go into a huge monologue about whether youth or the elderly are more valuable to society.

There is however one MAJOR change in this scene, but it's not anything that actually disrupts the original flow of events.

Ayanaokoji implies in his inner monologue that he can sense Dark Kushida already from this moment alone. He doesn't make it clear what it is, but he simply claims that when Kushida asks the entire bus if anyone can give up their seat for the elderly woman, there is apparently a red flag hidden between these words. It's not revealed what this is. Maybe Kushida had a brief change in facial-expression, or maybe her voice changed to something more authentic part way through. It's not clear.

What is clear though, although only to Ayanaokoji, is that the Goat Kouenji also noticed this Dark Kushida within her, Apparently giving a smirkbas Kushida spoke, however he didn't care for it, and simply continued polishing his nails.

Ayanaokoji Also says in his inner monologue that he sincerely hopes Kouenji isn't in his class, and he hopes people are more approachable than Horikita after they get-off the bus as-well. This isn't anything significant, but I don't believe the original story had him ponder the idea at all of being in the same class as anyone particular on the bus.
