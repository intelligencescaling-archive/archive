Premise:

The four players—Horikita, Ryuen, Ichinose, and Arisu—are friends with Flux of Lumariar in this event. When people find out they're friends with Flux, they can't help but think, "They're so different. How are they all friends? How is Flux friends with them? And how are they friends with someone who thinks so differently?"

Even though Flux and the four are close friends, their friendship, like any other friend group, is built on both mutual respect and mutual dislike. Some days, it feels like Flux is the glue holding the group together. Other days, the group is the glue holding Flux together. Without them, he would have isolated himself long ago.

The five take part in the event. Everyone believes a friendship like theirs can overcome whatever the event throws at them.

Can it?

# Conspiracy Situation

**meeting: passed :(Win condition: To expose the conspiracy early. Fails.)**

First days: The event begins. The five were selected into Island 1, a rich island nicknamed "Paradisail" by the players for its nature. The players quickly formed alliances, parties, guilds, and even nations on the first day.

Flux partied in the nation of Lumaria, which believed a bridge should be built to connect two islands in this event.

The four, however, did not party with any guild or nation. They decided to be hermits; they would help or assist some people, but largely maintained net neutrality.

For Ryuen, getting involved in other people's matters wasn't his thing, especially since he wasn't the ruler. Arisu believed Island 1's abundant resources wouldn't cause any conflicts, so there was no need to join a party. Ichinose was more interested in helping everyone while also having moments for herself, away from the civilization the players built. For Horikita, it was peaceful, because she wasn't the one in charge of anything, nor did she want to be.

However, Flux was ambitious. He told them about Lumaria, knowing they wouldn't change their mind on it regardless, about the bridge, a player from Lumaria, his origins, and his newfound group, and so on.

Days later, rumors from the hellscape of Island 2, claiming they have netherite and hate Island 1's prosperity, made the whole situation change colors. Flux urged them to cancel the bridge project and the idea of sending aid. No one listened, except for his followers.

So they decided to hold a meeting between nations. Flux selected Ichinose as the mediator, knowing she gets along with everyone and would be unbiased here.

**Dripstone assassination: Escape(Win condition: To expose the conspiracy early. Fails)**

After the three meetings, Flux seemed to give up on the idea of convincing others to cancel the bridge and aid, as well as the militarization of Island 1 and Lumaria. The first leader wouldn't concern themselves with his issue and even got demoted due to their attitude toward other nations in meetings.

Wanting a neutral space that belonged to no single nation, the five along with Flux's loyalists decided to build the meeting hall themselves as neutral ground for future talks. For Flux, it was a project to clear his mind for a while, even if he couldn't attend the meetings there the next day.

The four knew that Flux didn't digest his demotion well. For Arisu, it seemed Flux wasn't sharing anything with them anymore like they used to, plus them being hermits was also contributing to the growing distance between them.

At the newly built meeting hall, the four, along with Flux, his loyalists, and other nations, gathered for the meeting. Then something happened. Seraphim was killed. It caused an uproar in the meeting hall, but it didn't prepare everyone for what was coming next.

Arisu urged Ryuen, Horikita, and Ichinose that they should leave now. At first, they were shocked as to why.

Then, dripstone fell and three-quarters of all nation leaders died. The four realized it was better to run right now, especially for Arisu, whom the other three helped to escape.

**Ridge trial: Escape (No trial)(Win condition: To expose the conspiracy early. Fails)**

Arisu explains her chain of thought on what happened. In short, one of Flux's loyalists, 3belowzero, or even Flux himself must have set up the dripstone to kill them.

For the four, the possibility of Flux betraying them was unimaginable, but he was ambitious. Ryuen figures that his delusion of ambition could have driven him to make that decision, and now he is trying to frame them, meaning there is no point in attending the trial because it is likely to be completely one-sided.

Ichinose objects, but Horikita explains that escaping is the best option they have right now, especially since one of the three primary suspects has likely rigged the trial. Even though escaping might make them look more suspicious in the public eye, it is a miles-better choice than risking losing one of their own.

**escape to hellscape island: escaped**

The News: 3belowzero and one of Flux's loyalists were killed.

The four escaped to the poor island. Going from the paradise of Island 1 to the Hellscape of Island 2 was difficult to adjust to, but if they wanted to prove their innocence, they first had to survive.

According to Arisu and Horikita, their dear friend Fluxion was the true conspirator and likely used them as scapegoats. The killing of 3belowzero and Flux's loyalist would distract public attention from Flux's absence and suspicion in the conspiracy. Flux would become the king of Lumaria after this incident, and placed a bounty on their heads to get rid of any possible evidence.

there were times where headhunters almost got to them, but few nations were open to take the four in. Nobody trusted Ryuen, Horikita, and Arisu, but Ichinose's politeness and general rhetoric were enough to convince some that the four might be falsely accused and involved in the conspiracy.

Though, few nations only took them in due to ulterior motives against the Lumaria nations, which had been sending headhunters and disturbing the peace that Island 2 fought sweat and tears over.

One of them was Schood (or a similar name), the ruler of the largest empire on Island 2. He took the four into his empire. To say Ryuen and Schood got along was an understatement-they agreed about everything, especially how things should be resolved ;fights.

This time, Arisu and Horikita are much more cautious. They demand that any meeting space have an open roof and a fully transparent construction layout to prevent another trap. Following the meeting, the four manage to clear themselves of the suspicions held by Island 2.

Arisu takes charge of logistics in the empire temporarily as a token of alliance. Meanwhile, Horikita gathers evidence and builds the case that Flux is the true conspirator, aided by the other three.

That is when she remembers: The sand beach. Flux and his loyalists had planned the assassination of the Island 1 leaders right there during the construction of the meeting hall. She has proof, and she knows the exact nation that will listen to her claim—the Cass Coalition.

She sends two messengers. One is killed transferring the message (eliminated by Flux's men), but the other successfully delivers it, relaying Horikita's  proof and warning: if one of the messengers mentioned in the list  dies, it means Flux is actively hunting down the proof.

Upon hearing the messenger, reviewing the claims and solid proof, and sensing the urgency, the leader of the Cass Coalition takes immediate action. Realizing their facade has fallen, Flux and his loyalists flee.

With both Island 1 and Island 2 nations now united against Flux's faction for conspiring against the leaders, Flux's faction and the Infernos retreat to the infernal dimension to shield themselves from the impending assault.

however , once the nations of Island 1 and Island 2 ally, Flux's faction never stands a chance. It is a completely one-sided battle.

At last, Flux is arrested. He comes face-to-face with his friends, but they refuse to speak to him. Even Ichinose wants to reach out, but stops herself, knowing Flux deserves no mercy for what he put everyone on both islands through.
