Honami > BB in Planning

This is a short doc to make the case for Honami (COTE) being better than BB (DN) by feats, in terms of planning specifically.

I’ll use one plan from each - LABB for BB and CKA for Honami.

It is recommended that you read the two characters’ respective analysis and their source materials. It’s essential that you read them if you’re not familiar with their scaling and make your own judgments as for the sake of keeping this comparison concise, I won’t go through literally every single thing such as all the evidence supporting the two characters’ interpretations.

First I will describe the definition of planning that’ll be used for the comp. Planning is the character’s blueprint to achieve their goal. It usually comes down to the conceptualization of predetermined events. Think of it as the character’s ability to write an instruction booklet with the steps-by-steps (preparation process) as well as warning labels and safety information (contingency, coverage, etc). For example, to make sure you wake up before 9:10 AM, you’d need to set an alarm on your phone to ring at 9:00 AM. You might be concerned whether it’ll be enough, so you buy and set up an alarm clock that’ll ring at 9:05 AM.

Contrast with strategy - the set of principles guiding the way you will try to achieve your goal. The character’s “instruction booklet” will be written with those principles in mind. For example, to wake up before 9:10 AM, you’d need someone or something to make loud noises around that time.

Planning Aim

I won’t scale “planning aim” given it’s too result based and subjective IMO, but it’s important to understand what the two characters were trying to accomplish with their plan and their context/emotions that drove them to formulate their plans. This would condition L into thinking the last case would’ve a final puzzle L could solve to reveal the culprit.

Planning Preparation Process

Planning Preparation Process is basically the A->B->C->D steps. The planner’s ability to logically align many different tactics over the course of the plan’s lifespan. “Aligned steps” are when actions can’t be performed until another action is completed. If a number of tactics may have been performed in a largely disorganized way, One can also take into consideration the “acts” of each plan; such as, in BB’s plan, the crime scenes.

While the amounts of the components in BB’s plan is only slightly less than Honami’s, most of them did not need to be aligned to the same extent as hers. That’s because most of Honami’s actions had to be split over long stretches of time for a multitude of reasons. This is different from B’s crime scenes where he could do various things in whatever orders. BB also intended to produce four crime scenes in total and other than the one leading to the first crime for obvious reason, all the crime scenes contained the puzzles hiding clues pointing to the location of the next case, until the last.
As a result, Honami’s plan had a drastically more alignment than BB’s: 12 > 6 acts and 29 > 15 alignment of steps. Those numbers do not tell the whole story as there is a level of ambiguity in whether certain steps had to be aligned or not, although that argument can only go so far on its own when the gap in number is so large. More importantly, this can be written off as Honami’s plan having more long-term qualities/necessities with regards to her preparations compared to BB.

BB aligned his murders to a very rigid pattern of dates to serve his misdirections with regards to the letter “B” to ensure a split at the time of his death between “Beyond Birthday” and the “B” from the WH, from the perspective of L and his pawns. B also had to choose his victims and crime scenes according to this, creating its own set of difficulties in the process.

On the other hand, Hona aligned her tactics to hide behind ANHS’ schedule to conceal everything. ANHS’ environment has so many random, uncontrollable elements within its schedule and people with the ability to hurt you (Miyabi, Arisu, potential “white room” associations & co) in every corner are threats due to their cunning and resources, with Kiyotaka clearly being an interest for many of them: all of this limited the opportunities for Hona to progress her plan. Thus, Hona timed her tactics in accordance with the patterns within ANHS’s schedule that reliably sticks year after year as well as her ridiculous social insight to make difficult forecasts: nearly everything she did in relation to her plan that could’ve been ahead of time revolved around special days (exams, trips or promise) or took advantage of unique scheduling quirks (everything in Y2V9).

Basically, while B timed his acts around his deceptions, Hona had to time her acts around both deceptions and limitations due to the unpredictabilities in her setting’s schedules as well as limitations in opportunities. B’s main opponent, L, had a passive outsmarting style, whereas Hona’s potential opponent(s) were all considerably far more aggressive than L was throughout LABB. This meant L’s potential was significantly less disruptive to B’s prep process than Honami’s potential opposition(s). Both BB and Honami knew this about their respective opponents.

Verdict: Honami High Diff. The circumstances forced her to have the best preparation process possible. More acts and steps strung together with more dangerous factors needing to be considered.

B had to line up in his crime scene; the wara ningyo doll count, his closed rooms, his Ryuzaki persona and the various puzzles, but as I’ll describe in planning applicability, Honami is infinitely better at aligning all the layers of her plans when needs be.

Hona used her student council position to the fullest for the sake of her plan and she also used her social standings & insight in others without damaging her reputation, usually by scapegoating Kiyo and/or Miyabi + through her communication skills. That’s important, because Hona wasn’t burning her cards quickly & could spam them. Her usage of Mako and Nori before + during the Y2V10 Meeting are perhaps the best examples of it, as it was multifarious: Mako allowed Hona to walk to Kiyo’s room inconspicuously and bait Norihito into the meeting & Norihito helped making the meeting fun and predictably caught her and Kiyotaka on the act, to coerce Kiyotaka (and Norihito).

However, BB did not have Hona’s social resources in his favor and outside the Quarter Queen’s murder, he showed great use of many resources in the rooms of his victims. For instance, using the habit of Bridesmaid into an advantage to set up the books, which proves the planning was something of an adaptable flowchart. BB could integrate the lives of his victims into the plan, albeit the bookshelf could’ve been rearranged by B. Either way, this is still an example of B’s versatility with the assets he finds. Although the readers do not know the specific of their origins, it’s not difficult to guess the stacks of animals are likely a product of B’s resourcefulness with what’s at his disposal. B’s also... creative with the victims’ bodies.
While BB’s plan involved his suicide, it must be mentioned it was set to happen at the end of the plan and its execution, so it does not damage his ability to make adjustments at all.

Verdict: BB High Diff. Prolly BB’s most underrated plan subcat.
Planning Acclimation

The acclimation of a plan is determined by the character’s ability to alter a plan to be more befitting for their current environment, condition and potential hazards. Adapting the existing plan to the current environment as it changes or in the face of unforeseeable circumstances.

Most of the arguments for the planning versatility, with regards to being adaptable and able to improvise during the preparation and execution of their respective scheme applies here although there is a big difference that didn’t really matter as much for versatility. The C-D class alliance strategy to save her plan to bind Kiyotaka is a big planning acclimation feat for Honami since her plan and nearly everything she worked so hard to achieve fell out of reach after the 2nd year’s end-of-year exam. The Promise Meeting was “do-or-die” for Honami and she did... him, and do the other stuff about her class, and her plan too.

Verdict: Honami High Diff. Yeah... the C-D alliance strategy was a massive feat for this subcategory, no other way to look at it.

Planning Invincibility

Planning Invincibility is scaled by the planner’s ability to counter active threats. A planner may construct a plan with preventative measures that hinder their adversaries’ ability to hinder its setup. This has some overlap with planning versatility and acclimation as a plan with more leeway for adjustments can allow the planner to actively counter whenever necessary. Some may only consider preventative measures when scaling invincibility.

The Norihito misdirection of the Y2V10 Meeting was the blinder to conceal Hona’s intent to later coerce Kiyotaka during the promise meeting - this is a misdirection that could be only be seen through via EP, intuition and abductive reasoning; all three in combo. Kiyo would have needed to see through both Norihito and Hona as being dishonest, then extrapolate from this Hona aimed to be caught by Norihito who was definitely honest about his reaction when he saw Honami hugging Kiyotaka, so Kiyo would’ve had to know Honami could anticipate Norihito coming back to Kiyotaka’s room to seek advice about Mako from the latter without knocking on Kiyotaka’s door, then ties everything into deducing Honami has been plotting months ahead of time for the Promised Meeting the two agreed to have to coerce him if necessary into working with her, maybe even transferring to her class (at that time) and doing the naughty stuff, because otherwise it doesn’t make sense for Hona to intentionally do something like that. Also, the fact that Hona blackmailed Nori playing the “I’m Mako’s friend” card right in front of Kiyo adds to the misdirection because clearly, even if Kiyotaka wondered if she anticipated this turn of event, at first it would seem as if Honami’s target was Norihito instead of Kiyotaka.

BB’s cases created the idea that they were a set of murders and not suicides, when you make a murder in a closed room, you make it seem as if it were suicide, but all the investigation is part of a same deception process at the end, thus BB used the idea he constructed that this is a murder, in theory leading L and Naomi to forget the possibility of suicide in a room clause. The misdirection of putting L on the track that the last death is a murder and not a suicide.

While BB has the stronger misdirection to execute his plan, it’s not a gap at all, so it is important to discuss their defenses leading into their main execution.

Honami’s actions to disguise and conceal her changes and growth as a schemer from Arisu, along with all that as well as the winter break rumors from the (from Hona’s POV) unknown White Room threat blinded literally every outsider. Honami also took effective actions to neutralize Miyabi, another potential threat. All of which allowed her scheming to move without concerns, aside from Kiyo revealing his “true color” during the second year’s EoY exam. But even with that, Hona’s countermeasures to prevent enemy tactics from hindering the setup worked as intended.

B had his own set of preventative measures, mainly in the form of concealment like using the LAPD as a tool to provide Lawliet with  information of his choosing. Nonetheless, B’s lack of preventative measures regarding the agent(s) L sent was an egregious failure in terms of invincibility. BB’s plan relied on the fact that the agent(s) wouldn’t be able to discover his methods with the 4-1 nails and the wara ningyos which was rather risky when BB’s plan relied on the forecast that the agent(s) would be quite capable in their own right. BB also could not do an extremely precise assessment of Naomi’s abilities ahead of time, given he had no way to ascertain who L was sending as a pawn - BB should have considered a higher deviation versus his expectations. It might seem unfair to hold it against BB since his deception was super strong and the author stated Naomi figured him out due to her “good luck”, but if B relied on people he knew nothing about (beside being strong enough to be sent by L) to miss a gap in his defenses, it should be held against him.

Verdict: Honami High Diff. Her plan was harder to discern before the final phase and it also involved better thinking regarding her adversary; that’s what ultimately grants Honami this subcat.

Planning Applicability

Planning Applicability is for preparations the subcat “Knowledge Application” is to knowledge. Sorta. Generally speaking, you want your preparation to serve a purpose, or maybe more than a single purpose. In nearly all outsmarting scenarios, the opportunities to prepare are limited, so it’s essential to not only identify and act on those opportunities but to also make the most of them. Long story short, applicability asks how relevant the preps were to the aim.

Honami’s actions throughout Y2V9 are enough for BB, who is kind of ordinary in this subcat. The timing of Honami’s Rumor Mills #1 with Miyabi’s neutralization and the engineering of the friend nest Honami had aimed to create for Kiyo with her classmates as well timing this with the start of the KiyoKei fracture and breaking the ice between herself and Kiyo, in which she progressed the process for future contingency to, should it be necessary, try to coerce him with the promise meeting via Hona’s manipulations of Norihito to make him rely on Kiyo with his personal matters ahead of time to prepare the misdirection of the Y2V10 meeting while continuing to gather info on Kiyotaka to have a better understanding of him and his relationship with Kei while concealing any footprint of all this scheming from Arisu and any potentially harmful kouhais as well as teachers, pretty much eviscerates anything BB has ever done in his life with regards to his preparations’ serviceability.

Verdict: Honami Low Diff. B doesn’t have a performance for this subcat that’s remotely comparable to Honami’s Y2V9. The Y2V10 Meeting is enough for him honestly, but who cares.

Planning Contingency

Planning Contingency is measured by the character’s ability to create other plans or backup plans for future events that could occur for a potential outcome or to gain security or insight in case of a discrepancy.

Honami’s main contingency is her entire scheme in Y2V12.5. The coercion Hona created during the Y2V10 Meeting in the event she had won the end-of-year exam is important, as it meant Hona had considered the end-of-year exam when covering for the promise meeting on top of her potential gaps in understanding Kiyotaka (to an extent). Gathering the materials for her coercion contingencies in case she had won the exam and Kiyo wasn’t... enough into what Hona wanted Kiyo to do beyond just this and that involved a more complex set of mind games than for BB to put his contingencies in place... whatever they may have been.

According to the author, B covered for the events that L sent out 3 (or less) additional agents with a contingency. However, there’s no explanation as to what that contingency might have been and how B intended to blend them into the rest of his plan, especially when it comes to his final plays around his death which, in the manner it was executed in the novel, definitely would have required several adjustments should there have been more than one agent.

Verdict: Honami Low Diff. The process for Honami to both create and set her contingencies in place actually exists, but at least BB appears to have installed his contingencies in the right places, so he gets points for that.

Planning Formulation

Planning Formulation scales how swiftly a character can come up with a plan in a relatively short period of time.

By the level of intricacy in BB's planning, assuming he can create a smaller scheme in significantly less time than two months or so is to be expected from someone with his narrative. It can’t be said enough that B wanted to make everything perfect and he had OCD over the finicky details. In a different setting such as a DGR killing game, LG/TG game, COTE special exam, etc this is not practical at all due to time constraint and in many cases, unnecessary. The case for BB is that his PSI is obviously superior to Honami’s.

Honami’s CKA had some quickly made tactics such as the Y2V9.5 gym fabrication and the Kiyo/Kei nominations in Y2V10 but both are minor mid feats barely above average human level. However, Honami has a legitimate feat with the plan to extract information from Ichika she came up with in the middle of a conversation. It is not within CKA, but unlike any of the arguments one can make to assess BB’s aptitude in this subcat, it’s a formulation speed feat.

Verdict: Honami High Diff. Neither excels at this, but she has real feats and more often than not, that’s better than assumptions.

Planning Accuracy

Planning Accuracy measures how the abstractions of your plan translate into reality and everything connects together reliably.

Honami’s forecasting of everyone else’s actions were usually very precise. Some examples; Miyabi having her and Suzune compete for the SCP seat on the 2nd week of December, before its last day prior to the following weekend or him sending the photo to Kei via a third party after school but right before the winter break around the gym, nailing the place + timing with perfect accuracy. Hona’s anticipations of her classmates around the same time is arguably even more ridiculous, mainly the predictions of the Hokkaido trip grouping along with Norihito starting to see Kiyotaka as someone reliable, Ryuji asking for his help, Hona’s classmates asking Kiyo to investigate her discreetly in a pseudo-date as well as nailing the day that would happen and Kiyotaka telling Honami to pick where they would go first during the date... then there is the culmination of all this with Nori in the Y2V10 meeting - expecting that Norihito would return to Kiyotaka’s room without knocking his door; every single thing listed was planned as chain reactions from the onset, before the Hokkaido trip as a mix of foresight and manipulation.

BB’s planning accuracy was no joke either. For one, B was able to literally reproduce L's personality and foresee his actions despite never having met him directly before. This includes having a good idea that L would not send more than one agent, even if he made a back up plan in case he had sent two more. BB also calculated the reaction from the public to increase the chances of L being able to notice the killings to get on the case, expecting he’d be intrigued in a case the police were woefully unable to solve. While Honami had infinity emotional manip max, he had infinity logical manip max: much of BB’s plan revolves around the idea that L and his pawn(s) will solve the puzzle within one case then move on to the next for the three murders, leading to an “unsolvable” murder for the last death as it will not be an actual murder - this required precise info control from B to ensure the clues he would leave behind couldn’t be discerned by someone any less qualified than L / his agents.

The case for B taking accuracy from Hona is that his expectations of his foresights and manipulations were effectively abstractions on top of abstractions as BB lacked direct insight on the people he toyed with whereas Honami had that insight but her expectations were often much, much more precise than BB’s.

Basically, while B recreated the actors on his chessboard, Honami was able to link together much longer strings of manipulation and foresight within her planning.

With more direct insight B may have been more precise. With less insight, one can assume Hona could still achieve checkpoints, but on a smaller scale or with more info gathering in-between.

Verdict: Honami Extreme Diff. It’s very close. Her understanding of Kiyotaka was flawed even if it wasn’t entirely incorrect, but her expectations were at least somewhat a mirage. What saves Hona’s accuracy is two things: Her expectations about Kiyo’s movements were much broader than everyone else Hona aimed to control and / or predict (which she was 100% right about) and her expectations of Kiyotaka were largely based on traits that he does seem to truly have, so Honami’s researches were not weak.

BB’s accuracy wasn’t flawless either. L predicting the 4th murder case + his underestimation of Naomi in general, particularly with regards to Capoeira, showed a certain degree of weakness when it came to his estimations. Do note the Capoeira slip up doesn’t hurt his accuracy in itself, but more so what it says of BB’s assessment of Naomi for him to have that slip-up.

Their plans connected well, so these are all nitpicks, but Honami’s overall expectations can be said to be a bit more reliable. B taking this is valid as well.

Conclusion

Most of the subcategories are around High Diffs, but only one is a legit toss-up (Accuracy) and more are lower than that. None of this means B is overrated in planning; he’s so much better than the likes of Light and Nagito it’s not even funny. but Honami is one tier above BB in planning.
