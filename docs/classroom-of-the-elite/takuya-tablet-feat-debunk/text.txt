- The semantics and context dictate that takuya specifically HELD the tablet throughout the dialogue he had with d tsubaki, in which he had complete visual access to the tablet until tsubaki specifically made him step back (the original timeframe is believed to be 0.5 seconds, but through this correction, the new timeframe can be interpreted as ~15 seconds which massively nerfs the psi.) Essentially, Takuya had 30x more time to execute his calculations.
- Complete 3D visualization is not necessitated. Rather, only consideration of the ramifications of the 3rd dimension is required for TF. So, general terrain features and its effect on travel were accounted for, but not actively visualized. (vastly decreases the vsi correlation of the feat during Takuya’s calculations).
- The basis of visualization is completely restricted to a 50 square inch map and should not be treated as a visualization of a 35 square km island, inherently limiting the cpi/vsi of the feat by a significant margin.
- Differential calculus cannot consistently measure the meter-perfect line up of Riku and Kito due to a vast array of random occurrences that may fluctuate the calculation. Instead, there were multiple dozen meters of leeway for their confrontation to be premeditated, as opposed to the flawed precise meter claim in TF. This is due to the fact that Kito and Riku’s conflict would be initiated upon seeing each other (albeit in an area with poor visibility, which is why the margin of error is only some tens of meters). This massively nerfs the vsi/cpi required yet again. The actual difficulty of the equation that takuya executed would therefore be overstated as well.

These nerfs affect TF exponentially, reducing its potency by many hundred times.
