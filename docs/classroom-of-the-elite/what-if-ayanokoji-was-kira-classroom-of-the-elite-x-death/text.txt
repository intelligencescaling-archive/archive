What if Ayanokoji was Kira?

I made this one around 4 months ago, but it's a pretty interesting match-up so I'll post it here:

___

*Situational context:* Light found out from Rem that Misa Amane is being detained by L and is currently being interrogated—which is partly why Light comes up with and goes through with the memory loss plan in the first place.

Now, assuming Ayanokoji was the one in this situation after doing exactly what Light had done leading up to this scene, this is what would happen:

Koji would only have **Misa** lose her memories.

Koji wouldn't bother having L detain him along with her. He knows it would never clear suspicion at all since he can't express emotions physically and has a natural disposition to hide what he's thinking—something that'll only work to heighten L's suspicions even further.

What he would do after having Misa lose her memories is instruct Rem to find another person to conduct the killings in place of the *second* Kira and have them record a specific message in space of the first one.

Unlike canon where Higuchi received the book, it would be the guy who sold him out (Namikawa) in this case.

Why?

Because Namikawa is smart, he won't just kill without care like Higuchi did and actively aim to align his killing pattern with that of the 1st Kira (Ayanokoji) to mask himself which gives Koji some room to maneuver freely.

This is where Koji himself would prepare many deaths in advance in his own notebook and bury it like Light did. Only, these deaths will follow a completely different pattern separate from Misa's but still enough to SUGGEST that there are 2 Kiras.

It will factor in occasional mistakes and things of that nature.

The first deaths, however, will involve members of the police and political higher ups with the most important one writing out a letter before croaking.

Following this, Ayanokoji will go to the Kira task force head quarters. L won't give Ayanokoji info that he's detained Misa (same with Light) and test his deductive skills to see if their thoughts lined up or if he was actually Kira.

Koji will pass the test by being deliberately ambiguous at certain instances because he knows that L's reasoning is logical but more recursive and counterintuitive than others might initially assume.

L hates being wrong so the more Koji proves that he's right the more likely it is for his suspicions not to increase beyond their current level. He knows that L won't stop asking the same questions again and again so his intentional ambiguity at some parts will draw still attention to him but not enough to be concrete evidence of him being Kira.

Koji—after finding out Misa had been detained from L—would express his concern in his usual non-committal and slightly comedic way before slipping in a sly threat to L that if anything were to happen to Misa he would probably make L regret it.

This is when the delayed death from earlier will reach Watari's ears and he will relay the info to the task force.

When they read the letter, it will be *another Kira* expressing that L had detained the previous *second Kira* and tortured them for multiple days and that these new killings (policemen and politicians) are in retaliation to that fact.

They will list all the methods L used to torture Misa (Rem tells him) in the letter and say that he knows of them because he (Kira) **remembers** and he will stop at nothing to get revenge on the one that "killed" him.

The new second Kira will continue on to say that they hope this will reach the first Kira so that he can instruct them of what to do.

This fact will provide the answer L had been seeking after questioning why Misa had suddenly started talking Abt stalkers and wanting to die out of nowhere. It didn't match the Misa from before who didn't talk at all throughout the torture.

When L hears of this, he'll ask Koji if he remembers killing anyone, which, of course he'll say no. Then when L asks if Ayanokoji would kill anyone he says he might be forced to depending on the circumstances.

He'll go on to say that if L is talking in terms of Kira then he probably would since the crime rate had dropped noticeably after Kira's appearance.

This, of course, still places Koji as a prime suspect of being the 1st Kira, but seeing how the situation turned out, he will not be the *only one.*

Immediately after this, a broadcast from Sakura TV will pop up showing what happened to the police and politicians that died.

Even though Watari had the task force intercept the message, it was still broadcast due to a letter that the task force head had received from the new first Kira (Namikawa acting as Koji)

It had the names and faces of everyone involved with L's little squad—including Ayanokoji himself—and it said that if L continues to pester him then he will be forced to kill these innocent people. As an extra insurance he threatens each member's families as well, down to the babies, to let him know that this isn't up for negotiation.

Something like this will lead to L being isolated because no matter how justice centric Light's Father and everyone else is, the death of children will weigh heavily on their minds.

Aizawa especially will be an advocate to quit because his child's name is on the list.

Chief Yagami will ironically be next in line cuz if his children die because of his actions he literally can't live with it. Matsuda idolizes him so he's next to agree and Koji will simply decide after running it by L to see if he wishes to continue investigating (which he will)

___

This kind of seemingly heartless display will send Aizawa off the rails and have him attack L while cursing that if anything happens to his kid then he'll be the one to kill him with his bare hands.

He'll also express his hatred for L here which strengthens his threats but L won't budge and still continue his investigation because if the 2 Kiras roam free then a larger number of innocents will be in danger.

He'll apologize to them and tell them he enjoyed working with them but for the sake of the world he cannot let injustice go unpunished, even if the consequences are that severe.

He'll then say that after everything's over they can punish him however they like.

Here, everyone (Mochi included) will turn to leave but they'll just see Ayanokoji standing in the room and tell him to come along with them.

Koji won't answer them and simply ask L if he's sure that what he's doing is the right thing because if he doesn't stop here then the death toll will probably be alot higher than before, and the deaths of many innocents will be associated with his name.

Not only that, if he fails to catch Kira after he goes rogue, then, what'll he do?

L will say that he has no doubt he'll be able to catch both Kiras before that happens. Ayanokoji will ask him again if he's sure and when they lock eyes, Koji will nod—saying he understands— before walking towards the door and closing it in Aizawa and the others' faces.

He'll tell L that he trusts him and wants to help find both Kiras. When L asks if he's not afraid of death he'll say that he is but death from cowardice is something he doesn't think is appealing.

The purpose of this plan is not to earn L's trust. It is to isolate him and drag his name through the mud seeing as public perception has alot to do with how things play out.

If even the police and government publicly disagree with him then they'll be forced to withdraw support. Not only that, since Kira is threatening the life of children, L will eventually be driven out of Japan since there's a possibility that the new Kira will follow his example and threaten more civilian lives.

The new Kira (he assumes this is the one who possessed Misa before the transfer cuz of the information that Kira knew. He doesn't know Ayanokoji was acting as the new second Kira) will be way more likely to take civilian lives due to their impulsivity, especially after what L had done to them.

Koji only remains because he wants to counter any plan L might come up with that would uncover his identity.

___

[Koji's got a few contingencies in place:

1.) If Namikawa ever dares to attempt to write any of the names broadcasted on the television within that month then he will stab himself in the eye and die of a heart attack from the shock. It is one of the delayed deaths Koji had in place. The reason it is only confined to a month is because that is barely enough time for L and Koji together to hunt down the Yotsuba group and close in on Namikawa.

Higuchi will end up being a false lead here because of his attitude and Namikawa will take advantage of that fact by letting him know that he is Kira and that he wants Higuchi to act as his mouthpiece during meetings. They'll occasionally have differing opinions on certain topics but they'll settle on an answer (scripted) that seems like one had lost.

When Koji and L finally get past Higuchi and are about to get to Namikawa, he will die because he can't live past that month regardless. This not only causes the case to collapse, it allows Rem to retrieve the notebook and leave because she'll rationalize that his death is a warning to get out of there.

L now loses his only other lead of who could possibly be Kira and ends up feeling depressed like in canon. Since Koji is no light he will take advantage of that to convince him to release Misa because this is more than enough to prove that she was being used just like the first Kira. (One month)

2) If L still doesn't release Misa even though it has been proven that Kiras powers can be transferred after a mental or physical "death" then he will redirect Rem's anger towards L and have her kill him like in canon. Only, this time she will give the deathnote back to Ayanokoji and have him do it after telling him L's full name. (1 month + 3 days)

3) If L agrees to release Misa but still is adamant in his pursuit of Kira then Koji will start sending multiple anonymous messages slandering L and saying that he is still trying to catch Kira even though kids are in danger. Saying he's a heartless monster who thinks he's doing this for the sake of justice when in reality he's a stubborn fool that hates losing.

This will be constructed to sound similar to aizawa's griped about him and even if aizawa sees this it's more likely for him to agree with the message than realize the fact that this person is hinting to L that he's the one sending it.

After a while other people will send messages like these and flood the media with it to warp their perception of L. Someone will then send a picture of the building L had created as his hideout and propose the idea that he might be residing there. It'll take a while to gain traction but when it does people will start to question it and when they enter they'll realize that it's just a big building filled with nothing. Atleast one of them will search up info about the place and obviously realize that it didn't belong to any known company due to the apparent lack of staff. The theory of it being L's hideout will increase forcing L himself to leave via helicopter with Ayanokoji.

He'll drop him off somewhere around the bridge and by that time he'll have to accept that working in Japan will affect the case's progress so he can't stay for much longer but he'll tell Koji that he still thinks of him as a suspect after all this time, he just can't prove it.

Koji will tell him that it's a given he can't since he's not Kira. L will be forced to accept this loss and leave the case alone.

(This contingency would take 2-3 months to fully realize.)]

___

Note: It's important to know that even though Ayanokoji took the place of Kira, he did not take the place of Light Yagami. Light is at his home, uninvolved with the Kira case. And due to circumstances escalating this far, Souichiro would rather beat the shit out of him than let him set foot in the headquarters or hunt Kira on his own.
