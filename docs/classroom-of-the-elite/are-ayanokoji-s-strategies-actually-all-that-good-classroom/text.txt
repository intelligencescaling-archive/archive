Question- Do you Actually believe Ayanaokoji's strategies are all that good?

Listen, I've often been one to claim Ayanaokoji is VERY overated and gets demolished by Yuichi.

But here's the thing- I feel I can name every single one of Ayanaokoji's strategies in just a few sentences max.

Take the Deserted Island Exam. Yeah, it's a little complicated, but it just comes down to this-

Ayanokoji sees 2 Class A students emerge from a cave and one of them talks about how they are the Leader. Ayanokoji deduces the true Leader must be the other student, as the real Leader of Class A wouldn't boast about that.

Ayanaokoji sees Ryueen having a radio, and notices Ibuki keeps going to the same spot of their camp, where he finds an identical radio, realizing Ibuki is obviously a traitor.

Ayanaokoji tells Yamauchi to put mud on Horiktia's hair to speed - up her sickness, by bribing him with Sakura's email. Once Horikita passes - out, Ayanaokoji uses this as a way to change their Class - Leader position to himself.

That's it. Yeah, it's impressive. But you're really gonna tell me this stands on - par with Yuichi?

Even X Strategy or Love Letter Strategy aren't that hard to talk about.

X Strategy is just blackmailing Manabe with pictures of Karuizawa so that he can get some random information about Class C and try to trick Ryueen into believing someone - else might be the Strategic - Mastermind as no - one else would just run that fast as it would reveal too - soon.

Love Letter Strategy is just putting an anagram in the Love - Letter that Yagami would see, and then telling a bunch of people he hurt to go to the same room as him, tricking him into thinking he's been caught.

Like, I don't mean to undermine any of this. I'm not saying any of this is impressive. But I really don't see how it stands - up to Yuichi's later feats. I honestly believe Yuichi's strategy against Kamishiro Alone is enough to stand - up to these Strategies- Let - Alone his whole arc in All - Bet Gambling.

Not to mention, Ayanaokoji's Plans often rely on everyone - else just doing what he wants.

Take the Soudou incident. What if his Camera - Strategy just didn't work? What if the Class C students took a breather and just went onto call Ryueen? Unless Ayanokoji was recording them nearly admitting to their guilt, he'd be screwed.

And what about Sakayanagi VS Ryueen? Ayanaokoji literally relies on a "CLUE" he gave Hashimoto- It's the most absurd thing I've ever heard, because it's not even a real message. He's just hoping Sakayanagi Will happen to interpret in such a way into realizing that he just wants her to drop - out, and then furthermore believing that she will go ahead and do just that.

Or Y3 V1, Which I swear wasn't him doing anything. Yeah he got 100%,, but the rest of this supposed: "Plan," was literally just giving advice to Ichinose on how her Class could beat Horiktia's Class, and then just letting Ryueen just do whatever he wants. I'd hardly call it that complex .
