The trap isn’t just about catching Kiyotaka it’s about measuring how deeply Kiyotaka can analyze a layered deception. If Koji had openly exposed his knowledge of the marked Joker, Nagumo would have immediately recognized him as a major threat.

This strategy of Truth and Lie is a controlled deception, where the "truth" buries suspicions, and the "lie" provides hidden clues that only a genius could uncover.
