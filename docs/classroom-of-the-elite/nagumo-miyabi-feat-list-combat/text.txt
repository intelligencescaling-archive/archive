(V4.5)  Nagumo’s striking power was said to be either weaker or equal to that of Sudo’s so in other words his AP is relevant to his. This is the same Sudo who made Koji feel quite a lot of internal pain when slapping him on the back with 100% of his strength in Volume 5.

(V2) - While naturally Nagumo’s upper body/arm strength is stated to be comparable to Sudo’s, Nagumo was described by Suzune and Koji in V4.5 to be far more focused and toned in the legs as opposed to his upper body like Sudo + possessing physical abilities obvious by just looking at him.

Why is this relevant?

Because Sudo has an AP feat in V2 where he sends flying and destroys an entire wooden table (hence the term “splintered”) with his kick specifically. This means taking into account the toning of Nagumo’s legs, his kicks would scale even higher than what Sudo did especially considering he’s a prodigy soccer/football player as well; scaling to have insanely high Kicking Strength.

(Overall AP, Leg Strength)

(V5) - every single student in the soccer club is nimble, no expectations. Implying the students have above average agility at worst.

Hirata along with Shibata were said to be skilled players possessing sharp movements while you one said to be on a level beyond even that is Nagumo.

Among all the nimble and quick soccer players, Nagumo by far stands out the most and is practically a soccer prodigy. Meaning he possesses Agility greater more so than Sudo, Ryuen and surpasses even Manabu (yes cope lol).

Even more so, he reacted to both Hirata’s and Shibata’s movements at the same time and slipped past both without losing ball possession.

(Overall Speed, Agility)

(V5) - A show of his Kicking Strength is when Koji in particular was the only student he described to have a powerful shot good enough to score from midfield.

With that description + his uniquely developed lower body he clearly has high Kicking Strength.

In terms of stamina he’s supposed to be by narrative far above even Sudo and he actually has small but abstract feats to back that up as he’s participated in 5 events in the sports festival winning in each one showing zero signs of fatigue in fact he was shown to have a completely blank face after the event. Be aware he’s won 5 after the 100m sprint so there’s a chance he’s done more offscreen.

In terms of Travel Speed, by narrative he’s Top 4 in the verse as he was completely confident in facing Manabu in a race and that he always won his 100m race. Also due to the fact that Koji and Manabu never caught up with Nagumo before he reached the finish line in Volume 5.

(Overall Speed, Stamina, Kicking Strength)

(Y2V4) - Now this might not seem like a good endurance feat but Nagumo actually muttering a word before he goes unconscious from a blow against Ayanokoji (by being punched in the chest btw) is a decently solid feat especially when you compare his to Nanase for example who’s a fairly competent fighter being described to have her entire consciousness being blown away in an instant if she was punched in the forehead.

Also another important note is that Nagumo was never shown or stated to have any injuries in the aftermath and was perfectly fine during Y2V4.5 and onwards from a seemingly serious (not 100% ofc) punch from Ayanokoji as he showed zero hesitation and even admitted he was going to take Nagumo seriously.

(Endurance, Durability)

(V9) - In terms of statements and narrative, Nagumo was told by others that he actively went to all the trouble in making punishments of violence more lenient.

Question is why would he bother to do such a thing? It’s obvious that Nagumo has had experience in combat many times before in ANHS so he knows how to fight.

Further evidence for Nagumo’s experience in fighting is that despite Fujimaki, a classmate of Manabu who was leading the sport festival and was stated to be 2nd only below Manabu, Nagumo was not only taunting him but provoking him to fight.

The same Fujimaki who had his bulk praised by Koji.

Another powerful statement of Nagumo is that not only is he not against brawling but he likes serious fights. Considering that he had no qualms in provoking the built Fujimaki to combat, Nagumo has likely fought just as physically capable students in the past and given his statement, he’s won against them all.

Even more proof of Nagumo’s fighting experience by narrative is that despite Koji directing ALL his killing intent into Nagumo’s eyes, Nagumo only flinched once and immediately recovered and stood up to face him. This can be an Emotional Management feat too but the fact Nagumo only flinched makes you question how many fights Nagumo had.

Compare this to Kid Koji making a WR instructor freeze from just his glare. Nagumo not admitting his fear against a teen version is completely insane.

(Emotional Management)

In Y2V9, Fuka mentions there are aggressive people specifically in the third year. That’s another piece of evidence to strengthen Nagumo’s fighting experience narrative as Fuka implies third years are prone to use violence.
