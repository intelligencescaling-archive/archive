Nagumo’s Personality, Goals and Ideology

Miyabi Nagumo was born a gifted child, having always been better than everyone around him with little notice, having the ability to learn much faster than others despite never putting in particular effort and time, and being absurdly charismatic, naturally gathering big circles of individuals around him for all the possible reasons.
Nagumo had started observing that people around him began trying to take advantage of him for his qualities, and so it was during his childhood that Nagumo had begun to see things for the bigger picture and use his talents to control people and understand the human psyche. He became a social butterfly whose goal was to be ahead of everyone in terms of talents.
Nagumo destroyed any challenge he ever faced, never dealing with any sort of difficulty or roadblock in his “dazzling path”. No one dared to oppose him, and no circumstances were set against him. However, despite being a man who never knew defeat, Nagumo always questioned his built status. He decided to ignore his feeling of unease as long as he never met someone who could oppose him. That all changed when he entered ANHS. Nagumo’s competitiveness developed when he finally had seen some who he deemed to be truly on his level.
Nagumo’s feeling of unease when it comes to his ability has its origins in his own superiority over the foes he had in his past. He questions if he is truly someone remarkable among the crowds or just a “naked emperor” who never had a powerful opponent. Nagumo’s true motivation is to find the answer to that question by facing the most brilliant people he comes to meet in the most competitive environment a student can find in Japan.

Miyabi Nagumo developed a mindset fitting of his goal and pride, which is centered towards genuine “individualistic” meritocracy. He dreams of a society in which people get success not due to their in-built status, but because of their own personal abilities, REGARDLESS of their methods and convictions.

Ever since entering ANHS and understanding the system, Nagumo grasped it was the perfect opportunity to build an environment to envision his ideal society. Under his meritocratic stance which he was to prove as absolute through his own personal abilities, Nagumo spread an entire current in his year backed by his proven achievements to influence friends and foes alike. Nagumo understood how to abuse the ANHS system and how he can get his hands on influencing it himself, in ways no other people have done before.
Nagumo never went against his words or lied to himself and this principle was cemented when he became the most powerful figure in the ANHS, as he promised to his classmates he would. However, parallel to his past, he knows that he is not invincible and his ability has limits, looking for any means necessary to prove their existence. The one standing in between him and both his aspirations as well as his ideological fulfillment was only a single man, Horikita Manabu.

Mixed Training Camp (MTC)

(Y1V8) - The Mixed Training Camp was the first time Manabu and Nagumo ever had gotten to compete directly.
For Manabu Horikita, it meant to be on guard against any sort of strategy made by Nagumo. For Nagumo it was the start of a long term strategy to create an advanced multiple step trap system that later covers multiple objectives.

(Y1V8) - The reason why MTC worked so well is because of its great disguise as a regular exam in the eyes of everyone, but more importantly, Manabu can’t infer at least early on the possibilities of the main trap itself. The MTC trap relies on making use of the established exam rules to close any way to escape and build the trap over.
Here are all the important details Nagumo forged in advance:
MTC is a group-based training exam which is showcased as a direct competition of all years mixed together, focusing on mental development, meditation and physical training and following a specific schedule. It is held for 8 days where members of one group will experience daily life of all kinds in that group. All the students have to be split up based on gender.
After holding meetings amongst ALL the school years, the split students will divide themselves up into six groups, in total 6 male and 6 female. The upper and lower limit for each class is predetermined based on the number of students of each gender in a class year. What is very important is that each group must have students from at least two or more classes as a prerequisite, meaning that one class cannot form an exclusive group of their own.
It is not allowed to withdraw from a group halfway through or change members. If a student is forced to retire due to either sickness or injury, then the group must deal with that gap.
After the 1st years have established their groups, they will rendezvous with the groups of 2nd years and 3rd years and establish the groups numbering around 30 to 45 people composed of 1st years up to 3rd years.
All the formed small groups must assign someone from their group a leader.
The outcome of the special exam will be decided on a comprehensive exam that will take place on the last day of outdoor school. The outcome will depend on the 'point averaged' from the exam results of every member in the six large groups.
The Leader of a small group, which is the part of a large group that falls at LAST place, whose total falls below the predetermined border for the small group, will be expelled. If that happens, they have to choose one extra person from their group to bear joint responsibility with the leader and be expelled alongside them.
In the case of expulsion, as a last resort, one can buy a “Cancellation of Expulsion” and extend a lifeline to a student using 20 million private points and 300 class points, alongside 100 points per person of the expellee's class will be deducted either outcome.
In the way that it is presented to every student, but most importantly to the main 3 people Nagumo uses to make the trap possible, Nagumo is specifically able to infer IN ADVANCE the thought process and construction of strategies in order to reason exactly what each one of these people would do when they encounter specific information. What this means is that Nagumo ANTICIPATES not only EVERY OUTCOME of the exam before it even begins, but even their THOUGHT PROCESS, as he was laying foundations to very late stages of the strategy (such as the Ikari Momoko part of the strategy which requires every notable event up to that point to unfold perfectly) before the exam even started. But most importantly, the MTC itself, which SCP Nagumo submitted to the school WEEKS in advance after every detail of the plan was polished to perfection.
Every Anticipation noted with (A) was done by Nagumo weeks in advance. Everything else is something he adapted in his strategy during the exam.
