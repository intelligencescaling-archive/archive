Morty Prime feat list

Morty prime, the grandson of Rick prime is the current Morty we follow throughout the story. Called the Mortyest Morty, he has high adversity capacity and adaptability by experiencing multiple universes and trouble all at a young age.

Train Tactic (season 5 episode 6)
Morty after messing with Rick’s portal fluid accidentally gets a permanent portal open on the palm of his hand while the other side is connected to a psych ward patient’s thigh. Morty, unaware of his new portal mate initially meets up with him only for him to coerce Morty to be his criminal buddy. Morty does not agree leading to the dude headlocking Morty near a train track. Morty also sees that the dude  came to him with a jet pack. Morty to counter this sticks his hand on the train track so his palm where the portal is touches the train wheels. This does two things, it first sends intense pain to the psych ward person as his thighs can feel the impact of the train, and second it detaches his hand from his body. Morty knew the train track will be strong enough to rip human flesh apart and also knew that his hand wouldn't fall apart as there was a portal stuck to it and Morty knew the portal’s are indestructible through physical means, also Morty knew the energy of the train on his hand would be transferred through the portal to the psych ward dude so only his wrists will be damaged and not the hand itself. This leads to Morty’s hand being attacked with the portal still on his detached hand while Morty’s hand is detached due to his wrist being torn apart. Morty did all this and now the psych ward patient is in excruciating pain unable to move while Morty takes his hand and puts it in the psych ward dude's thigh where his side of the portal is. This led to a chain reaction of the portals inside the portal causing the portals to pull each other crushing the psych ward dude into nothing as the two portals pulling each other ended in a self destructive loop of pulling everything in and vanishing. Morty then puts his torn hand to the jetpack the dude brought and burns his injury to stop bleeding.

(mental fortitude, Stress tolerance, creativity, abstract reasoning, Strategy efficiency)

Deduction of parasites (season 2 episode 4)
The house gets infected with a parasite that clones itself as a friend and plants memories of being together, Rick realizes and locks the house down so the parasites don’t escape. As they try to remember who is real the parasites multiply and their house gets swarmed by parasites they all have memories of interacting, knowing there are only 6 people everyone gets paranoid. Rick, after trying to kill one of the parasites, gets flamed by the entire house of people and parasites and Morty is told to kill him. Morty gets flamed by Rick for being a shitty grandson and Morty from that realizes something. Morty can remember bad memories of Rick and none from the other friends, from this Morty reasons the parasites and only create happy memories not bad ones. Morty realizes this aligns with their goals as they would only want good memories to create friendship but because Morty can only remember good ones he assumes they can not make bad ones. With this Morty and Rick goes around the house killing families they can not remember mad memories of and gives guns to people he knows are real because they have bad memories of them.

(abductive reasoning, deductive reasoning, inductive reasoning, metacognition)
Rick’s memories (Season 5 episode 10)
During the events of season 5 episode 10, Morty using a memory device goes through every memory Rick has until that point. This gives Morty all the experiences Rick has inventing portal guns, scheming against galaxies and all the scientific knowledge of Rick. Morty after that full experience of Rick immediately realized Rick’s internal turmoil saying, “dead wife” in an empathetic tone showing his understanding for Rick's emotions. Morty also seemed to be knocked out by the information so its likely the memory device didn’t actually automatically feed Morty information but made Morty process the information on his own, meaning Morty understood Rick’s 70 year life (likely longer due to Rick spending time in places where time is warped) in a matter of hours or minutes.

(EU, Empathy, CPI, PSI, Knowledge, Experience)

Adaptation to the forest tribe (season 2 episode 6)
Rick and another scientist beefs over who is the better scientist, Morty gets sick of it and leaves Rick going to a forest where cannibal people exist. Morty meets them but adapts to their society and becomes their leader. Morty recognized their connection with nature and their social hierarchy formed around the belief that the forest has a will and they are following it. Morty acts like he can feel the will of the forest to become their tribe leader and Morty enforces their moral culture of attacking anyone who is disconnected with nature. Morty, although feeling discomfort to their culture, still adapted. Morty also learned their language and once he saw Rick brought them to his place so he could ask for help.

(Social adaptability, cognitive adaptability, VCI, Abstract reasoning, Social awareness, Social integration)
Existential Crisis fortutide (Season1 episode 6)
Morty, after his current world gets ruined by a failed love potion, is told by Rick to change to a different reality where their life is nearly identical but that world’s Rick and Morty was destined to die soon. Once they travel there they see Rick and Morty of that reality to die and bury their body and act as replacements. Morty after seeing his whole world destroyed is immediately moved to a different one where all of his friends and families are the same people but are like clones and aren’t his friends and family. Morty after realizing this is his life accepts his life and accepts his circumstances taking a nihilistic view towards the world.

(mental fortitude, cognitive adaptability)

Morty after his sister experiences existential issues with her family convinces her to stay by telling her life is too meaningless to get mad over family issues and uses his dead versions body buried in the backyard as evidence that giving a fuck is meaningless.

(existential intelligence, abstract reasoning, persuasion)
Morty’s rebellion (season 1 episode 10)
After Morty and Rick search for a different version of Rick that is currently framing his Rick for murder, Rick finds the evil Rick's hidden base only to get captured, Morty gets sent to a prison where other Morty’s also reside. Morty hears that the other Morty’s lost hope and has given up on changing their circumstances, he also recognizes that the Morty’s are currently believing in a one true Morty to save them. Morty, recognizing their lack of confidence, gives a speech. He says he is scared too to empathize with them while encouraging mob mentality, Morty says Morty’s have an identity independent from Ricks countering their lack of confidence and self worth. The Morty's, fueled with confidence and blinded by mob mentality, take down the guards and rescue Rick.

(EP, EU, mass manipulation, persuasion)
