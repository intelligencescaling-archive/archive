(high ball not sure)
This passage can be interpreted in a number of ways, we could say that it's possibly the 4th principle of the development of an object in space, i.e. movement, the fourth thing that defines this movement is the norm, i.e. in our case the speed, and this corresponds to "how". To calculate this, Arisu needs 2 things:
Total displacement
Elapsed time

It’s easy to have the Elapsed time, she needs the two moments, and just subtract (momentA) − ( momentB).

But for the Total displacement it’s harder, first she can just make an approximation by comparing the 2 point of the GPS of the Student, or make a rigorous formula to find the distance between 2 point which is:
√ (x2−x1)² + (y2−y1)²

In both case she need after finding the values displacement and time (found with arithmetic calculation), she need to make this final operation (division):
Total displacement / Elapsed time

After all that, she knows the speed of every student in motion, which means performing the above calculations hundreds of times over.
