My thoughts before rereading COTE

Today will be a post where I write my thoughts on Ayanokoji and COTE before rereading COTE.

Recently, seeing that not only Ayanokoji but also other COTE characters are scaled high, especially in this sub, surprised me. Honestly, people scaling Ayanokoji high was not surprising, but seeing other COTE characters this high as well inevitably surprised me.

So I decided to reread the COTE series from the beginning because it has been quite a while since I read some volumes. I first started the COTE series(Light novel) in the summer or fall of 2023 and caught up towards the end of 2024, so quite a lot of time has passed, and I decided to do a reread. Of course, whether I get bored and fail to finish it is another story. Anyway, every time I finish the volumes of 1 year, I will make a post and share my thoughts, and I am making this post to see if my thoughts actually change or not, and to see whether they change positively or negatively after reading.

My thoughts on COTE focusing on Ayanokoji

First of all, in my eyes, Ayanokoji is definitely not High Tier and is unfortunately overrated, at least that is my current view. I scale him between High Level Mid Tier and Peak Level Mid Tier.

So why do I not scale Ayanokoji higher?

1)Rivals:

When I use this argument, people sometimes brush it off saying 2022 argument, which is ridiculous.

In my eyes, there are 3 criteria to scale an SCD character high.

1st is the quality of their rivals

2nd is the difficulty of the situations they are put in, which also encompasses the 1st reason

3rd is their narrative feats

Now, Ayanokoji is quite good in terms of the 3rd criterion.

The 2nd criterion can also be called good, but the 1st criterion I mentioned (which also affects the 2nd) is unfortunately very weak.

Throughout the entire series, Ayanokoji has barely fought against a character who could push him even 10%.

I admit that COTE characters are generally scaled based on the mind games they show in school exams rather than 1v1s. And thanks to his feats in these exams, I can still see Ayanokoji above a certain level. If the school exams were not good either, Ayanokoji would drop quite a lot in my eyes in COTE.

However, even though school exams are important (and the difficulty of school exams already falls under the 2nd criterion I mentioned), the quality of rivals is also very important.

Even Takuya, the smartest character in the series who has gone up against Ayanokoji so far, barely pushed Ayanokoji at all. Aside from causing Sakura to get expelled, he had almost no impact on Ayanokoji.

In fact, I never forget when I was reading Y2V7, I said to myself

Well, this Takuya will probably do something in this volume, because in previous volumes he had only made one decent move against Ayanokoji in Y2V5, and I had not seen any other proper attack from him.

And what do you think happened at the end of the volume?

Takuya got expelled without doing anything.

That part disappointed me so much I cannot even explain it. In a part I was so hyped for, Takuya went to the student council without making any proper plan and then got expelled, that is it.

Still, I will try to read very carefully this time because COTE scalers still scale Takuya high.

Maybe there is some stupidity on my part, I do not know, but I will read very carefully this time.

So in short, Ayanokoji's rivals are really not good.

Of course, these are my thoughts before rereading.

Let's move on to the 2nd reason

2)The Narrative Method of the Series

In the COTE series, even though Ayanokoji narrates the story to us readers as it happens, he does not fully state his own thoughts.

Even though he sometimes tells us who he meets and talks to in the story,

in reality, he sometimes lies to us readers and sometimes does not tell us what is going through his mind at all.

And this is a trap that those who scale Ayanokoji high fall into to a large extent.

For example, Ayanokoji makes a plan and does not tell the reader about this plan until it yields results.

When the plan suddenly works, the reader cannot get that moment out of their head, and finding it both very impressive and cool, the reader unnecessarily overrates this part.

I admit that this narrative tactic I mentioned happens in other series too, but in COTE it happens almost all the time.

By using the same narrative tactic in almost all of Ayanokoji's plans, the author causes the reader to overestimate certain feats in their eyes.

That was the 2nd reason.

And now let's come to 3

3)Fanbase

Look, I cannot prove this, but I really have known SCD since 2022.

Well, it is not a community I follow very frequently, it has never been a community I followed excessively in my life, yet I have still been around almost since the beginning of SCD.

The biggest reasons for the initial formation of SCD were characters like Light, L, and Johan, however, around that time Season 2 of COTE also came out and COTE slowly started becoming popular worldwide.

The biggest proof of this is that while there were 5 years between Season 1 and Season 2, 2 years passed between Season 2 and Season 3.

2022 was a period when COTE and Ayanokoji became popular.

And those times were also when SCD was first forming, and Ayanokoji was a character present at the very beginning of SCD's formation.

In other words, COTE and Ayanokoji have been around since the start of SCD.

And in today's SCD, he is arguably the character with the most fans inside SCD.

About 4 or 5 months ago, I was debating someone.

They said that most COTE fans do not know SCD, but this applies to all other series too, meaning let's say if there are 100 Code Geass fans, 97 or 98 of them do not know SCD.
