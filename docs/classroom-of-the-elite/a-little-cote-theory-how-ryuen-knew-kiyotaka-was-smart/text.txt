A lil COTE theory

(TL:DR at end)

In Y1V4, right when the mails were sent about the status of VIPs, Ryuen and Ibuki approach Kiyotaka and Horikita.

There Ryuen mentions something -

>“That guy with you is considerably smart. Despite that, he hasn’t achieved anything significant, or gotten high grades. He has some excellent qualities, though I have my doubts,” said Ryuuen.

Based on this statement, Ryuen states that Kiyotaka is actually "considerably smart" despite being average in academics and not having achieved anything significant. Which begs the question, where did Ryuen even get the idea that Kiyotaka is considerably smart?

A potential answer to this is Kushida. We know Kushida was working together with Ryuen during the Zodiac exam. But, was she working with him only after she found she was the VIP and could use that to negotiate with him or even before that?

Kushida's number 1 desire at this point was to expel Horikita. At this point, she didn't even view Kiyotaka as a threat nor did she want him expelled. Her sole aim was Horikita and her alone. From Kushida's POV, this is a rather achievable target. After all, Horikita is extremely isolated from the rest of the class, while Kushida is the center of attention. So pulling some strings and making Horikita look bad or smth is entirely feasible. At the very least, Kushida held a massively advantageous position over Horikita.

However, this changed when the island exam ended. Horikita was credited with Class D's big win. Naturally, Horikita skyrocketed in popularity within her own class. Now, the big advantage Kushida held over Horikita, was now not as big. Furthermore, Horikita just became an even bigger opponent, since she would be seen as extremely smart for winning the exam.

If Kushida was in a position, where her initial advantageous position over Horikita, within the class, dissolved then what would she do? Look for someone outside her class. The best candidate for this is Ryuen, since he was stopped by Horikita during both the Y1V2 trial and the Y1V3 island exam.

So, Kushida reached out to Ryuen immediately after the island exam ended. That was why Ryuen knew Kiyotaka was "considerably smart", because Kushida told him about the midterm paper purchase.

Since the midterm paper purchase was also quite common, it would also fit the description of "hasn't achieved anything" too.
It makes sense that Ryuen would ask Kushida about Kiyotaka too, since Kiyotaka would have been one of his first suspects for being the mastermind.

Ryuen used this knowledge to test whether Kiyotaka was the mastermind or not. Since the mastermind wants to avoid taking credit, Ryuen thought that if Kiyotaka was the mastermind than he would end up giving away some reaction, if his abilities were brought to the light by Ryuen.

Now, there is also the possibility that Ryuen is indeed lying out of his ass and simply looking for a reaction from Kiyotaka regardless. But, I don't think that fits well enough with this situation.

This also creates the possibility that Kiyotaka knew Kushida would betray Class D after the island exam, even before all the Zodiac exam BS. But that's a different can of worms

TL:DR The theory is Ryuen was approached by Kushida right after the island exam ended and before the Zodiac exam. Ryuen then used Kushida's knowledge to see if Kiyotaka would panic having his abilities be recognized or not.
