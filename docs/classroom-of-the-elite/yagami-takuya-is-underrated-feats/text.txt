Feats & Statements

[Before Y2/V0] - As a student of the White Room’s 5th Generation, Yagami excelled in all parts of the program as he could even be touted to be the “Masterpiece of the 5th Generation” in his own right. This led him initially to be within the same tier(ish) as Current Ayanokoji since originally before V0, the main difference between the 5th Generation and the 4th Generation was that the 5th had communication and interpersonal skills curriculum added on while the 4th Generation primarily focused on individual skills. With the introduction of V0, while it is true that the 5th Generation’s “level” in terms of rigorousness is four compared to the 4th Generation’s Level 10, Yagami excelled so much in his generation to the point that he’s able to easily obliterate Ichika if he wanted who was considered the second strongest in the 5th. This leads me to reason that Yagami’s limit isn’t actually Level 4 if he excelled so much in the 5th Generation and is able to absolutely crush the second strongest with barely any difﬁculty. Level 6-7 seems to be more beﬁtting to his “limit” and anything over that would likely be too much. Essentially, what I’m trying to establish here is that Yagami's feats and statements in Y2 imply that he would succeed at higher levels for WR training, and just calling him "Level 4 fodder" is inherently disingenuous.

Y2V7….

Ah, the controversial volume that has led Yagami’s name to be run through the mud as he’s called an expelled clown despite having the most one-sided and expulsion in COTE. Well, let’s try to unpack this. But before this, I feel like many people don’t actually understand Y2V7 so I’ll try to explain the seeds planted that led to his expulsion.

Y2V7 Explained: As we’ve established, Yagami expelled one of Class 1-C’s honor students and pinned the blame on Housen after the events of the Uninhabited Island Exam. Tsubaki however isn’t entirely convinced that Housen did it and suspects that Yagami might have done something about it, so she and Utomiya want to get revenge on whoever really caused the expulsion. But they couldn't expel Yagami by themselves. Ishigami instructed them to use Ayanokoji against Yagami.
Tsubaki approached Satou with a plan to expel Kei and steal Koji, but then she threatened Satou when the latter refused. Tsubaki mentioned Yagami as the mastermind behind the plan, but this is just a ploy: the real goal was to have Satou conﬁde in Kiyo and get him to move against Yagami to protect Kei. Koji knew Yagami was being framed, but he moved anyway (he learned about Yagami's identity from the unknown source) Koji had Kei pass an encrypted message disguised as love letter: Kei's friend > Horikita > Yagami > Nagumo. The encrypted message detailed a Student Council Room Meeting at 3:00 PM. The plan was to ambush Yagami by feeding others information (Ryuuen, Nagumo, Sakagami, etc) so that Yagami could get expelled. Ishigami felt threatened that Yagami would eventually become too strong and disrupt the school harmony with his strategies, so he had to come to Koji to help him expel Yagami. Koji had connections, so he had to use all of them to crush Yagami before he could gain enough control of ANHS.

[Y2V7] - Yagami was able to successfully and immediately deduce that the Love Letter was an anagram sent by Koji, and everyone around the current vicinity was being controlled by him. That itself puts his deduction and analysis skills far above Nagumo and Horikita, who were unable to decipher the true meaning of the Love Letter.

[Y2V7] - The main question that could be asked is “How is Yagami being able to decipher the anagram and fall into Ayanokoji’s trap a feat towards him?” Well, the Love Letter could only work if Yagami was smart enough to even deduce that it was an anagram for a meeting to confront Ayanokoji. It doesn’t work if Yagami’s analysis skills were too low. Effectively, Yagami taking the bait by the Love Letter only proves that he’s a genius surpassing most people in ANHS that Ayanokoji has to create a trap that works on how smart he is; it was a perfect strategy that only a genius could be ensnared by.

[Y2V7] - Additionally, Ayanokoji had to use nearly every inﬂuential connection he had to expel Yagami. Remember when people would say that the only way that Ayanokoji could get expelled was if everyone used their collective efforts to expel him? That's what happened to Yagami; everyone from the ANHS adults to Ryuuen, Nagumo, Horikita, Ibuki, Tsubaki, Utomiya, and Ayanokoji had to get involved to unanimously expel Yagami. No one else had that many odds stacked against them, and the fact that Ayanokoji had to use these measures to ensure Yagami's expulsion only proves how dangerous the guy is. It shouldn't be something that he gets clowned on for, but rather, an inevitability of circumstance. The whole school had to gang up on him so that he could get expelled. Ayanokoji even admitted later on that he wouldn’t be able to expel Yagami had he not make the mistake of pushing students off cliffs during the Uninhabited Island Exam. Yagami
made one mistake and it was all over, but it wasn’t because he was “lmao get rekted lvl 4 WR trash” by everyone; it was a collective effort to defeat a common evil.

[Y2V7] - While getting ganged up on everyone, Yagami eventually snaps and goes on a rampage, declaring that from this point on, he would go all out to the point that even killing Ayanokoji isn’t out of the question. At this moment, Yagami appears to be using his Full Power. He immediately blitzes and one-shots Ibuki which knocks out her out cold; reminder that Ibuki has a strong argument to be considered the second strongest female character in COTE gets obliterated in less than a second. He then is about to make a victim out of Ryuuen, and had Mashima not intervened, he would have gotten a beatdown far worse than what Housen ever did to him. Nevertheless, he kicks Mashima instead in the knee then punches him in the face to knock him out.

[Y2V7] A teary-faced Ichika tells Yagami to stop his rampage to which he simply tells her to get out of the way. She once again reiterates like in Y2V4.5 that there’s no way she could stop him by force, but if it’s to protect Koji, she would try. Yagami falters for a moment before going in to kill Ichika, showing that he is absolutely capable of killing others if pushed to it.

Yes, had the instructors not shown up at the right time, then it’s very likely that Yagami would have killed everyone in the room including Ichika if they were determined to stop him.

Summary—

I mean, if you still think Takuya is a Level 4 Fodder Expelled Clowngami that could get nae-naed on by people like Nagumo, Arisu, and Kiryuuin, then I don’t think my conclusion will change your perception of him any more. Otherwise, in conclusion…

Yagami Takuya, the Masterpiece of the 5th Generation, stands high and tall one of the strongest overall characters in Classroom of Elite when you take into account the narrative, feats, and statements. Born in the 5th Generation WR with a difﬁculty level of four, he was able to amazingly surpass and excelled in all parts of the curriculum, given the title of the “5th Generation’s Masterpiece.” The 5th Generation added interpersonal and communication skills, which is the foundation for his Manipulation skills that are arguably the best in the verse. Despite many people downplaying his WR achievements because the difﬁculty level of the 5th Generation was stated to be Level 4, the fact that he was chosen and tasked to be the one to expel Ayanokoji with great trust from Tsukishiro even though there were “higher level students” in the room, his feats, and his statements indicate that Yagami’s Full Potential is not Level 4, but more akin to Level 6 or 7. Given his experience, physicality, social skills, manipulation, and other intangibles — he’s probably than someone like Shiro overall. Even if you don’t want to put him at number two even with his feats, he’s undisputably Top 3 in COTE.
His strongest points are Strategy, Prediction, Deception, Processing Speed (PSI), Working Memory (WMI), Manipulation, Methods, and Physicals.

It’s kind of crazy that in little more than a few months, Yagami was able to accomplish more than any other student ANHS ever did as a ﬁrst year. He completely controlled all classes from 1-D to 1-B, manipulated everyone in the Uninhabited Island Exam, outsmarted Koji in the Unanimous Voting Exam, and needed students as well as faculty to gang up on him to ﬁnally get expelled.
There’s no shadow of a doubt that if he stayed until the end of the year, he would have been able to enact the same level of control on the First Years that Nagumo has on the entire Third Years. His Strategies and Methods are easily on par with Nagumo’s in terms of how deceptive and effective they are in completely destroying his opponent. Added on with his manipulation, I think his overall strategies are the second best, but one thing that pushes Koji above is his meticulousness. While Yagami is more decisive, countermeasured, and deceptive with his plans — Koji’s strategies do not have any mistakes.

Ultimately, Yagami Takuya is an excellent example of what the WR after the 4th Generation has to offer. Behind the mask of an angel shows a monster, hellbent on proving that he can surpass the Masterpiece of the 4th Generation.
