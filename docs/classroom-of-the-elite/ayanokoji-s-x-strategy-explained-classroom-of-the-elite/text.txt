X Strategy

This is a description explaining what the X Strategy is. I made this for any new Koji scalers because the original analysis of the X Strategy had bad grammar didn’t understand the end goal and had bad readability. I might do a description for Love Letter next.

Ayanokoji deliberately and anonymously arranges the indirect bullying of Kei through texting Manabe on a different phone than his own, since he knew Manabe’s contact number, involving Rika, so they could get revenge on her in the Ship Basement where they won’t be discovered.

He did this after originally seeing Kei get confronted by Manabe and the others for allegedly shoving Rika in the cafeteria. He intentionally used the Indirect bullying method afterwards to make Kei emotionally vulnerable, then revealing the blackmail against Manabe—so Kei joins him out of desperation for revenge because of the bullying he had secretly created to make her join him.

But he also did this to frame two other people as the X mastermind Ryūen was looking for.

The first person is Yukimura, because he witnessed Manabe’s initial attack on Kei. He could have felt sorry for Kei, searched for Manabe’s phone contact through other students, and arranged for Manabe to do it again with a different phone while he was secretly recording for revenge blackmail. Also because he has high intelligence like the X mastermind.

The second is Hirata, because he knew Kei’s past, had Manabe’s number due to his popularity, and wanted to keep her bullies away from her—which he would have done if he had Manabe expelled through the blackmail. Also because he has high leadership like the X mastermind.

And Koji took it a step further. Way before the indirect bullying he eliminated the possibility that he was the X mastermind by deliberately acting incompetent around Ryūen‘s spy Ibuki during their time on the First Island Exam, so she would report back to Ryūen that he was too brainless to be the X mastermind.

He also, before this, framed Suzune as the X mastermind in the X mastermind’s first showing during the Sudō Incident, since she had been the one to set the false camera up. However, Ryūen confronted her multiple times afterwards to confirm that she wasn’t the X mastermind, so Koji had to switch to different targets.

And the final step of this strategy was that he didn’t do the indirect bullying just so Kei would join him; the framing had another purpose beyond just hiding his intelligence. He wanted to ensure that, even after the indirect bullying, Kei would fully depend on him. He accomplished this by using the indirect bullying so Ryūen would realise that the X mastermind and Kei were close, leading Ryūen to kidnap and abuse her in order to lure the X mastermind out. Koji beforehand ended his and Kei’s agreement to spark the feeling of abandonment in her, then waited for the precise moment—when Kei had nearly given up hope from the abuse—before saving her, making her entirely dependent on him.
