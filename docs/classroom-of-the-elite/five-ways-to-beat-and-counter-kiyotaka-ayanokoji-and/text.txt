Five ways to beat & counter  kiyotaka ayanokoji and characters who can

Kiyotaka  Ayanokoji has been one of the poster boys of overall SCD and edits. Whenever a new mid- or high-tier character comes onto the scene or gets introduced, bet there’s going to be an edit or a question like “X vs. Ayanokoji.”

His character is pretty good in both normal and “meth” SCD. Likewise, he can be used in various XYZ situations because of how versatile his kit is and how few weaknesses he has, while still not completely making every matchup one-sided.

However, I’ve seen people saying that beating Ayanokoji is impossible, while others claim that beating him is easy. Neither side really provides a good explanation to support their statements that gives idea of what's difficulty of beating ayanokoji  in first place and win condition etc.

So I’m gonna explain five win cons / ways to counter & beat Ayanokoji, since I have re-scaled his eval in my roster.

good to best

# 1. Seeing through the mask

While Ayanokoji’s ability to construct & fabricate a layered persona is crazy, it’s not completely immune to being perceived, as the series has shown multiple times where a few classmates, mainly Hirata, are able to sense that there’s something odd or wrong about Ayanokoji that they can’t quite put their finger on.

The series tells us about “darkness.” Before you call it “corny” or whatever, it’s a storytelling thing. Basically, it’s about the repressed self / scared self / shadow.

The first time the “darkness” term was used was in V3, where Ayanokoji was “masked” and we saw the self that he hides from everyone, including the reader. That is also a storytelling thing.

The second thing is in V4, when the story shows that he and Kei are similar in terms of having traumatic pasts & concealing their true selves, with the “scar” being mentioned as well.

Later in the series, we see Hirata also noticing Ayanokoji’s “shadow,” and then the story showcases Hirata's backstory. It also follows the same pattern of the scar(not literally this time), repressed self, shadow, etc.

Basically, the “darkness” in people in the story is what happens when something highly traumatic happens to a person and disturbs their normalcy forever after surviving it. And those who have had their normalcy disturbed can sense other people whose normalcy has also been disturbed.

So, a smart character with calibre comparable to Ayanokoji, especially someone with a highly disturbed past themselves, can sense Ayanokoji’s oddness and come up with measures to counter & beat him. However, they must be careful since they could fall into the pitfall of thinking they understand his dark nature underneath, like Ryuen and others.

A character who can pull this off and counter & beat Ayanokoji: **Patrick Jane.** *(Dead wife + good kit which specializes in STP.)*

# 2. Non-confrontational or direct style

A smart character who utilizes proxies, red herrings, and ploys in their schemes can counter Ayanokoji’s kit.

Ayanokoji’s approach is essentially: “I’m trying to understand my past, or should I say this person’s past, by thoroughly analyzing and breaking down people.”

This is how Ayanokoji’s ToM and psychoanalysis work. We saw this with Hirata, Ryuen, Ichinose, Nagumo, Arisu, Ichika, and Takuya.

A non-confrontational or indirect style counters Ayanokoji. However, it requires really good acting skills if it’s face-to-face, or strong concealment if the character is operating indirectly.

Example: **Johan in the Nameless Monster case situation.**

# 3. INFO advantage

A character whose kit revolves around an information network and spying would counter Koji’s outsmarting, as gaining information is the weakness of any character, and having established spying against him is especially bad.

Basically, the goal is to stay one step ahead at all times, since Ayanokoji is actually reliant & great on knowledge acquisition and application. However, the SC must not reveal or leak their hand during this approach.

Character: **Izaya Orihara from Durarara!!**

# 4.  Theory of the mind & Psychoanalysis

*"Ayanokoji is the most defective student in class D"*

One thing Chabashira got right about Ayanokoji is this. However, she doesn’t know the flaw, while as readers, we are repeatedly given hints about it: **his self-centeredness.**

Ayanokoji Kiyotaka’s self-centeredness is the prime driving force behind everything he does. It’s his maze and castle. Many of his actions and counteractions are taken because of his self-interest in things—his curiosity, self-centeredness, and contradictions.

*Your methods don’t matter. I don’t care what I have to sacrifice. As long as I have my victory in the end, I’ll be fine.*

If an SC has great ToM / psychoanalysis, they could figure out how Ayanokoji works and thinks, how he exploits people, and how his operations target them. From there, they could completely counter and beat him.

However, the SC needs to build a really accurate model of Ayanokoji’s mind without Ayanokoji realizing that he is being studied.

Example: **FY (Vol. 1 & 2).**

# 5. Quick Disposition, Anticipation, and Participation

One of the main factors that makes Ayanokoji a formidable opponent is his adaptability and thinking combination.

So, an SC who has strong anticipation, participation skills, and quick offensive skills is one of the best counters to beating Ayanokoji completely without giving him any time to adapt.

The SC should be able to construct schemes on the fly against Ayanokoji and improve them if Ayanokoji notices them.

Examples: **Chen ran (Inception instance) & Aki.**

# Verdict

These are five ways/counters to Kiyotaka Ayanokoji. However, these counters do **not** mean that Ayanokoji is weaker.

Notice the examples I gave and ask yourself: if Kiyotaka were to go against these characters, would that make him weaker, or would it simply show that he is capable of going against characters who possess these specific advantages?

Therefore **“Kiyotaka is impossible to beat”** is an exaggeration, but so is **“Kiyotaka is easy to beat.”** The actual difficulty depends on the opponent’s ability to exploit one of these win conditions *without allowing Ayanokoji enough time or information to adapt to them*.
