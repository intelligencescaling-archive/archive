This is a general outline of the theory, meaning it's not explained in depth (that's someone else's job). The information was obtained from various internet posts (Reddit and Discord) and some YouTube videos (Avent's video and some less relevant ones).

What is this theory about?

This theory was originally made to make TY's role make sense from the perspective of the ANHS destruction theory (which talks about WR's probing of ANHS before making its moves). This theory explains that Takuya actually let himself be expelled by Ayanokoji because he had already fulfilled his objective in ANHS and it was also part of a bigger plan.

Theory

Let's start by answering what many people ask: why would Takuya cause his own expulsion?

Well, this is due to two goals he set for himself:
Objective 1. Analyze the school system to find weaknesses.

In Volume 0, Atsuomi says he's interested in the structure of ANHS and that through that, he could find information about Kijima. This interest not only persists in Volume 0, but is also present in Takuya's intentions. He says he wants to have a solid foundation when it comes to the school system; he wants to conquer it. (“In the 2nd Year arc, when asked about his aspirations for the next year, he answered 'To analyze this school's system and conquer it. That will become the foundation for an order in the first years.”)

Objective 2. Analyze Ayanokoji's emotional growth.

Remember when Atsuomi visited Ayanokoji at school? Well, the conversation implies that the WR staff is interested in Ayanokoji's emotional and social growth. After all, such information is too useful to ignore, as it can provide valuable insight into how the ideal student behaves in human relationships.

—--------------------------------------------------------------------

Now the next thing: actions that Takuya takes to accomplish these goals:

Objective 1:

-Takuya becomes the leader of Class 1-B and negotiations with the other classes begin.
-In Y2V4.5, it is implied that Yagami has been testing the school's operations and how it reacts under certain conditions, such as student expulsions.
-He joins the student council as secretary, giving him access to information that a normal student wouldn't have. (such as the school's historical records since its founding, which allowed him a complete analysis of its structure and evolution.)
Objective 2: Throughout the volumes, Takuya not only tries to interact with Ayanokoji directly, but also uses others to see how Ayanokoji reacts to certain situations and not only that, he also observes the interactions she has with other people (like when he used Kushida to record some conversations between Koji and Nananse). He doesn't just limit himself to his goal, but also tries to understand how Koji affected the growth of other people (like Horikita).

—--------------------------------------------------------------------

So Takuya got expelled intentionally? According to this theory, yes.

Some say that during the island exam, Takuya attacked two students, sidelining them, not only to test the school's regulations, but also to "test" Ayanokoji, and that it's part of his plan for when the time comes for his expulsion. Aside from Takuya's contingency plans, he didn't really have any excuses prepared for when they accuse him of causing this (when questioned about his GPS location at the time of the accident). Aside from the aforementioned, it's said that Takuya caused this accident to involve Ayanokoji and get her attention.

Others say Takuya has listening devices in the student council room (Ichika confirms that Takuya did, in fact, possess these listening devices) and at several key points. This is how he knew about Ayanokoji's plan with Nagumo. That's why Takuya says he knew Nagumo would show up at the time of his expulsion.

Many emphasize that they find it strange that a WR student would act this way upon being expelled. After all, white room students are people who have endured harsh mental and physical training. It's strange that someone from WR would respond this way because of something like this (being expelled from the white room isn't the same as being expelled from ANHS). Do you really think that a human being trained to be perfect would commit acts out of pure rage and, because of that, make serious errors in judgment?
—--------------------------------------------------------------------
His hatred for Ayanokoji was a cover.

For an observer like Ayanokoji, the most logical and expected motivation for a White Room student would be envy and the desire to defeat the masterpiece. By presenting this facade of hatred, Yagami gave Ayanokoji a simple and believable reason for his actions.
This prevented Ayanokoji from further investigating Yagami's true, more complex motives, thus protecting the real mission and keeping Ayanokoji in the dark about what the White Room was really looking for.

—-----------------------------------

But what about the Y2V2 monologue? The explanation for this is that Takuya supposedly isn't telling the truth, that is: he's not a reliable narrator; he lies in his monologues just like Ayanokoji does.
