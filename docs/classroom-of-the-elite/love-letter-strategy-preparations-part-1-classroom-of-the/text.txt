Love letter strategy: preparations part 1

Nagumo's involvement in the Bounty Hunt exam had been a serious nuisance. Ayanokoji understood that the exam could only have been implemented through ***cooperation*** between Nagumo and Tsukishiro, showcasing the ***lengths*** Nagumo could go to. Nagumo would likely continue to involve himself until he was satisfied.

Leaving the situation ***unaddressed*** carried real risk. Nagumo held the influence and ability to ***disrupt*** the steady progress of the CTS plan. He had sway over Ichinose, an iron-clad dominance over his year, abilities that exceeded those of the class leaders, and information that could destroy the balance Ayanokoji had maintained between the classes for over a year. Nagumo would strike the moment he found an opportunity. Had he not been so fixated on a competition with Ayanokoji, he could have ***dismantled*** everything Ayanokoji had built with little effort.

When the time for negotiations came, Ayanokoji ***sensed*** a lingering spark in Nagumo's eyes despite his claims that he no longer cared. Ayanokoji therefore chose to turn the situation to his ***advantage by drawing*** Nagumo into the love letter scheme. He offered the competition Nagumo had wanted from the beginning, in exchange for a simple request: that Nagumo act ***unapproachable*** at the next student council meeting. He would later raise the stakes and make the offer feel genuine, Ayanokoji also offered his own expulsion should he fail. This doubled as an ***opportunity*** to earn private points, with the stakes serving as a ***disguise***. Ayanokoji had been conditioned by the White Room to seize every ***opportunity*** presented to him, and he did so here.

The negotiation was not without its twists. Nagumo was genuinely acting in an intimidating way. He refused the competition with Ryueen, who had ***challenged*** him with expulsion of anyone from his class, including himself, as a wager against ten million points. But he claimed that he would have ***accepted*** the challenge a year ago, since he would have found an expulsion, even one from another year, ***amusing***. He was ***flaunting*** his ruthlessness, trying to intimidate.

Ayanokoji ***sees through the act*** and remains unmoved, claiming that other students from his year must be displeased. Nagumo replies that nobody can complain, since doing so would cost them their ***chance*** at graduating from Class A. He does not stop his intimidating tactics.

Ayanokoji comments on his ***thoughtful*** strategy, to which Nagumo gives a non-committal reply and asks why he came here. Ayanokoji says he just wanted to talk, which Nagumo finds hard to believe. Ayanokoji replies that he is surprised too, since he was completely uninterested in Nagumo at first, and asks whether Nagumo knows why. This ***subtly drives the conversation.***

Nagumo replies that he doesn't know, but it certainly wasn't because Ayanokoji was ***afraid*** of his ***abilities***. Ayanokoji replies that part of the reason he didn't want to involve himself was Nagumo's flashy nature, the complete opposite of the previous student council president, Manabu. Nagumo probes further, saying that was only the surface-level reason.

Ayanokoji replies that he simply wasn't interested, ***striking directly to get a reaction.*** Nagumo says he would have been slightly ***offended*** if anyone else had said that, continuing his act. Ayanokoji then ***quickly changes*** his tone, claiming no offense. This is a subtle manipulative tactic: ***it gets Nagumo to take the initiative, to maintain the false sense of balance in the conversation.***

As expected, Nagumo brushes it off and claims he would have made Ayanokoji ***change his mind*** if he had been ***someone else***, taking the bait. Ayanokoji then says Nagumo's term as student council president is coming to an end, something he hadn't been bothered about until a ***few days ago.*** Nagumo probes whether Ayanokoji has had a change in heart, and Ayanokoji affirms it. Ayanokoji knew this was the perfect moment to get to the point, and he proposes a challenge. Nagumo refuses, claiming this ***isn't like him*** and he doesn't like it, unconvinced. Ayanokoji had already ***braced*** himself for this.

Ayanokoji replies that all this sounds convenient, and Nagumo agrees, since he had thrown out the three other opportunities given to him, and since he doesn't believe Ayanokoji was truly sick during the sports festival. Nagumo also reminds him of the island exam. Ayanokoji asks whether he wants to reenact it with the positions reversed, expecting nothing. Nagumo scoffs that a single punch won't make up for it and says he isn't worth anything. Nagumo already ***understands*** that Ayanokoji has a hidden reason behind the challenge, and, not wanting to play by his rules, refuses the offer. He claims he wants to ***shelve*** the discussion, since he knows he won't gain any intel from him, and continues the intimidation. He adds that he is the one allowed to challenge Ayanokoji, not the other way round.

Ayanokoji subtly drives the conversation in the ***desired direction***. He replies that he understands, and that is exactly why Nagumo is trying to shelve it here. He reminds Nagumo that the contest he was waiting for is right here, and asks whether he isn't satisfied with that. Ayanokoji ***tempts*** Nagumo ***openly***, since he knows there is little point in trying to convince Nagumo logically. He ***observes*** that Nagumo is being highly wary this time, unlike his earlier ***defensive, provocative*** demeanor. ***Even if Nagumo is unaware of it, his attitude proves he already sees Ayanokoji as a threat.***

Nagumo claims Ayanokoji isn't ***intimidated*** by him at all, even Manabu. Ayanokoji knows Nagumo holds Manabu in high regard and has an intense desire to compete with him because of his abilities. Nagumo can no longer fulfill that desire, and the only one who could potentially substitute for Manabu is Ayanokoji, which he exploits. Nagumo finally concedes and agrees to compete, but they don't have much time left, and he doesn't know whether there will be an inter-year exam in the brief time remaining.

Ayanokoji agrees that a year-wide competition is hard to arrange, as the battle between Nagumo and Manabu showed. He explains how the competition can become a reality even without an exam that allows for it, using Ichinose. Ayanokoji had already ***foreseen*** that Nagumo could use her against him, and he used this as a setup.

Nagumo is surprised by Ayanokoji's plan to exploit Ichinose. He approves of it, but probes for Ayanokoji's true objective. Ayanokoji is pleased with Nagumo's sharpness, since it saved him the bother. He then states his other objective: having Nagumo act ***unapproachable*** at the next student council meeting, to keep anyone from ***approaching him.***

Nagumo concludes that the competition must have been a ploy to get him to agree to the request. Ayanokoji replies that he is only ***half*** ***correct******:*** he truly came to challenge Nagumo, but the other half finds the request a bother. Nagumo calls the demands ridiculous for a mere challenger and asks whether Ayanokoji really believed he would agree.

Ayanokoji takes advantage of the ***direction of the conversation,*** claiming that agreeing would be the end of it and that he won't fight Nagumo even if Nagumo uses someone from his grade or takes a hostage. Nagumo believes him, but asks whether Ayanokoji would do the same even if the hostage were Kei, Ayanokoji's girlfriend. Without hesitation, Ayanokoji replies that it doesn't matter, completely unmoved, and Nagumo's smile disappears. Nagumo almost says it didn't seem like Ayanokoji was lying, then corrects himself, claiming his bluffs won't work on him. ***For an instant, though, he seemed to believe Ayanokoji, because of his unmoving, undisturbed, and unhesitating act.***

Ayanokoji ***admits*** he can't watch over all his classmates, even the ones closest to him, 24 hours a day, but claims that their disappearance will never ***convince*** him to compete. Ayanokoji isn't bargaining here; he is sincere, and even Nagumo can't deny it. Nagumo says he would be in trouble if he didn't lend a hand in the scheme he was coming up with. Ayanokoji replies that he already has another plan if this one doesn't get through. It was true: ***Ayanokoji stuck with the love letter plan, since it allowed for the most efficient and beneficial outcome.***

In the end, Nagumo accepts both the request and the challenge, since he is assured of graduating as Class A and ending past matters would be more satisfying. He asks whether Ayanokoji is okay with his decision, since he could ***hurt*** people close to him. Ayanokoji says it's fine, since Nagumo would have been ***involved anyway.*** Nagumo is shocked that Ayanokoji figured out his ***plan***. Ayanokoji replies that, even though they were a distance apart, he had a ***sense of Nagumo's plan for the future.*** (This conversation is rather vague, but it's safe to assume they are discussing second-year inter-class politics, with Ichinose as the main lead.) Ayanokoji had ***predicted*** that Nagumo's focus on him wouldn't disappear easily, so he decided to ***strike first.***

Nagumo asks whether Ichinose is the same. Ayanokoji replies that it doesn't matter whether it's Kei, Horikita, or Ichinose: none of them would move him. Nagumo finally ***acknowledges*** that he is indeed the person Manabu acknowledged, saying so with a serious expression. But Ayanokoji's reaction to his threat still disturbs him, and he asks whether Ayanokoji ***truly doesn't care about them.*** Ayanokoji replies that he would prefer none of his classmates were expelled, and that he would resist such a notion. Nagumo says that isn't an answer and asks about Ayanokoji's true feelings if someone special to him, like Kei, were expelled.

Ayanokoji knows what a ***normal*** response would be, but he is dealing with Nagumo and needs to thoroughly ***shape*** Nagumo's view of his ***persona***. So he replies that expulsion would be the end, nothing more, nothing less, and even claims it would make things more convenient for him.

Nagumo replies that he has some screws loose with that mindset. Ayanokoji observes that Nagumo is now truly shaken, or at least can't understand him. Ayanokoji then exits the room, saying he will contact him later.

What This Single Preparation Achieved:

This section of the love letter preparation is rather underlooked. Here Ayanokoji showed his capabilities to steer a conversation, predict reactions, read Nagumo's thoughts, and manipulate his views, showcasing social ability, perception, insight, deception, and more. He adapted to Nagumo's attitude, actively employed tactics to steer the conversation, and blocked every opening Nagumo could exploit.

From this single preparation alone, Ayanokoji:

1. Convinced Nagumo to take part in the love letter scheme.

2. Prevented Nagumo from interfering with second-year politics.

3. Used Nagumo as a pawn to develop Ichinose, the most underdeveloped class leader among the second-years.

4. Gained the opportunity to earn private points for a class transfer.

5. Used the competition as a disguise to earn more private points in the exam, without giving Nagumo a chance to figure out the transfer plan.

Overall, Ayanokoji made the best use of his resources, achieving multiple goals in one strike, and the achievement is even greater when paired with the execution of the love letter strategy.
