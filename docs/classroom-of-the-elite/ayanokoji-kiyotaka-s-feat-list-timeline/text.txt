Going by the given assertion, referring to the status Naoe once held as Minister of Transportation, we are able to extract a relevant time pointer using real life events as reference, as established by B).

This means that we can canonically assert that the now old Naoe has held this position before the year 2001, January => Last year of the post’s existence under that name is 2000.

We also have confirmation that by the time this interaction between Naoe and Atsuomi takes place, where the monologue is given, is after 2000, since the Ministry Land, Infrastructure, Transport, and Tourism (国土交通省, Kokudo-Kōtsū-shō) exists. Asama Hisashi is a Naoe underlying occupying the position of Vice-Minister. Additionally, we are also given Naoe’s age at the time - 68 years old:

Asama Hisashi. He is 71 years old—three years older than Naoe-sensei. He is currently serving as a vice minister of the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport, and Tourism, and is a leading figure in the Naoe faction.

Raw Japanese Text:
浅間久。年齢は直江先生よりも三つ上の七十一歳。 現在、国土交通省の副大臣を務めている直江派閥の重鎮だ。

As a result, we can estimate the absolute highest year value these reference statements take place in and find an upper ceiling for all events in Classroom of the Elite.

In Japan, the theoretical minimum age possible to become a Minister is 25 years of age. Even in the COTE universe, we have a similar example with Kijima becoming a powerful politician fresh out of University, launching ANHS soon forward, so we can conclude that this is realistic.
Assuming that Naoe occupied the function of Minister of Transportation at 25 years of age around the time it was replaced, meaning ~2000, we can infer that the latest date Naoe could have been born in for the given statements to remain valid is 1975.

2000 - 25 = 1975

Since we know that Naoe was 68 years of age during V0 Chapter 1, we can infer 43 years have passed since he held the position under this assumption.

68 - 25 = 43

We can now add this number of passed years to 2000 (the position for Naoe occupying the post) and we get that the latest time V0 Chapter 1 can take place is 2044.

2000 + 43 = 2043

Using the information from point C), we can reach the conclusion that the latest year in which Y1V1 can take place is 2064.

2043 + ~21 = 2064

=> According to A) and D), the highest valid year value for COTE Y1V1 is 2064.

This value remains consistent with point A), ensuring the statements are not contradictory.
Because ANHS was established 29 years before Y1V1, it means that the difference in years between ANHS’s establishment and Atsuomi’s thoughts is 29 - ~20 = ~9 years.

To find the highest year value for ANHS’s establishment we simply subtract 9 years from 2043, or subtract 21+9=30 years from 2064.

2043 - 9 = 2034
2064 - 30 = 2034

In order to find the years where those datapoints are possible, a python program that evaluated a year and then checked the weekdays of the dates in the datapoints was used.

Initially, this didn’t succeed because all it takes is one wrong datapoint to create a logical impossibility. It was then modified to look for possible years but only on a subset of dates. This was to identify the “bad” datapoints and investigate. For that, year candidate matches were looked into for all possible subsets of size eight to nine. Only one subset of 8 datapoints yielded several candidate years. Meaning those 8 datapoints were internally consistent and 2 are incorrect.
2 outliers excluded:
If February 11 is a Friday, then March 2 can only be a Wednesday in a common year or a Thursday in a leap year. It can never be a Tuesday like it was stated in Y1V10. Because the March 2 datapoint is internally consistent with the events that follow, the datapoint “Year N+1 Feb 11 = Friday” contradicts that “Year N+1 March 2 = Tuesday” and must be rejected. Similarly, the August 29th datapoint is impossible with any other datapoint. Thus it was also omitted.

All other datapoints are internally consistent with each other. Additionally, there have been no more that cause contradictions from Y1V10 all the way to Y2V12.5, potentially suggesting that some time after the release of Y1V10, the author has “fixed” in a precise timeline for COTE which he uses to guide himself on the inverse time progression per volume. While this is speculation, the chances of the data points accidentally being consistent with one another for such an extended period of time (Y1V10-Y2V2.5) are extremely low.
Another suggestive argument regarding the author eventually fixing in the timeline some time in the 2nd half of Y1 is the use of short stories such as H) and introduction of concrete timeline references such as A) in Y2V1, as those would appear shortly after such a decision.

After running the program for years 2000 - 2200 with coherent values, the following calendar years are possible: Matching years N: [2003, 2031, 2059, 2087, 2127, 2155, 2183]

Because 2003, 2031, 2087, 2127, 2155 and 2183 are automatically invalidated by direct contradiction with the established A) & D) year statements, 1 date remains:

N = {2059}

Conclusion:
2059 is the only year that matches both the elaborated arguments from A) to H) and the estimation using the data points, making it the single most plausible year to assert that COTE takes place in.

Reminder - This year is just more probable than others since it logically fits the internal datapoint structure of our own world. This is by no means an assertion that N = 2059 and it is set in stone.

After what has been established, it is self-evident that COTE does not take place in 2015.
However, the opening monologue of the series presents the timeline as the aforementioned year, the same of when Y1V1 has been published:

I know this is kind of sudden, but, please, it will only take a moment. I want your honest opinion.
[...]
So then, education creates an imbalance. The point’s spelled out there, in the incredibly famous work Gakumon no Sume. Even though this is the year 2015, the modern era, nothing about these teachings has changed. The situation has only grown more complex and fraught.

Raw Japanese Text:
突然だが、ちょっとだけオレの出す問題を真剣に聞いて、答えを考えてみて欲しい。
[...]
差が生まれるのは、学問に励んだのか励まなかったのか。
そこに違いが生じてくる、と綴つづってある。それが有名すぎる『学問のすゝめ』だ。

Since we know taking it in-verse as literal is not the truth, we can interpret alternate meanings to Kiyotaka’s monologue, especially with it being the only instance Kiyotaka breaks the 4th wall and addresses the reader directly.

This is interpretative, and regardless of how it is approached, including the belief that no narrative intervention/device is used, it merely remains a retconned piece of information which has been discarded for a different truth, the one established above.

B) Y1V11.5 - Chapter 5

Another aspect worth mentioning is the assertion that in the current time-period of Kiyotaka’s school life, the world’s population is 7 billion.

“There are definitely people who are even more excellent than I am in this
world. There are as many as 7 billion humans living on the planet, after all.
But the White Room is different.”

Raw Japanese Text:
「オレよりも優れた人間は、当然この世界に存在するでしょう。何故なら世界には70億にも達するほどの人間が生きているんですから。ですが、ことホワイトルームにおいては違う」

While matching the year 2015 for the world population, it does not aid in justifying COTE happening in the said year. Instead, it can merely be explained by the projected world population decline happening later in this century, which for futuristic reference needs to be accounted for.
This decline is expected to start after 2053, after a peak of 8.9 billion, according to United Nations estimates. The number of 7 billion aligns with the expectations of the world population around the year 2100, as low-end UN estimates claim that it will shrink below 8 billion below 2100, and mid-end calls that it will do so during the 22nd century. While this projection would be a more extreme form given the timeline of COTE being centered around the 2nd half of the 21st century, it can be justified in such a manner.
