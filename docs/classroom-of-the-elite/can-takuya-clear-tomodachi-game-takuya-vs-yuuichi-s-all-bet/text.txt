Can Takuya clear Tomodachi Game?

For me it depends.. if he's in Yuuichi's place then he clears the TG.
But if he fight Yuuichi he's not clearing the all bet arc. Doubt he can anticipate the Yuuichi's "special method".

Even a gambler with superhuman intuition/luck like Satone(basically Yumeko with better intuition/luck) didn't saw that coming.

What is Takuya gonna do during the coin toss game in the end of the all bet arc? Believe it or not Yuuichi's chance of losing during that coin toss game was 0% whether he win or not. It's like if he lose the battle he win the war, and if he win the battle he win the war too but with less gain.

Yuuichi was unbeatable in that coin toss game even though gambling wasn't his speciality and even though his opponent was a pro gambler with 100% winrate and superhuman intuition/luck. It's all because of the special method I'm talking about. If you're confused you should read TG manga then you will understand that special method Yuuichi used. He had everyone checkmated since the beginning of the arc and his acting was good that nobody knew he lost on purpose during the first game so that he could use his special method.

To beat Yuuichi, Takuya first need to see through Yuuichi's acting and this isn't gonna be easy. Secondly Takuya need to have a knowledge about the hedge bet strategy (which is the special method Yuuichi used) and lastly he need to gather more information about Yuuichi so that he can anticipate the special method Yuuichi planned to use (which is very difficult because all of Yuuichi's opponents in this arc have never met him before.. and Takuya will be in their place in this arc)

Better FSIQ statement doesn't mean he can clear the all bet arc. What he need is a good anticipation feat. Now tell me what is Takuya's best anticipation feat? And explain why do you think that feat justify the claim that he can anticipate the special method in the all bet arc. By the way, if he can't anticipate the Yuuichi's special method he will never clear the all bet arc.

Yuuichi is a mid tier SCD character sure but this is an arc where even smarter characters would have hard time to beat Yuuichi due to massive advantage he has in this arc. What I like about this arc is he didn't have that advantage in the beginning. If anything he was in disadvantage because he's not a good gambler but he turned that disadvantage into a massive advantage by losing the first game fooling everyone into thinking he would be the first one to lose when in reality he was planning to use the special method in the last game in which he has 0% chance of losing.

It takes a character with insane anticipation feat to clear this arc and Takuya definitely doesn't have any good anticipation feat that can justify the claim that he can anticipate the Yuuichi's special method. This is exactly why I said having better FSIQ statement alone isn't enough.. so Takuya is not clearing this arc.
