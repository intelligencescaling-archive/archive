**Thing to Note:**

To properly evaluate a feat you have to eliminate cognitive bias.

If you went in thinking (even subconsciously): "The feat has a flaw." you will find issue with things that don't even have any.

This logic also applies if you think, "Is this feat valid/impressive?" Because you are now subconsciously hoping it is and that will muddle your perception.

The right mindset to have going into a story is: "What *is* the feat? Does the narrative support it?"

In this case, you'll be looking for evidence from both the narrative context and the known/showcased abilities of the characters.

Why?

You're now searching for the feat first -> then checking if your interpretation is *consistent* with the established narrative -> then deciding the overall level of the feat.

If you are proven wrong in your conclusions then look from a different angle. Don't stubbornly stick to your own interpretation. It will lead you nowhere.
