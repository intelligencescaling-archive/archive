6. - A huge picture hanged on the fridge was that of a blue fruit (berry?), There’s a blue flower in a vase on her kitchen table, and blue is generally the most popular color between men and women. Jane inferred from this that blue was most definitely her favorite color.

Jane, after also revealing some slight inferences about her husband, asks why she suspects him of being the murderer; and while she denies it at first, she soon after stammers and slowly reveals that they (her daughter and husband) have been so ‘strange’ with each other, and that neither would admit anything is wrong.
