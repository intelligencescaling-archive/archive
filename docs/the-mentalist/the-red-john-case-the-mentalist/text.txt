The Red John case

So in this post, I will be explaining the Red John case without interjecting my beliefs on it as much as possible. I will list the opportunities that are presented to solve the case & then in short the win con with each season acting as a layer.

Pre-requisite:

The character having to interest RJ & him having to consider them a possible successor for the character for them to even start, of course they also need the sufficient AC to not break down & join RJ.

Context: Red John has been one of the most prolific serial killers in California though of course it ran much deeper than that single state & had catastrophic outcomes by the end of it & Red John himself being the most elusive killer that this verse has had to deal with. S1-4 is the recruitment stage(ROTR a.k.a Recruitment Of The Rabbit) & S5-6 is the getaway stage(DOTT a.k.a Death Of The Tiger).

Season 1:

During S1, the opportunities to solve the case arise from Jared Renfrew who claims to have been falsely accused of being a murderer & that if his case is solved, he will tell the detective the name of Red John. So this is the first challenge that our character replacing Jane is faced with that being solving the murder & getting Jared out of prison however the problem lies in the fact that Jared had already planned to escape earlier on after being freed & of course eventually being killed by Red John.

Then in the S1 finale. RJ strikes again killing a girl & kidnapping her sister with the help of his accomplice Dumar Tanner. Here another opportunity is presented where if they're able to solve the case then they'll obtain Dumar however considering his maniacal attitude & worship of RJ. It's most likely that he'd kill himself or RJ will arrange for him to be killed.

Win-cons: Secure Jared Renfrew and/or Dumar Tanner.

Season 2:

In S2, Red John appears in S2 EP8 when Bosco & his team have discovered Carter Peak's body which has some DNA of RJ's due to a fight that broke out between the 2. We learn that he had already had Rebecca Anderson join Bosco's team long before Bosco got the case so that she could inform him when the body is discovered, get rid of it & then get rid of Bosco's team. Finally RJ or one of his accomplices eliminates Rebecca & she was madly obsessed with RJ that she claimed that she'll never leak any info.

Next, RJ appears in the season finale once more here he doesn't do much other than kill the reporter who interviewed Kristina & then kidnapped the latter. While also killing the copycat RJ killers & saving the character replacing Jane.

Win-cons: Secure Rebecca Anderson & convince her to give up RJ.

Season 3:

In S3 EP3, Stiles gives the location of Kristina Frye who is then found mentally broken & in a state of believing herself to be dead.

At S3, specifically S3 EP9. A seemingly unrelated serial killer is revealed to be a disciple of Red John & is burned alive in the CBI headquarters tipping off the fact that there's a mole.

Throughout the season, Internal Affairs agent J.J. Laroche comes up with a list of possible suspects for who the mole could be & RJ has his mole frame Hightower who's also on the list of suspects in S3 EP16.

This culminates in the two-parter finale. The first is solving the bombing orchestrated by Anthony Gupta who is revealed to have been working for RJ & then narrowing down who the actual mole is with the possibility that J.J. himself could be the mole. Finally the mole has to be neutralized(Considering that said mole also died happily, it's possible that they might've killed themselves if arrested) resulting in the face-off against "Red John"

In order to tailor the scenario to who Red John is up against he very well might kill a close one of the character(For example: If it's Akiyama, he'd kill Nao) & tell the poser to induce rage within the detective resulting in Carter's death.

Win-cons: Try to cure Kristina, solving the identity of the mole & apprehending them while making sure they aren't neutralized & confronting "Red John".

Season 4:

Now in S4, our character has to find a way to obtain bail & investigate & expose both Timothy Carter & his wife, Sally Carter for the kidnapping of Debbie Lubin.

Then they'd need to reassemble the team in the next episode, this may seem optional initially but it's important since it could easily mean out character would end up being paired with BA members & RJ followers.

In S4 EP7, they'd need to set up the Fake Depression plan like Jane did via baiting Panzer into insulting RJ since RJ will view this as a hand in friendship.

Throughout the season they'd need to falsify their insanity in order to fool RJ. Then in the finale come in contact with Lorelai Martins however to get further they need sufficient the Social Intelligence.

Finally, they need to have apprehended Lorelai & if also possible her enforcer.

Win-cons: Apprehended Lorelai Martins & if piss her enforcer.

Loss: Both are eliminated. Leads terminated & case failed.

End of Recruitment.

Season 5:

During S5, the character has to have the sufficient SI in order to get Lorelai & pull the favor from Stiles. Then also have the sufficient memory, ToM, EI & Reasoning in order to narrow down the RJ suspects. Assuming the enforcer is alive, he'd most likely be killed by the BA.

In S5 EP13, the red barn case is discovered & it has to be solved in order for progress on the RJ case. The character has to be lucky enough to come across it.

In S5 EP16. Jason Lennon a conspirator of RJ has been arrested however upon his death, he seems to have no clue who RJ is, leading to a dead end once more.

Then they have to solve the season finale case though of course Miriam will end it just like in canon.

Win-cons: Have the sufficient SI, ToM, EI, Memory & Reasoning to narrow down the list while also being able to procure a favor from Stiles & be lucky enough to come across the red barn case.

Loss: Lacking in the above mentioned categories, failure to gain help from Stiles & being unlucky enough to avoid the red barn case.

Season 6:

Narrow down the suspect list, figure out why Partridge was killed in S6 EP1.

The mystery surrounding Kirkland in S6 EP4 & motive behind the fake suspects being hunted.

Solve the wedding murder & figure out if McAllister is acrophobic.

Use the tattoo clue in order to gather the suspects & narrow it down further in S6 EP6.

Apprehend Reede Smith or at least be able to clear him as a suspect & see through RJ's logical manipulation & figure what his phobia is in order to set the trap.

Be able to fool the FBI & arrive at the church, trick Oscar, making it past the check & injure McAllister. Eliminate Oscar & be lucky enough for Bertram's corpse to save the character.

Find & kill Sheriff Thomas McAllister also known as Red John.

Win-cons: Seeing through the body swap deception, fooling the FBI, tricking & killing Oscar, being lucky enough to be saved from the would-be fatal stab wound.

Loss: Failure to see through the deception, being captured by the FBI, failing to set up the trap & trick Oscar, failing to kill Oscar & being unlucky enough to be killed by the unnamed "nun".

Result: If failed at the DOTT stage then Thomas McAllister has escaped & fled the country. He will kill again.

Result: If all both stages cleared, The Blake Association has been neutralized.

Red John is dead.
