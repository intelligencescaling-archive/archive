Patrick Jane's Learning Ability

You've probably heard about the golf stunt from episode 20 of season 1 and the double bass stunt from episode 22 of season 3 of The Mentalist. However, this post won't focus on those specific stunts, but rather, they will be mentioned in passing. This post won't be a serious analysis, but I just want to share my opinion and hear what you think about it.

Patrick Jane is a character with some of the most impressive learning abilities. In episode 11 of season 2, it turns out that Patrick never went to high school, but at the same time his knowledge and skills are just incredibly great, he can literally be called a genius in all fields (except technology, forensics and weapons). His knowledge can be called encyclopedic, here is their enumeration: he knows at least two languages at a conversational level, that is, English and Spanish (in episode 9 of season 6, two elderly women said that Jane has a terrible accent, but this is not entirely true, I saw a post by one guy from Spain on this topic, he watched the series is in the original and said that Jane has an accent, but it's not terrible, but rather funny), knows at least partially several other languages, Italian, French and Russian (for example, in episode 3 of season 6, he translated the name of the restaurant from Italian), in episode 10 of season 4, Patrick stated that he remembers the contents of all Shakespeare's works by heart in chronological order, knows the length of all the largest rivers (he did not name the exact number, so it is unclear how many rivers he is talking about) and geological periods, is well acquainted with classical literature ("The Fax Machine Revolution: The Little Box That's Changed Our World" - 1 episode of season 2, "Communism and Development by Robert Bideleux" - 7 episode of season 2, "Jaws by Peter Benchley" - 11 episode of season 4, "Modern Man in Search of a Soul by Carl Gustav Jung" - 24 episode of season 4, "A Tale of Two Cities by Charles Dickens" - 20 episode of season 5, "The Greatest Baseball Stories Ever Told by Jeff Silverman" - 2 episode of Season 4, "Songs of Innocence and of Experience by William Blake" - 1 episode of season 3, "Moby Dick" - Episode 9 of Season 1, "Bleak House" - Episode 15 of season 5 and "The Purpose Driven Life by Rick Warren" - Episode 6 of season 2), knows the Bible very well (I don't remember which episode it is, but Van Pelt started reading a passage from the Bible, and Patrick was able to finish this passage from memory, name the exact chapter and verse), is familiar with classical music, theater and art, is well versed in expensive wine.

Jane's skills are no less incredible: in episode 6 of season 1, he beat professional Blackjack players (as mentioned in the episode itself), while one of them was a cheater who had a deal with a dealer (Patrick earned $2,500,000 from 100 dollars in just a few games), in episode 6. in season 1 and episode 1 of season 4, he showed good poker skills (he earned a million dollars in one day for collateral), plays chess well (he demonstrated this in episode 21 of season 2, it is worth noting that the game was not without flaws, there were problems + we were not shown the whole game, but apparently the authors were trying to repeat the game between Bobby Fischer and Boris Spassky, here's an analysis of the game from the series, if you're interested.: https://drive.google.com/open?id=1NFXRhoqtUY9thibrRx-oiNom4PdM5gC6AbJ2-Nnd-to ), plays golf very well (in episode 20 of season 1, Jane played golf better than professional coaches or at least on the same level with them, there is an opinion that up to this point Patrick did not know how to play golf at all, there is even an analysis on this topic: https://drive.google.com/open?id=1gyXguL7DNY\_P68F7GvW6b3R\_HB9nl1LNQwyf1tfbbOU But I I don't agree with him., Patrick probably knew how to play golf, he just couldn't remember the name "putter", and such carelessness in terms is quite in Jane's repertoire, but he clearly never played at the professional level, so we can conclude that the last time he played golf before the events of the main part of the series + he clearly I was tired from the long journey, so this feat is still very impressive. I also want to mention a moment, I don't remember which episode it was in, and maybe it was even a joke from Patrick, but I want to mention that a woman told Patrick, "You can't be talented at everything," to which he replied, "Yes. I can't swim cross-country and golf," meaning he also thinks he has problems with golf), plays the double bass in episode 22 of season 3, uses a computer (I'll explain why I mentioned this point in episode 7 of season 4. Lisbon, when she saw Jane sitting at her laptop, Just kidding: "Are you sitting at your laptop? What should we expect tomorrow? Laser guns?", but already in episode 22 of season 5, he calmly uses a laptop, I understand that this is not something impressive, but still), knows mathematics well, which was shown in episode 21 of season 3, possesses the skill of hypnosis (Patrick demonstrated this in many series, for example, in episode 2 Season 4, when he hypnotized a CBI agent, or in episode 18 of season 1, it is stated that he hypnotized Minnelli to quit smoking. Let me remind you that not all psychiators have the skill of hypnosis), plays Sudoku well, which was shown in episode 14 of season 2), can solve and compose ciphers, which was shown, for example, in episode 2 of season 4, is a good magician, can pick locks, which was shown in episode 2 Season 6.

And these are not all of Patrick's features, but only those that I could remember. All this knowledge and skills show the versatility of Patrick Jane, while I want to remind you that he did not go to school, he acquired all this through self-study, which cannot fail to impress. So Patrick definitely deserves a high level of learning ability.
