Apology post + Jane's traps in the Marquesan Mansion Poisoning Case

Anyway here's a trap setting feat from Jane.

In S1 EP15, a woman has been poisoned.

Info: The victim(Scarlett) was killed by poison applied on her lipstick, she died due to said poisoning.

A message written on a napkin in lipstick has been found saying "Now we're even". Thus the motive being possibly revenge.

She seemed to be in charge of a jewelry business & was also the chairman of the women's committee. Her office had a safe that only she could open.

There are 6 suspects, Victor(Husband of the victim) & then we have the group of "friends" that she used to be a part of, they are

1. Patience(Previously went against Scarlett in an election for the position of Chairman & lost)

2. Asra(Victor's account manager)

3. Jackie(Vice Chairman of the Women's committee)

4. Mandy(Member of the women's committee)

5. Heather(Member of the country club)

Asra had also run away from the gated community, clearing her accounts & her house as well.

Initially it seems as if Asra & Victor are having an affair giving either of them a possible motive however we later find out that Asra & Mandy are the ones having an affair.(They had previously brought the idea of an affair between Victor & Asra to Mandy. She laughed at the idea of it)

Asra confesses to taking money from the company reserves to cover some bad investments

Jane's first trap comes in when he tells Cho to inform Rigsby that the search warrant for the chairman's safe won't be here till tomorrow while Rigsby is interrogating Mandy.

The duo then pretend as if this info wasn't supposed to leak & Mandy who is the "gossip queen" goes against her word & leaks the info either way.

Around the same time, Grace & Lisbon visit Victor & inform him of Asra & what she had done with company reserves. Victor tells them that Scarlett used to go to a place to sell her handmade jewelry to a client. They get the location in Marysville which seems to be an antique jewelry market place. We also learn that the lipstick that the rat poison was applied on is very expensive & only sold in high-end stores.

While this is going on, Jane talks to Heather who opens up about her past saying that her 16 year old daughter died in a car crash & how that has affected her deeply.

However when Cho arrives there it turns out to be a dirt bike race & when meeting with said client, he attempts to run & after nabbing him, this client calling himself "Digger" who tells them that he & Scarlett were a drug business with the jewelry business being a cover for it & that around this time is when Scarlett got a call from a woman who sounded angry. They decide to call that number.

Meanwhile Jane & Rigsby are waiting inside the office of the chairman with the lights off & the trap that Jane set works perfectly when someone sneaks into the office & attempts to open the safe & then she receives a call from none other than "Digger, the woman who attempted to break into the safe & threatened Scarlett is Patience.

However she claims to be innocent & is let go due to the lack of solid evidence.

While Rigsby & Cho are tailing her, they run into Jackie who's wearing the same brand of lipstick that was used to kill the victim, before they can question her further, they hear screaming from one of the spas & enter it only to find Patience stabbed to death.

Considering all of this, there are only 3 suspects left those being Victor, Jackie & Heather.

So Jane sets up another trap by first telling Victor that they're going to pretend to arrest him & tells him to go along with it, he agrees to it.

Then he meets with Jackie & Heather telling both of them that Victor is the killer & that they're gonna be arresting him tonight. Of course since they can't keep the gossip discreet, Jackie leaks it.

Everyone arrives at the house to see Victor getting "arrested" while his son, Oscar is crying out for him. This emotionally triggers Heather who confesses to killing both Scarlett & Patience. We later learn that her daughter died because of overdosing in the car due to drugs from a friend's mom who got them from Scarlett & she killed her to stop the flow of drugs in the community however Patience then explained that she was going to pick up & continue the business so Heather had to silence her as well.

Jane's trap here was to emotionally trigger either Jackie or Heather into confessing to the murders & of course asking Victor to take part in said trap is in of itself a trap to check his innocence.

Note: I didn't go into the reasoning feats or ToM/EI feats in this episode. This case is also one of the easier ones in the show & this also acts as a breakdown of the case itself.
