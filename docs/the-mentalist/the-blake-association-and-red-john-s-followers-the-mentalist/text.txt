The Blake Association & Red John's Followers.

The Blake Association was created by Thomas McAllister in order to counter Brett Stiles whom he had resentment towards due to Stiles being a plagiarist.(Check out my last post for more context)

The BA focused on recruiting corrupt law enforcement members & worked on a strict need-to-know basis. Membership is signified by the 3-dot tattoo on their left shoulder & their code word, which is "Tyger Tyger".

I will now list every time Red John makes use of it.

1. Obtaining credentials from Reede Smith in order to kill Rebecca Anderson.

2. Being informed of Lisbon & Rigsby's supposed "deaths". He was able to reason on his own that Jane was deceiving him though it was helped by his great EU of Jane.

3. Swapping DNA records of his with a body he had on ice

4. Obtaining the concussion bomb & the lethal bomb for the suspect meeting

5. Moving Lorelai Martins so that Jane will be forced to break her out

6. Having Bertram assign Jane to Haffner's team

7. Having Bertram assign Partridge to more cases during S5

8. Having Reede Smith work a case alongside Jane.

9. Finally, he had Bertram encourage the involvement of Homeland Security's Robert Kirkland.

Now many people misunderstand the BA as a huge resource & info advantage over Jane, which is blown out of proportion since RJ himself avoids using the BA as much as possible. Why?

Let's look at what happened the first time he makes use of the BA, it results in Reede Smith realizing that RJ is part of the organization. So using it every single time would only result in exposing his membership, info which he had to keep hidden till he needed to leak it.

Why couldn't Reede go ahead & leak this info? There are 2 primary reasons

1. Reede doesn't have any proof that he helped RJ & therefore convincing other members would be a hassle.

2. The more important reason is that he has no idea where RJ lies in the totem pole, that is the Blake Association, which led him to fear retaliation.

Instead, he makes use of his direct followers who are outside of the BA(Proof being that S6 never brings up one of the previously confirmed accomplices having a 3-dot tattoo)

These followers are other criminals who RJ "accepts" & befriends, so that way he doesn't have to accidentally expose himself.

There were 2 adversities that the BA helped RJ with.

1. Protection from Brett Stiles.

2. Allowing to gain info on Jane & the CBI(Once again, RJ makes sure that Jane is able to solve the mystery on his own or straight up gives him the same info to work with)

This is why during his Death of the Tiger scheme(Which was the contingency for if the The Recruitment of the Rabbit scheme failed) he sets up the BA to be gutted & destroyed, the BA's destruction will lead to the dismantlement of the CBI thereby taking care of the 2nd adversity & he has already made sure to kill Stiles, which means the 1st adversity is gone as well the BA was created to counter is gone as well.

Red John operates without his influence for the last 2 episodes of the arc & does surprisingly well. Coming close to winning but ultimately luck favored the Rabbit.
