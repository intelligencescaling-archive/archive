In this post, I'll be explaining some misconceptions on RJ's scaling.

1. "He didn't intend to leak the tattoo"

This is the easiest one to debunk since RJ had already killed Partridge & gotten the body ready way earlier. Why would he do this if it weren't to fake his death. This part isn't up to interpretation. Red John used Tinsley to leak the tattoo.

2. "He could've checked the church"

RJ has already sacrificed the BA atp. All he has left are himself, the false nun, Bertram & Oscar. Bertram calls Jane while the latter is at the park to come to the church, Bertram himself isn't at the church yet. Jane barely makes it there & sees that they've arrived considering that Jane was a bit late, Oscar & Bertram must've made it there a few minutes prior since they themselves were pretty far away. This is why he had prepared the Nun contingency to make up for the fact that he doesn't have the sufficient time or people to check the church.

3. "Why didn't he have more accomplices there?"

The show itself states that Dennis Abbott has already started gutting the BA since S6 EP7. McAllister is legally dead & therefore has to be a lot more careful with his actions, the fact that he even got the people he did is impressive. Oscar, Bertram, the unnamed woman & himself that's all he can gather considering that the BA is in disarray.

4. "He read Jane's file"

This is another one I encounter sometimes. Jane suggests in S6 EP2 that the "killing" of his happy memory may have been helped by RJ reading Jane's file from Sophie Miller. However this is false since Jane further learns that "Jay Roth" met Sophie in S6 so after the "killing" of his happy memory, so unless RJ has a time machine of some sort, this is impossible. There's also the fact that Jane doesn't double down on his own hypothesis once he gets more context on "Roth" & Miller's meeting. The actual purpose of this meeting was to give Jane more clues to work with, so the suspect meeting can occur.

5. "He lost to the pigeon"

Now of course, most people are using this to joke,which is fine & even I do that but I do seem some people use this seriously. First of all, Oscar is the one who fails to notice & he's been portrayed to be incompetent(Failing to kill Reede both times) however RJ forsees this hence why the unnamed woman accomplice who's disguised as a nun. She gets close to killing Jane however he is saved from the 3rd attempted stab which would've been fatal by Bertram's corpse lying there & the woman stumbles due to it as well,which is what gives Jane ample time to grab a nearby pole & knock her out. This is obviously bad luck since, in the alternate universe where Bertram's corpse fell the other direction after getting shot, RJ would've won.

These are the common misconceptions I see sometimes. For those of you who have any other questions regarding his scaling. You can ask them here.
