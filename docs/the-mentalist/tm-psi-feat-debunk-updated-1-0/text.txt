assumptions of the feat

PJ used Zeller's algorithm

Zeller's algorithm is basically (as its name suggests) a mathematical algorithm that allows us to calculate what day of the week X day of X year fell on.
In the Doc of the feat we are told that Patrick Jane used this calculation method to know that May 31, 1976 was a Monday

“Well the series doesn't explain how Patrick Jane calculated it, so we use the simplest method as a basis.”
But here's the problem: The creators of this Doc assume that PJ used this algorithm to know what day it was, which is quite unlikely and in reality the idea that he used a much simpler method, such as the Doomsday rule, is never ruled out:

Doomsday method (made by JohnConway):

Century Anchor Day for 1900s: Wednesday
Calculate the Doomsday for the specific year (1976).
Let yy be the last two digits of the year (76).
The formula is: (yy + floor(yy/4)) mod 7.
   * yy = 76
   * floor(yy/4) = floor(76/4) = 19
   * 76 + 19 = 95
   * 95 mod 7:
      * 95 / 7 = 13 with a remainder of 4 (since 13 * 7 = 91, and 95 - 91 = 4).
So, the Doomsday for the year 1976 is the Century Anchor Day + 4 days.
   *    * Wednesday + 4 days = Wednesday (3) + 4 = 7.
   * 7 mod 7 = 0.
   * If we assign numbers to days (Sunday=0, Monday=1, ..., Saturday=6), then 0 is Sunday.
So, the Doomsday for 1976 is Sunday
Identify a "Doomsday" for the month of May.
check the list… and May 9 is a Doomsday (:3)

31 may - 9 may= 22 days

We know May 9th was a Sunday.
We need to find the day 22 days after Sunday.
22 mod 7 = 1 (since 3 * 7 = 21, and 22 - 21 = 1).
So, May 31st is 1 day after Sunday.
Sunday + 1 day = Monday.

Therefore, May 31, 1976, was a Monday.

The algorithm is simple enough that it can be computed mentally. John Conway(Doomsday’s rule creator) could usually give the correct answer in under two seconds.
