Patrick Jane's strategy

it's insane how underrated PJ is in terms of strategy. Ive seen some scalers putting LL, TT and L above him which is utterly insane. In this post ill try and explain one of his strategies from the episode Red Moon.

The Red Moon Case

The CBI are called in to investigate a murder. The entire scene is depressing, one of the many victims appears to be related to the paramedic who can't stop wailing.  Lisbon (L) is ready to pass on the responsibility of the case, but Patrick Jane (PJ) convinces her to take it.
Three different methods of murder.  He senses something about the way the murders were committed. There seems to be a pattern to them, a certain underlying logic. Not to mention, something about the ritualized way the murders were committed seems familiar to him.

*“This is a truly complicated maniac we’re dealing with”*\- Patrick Jane

Todd Johnson (TJ), the grieving paramedic, walks up to them and wows vengeance on the man who did this to his fiance.

At their make-shift HQ, Ellis Mars (EM) walks in. He demands to speak to the person leading the investigation. Grace Van Pelt (GvP) offers to talk to him. Mars, who correctly deduces that she’s not in charge, chooses to wait.

PJ and L visit Keeyle’s (K) (the victim’s) home, which is West of where she worked. She and TJ live together with her veteran grandfather, Hunnicker, now a depressed and disabled alcoholic (PJ notices the stack of bottles in the bin). They make do. Hunnicker gets his veteran’s check every month and TJ and K are paid well enough. TJ is here too, who is visibly shaken. L tries to talk him out of doing something he might regret. PJ has other ideas when it comes to revenge.

TJ claims to have spoken to K an hour before she was murdered. Asking her to pick up some chips to go with the beers he and Hunny were sharing. He blames himself for her death.

*“I couldve picked her up. You know, i often do…But last night, last night i was just lazy, i guess…It’sbasically my fault that shes dead…please find out who did this.  Just want to know why. Why”*

Cho and Rigsby visit the diner where K worked in. K’s friend mentions a trucker who kept harassing her. The cook, Rusty (R)  doesn't remember.

L and PJ try to find the place K was abducted. They talk about revenge. They find nothing and return back to HQ running into Ellis Mars.
EM believes that the murderer will strike again. He knows this because he is a “psychic”. The duo dismiss him. Before he leaves he tells PJ he knows that he has suffered deeply, and that he should be more open minded. He hands PJ his card.

PJ lies in bed, looking at EM’s card. He connects what his intuition made sense of earlier that day. A psychopath, a dead wife (fiance), a “psychic”. He remembers what Red John (RJ) told him after he saved him (at the end of Season 2)

*“Tyger Tyger burning bright, in the forest of the night. What immortal hand or eye could frame thy fearful symmetry”*

Has RJ set this up for him?

The next day Cho and Rigsby interrogate Gregor (G), the truck driver. He says he met her after her shift. She was agitated about something. He says that the cook harassed her and he tried to cheer her up. G was the last person to see her alive.

At the end of the interview he mentions how beautiful she looked as she rode away on her bicycle. It was like she was riding into the moon, which was full that night. It gave G a bad feeling.

Pj and L interrogate R at the local police station. He denies everything. L notices R is in full sleeves, despite the weather, she asks him to roll them up. R hesitates. She does it for him. Scratch marks, fresh ones. The Sheriff is enraged. He wants to get physical. L puts her foot down and takes R away to Sacramento.

They arrive at the station and Todd Johnson is there. He sees R and lunges at him, attempting to strangle him. L and PJ are summoned by Hightower. L wants TJ to be arrested, but PJ doesn't think he should suffer more. He offers to talk TJ out of revenge.

They go up to PJ’s cabin, where PJ explains that he doesn't believe R to be the guy and warns TJ about what will happen if he tries to take revenge, TJ doesn't seem to care. PJ sympathises.

*“Todd, you repeat what I tell you now and I will deny every word of it. If you truly want revenge, you have to be hard. You have to be dishonest and devious and cold. You can't let people see what’s in your heart”*

TJ apologies to Hightower, who lets him walk away. L suspects something.

GvP discovers that this murder wasn't an isolated incident. Multiple law enforcement officers have been killed in the same way, with the same gun, in one month intervals. It’s a serial killer targeting police officers. R was on vacation for the first murder and G was out of town, so it couldn't have been them.

Across 3 cases, they have next to nothing to go on. Except the monthly pattern, the gun and the type of victim (police officer). But this one seems to break the pattern. Why Keeyle?

The team discusses what it could be, meanwhile PJ goes through Cho’s interrogation of G. Cho suggests that she was used as bait, to lure the police in. Pj notices something. If what G said was true (she rode into the moon), that would mean K cycled off East, opposite to where she lived. They have been searching for the abduction site in the wrong place. They look West and find her bicycle and a button. The button seems to be a firefighter’s and the spot she was heading to is a traditional “make-out” spot. Could she have been having an affair?

They go over to the fire dept and find out that Ben Kitchen did not show up the night Keeyle was murdered. He burnt his face in a BBQ accident and was home alone. No alibi. The Sheriff calls L while they are interrogating BK, while talking he is shot by someone. The same MO.

The pattern is broken yet again. PJ thinks The Sheriff was killed because knew something about the murder. EM shows up again.

EM is disappointed in them because they didn't listen to him, and now what he predicted has some to pass. He tries hypnotising PJ. During this, PJ lets it slip that L was talking to The Sheriff when he was shot. PJ introduces EM to the team and leaves to go shopping. EM uses the info he got from PJ to manipulate L into thinking that he’s a real psychic.

They all meet up at K’s where EM looks around. TJ is disappointed and sceptical, but the local police assure him that EM is good. He somehow sensed that The Sheriff died while talking to L after all. EM finds a wolf tooth in K’s backyard. He connects this to the monthly patterns of the crimes and proposes a theory. The killer has a lycanthropic complex, that he is driven by the phases of the moon to kill and that the last murder did not break the pattern at all but in fact, simply signalled the start of a new lunar cycle. PJ is satisfied.

He mentions to EM and the local police (separately) that serial killers have a tendency to visit the scenes of their crimes. EM is then swarmed by reporters, before he leaves he hands PJ his new and shiner business card. PJ plucks a strand or two of L’s hair and leaves. L is annoyed.

Later that night EM goes up into the hills where K’s body was found, the police are there too. Mars panics and runs back to his car. He finds his car inexplicably locked. He hears the police after him and he runs down the road. PJ catches up with him in his car, offering him a lift. The police think EM is the killer. EM promises that he isn't and was only up there because he wanted to catch the killer himself. PJ says he believes him and offers to clear everything up. He takes EM to a motel. PJ leaves and calls Todd.

Todd, PJ and L meet up at HQ where a manhunt is being organised by the local police. They are convinced that EM is the killer because how else could he have known that The Sheriff spoke to L before he was shot. He must have been there. They even found his car by the hills and what was in the backseat but long strands of human hair. Seemingly Keeyle’s

TJ wants to know where he is but the police can't tell him. PJ offers to take him there.

PJ takes him to the motel EM is in and hands TJ a loaded gun. He takes him up to EM’s room and offers TJ revenge. TJ hesitates. He doesn't believe EM is the killer. He confronts PJ and asks him why he’s trying to frame EM. PJ drops the act and lets EM go. PJ tells Todd that he knows that he’s the killer, that the only reason why he didn't shoot EM is because he is certain of his innocence and that killing him would send him to jail. PJ deduces that Todd is a psychopath and killed Keeyle simply because she was annoying. And the pattern? Well, that’ s simple. As mentioned earlier, Hunnicker gets his veteran’s check once a month, he’s an alcoholic and drinks until he passes out, giving Todd an alibi. Todd is strangely calm and doesn't deny anything. He points the gun at PJ and shoots him. It doesn't go off. Jane took out the firing pin. Cho and Rigsby enter and arrest him.

At CBI HQ Todd seems strangely worried. He offers to talk but only to PJ, saying that they have a connection. PJ is hesitant at first but agrees. He goes into the interrogation room and finds Todd Johnson burning alive. Before he succumbs to his injuries he gives PJ a single message

*“Tyger Tyger”*

Simply confirming what PJ intuited at the start of the episode. The psychopath, the dead fiance, the fake psychic. RJ set this up to mock him.

Explanation

Jane realises what Mars was doing when he tried to hypnotise him and played along. Jane, already, has the entire strategy worked out. Down to the last detail.

He predicts correctly that Mars will use the info he “let slip” to gain the trust of Lisbon and the police. Jane needs him to be a part of the investigation.

Jane then goes shopping and returns with a wolf’s tooth, which he hides in Keeyle’s backyard. He correctly predicts that Mars and the team would head there next and correctly predicts what conclusions Mars will draw from it. That being that the most recent murder did not break the pattern, that the pattern was simply more complex.

Mars now gains more credibility and is now more popular (new business card)

He then sets Mars up by informing him and the police about how serial killers sometimes tend to visit crime scenes. He correctly predicts that the police will mistake Mars for the killer and that Mars will flee back to his car (which Jane locks, after leaving Lisbon’s hair in it, which he plucked earlier) where Jane will pick him up.

The move he made earlier now has new relevance, as the police reason that the only reason Mars knew so much about the Sheriff’s death was because he is the killer, just as Jane predicted.

PJ then takes Todd over to where Mars is and hands him a loaded gun. When Todd tries to shoot him, the gun doesn't work despite being loaded.

The reason why he didn't just hand him an empty gun was because Todd has killed before, he’d likely know the subtle difference in weight. Perfectly calculated.

Jane set this complicated strategy up, with nothing more than a tooth and Lisbon’s hair. Utterly insane.

in the end i'd just like to add my personal opinion. I think, with the ease with which PJ creates and sets up situations, the ease with which he manipulates people and the ease with which he's able to see so many moves in advance, that he is around Baku level in terms of strategy.
