Possibly underrated RJ feat

When it comes to discussing RJ’s manipulation feats people usually bring up Kristina Frye, Lorelai Martins or his formation of Blake Association.

I think people should focus more on his manipulation and recruitment of Timothy Carter for his Strawberries & Cream Scheme. While this isn’t exactly a dismissed feat i feel people don’t bring it up enough just because we cannot determine his methods with certainty. However-

Based on what we can infer from how Red John operates we can make guesses as to how he manipulated Timothy since we have a general idea of Red John’s methods.

Also for the sake of analysis i won’t consider things like “Red John could’ve simply blackmailed him by finding something incriminating on him or threatening his wife.” this theory is very likely untrue since we know Timothy and his wife Sally were not simple do-gooders and engaged in torture which RJ possibly facilitated in the first place. That theory weakens further when you consider the fact that Timothy was perfectly calm and acted like RJ perfectly. This is incredibly difficult to pull off- if in the back of his mind he was worrying about RJ’s threats. Unless he’s some God at acting to the point where he can convince PJ nothing suspicious is going on underneath- it should only be natural to assume Timothy was influenced by Red John to take his place in the mall. With that out of the way i have 2 theories as to how Red John manipulated Timothy Carter :

1. It’s highly likely Timothy was a sort of follower of Red John and part of his cult, Red John probably conditioned Timothy into believing this will be meaningful and his true purpose. He likely made Timothy feel “chosen” and framed his death as something to aspire to. It’s a very cult like way of thinking which tracks with how Red John’s followers treat him as evident with Lorelai. If true this displays RJ’s charismatic/persuasive nature as well as his emotional/psychological manipulation by making his followers depend on him- making them abide by his word as if law. While i don’t think this to be true i should mention Red John could’ve done something similar as he did with Kristina Frye- it’s possible he could’ve made him believe “you’re already dead” and conditioned Timothy into obedience by convincing his death as part of his fate which would be a great feat of emotional manipulation and fabrication/bluffing.

2. In the case that Timothy was not already a Red John follower it would make the manipulation of Timothy Carter even more difficult but not impossible. It’s also possible that Timothy was told by Red John to say what he did but Red John strategically withheld the possibility that Patrick may kill Timothy. By not telling Timothy of this possibility it would make Timothy’s willingness a lot more plausible and would also tie back to his torture- where i believe Red John helped cover up Timothy’s tracks and made Timothy feel like he owed Red John. Whether Red John did this before or after he started formulating the strawberries and cream scheme is up for debate but either way it’s impressive how he can turn a seemingly small favour to his complete advantage. It displays Red John’s trap setting, info control and emotional manipulation very well.

I should note these are just theories and not necessarily true but i believe it deserves analysis since people are too quick to dismiss feats just because they can chalk it up to calling Red John a fraud which i find lazy.
