Patrick Jane's strategy in the Miss Red case

An attempt to explain PJ's strategy in the Miss Red case. I think i may do one more of these strategy feats before doing one or two on his planning feats. Maybe ill do one on how he sets Rigsby up or on one of his battles with Erica Flynn.
If anyone would like me to cover one of his cases, I'm open to suggestions.

Jim Gulbrand (JG), CEO and founder of Gaia Matrix has been reported missing by his brother the day before. The police searched his yacht and found blood. The CBI are called in. Lisbon (L) and her team arrive on the scene. L, Patrick Jane (PJ) and Van Pelt (VP) board the yacht but Rigsby stays outside, he’s sick.

They examine the spot but there’s no sign of the body. VP briefs the team about JG. He’s a multi-millionaire, recently divorced, CEO of a company which he founded with his best friend. During the briefing PJ intuition tells him something is amiss.

He gets off the yacht and walks toward the end of the dock. The yacht is tired to the harbor by a thick rope, he looks around and sees the chain connecting the anchor leading down into the water.. Pj and L look at each other, he holds the rope. They're now on the same page. They both head to the control room and look for the right button. VP is a bit puzzled, PJ explains.

Why anchor and tie the yacht to the dock? Both serve the same purpose so why do both?

L finds the button and presses it, drawing back the anchor, with JG’s body tied to it. Rigsby nearly throws up.

PJ and L visit JG’s estate to talk to his brother, Keith. His brother, an amateur musician, explains how JG and his best friend started the company together. He mentions that JG is currently divorced but is dating a psychiatrist named Brooke Harper, who he met while visiting Keith in rehab (he’s a drinker). He loved her and planned on proposing.

PJ looks at the musical instruments around the house and correctly intuits the kind of genre Keith belongs to. Keith seems amused.

*“Yeah…kinda…yeah. You know, with an edge? How did you know, man?”*

*“Lucky guess.”*

Most of JG’s belongings will go to Keith, but Keith claims that he was with a woman the night JG was murdered. L asks Cho to verify.

Rigsby’s sick so Cho and VP head over to Gaia Matrix to talk to Rick (R), JG’s best friend. A wheelchair bound Stuart Hanson (SH) meets them at the door. He explains that he’s JG’s security chief. He takes them to meet R.

R handled the business side of things while JG took care of the innovations. R has been involved with the law lately and is under investigation by the SEC for security violations. VP wonders if this may have caused conflict between the two of them. R clarifies.

*“Look, you're not getting this. Jim was the creative brain behind Gaia Matrix. Without him, this company is in grave jeopardy. I have nothing to gain and everything to lose by his death.*”

PJ and Rigsby visit BH. She herself has no idea why anyone would want him dead. She was at her office the night he was murdered and has paperwork to back it up. Answering PJ’s question, the reason why she came in to work is because it’s her way of coping with the tragedy. Rigsby is too sick to contribute anything. She does a brief checkup on him and hands him a prescription for an antibiotic.

Back at HQ, Rigsby is still sick. VP asks him about the medicine BH prescribed. RIgsby looks at the prescription and reads it out. PJ notices something. He takes it, smiles, and shows it to the others, asking them if anything seems off to them. They read it, *“oseltamivir phosphate”*. Well, the odd thing was that they could all read it. It was written in a clear hand, not the classic doctor’s shorthand. That and the fact that BH was warm makes PJ believe that she’s not an actual doctor. L isnt convinced, but VP confirms this by looking through her records. Apparently the only Brooke Harper registered is a 64 year old woman. PJ compliments her on her looks, despite her advanced age.

Cho and Rigsby go back to the clinic but BH anticipates them and leaves before they can get to her. They check her apartment and there’s nothing incriminating there. PJ believes that she’s a conwoman, and the fact that she’s so prepared is because she’s done something like this before. But why kill JG? Did he find out about her?

VP examines JG’s financial records and finds out that he made a cash withdrawal of 10 mil before his divorce, but cant trace the money anywhere. Cho believes that BH knew about the money and that’s what she was after. But they have no idea who or where she is.

Pj reasons that because she’s been so meticulous, she must've had her next few moves already planned out, and asks to examine her last few credit card transactions. He has an idea.

VP and Rigsby meanwhile visit Kathryn (Kthy), the ex. She’s a selfish, snobbish woman who’s sued JG multiple times. When asked about the 10 mil in withdrawals she says that she suspected that JG had some money stashed away somewhere but couldn't prove it. She mentions that JG wore a key around his neck, for his gym locker he said. Perhaps that’s where the money is? During the time of the murder, she claims to have been at a wine tasting event.

Back at HQ, PJ is showing off his artistic talents by sketching out the materials BH purchased on a white board. BH used her credit card twice in two weeks. Once nine days ago and once today.

Nine days ago she bought a short haired wig, a bandage, a cap and a long sleeved t-shirt with pants.

Today her purchases were a blouse, a cigar box and a gun.

L correctly reasons that the purchases she made nine days ago meant that she wanted to pose as a man for some reason, Pj agrees. VP mentions the key to him, and how there was no key on JG’s body. PJ connects the two.

Nine days ago she dressed up as a man to get into the men’s locker room at JG’s gym. The fact that she’s still around meant that the key didn't fit. Her new purchases meant that she suspects the money is stored elsewhere and that’s where she's headed to.

JG was a member of a high-class backgammon club, judging by her purchases, Pj predicts that she’ll go there next.

Cho and Pj wait for her at the club. PJ entertains himself by playing backgammon against a prince. He’s up 20k. Cho doesnt seem happy. They spot BH sneaking in and PJ, before leaving, confesses to the prince that he’d been cheating. He explains that he’d been controlling the dice and gives him back his money.

They find her in the locker room trying out the key. She tries to distract them and hide the key but PJ sees through it. Cho arrests her.

Back at the station BH wants to know how PJ found her. PJ offers her a deal. He’d tell her if she told them who she is. BH agrees. She puts on an English accent and lies, fluently. Jane explains that he’d hid a tracking device in her bag when he’d met her at her office. She seems satisfied and explains that she had lied about her identity. PJ explains that he had lied too.

To break the stalemate PJ tells her what he’s figured out about her. That, maybe she’s not as mysterious as she thinks she is.

*“You manipulate people because you take pleasure in it. You enjoy being the smartest person in the room. You're interested in money, of course, but only so far as it allows you to continue conning people. You allow yourself one indulgence, not men, obviously. Not drinking or drugs because you fear that they will impair your abilities. You move around too much to have a pet. So my guess? Chocolate.”*

BH’s impressed but she maintains her lie. When asked about the key she says that JG gave it as a gift, a symbolic key to his heart. She says she has no idea about the 10 mil. PJ’s impressed.

VP and Rigsby follow Kthy, she seems to be waiting for someone at the dock. They wait and they find out the person Kthy is waiting for is R, JG’s best friend. They meet up and share a kiss. VP and Rigsby talk to them separately. Kthy has been seeing R since before the divorce, and the reason she’s here at the dock was because she was looking for the money. Rigsby points out to R that JG was cooperating with the SEC, going behind R’s back.

It would seem like JG found out about Kthy and R’s affair and anticipated Kthy trying to get all his money. He therefore took some out, and hid it carefully. He then volunteered to help the SEC in their investigation of R. This gives the both of them clear motives.

BH, R and Kthy look equally guilty at this point.

L points out that they need to find the 10 mil in order to actually convict BH, if she is guilty of course. PJ reasons that Stuart Hanson (SH), JG’s head of security should know where it is. They go over to speak to him.

They confront him about him, he is hesitant to say anything. PJ mentions BH to him and how she stole his key, he shows it to him and accidentally drops it.

SH confesses that JG did come to him, but the only thing he gave him was advice. Not exactly where to hide the money but a place that would have certain features. They leave.

PJ goes back to BH at HQ. He tells her how she got the key from JG, she simply made a copy of it and swapped it while he slept. He seems disappointed in her and mentions how she could’ve found out where the money was from SH. She’d have no problems getting it out of him. He leaves. BH notices that he’d forgotten the key behind. She takes the key and escapes.

PJ thanks L for allowing it to play out like this. They wait for a while and follow her to the train station using the tracking device Pj planted on her. She gets there with SH in a wheelchair. She’s armed. Cho, VP and Rigsby are inside. L and PJ are outside, L is agitated, PJ doesn't seem worried.

BH pushes SH forward on his chair and he heads her to the locker area. They get stuck by the gate, his wheelchair wont fit. BH leaves him there and goes ahead. She gets to the locker. The key fits.

The team is about to move in but PJ asks them to wait. They seem confused. They look over and see SH get off of his wheelchair and walk towards BH. He takes her gun and takes the money from her. He could walk the whole time. The team then moves in and arrests them.

Explanation

PJ knew something was off about the situation when BH didnt leave after the death of JG. It was a small, subtle thing. He figured out how BH took the key from JG, she simply swapped it, but that the key was not around JG’s neck meant that someone had to have taken the duplicate key, which meant that JG’s killer had to be someone else. The fact that BH was still around but had the key meant that she did not know where the money was. The fact that someone had the duplicate key meant that they had to still be around as well. Which means the money must still be there.

SH asks Jane how he knew about him, how no one in the six months time he worked there, ever questioned him or suspected him. PJ mentions that when he “dropped” the key, he checked the soles of his shoes and his shoes were scuffed.

He left the key in the interrogation room with BH, predicting that she’d take it and escape and go to SH. He predicted that SH would use the fact that BH doesn't know he’s not handicapped to his advantage and wait for a chance when she’s vulnerable to take her down.

They go to the train station, they try entering the gate, his wheelchair gets stuck because it’s too big, she leaves him there, goes forward, gets the money and gets countered by him.

The fact that PJ asked VP to wait while all this transpired was because he knew that this would happen, every step was predicted and planned out. He set the whole thing up with just a key.

What makes these final series of collisions even more impressive was that both Patrick and Lisbon weren't in the station when it all went down. They were outside in Lisbon’s car. Jane knew in advance, all the way back when he “left” the locker key in the interrogation room with BH how everything would transpire. How BH would go to SH, how they’d go to the station, how SH’s chair would get stuck, how he’d attack her and take the money.

I suppose the best way to summarise Patrick Jane’s strategic prowess would be a quote from this episode. When Cho asks Jane about his plan, he replies;

*“There’s no luck involved. The outcome’s a given. It’s all applied psychology and Newtonian physics”*
