His plan consisted of providing five possible locations of Hightower to each of the five suspects within the CBI who might be working for him. To carry out this plan, Patrick used different methods with each of them, but, in summary, he managed to distribute different information among the suspects. Additionally, he arranged hotel rooms with different numbers for each possible accomplice, so that any reaction would be crucial in identifying the real traitor. Jane informed each of the suspects about different rooms; these are the rooms and the corresponding names:

ROOM 405 - BRENDA

So how did RJ discover this? As we later find out in the sixth season, Gale Bertram was a member of the Blake Association, which was under Red John's control. Brenda was also working for corrupt organizations. However, we will focus on the information that Bertram and Craig gave to Red John. This means that when Jane informed both Craig and Bertram of different locations for Hightower within the same hotel and with vertically aligned rooms.

STRATEGY OBJECTIVE:

At this point, Patrick planned for Red John to sacrifice one of his pawns, but what he didn't foresee was that Red John would change his approach to sacrifice a pawn, using him to send Patrick to jail while simultaneously retreating. Now we should probably explain how he achieved this.

STRATEGY:

The situation he has been placed into forces Red John to verify which of these versions is correct. To do so, he sends an assassin to Bertram's room, initiating a vertical verification process to determine if Hightower is there.

However, Red John anticipates an alternative scenario: that it could all be a trap devised by Jane. In light of this possibility, he instructs the assassin to commit suicide if it is confirmed that they are being ambushed. This is reflected in the assassin's behavior, as she pauses for a few seconds in the room before opening the curtains. This moment of hesitation indicates that she was evaluating the situation, following Red John's instructions to act cautiously in case of a trap, before proceeding with her mission.

After the team discovers that the assassin left a rope, handcuffs, drugs, and a bag in the room, they conclude that her intention was to take Hightower to Red John. In response, Patrick Jane says, "maybe," indicating that he wasn't sure. This outcome was exactly what Red John had anticipated. It will be explained in more detail later, but the key point here is that this discovery helps protect Red John's strategy. By leaving those items at the scene, it reinforces the assumption that the assassin was there to capture Hightower. With this tactic, Red John has several objectives: he is testing Patrick while also setting up a way to have him arrested and then withdraw.

This detail is not mentioned randomly: it is carefully timed to coincide with Patrick’s mental state, which is oscillating between doubt and anger at that precise moment. By striking at this vulnerable point, Red John maximizes the impact of the comment, ensuring that the emotional weight overwhelms any critical analysis Patrick might attempt.
