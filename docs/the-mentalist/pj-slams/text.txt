Poker feat
After winning $250,000 in BlackJack, Patrick Jane is playing poker with Cho and Rigsby. Van Pelt asks him how he won. Patrick replies he memorized cards. He tells them he uses his memory palace for it and as an example, asks her to shuffle the cards so he can show the result of it.

Van Pelt was shuffling for around 11 seconds. Every place in his memory palace is the position in the deck (1-52) and every card has its own symbol.

So let's first calculate how many cards are missing from the deck.

Poker is played with 52 cards, as we can see, there are 14 cards in the deck that are not in the deck. I got this from 2+3+4+2+2+1=14. So, there are 38 cards in the deck (52-14)
So, as I said in the context, Patrick guessed the dealer's card BEFORE it finished shuffling. About 1 second before that, when she made the last shuffle. Because Van Pelt hasn't played with them-she doesn't know how to play poker, and which way of shuffling cards is most common among people who don't know how to play card games? That's right, from top to bottom! We were also shown the last second of her shuffle, and yes, she shuffled by hand. Shuffling is when you take a piece of the deck and move it to another part of the deck. Watching this scene, I counted 2 shuffles in the last second, which means that Patrick had to move 76 cards in his memory palace per second and had to keep in mind not only 38 cards, but also their location in the deck, so he had to hold 76 items. In addition, Patrick knew the entire deck even before it started shuffling, because he was able to name 2 cards in the deck without even looking at them.Now let's calculate Patrick's PSI. Patrick's movement of one card through his memory palace consists of 8 operations: perceiving the card, matching it with the symbol, matching the position of the card with the exact location in his memory palace, finding the symbol in the exact location, removing it from there, matching its next position after shuffling with the previous location in his memory palace. put it in the memory palace, find the specific place where it should be, and put it there. 76*8=608; Patrick's PSI is 608 operations per second. Patrick's WMI is equal to 76 objects in his head.
