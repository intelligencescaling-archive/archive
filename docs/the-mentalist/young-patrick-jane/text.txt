________________

Throwing Fire
The episode starts in the present day but we quickly go to Patrick’s teenage days (1986) after he gets knocked out by a baseball. Patrick is seen hosting a show with his father in front of many people. Patrick is blindfolded in the middle of the stage and his father asks him questions for him to display his psychic abilities. His father randomly grabs an item from the crowd. We don’t see anything until this part but the flashback instantly opens with Patrick guessing that the item is a cigarette case. We don’t know how he knows that so we can’t say that this is a feat. His father then asks him “What's it made out of, son? What kind of cigarette case, Patrick? What would you say? What's it made of?” Patrick replies that it is made from sterling steel. Cigarette cases can be made from a variety of materials. Silver, gold and steel were commonly used metals, but you can also find cases fashioned out of wood, glass and ceramics. And yet, Patrick managed to correctly guess the right material. After the second world war, the usage of metal cigarette cases dropped and people switched to cardboard packs and Patrick knows this. Knowing this, Patrick understands that since metal cases are not used anymore then the woman must have a special reason to keep the case with her, which is that it’s a souvenir. If she carries an heirloom with her, that would mean that it’s a recent death. So Patrick says to the woman that the case is from someone who has passed away recently. The woman agrees with her. Since the woman is not that old, it is more likely that her parents would not be dead yet, so one of her grandparents must have died. Jane guesses that her grandfather died at first, but after reading her face he understands that he got it wrong so he quickly covers his mistake and says that her grandmother died. Since the woman carries an heirloom, that would mean that she loved the person who died. And since it is very unlikely that the woman will love someone who didn’t love her, Patrick says that her grandmother loved her very much and even more than she knew. Patrick is right about his cold reading. Patrick also displays his social awareness in his reasoning.
(Social Skills, Social Awareness, EP, Cold Reading, Observation, Intuition, Prediction, VCI, Knowledge, Deductive Reasoning, Abductive Reasoning, Critical Thinking, Association Thinking, Abstract Thinking, Divergent Thinking, Linear Thinking, Lateral Thinking)

Patrick and his father are seen once again together. They watch over random people with binoculars to see Patrick’s reading skills. His father chooses a random person and Jane cold reads him. Patrick says “New mom, recently married. One of her first days out. His husband is gonna get her a better ring. They had to get married.”
(Cold Reading, Observation)
For his next show, Patrick’s father tries to convince Patrick to deceive a dying girl. Even though Patrick would gain a lot of money from this, he tries to convince his father to not do the act because it is morally wrong for him. But his father insists on doing it. Patrick and his father get a normal crystal and they display it as a magical ancient crystal with healing powers. To show its healing powers, Patrick burns his finger with a candle and when he puts his finger on the crystal, the burn mark vanishes. This is probably done by covering the crystal with something like aloe vera. The family of the dying child gets impressed by this. Patrick’s father says that they could stop by in sessions but the family insists on buying the crystal fully. While they argue, Patrick acts as he is concerned for the child and he acts as he convinces his father to sell the crystal when this was the plan all along. His father and Patrick gain 10 thousand dollars.
(EE, Acting Skills, Bluffing, Concealment)
