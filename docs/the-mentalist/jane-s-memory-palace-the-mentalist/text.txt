Jane's Memory Palace

I wanna talk about Jane's FSIQ. I don't use FSIQ so I might be wrong. This is mainly a feat for PSI, and VSI.

The feat itself:

Memory Palace. I used to think differently regarding this feat when Notme mentioned it in relation to Sherlock but now I recognize that he was right. The method of loci as a mnemonic technique is contingent upon walking through a visualized place and looking at all the information you encoded in that palace and decoding it. Which means that we can scale PJs PSI by how much is in the memory palace.

In S4E10 (Fugue in Red) he mentioned having all of Shakespeare's works in his memory palace and I think it is highly unlikely he just knows the names because that would be kinda yk just straight up weird and he paired it with a quote. This is strengthened through the fact he has shown information regarding Shakespeare's works before. Also narratively it just makes no sense whatsoever because he was bragging/flexing at the time and knowing all of the things Shakespeare did is not as big of a flex as knowing each word.

Countering counters preemptively:

“How do you know Jane processed it within a similar timeframe?”

The short answer is that I don't. It could be 10 or 15 seconds but I truly don't see it being longer than a minute and I can explain exactly why.

In season one, before the addition of the names to the memory palace we see him use his memory palace in seconds in the casino scene, and remember this is after placing all of Shakespeare within his mind's storage. Meaning he processed that in seconds so why would adding only a fraction more information increase the time substantially.

“I wouldn't call memory recall PSI”

Thing is that this isn't just memory recall. The trick with MP isn't just putting stuff there to remember later, it's putting shit there only after you encode the information you wanted to remember for you to decode at a later time. It's not just recall, it's decoding what you yourself put there.

“He remembered all those names over the course of season five”

He did in fact remember all the names over the course of S5 but the very recollection of who made up the list is not very important since him placing the names into his MP wouldn't be all too important in evaluating his FSIQ as would the speed at which he decodes what he's put in there after already storing it, so while remembering the names wouldn't scale all too high in PSI him putting it in his MP and then later decoding it would.

“He didn't need to use his MP for the names he had a board”

That board was explicitly shown to be misdirection with a fake list in case anybody were to ever break into his room at the CBI. Though even if you were to find a way to demerit this feat from being included In his MP calculations there would still be the 880k+ words from Shakespeare works.

Scaling:

VSI - His memory palace was stated to be a carnival circuit he traveled around in when he was younger. Since he simply put so much information into this memory palace it is quite safe to assume that the size of the memory palace grew in relation to how much he remembered.

PSI - As I said before he decoded amounts of information which should place him solidly above things like 17 card poker, AP calculations, and monitor hax.
