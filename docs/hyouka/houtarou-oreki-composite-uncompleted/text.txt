The first thing that caught Oreki's attention was the gojuon order Juumoji followed when selecting his victims. Another detail that caught Oreki's eye was the Kanya Festival Guide left at each scene. This guide wasn't just a pamphlet; it was Juumoji's target list. The pages left open marked the next victims. Chitanda's bringing of the manga "Ashes at Dusk" greatly aided Oreki in solving the case because this manga wasn't just an ordinary work; it was the cipher key to the Juumoji incident. The author of the manga, Anjou Haruna, had stated that she was inspired by Agatha Christie's "The A.B.C. Murders" for her future work, "The Kudryavka Sequence." This indicated that the Juumoji incident contained a literary reference, and like in The A.B.C. Murders, the victims were selected in alphabetical order. But this time, the alphabet was the gojuon.
In Agatha Christie's The A.B.C. Murders, the killer chooses his victims and murder locations according to the order of the letters in the English alphabet (A, B, C...). This, as the novel's title suggests, is a clear pattern.
In the Juumoji incident, Tanabe Jirou (i.e., Juumoji), while inspired by The A.B.C. Murders, used the gojuon order of the Japanese syllabary instead of the English alphabet. The gojuon is a table where Japanese vowels and consonant + vowel combinations are arranged in a specific order (a, i, u, e, o, ka, ki, ku, ke, ko, sa, shi, su, se, so... etc.).
Juumoji's victims (clubs) were also selected according to this gojuon order:
* A Capella Club (A)
* Igo Club (I)
* Uranai KEN (Fortune Telling Association) (U)
* ENGEI BU (Gardening Club) (E)
* O RYOURI KEN (Cooking Club) (O)
* KABESHINBUN BU (Wall Newspaper Club) (KA)
* KIJUTSU BU (Magic Club) (KI)
* KEION BU (Light Music Club) (KE)
* KOTEN BU (Classics Club) ([KO] target)
Oreki deduced that the pen name of the author of "Ashes at Dusk," "Ajimu Takuha," was also a cipher. This name was composed of the first letters of the names of the manga's three creators: Anjou Haruna, Jirou (Tanabe's first name), and Muneyoshi (Kugayama's first name). This meant that Juumoji was one of this trio. When Oreki put all the clues together, Juumoji's identity was revealed: Tanabe Jirou. Tanabe was both a member of the General Committee (giving him easy access to the crime scenes) and part of the "Ajimu Takuha" trio. Furthermore, on page 33 of the Kanya Festival guide he left at the scenes, both the targeted clubs and Kugayama's name were listed.
