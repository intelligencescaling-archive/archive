Repost of my Jack Kelly post

Jack Kelly, the main character of Newsies, has - in my view - a chance of not being fodder in SCD.

\- In Newsies, Jack is able to organize multiple groups/boroughs into going into strike as one unified group. He uses simplified economic terms to help the newsboys of New York understand the terms - as they're uneducated. This is an impressive task for Jack as he is incredibly uneducated himself. He does this in a couple hours, too, which shows great charisma. In summary, this feat is kinda like tactics and charisma.

\- He was able to improvise a speech spontaneously to keep morale high. This shows charisma and possibly could show a VCI feat.

\- He was able to hold his own in negotiations against Pulitzer, Pulitzer is an old-ish man who possessed an insane amount of education and wisdom compared to Jack - and I'm pretty sure that in some interpretations, Jack is literally captured and heavily coerced to oblige.

\- He was able to escape a place that was kinda like a boys home multiple times. This "boys home," or the Refuge, was a place with scheduled guards and had outside authority. This meant that he escaped recapture multiple times and also was able to evade and escape the Refuge through his intellectual abilities. In some interpretations, it's believed that he timed his escape during riots or issues, and also specifically evaded guards by their schedules. This is an amazing feat for an uneducated little boy. (He was able to repeat the process of escaping multiple times from, likely, the ages 13-17 years old.)

\- The Banner Headline Strategy shows strategic intelligence. Basically, he was able to create a strategy that helped the Newsboys strike exponentially. He, with the help of a writer, produced and coordinated the distribution of newspapers throughout New York - which alerted the governor. This allowed the Newsboys strike to work and stemmed from Jack's knowledge on the fact that awareness of the strike would likely help the strike.

\- He has great EQ (maybe ToM(Theory of Mind)) and was able to read the motivations, of people. His ability to know how to push emotional buttons shows an ability to manipulate. When he met a character he was able to precisely read the fear of said character simply through how they looked and expressed themselves. He also was able to read Pulitzer's psychological negotiation tactics.

\- He has great SQ, and is able to understand - and I believe engineer - some social interactions. In the Newsies, Jack maps out the social situation and understands that the Brooklyn Newsboys are the most powerful.

\- He was able to exploit a very egotistical, and hard-to-impress - character by reframing the Newsboys strike in a way that said character would like to join the strike. He basically tricked the character, Spot, into believing that him joining the strike would make Spot the big-celebrated hero.

Anti-Feats and Weaknesses:

\- He impulsively started the strike without a plan.
\- He has poor emotional control. (planned to run away to Sante Fe when the strike didn't go out well.)
\- Jack only planned the strike to be fast and coercive, which made the strike feel like it has blind spots. He didn't plan for food, money, etc. This makes some of his planning ability wary - as this could either be a feature of his plan or a bug.

Some of his weaknesses and anti-feats can be interpreted as him being stupid and impulsive because of his frontal lobe not being developed well - but that shouldn't be an excuse.

Where do you put Jack Kelly? Is he fodder or can he have a place in SCD?
