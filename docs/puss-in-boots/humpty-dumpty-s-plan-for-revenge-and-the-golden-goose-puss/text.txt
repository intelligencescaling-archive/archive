Full explanation of Humpty Dumpty's plan to exact vengeance and gain the Golden Goose

Context: Puss and Humpty grew up together as orphans in the city of San Ricardo, and dreamt of one day finding the magic beans told of in Jack and the Beanstalk so that they could ascend to the realm of giants and steal the golden goose's eggs, as wealth was something they both dreamt of. To that end, they spent their adolescence stealing every bean they could find in hopes of attaining magic beans. Over time they grew apart, with Puss becoming known as the town's hero while Humpty continued to be a thief. Eventually, Humpty tricked Puss into stealing from the people's bank with him so that they could finally leave together and start a new life. However, in trying to escape they lost the money anyway into a river, and Puss left Humpty behind to be arrested. Humpty spent the next 7 years in prison, where he finally got the beans from another prisoner, as he plotted revenge.

First, after being released from prison, he uses the beans to promise wealth and the capture of Puss to the town, and gains their loyalty. He further hires the outlaws Jack and Jill, and the thief Kitty Softpaws. He has a female cat seduce Puss, knowing well his nature as a womanizer and that this would be effective, and this cat would then bring Puss back to a fake home she lived in in the middle of nowhere, with the closest town being , thus compelling Puss to travel to that town. It is there that Puss will then encounter several more people hired by Humpty. Knowing Puss will be on the lookout for his next score, Humpty feeds two fake opportunities for Puss, stealing from a Church and an Orphanage, knowing Puss would pass them up due to his honor. This would, though, raise Puss’ interest in the third option they reluctantly posit, making it seem all the more enticing to a thrill-seeker like Puss as well as making it seem more legitimate as they didn’t want to say it. This option would be stealing the Magic Beans from the murderous outlaws Jack and Jill.

Upon attempting to steal the Beans, Puss comes across Kitty who was sent by Humpty. Kitty antagonizes Puss as Humpty knew Puss would respond angrily due to his competitive nature, blowing their attempt to steal the Beans. This also, however, sparks in Puss an interest in this mysterious cat lady with master burglary skills who can also go toe to toe with him in fencing. This was Humpty’s plan, and for it he indeed also has Kitty act consistently flirtatious with Puss to incentivize a fake romance to further distract Puss, clouding his judgement. Humpty approaches Puss and reveals that he and Kitty are working together, and leverages Puss’ desire to repay the money they stole from San Ricardo, using their bond to manipulate Puss by making it seem that Humpty regrets it as much as Puss does, as well as using Puss’ new infatuation with Kitty to draw him into Humpty’s fake plan to rob Jack and Jill of the Beans.

The three then go after Jack and Jill, with Puss and Kitty successfully stealing the Beans, albeit with a fair amount of resistance from Jack and Jill to further sell that this was a real robbery, as no resistance would have felt too easy. Humpty even goes so far as to trust Puss with planting the beans to reinforce that the two of them can put the past aside, as Puss knows that attaining the beans was Humpty’s greatest dream since childhood. They steal the golden goose and bring it down, however, Humpty had been purposefully leaving traces of them behind for Jack and Jill to find. Jack and Jill knock out Puss and travel back to San Ricardo, inciting Puss to follow them and ignoring why Jack and Jill would go there via his new/renewed emotional connections to Kitty and Humpty, where he is then arrested. What’s more, Humpty gives a pile of golden eggs to the town, knowing that the goose’s giant mother will have followed them and, seeing all the eggs, destroy the town while Humpty escapes with the golden goose. Thus, Humpty manages to get revenge on Puss, destroy the town that imprisoned him, and gain an infinite gold supply all at once.
