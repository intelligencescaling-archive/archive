[God’s Imitation] Tutorial feat explained

God’s imitation is a Chinese novel (how original 🤯) about a lawyer  Lin Sizhi transmigrating in the New World a world governs by games

At the start of his transmigration Lin Sizhi is given two choice either wait 2h before entering the New World or create a game to "judge" a criminal that will also enter the New World (Lin got also 2h to review the case of the criminal create the game and playing it)

In God’s imitation the game are here to test the human traits of the character The game Lin Sizhi created is a very good example of this

"The redemption roulette" is a really simple game

\-There is 2 people sitting in front of each other tied to their chairs

\-One is the innocent (Lin Sizhi) which is unconscious and the other is the shooter (the criminal let’s call him John)

\-There is a six chamber revolver between the two of them one announcement is made "there is 1 real bullet and the rest are perfect replica decoy, you have to shoot all six bullet to pass”

\-The shooter have 2 options either shoot himself or the innocent

\-There is two big concrete blocks on each side of John’s head if he shoot a decoy at the innocent the block will closing up on his head each time he shoot the innocent

\-He have 5 minutes to conclude the game

\-If he breaks any rules he died instantly

Now that the rule are explained I can explain how the feat unfold:

According to the predictions of Lin John is a self centered merciless bastard (he have killed an entire family in an car accident and never felt guilt) that is why he shoot the first threes bullet toward Lin the three happens to be decoy making the concrete block narrowing each time making John psychologically vulnerable because his chances of shooting the real bullet increased each time after the third shoot a voice announced that the 5th bullet will be a decoy making the true bullet only able to appear at the 4th or 6th shoot making the probability 1/2

Previously John made the assumption that after the third shoot he can only withstand the concrete block narrowing one more time anything beyond that will kill him so  he shot a another time Lin being at his absolute limit in terms of pain tolerance after seeing that it was a decoy John quickly shot himself for the fifth time and nothing happened relieved by the announcement being true John turn his revolver apologized to the innocent man he is going to kill

and fired without any glimpses of guilt but the bullet was a decoy the concrete block squeezed one more time  killing John

The trick is that it was never specified that the real bullet was inside the gun it was actually in Lin Sizhi pocket who reveals himself after John death

This game was designed in a way any other person could survive a selfless person a kind person a normal person a rational person even a suicidal person will survive but with John psychological profile Lin was sure that he will always shoot the other person the announcement mid game was here to ensure he will continue to shoot the innocent person resulting in dying cause of the trick

That feat show Lin quick thinking imagination trap setting emotional understanding planning

This feat only happens in the 5 first chapters showing Lin great intelligence right off the bat
