Introduction
Piotr Langer Jr. is a serial killer titled “The Sadist of Mokotów” and a son of an influential and wealthy businessman. He appeared for the first time in the “Chyłka” series by Remigiusz Mróz specifically in “The Final Appeal” and for the last time (as for now) in his own spinoff “Langer” in the book “Arachnia”, he is cocky and theatrical but careful.

“Wyrok” and “Ekstradycja”
In "Wyrok" Piotr Langer Jr, manipulated a kid to commit murder and forced Kordian Oryński to defend that kid in court by promising him that he will get Joanna Chyłka a chemotherapy, then he manipulated indirectly Kordian to prove in court that the prosecutor leading the case Karolina Siarka is forging evidence to convict the kid, that prosecutor just so happens to lead investigation against him. This is to discredit her and make it seem like her further attempts at pursuing him are just desperate moves to regain a career. He takes Chyłka to a fake medical facility for a day and moves her into a secret hideout in order to slowly kill her before the sickness does while making sure any attempt at looking into her disappearance would go nowhere and that everyone would assume she disappeared willingly to spend her last days in luxury out of country. During this time Langer has used a voicemail system to communicate between his secretary that was keeping track of what’s happening outside of his lair, she sent a message to the voicemail system Langer logged onto it and listened to the message, leaving no trace leading to Langer. Before he could kill her something went wrong and he had to make a deal with Chyłka, he gave her law firm "Żelazny and McVay" the list of all his underground criminal businesses while the law firm agreed to permanently represent him in court. After learning that Chyłka agreed for Langer to arrange the kid's murder in prison, Kordian decided to go against his law firm and to make sure he would end up in prison. Kordian asks Kormak to go through the files again and he discovers that Langer is laundering money using Fortnite by creating better accounts, filling them with items and selling them. He contacts the prosecution and as he's doing that Chyłka has been talking to Langer about it, and after she leaves he fakes his death and frames her for murder. She's arrested for it, taken to the station by transport but Langer uses his man to contact Commissioner Szczerbiński to create an opportunity for her to escape.
"Ekstradycja" - 1,5 year earlier Langer killed 3 people and framed Witalij Demczenko an Ukrainian living in Poland for it while forcing him into a coma and amnesia. In the time between his fall into coma and awakening he seduced his wife Oksana, he told her that they will live together. He made her contact Chyłka who escaped to Belarus and later to Ukraine and introduced the case to her saying that the true culprit is Piotr Langer. Chyłka contacts Kordian through a phone hidden inside a pack of cigarettes and Oksana and makes him take the case. Chyłka returns by misleading the authorities and the media that she's on a plane from Ukraine to Poland through an anonymous tip and driving to the NSI station to announce that Piotr Langer lives because he was recently seen in Ukraine alive and showing a fake picture that's good enough to fool everybody. Chyłka and Oryński investigate what the police could have missed in the Piotr Langer murder. They don't discover anything on the scene but by digging into Salus, a company Langer owned they discover that through the company money there has been funded rent on the apartment below Langer's and that 50 million PLN (If I'm not mistaken) just disappeared from Salus. Oryński was able to defend Witalij in court but Langer sent someone to threaten Oksana (because Witalij didn't know the connection between Oksana and Langer) to make him say in court that he remembered what happened and that he killed these 3 people. Oryński couldn't win the case with Chyłka and as she was taken away to prison, Oksana from an unknown place uploaded a video on which she confessed to killing Langer. Then after she was useless to him, he massacred her head against his red mustang.

“Afekt” and “Egzekucja”
Langer escaped to the Cayman Islands and purchased a property there with the money he extracted via Oksana. Langer knew about his rival, the shadow organisation "Konsorcjum"; during his absence, it grew in power. Langer knew that, since he left a red Mustang and Oksana's corpse as a clue that he was still alive, the Konsorcjum would catch on and scheme to take him out of the picture for good. Langer hid well enough that an agency of private detectives couldn't find a single lead on where exactly he is. Kordian Oryński went to the islands and got luckier than the investigators; he was able to find Langer, but before he could send a photo of Langer alive, William McVay (son of one of the owners of the previous law firm he worked at and was pronounced dead) turned out to be alive and Langer's associate. Langer pieces together that since Kordian showed up, that means Chyłka knows about that, and that if she's searching for him she has to have a reason cause she would much rather don't have to look at his face anymore. The only possibility was that Konsorcjum blackmailed her into finding him so they could remove him. Langer threatens Chyłka to tell her contact with Konsorcjum a fake address where he will prepare an ambush, or else he will make Kordian suffer.
The Konsorcjum's men showed up at the fake and real addresses; a gunfight happened, and Langer's men left Kordian inside, only to come back and take down Konsorcjum's men. Kordian got amnesia, so Langer devised a plan; he faked photos with dead Kordian and put the very same Mandela tattoo he had on Kordian and many other people. Then Langer went with one of his men, Szczerbiński (the policeman Chyłka had a romance with) and killed one of Konsorcjum's men in such a way as to make it impossible to tell who he was. Langer sent Szczerbiński to tell Chyłka that Kordian is dead in order to ignite in her the need for vengeance. Then Langer lets Kordian go into the wild in Poland, knowing that Chyłka would realise that a mysterious man without the documents from the news is Kordian. Chyłka tried to give the prosecution all she knows about Konsorcjum, but a man on the inside killed the case and leaked who gave the info, putting a target on her back. Chyłka contacts one of Langer's men to ask for protection; he agrees and under the condition that she will defend Szczerbiński in court via a proxy (she had her advocate qualifications taken away from her in the last book), to which she agrees. Langer secretly tells his men to install a tracking device in Chyłka's car, just in case. Langer provides protection not only to them but to their families.
In the trial, Langer plants another one of his men as the prosecutor to sabotage the offence; the only witness says that Piotr Langer Jr., a man who was thought to be dead, killed the man.
Chyłka and Kordian go to meet the prosecutor from the anti-corruption office, Anna Gabała, in an abandoned warehouse because she told them that she plans to falsely accuse Kreon's (The Konsorcjum's leader that nobody knew about) right-hand man and torture him into giving them info, while also leaking the false info that the prisoner will be transported and forcing Kreon to make a move. But she turns out to be one of Konsorcjum's members who had been planted in the office years earlier. Then Langer appears, he scares the shit out of Konsorcjum's members, extracts the info out of the highest ranked member and records what he says.
Then the police storm the warehouse and everybody besides Chyłka and Oryński get arrested.
Langer negotiates; he tells the presidential candidate that he will give away all the info he has on Konsorcjum, and in exchange he gets freed of all charges. Langer knows that Korodecki would guarantee himself a reelection by taking down a criminal organisation. He also reveals the existence of more people with the same Mandela tattoos and that tatoo was the only thing that was known for sure in the Szczerbiński case and that the witness was lying (the last thing didn't matter if it was true cause the first two things already guaranteed Langer would go free). Langer also says that the evidence in the disciplinary proceedings against Chyłka was faked and demands to get her back to the profession.

“Ozyrys”
Piotr Langer Jr. has been apprehended by Prosecutor Olgierd Paderborn for murder of the serial killer "Odra Butcher" with the evidence being footage on a cloud of that serial killer.
Piotr has devised a plan to escape with the help of his henchman - Mikhail but tweaked it after a serial killer "Osiris" who helped out Piotr the last time he was captured for being Osiris since he copied his M.O. reached out to him by lending him the final piece of proof that Langer's not guilty, his own DNA. Osiris provided Langer the resources to escape captivity. The lawyer Osiris sent has never told Langer who his boss is but the fact that the lawyer didn't take the case for money and that the lawyer's boss helped Langer once. Langer and Osiris understand each other without words, so Langer knew that Osiris would send him a letter via the lawyer.
The message was hidden in the page 5 of a document full of law speak which would scare off majority of the potential readers and was interwoven between information about the case. Osiris gave Langer information about his latest victim, so he could use it for leverage since the police won't be able to find it and knowledge that Osiris forced the therapist of Nina Pokora the woman he loves to change her way of thinking, so she would realise who saved her from the Odra Butcher and who she should be thankful for.
Langer revealed the identity of the victim and negotiated from Paderborn and Siarkowska the right to wear normal clothes and not a jumpsuit in shitty colour for the information of how he knows (without revealing the method of passing information obviously) and offered help in catching Osiris for rights to interact with other prisoners and access to the library. After they accepted, Langer gave them the name of another victim and Paderborn got a letter from Osiris.
The man Langer pointed the prosecutors to was not the next victim of Osiris but a pedophile, this way the media would talk about the situation as if Langer himself caught him. This however is a scheme orchestrated by Osiris, not Langer.
Osiris transported a phone to Langer in prison and Piotr told his plan to escape. The lawyer put out a statement on the internet to all the people who were fascinated with Langer in which he said that if they want to support Piotr Langer they should show it by putting on a red suit with a black tie, the same suit Langer chose to wear to court. During his speech in which he gave an explanation on why he doesn't confess to the crime he was able to notice that an experienced prosecutor is just pretending to be composed. He highlighted how he was proven innocent of many cases of horrible crimes as either a suspect or the defendant and claimed to be targeted by the corrupted justice system specifically and that the system wants him to be silenced, so the truth about the broken system was made to lock people up and not to show them the truth. That was obviously a speech for the people, not to the judges, as that wouldn't get him anywhere, while not giving anything concrete, so people would fill in the gaps.
Langer predicted that it would take long for the prosecutors to break Osiris's code in which he left a surname of his next victim, so he told Osiris to leave them a letter in which he will proclaim he will kill another and use terms only he (Piotr Langer) uses. When the prosecutors left the court during the trial his lawyer told the media that his client is treated like garbage by the justice system. The people were split, one group were supporters of Langer and the 2nd were the regular people who thought a death sentence was not enough. The situation was so heated one man jumped onto Langer when he was led to a police transport car with a knife and stabbed him multiple times.
The knife was fake and the man was placed there by Piotr through Mikhail and escaped, Langer got bags of fake blood through contraband and placed it under his suit. And the ambulance that took Piotr from court was bought and modified to look like an actual ambulance by Mikhail. After that Langer got in his red mustang refusing to get into a regular car, because he didn't want to just vanish. Langer knew that the prosecution can easily track his trail and realise where he is, and that didn't worry him. After Langer drove onto the road a police chopper was already in the air, and he knew the fun had just begun.
Langer drove on the main road in Poznań forcing everybody who chased him to drive from the same direction. Langer's only way of escaping was becoming invisible, and invisible he became. He drove into a private magazine hall and from it 11 red mustangs with the exact same licence plates and drivers in the same red suit Langer wore to court and spread like an illness.
Langer waited for the police to spread and after 4 people dressed like him and in old cars drove away (an unnecessary precaution since all the police focused on bait, but good for SCD either way). The baits were just regular people whom Langer got to participate by forging an online announcement that an ad for Ford will be recorded with the selected few.
Langer hid in a small house in a small village, the internet he used was provided by Musk's Starlink.
Langer recorded a video in which he proclaimed that he will show how ineffective this corrupt system of justice is by catching Osiris himself and that the information he gathers he will only pass to Nina Pokora, no meta data, no way to trace and sent it to media in order to influence Nina to contact him, since she may try to use that to capture them.
He spoke with Osiris on the phone, shared information while keeping things like his location for himself. Langer learned from Osiris that he kills people without emotions and immediately read from his voice that he's telling the truth. Langer thought about how he killed Osiris using his own M.O. and when Osiris said that the only person deserving admiration is him, Langer knew Osiris had similar thoughts.
Olgierd Paderborn, after he found out that the next victim of Osiris will be a prosecutor decided to start a press conference in order to tell people that Osiris is a fabrication and Piotr Langer was Osiris the whole time, in order to try and make Osiris attack Langer. Just like Piotr predicted.
