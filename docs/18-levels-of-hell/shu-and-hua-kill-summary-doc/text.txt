Now lemme also describe the skills just to let u know the cognitive load required in calculating this metaphorical game of chess

Qiu Yinong's Wheel of Time : can rewind time by 5 minutes, which creates a branching timeline from the original one. Can be used repeatedly to create a 5 min loop. At the end of this rewound 5 mins, or after withdrawing her skill, all the rewound members gain the memories of the other timelines of looping. Cannot bring back the dead.

Lin Xuan's Skills : Creates 6 different spaces, copying the currently occupied chamber. Think of these as parallel universes
2 spaces have slower timeflow than the normal ones
2 spaces have normal timeflow
2 spaces have faster timeflows than the normal ones

All people and objects, along with their skill abilities and personalities are replicated . Lin Xuan can transport himself or others across these different spaces. Lin Xuan can foresee the future with the actions on spaces with faster timeflow. Clearing a game or dungeon in one space counts as a clear in all spaces. Can eliminate Lies from anyone, etc..

And Hua was able to counter these with secret rules such as

1.Independent Clearance : Simply put, if there are two Chen Rans in the same space, and the first Chen Ran clears the Wooden Figures Game, then the second Chen Ran cannot continue to participate in... the Wooden Figures Game in that space

2. Furthermore, the consciousness of the first Chen Ran who clears it will not return to his body but will remain in a state of chaos. His consciousness can only return to the dungeon's third secret chamber after all other surviving players in that space have participated in the Wooden Figures Game, or if only one non-clearing player remains and is singled out
3. Mandatory Participation Mechanic : within two hours, if there are two or more surviving players besides the cleared player, two of them must be forced to participate in the next round of the Wooden Figures Game.

4. When the total number of corpses and living people is less than six, the consciousness of the cleared player will also remain in a state of chaos.
