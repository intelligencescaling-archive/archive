Yapping about Objective judgement of lies in Eighteen Levels Of Hell

\[The images are what I used to visualize the 18th level of Hell\]

Basically, whether you are judged to have told a lie or not is objective.

This means that even if you think that you are not lying or you genuinely don't know if you have told a lie or not. The system wouldn't care, you can be judged even if you didn't know you told a lie.

For example, you are in a room and you counted a total of 15 Players. After counting, and forgetting to include yourself you stated that "There are 14 Players in this room" you told a lie. The next second, all the other Players would have pulled their gun at you and killed you.

Another more cruel example is you see a table with a fruit on it, that fruit is orange. And you say, "That's an orange(fruit)" but other Players were more careful, looked at it upon touching it and realized it was a toy. You told a lie, since it was a toy not a fruit. The objective system doesn't care that you've been misled, if there was an actual judge it would simply say that, you could have simply checked the Orange(toy) yourself before confirming whether it was an Orange(fruit) or something else. Which means it was your own fault and you deserve to die.

Next more cruel example which already happened in the story many times. A Player transports you into another Parallel Universe, the people in that Parallel Universe are all fake people, but you said towards one of the people in that universe. "You are a true person." You can be judged by other Players and have told a lie..

Why? Because you experienced traversing to another Universe, you should have suspected that the other person is a fake therefore it was your own fault..

In fact this is basically how people are judged in court in real life, lets say you murder a person and you try to justify it by saying "He wanted me to kill him" or any other reason, the Judge doesn't care. It only looks at the evidence, since you killed someone that's all that matters and you are sentenced to prison..

...

Now it gets more complicated when it comes to Unknown Lies.. Unknown Lies are basically lies but you won't know or have no way of knowing whether that lie is true or not.

For example, \[Player A\] says "I have a younger sister before I came to Hell."(Lie).

\[Player B\] says: "\[Player A\] has a younger sister." From an objective perspective(in this verse's logic), \[Player B\] did not tell a lie. Since there was no way to know if \[Player A\] really had a younger sister and it was not \[Player B\]'s fault..

But later.. Lets say evidence appeared out of nowhere for simplicity.. \[Player A\]'s paper about his family was instantly shown for 4 seconds before \[Player A\] took it.. In that paper(document) it was directly stated that \[Player A\] is an only child.. If that is enough evidence(there might be a situation where it isn't) then if \[Player B\] says: "\[Player A\] has a younger sister." he would have told a lie..

Because once again, Hell doesn't care. In the objective perspective(This verse's logic), \[Player B\] should have read the entire paper and it was his own fault that he couldn't read or process that fast, therefore he is guilty and has told a lie.

That's a brief idea about the lying judgement mechanism in Eighteen Levels Of Hell:Lying Is Forbidden Here..

This system is one of the reasons why the story scales so high. Simply there are many things in this Story that give space to super high level outsmarting situations..
