Phase 1: Negotiation
1.1 Analysis of the situation: The Invisible Vise
After the deaths of the trio of sheep Trương Mỹ Lệ, Chu Thần and Tào Tu, the "Great Tycoon" board only now truly bares its fangs. Lý Tri Đồng realizes with a shock that she has fallen into a deadly trap, besieged by danger from all four sides:
1. First, the deaths of that trio were not only a purge, but also a "show of strength" by Trần Nhiên and Thu Ý Nùng. Their calculation, planning and ruthlessness have reached a level on par with, and no way inferior to, the Three-Star players. Amid a forest of seasoned minds, LTD painfully realizes that she is the weakest, the "least intelligent" link among the 6 remaining people.
2. Second, before the Great Tycoon game, TN and TYN had used their skills and revealed information about those 2 skills (pay attention to this detail). Because the skill is too dangerous for the 18 levels of hell, TN is marked by all 3 Three-Star players, who are determined to eliminate him at all costs. However, TYN's skill is different: it is an extremely powerful skill when it comes to mastery over time, yet it has one fatal weakness (namely that it requires shouting "I'm lying" and raising the gun to fire in order to activate) ⇒ The Three-Star players can fully rely on this weakness to blackmail TYN - a player whom all 3 judge to be enough to change their fate (receiving rewards from the lords of the levels outside the 18 levels in order to ascend, or escaping the shackles of reserve status), making her work for them if she does not want her weakness known to all 18 levels. That is, she could be killed by someone quick-handed before she even manages to raise the gun and start the skill (because her very statement "I'm lying" by TYN is itself a lie).
3. In order to win TYN over for their own team, the 3 Three-Star players will clearly have to fight and eliminate one another; this will be the basis for conflict among them
4. When the 3 Three-Star players fight, someone is needed to supply a lie for all 3 so that they can activate their skills and fight, to see who can kill whom

- Trần Nhiên is not a fool. He understands this situation thoroughly, and so he long ago launched extremely strong psychological pre-emptive strikes. By displaying his intellect and mettle while also amassing assets to become a "tycoon" with enormous cash flow, and by revealing himself as Company No. 8, he is not only a link in the great plan of the three sheep, but is also secretly sending three messages to the Three-Star players:
* I am very smart, and you have all seen that through the way I tricked the trio of TML to death, so getting me to speak a falsehood is as hard as climbing to heaven.
* All of you want to kill me, so why should I supply a lie, to serve as the tool for you to switch on your skills and fight over TYN?
* Besides, my Company No. 8 is swimming in wealth, and can also link up with TYN's company; if you want to ally and bring down my company, forcing me to speak a lie in order to survive for now, that is certainly impossible.

- At this moment, the scales of fate seem to have tilted completely to one side, pushing Lý Tri Đồng into an extremely disadvantageous position of "ambush on all ten sides". In the cold eyes of the three Three-Star players, she is the ideal "soft persimmon" to crush: her intelligence is not especially outstanding, she lacks the frenzied psychology of being driven to the wall like Trần Nhiên, and more importantly, her financial strength is completely outmatched by the mighty empire of Trần Nhiên's Company No. 8·.

All three old wolves of the Three-Star understand one truth very well: forcing Trần Nhiên to be a sacrificial pawn in their infighting is an impossible task. So the gazes of the whole group turn in unison and lock onto Lý Tri Đồng. Remember that the essence of the current game is a war of money, and a ruthless plan quickly takes shape: the five remaining companies (including the three Three-Star players, Trần Nhiên and Thu Ý Nùng) will form a closed alliance. They will be self-sufficient, circulating capital among one another, completely isolating Lý Tri Đồng outside the flow of finance.

Their goal is very clear: crush Lý Tri Đồng's company and push her to the door of death. Standing on the brink of bankruptcy and imminent death, she will have no choice but to become a tool: forced to utter a lie, turning herself into the platform for the arena where the other three slaughter one another.
While Lý Tri Đồng is still fumbling with a vague fear, the other five have long since seen through the board and mapped out two inevitable outcomes if she is driven to the end of her rope:

* First outcome: "A cornered dog jumps the fence". Pushed to a dead end, rage and despair will overwhelm LTD's reason. She will utter a lie to activate her skill, in order to drag others down with her. Outcome: She will certainly be torn apart by the Three-Star players immediately. Death is unavoidable.

* Second outcome: "Hold out and bide time". If LTD can still keep a bit of reason, she will notice a faint ray of hope. She will choose to hold out to the death, preferring to let the company go bankrupt rather than breathe a single lie. The survival logic here is very simple: if she dies and no one supplies a lie, the Three-Star players will lose the "fuse" for activating their combat skills. Keeping her alive is keeping the key to the game.

However, the old foxes of the Three-Star all rule out the first scenario together. After all, LTD is a Two-Star player, and her survival instinct will not allow her to be so stupid as to seek her own death without seeing the crux of how to survive.

At this point, again on the basis that LTD will most likely choose possibility 2, all players (except LTD) go on to consider another feasible option to replace LTD, and LTD will certainly have to choose this option if she follows possibility 2 (of course she has not thought of this part yet; the smarter players are working out, by logic, what other option she could take on her behalf)
That is, shifting the "fuse" target from LTD to Thu Ý Nùng. Why TYN? Trần Nhiên is too cunning and too rich to be coerced; the three Three-Star players hold the dominant position and would never agree to sacrifice themselves. Only TYN is the perfect choice to replace LTD as the scapegoat, supplying a lie for the crowd to slaughter one another.
And for TYN to willingly supply a lie, they clearly need to ensure that TYN feels their sincerity, and that she can rest assured that even if she lies she will not be judged. To do that requires one tool: the "future lie".

Specifically, they may together create 1 promise that places TYN in an inviolable position where no one can judge her, for example "After TYN lies, no one is allowed to judge her".

While Lý Tri Đồng is still fumbling to rearrange her chaotic thoughts, those hunters had long since seen through to the closing of the game. Her fate is now entirely in her own hands, but ironically, both forks before her lead to the abyss:
* First choice: She stubbornly resists the pressure of the other 5, leading to her company going bankrupt. In a state of distress and fury, she is forced to lie to activate her skill for revenge. She is torn apart by the crowd and dies without an intact body.
* Second choice: She too is squeezed by the 5 to the brink of bankruptcy, but her will to live rises. She firmly refuses to lie, but is forced to bow her head and accept the "future lie" agreement so that Thu Ý Nùng is the one who lies. At this point, the three Three-Star players will unleash their skills, turning the battlefield into a bloody melee. She must entrust her life to luck and to her own character.
After a long psychological tug-of-war, Lý Tri Đồng dejectedly realizes she has no way back and resigns herself to the second choice. However, it is at this very moment that a disastrous misunderstanding arises in her mind. She naively believes that the coming battlefield is only a one-on-one duel between her and Trần Nhiên - to see whose life is harder, to see who falls first. In her eyes, Thu Ý Nùng is the "juicy bait" that the three Three-Star players are hungrily contending for, and so will certainly not be killed. This "go big or go home" thinking makes her not too reluctant when she nods in agreement.
Moreover, another pragmatic calculation also pushes her to compromise quickly: time is money. If she keeps hesitating until she is squeezed dry by them before agreeing, then her financial strength will be exhausted and her company ruined - what would she have left to maneuver with? Better to accept early and preserve a little capital than to wait and starve to death. This very way of thinking became the perfect reason for her to talk herself into stepping into the trap.
At this step, Trần Nhiên has placed the final piece, completing the situation of "driving Lý Tri Đồng to death" and cutting off all her hopes of resistance. While Lý Tri Đồng is still dreaming of a fair battle of wits with Trần Nhiên, she has no idea that she has already put both feet in the door of death.
Let us together lift the curtain on the developments that follow, to witness the cruelty of the layout that Trần Nhiên painstakingly arranged.
1.2 Some information you need to know
Before stepping onto the life-and-death board of "Great Tycoon", these six final survivors had already clashed with one another in a brutal game earlier. Engrave an important detail in your mind: both Trần Nhiên and Thu Ý Nùng had revealed their trump cards - their skills - before the eyes of the others.

As analyzed in the "skill information" section, Thu Ý Nùng's skill is an extremely dangerous double-edged sword. To activate it, she must utter the sentence: "I'm lying". That sentence itself immediately becomes a fatal lie if she fails to turn back time in time to create a new timeline.

However, as was also mentioned, in order to kill the trio of TML, CT and TT, TN was forced to use the skill "Eternal Kingdom", and just as Tào Tu in the doc Great Tycoon (1) earlier went from a non-liar to a liar because the "causality reversal" effect was nullified, we can see that from the very moment TN used the skill, TYN immediately became a liar.

-An intuitive example of the mechanism of TYN's skill:
* TYN lies in timeline 0 on the subway to trigger the skill => TYN timeline 1; everyone timeline 1 (after 5 minutes return to timeline 0) => TYN still keeps her timeline 1 "state", so she will not be counted as lying when the current timeline is timeline 0.
* When TN activates Eternal Kingdom to wipe out the 3 Two-Star players => TYN timeline 0; everyone timeline 0 => EK has erased TYN's timeline 1 "state" as well as everyone's timeline 1, and when TYN returns to her timeline 0 "state" she will be counted as lying, and if anyone lied in timeline 1 it will also be counted as lying in this timeline 0.

Was this an oversight by Trần Nhiên? Not at all. In fact, this madman had seen through that latent danger long ago. Judging from the performance in the previous game, all three Three-Star players possess skills so freakish as to be unpleasant. When they are combined into a complete formation/combo, Trần Nhiên reaching the threshold of death was almost an unavoidable fate. At this life-and-death moment, the only trump card left in Trần Nhiên's hand is 'Eternal Kingdom'. Certainly, sooner or later, Trần Nhiên would be forced to unleash it as a last ray of hope. But if he did so, TYN would also immediately be turned into a liar. Standing between two doors of death, he chose to create a crazy scheme to break the deadlock, "Thin Gauze Covers the Sage's Eyes". Even before the trio of sheep were killed, Trần Nhiên and Thu Ý Nùng had secretly communicated through Morse code (please check the doc Great Tycoon (1) with the keyword "Morse"). A perfect plan was drawn up right under the opponents' noses, both creating a safe shield that lets Thu Ý Nùng escape judgment, and setting a vicious trap to send Lý Tri Đồng to her death.

In addition, it must be understood that when TN uses "Eternal Kingdom", all timelines are no longer independent but overlap one another entirely. For example, if LTD lies in timeline 1, then if "Eternal Kingdom" is used, in the main/current timeline LTD will also be counted as lying (because she lied in timeline 1)

“If Thu Ý Nùng lies, and the number of players remains intact at 6, then absolutely no one is allowed to judge her.”

Witnessing the terrifying counter-kill that Trần Nhiên launched from the brink of death, Lý Tri Đồng in her mind now felt only fear and astonishment toward Trần Nhiên, because she herself had been drawn into that bloody battle. If Lý Tri Đồng intended to judge her (supposing LTD now realized that TYN had lied), then the moment LTD touched the gun would already be a violation of the promise. And Thu Ý Nùng already held the gun in hand; in this life-and-death race of speed, how could LTD possibly win?
