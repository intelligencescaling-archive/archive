What it takes to survive in Hell for one month (Eighteen Levels of Hell: Lying is Forbidden Here)

Before everything, we must already include surviving the Tutorial.

Chen Ran's Tutorial is not the only one with a spawn killing trap, as we are revealed later in the story that Spawn Killing also happens at other Tutorials.

In order to pass this, you at least need Bottom Tier Trap Evasion, Reasoning and Thinking.

(The other traps might not be in the same difficulty as the three traps that were already shown, that's why Bottom Tier instead of Low Tier)

After Tutorial, they must survive a Novice Instance where Players are actively trying to kill Newbies as Newbies are ticking time bombs.

From what the narrative looks like, we can say that this at least needs Low Tier Social Intelligence and Mid Tier Reasoning+Thinking, Low Tier Scheming because the 3rd chamber will require a lot of strategies.

After clearing the Instance, you are faced with various troubles in the 18th Level of Hell, where Seeing Through People at Low Tier level at the very least is a must. You also need Mid Tier level FSIQ to memorize all the rules, +Info Gathering otherwise you'd just end up in some crazy situation without knowing.

In Hell you still become hungry so you still need to eat, to eat you need Points, to get Points you need to go to Instances.

So there goes, you have to go Novice Instances if you want to play safe or if you can't find one, then 1 Star Instance it should be. If you want high confidence of survival then you need Mid Tier level Social Intelligence.

And if you want to survive your next Instance with high confidence rate, you'd need to be Mid Tier overall.

Now you are back from the Instance, you have one big problem.

Every month in Hell there are 3 major events that are basically mass extinction to newbies.

This along with various other factors forces you to grow extremely fast otherwise you either die, or become a slave.

Among the three, the third major extinction event is the worst from what we have seen.

7 Star Players absolutely farming all the Newbies...

Imagine just sleeping and the moment you wake up you got sent to a virtual reality, or even worse you got hit by Gojo's Domain Expansion and your mind starts bleeding.

That's what happens to most Players who survive this far.

Yeh, you're not surviving this if you're not a 3 Stars Player due to sheer power level gap💀

So what do you need to become a 3 Stars Player? You probably need to go to 2 Star Instances to grow quickly or if you want to be sure, go to 3 Star Instances.

3 Star Instances likely requires one to be High Tier considering that it should be way more difficult than Lonely Revenge Instance.

So that's High Tier Reasoning and Thinking, probably Mid Tier scheming and Social Intelligence is still sufficient.

Now you still have to survive that third mass extinction event...

Anyways, you won't just magical come up with the method to survive in Hell.

Plus even if you have all this, you only have around 50%\~70% confidence rate of survival, so if you want to increase the odds...

So I think, if you want to increase the odds.

Very High Tier: Reasoning, Thinking, Trap Evasion,

High Tier: Social Intelligence, Deception,

This should give you likely\~Very Likely chances of survival.

This basically means that in order to survive Hell for at least One Month, you gotta be a High Tier or above.

Then, the second month would probably be way harder to survive as you need to choose between the two: Give up and be under a Floor Master or get hunted by the Floor Master's dogs, if you give up you'll be the one doing the hunting and you might get sent to crazy missions

Ofcourse you can survive without being High Tier if you are extremely lucky like Lin Xuan
