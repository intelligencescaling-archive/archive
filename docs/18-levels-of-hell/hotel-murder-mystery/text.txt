Basics - Gui Yu
Gui Yu's are basically Lies that everyone believes as the truth due to a forged physical evidence of its validity. Thus these cannot be judged as lies, until an unique objective evidence disproves the validity of it and is present in the same space as the Gui Yu itself does.
For eg : Assume there is a colony of underground humans who never knew who Micheal Jackson is, if someone has said that they are Micheal Jackson, and proves it by maybe singing pop songs, then anyone who denies that the guy is not Micheal Jackson, without any objective evidence, will be judged as a liar.
Meanwhile, everyone else who also says that the guy is Micheal Jackson won't be judged as liars. But if someone somewhere has discovered the id card of either Micheal Jackson or the guy, the Gui Yu is broken universally.
Those who had uttered the Gui Yu previously but not after it has broken won't be considered liars. Except the creator of the Gui Yu. If he had uttered the Gui Yu, then he would be considered a liar after it has broken, even though he hadn't uttered the Gui Yu after its breaking.
Those who utter the Gui Yu, not knowing it has been broken, will be considered liars. If the creator of the Gui Yu, had created it without uttering it at all, maybe by his actions instead of words, he won't be considered a liar even after the Gui Yu is broken.
Basics - Liar Killer Guns
Liar Killer Guns can only shoot at a person if the command "You Lied" is uttered and the person being shot has uttered it. Its shot can bypass Liar Killer Gun Protection.
Liar Killer Guns can protect its user from any kind of physical interaction with a living person. It can also protect from any incoming physical projectiles. Thus a wielder of Liar Killer Gun cannot be killed with force.
The Situation
Everyone wants to deceive each other into lying in this game. Everyone acts in the role they are given, and don't use physical force to kill each other. All their Liar Killer Guns are colourless, only gaining a tint of the Main Personality's colour when they kill someone. Everyone knows themselves if they are the main or sub personality, and whose main/ sub personality they are.
The Hotel they are in and the surrounding area are isolated from the outside world. This play of deception starts late into the night, and goes on to end at the next day's morning.
