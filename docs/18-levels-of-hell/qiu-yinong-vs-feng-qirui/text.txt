The Time Discrepancy

This created a time discrepancy.

That is:

Feng Qirui thought Qiu Yinong's Wheel of Time still had a minute left.

But in reality, it was only seconds away from ending.

---

Question 1

Why didn't Qiu Yinong kill Feng Qirui on the timeline she created within the future timeline, choosing instead to kill him on the true timeline?

The reason is simple.

If she killed Feng Qirui on the future timeline:

- His body would remain there rather than on the true timeline.
- Therefore he might not actually die.

But killing him on the true timeline meant certain death.

---

Question 2

Feng Qirui was hit by No-Distance Kill on the future timeline; why was his spirit drained on the true timeline?

The reason is also simple.

He wasn't hit by No-Distance Kill on the future timeline itself.

Instead:

He was hit on a separate timeline created by Qiu Yinong using Wheel of Time within that future timeline.

This is why Feng Qirui's spirit wasn't drained immediately upon returning to the true timeline.

Because the final Wheel of Time reset them to the double bed, a point where he hadn't been hit yet.

Simply Put

- The future timeline belongs to the instance.
- The time created by Wheel of Time belongs to Qiu Yinong.

The No-Distance Kill Feng Qirui suffered on the Wheel of Time's timeline was not affected by the instance's future timeline.

Therefore, once Wheel of Time ended, his spirit was drained.

---

Question 3

As everyone knows, Qiu Yinong's skill can only reset five minutes. Even if she ended the future timeline quickly and used the skill again, shouldn't it reset to the future timeline rather than to before she first used Wheel of Time for her plan?

The reason is simple.

One must be clear:

The future timeline is not real time.

It is a timeline created by the goddess of fate within the instance.

Players can control when it ends.

Qiu Yinong had retracted her skill on the future timeline, grafting the time onto the goddess of fate's future timeline.

Then she quickly ended that future timeline.

This caused the duration of the future timeline to be reduced.

Pushing the reset point further back.

Thereby resetting them to before she first used Wheel of Time for her plan.
