This is Hell, where lying is forbidden and yet everyone deceives each other. Since directly lying is forbidden, everyone finds a way to deceive each other into lying.

When someone lies, a player can either judge him/her or use his/her lie to activate a Skill.

Lies are judged objectively, meaning even if you think it's the truth. You can still be judged to be lying and die if what you said isn't true.

-Because of this, you cannot say anything emotionally since it would count as a lie. [emotional lie]
-You cannot say something that goes against common sense, since that would be a common sense lie, even if it was the truth you could have still lied v.i.a common sense lie.  [common sense lie]
-There are also unknown lies, which are basically just straight up lies but you won't know whether that person is lying or not lying until you judge him. Or it's basically bringing up information that you can't confirm whether it is true or not. For example  "My friend has a dog", you don't know if your friend has a dog and the other person also doesn't know it. If your friend has no dog then that counts as an Unknown Info lie.
[Unknown information lie]
- Future lies are when you promise something and was unable to fulfill it, for example. "I will kill you today", but the next day the person you vowed to kill is still alive. Therefore you have lied.
-Gui Yu lies are probably the most complex ones. These are lies that Weird Speakers specialize on, it is when you can turn something that is a lie into a truth until it is proven wrong.. For example: "I am at least 25 years old", you proceed to show an id that confirms it. The other Players said "You are at least 25 years old", plot twist: You are 20 years old, and that id was a fake id. But at this time no one is considered lying. Why? Because as I said lying is judged objectively, since you were able to bring a physical evidence(proof) you must be telling the truth. But.. The moment someone debunks your Gui Yu, it would turn into a lie. Those who have said that you are at least 25 years old before would not be lying, but after the Gui Yu was broken, if they say you are at least 25 years old again, they would be lying and can be judged. Proving a Gui Yu wrong requires 'Unique evidences' as for what counts as Unique? You gotta deduce it yourself.
[Gui Yu]

Dungeons or Instances depending on your translation, are basically escape rooms. You can view it as Dungeons from your average magic fantasy, except everyone fights intellectualy. The goal is still to defeat the Boss, an Instance has three main chambers. In each chamber you would need to solve a problem or do the clearing condition to proceed.

Hell has 18 levels, at the 18th level it's basically a city that all players stay in. But you can't stay in 18th level for a long time, otherwise more rules would be unlocked until you turn into a slave[Death row inmate] who basically lost its freedom e.g. you can only walk for 1 hour a day, you should maintain a smile 24/7, you cannot sleep outside, etc. etc.

There are countless rules in here, in fact rules might differ depending on the area you are on. Anyways, if you don't want to turn into a Death Row Inmate, you must keep clearing Instances/Dungeons(depending on translation) in the lower levels.

Now as to why this verse scales so high.. There are simply too many variables that are both complex and counter-intuitive.

For example, there are skills that manipulates causality, manipulates time, space skills, memory skills, virtual reality skills, illusion skills etc.

Stars are basically levels. The higher the Star, the more intelligent and powerful someone is.

Illusions and virtual reality is very common here. For example if you see a person and talk to him, and use any person pronouns you would be lying because you ain't even talking to anyone. There are also many Instances with memory loss, and trends in Instances are very insane. It is confirmed that killing yourself types of Instance are common below Three Stars.

Every statement uttered by a person could be tricking you into lying, anything you see could be tricking you into lying, basically you can't trust anything and must deduce everyone. You can also die by mechanism, not just by judgement.

If you are stick in a virtual reality and mange to escape, you're still not safe because.. Stacking virtual realities is very common here. Such as, Virtual reality inside a virtual reality inside a virtual reality and so on.

Every information you want to get, you must deduce ot yourself or you can pay people which isn't feasible. Everyone is very smart so "perfect logician" assumption is used a lot. What I mean by this, is that if you deduce up to a certain point you should assume the others would have reasoned or thought as far as you did.

Another is that almost every concepts used here are counter intuitive.

In a single month there are three events in the 18th level  that basically kills all newbies. In the third event high stars go and hunt newbies trying to kill them.

So yeah.. This world basics bullies all newbies.
