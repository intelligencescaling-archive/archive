Terminologies and Skill Effects

Heart Cultivation : Cultivation/Progression of the State of Mind and Thought to higher levels using specific procedures, which can offer benefits ranging from increased mental capacity, higher thinking functions and varied thinking due to various States of Mind resulting in varied ideas, and some amount of immunity to the Liar Killer/Whisperer Skills.

There are 3 main levels of Heart Cultivation : Id level, Ego level and Superego level, with various methods of heart cultivation to reach these levels. Each level of heart cultivation is so much higher than the lower one, the difference is described as the difference between the thoughts of an author and nd the thought process of the characters within his novel

No-Distance : Think of it as a mental domain expansion players can use to communicate in secret(can't be noticed from the outside) or pastiche mental attacks of various kinds. Higher levels of heart cultivation can enable players to stimulate parts of reality in this domain expansion.

Spring and Autumn Brushwork : Superego level skill of An Sheng. Two major  effects can be observed.
1. Life Editing : Before numerical inflation, An Sheng could edit players created by skills or dungeons, such as clones. After upgrading skill to high levels and achieving Numerical inflation, can edit a player’s Life, including their Loss of Ego trigger (with the condition the player doesn't know An Sheng can do this). The edited player will be counted as independent from the original.
2. Copy : Copy a player's life. This so-called copying requires a sample first. The copying effect is equivalent to an early-access screening. This effect also enables An Sheng to predict said player’s future actions.

Eternal Kingdom : True Self Level Skill of Chen Ran, meaning every other skill is lower in priority than Eternal Kingdom, whatever its rank might be. It has the following effects
1. Dispel : Decrypts all existing Skills within the entire space, regardless of whether they are covered by non-Skill secret chambers. This effect, simply put, means that 'space' here refers to large-scale spaces like dungeons and the Eighteenth Layer, not small spaces like a single secret chamber.
2. Prohibit : Prohibits other players within the entire space from using Skills for one hour, regardless of whether they are covered by non-Skill secret chambers
3. Silence : After using Eternal Kingdom, the holder is immune to the effects of any non-True Self Skill within that space for ten days. Note: This is a passive effect and can be actively toggled off.

Note: Silence is much stronger than State of Mind Skill immunity. For example, the Superego State of Mind can negate some effects of Superego Skills, such as the temporary memory loss from Wheel of Time, but it cannot negate time resets. Silence, however, can, and this passive effect can even be turned off.
