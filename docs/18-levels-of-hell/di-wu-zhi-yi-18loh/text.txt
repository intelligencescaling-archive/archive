Introduction (ch131)
Di Wu Zhi Yi (hereinafter referred to as Di Wu) is introduced as a pleasant character with a dazzling smile. He is a top-tier three-star player and was sent by the First Layer Captain to “protect” Chen Ran and Qiu Yinong.

Di Wu asks Chen Ran about using a Tracking Talisman and offers him advice, making a favorable impression that he would later build upon to spring a final trap on Chen Ran.

Chen Ran says that he was lucky that his Tracking Talisman with a 10% success rate worked well, but given the info Di Wu had from his handler, Chen Ran is not stupid enough doing such foolish things like wasting his limited money on low-rate items. And since Di Wu knows that Qiu Yinong’s skill is most likely related to time manipulation, he is even more convinced of this. He has probably even made a guess that her skill is something about rewinding time, so she can try using her low-rate Tracking Talisman until it works.

Fake Chamber (ch132 - ch136)
Twenty players were sent to the instance. Di Wu was a part of Chen Ran’s team (Chen Ran, Qiu Yinong, Cao Xiu, Zhang Meili and Di Wu Zhi Yi) and there were four full teams in total. And as soon as everyone was transferred to the instance, they immediately checked their watches to record the current time. This is essential since there are chambers in 18LOH which can erase your memories through the dungeon mechanisms (as we have seen in the previous instance).

It was a supermarket, but there were no goods on the shelves and there was purple smoke outside the market. At the entrance protective suits were neatly placed.

A man who called himself Lin Xiuyuan began to deduce the puzzles, but his deductions feel so smooth, he even has a perfect formula to solve the puzzle. Di Wu knows that some players could create their own fake-chambers so he guessed it’s a prepared trap for the newbies Lin Xiuyuan made before everyone came to the instance.

Li Zhitong solves the puzzle and orders her ally to enter the password and go outside the market wearing a protective suit. He did so, but when he got outside, he suddenly took off his helmet and died for unknown reasons. Since, out of the 4 teams, only Chen Ran’s team did nothing, Li Zhitong and Lin Xiuyuan insisted on making a player from his team the next victim. So Chen Ran, who deduced that this room is fake, goes outside and acts like he is terrified by something, puts off his helmet and “dies”. After that another player gets outside and realizes it was a trap, so he runs to Lin Xiuyuan and judges him to death, saying that he had lied to them.

As soon as this player says the command, Di Wu immediately kills him, so everyone understands that Di Wu had deduced everything long ago and he’s a dangerous player.

Duo Jie sends one more person outside, but as soon as he arrives at the door, he realizes that there is a wall labeled “Poisonous Gas”. Therefore he understands it was a trap constructed by Li Zhitong and Lin Xiuyuan and assisted by Chen Ran, who had been pretending to be dead. But there is a contradiction. The previous player, who had seen the wall, had deciphered the Gui Yu, which means that his judgement should have been to kill Lin Xiuyuan, because Lin Xiuyuan said earlier that “he deduced an unknown answer” but according to the fact that the answer was not unknown to him at all, that statement was a lie. So the player guessed that since it was a trap made by two people, they must have constructed different parts of it, meaning no one had actually lied. However he was wrong.

From the God's perspective, it was a three-layered trap. The first layer was designed to deceive those who went outside - they would likely die while claiming that Lin Xiuyuan and Li Zhitong were liars, because they would assume that the Gui Yu had already been broken and that the “masterminds” statement about deducing an unknown answer was false.

However, this is where the second layer comes in: if a player is wary enough and able to reason a little, they would arrive at the next conclusion - Lin Xiuyuan and Li Zhitong could have solved each other's puzzles. In other words, Lin Xiuyuan solved Li Zhitong's puzzle, and Li Zhitong solved Lin Xiuyuan's.

Nonetheless, this is not the correct answer either, because the “masterminds” were not the only ones who created the puzzles, their teammates also contributed (they had roughly one minute to prepare everything). Therefore, the statement that they were “solving each other's puzzles” is a lie, and that is the third layer.

And here comes another question: how could they know they were solving their teammates' puzzles? It’s actually quite simple - there was a hint hidden in the book, which reminded Lin Xiuyuan and Li Zhitong which puzzle to dispel.

As for how Di Wu figured this out, Chen Ran had mentioned it earlier, saying that he had already seen something similar in a previous dungeon.

Train Showdown (ch137 - ch )
After retracting the Fake Chamber, survivors ended up in the subway carriage. There are 25 stations with different names and there is an iron barrel outside the carriage. As soon as players arrived, all three-star players had already deduced the meaning of station names (I won’t focus on it cuz it was some sort of Chinese puzzle I don’t really understand). So Chen Ran deduces their destination too - the “second 11th station.”

When all the players sat down, the carriage moved to the second station. When the doors opened everyone rushed towards them, but stopped immediately, before going out of the carriage. This is some sort of trick which could help you to bait newbies.

Di Wu convinced everyone to let Chen Ran be the first one who will step out of the car, since he was the one who proposed his theory. Chen Ran agrees but with the condition: from this moment, each team should provide one player to go outside, starting with Chen Ran’s team.

After reaching their destination, Li Zhitong suddenly uses her skill: “Whisperer: Mount Tai Crushing Down.” As far as we know, using a skill requires sacrificing a guaranteed successful judgment opportunity and even though she was a Whisperer, the Gui Yu had been broken and the person who lied was dead. Di Wu immediately concludes that means there was the second Gui Yu created by her or there is a slave, providing a lie.

Soon, after the light returns, everyone sees all one-star playing lying on the ground unconsciously and two-star players look overexhausted, gasping for air. On the other hand, all three-star players act like nothing happens. Li Zhitong possesses a skill which could attack your mental state (could explain it later), so we can get a Mentality (AC) feat for Di Wu here, but it would be explained much better in later chapters.
