Deducing the liar is what will be covered in this short doc. The reasoning of the skill effects are short and easy to understand, as deductions from name and colour of the liar killers.

I’ll also take this chance to explain some basic terminologies even readers forget

To use a skill is to forego a Guaranteed Successful Judgement Opportunity. This mechanism is both subjective and objective, and needs the following criterias to be fulfilled

   * First, prove that the liar lied through your deduction. Even if someone lies but you aren’t able to prove it, you can’t activate your skill. Thus it is important to correctly deduce where and about what has the target lied.
   * This is unlike judging, where you can judge freely without needing proof. Instead the shooter uses his statement “You Lied” as a collateral.
   * Second, subjectively decide to forego the opportunity to judge that lie, i.e, you can’t judge the person to death if you have used a skill with that lie instead

Also I’ll explain Gui Yus’ and how they work

   * A Gui Yu is an unknown lie that has been proven true by circumstantial evidence. This means that nobody knows if the statement is true or false, but the creator of the Gui Yu has made evidences through physical or other means to forge the lie as truth
   * As long as a Gui Yu is not broken, anyone stating the Gui Yu statement is not a liar. Anyone who doesn't break the Gui Yu but proclaims the Gui Yu is a lie, is a liar
   * A Gui Yu can be broken by exposing anyone within the current space to the truth through valid evidence. Another Gui Yu can’t be made to break this Gui Yu and only true evidence can be used to do so. A Gui Yu break is effective across the whole space, regardless of whether the others have seen the evidence or not.
   * If the creator of the Gui Yu had uttered the Gui Yu before its creation or when it was created and sustained, the creator is judged to be a liar
   * If the listener/others utter the Gui Yu after it has been broken, they are also deemed liars. But if they had previously uttered it during the time it wasn’t broken, it isn't judged a lie even after the Gui Yu is broken.

Let me also explain how multiple personalities work in hell.

   * If the person had multiple personalities before death, each personality is judged as an independent person by the hell. But they can still occupy the same body.
   * Thus due to the nature of them being judged as a separate person, the liar killer skill also changes depending on who is in control of the body and each personality gets its own liar killer skill
   * When entering an instance, the personality in control matching to the instance gets to stay in control throughout the instance.
   * The personalities don’t share memories nor points earned

At last, let me explain the skill effects of all the 3 Three Star players

   1. Keep Silent : Causes a countdown timer of 5 minutes to appear in the hand of the players. Before the countdown ends, the player must either converse with someone or deduce information regarding the dungeon.

Converse here means that both people doing the conversation must reply to each other. The deduction done under the skill effect can be judged as lies. Circumvents the established rule that wrong deductions regarding the dungeon can’t be judged as a lie

   2. Emperor Emerges Across the Three Rivers : Allows the user to slowly weaken and usurp control the pre existing skills in space. The degree of weakening and usurpation of control can be done through deducing information about the dungeon/Instance.

After gaining control of skills present, the user can enhance the effects of controlled skills proportional to his star level.

   3. Disorderly : Causes the brain to receive the feedback of the second action before the action has even taken place. Thus the affected would never register their second action performed. Easily understood as a sort of time skip in the cognition of those affected
