What the Fuck was Sinful Hospital

Genuinely what was the author on when writing 18LOH😭. Sinful Hospital has to be one of the most difficult situations I have read and I'm not even halfway done with the web novel. At minimum you need to be a high tier character just to pass the first secret chamber. First you'll need to figure out the fake supermarket Gue Yu and the three layered deception between Li Zhitong and Lin Xiuyuan. Then in the actual first secret chamber you need to kill most of the low tier players, figure out the goal of the secret chamber, counter Li Zhitong's liar skill, and figure out Lin Xiuyuan's Gui Yu all while facing the three three-star player's liar skills. Those skills were losing a piece of common sense if you don’t talk for five minutes and your speech must be either deducing the secret chamber or conversing with someone, seeing your second action before you're first, and having those two other skills taken controlled by Di Yu Zhi Yi. The amount of reasoning and mental fortitude you need just to pass this is insane.

Next is the second secret chamber which focuses on stocks. I was so confused on this one since I know nothing about stocks or economics but in short you need to figure out that there are two company fives, one being another player and the other being the tycoon. Then you need to figure out and eliminate the player that controls company five all while getting rid of any remaining player that isn't the three-stars. After all that you could try to eliminate the three-star players but at most you can get one of them. They are completely immune to any emotional lie so you'd need to get them to tell an unknown lie which took Chen Ran and Qiu Yinong over 200 tries. The three players will immediately know if Wheel of Time was used through your body language and different behaviors you display.

Finally there is the third secret chamber which has multiple sub-chambers like in Lonely Revenge. In the first sub chamber you need knowledge in Chinese history to figure out which platforms are safe and not safe while trying to kill the three-star players. The next sub-chamber I can't even fully grasp. There was another group of players, the meteor illusion in which your bodies were being switched and there were npc version of you and the two other players, and then there is the three layered illusion, and finally the space decryption game where you need to lay a trap to kill Di Wu Zhi Yi and even then you need to figure the trap he placed using the boss's liar skill to occupy dead bodies to finally kill this man.

This is such a short and simplified version of this instance but the point is that no realistic or semi-realistic character is passing Sinful Hospital. The amount of layers of deception, manipulation, strategy, and reasoning there are I can't comprehend most of it. I fear what monsters Chen Ran is going to have to face in the future.
