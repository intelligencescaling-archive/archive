Introduction

The feat involves various versions of the same two persons. I will denote them as A, B, C alphabetically along with their names (eg Fake Chen Ran A, Fake Chen Ran B), and the original versions of themselves as Chen Ran and Geng Hao

The objective of this virtual game is to score the highest kill count. There will be a limited amount of other versions of the pair spawning in that room at every 5 min interval. The room is connected to a clone of the same room/chamber, and so on. These chambers/rooms are self-contained, which makes Liar skills used in these rooms not affect the other rooms.

These are fake versions of themselves, but have the same intellect and memory upto the entry of this virtual game. But fake versions produced are a copy of the previously produced batch. I.e, if one batch of them lie, the subsequent copies also carry that lie. Also the produced fake copies are copies of the original's physical state before they entered.

None of them can hurt each other without using their Liar Killer. But original version of the players can also be killed by the fake copies and vice versa, and all versions of them are driven by survival and self preservation (The copies won’t give up just because they aren’t original).

Thus ideally to win this game, Chen Ran has to score higher than Geng Hao, should not get killed by other versions of himself while also making a plan sufficiently robust enough to kill other n versions of himself. The way he achieves this is through a combination of First move advantage, information obfuscation, multilayered deception and exploiting his own weakness.

Reality and Fantasy

We see True Chen Ran and Fake Chen Ran A bet a cigarette on the outcome of their plan and since True Chen Ran won the bet, we can infer that he was able to anticipate Fake Chen Ran B and C's behavior in the faceoff.

The plan utilised the effects of Geng Hao’s Liar Killer Skill - Three Steps Forward, Two Steps Back. The true effects of the skill are as follows

1. When a person is hit by this skill, for every 3 actions they do, they do the last two actions in reverse, thus only the first action remains undisturbed

2. Eg: If I wanted to pet my dog through 4 steps
   1. Bend down
   2. Extend my hand
   3. Touch the dog
   4. Massage the dog
After getting hit by the skill, what actually happens are steps 2.1, 2.2 and 2.3. But then, I touch the dog and retract my hand, and staying in a bent down position

3. If only 2 steps are less is required to perform an action, it is not affected by the skill at all

4. After 3 steps are performed and 2 steps are reversed, a fantasy is triggered

5. This fantasy causes the affected person to hallucinate something they think will happen subconsciously. Eg : If I think the dog will wag its tail after I pet it, even if I had only touched the dog(step 3) and reversed my actions till I am in a bent position, I’ll hallucinate the dog wagging its tail. Even though I never massaged it.

6. The hallucinations can be differentiated from reality, as hallucinations won’t have emotions and feelings attached to it.

7. When the affected person speaks 3 sentences continuously : A, B and C, to retract 2 of their sentences, they repeat B,C again, retracting A. Then they repeat C again, retracting B too. This works opposite to the normal skill direction, with the 1st two sentences being retracted instead of last two. But you can’t judge someone as a liar with this if all 3 sentences were true

8. The effects, along with the skill name itself, makes it very simple but deceptive in nature, because the first effect can be guessed with the skill name, but the hallucinations can’t be
