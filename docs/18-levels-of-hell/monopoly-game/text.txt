Rules of the "Big Rich Man's Game" (Economic Game).
I. Game Structure & Objectives
* Nine players stand on nine separate suspended platforms (2m²), with a chasm below.
* Mechanism of life and death:The existence of the floorboard is tied to the existence of the company. If the company goes bankrupt, the floorboard disappears, and the player falls into a death abyss.
* Role:Each player owns a random company (numbered from 1 to 9).
* Target:Accumulate wealth, keep the company from going bankrupt, and eliminate rivals (especially the anonymous "Rich Man" enemy) through economic attacks.
II. Production Operating Mechanism (Supply Chain)
The game establishes a circular supply chain to force transactions:
1. Product:There are 9 types of products (ABCDEFGHM). One company's product is the raw material for the next company (e.g., company A sells to company B, B sells to C...).
* NoteThe company number is DIFFERENT from the product. Which player owns which company is predetermined at the beginning of the game; only the product type needs to be selected. (For example: A player owns company number 1, but their product is not necessarily A).
2. Trading Principles:
   * Transactions with the System (Market):
      * Purchasing raw materials: Price 100/unit (High).
      * Product Sale: Price 80/unit (Low).
      * Consequences:Profits will be extremely low or even result in losses if operating costs increase.
   * Transactions with Players:To optimize, company A needs to sell to company B at a price of approximately 90 (higher than the market price of 80, lower than the market price of 100).
3. Production process:
   * Production time: 5 minutes/batch/employee.
   * Basic costs: Materials + Employee salaries + Equipment maintenance.
   * Equipment durability: Decreases gradually with each production run. If it falls below 60, it must be repaired or replaced.
III. Stock Market Mechanism
This is the core part of eliminating other players.
1. Initial valuation:Each company has 1 million shares, priced at 10 points per share. The total value is 10 million.
2. Stock price fluctuations:
   * Discount:When owners sell off shares, lay off employees en masse, or fail to handle a natural disaster.
   * Price Increase/Recovery:When the company expands its production scale (hiring more people, buying more machines).
3. Mandatory Sales Rules:
   * If the owner holds shares at even numbers: 80%, 60%, 40%The system will automatically force a sale.20%Shares are offered to other players on the market for them to buy.
   * Tip:Players can buy back a small percentage of shares themselves to avoid being forced to sell (e.g., holding 81%).
4. Control:
   * If a group of shareholders holdsover 51%With shares, they have the right to convene a "Shareholders' Meeting" and decide on the company's strategy (expanding production or distributing dividends) regardless of the wishes of the owner (legal entity).
IV. Mechanism of Murder
The game forces players into an economic arms race; there's no room for standing still.
1. Cost inflation:Employee salaries and equipment maintenance feesgradually increasing over time(due to age and wear and tear).
2. Expansion pressure:
   * Without expansion, rising costs will erode profits -> Bankruptcy.
   * To expand (hire more people, buy new equipment costing 8 million VND), the company needs a huge amount of capital.
3. Stock dilution:
   * When expanding production on a large scale, the company willissue additional shares(For example: adding 1 million shares).
   * If the former owner does not have the money to buy these new shares, their ownership stake will be halved (e.g., from 51% down to 25.5%), resulting in a loss of control over the company.
4. Random event:An event (natural disaster, scandal, etc.) happens every 5 minutes. If you don't spend money to deal with it within 1 minute, the stock price will drop and the warehouse will be damaged.
Tactical Summary
* Method 1 (Crushing through cost):Attackers acquire >51% of shares -> Force the target company to continuously expand production -> Operating costs skyrocket -> Force the company to forgo dividend payments -> The target company runs out of cash to pay salaries/maintenance ->Bankrupt.
* Method 2 (Acquisition at Low Price):Spreading rumors or negative events/layoffs to drive the stock price down to the bottom (e.g., 5 points) -> Buying up all shares -> Forcing production expansion to make the price recover (15 points) -> Selling to take profits or using shareholder rights to manipulate the market.

1.2.3 The God of the Underworld Breaks His Tail to Survive:
