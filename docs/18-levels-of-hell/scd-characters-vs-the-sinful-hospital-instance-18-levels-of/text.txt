SCD Characters vs Sinful Hospital Instance

Earlier today I made a post about how crazy the Sinful Hospital Instance was and now I want to go over which characters can or can't clear the instance. Please keep in mind that I do not have a full understanding of the instance and I may or may not overrate it.

The Rules:

The character will be replacing Chen Ran in each situation. This means that they will have the same amount of knowledge about Hell, it's rules, and his liar killer. This also means they will be partners with Qiu Yinong

To clear they must not only figure out the story of the instance but kill every single player involved to keep Eternal Kingdom and Wheel of Time safe

To make things fair each character will have the sufficient knowledge needed to deduce each secret chamber otherwise unless they're Chinese or have a wide array of knowledge they will not clear the beginning of the First Secret Chamber

They will all be in character with the exception of willing to kill other players to survive

Joe Goldberg - stops at Fake Supermarket no diff. Bum level reasoning and impulsive that he's easily telling an emotional lie and getting judged

Oreki Houtarou - stops at Fake Supermarket no diff. No where near has the reasoning to dispel the Gui Yu and likely gets caught in a common sense lie

Walter White - stops at Fake Supermarket no-low diff. He is not getting through the deceptions and will get judged for an emotional lie

Dexter Morgan - stops at Fake Supermarket low diff. Might be able to see through the first layer of the deception but I don't believe he'll get further. I don't see him telling an emotional lie so a common sense lie will get him judged

Jibril - stops at Fake Supermarket mid diff. Despite what some may believe Jibril is not all that compared to the rest of NGNL. She has decent enough reasoning to see through the first two layers of the Fake Supermarket but I don't believe she'll see through the third. She won't survive because of her arrogant nature as a Flugal and looks down on humans. The other players will definitely see this and get her to lie

Light Yagami - stops at Fake Supermarket high diff. Simply won't be able to see through the third layer but it'll be tricky to get a lie out of him. Likely through attacking his ego

L. Lawliet - stops at the First Secret Chamber mid diff. He has the reasoning to get through the Fake Supermarket and most of the first secret chamber but just won't be able to figure out Lin Xiuyuan's Gui Yu under the pressure of the three liar skills

Ozymandias - stops at first secret chamber mid-high diff. This might be the craziest take but Ozy is not that impressive in reasoning and for that he won't be figuring out Lin Xiuyuan's Gui Yu

Akiyama Shinichi - stops at the First Secret Chamber high diff. He'll deduce through the three layers of deception in the Fake Supermarket and go through the First Aecret Chamber. But like always those three players fuck him over leading to judgement

Patrick Jane - stops at the First Secret Chamber high diff. Jane can get through it quite easily until you guessed it those three players

Shiro - stops at the First Secret Chamber very high diff. For arguments sake let's say she can be functional without Sora. She's clearing the Fake Supermarket and most of the First Secret Chamber with little difficulty. It's only when she needs to figure out Lin Xiuyuan's Gui Yu that she falls short. She can figure out but the problem lies with those three three-star players. Not only is Shiro a child but one vulnerable to emotional manipulation and deception. Those three players and there skills will get Shiro to slip up and tell a lie

Willard Wright - stops at the Second Secret Chamber high diff. He definitely has the reasoning and AC to clear the Fake Supermarket, First Secret Xhamber, and most of the second up until he has to get rid of Li Zhitong and Zheng Meili. Even if he does then he definitely won't be able to get rid of Duo Jie

Ranpo Edogawa - stops at the Second Secret Chamber high diff. He has the reasoning and to clear both the Fake Supermarket and First Secret Chamber with some difficulty. And he gets through most of the Second Secret Chamber until he needs to kill Duo Jie which he simply won't be able to do

Erika Furudo - stops at the Third Secret Chamber. The furthest we've gotten but Erika is a good all-rounder with the AC sufficient enough to clear the fake supermarket and the first and second secret chambers. I think she can actually most of the third secret chamber until the three layer illusion. Her reasoning is good but she won't figure out the second layer

CTW L - stops at the third secret chamber. L runs it again but this time with better reasoning and sufficient enough AC to get to the third secret chamber. Though I think he loses at maybe meteor illusion and definitely the three layer illusion getting maybe to the third layer

Osamu Dazai - stops at the third secret chamber very high diff. To keep it brief, Dazai simply won't beat Di Wu Zhi Yi in Space Decryption or maybe even three layer illusion

Fyodor Dostoevsky - stops at the third secret chamber very high diff. I just can't see him figuring out Di Wu's Gui Yu at the start of Space Decryption or even the three layer illusion

Battler Ushiromiya - clears high diff. The first person I believe can pass the Sinful Hospital Instance. He is a good all-rounder much better than Erika. He also has experience with illusions and word games

Sora - clears mid-high diff. Like with Shiro, Sora will be able to operate normally with her. Sora scales super high in pretty much everything I actually think he could low diff

Beatrice - clears low diff

Supreme Administrator - clears low diff

Comp Nobody - clears very low diff

This list is not in order of who I think is smarter. This instance requires such high tier scaling of certain categories that even if I scale a character high (for example Ozymandias) that because they don't scale high in reasoning they will lose. The problem isn't the instance. Sinful Hospital is not very hard to figure out for characters mid tier and higher. It is the players and info gap that are the problem. Two-star players at minimum showcased mid tier level feats and the three-stars can all be scale highly and liar skills that work really well with each other that increases the difficulty tremendously.
