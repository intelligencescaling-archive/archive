Sorting Alice in Borderland Part 1 into Difficulty Categories (Manga)

Rules are pretty simple for how I'm sorting these, and I'm only doing the first half of Alice in Borderland for now because I'm writing this close to midnight and I need to go to bed.

Sorting will of course be a case-by-case basis, so in the event that a particular Game Doesn't fit neatly into a category, it will be taken as a unique case and will be decided upon as fairly as possible.

Neg Difficulty- The Game is so simple That any average Joe could walk in there and easily clear the game without any issue.

No Difficulty- As long As you're aware of how to beat the Game, you won't have any issue.

Low Difficulty- Even if you Know the solution, anyone would struggle a bit when it comes to this Game.

Mid Difficulty- Only characters in the Smart Character Debate Community would have little struggle when it comes to this.

High Difficulty- Yumeko, Yuichi, and Ayanaokoji are all beginning to doubt their own abilities.

Extreme Difficulty- Only high end SCD characters like Baku and maybe Akiyama are clearing this.

Insane Difficulty- This Game is essentially impossible bar some kind of miracle kicks in.

I don't think Alice in Borderland is going to reach the high end of the Difficulty spectrum, But I'm setting up these rules in case of future posts, where maybe I eventually start ranking the traps in the Saw movies for all I know.

Here we go-

3 of Clubs- No Difficulty- This Game Isn't as simple as it seems. Honestly. You need to pay attention to where the flaming arrows are coming from, and on the off chance you happen to guess a question correctly, there aren't going to be any flaming arrows, which means you better be good at figuring out which direction is South. We also don't get to see what happens if this Game has more than 4 Players Or what kind of other paper fortunes existed, so there's no telling what difficulties might arise if you were thrown into this. Still, paying attention to the clues on the cards and paying attention to the direction the arrows are coming from should help.

5 of Spades- Low Difficulty. Figuring out the exact door Will be a struggle, as well as having to avoid the horse guy.

2 of Hearts- No Difficulty. I don't think this really needs to be explained.

7 of Hearts- Mid Difficulty. Regardless on if your goal is just to be the only survivor, or save everyone, It will be hard to get everyone to try and cooperate, And most likely someone is going to get aggressive.

4 of Clubs- No Difficulty. If you know the twist, explanation isn't really needed for why this gets this category.

4 of Diamonds- No Difficulty. Again, this is literally just a riddle. In fact, I'm pretty sure this manga didn't even invent this riddle, as I first heard this when I was a kid, so I think you could literally Chuck some random people into this room who've never even heard of this manga and they'd be able to solve it.

6 of Spades- Mid Diff. Honestly, this "Game" Could go even higher, as it's literally being thrown into a lion's den containing animals worse than and including lions, and having to survive. Your survival is pretty much entirely dependent on whether you actually brought weapons with you, but I don't think it's necessarily impossible to win if you go in not knowing what you're doing. After all, it is "only" a 6 of Spades.

4 of Hearts- Low Difficulty. You can't really say this is No Difficulty. You can just answer in the majority until question 10, but that will still give you painful electric shocks that will be hard to sit through, and even once you get to question 10, you'll have to start frantically looking through the pamphlet which doesn't exactly guarantee your survival if you don't have a clear head at the moment.

10 of Hearts- Low Difficulty. As long as you know the twist, you shouldn't have much problem with this. The only real difficulty comes from dealing with the other players that have been driven insane. At this point, which kind of has to be factored in, as this Game Have the label increase in difficulty specifically because of the state Insanity of the other Players at the Beach.

That's it for Part 1. I'll cover the rest of the Games in Part 2 I hope!
