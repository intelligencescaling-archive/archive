Sorting Alice in Borderland Part 2 minutes into Difficulty Categories (Manga)

3 of Spades- Low Diff. It's not quite clear what's going on here as it only lasts a few panels, But it's clear. The objective is to book it through an amusement park while there are people coming at you with weapons. So there definitely will be some difficulty to outrun/outthink those guys, but as long as you run like your life depends on it because it literally does, you should be fine.

6 of Diamonds- Mid Diff. There are no tricks here. You literally just have to play Blackjack against 5 or so other Players who could all individually be smarter or luckier than you, or they might not be and it ultimately depend on who you're up against. If you're up against the specific Players from the manga, you'll probably also struggle a bit, so you got to learn Blackjack quick and you got to think and maybe luck your way through this one.

7 of Spades- High Diff. Once the play starts collapsing in on itself, you're really going to struggle. If you react quickly, and begin running away the moment you read the instructions you might have a chance, But you probably won't make it out of the building without struggling a little bit.

King of Clubs- Mid Diff. Honestly, I don't think this one is really that hard. If you keep two goalies instead of one, make usage of the fact that you can be a human weapon by grabbing your opponents and shocking them which can buy your teammates some time to do something else, and if you ignore the items because they're rather useless compared to the potential 10,000 point pole in the middle of the battlefield and also your opponents set up the Game so they all probably know where they all are anyway- Point is, Yes this Game is a struggle But I think the main struggle comes from finding these loopholes I just listed. If you're aware of them going into the game, or if you just managed to figure them out quickly, you'll probably be able to devise a plan that'll get you a win. It's a struggle, but not as much as the 7 of Spades.

King of Spades- High Diff. Again, this one is very hard. It's especially difficult depending on what your goal is. Is your goal just to survive, or do you have to win? We find out that there's only one winner, or maybe you can have multiple winners if the final blow is being ambiguously done by multiple people, But the point is anyone who's not directly participating in the winning shot is not getting the 13-Day Visa. If your goal is to get this 13-Day Visa, well that's why this is in High Diff. If your goal is just to survive and hide until someone else can do it, or maybe help them out winning, Then it gets significantly easier, which is why this Only gets High Diff, especially seeing as there aren't any absurd mathematical loopholes you have to be thinking about when going on the battlefield- You just have to know that it is a literal battlefield. And obviously, being in excellent physical shake is going to give you a huge advantage here as Agni Was apparently able to dive out of the building that was getting blown up by a grenade and survive.

Jack of Diamonds- High Diff. I consider putting this higher, but I feel even low end SCD characters Could come up with some schemes or manipulation tactics to win here. You essentially just have to be really good at Mahjong (Akagi negs this), And be able to surpass or otherwise just trick the Jack of Diamonds himself. It's quite unfortunate that there can only be one winner here apparently, which adds the ambiguous scenario of does it count as "clearing the Game" if you and the Jack of Diamonds are both along the losers? I personally wouldn't count that as a win, so if you want to be the only winner, that's another reason why this is in High Diff.

King of Diamonds- High Diff. Again, I considered putting this one higher. I think this is objectively the most difficult Game as it has a lot to do with luck, but if Chishiya can clear this considering the position he was in, I think most SCD characters could. I think there's a huge chance this is going to result in a loss though.

Jack of Hearts- High Diff. Another difficult one, as this one also is quite luck-based and has to deal with manipulating a bunch of other players and not being manipulated by them. Winnable though.

Queen of Hearts- No Diff. Kind of bittersweet that the final Game is the only one to be a No Diff one. All you have to do is not drink the tea. You don't even have to be good at Croquet as it's made abundantly clear that you don't have to win 3 round of Croquet- You just have to play them. So just don't drink the tea. It should be pretty obvious that you shouldn't do that from the beginning, and even though Arisu did it anyway, he "technically" managed to fight through it with the "help" of Usagi with extreme psycological difficulty. So if you just don't drink the tea, this is incredibly easy.

9 of Hearts- Low Diff. Gotta throw in the bonus one too. Once you know the twist, getting through this is pretty easy, but even if you Know about the twist, It might be a little bit difficult to get the other players to cooperate. All you have to do is get them to pay attention to the chamber things, and then once everyone realizes what this Game Is all about, it just becomes more tedious than anything else. I'm putting this at Low Diff rather than No Diff As there's an ever so slight chance the other players might not be that cooperative, But it's quite easy and winnable even if you don't know the trick.
