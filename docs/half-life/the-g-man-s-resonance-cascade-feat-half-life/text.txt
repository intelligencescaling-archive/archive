Not sure why The G-Man was never discussed here

G-Man from Half-Life franchise is bascially a text-book high tier SCD character, and I believe he scales close to John Constantine, Fang Yuan, The Tenet Protagonist and Friend.

His Resonance Cascade feat is quite phenomenal when it comes to planning, strategy complexity, indirect and mass manipulation, psychological warfare, foresight, cold reading and psychological profiling.

Here's an extensive analysis and explanation of the Resonance Cascade feat:

The Nihilanth was an incredibly powerful leader of the alien forces in Xen. It hides out in the borderworld, actively keeping the interdimensional rifts closed to hide from the massive Combine Empire. Because of this psychic block, G-Man and his employers could not directly access Xen. To ensure that the employers could take control of Xen without a direct assault, G-Man designed a scenario by playing Black Mesa's top scientists, a hyper-advanced interdimensional empire, and an ancient alien god against each other, thus triggering a multidimensional domino effect.

The plan moves in action when G-Man personally delivered the Xen crystal (GG-3883) — the largest, purest and most volatile sample in existence — to Black Mesa Research Facility. By introducing a sample with such an extreme, unstable energetic threshold, he ensured that human ego and ambition would come to play. Instead of forcing the experiment, G-Man manipulates the facility's administrator, Dr. Wallace Breen, by exploiting his desperate career ambitions, succeeding at pressuring him to deliver results as Breen personally overruled all standards safety protocols, forcing the engineering team to boost the Anti-Mass Spectrometer to 105% even though senior scientists like Eli Vance adamantly objected to running the machine beyond its maximum safety buffer zone. Anticipating that the facility wouldn't back down from a breakthrough, G-Man walked up to Eli Vance moments before the experiment and whispered "Prepare for unforseen consequences." Deliberately letting a top scientist know that something catalysmic was bound to happen, yet positioned them so they were entirely powerless to stop it.

After outsmarting Black Mesa, G-Man purposefully chose Gordon Freeman to physically push the crystal into the beam. G-Man foresaw that Freeman possessed the exact survival capabilities needed to survive the ensuing chaos and act as his ultimate pawn.

After Gordon inserted the crystal, G-Man knew that the resulting Resonance Cascade factured spacetime, causing the "Portal Storms". He anticipated that the Nihilanth would use the fracture to invade Earth out of desperation to escape the Combine Empire. He also knew that this massive tear in reality would act as a beacon, luring the Combine to Earth, initiating the Seven Hour War and setting up the long-term cosmic agenda of his employers.

The Resonance Cascade was a perfectly executed trap. By creating the rift, G-Man used the apocalyptic scenario as an extraction mission and a crudible to test and recruit Gordon. He forced Gordon into a corner where his only path to survival was by traveling to Xen and assassinating the Nihilanth, tranferring control of the borderworld to the employers, and then acquired Gordon, his now hyper-lethal and un-tracktable asset, safely storing him in stasis to be deployed decades later to dismantle Combine assets.

When the military's Hazardous Environment Combat Unit (HECU) failed to contain the alien invasion and a Black Ops strike team failed at attempting to sterilize the facility with a themonuclear bomb as Corporal Adrian Shephard disarmed the warhead, G-Man personally reactivated the bomb (and later detained Shephard in statis), ensuring that Black Mesa, as well as all remaining evidence of his involvement including witnesses, was vaporized from the face of the Earth.
