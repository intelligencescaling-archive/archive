Yamcha's Plan

After noticing that his opponent first appears as a bat, then as a pale man, and witnessing him suck Krillin's blood, Yamcha concludes that he is a vampire. He immediately devises a strategy to defeat him.

Strategy

He needs Upa and Puar.

Since the enemy is a vampire, he decides to exploit the classic vampire weaknesses: garlic, protection against bites, and the cross.

Knock Dracula into the lake, resulting in a ring out (disqualification).

To deceive Fortuneteller Baba, Yamcha asks for two fighters to be allowed to participate. Goku argues that, because they are so small and weak, they should be allowed to fight together.

The Plan

Dracula will approach them, and Upa will blow garlic breath toward him.

Startled by the smell, Dracula will target the second fighter instead. At that moment, Puar will transform into a porcupine, preventing the bite.

While Dracula is stunned, Upa will form the shape of a cross with his body to frighten him even further.

As Dracula flees in bat form, taking advantage of his physical weakness, Puar will transform into a giant hand and swat him directly into the lake, winning the match by ring out.

C6 = Those weaknesses will be used.

Q5 = Will those weaknesses alone be enough?

Q6 = Then how should they be used?
