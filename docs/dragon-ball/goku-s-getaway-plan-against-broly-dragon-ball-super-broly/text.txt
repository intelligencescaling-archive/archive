Goku Getaway Plan/Strategy analysis

After Broly fights with Vegeta Goku figures Broly isn't a bad guy and he is fighting with his saiyan instinct rather than fighting to kill. On top of it Broly a saiyan is fighting for Frieza. It only makes sense broly is just being used by Frieza here without his knowledge. Goku also calculates Broly growth rate to his natural state and comes to conclusion that broly can't surpass SSJ blue

So Goku tries to calm down broly by dragging the fight and using SSJ Blue as a last resort. While Broly's main ability is his adaptability which can make dragging the fight illogical but one can grow only so much. Broly isn't that strong compared to Goku and Vegeta in terms of physicality on top of it Broly rapid growth is an ability not a natural process so there will be a point where his natural body wouldn't be able to handle the power he possesses also since broly growth rate is from his adaptability if he fights a weak opponent his strength would adjust to that of the opponent so Goku using SSJ Blue from the get go would only make Broly not hold back his power and he would feel threatened by SSJ Blue powers so his growth rate will increase more and would eventually surpass SSJ Blue and that's pretty much wraps for everyone. It's better to use low power to limit Broly growth as much as possible. It's also the reason why Goku could somewhat fight with broly in his base form when broly just a second ago was overpowering SSJ God vegeta.

Goku 1st fights in his base form knowing broly has no intention to kill. He charges at it when broly over powers his punch he spins back to absorb the impact of the attack. After some exchange Broly overpowers him Goku then uses kamehameha followed by barrage of ki blast to keep distance between him and Broly.

Broly catches up to him landing a massive blow Broly then powers up so goku follows it himself powering up to SSJ as the power gap now is way too big for skills to come into play with that 1st part of the strategy ends. In his SSJ form he again plays mostly defensive with offense sprinkled here and there.

Avoiding close quarter combat as he distances himself then attacks then distance himself then attacks. This has four benefits.

* This saves his own energy as he is mostly playing defensive, avoiding hits and the energy required for his own hits against Broly.

* This avoids physical damage from Broly attacks and recoil from hitting Broly.

* This drains Broly energy as he is constantly on offensive which already would require a great amount of energy addition to that since Goku is creating distance it would take even more energy than normal to play offensively.

* This also helps Goku attack potency since there is more kinetic energy into his punches and kicks due to the distance he himself created which gives him time to accelerate fully.

As expected Broly grows and surpasses his SSJ form so he uses the God form. He then uses an ability called GOD BIND to paralyze Broly movements and try to calm him down. It doesn't work instead broly once again grows in power Goku puts more power in GOD BIND but it also doesn't work instead Broly copies GOD BIND and reverses it on Goku as he prepares to land a punch on Goku. Since Borly engulfs goku by his own ki means his ki is also protecting Goku from attacks and For broly punch to land on Goku broly would need to turn off GOD BIND figuring this Goku prepares himself for the punch and by the time the punch land Goku was a heavy guard so Goku shrugs it off while also performing a counterattack immediately with a judo throw.

And Again after some exchanges Broly grows more stronger surpassing SSJ God. Piccolo, sensing the event taking place telepathically, talks to Goku about it as we learn that Goku was planning to use piccolo as a getaway if broly grows to point where they can't beat him.

Goku then uses SSJ Blue and gains upper hand on him as expected broly has reached his limit and can't catch up to Goku.

Frieza intervenes, killing broly father to trigger in SSJ. Making broly surpass his limit in unconventional methods. Broly far surpasses Goku SSJ Blue form. Vegeta asks to team up which  Goku agrees, yet both of them still weren't strong enough to handle SSJ Broly so Goku files towards Frieza vegeta follow his lead.

Making Frieza the target for Broly as a last screw you moment for killing Broly's father. Since Broly was consumed by rage he would fight anyone in his way. Goku then takes Vegeta's hand and teleports both of them to Piccolo.

Goku then asks Piccolo if he got any senzu beans so they can regain their HP to fight broly again but Piccolo replies he doesn't so Goku asks vegeta to do fusion dance to fuse and become strong enough to defeat Broly. At 1st Vegeta disagrees obviously but Goku uses Vegeta's weakness to get him to agree to do the fusion dance. Mentioning bulma life being on the line.

Vegeta agrees and they manage to perform it in 3 tries which is quite impressive considering the requirements to pull it off. Their breathing, Their power, posture has to match perfectly.

With it they managed to stop Broly at the End

Broly is sent back to the planet Vampa Goku tracks his ki meaning he already stored Broly appearance and probably fighting style too in his mind. He teleports there with home capsules and senzu beans so Broly can live a healthy life on that miserable planet so he can train with him. He finds Broly friends with him, he builds trust with them and convinces them and Broly to be his training partner as a guy with a potential like Broly would be a waste if he dies of some disease in the deserts planet that  is Vampa.

The strategy had high chance of success rate and with little low chance of failure the plan was there to cover for it.
