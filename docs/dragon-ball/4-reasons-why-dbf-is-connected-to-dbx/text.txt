6 Reasons Why DBF is Connected to DBX
DB Fusions is connected to Xenoverse by Virtue of the Appearance of Towa and Mira
In the DBXV Story Arc, Mira and Towa are the primary antagonists that have appeared in order to free the Demon Realm and make Mira the Ultimate Warrior who will rule supreme as King of The Demon Realm. In Dragon Ball Fusions they appear yet again out of a space-time wormhole and play key roles and important plot points in the Fusions Story to further their agenda. What is important to take away from all this is that the Mira and Towa that appeared in the world of DB Fusions had originally come from the world of Dragon Ball Xenoverse and experienced it’s events. Here are some reasons as to why

Reason 1
In Dragon Ball Xenoverse, one of the major themes of the game was Mira being the King of the Demon World. In Dragon Ball Fusions, this major theme of Mira being the Demon King is also present as both Towa and Mira himself are consistently referred to as the King of the Demon Realm Mira. The events and reasons as to why Mira is the Demon King are present within the XV story as Mira was meant to succeed Dabura the original king of the Demon Realm. This is never the case for Heroes Mira as Heroes Mira never consistently refers to himself as the demon king nor does he intend to become the demon king and instead the Title is held by Mechikabura and later on by Fu.

Reason 2
In Dragon Ball Xenoverse, one of Towa’s primary objectives is to collect energy from fighters across different timelines and use it in order to make Mira the most powerful being in existence and the unstoppable Demon King. In Dragon Ball Z Kakarot and then Fusions this plotpoint is extended and continued as Towa still seeks to collect energy to make Mira the ultimate being with that information even being a part of their character Bios. In Heroes, while Towa initially wants to make Mira the ultimate being, her attention shifts away from very early on in the series and she know longer cares to make him the ultimate warrior but instead focuses on Mechikabura and then later on Fu. The energy she collects in Heroes is not even used to make Mira stronger but rather it is used to free Mechikabura and the demon realm

Reason 3
During Dragon Ball Fusions, Towa mentions her encounter with Trunks' future self and how he consistently gets in her and Mira’s way. This would be Future trunks from Xenoverse and not Xeno Trunks from Heroes.
XV Future Trunks consistently challenges and opposes Mira and Towa throughout the story and events of DBXV. In the DBH events however, Towa is killed after her very first encounter with Xeno Trunks and later resurrects herself as Darkness Towa (a form she consistently stays in til she gains her demon goddess form courtesy of Mechikabura). Throughout DB Fusions Towa is never seen in her Darkness Form even once and Mira who consistently transforms, even during the early stages of the DBH story, never transforms a single time in Fusions even when being beaten back on the brink of losing. Towa and Mira’s lack of transformations would be inconsistent with the Heroes Setting but definitely consistent with the Xenoverse Settings as in that storyline Towa and Mira never use transformations. If Fusions Towa and Mira had come from DBH then Towa would have been in her Darkness form as after Towa meets trunks for the first time she dies and comes back in Darkness Plus and Mira would have had access to Transformations and wouldn’t be referred to as weak and incomplete by Towa.
Also during the events of Xenoverse Towa and Mira have shown to be consistently aware and cautious of Beerus and Whis. In Heroes however they display no awareness of either character during the early stages and when they are aware of them they display no such catiousness as seen in DB Fusions. In DB Fusions both Towa and Mira are aware of Beerus and Whis and display extreme caution around them which would be inconsistent with heroes but consistent with Xenoverse

Reason 4
In DBXV, Mira has a personal vendetta to challenge and defeat Goku. This vendetta is also shown in Fusions, as Mira is eager to face Goku upon seeing him but then reconsiders seeing as Goku is just a Child. In Heroes, this vendetta that Mira has for facing Goku is absolutely not present and he only ever cares about serving and protecting Towa rather than proving his power against Goku or becoming the Demon King Ruler. Also, he never met Goku (Xeno) till the Dark Empire Saga. At which point Mira can transform and Towa is consistently in transformed states but never displays these Transformations in Fusions.

Reason 5
As shown in Reason 3, Towa mentions how Trunks in the future got in their way and wants payback for that. This is an important quote because the story of Xenoverse and the “Demigra Incident '' actually takes place in Age 850 which is far in the future even compared to the Future Trunks timeline which was erased in Age 796 as confirmed in Xenoverse. However, when it comes to the Heroes storyline, it was never actually established that the story takes place in the far future like Xenoverse does. This would further imply that this Towa and Mira come from the Xenoverse storyline rather than Heroes’ as they fought Time Patroller Trunks far off in the future in Age 850 where the Xenoverse storyline starts from, while in the Heroes storyline it is never actually established that the story starts in a future point in time.

Reason 6
When Mira and Towa appear in Fusions they do not have the Masked Saiyan accompanying them to collect energy for Mira. This would imply that Mira and Towa appeared in the Fusions timeline before they recruited Bardock into their ranks, meaning that the story of Fusions takes place in-between Xenoverse 1 & 2 after the events of DBZ Kakarot where Mira’s body was revived. In Heroes, Bardock was freed from his mind control by Trunks during one of their first encounters and fought alongside Trunks to defeat the forces of the Dark Demon Realm which included Mira with all his transformations and even Xeno Dabura, and where Towa was then killed and later resurrected as Darkness Towa. As mentioned in the previous reasonings, the Towa and Mira who appear in Fusions have a distinct lack of transformations, so Bardock not accompanying Towa and Mira in Fusions would more likely put them in a time between Xenoverse 1 & 2 as Bardock not accompanying them would mean that Towa would already be in her Darkness form and Mira could spam his transformations if these versions of Towa & Mira came from the Heroes timeline.

Conclusion
The conclusion here is that based on the evidence presented, Towa and Mira and fusions definitely came from the world of Dragon Ball Xenoverse as many of their dialogue and actions are consistently tying back to their motives and experiences from the Xenoverse Games. This therefore also ties a connection to both worlds in short meaning that the world of Dragon Ball Xenoverse and Fusions are connected.
