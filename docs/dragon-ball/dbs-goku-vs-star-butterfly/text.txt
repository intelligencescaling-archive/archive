While Goku has multiple forms and techniques, Star has literally dozens of spells she can call on at any time. The sheer versatility of her arsenal is just something that Goku can’t predict easily. And the fact that she can make up entirely new attacks with a thought only means that she could adapt and counter throughout the fight just like Goku (albeit not as effectively as him. But with her superior power & durability, it’d be even more unlikely Goku could overpower her physically anyway). You COULD argue a potential win for Goku through Hakai. However, there is a problem with this: Star being more powerful. We have actually seen Hakai fail on beings who are stronger than the user, like with Freiza. So arguing Hakai is an instant win move is very questionable not only with Star, but any other matchup with beings from Dragon Ball that can use it. Since attacks & Hax in Dragon Ball can actually be overwhelmed and overpowered by beings who are stronger than the user of said abilities, so the exact same should apply to Hakai being used on Star. Not to mention how Goku has a primitive version of Hakai and has messed up the technique before, as he said it himself. So not only would it be Unlikely that Hakai would work, but there is also another major factor to discuss: Stamina. Throughout Goku’s fights, stamina has been a major issue, since he has a vast, but still finite pool of Ki. Hell, that was a major part of the Tournament Of Power & the Moro Arc. So while Goku has mastered techniques and forms like Ultra Instinct and Super Saiyan Blue, he has still run out of Ki and stamina many times, leaving him severely weakened & unable to transform or use Ki attacks. Star is also mortal just like Goku and has been shown to tire out plenty of times like in her fight with Meteora where she released her Butterfly Form’s power. However, there is a weird work around. Star obviously has a finite amount of stamina, but you could argue that she has an unlimited supply of Magic. Since she never appears to EVER run out of it in the whole series, with or without the wand. Even after she specifically is completely worn out after her fight with Meteora, she still appears to be able to fire a narwhal blast. Furthermore, when Eclipsa uses her unnamed spell on a Solarian Warrior, she herself appears to be the one worn out, and not her magic. So yes, Star herself has stamina like any other normal living creature, however, her magic theoretically is an endless supply. This means that both combatants waiting each other out would essentially be meaningless, since they both have finite stamina. And even though Star has theoretically infinite magic, Goku would STILL be faster even in base form. So we have a weird conundrum where; Goku Is Infinitely Faster, But Star Is Infinitely More Powerful & Mor Durable. With Goku Also Being More Experienced, But Star Having The Superior Arsenal.
So with that being said…

WHO WINS?

You could make solid arguments for both, and this fight could definitely go either way. But honestly, If I had like a gun to my head or something, and they asked who I think wins… I’d honestly have to go with Star more often than not… Since she holds the primarily physical advantages and has more effective and more plentiful ways of taking Goku down than he does for her.
