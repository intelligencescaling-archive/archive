My Research on Goku in SCD - Part 1

Kid Goku is quite fast at learning, especially when it comes to things he has never seen before, such as technology, capsules, etc. Whenever he falls into a trap, he does not fall for the same trap again. He also replicated the Kamehameha movement in order to use it himself.

His fluid intelligence is quite high. He learns how money works, learns techniques within seconds, and can even create techniques within seconds.

Goku is good at reasoning, although sometimes he does not reach the conclusions he should. He normally develops methods for things he does not fully understand, such as using touches to identify women. He somehow remembers people's scents. His strongest reasoning category is induction.

His critical thinking is inconsistent. He questions things several times, but not always. His lateral thinking, however, is something else. He is EXTREMELY good at it. He comes up with plans very quickly and in ways that someone would not normally think of. For example:

Using his staff to gain height.

Realizing that he cannot replicate Jackie Chun's drunken movements and instead behaving like a monkey to counter him.

Using his staff to help Bulma get through a dangerous trap.

Using the Kamehameha as propulsion.

Using his tail to descend slowly.

His EQ is mediocre. He knows how to provoke people and recognize basic emotions, but he is not particularly impressive in this area. What he has the most is emotional commitment, such as promising Upa that he would revive his father.

In terms of social intelligence, he is very good in one specific area: social cognition. Someone whose only person to talk to had died, and who then spent a year living only with animals, quickly understood things such as conversation, negotiation, making promises, provoking, threatening, identifying good people, and even seems to possess a sort of natural charisma.

His Theory of Mind allows him to predict actions. He knows that Uulong will try to make himself very small in order to escape and threatens him before he does it. He also knows that Jackie Chun expects him to perform a specific Rock-Paper-Scissors sequence and alters it at the last moment.

His long-term planning is almost nonexistent, while his short-term planning is very good. When the vehicle they were using to escape the cave runs out of fuel, he immediately gets out and uses a Kamehameha to propel it. He also remembers that it is extremely cold outside and first asks Android 8 whether he feels cold. After learning that he does not, Goku breaks through the wall, enters the area where Android 8 and the monster are, and the monster gets frozen.

His strategy works similarly. When he is in trouble, he almost always uses strategy to reach his objective. For example, he appears directly in front of the robot suit of the new Red Ribbon Army leader, provokes him, and moves out of the way so that the robot hits the glass with its own attack.

In Sensory, he is brutal. He memorizes Roshi's scent and later identifies the rock through that scent. He also seems to know the scents of all of his friends.

In anticipation, he alters the sequence against Jackie Chun and realizes that the ninja only has four tatami mats, so he throws the last one he has.

His manipulation is mediocre. He is very provocative, does things such as pretending to be dead, and uses other people's emotional reactions to produce a specific action.

In deception, he basically pretends that he fell from Karin's tower, while actually holding onto the railing.

In reading, his cold reading is mediocre, but his hot reading is very good. He constantly accumulates information and counters it almost immediately.

In adaptability, he learns technology and social functions, adapts to losing his tail almost immediately despite the imbalance caused by losing a limb, and then adapts again when he gets it back. He learns techniques within seconds, learns very quickly from his mistakes, and after training, his movements become completely different.

His countering is extremely fast in terms of Battle IQ. He adapts to his opponent and consistently overcomes them depending on how different the situation is. If the opponent is stronger than him, he tends to attack them in creative ways; if they are weaker, he may simply mock them.

Weaknesses

Goku is naive and depends on his physical power for most situations.
