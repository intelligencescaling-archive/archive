A short explanation on Li Gang's Unification War strategy.

This whole strategy begins in an alternate timeline which never existed. It doesn't make sense, but hear me out.

There is law in the world, called law of Unpredictability. This law changes future, as long as someone knows about it.

Timeless Demon God knew the future. In that future, Li Gang saw through Elemental Emperor's trap to force him to reveal his capabilities. He deliberately sets up a trap by putting a psychological cue on himself that regardless of what happens, he would attack Shu Dynasty long ago.

Coming back to current timeline, due to knowing future from TDG's memories, Elemental Emperor knew in advance that Li Gang would attack Shu Dynasty. Thus, he prepares a forbidden land long before Li Gang even gets a birth.

The formation had a special set up, which can help someone to parasitize body.

Elemental Emperor himself couldn't parasitize Li Gang, because he was afraid of any possible trap He Fan might have set up (since He Fan created this Parasitic method).

Elemental Emperor saves Huang and Lei during the catastrophe and choses them to parasite Li Gang.

Li Gang had protection of Immortals and He Fan. To eliminate that factor, Elemental Emperor chose Huang and Lei during the short gap Li Gang had created with his catastrophe plan which had absolute freedom of thoughts (otherwise everyone's mind are continuously monitored). Thus without Immortal's realization, Li Gang was trapped by Immortals.

Immortals thought they had sufficient time to protect Li Gang, but Law of Isolation appears before the scheduled time, blocking Immortal's interference.

He Fan couldn't help Li Gang, because Elemental Emperor could block him (another advantage of not becoming parasite to Li Gang directly.

Rules of Parasite -

1. The body can take control of your consciousness at any time. But they don't access your memories in first stage. They can't keep taking possession of your body for over 1 day as well.

2. The only one way to survive the parasite is when parasite willingly sacrifices it's life and let's you live.

Elemental Emperor's plan was perfect, since Huang and Lei both had lost everything and there was no reason they would let Li Gang live.

Now, despite such a dangerous trap, Li Gang creates a strategy, which not only ensures his safety with 3 layers of contingencies. At the same time, his strategy also ensures that his initial goal of unifying the Northern Continent doesn't hinder due to Wu Dynasty's sudden gain of power.

During the maze of death, he deliberately uses only 10k warriors, because he knew Lei and Huang would ensure he remains on battle ground till they set up spatial formation to trap him within. This helped Li Gang later because Wei had large army to spare which was helpful in keeping Wu dynasty in check.

Li Gang had also obtained help from He Fan letting him know of Parasite method rules. However, due to info asymmetry, Li Gang didn't know whether it was from someone trustworthy or Elemental Emperor who was enemy. This strategy also ensured he confirm the validity of those memories.
