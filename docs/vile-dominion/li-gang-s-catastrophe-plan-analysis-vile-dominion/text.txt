# The Catastrophe Plan: Li Gang's Grand Strategy

The Catastrophe Plan was Li Gang's largest and most comprehensive scheme, spanning from early childhood to the destruction of Azure Mountain. Unlike ordinary plans that pursue a single objective, the Catastrophe Plan was designed to solve multiple problems simultaneously:

* Identify the hidden forces manipulating his life.
* Confirm the existence and involvement of the Elemental Emperor.
* Determine the relative strength of major players.
* Escape thought manipulation.
* Gain temporary freedom from immortal oversight.
* Master the Law of Unpredictability.
* Create conditions for future independence.

The plan was built upon a simple realization: Li Gang was being guided by forces beyond his understanding.

# Stage 1: Discovering the Board

As a child, Li Gang discovered a book concerning the Elemental Emperor and another concerning poison cultivation in the family library.

Several details immediately appeared suspicious.

His father personally arranged the library despite normally being too busy for such tasks. The Elemental Emperor book contradicted his family's beliefs, despite the Li family treating the Elemental Emperor as a divine figure. Most importantly, the poison cultivation path appeared precisely when Li Gang desperately needed a cultivation route that required little talent.

From these observations, Li Gang concluded:

1. His thoughts were likely being monitored.
2. People around him were being influenced.
3. Someone was deliberately paving a path for him.

The book also mentioned a treasure associated with the Elemental Emperor.

This led Li Gang to suspect that he had become a pawn in a conflict between extremely powerful entities. He therefore decided that before being drawn into that conflict, he needed support from someone capable of standing alongside those hidden forces.

Now there are few other observations:

1. Fluids in the world disappears and appears out of nowhere (like water is normally kept in vessel with water attracting attributes.
2. Light illuminates the whole world and every corner. Even enclosed room has light.

He used them to conclude that world has higher dimensions. Since there are higher dimensions, then higher dimensional beings may also exist. He also believed that his enemies are likely from their and that's why they can't directly take actions. Since Emperors disappeared for no reason, he believed they likely ended up going to those worlds. Note he also verifies and changes these conclusion later on. But I won't be discussing them, you need to read the novel to get those details.

This became the foundation of the Catastrophe Plan.

# Stage 2: Acquiring a Shield

When Li Gang encountered Wang Xueyao, he saw a potential solution.

There is no evidence that he knew about her beforehand. However, once the opportunity appeared, he immediately incorporated her into his calculations.

His interactions with Wang Xueyao were carefully designed to lower her guard, establish psychological leverage, and create a relationship that could later be exploited. Through this connection, Li Gang hoped to influence not only Wang Xueyao herself, but also the hidden powers observing him.

His ultimate goal was simple:

If Wang Xueyao truly possessed strength comparable to the forces manipulating his life, she could serve as a shield while he investigated the truth.

# Stage 3: The Monarch Inheritance Test

The Monarch Inheritance was not merely an opportunity.

It was a controlled experiment.

Li Gang intentionally remained under Wang Xueyao's protection for years while observing whether any threats emerged. His hypothesis was straightforward:

If no attacks occurred while he remained beside Wang Xueyao, but attacks began shortly after he left her protection, then the hidden forces feared Wang Xueyao's strength.

When danger appeared immediately after he separated from her, Li Gang treated it as confirmation.

The experiment provided two conclusions:

* The Elemental Emperor was likely connected to the Monarch Inheritance.
* Wang Xueyao possessed enough power to deter him.

With this information, the Catastrophe Plan entered its next phase.

# Stage 4: Creating the Deliberate Loophole

Li Gang understood that hidden interference could not be detected through a flawless plan.

To reveal interference, a controlled weakness was necessary.

This weakness was Huang Shu.

Li Gang deliberately constructed the Monarch Inheritance strategy so that Huang Shu's survival represented the sole meaningful loophole. He simultaneously manipulated events across all four regions to ensure that Huang Shu's death should have been inevitable.

Under normal circumstances, survival was virtually impossible.

However, Li Gang never intended Huang Shu to determine success or failure.

Even if Huang Shu survived, the primary objectives of the plan would still be accomplished.

Huang Shu existed as a diagnostic tool.

If the impossible occurred, it would reveal the presence of an outside force.

At the conclusion of the Monarch Inheritance plan, Li Gang learned that Huang Shu had survived.

This result demonstrated that hidden interference had occurred and provided one of the foundations for his later understanding of the Law of Unpredictability.

It should be noted that, Li Gang's plan could have continued even if Huang Shu died. However, Li Gang wanted to make things easier for Huang Shu, since Li Gang had likely deduced Law of Unpredictability too, but he was acting like he was too stupid for that, he had deliberately suppressed his cognition. As a result, he needed a convincing reason to "know" Law of Unpredictability and he used Huang Shu as the key pawn to make this realization at the end of this whole scheme.

Because if Huang Shu survives, despite having possibility of like 1 in a million, then it would mean that things in this world don't go as you predict. Even though Li Gang wouldn't know Law of Unpredictability, in the future, he would take actions considering them all, thus effectively countering it.

# Stage 5: Manufacturing a Catastrophe

The central feature of the Catastrophe Plan was the deliberate creation of a disaster.

Li Gang intentionally attracted hostility from every possible direction.

He concealed his true identity, manipulated public perception, created enemies, and placed himself at the center of multiple conflicts.

This behavior was not reckless.

It served a specific purpose.

Li Gang believed this method would attract Elemental Emperor's attention and he would later use it as a means to target his villages. Li Gang knew that Elemental Emperor wants to force Wang Xueyao to leave, as a result, he would unknowingly "cooperate" with Li Gang.

The intended result was the eventual destruction of Azure Mountain and the targeting of those connected to him.

Li Gang anticipated this possibility long before it occurred.

This was one reason he arranged for Wang Xueyao to protect his family.

The catastrophe itself was never the goal.

The catastrophe was bait.

# Stage 6: Forcing Every Hidden Player Onto the Stage

Li Gang understood that different factions possessed same objectives. The fact that they all are high level variables around him, he must possess something in their interest, and the only thing he could think of was his Special Ability. He wanted to use it as a bait to expose everyone to fight themselves. Note, he also considered all possible situations, even the ones where they all don't fight. It would still lead to his benefits in the end.

He brought Elemental Emperor into the game via Monarch Inheritance scheme (also read Stage 3 test)

He brought Wang Xueyao in the game by deliberately staying with her for 5 years in Monarch Inheritance, cultivating feelings, seeing through her scheme and making her think that he had fallen for her trap so that he could convince her into protecting his family for 10 years, which would help him engineer the situations in Stage 9.

He brought Immortal Zhu into the game via Stage 7 and Stage 8 deception and emotional manipulation.

# Stage 7: The Timeless Demon God Deception

The Central Continent phase introduced another critical component.

After receiving Lin Fan's memory inheritance, Li Gang made an extremely unusual decision.

He absorbed the memories but deliberately refused to process them.

The information remained inside his mind in an unprocessed, effectively encrypted state.

To ordinary cultivators, such behavior made no sense.

The immortals therefore reached a different conclusion.

They believed only the Timeless Demon God would possess enough confidence to ignore such an inheritance. Combined with Li Gang's knowledge and behavior, this caused them to increasingly suspect that he was the Timeless Demon God.

Li Gang encouraged these misconceptions.

The false identity became another layer of protection and another source of instability among his enemies.

# Stage 8: The Open Trap

The next phase involved Li Gang's declared goal.

Publicly, he promoted peace.

Privately, memories existed showing a different objective.

The contradiction was deliberate.

The immortals immediately recognized it as a trap.

If they attempted to interfere, they would expose the possibility that memories themselves could be read and all active thoughts could be altered.

The scheme was obvious. But they were forced to fall into it. If they don't, they would avoid their objective.

Li Gang forced them into a position where avoiding the trap was nearly impossible.

More importantly, he successfully provoked them into abandoning their usual caution since it was an insult to them. Imagine you have highly reputable position and you are forced to follow the will of an inferior being like a slave despite knowing what he intends to do. It was a provocation that created an emotional instability.

# Stage 9: The Catastrophe

Everything culminated at Azure Mountain. As Li Gang had engineered. Elemental Emperor manipulated the Jade Harbor City warriors into attacking Azure Mountain. Wang Xueyao provided resistance just as he had requested beforehand.

However, Elemental Emperor was also strong. He blocked Wang Xueyao, while the Warriors attacked and massacred Azure Mountain villages. It was all within Li Gang's expectations. He even foresaw that his family would be saved. Because no one can see future. So, in a certain unforeseen circumstances, they would need a leverage on Li Gang. As a result, they would protect his family and take them away in order to threaten Li Gang anytime in future!

Upon witnessing the destruction of Azure Mountain villages, both Li Gang's conscious and subconscious mind shut down due to despair. This was all orchestrated by Li Gang. Because he wanted to trigger his own Special Ability without even knowing that he had a Special Ability and he can use it! The triggering condition was shut down of Conscious and Subconscious thoughts.

This activated his Special Ability.

Li Gang wanted to force Immortal Zhu to take actions. He already knew that Wang Xueyao was stronger than Immortal Zhu (reason unknown) and his fabrication of Timeless Demon God identity gave Immortal Zhu enough confidence. Since Timeless was an enemy of Wang Xueyao, however, Immortal Zhu failed to realize that Wang Xueyao believed Li Gang is Creator because of his Special Ability which was Creator's Will. He couldn't have possibly known about Creator, but Wang Xueyao was Creator's student.

Note - Li Gang is not Creator. There is another layer of scheme on top of everything Li Gang did from Creator.

Li Gang saw through that this world is only 3D and there exist higher dimensions in the world. The Boundary (also known as Fourth Wall) protects this world from higher dimensions to some extent.

He also knew that once he forces Wang Xueyao into action, whom he had already figured that she is higher dimensional being, the world would break. But Wang Xueyao who already believed she had controlled Li Gang, as per Li Gang's deliberate falling into her trap, wouldn't cause world destruction since she needs something from him. So she would leave the world, helping Elemental Emperor too, this was also the incentive for Elemental Emperor into following the direction of Li Gang's plan.

All this would cause a large scale catastrophe in the world, since the battle between two higher dimensional beings can't possibly leave a lower world unaffected.

Lots and lots of warriors would die, and their dead body would become nutrition for Li Gang in improving his talent, since your blood possesses Ether which creates connection with Soul, helping in improvement of talent. He needed billions of dead bodies for that to happen.

# Stage 10: Freedom Through Chaos

The activation of the Special Ability immediately destabilized the balance among higher powers.

Immortal Zhu intervened.

Wang Xueyao responded.

The Elemental Emperor remained hidden.

After the battle between Immortal Zhu and Wang Xueyao, Immortal Zhu died.

This chaos achieved one of Li Gang's most important objectives.

An immortal responsible for monitoring the world died.

As a result, Li Gang obtained a brief period during which no one directly oversaw his actions.

For the first time in years, he possessed genuine freedom, where he could take actions and make decisions as he wishes! Since Immortal Zhu just died, no one can oversee the whole world and no one would be reading his mind.

# Stage 11: The Final Results

The Catastrophe Plan produced several outcomes.

Li Gang confirmed the existence and interference of hidden powers.

He verified the Elemental Emperor's involvement.

He identified the relative positions and motivations of major players.

He successfully manipulated immortals, Wang Xueyao, and the Elemental Emperor into acting according to his predictions.

He obtained temporary freedom from immortal supervision.

He escaped the worst effects of thought manipulation. Since he is able to confirm how the thoughts are being manipulated and after knowing that he needs to have high bottomline in order to resist thought manipulation, it became easier to escape control. Note that, Li Gang had already seen through this in Awakening Ceremony, but he didn't possess enough ability (since he was just a mortal). Therefore, he put the psychological cue of knowing himself as much as possible, since it would help him understand which thoughts belongs to him and which are being altered, allowing him to react and adjust them accordingly. And with higher bottomline, it became easy.

Especially, Li Gang is unaware that he had pulled off this plan. It was essential part of whole plan. Since if Li Gang knew about it, then he would be more evil with low bottomline and his thoughts could still be manipulated. But since Li Gang orchestrated everything in a way that he never knew everything happening was planned by him, his ideology was still righteous, maintaining higher bottomline to resist thought manipulation.

He also gained the insights necessary to counter the Law of Unpredictability via Huang Shu.

Now, the whole Catastrophe plan is part of his over all strategy with unknown goal. This strategy is still ongoing.
