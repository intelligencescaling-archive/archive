Vile Dominion Volume 1 summary (Monarch Inheritance feat)

LG's plan was simple... He first orchestrated Azure Mountain Warriors to assemble. How he did it?

First WXY killed a Spiritfire wolf, Li Gang took the dead body to Huang Weimin, who was the leader of Shadow Phoenix faction. He then cooperated with him to take down Spirit Dragon faction. In this time, he got the identity of Ren Ying, provided to him by Huang Weimin, he also obtained the disguise technique from him since his sect was better at spies and assassination. Ren Ying was originally a member of thunder faction.

With this identity, he went to Spirit Dragon faction, to get closer to Long Yifan (was that the name? dunno lets say that was the name).

Now, LG already has a disguise technique, so he sent a head to Long Yifan, disguising it to his own head, thus winning his trust.

Now, it was his another foresight. Because he knew that news travel very slow in this small world. The connection wasn't large. He understood, it would take long time for his fellow villagers to gather.

When he got hold of the map from Shadow Phoenix faction, he altered it. He marked one of the position to Azure faction (where his own villagers stay).... and then spread it throughout.

As a consequence, all the Warriors from Azure mountain, they thought that there own people are already gathered at that place... and thus, they went there to gather.

As more and more people started gathering there... they automatically formed a place to stay.

This place was chosen by Li Gang, since it was closer to Region 23.... where that bloody pond was present.

Now, Li Gang also helped Long Yichan/yifan whatever his name is, to win various resource points from Shadow Phoenix faction, resulting in him gaining more trust.

At the same time, he orchestrated hero saving beauty with Tang Liuyue, in order to get her trust.

In this way, Li Gang got control over Moonlit faction (through Tang girl), Shadow Phoenix, Spirit Dragon and Thunder faction (through fake identity of Ren Ying).

Now, Tang Liuyue made a condition... she would trust him only if he got her that particular treasure (it was some kind of plant). This plant had weird way of growing.. it feeds on lives of people. LG got the plant from Shadow Phoenix faction... but it had to be nourished.. Thus, LG used thunder faction for this. He posted it on their mission... and prepared enough reward.

Whoever took the mission, got sacrificed by Thunder faction in order to grow it.

(Yes, Li Gang gave the plant to thunder faction)

At the same time, Li Gang also revealed the news of that bloody pond to Huang Ling... it was great attraction to her since it could stabilize her foundation

But before revealing it, he had already shared it's benefits with Moonlit faction, and asked for their help in return.

During this time, the news of his death also reached Li family. Li gang had already held back many Warriors of Azure faction and prevented them from going to that place. He did it because the Warriors weren't stupid. After seeing that place being marked as Azure faction, and it wasn't a gathering place at all... they realized someone schemed to make them gather here. They didn't know what was the purpose, but they would definitely leave once everyone gathered. Hence, LG stopped them from gathering.

LG prevented various warriors from gathering... and with their righteousness... those who gathered wouldn't be willing to leave that place until most of the warriors reunite there. After they gathered, LG put some spies in there (his trusted ones from the village)

He controlled the flow of communication. When the news reached Azure faction (of his death)... he prepared to manipulate LYX into going to region 23.

When Li Yuxong went to ask for map of the small world, the person incharge said only 23 maps are left.

When LYX was leaving, he heard many people's murmurs... in the murmur, one guy said "23".

When LYX left the place, this 23 was deep rooted in his subconscious and when he saw the map and thought of going to somewhere, he found that there was Region 23 marked near the place.... he decided to go there first. Note that... this entire plot was foreseen by Li Gang when he heard about Huang Ling needs that bloody pond near region 23. He made the scheme at that time.

As he went towards there, Li Gang (now Ren Ying) was already present on his way as a disciple of Thunder faction with two others. During one of the moment to scout the area (for the place where corpse blossom plant, which was the one Tang girl requested, could be present), Ren Ying sent LYX to one of the cave. He smelled a bloody stench, and it left deep impression on him.

Before he could explore further, he was called back by RY, saying he found the plant.

This time, as LG had planned, it was a trap to lure disciple and sacrifice to corpse blossom plant.

LG had deliberately chose one of the disciple to be from formation master... this allowed them to easily escape this situation, and LG managed to conquer the plant with Li Yuxong being a distraction. At the same time, as part of contingency, he had asked Huang Shu for help.

Huang Shu came and blocked Thunder disciple for him.

LG had asked to splash and cause bloody stench in the air when killing the disciples of thunder faction.

Huang Shu did the same.. LYX, who was busy escaping, suddenly thought of the cave where LG had asked him to go.

They had the same stench.

This made LYX to take various distractive routes and go there. Once again, subconscious manipulation.

As soon as he went there, he was trapped by the love poison.

He and Huang Ling became couple, as a part of LG's plan.

Huang Shu and others were also trapped by Moonlit faction and were killed.

LG managed to get corpse blossom plant and gave it to Moonlit faction, winning their trust as well.

Moonlit faction also attacked Huang Shu, smeared beast attracting smell on his clothes and led him to get killed by beast tide.

After this arrangement, he asked thunder faction to attack Ren Ying, as he 'coincidently' met Li Yuxong, who happened to help him.

Huang Ling didn't trust him, but LYX did. Thus, he managed to win Huang Ling to the side of Li Family, which would later help him block Jade Harbor City's attacks. Next, with all the 4 factions under his control, he caused Spirit Dragon faction, Shadow Phoenix factin, Moonlit faction in 3-way battle.

With Thunder faction watching from sidelines and attacking as well.

This caused heavy damage to the 4 factions.

After this, Li Gang gave a tip to attract Spirit Dragon faction, and split them into two (which helped Shadow Phoenix and Moonlit faction to attack them easily)

One of the Spirit Dragon faction attacked Huang Ling (and this was also what Shadow Phoenix faction wanted since Huang Weimin was against Huang Ling)

This caused Huang Ling's suspicion high... she was rank 6, so how could she not see through the fact that only 3 people were aware of the route they were taking.

But it was too late, LG poisoned LYX, and thus controlled Huang Ling through his threat.

He gained Soul Pond as a result.

At the same time, Tang Liuyue was also scheming against LG... she had long collaborated with Wang Xueyao.

When they wanted to return for Inheritance... she asked multiple times when LG would return, and this raised suspicion in LG's heart. He was too cautious.

When he visited Tang Liuyue later, he directly asked question... and he guessed that there is problem with her based on her behavior.

He wasn't sure, but he still poisoned her decisively. If she didn't plot, then he would give her antidote, and if she did, then no antidote.

After the Inheritance began, in the perseverance test, he forced himself to be heavily injured, he already noticed that many warriors, when injured are fed with high rank vitality pills.

So, when he was fed with the pill, he didn't swallow it, he coughed it out and kept it. The injury wasn't heavy enough to require high rank pill.

This was again his contingency against TLY, in case she wants to betray him. Later, he deliberately lured her to a place where no one was present, to test what she wanted. As expected she backstabbed him.

However, before all this, he had already asked Huang Ling's help, as a contingency in case WXY also attacks.

She was the strongest person there after WXY.

After she backstabbed him, he delayed time till Huang Ling is present, and then jumped off the cliff. When WXY wanted to kill him, Huang Ling blocked her. Later, when TLY went for next exam, which required Spiritual Essence utilization, her poison flaired up and she died "accidently".

After the Inheritance ended, due to unknown stance of Shadow Phoenix Sect (Huang Ling sided with Li family), other factions dare not attack Azure faction, leading them to benefit without danger.

He reduced the competition of all 4 factions by letting them fight amongst themselves and kill themselves. This allowed Azure faction to benefit massively. At the same time, he prepared the contingency of Huang Ling, to block others from attacking Azure faction.

No one realized Ren Ying and Li Gang were same person, so no one could target Li family with that excuse.
