* LELOUCH VI BRITANNIA

Amidst the heated battle, an enemy turns off their IFF (Identification Friend or Foe) and is operating on their own. The same enemy seems to be the one causing havoc among the Black Knights as Lelouch receives emergency calls from his troops, as they die mid-speech suggesting that this singular enemy is obliterating Lelouch's men.

Identification Friend or Foe is an identification system designed for command and control. It enables military and civilian air traffic control interrogation systems to identify aircraft, vehicles or forces as friendly, and to determine their bearing and range from the interrogator.

This means that LL can't trace his movements through the military radar, but based off the pattern of destruction it leaves among LL's men he inductively predicts that it's heading towards his location. This unforeseen circumstance is quite extreme and Lelouch has to adapt to it amidst his strategy, as he prepares for its arrival the enemy Knightmare is identified as a prototype based off the Lancelot; The Vincent. This enemy was extremely formidable but Lelouch with the help of Urabe and Kallen was able to hold him off until the finale of his strategy was executed.

Finally, C2 had finished planting the bombs according to Lelouch's command within Babel Tower which were then blown up by Lelouch, destroying the upper half of Babel Tower. What Lelouch had done here was genius, foreseeing Calaris’ strategy as aforementioned, he had known that there was no way to win with all escape routes occupied by the enemy, so what did he do? He used the building itself as a road to the Chinese Embassy. As hinted from command 2 assigned to p4 to “block the stairway’. Lelouch had effectively limited the control of the enemy's positioning by preemptively blocking out certain routes. This way the enemy's positions would perfectly align with his preemptive detonation at the base of the tower. Doing this meant that the tower would fall and collapse, resulting in…

1-) The enemy sutherlands on the top floors falling with it and meeting their end.

2-) The falling tower's trajectory was aligned in a manner where it would fall directly onto the G-1 Base; crushing Calaris, figuratively and literally.

3-) The perfect alignment of the collapsed tower means that the Black Knights at the lower levels of the building can use it as a road to escape to the Chinese Embassy.
