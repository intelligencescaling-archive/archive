“Lelouch needed to deactivate the explosion of the F.L.E.I.J.A, to do so
he had to enter the data of his composition, considering that the F.L.E.I.J.A changes its supercomputer compositions constantly, Lelouch had to enter data with an equal reaction in order to be able to break into the critical condition of the F.L.E.I.J.A. In no material of Code Geass (to my knowledge) is it ever stated that the ever changing composition data had to be entered by Lelouch in the Fleija Eliminator. This effectively debuffs Lelouch’s FSIQ feat from beyond supercomputer levels to just pretty impressive but still human level CPI. What is Fleija’s “Composition”?

This section has no impact on Lelouch’s feat itself as we have already established in the prior section that the composition has no connection to the environmental data that Lelouch had to enter.

First of all let’s establish some facts. Fleija is an acronym for “Field Limitary Effective Implosion Armament”. Basically it’s a bomb. Now let’s look at these two statements and dissect them.

1. The Fleija changes its composition from time to time.
2. If you hit it with a reaction corresponding to that, you can stop it from achieving criticality.

These two statements were said by Nina, the one character that should know everything about this warhead. She says if one hits Fleija with a reaction corresponding to its composition, the warhead will not achieve criticality. I have put the important words in bold for ease.

The chain of logic is therefore like this:
Reaction => Composition => No Criticality

So the three words are connected as shown above. What is the criticality of a bomb you ask? Critical mass is defined as the amount of material at which a neutron produced by a fission process will, on average, create another fission event. Essentially “criticality” means the bomb will reach a stage of chain reaction which will cause the explosion.

This begs the question, what kind of reaction can stop the bomb from achieving criticality? The reaction in question is either a nuclear reaction or a chemical reaction given its connection to the concept of criticality.

Lastly, what does this suggest about the word composition. Composition in my opinion very obviously refers to the chemical composition of the bomb itself. Nuclear warheads in real life contain radioactive materials like Uranium, Plutonium, or various isotopes of these materials. Fleija most likely changes its composition by changing the specific isotope present at any given moment to a different one using a method called enrichment. Therefore the reaction needs to account for that specific isotope to stop it from achieving criticality.

Hence, I believe that the composition of Fleija has nothing to do with a supercomputer.
