Note
Why did I create this? Well to simply put it, a lot of people kept bullying Lelouch after the Sahej drama. So it’s about time I place him back into his intended spot in the SCD.

I will analyze Lelouch’s battle strategy because I am a massive larper and was always in love with the military aspect of Code Geass. There are some base level tactics involved which, when paired with his interesting plans and strategies really do hold him up well in his tactical ability. Although his feats don't hold up to the level of real life military battles, anime isn’t intended to replicate the intricacies of politics and warfare.

Also, each paragraph will not have specific text colour coded for specific parts of cats demonstrated. If an individual displays a better manipulation feat but with some portions of reasoning or psychology, the paragraph dedicated to explaining the manipulation feat will be coded Purple.

This will also include Lelouch of the Resurrection. However, I will do it later

DM Rian for questions, clarifications or feedback. This is my first document and I know there are some flaws but I’m not exactly the best at identifying such.

Feel free to credit me if you’re using my document for Lelouch scaling. I would really appreciate it.

Enjoy the document!
Episode 1-3
* When Lelouch was trapped inside the truck driven by Kallen and another unknown lackey, he was able to realize that the truck was heading to the Ghetto through the old tunnels of Tokyo based on his cell phone being out of service, the darkness encompassing the trucks and the terrain of the roads. Although this seemed only to be unrealistic in hindsight, in reality Lelouch’s reasoning made sense. He already knew the roads from the Casino to Ashford Academy considering he was able to travel there multiple times (as stated by Shirley to have been gambling again) not to mention that he had lived during Britannian occupation for roughly 7 years which would’ve most likely implied that he had been familiar with the smoother roads around the Britannian sector of Tokyo. It can also be reasoned that Lelouch used his understanding of the discrimination between the Japanese or “Elevens” to understand that they would be most likely running through the Ghetto areas which would’ve had rougher and older routes terrorists would depend on. Terrorists wouldn’t also depend on the usage of open roads to evade capture by the Britannians as there was a possibility there would’ve been checkpoints leading to the Ghetto.

Shinjuku Battle
After Lelouch’s capture of Villetta’s knightmare frame, his contact with Shirley has led him to realize that the Britannians had suppressed all information about the Shinjuku Operation. Despite how trivial this seemed, this allowed for Lelouch to understand that any use of reinforcements would be impossible without proper media explanation, serving as a tactical advantage in his favor should he inflict casualties on his opponents and eventually back the Britannian command into a corner where they may eventually depend on either the encircling forces or reinforcements which would impact Clovis’ media reputation. He contacts Kallen through a radio, telling her to follow his orders despite knowing she wouldn’t trust him. But knowing the dire situation the Japanese Resistance was facing, he told Kallen “It doesn’t matter who I am! If you want to win, then trust me!”, emphasizing the importance of the survival and success of the resistance. After baiting Jeremiah and his subordinate into a train tracks, Lelouch used his Knightmare frame to ambush the subordinate, before letting Kallen finish them off. Lelouch also had the Resistance including Ohgi capture the supply train containing Sutherlands, offering it to them under the condition they follow his orders. He knew that an act of goodwill would entrust most if not all of the Resistance into following his orders even if he was a mysterious individual, granting himself control of an opposing force to face the Britannians.

Lelouch also had to visualize off of the map and understanding of the Ghetto, including its nearby walls, buildings and routes as a means to time and prepare his bait such as using Kallen to set up his first trap. Lelouch had also timed Ohgi and his forces to launch ambushes on the approaching Britannian forces with impressive accuracy, as he had to time the speed and distance in order to justify the effective time to launch such an ambush. This process would continue similarly in dividing and conquering the Britannian forces effectively, knowing that not only the Britannian Command will be surprised of the resistance mounted but would also attempt a desperate counterassault. And surprisingly, Lelouch had continued to effectively coordinate and plan out such moves while relaying moves against a larger force which could’ve easily overwhelmed him. The orders weren’t rudimentary as well, as not only he had to catch Britannian forces, vehicles and aircraft off guard but also put into account the weaponry and methods used as shown when Lelouch ordered the resistance to apply “UN Ordinance” against a Britannian Armored Personnel Carrier or use anchor against an Attack Helicopter.

Lelouch stated that there were five possible responses the Britannians could’ve provided which not only meant he had to identify the logistics, manpower and capability of the Britannian forces involved in the encirclement to make a feasible counterattack but also implied that he had already foresaw such a move as he had already developed and relayed orders to counter such in a short timeframe while Clovis was preparing and transmitting orders.

Upon Lelouch’s discovery that the Britannians had broken the encirclement with the intention to strike at the heart of the resistance, he ordered Ohgi’s group to make a withdrawal through the underground tunnels while allowing for the Britannians to slowly stream into the center en masse. Eventually falling victim to Kallen who he ordered to destroy the ground behind them as a means to destroy the entire Britannian force in one move. Lelouch had to account for how Prince Clovis would act out of desperation by throwing all his forces en masse at the terrorist suspected base of operations to ensure Kallen can destroy the ground and activate his trap  of destroying the entire ground and eliminating a massive portion of the Britannian forces.

Lelouch managed to turn the entire situation from terrible for the underequipped and uncoordinated Japanese Resistance into a tactical Japanese victory, stopped only by Suzaku and forced to use Clovis in order to prevent further conflict. And note that Lelouch was 17 and although he had stated that he intended to destroy Britannia later, to be able to instantly adapt and fight against Clovis who although had shown to be a massive walking anti feat in military battles, had been supported by subordinates who were possibly more experienced than him and performed optimally without Clovis’ micromanagement. Lelouch was severely outnumbered and would have faced any possible issues and counterattacks yet still managed to come out on top. And all of this occurred without a Geass (excluding the takeover and hijacking of a Knightmare frame)

Orange Strategy
During the transport of Suzaku towards his court, Zero created a replica of Prince Clovis’ personal transport vehicle with the intention of driving it towards the convoy. He was aware that there would be a massive publicity and coverage of the suspected murderer of Prince Clovis being transported, and as such would mean that Zero would be able to gain public traction should he stop the convoy. With that in mind, he introduces himself to the Britannians, before revealing to Jeremiah and Villetta the capsule intended to contain C.C at the back of the car. Although the plan to make himself go public but also save Suzaku from suspicion had a massive risk, Zero was able to calculate that Jeremiah wouldn’t be aware of the contents of the capsule he hid inside the vehicle.

Zero knew from his experience encountering the “poison gas capsule” as mentioned by others that only the highest ranking members such as Prince Clovis would believe that what was actually inside the capsule was just a test subject with extraordinary powers beyond human comprehension. As such, Zero had taken advantage of the cover story of poison gas to create the belief that he had taken almost everyone in the bridge including civilians as hostages should he be shot at. Thus the Britannians were forced to disengage and listen to Zero’s demands. As to further reinforce his publicity stunt, Zero admitting that he murdered Prince Clovis, not only solidifying his notoriety among the Britannians but to also save Suzaku from being found guilty in a rigged court should his plan fail. Zero also takes advantage of the values of the Britannians and their views on Elevens, arguing that in order to save multiple Britannians, Jeremiah had to release Suzaku.

Despite Zero’s bluffs, Jeremiah insists on stopping Zero despite the threat of poison gas. In response, Zero questions him if he would like the public to know of “Orange”. Despite the insignificant code in hindsight, his plan would work almost flawlessly after using the Geass in conjunction to force Jeremiah to let the group pass. He knew that not only would there be suspicion should he instantly let Zero go after he mentioned “Orange” due to the world of political maneuvers and scandals, but would also portray to the Japanese Resistance that Zero had some semblance of political influence and power which could serve to their advantage. By specifically ordering Jeremiah to “Let them go with all of his might”, Jeremiah’s desperation as he orders his column to stand down and let Suzaku be released reinforced such a suspicion. This also meant that should any Britannian act out of line, Jeremiah’s response could also indicate that he was indeed involved in Orange and was attempting to save his skin.

After Lelouch releases the “poison gas” which is actually just a purple smoke screen, he makes his rapid escape under the cover of Jeremiah (who is still Geass’d) who continues to prevent any moves made by the Britannians in preventing their escape. After the success of Suzaku’s rescue, much of Ohgi’s group came to the realization that under Zero’s leadership, they would be able to start the process of liberating Japan. Not only was Lelouch able to save Suzaku while making a name for Zero, he ultimately pinned suspicion on Jeremiah who had been the de facto leader of Area 11’s Administration until Cornelia’s arrival.

Introduction of the Black Knights
Despite the precarious nature of the JLF Hotel Hostage Situation, Zero had noticed that Cornelia hadn’t conducted a large-scale rescue mission on the hostages. Compared to how Cornelia had acted during the Saitama Ghetto, it didn’t make sense why she would be indecisive at this moment as she has been shown to sacrifice civilians as a means to not bend to terrorists. He deduces that Cornelia must’ve had an individual among the group who she had sentimental or emotional value towards, hence why such a rescue attempt could risk her life. Zero’s memory of his time during the royal family had led him to deduce that perhaps her sister Euphemia was among the hostage group, hence why she was unwilling to act compared to her previous actions in the Saitama Ghetto.

Zero’s entry into the Britannian lines was intended to offer Cornelia the choice of letting him pass in order to negotiate with the JLF and save Euphemia. Due to Zero’s notoriety as being Clovis’ killer, this meant that the JLF would be willing to discuss with him and as such would also allow him to enter. Cornelia knew that Zero was able to create such cunning plans such as the Suzaku’s Rescue or the Battle of Shinjuku Ghetto which would mean that Zero was ultimately Cornelia’s last hope in saving Euphemia.

His willingness to risk discussing with the JLF meant that should he betray his promise to Cornelia and kill Euphemia, Cornelia would be able to eliminate a massive portion of terrorists including their equipment and one of Britannia’s most wanted individuals. Similarly, Zero understood that due to his enigmatic nature, the JLF would be intrigued in meeting with this masked individual, especially one who could inflict such victories against the Britannians.

Zero ultimately offers himself to handle negotiations, knowing that Cornelia would have a strategic and emotional reason for letting him in. The strategic reason being the JLF having interest towards Zero. While the emotional reason being to ensure Euphemia’s safety. Zero has the JLF members commit suicide and disarmed the remaining hostage takers with the Black Knights. After destroying the entire hotel with the aid of Suzaku’s destruction of the structure, he uses the media broadcast to announce the Black Knights to the world, emphasizing them as “Knights of Justice”.

Compared to other Japanese resistance organizations, Lelouch advertised his Black Knights as a means to defend the weak, whether they were Britannians or Japanese. Zero knew that much of society would look up to these supposed “Knights” as they stand on a moral ground compared to both the Britannians and JLF. Lelouch had not only taken advantage of the developing hostage situation to advertise the Black Knights but had also strategically taken advantage of Cornelia’s emotional weakness to orchestrate such a plan in the eyes of the media.

Later on, this advertising of the Black Knights and use to launch drug busting operations and defending the weak would result in a rise of recruitment numbers and even some Glasgows provided by supporting resistance factions.

Battle of Narita
The Battle of Narita feat solidifies Season One Lelouch in terms of his Planning and Strategy feats,

Lelouch knew that Cornelia would operate in the field during the assault on the JLF Headquarters, while also acting on the intelligence provided by Diethard to test the Black Knights as to not only solidify Zero as a “miracle worker” but to also capture Cornelia, the crown jewel of the Britannian Military. He placed all of his forces on the very top of the mountain while drilling onto the ground to prepare the Radiant Wave technology provided to the Guren II. Upon realization among the BK of the assault underway, they begin to question him. Zero states that “Even messiahs need to perform miracles to be acknowledged”.

And to solidify his leadership and ability to not only towards his allies but to the Kyoto group who backed the BK, they needed to “produce a miracle of their own”. As Tamaki continues to question Zero, Zero pulls out his pistol, stating that if anyone could win this battle other than him, they can shoot him. He calls for the BK to follow him in battle, aware that the only person that can truly make a miracle out of this situation was himself. Should they shoot Zero, their loss was almost inevitable. Thus much of the group follows him into battle, knowing it’s a life or death scenario and that they’re backed into a corner. Through using the Black Knights’ fear of being backed into a corner and annihilated by Britannian forces, Lelouch forces them to not only fight for their lives but once victory had been achieved, the Black Knights would see Zero as a true messiah capable of creating miracles.

Lelouch had already studied the defenses and headquarters of the JLF position, and knew from Cornelia’s personality that she wouldn’t be interested in attempting to steal credit from her subordinates. Instead, Cornelia would instead stick to “a very logical deployment” to maintain her forces’ effectiveness within the battle which Lelouch anticipates.

Alongside that, Lelouch had anticipated that Cornelia would support Darlton’s formations with reserves to support the final breakthrough into the headquarters, making her dependent on her more quality Royal Guard. Lelouch’s landslide strategy was unconventional to say the least, as the Radiant Wave technology had never been used for mass production and especially not with the intention to create a landslide out of all situations.

As the Britannians made their advance towards what seemed to be the final remnants of the JLF with excitement, Lelouch uses this to his advantage to order a massive attack on Cornelia’s forces spearheaded by the landslide which was use to great effect in eliminating and splitting off a large portion of the Britannian Army from Cornelia. This also meant that the terrain of the landslide would bog down Britannian forces to a point where there was a limited timeframe for Lelouch to easily capture Cornelia as he didn’t seek a “Battle of Attrition”. A common strategy used during warfare by a smaller guerilla force to combat a larger force. However Lelouch had also integrated the idea of decapitating the Britannian leadership as to achieve a strategic victory. Without Cornelia’s leadership, Lelouch would guarantee a Black Knight victory as Cornelia would be considered a sizable threat even without a substantial force.

Lelouch had realistically underestimated the impact of the landslide which had not only demonstrated his realistic feats but also his abstract thinking. His implementation of the landslide strategy despite only having three battle experiences in his life also indicated creative ability to its fullest extent, utilizing the Radiant Wave Tech provided to create such an effective tactic. By the end of the landslide, it was stated only 20% of Britannian forces remained operational. Lelouch ultimately took advantage of Cornelia’s rational and strategic mindset to justify directing the landslide at a specific direction as to not only sacrifice some elements of the Japanese Liberation Force but also be used to eliminate a significantly large portion of the Britannian Army.

Note that the direction of the main Britannian spearhead was directed at the Japanese Headquarters. So not only had Lelouch predicted such an outcome considering Cornelia’s tactical prowess, but had planned it out from the very beginning.

Lelouch had also foreseen Cornelia regrouping with her forces through a canyon one way or another. Although he didn’t anticipate the arrival of Tohdoh, he could easily integrate their involvement into his strategy as he had positioned Kallen to ambush Cornelia before using his forces to eventually link up and disarm her Knightmare frame. Note that Lelouch had not only outmaneuvered an experienced leader after experiencing her in the field for once but had forces that weren’t trained to the degree of the Britannians, hence why they depended mainly on ambushes and a lightning battle to kill or capture Cornelia. His positioning of Kallen into the canyon was designed to not only slow her down using their best and most dedicated pilot alongside shooting at the side to guarantee Cornelia’s loss despite the arrival of Suzaku. Cornelia later admitted the Britannians had lost to Zero for once.
