Introduction
This feat was performed in R2 E10 by Xingke as he used the Shen Hu to intercept the Black Knights while they were escaping to rendezvous with the Indians. Xingke, driven with the motivation to save the Empress, uses the Shen Hu to defeat Kallen Kosiki in a 1v1 knightmare battle
________________________________

Explanation of the feat
The Shenhu is a knightmare designed by Rahkshata and her team, it was constructed at the same time as the Guren however with a different style of construction, it wasn't built with the same Radiant wave system but was instead built more around the knightmares overall specs, however, Rakshata's team got a bit too carried away and ended up constructing the Shenhu with specs that no human could ever learn to pilot as stated by Rakshata herself in R2 E10:

"A one of a kind knightmare that no pilot could master(...)and piloting it takes skills which are almost beyond human"
* Rakshata

What Rakshata's statements provide is that just piloting it alone takes "skills which are almost beyond human" however as shown with Xingke's first displays, he manages to defeat Kallen, while it is true her Knightmare fuel was depleted, as Tamaki stated, she had even managed to fight off the knights of the round whilst in a similar condition meaning that Xingke managed to fight Kallen on a level surpassing that of the knights of the round.

Further statements also reinforce this as Lelouch, who's watched Suzaku fight numerous times in the lancelot, even compared their skills to each other and even then, Suzaku was fortunate to have a very high compatibility with the Lancelot, a statement not given to Xingke which further proves Xingke's ability to master the Shen Hu's functionality.

Furthermore, Xingke even noted how the Shen Hu was only at 40% output which drastically heightens this feats prowess as he managed to perform a feat better than the knights of the round by beating an energy depleted Kallen which they could not while performing at only 40% output.

Finally, Xingke managed to master the Shen Hu to such a pristine level IMMEDIATELY as he'd intercepted the black knights and entire hour before an encounter was feasibly possible, while Xingke had indeed predicted Lelouch's precise route of travel for their escape, this amount of a time difference wouldn't have given Xingke the luxury of time to practise, nor did he even know of the Shen Hu's existence prior to this.

In summary, Xingke managed to master the Shen Hu, a knighmare that would've taken "almost beyond human" levels of capability just to pilot which she would've known from pilots practicing with it. However, Xingke not only managed to pilot it first try but also managed to immediately master it's functionality to such a degree that he could beat an energy depleted Kallen with only 40% of the Shen Hu's output, something not even the knights of the round could achieve with maximum output, putting this feat easily at an unrealistic level.
