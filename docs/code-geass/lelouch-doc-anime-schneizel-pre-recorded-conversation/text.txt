1st segment: The preparation for the feat

The conversation lasts for 1 minute and 49 seconds and encompasses the ideological debate between Lelouch and Schneizel as their logistical battle has come to a close. We are able to tell from precise timings of Lelouch's words that he knows every single word Schneizel will say as even 1 different word would knock three entire recordings out of sync and Schneizel will deduce that it's a recording. Lelouch would have achieved this by using his memory to recall every interaction he's ever witnessed Schneizel partake in and find all of his most common word patterns, gestures, talking speed, and how his emotions affect every variable down to perfection. He'd find the most common and relevant patterns in Schneizel's behaviour and then perfectly implement that into this conversation structure. Other variables he had to consider when formulating this are: How he perceives Lelouch, his understanding of Lelouch’s and his own philosophies, and how the topics of conversation would shift during an interaction between the 2.

Debunk: But Schneizel and Lelouch are half siblings so they'd know each other very well

Rebunk: They actually didn't, in the picture novels (which are canon to the anime continuity) it shows that during his childhood, Lelouch spent most of his time with Nunnally and Euphemia, while Schneizel spent most of his time with Clovis and Cornelia, the most politically active of the children. While they did interact, it was mostly through chess. It wasn't really any deep philosophical bond as Lelouch was just a child and Schneizel was just a teenager with minimal political experience at this point.

As for the formulation of this recording, due to time constraints, we can assume that Lelouch had this conversation memorised in his head beforehand and simply recited it to the recorder. This means that Lelouch kept a perfect track of time in his head when doing certain responses down to the milliseconds in terms of accuracy in order to align everything perfectly.

Lelouch: "I have a question. Were you intending to use the Damocles as a tool to dominate the world?"

With this question, we can determine that Lelouch has deduced Schneizel's entire world domination plan, aiming to use the presence of the Damocles to control the rebellious nature of humanity as a whole through total power.  Lelouch also understands that Schneizel sees himself as a higher power individual, not necessarily a God like Cornelia inferred but someone with higher power than everyone else. We can infer this as he specifically mentions that Schneizel will "use the Damocles as a tool", implying the FLEIJA's to only be a single piece of an arsenal for Schneizel, the true symbol of power

It also shows that Lelouch knows that keeps his ego in check as even with this self perceived "higher power" he still fully understands his own intentions and WHY he is doing what he's doing.
