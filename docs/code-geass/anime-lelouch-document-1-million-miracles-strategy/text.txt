Explanation of the plot

This strategy mainly takes place during episode 8 of Code Geass' second season, right after Lelouch regains his motivation and saves the Black Knights via his methane hydrate reaction tactic.

At 21:36 during episode 7, Lelouch arrives aboard an aerial knightmare piloted by Rollo, now donned in the Zero costume as he calls a ceasefire before announcing his acceptance to join Nunnaly and her plan to recreate the Japan special zone, disabling Suzaku's ability to retaliate. Lelouch then orders the black knights to cooperate willingly.

During episode 8, 2:16 in, Lelouch presents himself to Anya, Gino & Suzaku. Suzaku expresses how despite Lelouch’s acceptance to join the zone, the blood on his hands doesn't go away, however after Gino brings some sense to Suzaku, he begrudgingly accepts Lelouch's actions. The black knights are then shown going back underwater as Villetta suspends any advances until further orders as a bluff to let them escape.

At 2:54 the black knights are discussing Lelouch’s decision to join the special zone with Tohdo beginning to question Lelouch’s choice and how it may not align with what the Japanese people would want. Zero enters to Kaguya's delight, after a small exchange, they begin questioning him directly on his choice. Lelouch speaks about how there's only a chain of violence, something the Black Knights speak against, Ohgi claiming they fight because they must. Tohdo pushes the point, adamant that the Japanese cannot “become friends with our conquerors”. To this remark, Lelouch asks Tohdo “What is Japanese?”

At 3:51 the scene shifts to a Britannian announcement to the Japanese about processes applications to the special zone, something that receives a mixed reception from the populace, remembering the old massacre from before as well as the established benefits of becoming an honorary Britannian instead, losing a sense of their national pride in the process. As a result of this, no Japanese citizens have applied, on top of this, with no words from Lelouch as of late, Britannian media suspects he may be receiving backlash within the black knights organisation.

At 4:29 - The scene shifts to Nunnaly and her Personal assistant, Miss Lomire as she tells Nunnally to make sure to stick to her pre-assigned script and that she isn't a ruler but instead a reflection of the emperor within her zone, the scene quickly moves to Suzaku and Rivelz where the former states his leave of absence from school as a result of the special zones recreation. The rest of the council tries to use this as a way to have Suzaku try to relieve Kallen of her crimes as a Black Knight diplomatically, Lelouch chimes in, talking about the importance of family and friends and his relationship with Nunnaly as viceroy proving influence of Suzaku's word. Suzaku inner monologues, suspecting Lelouch of hiding his identity despite the seemingly valid insight from the OSI and testimonies from other students. At 5:48 - Suzaku’s monologue continues as he rhetorically asks what Lelouch's reasoning was for killing Euphemia. He is interrupted as a Japanese man hidden as a soldier attempts to assassinate him. With the geass for him to live activating, Suzaku defends himself and curses Lelouch for giving him this command despite knowing his want for death.

At 6:12 Lelouch is on a call with Diethard who is in the Chinese federation due to his self-exile as part of their broader plan to take over the Chinese federation and form the UFN to counter Britannia. Diethard states that  he's had the negotiations with the Chinese to have the iceberg ship used for the evacuation of the Japanese people from Japan, with Diethard mentioning how Sayoko has already been sent to the ship. Lelouch congratulates Diethard for his work, pampering him with comments such as “you really are an expert at this” “an outstanding job” and “You're truly irreplaceable”. Clearly bringing glee to Diethard. Diethard then asks if the next stage shall be consistent, again referring to the Chinese takeover plan and UFN formation. Lelouch mentions how while their tactics have changed, the objective has remained unaltered. Lelouch then tells Diethard to keep to his work on his end before hanging up.

Rollo asks Lelouch if he has forgotten their promise to protect the school. Lelouch remarks that he hasn't forgotten and that he will protect the school as they both live there as brothers and he won't let anything get in the way of that and rejects the Britannians wanting to control their destiny. Rollo adds how he's on Lelouch's side now to protect him and the school, aware of how it'll be destroyed if Lelouch was discovered with his memories intact. Lelouch also brings up the threat of V.V, Rollo mentioning how he'd be killed as a traitor as Lelouch adds how he'd die as well, showing their alignment of destiny as Rollo mentions. Villeta comes into the meeting, referring to Lelouch as Zero, however this is quickly shut down by Lelouch, aware that all their lives would be in jeopardy if his identity as Zero got out. He asks Villetta if she misses Ohgi before the scene transitions.

At 10:48 Lelouch holds a meeting to discuss terms relevant for the special Zone. He mentions the presence of the knights of the round but also notices Nunnaly's lack of presence for the meeting. Suzaku mentions that it's not necessary to stress her with the details. Lloyd buts in, asking if the Zero in front of them is the same Zero as before or if it's a new person behind the mask. Lelouch mentions how Zero is not determined by his identity but by the weight of the user's actions. Lloyd verbally notes the philosophical response before Gino puts the meeting back on track, double checking with Lelouch to ascertain whether his joining the special zone is of full consensus from the black knights.

Lelouch states that he has more than just the black Knights, but he's also gathered an extra 1 million citizens that will agree to participate. However, Lelouch adds on a single condition. He states that if the 1 million citizens and the black knights are to participate in the special zone, then they must allow Zero to go into exile as a punishment for his rebellious actions. Suzaku asks what shall become of the black knights. Gino interjects, believing Zero doesn't care about them and that he simply wants to save his own skin. Lloyd mentions how if Zero was to go through with this request, then the people he swore to protect shall have him strung up for his betrayal. Lelouch replies by mentioning that this is the reason he's kept this clause as a top secret.

“Area law, section 12, paragraph 8” Miss Lomire mentions this legal section and how it states that an area viceroy does have the authority to exile rebellious elements if they deem necessary. Suzaku confronts her, asking if she's willing to let Zero go. She responds by simply stating that she's referring to their legal basis of action. Lelouch uses this moment to state that they should announce this during the opening ceremony. Gino mentions how this is a very good deal for them since if Zero runs, as the leader of the black knights, the rest of the organisation would simply disband out of fear. Suzaku understands but questions where justice is to truly punish Zero for his crimes.

At 12:43 Suzaku mentions how legal counselors have confirmed the logic behind exiling Zero. Nunnally decides to agree to this logic saying it may be for the best and how her desire to forgive is merely a personal matter. Suzaku solemnly speaks out to Euphemia, saying how even after her death, her dream of a more peaceful Japan will still be realised. Suzaku notices how another candle has Euphemia’s name engraved, this is shown to have been Lelouch, who is now leaving with Rollo, as he mentions how nothing is holding him back any longer.

At 13:45 the opening ceremony for the new special administrative zone of Japan has begun, the commentator stated how due to the surprising and sudden amassing of people, personal ID checks would have to wait until after the opening ceremony. Gino mentions how a riot may ensue once they realise Zero betrayed them and notes how this would be a free excuse for a Full-Scale crackdown (referring to a genocide of the Japanese)

The scene shifts to Xingke leaving Brittania due to his role as acting consort ending. He mentions how his request to Britannia the day before will be able to set up their plans for the future, referring to the request for safe passage of the iceberg ship as planned beforehand by Lelouch.

Nunnally greets the attending citizens with an opening speech, once this has concluded, Miss Lomire makes the announcement regarding the rules for the special zone such as criminal charges being either reduced or dropped entirely if below level 3 and then concludes by mentioning the federal law behind the exile of Zero as mentioned before during the meeting.

This is when Zero makes his presence known on the monitors, thanking them for their lenient actions, Guilford remarks that if anything gets radical, then Miss Lomire has given full authorisation to kill as many Japanese as possible to reduce the riot. Suzaku calls out that he'll personally escort Zero out of the country, however, Lelouch counters that he wishes to not be in Suzaku’s debt and instead proposes a question. “What does it mean to be Japanese” Nation, language, territory, bloodlines are all answers he coins but Suzaku rejects all of these, saying it's in the heart. Lelouch then states that that means that they're in agreement and goes on by saying how “It's a feeling within, a sense of belonging, dignity, pride. The culture is carried by heart. You're Japanese no matter where you're from” Suzaku questions how this question is relevant to him running away.

Suddenly, everyone attending the special zone uses a button triggered contraption to create a large smoke screen, Nunnaly is withdrawn with a few guards and Anya, leaving Suzaku and Miss Lomire behind. Guilford readies his men to fire but Suzaku stops them as they have made no violent moves yet. Zero appears from the smoke screen but has over 1 million copies. Gino immediately deduces what Lelouch's true intentions were, and mentions how as a result of this scheme, he managed to find a way to legally move over 1 million people. Lelouch then calls out to the other Zero’s as a result of the single piece of legislation used, their joined identity as Zero results in them all falling under said legislation, allowing them all to join him in exile without legal consequence.

Miss Lomire states that it's simply a bluff and that Lelouch has no way to transport 1 million people, it's at this point that the request made by Xingke reaches Miss Lomire from the port authority. The request regarding the safe passage of the massive Iceberg ship, however with Xingke having already left the country, no changes to the port's decision can be made As there is no current contact to reach.

As a last ditch attempt to prevent the event, some soldiers aim to shoot down the real, however, as a result of the mass Zero’s the leader cannot be identified and that risks the massacre of citizens, breaking the legally binding agreement made by both parties.

Miss Lomire states that they can’t let 1 million labourers leave the county. Suzaku requests for Lelouch to order all the Zero’ to remove their masks using the threat of another massacre to sway him but as Tohdo monologues, the massacre will only occur as a result of Suzaku’s order and even if they were all unmasked, Zero’s true identity would still be a mystery meaning nothing would change.

With Suzaku now in charge due to Nunnaly being withdrawn, he mentions how while Zero alone being exiled is fine, over a million more people is far more unacceptable. Lelouch counters with how if the black knights were to leave the country, Japan would be in peace and Nunnaly wouldn't be in danger of rebellion.

After Suzaku stops Miss Lomire from trying to kill a Zero, under the basis of this not being what Nunnaly or Euphemia would have wanted and how if they break their exile agreement, the people shall never trust them again. He speaks to Zero, making him promise to lead the people to salvation. He agrees and has Suzaku promise to let the Japanese still left in Japan find salvation in Suzaku’s hands. To which Suzaku agrees.

Zero makes his presence known on the iceberg ship and calls all of the Zero’s to him under the order made by Suzaku.
