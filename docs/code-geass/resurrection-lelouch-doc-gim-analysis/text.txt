GIM analysis explained

1:20:20 - Lelouch regains his volition after C.Cs outburst. Immediately formulating progressive ideas "Let us consider the enemy's mind" this shows Lelouch's willingness to encompass a methodology following a critical and abstract analysis with emotional and psychological Intelligence, coming up with this immediately already shows his ability to evaluate the best outcome despite the circumstances laid out before where not even Lelouch could form a counter plan with the means of conventional Warfare. This already puts the adversity far above anything seen in CG as even Schneizal who is superior to Lelouch in conventional Warfare and possibly the best in SCD to my knowledge under these circumstances wouldn't be able to best Zilkstan currently, displaying Lelouch's ability to think outside of just the battle itself and at the bigger picture allowed him to immediately overcome this mental block

1:20:23 - Lelouch immediately reasons that Zilkstan has someone in possession of geass, something not even C.C was confident in as she says "are you sure?" Clearly relying on Lelouch's judgement. Even though Lelouch was told of Zilkstan's military might as they were able to "beat a britannian army twice their size" and "they have the home advantage" as stated by Cornelia at 55:11, them being outnumbered "600 to 1" as stated by Ohgi at 54:55 and on top of this their forces have "hardly enough supplies to go around" as stated by Tamaki at 55:01. Further reinforcement of this comes from Gilford at 55:17 "The fact we're even here is a tremendous risk it would be far better if we try the diplomatic route" showing that nobody was even remotely thinking of battling Zilkstan showing that them simply being outmaneuvered by a phenomenal military which was hundreds of times larger than them is a very obvious plausibility when explaining their current circumstances.

With all of these odds against him, Lelouch still discards all these and is confident they have a geass, showing his ability to think of everything and not only believe the evidence presented to him beforehand. With the amount of evidence suggesting simply a phenomenally strong military. Lelouch being able to diverge away from the evidence to even assume geass is a great display of Lelouch's ability to always consider more options and even look beyond the observable and logically sound in order to reach his conclusions.

1:20:30: after Lelouch self ascertains the usage of geass, it only takes Lelouch 7 seconds before he formulates different possibilities for what the geass could be, bear in mind Lelouch was also speaking to C.C during this period and so was creating ideas whilst communicating, showing impressive multi-tasking. This feat gains its most impressive standing via adversity. With the possibility of geass being literally anything, labelling ideas as noteworthy becomes a troubling task as they're so many different ways any 1 concept of geass can be utilised in the scenario whether directly or Indirectly, essentially making the options limitless and consequently making judgements on what is more plausible becomes difficult.

At 1:20:32 Lelouch states his first geass possibility, being 'read minds' and also began expansions upon the details and criteria for this geass possibility. This gives us insight into how he's analysing each geass possibility with multiple factors and criteria for each possibility as shown when Lelouch had 2/a & 2/b having at least 2 factors for just the mind reading possibility.

1:21:00: Lelouch is now shown giving 8 different plans to C.C, this is shown only 28 seconds after the first geass possibility was made. This is where deeper analysis shall be made. Now for each plan Lelouch would've had to account for 3 variables. The geass itself, the person wielding it, and how it'd operate methodologically. Now 1 Geass, 1 Individual & 1 Methodology will make what that doc will refer to as a GIM. Using the mind reading GIM as an example. Lelouch made mind reading with 2 variables 2/a & 2/b this means Lelouch did a GIM analysis on each variable meaning that the mind reading GIM now becomes 2 mind reading GIM. Since Lelouch considered these 2 variations very easily and were coined in less than 3 seconds, it's safe to assume he could've made more variants for Mind Reading. But this won't be considered but instead it'll be used as a benchmark so each GIM Lelouch will have 2 variations to align with Lelouch's evidence of how he's thinking thus far.

Since lelouch had made 8 plans during the 28 second difference. Each plan would cover at least 1 GIM but with 2 variation for each GIM, we can assume each plan will cover a total of 2 possibilities. Now doing basic maths we get 28 seconds ÷ 8 plans = 3.5 plans per second and a GIM variation was aligned into each Strategy in an average time span of 1.75 seconds (3.5 seconds ÷ 2 GIM variants) and this is assuming that Lelouch was strategizing while formulating new ideas simultaneously. To facilitate this, you can half the time to allow a 50:50 split between GIM creation & Planning formulation. With this, each GIM took only 0.875 seconds to analyse with a following 0.875 seconds to formulate a plan to best encompass and test each GIM variant. Also to note, 0.875 isn't a high ball as it only considers if 2 variables were used for each GIM, if you use 3 variables for each GIM, high balling it to factor in the possibility of another Mind Reading variants being formulated off screen and then setting that as your benchmark, then the number decreases to 0.593 seconds for the GIM analysis for 1 variable and another 0.583 seconds for formulating a plan to test it whereas if you use 1 variable, using that as your benchmark and assuming only mind reading had 2 variations, it'd be 1.75 seconds for GIM analysis and another 1.75 seconds for planning formulation. All this is also calculated with no breaks, meaning under these values, Lelouch was thinking constantly with no pauses in his analysis meaning that these could even be lower to account for any moments where Lelouch wasn't thinking however this is incalculable so I won't be following this approach.

1:21:24: This next part of the feat starts once Lelouch shows that he recognises Tamaki's voice when they make a surprise reach to his channel despite communication supposedly being down. Hearing that he and Ohgi "took over an enemy knightmare". Later at 1:21:33 Lelouch says "gentlemen" This'll be used as the end point of this thinking process I'll briefly explain. During this 9 second time span between 1:21:24 and 1:21:33, Lelouch formulates a tactic to use a communication broadcasted to all channels in order to make an announcement.

He makes this announcement on every channel available so everyone will hear him. He announces about a "final phase" as a bluff and uses this to "force the enemy to risk an order" as stated by Cornelia at 1:23:11. This order is forced due to paranoia inducement. With Lelouch mentioning a mysterious final phase, this implies that the enemies current formation and tactics are about to be countered. With the paranoia of possible defeat looming, this puts pressure on the commander (In this case Fortinard) to respond in some way, henceforth forcing an order to try rearrange in order to adapt and prepare for this “final phase” despite it never actually coming. This also gives an opportunity for everyone else to respond to the changing battlefield in order to unilaterally relieve pressure on a majority of his fleet. This feat shows Lelouch's ability to change his thought patterns and immediately alter to formulating ideas to create lifesaving tactical maneuvers by completely understanding how the enemy would respond, this prediction is proven true via Fortinard's statement at 1:23:02 "inform all forces that the perimeter has taken priority" showing him reacting to Lelouch's announcement just as he foresaw.

1:26:00: After a display of Shamna using her geass to counter 2 of Lelouch's plans, he reasons that mind reading is eliminated and can narrow down the user of Geass to someone in a position of power as they are able to "mobilise the military at will". Now he begins to further converge on who the geass user is, eliminating king Shalio from the analysis via his occupation with Suzaku, now stating it to be Princess Shamna or general Fortinard. He later encompasses Shamna's advisors into this array of suspects (There's 6 advisors so the total who are shown on screen so possible geass holders now raises to 8)

1:26:25: Lelouch ponders why Zilkstan hasn't used Nunnaly's Intelligence, reasoning that "she herself is the objective" and further evaluates that the geass holder “doesn't care whether the kingdom is jeopardised". Lelouch then appears to reach a conclusion regarding the motivations of his enemy at 1:26:43 but his thoughts are interrupted by C.C so we never get conclusive evidence on what this conclusion exactly was however based on future events, we're led to believe that it was as early as this point that he deduced that Shamna wished to use Nunnally to gain access to C’ world expand the power of herbgeass. With Lelouch having nearly no concrete evidence on anything at this point, this speaks to the volume of this feat.

1:26:23: Lelouch states that he's narrowed down the possible geass number to 22. And he remains with the 8 geass suspects (Shamna, Fortinard, 6 advisors). The number of methods of ambush used by Shamna is a total of 7 up to this point (Cornelia twice, Suzaku & Kallen, Sayoko & others twice, countering 2 of Lelouch's plans) putting this all together. Now with the calculation 22 × 8 × 7, we get a total of 1,232 GIM possibilities and as a result of the 2 variations for each Geass, the calculation changes to 44 × 8 × 7 which is 2,464 possibilities. You can choose to either pursue the 1232 route or the 2464 route depending on whether you personally believe the 2nd variations were consistent or not.

Now between 1:26:24 & 1:26:44 Lelouch analyses all the GIM possibilities & variations in a 20 second process and narrows down to 2. Doing more maths, this results in 2 outcomes depending on whether you scale the extra variations route or not.

1232 route
20 seconds ÷ 1232 GIM = 0.0162337662 seconds per GIM analysed.

2464 route
20 seconds ÷ 2464 GIM = 0.0081168831 seconds per GIM analysed

This is phenomenal, considering this is an entire set of different battle tactics and adaptions over an extensive time period with each ambush occurring at different periods. In summary this means Lelouch was able to critically analyse this amount of information in a situation as adverse as his with a power as abstract, complex and unpredictable as geass is within only 0.01/0.008 seconds and did this constantly for 20 seconds showing how he was able to go in depth into every GIM scenario for every individual, geass & and method, making sure all variables aligned or not.

What this means is that each GIM is a 3 premise reasoning process

P1 - The geass aligns/doesn't align
P2 - The individual aligns/doesn't align
P3 - The methodology aligns/doesn't align (P3 is done for all 7 methodologies used)

Conclusion - the GIM is/isn't a possibility

This means that with each possibility having 9 premises for each reasoning process, this would result in:

1232 route
1232 × 9 = 11,088 premises in total

2464 route
2464 × 9 = 22,176 premises in total

By the and of the analysis, Lelouch has reached 2 final possibilities and after enacts his final tactic vs Shamna to defeat her and save Nunnally, seen in the summary of the Shamna strategy analysis below
