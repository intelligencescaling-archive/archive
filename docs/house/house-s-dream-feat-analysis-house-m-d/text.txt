House Dream feat:

In season 3 episode 16 House dreams about the face of a soldier whose he saw 10 years ago (for ten seconds). His next patient then turns out to be this soldier (this is probably a coincidence as while House’s mind has insane foresight I doubt it’s to a precognitive degree).

Dream explanation:

In a later point in the episode House dreams about not being able to sleep, then going into the hospital where Chase and Foreman are asleep on the couch. He then goes into his office where they tell him they put him on sodium bicarbonate to flush the potential uranium out of the patient’s system while House was asleep.

He then goes to the patient where Cuddy is standing over him asking who approved a sodium bicarbonate infusion. Cuddy then says that the patient has gone unconscious and his skin has lost all colour. House then says he’s bleeding in. Chase then says there’s no signs of bruising or internal haemorrhaging and then Foreman says how paralysis must’ve reached his diaphragm so he’s not able to oxygenate his blood. House then says he can’t oxygenate his blood because he has none left.

The rest of the team argue on how there’s no way he could’ve lost his blood and refuse to get the blood transfusion to save him. The urine bag attached to his leg then bursts and starts to spill out which causes everyone to look in concern. House continues to demand blood as he starts to nose bleed.

House then realises it’s a dream as Cuddy is there and it makes no sense for Cuddy to be there outside of his dreams as he always dreams about Cuddy.

House then wakes up, having urinated in his bed.

Feat explanation:

After this dream House goes into the office the same time as he went in the dream and everything happened EXACTLY as he dreamt it. And I mean EXACTLY. Foreman and Chase were sleeping in the exact same positions as they did in the dream. They gave the patient the exact same amount of sodium bicarbonate as he dreamed. They said the exact same words as in the dream. What happened to the patient in the dream happens in real life, etc.

Except House intervenes as soon as he gets into the hospital instead of after talking to his team, saving the patient’s life by revealing what the disease that he had was.

Now this covers a lot of categories.

1 – EU & Anticipation

House understands his team to such an extreme extent he can anticipate their exact expressions, dialogue, movements, actions, etc. With PERFECT detail (I’m not even exaggerating this, he literally does this in the episode).

2 – Medical Knowledge, Crystallised intel, Prediction & reasoning

House has so much medical knowledge that he even without knowing what the disease was he knows how the patient’s body will react to the sodium bicarbonate and by the reaction he can compare it to how other specific diseases would react to it and narrow down which disease it might be. Also in the dream his subconscious had already deduced what disease it was as it gave House a nosebleed which was related to the patient’s disease.

3 – Memory

Also he remembered the face of a man from a decade ago he saw for 10 seconds from afar.

Basically House has stupidly Insane Anticipation, Prediction, Emotional understanding, Crystallised intel & Knowledge, he probably gaps BBC Sherley, PJ, Aki, Hannibal, etc in all of these.
