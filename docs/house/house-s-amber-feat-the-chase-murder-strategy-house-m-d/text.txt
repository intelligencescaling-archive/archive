House's Amber feat

Hello everybody! Back with another House analysis. Today, I'm going to explain what I believe to be one of House's greatest feats in intelligence - What I like to call the Chase Murder strategy.

Though I've said it's a strategy here and a feat in the title, that's because I'm not analysing the strategy implemented, but House's method of doing so.

**HUGE SPOILER WARNING FOR HOUSE M.D.!**

The character on the second slide is Amber Volakis. Amber is introduced to the show in season 4, as a contender to get in House's team. In this season, she character uses herself as manipulative, deceitful and willing to do whatever it takes to get in Houses team, though eventually doesn't make the cut. But she doesn't get kicked off the show, no sir. Amber returns a few episodes later after being kicked off as Wilson's new girlfriend, though I'm not gonna explain that drama here.

It's revealed at the end of the season that Amber was the woman who was badly injured on the bus. She dies, leaving Wilson heartbroken and incredibly upset. All we need to take from this is that Amber is dead by the end of the season.

But I lied. The photo of Amber is one from Season 5. How does that make sense? Spoiler: It's not actually Amber. In season 5, House begins to have vivid hallucinations due to his abuse of Vicodin. The most prominent vision he sees is of Amber, who died months prior. House sees her, because she represents the deepest, darkness side of Houses psychology. The "cutthroat bitch," as she's commonly referred to. So why is she important here?

Towards the end of season 5, Chase has a bachelor party to celebrate his wedding with Cameron. House insists upon planning the party, which he eventually does. To Wilson's distrust. Though, House does win Wilson over when he introduces a stripper, Karamel, who both House and Wilson knew ten years prior. Later, Chase ends up licking Karamel (can't remember why), and he falls into anaphylactic shock. Why? Karamel's body butter (what strippers use to make themselves look shiny) is strawberry flavoured, and Chase is allergic to strawberries. Though Chase does survive.

You might be wondering how House and Amber tie into this, and I'll answer. House knew this was going to happen. In fact, he planned the whole thing. And was using Amber to do it.

Amber, like I said, represents House's "dark thoughts," the things House keeps to himself as they're usually inappropriate and evil.

This manifests at and before the bachelor party, and Amber reveals to House in the tub that the whole ordeal was orchestrated by the two of them because they were jealous of Chase.

Now, why I think this is an amazing feat by House:

Amber isn't real, very evidently. She's very clearly a persona made by his mind. All of Amber's thoughts are House's thoughts. And Amber was deceiving House. House was deceiving himself.

House had been crafting Amber for moments like this, she is a mechanism where he can bring himself to do awful things he secretly wants to do. To simplify all of that, House created a system where he could withhold information that he already knew from himself, so he could abuse it without him possibly messing it up due to his own moral concerns. In a few bullet points, House;

 \- Created a version of Amber so accurate to his past perceptions of her that he was able to incorporate her deception and sweeter outer personality to make the strategy work.

\- Subconsciously manipulated himself to orchestrate a chain of events with absolute precision.

\- Proved to himself that he has excellent memory, as he was able to use Amber to remember a detail, pretend that he had no idea, which he then hid from himself.

\- Didn't make Wilson suspicious of him, who was already incredibly wary of Houses adamancy to set up the party.

\- Knew who the first and subsequently only stripper Chase would get that close to would be, so that he could exploit his allergy.

This is an amazing feat, which buffs House's resistances, deception, manipulation, thinking etc even higher, and many other cats where you think this could apply. This version of House is impossible to read correctly, as he himself doesn't know the truth if he applies something like this, which buffs his EM (which would be several average otherwise) ER and EU (Though already high tier in it).

So yeah... House is extremely broken and definitely a high tier.
