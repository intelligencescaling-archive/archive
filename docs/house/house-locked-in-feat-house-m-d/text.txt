House Locked In Feat

The episode starts with Lee in the hospital, where Dr. Kurtz was going to use him for an organ transplant. However, House could tell Lee was still alive, so he called him out on it. Kutz ignored it and told House the dude was braindead, and House could see the signs on the monitor indicating activity in Lee's brain. When Kurtz tried to dismiss the signs as random, House keeps insisting. Then, House points out Lee's eyes following Kurtz, and when he keeps saying it doesn't matter, House faces Lee directly and tells him he knows about locked-in syndrome. **(VSI, WMI, Inductive Reasoning)**

Then, we cut to the intro, and Dr. Kurtz is finally on House's side after checking out Lee's eyes. Kurtz makes the temporary system of "1 blink = yes, 2 blinks = no" for asking questions. Kurtz also explains the accident Lee's in, saying he had brain damage from the crash. However, House, pretty much on instinct, wonders whether it was pre-existing brain damage which caused the crash instead. He starts observing Lee's body to confirm, and his hands were completely fine, meaning Lee didn't move or protect himself from the crash. House argues a bit more with Kurtz before the latter leaves. **(VSI, Body-Kinesthetic Intelligence, Deductive Reasoning)**

After an emotional scene with Lee's girlfriend (Molly), we cut back to House, but now the team's here. A bit of talk later, 13 points out a tumor could explain the issues, so House sends them to check. Lee starts praying in his mind, House somehow deduces he is, and tells him to stop it. This one's particularly interesting, because it implies House understands Lee well enough already. **(Emotional Understanding, Emotional Facilitation, PSI, VSI)**

They take Lee to the MRI, where he puts on a headset that puts him in a quiet place, in this case a beach... then House is there, apparently. He talks with Lee about the incident, about God, and it's clear this is in Lee's head because House is mentioning things like the car door and him going to church. Impressive for House to get in Lee's head so well. **(Emotional Understanding, VCI)**

After the operation, House enters Lee's room and explains what's going on. He has cancer, but his doctors think it's an infection, which is bad because the antivirals could kill him. However, House tells Lee that he plans on keeping him on the antivirals regardless until they almost kill him, so that House can be proven right and is given control over the treatment. Lee then almost immediately starts seizing. House starts arguing with Dr. Kurtz over the treatment, and House starts manipulating Molly into agreeing with him to give him plasmapheresis. **(Emotional Manipulation, Strategy, Short-Term Planning, Logical Reasoning)**

While taking Lee to a room to get him plasmapheresis, he argues with Taub about him wanting to resign and then regretting it but House deciding for him that he's leaving anyway because he no longer has incentive to innovate and surprise House, "unless I'm wrong, save the cheerleader, save your world", direct manipulation tactic **(Emotional Understanding, Emotional Manipulation, Logical Reasoning, VCI)**. Then, they're setting up everything for the treatment, and House comes up after being warned of something. He decides it's a good idea to biopsy Lee's brain. While receiving anesthesia, Lee's back on the beach with House for a bit. After that, Chase asks him a few questions, but suddenly Lee can't blink.
