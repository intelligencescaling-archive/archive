House No Reason Feat Analysis

**Source: S2E24 "No Reason"**

In the start of the episode, House was shot by his patient and went unconscious.

After waking up from surgery to treat his wounds, he talked a bit with Cameron about Harpo, a patient who has a massively swollen tongue. When she mentioned she biopsied his tongue, House says that the biopsy was negative, including the blood tests. Cameron call out that she never mentioned that, but House says "you don't biopsy a tongue unless you have to, and you don't have to unless you've already come up with nothing on a routine battery of tests which don't involve torturing the patient". He then mentions increased intercraneal pressure, as he knew the next step after that was a lumbar puncture, which would give a definitive answer, and since they don't have one, it means they didn't do an LP, and the main reason they don't do an LP is because of increased intercraneal pressure **(WMI, Knowledge, Inductive Reasoning, PSI)**.

After a while, House is talking with the attempted killer, and the guy reveals his wife was House's patient, and he did cure her, but he convinced him EVERYTHING was relevant, convincing him to tell House about his affair. House told the wife about the affair, the wife then killed herself **(Direct Manipulation)**.

Quickly after that, he's talking with the wife of Harpo (refer to the first paragraph), and he quickly analyzes his wife to see if she has anything to do. He compares both their beauties, economic status (through looking), anything to see why they're together. He then tells her the most likely cause of Harpo's infection is an STD, deducing she had an affair with someone **(Inductive and Deductive Reasoning, VSI, PSI)**. However, after a while of talking to Chase, Cameron, and Foreman, he's notified that the woman he just spoke to was dead, since Harpo is a widower, meaning he hallucinated the entire conversation with a woman he never met or saw **(insane VSI)**.

After that, he's talking with Wilson about what just happened, while reading his surgical file. After reading it, he notices he was given almost no anesthesia but a lot of ketamine. He realizes he was put in a dissociative coma during his surgery, which is causing the lack of pain in his leg and altering his mental state **(Inductive Reasoning, PSI)**.

While talking to Chase, Cameron, and Foreman about Harpo, he makes an allegory about the chest lymph nodes. "What do you do when your trash can's full? You dump your trash in the neighbor's trash can. But it's still light outside, he'll see you, so you go down the back alley and dump it in his garage", to which Chase IMMEDIATELY realizes what House meant and says they'll check the lymphatic system in the chest. Chase explains that "the saliva glands from the tongue are connected to the lymphatic system in the lungs, it's the next lymphatic system over". After surprising House and leaving, the killer wonders how he knew, to which House replies that he hired him because he's smart, and the killer says that maybe the question wasn't as tricky as he thought, letting House realize his mind keeps deteriorating **(Inductive Reasoning)**.

While talking to Wilson about his deteriorating intellect due to the surgery, Wilson seems surprisingly fine with this, as if he's completely sure it was ok. House, knowing Wilson always struggles with moral choices, finds this very suspicious and realizes Wilson had known for days, meaning Cuddy also knew for days about the surgery, which means this was planned by her to cure his leg pain **(Emotional Understanding, Inductive Reasoning, PSI)**. However, suddenly, after hitting Wilson, House realizes he's hallucinating and is woken up by the killer **(VSI because he just hallucinated the whole bathroom and the hospital main floor and Cuddy's office, picturing them perfectly)**.

Then, he's talking with Chase, Cameron, and Foreman in the ICU about a test, but suddenly he's in the stairwell with them talking about the test being negative again. He starts jumping between steps and running up and down the stairs to realize he has no idea what happened between him in the ICU and him now, so he knows he's also having blackouts. Then, he's talking with Cuddy about taking himself off the case because he's losing it, but he realizes Cuddy jumped up when he entered. Cuddy says she thought he was going to attack him again due to what happened with Wilson, but House knows that was a hallucination, meaning THIS is a hallucination, and wakes up **(Inductive Reasoning, PSI, VCI)**.

House is then talking with the killer about what's real and what's not, and he figures out a way to distinguish what's real. He's going to throw ideas at them, and if they call it out for being false, it's real **(Strategy, WMI)**.

Suddenly, at the end of the episode, House starts realizing that, every time Chase, Cameron, and Foreman come to him with test results, he knows them already. Every time he throws an idea, they're all on board, they haven't been questioning him at all. As long as the hallucination makes sense, his mind lets it keep going, so he kills Harpo to wake himself up **(Inductive Reasoning, PSI, VCI, Intrapersonal Intelligence)**. And IT. WORKS.

However, what was unexpected is that HE HALLUCINATED THE WHOLE THING. The entire day, the entire case, everything from the shot on, he all hallucinated it. He solved a day worth of a case in just minutes while being taken to the ER for the shot. He asks for ketamine, he now knows the method to cure his leg somehow without even talking to Cuddy about it.

**Insane EU and EM, Inductive Reasoning, VCI for picturing the entire hospital perfectly, PSI for doing an entire day worth of a case in literal minutes, Intrapersonal Intelligence for knowing his own mind so much he can do all of this.**

And then the almost supernatural aspect of knowing the ketamine method to cure his leg before even hearing about it, but that could just be absolutely bonkers Reasoning.

Honestly, insane overall FSIQ feat.
