Machiya FSIQ (psi-wmi-vsi) feat - Island feat

This feat's purpose is to scale, mainly, Machiya's PSI and WMI, and secondarily his VSI.

# Context – What Happened:

Rascal (the murderer) declares that they must identify him within 3 minutes, or he will kill the captured child.

* Machiya begins concentrating (shown across multiple panels).
* Machiya: *"Give me silence, I'm in the process of editing."*
* Mai: *"Looks like Machiya is editing everything he witnessed today in his mind, turning it into a film."* (Panel showing Machiya processing everything.)
* Immediately in the next panel, Machiya finishes his recall, reacting with *"!!"*, and then immediately begins explaining his reasoning feat.

# Feat Summary:

Machiya recalls an entire day’s events with photographic detail, as though replaying a film in his mind—including even minor details, as explicitly shown in the panels and his reasoning. This means that he had to adjust every single memory to the right angolation (for the right point of view). Simultaneously, he solves a complex case on the spot. Both in a really short period of time. But exactly how much time?

**Speed calculation** (if you do NOT want to crack your balls and just wanna skip the speed calculation part, the short answer is that it was done in 10.3 sec.)

**ADVERSITIES IN THE FEAT**

\-*Unpredictability*: He had to instantly begin recalling everything at an unexpected moment when Rascal demanded proof of his identity to spare the child’s life.

\-*Memory Load*: The arc was highly detailed, requiring retention of a large volume of information. It was around 300 panels worth of material.

\-*Incomplete Information*: Many events left unanswered questions and unresolved logic, forcing him not only to recall but also to reason through and resolve them in real-time.

\-*Dual-tasking*:  He simultaneously performed an intricate reasoning process while recalling every event.

\-*Physical Exhaustion*: Right before the feat, a panel shows Machiya visibly tired from running around, reinforcing the idea that he performed this under suboptimal physical conditions. This aligns with the third interpretation, as Machiya is shown fatigued just two panels before Rascal begins questioning him.
