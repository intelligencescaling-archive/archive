Evbo's parkour skills (Season 1 only)

Let's start from the beginning of the series, when Evbo was barely able to make 2 block jumps. He was self aware of his own weaknesses however he strategized on the spot by bribing the parkour pro with a 360 jump. He manages to perform one despite never doing anything as unique as a 360 before and with that he makes the last jump in the course a 1 block instead of a 2 block, improving his chances of victory. The Parkour Pro even says that it was the coolest thing he has ever seen a parkour noob do.

In parkour prison, he learns to do diagonal jumps in quick succession and also manages to perform a 360 as a bribe again, getting the hang of it. He manages to leave parkour prison but is forbidden from ranking up ever again, however when he buys his wood house he meets the Old Man who sends him to the outskirts to get a barrier block, during his journey he meets 2 Parkour Pros and shows a backwards jump to both of them, which they both attempt to do and fail. This is impressive because Parkour Pro's are quite literally born more skilled at parkour than Parkour Noobs, yet Evbo manages to make a jump that even they can't make.

When he ranks up to be a parkour pro, he makes many jumps that for normal noobs would be difficult, however along the entire course he doesn't make any mistakes until the 2.5 block stair jump, which he states is the hardest jump he's ever seen. However when he does fail and land on a lower block he immediately understands why he failed, because he jumped too early. Learning from his mistake he manages to make the jump the second time. When he makes it to the last jump, which is made to be impossible, he remembers the barrier block and uses it to make the jump and become a Pro.

At the Parkour Pro level he gets challenged to a parkour battle by a parkour master and despite not even knowing about the existence of parkour battles upto this point, he manages to perform a 3 block vertical jump, which he once against states was the hardest jump he'd ever seen. Then he performs another 360, remembering how his unique jumps are not something other players do in Parkour. And we see this pay off as the Parkour Master fails the 360, later Evbo does a 2 block jump with a 360, which he purposefully made harder to prevent the Parkour Master from potentially learning how to perform a 360 just from his first failed jump, just like Evbo did with the 2.5 stair jump in the ranking up course. A 2 block 360 is also something Evbo had never done before, however he had become skilled enough at normal 360's that he was able to do it first try. He even threatens the Parkour Master with a 3 block 360, not only confident in his own skill but also aware of how the Master wouldn't be able to perform one, thus the Parkour Master gives up

Later on during his ranking up to be a Parkour Master, he not only notices the impossible jump, he copies it and then finds the invisible block. Later on he starts stocking up on daily tasks, allowing him to practice for 1 hour, he then reconstructs the parkour course from memory and then goes to rank up.

While ranking up he parkours on invisible blocks, trapdoors, doors, ladders and while he's doing the course he gets locked into a parkour battle but he not only performs the same jump as the Pro with ease but he performs a jump he had never done in his life before, which he knew the Pro also wouldn't be able to do due to it's uniqueness, a 2 block downwards backwards diagonal jump

Near the end of the course he manages to perform multiple chain jumps, despite never even seeing them before. When he makes it to the Parkour Master level he does a 3 block 360 and then a 2 block 360 right after, despite never doing the former at all and also never doing a 360 jump combo.

When the Parkour Master he defeated tells him to go a secret house in the Parkour Master level, Evbo considers that it might be an ambush, however he knows it's very unlikely because if the Parkour Master wanted him gone, he wouldn't have given him the totem of undying before. After he learns the truth of how players get assigned their rank he goes to fight the Parkour Champion, however he first has to fight a Parkour Pro and 2 Parkour Masters, all these battles are simply made to waste the amount of blocks Evbo has, later when the Champion arrives he performs a 5 block jump, which Evbo knows for sure is impossible.

Due to Evbo losing the first Champion battle, he gets demoted to Parkour Noob but upon getting the diamond boots from the Parkour God he makes it back to the Parkour Master level and makes a plan with his Parkour Master friend. He goes to the bank and bribes the guard with a backwards 360, he then challenges Seawatt to a Parkour Battle where he makes a two 3 block brewing stand jumps, remembering Seawatt's words about how brewing stand jumps can deal any potion effect. He then applies slowness to Seawatt, threatens him with a jump that is impossible with slowness which makes Seawatt spill the beans . Seawatt tells him about the Champion's barrier blocks and also about the secret rule of Parkour Battles. Evbo then negotiates, Seawatt will send Evbo to the arena again where he will fight the Champion in a 1 on 1, without any other fights. Evbo makes a threat that if he doesn't arrive in the arena alive, Evbo's friend will kill Seawatt. Seawatt, fearing for his life, complies and Evbo arrives at the arena once more.

Evbo uses the secret rule to make a harder jump than the Champion's "5 block jump" which in reality is a 4 block jump, Evbo then counters the Champion's invisible parkour combo with a 1 block vertical flower pot jump into a backwards 360 candle jump, which was harder than the Champion's. The Champion then builds a Neo Jump, which Evbo states are the hardest type of jumps on their own and he knows he can't do them but he still manages to counter with a series of jumps that, on their own, aren't harder than a Neo Jump but together are.

The Champion then creates a 3 block Neo Jump over the void, a jump that both he and Evbo will have to do. Evbo, knowing he doesn't have enough blocks to build a harder jump, has to perform it. He knows his chances of failure are massive but he still focuses and stays determined, ultimately making the jump and beating the Champion.

Overall Evbo amazing learning capabilities for Parkour, especially when you consider that Noobs are born with less mastery of Parkour but through sheer determination and practice he's able to surpass Pro's, Master's and finally the Champion. He learns to perform hard jumps very quickly and is a master of unique jumps such as 360's and backwards jumps. Parkour on it's own requires good VSI. Evbo also shows good EM and AC as he's able to control his emotions during the final Neo Jump. He also has decent Manipulation and Planning with him threatening Seawatt in order to get a fair 1v1 with the Champion. His strategy and thinking are decent.

Overall ranking of Season 1 Parkour Evbo: Middle of Low Tier
