Aizen vs Zetsu in manipulation

I decided to do this after posting it all on X, so here is the thread.

I focused mainly on direct, indirect, logical, emotional, mass and individual types of manipulation. My main method was to primarily focus on the circumstances of said feats, the lasting effects it has on the targets, the active manipulation, and the likes for characters.

First we have direct manipulation.

Zetsu’s main feat of direct manipulation comes from that him manipulating Indra and Madara.

Where for Madara he acted on behalf as Madara’s will, and acted compliment for him, then eventually, back stabbed Madara when the time came, he also manipulated Indra who lost the fight against Ashura into temptation, however his methodology is unknown for this last one. All we know for sure is after Indra lost and Indra was self isolating, only relying on his own strength, Zetsu used that to his advantage and it led to the eventful fight between Indra and Ashura.

While notable, taking advantage of a character’s ignorance and their isolation. But how does Aizen compare? Aizen doesn’t utilize direct manipulation as he does with indirect manipulation, but he has showcases of it. A few examples included how he painted Gin as the enemy in front of Toshiro, which for a genius like him, Toshiro will question things, but because of Aizen’s framing, it led to Toshiro maintaining an attention on Gin the whole time. On top of faking his death, creating practically the perfect alibi, Aizen manipulated Toshiro in the long run so he couldn’t look even deeper. Another one is his manipulation of Momo, Aizen already has immense charisma, but he manipulated her in a way that made her completely emotionally dependent on him, when a time of crisis happens, Aizen placed his jacket on her to signal protection, then calmed her down as well. That one scene is an example, but Aizen did things like that from the moment they met, but anyhow, Aizen set up a series of events would cause Momo to be the first one to see his staged death, and she will stop thinking rationally, Aizen then sets up a series of events after the fact, but those will be detailed in logical manipulation, indirect manipulation, and the likes, but one of the feats included him making a fake letter to get Momo to try and kill Toshiro. This is also to show the long term psychological effects he has on Momo, where even after there is proof of his betrayal, she still tries to rationalize it, showing Aizen’s long term control over his targets.

Overall, Aizen takes direct manipulation, due to his versatility and how deeply he broke his targets of the longer running bits for his manipulation.

Now for indirect manipulation.

Zetsu’s main feat for indirect manipulation comes from his tampering with the Uchiha Stone Tablet, rewriting Hagoromo’s original message so someone like Madara would get a certain conclusion from it.

However this feat primarily falls under the fact that Madara has no reliable way to verify how authentic the tablet is beyond taking it as true.

As for Aizen, he has many forms of indirect manipulation, such as how because of his understanding of Momo and Izuru, when Momo would charge at Gin, Izuru would jump in and protect Gin, causing the two to fight and it would lead to Izuru almost killing Momo, thank also the fact Toshiro gave the information to Momo.

Another is the constant use of it in Ichigo’s growth:

\-Tampered with Uryu’s competition to summon a Gillian for Ichigo’s power growth to skyrocket.

\-Instigated the Ichigo and Grimmjow rivalry indirectly.

One of Aizen’s other methods include how he can leave behind a Hollow to cause destruction and allows others to take care of his dirty work as another form of indirect manipulation, causing self hatred from Rukia in the coming years that Aizen saw and was able to use to his advantage later.

This shows his indirect manipulation is once again incredibly versatile and potent, dwarfing Zetsu by comparison.

Next up is logical and emotional manipulation.

As established before, the main Zetsu feats for logical manipulation comes from his Uchiha Stone Tablet rewriting.

Emotional manipulation wise, there isn’t much apparent aspects to it, other than when he tried it on Naruto… which didn’t work out.

As for Aizen, we already saw how his logical manipulation is versatile, evidence tampering, fabricating letters and his death, influencing decision making based on framing that is intended to work on geniuses, but another one is how he brought up Renji’s past with Rukia and on top of that give way of Ichigo’s location to instigate the Ichigo and Renji rivalry.

As for emotional manipulation, Aizen was capable of on multiple occasions taunting his targets in ways to get them to make irrational decisions, rage baiting Hiyori to charge head first towards him only for her to get sliced in half by Gin, destroying Ichigo’s morale thoroughly, destabilizing the Gotei 13 and Vizard’s team work to cause them to break apart the team work and leave them vulnerable, using Orihime’s desires to protect her friends against her by putting her in a multi layered psychic cage to break her will.

Once again, by how thorough Aizen’s feat is, he takes it.

And finally for individual and mass manipulation. Both Aizen and Zetsu because of their circumstances employ mass manipulation more times than individual, but to knock individual out of the way, since Aizen won direct manipulation, he will take this one too, especially since the only evidence of Zetsu’s individual manipulation by feats is how he conducts himself with Madara, but Aizen’s manipulation of Toshiro was better because of how Toshiro is much smarter than Madara as a character, and Aizen’s manipulation of Momo was far more destructive to Momo as an individual.

But let’s move on to mass. Zetsu’s main feat will come from by manipulating the Uchiha Stone Tablet, and choosing Indra’s bloodline  for who he will use, for generations, he was able to shape Shinobi History. This is arguably Zetsu’s best feat, but as we know not everyone was under his control, Naruto broke the cycle, Itachi went against the Uchiha and killed a lot of them, and more which shows Zetsu’s control wasn’t absolute.

In contrast, Aizen has multiple mass manipulation feats, using the narrative framing of Ichigo and the gang rescuing Rukia, Aizen disrupted them and made chaos across the Sereitei so he can act more secretly and give himself more freedom. Another time, is how mid fight, he was capable of destabilizing the Gotei 13+Vizards’ teamwork, leaving openings for Aizen to take them down, or how he manipulated Central 46 with false alibis using a stand in for himself, alongside leaving Kisuke with the crime scene and his research would cause them to then banish Kisuke, or how his Orihime capture plan, led for his long running strategy of isolating Gotei 13 members in Hueco Mundo.

I can go on with other feats, but Aizen CONSISTENTLY shows more apparent mass manipulation compared to Zetsu.

Overall, by a perfect score, it’s very apparent that Aizen is the superior manipulator to Zetsu, the only reason Zetsu’s manipulation works is because of his historical nature, nobody can counter his manipulation because they are so disconnected from history that people won’t think about it that much, but Aizen has more active control over his targets across all domains of manipulation.

If they were to switch places, Zetsu would likely struggle against Shinji and be incapable of executing the Turn Back the Pendulum strategy Aizen did, but Aizen by contrast would speed run Zetsu’s whole plan.

The winner is Aizen.
