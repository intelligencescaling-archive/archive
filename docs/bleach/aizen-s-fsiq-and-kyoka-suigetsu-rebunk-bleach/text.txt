The thing to take note of the context is that Tokinada copied Kyoka Suigetsu from Aizen, because his Shikai’s ability is that, he copies other Zanpakuto. And while it is true that the Shikai he copied can be stronger or weaker because of the spiritual pressure put into it, that doesn’t automatically translate that for its core fundamental aspects being erased. Examples to note, Tokinada must consistently put the targets under an illusion. Characters can still further develop their Zanpakuto abilities, Ichigo, Toshiro and Renji being examples of this.

If it was just that, why would Aizen still need to show his sword to put you under his illusion? Why does he need to put in so much effort on hiding the fact you need to touch his sword to escape the illusion? Heck, Yamamoto detected a flaw in the illusion, and Unohana intuitively figured out something was wrong when analyzing Aizen’s corpse. It should be noted as well that in Turn Back the Pendulum, Aizen had someone take his place and perfectly mimic his movements, speech patterns and more, then casted Kyoka Suigetsu to make it seem like it’s him. If Kyoka Suigetsu required no cognitive load and is just raw power, why would Aizen go through all this trouble and just “I made this illusion and you just thought you saw me the entire time lol”? However, it does imply Aizen needs consistent modeling to go by for it to work aka a mental model, aka he needs FSIQ.

For more consistency, even Kido in Bleach require that you need some form of visualization before casting it. In chapter 81, Ganju’s tip for Ichigo was to specifically visualize a big heavy circle then see yourself diving into it, and in a Q&A with Kubo in KlubOutside, he described it as “healing according to one’s imagination” and he notes how you need medical knowledge for that. Considering there are also mind hax type Kido and Aizen would technically fall under a Kido type Zanpakuto, alongside the statement, it would further support Aizen needing FSIQ for it to work. Unohana even states that despite their difference, medical Kido is not any different to real world medicine, which makes sense since they need medical knowledge to do the feats they do.

For those who don’t know, Zanpakuto have different types and specifications for how they work, we got melee based ones, these extend to Kenpachi, Renji, Ikkaku, nothing special going on there, it’s just a weapon change basically, the in between ones as I call it, these ones do make changes but not too dramatic like projectile types, such as Byakuya or Jirobo, but then we have the Kido type, which are the ones with Kido type abilities, such as Momo’s Zanpakuto, and while not denoted as one, Kyoka Suigetsu can fall into one of them, consider as well how there are illusion based Kido and that Aizen has a preference for using Kido.

Naturally not a universal thing, as characters like Soi Fon have unspecified types, but considering how we have a consistent basis for a mental model, imagination, visualization is needed for spells such as Kyoka Suigetsu, it makes it is needed.

Lastly, the way it debunks the statement is just the surface aspect of it, sure it says he needs to observe it, but why would he need to perfectly reproduce those aspects of the illusions happen to his will? What other part would govern those aspects other then at the very least a mental model? If he is using pattern recognition skills, why wouldn’t he need the mental model? Or as defined in FSIQ, visual spatial index+working memory.

The statement implies that he needs good FSIQ to make a mental model of what he wants to replicate for the illusion, and the same would go for Aizen as a result, as seen by how both he and Tokinada need to maintain coherence of the illusion aka a cognitive load.
