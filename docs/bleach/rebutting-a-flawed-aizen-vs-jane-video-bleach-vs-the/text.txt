Aizen vs Jane flawed video

I love both Sosuke Aizen and I also love Patrick Jane, both as characters and smart characters, so this isn’t coming from a place of bias, as I do my best to research deeply as possible. However the video is massively flawed.

 The main flaw comes from the following fact: We know how Aizen operates in canon when opposition are trying to deconstruct his narrative. I will reference different points in Turn Back the Pendulum, Soul Society arc, and Thousand Year Blood War, to show Aizen’s methodology and how he would beat Jane if the way is going to be that of the average Mentalist episode, or even Red John.

In the video, he claims Aizen would want to prove himself to Jane, and any lie he makes targeted at Jane, will cause Jane to see through him, due to the fact Aizen wants to prove himself to Jane. Which will be Aizen’s downfall.

However, among all the things Aizen can be taken down with, literally just that isn’t the case if you read the manga.

We see in Turn Back the Pendulum, Aizen kept himself lowkey as he tried to determine Kisuke as a threat level, and find a way around Shinji, as he used Shinji’s distrust. When Aizen made his plan, it resulted in Shinji being Hollowfied and faced the risk of death or just being executed by Central 46, and with Kisuke, he implicated multiple pieces of evidence at Kisuke, as well as a solid alibi, and Kisuke would have been banished without powers, if it weren’t for Yoruichi saving Kisuke, which even with that, Kisuke couldn’t return to Soul Society if he so desired.

In Thousand Year Blood War, a key point is that when he was in Muken, Aizen immediately placed Yhwach under hypnosis to determine if it works, it did and Yhwach could never break from his control, as it remained during the final fight.

However since abilities are restricted, that’s fine, because in Soul Society arc, Aizen implicated Gin as the threat through indirect means, causing Toshiro to go after Gin and miss Aizen, Aizen’s fake death further helped his plan.

Jane is actually an easy target for Aizen comparatively, since Jane is difficult to work for most CBI, except the main ones, regularly breaks the law, gets on everyone’s bad sides, and is willing to do things like feign insanity and self isolation just to get close to Red John. This would actually back fire against Aizen, since Aizen can effectively play the part of an innocent man, with someone else as the master mind, then it turns out to be Jane when everyone least expects, because Aizen planted fake evidence long in advance that proves Jane’s guilt.

Wouldn’t Aizen want to prove himself to Jane? No, Aizen’s narrative point is that he wants an equal who sees things from his perspective, which isn’t him wanting to be validated, because as someone who was ONE OF the strongest, smartest, talented and such, he never knew the experience of someone who can understand him. This is why he dislikes Kisuke, while respecting his intelligence and recognizing Kisuke is the smarter one, Aizen hates that Kisuke never took independent action and just accepted the way the world is. This is in fact what makes Aizen and Ichigo’s relationship so complex.

Jane has no such desires similar to Kisuke if anything, at most Aizen will be disappointed at best, because Kisuke and Ichigo are two very unique people:

\-Kisuke is the only one who saw the Reio, and is smart enough to take independent action but he saw the same thing Aizen did, and didn’t do it.

\-Ichigo is also someone of immense power and potential, but also had different experiences compared to others that come together to form the unique perspective he has, on top of the fact Ichigo isn’t bound by the same standards of Soul Society. This is why Ichigo is the ONLY character who can see things Aizen sees, nobody else can.

Even so, he won’t let it consume him, because we know the moment he had the chance he got Kisuke to be banished, he wouldn’t want validation. The only way to say validation is an idea is when Aizen was in Deicide, but as the video said, no supernatural abilities, so this is just genuine character assassination for Aizen.
