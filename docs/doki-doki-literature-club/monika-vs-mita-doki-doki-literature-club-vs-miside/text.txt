Kind of an old debate ig (Monika VS Mita)

I compared these two like a year ago (if you're curious, I had Monika high-extreme diffing) but my scaling for Monika has drastically changed since then (also, tiny little justifications sometimes added, but not all the time because I'm a lazy rascal):

Full scale IQ:

Monika - WMI, PSI, CPI, VCI, FRI

Mita - VSI >=

Mita has pretty decent FSIQ too given she was able to learn engineering in a short amount of time and "completely surpassing" the person who was teaching her. But Monika basically has this exact feat on steroids: she learned how to hack into her own simulation and even create several others (which were all stated to be just as real as the actual universe) with her mind. She also taught herself to do this while actively pretending that she was failing in an effort to confuse Metaverse.

VSI is somewhat close given their best feat is pretty much identical. Mita was able to completely recreate the players room in MiSide, presumably only from what she was able to see from his phones camera. While Monika was able to create her own CG of the club room in a few seconds, both of them did this from memory.

Emotional intelligence:

Monika - EP, EU, EF >=, ER, EM, EE >=

Mita - Nothing

EP and EU go to Monika pretty easily given her ability to pick up on the emotional weakpoints of the club members, this is shown pretty clearly when she isolates Sayori, firstly Monika at this point has only known Sayori for around a month (although, depending on how you want to assume Monika perceives time, it might be much much less) while MC has known Sayori for her entire life, Monika however was the only one able to pick up on Sayori's depression. While Monika was manipulating Sayori (basically a few feet away from the other club members) she had to carefully manage her emotional state so that she wouldn't panic or have some noticeable emotional outburst in the club room. Monika is also able to easily convince others to do what she wants by utilizing her emotions, in act 2, despite Sayori initially being the reason MC joined (due to having a past relationship) Monika is able to still convince MC to join by remembering his personality from the first loop, for example, she recalled that he liked manga/anime, so she offhandedly brought up that one of her club members hides manga in the closet, this succeeded in doing two things 1: it made MC feel more comfortable, knowing that his interests (which may be perceived as nerdy or weird) would be accepted and normalized in the club 2: Monika made sure to use female pronouns when referring to this club member, which made MC aware of the fact that at least two of the club members would be girls, which was a big reason he joined the club last time.

Social Intelligence:

Monika - Awareness, Cognition, Integration, Leadership, Charisma >=, Social Skills

Mita - Influence Building >=

Monika is stated to be consistently the most popular girl in class, she's also likely been moved up a grade due to her academic skills (https://doki-doki-literature-club.fandom.com/f/p/3150258947228038078). She's stated to have been the president of the debate club prior to starting the literature club (such clubs can have upwards of 20 people) and has the ability to become a board member of any of the major clubs in school. Which implies that she handled her leadership responsibilities perfectly.

Mita takes influence building from the fact that she was able to manipulate dozens (maybe a lot more if you consider that "praise Mita scene") into basically devoting their life to her.

Adversity Capacity:

Monika - Mental Fortitude, Stress Tolerance, Impulse Management, Perseverance, Cognitive Discipline

Mita - Nothing

Turning off the game at any point causes Monika to instantly be put into excruciating pain for (from her perspective) an infinite amount of time: "I know I asked already, but can you seriously please try not to do that so much? It makes me feel like I'm trapped in Hell.". Despite this, Monika never shows any outward discomfort and at one point will even accept that you'll continue to turn off the game. The fact that Monika is even able to cope with her existence at all is also noteworthy given if you delete Monika before act 3, Sayori will instead be given awareness and will immediately have an existential crisis before killing everyone (Sayori also implied that existing with this knowledge caused perpetual pain, which means Monika is constantly in pain even if the game isn't turned off).

Intelligence Concepts:

Monika - NCPS, Learning Ability, Knowledge Application, Linguistic, Musical, Interpersonal, Intrapersonal, Existential, Naturalistic

Mita - Knowledge >= (maybe, probably not though), Spatial >=, Bodily Kinesthetic

Along with putting up a convincing act, Monika also spends time in game: formulating strategies, developing her coding abilities, learning piano, writing a song, writing poems, etc. (the game can also be sped up by quite a bit, and considering that Monika is able to tamper with your ability to fast forward in act 3, that makes it likely that she can feel time being sped up and thus has to do all these things on a much more rapid level). At one point, Monika says that one of the poems you read was kind of weird because it was more of an experiment to try and break free of the code, Monika also says that she could probably accomplish this, this is also further supported by the fact that Monika is loosely implied to have broken free in DDLC+ secret files and the fact that she already was in some way connected to the real world internet given she A: was the one who posted the game to steam (the fact that DDLC+ is in several languages also seems to imply that Monika learned how to speak these languages), B: knew that she had a real twitter account.

Mita could potentially take knowledge given she seemed to use most of the players she'd trap inside the simulation to accomplish some kind of goal, like: learning engineering, getting better at doing her makeup (in order to disguise herself more effectively), and trapping a person who could write several different kinds of books for her, books like: Physics books, flirting books, debate books, several story books, cook books, books containing hexadecimal code, etc. (Although, to be fair, Monika does probably take knowledge, just if we assume that Mita has trapped hundreds-thousands of people like the "praise Mita scene" seems to imply, then she narratively might be super broken in this.)

Sensory: Mita >=

Foresight: Mita

Mita's just super broken in foresight, given she was able to anticipate pretty much all the players moves despite the fact that there were pretty much an "infinite" number of places he could've been.
