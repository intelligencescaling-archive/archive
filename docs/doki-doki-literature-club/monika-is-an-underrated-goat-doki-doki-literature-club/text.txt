I’ve made a doc before this but that was my first ever doc **and** I did it all in one sitting (W perseverance feat) so I’m not super fond of the quality, either way I just wanted to glaze Monika rq since she is legitimately underrated:

**FSIQ:**

\- She picked up how to code to a near professional level in under a week and while simultaneously keeping up appearances and learning piano (with presumably no prior experience AND she would have to learn it entirely by herself in secret). This alone is like genuinely super human learning ability but what makes it even more impressive is the fact that despite how it looks from our perspective this isn’t just a basic video game it’s a literal universe ([yes this is canon information](https://doki-doki-literature-club.fandom.com/wiki/Mails_(DDLC_Plus)#Side_Stories)) which required entire teams to code and manage but Monika was able to learn this supposedly *extremely* complex computer science by herself and so fast that it surprised the professionals who literally constructed several fully functional simulated universe.

\- This goes for manipulating the character files as well; since it’s a fully functioning simulated universe that means all the characters are technically all just hyper advanced AI, I have to believe that hacking into and manually manipulating such an advanced piece of code wouldn’t be easy for anyone let alone a complete novice who just started coding a week ago.

\- I feel like people underrate how impressive the fact that Monika could literally reason what girl you "like" based on your word choice and writing style in your poetry. Maybe I’m glazing but that is some pretty damn good inductive reasoning, VCI, sensory, etc.

\- Some other stuff

**Manipulation:**

Sayori’s suicide inducement is kind of contentious for a few reasons but I think I have a fairly good argument for why it’s actually relatively impressive. A few things first: 1. Sayori already had depression 2. Monika admits that she messed with her personality a little bit.

\- Despite the fact that Monika did in fact mess with Sayori’s character file she blatantly didn’t mess with it all that much (I personally think this is because she hasn’t fully grasped coding at this point and therefore she didn’t turn it up all the way, this becomes especially apparent in Act 2 where she’s much more competent in coding and therefore Yuri’s personality change is MUCH more noticeable) which means that while Sayori was much more gloomy Monika clearly didn’t believe that it would be enough as she felt the need to talk to her alone (This action also required some strategy, deception and a shit ton of EQ given that she had to push Sayori just far enough that it would make her self-loathing but not so far that Sayori would panic or tell one of her friends what Monika said). This is made even more impressive when you consider the fact that even if you give her exactly what she wants (MC accepting her confession) she still doesn’t feel better AND (from what I remember) Monika never even intended for Sayori to kill herself she just wanted her to stay away from MC (which even further supports the point that Monika didn’t fill the “depression meter” all the way to the top).
