Monika's FSIQ is kind of goated

Some of her feats in FSIQ:

Monika is perpetually receiving information from the simulation she's currently in, on top of that she is also receiving information from VM2 and CS (two other simulations), all of which are considered real universes with their own space and time as to best replicate conditions in Metaverses reality, so it'd be fair to assume that at the very least Monika is constantly absorbing 3 observable universes worth of information.

* She's also stated that she found the twitter account created for her which implies that she also has direct access to the real internet (which makes sense given the feat below).

There's a joke in ACT 1 where Natsuki makes reference to the fact that Monika's name kind of sounds like the Japanese word for squid ("Ika") if you play the game in English (and presumably other languages) she just says that the joke doesn't make sense in translation. However, if the game is played in Japanese Monika says something completely different, instead just telling Natsuki not to say her name like that and how it'll make the translators job more difficult. Basically this shows that Monika is capable of genuinely understanding and speaking whatever language the player chose to play in, which makes sense given how Monika is also the one who put the game on steam and the metafictionality of the game.

Given the fact that Monika isn't given a "route" this also should mean that the CG in ACT 3 was created by Monika herself which would require her to remember the layout/size of the classroom, and all around be capable of designing a good looking CG in a short amount of time.

Monika is able to notice when the player tries to fast forward or skip in ACT 3 and turns off the function, this implies that if the player had played through the entire game with the auto skip function on Monika would've also noticed this and would have to adapt to the speed (which means she'd have infinitely less time to: develop her coding abilities, write poems, write her song lyrics, master the basics of piano, formulate her plans and strategies, etc) and yet she's fully capable of doing all these things.

On Day 2 of Act 1, when the club members first share their poems with each other, after reading a poem written by the Protagonist

* After reading girls’ poems for the first time, Monika understands what styles Yuri and Sayori like (Monika saw Natsuki's poem the day before) and even explains her point of view about their literary nature. Monika can tell which girl would like his poem based on the poem's style and choice of words, by understanding how similar it is to the poems style and preferences of other girls. The accuracy of Monika's analysis of poems can be explained by the psychoanalysis of the club members' personalities that she has compiled for herself.

The fact that Monika was capable of "creating and destroying" several different universes shows that she pretty much completely understands the code that developed them and is able to replicate it to a similar level of quality what dozens of highly skilled engineers struggled to create in the first place. She also had to teach herself these things completely alone and while hiding her intentions by purposefully making her experiments look "clumsy".

Monika saw the image of Sayori hanging herself, just like the Player. She notices that Sayori's fingertips are bloody. From this, she concludes that this is due to Sayori improperly performing the hanging procedure, using a chair, slowly choking, and clawing at the rope, bleeding her fingers in the process. At first, Monika assumes that Sayori did this by changing her mind, but then she thinks that the most logical reason for Sayori's actions is survival instincts, self-preservation.

It can be concluded that the title of President (Monitor Kernel Access Function) does not give users the knowledge of why they are self-aware.
