Terminology explanation of Mahouka world

Weeds

Weeds were described by the second course students in the First High School. Shiba Tatsuya was one of the weeds in the First High as well. It represents the class differences in the First High. Basically, the second course students would not gain much more respect in the First High because of their lower course.

CAD

CAD, the Casting Assistance Device. It is a machine researched & developed using Magical Engineering Technology. A device that simplifies the magic activation process and stores the programs that are required to use magic inside.

Four Leaves Teology (FLT)

A domestic CAD manufacturing company. Originally, the magic engineering parts manufactured by this company were more famous than the finished products. But after developing the ‘Silver Horn’, it became a well-known CAD manufacturing company.

Taurus Silver

Silver is a talented technician who has advanced TEN YEARS of specialized CAD software technology in just one year. However, Tatsuya is the main character in Taurus Silver this role.

Eidos (Individual information body)

In modern magic, individual information bodies refer to ‘information that accompanies things and phenomena.’ And ‘magic’ means modifying individual information entitles.

Idea (Information body dimension)

In modern magic, it refers to ‘a platform used to record individual intelligence bodies.’ The original form of magic is to export magic formulas to this platform.

Activation Sequence

It is a blueprint for magic, a program used to construct magic. The activation sequence data file is stored in CAD in a compressed form. After the magician inputs the psions expansion program, the activation will be converted into a signal according to the data content and sent back to the magician. It replaces the mediums used in ancient magic and allows faster and more efficient use of magic.

Psions (Thought particles)

Non-material particles located in the dimension of supernatural phenomena are information elements that record the results of cognition and thinking. The ‘Individual information body’ that became the BASIS of modern magic theory, and the ‘activation type’ and ‘magic type’ technologies that became the backbone of modern magic are constructed from psionics.

Pushions

Immaterial particles located in the dimension of paranormal phenomena. Although its existence has been confirmed, its form and function have not yet been successfully analyzed. The ordinary magicians can only ‘sense’ the activated pushions at most.
