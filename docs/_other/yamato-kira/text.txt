Project Burning Reaper: A UN black-budgeted division responsible for human experimentation on children in government built orphanages

The Ryo Home: One of the many orphanages around the world built by the BHA, Kira was one of few survivors.
Dr. Jakub Ztratil: The head physician for the Ryo Home, in charge of the most dangerous experiments.
Dr. Amari Toshiaki: Head of the BHA Japan division.
Takagi Shinkichi: A 16 year old enforcer of the Ryo Home, sent to Kireina to find and destroy clues that Kira might have left behind before his arrest.
Yamato Taizen: An influential politician, Yamato Kira’s adopted father who was chosen by the BHA.
Kireina Academy: A prestigious school where students from wealthy/influential backgrounds, or talents are accepted.
Zhou Yuxuan: A Chinese prodigy, and a great detective, he was able to catch and arrest Kira after the murder of a student named Fuku Mayas.
Naoki Hachina: A 16 year old student at Kireina, the bastard of a man who is in charge of Japan’s gambling rings.

FEATS AND STATEMENTS
—-------------
Yamato Kira was able to remember every detail of his life, starting from his toddler years.
(Memory)
—-------------
During Kira’s years in the Ryo Home he was put through multiple experiments that were designed to ring out the weakest of the children. Some of the experiments include, “The Forest Experiment”, “Sound Room Experiment”, “Isolation Experiment”, and the “Sea Water Experiment”.

The Forest Experiment had children blindfolded and put into a truck where they were then sent to a random part of the forest the Ryo Home owned, this is when they were instructed to make their way back to the orphanage while being hunted by operatives of the BHA who were former special forces. Kira was able to calculate where he was going using the setting sun as a reference before being blindfolded, while in the truck he kept in mind the turns, speed and duration before he was dropped off. There he had to avoid armed guards by setting traps such as false trails, sound diversions, and even using a sharp rock to make himself bleed and leave a trail.
(WMI, Determination, Trap Setting, Perception, Intuition, Memory, Decision Making, Observation, Information Gathering, Adaptability)

The Sound Room Experiment had children strapped into a chair where scientists played various sounds from speakers designed to break them down. Kira was able to survive this by being able to limit his emotions.
(Composure, Cognitive Discipline, Stress Tolerance, Mental Fortitude, Impulse Control.)

The Isolation Experiment has children locked into a room alone with enough food and water to last them the duration of the experiment, they are also given toys. Kira was able to survive this experiment as he realized that the scientists wanted them to not use the toys for playing but to make their own friends. Kira grabbed a marker and drew faces on the toys and he treated them like real people.
(Composure, Adaptability, Mental Fortitude, Deduction, Observation, Improvising, Creativity.)

The Sea Water Experiment had kids locked into a room with nothing but seawater and a stove. Before the experiment the scientists taught the kids how to make seawater drinkable. Kira survived this experiment as he remembered the lessons and used the stove to boil the seawater and make it drinkable.
(WMI, Memory, Practical Intelligence)

The Ryo Home taught all the children Judo, Karate, Taekwondo, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Aikido, Muay Thai, Krav Maga and Kung Fu, Kira is the top belt in all of these martial arts.
(Bodily Kinesthetic Intelligence)

The Ryo Home taught all children English, Chinese, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Arabic, Russian, and Hebrew.
(VCI, Linguistic Intelligence)
