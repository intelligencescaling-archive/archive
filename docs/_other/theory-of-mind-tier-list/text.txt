Theory of Mind tier list

Theory of Mind - The human ability to understand that people have mental states different from our own, informed by their own biases, experiences, and emotions, which allow us to make inferences about their behavior and navigate social situations. (Basically EP + EU)

\*Mainly going off what I've read/seen so far from the given characters

**Tier 5 - Average**: Exactly as the title implies

*Beyond Birthday -* Badly imitated L, presumably based on past experiences with him. Generally very socially offputting.

**Tier 4 - Aware**: These people are more conscientious than the average person but still firmly mediocre in a fictional context.

*Joe Goldberg* \- Generally perceptive of his love interests and other people, though his deluded mind often leads him to erroneous conclusions. (See S5, where he really thinks Bronte will run off with him after his insane behavior; or the first novel, where he falls for Beck's trap). But very good social skills, implying some cunning there.

*Ryuuen* \- Utilized his knowledge of psychology to torture Karuizawa and resurface her past trauma, also formed a basic profile of Ayanokouji.

**Tier 3 - Profound:** These characters have an immense ability to model the minds of others. These characters would be the peak of human cognition in terms of emotional intelligence.

*Dexter Morgan -* This is mainly for serial killers, but Dexter possesses deep knowledge of criminal behavior, which he often uses to make predictions about his targets or reconstruct crime scenes in his head after minimal analysis.

*Novels Sherlock Holmes* \- Though heavily carried by his observation skills and reliance on stereotypes, Sherlock Holmes has reconstructed crime scenes casually, even factoring in the behavior of characters he should have no information on.

*Inspector Lunge -* Lunge obsessively models the criminals he's chasing, almost becoming them in order to understand and catch them. Within the Monster universe, he's renowned and even wrote a book on the matter, though it's only Johan's near supernatural mind that made him look impotent.

*Kiyotaka Ayanokouji (Y1)* \- Ayanokouji is implied to have a wealth of psychological knowledge exceeding professional psychologists. In terms of application, we see him utilize this to gradually force Horikita and Sudo to develop as characters and predicting many of Ryuuen's actions after studying him for months.

*L* \- Profiled Kira's psyche with near perfect accuracy based just on his killing patterns. His absurd prowess as a detective implies deep knowledge on criminal psychology. Matched Light in their "I know you know I know" mental battle during the tennis ball scene.

*Walter White* \- Predicted all of Jesse's actions in his Lily of the Valley plan, which is impressive since Jesse, unlike the other characters, is far more capricious and prone to mood swings, making him harder to read. Pinpointed that Gus was the kingpin after reading his expressions of a reflection.

*Light Yagami* \- Almost perfectly profiled L despite only having a short speech to go off of, predicting several of his tactics and even exactly recreating the profile of Kira that L had made, showing extreme metacognition. Noticed Rem's microexpression out of the corner of his eye. Perfectly profiled Near, anticipating his exact, highly specific plan months before he ever formed it.

**Tier 2 - Mindreaders** \- These characters are simply superhuman in this domain, with the least impressive feat among them possibly being outright reading someone's mind without any sort of power.

*Agent 47* \- Several of 47's possible plans presented in the game require an absurd understanding of his targets with minimal information. Outperformed a world class psychiatrist in treating Silvio Caruso and used his psychological knowledge to deduce that Silvio killed his mother based off subtle verbal clues. His keen senses allow him to know exactly who and who wouldn't be keen enough to spot him, even through floors.

*Hannibal Lecter -* Stated to be far and above the smartest person in the world when it comes to this (alongside Will). Chilton Framing plan requires a deep understanding of the FBI's thought process. Mindbroke Will.

*Mark Hoffman* \- Predicted the actions of dozens of people with impossible precision.

*John Doe* \- Predicted the investigation against him a year in advance. Perceived the emotional state of a detective watching him through a one-way glass. Accurately explained what a victim of his was hallucinating.

**Tier 1 - Soulreaders** \- These characters are broken in this field, knowing the people they across as they were, are, and will be better than the victims could ever hope to know. If the human mind were made up of particles, these characters would be Laplace's Demons, being able to track and predict each and every last particle.

Johan Liebert - As a child, he predicted his sister's adult psyche years in advance in accordance with how the trauma of shooting him would affect her. Perfectly impersonated his sister. The Another Novel states that if the mind were a set of axes with a center point, Johan is able to infiltrate and remove that center point with whatever he pleases. As a child, Johan could identify, communicate with, and toy with serial killers with expressions and simple messages alone.

Jigsaw - Jigsaw is able to predict the actions of people he's never met with near impossible accuracy, at times down to the second. This is even the case when they're under the effects of never-before-seen poisons, which would inherently make them more illogical and thus harder to read. Most blatantly, he'd predicted the dying words of an FBI agent he'd never met and had minimal information on in the climax of a given plan he knew Hoffman would craft, knew those same words mailed to him in advance would trick Hoffman into setting that plan in motion, and knew that those words actually spoken by that agent would fully conceal the deception in Hoffman's mind.
