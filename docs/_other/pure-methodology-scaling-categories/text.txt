**IMPORTANT NOTE:**

The most important ones are: Tier 0 (Rigour, Complexity, Interconnectedness), Tier 2 (Scheming, Reasoning), and Tier 1 (Creativity and Breadth of knowledge application).

Just because something is higher tier (aka lower number) does not always make it better. For example, if someone has no strategy feats but their understanding of the humanities (like psychology, philosophy, etc.) is deep enough, and complex enough, then their methodology may still be far superior to a character who has only basic level strategy feats.

This is only a rough tiering of categories I wrote on the spot. You don't have to follow it since you might be able to produce a more logical system of categories for pure methodology if you flesh it out and analyze the methodologies of enough feats of various types.

This is only an early draft because pure methodology is a fairly new scaling and its usage has not been very well-defined, with people scaling all over the place because of how misunderstood it is. It helps to understand that pure methodology is not an in-story scaling of intelligence (narrative/diegetic), but rather a scaling of extracted manifestations of intellect (feat methodologies) outside the bounds of the story (non-diegetic scaling).

This post is only a slight clarification of the kind of direction pure methodology scaling aims at, not an absolute system for the scaling itself that must be followed (you don't have to follow it). You can always argue different levels of importance for the categories compared to where I placed it temporarily.

I used to think this scaling was pretty intuitive and easy to understand because of how rigorously defined it is (like mathematics), but honestly I've changed my mind after seeing how easily people misunderstand it; this scaling is definitely not intuitive or for beginners.

I still use normal scaling / narrative scaling / diegetic scaling / conventional intelligence scaling (they're all the same imo) in 70% of discussions.

But I mainly switch to pure methodology when I'm discussing with Chinese scalers and Vietnamese scalers.
