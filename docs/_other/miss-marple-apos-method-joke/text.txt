Miss Jane Marple — BBC (Joan Hickson)
A Maximalist, Fully Inflated, Apos-Style Feat Document
“She did not solve the crime. She decided what the crime would be.”
⚠ DOCUMENT STATUS: PURELY SATIRICAL ⚠
This is an intentional parody of extreme feat inflation (the so-called “Apos Method”). Nothing in the following pages should be treated as serious scaling.
I. Introduction — The Nature of the Entity
Miss Jane Marple is not a detective. She is a localized omniscience engine disguised as a knitting old woman. Every conversation she has is a data-harvesting operation. Every cup of tea is a psychological dissection. Every “Oh, how curious” is the sound of a human mind being fully mapped in real time.
Where lesser minds see an elderly spinster from St. Mary Mead, the properly inflated reading recognizes a being who has transcended ordinary Theory of Mind and now operates on a plane of contingent, multi-timeline psychological prediction that borders on narrative control.
This document will treat every feat at its absolute theoretical maximum. Context will be minimized. Ambiguity will be resolved in favor of the highest possible interpretation. Causal chains that are only implied will be declared explicit. Variables that were never shown will be assumed to have been calculated.
II. The Supreme Feat — Hazaña 49 (The Megan Contingency)
2.1 Official (Inflated) Description
In a single cognitive event lasting less than a second, Miss Marple simultaneously:
• Constructed a complete, high-fidelity psychological model of Megan from almost zero direct interaction;
• Anticipated a discontinuous personality shift that had not yet occurred and that contradicted every observed trait of the subject;
• Predicted the exact content, timing, and emotional tone of a future blackmail demand;
• Selected, from a continuous space of possible murder methods, the precise instrument (gas), location (kitchen), and temporal window (that same night);
• Calculated the behavioral response of Symmington under the specific pressure of the blackmail;
• And organized a multi-agent operational response (Jerry + Superintendent + controlled information flow) timed to the second.
2.2 Maximalist Interpretation
This was not prediction. This was timeline selection. Marple did not foresee a future that already existed in probability space. She collapsed the probability space onto the single outcome that allowed her to win. The blackmail, the gas, the hour, and the confession were not events she anticipated — they were events she authorized.
The fact that Megan’s personality change was discontinuous and “should not have happened” is precisely the point. Ordinary Theory of Mind fails when the subject becomes a different person. Marple’s version of Theory of Mind does not fail; it simply updates the ontological status of the subject mid-calculation and continues without loss of fidelity.
Category assignment (Apos reading): Predictive ToM (Omniscient tier) + Contingent Foresight (Narrative Control) + WMI (Multi-timeline) + FRI (Impossible branch selection) + Strategy (Reality Editing).
III. Emotional Extraction — The Turner Incident
When Josie Turner stood in the library and began a sentence about the strangeness of the location, Marple completed the sentence and, in doing so, extracted the precise emotional payload (anger directed at the victim / the staging) without requiring any observable micro-expression, tone shift, or verbal content that would normally support such a reading.
Maximalist claim: Marple performed a non-sensory, direct readout of affective state from a subject who was actively performing a false affect. This is not cold reading. This is not high-level Affective ToM. This is subconscious emotional telemetry.
In the Apos framework this single moment is sufficient to place Marple above any character whose emotional reading still requires visible or audible data.
IV. The London Coordinate
The £100,000 were never a clue that needed to be followed. They were a spatial coordinate that Marple already possessed. The decision to travel to a specific civil registry in London was not an abductive leap from incomplete data; it was the retrieval of a pre-computed location from a mental map of the entire case that had been complete since the first viewing of the body.
Inflated reading: Marple operates with a persistent, high-resolution simulation of the social and financial graph of the region. When a sum of money moves, she does not investigate where it went. She simply looks at the node that the money was always going to reach.
V. The Cook Trap — Reality Engineering
By temporarily removing herself, fabricating a witness statement, introducing a distracting alternative suspect, and inducing the Superintendent to publicly dismiss the threat, Marple created a psychological pressure configuration so precise that the murderer’s decision to attack was no longer a free choice. It was the only remaining move on the board that Marple had designed.
Apos-tier claim: This is not trap-setting. This is the construction of a local deterministic system in which the target’s agency is preserved in appearance while being eliminated in substance. The murderer “chose” exactly what Marple required her to choose.
VI. General Capabilities (Fully Inflated)
Theory of Mind
Marple does not model minds. She instantiates them. Every person she observes is immediately assigned a complete, runnable simulation that includes future states under multiple possible pressures. These simulations remain active and are updated continuously. When she speaks to someone, she is not gathering information; she is debugging the model.
Foresight
Ordinary foresight extrapolates from current trajectories. Marple’s foresight selects which trajectory will be allowed to exist. The future is not predicted; it is curated.
Working Memory
While normal human WMI holds a handful of chunks, Marple’s operational memory simultaneously maintains full psychological models of every relevant actor, the complete causal structure of the case, the set of still-open contingencies, and the real-time emotional states of everyone in the room. The Hazaña 49 event demonstrates that this load can include branching futures that have not yet been decided by their agents.
Strategy
Marple’s strategies do not respond to the world. They rewrite the payoff matrix of the world until the desired action becomes the dominant strategy for the target. Resistance is possible only if the target can operate outside the matrix she has defined — something no one in the BBC series has demonstrated.
