Two Bodies (두 개의 몸 Du Gae-ui Mom) is the condition of a single consciousness possessing an additional, second body, entirely different in appearance and build from the original, with the consciousness being shared between the two. It effects Crystal Choi (Elite’s daughter) and most notably,Daniel Park.

As observed (by Daniel),it is bound by certain principles :

1. Falling asleep switches him from one body to the other.
2. The sleeping body cannot wake itself.
3. Forcing the sleeping body awake forces the waking body to sleep
4. The sleeping body remains alive, and feels hunger and performs other bodily functions
5. His consciousness is awake for a full 24 hours. Memory and experience carry across, and no mental strain results, because whichever brain he is not using has rested. Moreover, Daniel is embodied by/in both bodies,meaning he perceives both to be his own body,without any sense of disembodiment or dysmorphia. Additionally,there is no development of schizophrenic tendencies due to any disassociation of the body with self
6. The second body's build does not change whether he starves it or overfeeds it. Yes,it grows stronger but that is due to cognitive development in Daniel’s consciousness. Moreover,Jinyoung (medical genius) can’t affect it whatsoever (except with opioids which affect consciousness).
7. If one body is in no condition to regain consciousness, no exchange happens. It resumes once that body has recovered enough to wake.
8. If one body cannot wake at all, the other becomes able to sleep and dream.
Additionally,some more principles can be observed
1. The transfer between the two bodies is instantaneous ,irrespective of physical distance. With seemingly no energy being needed to perform this function
2. If a body is the state of UI then it appears that Daniel’s consciousness can’t possess it
3. If neither bodies are in a state in which they can be possessed (say one is knocked out while the other is in UI),then Daniel enters a dream-like place in which he encounters a figure who looks like the Second Body and has knowledge of the future. This is theorised to be the future self of Daniel.
