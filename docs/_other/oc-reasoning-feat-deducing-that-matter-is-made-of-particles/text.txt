P98: Every prisoner guesses the hat color corresponding to their position in that pre-chosen representative sequence.

P99: Because the real sequence and the representative sequence differ in only a finite number of hats, all but a finite number of prisoners will guess correctly.

C46: Choice allows an infinite group of isolated observers to predict unknown, non-local information with near 100% accuracy without communication. Because physical universe causality forbids non-local prediction engines, reality cannot consist of continuous infinite choice structures.

P100: If physical matter or space were continuous, even a single cubic centimeter of space would contain an uncountably infinite number of continuous points, and thus an infinite capacity for information. This would mean that it would collapse into a black hole.

C47: Evidentally, this is isn't the case, which is further proof of C43.
