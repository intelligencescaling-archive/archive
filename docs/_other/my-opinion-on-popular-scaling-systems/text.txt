My Opinion On Popular Scaling Systems

This is gonna be my opinion on the most popular scaling systems (SS). Let's go.

Narrative SS
SS with pure narrative POV regardless of anything else (how absurd something would be etc.). Even takes into account implied meanings.

My opinion:
logical coherencies within its principle 10/10 in theory, -10/10 in practise (due to usual narrative POV problems)
fun scaling with this 6.5/10. Too much aesthetic involved. Really good for glazing purposes though (can scale Johan and PJ high so yuppy). Miss scaling FSIQ….
structural scaling creativity 0/10. No nuance at all
structural scaling complexity 0/10. Bruh

Overall: 0.25/10 I guess. At least it's coherent in theory.

Proven Ability

Says “When determining the winner in an outsmarting scenario, PA scaling only measures the outcome for any scenario given by the user, it’s not an end-all be-all type of scaling where there’s one type of scenario everyone must stick with, scaling NFS and FS and RLS and MB in this scaling are all valid” and then procedes to say (the very next line) “General Outsmarting in this system is scaled not by a scenario, but by comparing the characters' ability directly and how they would fare against each other”.
Which at worst it's a major contradiction, and at best it was meant as in “we ALSO take into account more stuff other than scenarios”. But whatever no big deal
“So as explained, PA scaling doesn’t concern feat-to-feat comparison, but rather comparing the thought process and outsmarting style of characters”. Who knows what that even means. This is basically saying “we don't compare feats, we instead compare feats”. No feat definition was given btw, so it's not even that I'm cherry-picking a bad out of context line.
For the rest of it, it's basically a whole bunch of “You can't just use statements and implied meanings as feats, you at least gotta have some evidence to back that up”.
“When it comes to Hax and Gadgets, the default is to not include them when comparing the characters in outsmarting, however, when it comes to Hax, as in some cases they’re considered a part of the character themselves, they can be used when distinguished from the character’s base form”. Now we use Haxes in smart-characters-debate. Bruh.

My opinion:
logical coherencies within its principle -10/10 in theory, -10/10 in practise (due to usual narrative POV problems)
fun purposes with this 8.5/10. Same as with Narrative SS, but I no longer can glaze Johan as much, but PJ and others get pumped. Miss scaling FSIQ….
structural scaling creativity 0/10. No nuance at all
structural scaling complexity 0/10. Bruh

Overall: -1/10. Worse than Narrative SS.

STOP SCALING
Version: “Only explained feats by the characters”.
Meaning that statements are irrelevant.
Meaning that off-screen feats are also irrelevant.
Meaning also indirectly that computational skills (like processing speed) and much more, would not be counted as intelligence, since they have nothing to do with being "explained".
In short, it's merely about scaling the thought process of the characters.

My opinion: it's basically a user-friendly solution if you want to get rid of lots of bullshit. But it's still obviously wrong because it still does take into account the narrative POV (and, additionally, because it denies some offscreen feats). So it's still highly incoherent philosophically, but methodologically it's much better than the others.

logical coherencies within its principle -10/10 in theory, 4/10 in practise (due to usual narrative POV problems, but being heavily contained)
fun scaling with this 3/10. Thought process isn't fun at all to scale
structural scaling creativity 1.5/10. I guess
structural scaling complexity 0/10. Bruh

My score: 3/10

METHODOLOGY SCALING
Basically STOP Scaling but trying to appeal to more people by bringing in much more narrative BS, and so introducing new contradictions.
It's definitely not as bad as PA, but still.
My score: 1.5/10
