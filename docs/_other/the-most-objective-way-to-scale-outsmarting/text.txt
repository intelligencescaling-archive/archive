The Most Objective way to Scale Outsmarting.

It's not Methodology it's not proven ability and it's definitely not normal/Narrative Scaling.

In fact I wouldn't even Call this A Scaling system, it's just a Filter that you can apply in your own Scaling system to Stay Close to a characters Objective Scaling.

And how do we do that?...  Complete Evidences.

And Anything can be used as a Complete Evidence Explanation, a Statement, an Expression, a Comment Anything. If it Undeniably Proves A feat or just an Aspect of the feat you are trying to prove, which itself can undeniably prove the entire feat it's a Complete Evidence.

And the word "Undeniably" Means something entirely different than what you may think.

"L Stopped World War 3 at age 8" Is an Undeniable Evidence, But can You Scale it While Sticking Close to L's Objective Scaling? You can't.

Why? Because that Sentence in of itself doesn't give you anything to work with. 10 Different people can look at this same Sentence with 10 different perspectives To Conclude 10 Different things entirely All While Staying under the Contextual Framework Set by the Author.

How he stopped it? Diplomacy? Hacking? Blackmail? Luck?

Was he alone? Did adults do the work and he just pointed?

What does "stopped" mean? Prevented a launch? Talked down one general?

What scale of intelligence / influence does that require?

10 people can take the same sentence and get 10 different L's, all still inside what the author wrote. That's not Complete Evidence, That's an "open" statement.

A Complete Evidence would be something like: "L, alone, at age 8, with no outside help, intercepted and decoded X in Y minutes, and that action alone prevented Z." Now you have mechanism + isolation + time + causal link. You can't get 10 L's out of that anymore.

If You Wish to Prove/DisProve Something Just Ask Yourself 2 thing's and You'll know the Answer before you engage.

1. If you are using just 1 Evidence is that Evidence Complete Enough to prove the exact interpretation you are going With even if it wasn't Fully Explained?

2. If you are Using Multiple Evidences Which Don't prove Anything on their own, but when you combine everything together and try to come up with 10 Contradictory interpretations to your one, Can they be Disproven?

In Evidence Hierarchy the first type of Evidence is the best to prove a Feat because Trying to Disprove it would be like trying to disprove 2+2=4.

The Second type of Evidence is Below the first type but is Still the most Logical method to prove Disprove Something when you are Dealing with Ambiguous data.

That's Popper + legal cross-examination, It's good because it kills the most common SCD fallacy:

Stacking 5 vague statements and pretending 5 x vague = 1 solid.

It doesn't, 5 vague statements that all point the same way are still abductive. They make your interpretation More Likely Not Proven.

And this is why you Shouldn't try to prove a Feat or your scaling for a character Like this:

"L Stopped World War 3 at age 8 his Author obviously Made this Statement to Make L look Smart to the Readers, So Going by Scale L with just this one Statement Gets Close to Top Tier.... Wdym I can't prove that Who needs to prove Interpretations in SCD Anyways Are you Dumb?"

In Short: Use Deductive And Inductive Reasoning to Prove Why you Scale a character Where you Scale them or/And Why you believe in a feat, not Abduction because in Abductive Reasoning Multiple Answers Can Exist in a Super Position, and That's not Objectivity.
