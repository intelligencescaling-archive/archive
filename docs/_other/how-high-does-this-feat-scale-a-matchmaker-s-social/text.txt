I'm writing an intelligent character fanfic based on an Ultimate Matchmaker—Since nobody has really written an SCD-like/adjacent story like that

The Problem

Context: High school, 11th Grade

The POV character (lets call them MC) has consistently observed that his friend Ren likely likes their classmate Aoi. As a good friend, MC decides to try to kickstart a relationship between Ren and Aoi.

Aims:

Get Ren and Aoi to exchange contact information (preferably phone numbers) and work closely together.

Ren is one of those edgy wannabe 48 Laws of Power-glazers and such so MC has some faith that Ren could be able to charm Aoi if he is in a close enough situation.

Thinking & Reasoning:

1. Ren is shy and indirect concerning his crush.

2. Aoi doesn't appear to be interested in Ren.

3. Aoi and Ren are not related or connected in any way at all.

4. Therefore, Ren asking for Aoi's contact information will likely be rejected.

5. Therefore, Ren and Aoi are not going to exchange contact information at all in the foreseeable future.

6. Therefore, they need to be put into a situation where exchanging contact information is mutually beneficial.

7. Therefore, they need to be put into a situation where they need to communicate about something that affects both of them.

8. The best situation would be something at school.

9. Therefore, MC needs to manipulate a teacher into creating a situation where they are in a 'group.' Preferably of 2 by themselves, so Aoi is more dependent on Ren.

10. One of MC's classes, Philosophy class, is viewed as boring by the majority of the students. The teacher knows this.

11. The teacher is also quite neurotic and anxious, so he likely feels bad that his class is unenjoyable.

12. Therefore, the best path is for MC to persuade the Philosophy teacher to hold something with groups that will be seen as enjoyable.

13. The best proposal that is seen as fun and enjoyable would be a game.

14. MC can connect the game to Philosophy by making it a social deduction game and propose that those two are the 'Imposter' role.

15. MC can make the game last several days, so that they are forced to exchange numbers.

16. MC can make this a reality by directly manipulating the Philosophy teacher via a calculated conversation.

17. MC can connect the ideas of epistemology with the idea of the social deduction game and also play the emotions of the teacher under the guise of a concerned and caring student.

18. MC can suggest that this game lasts several days and happens in the background of the class to further incentivize the teacher and also secretly prolong the game so Ren and Aoi spend more time alone with this secret and colluding.

18. Once the teacher agrees or is interested, MC can suggest that Ren and Aoi are the chosen imposters.

Outcomes:

MC is able to talk to the teacher one day after classes and is able to successfully persuade them into running this game and choose Ren and Aoi. Furthermore, MC is able to get the teacher to take all the credit, leaving him out of it completely. Nobody except the teacher knows that MC is the one behind the Game.

The Game goes ahead for a school week. Ren and Aoi officially exchange contact information and text each other for a couple hours after classes everyday to plan what to do next for the IRL Social Deduction Game.
