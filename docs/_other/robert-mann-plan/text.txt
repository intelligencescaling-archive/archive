STEP 1: SCOTLAND AND THE GENTLEMEN
In 1933, Robert Mann created the Gentleman Movement, one about conserving classic culture and families and chivalrous practices, and spread it around Wessex and Mercia using his resources and his charm. He needed the support, he needed the fame, because he was about to unite the peoples.
Soon enough, most of Wessex was supportive of the Movement. Eventually, Mercia was also on board. Robert started talks with the kings of Wessex and Mercia, slowly getting closer to the king of Scotland.
Once Robert got close to the king, he went and talked him into absorbing Wessex-Mercia, for which he even managed to convince the king of Wessex using the Gentleman Movement as a motivator, their people being more united than ever.

Thus, in 1934, Wessex-Mercia officially became part of Scotland, having united the British Isles for once, just one year after Robert started the Gentleman Movement in Wessex.

STEP 2: EXTREMISTS AND WORLDWIDE INFLUENCE
Robert couldn’t just be a local British celebrity, no. He needed to look further beyond, beyond the Isles, he needed to get Europe. But first, he needed to radicalize his people, but how? He needed something strong, something powerful. Then, he got a call. Hans Schraiber. He had the same idea. So, Robert uses his resources to fund Hans’ research on a drug to allow for this to happen. Once Hans’ drug was done testing on soldiers, he’d use it.

When the drug proved effective, Robert received the samples and started creating extremists of the Gentleman Movement. At first, it was slow, but then he realized he needed to become known in Europe quickly, so he taught the extremists how to use the drug and the method of “radicalization” to make it snowball. After that, Robert took a trip to Europe and started spreading his influence, making businesses and connections, making him even richer and more influential. In a matter of only a few months, he had become one of the most famous men in Europe.

Oh, but something back home was brewing, and he wasn’t expecting it.

STEP 3: MANN INDUSTRIES AND THE COUP
Seems like the extremists back in Scotland have grown too large, whatever shall Mann do? Use them, of course. But he can’t be publicly associated with them, so he leaves his assets in Scotland and moves to the perfect place to escape to: Ethiopia. A particular man lived there, Hargos Hadgoop Mebret, known as “The Ultimate Diplomat”. He arguably had an even bigger charisma than Robert, but he was also quite intelligent (which ended up being an issue in the long run) and had tons of connections. He was also, at the time, the husband of both the king and queen of Kenya. However, what got Robert invested the most in the end was none of those things… it was his immortality and strength. This man seemed unbeatable and ageless, he should be in his 80s at this point! So Robert started looking into him…

Meanwhile, Robert’s assets were being handled as he ordered, creating a weapons manufacturing company called Mann Industries, to sell weapons to the extremists and delve into the business of other nations secretly. When Mann Industries looked into Norway, they found something interesting: a pattern of prison guards talking about a coup, and many mentioning Hargos… Robert got told this immediately and he started thinking. A coup in Norway, using Hargos… but how…?
They could be talking about kidnapping Hargos and then blaming the Russians for it, that would make everyone mad… and then, they would fight… Russia would be gone if they were facing so many nations… and they’d be weaker… even the Dutch.
…less competition, less resistance. And the way to Norway would go through the Isles. If he got kidnapped with Hargos, and they dropped him off in the Isles, nobody would know! He wouldn’t be associated with the extremists and he’d keep his public image! Wait, but how would he handle Hargos returning but him being nowhere in sight? Hm… maybe he could fake his death, yeah. If he’s dropped off in the Isles and fakes his death, he could become a martyr, and he’d be able to research what makes Hargos immortal and control the extremists by himself.
…excellent plan, let’s execute it.

And thus, Mann Industries contacts the Norwegian insurrectionists and offers to fund their operation, IF they do what they tell them. Since the Norwegian men don’t know who’s behind this, they just think it’s a random warlord, which is honestly possible in their eyes.
The operation starts.
In January 1957, a ship arrives and forcibly takes Hargos and Robert to the Mediterranean Sea, not without a fight. The king of Ethiopia obviously tells the other nations to look for the ship, and they actually do find it, they just don’t manage to intercept it, but at least they know where it’s going. However, the last point they’re seen close to is in Paris. A little bit later, Robert is dropped off in Scotland, and Hargos is taken to Oslo. Thanks to Ethiopian spies, the news spread out that Hargos had been kidnapped by the Russians, and that Robert Mann died in Finland. Shortly after, the jailbreak happens, where the guards rebel and allow Hargos to be set free and return to Ethiopia safe and sound, although the nations are now furious with Russia and commence the war Robert had predicted. Nations are sometimes too quick to rage.

STEP 4: NEW KING AND THE REVELATION
The Russian War breaks out. Practically all of Europe gangs up on the Russians, and Norway is now independent and under Scottish influence. Robert will make sure to use that for later, Norway has some resources. As Robert expected, all the nations that participated became slightly weaker, and no one’s looking at Africa. But that’s for later.

Robert goes back to spreading the extremism in Scotland, at this point 95% of the population has become extremist, and Mann Industries has made sure to equip these maniacs well. Robert then goes to the king of Scotland and kidnaps him, his guards were extremist anyway, they won’t disobey. The king stays in a dark room with bright lights, a strange feeling of compliance, and a charming voice guiding his confused mind. It helps deal with everything, at least he can be sure the throne will be safe with him. He stays in that room for almost 5 years, and comes out essentially brainwashed, following Robert’s orders at every point. Now that Robert has guaranteed power with an influenced king, he can start looking into Hargos’ immortality uninterrupted.

A lot of things don’t add up for Robert. No one had previously noticed Hargos’ immortality, there’s no noticeable source for it, but Hargos does speak of a certain “Goop” as if he was the Lord… this could be the key to the power, Hargos had contacted “Goop” and he gave him immortality! He needs to contact Goop.
In the middle of the night, on the rooftop of a house, Robert screams for the name of Goop, and suddenly… a figure appears. He can’t understand it, it’s completely abstract, but it claims not to be Goop, however it was surprised that Robert knew of him. It calls itself “the Watcher”, and tells Robert that he’s the source for Hargos’ immortality. Of course, Robert asks why and how it works. After a long explanation, Robert wants to be immortal as well, and the Watcher agrees suspiciously quickly. Then, a massive storm covers all of Europe, but Robert doesn’t care.
He begins experimenting. Is he immortal, or is he just ageless? He falls off increasing heights without so much as a scratch, and he even blows himself up inside a house, although that one did make the news even if no one knew he was there. He replaced all his limbs with mechanical ones intended for the Assaultrons, and after he finished all of this, he got confirmation of a few things: the entire population was now his, and the Venetian ships had arrived. Time for the next plan then.

STEP 5: THE WAR
Now that the nations are weak and tensions are still high between the Genoa Pact and the Pact of Madrid thanks to Venice and the Papal States, it’s time to take advantage. Africa is weak, and Scotland has a small colony in there that nobody’s been paying attention to. Robert decides to invest in it, sends tons of resources and people to it, he needs it stocked up like hell. No one suspects a thing if it’s over several decades. After all, he has all the time in the world now, he can afford it.

Robert waits, the world keeps going on, he keeps sending resources, and he rehearses the plan for the year 2000.
First, the Venetian ships, with extremists from Venice, will go to Sicily and attack it, then the Papal States will blame Venice and soon enough, the two sides will be put against each other. Robert knows the leader of the Papal States is unstable, he’ll be the first one to bite. Venice will follow. Then the others. As soon as that happens, contact the colony, make sure Portugal isn’t looking, and start expanding. The goal? West Africa. But that won’t be the only front. Scotland has Vietnam and some of Australia. The Dutch are very observant of the East Indies, so Australia will be the last. Scotland will start with West Africa and the Philippines. When everything’s done, the Netherlands should be very weakened after facing France and Austria, and Scotland wouldn’t even get involved in the war thanks to the Dutch being too busy to attack. Everyone will be weaker and Scotland will be better than ever to face the 21st century. King Robert the Great will become a legend.

In the end, it all worked out. Scotland shall become the next world superpower.
