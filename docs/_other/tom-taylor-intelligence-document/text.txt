Introduction
This is going to cover some details that are needed to understand the full picture of what is going on. The Unwritten follows the character known as Tom Taylor(Otherwise Thomas or Tommy). He is a celebrity, because his father Wilson Taylor, wrote novels surrounding a fictional persona of Tom, namely Tommy, with the novels being a harry potter style series. The Cabal is a group that rewrites history using the power of narratives, which Wilson was a part of before ultimately cutting ties with them. Wilson decided to make a weapon to fight the Cabal, namely his own son, Tom(my) Taylor. Magic and power exists only through a system of belief from the Leviathan. The Leviathan is a figure that encompasses the collective unconsciousness of all authors and people that are tied to stories. We will next cover the cosmology of The Unwritten to make it more clear.
