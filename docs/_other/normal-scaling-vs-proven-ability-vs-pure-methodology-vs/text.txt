Normal Scaling vs Proven Ability vs Pure Methodology vs General Methodology vs Powerscaling vs Writing Scaling vs Morality Scaling

NS (Normal/Narrative Scaling) vs PM (Pure Methodology) vs PA (Proven Ability) vs GMS (General Methodology Scaling) vs Powerscaling (PS) vs Writing Scaling (WS) vs Morality Scaling (MS)

The first 4 scalings are intelligence-focused scalings (or scaling "systems"), but the 5th is a broader power-focused scaling and the 6th and 7th is entirely unrelated but useful for understanding how these rankings work.

Scope of evaluation: WS > PS >>> NS > PA >> GMS >> PM >> MS

Number of categories: PS ≈ WS >>> NS >= PA > GMS >> PM ≈ MS

Perspective/Measurement: PM evaluates the method of the character's feats extradiegetically/non-diegetically (extracted outside the bounds of the story), PA evaluates the proven intelligence of the character diegetically (within the story). NS evaluates the intelligence of the character diegetically without requiring the level of proof that PA requires. GMS semi-diegetically scales the character by the process of their feats if there is sufficient explanation behind the process. PS evaluates the power of the character diegetically. WS evaluates the well-writtenness of the element of the story extradiegetically/non-diegetically (the element evaluated is not within the bounds of the story itself.) MS evaluates the virtue/evil of the character semi-diegetically (the framework of evaluation is non-diegetic but the character is analyzed diegetically.

Precision/Unambiguity/Objectivity: PM >> GMS >=< PS > NS >>> PA >>> WS >>>>> MS

System design: PM >> GMS >> NS > PS > WS > PA >>> MS

Personal preference (isolation): PM > GMS > NS > WS >>> PS >> PA >>> MS

Personal preference (current usage): PM > NS > GMS > WS >>>>> PA >>> PS >>>>>> MS

MS = Morality Scaling

WS = Writing Scaling

PS = Powerscaling (encompasses intelligence)

GMS = General Methodology Scaling (intelligence scaling subset)

PA = Proven Ability (intelligence scaling subset)

PM = Pure Methodology Scaling (intelligence scaling subset)

NS = Normal/Narrative Intelligence Scaling (powerscaling subset)
