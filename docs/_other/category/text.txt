FSIQ Categories:
VCI (Verbal Comprehension Index) - The ability to communicate through words and utilize language effectively. (Yes, it can be different languages)
VSI (Visual Spatial Index) - The ability to evaluate concepts, comprehend, and manipulate visual/spatial relations/shapes.
PSI (Processing Speed Index) - The ability to accurately and quickly identify visual information/objects, and the speed of mental processing.
WMI (Working Memory Index) - The ability to consume, process, and manipulate visual information, typically to solve problems.
PRI (Perceptual Reasoning Index) - The ability to consume visual information, organize it, and interpret it to solve problems at the task.
CPI (Cognitive Proficiency Index) - The combination of various cognitive functions, including WMI, PSI, and PRI. The ability to be proficient at the Index’s mentioned.
FRI (Fluid Reasoning Index) - The ability to detect underlying conceptual relationships visually, think abstractly, and use reasoning to solve novel problems.
RCI (Reading Comprehension Index) - The ability to comprehend and interpret words after reading and answer questions.
TRI (Total Reading Index) - The ability to comprehend and identify written or visual information effectively.
NVI (Non-Verbal Index) - The ability to reason non-verbally, comprehend spatial information, and mental processing.
QRI (Quantitative Reasoning Index) - The ability to solve problems involving numerical reasoning and quantitative reasoning skills.
GAI (General Ability Index) - The combination of various cognitive abilities, excluding working memory and processing speed.
AWMI (Arithmetic Working Memory Index) - The ability to calculate and perform mental arithmetic tasks while retaining information stored within memory.

EQU/EU (Emotional Understanding) - The ability to comprehend complex emotions beyond the facial surface and body language of an individual, typically used to predict an individual or group's actions, possibly by creating a profile.
EQP/EP (Emotional Perception) - The ability to perceive emotions in others through facial expression, body language, voices, pictures, and reasoning.
EQF/EF (Emotional Facilitation) - The ability to detect and utilize the emotional impact one possesses on others, and to manage their emotional state accordingly.
EQR/ER (Emotional Realization) - The ability to detect, recognize, and comprehend the emotional and cognitive setbacks in one’s self and others.
EQA/EA (Emotional Awareness) - The ability to recognize, comprehend, and be aware of the current emotions of others or one’s self.

Strategy Categories:
Strategy - Can include multiple tactics that can be combined into one strategy and/or steps taken to achieve a specific goal but requires active critical thinking and decision making.
Strategy Fortitude - Strategy Fortitude is measured by the ability to succeed, it also focuses on the tactics the character implements into the strategy and to maximize its resilience and shielding. To comprehend this more accurately ask yourself this one question. Is the character’s strategy LIKELY to succeed?
Strategy Building - Strategy Building measures the ability to construct and create a coherent scheme while navigating limitations and setbacks they encounter.
Strategy Logistics - Strategy Logistics measures the ability to ensure everything revolving around execution, alignment and active/efficient resource management. It also measures how well the characters manipulate and orchestrate the field around them by bringing their strategy to life.
Strategy Orchestration - Strategy Orchestration measures the ability to manipulate the field in which the strategy is applicable to control all of the elements within it.
Short Term Strategy - A Strategy that lasts a relatively short period of time, could last potentially a few days or weeks.
Long Term Strategy - A Strategy that lasts an extensive period of time, could potentially be a year, or a few months.
Strategy Complexity - Strategy Complexity measures the quality incorporation of additional layers, and overlaps of tactics within a strategy to increase the difficulty to decipher and counter it. Or in simple terms, the amount of layers within a strategy for it to be increasingly more tough to counter.
Strategy Adaptability - Strategy Adaptability measures the characters ability to leave remaining space within a strategy to adjust to unforeseen and unfavorable situations.
Strategy Vision - The aim and long-term objectives of a strategy.
Strategy Efficiency - Strategy Efficiency measures the ability to execute the strategy and achieve the characters goals with the minimum amount of resources and time sacrificed when the situation requires it.
Strategy Effectiveness - Strategy Effectiveness focuses on the end result of the strategy and considers how accurate the strategy was in achieving the desired outcome.
Don’t confuse Strategy Effectiveness and Efficiency: ) Effectiveness: Executing and covering the RIGHT things. And also looks at the RESULT. ) Efficiency: Executing and managing THINGS right. And also looks at the PROCESS.
Strategy Execution - Strategy Execution focuses purely on tactical implementation and how well the characters thoughts translate into actions, and the deployment of the strategy and the means and process of achieving their goal.
Strategy Resilience - Strategy Resilience is measured by how well the character's strategy can withstand resistances, regardless of attempted action.
Strategy Control - Strategy Control is measured by the characters ability to remain in control regardless of setbacks and to adapt to novel situations.
Strategy Length - Strategy Length measures the complete length of the character’s strategy once executed.
Strategy Shielding - Strategy Shielding is measured by how well the character can handle and cover for any flaws or discrepancies in their strategy that could potentially cause it to fail. It also involves countering any action taken by the opposition instead of ignoring it.
Don’t confuse Strategy Shielding and Resilience: ) Resilience: Focuses on WITHSTANDING resistances and IGNORES attempted action. ) Shielding: Focuses on COVERING resistances and focusing on COUNTERING attempted action.
Strategy Foresight - Strategy Foresight is measured by how far ahead the character has planned out their strategy and foreseeing possibilities and actions the opposition could potentially do.
Strategy Alignment - Strategy Alignment is measured by how well the character can make elements and components of the strategy align with each other to create a Linear Strategy.
Tactical Complexity - Tactical Complexity is measured by the number of layers and tactical overlap within the character’s strategy, it can make their strategy increasingly more difficult and complex to see through and comprehend.
If you require a more in-depth analysis on strategy then check out Ace’s New Strategy Doc and Loy’s Guide To Strategy Doc.
Planning Categories:
Planning - Can include multiple strategies or tactics created at different period of time compiled into one coherent plan and/or fully planned and mapped out beforehand and does not require active critical thinking and decision making.
Short Term Planning - A plan that lasts a relatively short period of time, typically a few weeks or days.
Long Term Planning - A plan that lasts an extensive period of time, typically a few months or years.
Planning Intricacies - Planning Intricacies is measured by how well the character can create a complex plan and how many layers it includes.
Planning Aim - Planning Aim is measured by how accurately the character can achieve the goal of their plan and solve problems that contribute to their goal.
Planning Process - Planning Process is measured by how well the character can solve problems they encounter within a plan and to organize the plan accordingly.
Planning Preparation Process - Planning Preparation Process is measured by how well the character can logically align different tactics over the course of the plan’s lifespan.
Planning Formulation - Planning Formulation is the ability to create a coherent and comprehensive plan within a relatively short period of time.
Planning Coverage - Planning Coverage is measured by how well a character can take into consideration various aspects when developing a plan, this can include things like potential hazards, variables and outcomes. As well as ensuring maximizing succession of the plan.
Planning Versatility - Planning Versatility is measured by being versatile with objects, information, resources, or the character throughout the course of the plan.
Planning Risk Calculation - Planning Risk Calculation is measured by the character’s ability to evaluate and organize different or unique choices when presenting with a more challenging situation.
Planning Contingency - Planning Contingency is measured by the character’s ability to create other plans or backup plans for future events that could occur for a more predictable outcome and/or to gain security or insight just incase of a discrepancy.
Tactics (Can be included with planning sometimes so I’ll include it here) - The ability to create short term objectives and are usually straightforward, typically located within a plan.
Planning Invincibility - Planning Invincibility is the ability to construct a plan with as few loopholes as possible and being able to consider unexpected possibilities.
Planning Accuracy - Planning Accuracy is the ability to determine if all components of the plan connect correctly and the details being considered.
