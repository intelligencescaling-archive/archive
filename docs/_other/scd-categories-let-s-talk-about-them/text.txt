SCD categories… let’s talk about them.

So…. I don’t know how to start this, but let me just say : the current community’s frameworks are flawed, and we are being separated.

You will often see people using a framework like this :

“FSIQ - PSI, WMI, VCI, VSI, FRI

FSEI - EP, EE, EU, EF, ER, EM (EP later overlaps with sensory or observation categories, EE shouldn’t even exist as a category, EM is part of ER)

FSSI - social cognition, social awareness, social skills, charm, charisma, influence, relationship management, etc (social cognition and social awareness overlap, charisma and influence overlap, relationship management is too specific to be a category)

FSAC - cognitive discipline, impulse control, stress tolerance, mental fortitude, composure (cognitive discipline and impulse control are two narrow concepts that are part of a much broader concept, which is discipline. Stress tolerance, depending on how you interpret it, overlaps with mental fortitude, and is also a narrower concept of a much broader “physiological arousal”. As for “composure”, it overlaps an insane amount with later categories like concealment (basically meaning the same thing)

Thinking - (way too many categories to go over, but the overlap is insane). I’ll instead go over the definition itself. Thinking is the process of using the brain to form ideas, reasoning, problem solutions, judgments, or reflect on information. As you can most likely understand by now, this is deeply flawed, as almost all SCD categories are “thinking”. It also doesn’t help to change this with “cognition”, as that’s an even broader concept, which many of the performative scalers like to use now to be all different from the rest (going back to one of my earlier points about separation in the community).

Reasoning - deductive, inductive, abductive, and sometimes more sub-categories. (No explanation needed for why this is bad, but I’ll still give it. It’s a narrower concept of thinking. It’s either keep thinking and remove reasoning or remove thinking, keep reasoning, and add other forms of thinking to make sure everything’s covered (though this would be efficient and a waste of time…. And sanity)

Strategy - logistics, versatility, tactical complexity, shielding, building, fortitude, effectiveness, alignment, resilience, etc. (if you had to ask me, I’d say this is the category that almost no “scaler” actually has a proper framework of… or even understand what the sub-categories mean… logistics and alignment overlap heavily… resilience… fortitude? I think you get the point)

Planning - contingencies, coverage, intricacies, prep process, risk calculation, invincibility, accuracy, aim… (again, do I have to explain?)

{As a side note, I know some tend to just combine strategy and planning into scheming, but there’s a problem with that which I’ll cover for another later post. }

Deception - lying, bluffing, sophistry, concealment, acting skills, disguise, smearing, fabrication, omission, info control, misdirection.  (There’s more overlap here, such as acting skills/disguise, smearing/fabrication, info control/omission, and more. Bluffing is also too specific for a category. Lying and sophistry could just be combined into “verbal deception”.)

Manipulation……. THE DUMBEST CATEGORY OF THEM ALL - logical, illogical, emotional, psychological, individual, mass, indirect, direct. What the hell, you guys? This is so stupid. What do you mean “illogical”? Psychological and emotional already overlap heavily. And what about logical manipulation? Indirect? These aren’t even broad forms of manipulation. I’m so sick of how almost nobody is saying anything about this. The worst designed category by the community. A true reminder that 99% of SCD community is just sheep.

Psychology - theory of mind, hot reading, cold reading, psycho-analysis, psychological warfare, psychological exploitation, psychological insight, profiling…. DO I HAVE TO EXPLAIN????????? The overlap here is insane, and a lot of it is covered already!!!

ETC”

I think you understand already. This is bad. Overlap is never a good thing. We’re missing out on many necessary categories, we’re focusing on unnecessary categories (or ones already covered by other broader ones), and more.

I get that many scalers aren’t exactly in the 99.99th percentile of English proficiency, and that prevents us from making a perfectly coherent and logical framework that avoids most overlap, but what about those who do have the linguistic (and technological) tools needed to do something about this?

Don’t even get me started on categories like field skills, setting traps, or whatever else.

WIS already has one that works perfectly : strength, speed, durability, endurance, intellect, experience, skill, hax, etc.

Why don’t we?

Continuing with what I said earlier about “performative scalers”, they’ll have something like :

Cognition

Intelligence concepts

Self management

Social engineering

Etc, etc, etc.

And this is arguably worse than the more-used one.

I know SCD is never that serious, and I’m kinda cringe for coming out like some crying kid just because of a trivial matter, but there’s no point in staying anymore if nobody will act rationally. You’re scaling smart characters, but you’re not smart yourselves? The irony.
