Another small feat from my OC (side character this time)

**CONTEXT:**

**This feat is for a side character named Grock, who's the main antagonist of the current arc. He is the secret director of the Enforcers (Secret Police type organisation) as well as public face of the police force and the leading advisor to the president in the war they are fighting against an enemy country. Right now his goals are:**

**1. Capture/arrest Sigerson (the MC)**

**2. Capture/arrest the mastermind behind the war**

**3. Repel the rebels' pressure (I forgot to mention they are in a sort of civil war in the background)**

**4. Keep applying pressure on the enemy country, since they have the momentum in the war**

**The 3rd and 4th goals don't really have much to do with this feat though. The 3rd goal just means the number of Enforcers he has available to use is significantly lower than usual (disadvantage). Also, the situation he's in is also kind of a handicap; he has to stay behind and defuse bombs hidden around in ammunition factories (someone is trying to sabotage their ammunition supplies in the war). The country's current state is split into three factions: the criminal underworld, the rebels, and the Enforcers/government. The first two are always hidden and struggling against the Enforcers. Neither can infiltrate the other (I thought I might make this clear).**

The simple scheme he uses to try and trap Sigerson is basically this:

1. The few Enforcers he has on hand, he splits into equal groups and spreads around the country in the most efficient way so communication is extremely fast (due to informants and telegram offices). This placement allows them to communicate to one another extremely quickly if something happens. He also shuts down the telegram offices to prevent Sigerson or any accomplices he may have from communicating with each other from long distances, so only the Enforcers have access to long distance messaging.

2. Since Sigerson had never been spotted or heard of by public eyes, Grock suspects him of gaining the help of either the criminal underworld or the rebels. He determines it must be the former after arresting one of the mastermind's henchmen, who provides proof that Sigerson is working with an infamous criminal.

*Some people might say that the henchman was lying, but remember, he is working for the mastermind behind the war, not Sigerson. The mastermind wants to harm Sigerson as much as Grock wants to capture him, so of course they wouldn't lie.*

3. Now, he has to try and track Sigerson down from nothing. How does he do this if he can't directly infiltrate the criminal network? Firstly, he had information on Sigerson, since the MC is technically a detective and worked under him before. During all the times he worked, Grock found a pattern in his career cases, that is, Sigerson never backed down from opposition or let anything come in his way from solving the cases. Additionally, Grock found that he would never leave a case to luck, so Sigerson always required solid proof before actually moving on to convicting criminals. Since Sigerson didn't know that the mastermind's henchman was arrested yet (Sigerson's goal was to arrest the henchman btw), Grock could use this to his advantage by not releasing the news to the public.

*Of course, even if he did release the news to the public, it might be fake, but Sigerson knew it could never be fake and Grock knew that Sigerson knew this. This is because the president of the country (the country is sort of dystopia btw) always used scapegoats to pin misfortunes on, so it was nearly guaranteed that the president would again try and pin the unwanted war on the henchman to boost the citizen's morales and show that they were competent in catching the criminals that started the unwanted war. If the president wanted to show this to the citizens, he would put it in the news. So since Sigerson knew all of this, he would definitely stay and try and get the news himself to confirm his target's arrest.*

4. Grock then chooses to withhold the news from a certain city, and provide the news to another, so he will know where Sigerson will travel to. This allows him to reorganise his Enforcers and also it might allow him to arrest Sigerson there at the city (even if he doesn't, it's fine because it wasn't intended to fully capture him). He makes the information flow blockage less suspicious by turning into a lack of paper in the cities rather than a complete shutdown, which is way more suspicious (this doesn't really do much tho).

5. Now that he has reorganised his Enforcers, he has also diminished the area he has to barricade, which is the area surrounding the main city and the one that holds the news. He barricades these two cities.

6. He plans to start closing the net to cause more interactions between the criminals and citizens, which in turn will lead to more information he can use to finally track down Sigerson's exact whereabouts and arrest him.

This simple scheme, of course, isn't enough, since an unpredictable event happens later on in the story, so Grock is unable to carry out the 6th step.

At the end of the arc, Sigerson successfully misdirects Grock and escapes from the barricaded city, and so this feat really doesn't do much to buff Grock's scheming efficiency.

Do also note that it was already extremely hard for Grock to keep up with 4 goals at the same time, and he was already at a disadvantage since the rebels' pressure meant he only had a limited number of Enforcers and resources available to use plus he had to defuse a bunch of bombs as well.
