**OXYGEN TANK GAME FEAT**

Despite being heavily wounded by his previous gamble (during Bonfire Game), Shigure continued to participate in the "Leader" Games.

Rules: Oxygen is not so simple. There are two players, and both players are reverse to each other. They cannot see nor turn their heads around. Both players are able to dedicate oxygen tanks (which is only enough for normal breathing, and only amounts up to 10 seconds of air). They each have 30 oxygen tanks.

Both players have to predict which number combination the other player will choose to place on the screen.

Shigure's opponent was Haruno "Odysseus."

Shigure's first thought was saving as much oxygen as possible. However, he was heavily wounded, and he knew he could not bear too much physical exertion.

Shigure knew that Haruno would know that he knew about his inclination to complexity, and thus would choose simpler numbers. However, Shigure also knew it was safe to assume that Haruno also knew that he would think of this.

Shigure also knew that Haruno wasn't capable of writing certain numbers: 0, 9, 2, 5, and 1. The reason why is he knew that Haruno's arm tendon was slightly strained, and he would be inclined to write numbers with less pain naturally due to the finger motions used in those muscles. He had also realized Haruno didn't have a preference to those numbers, but he kept in mind that Haruno would know he knew.

Shigure then also reasoned that the number combinations could be near infinite, but the time to write was only 10 seconds. So the most common number written would likely be 3 to 5 digits.

Shigure then guessed what Haruno wrote: "0."

He was correct. His reason? He knew that Haruno would have thought of everything similarly, and the game did not state that you had to write something down. Also, it was a technical impossibility that you could write any number combination, as this was unfair to both parties.

There were rules that he had not been told, but Haruno had been by the referees.

Shigure wrote down his number, "30."

Haruno guessed correctly.

However, during his next turn, Haruno realized that Shigure was only on his 12th Oxygen tank, while he was on his 23rd. So, Haruno reasoned that Shigure was holding his breath.

So Haruno began holding his breath. However, he was still running out before Shigure.

Shigure also had been guessing all of the numbers correctly, as he had deduced that Haruno was writing in only binary to message the referee that he needed more air. Basically the referee was giving Haruno's oxygen tanks more air.

So Shigure had forced himself to learn binary during the game itself, as he only knew the basic mechanics of Binary. However, he reasoned that Haruno was forced to use a modified form of binary, as the game had constraints on how many numbers one could write, so Shigure had to actively condense binary code and guess half of what Haruno was writing. Shigure already had a basis in learning what Binary was, and was able to create systems following his analysis of Haruno's tastes and theorywork. He created several hundred variations of binary, alterations considered, personality patterns, number choices.

He was basically forced to calculate hundreds of binary combinations and codes. The binary code wasn't all of it either; Shigure was able to link the possibility of a code within a code, and Haruno's own preference for morse code, to realize it was a mix of binary and morse code. How was he able to guess this? Firstly, he had developed a mirror mind: thinking like Haruno, understanding his surroundings, and creating a backstory to get a better picture. With this he was able to infer possible writing styles, thought processes, etc.

Haruno was on his last oxygen tanks, and Shigure was winning by over 14 points.

And shigure was only on his 16th oxygen tank!

How? Shigure had not been holding his breath. He had been using empty oxygen tanks and taking very small breath's from his remaining oxygen tanks. Meaning he actually had 22 oxygen tanks. But not to arouse Haruno's suspicion and to make the refereee accomplice not notice, Shigure let air out of several oxygen tanks. This was to also prove his theory on the referee working with Haruno, since he was running out of oxygen tanks it would appear to that referee their plan would start.

Even in a wounded state (bruised lung, a broken rib, etc) and in pain, Shigure was able to compose himself to the point it looked he wasn't hurt at all. HIs heart rate didn't even change.

Shigure beat Haruno: final score? 29—15.
