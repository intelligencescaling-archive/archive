# Influence

**Influence** for the most part is just a different collection of different categories such as Mass Manipulation, Common Knowledge, Direct Manipulation, Indirect Manipulation etc.

Now I would like to create some distinction, influence is a collection of these cats, but a character can be better in these cats than the other. So the confusion comes how do we consider influence.

From my last post I mentioned Environment, Time Complexity and Struggle. These right here can be determined to build influence and used for comparison.

# Why consider Influence?

**Influence** is a great category, it shows a character's ability to build connections while showing his control and dominance over other cats.

I would now explain some categories and how they affect influence

# Mass Manipulation

This is one of the categories which build influence. Characters who are decent at this are able to highly influence other characters in majority. Friend is one of the best examples of it.

# Emotional Intelligence

Emotional Intelligence is better when one to one communication comes in play. It shows a character control and how he can direct his influence over others.

# Direct Manipulation and Indirect Manipulation

To achieve ones's goal they need to have a direct one to one confrontation, while having the ability to give others assurance while sacrificing others. Indirect Manipulation comes into play to indirectly lead a character's death while showing other under your influence that it had nothing to do with you. Directly manipulating others to convince them to sacrifice to save a majority of other people shows leadership skills.

# Why consider other cats?

Well characters who build influence have to be great in all of them, you have to be it's just that. Johan is better in Direct Manipulation but he hasn't shown much influence by his own rather convincing other powerful people to build influence for him.

# What to consider in Influence?

Finally I would say these things.

1. Time complexity, how fast can someone build influence and how long does it lasts

2. Environment meaning the place where they are building influence which can become harder considering different environments.

3. Struggle, represents the struggles the character and how he overcomes then while building influence

4. How big is it? No I am not talking about the other thing. I meant to say how big is the influence, larger the influence, larger the risk of exposure.

# Example - Red John

Red John is one of the best examples when it comes to influence.

Now explicitly not shown, but this is Theoritical

# Timeline:

Red John escaped in 1980's from Visualise. He peaked at 2000's. Considering police timings and all he beautifully built an influence known as Blake Association in 10 years or so.

Theoritical framework is that Red John became the first responder to crimes commited by politicians. He hid those crimes and used his Direct Manipulation, Emotional Intelligence and Social Skills to create connections with other. His Direct Manipulation allows him to get through other people and make them do whatever he likes, but his charisma creates a promising leader to his organisation.

Indirect Manipulation assures others that his organisation only means he'll target those in necessary. He spreads lies about them to get them killed.

# Time complexity

His influence lasted for 25+ years.

# Size

The size of influence was big, he had the entire US government involved in it, and the fact that no one ever found out shows his leadership skills.

# Personality issues

Where these influential characters loses is considering they are safe. Red John lost to his own ego. While he does have great Emotional Intelligence, being sociopath makes him a narcissistic character. He likes to fiddle and play which is why he lost in the end.

# Misconceptions

One common misconception is that "more money means more influence". That's completely wrong, while money is a good factor which builds influence, it essentially all comes down to a person's skills to spend that money rather it building influence. "An idiot can have infinite money but can still be a chess piece".

Influence can be built very easily, that's false it requires planning, and a lot of other factors I mentioned above.
