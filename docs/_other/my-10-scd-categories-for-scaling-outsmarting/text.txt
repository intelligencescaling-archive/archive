Very much familiar to scd, but new to posting. Here, I have listed the 10 scd categories I'm using for scaling.(Outsmarting only)

I have seen variable categories used by everyone while scaling such that it changes as per the characters scaled.

 So I have come up with a fixed amount of categories to be used to scale characters, which also has its own sub categories.

 I won't use some extra categories i.e.TOM(EU), inter EM(Literally a manipulation category), risk assessment(just prediction, anticipation or premonition with adversity), etc.

 I'll try to give my own explanation about the difference between strategy and planning.

 I won't use the ee category in FSEQ since it comes under  FSSQ.

 I'll try to avoid sub categories that consists of two categories like strategic foresight which is foreseeing the opponent's ploy while strategizing and making using of it. I consider it as an unnecessary category since if someone is good at foresight that is more than enough to nominate them in that category and same for strategy and if you are talking about being good at two categories separately doesn't mean they would be at combining those qualities, there is another category known as insight which is supposed to be specialized at that quality.

  SCD Categories:

1.FSIQ: Cognitive abilities.

\>VSI:  Visualising the whole structure without the need to fully look over it.

\>VCI: Verbal reasoning that requires decoding and linguistic efficiency.

\>PSI: Measure of time taken to reason through a problem.

\>FRI: Fluid reasoning skills.

\>WMI: Measure of memorizing skills.

\------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.FSEQ: Emotional cognitive abilities.

\>EU: Emotionally understanding the target through observing their psychological nature

\>EP: Tracking the target's emotional state and psychological modification. You must emotionally understand the target to perceive the target's modifying emotional state.

\>EM: Managing your emotional state under dire situations.

\------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.FSAQ: Being calm and adverse under extremely dangerous situations.

\>Stress Tolerance: Managing yourself mentally under a stressful situation.

\>Physical Tolerance: Enduring physical pain.

\>Self Awareness: Being aware of your own capabilities.

\------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.FSSQ: Social skills.

\>EE: Socially engaging with the target while being aware of them emotionally.

\>Cold Reading: By intuitively understanding the target's nature and situation, speaking the right words that matches the target's situation.

\>Persuasion: Persuading the target or bringing the target's decision partially under your control.

\>Fear induction: Inducing fear within the target through threatening.

\>Charisma: Influencing the target through their charm and soft speaking skills.

\>Verbal Seduction: Socially seducing the target.

\------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.Strategy: Combining tactics in a consecutive manner which are dependent on each other. Depends on the quality of tactics rather than the way of combination, since they are supposed to be dependent.

EXAMPLE:

Akiyama's Contraband strategy-

(Akiyama deceived Akagi and other two members that they didn't deposit the money in northern atm but hid it(1st tactic) to ally and make them follow his instructions so they hoped for a win themselves.

 Akiyama got the tape recorder from his allies beforehand to record his instructions(2nd tactic).

 Yokoya realising something fishy going on isn't supposed to be any kind of alignment required for Akiyama to consider this as a plan, but it is something Akiyama needs to shield through some tactics. And that is done by making contact with one of the members already beforehand who wouldn't contact the other two such that even if the other two were caught for deceiving, he wouldn't get caught and would gain trust from Yokoya(3rd tactic).

 Akiyama took a speaker with him in the inspection room and used the as a medium to turn the atm card of Akagi pale, such that it looks pale enough to make the lgt officials to bring them another card, while in reality it could be used, letting akiyama take the pale card and Akagi take the newly issued card, so Yokoya wouldn't need to get any doubt(4th tactic).

 As a result, the rest of the process was done smoothly by the traitors of northern country to carry out the rest of the steps.)

If you read yokoya's plan written in the planning category and Akiyama's strategy, you can understand that Akiyama made a feat that requires consecutive, dependent tactics whereas Yokoya made strategies which are practically independent but were aligned through some purposeful interference which made it a plan.

Types:

(1)Scheme strategy: Initiative strategy

(2)Counter strategy: Counter moves

\---

\>Tactics: A flexible intellectual move to chance upon an initiative, setting or evading traps.

\>Creative Combinations: Combining creative tactics that facilitates high probable success rate without the target realising.

\>Psychological Combinations: Combining psychological tactics, totally shielding your initiative in your target's thoughts.

\>Complex Combinations: combining complex tactics or combining a high number of tactics creating a complex block of shielding.

\------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.Planning: Aligning independent events/strategies/tactics within the span of contingency. It depends on the way strategies are aligned rather than the strategies itself. In simple words planning equals alignment of events aka "event manipulation".

EXAMPLE:

Yokoya's Contraband plan-

(Yokoya manipulated Kikuzawa to follow his strategy{considered a strategy by quantity and a tactic by quality due to the repetition of the same move}.

Akiyama figuring out that ploy in the midst of the game is the necessary alignment needed for Yokoya to carry out his next tactics independent of the first one.

 That is, manipulating the three members of the southern country he observed very well to steal the money from Southern atm to northern atm, accomplishing his goal. This is, by overall, the plan of yokoya made through the alignment of two independent strategies).

The advantage of planning over strategy is that there is an automated deception made.

Types:

(1) Scheme: Initiative plot

(2) Plan: Counter plot

\---

\>Strategic Alignment: Aligning varied strategies to reach the goal.

\>Tactical Alignment: Aligning varied tactics to reach the goal.

\>Event Alignment: Aligning various events by foreseeing and manipulating it to reach the goal.

\------------------------------------------------------------------------

7.Deception: Concealing any prominent information through tactical ways.

\>Verbal: Social deception.

\~Bluff- Lying through words.

\~Seduction- Seducing the target through words.(SQ)

\>Non-verbal: Deceiving through schemes and counters.

\~Strategic shielding: Shielding your motive through strategies and tactics.

\~Event shielding(important): Shielding your motive by manipulating the events around or linking the target.

\------------------------------------------------------------------------

8.Manipulation: Taking control over the target's actions through deception or influence without the target realising it.

\>Verbal: Social manipulation.

\~Psycological- Psychologically manipulating the target through words

\~Logical- Speaking logical explanations to bring the target's decision under your control.

\~Influential: Giving the insightful explanation that meets the expectation of the target to get them under your control.

\>Non-verbal

\~Psycological

\~Logical

\~Influential

\------------------------------------------------------------------------

9.Insight: Understanding the information of the situation faced through an intuitive or logical way and thinking through the most efficient solutions.

For example, the plan made by Yokoya in contraband requires a good amount of insight to think through the right goal.

\>Intuition: Reasoning through instinct.

\~Decision making: Choosing the right decision.

\>Observation: Finding important cues through sight.

\>Perception: Reasoning out the important information through the observations made.

\>FRI(FSIQ)

\>PSI(FSIQ)

\>Adaptability: Improving insightfully as the time goes on in a particular event.

\>Knowledge and application: Possessing information of the environment and its technologies to apply it in an insightful manner to carry out plans.

\---------------------------------------------------------------------

10.Foresight: Seeing through the future events through an intuitive or logical way.

\>Prediction: Seeing through the future in logical steps.

\>Anticipation: Seeing through flow in which the structure of a particular event unfolds, not the logical calculation of steps in which the event unfolds.

\>Premonition: Seeing through the future in an intuitive way.
