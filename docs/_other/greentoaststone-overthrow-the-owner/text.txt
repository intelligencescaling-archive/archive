Firstly, the group reported the then owner’s posts for internet fraud due to the obvious scam nature of his “TOEI deal”. GTS requested the group members send proof of their reports via screenshots of emails - by doing this the members are showing their loyalty to the cause since if they were to turn against it, the screenshots could be used as leverage.

Another stroke of luck hit when the former owner revealed in a post that they had actually sold the subreddit to the new owner- violating rule 5 again, this allowed the group (and a few others contacted individually by the members) to take advantage of the situation and file a second report wave.

GTS had noticed a new moderator with an account of only an hour since its creation- they immediately speculated that it was the new owner’s backup/contingency account and GTS took a calculated (and ultimately correct) risk in removing their permissions due to figuring out that the owner wasn’t in a rush to ban any opposers.

Following that they created a post to stand against the owner, further displaying his opposition to the new owner and gaining support from the subreddit. This move acted as a failsafe- if GTS was removed, then the subreddit would fall to the owner, letting everyone know to abandon ship to r/smartcharacterdebates an alternate smaller subreddit which in a worst case scenario could be rebuilt. As a contingency, GTS had another moderator they could trust in case GTS was removed.

The reddit support team soon contacted GTS, informing them that they were now top moderator and the new owner was swiftly removed as well as the previous one who had sold off the subreddit.
