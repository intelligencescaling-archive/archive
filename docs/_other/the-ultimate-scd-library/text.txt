The Ultimate SCD Library
A document created as a library to gather various documents specifically focused on Smart Characters Debates (SCD), with some documents related to both Writing and WIS/Powerscaling, although that is not the main focus. Additionally, more SCD content will be included beyond character documents, such as SCD Categories Docs, Scaling Systems, Tier Lists, and potentially more.
Made by: hypervenomxtreme2.0
Edited by: AgentMcBacon

The characters' verses will be organized in alphabetical order. Only verses that have at least two characters with at least one document will receive their own section. Verses with only one character will be placed in the Miscellaneous Section, also in alphabetical order, until documents for another character (or more) from the same verse are added. Additionally, characters who have docs covering more than one of their versions (for example, a book version, a TV series version, and others) will also be included. If you would like a document to be added, please contact the Discord accounts hypervenomxtreme2.0 or mcbacon_ so the document can be submitted.
