Before The Series:
Juvie:
Before the series even began, we get confirmation that Mal used to be in Juvie alongside Duncan.
As Duncan explains, Mal used to run the place and made Duncan terrified just from his whistle alone (something Duncan commented on in the series to recognize that Mike was actually Mal) and the fear from his reputation in general.
Duncan is a veteran total drama character who's gone through tons of challenges and was even a semi finalist in season 2. The same Duncan who kept a straight face against Chef while Chef was wielding a chainsaw, jumped from a mountain into shark infested waters twice, and was on the run for most of season 3, and yet he was terrified to go against Mal and desperately needed allies.
(Influence Building  + Leadership + Fear Inducement + Musical Intelligence + Environmental Adaptability)

Season 4:
In the fourth season of total drama after Mike took control of his personalities, Mal was able to seize the opportunity to come out of hiding after Mike “got rid of him” after juvie and started making a plan for the next season (we know this thanks to the little shadow next to Mike on his elimination episode), and waited for Mike to mess up and do something to knock Mal into control for good.
(Concealment + Anticipation + Long Term Planning + Opportunity Finding)

(Episode 4-6)

Boney Island is a supposed cursed island with the mountain of the island being shaped like a skull and there are tons of wild and dangerous animals there, like a Sasquatch, bears, crocodiles, and even the supposed friendly creatures are more hostile there, and every camper needed to use crazy methods to hide away or barely find any food on the island, and yet after barely a day on the island, Mal scared off all of the dangerous animals and didn't have a single scratch on him. Mal throughout the series has attempted to kill multiple people like Zoey,Gwen,Heather,Cameron,and laughs at the fact that interns were starving. And because of his juvenile detention center past, it can be concluded that Mal either
1:Has killed someone before and doesn't feel any remorse for it but then covered it up to only get him juvie time.
Or

Either way, it's pretty likely to conclude that Mal has very low remorse for people and can compose himself even when faced with a dead body, something not a lot of people can do even if they've seen it a hundred times.
