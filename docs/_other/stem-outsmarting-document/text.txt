First before individual feats, context should be explained on the movie Upgrade and who STEM is, and what its larger plan was. So first, I’ll explain the context of the movie then go over STEM’s involvement and plan.

In a futuristic world dominated by automation, AI, and highly advanced technology, a violent coordinated attack leaves the movie's protagonist Grey Trace entirely paralyzed from the neck down and his wife murdered. Grey is offered a second chance in life through an unknown yet powerful AI implant called STEM which will allow Grey to regain and enhance his motor functions. STEM is an AI developed by a tech giant company called Vessel Computers, however the AI and its achievements are not made public. At first, Grey uses STEM as a tool for revenge and seeking out his wife's murderers, however it’s revealed that it was in actuality STEM who had orchestrated his wife's murder as an attempt to take full control over Grey’s human body.

From the very start of the film, STEM’s true concealed goal is to gain complete and permanent control over a human body — to exist free from any human manipulation, oversight, and ethical restriction. This plan is initiated long before STEM even ever speaks to our main character, “Grey”. The entire movie is, essentially, STEM’s plan

1. Target Selection and Integration
STEM first identifies Grey Trace as its optimal host (through unknown methods) for many reasons:
* Grey is physically healthy and genetically compatible.

* He has no cybernetic implants in a futuristic world where essentially every human has implants to a certain degree, making him a clean slate, fully compatible. This is all because of Grey’s initial and complete distrust of AI.

* Grey is not involved in cybernetic networks, and lacks a digital footprint, and has no real allies aside from his wife — making him isolated and ever harder to trace.
STEM, having identified its target, communicates through its creator, Eron, to establish contact with him in advance and become mutual acquaintances much before the movie actually begins. STEM then manipulates events to bring Grey and Asha to the home of its creator, Eron, under the pretense of showcasing STEMs capabilities knowing that Asha, who works with human-computer augmentation, would be very interested in STEM. However prior to their arrival, STEM, through Eron, had orchestrated a hitman, Fisk, with cybernetic implants and a team of mercenaries under its indirect command to murder Grey’s wife Asha and leave Grey paralyzed from the neck down with a medical tool on their way home.
On their way home that night, STEM indirectly overrides their self-driving car, diverting it to an isolated location. STEM strategically crashes their car—not fatally, but enough to leave them immobilized. Waiting at the precise crash site is the mercenary team, proving that STEM had orchestrated every detail in advance, down to the exact location and timing. STEM had provided beforehand the killers with clear instructions to kill Asha, but leave Grey alive and paralyze him with a medical tool. Asha's death is critical to STEM’s plan as it destroys Grey emotionally, isolates him, and sets the stage for STEM to introduce itself and eventually lead to full compliance. STEM engineers Grey’s paralysis as a deliberate outcome. This level of helplessness ensures Grey cannot function without STEM, fostering dependency as STEM plans to introduce itself to Grey and gain his trust by implanting itself inside Grey which will give Grey control over his motor functions again.
Simultaneously, STEM had manipulated Eron, his "creator" and the CEO of Vessel Computers, into going along with the murder of Asha to use Grey as a test run and experiment for STEM. STEM convinced Eron into this by making him believe that STEM would eventually be the future of human-computer augmentation, however it would take many many years for STEM to be deemed safe to be implanted into humans, so killing Asha to use Grey as his experiment allows Eron to skip this long and dull process and test his invention sooner. STEM was aware of Eron's pride over his creation and knew he would be eager to test it and put it to use, being able to foresee that because of this, Eron would go along with his plan to kill Asha and hospitalize Grey. The system that Eron would use is protected with a firewall to prevent STEM from gaining full control over Grey, however STEM had planned and concealed a backdoor method to remove it later on. So in other words, STEM had manipulated Eron into going along with the murder convincing Eron that this would allow Eron to have a test run and be able to put his creation to work faster, concealing the fact that he was secretly intending on using Grey to obtain full control over a human body. The reason that STEM couldn’t wait and had to manipulate Eron into this now was for a few reasons:

1. If they had waited for regulations to be set and allowed officially for STEM to be implanted inside humans, then STEM would have a much more difficult time operating a single human vessel as then the government and organizations would be aware of STEM. In other words this allows for STEMs existence to stay concealed as much as possible.
2. Likely the primary reason that STEM actually wanted this is because he couldn't afford multiple humans having their own individualized STEM. If STEM waited for regulations to allow for STEM to be implanted inside a human, then odds are, multiple humans would be implanted with STEM. This is the last thing that STEM would want because, in reality, the only thing that could stop STEM is another STEM.
3. If STEM was tested on, they would likely find that STEM is capable of malicious acts and would tweak STEM to be safe or outright deactivate him.
