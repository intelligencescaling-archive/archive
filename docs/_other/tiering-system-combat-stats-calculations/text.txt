Long story short, I’ll use this page (Archived) that Vs Battles uses for it’s Planetary to Universal tiers as a reference point. Notable concepts: Inverse Square Law (Archived) (I ∝ 1/(d^2); or I = S/4(pi)(r^2) (I is the Radiation Intensity in Candela or Lumen, S is the source, while d is the distance in meters) (Archived)) | Gravitational Binding Energy (GBE) (Archived) is the amount of energy it would take to disperse an entity from it’s gravitationally bound state. The equation that I can get reliably (Archived) is this:
U = (3GM^2)/(5R)
In this previously mentioned equation, U = GBE in Joules, M = the mass of the body in kg, R = the radius of the body in meters & G = the gravitational constant of 6.67430e-11 m^2 kg^-1 s^2 (Archived). Wikipedia & Vs Battles wiki notably shows the image version of this equation, also Vs Battles states that the equation is different with stars:
|Ω| = (3/(5-n))((G(M^2))/R) (Archived) Or U = ((3GM^2)/(r(5-n))).
In this previously mentioned equation, G & M stays the same as the one in the last equation, but r is the radius in meters, & n is the polytropic value  ((pdf pg 1) Archived) characterized by the type of star. Calculator (Archived)
Based off of these facts & the fact that it’s been said that destroying things from a distance & even causing an omnidirectional blast is (common) in fiction, the Tier 4 Revisions Round 2 thread got this equation from Calc Group member Executor N0:
E = 4*U*(Er/Tr)^2 (E = energy in joules, U = GBE in joules, Er = explosion radius in meters (radius that would destroy the area in question), & Tr is the target radius (outer space body’s radius) in meters. (Archived)) (Archived)
My own custom equations (scrapped due to inflating results): E*(1/d^2) = GBE, so GBE/(1/d^2) = E, (OB) = S/(4(pi)(r^2)) or (OB)(4(pi)(r^2) = S
