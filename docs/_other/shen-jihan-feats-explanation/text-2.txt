\*\*Psychological Trap Evasion:\*\* Humans typically feel "off", misunderstood or awkward when something is unresolved and end up replaying the scene to find a solution or better outcome after reexamining past interactions out of a fear that they appeared stupid, offensive, or boring, it causes them to worry about social disapproval. This bias makes people believe that a past event was \*\*predictable\*\* or that they "should have known better" at the time, which often leads to unfair self-judgment -> leading them towards a normal anxiety response wherein the brain tries to prepare for the future on its own.

Meaning, you unconsciously try to predict the future in an attempt to remedy a perceived wrong in the past.

In the dream world this was definitely detrimental to Shěn Jîhān so now that he wasn't inside it anymore + was subject to unbearable pain + had to deduce why Xiaobai needed him, his guard should have fallen afterwards but it didn't.

The only reason is because Shěn Jîhān recalled the series of tricks Xiaobai had pulled throughout the dream to trap him. Especially the old man giving him a quest post mortem by saying: \*\*“There is a man waiting for you up ahead. You will find what you need there.”\*\*

Saying "you will find what you need" rather than "you will get your answer" was one of the subtler psychological traps.

People are always more attached to answers THEY come up with than what is directly told to them by an individual. It's the same reason people learn faster and more efficiently during practicals rather than simple lectures. Using the "need" terminology already places importance on Shěn Jîhān's desire to receive an answer so it becomes an emotionally salient memory and those are the ones that're harder to forget since the 'feeling' alone is enough to influence subconscious thoughts.

It's even more wedged in because Shěn Jîhān needed to figure out the \*answer\* to the first looping mechanism or he'd die. (He knew he would because the red stop sign turned into a hexagonal shape at one point. Red means stop, 6 is the number of turns. This was the case because the "Exit 8" movie was an infinite loop so had he failed at the 6th loop then he would be killed on the spot. Him abandoning the possibility of infinite loops here is the reason why he expected the same thing to apply to the loop by the fountain)

It's why it took Shěn Jîhān 3 loops to figure out that this was one of the many wedges that Xiaobai had imposed upon him throughout the dream state and chose to eradicate them to escape.

This eradication of biases in itself was a trap within many layers of traps that Xiaobai had been left to fall back on. Had Shěn Jîhān believed that the dream world was the only one adhering to the \*\*"law of uncertainty"\*\* then he would die for sure in this new world the moment he expressed any form of relief before deducing the correct answer and once again pre-emptively eradicating the wedge.

Xiaobai didn't want Shěn Jîhān acting too quickly after being subdued by the psychological wedge. He was supposed to wander aimlessly “逍” to a far away/remote “遥” area and wait for an order which correlated with Xiaobai's decision to not provide any sort of information.

When Pai Xi swaps with the mosquito he kills after burying the token, Shen Jîhān deduces that he was not the mosquito itself and when he receives the task from Pai Xi to deliver a vial to Pai Xu, he knows that the vial itself contained liquid from the purple river since the sound of faint dripping was heard when he was behind the bushes at the time.

To Shěn Jîhān, it was obvious that Pai Xi wanted to pressure him into doing something for them and is the reason why he first gives an impossible task that when failed, he will be obligated to do something for him from a disadvantaged position.

Shen Jîhān later sets up a trap that both defies what Xiaobai intended for him to do, and hurt Pai Xi by dropping stones and leaves on top of a mega-mice hole to rile them up by jumping into it and launching himself onto the tree (high enough where a fall would hurt).

The various attacks draws Pai Xi's attention and when he sees the direction the rats were running from he is led to go back and check up on Shěn Jîhān.

But since he wasn't on the ground, Pai Xi swapping places with the bug close to Shěn Jîhān's height caused him to fall and break his foot inside the hole (due to landing awkwardly on the stones)

For this plan specifically Shěn Jîhān sidestepped the law of expectation, predictability and uncertainty and successfully created a plan of action that worked by adapting to each change as soon as they happen.

So he:

Jumped into the rat nest -> used that boost to climb the tree -> used the tree as a hiding place, a vantage point and a trap -> saw what skills were used then adjusted himself to hide according to the proximity of the most dangerous + farthest ones while still staying above the hole -> had Pai Xi break his leg by teleporting with the bug -> then getting attacked by Ye Min -> this was to entice the person who sent Pai Xi, Brother Dong, into action.
