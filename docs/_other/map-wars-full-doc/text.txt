That doesn’t help Harold very much, he doesn’t know too much about the law, but it’s a nice detail about the guy. Harold just does the usual, spends the whole week talking with the guy to build some rapport, talking about common interests, and Ido actually shares his interest in psychology from his childhood, and Harold tells him that, as a prince, he knows a bit about that and is glad to teach him.

Anyway, after that, it’s time for…

THE GRAND FINALE
Yes, it’s time. This is what all this was for. Harold will do a few things.
First, he’ll need to gather the ministers in a place and bring “the boys” from Scotland to give them a good ol’ therapy session, MK Ultra style (scopolamine, ECT, and hypnosis).
Then, he’ll call Prince Félix to Amsterdam, then Harold will steal an old jewelry piece from Natasja (which, 100 years ago, belonged to Félix’s grandfather) and make it seem like it was a regular thief, but he’ll present “evidence” that it was actually Félix. This will ruin Félix’s reputation and make him unsuitable for marriage with Eva, so the marriage will be cancelled by the Council of Ministers and Queen Natasja. This will force Cees to have to go back to looking for a new candidate.
A few weeks after this, Harold will have Natasja and Cees assassinated, they’ve served their purpose. Eva will inherit the throne as queen and appoint him as Chairman of the Council of Ministers (which is now under his control) for an indefinite term. And that’s how he’ll get the control of the Netherlands for good (or at least until Eva grows older and he gives her a daughter, which he’ll make sure to raise to be obedient and single to make things easier).

Let’s get to it.

First step. Robert brings some of his guys from Scotland who are usually in charge of the brainwashing process, then he gathers 7 of the 8 ministers.

Thanks to the scopolamine, the several weeks they spend being manipulated and influenced don’t even register in their minds (scopolamine makes it incredibly difficult to create new memories for 24-48 hours after being dosed). They just think their weeks were completely normal and nothing happened. This was the easy step, because Robert was already incredibly experienced with this process. The next part was the difficult one, but he already knows what to do, it’s just a matter of executing it correctly, of leaving the evidence in the correct order to make it seem like it was simply a very skilled thief.

Second step. Harold gets to work and plans it out. Natasja has the jewelry stored in her own room, so he’ll need Félix to be there at the time of the “robbery”, then he can plant the evidence framing a common thief and then show the proof that it was actually Félix who stole that because of the significance it has to him. So, he summons Félix to Amsterdam for a meeting; they meet at the royal palace with Natasja’s approval. They go near Natasja’s room, then Harold excuses himself for a moment and sets the scene as if Félix was desperately looking around for the necklace. After getting the necklace, he slips it into Félix’s pocket and they keep talking. Eventually, Félix leaves and Natasja stumbles upon the scene of the robbery. At first, they blame the clearly sloppy robber and do a search around town, but when nothing comes, Harold goes to Foreign Affairs and tells Mart Bodde through radio that he heard the whole thing when Félix excused himself, so they should search Félix before he leaves the Netherlands. He actually contacts the police as his own investigation, which avoids exposing Harold as the man with the main evidence, and they search Félix at the border. Indeed, the French prince had the jewelry and was arrested for theft of royal jewelry. The newspapers go haywire with the news that Princess Eva’s fiancée tried to steal from the Dutch queen, while the French king wonders what kind of idiot his son must have been to do it. Soon enough, after being released, Félix is disinherited and Harold makes the Council of Ministers cancel the marriage altogether as the prince is no longer suitable for marriage, which everyone actually supports, even King Cees.

Third step. Everything is in place, and there’s only one thing left to do. The ministers are controlled, Félix is out of the equation, and a new candidate is not yet on the table, so the only thing left is to finally take out Cees and Natasja. So, what does he do? He just makes one of his guys pay a hitman in Amsterdam (to avoid Scotland being connected to the assassination) and has them killed. Of course, this causes a huge commotion and people go wild about it, but Harold makes sure to give Eva all the help and reassurance she needs to be able to function as the new queen she will become. After three weeks, Harold is appointed as Chairman of the Council of Ministers with most of the Parliament and the entire Council agreeing to it.

“Harold IV of Scotland” aka “Robert Mann” has officially become the Chairman of the Council of Ministers of the Netherlands.
