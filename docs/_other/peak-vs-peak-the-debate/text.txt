That's a great versus match-up—both characters are brilliant, manipulative, and mad in their own way. Let's break it down by intelligence type and achievements:

🔬 Edward Richtofen (Call of Duty: Zombies)
Strengths:
* Scientific Genius: Richtofen is a brilliant Nazi scientist, specializing in teleportation, mind control, Element 115 manipulation, and quantum physics.

* Multiverse Awareness: He's aware of and manipulates alternate timelines and dimensions.

* Long-Term Planning: In the later iterations of the Zombies storyline, he demonstrates extremely complex long-term planning involving alternate versions of himself and others.

* Madness/Insanity: His madness gives him unpredictability but doesn’t undermine his intelligence—in fact, it sometimes enhances his creativity.

Achievements:
   * Built and used teleporters, DG-2 weapons, and brainwashing devices.

   * Manipulated events across multiple universes.

   * Orchestrated complex timelines to try to "set things right" (even if his motivations were selfish or unclear).

________________

🧠 Emperor Palpatine (Darth Sidious, Star Wars)
Strengths:
      * Political Mastermind: Orchestrated the fall of the Galactic Republic from within as a senator and later Chancellor.

      * Force Mastery: Unparalleled Sith knowledge, including manipulation of the Force, Sith alchemy, immortality pursuits.

      * Strategic Genius: Manipulated both sides of a galaxy-wide war (Clone Wars), turning a republic into an empire.

      * Legacy Manipulator: His influence lasted beyond death (e.g., contingency plans, "Operation: Cinder", resurrection in the sequel trilogy).

Achievements:
         * Overthrew a republic that lasted for millennia.

         * Trained multiple Sith and dark-side users, all while hiding in plain sight.

         * Outmaneuvered the Jedi Order and wiped them out.

         * Nearly returned from death using forbidden Sith techniques.
