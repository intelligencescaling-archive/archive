SCD Categories
Intellectual Proficiency:

Full Scale Intelligence Quotient -
Working Memory (digit span, picture span, arithmetic)
Processing Speed (coding, symbol search, cancellation)
Visual Spatial (block design, visual puzzles)
Fluid Reasoning (matrix reasoning, figure weights)
Verbal Comprehension (vocabulary, similarities, comprehension, information)

Emotional Intelligence -
Emotional Interpersonal (emotional perception, emotional understanding, emotional facilitation)
Theory of Mind (affective ToM, cognitive ToM, emotion recognition, profiling)
Emotional Intrapersonal (emotional management, emotional engagement, emotional realization)
Adversity Capacity (mental fortitude, stress tolerance, impulse control, perseverance, cognitive discipline)

Social Intelligence -
Awareness (social cognition, self awareness, composure, experience, judgement, situational, empathy)
Networking (social integration, social skills, social mentalities, responsibility, behavioral approach, relationship management)
Influence (communication, leadership, influence building, charisma, aura, reputation, teamwork)

General Intelligence -
Crystallized (application, academics)
Fluid (neuroplasticity, inference)
Multiple (intrapersonal, interpersonal, naturalistic, existential, musical, visual, verbal, logical, bodily)
Cognition (problem solving, learning ability, attention span, novelty, knowledge, adaptability, memory, multitasking)
Sensory (observation, perception, intuition, heuristics, synthesis, guesswork)
Insight (analysis, psychoanalysis, abstraction, dots connection, pattern recognition)

Thinking -
System 1 (faster, association, concrete, holistic, divergent, creative)
System 2 (focused, linear, convergent, abstract, application, strategic)
Metacognitive (critical, reflective)
Reasoning (deductive, inductive, abductive, causal, perceptual, conceptual, decomposition)

Scenario Proficiency:

Manipulation -
Direct
Indirect
Methods (seduction, love bombing, coercion, scapegoating, goalpost moving, reinforcing, discrediting, smearing, triangulation, guilt tripping, gaslighting, demoralization, etc.)
Psychology (emotional manipulation, cold reading, hot reading, psychological warfare)
Engineering (logical manipulation, persuasion, negotiation, social engineering)

Deception -
Discursive (Bluffing, Omission, Misdirection, Sophistry, Reframing, Fabrication)
Operative (Acting, Concealment, Espionage, Disguise, Framing, Unpredictability)

Scheming -
Strategy (Alignment, Flexibility, Effectiveness)
Planning (Preparation, Formulation, Contingencies)
Tactical Prowess (potency, synergy, management)
Methodological Expertise (innovation, upcycling, optimization)
Foresight (prediction, anticipation, risk assessment)
Execution

Field Skills -
Crafting (Information Gathering, Decision Making, Creativity, Trap Setting)
Counteraction (Information Control, Improvisation, Cunning, Trap Evasion)

Situations -
Fixed (Lesser Weaknesses, Higher Strengths)
Real Life (Resourcefulness, Multi-Agent Complexity)
Non-Fixed (Cat and Mouse, Mind Control)
Versatility (Intellectual Proficiency, Scenario Proficiency)
Potential
Countering
Advantages
Frameworks (contextual adversity, complexity, opposition)
