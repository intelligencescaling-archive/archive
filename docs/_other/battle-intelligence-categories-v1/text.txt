Overall CS
Cognitive Processing (CP)
Cognitive Processing is the act of processing the sensory information of which you are provided.

Cognitive Attention (CA)
Cognitive Attention consists of utilizing your cognitive resources towards certain details while filtering out irrelevant information.

Cognitive Memory (CM)
Cognitive Memory involves the retaining, storage, and retrieval of information when presented with it. This includes the capability of recalling details from memory when needed.

Cognitive Knowledge (CK)
Cognitive Knowledge is the compilation of known facts to build an internal depiction of it within your cognitive space to use later.

Cognitive Learning (CL)
Cognitive Learning comprises accession of information obtained through memory and using it to create a process of improvement or adaptation. This utilization of learning as a form of adaptation can be either intentionally or unintentionally

Cognitive Thinking (CT)
Cognitive thinking is where concepts and mental representations are considered and manipulated, this can overlap into other categories as well, such as reasoning,  decision-making, problem-solving, and more.

Overall Management
Risk  Management (RM)
Risk Management is the identification and minimization of risks
Capability Management (CM)
Capability Management is the managing of your capabilities so that you don’t lose your position
Neutralization Management (NM)
Neutralization Management is the managing of your own inner impulses and actions while in combat, it is the act of neutralizing thoughts that might lead you in a worse position.
Knowledge Management (KM)
Knowledge Management is the managing of your knowledge, whether that’s by concealment or usage of it in more manipulative ways in combat

Situations

Fixed Situation
A Fixed-Situation is defined by a set rules and regulations to follow when in combat, a restrictive system.
Non-Fixed Situation
A Non-Fixed Situation is primarily just a situation not defined by regulation or rules, combat where everything goes
