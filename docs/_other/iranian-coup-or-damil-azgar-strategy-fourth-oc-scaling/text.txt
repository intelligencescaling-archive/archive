Fourth OC scaling about one of main villains. Kinda risky, because of mentioning real life people, but anyway let's start.

Name:Hassan Karenzadeh(Real name Hassan Reza Pahlavi). Age:29

Nicknames:The true Sphinx, The last Pahlavi.

Little background: Hassan's father was the son of the former leader of Iran, Mohammed Shah Pahlavi. After their dynasty fell, unlike other members, Hassan's father didn't manage to leave the country in time (his watch was slow), leaving him no other choice than to take a fictitious name and hide from the new government with his wife. During that time, they had a son, Hassan. Unfortunately, his family lived in complete poverty, and when Hassan was 8, his father fell ill, which led to his death. The same fate befell his mother when he was 16. From that day, Hassan decided to achieve two goals.

1. Take revenge on the political elite of the country, believing that his parents' deaths were their fault. Especially, he wanted to take revenge on the main figure of Iran, Ali Khomeini. (In my previous post, I didn't want to write his name because I thought that post could get banned, but I decided to take the risk.)

2. Become the new leader of Iran and restore his dynasty.

The plan had four stages: Gain as much influence in politics and become the mayor of Tehran, destabilize the situation in the country by raising the level of racial hatred, assassinate Khomeini, and carry out a coup to seize power in the capital.

When he turned 22, he started his journey to gain influence in politics. He quickly climbed the career ladder, becoming mayor of several cities and building a reputation as a trustworthy politician who fought against corruption and served as a human rights defender until he finally became the mayor of the capital, Tehran.

After that, he began to think about how to destabilize the situation in the country. Being the mayor of several cities gave him access to information on various citizens, especially those who showed deviant behavior. He was interested in a file on a certain Damil Azgar Horasani from Hamadan city, a former prisoner who was arrested while attempting to kill a person but wasn't severely punished because the victim dropped the charges after Azgar paid him compensation. Hassan decided to secretly meet with him when Azgar was in a teahouse. Hassan sat at the same table where Azgar was and started a small talk with him. Hassan introduced himself as a merchant who traveled across the cities to sell his goods. Their conversation went normally, but Hassan slowly began to change the topic and talk more about laws and crimes. Hassan stated that during his travels, he had heard about many cases and said something like this: "If something happens to poor people, the police are reluctant to take on the case, but if those people are also ethnic minorities, the police won't even open a case."

After that, Hassan finished their conversation and left Azgar alone. Hassan did it on purpose because he wanted Azgar to think about his words and draw the right conclusions from their conversation. Hassan was sure that, considering Azgar's deviant behavior, he would 100% fall into his trap and act as Hassan wanted him to. It actually worked, as Azgar understood that he could use this information, and he started his bloody trip across the cities of Iran. So now Hassan just needed to wait until someone finally found out about the new killer operating in Iran and the police started searching for him, but the longer Azgar remained free, the better the outcome would be for Hassan.

When Azgar was finally caught by Ryuichi (the main character of this story, whom I mentioned in a previous post) after his investigation (in which Hassan played a major role), the number of killed victims was over fifty. Hassan made sure that all newspaper agencies and news programs talked about Azgar's case, especially about how Azgar chose his victims and how the police didn't do much to search for him. Another positive outcome for Hassan was that all the reporters were discussing his role in the investigation and how he paid high compensation to the families of all the victims, causing his reputation to rise even higher.

This case led to strong destabilization in the country, as many ethnic groups were angry that the police didn't even try to catch Azgar. This resulted in major protests all over the country. People went out to rallies, and workers refused to work, and so on. This forced the political elites of the country to work hard to calm down the citizens by giving an official apology in the news and declaring that all police officers who showed negligence would be punished. However, the situation was still tense, and could lead to mass unrest with possible clashes between people, so they announced that on the following Friday (it was declared on Sunday, so it would happen in five days) to honor the memory of the dead, they would hold a prayer in the Blue Mosque of Tabriz, where, in addition to representatives of national minorities, the Ayatollah himself would be present. That was what Hassan was waiting for; now he was aware of where the Ayatollah and many important figures of the country gathered, so when the time came, Hassan could commit assassination by blowing them all up with a pre-prepared bomb.

Despite the plan going smoothly, there was still a small problem. Ryuichi, while investigating Azgar's case, found out about Hassan's plan. To ensure that Ryuichi wouldn't interfere, Hassan framed him by placing drugs in his hotel room. Moreover, he provided information to the police that Ryuichi was staying in the country illegally and that his passport was fake. (In that story, Ryuichi was still a minor, and because he was an orphan without a legal guardian, he couldn't enter Iran normally, so he had to fake his passport.) Because of this, Ryuichi was sent to a prison situated in the Khorasan-Razavi region, where he would be executed in five days.

Now that all obstacles were overcome, the only thing Hassan needed to do was patiently wait until 10:00 AM next Friday when he would give the command to his people to remotely activate the bomb that was placed at the entrance. When the news of the successful assassination spread across the country, Hassan would have the opportunity to start a coup in the capital, justifying it by saying that it was necessary to restore order and prevent mass unrest, as both sides of the conflict would blame each other for the assassination. After all of this, it would be easy for Hassan to become the new leader of Iran and restore his dynasty.

So now Ryuichi has only five days to escape from prison and prevent the assassination, or not only will he die, but there will also be many civilian deaths.
