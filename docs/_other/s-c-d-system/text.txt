Axioms of Outsmarting
Complexity Scales with Scope:
As the scale of influence increases, for example, from local to universal, so does the complexity of systems being manipulated. Metrics like Multi-Agent Complexity (MAC) or Systemic Scope (SS) reflect this escalation.
Multi-Factorial Nature:
Outsmarting is multifactorial in nature—a combination of several factors such as creativity, foresight, adaptability, and sophistication of opposition. This is why formulas are mostly additive or multiplicative.
Interaction of Elements:
Outsmarting is nonlinear; factors often interact with each other dynamically, creating multiplicative relationships.
Example: A character's Conceptual Influence (CI) is far stronger when combined with Foresight Depth (FD) because manipulating abstract systems requires a vision for long-term effects.
Sophistication of Opposition Matters:
It is significantly more impressive to outsmart a highly sophisticated opponent or system than to exploit a simple one. Metrics such as Opponent Complexity (OC) or Meta-System Complexity (MSC) capture this distinction.

2. Empirical Basis
The structure of these formulas is drawn from observations in both human and narrative intelligence.
Inspiratory Sources: Real-World and Fictional
* Game Theory: Multiplicative terms, like AV+CII×MAC, are inspired by how multiple strategies interplay in competitive scenarios.
* Complex Systems: Additive relationships reflect independent metrics, such as creativity and foresight, working together synergistically.
* Hierarchical Systems in Stories: Many narratives—from chess to galactic wars—illustrate an upward escalation in size and complexity, justifying hierarchical frameworks.
Examples:
* Tier 1 (Localized):
In chess, Tactics and Psychological Insight predominate, but the complexity of the opponent determines the challenge.
* Tier 4 (Universal):
In a cosmic manipulation story, Conceptual Influence (CI) and Systemic Scope (SS) dominate as they reflect universal systems of causality and time.

3. Logical Abstraction (A Priori Reasoning)
   * Why Additive Terms?
Additive terms capture independent contributions.
Example: Foresight and Creativity may operate independently, but both contribute to overall effectiveness.
   * Why Multiplicative Terms?
Multiplicative terms capture interdependent relationships.
Example: Manipulating abstract concepts (CI) is amplified by the breadth of influence (SS) because they act synergistically.
   * Why Escalating Complexity?
As tiers rise, formulas incorporate more dimensions, reflecting an increasing dimensionality of influence, such as moving from two factors in Tier 1 to four in Tier 6.
      * Tier 1: Focused, localized variables like Tactics.
      * Tier 6: Requires handling ultimate principles, recursion, and paradoxes.

4. Philosophical Basis (Derived A Priori)
Scope of Intelligence and Outsmarting
These formulas align with philosophical theories of intelligence:
      * Abstraction and Creativity (Immanuel Kant and Bloom Taxonomy): Higher intelligence synthesizes concepts across diverse domains, represented by CI × MCD.
      * Emergent Complexity (Aristotle and Complexity Theory): Larger systems exhibit emergent properties that cannot be reduced to smaller subsystems. For instance, the EC metric applies specifically at Tier 3.
Sophistication of Systems:
The increasing complexity from localized systems to universal or multiversal frameworks mirrors the evolution of abstract thinking:
      * Tier 1: Empirical, direct logic (e.g., explicit manipulation).
      * Tier 4+: Transcendence into conceptual or ontological spaces.

5. Tier-Specific Justifications
Tier 1 Formula (Localized):
OI=(TAC+PI)×OC
      * Why Add TAC and PI?
Tactics (TAC) and Psychological Insight (PI) contribute additively.
      * Why Multiply by OC?
Outsmarting scales with the complexity of the opponent; a simple tactical plan becomes exponentially harder against a sophisticated adversary.
Tier 3 Formula: Galactic
OI=(SA×EC)+SF
         * Why Multiply SA and EC?
Systemic Adaptability (SA) relies on handling Emergent Complexity (EC), making their relationship interdependent.
         * Why Add SF?
Strategic Foresight (SF) serves as a baseline capability that operates independently of the other metrics.
________________

Tier 6 Formula: Cataphysical
OI=(UPM×MCD)+(RMC+ANLM)
            * Why Multiply UPM and MCD?
Ultimate Principles (UPM) require mastery of Meta-Conceptual Systems (MCD) to function effectively.
            * Why Add RMC and ANLM?
Recursive Multiversal Complexity (RMC) and Absolute Non-Logical Mastery (ANLM) are complementary but remain independent skills.

Tiering System

General metric across tiers Opponent Complexity = (Base Complexity (BC) + Domain Expertise (DE)) × Context Alignment (CA)
               * Base Complexity (BC):
The intrinsic difficulty of outsmarting an average individual.
               * Domain Expertise (DE):
The opponent's specialized skills, such as athletic, intellectual, or creative abilities.
               * Context Alignment (CA):
A multiplier that adjusts how effectively those skills apply to the specific scenario.
Tier 1: Localized Outsmarting
Scope:
Manipulation of localized systems, such as individual opponents, small groups, or confined rule-based frameworks like games or localized schemes.
Key Metrics:
                  * Tactics (TAC):
Precision and efficiency in executing strategies within well-defined systems.
Example: Winning a chess game or navigating a single negotiation.
                  * Psychological Insight (PI):
The ability to read and exploit human behavior in small-scale interactions.
                  * Opponent Complexity (OC):
Intelligence and strategy level of the opponent.
Formula:
OI=(TAC+PI)×OC
 Tier 2: Local-Global Outsmarting
Scope:
Manipulation of multi-agent systems spanning local, societal, or planetary dynamics, such as politics, economics, or societal influence.
Key Metrics:
                     * Adaptation Velocity (AV):
Speed and efficiency in dynamically shifting strategies.
                     * Creative Innovation Index (CII):
Novel solutions to address complex systemic problems.
                     * Multi-Agent Complexity (MAC):
The sophistication of the systems and agents being manipulated.
Formula:
OI=(AV+CII)×MAC
Tier 2.5: Global Outsmarting
Scope:
An extension of Tier 2, covering fully global systems, integrating elite multi-agent dynamics and layered strategies.
Key Metrics:
                        * Systemic Foresight (SF):
Ability to anticipate long-term outcomes across multiple agents and systems.
                        * Creative Innovation (CII):
Expanded from Tier 2 to address global systems.
                        * Opposition Complexity (OC):
The sophistication of opponents requiring layered strategies.
Formula:
OI=(SF+CII)×OC
Tier 3: Galactic Outsmarting
Scope:
Interstellar manipulation involving emergent phenomena, large-scale diplomacy, and long-term strategy spanning civilizations.
Key Metrics:
                           * Systemic Adaptability (SA):
Ability to adjust to galactic-scale systems with emergent properties.
                           * Emergent Complexity (EC):
Complexity of large-scale, interconnected systems.
                           * Strategic Foresight (SF):
Multi-generational or long-term planning.
Formula:
OI=(SA×EC)+SF
Tier 4: Universal Outsmarting
Scope:
Manipulation of universal systems, such as time, causality, and space, across vast systems and scales.
Key Metrics:
                              * Conceptual Influence (CI):
Ability to influence or manipulate abstract systems like time, space, or causality.
                              * Foresight Depth (FD):
Long-term planning across universal scales and timelines.
                              * Systemic Scope (SS):
Impact across entire galaxies, universes, or cosmic systems.
Formula:
OI=(CI+FD)×SS
Tier 4.5: Multiversal Outsmarting
Scope:
Operating across multiple universes  and branching timelines, leveraging multiversal frameworks.
Key Metrics:
                                 * Multiversal Navigation Efficiency (MNE):
Success in traversing and influencing multiversal structures.
                                 * Probability Optimization (PO):
Exploiting probabilities to ensure desired outcomes across timelines.
                                 * Systemic Influence (SI):
Manipulating systems across universes and timelines.
Formula:
OI=(MNE×PO)+SI
Tier 5: Metaphysical Outsmarting
Scope:
Manipulating ontological structures and the foundational rules of reality itself.
Key Metrics:
                                    * Ontological Manipulation (OM):
Altering the fundamental rules of existence.
                                    * Paradox Exploitation Rate (PER):
Leveraging paradoxes to create outcomes that defy conventional logic, where logic exists in a metaphysical sense and not its formal scientific sense.
                                    * Meta-System Complexity (MSC):
Complexity of the metaphysical systems being manipulated, including extended metaphysics aka pataphysics which is where imaginary solutions and absurdities  are introduced.
Formula:
OI=(OM+PER)×MSC
Tier 6: Cataphysical Outsmarting
Scope:
Mastery over ultimate principles—beyond metaphysical systems—such as narrative, modality, and constants.
Key Metrics:
                                       * Ultimate Principle Manipulation (UPM):
The ability to manipulate absolute constants or frameworks present in an ultimate reality.
                                       * Meta-Conceptual Dynamics (MCD):
Creation and control of systems that go beyond abstraction or meta-causality.
                                       * Recursive Multiversal Complexity (RMC):
Thriving in and resolving infinite recursion loops where said loops interact on an absolute scale. (Cosmogony has more accurate usage than multiverse)
                                       * Absolute Non-Logical Mastery (ANLM):
Functioning effectively within paradoxical, meta-logical, or incomprehensible frameworks. Where incomprehensible=Ineffability.
Formula:
OI=(UPM×MCD)+(RMC+ANLM)
