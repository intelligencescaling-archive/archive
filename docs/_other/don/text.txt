My mini analysis on Don's plan:

This plan was absolute GENIUS. Don literally orchestrated the entire movie.
First, he intentionally leaked info to the cops about the drug deal, causing that epic police chase. Why? Because Don WANTED to get hurt so they would put him in the hospital.
But before any of that even started, he kidnapped Jasjit's son, Deepu, and handed him over to Vijay’s custody. He did this so Vijay would have something to care about, more emotional stakes, making the whole "Vijay act" totally believable later. (And for the people saying Vardhaan kidnapped the kid,, uhh, no he didn't! When Vardhaan was threatening Jasjit, only the wife spoke, and we find out later Vardhaan had zero clue about the son. Someone else already grabbed Deepu, and that someone was Don). Don killed two birds with one stone here: it helped his acting, and he knew it would trigger Jasjit to seek revenge and steal the disc.
While Don was in the hospital faking a coma, he profiled Vijay EXTREMELY well. He didn't even look at him, but he perfectly copied everything how Vijay moves his hands, his accent, his paan addiction. Then after the doctors made fake wounds on Vijay, don switched places with him. He added some injection or smth in his oxygen thingy. ("what abt the cameras?". Vardhaan was the only one who knew about Vijay except some doctors. It was his scheme to catch Don so ofc he would disable the cameras)
Then he created more chaos using fake discs. When Vardhaan was literally about to kill Roma and Don, Don used his brain and said, "Hey, there's a huge meeting at this specific spot and Singhania is gonna be there. I'll tell you the exact location when I find out." This stopped Vardhaan from pulling the trigger. Plus, it got Singhania killed, meaning Don didn't have to follow his orders anymore AND to delete possible future threats. Right after Singhania died, Don stole the key from his pockets (more on that in the Don 2 analysis).
He knew the cops would show up at that location. He literally got himself arrested on purpose just so he could seduce Roma and use her for his "innocent Vijay" act. He even anticipated Vardhaan's fake death just to expose him.
After the big final fight with Vardhaan, Don handed the police a fake disc. He also intentionally dropped a huge clue for Roma to figure out he was actually Don (while still pretending to be Vijay)he said "i like wild cats" . Why?
