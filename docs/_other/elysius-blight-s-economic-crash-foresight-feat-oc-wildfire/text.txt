Another OC's foresight feat (more reasoning yay)

This feat is for Elysius Blight (my OC) and his prediction of the entire economic crash in the country.

**CONTEXT:**

**A railroad company has begun a VERY amibitious construction project in the mountains, where they think will be safe because hardly any storms or accidents occur there. Blight then visits the site himself, talks with a worker there and gathers information before predicting an economic crash.**

Here is his chain of reasoning:

P1: Temperature inversion occurs when cooler air is trapped to the ground by warmer air, and it also causes sound to travel further

P2: A worker could not hear a voice from far away as usual

P3: The weather conditions are normal except for P2

**C1**: A temperature inversion has lifted

P4: Temperature inversions act as a cap that delays storms

P5: Birds and insects are hurrying back into their nests and dens

P6: Animals can sense if a storm is coming more accurately than humans

P7: Anvil shaped clouds (*Cumulonimbus incus*) can be seen coming towards their direction

P8: *Cumulonimbus incus* are thunderstorm clouds that create the right conditions for strong storms

P9: The last storm recorded there was also caused by the temperature inversion lifting two years ago

**C2**: A strong storm will blow over the construction location

P10: Many Custodians have invested massive sums of money into the construction project

P11: They expect the project to go well with all the advertising/marketing that went on before the actual project began

P12: The Custodians who invested are very tight with their money

**C3**: They won't allow the project to be cancelled

P13: The owner of the company behind the project is known to fold easily to pressure

**C4**: The owner of the company will not shut down the project

**C5**: The workers working on the construction site will have to keep working in despite of the storm

P14: The weather might cause a plethora of accidents relating to machinery, preventing further progress

P15: The workers might rise up against the idea of continuing construction in the harsh weather, also hindering progress

P16: In the unlikely situation of nothing happening, the laying down of foundations will be stopped, due to eventual soil erosion along with wet terrain

**C6**: The project will not be moving forward and will look like a disaster

P17: There is a manipulative businessman

P18: The businessman has access to a majority of the news outlets in the country

P19: He wants the railroad company for himself

P20: He also sees that the project is crumbling apart without much support

**C7**: He will spread the word of the weather to discourage the investors (using news), stir up panic, and watch the company collapse to take over it himself for a cheaper price, taking his manipulative personality into account

**C8**: The stocks for the railroad company will fall

P21: Someone is bound to fear that the other railroad companies will fall

**C9**: The other railroad companies' value will also fall

P22: The bank has lent massive amounts of money to fund the railroad's project

P23: The project will fail

**C10**: The bank will lose a massive amount of money that they spent on the project

P24: From **C8**, the investors who invested in the project will all begin to panic and start selling their stocks

**C11**: There will be a big supply of stocks but little demand (important for later on)

P25: From **C10**, the bank has lost a massive number of assets (mostly money from the project funding)

P26: Banks never keep their deposits in cash

P27: Most deposits are lent out, especially to the project this time

**C12**: Someone is bound to fear losses because of the banks falling value

**C13**: This will cause a chain reaction and everyone will come to withdraw their money from the bank

**C14**: The bank will not be able to distribute enough cash to every withdrawer

**C15**: The country will be plunged into economic unrest and instability

P28: The government relies on its economy to make money and fuel the country

**C16**: The government will act to provide emergency measures to fix everything up

P29: There are foreigners that do business with the country

**C17**: This will reach foreign ears

P30: The news of emergency measures shows a very negative part of the country's economic state

**C18**: The foreign investors will start dropping

P31: The foreign investors assets have to go somewhere after being sold

P32: The country's people will not take it

**C19**: The foreign investors' assets will be taken by the government

P33: From **C11**, the banks have very limited amounts of cash/assets to give now

P34: They fear that others are also going to go bankrupt in this period of uncertainty

**C20**: The domestic banks will hold back their loans

**C21**: Money will be scarcer

**C22**: Interest rates rise

P35: Smaller business require loans to survive

P36: Higher interest rates will make loans harder to get

**C23**: Smaller businesses will shut down from the lack of money during this financial crisis

P37: Many workers work for small businesses (aka the poorer class)

**C24**: Many workers will be unemployed

P38: The government's power cannot solve everyone's financial problems and the bank's lack of money so quickly

**C25**: For those still employed, their wages will be lowered

**C26**: The citizens' spending will be reduced

P39: The shops rely on the citizens' purchases to make money

P40: Shops also buy merchandise from manufacturers

**C27**: With limited customers, the shops will cancel orders from manufacturers

P41: Sufficent time would have passed by now for the government to stabilize the banks to resume careful lending

P42: In order to not fall during this time, a company must have enough money prepared beforehand

**C28**: Only the strongest and most well-prepared companies will survive this economic crisis

P43: Companies would need trains eventually to transport goods around and passengers will also need trains to transport them around

**C29**: The companies to invest in would be the surviving companies and railroad companies because they will never be replaced so quickly (**C8**, **C9**, and **C11** also mean that the cost of their stocks will be pretty low and easily attainable)
