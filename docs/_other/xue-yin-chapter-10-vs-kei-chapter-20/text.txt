Xue Yin (Chapter 10) VS Kei (Chapter 20)

I would highly recommend reading up to the aforementioned point in u/Ancient-End2474's new webnovel 'The Endless Food Chain' before reading any further to avoid spoilers. Obligatory W novel 🙏

Incredibly chaotic opener, I really did not expect it to be quite so brutal, Xue Yin just slaughtering people left and right 😭 I was happy that it was easier to follow than expected; not all the reasoning felt airtight but there where some very impressive inferences regarding the copycats, memory tampering, hidden motives of the examiners etc. His plan to intentionally bait the last copycat into removing all the talismans and trapping them using the final Clock command in order to escape the house, while anticipating the memory tampering and using the blood from the first copycat to remember the identity of the second was a genuine treat to read.

I decided to put him up against one of the first OC's I scaled, Kei from 'Kei and Satoshi' by u/HourFrame9349 as I thought it would be the closest matchup. Anyways, here's my distribution.
