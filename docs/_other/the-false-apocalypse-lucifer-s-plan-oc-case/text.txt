Case: The False Apocalypse

General Premise

The world begins experiencing an escalating series of alarming phenomena:

Wars spread across the globe.

Human violence and wickedness steadily increase.

Famine and humanitarian crises worsen.

Unexplained anomalies begin to appear.

Some stars disappear from the night sky.

Natural disasters become increasingly frequent.

A global leader rises whose influence continues to expand.

Many observers begin noticing disturbing parallels with the prophecies of the biblical Book of Revelation.

However, one fundamental problem remains:

\> No one knows whether these events truly mark the beginning of the Apocalypse.

\---

Available Information

The protagonist has access to:

The Bible and other ancient theological writings.

Historical records of alleged prophetic events.

Religious testimonies, traditions, and partially preserved documents.

Yet every source presents severe reliability issues:

They have been translated countless times throughout history.

Many words may have changed meaning.

Different versions contradict one another.

There is no certainty regarding which passages preserve the original message.

As a result, every prophetic interpretation carries a high degree of uncertainty.

\---

World Conditions

In this universe:

Demons exist.

Demonic attacks are a normal part of the world's nature.

Demon hunters actively combat them.

Therefore, the mere appearance of demons cannot be considered evidence that the Apocalypse has begun.

\---

The Central Problem

The protagonist must determine which of the following hypotheses is true.

Hypothesis 1 — A Preventable Apocalypse

The Apocalypse is a warning.

Its signs appear in advance so humanity has an opportunity to change.

God may delay, alter, or even cancel the final judgment if humanity genuinely improves.

\---

Hypothesis 2 — A Self-Fulfilling Prophecy

Humanity itself can trigger the Apocalypse.

Attempts to stop the prophecies through violence, persecution, or desperate intervention may ultimately bring about the very future everyone is trying to prevent.

\---

Hypothesis 3 — An Inevitable Apocalypse

Everything is predetermined.

No human action can alter the final outcome.

\---

Critical Restrictions

1. Unknown Timeline

There is no known deadline.

The First Seal could open tomorrow—or decades from now.

\---

2. The First Seal Marks the Point of No Return

There is widespread agreement that:

\> Once the First Seal is opened, the process can no longer be stopped.

Any irreversible mistake made beforehand could doom humanity.

\---

3. God Is Omniscient

God knows every human intention and action.

Therefore, attempting to:

manipulate humanity,

establish a dictatorship,

force people to become morally good,

eliminate potential prophetic figures,

only worsens the situation.

\---

4. Prophecy Contains Traps

Attempting to kill the supposed Antichrist, destroy prophetic symbols, or interfere directly with central elements of the prophecy does more than fail.

\> It may actually accelerate the true Apocalypse.

\---

The Fundamental Anomaly

During the investigation, the protagonist discovers something deeply unsettling.

Some events appear consistent with God's will.

Others clearly do not.

Still others seem to be nothing more than natural consequences of human behavior.

Distinguishing between them becomes extraordinarily difficult.

The investigator must determine:

which events originate from God,

which are orchestrated by another agent,

and which are purely the result of humanity's own actions.

At the same time, countless religious documents have been altered over centuries.

The protagonist must reconstruct the closest possible version of the original Bible by separating:

authentic teachings,

later corruptions,

intentional manipulations,

and hidden theological traps.

\---

A New Hypothesis

If these events do not appear to be entirely God's work...

\> Who else could be creating an imitation of the Apocalypse?

One possible answer emerges:

\> Lucifer.

\---

The True Conspiracy

The protagonist concludes that Lucifer has been manipulating world events.

His actions include:

fueling global wars,

amplifying hatred among humanity,

placing a demon—or another loyal agent—in the position of a global leader.

All of this serves one purpose:

\> To create a perfectly convincing illusion that the biblical Apocalypse has already begun.

But Lucifer has also prepared elaborate traps for every type of investigator.

Those who attempt to stop the Apocalypse may unknowingly trigger the real one.

Those who refuse to act allow humanity's worsening condition to eventually bring about the genuine Apocalypse anyway.

Those who try to capture Lucifer mysteriously disappear, along with many innocent people, creating the illusion that the righteous have been taken to Heaven.

If Lucifer realizes someone has discovered his deception, he begins artificially accelerating events:

counterfeit Four Horsemen,

false heavenly trumpets announcing the end,

fabricated prophetic signs,

increasingly convincing imitations of Revelation itself.

\---

Lucifer's Plan

Lucifer has no intention of directly initiating the Apocalypse.

His objective is far more sophisticated.

He wants someone intelligent enough to:

1. recognize the biblical parallels;

2. conclude that the Apocalypse has begun;

3. identify the world leader as the Antichrist;

4. violently intervene to stop the prophecy.

That desperate act would become:

\> The final straw.

The combination of:

humanity's growing wickedness,

escalating violence,

and direct human interference with the prophecy,

would finally compel God to initiate the true Apocalypse.

\---

The Solution

Eventually, the protagonist reaches several crucial conclusions:

1. Every piece of evidence is circumstantial.

2. No prophetic interpretation can ever be considered absolutely certain.

3. Violent intervention against supposed prophetic symbols is morally unacceptable.

4. Trying to "save the world" may ultimately destroy it.

Therefore, the protagonist chooses:

not to hunt the supposed Antichrist,

not to seize control of the world,

not to impose their interpretation upon humanity.

Instead, they decide to:

\> Help people directly, relieve suffering, and become a genuine example of goodness without seeking power.

\---

The Final Danger

If the protagonist acquires enough charisma, hope, or influence, another danger emerges.

They could unintentionally become:

\> The true functional Antichrist.

Not because they are evil—

but because they have become humanity's moral, political, and symbolic center.

Realizing this, the protagonist makes one final decision:

\> To reject any position of global leadership.

They abandon power entirely and dedicate their life to quietly helping the most vulnerable.

By refusing to become the center of the world, they break every contingency Lucifer had anticipated.

\---

The Greatest Challenge

The investigation itself becomes part of the danger.

Investigating too deeply risks accelerating the Apocalypse, especially when genuine acts of God are mistaken for deception.

Ignoring the problem, however, allows the world's corruption to continue escalating until the true Apocalypse becomes inevitable.

The investigator must also realize one final truth:

Lucifer cannot directly destroy the world, because within the prophecy he is destined only to become the Beast of the Apocalypse.

Instead, he manipulates humanity into creating the exact conditions that lead God to begin the real Apocalypse.

Thus, Lucifer becomes the indirect architect of the end of the world.

\---

Final Objective

The protagonist must determine:

1. Whether the Apocalypse is genuine or a fabricated imitation.

2. Whether prophecy is preventable, self-fulfilling, or inevitable.

3. Who is truly responsible for the world's unfolding events.

4. How to prevent humanity from accidentally triggering the real end of the world.

All without making a single irreversible mistake.

Situation: Lucifer is imprisoned. He can only send demons out, but cannot leave himself for more than one minute. The demons seek to free their king and will prioritize carrying out his commands above all else, even killing traitorous demons if necessary. However, Lucifer will rely primarily on his most loyal demons.
