About:
This doc is mainly maintained by Cucharoth
It’s a compilation of everything we know about Radish ASMR~

Radish ASMR is a youtube channel created on oct 15, 2022, It started uploading ASMR type videos with strange audio glitches or straight jumpscares, since then has been developing lore through videos and hidden messages.
Around the end of February 2023, a group dedicated to explore and decipher the hidden messages and clues was created.

In the timeline below you will find a light version recap of every event that has happened, the story it’s still being developed so it’s being updated as it goes.

You will also find in full detail every video and event we have knowledge of.

To anyone new reading this, we do not claim to know everything and it is very likely we’ve missed something. We encourage you to rewatch and compare your findings, if there’s something that is not written here let us know in the discord~!
