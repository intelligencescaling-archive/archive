EXPLANATION: Illogical Reasoning, Fallacious Reasoning, Sophistry

They all mean the same thing.

Illogical reasoning is an uncommon term. Fallacious reasoning sounds insulting.

Sophistry is the most suitable term to use, even though it's still an insult in normal speech.

It means false reasoning that sounds correct.

In the context of intelligence scaling, sophistry refers to how well you can use correct-sounding logic to influence people's thoughts or stump them.

Let me give you some examples:

Low tier sophistry: "It's true because I said so!"

This is clearly poor reasoning, and it's low tier because you can easily see through it, but it still stumps some people and makes them confused.

Mid tier sophistry: "You, the biggest liar on the planet, are calling me a liar? That just proves I'm telling the truth!"

This is very effective and can convince a large part of the masses, but it's still easy to see through. Just because your opponent is bad does not mean you are good.

High tier sophistry: Person A: "Why did you kill him?" Person B: 'I didn't ki-' Person A: "I didn't ask whether or not you did it, I'm asking WHY you did it! Answer me! WHY? How would you feel if it was your family? He was just a child!!"

The person just assumes that he killed him, preventing him even the opportunity to deny the accusation. Instead of asking "did you kill him? why or why not?", he says "you killed him. why did you do it?" Added to this is emotional argumentation, interruption, rhetorical question, and a barrage of information that makes it hard for the person to deny the accusations. In a legal court, this wouldn't be as effective because of the judge's interference, but in public, he can gather the crowd on his side with his fallacious speech.

Higher tier sophistry: "If you had nothing to do with the crime, why were you the only one near the crime scene? It's almost as if you're the criminal and only reported the crime to bring suspicion away from yourself huh?"

This is very difficult to see through and refute in the moment. The person is throwing a string of speculations at a potentially innocent person, but the probabilistic logic makes the audience assume the person is the criminal automatically, with little room for doubt, and too much information for the person to refute easily.

Highest tier sophistry (very subtle and effective if combined with prior manipulation): "Look at what happened when we listened to you last time! Your suggestions are doing nothing but making it worse for us. Stay out of this!"

Just because someone makes a mistake once does not mean his opinion is completely worthless, but the person on the receiving end will find it almost impossible to counter. If he says "I just made a mistake once, I'll be right this time!" it will be admitting to the mistake and people are not going to trust him because it sounds like desperation. So it's very difficult to counter, and even see through it in the first place.

All categories and subcategories are connected in some way. Sophistry is strongly connected to VCI, Reasoning, Emotional Intelligence, Social Intelligence, Manipulation, and Deception.

Sophistry can even be considered a subcategory of misdirection. It's basically just misdirection through false reasoning in speech. Whether you use it or not is up to you, but it's probably one of the hardest subcategories to scale. It's easier to just scale the main categories like deception, since sophistry is part of them anyways.
