1. Context & World Setting
Román is a young man living in a small village within a medieval world. He is a lover of books and history. Rumors begin to spread in his village that a major war is brewing: three kingdoms have formed an alliance to eliminate the most powerful nation—the Kingdom of North—along with two southern kingdoms.

	Román realizes that if war erupts, his village will likely be caught in the crossfire, used as a battlefield, or its people forced to fight as cannon fodder. He decides to design a master plan to evacuate his people safely.
2. Executing the Strategy & Infiltrating the Court
Román initiates his plan step by step:
* Intelligence Gathering: He investigates the three-kingdom alliance, collecting vital information about troop movements, key participants, and strategic vulnerabilities.
* Reconnaissance in Roan: He travels to Roan and gathers rumors and facts from local villagers.
* The Interruption: Leveraging his knowledge of history, he knows Queen Dahlia attends a prestigious annual event around this time. Román interrupts the event, threatening that he brings vital intelligence and demanding an assembly with the Queen.
Román is immediately captured and imprisoned. Although Dahlia could easily execute him for such blatant disrespect, she is intrigued by a stranger daring to threaten her—one of the most feared monarchs in the world. Curious, she grants him an audience in the throne room.
3. Audience in the Throne Room & Negotiation
When Dahlia demands to know the reason behind his scandalous entry, Román reveals the secret alliance between Azvar, Tristen, and Vargand. He gives precise coordinates of hidden enemy troop encampments to prove his credibility.
Dahlia dispatches scouts to verify the claims. Hours later, the scouts return and confirm that the enemy troops are exactly where Román indicated. When Dahlia asks why he is giving her this information, Román explains that he wants to protect his village. He sets three non-negotiable conditions before sharing the rest of his intelligence:
1. Román's village must be relocated to a safe location untouched by the war.
2. He must be granted full freedom as soon as he finishes sharing his knowledge.
3. No actions or retaliation will be taken against him or his people afterward.
4. Private Assembly & Mind Games
Dahlia agrees and orders his handcuffs removed. Román requests that she gather her most trusted advisors, generals, and personal guards in the council room. Once everyone is assembled, Román addresses the room:
Román: "I am only here to notify you that I have come to provide very fresh and juicy information that will give you an early advantage before everything begins."
Suddenly, Román changes his mind and insists on speaking with Dahlia in private. Dahlia agrees, leading him to a private chamber. There, Román discloses the second piece of intelligence: exact soldier counts, armament details, and tactical traps prepared by the rival kingdoms. However, he refuses to reveal the final, most crucial piece of information, stating that doing so immediately would leave him with no leverage to guarantee his survival.
5. Personal Conversation & The Dinner Request
While waiting, Román engages Dahlia in casual conversation. He asks about her succession, and she reveals she took the throne at age 12 as the 13th heir following her father's death. Román shares his love for history and tells a story from when he was 7 years old:
"I bet with friends over who could climb highest up a giant tree in the center of our village. As an ambitious and foolish child, I climbed to the very top to win, but ended up falling and staying in bed for three weeks. I learned that no matter the field, the higher you climb, the harder you fall."
Román then makes a simple request:
Román: "Could you bring me some food? I haven't eaten since yesterday and need something in my stomach to continue this conversation—preferably a large roasted chicken with potatoes."
Dahlia consents and commands a stern guard named George:
Dahlia: "Bring him a plate of chicken with potatoes, just as he requested."
6. Observing George & Psychological Insight
After George leaves, Román asks about him. Upon learning his name, Román notes:
Román: "I like him. You know, I've noticed how the others look at you. Your subjects always have tense shoulders, clenched jaws, waiting and hoping their next word doesn't make you angry enough to order their execution. But that man seems to have your genuine trust, and he is loyal to you out of free will, not fear. A serene face without trying to fake it, despite having the sternest look I've seen in a long time; firm yet relaxed shoulders; looking you in the eyes without fear. I assume he was a faithful servant to your father, and when you took power, he continued to swear eternal loyalty. He seems deeply indebted to your family, and I can guess why."
7. Relocation of the Village & The Hidden Weapon
Dahlia orders her troops to relocate Román's village to a remote, treacherous region filled with wild predators, assuming the peaceful villagers will be helpless and dependent on her mercy. Unbeknownst to Dahlia, this was part of Román's calculation.
After dinner, Dahlia schedules the journey to the relocated site for the following day so Román can confirm his people's safety before delivering the final piece of intelligence. She places heavy guard detail outside his room. However, during dinner, Román had secretly concealed a chicken bone. Throughout the night, he sharpened it into a deadly spike and hid it inside his boot.
The next day, as they walk through the corridors, Román purposely bumps into George, apologizing politely. They board the carriage together with Dahlia. During the ride, Dahlia asks what would happen if his village suffered harm. Román silently pulls the sharpened bone from his boot and holds it against Dahlia's neck, promising to strike if his people were hurt.
Román: "How can I be sure my people are safe and that you won't kill me as soon as we arrive?"
Dahlia: "That is how trust works. Trust that you will fall backward and the person behind you will catch you, darling."
8. The Empty Camp & Exposing the Traitors
Upon reaching the relocation site, they find it completely deserted—only extinguished campfires remain. Román had instructed his villagers beforehand to play the part of unskilled farmers, but immediately evacuate the area as soon as Dahlia's guards left them, eliminating any hostage leverage.
Román forces Dahlia to ride away with him on a horse to a safe distance before ordering her to dismount. He informs her that the final, most crucial piece of intelligence will arrive by mail in two days.
Román: "That is how trust works. Trust that you will fall backward and the person behind you will catch you, darling. Oh, and don't tell anyone else about this or let them see: secretly tell George to check his right pocket."
Dahlia returns to her men and privately tells George to check his pocket. Inside, George finds a hidden note placed there when Román "bumped" into him in the castle corridor:
"Wait for my letter marked with an 'X'—only the two of you—and open it without telling anyone."
Two days later, a letter arrives for Dahlia. Upon opening it, the message reads:
"There are pests among your ranks. George is trustworthy—he must accompany you at all times. Sincerely, Román."
How the Master Plan Unfolded:
* Uncovering the Spies: Román's initial investigation revealed that the enemy kingdoms had placed spies within Dahlia's inner circle.
* The Council Trap: When Román requested to gather all of Dahlia's advisors and generals in one room, he closely observed their facial expressions. The traitors working for the enemy kingdoms grew visibly nervous upon learning an informant had arrived with intelligence about the triple alliance.
* The Secret Note: By slipping the note into George's pocket during their hallway encounter, Román ensured that Dahlia and George would establish complete mutual trust and root out the infiltrators together.
