How to actually be better at outsmarting, No BS Guide.

We won't go Cat By Cat but the only 2 cats You need to Work on if you want to be more Capable in outsmarting are Thinking + Reasoning (To Determine both the past and the Future)

If I asked you what's the Core of outsmarting in a nutshell most People would answer Deception, and you would not be Wrong to Say this, so let's look at it more deeply.

In Fiction we have seen 2 different type's of deceptions:

1. The Opponent wasn't smart Enough or had made an assumption which made sure they don't see through the truth:

In this type of Deception Technically Your opponent has all the necessary information to see through your next move but they aren't able to.

2. The opponent was deceived because they missed A key info, now if their reasoning abilities are crazy enough to still see through you then that's am exception.

In Summary both Deception Styles Follow This Framework:

Established Fact (True or incomplete) -> Reasoned Conclusion (False if Establishment Fact Was True/ True If Established Fact was incomplete) -> Action Taken Based on the Conclusion -> That Action Ends Up in Making you Win Since You predicted their Reasoning From Established Fact.

Now look at all these elements Deeply:

1. Established fact:

It can be established by you or your opponent.

If established by you it's easier to execute but has lower success rate.

If established by the opponent Harder to Execute but higher Success Rate. (The reason why it's harder to execute is because you are the one who needs to form the true conclusion from the established fact and use ur opponents assumptions regarding it to use it against them.

Let's look at some examples:

1. Established fact established by you the MC:

Baku madarame in Air Poker Established the Fact That He Broke The Glass right in Round 1 and He established the Fact That he bet All his BIOS in Round 5 -> Lalo Forms The Conclusion that Baku had been Saving Oxygen Ever Since Round 1 therefore he has more reserved Air And therefore Lalos previous conclusion About Baku's Breathing Rate was Wrong And now By Betting Everything Baku was trying to make him Fold So that He Could Win The Endurance Battle (Lalo was fooled by this because Baku saw through Lalo's assumption that Baku might bet on Fukurou's Mistake, by betting All his Air Bios Baku Re established that Assumption while also making lalo think it was Bait) -> Lalo Checks And Losses the Round.

2. Established Fact Established by Your Opp:

In Tower of karma Suteguma Sends Hyougo To Enter 8 in the terminal -> Baku See's through Suteguma And Realizes it was A fake guess But Suteguma Still thinks that Baku Thought it was Real -> Baku Usses That Assumption Against Suteguma by guessing 13 in the Dotty And misleading him into thinking that Bakus number was 6 or 7.

Since The Move Was made by the opponent themselves the Assumption is Already planted within them therefore it becomes easier to execute deception on them.

So Basically ur reasoning has to be Strong enough to determine what the opponent thinks What you are what you could be planning to do, and use your thinking to think of creative ways to use the Already established assumptions in your opponents mind.

It's as simple as that, all deceptive events weather in fiction or real world follow this framework of doing something your opponent either doesn't think you'd do or couldn't think you'd do, tho the situations do get complex based on the variables.
