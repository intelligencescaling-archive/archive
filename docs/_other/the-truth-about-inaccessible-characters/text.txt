The truth about Inaccessible Characters

I hope you already guessed it.

The characters who supposedly layers of no diffs Ang Xiao out of existence—Vereiya, Long Chian, Ocrevius, Sou Loang, Zha Hiangtan, etc. etc.

They never existed, at least not in the way you think they did.

They were fanfics by the people who introduced them and claimed they were actual works.

# They never existed.

**Evidence 1:** We were never told any reliable evidence that they existed.

**Evidence 2:** Standard was overly complicating the method to access them even though it's actually very simple if said properly. Even if you do the method, it wouldn't lead you to those inaccessible works, instead it would lead you to jazz and some music.

**Evidence 3**: my friends contacted many experts, one among them who did the most contribution went so far as to even contact the figures behind wuxiaworld. The result, couldn't have been more dissapointing, **they don't exist**, or rather there was no reason for them to exist. Just imagine this, which author would write a work with 3000+ chapters for several years with so much effort just to hide it from the public. In truth the Inaccessible Works in writing tournaments does exist but they are very very short and there's no reason for a work with such top tier effort to be in there.

**Evidence 4:** The biggest evidence of all. The people involved admitted it themselves after a long time. They never existed.

# The true inaccessible fictional works:

I also did my own part and went around several writing groups for that one small hope, from facebook, to youtube, to tiktok and several sites to search for the private works with smart characters, I've read nearly a hundred but most of them are short and are Low Tier with Mid Tiers to minor High Tiers at best.

In truth Inaccessible just means there is a privacy. For example, Morris the blind man(before it was released I included it in my joke post of Fang Yuan vs smart characters where I had everyone defeating Fang Yuan and everyone after Niu Youdao were private works or OCs.)was originally a private work but as you can see author decided to make it available to public.

These are the fictional works I found which are all private:

Xuan Luo: from Endless Wonders which I convinced the OC authors to translate the unreadable source into a readable version. The story revolves around absurd situations and is overall a scattered story, with smart characters trying to outwit each other across such a large cosmology. This was originally a powerscaling OC that then became an SCD OC.

Gu Shiyan: original name was Gushan, The first work of the writers of Xuan Luo, it's a Dr.Stone fanfic originally but the world is more cruel and there's a lot of scheming going around. This one is already completed but once again in an unreadable form.

Reverie: From a fanfic of a Philippine tv series named Encantadia(no tiny bit of scd in here), written by a UP Dilleman student, Reverie is a strategic genius who transmigrated in this mythological dimension. Despite him being a genius in strategy, he simply lived a normal life in the modern world, struggling in academics and we were shown that the world simply didn't prefer his type of intelligence. But when he Transmigrates into the new world, he is now able to utilize it and scheme his way up. The ending was also very mature and well done.

Yu Fenxie: Honestly high potential to be well written but author is just so lazy... the author is in this subreddit but is just lurking, Valkyrie something.
