OC scaling. Copycat strategy/planning feat.

"The shadow of the Ripper looms over Tokyo and threatens to devour it whole!"

Hi guys, I saw that sometimes people here like to scale their own characters, so I decided to do the same. I hesitated for some time, but then I decided that the community needs something new, so why not? The worst thing that will happen is that I will disgrace myself, so there is nothing to be scared of.🤣 Sorry for my bad language; I am not a native English speaker, but I will try to write as detailed as I can.

This strategy/planning belongs to one of my characters, who is the main villain in one of the stories—Umitaka Shun, 24 years old.

So let's start with the actions and strategy itself. I thought about writing a little background but then decided not to do it because there will be a lot of text, so I will just describe the main events.

Khm khm, so what does Umitaka actually want? Nothing too much, just to start the Apocalypse, because he truly believes that if he succeeds in that, then the higher powers will reward him with one wish, which he will use to bring his dead elder sister back to life. To make it clear how much he is insane: when his sister died, he secretly stole her body from the coffin and embalmed her corpse, making a wax figure of her, absolutely not wanting her to leave him. As you can see in the second picture, there are points where Shun wanted to commit his crimes in order to paint a large pentagram on Tokyo. His other targets that he thinks can make the Apocalypse come to reality are to kill the "Whore of Babylon," set Rome on fire, and kill seven emperors to start the last battle between Good and Evil.

Why do those emperors need to be killed, and who are they? How did he decide to burn Rome, and what is going on? So let's go in order. In the Revelations of John the Theologian, where he described the future Apocalypse, it was spoken about "The Beast with Seven Heads," who, when the Apocalypse started, was burned. Some people think that The Beast represents Rome and its heads are seven emperors who oppressed Christians (Nero, Diocletian, etc.). Now, let's see the situation in Tokyo. The city is under the control of seven Yakuza gang leaders (seven emperors, seven heads of the beast) and one Triad ruled by Han Junwei (important for the story), In the Chuo district was situated the nightclub "Rome," where gang leaders often rested (the body of the Beast), and the owner of the nightclub is former prostitute Sawamura Eiko (The W*ore of Babylon). Moreover, Umitaka needed to perform his murders in such a way as to create a large pentagram painted in Tokyo (if we connect those kills with lines). So, Shun started formulating a plan, thinking about how he could accomplish all the conditions without being caught while fulfilling them. After a long time, he finally came up with a plan. He needed to become a copycat of a famous serial killer (Jack the Ripper) in order for his plan to succeed. Now I will explain his first steps in his plan:

1. On August 31st in the Shinjuku district, Umitaka killed 27-year-old prostitute Nagasaki Mayumi. The victim was killed in exactly the same way as the Ripper's first victim. Many details were similar in both of these murders: the weapon, the time, and even their names were somewhat similar (Mary Nichols, Mayumi Nagasaki). But Shun also threw Mayumi's wallet in the garbage, not wanting the police to figure out too quickly that there was a copycat of the Ripper in town, making them think it was a robbery gone wrong.

2. On September 8th, he again committed murder, this time killing 19-year-old Chikazawa Emi (Annie Chapman) in the Arakawa district. The crime was a total copy of Jack's second victim, and now the police were fully aware of his existence and understood how he chose his victims. However, Shun wasn't too worried because it was the perfect time for the next step of his plan.

In the period between September 8th and 30th, Umitaka met with Han Junwei to offer an agreement. Umitaka understood that in order for his plan to succeed, he needed to ensure that five of the Yakuza gang leaders would be located there on the night of September 30th at "Rome." He and Junwei made an agreement that Umitaka would help him get rid of the Yakuza leaders so Junwei could take control of the city, and Junwei would help Shun complete his mission.Han made a phone call to Sawamura Eiko and asked her to gather five bosses on September 30th at the club. On September 30th, the Tokyo police were ready for another attack on women, so they were observing all prostitutes with names starting with E and surnames starting with S, but something unexpectedly began to happen. At 1:00 AM, the police received a call reporting that the building of the nightclub "Rome" in the Chuo district was on fire. After the fire was extinguished, the police found not only the body of the owner, Sawamura Eiko, but also the bodies of five yakuza gang leaders. After Shun successfully killed everyone he needed in "Rome," he quickly moved to the Toshima district, where he met with Junwei, who at 1:45 had already killed a girl named Edamura Kanami (Catherine Eddowes), as discussed in their agreement. There were also other parts of the agreement. Firstly, after Umitaka helped to get rid of those yakuza leaders and after Junwei started his campaign to take control of the city, he would have to kill one of the two remaining yakuza leaders in the Sumida district. Secondly, the last leader must be killed by Shun himself, as he needed to kill him directly in Shinjuku to complete his Pentagram painting. Finally, Junwei must pay 100 million to Shun after he finishes with everyone in the Rome nightclub in order to prevent Junwei from getting caught (because even if Shun gets arrested, he can lie that he was hired as a hitman by other people, so Junwei won't get caught. This clause was offered by Junwei himself). Umitaka also made an agreement with Junwei for another reason. He understood that after what happened in the nightclub, they would suspect that the entire series of copycat killings was organized by one (or all) of the remaining gang leaders. However, after Junwei started his campaign to take control of the city and destroy other gangs, he would become the main suspect, and the police would begin preparing for a special operation to apprehend Junwei and his gang (In Shun's eyes, it represents the final battle of Good and Evil.) Meanwhile, Shun will have his opportunity to kill the last emperor (gang leader) in Shinjuku while the police will be busy dealing with the Triad.
