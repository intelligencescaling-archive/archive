FSIQ tier list

Don't take this too seriously. I went off memory of the characters I'm familiar with and ONLY to the extent I'm familiar with them.

**Godly -** The level reachable almost only by sheer power

Freddy Krueger. Shouldn't be too contentious due to the nature of his powers. He's absorbed thousands of souls worth of knowledge, maintains the dreamworld, a structure layers into infinity, with his mind, has perfect memory, etc.

**Supergeniuses** \- Characters who are beyond the limits of any real world human by any stretch of the imagination

Lex Luthor - Again. Shouldn't be controversial. Essentially replicated the Big Bang, created a clone before he hit puberty, learned the Kryptonian language, can react to speedblitzes from Superman and counter, etc.

John Kramer - Absurd autodidact. Became proficient in dozens of fields over the time frame of perhaps a month, even making advances in some of them. Absurd ability to make impossibly precise predictions hours if not years in advance.

John Doe and Hoffman - Kind of like Kramer, but on a far smaller scale. Self taught in medicine, theology, and forensics. Wrote over hundreds of books over the course of a few years just as a contingency plan. Hoffman, even before learning under Kramer, learned engineering over a short period of time to make a Jigsaw-level trap good enough to fool the police.

Michael Myers (Comp) - Can make impossibly precise predictions like Kramer, albeit over a smaller time frame. Mastered driving in seconds.

**Blatantly Superhuman** \- Characters who obviously have superhuman intellect, albeit if you squint your eyes you can kind of see them as realistic

Agent 47 - Mastered dozens of languages. Can effortlessly parallel process an immense amount of sense data to visualize people across hundreds of meters. Perfect memory.

CTW L - Implied to have the same feats as Canon L. Read through several pages in seconds and memorized its contents. Can monitor hax with 50+ monitors. Knows several languages.

Canon L - Slightly inferior to CTW L. Became proficient in stock trading at the age of 6 while illiterate. Can parallel process. Relative to Light. His work as a detective has an impact on crime rates at least in the single digits, implying absurd proficiency. High level of neuroplasticity means he grows far smarter with time.

Canon Light - Can parallel process several monitors. Processed several days worth of memories at once. Memorized dozens of cameras in a short time frame. Pinpointed exactly which deaths benefitted Yotsuba co in a few weeks.

Novels Sherlock - Mastered chemistry and anatomy without ever attending a single class or reading a single book on the matter. Implied to have perfect memory. Often solves crimes instantly upon hearing the details, rarely having to leave the room. His unconscious intuition, he explains, allows him to make extremely complex deductions near instantly with a glance.

Near - Can parallel process dozens of monitors. Stated to be as knowledgeable as Light. Implied to be as good a detective as L.

Joe Goldberg and Dexter - Parallel processing via Rhys/Brian (lol). Joe taught at an ivory league college with zero prior training effortlessly. Dexter's a star forensic analyst and is stated to have gone through med school easily.

**Geniuses to somewhat Superhuman -** As the tier implies, these characters stand at the pinnacle of human intelligence, often with savant level feats in some areas, or perhaps slightly above.

LN Ayanokouji - Perfect memory. His knowledge far surpasses what one could learn in a lifetime, and he learned to a level above professionals several fields, which at the very least includes psychology, martial arts, chess, classical literature, and so on.

Johan Liebert - Mastered several languages as a child. Later went on to effortlessly start a successful criminal bank before he hit 20. Superhuman insight into the minds of others.

CTW Light - Memorized hundreds of names in an instant. Beat CTW L at chess, albeit the former may've been holding back.

Anime Yumeko - Extremely observant with savant-like memory, can make complex deductions under pressure

Walter White - Absurd proficiency as a chemist. Learned how to cook meth to a level that still far surpasses real world scientists who work in labs (while operating out of a crappy RV).

Amanda - Implied to have learned medical skills from Kramer to an applicable degree over a fairly short period of time

**Average to Gifted**

Anime Koji - Very observant. Deals with ANHS's special exams with novel solutions.

Beyond Birthday - Can craft extremely elaborate crime scenes with abstract clues. Almost outsmarted Naomi, an FBI agent.
