I want to make things clear about Zones, because it's gonna be important later
"A "Zone" in Archie Sonic can mean one of 4 things: A region within the main universe(Called "Prime Mobius"), an alternate universe, a pocket dimension, or an alternate timeline.

"Region Zones." These are regions within the Prime Mobius universe. Examples include: Marble Garden, Lava Reef, Ice Cap, etc.

The second one is called "Alternate Reality Zones." These are different universes separate from the main one. Examples include: Anti-Mobius, Planet Freedom, Sol Zone, etc.

The third one is called "Pocket Zones," which are exactly what they sound like: Pocket realities. Examples include: The Zone of Silence, Twilight Zone, Collision Course Zone, etc.

The fourth and final type is called "Alternate History Zones." These are simply alternate timelines branching off from the main one. Examples include: Light Mobius, Dark Mobius, 3437 P.X.E, etc."
