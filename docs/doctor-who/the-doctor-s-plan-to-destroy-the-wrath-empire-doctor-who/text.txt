Firstly,  some backstory

In a galaxy far far away, Kylo Sorsha was a prince of the Royal House of Sorsha in the Drashani Empire. He was also a pyrokinetic, To end a civil war, he was pledged to marry Princess Aliona of the rival Royal House of Gadarel.  However, he found out that she had agreed to the marriage to get the chance to assassinate him and his royal family, using pheromones to manipulate his own emotions.  The plan failed due to the intervention of the doctor and she was killed. However, Kylo's hand was severed and he was stranded on Sharnax, presumed dead He lived on Sharnax for more than 25 years. However, Sharnax's atmosphere was mildly acidic, and after spending so long on the planet, he became hideously disfigured.

Now, prince kaylo swore he take revenge,  he managed to genetically engineer a new army called the wrath and launche a galactic conquest,  but the doctor stopped and reprogrammed the wrath to become a force for good by using his own moral code as a template for their future behaviour. However, they had taken this new morality to the extreme and were attacking everyone for any perceived sin.
