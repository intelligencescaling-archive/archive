17 minute doomsday strategy

This strategy takes place in the series 5 episode 1 The Eleventh hour.
A guy begged me to put it here so like anything for him.

Anyway

Context: The Doctor has exactly 17 minutes before the Atraxi destroy planet earth in order to certify prisoner zeros execution. But if the atraxi were to locate and capture prisoner zero before the 17 minutes were up they would spare the earth and just eliminate prisoner zero.

This strategy was formulated by The Doctor in literally a couple seconds
Firstly he sends Amy and Rory to the nearby hospital where he expects Prisoner Zero will be. He anticipates this due to the fact his disguise has recently been discovered meaning he would have to find another disguise quickly as he thinks they only know one of his disguises. So he would go by hr to hospital where there are all the coma patients that he uses as disguise. He makes Amy and Rory go to the hospital to evacuate the place to make sure prisoner zero takes as little casualties as possible. While they're doing that, The Doctor finds the nearest laptop and hacks in on a confidential call. You see the sun was getting dark randomly and every single Tv on the planet is talking about the end of the world with a big eyeball on the screen. He knows there will be a conference call about it, but it's an extraterrestrial threat on earth so the doctor knows it’ll be astronomical companies and people on the call. He then hacks into this secret call in only a couple seconds. Now he knows they’ll instantly kick him off the call but he understands they're all absolutely terrified at this moment due to the extraterrestrial threat. So the Doctor abuses this fear. He shows them Fermat’s theorem and the proof that backs it up (see here if you want information on what that is). Then he shows them a diagram on why electrons have mass and How to make faster than light speed travel with only two diagrams and a joke at the end (which is considered literally impossible by scientists). Doing all this shows them that he is a genius far ahead of their current intellect and they'll be terrified due to the extraterrestrial threat so the Doctor, by showing he’s a inhuman genius, makes them trust him to be able to deal with this threat and they begin to do everything he says without much question. Then the doctor in only a couple seconds formulates a computer virus and sends it to all their computers and tells them to send it to every single device they have access to. Since this seems sketchy as hell the doctor turns to the random person (Jeff) next to him while he’s on the call and tells him to explain why they listen to him. He turns down the laptop but keeps the volume on. He tells Jeff that he has to convince them to get working and saying that he believes in Jeff to be able to convince them and that he will be a hero after this and he’ll have immense job offers by the end of this. And tells him today is the day he saves the world. This achieves two things.
It convinced Jeff to do exactly as The Doctor says and continue to send the virus everywhere.
It indirectly makes everyone on the call hearing this in the background think of him as a genuinely nice guy who wants to save the world and has no alternative motives.
Also he needs Jeff to keep sending the virus around as he goes to visit the hospital. So he steals a fire truck and goes to the hospital. When Amy calls him and says they can’t get into the hospital the doctor is able to think of a way to get into the hospital. He tells Amy to steal a nurse's clothing and with Rory (who's also in nurses clothing) go inside the hospital and evacuate people. Then The Doctor reaches the hospital and confronts prisoner zero. He starts the conversation by asking about how he escapes Prison using the crack. This is simply to buy time as there are 2 minutes before the code is activated at the exact same time as the doomsday clock reaches zero. Now the computer virus is a line of code that resets all numbers on any digital device to zero. This works on every single device on the planet. The Doctor made this code intentionally simple so that the Atraxi wouldn’t have any problem tracking it, now The Doctor confronting Prisoner zero was necessary as they’d track the virus to the phone which would be right next to prisoner zero. Also on the phone, is every single image of every disguise prisoner zero has taken so that the Atraxi knows what he looks like despite him being in disguise. But Prisoner zero had a contingency and took the form of Amy who collapses as Prisoner zero turns into The Doctor and Child Amy. The Doctor realises that because The disguised prisoner zero takes a due to a psychic link and he turns into what the people are thinking. Since He turned into The Doctor and child Amy, Amy can hear his voice which is why she’s thinking about him. Using this The Doctor tells Amy to think about Prisoner zeros' true form in as much detail as physically possible, this makes prisoner zero turn into himself as that’s what Amy is thinking about. Which the Atraxi instantly pick up on and capture him.
Next The Doctor calls back the Atraxi and meets them on the roof. He asks them if the world is protected and how many alien threats have attacked the world and who stopped them. They go through earth's history to see that it was him who had stopped every single extraterrestrial threat that had attacked the world. He then tells them to “Run” with the knowledge on who The Doctor is, they become absolutely terrified of going near to earth at all and fly away completely away from the planet. Which means they will never put the earth in such extreme danger again.
This scheme accomplished a couple things: Eliminate prisoner zero, have minimum casualties, make sure the Atraxi never attack earth again and save the world from total destruction. Everything in this strategy was executed perfectly and all situations were adapted to perfectly with no flaws. He had calculated before this strategy the fire power of the Atraxi and how long is takes them o power up a beem to destroy the world and the abilites of Prisoner zero. This would be calculating the risk as he knows how strong the enemy’s are and how much risk would be needed to beat them.
