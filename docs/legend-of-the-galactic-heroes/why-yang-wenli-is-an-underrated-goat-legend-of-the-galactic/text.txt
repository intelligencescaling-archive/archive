TLDR:
Yang is always facing extreme adversity since he's always at a numerical, strategical, tactical, political disadvantage in the series. His opponents is also usually Reinhard (Who's also a military genius) and his men (Who are stated to be talented military generals in their own right). Yet they all still get clapped by Yang and he remained undefeated in battle until the end.

That's why, out of all the military generals, I consider Yang my GOAT. Thrawn is cool but he got outsmarted by the rebels a few times in some canons despite having the advantage. Yang's always at a MASSIVE disadvantage and always comes out on top.
