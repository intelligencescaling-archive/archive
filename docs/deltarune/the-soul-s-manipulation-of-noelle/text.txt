The SOUL’s Manipulation of Noelle

Deltarune’s Weird Route from an SCD perspective
Doc made by ForAllPosterity
!! SPOILER WARNING FOR DELTARUNE CHAPTERS 1-4 !!

Context:

Deltarune, at least in the current understanding, appears to be a field of connection between W D Gaster and The Player. Gaster created Deltarune from nothing as a means to fulfill a yet undisclosed purpose and has started a partnership with us, the player, in order to fulfill that purpose.

The player’s avatar is expressed through a red, heart shaped object which is referred to as the “SOUL” (all caps) and sometimes even just as “You”. It was created as a proxy between The Player (as in us, in the real world) and Gaster during the opening sequence of Chapter 1. The SOUL goes on to possess Kris Dreemurr, a human teenager living in Hometown and the rest of Deltarune Chapter 1 plays out as usual, featuring us controlling Kris as they discover the first Dark Fountain and subsequent Dark World. At the end of Chapter 1, we get our first scene of Kris ripping the SOUL out of their body, which shows us as players that the SOUL and Kris are fundamentally two different entities (you do NOT play as Kris, but as the SOUL)

After this, the game just continues on as any normal game does, with Gaster and even the SOUL itself not really doing much of anything as we just experience the story of Deltarune and it’s characters organically.

Deltarune’s central and recurring theme seems to be around fate, specifically, “your choices don’t matter”. The game keeps its word in that regard, giving the player no major choices that result in different routes or changes. You can’t even kill enemies like you could in Undertale to do a Genocide Route, enemies in Deltarune simply run away if their health gets too low.

This is all true except for one major point, the Weird Route (also known as the Snowgrave Route) which starts in Chapter 2. The Weird Route begins the moment Noelle, Kris’s childhood friend, joins your party after Susie and Ralsei go off on their own adventure. The events of the Weird Route after this are what’s gonna be covered in this doc.

Before that, though, I’d like to get the big “Why?” out of the way. Why does the SOUL start the Weird Route? What purpose does abusing Noelle serve? It’s unconfirmed as of Chapter 4, but this doc will be working with my personal favourite theory

Theory: The Weird Route is the SOULs second time playing Deltarune. It saw the original ending, whatever that may be, and then went back to prevent it by any means necessary. To achieve this, it decided on using Noelle.

The “why” of it doesn’t really matter for the purposes of this doc and it doesn’t validate or invalidate the feats we have in front of us, it’s just good to point to a grand purpose to the SOULs plan that gives its established feats more meaning.

Before we begin to look into the feat, we first need to learn who the feat was performed against.

Noelle Holiday:

Noelle Holiday is the 2nd child of Carol Holiday who is the mayor of Hometown (the town which Deltarune takes place in). Her sister, December Holiday, is missing under unknown circumstances and her father, Rudy Holiday, is bedridden in the hospital with an unknown illness. Noelle has a crush on a girl in her class named Susie, who happens to be a part of the main trio of the game and she’s become estranged with her childhood friend: Kris Dreemurr. Noelle in general is a shy, meek person and feels self conscious about that fact. Interestingly enough, though, she’s someone who enjoys scary movies and all things horror, citing that she enjoys “controlling” when she gets scared as she can just turn off the TV if she ever got too scared.

These aspects of her character are all important to know as these virtually all become exploited and used against her as the SOUL turns Noelle into a mindless puppet.

Another important thing to note while you read is that Noelle isn’t aware that the Cyberworld she’s in is real. Or at least she’s unsure as Susie in the normal route is able to convince her that it’s all just a dream to which she believes her.

Skip to the Analysis section of the doc if you’re already aware of the events of the Weird Route

Chapter 2:

“The forbidden path began with ice magic.”

The Weird Route begins as soon as the player resumes control over Kris after Noelle joins your party. After this happens, immediately backtrack and get into an encounter with Noelle

During these battles (there’s a lot more), the player is required to not defeat any monsters with Kris but instead, entirely rely on Noelle’s ice shock in order to defeat the monsters.

The thing that makes Ice Shock so special is that it freezes enemies in place even after the battle is done. As previously mentioned, monsters in Deltarune run away whenever their HP gets too low, preventing the player from being able to kill any monster. This is always the case except when it comes to Ice Shock, it’s the only move in the game that allows you to kill an enemy.

After the player backtracks all the way back to Spamton’s trashheap only to find that he isn’t there, they then continue forward with Noelle, using Ice Shock on every single enemy that they come across. Along the way, Kris and Noelle come across one of the mousetrap puzzles that are littered throughout the chapter. Instead of solving it for Noelle (who has a phobia of mice), the player must elect to walk away from Noelle, who’s trapped on the other side of the puzzle.

Noelle begins to plead with Kris to do the puzzle for her and it’s here that the player gets the first crucial choice in the Weird Route, the first “Proceed” option.

Instead of doing the puzzle for her, the player simply and repeatedly chooses “proceed”. This makes Noelle confused at first, but eventually she relinquishes and runs across the puzzle in fear. Afterwards, she breathes a sigh of relief and hopes that nothing like that happens again. She also wonders when exactly Kris became so authoritative, which isn’t like them at all.

After continuing onwards, freezing more enemies, Kris and Noelle eventually come across a salesman NPC who tries to sell them “Dating Shoes”, insinuating that Kris and Noelle are a couple. Noelle interjects and nervously says that her and Kris are just friends. The player, however, says that they are “something else”, which causes Noelle a deal of confusion over what Kris meant by that exactly.

After walking away for a moment, the salesman stops them and suggests that since they’re “something else”, maybe they would be interested in the Freeze Ring instead. The Freeze Ring is an item that can only be equipped by Noelle that increases attack and magic stats. Noelle initially declines but the salesman beckons that “you can’t get stronger without good equipment!” which causes Noelle to have this reaction:

But afterwards, she still laughs off  the idea of actually getting the ring, thinking that Kris would never buy her something like that. Since Kris isn’t able to buy the Freeze Ring, the player then makes Noelle try and get the ring instead. Noelle is of course confused at what Kris means since they don’t have enough money to buy it, but the player simply keeps choosing the “Get it.” option to Noelle, who remains confused for a while before finally picking up what Kris was putting down.

After saying “Get it.” a few more times, Noelle relinquishes and uses Ice Shock on the salesman NPC, murdering him. The player gets the Freeze Ring after this happens. Interestingly enough, Noelle doesn’t remember using Ice Shock on the salesman NPC and is confused as to what happens to him. Her memory of attacking him was completely gone after only a few seconds.

Despite that, she still thanks Kris for helping her get stronger.

The player equips the Freeze Ring to Noelle and they continue forward. Eventually, they come across a laser puzzle where one character has to stay on a pressure plate in order to stop the laser gate so the other character can walk across and then step on the pressure plate on the other side, allowing the other to walk past it, a trust exercise. During this puzzle, however, Noelle’s walking animation can be seen playing for just a second while she’s on the pressure plate, indicating that despite her apparent gratitude, she still had the thought of outright killing Kris.

Analysis:

As previously stated, the theory this doc is working with is that The SOUL saw the original ending of the game and is now playing through Deltarune again in order to prevent the ending from happening.

To explain why I think this, we have to discuss The Prophecy that was revealed in Chapter 4. All I’ll say is that the final panel of The Prophecy was destroyed by Susie before we the player gets to see what it is. All we can tell is that the ending to The Prophecy (which is what the game is acting out) is a bad ending to the degree that Susie destroyed it out of anger. Interestingly, Susie then states “Kris would NEVER do something like that…”, whatever “that” means…

Theory: The Prophecy’s ending is the Weird Route ending. The ending to the normal route involved breaking free from The Prophecy (likely using Susie) and the SOUL didn’t like that, so it’s now gone back to replay Deltarune in order to make the original Prophecy come true. In order for that to happen, it needed to make Noelle stronger.

The moment Kris and Noelle are reunited in Chapter 2’s Cyberworld, the SOUL, using its control over Kris, makes them backtrack while killing enemies along the way. This accomplishes two things.

1: It gets Noelle used to the idea of battling. Specifically, it gets her used to Kris laying back and guarding them which generates enough TP for Noelle to use Ice Shock. Repeatedly doing this makes Noelle get used to her being the one attacking and always dealing the final blow, which increases her LV (Level of Violence)
2: The repeated encounters start making Noelle get used to them as well as getting used to the SOUL’s commands. This will eventually manifest to her acting on pure instinct after she hears the SOUL talking through Kris to the degree that her mind shuts off, performing Ice Shock and killing monsters in cold blood without even remembering what happened as the SOUL’s voice alone gradually makes her lose herself.

The player makes Kris go all the way back to Spamton’s trashheap in an attempt to get the Thorn Ring. This showcases that the SOUL knew about the Thorn Ring on their first playthrough yet wasn’t able to get it (which is also true in the normal route of the game). This is because the SOUL wanted to unlock Snowgrave on Noelle which you otherwise can’t via normal means because Snowgrave costs 200TP, 100 more than the maximum TP you can generate.

As they defeat more and more enemies and Noelle gets more and more used to the SOUL’s commands, she begins to justify to herself why she should be fine with it. From her POV, her childhood friend whom she hadn’t been close with in quite some time has suddenly appeared in her “dream” and started commanding her to battle. Noelle, who has always been meek and timid is of course initially scared, but eventually comes to be thankful of Kris for making her stronger.

But it isn’t enough as Noelle still subconsciously feels confused, worried and scared over her new situation. Her ever increasing LV causes her to contemplate killing Kris to make it stop, to which the SOUL interjects as she finishes her inner monologue. This causes Noelle to snap out of it and ends the thought right there. Interrupting Noelle as she’s thinking in a way that makes it seem like she was either speaking out loud or worse, having her mind read stopped that thought process in its tracks which focused Noelle’s mind back onto the oncoming battles instead of thinking of a way out, showcasing the SOUL’s masterful manipulation over her inner emotions.

After the SOUL makes Noelle kill the salesman (which is the first showing of Noelle’s mind blanking before and during killing someone) and gets the Freeze Ring, her confidence in battle and her LV eventually increases. She’s now no longer nervous in fights and hones her focus into becoming stronger, exactly as the SOUL wants her to do.

Noelle eventually begins taking initiative on things she normally never would such as “solving” a mice puzzle by herself. This shows that the Freeze Ring encounter and the following mice puzzle where the SOUL made Kris gradually push Noelle into the laser gate (+whatever battles followed that) fully manifested Noelle’s confidence within herself.
