Hello. Over the past few months, I've been making a lot of posts and edits about W D Gaster, the enigmatic overarching antagonist\* of Undertale/Deltarune from the perspective of his feats of intellect. Anyone who's played UT/DR or otherwise knows about it will observe that the details on Gaster are scarce and so some of you might be wondering how exactly I can scale a character we know so little about. Well, I think we know more about Gaster than you'd think and that's what this post is here for: To give an overview on the basic details of Gaster that we have pretty strong confidence in being correct and measure the implications of those facts to his outsmarting capacity.

\*(I don't actually think Gaster is the antagonist. Antagonist's are by definition opposed to the main characters or protagonists and Gaster very much is not, he's on our side the whole way. But that's hardly important for this post)

**Part 1: The Basics**
Some of you may only know of Gaster's relevance to Undertale but struggle to think of how he's relevant to Deltarune. In actuality, he is far more relevant to the latter than the former and [These.](https://youtu.be/RwDrTL3jhoQ?si=ROidv7k818diB6If) [Four.](https://youtu.be/gyZM91y8lyg?si=FrvQwyWLR8KgTZ0h) [Videos.](https://youtu.be/rxdn3jG6O9E?si=kdOtFuHGbfdSs6x_) [Cover.](https://youtu.be/IwKz6JuWeSU?si=lOPPKphbMEXjE-p6) exactly why that's the case in way more detail than I ever could. If you're confused on the very rudimentary details of Gaster's pertinence to Deltarune then even just the first video is enough.

"DEVICE Theory" also holds importance and is something I'd regard as a basic detail of Gaster even outside of dumb SCD stuff. It's the only thing in the DR comm that actually comes close to the scientific definition of a "theory" and not dumb stuff like "Dess groomed Kris theory" 😭. But anyways, all you need to know about DEVICE theory is that an examination of the games code (especially the SURVEY\_PROGRAM) reveals certain labels applied to certain aspects of the games code in a way that stands out from how traditional code is written, these differ in the fact that they're written in all caps, something that's Gaster's calling card. What the theory ultimately suggests is that Gaster has built a DEVICE to connect us the player to the world of Deltarune, but not necessarily that he created the world of Deltarune itself. Given what the narrative has suggested in later chapters (this theory was made during ch2 era), it's actually far more likely that the world of Deltarune we see is actually a copy of a previous world, but the implications and canonicity of that are still unknown.

One more thing, I will be taking the chapter pre-release tweets as canon to Deltarune. Toby explicitly says that only things within the actual game itself are what he considers "canon", but the chapter 1 pre-release tweets (the most important ones) literally directly lead up to the events of the Goner Maker sequence verbatim, hence why I consider those canon.

**Part 2: Motivations**
Why does Gaster do anything? Well this is mostly already covered in the basic details section but I figured I might as well spell it out here. In short, Gaster is using the world of Deltarune to fulfill an "experiment" involving the creation of a "new future". I have my own theories as to what this could mean, but I won't insert my own fanfiction into this scaling as these are really all that's known.

Gaster builds the DEVICE around the world of Deltarune, containing it and making it accessible to The Player/SOUL and sets up the conditions required for this experiment to be possible. The word choice Gaster uses for his experiment ("Then, using your responses, we will approach it's realization"), ("The halfway mark has been attained") suggests that this experiment reaches it's realization at the end of the game. The ultimate result that Gaster is after and what it'll look like remains unknown. The important part here for scaling purposes is that I'm taking the characters within Deltarune to have their own agency that Gaster contorts into bending to his will instead of them being entirely pre-programmed robots. The reason why I believe this is because this is meant to be an experiment, if everything was predetermined then there wouldn't be anything experimental about it. Although this interpretation could prove to be false but either way it doesn't really debunk his scaling as he's still controlling the player and their actions/reactions.

**Part 3: Undertale/Pre-canon Deltarune**
With that out of the way, we can begin to examine the actions of Gaster before the events of Deltarune began.

Undertale:

* Before his shattering, the events of Entry 17 took place. This shows Gaster already experimenting with Dark Worlds well before Deltarune began and seems to be the beginning of his grand "experiment" for Deltarune ("This next experiment seems very, very interesting)
* After Gaster was shattered across time and space, he began to apply what he learnt via his Entry 17 tests. But first, in order for his Deltarune to be fulfilled, he needed to establish a connection with a being on a similar "level" to him, one that views the verse as a mere game and story. In order to confirm that such an entity even exists, he modified the game of Undertale, his former home. By adding rare Fun Value events into the game and hiding details about himself in the form of Goners, Gaster could be certain that anything that managed to find all of them could only be something that has access to the game's code itself and can modify it at whim, same as he can.
* An entity that would go through all of that would also be a curious entity, wanting to know more about this Gaster figure they keep hearing about tucked away behind secrets and code. Such an entity would gladly accept an invitation from Gaster himself, which is exactly what ended up happening.
* With the preparations complete, Gaster was ready to contact this entity: Us, the player (aka the SOUL). Gaster took over Toby Fox's twitter account and reached out to us, getting us to download the SURVEY\_PROGRAM so that we can begin "to approach \[the experiment's\] realization"

Deltarune pre-canon:
Before I segue into the Goner Maker sequence, I feel like it's important to highlight the stuff that we can safely say Gaster had a role in before the actual events of Deltarune began. Whether or not you subscribe to the theory that the world of Deltarune is also just a mere video game and things that happened in the past aren't actually "real" to that world, it's undeniable that the characters themselves treat it as though it is, so everything I say here is still applicable.

* As previously mentioned, Gaster built the DEVICE to contain the world of Deltarune and modify it to his whims
* Gaster set up the prophecy, the backbone behind all the events of Deltarune. This legend would deeply impact everyone in Hometown, building an entire religion around it and even making certain characters seek to fulfill it themselves and other characters (The Fun Gang) to try to subvert it. The Prophecy is kept vague enough so that there is no time limit and several of it's panels and aspects can mean different things, making it so that it can essentially be fulfilled no matter what. But since it's so vague, any detail of it going wrong would destroy it completely, making it easy to subvert with enough effort (and if Kris & Ralsei stopped being a bunch of bitches). This dichotomy is the heart of all conflicts within Deltarune and the bedrock behind every important characters actions
* The only other thing of note is that Gaster played a role or even direct caused December Holiday's disappearance. This has many many different effects and is the pivotal event that the whole story spins around. It made Kris vulnerable to accepting the SOUL and being part of the Knight's Conspiracy, it's the reason why Carol started the Knight Conspiracy plan to begin with, it left Noelle in a state of mental ruin that allowed her to be vulnerable to the Weird Route, it kicked Asgore off the police force and left him open to the events of Chapter 5. So many different characters and events set up from one event that happened YEARS before the story began, insane foresight.

**Part 4: The Legend of DELTARUNE**
We can finally begin with events that we actually see within the game (though they're still mostly set up ahead of time)

Chapter 1:

* After being charmed by Gaster and led into accepting the SURVEY\_PROGRAM, we're met with the Goner Maker sequence, where we are tasked with making a Vessel. After answering a bunch of questions and naming the Vessel and ourselves, the Vessel in unceremoniously stripped from us from an entity other than Gaster himself (evidenced by the completely different text in both translations of the game, the different font size etc). Yet Gaster himself completely ignores this before during and after the fact which is weird. He has ultimate dominion over the game, how could anyone else outright steal something from him? Given the fact that the SOUL going into Kris is the catalyst for the entire game, and Gaster himself states that everything in the game other than the Eggs from the Forgotten Man isn't an issue, it's safe to conclude that the Vessel getting snatched was the plan all along.
*  The Knight Conspiracy plan set up from before kicks into action with a tiny, innocuous act. Noelle steals the chalk box from the supply closet which caused Alphys to send Kris and Susie to go fetch it, which led to them finding Castle Town and the subsequent Dark World in the unused classroom next to it (it's also likely that Noelle is the one who closed the door on them but who knows). This begins the chapter 1 Dark World adventure that introduced the Fun Gang and later incorporated Susie into it after her character development arc.
* During this chapter, we find out about Jevil, Seam, the Knight and this "strange someone" that introduced Jevil to the shadow crystals. This begins a side quest arc to collect every Shadow Crystal by defeating the secret bosses of every chapter. These secret bosses in question all hold thematic relevance to Deltarune so I don't think this is truly just a side quest but rather an important part of the story and Gaster's plan as a whole.

Chapter 2:

* Noelle once again kicks off the events of the chapter by inviting Kris and Susie to the library to study for their project (most suspicious girl of all time I swear to God bro she's involved in all this). In the libraries computer lab lays a Dark World ready to be sealed.
* In this Dark World we see the beginnings of the Weird Route, a divergent timeline from the Main Route that involves psychologically manipulating Noelle and forcing her to get stronger. To facilitate this divergent timeline, Gaster manipulated Spamton into gaining a large amount of success before stripping it away from it, leaving him with a desire to get "bigger" before providing him with the Thorn Ring, a crucial item needed for the Weird Route to succeed. He also gave Spamton a shadow crystal so this manipulation of him has effects in both timelines.
* At the end of the chapter, Kris opens a Dark Fountain in order to fulfill the prophecy.

Chapter 3:

* It's revealed here that Kris/The Knight and Tenna made a deal beforehand in order to occupy the Fun Gang for enough time until the Knight arrives
* During the Dark World, Kris comes across the original game within the S Rank room which foreshadows the Weird Route directly to the player so that the player can go through the game again to fulfill it, another aspect of the divergent timelines plan.
* The Roaring Knight shows up at the end of the chapter, cleaves Tenna (fulfilling a prophecy panel) and kidnaps Undyne instead of Toriel due to Kris's intervention

Chapter 4:

* Susie attempts to uncover who the Knight is but fails due to Carol's concealment and manipulation over Kris
* The chapter 4 Weird Route scene happens where the player gains control over Kris in order to revert the rationalization process that Noelle went through in order to get over the Weird Route events of Chapter 2
* Gerson Boom is introduced in this chapter and is an important stepping stone for Susie as well as revealing the underlying purpose of Deltarune via his Lord of the Hammer narrative
* This chapter is where the Prophecy and the Final Tragedy is revealed which facilitates Susie to want to subvert the prophecy, just as planned

Chapter 5:

* Carol uses the festival as a disguise in order to investigate the shelter undetected
* Gaster manipulates the water levels of the lake, making it shallower so both Main Route and Weird Route lake scenes are possible, with the Weird Route skipping the chapter entirely and bringing it to Chapter 7B
* The last shadow crystal boss in Mad Mew Mew is found within this chapter
* In the Main Route, another Prophecy Panel is fulfilled and Asgore also ends the chapter being kidnapped

And that's where we're at as of now. There are so, so many things that I failed to mention as I only wanted to bring up the more important things regarding his plan, but truly every single thing in Deltarune was either planned directly for Gaster or accounted for. Everything except the Forgotten Man's eggs (not the FM himself, just his Eggs for some reason). Even going through the game without getting any achievements isn't an issue to Gaster, whom only expresses curiosity and admiration if you're able to do that.

**Conclusion:**
Despite not having any on-screen apperences, we can make confident assertions of Gaster given the basic details around him and use it to scale him. Basically, he planned the entire game of Deltarune, everything that happens within the plot is all under his control and watchful eye. He exploited the player themselves and threw them into his experiment, manipulating them and the characters in the world of Deltarune themselves in order to reach it's realization. He's able to accurately predict the actions of characters years ahead of time and facilitate their emotional development so perfectly that nothing the player does can ever hope to break his experiment.

And through it all, there's still so much we don't know about him and his plan since Deltarune is unfinished, like what's up with the Vessel and where does it fit into his plan? What does the Knight and Carol want exactly? etc. But all of this scales him very high and I hope my takes around him make more sense now.
