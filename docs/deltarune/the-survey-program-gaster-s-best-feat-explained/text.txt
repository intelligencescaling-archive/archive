The SURVEY_PROGRAM -- Gaster's best feat explained

As per my last post, Gaster is my new t1 in SCD and slams everyone else I know well enough to scale. Time to explain why that is exactly

As Entry 17 reveals, Gaster was looking to create the Deltarune experiment long before he was ever shattered, while he was still just a regular monster working as the royal scientist under Asgore. One day, he "fell into his creation" (likely just the Deltarune experiment) and was "shattered throughout time and space". This is when Gaster became the being we see at the beginning of Deltarune and in the pre-release tweets, an entity that exists on the same layer of reality that we do as players at least according Deltarune canon.

![img](a3evv4ywwqzh1 "\"This next experiment seems very very interesting\" (deltarune.com 2015 snapshot)")

After this, Gaster started enacting his experiment that he was already working on in Undertale. To do this, he needed to contact the entity he detected messing with the timelines in Undertale, an entity that's on the same level he now is, the player. I explain how Gaster manipulated/recruited the player on my analysis on [his Undertale feats](https://www.reddit.com/r/IntelligenceScaling/s/YMGboamdf8) so check that part out so I don't have to repeat myself.

So Gaster first started with a plan to fulfill some unknown experiment, then fell into his creation (maybe on purpose) and then sought out the player in order to fulfill this exact experiment. From this we can determine that Gaster had already had a hypothesis in mind before ever becoming shattered and this will be important to realize later.

Ok so Gaster led the player into what's essentially a trap to satisfy his own plan (he did it out of love tho trust) so what? This is where Gaster truly becomes OP once you realize what this trap really is, and for that we basically need to go over what DEVICE theory covers which explains what SURVEY\_PROGRAM was all about.

If you didn't know, before Deltarune's demo was fully released (chapters 1 and 2), Chapter 1 was initially released in 2018 under a program named "SURVEY\_PROGRAM", an examination of this file (which is in the proper Deltarune game too) reveals certain details about the code that come across as strange. They're strange because certain objects or rooms within the code are labelled with all caps, something that is uniquely Gaster's style within the story. Their labels are:

IMAGE
ROOM
PLACE
PROCESS
DEVICE
AUDIO
UNUSED

Every single one of these labels is used for things that run outside the story itself, such as save menus, the dogcheck (crash handler), the Vessel Creation segment at the beginning of the game etc.

To not bore everyone here with more details, I'll cut to the chase. These labels showcase that Gaster had built a program that connects us as players in the real world to the fictional world (from our POV) of Deltarune, not the world of Deltarune itself (since they don't have all caps labels for the code).

![img](yfy8vd491rzh1 "mollystar's diagram of reality (outside the box), the DEVICE/PROGRAM (green box), Light World and Dark World ")

Gaster, ***likely before he was even shattered***, figured out a way to connect us as players, real living entities to a world that appears fictional to us. He built a conceptual prison around this reality and turned it's existence into a mere video game to us. Within this world, Gaster has played with light and darkness itself to contort this world into containing more and more nested layers of reality, tampering with the connections between these realities and organically introducing even higher realities than the Light World into it. He manages these many different layers of realities all at once, foreseeing what characters that appear fictional to him (yet still have their own agency) will do decades after his own actions and accounting for the arrival of another meta-entity into this world + how it'll influence it thereafter.

Word salad but long story short, Gaster played with the conceptual frameworks of reality, fiction, light and dark itself, building a program around all of it and connecting us to a world all in order to fulfill his experiment.

The fact that all of this was built on his intellectual engagement of such metaphysical things proves that Gaster is just a whole different beast, and the fact that he probably planned all of this before his "ontology" 😭 allowed for him to meaningfully, physically interact/build his experiment proves that. This guy slams Akane Kurashiki, Composite BSD and Sora all at once with and it's not even close
