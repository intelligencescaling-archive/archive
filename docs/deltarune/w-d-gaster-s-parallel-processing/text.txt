W D Gaster's Parallel Processing

**Spoilers for Deltarune up to Chapter 5**

Back with more Gaster scaling. This time for an argument that suggests that he may be parallel processing the Undertale and Deltarune universes at the same time. Or, at minimum, the Main and Weird Routes

By "parallel processing", I mean the conscious comprehension of two different stimuli at the exact same time, which is something that human beings cannot do at all, we can only ever consciously focus on one thing at a time. Therefore, parallel processing is among the highest forms of CPI.

Here's what I'm using for this scaling:

**Part 1: A parallel story**

https://preview.redd.it/eqpuxjcm7tmh1.png?width=786&format=png&auto=webp&s=0b03a62fbbec7e6ddc9413a2393a400fcf185112

As directly stated by Toby on the [deltarune.com](http://deltarune.com) website, Deltarune is a "parallel" story to Undertale. Not a sequel, not a prequel, but a parallel story. If we take this literally, then that means Deltarune and Undertale run together side-by-side at the same time. However, this is strange given what we know about characters and their direct ties to Undertale. Entry 17, that happens in Undertale, goes over the creation of Dark Worlds, which firmly places Deltarune as happening *after* Undertale. However, Sans travels from Deltarune *to* Undertale as confirmed by Chapter 5, meaning that Undertale happens *after* Deltarune from Sans' POV...what gives?

Frankly I have no idea. But just know that UT/DR's timeline goes in some very strange directions. Needless to say, I think it's best to just take Toby's word for it that Deltarune really is parallel to Undertale.

**Part 2: Always listening**

https://preview.redd.it/34lsvgq88tmh1.png?width=1366&format=png&auto=webp&s=d40f6f0b9749a456d4bc77dbc6172ec32181c7b5

This line is spoken by one of the Goners in UT talking about Gaster. Obviously this line implies that Gaster is directly listening in on everything that's happening in Undertale. This is strange considering the story of UT still takes place within Gaster's past despite him being shattered before the story began, given his DR ch1 pre-release tweets directly call out how we've been looking for him within UT.

Still, best to keep things simple and just take what the game is telling us at face value. Gaster is very much actively keeping track of us in Deltarune, here's the proof:

[Said after the end of Chapter 4](https://preview.redd.it/slthbvju8tmh1.png?width=750&format=png&auto=webp&s=51821b0c7750d57092706c457fb52e921cb8aac2)

So if Undertale is parallel to Deltarune, and he's keeping track of both the verses...yea you see where this is going.

**Part 3: Weird Route**

https://preview.redd.it/29jvirl79tmh1.png?width=1966&format=png&auto=webp&s=9a6b49d15c8037eff86cfa87c0f6dc7922faab07

This image has made me happy for many different reasons. Mostly, it confirmed beyond a shadow of a doubt that Gaster planned the Weird Route. But something else about chapter 5's weird route stuck out to me. As highlighted by my [Festival Day analysis](https://www.reddit.com/r/IntelligenceScaling/s/itjYYfC4ue), I believe that Gaster deliberately altered the lake's water levels to make BOTH lake scenes on both routes possible. This in conjunction with Toby revealing that 7B is releasing at the same time as 7A makes me think of one thing: The Main and Weird route's are happening at the same time.

This would make sense, Gaster says the exact same "halfway mark" line at the end of ch4 no matter which route you're on, which reveals that he's actively keeping tabs on you no matter what. Given his "always listening" narrative...yea once again you see where this is going.

**Conclusion:**
At bare minimum, Gaster is parallel processing two entirely different realities at the same time. Up to 3 if you include Undertale or even 90+ if you count all of Undertale's possible endings as different realities, but that's a stretch.
