The Second Vessel - W D Gaster's Emotional Manipulation of Kris Explained

Analyzing Gaster is the most fun I've had in SCD in a long while so expect to hear from me about him a bunch. This feat in particular is honestly among one of my favourites as well, mainly due to how it connects to Deltarune's writing but as a feat by itself, it's pretty cool. Obviously since Deltarune is unfinished, this post will contain a lot of theorycrafting and is subject to change especially with the release of Chapter 5 right around the corner.

**Background:**
Deltarune is an experiment created by Gaster and performed against The Player in order to fulfill a as of yet unconfirmed goal (see my Fate Engine theory on my profile for what I believe the end-goal to be). In order to fulfill this experiment, Gaster needed to rope The Player into it and establish a connection with them in order to place them into the world of Deltarune. In order for The Player to be in the world of Deltarune, it needed a Vessel to hold The SOUL (the player's avatar) within it. Now, you already create a Vessel with Gaster at the beginning of the game but for reasons unknown, this Vessel is discarded in favour of The *other* Vessel: Kris Dreemurr. For the sake of this theory, we'll just say that the SOUL going into Kris instead of the Vessel you create at the start was the plan all along (there's strong evidence for it but I won't get into it rn).

So now with that established, we can talk about *how* Gaster prepared the way for Kris to become The Vessel to the human SOUL.

**Set-up:**
Like most things in the Deltarune story as of now, it all seems to go back to a certain December "Dess" Holiday, oldest child of the Holiday family and specifically, her disappearance. Roughly 5-10 years before the start of the game, Dess vanished under mysterious circumstances, her absence leaving a strong mark on several key characters that we know of and is essentially the driving motivation behind *most* character's goals.

The crucial element to this in regards to Kris is that the Chapter 4 egg room along with other key visual storytelling/foreshadowing seems to suggest that Kris played a role or otherwise witnessed this disappearance first-hand. As the backbone and seemingly origin point to everything wrong with Kris, I posit that Gaster deliberately used this event and Kris's involvement in it in order to set up their acceptance of The SOUL *years* in advanced.

Of course, the Dess Disappearance caused a lot more to happen than just this. Just to list a few:

* Caused Asgore to be removed from the police, setting up his "inferno of jealousy" things that we'll definitely see in ch5
* Caused Carol to become obsessed with getting her daughter back, starting the Knight Conspiracy thereafter
* Set up Noelle's manipulation in the Weird Route/made it possible to begin with
* (Probably) created The Roaring Knight if you believe in Dess-Knight theory

And more. Point being, this was a multi-layered plan that set up a LOT of stuff but for now, we'll only focus on the Kris aspect of it.

**A Brief Psychoanalysis of Kris Dreemurr:**
What we know of Kris before "the incident" is scarce, but very telling. To be brief, Kris *used* to have a strong relationship with basically everyone in Hometown, especially Noelle. But at some unknown point (almost certainly after the Dess incident), Kris became closed off. They stopped maintaining their relationships and eventually, became incredibly isolated.

That's what everyone sees on the outside, what nobody sees in the utter grief and guilt Kris feels over what happened all those years ago. The trauma of the incident has affected them so much that they have literally blocked it out of their mind entirely with a symbolic "tree" that now comes back to haunt them in the form of the Forgotten Man.

The most extreme part of it all, though, is Kris's self-loathing. The guilt they feel developed and refined over the years to manifest itself in probably the most insane example of self-hatred I have ever seen, where they *WILLINGLY* gave up control of their entire being just so they didn't have to go through the pain of even one more day of existing as themself.

One last thing, there's scarce supporting evidence for it, but it seems likely that Kris used to have an original soul but doesn't anymore, not since The SOUL (the player) took over their body at the start of the game. What this implies is that Kris killed themselves just to fulfill the plan of the Knight Conspiracy to bring back Dess, unreal levels of guilt.

With all that out of the way, we can start to see how insane this feat of Gaster's is.

**Summary of the feat:**
In order for his experiment to be fulfilled, Gaster orchestrated the total override of Kris Dreemurr, facilitating their complex psychological development which made them willingly give away their autonomy just to make the guilt they feel go away and potentially even killing themselves over it. Gaster did all of this just so The Player could have an avatar in his grand experiment he's performing against them and Gaster did all of this YEARS in advanced.

Whatever the SOUL's purpose is to Kris, it is so strong that NOTHING the SOUL does ever makes Kris fully give up entirely. Not forcing them into psychologically torturing their childhood friend, not hurting the people that they care about, nothing, that's how deep the manipulation runs within Kris (although we are starting to see Kris try to take back some autonomy of their own, but thus far it's never been enough to change anything). And this is all thanks to something Gaster did years ago and predicted everything else that came after it.
