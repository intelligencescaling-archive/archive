Kris didn’t have a single reaction to having his soul being ripped out. Now In Undertale your soul is tied to your very being and it is your will.

“That's your SOUL, the culmination of your being! Within, it holds your WILL... your COMPASSION... and the FATE of the world” -Ralsei towards Kris.

1.Your soul is tied to your very being, this would mean both physically and Mentally. As Being means your existence physically and non-physically. This meaning having your soul being ribbed would be physically beyond agonising, as your practically having something that is tied to every single part of your (internal and external) being ribbed out. He would feel things like his entire spinal cord, his nervous system, his brain, his skin and basically everything that in the body having something ripped out of it at exactly the same time all at the same pain. Your will is defined as “the faculty by which a person decides on and initiates action.” Since your soul is your will, being able to handle the pain of ripping out your will. Is practically ripping out a giant part of your mind and this would cause an INSANE amount of mental stress on your brain. As your literally feeling a giant part of your mind die. Note that Kris does this multiple times.

2. Kris,after ripping out his soul is still able to walk and do basic decisions. This is only possible for Kris since it’s implied him that Kris and his soul are separate entities but are just extremely connected. Kris by ripping out his soul is practically ripping out a giant part of his own being, to be able to rip out a giant part of yourself and keep moving momentarily afterwards is impressive as hell. Now his soul his 100% of his will (we know from the statement above), so how is it physically possible for him to move for periods of times without it? This would Mean he has infinite willpower. Since his soul is 100% of his willpower, it would have to be infinite since if you remove something (no matter how large) it will still be infinite. This is also backed by the fact it’s shown that his soul can be used seal the dark fountains which can pierce endlessly into the sky and well Endless = infinite.

So Kris has infinite willpower, this means no matter how much Kris experiences he willpower will mean he can always persevere
no matter what.
