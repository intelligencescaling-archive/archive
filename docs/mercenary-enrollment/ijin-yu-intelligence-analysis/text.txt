Introduction
Yu Ijin is the central protagonist of the Mercenary Enrollment series. A gifted young mercenary and survivor of a plane crash, he opens the story when he returns to Korea after a decade to reunite with his family. His sole intent is to keep a low profile, but this is increasingly thwarted by school bullies, kidnappers and international criminals. This doc will be going over the many feats of intelligence he displayed throughout the story. I will try to go over all of Ijin’s feats in the story in as much detail as possible.

Ijin’s Past + Prologue
Has 10 years of experience. From the age of 9, after being rescued out of a plane crash, he was trained by The Camp, a special experimental unit under Iron, a global private military corporation. By the age of 15, he received the title of 001 of the numbers, a group of 40 out of the 1,000 individuals who survived the hellish training of The Camp, as well as death-defying missions and dangerous battlefields. Mad Dog even refers to Ijin as “the perfect soldier” in episode 134. His training from 004 allowed him to power through pain in order to continue fighting, even if cornered and on the brink of death. From what’s been shown so far throughout the story, Ijin has been trained in martial arts, knife combat, marksmanship, assassination, leadership skills, teamwork, espionage, hacking, and military tactics. He also learned English and Korean, as well as another language during his time as a mercenary. (Experience, Knowledge, Crystallized Intelligence, Linguistic Intelligence, FRI, CPI, Mental Fortitude, Perseverance)

During Episodes 160 and 161, When Immortal and his band of pillagers attacked the mercenary camp Ijin lived in for 5 years, he pretended to be a civilian in order to surprise attack them, before taking out the soldiers surrounding the civilians and killing Immortal. (Cunning, Impersonation)

In Episode 0, Ijin went to rescue the RoK lieutenant named Kang Hamchan. When they faced two soldiers, Ijin immediately disarmed and incapacitated them without taking out his gun in order to not give away their location. He also took into account the of the other two men and their weak states, having everything calculated. (Fast Thinking, Decision Making, Observation)
