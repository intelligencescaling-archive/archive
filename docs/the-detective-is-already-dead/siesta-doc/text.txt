(V2) Bioweapon

How did Siesta figure out about SPES’s bioweapon used by Hel?
Okay, so there’s not just one big “aha” moment where Siesta suddenly knows everything about SPES’s bioweapon plan. It’s more like a whole chain of little things adding up observations, deductions, and just her being way ahead of SPES’s twisted schemes.
Pattern Recognition from SPES’s Past Moves
Siesta’s faced SPES enough times to basically know their playbook by heart. Their style? Big, scary, chaotic threats that mess people up psychologically. Like, seriously, alien tails, Cerberus, the chameleon guy, and that mind control girl (what was her name again?). SPES doesn’t do subtle, they go all-in with these crazy bio or superpowered stuff.
So if Siesta noticed SPES suddenly putting more resources into research, especially bioengineering stuff, that’d be a huge red flag. The jump from just “weapons” to “bioweapons” is obvious if you’ve got her level of pattern recognition. It’s like solving a Rubik’s cube, you see the colors start lining up way before anyone else.
Behavioral Cues from SPES Members
Siesta’s strength is catching all those tiny details most people miss. SPES agents probably never outright said, “Hey, we’re making a bioweapon,” but their behavior gave it away. Were they carrying weird medical gear they didn’t really need? Acting overly cautious about hygiene? That’s not normal, even for criminals.
Little things like specialized supplies, strange lab equipment, or documents with vague scientific jargon weren’t just random, Siesta saw those as breadcrumbs leading straight to something dark. She’s a pro at reading these clues and putting them together.
The Informant Network’s Role
Then you’ve got Siesta’s huge network of informants. She’s got people feeding her info constantly. Even vague whispers about “weird incidents” or “unexplained sicknesses” would have set off alarm bells for her. SPES might try to hide their tracks, but nothing stays secret forever.
Siesta’s skill? Taking these scattered hints and weaving them into a clear story. One informant mentions a shipment of rare chemicals, another hears about secret “experiments” put that all together, and bam, you’ve got a roadmap to SPES’s dirty secrets.
Decoding SPES’s Motives
Knowing your enemy is half the battle, and Siesta nails this every time. What’s SPES really after? Maximum fear, chaos, and despair. A bioweapon fits perfectly into that. If Siesta saw SPES hiring more scientists, funding sketchy projects, or hoarding resources, connecting the dots wouldn’t be hard.
But it’s not just logic. Siesta knows psychology too, human and not-so-human. She gets that bioweapons terrify on a global scale. That’s exactly why SPES would go for it: “Go big or go home” is their motto.
Reading Intentions and Intuition
Here’s where Siesta’s gut really kicks in. She has this almost supernatural sense to read people and predict their moves. SPES’s recent experiments and their obsession with bio-enhancements? Those screams of escalation would be deafening to her.
