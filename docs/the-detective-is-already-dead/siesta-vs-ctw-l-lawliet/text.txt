* FBI bluff: Convinces the President and FBI that there are multiple Ls, turning them from hunters into protectors (L Change The World). This is a brilliant short‑term deception strategy that reverses the situation.
* Hot air balloon escape: Pre‑arranges a balloon and meteorological data to flee undetected – he analyses wind patterns and prepares the escape route in advance (L Change The World).
* Trap for Blue Ship terrorists: Uses a fake transmitter, a pill with a tracker, and a dummy data chip to outmanoeuvre them (L Change The World). He plants multiple layers of deception.
* Cabin trick: Leaves shoes in a fitting room to mislead terrorists, forcing them to hold a meeting that he observes (L Change The World). He uses misdirection to gather intelligence.
* Contingency planning: Leaves a second data chip, plants a dummy bug, and sets up a decoy (L Change The World). He prepares for failure.
* Negotiates with the President: Uses psychological warfare and threats to manipulate the most powerful man in the world (L Change The World).
* Confusion strategy: Observes that the Blue Ship terrorists are amateurs and devises a strategy to confuse them with a falling bomb (L Change The World).

Limitations:

* L's strategies are reactive – he responds to threats rather than initiating them.
* L's strategies are short‑term – they cover a single operation, a few days, or a few weeks.
* L's strategies are defensive – he aims to escape, survive, or protect.

Siesta's Strategy Feats

Multi‑Year Strategic Planning:

* Six‑year Heart Transfer Plan: Orchestrates her own death, consciousness transfer via her specially made heart, suppression of Hel's personality, emergence of Nagisa Natsunagi, and the formation of the anti‑SPES team (Vol. 2, 3, 4). This is fate‑engineering, not just planning. It involves:

1.   Predicting Hel's actions
2.   Planning her own death
3.   Weaponising her own heart
4.   Preserving her consciousness
5.   Engineering Nagisa's future role
6.   Building the final anti‑SPES team

    This spans six years and involves multiple independent actors (Hel, Alicia, Kimizuka, Charl, Fuubi, Mia).
* Bible Trap: Feeds a false prophecy to SEED via the Phantom Thief, turning the enemy's reliance on foresight against it (Vol. 4). This strategy:

*   Uses SEED's belief system as a weapon
*   Manipulates the Phantom Thief into stealing the Sacred Text
*   Creates a false future that SEED relies on
*   Results in SEED underestimating Siesta's true intentions

    This strategy spans years and involves Mia's cooperation.

Posthumous Strategies:

* Unconscious heartbeat signal: While in a coma, hears Kimizuka and Natsunagi's frustrations and deliberately causes a heart anomaly to summon Stephen – strategic action without consciousness (Vol. 8). This requires:

*   Auditory perception while unconscious
*   Voluntary control of heartbeat
*   Understanding of Stephen's monitoring system
*   Timing the signal when her friends are present
* Posthumous AI and cryopreservation: Arranges for her body to be preserved, an AI to be installed, and her will to be executed after death (Vol. 3). This includes:

1. Cryopreservation of her body
2. Installation of an AI based on her memories
3. Programming tasks for the four legacies
4. Timing the AI's activation for one year after her death

* Unmanned plane: Arranges an identical plane at exact coordinates three years in advance to foil Togenkyo's attack (Code Red). This demonstrates time‑spanning strategic execution that continues after her death.

Pre‑emptive Strategies:

* Pre‑emptive hijacking resolution: Solves the plane hijacking before the plane even takes off, plants a bug, lands a crippled airliner with zero casualties (Code Red). This is the embodiment of her philosophy: "First‑rate detectives resolve incidents before they even occur."
* 100,000+ scenario simulation: Accounts for every plausible future, ensuring her plans are robust against unforeseen events (HS21). This is strategic planning at a superhuman scale.

Therapeutic Strategies:

* Designs personalised trials for four legacies to overcome psychological curses – a therapeutic strategy disguised as a crisis (Vol. 3). Each trial is tailored to the individual's core psychological obstacle:

*   Nagisa: Infinity Mirror ritual to integrate Hel
*   Saikawa: Confrontation with the truth about her parents
*   Charl: Breaking free from indoctrination
*   Kimizuka: Rebellion against accepting Siesta's death

Global Strategies:

* Manipulates Arsene the Phantom Thief into stealing the Sacred Text and handing it to SEED, indirectly having SPES under her control for over 5 years (HS21).
* Orphanage rebellion strategy: As a child, she organises a resistance against SEED's facility, builds a secret base, recruits allies, and eventually escapes with the Seed (Vol. 3/HS21).

Why Siesta Wins Strategy

L's strategies are impressive for short‑term tactical scenarios:

* They are reactive – he responds to threats.
* They are short‑term – a single operation, a few days.
* They are defensive – he aims to escape or survive.

Siesta's strategies are fundamentally different in nature:

1. Temporal scale: Siesta's strategies span six years (Heart Transfer Plan), three years (unmanned plane), and even posthumously (AI, letters). L's strategies cover days or weeks.
2. Proactivity: Siesta initiates strategies; she doesn't just respond. She plans her own death and resurrection. She manipulates enemies (SEED, Phantom Thief) into acting against their own interests. L responds to existing threats.
3. Posthumous execution: Siesta's strategies continue to operate after her death. Her AI, letters, and unmanned plane execute her will without her presence. L has no equivalent.
4. Unconscious execution: Siesta's heartbeat signal is a strategy executed without consciousness. She acts strategically even when asleep. L cannot act when unconscious.
5. Scale: Siesta's strategies involve global conspiracies (SPES, Phantom Thief), metaphysical systems (SYSTEM), and multiple independent actors across years. L's strategies involve a terrorist group and a few allies.
6. Psychological engineering: Siesta's strategies are therapeutic – they heal psychological wounds and shape people into who they need to be. L's strategies are purely tactical.

L's strategies are clever; Siesta's strategies are architectural. She doesn't just solve problems; she designs the entire system in which problems are solved.
