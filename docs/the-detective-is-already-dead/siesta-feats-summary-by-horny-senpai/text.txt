Feats from Volume 1

PART 1: THE PLANE HIJACKING — FOUR YEARS AGO

Narrative Context: Kimihiko Kimizuka, an eighth-grader with an uncanny talent for stumbling into trouble, is on a flight carrying a silver suitcase given to him by mysterious men in black suits. He has no idea what is inside. Mid-flight, a cabin attendant asks, "Is there a detective present amongst the customers? " Kimizuka tries to ignore it, but the girl seated next to him—a silver-haired beauty in a militaristic dress—raises her hand and declares, "Yes, I am a detective." She immediately grabs Kimizuka's hand and drags him toward the cockpit, declaring him her assistant. The hijacker, a man codenamed Bat, has taken control of the cockpit and demands the detective deduce his motive.

FEAT 1: Pre-Mediated Asset Placement and Manipulation of Kimizuka.

Siesta did not meet Kimizuka by chance. She had already identified him as a useful pawn and arranged for him to carry her weapon—a musket gun—aboard the flight. The men in black suits who gave Kimizuka the suitcase were her contacts (group of people who assist Turners with their tasks also known as Black Robe). Siesta knew Bat would hijack the plane and that she would need her weapon, but she could not carry it herself (likely due to security or to maintain her cover). By having an unwitting civilian transport it, she bypassed restrictions and placed the weapon exactly where she could access it during the crisis. She also ensured that Kimizuka—a boy with a "troublesome nature" of attracting incidents—would be seated next to her, ready to be recruited. When Kimizuka retrieves the suitcase mid-battle and throws it, Bat destroys the case with his feeler, exposing the musket. Siesta catches it and shoots Bat's feeler in a single fluid motion.

Key Dialogue:

"It was I who instructed you to bring the suitcase onto the flight. ”

SCD Categories:

* Strategic Planning: Siesta orchestrated events before boarding, placing a critical asset (the weapon) and a potential assistant (Kimizuka) in position without either party being aware of her involvement.
* Foresight & Prediction: She anticipated Bat's hijacking, the need for a weapon, and the utility of Kimizuka as a wildcard. This level of prediction suggests deep intelligence on SPES movements and individual behavioral profiling.
* Social Manipulation: She used Kimizuka as an unwitting courier and then immediately integrated him into her operation, demonstrating an ability to turn strangers into assets within seconds.

FEAT 2: Instant Psychological Assessment and Recruitment of Kimizuka.

The moment Siesta raises her hand to answer the detective call, she turns to Kimizuka and, without asking, declares him her assistant. She gives him no choice. She even provides him with a cover identity on the spot: "I'm Siesta, and this is my assistant Watson. We grew up together at Baker Street. ". This is a blatant lie designed to confuse Bat and establish a dynamic. Siesta's decision to recruit Kimizuka is not random; she has observed him i.e the fact that even though the rest of the cabin shows signs of distress, he was relatively calm and carefully observing others to figure out what he might have gotten into this time around. She knew about the suitcase also and likely recognizes his unique "trouble magnet" nature as a potential asset in unpredictable situations. She immediately establishes a hierarchy and begins using him as a sounding board and extra set of hands, even though he is initially useless in combat.

Key Dialogue:

"Perfect, you—be my assistant.”

Siesta says while pulling me along with her.

SCD Categories:

* Social Influence & Manipulation: She seizes control of the social situation, assigns roles, and creates a false narrative to confuse the enemy—all while maintaining an air of absolute authority.
* Strategic Planning: Integrating Kimizuka immediately gives her an extra pair of hands, a potential distraction, and someone to carry out simple tasks (like retrieving the suitcase later). She is building her operational team in real-time.

FEAT 3: Psychological Deduction of Bat's True Motives and the SPES Connection.

Bat presents a game: if Siesta deduces why he hijacked the plane, he will surrender. Siesta initially gives a flippant answer: "You hijacked the plane because you were bored to tears." Bat laughs and concedes defeat, calling it a draw out of respect for her fearlessness. However, Siesta then reveals she knows the deeper truth. Bat was ordered by SPES to hijack the plane and die in the ensuing crash. He went along with it initially but, at the critical moment, feared death. To save himself without appearing to betray SPES outright, he staged the "detective game" as a pretext for surrender. He wanted to be taken into Japanese police custody, where he believed SPES could not reach him. Siesta deduces all of this not just from Bat's behavior but from her prior knowledge of SPES and his specific circumstances.

Key Dialogue:

"It's likely that you were ordered to hijack this plane. That party ordered you to die in the plane crash—am I wrong?"
"But you're scared of dying, and you don't want to die. So you used us to give yourself a reason not to die."
"I know everything, from the fact that you boarded this plane, planned to hijack it, and even about the people who ordered you."

SCD Categories:

* Deduction & Reasoning: She sees through the surface-level game to the underlying psychological conflict (fear of death vs. loyalty to SPES) and organizational dynamics (SPES punishes betrayal with suicide missions).
* Knowledge & Expertise: She possesses detailed intelligence on SPES, including their methods of punishment and individual operatives like Bat. She understands the Latin meaning of SPES (hope) and their goal of spreading aid (which is actually destruction).
* Philosophy & Methodology: Her statement "A first-rate detective will solve an incident before it happens" underscores her proactive approach to investigation.

FEAT 4: Combat Evasion While Protecting a Non-Combatant and Managing Injury.

When Bat's true nature as a half-Android is revealed and his feeler attacks, Siesta's first action is to shove Kimizuka out of the way, taking a wound to her own shoulder. She then grabs Kimizuka and physically leaps around the aircraft cabin, dodging the feeler's strikes while carrying him. This requires exceptional spatial awareness in a confined, chaotic environment, as well as the physical strength and agility to move two bodies through the air while tracking an unpredictable, snake-like appendage. She maintains this for an extended period, all while bleeding.

Key Dialogue:

"Assistant, get down."

Siesta hugged me and jumped up high to avoid the enemy's attack.

SCD Categories:

* Combat & Physical Capabilities: Enhanced agility, reflexes, and endurance. She fights effectively in a three-dimensional space (the narrow aisle of a plane) while burdened by an injured shoulder and a passenger.
* Tactical Awareness: She prioritizes protecting the asset (Kimizuka) even at personal cost, recognizing his potential value or simply adhering to her detective creed of protecting others.

FEAT 5: Creation and Deployment of the "Red Bullet" Blood Contract.

After Kimizuka throws the suitcase and Siesta catches the musket, she shoots Bat's feeler. But this is no ordinary bullet. Siesta explains that the bullet was "made from my blood" and that anyone who touches her blood "cannot disobey the master." This is a permanent, biological/paranormal binding effect. The feeler will never be able to attack her again. This feat demonstrates that Siesta possesses unique properties (her blood acts as a weaponized binding agent) and that she prepared this specific countermeasure in advance, anticipating she would face an Android whose power was tied to a specific body part (the ear/feeler). The effect persists indefinitely; four years later, Bat's feeler cannot harm Natsunagi because Natsunagi carries Siesta's heart, which still bears the "master" signature.

Key Dialogue:

"I just shot you with the 《red bullet》 that was made from my 《blood》, you know? Anyone who touched this blood cannot disobey the master—your feeler can never attack me again.”

SCD Categories:

* Strategic Planning: Pre-emptive creation of a specialized counter-weapon tailored to Android abilities. She knew she would face Bat and prepared exactly the tool needed to neutralize his primary threat.
* Knowledge & Expertise: She understands her own unique biology and how to weaponize it. She also understands the nature of Android "cores" well enough to create a binding effect specific to a body part.
* Posthumous Influence: The blood contract remains active long after her death, protecting Natsunagi and confirming the heart's origin. This is a deliberate or serendipitous long-term contingency.

FEAT 6: Strategic Non-Lethal Resolution and Asset Preservation.

After disabling Bat's feeler, Siesta does not kill him. Instead, she shoves the musket into his mouth, says "Bam!" to simulate an execution, and declares him dead. She then explains her reasoning: by faking his death, SPES will believe Bat perished in the hijacking attempt, and they will not hunt him down as a traitor. Simultaneously, she will have him taken into custody by "reliable people" (Fuubi Kase who's secretly a fellow turner and the Japanese police), where he will be protected and, importantly, available as a potential source of intelligence or a future asset. This decision proves crucial four years later when Bat, still imprisoned, identifies Siesta's heartbeat in Natsunagi, kickstarting the main plot.

Key Dialogue:

"Now you will not be targeted by your colleagues. After all, you are now dead."
"And you'll be hidden by the Japanese police. Don't worry, there are reliable people."

SCD Categories:

* Strategic Planning: She chooses to preserve a defeated enemy as a long-term asset rather than eliminate a short-term threat. This requires thinking beyond the immediate battle to future operational needs.
* Social Influence & Manipulation: She as a Turner, has the ability to contact others acting within law enforcement (Fuubi Kase in this situation) who are willing to operate outside standard procedure to imprison a non-human entity secretly.
* Foresight & Prediction: While she may not have predicted the exact chain of events (she actually did but it wasn't revealed yet in the series), preserving Bat aligns with her pattern of leaving "seeds" that can be harvested later. Bat's ability to identify heartbeats becomes the key to confirming Natsunagi's connection to Siesta.

FEAT 1: Immediate Poison Detection and Preemptive Counter-Action.

Upon arrival, the owner apologizes that the air conditioning is broken and offers cold drinks. Siesta accepts politely but, as Kimizuka reaches for his glass, she stomps on his foot—hard—to stop him. When he complains, she stomps on him again, causing him to trip and spill the entire tray of drinks. While pretending to help the owner clean his stained clothes with her handkerchief, she picks his pocket and steals a small vial—the antidote to the poison he had laced the drinks with. Later, in Mary's room, the owner releases a paralytic poison gas, expecting to immobilize Siesta and Kimizuka. Siesta, having already taken the stolen antidote, is unaffected. Kimizuka and the owner collapse. Siesta then restrains the owner and opens a window to ventilate the room, saving Mary from further poisoning (Mary wore a respirator to avoid the gas).

Key Dialogue:

"I was really shocked when you actually planned to drink what was clearly a spiked beverage."
"So back then...when you wiped his clothes with your handkerchief, you already stole that antidote."

SCD Categories:

* Deduction & Reasoning: She identified the threat before any overt evidence—perhaps from the owner's excessive hospitality, the lack of air conditioning (which kept windows shut to contain gas), or subtle behavioral cues.
* Sensory & Perception: She likely noticed micro-expressions or inconsistencies in the owner's story. The emphasis on "expanding horizons" and training sight/hearing from earlier suggests she constantly scans for anomalies.
* Strategic Planning & Deception: She used a staged accident (the tripping) as cover to steal the antidote, ensuring she could neutralize the poison threat without alerting the culprit. This is classic misdirection.

FEAT 2: Interpreting Non-Verbal Communication from a Comatose Victim.

After subduing the owner, Siesta explains that she was certain of his guilt when she saw Mary's eyes. Though Mary was unresponsive, she was blinking furiously in a rhythmic pattern. Siesta recognized this as deliberate communication—an attempt by Mary to expose her stepfather's crimes. The owner, driven mad by grief, had been poisoning visitors to add "friends" for his daughter, never realizing Mary was trying to signal for help. Siesta's ability to distinguish voluntary blinking (a conscious attempt at Morse-like code) from involuntary spasms demonstrates exceptional observational acuity and an understanding of locked-in syndrome behaviors.

Key Dialogue:

"I was convinced when I saw Mary's eyes...she was blinking away furiously...there was definitely some will in there. I supposed she wanted to expose the mistake of her stepfather.”

SCD Categories:

* Deduction & Reasoning: She correctly interpreted a subtle, non-standard form of communication that everyone else dismissed as random movement. This is akin to breaking a code in real-time.
* Sensory & Perception: She paid close attention to the victim's eyes, the only part of her body capable of movement, and recognized the pattern.
* Empathy & Psychological Insight: She understood that Mary would want to expose the truth, attributing agency to a person others saw as a vegetable.

FEAT 3: Deliberate Training of Kimizuka's Observational Skills.

After the case, as Siesta carries the partially paralyzed Kimizuka on her back, she scolds him for his carelessness and reiterates the lesson she had given earlier: "Did you forget what I said on our way to that western house? The importance of expanding our horizons, training our sight and hearing." She emphasizes being sensitive to human stares and other subtle cues. This is not idle chatter; it is a deliberate training regimen. Siesta is molding Kimizuka into a more effective assistant by instilling her core investigative principles. Two years later, when investigating Yui Saikawa, Kimizuka explicitly recalls this lesson and uses it to deduce the secret of her Left Eye.

Key Dialogue:

"Did you forget what I said on our way to that western house? The importance of expanding our horizons, training our sight and hearing. For example, you should be more sensitive to human stares.”

SCD Categories:

* Philosophy & Methodology: She articulates a core tenet of her detective work: sensory training is paramount. This provides insight into how she operates.
* Foresight & Prediction: She is intentionally developing Kimizuka's skills for future challenges, knowing he will need them when she is not around to protect him.
* Social Influence & Mentorship: She is actively shaping Kimizuka's growth as an operative, ensuring her methodology survives through him.

PART 3: SIESTA'S DEATH — ONE YEAR AGO
Narrative Context: Siesta, Kimizuka, and Charlotte are on a small boat heading to a SPES-controlled island to rescue an imprisoned ally. Siesta briefs them that they must work in pairs due to their complementary weaknesses: Kimizuka is smart but physically useless; Charlotte is a combat monster but intellectually lacking. Siesta will operate alone. She tells them to "get along well—now, and forever". During the mission, Siesta faces the enemy alone and is killed. Her body is never recovered.

FEAT 1: Deliberate Self-Sacrifice with Full Foreknowledge (in order to complete her Heart transfer plan “HTP”)

Charlotte later reflects, "Maybe Ma'am knew that she would die on that day.". Siesta's behavior before the mission supports this. She deliberately separated from Kimizuka and Charlotte, sending them on a safer task while she confronted the main threat. She gave them a final instruction that sounded like a goodbye. During her temporary return on the cruise ship, she admits, "I didn't actually plan on journeying with you for three years... If I had formed intimate relationships with humans, I would have lingering regrets." . She knew the mission was likely suicidal, but she accepted it as necessary. Her death was not a failure; it was a calculated step in a larger plan to position her allies and legacies for the final confrontation with SPES.

Key Dialogue:

"So you two, get along well—now, and forever."
"Maybe Ma'am knew that she would die on that day.”

SCD Categories:

* Foresight & Prediction: She accurately assessed the threat level and the probability of her own survival. She did not walk in blind; she walked in knowing the cost while calculating it into her LTP.
* Strategic Planning: Her death was not an end but a transition. By removing herself from the equation, she completes her 6 year long Heart transfer plan and forced Kimizuka and Charlotte to grow independently and set the stage for the activation of her posthumous legacies.
* Philosophy & Methodology: She consistently prioritized the mission and the protection of others over her own life, embodying her detective creed: "protect the interests of the requestor before anyone else, no matter what.”

FEAT 2: Establishment of a Posthumous Legacy Network.

Before her death, Siesta planted multiple "legacies" (physical objects, data caches, or instructions) in various locations around the world. These were designed to be found and used by her allies to combat SPES after she was gone. One such legacy was hidden on the luxury cruise ship owned by the Saikawa family. Charlotte, working with intelligence agencies, spent a year decoding Siesta's final communications to locate this specific seed. The existence of this network proves that Siesta had been preparing for her own death for some time, ensuring her war against SPES would continue without her direct involvement.

Key Dialogue:

"It's the legacy Ma'am left before she died, so that 《SPES》 could be defeated...it's still somewhere in this world. One of them is on this ship.”

SCD Categories:

* Strategic Planning: This is the ultimate long-term strategy: creating an operational infrastructure that activates posthumously. She set events in motion that would unfold long after her death.
* Foresight & Prediction: She anticipated that her allies would need guidance and resources after she was gone, and she provided them. The cruise ship legacy specifically draws all four key players (Kimizuka, Natsunagi, Charlotte, Saikawa) to the same location.
* Posthumous Influence: Her planning directly causes the events of the cruise ship arc (she was the one who leaked the info to SPES in the first place), uniting the four-person team she designated as her "final hope.”

PART 4: DISCOVERY OF SIESTA'S HEART IN NATSUNAGI
Narrative Context: One year after Siesta's death, Nagisa Natsunagi, a high school girl who received a heart transplant one year ago, experiences a "memory transfer" phenomenon. She feels an inexplicable, overwhelming urge to find a person she does not know—person "X." She tracks down Kimizuka, believing him to be a famous detective, and asks for his help identifying X. Through a visit to the imprisoned Bat, it is confirmed: the heart beating in Natsunagi's chest belonged to Siesta. Bat's feeler, still bound by Siesta's blood contract, cannot attack Natsunagi's heart.

FEAT 1: Heart Memory Transfer and Compulsion to Reunite with Kimizuka.

The heart transplant recipient, Natsunagi, inherits not just an organ but a fragment of Siesta's consciousness and will. She feels an irresistible need to find "X," which turns out to be Kimizuka. When they first meet, Natsunagi acts out of character—grabbing Kimizuka, inserting her finger in his mouth, pressing his face to her chest—which she later realizes was the heart's influence. Siesta's heart wanted to touch Kimizuka, to be near him, and to get his attention. This demonstrates that Siesta's emotional attachment to Kimizuka was so profound it imprinted on the physical organ and transferred to a new host. The heart's "memory" actively sought reunion.

Key Dialogue:

"This is the desire of this heart! I, this heart, has been looking for you all this while Kimizuka Kimihiko! All for the sake of meeting you again...that's all!"
"Memory transfer. That was what you said, Kimizuka. Actually, that was not me. The original owner of this heart definitely forced me to do that."

SCD Categories:

* Posthumous Influence: Siesta's will literally outlived her body, compelling a stranger to seek out her former partner. This is the most visceral form of posthumous influence in the narrative.
* Emotional Depth & Connection: While not an "intelligence" feat per se, it reveals that Siesta's bond with Kimizuka was deeper than she ever admitted, contradicting her stated philosophy of avoiding fetters. This makes her later confession during the cruise battle more impactful.

PART 5: THE CRUISE SHIP BATTLE — SIESTA'S TEMPORARY RETURN
Narrative Context: SPES agent Chameleon has abducted Natsunagi and threatens to sink the cruise ship. Kimizuka confronts Chameleon alone on the burning deck. He is losing badly. Natsunagi, unconscious and bound, suddenly drops into the casino below, lands on her feet, shoots Chameleon's tongue, and greets Kimizuka with "It's been a while." She speaks with Siesta's cadence, mannerisms, and knowledge. Siesta has temporarily taken control of Natsunagi's body.

FEAT 1: Conscious Manifestation and Bodily Control from the Heart.

Siesta's consciousness, dormant within the heart, awakens and overrides Natsunagi's personality. This is not a mere memory; it is the full, active mind of Siesta, capable of combat, deduction, and conversation. She wields Natsunagi's body with the same skill she had in life, firing a gun with precision and moving with agility. This manifestation is temporary and requires Natsunagi's consent (as revealed in the "Girl's Dialogue" section). Siesta explains that Natsunagi asked her to save Kimizuka. This feat proves that Siesta's "death" was not absolute; a core part of her identity survived within the transplanted heart and could be activated under extreme duress.

Key Dialogue:

"It's been a while... Yeah, you're not napping again?—Siesta"
"Actually, I was asked by her. She hoped that I'd save you—so she asked.”

SCD Categories:

* Posthumous Influence: The most direct form of posthumous action. Siesta's consciousness returns to the physical world and engages in combat.
* Combat & Physical Capabilities: She immediately demonstrates proficiency with a firearm and tactical movement, despite being in an unfamiliar body.
* Social Influence & Reciprocity: She was moved to act by Natsunagi's earnest plea, showing that even the "rational" detective can be swayed by genuine emotional connection.

FEAT 2: Seamless Combat Coordination with Kimizuka After One Year.

Despite a year of separation and the fact that she is inhabiting a different body, Siesta and Kimizuka fall into their old combat rhythm instantly. They flank Chameleon, swap positions to optimize Siesta's immunity to the tongue (due to the blood contract), and fire in unison. They communicate with minimal words, relying on years of shared experience. Siesta dodges Chameleon's invisible attacks by using her trained senses and Kimizuka's verbal cues. The scene is a direct parallel to their first battle on the plane, emphasizing that their partnership is deeply ingrained.

Key Dialogue:

"We flanked Chameleon from both sides."
"Assistant, hang on." [She grabs him and leaps, just like four years ago.]

SCD Categories:

SIESTA'S FEATS FROM VOLUME 7

This volume is set one year after the "Great Disaster." Siesta has opened the Shirogane Detective Agency with Kimizuka as her assistant and Nagisa as a detective. VOLUME 9

Natsunagi, faced with the moral dilemma of whether to reveal the painful truth about Marley's past (that she is a vampire and Scarlet's childhood friend), chooses to honor Marley's request as a client and pursue the truth. ONE YEAR AGO — SIESTA'S ABDUCTION AND THE BATTLE AT NIGHTLESS CASTLE
