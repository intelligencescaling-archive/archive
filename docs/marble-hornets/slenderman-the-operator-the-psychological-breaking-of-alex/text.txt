Slenderman (The Operator) psychological breaking of Alex Kralie

***To be clear, the operator and slenderman are not the same characters, but they’re so tied together that it hard to bring The Operator up without “slenderman.” This is because of the impact Marble Hornets had on Slenderman as a character.***

***The Operator is the main antagonist of influential webseries, Marble Hornets; which in 2006, Alex Kralie was shooting a student film project entitled the same name as the webseries, which gets interrupted by The Operator as he torments the group, and especially Alex Kralie, who becomes obsessed with it.***

***Before going forward, this analysis might contained spoilers for the story of Marble Hornets (which I recommend):***

***what was broken down by The Operator:***

 Due to Alex’s hard work he put into Marble hornets, this piece of his creation that he worked on so hard since high school, and was so excited to see finally it all happening after many years of work; when the operator came in, his sudden presence, strange appearance, and strange effects it had cause great damage. Alex Becomes obsessed with it, The Operator becoming the new “Marbles Hornets” for Alex, it overriding the project he written for years so he can record The Operator and fight back. The gaps in memory which the operators causes made him want to record as much as he can of this creature that came in and ruin his life all of sudden.

After observing it, Alex realised that it causes some disease which spreads (The Operator sickness) and was going through the rest of his crew members, his assumption from this being that The Operator feeds off his presence being known, spreading itself; so to prevent that, Alex decided that he was going to eliminate the other cast members, saving them and isolating The Operator influence from spreading to just himself, which Alex believed was attached to him, protecting him some weird way. In reality, Alex had already fallen into the trap of The Operator.

To also add, the reason why he believed he had such a connection with The Operator and to add more to his reason was because of his filming of The Operator, maybe attracting it to him. Further reasons for why The Operator chose Alex is because of his rather high way of thinking of himself, his logic never being wrong. The Operator feeding into this side of him, the inferences which Alex makes not actually being wrong, just wrong in a way that benefits The Operator and convince Alex.

When Alex began eliminating his cast members, he failed when some of them, succeed with others, although he believed that everyone was gone beside Jay, who he was gonna kill before deciding not too. One of Alex’s victims, actually two, end up surviving his onslaught, The Operator saving them; Alex assuming their death because The Operator usually takes them after Alex is done with them, it doing with his previous victims; so when it took these two, Alex assumed he succeeded, and because of his high thinking of himself, he didn’t think much about his killing method fatalities.

After the events in 2006, Alex ends up ditching town, moving to another College where his girlfriend was at, the memory wipe taking place on Alex, forgetting about The Operator for years until one day when his girlfriend found the old camera which he used to record back in 2006, containing a videotape in it which was hard to make out. What was in this video tape? My guess was that it contained a video of The Operator, logically considering the Operator shows up right after. But why? Well, I think because the conclusions Alex came up about The Operator wasn’t fully wrong, The Operator does get starved when it doesn’t have prey, which ties into the fact that he needs awareness of his presence. This is proven by the mini series: ***Rosswood*** where the operator was a lot more aggressive in it because of the fact that he hadn’t had a victim in a long time.

To build on this, The Operator seems to be restricted to Rosswood park when he doesn’t have anyone he’s attached too, so by having this tape in the camera, it attached him to someone, making him be able to leave Rosswood, and get to Alex Kralie. But why did the Operator stave himself? Well, my assumption is because the longer he gets them affected, the better the meal is for him. The Operator being restricted to Rosswood is likely another inference Alex made, which is why he ditched town

After Alex’s strategy had failed to get rid of The Operator, Alex emotions really began to flourish here, feeling immense guilt for what he’d done before, and gaining a sense of suicidal thoughts in him, but around this time, Jay starts up his investigation, which is the main Marble Hornets channel, which Alex finds, leeching him onto why The Operator could had survived… because his killing fail, specifically with a victim named Tim Wright, who still lived in the area where The Operator is at. He learns that the Operator isn’t as trustworthy as he thought, but his thought process likely went down a line that criticise his past failure, evident by how he said: “I should had went harder!” About said Crew Members killing. The Operator pushes him to focus on his own mistakes rather than on it.

This new revelation of information which Alex figured out gives Alex a new goal, a redux version of his old goal of killing everyone. Alex is convinced that he failed because of some mistakes he made, and he’ll pay for these said mistakes by succeeding this time. Now that The Operator victims are more prepared for being better meals for him, and Alex is now out to hunt them down for The Operator to eat Unknownly to Alex, before giving himself away to him.

So where does Alex Kralie ends up mentally? He’s suicidal, and he has a goal in mind, which is killing everyone related to The Operator, then himself, “officially killing it off” when in reality, it’s actually giving him a rather nice meal. There’s some factors about this, such as how Alex plan to kill himself would honestly work against the Operator, so it isn’t masterfully done or anything, but overall, it still was a good enough meal for The Operator.  Also, to let it be clear, the miniseries: Rosswood, did made it out that Alex has a clear fascination with the supernatural aspects of things for filming reason, which gives more of a reason of why he was chosen.

***Overall, The Operator drove Alex down into suicidal, aligned obedient thoughts for him to be the perfect victim of his, feeding it a good meal.***
