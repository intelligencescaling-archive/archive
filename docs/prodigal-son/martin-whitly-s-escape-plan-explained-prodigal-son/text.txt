Martin Whitly escape plan explanation

In Clairemont Psychiatric Facility, home of renowned serial killer Martin "The Surgeon" Whitley, the place is sectioned off into areas requiring varying colors of key card to gain access, namely red, blue and green Were a prisoner to escape, they would require one of all three key card.

–First off, Martin allies himself with another inmate vying for escape, called Friar Pete. With Friar Pete's aid, Martin learns of another inmate who has both a green and a blue key card. In his own time, Martin sees that the main guard keeping check over him has a red card on him at all times. Martin then talks to the inmate with the two cards, informing him of Martin's own proximity to a red card, and arranges a possible agreement, wherein the other man lends him a sharp object to shank the guard in exchange for cooperation between the two. When this comes, Martin yells alerting nearby guards that the other inmate is trying to shank him, and in the commotion steals the two key cards, eliminating the need other inmate, what's more if he tries to claim Martin stole from him he would have to admit he'd stolen the key cards himself. (Bluffing, Logical Manipulation, Strategy.)

–Martin begins working in the facility's infirmary, though initially only as a cleaner. Over time he endears himself to the main doctor, Vivian Capshaw, seeing as he himself is a surgeon of renown who's works and contributions to medical knowledge she herself studied. He uses his own genuine care for his family to blindside her understanding of the psychopaths in the facility as unfeeling by using his own genuine care to enhance his acting skills in this situation, helping to convince her that despite all he's done he does still have a heart, and over time, through supporting her in her work and self image and offering advice, he even manages to seduce her into an affair with him. He then has Friar Pete sneak off into the supply room one night when Vivian will encounter him, thinking he's trying to escape but, under Martin's orders, Friar Pete blackmails her using the knowledge of her affair with Martin. This serves multiple purposes, as it plants the seed of Friar Pete as an antagonistic force which will be useful later, as well as forcing Martin and Vivian closer together as they now being tested together, for if this gets out it would be terrible for them both, so now Vivian basically has to trust Martin as she has little alternative. What's more, it further destabilizes Vivian's mentality after she was shaken up simply from the prospect of an affair with a serial killer. (Emotional Facilitation, Acting Skills, Social Skills, Seduction, Emotional Manipulation, Indirect Manipulation, Psychological Manipulation, Strategy and Planning.)

–Finally, Martin had had Friar Pete sneak rat poison into the cafeteria food. This poisoned a large number of both inmates and guards, making the getting out part simpler by thinning the heard of security. What's more, the pileup of patients stretches Vivian's resources, and what she's capable of. With her meeting Friar Pete the other day in the supply room, and now thinking of him as antagonistic, she makes the simple abduction that Friar Pete had poisoned the food, further fueling her us vs them mentality in regards to Martin as her only remaining ally and, with how much there needs to be done and how much he's done to trust her, she now has no choice but to release his handcuffs and let him help. She then needs more supplies, so hands him her red key card to go to the supply room, and with it Martin locks her in the infirmary, and meets up with Friar Pete and some other guy working with them to finally escape. (Indirect Manipulation, Logical Manipulation, Setting Traps, Strategy and Planning.)
