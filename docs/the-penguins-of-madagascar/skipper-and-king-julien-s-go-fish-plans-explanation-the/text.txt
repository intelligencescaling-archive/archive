Skipper and King Julien's Go Fish plans explanation (The Penguins of Madagascar)

Context: the Central Park Zoo has quit giving fish to the fish eating animals, instead giving shitty bland fish cakes. Skipper hates this of course. King Julien however, loves this as he hates fish stink.

Skipper thus creates a plan to steal fish from a shipment to a restaurant. Julien plans around it.

Now, at the end of the episode it is revealed that Skipper was actually Julien in disguise, and that likewise, Julien was Skipper in disguise all along. I'll be real here, I have no idea how they managed to just switch places like this with both of them just letting the other do so. For the sake of simplicity (and Julien agenda) I'm going to say that everything from 5:43 in the episode onwards is after they switched. Insane Disguise and Acting Skills feat from them both.

**Skipper's Plan:**

–First they stole several crates' worth of the fish cakes.

–Skipper and the lemurs replace the crates of fish being shipped the night they arrive at the pier with some of their fake crates.

–Then, they hid in the penguins' car and waited for Julien and the Penguins to return to the zoo with their haul, and switch the crates as soon as they park, with the fake fish crates placed nearby where the Penguins enter the zoo.

Basically, this is done to both attain the real fish using the lemurs while also diverting Julien with his own fake heist, being enforced by Skipper's fake attempt to steal the crates. (Fabrication, Misdirection, Concealment, Bluffing, Indirect Manipulation, Logical Manipulation, Anticipation, Trap Setting, Tactics, Preparation, Overall Strategy and Overall Planning.)

**Julien's Plan:**

–Julien anticipates that Skipper will attempt to switch the real crates with crates of fake fish cakes, so he fills up several crates' worth as well in preparation

–For the heist itself, he recruits a flamingo from the zoo for areal surveillance

–With the surveillance's cue, Kowalski will access a stoplight from the truck's route and force a red light

–Private and Rico will then drill a hole into the truck to steal the fish

The plan goes wrong, because dumbfuck Kowalski messed up with the lights, letting the truck go and accidentally making Rico and Private go into a truck full of angry dogs. Julien adapts and manages to extract them both, then delivers them a speech in the vein of what Skipper would about their nature as fish eaters to motivate them. This gives them the motivation they needed to continue on with the mission and improvise a way to success. (Improvisation, Social Skills, Charisma, Emotional Facilitation.)

–Julien anticipates that Skipper will switch the crates once they return to the zoo, so he switches the real fish crates in the car with the fake fish crates, instead placing the real crates in the pretzel cart often used by the Penguins, all before returning to the zoo.

–All of this however, is offset when, after both Julien and Skipper unmask themselves, it is revealed that they both only had fake fish all along. It was the flamingo that stole all of the real fish.

This was Julien's true goal all along. This was why he recruited the flamingo, knowing that he was greedy enough that, knowing there was a fish heist on, he would double cross the penguins to steal the lot. Everything else he had done was in order to distract the Penguins, making them believe they had truly fought a hard battle obtaining the fish as well as misleading the lemurs with multiple diversions. However, it was all along a massive diversion for them all for the fish to go to the flamingo, who is far away enough from the lemurs to not offend Julien's nose like the penguins. (Emotional Understanding, Fabrication, Misdirection, Concealment, Bluffing, Tactics, Trap Setting, Anticipation, Prediction, Preparation, Overall Strategy and Overall Planning.)

**Final Chronology:**
–Skipper and Julien switch places

–Julien and the Penguins recruit the flamingo

–Both groups collect crates of fish cakes

–Skipper and the lemurs swap the real crates the night they're shipped into New York for the fake crates

–Julien and the Penguins have their heist

–Julien and the Penguins switch the crates they got from the shipment with their fake crates

–Julien and the Penguins return to the zoo and Skipper and the Lemurs switch the crates the Penguins had with more fake ones

Somewhere along the way, the flamingo stole the real crates from Skipper and the Lemurs.
