Bull's feats during the first arc of Bakuchi Gui

For some context, the main character Bull is searching for his missing brother and goes to visit the place where his brother disappeared, a sports bar named "ZES". The sports bar is considered an entrance to "The Swamp", a massive illegal gambling den considered to be an urban legend. The bar is filled with violent people who aren't afraid to kill so Bull can't just kidnap the boss of the bar, Fukutomo, and interrogate him or anything like that. He has to win against him in a game in order to get the information and Fukutomo loves baseball betting.

When exiting the bar, Bull sees a girl call the cops but when they arrive they enter the bar and then leave without arresting anyone, so Bull realizes he can't rely on the cops at all. The girl who called the cops runs away and Bull notes she is quite virtuous.

Bull continues to come to the bar over a period of three days and during this period he sees that each day a Dracula-themed cylinder with many holes is always behind the counter but never in the same spot, meaning it was used every day, he then finds and buys this exact same Dracula Game toy in a shop and dismantles it, learning the internals perfectly. He also notices how Fukutomo looks at the vulgar things happening in the bar like a king, however he notices something that doesn't line up with this kingly aura, a cheap bento box. So Bull goes to that bento shop, shows the girl working there a picture of Fukutomo and asks if he visits often which the girl confirms. Bull then lies about Fukutomo's birthday coming soon and him wanting to make a game where he gets to choose his lucky prize. Bull tells the girl to tell Fukutomo that his lucky color is red.

Bull finally comes to the bar to play a game against Fukutomo. Fukutomo agrees to play a game with Bull.

Bull prepares a hidden cheat to use in the baseball betting game, the rules of which need you to guess whether the hitter hits or misses and you win if you guess correctly 5 times in a row. He notices that the game is being shown in a radio broadcast so before coming here he disguised himself as a mechanic that wants to fix the antenna and added a homemade delay device to the antenna cable. So the game shown in the bar is 4 seconds behind the actual game, which Bull can hear through a hidden bone-conduction speaker in his ring which is linked to a radio tuner in his pocket, so that he can hear the game through the speaker and be 4 seconds ahead, allowing him to win the baseball betting game.

However Fukutomo doesn't want to play that game, claiming that he's tired of baseball. This is because Bull has been coming to the bar for three days and agreed to the conditions of the game way too quickly, causing Fukutomo to suspect that Bull may be cheating in some way and decides to play the Dracula game. A game where they stick crosses into a Dracula-themed cylinder with many holes, whoever puts a cross into the wrong hole gets shocked and loses. For every cross inserted into the holes, a nail gets put into a nail gun and whoever wins shoots the nail gun at the loser. The one who goes first is decided by Fukutomo flipping a coin and on each turn they have 30 seconds to insert a cross or they lose

However Bull had prepared for this earlier when he saw the Dracula Game being used every day, as mentioned before, so he had a plan to win this game too. However Fukutomo isn't actually Bull's opponent in this Dracula Game, instead it is the girl that called the cops earlier, who wants info on the Swamp and is willing to play the Dracula Game in order to get it. Fukutomo flips a coin and the girl goes first. Putting in a cross successfully

The structure of the cylinder is simple, there is a switch behind one of the holes which, if hit, lights up the eyes of the Dracula head and causes a shock. The Dracula head can be rotated which moves the switch too. So since the ends of the crosses are deeper inside than the switch, the first cross serves as a sort of barrier, meaning the switch will get stuck at the place where the cross was inserted if it's being rotated and collides. So by secretly rotating the Dracula Head and by extension the switch in a counter clockwise direction, Bull knows for certain that the hole immediately right to the previously inserted cross is safe, so he puts in a cross successfully.

The girl inserts a cross into a different hole, which is left of the first 2 crosses. Bull's strategy of secretly rotating it clockwise in order to eventually find a barrier and locate the switch can still work. However the only problem with this plan is that Bull cannot bring himself shoot this girl with nails. Bull inserts a cross into a hole right next to his previous one.

The girl tries to make a deal, if she wins she will look for Bull's brother and if Bull wins he will give her info on the swamp. She inserts a cross and doesn't trigger a shock. Bull rotates the Dracula Head and it hits a barrier, so now Bull knows the location of the trigger hole.

Bull realizes that Fukutomo probably foresaw that Bull might have some sort of cheat even for the Dracula Game and thus chose this girl as an opponent in order to test Bull's resolve. However Bull cannot bring himself to win, so he asks the girl to aim for his thigh and inserts a cross into the trigger hole. At this point there are 6 nails in the nail gun. Bull asks for some whiskey and chocolate to dull the pain, it is important to note that the chocolates are all different colors. Bull drinks the whiskey and eats some of the chocolates.

The girl, who is named Rin, then shoots the nails into Bull's inner thigh. Fukutomo turns to leave but Bull stops him, claiming how he will play any game in order to get information on his brother. He then takes 7 chocolates, one of each color, and proposes a game to Fukutomo. The game is simple, Bull writes down a prediction of which color Fukutomo will pick on a piece of paper and then Fukutomo picks a color. If Bull guesses correctly, he wins. Bull provokes Fukutomo who agrees to play, noting how Bull is getting desperate and that his chance of winning is only 14%.

Fukutomo chooses the color red, which is what Bull wrote down. This was done through Bull's previous visit to the bento shop and the girl's message about Fukutomo's lucky color being red influencing him to pick that color of chocolate, ultimately making him lose the game.

So in summary, Bull prepared a cheat for the baseball game, anticipated that he might have to play the Dracula Game and prepared in advance by studying it's internal mechanisms clearly. But there is a small chance to still lose by inserting a cross into the trigger hole on your first turn, so Bull sets up another game involving the bar's chocolate and secretly manipulates Fukutomo into choosing the color red. He had prepared a way to win each of these 3 games.
