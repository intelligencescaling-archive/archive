Baku Madarame(manga + spin-off) vs Akagi Shigeru(manga + ten) in EI

Who do you think will win in a showdown between Baku Madarame and Akagi Shigeru in EI? As already stated in the title, Baku is used from the manga + spin-off, and Akagi is from the manga + ten.

A standard set of categories is used: EU (emotional understanding), EP (emotional perception), EF (emotional facilitation), ER (emotional realisation), EM (emotional management) and EE (emotional engagement) + empathy

Please share your opinion and, if possible, explain the distribution of categories.

My opinion is:

EU - Baku Madarame>=(This is really close, Akagi has a very good feat with reading Washizu's past and personality during the Washizu Mahjong arc. But I gave this rating to Baku because of his feat in the spin-off, he never met the players that Judge Yaku told him about, but he understood them perfectly. Madarame knew exactly their emotions, body movements and actions in the game)

EP - Baku Madarame(I don't know why people say that Akagi has insane EP, because he doesn't. His feats in the Washizu or Ishikawa arcs are EU! Baku takes it because of reading Suteguma Satoru's emotions, who was described as "unreadable", although I think this statement is overrated, and also because of the feat in the Hanged Man arc. But they are both victims of Patrick Jane in EP anyway)

EF - Akagi>=(This is very debatable. Akagi has an incredible feat in Ten, when he literally convinces Takashi that gambling is the main goal of his life, which makes Takashi obsessed with gambling. He also had a good feat during mahjong against Washizu, Akagi quickly makes Yasuoko a good player who makes bold and thoughtful moves himself. I heard from Akagi fans that Yasuoko did not know how to play mahjong at all and Akagi was able to teach him in a few minutes, but this is not true. Yasuoko obviously knew how to play mahjong, but it was Akagi who helped him realize his potential. Baku also has very good feats, he constantly pushed Kaji and Marco to develop, they even became obsessed with him and eternally devoted to him, which is why the same Kaji became a very good gambler. But I rate Akagi a little higher in this regard. Both victims of Tokuchi Towa in EF)

ER - Baku>=(Honestly I'm very unsure, speaking of which. Both characters have very good self-awareness, but... I'd give it to Baku because of their more clearly defined goals)

EM - Akagi(Both have insane emotional control, but Akagi literally didn't show any even when he woke up after dying during mahjong with Washizu)

EE - Baku>=(Both are obsessed with gambling and constantly bet their lives. But Baku has more understandable and clear goals, so I'd give it to him)

Empathy - Baku(Akagi doesn't show normal human emotions, I'm not even sure he can fully empathize with other people)

In the end, I think Baku wins very high diff. Those who have seen my comments and posts probably know that I don't rate Akagi as highly as most people, if you disagree with my opinion, then I'm always open to debate and discussion on this topic.
