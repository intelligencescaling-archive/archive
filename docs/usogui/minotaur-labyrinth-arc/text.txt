Minotaur Labyrinth Arc

The key to understanding Labyrinth Arc is to feel it. There’s no need to completely follow the squares Baku goes through.
Rules:
Everyone has a unique path through the labyrinth
I gain 1 MP everytime you open a door. You stop when you open a locked door
When two players meet at a room, they present their MP, the presented MP is subtracted from their total MP. The person who presented the higher MP can be violent towards the person who presented less MP. If both present the same MP, both can be violent.

7) Baku’s Deduction and Evasion of a Trap
Baku using his hidden phone in F3 has now deduced Minowa’s initial starting point and locations.
Amazingly, Amako doesn’t care about Baku almost reaching his goal. Amako should have deduced this by now. Why isn’t Amako worried in any manner?
Curiously Minowa and Amako both haven't passed through C2. Almost like they are deliberately avoiding it. Baku considers the possibility of a trap. It may or may not be existent.
What exactly can the trap be? This is a water storage facility, overloading a room with water would be apparent to the referees and would not achieve much when another door is opened. But what if they can drain the oxygen from a room?
In Fluid Mechanics, As velocity of water increases, pressure decreases. This is a consequence of Conservation of Energy. So if in an adjacent room, water was drained fast enough, rooms direct in connection would lose pressure! A loss in pressure would mean oxygen would be drained.
Using this Baku deduces, the most probable trap IF it exists is the draining of oxygen. (Scientific Knowledge, Abductive reasoning, Foresight EU, FRI, VSI)
So as soon as he reaches C1, he puts a notepad piece, the thick aspect of the book, to block the door from completely closing, allowing air to flow through. He opens C2, the oxygen doesn’t fall down enough for him to suffer any serious ailments, using this he goes to D3 without suffering any problems. (Foresight, Strategy, Trap Evasion, FRI, VSI)
