Baku's blunder in Air poker Round 1 | AP Round 1 debunk

I never understood why people don't talk enough about this.

In AP Round 1 When the round started both Lalo and Baku were on there first Air bios, after paying the ante both were left with 23 Air bios.

After the cards were revealed Lalo made the first bet of 1 air bios. He got to bet first because his number was smaller.

When Lalo made that bet Baku calls then Raises to max betting 4 bios in total, Lalo then calls and raises to the max betting 8 bios, then Baku calls and the betting ends there with the pot of 16 air Bios.

What I do not understand is what was the need for baku's raises?

"He was trying to see if Lalo knew the Law or not"

That doesn't make any sense. Baku knows Lalo's personality enough to tell that Lalo would try to provoke Baku till the very end as Lalo knows Baku won't go above 8 bios bet. In this case the person who ends the bet and leaks the info that he doesn't know anything about the Law is Baku.

"He was trying to get a read on Lalo"

That was the least important thing at that point, what he should have been focusing on was to see the results with the least price. And because Lalo got to bet first he had gotten that opportunity. If it was Baku who had to bet first then he'd obviously be forced to bet 1 bios because he would want to see the results. And in this case if Lalo had raised Baku would be forced to call. But that didn't happen and Baku had the advantage of just calling and ending the betting round with just 4 bios bet by each side.

The only plausible explanation I can think of right now is that Baku got provoked by Lalo which is completely against what Baku's entire character has been established to be.

There was literally no other reason to Raise on Lalo's first bet let alone Raising twice. Baku at that time didn't knew About the law himself. Lalo's 1 BIOS bet could have been just a forced move because he it's plausible he didn't wish to fold.

I know many Usogui fans would try to down play this anti feat and come up with some Explanation that Sako directly told them about in their dreams. but if you actually look at how bad this anti feat is, I don't think it's too much to say that it Lowered Baku's impulse control and Judgement to an average human level and nerfed his deductive Reasoning to even below that. The simple maths of addition and subtraction literally make's this feat impossible to defend.

And AP is filled with such anti feats throughout the game. When you actually bother to look a bit closer you'd see the flaws.

AP nerfed Baku in Usogui the most, and ironically many think the exact opposite.

Baku Fell for such a simple bait and reduced his own options so badly that Kaji had to come and save his ahh in round 2 otherwise he would have been done for.
