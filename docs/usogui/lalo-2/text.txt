 Introduction
The aim of this report is to explore the character of Vincent Lalo as a whole and provide sufficient information and argumentation as to why his “cognitive prowess” is at a level above that of the main cast from Usogui (Baku and Souichi).
To achieve that, the main areas of this study focus mostly on diving deep into his capabilities showcased from his actions and analyzing his performance during the span of the series (mainly against Baku).
 Assumptions and Definitions
Before we can do anything the first issue that needs to be enlightened is how exactly do we measure who is “smarter”. For this assessment we use the term cognitive prowess. Cognitive Prowess refers to exceptional mental abilities and intellectual skills that allow an individual to understand, interpret, and manipulate complex information, solve problems, anticipate and strategize effectively, and influence others using psychological insights. It is a sum of utilizing your brain’s capabilities as efficiently and effectively as possible towards achieving your goals.

Especially in our examples because the gambles happening during Usogui we are about to explore include many elements whose influence on the match cannot be directly and fully controlled by the 2 main fighting sides. Simply said this isn’t chess, where people have full control of their pieces and all mistakes done by a person are his own fault. We can be sure that the win of 1 means his performance was superior and therefore winning becomes a proper factor to compare who is “better” at chess. Therefore, the main factor being reviewed when comparing Lalo later on is performance.
Measuring the performance of characters during games (mainly for when we dive in Baku vs Lalo) will be done by overviewing the following components and observing who showed more efficient results:
Strategic depth: We assess these criteria by the complexity and effectiveness of the strategies employed by each player. Therefore looking for multi-layered strategies that anticipate and counter the opponent’s moves.
Analytical thinking: Analyzing how each player breaks down and understands complex situations. This includes their ability to identify patterns, weaknesses, and opportunities within the game.
Predictive skills: Measures each player’s ability to foresee future moves and game developments based on current information. Successful prediction and preparation indicate strong cognitive prowess.
Problem-solving abilities: We evaluate how each player handles unexpected challenges and complex problems within the game. Look for creativity and efficiency in their solutions.
Psychological Manipulation: With this we consider how effectively each player can influence and manipulate the opponent’s perceptions and decisions. This includes bluffing, feints, and other psychological tactics.
Adaptability and Flexibility: Assessing each player’s ability to adapt their strategies in response to changing game dynamics. Flexibility in thought and action is a key indicator of cognitive prowess.
Emotional Intelligence: definition we use for our situation (the games) is how characters observe each player’s ability to manage their emotions and maintain composure under pressure. Also, we consider their ability to read and influence the opponent’s emotional state.
There are other factors which people can use to distinguish how people perform, but I believe those to be enough and sufficient.
Overall
The second way we will measure characters’ superiority is by output. This means who has done more during their clash to narrow the odds in their favor.  It’s important to know that this is done in the field the characters operate. For this analysis we will stick with the display of maneuvering the surroundings according to your own will. Being able to influence and shape your situation and setting in accordance to your own will.
 For example, when people are asked who is more of a genius between a physics scientist or a businessman, they will often point at the first. This whole question is wrong to begin with since they both work and compete in different fields. If we still want to compare them (which will be essential when talking about Lalo) it’s best to differentiate them based on their success in their own fields. This means how far they have gotten in their domain of expertise. If the genius from our previous example is to be Einstein and the businessman Andrew Tate, we can than say that the former is more successful, because of his output (all his (changing the perception of the world) findings and inventions) and prestige (world-wide renown and being the most famous scientist in the world of all time) in his profession are far greater than that of Mr. Top G (there are hundreds of people with greater monetary power and influence than him).
Of course, competition size plays a factor. For example, we may say that Messi is a greater genius than Rayna Vallandingham (13 times taekwondo world champion and Black belt) because the amount of people competitively participating in the sport of football is far greater than that of taekwondo.
The 3rd thing of importance is difficulty in the field. Becoming the richest man in the world may be a lot harder than becoming the physically strongest. Although that’s for debate I only put it here to demonstrate what I mean.
This overall measurement will also be essential when talking about Lalo (will be used more when we compare Lalo vs Souichi) because unlike with Baku he hasn’t directly engaged with someone in combat (a game, competition, you call it).

Way of thinking: Here we jump into what I call his extreme godlike rationality. The boss of Ideal is the most rational person in all of Usogui. To convince you that we need to first define what rational means. A rational person is someone who consistently makes decisions based on reason, logic, and factual evidence rather than emotions, impulses, or biases. This person systematically evaluates information, weighs the pros and cons, considers potential outcomes, and chooses the course of action that maximizes benefits and minimizes risks or costs. Rationality involves clear thinking, critical analysis, and a commitment to objective truth, allowing individuals to navigate complex situations effectively and make sound, informed decisions. Mr. Lalo has no regard for other people’s thoughts on his actions or the way he dos things.

1.Constructing the Sextant
Lalo would have needed to create a sextant capable of accurately measuring angles. The sextant consists of an angular scale and a movable arm (called the index arm) that allows the user to measure the angle between two objects. The key component is a mirror system that reflects the image of the celestial object onto the horizon, allowing the observer to align them perfectly and measure the angle.
2. Measuring the Altitude of the Sun
Lalo would align the sextant to measure the altitude of the Sun at a specific time of day. The altitude hhh is the angle between the horizon and the Sun. Mathematical Calculation: Suppose the sextant reads an angle hhh degrees above the horizon. This angle is the altitude.
3. Recording the Time
To calculate the coordinates, precise timing is crucial. Lalo would record the exact time (usually in Universal Time, UT) when he measured the Sun’s altitude.
4. Calculating the Declination of the Sun
Declination δ\deltaδ is the Sun's angular distance north or south of the celestial equator. The declination changes daily and can be looked up in an astronomical almanac. However, since Lalo knows he is on an isolated island, he would have had to estimate this based on the known date and time using a rough approximation or pre-existing knowledge. Mathematical Calculation: If δ\deltaδ is known, he uses it as a reference in further calculations.
5. Using the Formula for Latitude
The latitude ϕ\phiϕ can be calculated using the formula: ϕ=h+δ−90∘\phi = h + \delta - 90^\circϕ=h+δ−90∘ where hhh is the measured altitude of the Sun and δ\deltaδ is the declination.

6. Determining Local Noon (Optional)
Another method Lalo could have potentially used is determining the Sun’s highest point in the sky (local noon) to calculate latitude. At noon local time, the Sun’s altitude is at its peak. Mathematical Calculation: The formula remains the same: ϕ=90∘−(h+δ)\phi = 90^\circ - (h + \delta)ϕ=90∘−(h+δ)
7. Calculating Longitude
Longitude is more challenging to calculate accurately without a precise timekeeping device. However, Lalo could estimate his position by noting the time difference between local noon and Greenwich Mean Time (GMT). The sign (East or West) would depend on whether the local time is ahead or behind GMT.
8. Considering the Precision and Limitations
Since Lalo is using a self-made sextant and estimating time, his calculations might not be perfectly accurate. Still, he would be able to get a fairly good estimate of his location on Protoporos Island. Small errors in angle measurement, time recording, or declination estimation could lead to significant differences in his final coordinates.
