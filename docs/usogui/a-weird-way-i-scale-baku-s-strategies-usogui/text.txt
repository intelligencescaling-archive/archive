Weird Way I scale Baku's strategies

As some of you may recognize, im not the biggest believer in Baku’s leap second feat and more or less consider it one of his weaker showings of schemes which I know is in heavy disagreement with conventional notion. Other than that I also scale other of Baku’s strategies very differently from others.
I think SCD is all about disagreements and discussions so I come here to offer my ranking of all of Baku’s strategies and my reasoning for where I scale them. Before I explain my ranking, no I dont hate Baku as a whole and in fact I scale him very highly.

1. Pandora’s Box

This is his whole plan to use the L-file to extort powerful people of both money and Karamete resources. The trap itself is relatively simple but is insane in both efficiency and effectiveness but most importantly scope. This is the largest system we see Baku operate in (maybe aside from the final vice confrontation) and we see Baku predict the dynamic interaction of Kakerou, Police, Governments, IDEAL, Kurama Group, TV hosts, Threatened Politicians and KY participants.

1. Minotaur Labyrinth

Best gambling strategy for Baku I think. Great MAC with not just Minowa Amako but Kadokura and Nangou intervention as well. Baku had very little information while Amako had Hax literally made for the game. Baku was physically the weakest while he had set traps to get both Amako killed and for him to win the game. Best methodology feat for him imo, so i might be a bit biased.

1. Shell Gamble

The gamble itself is mid af. Nothing notable aside from Baku luring out Sasaoka and feeding him the seed of the L-file. This not only facilitated Baku’s later pandora box trap but Baku had likely formulated his alibi transfer trap as well here. The reason it's not higher is because we don't know how confident Baku was in his predictions. The whole point of Baku’s seed planting style of scheming is that you plant multiple seeds that are likely to fail, so in the story Baku might have planted multiple seeds in his teenage years and the labyrinth one just so happened to bloom. If so that decreases the modality of confidence Baku had assumed in this scheme so it lowers the scale a bit.

1. Tower of Karma

Decently impressive MAC with Kurama Intervention. Also had to pull off multiple deception mechanisms while managing noise of the macro game too (deducing Suteguma and IDEAL’s preparations and accounting for it was necessary to properly strategize against him).

1. Leap Second

Well the most controversial take here. I'm willing to explain it but the two main reasons are the 3ND fallacy and alignment to Baku that was handed to him. Baku does face insane Intellectual adversity, but like he kind of gets wretched by Souichi throughout and only wins because of the LS + memory loss alignment handed to him by luck.

1. Abandoned building

Insane adversity he faced, he basically had nothing and yet had to counter Q-taro. He also had to profile and manipulate Marco perfectly so that he would become his ally. Mid MAC because Q-taro decided to send in his soldiers in waves so the complexity Baku needed to manage didnt really stack. But then again it was one of the games where Baku had to deal with multiple people at once and not just a single opponent like the rest of his gambles.

1. Air Poker

I scale AP low because this is a strategy scale, not a general scale. AP is mostly for CPI, AC and Reasoning. Aside from the final trap which was good, Baku makes a big-time decision-making anti-feat in R3 which knocks down the strategy for the whole arc.

1. Paper Labyrinth

Pretty creative to use the paper chemistry to create an impossible wall but other than that, most of the arc is just Baku reasoning the trick, and the final trap was formulated in the Shell Gamble, not here. Overall though Baku formulated a pretty neat trick that's really difficult to see through and multiple verbal interactions to facilitate his final trap and information gathering so he can later escape the gamble after he wins.

1. Hangman

Actual game with no consideration for Macro strategies like him predicting IDEAL intervention and using Marco, what Baku did was mostly reasoning. Him reasoning out the cameras were impressive but the strategy part was a pretty simple trap.
