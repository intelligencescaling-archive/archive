How Do You KILL A Time Traveler? (My First SCD Analysis)

So I was watching Undertale/Deltarune content the other day and ended up coming across this video. It's a pretty interesting hypothetical about Sans and Papyrus trying to solve a temporal anomaly, (presumably Flowey) while avoiding the anomaly's attention so as to not jeopardize the operation. I thought it might be SCD viable, and it's been a while since I've done anything here, so I decided to look at Sans from an SCD perspective.

I will be using Proven Ability as that is the system I am most comfortable with. (excluding Normal)

I will also be using the new categories designed by u/Nemo-Lemon01 [in this post of theirs.](https://www.reddit.com/r/IntelligenceScaling/comments/1q4srni/new_categories/?utm_source=share&utm_medium=web3x&utm_name=web3xcss&utm_term=1&utm_content=share_button) (because I am too lazy to dig up old docs)

I will be including feats from the game alongside the video. (Sans already has little to no feats anyway) Stuff from the video will be in *italics,* and stuff from the game will be normal.

This is my first time analyzing, so feedback is welcome

**Cognitive Core**

Cognitive Proficiency (WMI, PSI)

* *Sans was able to come up with a plan to combat the anomaly in the short time he and Papyrus has in their safe zone*

Abstract Proficiency (VCI, VSI)

* Sans doesn't really have any notable VCI or VSI feats

Memory

* Nothing really notable.

**Intellectual Core**

Thinking (Generative, Evaluative, Metacognition)

* Decent planner?

Reasoning (Abductive, Deductive, Inductive)

* Capable of accurately guessing how many times he's killed you through your facial expressions
* *Deduced the anomaly's habits through its continuous resets of the timeline; the anomaly is naturally curious, apathetic, and seeks to exhaust its knowledge of the world.*
* While in the Last Corridor in any route other than the Genocide Route, if you reload your save before Sans's judgement and speak to him again, he will deduce from your facial expressions that you've seen this before, and identify you as a time traveler.

**Social Intelligence**

Cognition

* Decent judge of character, especially in regards to emotions and thoughts

Awareness

* Great at picking up emotions with only minor expressions

Engineering

* *Through information control*, *Sans was able to deceive the Underground completely, and by extension the anomaly*

Skills

* Well liked among his contemporaries
* Works on his spare time as a comedian, great with and popular with people

Integration

* *Successfully blended into the Underground alongside Papyrus with little issue*
* *Good with people, as seen with his job as a comedian*

**Emotional Intelligence**

Emotional Management

* On one hand, he's able to feign happiness to keep up appearances
* ...but he's also abysmally lazy and is unable to manage his emotions to actually do something helpful in the other routes besides Genocide

Emotional Understanding

* Similar to EP

Emotional Perception

* Sans's most pronounced specialty.
* Capable of making accurate guesses as to how many times he's killed you based on your facial expressions when confronting him.

Emotional Engagement

* Sans is overwhelmingly lazy, and only really jumps into action at the end of the Genocide Route. So his EE is very lacking.

**Intelligence Concepts**

Fluid

* *Came up with a suitable strategy to counter the anomaly*
* Decent reasoning skills, especially with psychology

Crystallized

* Sans is adept at quantum physics, and is implied to have a scientific background, shown with his close relationship with Alphys.

**Theory of Mind**

Affective

* Nothing groundbreaking, he can understand emotions well enough to guess what someone is thinking.

Cognitive

* Similar to affective

Mental States

Mental Fortitude

Impulse Management

* Held himself back from killing Frisk until the end of the Genocide Route????

Perseverance

Cognitive Discipline

* Similar to Impulse Management

**Strategy**

Forecasting

* *Anticipated that the anomaly has likely seen lots of possible routes through their ability to reset the timeline and planned accordingly; by forming a strategy that can remain inconspicuous regardless of the resetting timelines*

Resource Optimization (Logistics + Efficiency)

* *Through Papyrus, Sans is able to control the anomaly's information regarding the two skeletons while also being able to use Papyrus as a backdoor to learn more about the anomaly.*

Adaptability

* Little to no adaptability, Sans remains consistent throughout all the routes, excluding Genocide.
* *Because of the timeline resetting, Sans is unable to adapt meaningfully because his memories are also reset. In fact, adapting the strategy could potentially be detrimental as the anomaly might notice a slight change which could ruin the whole strategy.*

Strategic Intent

* *Papyrus's favorite food is used to quickly identify the anomaly in the event that they reset the timeline and wipe the skeletons' memories.*
* *Deliberately not adapting the strategy so as to avoid alerting the anomaly.*

**Planning**

Structural Depth (Coverage + Intricacies)

* *The plan accounts for timeline resets, memory wipes, interference from non-anomalies, and the anomaly trying to gain information on the skeletons.*

Robustness

* *The plan was solid all things considered... but it was only meant for one anomaly. Frisk's arrival rendered the plan unviable.*

Contingency

* *No contingencies. Sans kind of sucks at this.*

Preparation

* *Minimal preparation, Sans and Papyrus only prepared the strategy while in their safe space.*

**Manipulation**

Psychological

* Probably irrelevant, but Sans's scare tactics throughout the game can help in avoiding a Genocide Route (obviously this depends on the person playing)
* Takes advantage of Frisk's possible guilt to get them to spare him, and allow him to kill them easier

Cause & Effect (Logical + Indirect)

* *Sans is aware of the anomaly's thirst for knowledge, and uses it to indirectly manipulate them with logic into believing what the skeletons want them to believe (this is more deception to be honest)*

Exploitation (Direct + Emotional)

* A minor feat, but Sans is able to directly manipulate Frisk into loading saves during his judgement of them, as well as lead them into a prank through taking advantage of their curiosity. Admittedly though, Frisk initiates the event on a whim, and Sans just picks up that you reloaded based on your expression and manipulates you from there
* Emotionally manipulates Frisk into sparing him, letting their guard down.

**Deception**

Information Control (Info + Concealment)

* An underrated category for Sans
* No one in the Underground knows where Sans and Papyrus came from
* *Successfully convinced the anomaly and the population of the Underground into buying the skeletons' false info.*

Reality Fabrication (Fabrication + Acting Skills)

* Kept up appearances as a harmless comedian
* *Convinced the anomaly to trust Papyrus as a friend through a fabricated persona*

Focus Redirection (Misdirection + Bluffing)

* *Through Papyrus, Sans misdirected the anomaly to avoid them from figuring out the plan*

Pattern Breaking (Unpredictability)

* *Because breaking the pattern would alert the anomaly and raise suspicion, Sans isn't really unpredictable*

**Sensory**

Synthesis (Intuition + Insight)

* *Sans knew from intuition and his insights what the anomaly was likely going to do*
* Sans's intuition and insights on psychology help him predict what a person has done based on minute facial expressions

Perception

* Sans excels at perceiving emotions and using them to make educated guesses on what people have done/been doing.

Observation

* Sans picks up on the smallest of expressions, especially in Frisk's case who's face is always empty.

**Foresight**

Logical Prediction

* *Sans predicted the anomaly would reset his memories while still remembering everything themselves whenever it was in a unfavorable position, so he devised a strategy to avoid raising suspicion over the course of hundreds of resets*

Anticipation

* *Same as above*

Probabilistics

* ...same as above?

**Counteraction**

Tactical Fluidity (Tactics + Adaptability)

* *Little adaptability, but Sans's tactics helped him and Papyrus from having their plans be discovered by the anomaly over the course of potentially hundreds of resets*

Profiling (Profiling + Cold Reading)

* Sans is able to accurately deduce what Frisk has done through their facial expressions
* *Sans was able to profile the anomaly's habits and motives*

Adaptive Resilience (Overcoming Limitations)

* *Nothing much here, Sans and Papyrus couldn't really do anything to overcome the anomaly's control over the timeline*
* Sans is naturally lazy. He doesn't really overcome anything in game.

Threat Detection (Traps Evasion)

* *Sans's strategy to avoid raising the anomaly's suspicion*

**Field Skills**

Data Acquisition (Info Gathering)

* *Through Papyrus, Sans is able to gain information on the anomaly*
* Has connections to the Royal Guard and Alphys, which can act as sources of information
* His dialogue during his battle implies he has info on spacetime anomalies, based on reports from an unnamed source

Contextual Rigging/Induced Error (Setting Traps)
