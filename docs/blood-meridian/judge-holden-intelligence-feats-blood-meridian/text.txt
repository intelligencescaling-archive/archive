Judge Holden intelligence feats

This will cover some parts of his intelligence and knowledge.

1. **Meeting the Gang:** When a group of Native Americans pursued the Glanton gang, Holden used his considerable knowledge of chemistry and natural resources to craft crude gunpowder. Extracting potassium nitrate from bat guano and manufacturing charcoal, he led the gang to a region of volcanic activity where he gathered native sulfur. Mixing these with charcoal, potassium nitrate, and urine, he successfully created a functional, albeit rudimentary, gunpowder. With this, he staged a false surrender, only to ambush and massacre the approaching natives once they were in range.
2. **Framing the Priest:** He entered a revival meeting, falsely accusing the priest of being an imposter, a zoophilic criminal, and a pedophile. He stirred the crowd, inciting them to attempt to kill the priest on the spot. Which he succeed in doing.
3. **Manipulating the Glanton Gang:** He present himself as a consultant to the gang. Even the leader, Glanton, falls under his sway. At one point, when Glanton wanted to attack a weapons dealer for charging too much, Holden calmed him, paying for the revolvers himself. It’s clear that Holden doesn’t show loyalty to the gang but uses it and its violence for his own ends. His ultimate goal seems to be guiding the gang on brutal excursions, possibly orchestrating its eventual destruction.

Skills and Knowledge
1. **Philosophy:** Seen many times in the books during his dialogues.
2. **Law:** Holden demonstrates an intricate understanding of the law, and even negotiates the pardoning of criminals, manipulating legal structures to his advantage.
3. **Linguistic:** He speaks multiple languages including English, German, Spanish, Dutch, and various Native American dialects.
4. **Polymath:** Holden is knowledgeable in multiple fields: geology, chemistry, biology, history, botany, and even drafting.
5. **Artist:** His drawings are unnervingly lifelike, so much so that they disturb anyone who views them.
6. **Marksman:** A good marksman, is equally proficient with dual revolvers, knives, and even a Howitzer cannon.
7. **Music and Dancing:** He’s proficient at playing the flute and is also a skilled dancer.
8. **Equestrian Proficiency:** A capable horseback rider.
9. **Poetic Knowledge:** He is well-versed in poetry.
10. **Craftsmanship:** Skills include building a clay kiln, crafting a parasol from animal hide and bone, and even taxidermy, as he stuffs the birds he hunts.
11. **Sleight of Hand:** Sleight of hand is demonstrated through his coin tricks, including the classic coin-behind-the-ear trick and others.
12. **Architectural Expertise:** Explains the history and architecture of an old church in such detail that no one believes he hadn’t visited it before.

Anti-Feats and Mistakes
1. **Outdated Practices:** Holden demonstrates some limitations, particularly in his outdated methods. For example, he uses a skull-measuring device to diagnose mental disturbances, a practice that was already considered archaic at the time.
2. **Failure to Corrupt The Kid:** Holden’s attempts to corrupt and manipulate the Kid ultimately fail. Despite his vast intellect and manipulative prowess, he cannot break the Kid's spirit or make him submit to his philosophy.
3. **Failure to Kill the Kid:** Holden chases the Kid with the help of the Idiot but ultimately fails to kill him.

Supernatural/Unexplained Abilities (Hax)
*He’s apparently knowledgeable of events before they unfold. As seen when he knew where would the gang would be in their first interaction.

###Conclusion

Overall, he’s decent, but definitely not a high tier.
