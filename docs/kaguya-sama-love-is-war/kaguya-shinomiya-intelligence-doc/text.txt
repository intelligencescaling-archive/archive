Feats
[Chapter 1] She excels in theater, music, and martial arts. After half a year, Kaguya and Miyuki develop feelings for each other and begin to plan ways to get the other to confess said feelings. Chika Fujiwara, the student council member secretary, wanted to give a pair of movie tickets she won to the two of them, since her parents won’t let her go due to family restrictions. Chika also brings up a myth about if a boy and girl watch that movie together, they’re guaranteed to start dating, which Kaguya had predicted and planned beforehand by carefully preparing a fake prize, slipping it into Fujiwara’s mailbox, and finding a movie on the weekend on one of Miyuki’s rare breaks, all to use to her advantage in order to have Miyuki ask her out. She also sees through Miyuki’s strategy of leaving it up to her and uses the skill “Feigning Innocence” to throw Miyuki off. (Knowledge, Academics, Musical Intelligence, Bodily-Kinesthetic Intelligence, Acting Skills, Overall Planning, Long Term Planning, Strategic Thinking, Tactics, Prediction, Anticipation, Seduction, Love Bombing, Indirect Manipulation, Logical Manipulation, Emotional Manipulation, Fast Thinking)

[Chapter 2] Kaguya and Miyuki began to play a game of Old Maid, a game where players take turns drawing cards and all pairs are discarded. The winner gets to make a request of the loser. Her goal was to throw the game and manipulate whatever request Miyuki was going to make via tears. Miyuki thinks of Kaguya in a swimsuit or maid outfit. However, Kaguya immediately shuts down the option of making lewd requests, implying that she knew what Miyuki was thinking. (Overall Planning, Tactics, Emotional Manipulation, Emotional Perception, Guesswork, Seeing through People)

[Chapter 4] She casually answers each one of Chika’s riddles correctly, such as correctly guessing which number goes to which letter/direction (East is 3, West is 9, South is 6, and North is 12), something that Miyuki fails to accomplish. (Analytical Thinking, Critical Thinking, Associative Thinking, Abstract Thinking, FRI)

[Chapter 7] Had prepared an entire manual for any counter arguments Shirogane could make against going to the beach. (Planning Coverage, Planning Preparation, Anticipation, FRI, Strategy Shielding)

[Chapter 10] She tries to have Shirogane confess to her in his sleep by manipulating his dreams. She could also figure out how deep of a sleep he is in by touching his eyelids due to the eyes moving rapidly during REM sleep. When in REM sleep, the brain continues to work, causing dreams. And the brain is still able to process auditory information during this time, allowing Kaguya to manipulate Shirogame’s dreams by speaking to him. (Crystallized Intelligence, Love Bombing)

[Chapter 11] Formulates a plan using her servants to convince Shirogane to buy a smartphone. That way, he can attempt to get her contact information  later on. In order to get Shirogane to show a childhood photo and lose his bargaining chip over her, she pretends to cry and call him cruel for it. (Short Term Planning, Indirect Manipulation, Influence, Direct Manipulation, Acting Skills, Guilt Tripping)

[Chapter 12] She creates a false love letter and accepts to go on a date with a random guy in order to have Shirogane stop her and have him admit his feelings towards her. (Fabrication, Short Term Planning)

[Chapter 17] She bit down on her tongue to prevent herself from smiling at Shirogane. (Impulse Management, Composure, Mental Fortitude)

[Chapter 20] Is able to speak French Fluently, only having difficulty with pronunciation because one rarely uses naval vowels in Japanese. It’s to the point where she was able to terrify Betsy, who has two victories in the French Debate meet, and uses quick thinking and logic to deny her opponent’s most basic way of thinking. (VCI, Intimidation)

[Chapter 21] She deduces that Shirogane knew that it was going to rain today because he took the train instead of riding his bike to school like how he usually does. (Observation, Deduction, Abduction, Convergent Thinking, Critical Thinking, Logical Reasoning, Logical Thinking, Analytical Thinking, Logical-Mathematical Intelligence, Linear Thinking, Logical Thinking, Logical Reasoning)

[Chapter 30] Has been second only to Shirogane in terms of the school exams for the past three exams, which were about science and liberal arts. (Academics)

[Chapter 31] Having known Fujiwara since junior high school, she is able to anticipate her behavior more easily than tomorrow’s weather forecast. She knew Fujiwara would grab a psychology test book from the library, so she already reviewed the book. And since she already knows the significance of the questions, she won’t get caught providing a revealing answer (such as with Page 47, question 2, which asks “who tapped your shoulder while you’re walking down a dimly lit road”, which would be the person you like). (Emotional Perception, Emotional Understanding, Prediction)

[Chapter 34] In order to make Shirogane ask her for a ride during a thunderstorm, she manipulates him by saying how a contributing member of society would never miss work over something like that, and that he should either pay for a taxi, which costs more than he makes on his job, or ask someone to give him a ride. However, immediately after this she gets a notification that the trains have resumed service. In order to prevent Shirogane from finding out, she replaces the battery in his phone with a dead one, all within one minute before Shirogane returns to the room. Kaguya has long predicted a wide range of scenarios and prepared the necessary tools to entrap Shirogane. This dead battery is one of them. An item to be deployed in a situation in which Shirogane's phone must be disabled. (Individual Manipulation, Direct Manipulation, Logical Manipulation, Persusion, Setting Traps, Strategy Fortitude, Planning Coverage, Strategy Shielding)

[Chapter 39] Kaguya comes up with the idea to get closer with Shirogane’s sister Kei, in order to also get closer with him. She presents herself as helpful and generous to Kei, projecting an image of the type of girl a person would want as an older sister. (Self Awareness, Relationship Management).

[Chapter 54] The day after giving Shirogane cake and a fan for his birthday, she goes to tell Chika that Shirogane’s birthday was yesterday, so that Chika would believe Shirogane is in love with her and spread that rumor to make others believe it as well. Shirogane attempts to counter this by explaining that he personally didn’t tell her his birthday, and that she only found out because it was in the student. He also questions why she didn’t share that information with their friends. She had a counter to this, but was distracted by Shirogane fanning himself with the fan she got for him. (Direct Manipulation, Individual Manipulation, Event Manipulation, Verbal Deception, Information Control, Strategy Shielding)
