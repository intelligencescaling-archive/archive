Kaguya Shinomiya (四宮しのみや かぐや, Shinomiya Kaguya) is one of the two main protagonist and the titular protagonist of the Kaguya-sama wa Kokurasetai series, the same creator by the Author of the 2020 supernatural drama manga series, Oshi No Ko. She is a student at Shuchi'in Academy, a member of the Archery Club, the daughter and member of the Shinomiya Family and the Vice President of the 67th and 68th Student Council.
