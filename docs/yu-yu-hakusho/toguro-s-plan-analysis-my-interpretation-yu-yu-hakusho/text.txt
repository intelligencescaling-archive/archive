Toguros plan analysis (my interpretation)

As stated by the last chapter of the arc Toguro wished to become a demon 50 years before the start of the manga. Many people will have their own way to interpret his real plan since it’s rather vague what he really even wanted but I will try my best to explain what I think was his real goal all along

As the tournament progressed (the one 50 years ago) I assume that he felt that deep down he started getting detached from Genkai and humanity in general due to his obsession with Kairen the demon that murdered his attempt to protect humanity by fostering warriors that would protect and carry its values in the form of his dojo. I take this symbolism for how his humanity was killed of Kairen the moment he lost his dojo which could be a metaphor for his humanity. Since his humanity is lost and his team is ”human” that would make them incompatible. That’s why I think that he chose to become a demon becouse in his mind he had already became one. He likely felt great pain litteraly becoming what took his humanity away (he took Kairens body and made it his own), feeling responsible for the death of his students he decided he wanted to be taken down by Genkai or someone close to her to finally end his suffering for good.

I believe that he had anticipated that someone like Genkai would want to live on in another by teaching a new generation to do what she did since he understood she valued life and humanity and would want to have it protected. He endured 50 years of psychological torture until he found out about Genkais student or Yusuke he decided to set a plan into action. He met Sakyo from unknown circumstances but we could assume that he chose Sakyo since he could be used for the set up. As he was hired by Tarukane he knew about Sakyos set up with the gamble and decided to follow his orders to get to the outcome to be able to get to fight Yusuke before the tournament. During the fight he was able to display his far superior power to Yusuke which was a plan to make his offer at chapter 51 unable to fail since he not only managed to show Yusuke the gap between them but also use psychological warfare by blitzing him putting him in a state of pure shock and fear which made his fear more instinctive. Toguro knew about the amp humans could get from emotion by this point since Genkai told Yusuke about it during their training which should mean she had experienced it herself which Toguro was likely there to witness since her time with Toguro was the most emotional and personal relationship from our knowledge. He likely knew that she would chose someone similar to her to become her aprentice which is why he targeted Yusuke and not Kuwabara who had shown the amp infront of his eyes (also becouse he wanted to be beaten by Genkai). He most likely understood that the dark tournament would be the perfect place for such great emotions to unleash causing him to rapidly grow in power over the tournament. He knew that Genkai wouldn’t decline the offer since she wouldn’t wanna risk her student dying which would mean her legacy would be destroyed and becouse he knew she missed him deep down in her heart. He used this knowledge to further empower Yusuke by making the deduction that she would pass on her power to Yusuke since she would logically know that she would lose to Toguro (current toguro>Prime Toguro~Prime Genkai>Genkai) and Yusuke would be the best shot since he’s younger and therefore has more potential to grow that power into something bigger (this theory is supported by him asking Genkai if she had ”passed it on”. As his plan goes as expected he decides to take into action to kill Genkai which he does all while never breaking character and holding up his persona despite how close they were. He likely had predicted that at this point Yusuke would be extremely focused due to how tense the tournament is (keep in mind its over mere days so the change of his psychological state is likely very great). Therefore he knew that Yusuke would instantly come to the location that Genkais aura vanished. He was likely trying to replicate what Kairen did to him by killing Genkai which he knew would shatter Yusuke making him determined to kill Toguro. As the final match comes to a close he manages to use Yusukes emotions to kill himself by pretending to kill Kuwabara just like Kairen killed his students. His plan to be killed of by Genkai is successful since he sees Yusuke and Genkai as one of the same person in a way which means that him being killed of by Yusuke/Genkai was also a plan to prove to himself that he was wrong in choosing to abandon humanity due to it having to separate him and Genkai from each other. By being killed by Yusuke who overpowered his demon enchanted power with raw human emotion he metaphorically proved to himself that he was wrong in choosing demonhood and leaving his human side. Before he dies he meets up with Genkai for one last time for a final goodbye. As they speak he mentiones that Yusuke has massive potential but that he could fall into the wrong path just as he did since he sees Yusuke not only as Genkai but as an extension of himself as a human which is why he smiled at his death since he thought that he had in a way redeemed himself by making up for his mistake and leading the next him to take on the same challange as him but with a different outcome, in simple terms he was rewriting the mistakes from his past by changing to future to fix what he regretted. He likely wanted Genkai to live due to his feelings for her which he convinces her to do by bribing up the topic of the parralel line between him and Yusuke which gives Genkai 2 reasons to stay alive
1:Yusuke is very young and he could become like Toguro if worst comes to worst
2:She liked Yusuke becouse he reminded her of him and felt like she had to continue to teach him in order to ensure that the conflict between Toguro and her would end for good
