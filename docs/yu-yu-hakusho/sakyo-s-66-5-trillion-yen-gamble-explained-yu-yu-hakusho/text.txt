Sakyo 66.5 trillion yen gamble feat explained

It’s revealed that Sakyo planned the events of the arc from the start by hiring Younger toguro to Tarukane as a body guard. Knowing that Yusuke and Kuwabara would be able to take down the guard the Younger toguro hired he likely used his intution to guess that Tarukane would ask about the competence of his gang. The logical step after that question would be to affirm Tarukane that it’s fine. Knowing that Tarukane is as money hungry as he is he expected that he would take every chance he would get to lower Toguros salary which could be done by making him decline a order to justify. Knowing about his hobby of experimenting he guessed that he would go down to make him challange one of his experiments thinking that they would scare Toguro off. But knowing the true power of Toguro Sakyo knew that the experiments wouldn’t stand a real chance which is why he planned all of this in advance in order to have Toguro kill it off. Sakyo would then use Tarukanes greed as a part of his plain moving forward to guess that he would try to profit from Toguro which would get him as a part of the secret black club to finally come in contact with Tarukane. Knowing that Toguro would have given him the evidence that his normal men wouldn’t be enough due to the implication of the guard instantly getting killed that he would have a plan that would go something like this
1:Set up intruders vs The Toguro gang
2:Make people gamble on it
3:Present the intruders as extremely formidable due to the video evidence showing it
4:Have the memebers get a false fabricated reality that the intruders will win
5:Use Toguro last in order to throw them off guard and finally win the gamble

As he asks the rest of the Black black club if they will bet on the demons or the intruders most of them naturally bet on the demon since there were over 10 of them while there were 3 humans who are generally physically intherior to demons. This is when Sakyo decided to bet 5 billion yen on the intruders not only becouse he knew he would win but becouse he knew that placing such a big bet on them would make him stand out compared to the others that were betting on 500 million yen or less. After winning the 1st gamble Tarukane owes him 10 billion yen since he proposed a 2x bonus if the gamble is successful. On the second gamble knowing about Yusukes reputation he bet on 20 billion yen on the intruders winning against the triad which would mean he has won a total of 50 billion yen from gambling. He used this strategy of getting money not as a means of financial development but as a means to make him accept his next offer. As that is done he likely knew that he would use Toguro in the 3rd gamble due to his showings of power that would be displayed at the basement floor of his mansion. Sakyo then proposes a bet off 66.5 trillion yen on the intruders. Normally accepting such a bet would be absurd since it’s the entire fortune of his criminal empire but since he already lost almost 50 billion yen even counting his winnings he likely predicted that a greedy man like Tarukane wouldn’t want to lose money in this game no matter what and that he was far to confident with Toguros display of power to be nervous about it.

In the end this makes Tarukane play right into the hands of Sakyo since he hired Toguro from the start to set up the means for him to get the entire fortune of Tarukanes criminal empire via a gamble

Short explonation:Sakyo set everything up from the start from the display of Toguros real strength to Tarukanes decision to make money off of the black black club while purposely making him lose a ton of money and making himself a challanger from the POV of Tarukane in order to get him to accept his 66.5 trillion yen gamble which was garuanteed victory due to him being the real employer of Toguro
