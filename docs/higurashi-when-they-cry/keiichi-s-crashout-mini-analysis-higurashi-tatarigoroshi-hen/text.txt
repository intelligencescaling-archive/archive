Keiichi’s crashout Mini Analysis | One of the most ABSURD feats ever performed by a low/mid tier [Higurashi Tatarigoroshi-hen]

On this occasion, I’d like to talk about a feat that I haven’t really seen anyone discuss in general, which belongs to a low-tier character from the 07th Expansion (the same verse of Umineko).

To give a bit of prior context so the scene makes sense: this takes place after Satoko’s route, in the Tatarigoroshi-hen arc, where Satoko’s uncle returns from Okinomiya and starts living with her again, subjecting her to both physical and emotional abuse.

Keiichi in this route is very close to Satoko, so as he sees how his friend gradually loses her cheerfulness and the light in her eyes, he becomes more and more angry, actively searching for a way to solve the problem of her uncle’s presence so she can return to a normal life.

However, one day Keiichi reaches his breaking point. This happens when Satoko begins to show at school the psychological scars left by the constant daily abuse, which pushes him into such an extreme state of rage that he performs the feat I want to explain in this post, which I’ve dubbed “Keiichi’s crashout.”

I’ll go image by image explaining what’s happening:

- - - -

(1) This is the moment when Keiichi gets so angry that he breaks his internal restraints, reaching a state of calm in which emotions gradually leave his body. He starts to perceive reality in different colors, expanding his vision and the volume/capacity of his own mind, even stating that his mind grew wider than the classroom.

(2) Even though you might think the previous part is metaphorical, it’s actually much more literal than it seems. Keiichi’s mental capacity in that state increased so much that his soul was no longer bound to his tiny body of flesh, where the skull limits the size of the brain. Instead, he could now see himself from above, floating in the air. It was a sensation of release, in which he mentally surpassed the confines of the flesh and expanded his consciousness like roots in all directions, reaching a state of “transcendence,” where his mind is described as being “sharper than a knife."

(3) With that transcendental mental state, Keiichi displays an absurd level of emotional control, being able to voluntarily expel all emotions and useless information from his brain that could hinder him from achieving his goal. He can then use every single cell of his brain to think about how to eliminate him in the most effective and direct way possible with the means at his disposal.

(4) And here’s where something happened that genuinely blew my mind the first time I saw it. After having seen Satoko’s house and its surroundings only a couple of times (maybe two or three), he was able to perfectly reconstruct the entire house and everything around it within his mind.

(5) He didn’t just reconstruct the terrain; he also reconstructed the number of pedestrians and the times at which they move through the area. This implies that he was mentally reconstructing the scene in four dimensions, taking the temporal variable of a given time span X into account. He goes as far as identifying patterns in the civilians’ seemingly random behavior, creating algorithms in his head and calculating the most efficient way to commit the act, ultimately concluding that it would take him 25 minutes to execute Satoko’s uncle in a direct attack.
