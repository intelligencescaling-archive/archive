Jalaketu West’s The Other King Tactic analysis

Context : Jalaketu West or The Comet King had realised that even without being in hell,he wasn’t of hell. He thus couldn’t use the Shem HaMephorash to destroy it . Thus the only thing left for him to do was to do enough evil so that he died and went to hell

Context#2 : Elisha Ben Abuyah is a Judaic figure. He was one of the greatest Rabbi of history. However,when he saw that a boy who did good was killed but another who did evil was harmed. He decided to act against God and blaspheme against the unjust world. He was thus punished by God to never enter heaven . Nobody,not even Uriel know his fate. His name has been recorded as Auter or The Other since this can’t conceivably be Elisha Ben Abuyah.

Context#3 : The Comet King had recently defeated a death cult in Las Vegas ,however the cult lived.

Context#4 : Gadiriel was an angel who deeply loved humanity and guided it throughout history,the current state of USA was due to her. She has an exceptional ability to charm and create golems.

————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————

The Comet King had convinced Gadiriel to create another body for him. This was >!likely!<done by justifying to her that through his plan he was doing the most good by saving all the sinners from hell. He used this golem to act in his place as The Comet King,fooling everyone as Gadiriel’s golems were perfect. He further concealed this golem not being him by faking depression due to the death of his wife,robin. He then established himself in the defeated Las Vegas death cult as their former necromantic leader,who had gotten stronger through death and lead them in a conquest of the west. As anticipated,this resulted in his advisors and children asking him to beat the necromantic leader once more ,which he had the golem half-heartedly accept . In the fight against the golem,he faked his death and made everyone believe that he had died due his terrible state. He created the image of a near fatal wound to provide a reasonable cover for why he didn’t act more (which was done to prevent chances of being found)

As the necromancer,he initially acted as just any other conqueror making people flock to him due to Novelty and Quality . He then ensured that after they had joined,they couldn’t leave due to fearing for their life and feeling that everyone else would betray them to save their own son. This allowed him to enact terrible atrocities with impunity. He was thus dubbed ,The Other King due to having been the other King who defeated the comet King and his atrocities blaspheming God like Auter’s did .

Now,a keen minded Kabbalist would link Elisha with The Other King due to self evident traits like both having been called other,both blaspheming against God and the kind of strength needed to beat Comet King isn’t just present in some old necromancer but a great Kabbalist who turned against God ,chiefly he divided the messiah into two while Elisha divided God into two.

————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————

The Layered Deception :

1.The Other King is the necromancer defeated by Comet King but risen and made more powerful

2.The Other King is Elisha Ben Abuyah acting as the necromancer to take control of the cult

The first layer fed into the second . It answered all the questions like how could a necromancer beat THE COMET KING or what happened to Elisha Ben Abuyah or why isn’t the Other King more active.

The Layered Manipulation :

1st. It was inconceivable to imagine that The Comet King had become The Other King and committed the atrocities,furthermore it was such a terrible truth that even those knew didn’t want to accept it (like Jinxiang)

2nd. Those who saw through all of it were convinced to not act since it was all for the ultimate good

The trick worked on the ego of somewhat intelligent people to make them think they had seen through ,the Auter.
