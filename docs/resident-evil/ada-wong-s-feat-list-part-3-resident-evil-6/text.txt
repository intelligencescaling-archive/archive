Ada Wong's Feat list part 3. Resident Evil 6

This is her feat list in her story in
Resident Evil 6.

Chapter 1 – “Into the Submarine”

Plot summary: Ada receives a mysterious message, infiltrates a submarine whose security recognises her biometrics, discovers past orders assigned to her which she never received.

Feats
(Inductive Reasoning):
Ada deduces that the orders (six months old) to capture Jake Muller must have been carried out by someone impersonating her. This shows inductive reasoning (“someone else did these things in my name”).

(divergent thinking):
She navigates the unusual submarine environment, including puzzles and biometric security systems keyed to her identity. Her ability to interpret that “this submarine is built for me/against me” shows non‑linear thinking.

(Knowledge Application): She uses her espionage experience to infiltrate deep into a hostile facility, recognising the significance of the mission even though she wasn’t originally involved.

(Decision‑making and foresight):
 Choosing to investigate despite initial refusal her curiosity plus risk‑evaluation show she generated options, assessed risk and took the mission anyway.

Sensory / observation: She notices the biometrics system keyed to her, realises her identity is compromised, and reacts accordingly (escape sequence).

Chapter 2 – “Tall Oaks / Cathedral Lab”

Plot summary: Ada follows leads to the U.S., enters the Tall Oaks cathedral laboratory region, meets up (briefly) with Leon S. Kennedy and Helena Harper, uncovers a videotape labeled “Happy Birthday Ada Wong” showing her clone’s origins.

Feats

(Inductive and Deductive Reasoning):
 She pieces together clues: the submarine mission, the orders she never received, the videotape, and realises there is a clone‑plot going on.

(Quick Thinking and Fluid Reasoning Index):
In the labyrinthine lab/cathedral environment, with puzzles (e.g., serpent‑emblem puzzles) and enemy threats, she mentally navigates both stealth and puzzle mechanics.

(Knowledge Application):
She uses her familiarity with spy‑craft and infiltration to trace the research facility, understand its significance (a clone of her, a massive bioweapon plot).

(Decision‑making and Long term Planning): Ada shifts her objective from simply servicing a mission to destroying the infrastructure behind the plot. She decides the data about herself must be purged.

(Strategy lenght  / planning versatility): Recognising that she cannot just steal data — she must blow things up (later) — shows foresight and strategic planning rather than reactive action.

Chapter 3‑4 – Confrontation & Escape

Plot summary: Ada assists Leon & Helena in confronting Derek C. Simmons (via rooftops, helicopters, etc.), pursues her clone Carla Radames, accesses the Quad Tower lab, and destroys the facility containing her clone’s cocoon and research.

Feats

 (Trap‑setting and counteracting):
 She sets up or participates in a coordinated strike with Leon & Helena, uses diversion, assumes the role of controlling the field (e.g., via helicopter, sabotage).

 (Deductive reasoning):
She figures out Simmons is behind the clone‑plot, realises Carla is the fake Ada, then plans to destroy the root facility rather than chase every symptom.

(Quick Thinking and Tactics Versatility):
 During the lab collapse, escapes, environmental hazards, she mentally adjusts on the fly, using her tools (grapple, stealth) to navigate chaotic terrain.

(Knowledge Application):
 She understands the biomechanics/bioweapon threat of the cocoon research, recognises the significance of destroying it before it hatches.

(Decision‑making and long term Strategy):
She opts to destroy everything (lab + cocoon) rather than simply extract data or let someone else handle it. This is risk‑heavy but aligned with her long‑term goal of eradicating the threat.

(Observation):
She observes the architecture of the lab, uses her grappling hook and infiltration tools to exploit ceilings/vents/ropes rather than frontal assault.

Indirect Manipulation: Through stealth and infiltration, she manipulates enemy movements, stays hidden, uses environment to her advantage (rather than brute force).
