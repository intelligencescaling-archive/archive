Jacopo's feats in the revolution

Today I'll be analysing the feat of Jacopo Bearzatti from The House In Fata Morgana : Requiem for Innocence. (RFI is a prequel to the base THIFM)

At the beginning of the story, we see 19 yr old Jacopo. Who was among the slum kids and a 'thug'. He with other people planned to revolt against the Lord Barnier. Or atleast plunder him. So he went inside as a 'slave' to not only free other slaves but to plunder or possibly kill Barnier. As soon as he enters the party he sees a Morgana (a 9 yr old at this time) tied to chains with brutal cut marks. The party was to drink her blood since she was a 'saint'. Here, he also meets Gratien. A quite strong and muscular slave who quickly under Jacopo's plan and challenged him infront of Barnier. Barnier was a sadistic and cruel lord who loved to torture his slaves in a party with other nobles. So he agreed to their 'battle to death'.

In reality Gratien's plan was something else. As Jacopo was a slim kid, he was no comparison to Gratien. So naturally Gratien should've won easily. But he held back and repeated a certain pattern of moves. Jacopo initially thought that Gratien wanted to impress the lord but soon after seeing the continuous patterns of moves in sword-fighting he immediately understood his plan. And began following the patterns and showing his 'competence' when infact he was inexperienced in Sword-fighting. After some more moves Jacopo's blades strikes from above to Gratien's and breaks Gratien's blade off. The broken part flies off in the party which was the initial 'motive' of Gratien. Because the blade is no more usable the fight ends in victory to Jacopo. After leaving the party, Jacopo meets Gratien again and asks him why his 'initial motive' was so stupid and why he had to risk Jacopo's life for that. (As there was no guarantee the blade would definitely hit a noble). And that there was definitely something else he wanted to achieve. Where Gratien reveals that he wanted Jacopo to win and that was the motive. Not the blade flying off which was secondary. By winning against the strongest Slave in the lord's Manor, Jacopo(who is a new slave here) would get approval of other slaves faster and would get influence over them.

**(Pattern recognition, intuition, situational adaptability feats by Jacopo. Scheming (plan formulation, strategic efficiency), misdirection feats by Gratien).**

Now with the influence over other slaves and Gratien (who as a stated before was strongest possibly), Jacopo started his revolt on another day when party was held. Barnier was going to execute the Girl Morgana who was still tied to chains and seeing her like that Jacopo couldn't control himself and defended her completely giving up the 'suprise' advantage of their attack. (Empathy feat and Strategic advantage antifeat possibly?).

Seeing that Jacopo had launched to protect the girl, Gratien using the other slaves attacked even though now the guards were prepared. But because of preparation, Gratien and the slaves won, whereas against the Lord who was way more experienced than Jacopo, Jacopo managed to make him flee and overpower him but failed to kill him. This was all while having no experience of swordsmanship and trying to protect the girl.

 **(Learning ability, pattern recognition feats by Jacopo. Situational adaptability feat by Gratien).**

Now that the Lord had fled, the slaves and Jacopo plundered him and escaped with carrying the young girl who was chained. Jacopo couldn't manage to control all the slaves after this as he himself was a slum kid and had no power and money. So he sent them off from the city with the resources their share was. Gratien joined in with Jacopo and they went to the Slums side of the city. Here he gives the young girl to a brothel, where his friend Maria lived and worked.

Time passed, as Jacopo and the girl who was quite distant formed a bond and the brothel took care of the girl, Morgana.

After three years in a festival, Jacopo meets a woman who claimed that Jacopo was her child and illegitimate son fo the previous lord. Jacopo didn't knew about his family and the woman had quite similar skintone and face to Jacopo. Jacopo didnt agree to take the 'heir' position and left. One more character was in the brothel - Ceren. Keep her in mind.

After a month or so, Morgana's birthday came, and Ceren proposed a birthday plan for her. Eventually the party happened and on that day, Ceren had gifted a strong perfume to Morgana. So strong it could be smelled from outside. (Jacopo commented). Morgana left for bathroom and that time unfortunately a group of bandits attacked. Killing and plundering the brothel. Jacopo got stabbed in the guts as he looked for Morgana who had been abducted by the bandits and sold off. Only 4 women were left alive (including Maria) and even Ceren was missing. Jacopo filled with regret decides to meet the woman who claimed him as her child again with a plan.

**(Empathy, observation, decision making, EF feat by Jacopo).**

Meeting the woman again who had claimed him to be her child and illegitimate son of the previous lord, Jacopo had completely saw through her.

*Premise 1 -  When asked why she only came now after so many years, she said that she couldn't meet him and he would've been killed as a baby.*

*Premise 2 - when asked why she left him, she said that he was abducted by the group bandits.*

*Premise 3 - "were you a maidservant to the lord's Manor?" "...Yes, I was. The lord even gave me the ring of the family as a gift."*

*Conclusion 1 - her story was fabricated and she was decieving Jacopo using words like 'mother's love' or 'you would've been killed' to confuse him.*

*Conclusion 2 - her skin tone and face was similar to Jacopo which meant either they were from the same land or place. Her child is probably dead or never existed and because Jacopo was an orphan and looked similar she did this. She was removed from the servant position (the ring proved that she was indeed a servant with intimate affairs inside) and now she chose Jacopo to take revenge.*

*Conclusion 3 - She chose him and that too right now because he had made his name through the 'revolution' he had done 3 years ago. This made her digging up information of Jacopo easier.*

After the revelation to the woman, she confessed that indeed what he had said was true.

(Abductive reasoning, deductive reasoning, Seeing through, EP, EU feats by Jacopo.)

Now Jacopo decided to use this fabrication to compete for heir spot and kill the Lord Barnier.

He then convinced Gratien and other people who 'revolted' with him by saying, "we have a noble and that is me." By using the name of 'blood' Jacopo could now compete against nobles.

He then used this name and fabrication to lead the citizens into revolution as the citizens didn't liked the Lord and the fact that their leader was of noble blood yet had grew up in the slums which made the story even more inciting for the crowd.

(Fabrication, strategic thinking, planning effectiveness, Linear thinking, logical manipulation, mass manipulation feats by Jacopo)

He then again led them to Barnier and fought against them. Here Ceren tries to stop Jacopo which shocked him. And the lord eventually pierces Ceren with her sword and she laughs again. While she was pierced Ceren held onto the sword saying, "I want you to be with me." To the lord. Using this opportunity in a split second, Jacopo swung his sword and beheaded Ceren and cutt off Barnier's throat. Killing both.

*Observation - Ceren gave strong scented perfume or something like that to Morgana on the day of Attack of bandits. She disappeared. Now she appears again to stop Jacopo from killing Barnier. Ceren always laughed even now while she was dying, even after her parents had died when she was young, infront of her.*

*Conclusion - Ceren was working with Barnier and she used the scent to signal the attack. She was mad as she always laughed and never felt sadness or sorrow even though her parents died. Barnier was scheming and had planned all this using Ceren to lead Jacopo here. Even the attack by bandits was done by him.*

(Logical reasoning (abductive, deductive and inductive), adaptability, decision making, EU, observation, EM feats by Jacopo).
