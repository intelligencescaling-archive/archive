Niu Youdao - The Interlocking Stratagems

Background Introduction:
The Ethereal Pavilion was established by the Nine Great Sovereigns. Every time the Sacred Realm opens, they force the various sects to send disciples inside to compete for Spiritual Seeds within a one-year period. The Spiritual Seed is an extremely rare fruit that grows within the Sacred Realm, and whichever faction collects the most will claim the top spot.
* Niu Youdao was originally just a rogue cultivator, not belonging to any sect. However, because he had too many enemies, the elders of the various sects proposed to the Ethereal Pavilion that, in addition to sect disciples, rogue cultivators should also be dragged into the Sacred Realm to trim their numbers. Conveniently, this would also make it easier for the Ethereal Pavilion to control the martial world.
The manager of the Ethereal Pavilion, Sha Rulai, agreed and requested the sects to register the names of the rogue cultivators who must enter the Sacred Realm. Niu Youdao’s name was listed the most times.
Upon receiving the news, Niu Youdao understood clearly that this time, he was being targeted by the entire world.
* Before entering the Sacred Realm, he prepared extensively. The Moon Crest Pavilion—the assassin organization currently cooperating with him—secretly transacted with him, informing him that among the sect disciples, there would be people from the Ethereal Pavilion blending in, while also providing secret hand signs for mutual recognition. In addition, there were Yun Huan and Yun Ji, both of whom possessed their own forces, but due to various circumstances, they could currently only stand on the same side as Niu Youdao.
* On the opposing side, the three major sects of the Zhao State and the Jin State had nearly resolved to kill him within the Sacred Realm.
* Meanwhile, the three major sects of the Yan State temporarily cooperated with Niu Youdao because Shang Chaotong, who held 90% of the Yan State's military power, was listening to him. However, this relationship was highly fragile; the moment interests shifted, they could turn their backs at any time.
* Upon arriving at the entrance of the Sacred Realm, Niu Youdao immediately encountered the mass of sect disciples from various countries gathered there. The rule of this place was that no fighting was allowed.
In the mountain valley outside the entrance, the various forces had divided up territory many years ago. At this moment, the people of the Zhao State were forcing the Wandong Heaven Mansion of the Yan State to leave their residential area, creating an extremely tense atmosphere.
Three elders of the Zhao State directly blocked the entrance, refusing to let Niu Youdao pass through.
Niu Youdao understood very well that if he endured it today, then after the Sacred Realm concluded one year later, this crowd of people would still be standing outside the door to block and kill him. Since it was already like this, there was only one way to solve it.
He directly drew his sword and slaughtered every single elder and disciple of the three Zhao State sects right there in the valley.
The surrounding crowd was entirely stunned; no one expected Niu Youdao to dare kill people in an area managed by the Ethereal Pavilion.
The commotion was so massive that Sha Rulai came in person to interrogate him. However, thanks to his prior preparations, Niu Youdao ultimately escaped the death penalty.
Even so, Sha Rulai still punished him for causing a disturbance.
The punishment was that if he failed to secure the Number 1 spot in the Sacred Realm this time, he would be executed.
* To claim the top spot, one had to collect the most Spiritual Seeds, but the biggest problem was that Niu Youdao had almost no one truly trustworthy.
The next day, during discussions, due to a conflict of interest, Zhu Fengping, an elder of the Yan State, lost face and immediately turned his back. He even played dirty, using his influence to separate Niu Youdao from the remaining two sects of the Yan State, deliberately stating that without the support of the three major sects, it was impossible for Niu Youdao to get Number 1, and his end would only be death.
The remaining two elders, Yan Li and Shan Hai, also turned their backs immediately. Even a few people who had previously followed Niu Youdao abandoned him for the other side, feeling that he no longer held any value to rely upon.
From that moment on, Niu Youdao was completely pushed into an isolated position within the Sacred Realm.
To deal with the current situation, he could only begin layout out a plan.
The first step in Niu Youdao's plan was actually not to search for Spiritual Seeds, but to secretly sneak to the place where the Ethereal Pavilion disciples were bathing to steal a set of uniforms, and then he released a wild boar to carry it away. When it was discovered, they only saw the boar wandering around while the clothes had vanished. His purpose was very simple: to prepare in advance for a long-term planning step further down the line, to be used to escape a fatal deadlock when necessary. From beginning to end, no one knew about this matter, including Yun Ji and Yu Zhaoxing. Currently, only a few people remained by Niu Youdao's side:
* Yun Ji and her son, Yun Huan
* Yu Zhaoxing
The head count was pitifully small. Wanting to compete for Number 1 through normal means was nearly impossible. The other forces all had vastly superior numbers, while his faction combined consisted of only a few people. Most importantly, almost every single sect inside the Sacred Realm currently wanted to take a bite out of Niu Youdao. After pondering over the forces participating this time, Niu Youdao began to construct a chain of interlocking stratagems.
The first step of the plan targeted the overseas cultivators.
He led his people to search for their traces, and after a few days, finally discovered a group of three disciples from the Tianxing Sect operating near the overseas region. Niu Youdao proactively approached to greet them. During the conversation, he secretly made the secret hand sign of the Moon Crest Pavilion to test them. The moment he saw the sign, one man among the three immediately caught the meaning. His name was Xu Fuhua, a spy planted by the Moon Crest Pavilion inside the Tianxing Sect. Xu Fuhua directly moved his hand to kill the remaining two fellow disciples. Only then did Niu Youdao assign him a task: to investigate the whereabouts of the overseas cultivators.
The reason Niu Youdao told Xu Fuhua to kill those two people was so that after returning, he could report to the Tianxing Sect that they were ambushed by the overseas cultivators, and two disciples were killed by them.
As long as this news spread, the entire Tianxing Sect would automatically help Niu Youdao hunt down the whereabouts of the overseas cultivators. He barely needed to waste any effort searching by himself.

10 days later, Niu Youdao went to meet Xu Fuhua again.
At this time, Xu Fuhua already had three additional Tianxing Sect disciples accompanying him. Niu Youdao simply came over to ask a few questions and then left very quickly, looking outwardly as if it were just a chance encounter. However, his true purpose had two layers.
First, it was to intentionally let those disciples see him, so they would report Niu Youdao's whereabouts upward to the Tianxing Sect elder.
Second, it was to use this meeting as a signal, informing Xu Fuhua that the plan on the Tianxing Sect's side had concluded, and there was no need to continue acting, so as to avoid exposure.
After dealing with the Tianxing Sect side, Niu Youdao’s interlocking stratagem began moving to the next part. Following the direction pointed out by Xu Fuhua, he led his people to search for the Wei State cultivators. A day later, they finally encountered a group of Wei State disciples.
Niu Youdao proactively came forward to ask about their leading elder, Yu Xianding. The Wei State disciples were extremely defensive. Everyone knew Niu Youdao was being targeted by the Ethereal Pavilion; if pushed to a dead end, the matter of robbing resources and killing people could entirely happen.
Niu Youdao understood their thoughts, so he only smiled: "I am not yet foolish enough to touch a team that clearly has an elder backing them up from behind."
He only asked for Yu Xianding's whereabouts and left it at that. After a moment of hesitation, the disciples still pointed him in a direction. Niu Youdao immediately led his people away. His true purpose was not to find Yu Xianding, but rather to use the Wei State's forces to locate more of his spies. Inside the Sacred Realm, the various forces all chose to disperse to search for Spiritual Seeds. The scope was too wide to gather entirely in one place; running back and forth would be an extreme waste of time.
Therefore, the elders of various countries usually designated an approximate direction for the next assembly, and after meeting up, they would disperse again. The Wei State disciples did not know exactly where Yu Xianding was, but the place they pointed out was certainly the next assembly location for the Wei State forces. By nightfall, Niu Youdao as expected encountered three Wei State elders. He said he only wished to lodge for a night because he feared being assassinated along the way. Seeing nothing suspicious, the three elders agreed. At night, while gathering firewood, Niu Youdao secretly approached a disciple named Xia Xinru.
He was also Niu Youdao’s spy. Niu Youdao lowered his voice: "When setting out tomorrow, eliminate all the people traveling with you."
Hearing this, Xia Xinru knitted his brows: "Can't do it, I don't have enough strength to kill the entire group."
Niu Youdao replied: "Leave a mark, I will help you."
The next day at noon, following the mark left by Xia Xinru, Niu Youdao tracked them down and directly slaughtered four Wei State disciples. Afterward, he let Xia Xinru return to report the news that they were ambushed by overseas cultivators.
Not long after leaving with Xia Xinru, Niu Youdao encountered a group of Zhao State cultivators. This time, he took out the Ethereal Pavilion uniform he had stolen previously and put it on, pretending to be a person from the Ethereal Pavilion to deceive the Zhao State cultivators. He demanded that they call Chen Zifei to come meet him. After Chen Zifei appeared, everything went exactly the same as before. Slaughtered completely.
However, this time Niu Youdao did not tell Chen Zifei to shift the blame to the overseas crowd. He asked how the current situation of the three Zhao State sects was.
Chen Zifei replied that after losing their elders at the entrance of the Sacred Realm, although the three Zhao State sects still cooperated to handle common matters, they no longer had anyone with enough prestige to decide major affairs.
They had thought about joining other forces but feared being used. Hearing this, Niu Youdao immediately changed his mind. He began to secretly track the movement direction of the Zhao State cultivators through the marks left by Chen Zifei. After a period of time, he finally traced down the whereabouts of the overseas cultivators. However, Niu Youdao was in no rush to appear. He knew the time had not yet come.
Three months later, Niu Youdao’s group of four had nearly searched through all the various forces secretly once over.
This time, the target was Li Yude—a disciple of the Qi Cloud Sect from the Jin State. Everything initially proceeded just like the previous times: eliminate all accompanying persons, then pin the blame on the heads of the overseas crowd.
However, variables also began to appear.
According to Li Yude, the overseas crowd this time was extremely overbearing; after being framed too many times, they would definitely turn back to investigate to the very end. Even he began to fear that he would be exposed, affecting Niu Youdao's plan. Not long after, Niu Youdao discovered that he had lost track of the spy line on the Ling Sect's side. The chain of plans that was originally running smoothly was suddenly cut off midway. Wanting to turn back to find Li Yude was also impossible. Using the old method to contact other spies also failed. Every single time, the rogue cultivators were perfectly fine, while everyone else was completely dead. It defied common sense too much. If it continued, it would easily cause others to discover that someone was intentionally orchestrating a layout from behind.
A few days later, Niu Youdao began using smoke fires to intentionally lure people close, but repeatedly failed. Until one day, while observing from up in a tree, he finally saw a group of overseas cultivators being hunted down by disciples of the seven countries to the point of running like battered dogs. With just one glance, Niu Youdao knew his plan had succeeded.
He immediately took the initiative to meet the leader. That person was named Hong Gaitian. One of the four major leaders of the overseas faction this time. Niu Youdao directly proposed a cooperation. He said he could help the overseas crowd escape from their current predicament.
Right now, the seven countries had secretly united to hunt down the overseas faction; if they didn't cooperate, sooner or later the overseas crowd would be squeezed to death inside the Sacred Realm.
Hong Gaitian asked in a cold voice: "Why did the seven countries suddenly target the overseas faction?"
Niu Youdao replied:
* "Because this time, the timing of the Sacred Realm opening was not right. The war among the seven countries was forced to a halt by the Ethereal Pavilion. Afterward, the Ethereal Pavilion even modified the rules, dragging a massive number of rogue cultivators into the Sacred Realm. According to the Ethereal Pavilion's intent, that number of rogue cultivators must have their numbers trimmed. But upon entering here, the majority of rogue cultivators have already been solicited and absorbed by the seven countries. If too many rogue cultivators die, the strength of the various countries will suffer a loss."
Niu Youdao looked at Hong Gaitian and continued:
* "Therefore, they need a group of scapegoats. You overseas people are the best choice. As long as enough overseas people are killed, then the rogue cultivators of the seven countries won't need to die. If they fail to complete this matter, upon exiting the Sacred Realm, the Ethereal Pavilion will not let the seven countries off easily."
Niu Youdao’s entire previous plan was, in truth, to create a pincer formation.
One side was the seven countries.
The other side was the overseas cultivators.
He secretly used his spies within the various sects to kill the accompanying disciples, and afterward, all of it was blamed on the heads of the overseas crowd. From the Tianxing Sect, to the Wei State, and then to other forces, the news that "overseas cultivators ambushed and killed people" continuously spread out.
Initially, it was just a few small groups hunting them down.
But the further it went, the larger the scale became.
Gradually, all the forces of the seven countries began to regard the overseas crowd as a target that needed to be wiped out. Waiting until the overseas faction was truly backed into a dead end, only then did Niu Youdao appear.
He directly took the matter of "the Ethereal Pavilion wanting to clear out rogue cultivators" as a springboard to persuade Hong Gaitian.
According to his words: this time the Ethereal Pavilion intentionally dragged a massive number of rogue cultivators into the Sacred Realm; if fewer people died, then after going out, the Ethereal Pavilion would not be satisfied.
But the issue was that the majority of rogue cultivators currently had already been absorbed by the seven countries.
Therefore, the seven countries did not want the people of their own factions to die, so there needed to be another crowd to step out and shoulder it instead. And the overseas faction was the most suitable choice.
These words immediately caused Hong Gaitian to understand the logic behind the hunt.
Because everything was entirely logical.
The overseas cultivators originally had no enmity or grudges with the seven countries to the point of being targeted for such frenzied hunting. If there were no underlying interests driving it from behind, this matter could not happen.
At this moment, the second framework part of Niu Youdao’s interlocking stratagem truly kicked off.
He used the status of a person from the seven countries to tell the overseas faction about the internal situation. In reverse, he also used this matter to exchange for the trust of the overseas crowd.
Because currently in the eyes of Hong Gaitian: Niu Youdao was the only person on the seven countries' side willing to tell them the truth, and furthermore possessed extremely vital information; if they cooperated, they could find a path to survival.
As for Niu Youdao, he also needed to borrow the strength of the overseas faction to continue completing his subsequent layout.
From this point in time onward, he began standing between the two factions.
One side was hunting.
One side was being hunted.
As for Niu Youdao, he secretly controlled both sides to proceed in the direction he desired.

However, Hai Wuchang immediately said in a cold voice: "Cooperation? On what basis must we hand over Spiritual Seeds to you to help you take Number 1?"
Niu Youdao was very calm. He said that currently, having even more Spiritual Seeds did not necessarily carry too much meaning. To put it plainly, the final reward still had to rely on the Ethereal Pavilion's ranking to be distributed. The thing Niu Youdao needed was merely the title of Number 1 to keep his life. As for the rewards behind it, he held no interest.

Your Four Seas forces were originally the opponents of the seven countries, correct? But any random country concentrated together is also stronger than you. If inside this Sacred Realm you truly strike too ruthlessly against the seven countries... then after going out, you will certainly be hunted down until you die."

After going out, the seven countries might not necessarily have the free time to take revenge on the entirety of the overseas faction.
