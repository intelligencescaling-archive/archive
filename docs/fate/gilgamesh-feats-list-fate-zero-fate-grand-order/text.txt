**\[Fate/Zero\]**

\[Episode 8-12 - Chapter 17-33\]

Gilgamesh asked Kirei to investigate the reasons for the participation of all the other masters in the war, saying he just wanted to know if any of them was more interesting than Tokiomi.

But \[In Episode 12 - Chapter 33\] it turns out that Gilgamesh wanted Kirei himself to see the truth about himself.

Gilgamesh : "Therefore Kirei, having you tell about what you saw, and heard, and your understanding of things from your own lips... already has considerable meaning for me"

"The thing you spoke about the most is the thing that most captivated your -interest-"

"People are toys, lives are stories"

"First of all, let's set aside the person you've deliberately avoided speaking about. An interest that one is aware of simply a fixation, no more"

"In your case, we should pay more attention to the interests you're unaware of"

Gilgamesh also noted how passionately Kirei spoke of Berserker’s master \[Kariya Matou\] since he spoke in details on his situation and then Kirei answered saying that “his situation is the most complex” on which Gilgamesh corrected him saying :

"Hmph, No. It was only in regards to this man that you ordered that Assassin to investigate until his situation could be seen to be complex"

"In accordance with your unconscious interest"

Making Kirei question his own judgment, until Gilgamesh asked him to imagine a scenario where Kariya survives to the end, and after imagining it Kirei asked what is the point on which he said “None”.

And Gilgamesh explained that all of his speech was simply to show Kirei that he finally felt the joy of doing something.

Gilgamesh : "Think about it, the fact that Kotomine Kirei never recognized the pointlessness in that speculation"

"Don't you think there is clear and unshakable meaning in that?"

Kirei : -Sigh- "Explain it to me Archer"

Gilgamesh : "If...say... you had the same presented with the same problem, but about the other masters... you would have recognized the pointlessness of it immediately, and refused to do it since it was useless"

"But that's not what happened with Kariya. You abandoned you usual no-nonsense way of thinking and gave yourself up to drawn out, worthless fantasizing"

"You forgot that it had no meaning, it was in vain, but not painful"

"In other words, you did because you found it -Diverting-"

"Let's celebrate Kirei, you finally understood the meaning of -amusement-"

On which Kirei denied his words saying this joy is sin, until he was proven wrong by the grail itself giving him his command spells back meaning all of Gilgamesh speech was right, which was anticipated by Gilgamesh.
