Introduction:
The Demon God King (魔神王, Majin-ō?), the aggregate form of Solomon's seventy-two Daemons. To begin with, the 72 Demons were "a system to efficiently promote proper reason" invented by the founder of (human) Magecraft, Solomon.

Goetia was originally created as a magical ritual to look after mankind after Solomon's death, the Human Order Correction Ritual. Said system grieved and raged against the relationship between King Solomon and humans, becoming a curse that takes action to conquer “the imperfection of humans” that King Solomon continued to ignore. It eventually changed into the Human Order Incineration Ritual. A magecraft with its own will that was devised in order to attain true wisdom, and is now attempting to reach the origin while making use of the entire human history.

After King Solomon’s death, the 72 demons that were sealed away inside his corpse awakened to their own ideology, swindled Solomon’s name (although, from their point of view, they are the true King of Magecraft Solomon), and embarked on a new venture to convert the entire mankind into energy. And thus, Goetia resumed activity by making King Solomon’s corpse into his nest, but there was a great dilemma in his way of being.

In its original Greek, the term "GoetiaWP" refers to the act of sorcery itself; Goetia was the original Thaumaturgical Foundation used in Modern Magecraft. The genetic curse that he implanted in the seventy-two Thaumaturgical lineages that hosted his Demon Gods was the basis from which the first Thaumaturgical Crests developed. Said Magic Crests were from the outset a means by which he connected his existence with that of his agents.

Fgo Fourth Singularity: The Mist City: London Londinium Knight
[Section 13]
Solomon, the King of Kings and Grand Caster, manifests as the ultimate authority over the 72 Demon Gods, addressing Chaldea and its Master, Fujimaru. He observes humanity with disdain, labeling them foolish, stagnant, and incapable of meaningful progress. Though he possesses unparalleled vision, able to see all possible futures, even he struggles to perceive Chaldea, and thus the human survivors remain alive—pitifully defiant in the void of history.

He asserts his absolute superiority, explaining that he revived as a Heroic Spirit of his own volition and is unbound by any Master’s control. Solomon reveals his grand plan: the eradication of humanity across all eras using his Demon Gods, anchored in time and wielded through his Third Noble Phantasm, Ars Almadel Salomonis, whose light carries the destructive power of millions of holy swords.

Despite his overwhelming power, he acknowledges the Chaldeans’ persistence, allowing them to survive for now. He contrasts the degraded human summoning system with the original Heroic Spirit Ritual meant to protect humanity, emphasizing the gulf between his freedom and the limitations of ordinary Servants.

Throughout, Solomon mocks human fear, attachment, and hope, questioning why they cling to life when death is inevitable. He exults in the destruction he can enact, describing it as both joy and the greatest form of salvation—humans exist only to be utilized or discarded according to his will. He issues a chilling warning to Fujimaru: surrender is the easiest path, for resistance is ultimately futile against the King of Kings and the inexorable might of his Demon Gods.

Fgo Final Singularity - The Grand Temple of Time: Solomon
[Section 1]
Burdened by their boundless clairvoyance, Goetia confesses that they can no longer bear the endless slaughter and ceaseless screaming echoing through past and future alike. As king, they cannot turn away; the horrors of the world are forced upon them, etched indelibly into their memory. Disgust and revulsion grow with each revelation, until a single conclusion forms: nothing can be fixed so long as life continues as it is. History, ecosystems, even time itself are insufficient targets for erasure — the planet must be rebuilt from true nothingness.

To achieve this, they plan to incinerate all life, harvesting the immense energy produced not just from the present, but from the past, present, and future simultaneously. Three thousand years of the planet’s most potent energy will be gathered, bound, and controlled to fuel this recreation. Across the land they scatter portents and kindred agents, leaving messages of their indignation and a path for their successors to follow. They will construct a temple and weave bands of light, gathering every resource and every second of eternity to destroy and forget humanity. At the end of this path lies the Throne of the King of Mages — the Final Singularity where they await.

[section 13]
Once, there was a king, omnipotent and omniscient, whose powers were granted by God. His eyes could pierce past and future alike, knowing all that could be known. We were his shadow — his guardian spirit — and we had no choice but to synchronize with his vision. Through his eyes, we saw everything: sorrow upon sorrow, betrayals and pillaging, countless ends.

We have seen enough. Even gods can find no conclusion on this planet except annihilation. We no longer care for humans or their future. What we seek now is a perfect environment to raise a true, healthy sapient being. This planet is a mistake, a madness built on life with a fixed duration.

I will go back — 4.6 billion years into the past — to witness the birth of this celestial body. I will absorb its energy and make myself into a new planet, rewriting the book of Genesis and creating a world without death. This is our grand deed.

We did not destroy mankind out of hatred. We needed energy to travel to the past and perform the infinite, instantaneous tuning required to control a planet’s birth. Such a feat demands 3,000 years’ worth of magical energy stored by all sapient beings. By converting all human history — from 1000 B.C. to A.D. 2018 — into magical energy, we gained what was necessary to reach the beginning.

Humans were fuel, nothing more than the propulsion that would launch us to genesis itself. The Throne you see before you is the convergence of all your lives — our bands of light are the infinite accumulation of human history. They cannot be overcome by mortals. This is the scale at which we move: to remake the planet itself.
