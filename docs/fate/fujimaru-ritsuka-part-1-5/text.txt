[Chapter 13]

Wu Zetian decided to kill Ritsuka and his team [Astolfo + D’eon] and the fight started with Wu Zetian having an advantage thanks to Caster helping her, and Wu Zetian used one of her spells to disguise as one of her soldiers making her almost impossible to locate while fighting her.

And since fighting all her soldiers will be a waste of time and most likely end up making them lose a lot of energy, Ritsuka realized they need to locate Wu Zetain immediately and kill her, therefore he ordered young Fergus to use his Noble Phantasm to crush the whole floor, and ordered Astolfo to save the hostages and ordered D’eon to kill Wu Zetian.

Ritsuka idea of this :

1- Fergus’s noble phantasm is able to destroy the whole floor easily even if he is in his child version.
2- Wu Zetain’s soldiers aren’t as powerful as her, meaning once a powerful attack comes up they won’t be able to defend themselves unlike Wu Zetian, meaning she will be the only one to survive the attack.

Ritsuka predicted that she will avoid the attack to not get hurt and he knows that D’eon will understand his plan once Fergus crushes the floor and sees that one of the soldiers survived and jumped meaning that soldier is without a doubt Wu Zetian since she is the only one capable of surviving that.

And D’eon successfully understood that and stabbed Wu Zetian who jumped to the air to avoid the attack while she was still in disguise, marking the success of Ritsuka’s tactic.

[Note above all of that, Wu Zetian is historically one of the smartest and cunning Chinese figures, making this one of the best Ritsuka’s outsmarting feats]
