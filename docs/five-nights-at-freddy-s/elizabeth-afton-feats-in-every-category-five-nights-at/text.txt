Elizabeth Afton (Gamecanon & Gametheory) Feats in Every Category

I’m only accounting for categories in which she has feats and the theories I agree with.

Side Note: Elizabeth is Circus Baby. Common sense tells us this, since we know that every child who dies inside an animatronic possesses it due to remnant—and Elizabeth died inside Circus Baby, as confirmed by the minigame and circus baby’s blueprints, and is therefore Circus Baby. Additionally, Circus Baby having Elizabeth’s green eyes after killing Elizabeth obviously means she is possessed by Elizabeth. There is no Circus Baby AI; in her own words, she likes to pretend. She is pretending to be an AI so Michael doesn’t immediately try to retrieve her, allowing her to use him to escape. She also refers to Scraptrap as Daddy because she knows he is her father. Remember, Scrap Baby isn’t Ennard, and Circus Baby’s AI would only know William Afton without the suit through facial recognition—so this has to be Elizabeth.

Strategy Deception Manipulation Thinking Trap Setting & Planning: She needed a way to get herself and the other Funtime animatronics out of the Circus Baby Entertainment and Rental bunker, so she devised her first plan. She convinced the animatronics to allow themselves, on Night Four and Night Five, to have their endoskeletons dismantled by the Scooper, one by one, so they could merge into Ennard—a form slim enough to fit inside Michael after his internal organs were removed by the Scooper. With that achieved, she moved on to her second plan: luring Michael into the Scooping Room. She would do this after her Ennard plan had succeeded by lying that Ballora was pursuing him and offering to help him evade her—but only if he followed her instructions into the Scooping Room, under the pretense that Ballora feared the room so he would be safe there. To make her deception credible, she had previously exploited Michael’s trust: she guided him through confrontations with the other Funtime animatronics, which she had deliberately made appear hostile, acting as a white knight to ensure he would trust her. She also made sure Michael beforehand would remove her chip, allowing her to communicate with him while in the Scooper control room so she could guide him and prevent him from becoming suspicious. After recognising that Michael Afton was the one working, she deliberately acted as the artificial intelligence of Circus Baby, pretending that Elizabeth was inside but not the one speaking, and that she didn’t recognise him. Knowing that Michael was sent by William—since he would never normally work as a technician—she realised William was trying to retrieve her. She then made Michael believe he would have to comply with her to see Elizabeth again, so he could retrieve her for William.

EM: After seeing her brother for the first time in weeks or years, she composed herself convincing him that she was just an artificial intelligence that didn’t recognise him.

VSI & Learning Ability: After being separated from Ennard, she rebuilt herself a body using metal scraps. With zero prior engineering knowledge and while learning how to assemble components for the first time, she managed to put each piece of metal together to turn herself into Scrap Baby.

Stress Tolerance Mental Fortitude Determination & Perseverance: She was abused by her father. She was manipulated by him into making Circus Baby her favorite animatronic because he made it for her, then endured watching her father neglect her and forbid her from seeing the very animatronic he made her love. She then endured being crushed to death by her own favorite animatronic as her father watched, followed by total isolation in the bunker as Circus Baby for weeks or years. Yet despite all of that, she is still determined to escape.

Reasoning: She deduced that Michael was sent by William to retrieve her due to Michael’s odd choice of career. Even though she was wrong, she deduced that William Afton had lured the animatronics to the Freddy Fazbear Pizza Place and hired Michael Afton so he could use the animatronics to get rid of him. Which is why he was specifically luring the animatronics to the building where Michael worked—because the animatronics would mistake Michael for William, who they want revenge against.
