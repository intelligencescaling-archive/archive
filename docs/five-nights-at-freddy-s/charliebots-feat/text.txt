Charlie Bots analysis
This document idea is to explain the charlie bots that appear in the novels of the universe of five nights at freddy’s, this animatronics were created by henry emily.

The charlie bots
 In different parts of the novels we can see charlie bleed, she needs to drink and eat to stay alive, she needs to do basic human necessities, this would mean henry managed to build an animatronic that can’t be distinguished than real humans, This charlie is able to grow muscle, forget and remember things, she has a memory, she can feel human emotions, she can feel love (she falls in love with some of the main characters), she can feel fear (the sequence where she is being followed by foxy), she can feel happiness (when she reencounters with her childhood friends), she can feel nostalgia (when she remembers the old freddy location), she can feel anger and hate (she is shown to be angry and hate william afton while the story happens), this means that Henry while creating this animatronics was able to create human emotions into this animatronic and memory system inside this, where charlie will get and lose new memories, exactly as a human, this shows that the understanding of anatomy and mechanics of henry was unrealistic inside of his own universe.

Charlie memories
Charlie explains and is shown to remember almost everything before her real version died, this means that henry managed to replicate memories of another person into an animatronic, this would mean henry knew everything charlie was doing while she was with him or when she wasn’t, this also means henry had to visualize everything from another perspective with a different height while analyzing every detail in every room he was and every room he passed by, every place charlie was and know where charlie was in every moment while maintaining every detail of everything, it was stated by charlie that henry sometimes ignored her because of the focus he had in his job, that would mean that henry knew everything charlie was doing while maintaining a perfect focus inside of his job, charlie described it as ‘’it looked like he was in another reality’’, henry managed to open the freddy’s restaurant while taking care of charlie, this means that henry would need to have a perfect memory, keep perfect details of every room and everything he sees and visualize every place where charlie was with a different perspective, different height and different movements than his own self.

Henry emotional understanding of charlie
While creating this animatronics, Henry introduces on them the memories of every one of them with the help of someone (who will help him after his own death), every memory that charlie has is filled with feelings she feel in the moment, happiness, sadness, anger and more, this means henry knew how charlie felt in every moment and how her entire personality was, this needs a really in depth analysis of her own emotions and actions, a complete understanding of charlie in any moment to be able to copy that into something else.  This would also mean understanding things of another human emotions as he can replicate the human mind in its entirety, being able to create emotions such as love, an emotion charlie never felt to another person (romantically), he managed to create a personality that could evolve based on charlie original personality
