William Afton feat list

William Afton intelligence feats (Game Canon):

**"It is not your flesh that sustains me. It is your FEAR.”**

(Dittophobia) He planned ten years ahead, predicting that Rory would escape his dittophobia experiment. To maintain control, he created a tape that manipulated Rory into willingly returning to the chamber after his escape and reactivating the gas. The tape would automatically rewind if Rory tried to flee again, making his escape an integral part of the experiment’s design and trapping him in an endless cycle. This feat showcases \[PS (Problem Solving) CT (Creative Thinking) EU (Emotional Understanding) LT (Linear Thinking) Foresight and ST (Strategic Thinking)\].

(FNAF SL) He designed and built the Funtime Animatronics to lure and kill children when they were alone, hiding the bodies within themselves. He also designed and built the Scooper to grant immortality through remnant and installed security cameras to watch over Rory from a secret room in the Entertainment and Rental. He additionally made these Funtime Animatronics after working with Henry Emily for years possibly learning from him and surpassing him. This feat showcases \[LA (Learning Ability) LC (Learning Capability) VSI (Visual Spatial index) EU (Emotional Understanding) CT (Creative Thinking) ST (Strategic Thinking) and LT (Linear Thinking)\].

(SOTM) He manipulated an unknown MCM higher-up into betraying Edwin Murray by providing him with staff contacts in exchange for a management position at Freddy’s. This allowed him to manipulate the staff into selling Edwin’s work to Fazbear Entertainment by promising them the possibility of a job at Freddy’s. This feat showcases \[EU (Emotional Understanding) ST (Strategic Thinking) PM (Psychological Manipulation) DIM (Direct Manipulation) IM (Individual Manipulation) MM (Mass Manipulation)\].

(FFPS) He manipulated Susie into following him by preying on her curiosity, convincing her that her dead dog was somehow alive and waiting for her in the saferoom, knowing she wouldn’t be able to resist checking. This feat showcases \[CT (Creative Thinking) EU (Emotional Understanding) PM (Psychological Manipulation) IM (Individual Manipulation) DIM (Direct Manipulation) AS (Acting Skills) LT (Linear Thinking) and ST (Strategic Thinking)\].

(FFPS) After being springlocked for 30 years, he still remembers his past and speaks to Michael: “Fascinating, what they have become,” referring to the Missing Children haunting the animatronics. “You may not recognize me at first, but I assure you, it’s still me,” he tells Michael, revealing that he is still his father. This feat showcases \[LTM (Long Term Memory) and SA (Self Awareness)\].

(UCN) He defeated 50/20 Mode, which involves overcoming 50 animatronics, each with unique mechanics, all set to their maximum aggressiveness simultaneously. This feat showcases \[CPI (Cognitive Proficiency Index) PSI (Processing Speed Index) WMI (Working Memory Index) PR (Pattern Recognition) and Adaptability\].

(SOTM) He tricked his contractor, Edwin Murray, into bankruptcy to take over his costume manor by constantly changing designs at the last minute until he couldn’t afford the costs. This feat showcases \[CT (Creative Thinking) LT (Linear Thinking) and ST (Strategic Thinking)\].

(FNAF 2) in Save Them he was a Night Guard most likely under a false name that picked more victims using a Springlock Suit he covered up the murders afterwards as shown on night 1 the bloody table from Party Room 4 is missing. This feat showcases \[CT (Creative Thinking) LT (Linear Thinking) EU (Emotional Understanding) PM (Psychological Manipulation) Deception and ST (Strategic Thinking)\]

(FNAF 2 + FFPS) He murdered Charlotte Emily in a drive by when no one was watching and made sure to depose of her body in the back alley. This feat showcases \[SIA (Situational Awareness) and LGT (Logical Thinking)\].

(FNAF SL) He successfully covered up Elizabeth’s death by disguising it as a gas leak incident, ensuring the facility’s closure without arousing any suspicion from the public or the authorities. This feat showcases \[SOS (Social Skills) Deception and MM (Mass Manipulation)\].

(FNAF 3) He exploited Shadow Freddy to lure the old animatronics into the saferoom using their curiosity so he could dismantle them. \[CT (Creative Thinking) LT (Linear Thinking) ST (Strategic Thinking) EU (Emotional Understanding) IDM (Indirect Manipulation) and PM (Psychological Manipulation)\].

He used Spring Bonnie which concealed his identity footprints and fingerprints, to gain children’s trust, and lure them into the safe room to kill them—a blind spot for the cameras hiding the corpses into the animatronics so the authorities couldn’t find them. He also framed the incident on someone else to deliberately get the case closed. This feat showcases \[CT (Creative Thinking) EU (Emotional Understanding) PM (Psychological Manipulation) MM (Mass Manipulation) DIM (Direct Manipulation) LT (Linear Thinking) and ST (Strategic Thinking)\].

(FFPS) He conceals himself within the Nedd Bear animatronic, waiting patiently for it to be purchased. Once it is bought, he reveals himself as a hidden opponent, turning the buyer’s acquisition into an unexpected confrontation. This feat showcases \[CT (Creative Thinking) LT (Linear Thinking) and ST (Strategic Thinking)\].

(Security Breach) He manipulates Vanessa into making false accusations against her mother during a custody battle to win. This feat showcases \[DIM (Direct Manipulation) and ST (Strategic Thinking)\].
