William Afton framed Jeremy Fitzgerald (FNAF 2 Theory)

After Jeremy Fitzgerald is hired, the DCI takes place. I find this to be no coincidence, but rather a deliberate attempt by William to frame Jeremy—and here’s why:

William changes his perfect *modus operandi*. The MCI takes place before the DCI, and we know that during the MCI, William lured children while wearing the Spring Bonnie suit, killed them in the backroom blind spot, and hid their bodies inside the animatronics. However, in the DCI, William appears to have killed in open areas, possibly while wearing Spring Bonnie or Fredbear, remained at the crime scene as a night guard, and did not hide the bodies in the animatronics—almost as if he wanted the crime to be discovered.

But why would William want people to find out about this crime? Because he committed it under a new alias—a new identity. It is most likely that he lost weight for this identity using novel parallels. I also believe he acted under a new identity because, on Night Five, William is most likely one of the suspicious previous employees, as he may have already been convicted or charged based on the MCI poster. This would mean he had to appear as a different person. That person would most likely be someone already working for Fazbear Entertainment—namely, Jeremy Fitzgerald.

You see, the DCI is specifically planned to occur as soon as Jeremy begins his night shifts, and William carries it out while posing as a night guard—the same position Jeremy currently holds.

On Night Five, Ralph tells Jeremy to stay put because the building is on lockdown. I believe this lockdown exists because William Afton is intentionally keeping Jeremy inside the pizzeria for longer so that, when law enforcement—whose investigation began on Night Four—moves in, they conclude that “Jeremy Fitzgerald” was responsible for the DCI and arrest him. William may even be using the lockdown to reinforce this narrative, as Ralph is surprised to find Jeremy still inside on Night Six, despite the investigation presumably being underway and the location already being closed.

The one flaw in William’s plan is that he did not anticipate law enforcement failing to conclude that Jeremy was responsible, because they did not follow the circumstantial logic of access + timing + presence = guilt.

TLDR: William commits the DCI at the exact time Jeremy starts working, while posing as a night guard—the same role Jeremy has. He remains at the crime scene so the police can see that “Jeremy” was present, including on every night the DCI was being planned, according to Ralph’s recordings. So when the police look at who started working right before the murders? who was there at the crime scene? who had access to the backroom? who was always there during the planning? Jeremy fits perfectly.
