When it comes to purposefully articulating and curating feats or examples of intelligence within media, some authors struggle within the realms of methodology or have an over reliance on narrative and statements, but Scott Cawthon had his pinnacle within William afton showing not only in detail, but through relying on you as the readers own intelligence to logically process and understand the layering and depravity of Aftons inherent narcissistic and sadistic nature and the scope of his autonomy and how he utilises to compose fluid and horrifying strategies, creating multiple symphonies of deception and manipulation asking you a simple question of did you understand what he's conveying, what afton did, how he did it and the underlying dimensions and layers of invincibility and contingencies he had left in his psychological deconstruction and brainwashing on Rory's senses, sense of being and his identity.

Scott left a beautifully layered strategy and plan and gave you the right clues to piece together the monstrosity that is called William Afton and what he is truly capable of in the scope of his mind and ability to understand the soul and the nature of people, playing "God" for his own twisted sense of being, perfectly manufacturing an image of a theatrical genius that sees those around him as nothing more than caricatures and results of his own machinations and mastery over despair and the human heart painting the image of a genius beyond his time, and those who are still human in nature and in essence essentially giving Afton the imagery akin not to just an evil genius or generic edge lord with cool feats but a methodical psychopath who truly believes he is above those around him. A human who doesn't recognise his heart beat which is why he fluidly executed feats like this.

For context and initial analysis and insight into these feat, Rory is physically [17] whilst he is mentally reduced to that of an adolescence, a boy. No older than 10 in his mental capacity and ability to reason and grasp novel concepts and form acute and knowledgeable insights and reasoning onto the scenarios and things surrounding, but it is the [How] Rory was reduced or rather kept in this state that adds on layers and aspects of manipulation, planning, foresight and physiological understanding of this boy that adds onto the depth of aftons showcase and methods in which he took to get here.

William's extensive and meticulous planning and degradation of Rory's sense of self and his view on his self has gone on for so long that the boy can not recognise his own age, carefully and masterfully stringing the boy along like a puppet warping his view making him none the wiser to the truth of his own body, his own conscience and autonomy completely shutting him off from the outside world and any lines of help, he even professionally makes hallucinations based off of gas that targets the neural pathways of the brain. Separating reality from the things Rory's mind will construct out of fear and due to being drugged. He articulates the whole thing to establish uncertainty and fear within Rory because he understands Rory will eventually want to escape.

After the gas begins to wear off, Rory sees the machinery that controls the mannequins that give him intense fear, horror, and vivid psychological reactions and terror, the very things that reduced him to bordering on the edge of induced insanity, that mocked his innate terror and lack of knowledge and grows a desire to escape, it's natural isn't it?
