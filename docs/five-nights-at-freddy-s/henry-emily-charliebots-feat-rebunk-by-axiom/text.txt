1. Lets introduce solipsism rq:

What is solipsism?

Ramona’s argument basically argues “since we might be perceiving a false reality, nothing within that reality can be measured.” Which is textbook solipsism as denying an object because “reality might be altered”. Based on their logic, if we apply this to any story: No action is trustworthy, no fear is valid, and no behavior can be interpreted. But the story still continues, and the narrative structure and in-universe consistency still allow us to measure performance even within those altered perceptions. Even if under illusions, characters respond to internal consistency.
This is basically philosophical skepticism to absurdity: If carried to its conclusion, nothing can ever be assessed, not even the illusion disc themselves.

2. Mathematical jargon/counterpoint: observable output /=/ invalid input
Let:
R(x) = Robot’s emotional behavior function
I(x) = Illusion disc’s visual modifier
O(x) = I(R(x)) = What the reader perceives.

Ramona is saying:
“We can’t trust O(x), so R(x) is invalid”

But this ignores a vital rule in applied in mathematics: “If Multiple independent characters interact meaningfully with O(x) and respond consistently, then R(x) is functionally real in universe even if masked by I(x). As basically this implies that charliebots responses are narratively invariant across the illusion disc and her actions still affect the story logically and casually, which means theyre valid to scale.

Extra jargon point:
Lets have:
        Illusion discs = filter f
        Real Emotional behavior = function e(t) over time
        Emotional impact on the other characters = measurable variable m
If- m = f(e(t)), and m /=/ 0, then the behavior is real enough to affect the system and concludes that:
Observable -> valid. Impactful -> canonical, Illusion /=/ nullification.

3. Syllogism overview
P1: If a character’s behavior consistently affects others, follows internal logic, and has measurable consequences, it is functionally valid for scaling.
P2: Charliebots actions meet those criteria REGARDLESS of visual distortion via illusion discs.
C: Therefore, her feats and by extension. Henry’s emotional programming are valid for scale.
