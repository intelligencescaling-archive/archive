Henry Emily (Gamecanon & Gametheory) Feats in Every Category

I’m only accounting for categories in which he has feats and the theories I agree with.

VSI: He created the blueprints for Lefty, meaning he fully visualised every individual component in his mind, assembling them piece by piece. He created the Freddy Fazbear’s Pizza Place originally visualising it in his head.

Strategy Deception Manipulation Thinking Trap Setting & Planning: He created Freddy Fazbear’s Pizza Place, hired Michael as the manager, and lured the animatronics to the back alleyway by creating mimicked children’s cries and a mimicked William Afton voice signal, making the animatronics believe William Afton was hurting more children so they would go to stop him. He also hired Michael because the animatronics would mistake him for William, further reinforcing the deception that William was there. He then had Michael salvage the animatronics to “help” his restaurant, when in reality the animatronics were being salvaged for his plan, and he knew they would go after Michael every night. So, on the final night, when all the animatronics were salvaged, he activated high-powered remote burners built into the floor to set the entire building on fire and free the animatronic spirits. He carried out this entire plan after previously discovering Afton’s research on remnant and how its effects could be nullified at high temperatures. He created the Security Puppet to monitor children wearing security wristbands and intervene if someone attempted to take the children away, in order to prevent child kidnappings. He built Lefty to specifically capture the Puppet/Charlotte Emily, just in case she wasn’t lured by the mimicked children’s cries or mimicked William Afton voice. He programmed Lefty to use a special lullaby through its microphone and to emit the lullaby’s frequency from its left eye, allowing her to be lured from afar. She would be drawn into its internal compartment through the mouth, the suit would be sealed shut, and steady voltage would be applied to the microphone to keep the lullaby playing and pacify her. Lefty would then transport her to the Pizza Place and extract her from the suit when the fire started, freeing her spirit.

EU Hot Reading & Foresight: He understood Elizabeth enough to predict she would be the one giving a speech in his pre-recorded recordings. He also understood William enough to predict he would knowingly enter the trap out of curiosity, even though he made the signal as William Afton in his pre-recorded recordings. Although he originally created an escape plan for Michael, he understood him enough to predict that he wouldn’t want to escape in his pre-recorded recordings. He also predicted the potential failure of his plan if Michael simply threw the animatronics back into the alleyway, and created a pre-recorded recording in advance in case that happened—firing Michael and hiring a new manager who would salvage the animatronics as a failsafe for his plan. He predicted the possibility of child kidnapping in his restaurant, so he created the Security Puppet to monitor children wearing security wristbands and intervene if someone tried to lure them away. He predicted the possibility that his daughter, the Puppet/Charlotte Emily, might be lured there by the children’s cries, since it is her nature to protect the innocent. However, he also anticipated that she might not be drawn in by the mimicked children’s cries or mimicked William Afton voice, and thus he built Lefty just in case.

Reasoning: He deduced that William Afton was the culprit behind the MCI because he knew William understood how to wear a Springlock suit from their old Fredbear and Spring Bonnie performing days—specifically the Spring Bonnie suit, which was used in the MCI—which he would find suspicious. He also knew that William understood child psychology from those same performing days, which the MCI culprit used to lure the children away. By putting this together, he realised that William was the one behind it all.

LA: After Edwin Murray was no longer available for contracting, he had to make Fredbear himself. With zero prior engineering knowledge, he learned how to assemble components managing to create the Springlock suit known as Fredbear on presumably his first attempt.
