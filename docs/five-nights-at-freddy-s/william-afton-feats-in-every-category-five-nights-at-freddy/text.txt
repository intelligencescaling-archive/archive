William Afton (Gamecanon & Gametheory) Feats in Every Category

I’m only accounting for categories in which he has feats and the theories I agree with.

VSI: He created the blueprints for the Funtime animatronics, meaning he fully visualised every individual component in his mind, assembling them piece by piece. He also visualised the Circus Baby Entertainment and Rental bunker to add a secret room that you can enter using the code 1983 on the keypad.

Strategy Deception Manipulation Thinking Trap Setting Trap Evasion WMI & Planning: He deliberately used the Spring Bonnie suit during the MCI to conceal his identity on camera footage and also mask his footprints and fingerprints, while simultaneously exploiting the fact that Spring Bonnie was a familiar, friendly children’s animatronic to gain the children’s trust and lure them into the safe room—a known blind spot in the camera system—where he killed them and hid their bodies inside the animatronics to prevent discovery by the police. He also carefully timed everything, ensuring the children were isolated and their parents were completely out of sight before acting. After finalising the Funtime animatronics, he needed test subjects to see whether they would function the way he intended: luring victims to a private location, murdering them, and concealing their corpses inside themselves. To accomplish this, he opened Circus Baby’s Pizza World. However, he also wanted to witness the process firsthand in case an animatronic failed offscreen. Prior to opening the location, while working on Circus Baby, he told Elizabeth that he was making Circus Baby specifically for her. This caused her to grow attached to the animatronic and beg to visit Circus Baby’s Pizza World when it opened. Once she was at the Pizza World, he deliberately denied her entry to see the animatronic, making her want to approach it even more—especially since it had been made for her. He then walked away, allowing Elizabeth and Circus Baby to assume he had left. Elizabeth then snuck over to Circus Baby, while he watched from afar as Circus Baby killed her and concealed her body inside itself. After confirming that the process worked, he shut down the Pizza World, lying that it was due to gas leaks. Murdering children was not his primary goal; harvesting remnant to study it was. Elizabeth’s remnant, along with that of the other children taken that day, was more than sufficient. But why couldn’t he do this at Freddy’s? Because he was already under suspicion there for the MCI. After covering it up, he would enter the bunker again and study how remnant works in order to build the Scooper and make himself immortal, which is why he doesn’t die in FNAF 3—because he used the Scooper beforehand. He also used the Funtime animatronics for this murder massacre because it was easier, and he could shift the blame to faulty programming if one of the animatronics were caught. He executes the DCI when Jeremy starts working, disguising himself with a fake badge, weight loss, and a false alias to impersonate Jeremy, framing him as the culprit behind both the MCI and DCI, to tarnish Freddy’s reputation and get a clean streak for himself. He even lingers at the crime scene so the police will see Jeremy was present, including on every night the DCI was planned, as confirmed by Ralph’s recordings. So when the police ask: who started working right before the murders? Who was at the crime scene? Who had access to the backroom? Who was always present during the planning? Jeremy fits flawlessly. He knows he needs to get Circus Baby/Elizabeth Afton retrieved from the bunker, but also knows she would try to escape through him if he went down himself. So, he instead sends Michael Afton to retrieve her, so Michael could then report back what happened if she escaped, or she, as Michael comes to him—and he retrieves her without having to do any work himself. He tricked his contractor, Edwin Murray, into bankruptcy to take over his Costume Manor by constantly changing Fazbear Entertainment designs at the last minute until he couldn’t afford the costs, so he could then purchase the Costume Manor for himself. He also took it a step further: he manipulated a head administrator at MCM to give him MCM worker’s contacts in exchange for management over Freddy Fazbear’s Pizzeria, allowing him to message them and manipulate them into leaking MCM information for better pay and positions at Freddy Fazbear’s Pizzeria, enabling him to gain more assets and, ultimately, acquire Edwin’s house as well. He planned Charlotte Emily’s murder. Knowing he needed to remove Charlie from camera coverage and neutralise the Security Puppet, he bribed other children with money to trap the Puppet inside it’s box by stacking a second box on top, preventing it from intervening, and had the children lock Charlie outside the restaurant. This allowed him to pull up in his purple 1970 Dodge Challenger from outside, kill her, and dispose of the body in the back alley—since the outside of the restaurant had no cameras. The rain further worked in his favor, washing away most of the physical evidence. He realised Susie’s dog could be the perfect way to lure her during the MCI, so he ran over the dog after luring it to his car with a honk, allowing him to exploit the depression Susie would feel afterward to lure her to the backroom. He killed the board members through the use of the Funtime animatronics after they discovered the Circus Baby blueprints. He lured the animatronics to the backroom using Shadow Freddy.

PSI BKI Decision Making Intuition & Spatial Intelligence: He piloted a purple 1970 Dodge Challenger 200 miles per hour through rain while intoxicated, threading through traffic and narrowly avoiding every oncoming vehicle.

Stress Tolerance Mental Fortitude Determination & Perseverance: He watched his own son kill his other son with an animatronic he helped create. He watched his own daughter be slaughtered by an animatronic he created just for testing. He was springlocked to death, having to slowly endure every part of his body being impaled and bludgeoned. He lived in a near-eternal nightmare, constantly dying over and over until escaping through the 50/20 mode. He watched his own son try to kill him by burning the restaurant down. And he watched his old business partner try to kill him the same way. Yet he is not insane and is still determined to keep coming back.

EM: He composed himself in front of a Fazbear Entertainment board member, listing off other details about Circus Baby to shift their focus, even though he knew they were aware of the Circus Baby blueprints and, therefore, knew about his plan to use the Funtime animatronics to recreate the MCI. He killed the board members afterward through the use of the Funtime animatronics—not in the way he recreates the MCI, but more as in forced hanging, as we know.

EU: He understood that Susie cares about her dog from her previous visits to Freddy’s, and that it would be a good way to lure her during the MCI. So in advance, he ran over the dog after luring it to his car with a car honk. Then, during the MCI, he exploited the depression she was feeling from the past event to make her think it didn’t happen and that the dog was still alive, allowing him to lure her to the backroom.

Reasoning: He saw through Henry Emily’s Freddy Fazbear’s Pizza Place trap because Henry was luring the animatronics through a call as William Afton, and he is obviously William Afton. He knew he didn’t make the call, so he realised Henry was impersonating him to set it up. He specifically knew it was Henry because it came from a Freddy’s location he knew he didn’t create, and the only genius who could have created a new location while being involved with Fazbear Entertainment was Henry.
