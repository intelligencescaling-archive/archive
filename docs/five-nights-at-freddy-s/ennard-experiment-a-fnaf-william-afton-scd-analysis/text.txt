Ennard experiment | A FNAF/William Afton SCD analysis

***FOLLOW ME MINIGAME: the birth of Ennard experiment***

This is generally a complex situation to talk about, especially due to my history behind my analysis of this minigame, and me trying to figure out what was going on. My main goal was trying to punch out the anti-feat for Afton here, but obviously I kinda just accepted that this is clearly an anti-feat and can’t be seen as anything else without reaching to the stars for that miracle. But, in my journey of trying to figure out “follow me” beyond this, I ended up with a conclusion that I’m happy with. But before we get into that, why is Follow me important? Well, besides being the birth of Springtrap, this is also the minigame most believe is the origins for the very popular moltenMCI to be from, which means that Afton in follow me was breaking them down for the purpose of melting them into remnant to inject into the funtimes, funtimes becoming Ennard, Ennard becoming molten Freddy, hence MoltenMCI. But what was  Afton’s goal? Well it was just having  full control on everything, specifically, Ennard, while eliminating threats. Now, I’ll like to start this all off with the dialogue Micheal had at the end cutscene:

“**Father, it’s me, Micheal. I did it, I found it. It’s right where you said it would be. They were all there, they didn’t recognise me at first, but then… they thought I was you. (Sigh) and I found her… I put her back together, just like you asked me too. She’s free now. But something is wrong with me, I should be dead. But I’m not.**”

What does this dialogue imply besides the obvious, which was he was sent there by William on purpose and he put back together Elizabeth Afton. Well, the line that caught my attention the most for the analysis was “they were all there” which is a very interesting line, because it’s clearly more than what it seems on the surface. What is it? What does it mean? Well, I think Micheal was talking about the MCI spirits, some event from the past he could had been curious about, and was strangely told about them in a blockage manner by his father, but learning their spirits was in the funtimes, and this had to be a planned event by William because of the mere fact of MoltenMCI which I had brought up, which can only be possible if follow me happened before Sister Location, basically the line of events I had covered.

As I had mentioned before, William’s overarching  goal in “follow me” was him preparing the creation Ennard while separating control from, not mentioned before, Charlotte Emily/The Puppet, which up till this point in the timeline, had the main control over the spirits for the MCI under her guidance. William would go in, destroying the classic animatronics and collecting their remnants to inject into the Funtime animatronics, which at this point, the Funtimes AI had this rebellious nature, which ended up  mixing with the spirits of the MCI that was shattered inside all the Funtimes together, this being something William intentionally did, meaning he knew this would definitely stir things up. Then, William would send in Micheal Afton before his springlock failure, knowing his similarity in appearance with Micheal, something Micheal pointed out, and this would lead to his death while also leading with putting Elizabeth “back together” (Ennard) via Elizabeth’s manipulation on Micheal, which leads towards the ending of Sister Location to happen.

If you asked me personally, I think William likely foreseen the processing of Elizabeth/Circus Baby’s actions in Sister Location;  I’ll also like to mention how the wording of putting her back together just aligns so well with the process of Ennard that I think no way William didn’t foresee it coming to life. Anyways, With this, the creation of Ennard would happen and Micheal is theoretically “dead”; Elizabeth ends up being the leader inside Ennard like William wanted, and  this is where William would had likely came in, the others inside of Ennard would think William is dead because of the scooper (this being Micheal), William would had came in to gain  control over Ennard through Elizabeth, his most loyal child, with all the MCI spirits inside Ennard while Micheal is dead. But, obviously it didn’t turn out prefect; William getting springlocked messed things up here for him, leading to Micheal surviving, Elizabeth losing control of Ennard, creating molten Freddy, and Charlotte eventually coming back into the picture with Happiest day.

Beside that, I’ll like to mention how William also understood how the MCI would react after being split up and injected into the Funtime Animatronics as shattered pieces, and how this would synergy with the rebellious programming of the Funtime Animatronics AI, which would lead to his understanding of Circus baby’s goal and manipulation she’ll pull with Micheal Afton, which is why he sends down Micheal to his death, since he knew it’ll lead to his death for the understanding he had above.

William Afton knew that Ennard would use Micheal as a skin suit, and his overall “mini-goal” here was actually to put them back into the CBEAR facility. Circus  baby actually said that she was out before, but they always put them back inside the CBEAR facility. Micheal’s own motive would also play apart of this, William likely knowing Micheal had this lurking suspicion about the MCI and the DCI, which is why he came into the FNAF 1 job, William likely figuring that out, leading to William to tell Micheal about the MCI and Eliza down in the CBEAR facility to manipulate him. Lastly, I’ll like to mention how the obscurity of Micheal’s word could definitely be an alignment of how William worded it for Micheal, making it very complex to understand due to the secretive nature he present to Micheal, this bowing use to further manipulate Micheal because it’s like trying to figure out the fitting pieces for the puzzle, which Micheal ended finding.

***Why is called Ennard experiment?***

Well if you haven’t connected the clues, it’s because I think Ennard creation was something Afton wanted due to wanting a creature he has full control with through Elizabeth while separating outside forces from it, but because he also likely wanted to experiment with this abomination as it quite likely juice with Remnant, sentient AI and etc before a backfired event happened, leading to his ultimate demise.

***So what did William succeed in with this feat?***

Foreseeing the process of Elizabeth manipulation on Micheal, creating a time gap of control from Charlotte with the MCI, foreseen the creation of Ennard, understood the nature of the MCI and how their predicament Synergised with the AI rebellious nature, and understood Micheal’s motive, reasoned out the events that would take place.

***What he failed with the feat:***

Didn’t seen his own death happening in this which created blockage with the end goal of the plan, leading to failures which he couldn’t prevent such as Micheal survival, Molten Freddy creation and his original end goal.
