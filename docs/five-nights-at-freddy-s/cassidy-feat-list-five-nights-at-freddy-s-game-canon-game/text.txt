Cassidy feat list (Gamecanon & Gametheory)

**“IT’S ME”**

I will be using three game theories: CassidyTOYSNHK, CassidySpringlock, and GoldenCassidy.

\[FNAF 3\] Used William Afton’s own fear of spirits against him, to cause him to jump into the Spring Bonnie suit while there was water leakage, to activate the springlocks making him die and possess Spring Bonnie.

This feat showcases \[DIM (Direct Manipulation) IM (Individual Manipulation) STM (Short Term Manipulation) PM (Practical Manipulation) ST (Strategic Thinking) FMM (Fear Mongering Manipulation) and CT (Creative Thinking)\].

\[FNAF 3\] Predicted that William Afton would lure the animatronic bodies to the blind-spot backroom one by one to dismantle them, and instead chose to go to the backroom as a spirit to evade this, unlike the other animatronics.

This feat showcases \[TE (Trap Evasion) EU (Emotional Understanding) SE (Strategy Efficiency) PS (Problem Solving) LAT (Lateral Thinking) Prediction and Anticipation\].

\[General\] Was betrayed by William Afton disguised as Spring Bonnie, who promised her cake, and endured a springlock failure that led to her death, hardening both her mental fortitude and stress tolerance.

This feat showcases \[CI (Crystallised Intelligence) MF (Mental Fortitude) and ST (Stress Tolerance)\].

\[UCN\] Instantly learnt how to replicate the attack patterns and abilities of multiple animatronics to confuse and overwhelm William Afton.

This feat showcases \[LA (Learning Ability) SV (Strategy Versatility) SD (Strategy Deception) CT (Creative Thinking) LAT (Lateral Thinking) IDM (Indirect Manipulation) PRM (Practical Manipulation) GM (Gaslighting Manipulation) PD (Practical Deception) and AD (Acting Deception)\].

\[UCN\] Designed a purgatory to trap William Afton after he burns down in the FNAF 6 Pizza Place. The purgatory is meant to make him suffer repeatedly as vengeance, with her visualizing four different offices (the default office, Sister Location office, FNAF 3 office, and FNAF 4 office). Convincing 59 other animatronics to join this purgatory as enemies to torment him, and making the only possible exit 50/20 mode to truly make him suffer. This purgatory was also created with an understanding of Afton’s psyche, forcing him to relive the consequences of his actions to break him mentally by having him die and come back multiple times, with voice lines tailored to his past to sharpen the psychological damage.

This feat showcases \[TS (Trap Setting) SS (Social Skills) CT (Creative Thinking) ST (Strategic Thinking) PC (Planning Convergence) SC (Strategy Complexity) LTS (Long Term Strategy) VSI (Visual Spatial Index) EU (Emotional Understanding) GTM (Guilt Tripping Manipulation) LTM (Long Term Manipulation) and Determination\].

\[TWB\] Knew how important Ralph’s daughter was to him after only meeting once, and psychologically attacking him with multiple it’s me messages, calming him down when he was panicking by implying she was going to be safe, letting him fall asleep peacefully.

This feat showcases \[CR (Cold Reading) EU (Emotional Understanding) DIam (Direct Manipulation) PRM (Practical Manipulation) IM (Individual Manipulation) and EF (Emotional Facilitation)\].

\[FNAF 1\] Uses her hallucinogenic ability to show the MCI posters on the wall, helping Mike figure out what happened and realise that the animatronics are possessed countering Ralph’s claims.

This feat showcases \[LT (Linear Thinking) PC (Planning Convergence) CT (Creative Thinking) and Countering\].
