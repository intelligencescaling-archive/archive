Jenny & Marny murder Strategy

Tactic 1: Separation and first kill

Ghostface makes a noise upstairs, causing Jenny to go upstairs to check. While Jenny is upstairs, Ghostface silently manages to take Marny while distracting her with a call made by Jenny as a prank

Tactic 2: Lure and second kill

Jenny hears Marny make a noise in pain before the phone is hung up, this lures her downstairs, her belief it to be a horror prank by Marny to fueling this tactic indirectly, leaving the phone at the foot of the stairs where Jenny can see, Ghostface calls and after talking briefly, they use Marny as a way to break the glass window in front of Jenny in order to get into chasing distance, soon outpacing her, they use the garage door to break her back, immobilising her before killing her.

While a simple feat at face value, it's efficiency cannot be ignored, with the plan going off without a single fault, knowing that the noise made upstairs would be loud enough for them to hear and be worried by, but not loud enough to where they immediately call law enforcement out of worry, showing they were observing them before and genuinely aware of their level of focus. After Jenny is lured upstairs, they're able to take Marny without her making a single noise, meaning it was swift and expertly concealed considering Ghostface was already in the house to make the noise.

For the second tactic, the exploitation of the girls’ horror movie love is greatly used, by using their observations before the attack to their fullest extent, the phone at the bottom of the stairs is great for during Jenny towards where they want her to go as she searches the house, thinking Marny wants to jumpscare her before they lure her where they want and attack using Marny as a way to get in right next to Jenny.

Now even though this was foreshadowed earlier by Sidney, when she said “They all slip up” and the Reasoning is sound. This is very out of character for Jill who was such a great character when it comes to word articulation and focus on detail as shown in all the feats prior. With the original writing of the ‘Scream 4’ actually having Jill succeed and survive the movie shows her winning was on the cards until they decided to alter the script and include this slip up to lead to her eventual death. So imma call plot armour on this one.
