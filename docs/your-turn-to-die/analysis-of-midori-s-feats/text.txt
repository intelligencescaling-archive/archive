Introduction Of The Banquet

(No feats here only an introduction to the Banquet and its rules, you can skip this if you wish)

“The Banquet” is the last sub-game of the first part of the third chapter of the game “Your turn to die”, its rules are quite simple to understand:

R1: The players must choose two challengers who will enter inside two newly made coffins, after the challengers have been decided, the players must advance to the room where the game will in fact occur.

R2: In the main room, the players will find a giant mechanism similar to a revolving cylinder of a gun, the challenger chosen by the players and the opposing challenger are located in two different coffins between the ones in the image above.

R3: There's a total of nine different coffins, seven of these coffins containing seven different people or dummies inside it who were chosen by the players. While seven of these nine coffins contain non important beings to this game, two of these coffins contain the challengers of the game, the players must locate the coffin where the opposing challenger is and defeat him while at the same time avoiding hitting the challenger chosen by them.

R4: After selecting one of the coffins, a drill will appear from the ground below the coffin and kill the unlucky being who is inside it, if it is the opposing challenger, then the players win, if it is the challenger who was chosen by them, the opposing challenger wins. The opposing challenger can also choose any coffin of their choice and Maistre, the one managing the game, will choose the same coffin chosen by the opposing challenger, making the choice for him since he can't go out of his coffin to choose a coffin to hit with the drill.

R5: Both opposing sides have a chance to use a hint, this hint is the spherical-looking thing below the monitors that are below the coffins in the image above this text, depending of who is inside the coffin, that thing can shine in two different ways, Red, which is equal to doll, which means that there is a doll in the coffin where the hint was used, and blue that represents human, this means that there is a human inside the coffin where this hint was used.

R6: When the opposing challenger’s location is identified by the players and they kill him using the drill, the players will win the game, but if the opposite occurs, the challenger of the player’s side is located and killed by the opposing challenger, then the opposing challenger, or in other words, Midori, wins the Banquet game. The game will persist for an undetermined amount of rounds, being more specific, the idea of the game coming to an end soon or not will depend if the opposing challenger’s location is discovered quickly by the players, or if the opposing challenger ends up killing the challenger chosen by the players quickly.
