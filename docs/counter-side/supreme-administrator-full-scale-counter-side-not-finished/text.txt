ADMINISTRATOR'S PAST

The Supreme Administrator was never born a genius. He admitted it himself. He was dumb, ordinary, the kind of man who would rather keep his head lowered than raise it. But when the world kept falling apart, when promises kept breaking, he forced himself to change. He pushed his mind until it became something more than genius, something that could survive in spaces where ordinary thought would collapse. He hated it. He hated raising his head, hated being the one who saw everything unravel, but there was no one else who could carry it. He never wanted to become what he is, but he had no choice.

Each time a world collapsed, he jumped to another. Parallel dimensions, endless variations, and every time he arrived, the first thing he saw was a dead person’s face. Faces of those he failed, worlds he couldn’t save. He carried them all, stacked across years that refused to fade. “I wish I could turn back time,” he admitted, “but I can’t. I have to move forward. So many years passed, and I can’t simply forget them. I didn’t even want to be what I am. I just have no choice. I wish someone else would replace me, but I won’t do that. I won’t let anyone else suffer like I did.”

Even as CEO, even as Administrator, he confessed he was only a man. There were countless things beyond his control. He couldn’t undo the tragedies, couldn’t rewrite the endings, but he could give victims one last respite. That was the least he could do, to bear their despair himself, to walk through their final moments so they didn’t die alone. Lee Sooyeon confronted him about it, asking how many times he had endured such torment. She said most people would break after a couple of tries. He smiled anyway, because that’s what he did, he smiled, even as he broke.

His AI son, Sysop, saw it for what it was. “My Father is a near-perfect being. However, if phrased differently, it implies that he is not perfect. Always one step short. He lost everything because he could not take that one extra step. That is why I am assisting Father in taking the one step he is lacking.” Those words cut deeper than any enemy’s blade. Near-perfect. Always short. Never enough. That was the truth of the Supreme Administrator. He's not a god, but a man who kept missing the final step, and carried the weight of those failures across infinite worlds.

He was tired. He was old. He remembered broken promises and wondered what might have changed if he had acted sooner, if he had taken risks he avoided. But regret never stopped him. With each failure, he promised again. He would not give up. He would move forward, no matter how many worlds crumbled behind him. He smiled through the pain, because if he didn’t smile, he would collapse. And collapse was not an option; not when there were still people left to save.

HILDE - Hilde (힐데) is the deuteragonist in Counter:Side. She is a senior employee of the Coffin Company, serving as the commander of its Fenrir Squad, and a former division leader of the now defunct Old Administration's 1st Assault Division [Fenrir]. She is significantly older than she looks, and some within the Old Administration have outright called her immortal. Hilde is also an Apostle of Tammiel. Having been in conflict with Tammiel and the Demon Lords since time immemorial, Hilde has a strong devotion to humanity and an unshakeable duty to protect whatever world she is residing in. She is not one to give up and falter away from danger and failure, no matter how big, and shows no signs of doing so even after enduring countless losses in Qliphoth Games. To that end, Hilde takes Qliphoth threats very seriously and will mercilessly hunt down anyone who risks increasing the global Corruption rate through Qliphoth Factor— only in the most desperate situations will she even consider activating her own Factor in a conflict. Still, even someone as typically resolute and unbending as Hilde is not immune to sentimentality. For starters, although she does not appear to be too fond of the current Yoo Mina, Hilde clearly holds Mina's previous iterations dear even though the Cursed Wolf is the original holder of Qliphoth Factor. Near the end of the Administration Failure, Hilde reluctantly abandoned the majority of her comrades in order to look for the Yoo Mina of Fenrir Division in hopes of running away from the conflict with her; after the incident, she repeatedly claimed that the "old" Mina no longer means anything to her, insisting that she was dead, though the attitude she gave off each time suggested otherwise. As observed by Asmodeus, this was far from the first time Hilde took off on her own for the Wolf's sake. In addition, the Administrator noted how obsessive Hilde can be when it comes to the Wolf, pointing out to how she named Fenrir Squad after her. Similarly, Hilde spared Joo Shiyoon's life and took him in as her apprentice— all in spite of the fact that Shiyoon, like his parents, whom Hilde killed mere moments before taking him in, was an inheritor of Dragon Blood. While Shiyoon's apprenticeship was arguably more practical, Lee Sooyeon implied Hilde was being more sympathetic than usual by calling her "indecisive." Though frequently described as reckless due to her tendency to tackle threats head-on, Hilde has time and time again proved to be a very capable commander who values teamwork and camaraderie. She may be distant and aloof but is more caring towards her teammates than she makes herself out to be; because of this, Fenrir Squad's mortality rate has consistently been far below the TASKFORCE industry average despite having a high injury rate. Outside of combat, Hilde can be friendly and sometimes provides her colleagues with meals as gratitude for their assistance. That being said, Hilde is still strict and has little to no tolerance for nonsense and carelessness. Within Coffin Company, she finds Mina to be bumptious and is disdainful of Shiyoon's laziness and cheekiness, and during her tenure at the Old Administration, she cringed at Sooyeon's habit of naming her attacks and would punish her if any of them were named after her. Hilde's true name, Freyja, [Queen of valkyries] is named after the Norse goddess of love, fertility and war. In folklore, Freyja presides over a divine field called Fólkvangr, where half of those who die in combat are taken to (while the other half are taken to Valhalla as mentioned above). Following her Awakening Collapse, which severely and irreversibly wounded her best friend Shin Narae and ultimately led to the deaths of Narae's family, Mina developed survivor's guilt and grew depressed. To make matters worse, Mina became the sole caretaker of her sister, a Corruption victim currently hospitalized at a relief facility in Old Seoul. Because of this, Mina became desperately fixated on making money to pay for her sister's medical bills to the point where she would use violence against her employers whenever her pay was not delivered to her on time. As she rarely confided her reasons for needing so much money, Mina was often perceived as nothing more than another moneygrubbing mercenary to her coworkers. After encountering a vengeful Narae, who had transformed into the Replacer Bishop, and witnessing her best friend's violent death, Mina's emotional stress only worsened and she began to go on an unhealthy working spree, leading Joo Shiyoon to diagnose her with PTSD. As she was already suffering from immense guilt, Mina was extremely unwilling to face the Regenerated Bishop or put her through any pain and suffering even while fully knowing that the new Bishop was not Narae. For a while after the Replacer Incident, Mina regained her self-confidence and started opening herself up to others around her like she did in her pre-Counter life. While she was still needy for money, she didn't feel as ashamed about it as before and was more willing to talk about her need to take care of her sister. Nevertheless, she still felt embarrassed when her financial issues were brought up to her. Following the Second Qliphoth Game, Mina not only fell into depression again but also grew nihilistic. She came to believe that, as the Wolf, she has no real control over her life and fate, and nothing she does of their own accord will matter since something else will inevitably change her trajectory. Furthermore, the realization that her sister was in fact the world's greatest threat severely conflicted her feelings about her. Though she is worried about the future now that she is aware of her and her sister's true nature, Mina is currently satisfied living humbly and remains committed to finding a resolution to her situation. Supreme Administrator (최고 관리자) is the overarching protagonist of Counter:Side’s greater mythos and the founder of Coffin Company as well as the architect behind the Divergent Curriculum of the Administration Counter Academy. Though outwardly appearing as a composed and calculating executive, the Supreme Administrator is a being whose existence spans over 160 million years, evolving from a human genius into a myth recognized across parallel worlds. At the age of 18, he created the first prototype Terrabrain, demonstrating civilization-level systems engineering before reaching adulthood. At 25, due to external forces, he ceased aging physically, locking his body in its prime while his mind continued to evolve across epochs. Their conflict spans millions of years and operates as both rivalry and paradoxical preservation. Over time, the Supreme Administrator has become a mythic constant parallel to Tammiel, recognized across universes not for inherited divinity but for accumulated transcendence.

ORGANIZATIONS:

The Old Administration - The Old Administration was a secretive political-military-industrial complex built with the purpose of preventing the spread of the Counterside. The Old Administration was practically annihilated during the Administration Failure, after which the wider world came to know of Corrupted Objects and the Counterside. Its existence is still somewhat shrouded in mystery, with some contemporary debate on if the Old Administration existed at all and in what capacity. The Old Administration was founded by the Administrator after he and Hilde left the previous World they were inhabiting behind. Its exact foundation date is unknown, but the organization had been in existence for more than forty years before its collapse. As it was meant to be a secret organization, the Old Administration took great lengths in covering up their existence as well as the knowledge they held; those who left were required to undergo a memory wipe and have any records pertaining to their involvement with the group erased. The reason for all this was to prevent mass panic within humanity. The Old Administration used a technology called "awareness distortion filters" to hide its facilities and buildings. These filters distort the people's perception of things, making them unable to see things affected by them. They were generally effective in keeping the public from learning about the Old Administration, though there were occasionally times when some people came across Administration assets. If an outsider gave considerable attention to what they discovered or got caught in Administration activity, workers would erase their memories about them. The Old Administration's combat forces consisted of divisions that were established both within and outside of the organization. Some are operated only as needed by the Administration while others proposed solidarity with forces that have reached the truth of the world (i.e. knowledge about the existence of the Counterside, Corrupted Objects, etc. before the Failure) in their own fields. The roles and positions of a division's members were sometimes left up to calculations and predictions made by Hive Control. Each division mobilizes to their respective roles, and the orders from Command are there to respond to unexpected variables. Battles are left to the discretion and active judgment of the Division Commander.

The “Current” Administration - The Administration is an expansive political-military network. It is the successor of the Old Administration following the Administration Failure. Unlike its predecessor, however, the Administration's existence is publicly known throughout the world as it functions like a supranational government entity that ordinary citizens can interact and communicate with. Nevertheless, the Administration still holds a number of secrets of its own— including information about the Counterside and advanced technologies that have yet to be released to the rest of human society. The Administration was established following the collapse of its predecessor during the Administration Failure. It accredits TASKFORCE companies with the goal of combating interdimensional corruption. The Administration's main headquarters is in the Ground One and classifies Counterside Effects by their level. It has access to satellite data to better document Corrupted Objects and help redirect TASKFORCE companies to where they are needed. The Administration Executive command is responsible for assigning free contract missions to TASKFORCE companies. It sometimes gives out plaques of appreciation to them for jobs well done. Counters are rated by the Administration Counter standards and given a ranking based on their CRF output. There, Counters take a grade assessment test The Administration used to only call up Grade C counters as reserves, but due to a serious manpower shortage, even Grade D counters are being considered for active duty. Additionally, the Administration requires all registered Counters that are minors to attend and graduate from its Counter Academy. Although the Administration operates globally, there are places known as Anti-Administration Zones that either prohibit Administration forces from entering or have not implemented its Anti-Corruption System. As with its predecessor, the Administration uses technology that is far more advanced than what the rest of the world has. Due to its background, it specializes in anything related to Eternium and Counters, particularly dimensional ships. Because of this, it was able to help the world overcome the Grand Purification War and recover from it afterwards in less than twenty years. Despite this, the Administration still keeps much of its technology secret, only releasing some information and blueprints to the public when they deem it safe to do so; sometimes it even makes efforts to prevent competition from outside groups. This is because of the risk of data corruption, which can occur if technology goes past what the current era can allow.

THE COUNTERSIDE

DEMON LORDS - The true origin of the Demon Lords is unknown. However, in the words of the Administrator, a Demon Lord is a being that "becomes one with the world by intertwining with fate itself," which indicates that one can arise if an entity consumes an immense amount of possibilities. Although it appears to be a destructive being that spreads Corruption throughout worlds, the Demon Lord is also inherently a pillar that maintains the balance of its home world (along with any others it has absorbed into its Qlipha Dimension), keeping it from collapsing. Ultimately, the Demon Lord aims to absorb the Normalside into the Counterside by raising the Global Corruption Rate of each world to its maximum. For the most part, they seem to endeavor in the background, either idly killing time (e.g. Rosaria) or scheming in the shadows and setting up pieces big and small (e.g. Edel, Thagirion). Not every world faces a Demon Lord or enters a Qliphoth Game as it falls under Corruption; some worlds such as the Pathfinder World are just too weak to have an Antagonist, let alone a Wolf, and are left to die at the hands of the Counterside and its strange causality. While their powers and appearance vary from individual to individual, the Demon Lords seem to generally possess abilities that put them well within the threshold of being a threat to a world individually, going beyond even the combined might of multiple high level Counters (the Administration-run world being a strange anomaly, attracting and being host to numerous Demon Lords to face in the Game). Demon Lords are nigh-immortal, and the only person who can slay one outside a Qliphoth Game is the Antagonist.[citation needed] Should the Antagonist succeed in doing so, they will end up inheriting the Demon Lord's powers and title, thus becoming the new Demon Lord of that respective Qlipha. Should they fail, their world will be consumed by the Counterside, and they will be transformed into a Hollow God. As long as one has succeeded in the Demon Lord's role, they would still retain the authority that all Demon Lords have, namely the ability to resurrect the dead and use them as Apostles.

CORRUPTED OBJECTS (C.O.) - Corrupted Objects are mutated organisms that have been heavily exposed to Corruption rays. They inhabit the Counterside and are exceedingly hostile towards non-Corrupted beings. Any organism—whether it be a human, animal, or even plant—can transform into a Corrupted Object when exposed to enough Corruption rays. The stronger or more concentrated the Corruption ray is, the more mutated the resulting C.O. will be. While they take on many different forms, standard C.O.s have dark grey carapaces with red eternium crystals protruding. Higher-category C.O.s are more ferocious in appearance and sport purple or green eternium crystals on their bodies, while C.O.s that emerge from large bodies of water are blue or white. Other variants include Volcanic and Jungle-type C.O.s. Machines can also be corrupted, though this is a rare phenomenon. Corrupted Objects can breach Normalside through points of reality collapse called Counterside Effects and use Eternium as a catalyst for entry. Their presence within a local area is usually part of a Corruption Disaster. The danger posed by a Corrupted Object is expressed by its Category, to which the Administration issues alerts depending on the severity of C.O. incursion, with the level of alert being derived from Counterside Effect (CSE) levels. Despite their nature, Corrupted Objects are edible by themselves. Additionally, some harbor Solocao, a type of fertilized C.O. egg that secretes a special hormone to enhance its host C.O. Described as having "the bitterness of cacao and the sweetness of honey," Solocao contains no Corruption-related substances but needs to be mixed with Solonila, an extract found on Solocao Queens, in order to make it safe for human consumption. However, if left alone, the Solocao will instead hatch and produce a unique kind of Category 3 C.O. Nevertheless, the chocolate that the C.O. produced upon death is still perfectly harmless and edible, though it contains chemicals that inhibit cortisol production and promote positive feelings. C.O.s that carry Solocao or Solonila are brown in color.

SHADOWS - Shadows, or Demon-type C.O.s as named by the Administration, are former Counters that have been turned into Corrupted Objects due to too much exposure to Corruption Rays or severe injury from a Corrupted Object. The more powerful the Counter was before succumbing, the more powerful the Shadow will become. Certain Shadows (such as Ministra) possess "corruption cores" which emit and distort spacetime in the Normalside even while living in the Counterside, causing Counterside effects. "Demogorgons" are ancient Shadows that appear when certain conditions are met. Shadows are more likely to appear in front of fresh Counters visiting the Counterside for the first time, and single handedly raise the death rate for new squads. Fresh Counters "reek" of the Normalside, which is irresistible to Shadows. The Administration keeps guidance squads in place to make sure new rookies do not die to Shadow attacks for this reason. Once attached to someone, a shadow never goes away voluntarily. If it does, it will come back as a bigger threat. High-category Shadows also have the ability to give orders to lower Corrupted Objects using their unique Corruption Ray. Many Counters become Shadows and their personalities are twisted based on their last thoughts before becoming a Shadow. For example, Rita Arsenico's last thoughts before becoming a Shadow was her love for Dash and desire to protect her while Dash's last thoughts before becoming Spira was to return home to the Normalside. Because of this, many of the Counters who become Shadows end up losing their moral compass and become destructive or evil. Lyudmila is one of the most noticeable rare exceptions, as she was able to maintain her moral compass, mainly because her last thoughts before becoming a Shadow was more noble (to protect her comrades from incoming C.O. and her dedication and loyalty to her comrades and Administrator) when compared to other Shadows. Because of this, Lyudmilia ended up being very unique among many Shadows to the point that she earned some admiration to the Administrator. The Administration's database provides various information to authorized corporations and organizations, and lists known shadows that must be avoided at all costs. However, it hasn't clarified its stand on whether or not Shadows should be treated as equal to their original Counters.

DOPPELGANGER - A Doppelganger, as named by the Administration, is a unique type of Shadow that originates from Shadow Palaces and has the same appearance and abilities of their original non-Corrupted counterparts. They are created from Qlipha energy when Corruption Rays permeate throughout a particular space response to an active entity's energy. Doppelgangers are incomplete Shadows that vehemently seek to absorb their original counterparts to become whole, though they can still form even if the original is dead. They also require a constant source of Eternium to stay alive unlike most other C.O.s and can cannibalize on each other if necessary.

HARVESTERS -  Harvester is a new type of C.O. Its name was coined by Swan Claire due to its ability to steal possibilities from other entities and evolve from experience. Harvesters are self-aware and capable of human speech like Shadows but can also cause Counterside Effects at will and mimic the skills of their opponents. Harvesters first appeared during the conclusion of the Second Qliphoth Game. After the defeat of Nehemoth, a total solar eclipse occurred, and a portion of the Tree of Sephiroth emerged from the far side of the moon and emitted enough energy to create a rift in the dimension. This "purified" a Corrupted Area in the Charade-Lotus Management Perimeter Zone, terraforming it into a lush and habitable area, but the residue left behind by Sephira's power of creation resulted in the creation of Harvesters.

CSE - A Corruption disaster, or Counterside Effect (CSE for short), is a phenomenon where a point in the Counterside overlaps with the Normalside. During a disaster, a local reality collapse can occur and allow monsters known as Corrupted Objects (C.O.) to breach through dimensions and wreak havoc onto the Normalside. Counterside Effect is caused by an influx of Corruption rays originating from the Counterside and breaking into the Normalside. However, effects are also reflective of emotions engraved in human instinct, indicating that they are, to some extent, manmade and not as spontaneous as one would believe. In fact, it is possible for someone to control the flow of Corruption rays in order to cause a specific type of Counterside Effect. Sometimes, the Counter Reality Force caused by humans' collective subconsciousness, specifically their negative emotions, can trigger and affect CSEs. Naturally, CSEs usually occur on the ground where humans are most prevalent but can also happen in the stratosphere. Data corruption occurs during a Counterside Effect if a world uses technology that goes past what its current era can allow. The Administration uses a system known as the "Counterside Effect Alert" in its controlled cities in order to warn residents of potential disaster and to hunker down during these events. The highest known Counterside Effect Alert level is 6. COUNTERS

What are Counters? -

A Counter is a superpowered human using a Watch, born with unique abilities. Unlike ordinary human beings, most Counters have innate resistance to Corruption and can roam freely the Counterside or High-level Corrupted Areas, though how much resistance they have varies from Counter to Counter. Their name comes from their eponymous Watch which "counts down" the amount of Counter Reality Force they have left before they start succumbing to the effects of corruption in an area. Most Counters share the same basic physical attributes such as super strength and enhanced durability and agility and usually have additional powers that augment their combat capabilities as well as survivability. Among the most common Counter abilities include accelerated healing, firepower amplification, weapon and body fortification, energy projection, and pyrokinesis. Other Counters may have powers that are rare or unique to them. A Counter's ability weakens the further it is from their body. While most Counters who use weaponry are equipped with modern standardized weapons (such as firearms), others may have special equipment called "Unique gear" made exclusively for Counters. Such paraphernalia can make a Counter even more powerful than other Counters of the same grade.

Counter Watches -

A Counter's Watch counts down whenever a Counter uses their unique abilities, emits energy, resists corruption or "defies reality". The Watch measures a Counter's CRF and can be recharged using eternium crystals. As staying in the Counterside is inherently hazardous, the watch continuously moves little by little. When a Counter's Watch reaches zero, the Counter will turn into a Shadow. A person can obtain a Counter Watch if they go through a life-changing and usually traumatic event (chosen by the Administrator personally as he created those watches). When this happens, the Watch will suddenly appear on the person's wrist.  Simply having a pre-existing watch does not automatically turn a person into a Counter; a Watch without an owner "chooses" who has the potential to become a Counter, and thus it is useless to the unprepared. Otherwise, a watch will usually break or disappear after its original owner dies. As their nomenclature suggests, watches usually take the form of wearable timepieces. This is not always the case, though, and Watches can represented either in the form of other objects or a unique mark on the Counter's body; for example, Yuna Springfield's Watch takes form of a magical staff, while the Watches of the elite members of the Elysium Philharmonic (which were called "Stigmatas" in their original world) are represented as tattoos on their bodies.

Counter Reality Force -

The Counter Reality Force (CRF) is the power to change reality and grants Counters the ability to use their powers and resist corruption. Although CRF is present in anyone who possesses possibilities—including regular humans—Counters are the only ones that can use and manifest it at their discretion. This is because ordinary people unknowingly spend their CRF in increments over their lifetime, slowly changing their realities in the long run. Counters, on the other hand, can use their Watches as a catalyst to use bursts of their CRF at a time. A Counter's possibilities are expended when one uses their Counter powers. A Counter's CRF output is linked to their psychology, so Counters can exert more detailed CRF if they can control their mental state. However, CRF consumes Eternium and as such must be used sparingly in order to not eventually succumb to corruption (see Watch below). CRF and Counter powers can be suppressed using certain technologies such as the "Mind Jammer," a device that causes severe tinnitus in Counters. Counters are rated by the Administration Counter standards and given a ranking based on their CRF output— from lowest to highest, the ranks are Grade D, C, B, A, and S. It is important to note that Counter grades are determined based on the Counter's CRF output and not their overall combat skills. For example, Nanahara Chifuyu and Ciel Marronier are registered as Grade D and C Counters respectively despite being some of the strongest and most skilled fighters.

Awakening Collapses -

An awakening collapse is a rare phenomenon that occurs when a high-grade Counter receives his powers for the first time. An explosion, usually with a blast radius of a few dozen meters, emitted the person turning and corrupts the surrounding area. People and structures in the vicinity will be damaged, but the newly-awakened Counter will be unharmed. Yoo Mina's awakening collapse is unique in that the blast radius of her explosion was more than a kilometer long.

Administration Counter Academy -

The Administration Counter Academy is an educational institution exclusively for students who are Counters. It teaches Counter training in addition to both primary and secondary education. Courses in the Counter Academy are categorized into two types of classes: general and combat. General courses teach subjects typical to regular schools while combat courses train their students in using their Counter abilities. Due to the nature of Counters, all students are strictly required to take combat courses. In order to graduate, students must meet a certain score in combat training. Recently, Dive Training Program was added, where it would acclimate Counters to the Counterside by going to shallow levels at first. The only ones that can go on the Dive Training are high schoolers. Students can continue working as long as they fulfill the mandatory courses. There are many students that transfer after a stint of being mercenaries. All registered Counters that are minors are required to attend and graduate from the academy. The Academy encourages its students to have at least one club activity. This facility creates a virtual environment that mimics real combat zones that students can enter and receive combat training via the use of a holographic mass generation system. Originally, students received training in actual disaster zones, but this was considered too dangerous by the Administration and thus the simulation center was built. It hosts a number of sporting events, including Field Day. It is located on the Blue Rose Islands, namely the twin islands known as the Stadium Islands.

The Supreme Administrator himself also created the curriculum as “Divergent” unlike public schools. Each student chooses a field of intellect. By the highschool years they have the equivalent of masters degrees as a “casual coursing” at the age of 15. Instead of worksheets, students complete their daily education in 2 hours which involve analysis, WAIS-V, Logical Reasoning, and Sensory. The rest of the day was focused on training Counter abilities. Because of that, they proved the government curriculums can't innovate, and became the top school in the world. The curriculum changes entirely each year, and implements some from past years. Due to students not being forced to work, and had the ability to learn at any rate they want whether it be making their own businesses and/or having Adversity, every teenager at least had more academic and social knowledge than every professor in a college combined. Each student by the 8th grade eventually surpassed their own professors. With that, the rest of the school day for them is counter training.
