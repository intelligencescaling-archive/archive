Introduction

Otto Apocalypse is the main antagonist of Honkai Impact 3rd, HI3 for short, a popular gacha game by HoYoverse.

He is the 227th Overseer of Schicksal, which represents the church in HI3, and using his position and intellect, Otto manipulates everyone and plans almost everything that happens in the entire story of HI3 as a mastermind from the shadows.

He was born as the youngest son of the House of Apocalypse in the 15th century. However, he was nothing but a sickly, useless, boy with no hope of inheriting the seat of the overseer. Though fate did not spare this sickly boy as he was eventually forced into the role of a villian.

Although everyone over the 500 years of his life perceives him as a selfish person everything he does serves only one purpose: the revival of his love Kallen Kaslana who was his only light in the dark and cruel world; she was akin to a god in his eyes.

“No one shall get in the way of my obsession.”

Terminology

Honkai: Higher dimensional energy that creates natural disasters, monsters and plagues that try to eliminate humanity. Honkai also has a strong corrupting effect that can break almost any mind.

Herrscher: Humans who are chosen by the honkai to be their apostles. They are granted power to bend physical laws and affect higher dimensions. Most of the time these humans lose control over themselves because of the Honkai’s corrupting effect.

Herrscher Core: An imaginary singularity in the form that is similar to a gem, through which the Herrschers draw their powers. These cores form when a massive amount of Honkai Energy is condensed.

Schicksal: The organization that rose after the fall of the Roman Empire and fights against the Honkai. It is the counterpart of the real life church in HI3.

House of Kaslana: A linage from Kevin Kaslana, who was the strongest warrior in the previous era, and his descendants that mimic him joining the fight against the Honkai throughout the current era. They are the part of the Schicksal's three houses that holds power over the biggest military force and their role is that of the “knight”.

House of Schariac: The second of the three houses and it has the theological power of Schicksal. They too have a military force but it consists of women who are able to hold the holy blood. Their role is that of the “divine”.

House of Apocalypse: The last of the three houses. They lead Schicksal and take the role of the “overseer/bishop” but they don't have their own military force.

Valkyries Order: A Schicksal fighting force consisting of women who are able to wield Honkai Energy as their weapon.

Anti-Entropy: A former branch of Schicksal that broke away from it and became an independent organization.

Previous Era: A highly technological advanced human civilization 50,000 years ago that lost and were obliterated by the Honkai.

Current Era: The era after the Previous Era.

World Serpent: on the first glance World Serpent is an underground sect that holds secrets of the honkai and collects and develops honkai artifacts but in reality it is an organization established by Mobius from the Previous Era and is now led by Kevin Kaslana with the goal of reaching project STIGMA.

Divine Keys: Weapons developed by the Previous Era that draws power from Herrscher Cores.

Sea of Quanta: A metaphorical sea that holds infinities bubble universes/worlds

Imaginary Tree: The origin of all that is and holds infinite leaf universes/worlds and timelines

Imaginary Space: The “space” outside of the Imaginary Tree

Side Note - The terms “universe” and “world(s)” are used interchangeably in HI3. The bubble and leaf worlds are all separated space-time continuums so they have the same attributes as universes.
