Now, for obstruction:

•R is the radius of the Sun/Star, r is the radius of the Planet, and d is the Planet to Sun distance. We need to find z (the size/radius of the object which will block all the light) and y the distance that object is from the Planet (which we have already). In order to do this, we also need to determine x which is the distance at which the umbra becomes a point. We can take advantage of the properties of similar triangles to write:

R/(d+x) = r/x.
Therefore:
x = rd/(R - r).
•Substituting in the radius of the Sun, the Planet to Sun distance at perihelion and the radius of the Planet (using Earth), we get:
x = 6371×149600000/(696340-6371).
x = 1381368.72816km.
•In order to obstruct all sunlight from the planet, the object needs to have a radius of:
z = (6371(y+1381368.72816))/1381368.72816 = (6371(15968.5781455+1381368.72816))/1381368.72816.
z = 6444.64855544km (radius of the Solar Disk).
Volume of sphere = 4/3πr³ = 4/3×π×6444.64855544³ = 1.12e+12km³ (volume of the Solar Disk).

Invincible no-sells a super-nuke calc:

•Inverse square law states that the intensity of a source is inversely proportional to square of the distance away from the source, i.e. a source gets weaker in intensity on a body the further the body gets away from it.

Yield : 9.56 petatons.

Distance Invincible was from the nuke : Around 0.5m.

Surface Area of the explosion at point in which it hits Invincible: 4πr² = 4×π×0.5² = 3.14159265359m².

Intensity: Yield/Area = 9.56/3.14159265359 = 3.04304251192 Petatons/m².

Body surface area: √(height×weight/3600) = 2m².

Cross-sectional area ≈ Body surface area/2 = 1m².

Total yield energy taken by character = Intensity×Cross-sectional area = 3.04304251192 Petatons of TNT (Multi-Continental level).

FOR FORCE:

P: 2I/c.

•P is pressure in Pascals//N/m².
•I is intensity. = 3.04304251192 Petatons/m² = 1.273208982e+25J/m².
•c is the speed of light. = 299792458m/s.

= (2×1.273208982e+25)/299792458 = 8.493936e+16Pa.

Force: PA = 8.493936e+16×1 = 8.493936e+16N//8.66 trillion tons of force.
