Tokuchi is overrated.

If your goal is just to hate, scroll on. If you want to see a new perspective? Read the full thing first.

Let me make this clear out the gate: Tokuchi Toua is incredible. And easily in my Top 5 favorites in SCD. There's actually nothing I dislike about him at all honestly. However, this doesn't mean I don't think he's also overrated in SCD as a whole.

Why do I think Tokuchi is overrated? Well first I want to make sure I have properly represented the position of people who consider him High Tier and why. There have been people who made objections towards Tokuchi's praise by saying "You all scale (x) character low because they only go up against trash opponents, but what about Tokuchi? Has he ever gone up against someone who WASN'T fodder?" And the rebuttal is "Sure, he may go up against fodder. But look at the position he's in and it's difficultly. He's having to carry an entire trash baseball team to victory essentially by himself and apply his observation, perception, strategy, and planning to a game like baseball making it exponentially more difficult. AND hes countering his boss who's trying to send him into debt at the exact same time he's carrying scrubs and crushing opponents who are better. Two opposing strategies at the same time. To pull all of that off and win, makes him certainly high tier due to difficulty, execution, and how well formed the strategies are." Have I represented this position fairly?

Except, I have one problem with it. The situation Tokuchi is in, is actually NOT difficult. I know it sounds crazy. But let me explain. I'm just offering a new perspective. So first I ask myself the question "Could even the BEST people readers, gamblers, and strategists (for sake of example let's assume they also have great pitching skills) use their abilities to pull off anything close to just Anime Tokuchi has done if they were in the MLB? Could they even come close?" And the answer is no. I think we can all fairly say that. But why?

The reason why is because the skill level, the sheer FOCUS, the adversity capacity, and the resilience that actual world class athletes have is MULTIPLE LEAGUES ABOVE the people in One Outs. NONE of the stuff Tokuchi does would fly irl. MAYBE at best, getting people to miss by faking them out and reading them based on previous swings. But everything else? No. Especially many of the rules he breaks.

So what do I ask myself next? "How much lower do I have to bring the skill level of MLB players in order to successfully allow a Tokuchi Toua to be able to demolish an entire time WHILE ON the worst team in the league himself through sheer people reading and strategy?" And I'm ngl, I truly cannot see that happening even against a good highschool team. Cause I've seen them play.

Essentially, Tokuchi is playing against SUCH fodder, that he looks other worldly by comparison. He's an alien surrounded by sub humans. Is it really that difficult when the skill level he's going up against would only work if his opponents were 10 year olds? I saw someone use this analogy with Hannibal and I think it kinda fits here: "If you need to come up with these incredibly complex strategies to take candy from a baby, are you really that good?" Now let me be fair. Tokuchi's SITUATION is high stakes and requires him to actually try, however anything he does only works BECAUSE the writer says so and because his opponents are dog water.

Tokuchi has earned the nickname "Tokuchi the mind reader". And that's not right. Yes, Tokuchi's observation skills are brilliant. Yes, his perception and even cold reading skills are brilliant. His EP is brilliant. Those are the categories I love about him. However, he's reading people that are relatively EASY to read. It's an emotionally charged high stakes situation where these people SHOW their hand readily because they're lower than low tiers.

L from Death Note is the actual mind reader. "wHaT iS tHiS gUy sAyiN-" Just listen. If we consider the position L was in from the VERY BEGINNING it was already much more difficult. He's having to take seemingly random heart attacks, find the connection, deduce the motive, determine if it's murder, if it is then understand the killer's thought process and emotional state, and who he his. L without seeing or meeting Light was able to QUOTE VERBATIM Light's thought process. L in episode 6 when talking to the NPA, quotes Light and says "but all those who oppose me by hiding criminals, you are TRULY evil." Light in episode 2 quite literally says "Those who would oppose that god, you are TRULY EVIL!" Wtf? How? Not only is his situation harder than Tokuchi's but the feat is far more impressive. Especially when we consider there are plenty of other explanations before we get to "One person. Murderer. Childish morality. Thinks he's God". There's SO MANY other possibilities before reaching that conclusion, ESPECIALLY when starting completely cold. And to flawlessly and continously not just predict and understand Light's entire psyche, but everyone around him, consistently? He's the true mind reader. L scales higher than Tokuchi.

If you disagree? Thats fine. I know I typed a lot but you should read the full thing, that's all I really ask.

Tokuchi is STILL in my Top 5 despite all this. I just think Akiyama scales higher. And so does L.
