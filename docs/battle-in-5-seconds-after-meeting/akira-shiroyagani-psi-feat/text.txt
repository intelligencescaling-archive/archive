After the 3rd trial, Akira Shiroyagani was trying to find the person who killed Mr. Shirasagi(the boss)’s Daughter, Reiji Izuka

Akira deduced that Reiji was still alive. He wasn’t aware of this before, but he was able to deduce it based on the boss’s wife’s wording.

Later, after Akira goes to find Reiji, he ends up at the location where Reiji is hidden. This is where the actual feat starts.

Reiji has purposefully hidden in a populated part of the city as a smokescreen. This would make it near impossible for Akira to figure out who he is due to his time constraint.

This is where Akira performs the feat:

Keep these things in mind:
-Akira had to psychoanalyze thousands of civilians at the same time in a matter of a few seconds. Due to his time constraint, he had to figure out who it was as soon as possible because Reiji’s ability was crucial to defeat his partner.
-On a work day in a populated Japanese city, there’s probably hundreds of thousands of people out and about. In a part of the city like that, there’s presumably thousands of people outside. Reiji picked that specific part so that Akira couldn’t figure him out, as he’d be hidden.

But, Akira DID figure him out.

First, he had to psychoanalyze thousands of people all at the same time. He took around 2-3 seconds for his feat. We can assume he analyzed ~300 people per second. Not to mention the people on balconies, already at home.

Not only that, but his psychoanalysis turned out to be correct.

So for the first part of the feat:
-Akira had to psychoanalyze thousands of people all in a matter of a couple of seconds due to his time constraint.
-His psychoanalysis turned out to be correct, as he managed to narrow it down from thousands of people to simply 3.

For the second part:

Akira had reduced his list to 3 people, but how did he single out Reiji out of the three?

He accidentally stepped into the road, and was almost hit by a car.
Now, why is this important? Because this event is how he figured out the true Reiji.

Akira had been almost hit by a car. The average human reflex is ~0.25 seconds. Akira’s is faster than that, but we’ll take the average just to ensure accuracy.

So, simultaneously while dodging the car in that quarter of a second, he had to observe all those thousands of people again. Including the 3 suspects. Keep in mind, he formulated this entire strategy in 0.25 seconds. He knew Reiji wouldn’t look as he had to stay vigilant and observe his partner, which he used to his advantage.

Akira stepped in front of the car -> which led to the car honking -> which caught everyone’s attention except Reiji -> right during Akira was dodging the car, he re-analyzed everyone’s behavior in that quarter of a second and immediately pinpointed Reiji out of those thousands of people.

In conclusion:
-Akira psychoanalyzed thousands of people in ~3 seconds.
-Then, Akira observed the facial expressions and where those thousands of people were looking, in ~0.25 seconds.
-In that quarter of a second, he has formulated that entire strategy.
