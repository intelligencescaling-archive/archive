SPEED:

First let's gauge her speed without magic. Star even without any magical enhancements has shown to have extremely impressive speed. As seen when she dodged a bolt of lightning while chasing Ponyhead. Lightning is ionized air traveling downward, and since she clearly moved out of the way out of it before it hit the ground, she would have to be moving at 270,000 mph, or Mach 352. This would make her massively hypersonic. But she actually has much more impressive scaling if we compare her to Marco. As Star was testing Marco, she released “Spider With A Tophat Blast”. It's important to know the weapon that Spider with a Tophat has. This weapon has been described as not only a minigun on multiple occasions, but also as a Laser Cannon, meaning that it obviously fires Lasers. Now there are 2 types of lasers, Plasma Lasers, and Light Lasers. Plasma lasers are not composed of light, and instead by plasma. This causes them to move significantly slower than light, at around 90,000,000 mph/Mach 118,110 (Which is massively Hypersonic). However, light lasers are obviously composed of light beams. Which travels at the speed of light, or around 670,000,000 mph. However, I’d be led to believe that these are Light Lasers and not Plasma lasers since the bullets in question have shown to not explode on contact, and can be deflected by reflective surfaces like metal swords. So using this, we can scale Marco’s (and by proxy, Star’s) reaction time to FTL. But since Marco was deflecting them at the firing rate of a minigun, this speed feat can MASSIVELY increase.

Marco Blocks Laser Gun

Since the firing rate of a minigun can range from 2,000 - 6,000 Rounds Per Minute, or anywhere from 50 - 100 rounds per second.

If Marco did indeed deflect ALL of these bullets within the span of a second, this means his reaction is around 100x The Speed Of Light! Since he is deflecting these bullets at the rate they are firing at, this correlates to how fast he is deflecting them. And since Star keeps up with him on the regular, she should logically scale to Marco’s reaction speed. So using her highest ends she’s Massively faster than light, or MFTL. Now let’s scale her with her magic, AKA: Her Butterfly Form. Now, there is a way that you can calculate Star’s Multiplier, but this is pretty shaky. So, Meteora was able to absorb most of the Souls in the Kingdom of Mewni. And it’s heavily implied that her Growth is relative to how many souls she consumes. So, we could derive a sort of Multiplier through how much she grows. Thanks to Reddit User Murphy Paradox, they were able to calculate that Meteora grew by a factor of around 4.4x what her value was during her fight with Moon.

So using this logic, we Could say that her Butterfly Form is at bare minimum a 4x Increase from her base form. This is because she was shown to be relative to meteora.
