Pathways and Potions

In LOTM,Pathways are the main power system in the story,each pathway has a specific theme surrounding it and has Sequences which a person must climb through,lower the sequence the weaker the person is,Sequence 9 is the weakest and the higher the number the stronger the person.A person must drink a potion of a specific pathway so that they become a Beyonder of that Pathway.Beyonders are paranormal people who have drank a potion that gives them the powers of a specific pathway they chose,in LOTM there are 22 Pathways a person can take with each pathway having their strengths and weaknesses.

Important Pathways

For this Doc,you only have to learn about 4 Pathways and their lower Sequences

Seer Pathway

Sequence 9 “Seer”- Seers gain a massive increase in effectiveness of Divination Abilities compared to normal humans but at the cost of having no physical upgrades.They also gain Spirit Vision due to their enhanced Spirituality and an increased memory as well.

They also gain an ability called Spirit Vision,which allows the user to see different parts of a person's soul,they can also see a person's health and emotional state through colours in their body.

Sequence 8 “Clown”- Clowns gain increased physical capabilities allowing them to fight other Beyonders.Clowns gain near control of facial muscles allowing them to show facial expressions that they want to show even in mild pain,this ability can't function if the Beyonder is in extreme pain or shock.Clowns also gain an inhuman sense of coordination and balance.They still aren't as physically capable as other Pathways dedicated to fighting.

Clowns now also have danger intuition which allows the user to see a physical action that will be taken against them only a second prior.Think of it as Spiderman’s Spidey Sense but a bit weaker.

They also gain an ability to turn Paper into lethal weapons and can throw them like shurikens that can even stick to solid stone.

Spectator Pathway

Sequence 9 “Spectator”- Beyonders of the Spectator Pathway gain greatly enhanced inferential,analytical, observation and identification abilities along with enhanced Spectators also gain enhanced vision which enables them to see people from very far away.

They gain an ability to deduce a person’s mind, behaviour,actions etc just by their body language.

Sleepless Pathway

Sequence 9 “Sleepless”- Beyonders of the Sleepless pathway gain an ability called Nocturnality where in the night Sleepless Beyonders get increased physical and mental capabilities.They also only need to sleep for 3-4 hours per day.They can also use divination but it's much inferior than the Divinations of Beyonders of the Seer Pathway

Sequence 8 “Midnight Poet”-Midnight Poets gain an ability called Midnight Poem where they can induce different effects related to the night to other people or Beyonders when they sing their midnight poems,these effects can be Tranquility, Pacification etc.Their Nocturnality also gets boosted to the point where in deep night they can punch wood without any injuries.

Sequence 7 “Nightmare”- Nightmare’s main ability is to put people to sleep and invade their dreams which can be useful for killing other Beyonders or to pry out information.They can also use Midnight Poem without using their throat.Nightmares can also use tentacle-like limbs that protrude from their back to fight in close range situations.

Assassin Pathway

Sequence 9 “Assassin”- Assassins gain physical enhancements to their bodies,their body is also as light as a feather while they can also release their whole strength into one blow dealing huge damage.They can also hide in shadows reaching a level of concealment,while also gaining heightened senses and night vision.

Sequence 8 “Instigator”-They gain even more physical enhancements to their body.Instigators can instigate people indirectly causing other people to cause violent crimes.They can start fights between people,spread murders,conspiracies etc.They can also instigate people to believe their lies easier.

Sequence 7 “Witch”-Beyonders that reach Sequence 7 turn into women regardless of their prior gender,Witches have significantly increased charm,they can use various forms of Dark Magic like using Black Flames,Frost Magic,Mirror Magic.Witches also gain Divination abilities mainly mastering the use of Magic Mirror and Staff Divination,they can also interfere with other people's Divination making them a counter to Beyonders of the Seer Pathway.

Sequence 6 “Pleasure”-They gain an even larger physical enhancement to their body.They gain invisibility and can make thin,almost invisible threads that can restrict enemy movement.Pleasures gain enhanced charm beyond the human norm.Their capabilities of Dark Magic also get increased.They gain a three layer defense mechanism that protect them from death which encompasses of Black Flames,Frost and Invisible silk.

Sealed Artifacts

Sealed Artifacts are items that offer a wide variety of powerful effects at the cost of a significant drawback. The drawbacks occur because sealed artifacts are in a state of "having lost control." They are either man-made or natural.

Their powers correspond to certain Sequence abilities of Beyonders.

Sealed Artifacts usually come about in one of two ways: as a result of the death of an out of control Beyonder or made by a Sequence 6 or above of the Savant Pathway.

Sealed Artifacts have grades from 0 to  3 that correspond to their danger and power level.

Grade 3- Considerably dangerous. They have to be used carefully. It can only be applied for operations that require three or more people. Their abilities and power levels are equivalent to a Low-Sequence Beyonder (Sequences 8 or 9).

Grade 2- Dangerous. They can be used with care and moderation. Their ability and power levels are equivalent to a Mid-Sequence Beyonder (Sequences 5, 6, or 7).

Grade 1-Highly Dangerous. They can be used in limited ways. Their ability and power levels are equivalent to a Saint (Sequence 3 or 4 Beyonder).

Grade 0-Extremely dangerous, most important, and most confidential. They are not to be inquired, disseminated, described or spied on. Their abilities and power level are equivalent to an Angel (Sequence 1 or 2 Beyonder).

Grade:0

Code:0-08

Power

Anything written by it, within logical boundaries, will come true. The people written about will be unaware that they are participating, instead thinking that they are just going about their everyday life. They will not realize that they are being manipulated and controlled.
