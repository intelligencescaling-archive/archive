Definitions:
Anchor: A stabilizer for one not to lose control during their status of Godhood. Sequence 4-0 Anchors can be the deity’s believers, memories, identity, avatars etc… Anything that makes them significantly a real being.

Bug: a loophole

To graft: to bend reality into something

Marionette: a body whose movements and actions are mentally controlled by the owner. Typical of Klein

Avatar: a body whose movements and actions are mentally controlled by the owner. Typical of Amon

Beyonder: an animal(including humans) with supernatural powers.

Sequence: the level of the characteristics of a certain beyonder. They span from Sequence 9(weakest) to 0(strongest, a deity)

Above The Sequence: a being who has surpassed the Sequences by merging with a Sefirah(e.g. Sefirah Castle). An example of Above The Sequence is Celestial Worthy, as he has merged with Sefirah Castle.

Pathway: the type of path a beyonder can choose when advancing in sequence.  For example, the Sequence 9 of the Fool Pathway is called “Seer”, while the Sequence 9 of the Error Pathway is called “Marauder”.

Card of Blasphemy: an artifact that, when activated, becomes a piece of writing where all the ingredients for Sequences potions, names, powers and rituals from Sequence 9-0 are written. Each one of the 22 Pathways has a specific Card of Blasphemy. Each Card of Blasphemy embodies the characteristics of the Pathway.

Tentacle: a literal tentacle that grows under corruption from Above The Sequence entities. For example, during the awakening of the Celestial Worthy.

Terms that are intuitive such as “Fog of History” won’t be given a definition, as they are explicitly what the name tells.

The powers and identity of The Half Fool Antigonus (Half Sequence 0 The Fool) are sealed and concealed with darkness in the Astral World by Amanises. In this way, although Amon is able to unlock any seal with the Door Authority, he is not able to find the location of the seal due to Amanises’ concealment powers.

Klein Moretti is currently a Sequence 1 Attendant of Mysteries and has not yet attained the status of Sequence 0 The Fool, meaning he does not yet possess all the powers previously enlisted.

To ascend to Sequence 0, Klein goes to the peak of the Hornacis Mountain Range in the Astral World, the location protected by Amanises’ concealment, in order to attend the Apotheosis Ritual and merge his identity with The Half Fool Antigonus inside a palace.

The process of identity merging requires Klein to remember all of his memories, in order not to lose his own identity in the process. The identity of Antigonus is corrupted by madness. To make an example, in the Lord Of The Mysteries history Antigonus became so mad that during the War of the Four Emperors he attacked his own nation.
Thus, Klein’s identity merging is not only an act of  Working Memory and Emotional Realization, as he has to remember his own self through the recollection of his memories, but also of Emotional Management and AQ/AC, as it requires Klein to fight Antigonus' upcoming madness.

(WMI, EQR, EQM, AQ, Mental Adaptability)

Disguised as The Fool’s Angel of Time, as described in the Holy Book of The Fool, Amon strikes the bell and steals a portion of Klein’s anchors. On the other hand, Amon, aware of Klein’s possession of the Magic Wishing Lamp and having reasoned how The Genie would have helped Klein, had done this on purpose to slow down Klein’s ritual and urge him to disband the Realm Of Mysteries into reality (Having the Realm of Mysteries disbanded into the external world can help Klein significantly as he is currently the one to have major Authority over Sefirah Castle) to start taking control of it. Lastly, Klein, having reasoned Amon’s scheme, wasn’t worried about disbanding the Realm of Mysteries as to unleash his maximum powers too early, but wanted to wait for a proper stage of the Apotheosis Ritual and the moment where doing so would have amounted to the minimal amount of losses and the major amount of benefits.

Hence, Klein isn’t able to continuously avoid the characteristics from getting stolen by himself.
 Therefore, Klein needs to immediately take The Fool characteristics out of his body before Amon reaches the body and steals them.

Due to Klein’s previous plans, Amanises had already the Twilight Giant’s Sword in hand prior to this event.
 As shown before, Amanises destroys the bodies wherein the Celestial Worthy’s Will is awakening too abundantly, as they are bodies out of control—dangerous ones.

A supernova is a catastrophic stellar death when the core of a massive star collapses under its own gravity. This gives rise to a neutron star or a black hole, accompanied by a shockwave and neutrino burst. Formation of plasma and strong radiation emission are a consequence of the outward propagation of the shock. The core collapse and shock formation are rapid processes happening in milliseconds, while the outward explosion and visible light emission take place over a range of days to months. The supernova process is thus described by fast inner dynamics accompanied by slow outer expansion (NASA, "What Is a Supernova?"; Janka, 2012).

The released mass in a typical Type II supernova is 1 to 10 solar masses, equal to approximately 10³⁰ kg. Whereas atomic and subatomic processes of the supernova are photon emissions and electron transitions from femtosecond to nanosecond timescales, the macroscopic explosion is driven by gravitational collapse, shock propagation, and neutrino emission, which all evolve on timescales ranging from milliseconds to seconds. The explosion is then macroscopic hydrodynamic and neutrino-driven ones, and not atomic-scale reactions (ESA Science, Supernova Observations; Burrows & Lattimer, 1986).

Observing real-time quantum effects in a supernova in principle would require time resolution on the femtosecond or attosecond scale to monitor single photon emissions or changes in particle states. This level of accuracy is not necessary and is impractical in simulating a supernova, for simulations focus on macroscopic bulk plasma processes, radiation transport, and hydrodynamics. Models do not attempt to chart discrete quantum jumps for all particles since the explosion is not regulated by such processes (Science, "Attosecond Physics"; Summa et al., 2016).

With the ejected mass of about 10³⁰ kg and an average atomic weight of about 10⁻²⁷ kg per nucleon, the supernova ejecta have about 10⁵⁷ particles. This is a figure for the number of nucleons (protons and neutrons) in the ejected mass, and it is a fair estimate of particles considering usual conversions of mass to nucleons (Standard atomic mass calculations; Janka, 2012).

It is incorrect to assume that every one of these 10⁵⁷ particles encounters one single interaction every attosecond. In the star's dense core, rates of interaction go from extremely low to fairly high, but even then, neutrino and photon interactions fall far short. Practically, total interaction rates during the most dynamic phases of a supernova are likely to be between 10⁴⁵ and 10⁵⁰ per second, as determined from neutrino transport and high-density plasma physics.
(Janka, 2012; Burrows & Lattimer, 1986; Summa et al., 2016).
