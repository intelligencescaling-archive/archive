Franklin Saint's "Capture Teddy" Strategy

Welcome to an analysis of Snowfall a fairly underrated show that contains 2 pretty cool SCD characters that I hope to dive into

SPOILERS FOR SNOWFALL

This strategy is initiated after Louie (Franklins Aunt/ old business partner/employee) cuts out Franklin from his deals with Teddy which makes Franklin quit selling crack. Then Teddy (Franklins old CIA agent, business partner/boss) breaks into his home steals his bank statements and uses his CIA connections to wire 70 mil that he made selling crack  from his offshore accounts into CIA funds. Franklin has an extremely emotional reaction and crashes out. After the crash out he forms a pretty cool strategy to restore his 70 mil.

Franklin's Objective: Capture Teddy and restore his 70 million dollars.

First he steals a quick 5\~ mil from Louie and starts a gang war with her. This would announce to the world and thus Teddy that him and Louie were fighting and allow him to keep making money to keep himself afloat after being wiped clean. By announcing to Teddy that him and Louie were fighting Teddy would let his guard down and keep doing deals with Louie allowing franklin an in to get to Teddy rather than him just going ghost and using his CIA connections to disappear completely.

Franklin then uses his own connections to search for any of Teddy's weaknesses. Using his girls mum called Cassandra ( an elite scammer and con artist) he manages to find out about Teddy's father. He then uses Cassandra to bait Teddy's dad out of his nursing to his home address by having her seduce him. He then gets on the phone and threatens Teddy by threatening to kill his dad. Ideally he hoped Teddy would give the money back there and then but he knew that was unlikely.

After Teddy tries to call his bluff and refuses to give him the money Franklin coldly stabs and kills Teddy's father and holds the phone to him so Teddy could hear his fathers painful dying breaths. Then Franklin continues to threaten Teddy's wife and kid who he knows Teddy cares about way more. Franklin makes sure to wear gloves and burn the crime scene down to leave no evidence.

This series of actions allowed Franklin to achieve a number of objective to further his overarching one.

\>He psychologically rattled Teddy by utilising his tumultuous relationship with his father. Franklin made an accurate psychological evaluation that this tumultuous relationship would lead to a raw and chaotic flurry of emotions to stir within Teddy making him act recklessly

\>He emotionally manipulated Teddy into seeking revenge on him for killing his father

\>He logically manipulated Teddy into believing he needed to eliminate Franklin in order to procure his and his family's safety.

P1: Franklin found and killed my father who I thought was safe over his 70 million

P2: Franklin found out the name of my child and threatened my ex-wife and kid

C1: Franklin poses a threat to me and family

P3: Franklin left no evidence of his crime

P4: Franklin possesses critical information about me and the agency

C2: I cannot use law enforcement to eliminate Franklin as any information I reveal about his wrong doings could be used against me and the agency if he were to reveal my crimes

P5: Franklin is quickly running out of money and will soon have nothing left to lose

P6: I can't hide my family with CIA connections forever as my ex-wife dislikes me and won't want to stay with me under CIA security for extended periods of time

C3: I have to eliminate Franklin quickly before he loses everything and does the nuclear option of revealing all that he knows to law enforcement and before my Ex-Wife leaves my 	    grasp so that Franklin does not obtain her as a hostage.

The combination of these points would cause Teddy to believe he had to personally strike back against Franklin without ever suspecting Franklin had a deeper strategy guiding him. This tactic allowed Franklin counterattacking possibilities and actively prevents Teddy from using his CIA connections to completely disappear from Franklin. Franklin's brutal actions would also ensure Teddy's strike against him would be far more clumsy than they otherwise would be due to Teddy being emotionally rattled.

Franklin then engineers a scenario that would allow him to control Teddys strike back and counter act it with minimal risk.

Franklin tips off a rival gang leader about how Louie ordered a hit on them. This causes the rival gang to kidnap and torture Louie. He would then come and save her utilising the white knight manipulation method and the torture to emotionally break her down so that she would submit to him. He then uses her to set up a meeting with Teddy where she would claim to have captured Franklin and deliver him to Teddy. Teddy would buy into this story due to his judgement being clouded due to Franklins previous actions and the pretence would be believable to him as he would know Franklin and Louie had been fighting for a decent amount of time now escalating to the point of many deaths including Louie's own husband.

He then instructs Louie to have him beat up and tie him up to be dropped at a discrete location for Teddy to pick him up.

Franklin then calls El Oso (Franklins Friend/ business partner) who he judged as loyal. He tells him the location that Louie would be dispatching him at so that he could camp there and get the jump on Teddy when he comes to pick up Franklin.

Contingency: In case anything went wrong and El Oso couldn't camp there Franklin knew El Oso and Teddy were still very close so El Oso could use his info on Teddy to track him down.

This contingency is actually what saves him.

Due to a complication with the DEA El Oso is late to the pick up and has to break into Teddy's secret base whilst fighting off a KGB member. El Oso wins and hands both of them to Franklin who has now captured Teddy and gained another powerful asset. Franklins final component of the strategy was to torture Teddy so that he would give up the money so Franklin Saint's 70 million dollars of drug money would be restored.
