Chou Manipulation
Objective: Friend needs to accomplish 4 things: Remind his old teacher of the spoon bending incident in the year 1971, get rid of any competent detectives on his tracks, get members of the police on his side, and make sure his identity as Katsumata is secure.

Context: Detective Chou, the world renowned detective, has started investigating Friend’s cult. Friend has kidnapped professor Shikishima through blackmail using his daughter. Friend notices Chou and has to do something about him, otherwise Friend might have to expedite his plan, which Friend doesn’t want.

Friend learns about Chou investigating the Friend cult. He has to come up with a strategy that gets rid of Chou; instead of seeing this as a liability, Friend instead sees this as an opportunity to accomplish numerous objectives and increase his security.
He first draws a Friend symbol on a wooden plank beside Shikishima’s house. Knowing that Chou is a world renowned detective, he concurs that Chou loves doing his work and has a strong sense of justice, thus he will investigate the case of the missing professor. To draw further attention to the symbol, he makes multiple students of professor Shikishima wear the shirt via utilizing 13 (at that time a student who’s very close to Shikishima). Now that Chou has noticed the Friend symbol, Friend reasons that he will start to investigate the Friend case too. He lets some rumors escape from his cult to further increase Chou’s suspicion (or Chou just learns them naturally, either works). While Chou is away, Friend goes to Yama (Chou’s sidekick detective). He emotionally perceives Yama’s weak psyche in relation to Chou. He reads Yamane and notices that he has an inferiority complex with Chou. He uses this to quickly brainwash him and take control of his actions. He uses Yama to gather more info on Chou so he can later manipulate him further. Friend confirms his suspicions of Chou being a hard worker, but he also learns that Chou has failed repeatedly at being a family man. Friend next reasons that Chou would seek out a theologist since Friend’s cult is in fact a cult. The theologist would soon tell him about the rumor floating about one of Friend’s members killing Pierre (another religious figure). Chou would then go to Pierre and try to learn any info he could, Friend thinks. So Friend gets a cult member with good acting skills to impersonate a Pierre follower and feed Chou info (we know that it’s a fake fan since Katsumata himself later contradicts some of the info he gives). The fake cult member gives Chou Katsumata’s fake name (Hattori). Friend reasons that Chou will further investigate and try to find Hattori until he finds the school that he went to (since it’s also the only school that Katsumata disclosed information on). Friend then fabricates all the school records, making sure to include “Hattori Fukubei” as one of the names (could have done this way before Chou too, in fact he most likely did). Friend also accounts for any holes in this plan; for example, if Chou had found that Kenji was Hattori’s classmate. He used his connections to send Kenji’s corporate manager to go berate him. Chou, being a bad family man, would see Kenji getting chewed out by his manager and not want to interfere since he wouldn’t want to cause a family man like Kenji to be in any more of a hassle than he already is. Friend further reasons that Chou would go to Hattori’s teacher and ask him if he knows anything about Hattori/the year 71’ which is where the next part of Friend’s new strategy arises. Chou would force the teacher to remember the spoon bending incident of 71’, prompting events to come. Chou would investigate further and further until he got to the bottom of the case or close to it since he’s a legendary detective. Chou would report all of this evidence to Yama since Yama is his right hand man; he would likely leave plenty of notes on the case too. Now most of Friend’s goals are complete except for one. Yama calls Friend to relay everything to him; Friend is satisfied with the results so he orders Yama to kill Chou with the new virus, something that Chou can’t possibly notice. Yama kills Chou and steals all his notes on Friend. And just like that, Friend’s goals are complete. He accomplished 4 goals at once with an improvised strategy that he had to think up on the spot, which also segways and improves on the next strategy Friend deploys. Friend indoctrinated an important police member to work for him and deal with important info control by emotionally manipulating him; he killed the legendary detective Chou and got rid off one of the only people who could investigate further into the Friend case; he collected notes from Chou which detailed the errors in Friend’s concealment and fabrication (If any, we don’t know for sure if Chou discovered anything about Katsumata. We only know that he discovered Hattori and Sadakiyo.) by manipulating Chou in every type of way; and he reminded his old teacher of the spoon bending incident, increasing his concealment further by having Chou do it instead of himself, which also adds onto another strategy which he had to come up with on the fly.
Extra side note: Chou most likely wasn’t able to find out the identity of Katsumata based on the notes. It’s also highly likely that he got misdirected really badly.
