Friend debunked

Point 1
Friend is carried by information asymmetry.

Friend operates with a major informational advantage. He has access to everything, while his opponents do not, so he has an epistemological bias. Imagine two people competing in a math contest, but one person does not know most of the equations while the other does, so the latter has the advantage. It is similar here.
Most of his feats boil down to a knowledge gap due to him having thugs everywhere. He does not outsmart because he reasons better, but because he has more access and control.
Thus, it heavily downscales his feats because there is a power and informational gap. We do not know how he scales in pure intelligence without an informational advantage.
This brings me to my next point.

Point 2
Friend is a politics merchant.

He uses his influence and power to achieve everything. He manipulates variables, and that may seem like an intelligence feat, but with the access he has to surveillance and information, his control of variables requires pretty much no intelligence, just common sense and maybe elementary reasoning.
For example, him making everyone figure out his identity.
Everything he does in this is just elementary reasoning that a child could do.

Point 3
He relies on pre-existing scripts.

Everything Friend does, including NBOP, is based on Kenji's ideas in BOP. Friend has to use this book to plan everything, showing his weakness in planning.
This shows that Friend is only good at executing but cannot create his own frameworks.

Point 4
His manipulation is trivial.

Him manipulating people to be in his cult is a low-tier feat. Real cult leaders have done this, and it is very easy. Pretty much anyone with an IQ above 90 can do it. Broken people look for something to find comfort in. Pretty much anyone can approach them, pretend to be some messianic figure, and manipulate them. The fragile mind is weak and can easily be bent. So Friend's feat is essentially nothing.
His manipulation of the main characters is also trivial.
"WOW, he manipulated Kanna by reminding her of her childhood. He is so smart."
Literally anyone can do that.
It is a below low-tier feat.

Point 5
Power-manipulation cheat code.

This is the thing that gives the illusion of Friend being good.
The more power Friend has, the easier he can manipulate people.
The easier he can manipulate, the more power he gets.
Literally everything he does is based on this, and it is a low-tier feat.
Without this, he would have nothing because he never does anything with his brain. He is a power merchant. Without power, he is below Yumeko.

Point 6
Bad modeling.

Friend's planning models are mediocre and leave out crucial variables. A good planning model has to account for everything. Friend's models do not. Instead of considering probabilistic scenarios and thinking in terms of Markov chains, his models are linear and straightforward. They have no recursive elements. Hence why Kenji beats him, because Kenji is the outlier that Friend never accounts for.

Point 7
Closed system merchant.

Friend only thrives in a closed system environment where he controls the variables. Put him in an open system environment where he has to face unknown variables, like STL or basically any post-volume 2 scenario in RI, and he would get destroyed because he is featless in that area. He needs control of variables to thrive, which downscales him because he needs advantages of control and power to even stand a chance against low or mid tiers.

Point 8
Friend does not work alone.

Everything Friend did, he did with help. We have never seen him outsmart alone. He always needs his men, so his outsmarting is not even fully his.

Point 9
Messy layers.

Friend's plans require entities like subordinates, engineers, scientists, and others.
This adds extra layers of human agency, which are obviously flawed, so this downscales his plans.

Point 10
Anti-feats.

Friend ultimately failed in spreading the virus, since a cure was found, stopping Kenji, and detonating the bomb.

Conclusion: Friend is an overrated mid tier who gets neg-diffed by Fang Yuan and mid-diffed by Baku.
