Explaining and rebunking Friend’s VR feat (minor spoilers for 20th CB)

VR stands for Virtual Reality and is a section of Friend Land/World, likely being built around 2002-2010\~.

A lot of people have tried to downplay this feat through disingenuous arguments and total lack of comprehension.

this is a response to those inaccuracies and an explanation of the feat and why it scales so high.

The first “debunk”: Friend didn’t make the VR.

This is one of the worst arguments you can make. The VR is a recreation of a town that by now is 30+ years old. It has thousands of ai that match the people in that town perfectly and act accordingly. It also contains memories that only Friend would know about such as the Hanging Hills, the 1971 hanging, and any such events.

No one else, even if they lived in that town (most would be dead by now btw), could recreate all the events in the VR, especially the ones concerning the Kenji/Friend factions. Friend would need to recreate them himself, because those memories are deeply private to Friend himself and need to be captured perfectly for his plan to work. Urasawa also blatantly hints that Friend is the one who created it (twice), one time by one of the side characters who says “only the creator of the game can move like this” and the other time by the narrator who says that the VR world is a “recreation of Friend’s mind/memories”.

The second debunk: “Friend didn’t do any complex cognitive process to make the VR, he just used the PV machine mechanic to upload his memories into the game, thus making the levels”

Equally bad. Not only does this contradict the inclusion of ai but it’s also not how memory upload works. The PV machine allows your consciousness to recreate a movie style version of your memories (manually btw). The VR on the other hand is a fully interactive system where you can explore city wide areas with complete freedom and interact with the ai’s (some of which have special functions btw). The environment changes every second, so there’s absolutely no way Friend could have uploaded a single view of the town. It has real world physical laws in it.

Now onto why this feat is so good:

EU/ER) The VR has a perfect copy (1:1) of hundreds of people on screen and most likely thousands judging by how big the town is. They are PERFECT. They do actions in EXACTLY the same way the real world counterparts did them. A lot of the time these are things that Friend didn’t see, so they’re not just recreated to look perfectly, they really have the qualia of their real life counterparts. This includes people that Friend (as a child) probably would have barely even seen, such as the bartender/bar (a place friend could never even enter), yet even that is recreated perfectly. For at least 5 years, every single AI in that town, when not interfered with, acted exactly the way their real world counterpart did. Even Yoshitsune comments that the ai are perfect and that the simulation is perfect when he meets his younger self and Mon chan (his childhood friend). Friend knew Yoshitsune so well that he predicted what he’d be doing on a random day 30 years ago better than Yoshitsune himself did (and got it right with so much accuracy that it made yoshitsune cry) As for ER, Friend makes an ai version of himself as well. Equally perfect ER.

VSI/knowledge/sensory/intelligence) As I mentioned previously. Friend recreates a large town (possibly more than that) that he saw 30 years ago in perfect detail. It functions perfectly for at least 5 years, its physics engine is 1:1 with the real world enough to where it follows everything accordingly for 5 years, simply think about how insane of a feat that is. We are in the year 2026 and still can’t even come close to simulating real life, yet Friend, most likely by himself, codes this entire engine in the early 2000’s while working on global domination. The VR has sound temperature taste touch dust and water physics shadows wind weather etc, absolutely unreal for the time. Speaking of the senses, they are perfectly reconstructed too, meaning Friend has nigh perfect sensory input and memory. Millions of different tiny variables recreated perfectly to match the real world in 1969-1973. A lot of these variables Friend would have to recreate apriori of course but a good deal of them would be his own experiences. VSI is obvious ofc. And judging by the donkey scene it’s most likely that the VR extends beyond the town itself. The knowledge required is insane. The school is recreated along with all the teachers thus Friend would most likely have to know a great majority of all majors and fields related to education. He would need to know all the different types of insects, vegetation, etc (or research about it to actually make the simulation). The implications are endless.

AC) finally, as we see in the scans, the VR is directly linked to memory and your consciousness (and brain waves). Whenever someone is forcefully taken out of the VR they suffer enough backlash to chew their own wrists in order to commit sui. For reference the students who did that absorbed only a part of 3 days of memories. Friend, Kenji, and Manjoume would go on to absorb 59, 5, and 3-5 years respectively. The students also had anesthetics, which Friend and Manjoume didn’t even have (Kenji neither I think? I don’t remember).

The VR functions as part of Friend’s scheme but I won’t be covering that because it would take too much time. These aren’t all the impressive parts of the feat either but they are the most important ones imo. Do with this feat what you will, there are countless things you can draw out of it.
