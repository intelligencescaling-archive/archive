Friend AQ/AC Mini-Doc
(Work in Progress; Subject to Change)

Friend’s AQ/AC has been severely underrated as of recent when in actuality, it’s fairly good. I will be showing some of those feats in this doc. This analysis will feature PE Friend and OG Friend, the feats for all three versions are fairly similar. The original friend doc addressed most of these points but I want to expand on them a little more. KT Friend might have some extra feats in AC that I might add in post.

Feat #1 (PE exclusive): Death of Fukubei

As seen in chapter 175-176, Fukubei dies from hanging himself. Katsumata and Yamane are sadly too late to save him and so he was never saved from that tragic fate. Katsumata, without hesitation, inherits the title of friend and becomes the new leader. He does this at AGE 11. Compare this to Kenji and Yoshitsune who’re scared to go into an abandoned house. Katsumata (PE) on the other hand manages to dispose of the body, hide the evidence, and take on the role of friend (while continuing and realizing Fukubei’s and Kenji’s plan ☠). He also manages to adapt to every relationship that Fukubei had without anyone noticing.
(Stress tolerance, mental fortitude, perseverance, multitasking, adaptability, decision making)

Feat #2:PV (chapter 176)

PV is a feat in which Friend manages to upload 50 years of memories from MULTIPLE perspectives into a machine in a single second (possibly two). The specifics of PV are quite complicated and vague so this feat won’t be as concrete as the others.  As for the AC aspect, Friend managed to show all events in the story from multiple PoVs, at one point in the process he did multiple of them at once. It’s not specified but the machine that was processing his data wasn’t fast enough to keep up with friend (☠) so it forcefully shut down which caused Friend to feel immense pain (the machine literally made friend’s brain forcefully disconnect from the system), yet not a second after that friend gets up like nothing happened and wonders about what his intuition was telling him.
(Mental fortitude, top 1 multitasking, cognitive flexibility, cognitive discipline, etc)
Extra: Kenji, when absorbing a year's worth of info started bleeding out of his nose and nearly had an aneurysm (might have even killed him). Friend on the other hand, when processing 46 times that amount only did a slight yelp and was fine immediately after. (Insane mental fortitude) (chapter 15 of 21st cb)
Feat #3: Coffin and Rebirth (the best AC feat)

In order to execute his Rebirth plan, Friend had to stay in a coffin for four and a half days. Those four and a half days he had to stay completely still, not to move a muscle, otherwise everyone would see him. He couldn’t eat, sleep, or drink on account of people seeing him (sleeping would make him move). Coffin scene also took place in summer of 2015 in Japan, coincidentally Japan had a heat wave at that time, reaching lows of 32C at noon and often exceeding 35C, keep in mind that Friend was wearing a full bodysuit with a mask during that whole time. And after two whole days of doing nothing, Friend managed to walk out of the coffin, walk up to the pope, and, without any second thought, body-block the pope while receiving a bullet to the shoulder. (He had to sit in that coffin thinking about how when he gets out of it for the first time in two days, he would need to immediately go and body-block someone, then get shot in the shoulder. Absolutely insane AC). Then afterwards he didn’t scream nor show any sign of him feeling pain (verbally). While composing himself he pushed himself away from the pope’s bodyguard WHILE talking to them coherently, walked a couple meters to the right, fell down on his knees, and held up HIS ALREADY INJURED HAND to once again reinstate the Friend symbol.
(Impulse Management>=Mental Fortitude>=Stress Tolerance>Perseverance>=Cognitive discipline)

(The coffin is also slightly tilted, so Friend had to nearly stand in that coffin, flexing his back foot muscles as hard as possibly since his shoes are sticking out of the coffin)

Some other noteworthy feats:

1. Killed billions of people without any sign of weakness. (MF)
2. In BNYE he stood on top of a round object (the 1970 exhibit statue) while explosions were happening everywhere (falling buildings, virus spreading, etc). He then clashed with Kenji, causing a giant explosion. If any of this had gone wrong, Friend would have died in an instant, yet he managed to stay composed throughout the whole thing. (MF, ST, Perseverance)
3. All events in the story had to be predicted and calculated very carefully; if Friend made even one mistake his whole 50 year plan could be foiled. (ST)
