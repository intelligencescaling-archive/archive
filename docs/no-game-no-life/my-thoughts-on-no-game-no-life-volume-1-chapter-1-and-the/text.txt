My thoughts on No Game No Life Volume 1: (Chapter 1 + Rock-Paper-Scissors Game)

Chapter 1:

We then cut back to Sora and Shiro in a tavern, playing a game. A poker, which their opponent got a full house, but lost since the duo got a Royal Flush. Of course, they cheated, and it was off-screen, but one thing to take note is that they bet everything on the line, telling the opponent that he could do anything he wants with them if he wins. Their lives were in risk, yet they stayed calm & cheated.

And of course, they needed a place to stay, so they went to the counter.

Sora, without having any understanding of the value of money, was able to see through the tavern owner that he was trying to swindle him with the price. Then, Sora was able to coerce the owner to give him a really good discount after somehow figuring out he has been embezzling customers’ money. This shows Sora’s ability to see through lies

Later, they go back to the duo looking at the match between the Royal Candidates, and they actually saw through Zell’s cheats quickly. Which tells us the duo is pretty sharp and observant.

Bandits came, and wanted to play games with them, but the duo made the risky bet again, risking their everything. It was stated that due to Sora’s ability to adapt to any form of game, he could accept every requests of the bandits as if he knew he would win. They then could get the most important information they needed (including 10 covenants)

\>“Uhh, no I didn’t. Anyway, I’m Sora. I’ve never had a girlfriend in my life, and I’m looking!”

I laughed a bit at this line, this was like right after Steph entered the room. Not a feat or anything, just wanted to put this in.

Now that Steph came in the room pissed, Sora started taunting her by calling her grandpa foolish king etc, then challenged her with a game of Rock-Paper-Scissors. The taunt was most likely to manipulate her into accepting the game.

So… this is how the game went.

1.      Sora taunts Steph into accepting his game terms. Despite Steph having the chance to change the game rules (according to the ten covenants) she accepted it because of how Sora leads her into playing the game

2.      Sora states that he wants a favor, and then talks about how he wants a place to stay. But when Steph asks to confirm it, he only smiles. (the reason will come later)

3.      Sora says he will only play Paper, and playing anything other than that would count for his loss, but if he wins with anything other than Paper, it would be a draw, since both would lose.

4.      This manipulates Steph into thinking that Sora only wants a place to stay, so he is aiming for a draw. For Steph, playing Rock or Scissors would have given her the best chance at winning. But for Rock she had a 1/3 chance of losing

5.      This makes Steph into thinking that Sora is trying to manipulate her into playing Scissors, only for him to play with Rock.

6.      But just then, Sora smirks at Steph with a confident gaze, this leads Steph into rethinking, and makes herself believe that Sora would have no reason to play anything other than Paper, since Paper would give him 1/3 to win, 1/3 to lose, and 1/3 to draw. However, the other two options would give him 2/3 to lose and 1/3 to draw.

7.      After that , Sora plays Rock and draw the game.

So, with subtle actions and words, Sora was completely able to predict Steph’s actions and thought process, as well as winning the game.

And remember about Sora’s favor? Sora didn’t actually confirm the favor, so now he can asks Steph anything he wants since wanting a place to stay was just a bait.

And then the comedy scenes went on, regarding Sora’s favor, and some other stuff. That’s all for chapter 1.

In summary, The duo are pretty good at adapting to new places, both of them are quite sharp and observant. They also don’t waste any time to be able to get the info they want to fit into the new environment

As for the Rock-Paper-Scissors Game, it wasn’t anything outstanding, but it was a pretty decent short game. We saw a glimpse of how Sora is able to manipulate and predict the actions of others, as well as abusing the loophole as he wants.
