STAR-GRAIL:

The Star Grail (星杯, Hoshi-hai), also known as the Sunya Star (スーニアスター, Sūnīasutā), is a conceptual device with the concept of absolute supremacy. A device that bestows its owner the sole Throne of the One and Only God and absolute power over all other races.

At the climax of the Great War, the Suniaster was finally able to manifested itself through the convergence of power from the eruption of the Source of Spirit Corridor, which had been detonated by the Stalemartyr.

Explaining more on Star-Grail:
It is the very manifestation of the power of the currents within the Source of Spirit Corridor, which are, unequivocally, the power of Spirits.The Spirits are the life force and/or power themselves and are the driving force of the world. Hence, the Source of Spirit Corridor is said to be the source of all things and the flow of all creation.

The Suniaster is more or less the core of the world, All Exceed are born from the Spirit Circuits which in turn come from the Suniaster. The war was faught in order to try and obtain the Suniaster and become the One True God.

As Star-Grail (Suniaster) is the source and origin of everything, it is where all existing powers and abilities come from. All the powers that exist in the universe are said to be only fragments that have fallen from it.
