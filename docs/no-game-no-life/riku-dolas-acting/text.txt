(V6) - Riku Dola has a unique form of acting which is deeply involved in his ability to freely manipulate the strength of the ‘lock’ on his heart at his own will from merely locking it to locking it “several times harder than usual”. From closing his feelings entirely, Riku is able to use other emotions to play and act a different persona at his own choosing from a cold, calculating and icy character who’d show no remorse ordering a fellow partner of his to kill themselves for the sake of the war to a meek and sorrowful character able to pour out sympathy and weakness.
This heart-lock persona is potent enough to where it’s able to even fool Riku’s own memory and other vitals as Riku himself had trouble telling whether his acting was fake or not when using it against Schwi.
