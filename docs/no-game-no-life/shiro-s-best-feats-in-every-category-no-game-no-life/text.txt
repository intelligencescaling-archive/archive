Shiro’s Best Feats in Every Category

I’m only accounting for categories in which she has feats.

VSI: (EP1) She can visualise 10¹²⁰ possible chessboard states for reference that’s more than every atom in the observable universe 10⁸⁰

WMI: (EP1) She can calculate 10¹²⁰ possible chessboard states.

VCI: \[EP9\] She goes through her past memories to figure out that Sora was wiped from people’s memories by deciphering his past verbal hints, also discovering from them that Sora played a game with Chlammy Zell that involved stealing each other’s existence, and that the game is still being played.

PSI: (Volume 9 Chapter 3) She outstripped a cluster of Ex-Machina, with the cluster having processing speed that can exceed infinity; this makes Shiro’s processing speed above theirs.

FRI: (EP5) She assested a male-to-female ratio using data on time-based variables, occupations, and the employment rate in the area, along with the general trends she’s observed in the past, to predict whether a man or a female would enter the alleyway she was in to win the bet against Stephanie.

EU: (EP3) She understood, judging by his struggle, that Sora was having a breakdown from the sentient chess match with Chlammy Zell, so she calmed him down.

EP: (EP7) At the age of three, she could tell just by looking at Sora’s smile that it was fake—and he was only smiling because everyone else in the room was smiling.

Stress Tolerance Mental Fortitude Determination & Perseverance: (EP1) She defeated 120 players using only her feet to control the mouse and keyboard, after gaming for five straight days without sleep.

Deception: (EP11) She pretended to be under Izuna’s control in *Love or Loved 2* by faking being hit by Izuna’s bullet—timing her fall to be in synchronicity with the gun before it fired—deceiving Izuna and even temporarily fooling Sora, whom she pretended to target by chasing him down.

Spatial Intelligence & Knowledge: (Volume 3 Chapter 3) While going against Izuna, she uses pursuit curves to anticipate the most likely path Izuna would take if she were to move, applying backpropagation to account for the possibility of Izuna retracing her steps, and using the Dirac delta function to estimate how Izuna's presence umight diffuse in the environment as she moves or hides. She also uses particle filters to update the probability distribution of Izuna's location, and finally, linear discriminant functions to classify and determine potential hiding spots or movements.

Strategy: (EP12) She exploited the love gun mechanic as a countermeasure, ensuring a win even if she and Sora were taken out.

Planning: (EP12) She planned for Stephanie to defeat Izuna in Love or Loved 2 by exploiting the love gun mechanic. First, she hand-wrote Stephanie’s intended actions, then made Stephanie forget them by ensuring she was hit by a love bullet, which caused her memory to lapse until another bullet struck her. Afterward, she led Stephanie to the predicted location of the Bunny Girl NPC, having calculated the NPC’s movements in advance, and ricocheted a bullet off Jibril’s shot to hit Stephanie, restoring her memory so Stephanie would then take down Izuna after Izuna had defeated her and Sora.

Learning Ability: (EP2) She learned Immanity-go’s writing system in less than twenty minutes. The spoken language is identical to Japanese, but the writing system is different, having different characters for hiragana. In the light novel, the writing system also uses Latin grammar and Ancient Chinese syntax.

Sensory: (EP5) She knew a donut had a shortage of sugar, salt, and butter just from a glance, meaning her eyes were good enough to see each granule of sugar, salt, and butter on the donut.
