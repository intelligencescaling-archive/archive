\[EP12\] She planned for Stephanie to defeat Izuna in *Love or Loved 2* by exploiting the love gun mechanic and sand-writing to Stephanie what she was going to do, then making her forget her actions until a bullet hit her, causing her to be shot by one of the bullets. She then led her to the predicted location of the Bunny Girl NPC after calculating the NPC’s movements in advance, and ricocheted a bullet off Jibril’s bullet to hit her, making her remember what she was supposed to do to take out Izuna.

This feat showcases \[STP (Short Term Planning) CT (Creative Thinking) ST (Strategic Thinking) PC (Planning Convergence) NCPS (Novel Complex Problem Solving) TS (Trap Setting) LT (Linear Thinking) SIA (Situational Awareness) PS (Problem Solving) LGT (Logical Thinking) Foresight and WMI (Working Memory Index)\].

\[EP9\] She goes through her past memories and deciphers Sora’s past verbal hints in them to come to the conclusion that Sora played a game with Chlammy Zell that involved stealing each other’s existence, and that the game is still being played.

This feat showcases \[VCI (Verbal Comprehension Index) CT (Creative Thinking) PS (Problem Solving) FRI (Fluid Reasoning Index) LGT (Logical Thinking) LR (Logical Reasoning) SIA (Situational Awareness) WMI (Working Memory Index) and Memory\].

\[EP11\] She pretended to be under Izuna’s control in *Love or Loved 2* after faking being hit, by Izuna’s bullet deceiving Izuna and even temporarily deceiving Sora, whom she pretended to target by chasing him down.

This feat showcases \[AS (Acting Skills) DIM (Direct Manipulation) PM (Psychological Manipulation) and Deception\].

\[EP5\] Assessed a male-to-female ratio using data on time-based variables, occupations, and the employment rate in the area, along with the general trends she’s observed in the past, to predict whether a man or a female would enter the alleyway she was in to win the bet against Stephanie.

This feat showcases \[VCI (Verbal Comprehension Index) CT (Creative Thinking) LGT (Logical Thinking) AR (Abductive Reasoning) LT (Linear Thinking) PR (Pattern Recognition) SIA (Situational Awareness) NCPS (Novel Complex Problem Solving) ST (Strategic Thinking) PS (Problem Solving) WMI (Working Memory Index) and Foresight\].

\[Volume 1 Chapter 2\] After being thrown into Disboard she adapted and learned the entire written language of Imanity in just fifteen minutes becoming fluent in eighteen languages, including their classical forms so she could speak and understand people in Disboard who use imanity.

This feat showcases \[LA (Learning Ability) LC (Learning Capability) ST (Strategic Thinking) LGT (Logical Thinking) LT (Linear Thinking) and Adaptability\].

\[Volume 3 Chapter 3\] While going against Izuna, she uses pursuit curves to anticipate the most likely path Izuna would take if she were to move, applying backpropagation to account for the possibility of Izuna retracing her steps, and using the Dirac delta function to estimate how Izuna's presence umight diffuse in the environment as she moves or hides. She also uses particle filters to update the probability distribution of Izuna's location, and finally, linear discriminant functions to classify and determine potential hiding spots or movements

This feat showcases \[VSI (Visual Spatial Index) CT (Creative Thinking) LGT (Logical Thinking) LT (Linear Thinking) ST (Strategic Thinking) Knowledge and Foresight\].

(EP7) At the age of 3 when she first met Sora she knew he was using a happy facade because everyone else was smiling while really he felt empty she also knew his name meant Sky and used this knowledge to create a double entendre.

This feat showcases \[VCI (Verbal Comprehension Index) SOA (Social Awareness) EU (Emotional Understanding) and CR (Cold Reading)\].

\[Volume 1 Prologue / EP1\] She defeated a chess program that had shut out the world’s best grandmaster of world chess and was capable of exploring two hundred million moves per second. She did this by, alternating between the first and second moves, to highlight the program's imperfections then after identifying those weaknesses she defeated it twenty times in a row.

This feat showcases \[NCPS (Novel Complex Problem Solving) PS (Problem Solving) CT (Creative Thinking) ST (Strategic Thinking) LGT (Logical Thinking) LT (Linear Thinking) and TS (Trap Setting)\].
