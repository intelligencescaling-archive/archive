Why didn’t Tsukumo bring Midzuno with him during the first July 22 Hand Plan?

My memory of Massacre Happy End is a little fuzzy right now, so I might be missing some detail or rule that explains this. If I am, feel free to correct me.

During the first July 22, after Tsukumo figures out the Time Loop, he is literally with Midzuno before confronting Makoto, but he specifically tells her to go back and then approaches Makoto alone. At this point, Tsukumo has already concluded that Shiori is being carried through the loop and that physical contact can bring other things along with the person traveling through time. His entire Hand Plan is based on this assumption: he handcuffs himself to Makoto because he believes remaining in direct contact with him at midnight could drag him into the loop as well.

So why send Midzuno away?

She would not even need to touch Makoto from the beginning. She could simply remain nearby as backup and an independent witness while Tsukumo carries out the exact same Hand Plan.

She could also come prepared as normal police backup, including bringing a firearm or some other means of defending herself if necessary. The point would not be for her to shoot Makoto immediately, but to make it much harder for him to simply attack Tsukumo, sever the connection, and walk away without consequence.

If Makoto cuts Tsukumo’s hand off, Midzuno personally witnesses an extremely serious assault and can immediately intervene. She could order him to stop, restrain him, call for additional officers, and—if possible—maintain direct physical contact with him until midnight.

This creates a much worse situation for Makoto than in the original plan.

1. If he stays attached to Tsukumo, Tsukumo continues his experiment.

2. If he cuts Tsukumo’s hand off and tries to escape, Midzuno is already there to stop him.

3. If he attacks Midzuno as well, he is now openly attacking a second police officer who is in a position to defend herself.

4. And if Midzuno manages to restrain him and maintain physical contact until midnight, she becomes a second test of the exact same hypothesis Tsukumo was already testing on himself.

Tsukumo does not need to already know that multiple people can enter the loop. That is not the point. His own plan already assumes that a human in direct physical contact with Makoto may be carried through the reset. Having Midzuno there simply gives him redundancy and a second chance if Makoto manages to separate himself from Tsukumo.

If Midzuno touching Makoto does not work, almost nothing is lost compared to the original plan.

If it does work, the payoff is enormous: Midzuno enters the reset while remembering the previous timeline and becomes an independent police witness to both the Time Loop and Makoto severing Tsukumo’s hand.

What makes this stranger to me is that Tsukumo later comes up with essentially the same general idea anyway. After the reset, he tells Midzuno to hold Shiori at midnight so she can personally experience the Time Loop instead of merely trusting his explanation.

So is there an actual reason Tsukumo needed to confront Makoto alone during the first July 22?

Was there some mechanical limitation he knew about, some risk caused by Midzuno being present, or is sending her away simply a suboptimal part of the Hand Plan?
