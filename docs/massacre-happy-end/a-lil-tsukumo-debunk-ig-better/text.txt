This is the interpretation. If you've read it, or already know it in some way, we can start debunking it.
Okay, so let's go step by step, and I'll raise some questions along the way.
The first thing you have to understand is that there are two Tsukumos who live through July 22. I'm going to call them Tsukumo 1 (the original one, before the time reset) and Tsukumo 2 (the one after the time reset on July 22).
The plan starts when Tsukumo 1 overhears a conversation between Midzuno and a random police officer. The officer jokes that if Midzuno cares about him that much, why not handcuff him to herself? After hearing this, Tsukumo 1 gets the idea of handcuffing himself to Kusakabe. (This happens at around 02:47, give or take a little.)
After 14 hours and 22 minutes, Tsukumo 1 comes out of his room and says, "Something disappeared." He concludes that the "something" is Suzuhara (Pics. 1, 2, and 3). He goes to her place and confirms he was right. Kusakabe killed her after the incident at the hospital.
After that, he goes out with Midzuno to look for Kusakabe. While they are heading back to the department, he sees Kusakabe on a bus and goes after him, telling Midzuno to return without him. He catches Kusakabe and handcuffs him to himself.
He then explains his speculation that a time loop may exist and that Kusakabe controls it. Afterward, he drags Kusakabe in front of a CCTV camera and says that if the time loop is real, they will go back in time and be caught on camera. (Pics. 4, 5, 6, and 7.)
However, Kusakabe cuts off Tsukumo's hand and breaks free. Tsukumo then says, "You know the plan now, and it will never work again. But if my hand stays with you, my hand will disappear, and everyone will know that a time loop exists." He stands up and tries to throw his hand toward Kusakabe so it will travel through time, but he is too late, and the time loop closes. (Pics. 8, 9, 10, and 11.)
After the time loop closes, we see Kusakabe return home to remove the handcuff. While he is there, he confronts his father and then kills him.
After that, we see the beginning of Tsukumo 2's timeline and how he comes up with the idea.But this time, instead of spending hours planning on it, he notices that one of his handcuffs is missing. He guesses that his other self (Tsukumo 1) already carried out the plan and failed. He also assumes the handcuffs are probably still on Kusakabe, so he rushes to the most logical place he can think of: Kusakabe's house.
When he gets there, he finds a corpse and a broken handcuff. (Pics. 12, 1, 2, 13, 14, and 15.)
Fast forward to Chapter 26. It is now July 24, and Tsukumo is under house arrest.
We see Yae finding Kusakabe in Tayomi. She says she wanted to see him, but Kusakabe tells her to leave him alone. Yae insists on staying, so Kusakabe reveals the truth about himself by killing someone using the "organ method." (Pics. 16 and 17.)
After some events that aren't important to this discussion, we reach the final pages of Chapter 27. We see Kusakabe and his original sister walking when a car suddenly crashes into them and kidnaps them both. They wake up inside a shipping container, where it is revealed that Tsukumo is the one who kidnapped them.
By this point, Tsukumo 2 already knows about the time loop because of the broken handcuff. (Pic. 20.)
He then explains how he found Kusakabe. After Kusakabe escaped, Tsukumo assigned someone to keep an eye on Yae. When he learned that she had gone to Tayomi, where the mysterious deaths were occurring, he realized that Kusakabe was probably there as well. Of course, he didn't know Kusakabe's exact location, so he had to search for him by car.
Tsukumo then says that he also figured out the existence of the time loop.
When Kusakabe asks what he is planning, Tsukumo says, "As far as I understand, you are connected to the mysterious deaths that happened yesterday."
He then asks, "Kusakabe Makoto, is this your doing? How did you kill them? Did you use the time loop?" (Pics. 18, 19, and 21.)
