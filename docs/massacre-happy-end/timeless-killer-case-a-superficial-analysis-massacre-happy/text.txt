Timeless Killer Case | Superficial Analysis

The main reason for this is the inherent logic of the "Time Loop" Curse.

According to this rule, if Makoto does not kill at least one person on a given day, time will reset. Fully aware of this mechanism, Makoto always executes his murders between 22:00 and 23:00. This ensures that even if he gets caught, time will rewind, allowing him to return flawlessly without repeating his previous mistakes.

The only way to bypass this stalemate is to either manipulate him into neutralizing the curse altogether, or trick him into killing someone else to trap him in an Infinite Prison Loop governed by the curse's mechanics. However...

1. The Time Limit (29-Day Countdown)
Makoto Kusakabe never wanted to be a killer in the first place; he was fully conscious of the gravity and immorality of his actions. His sole objective was to ensure his sister Shiori received her donor surgery at the end of 29 days.

Once that goal was met, he intended to cease all killings and start a new life with her.
Solving a supernatural phenomenon like the "Time Loop", identifying Makoto as the culprit, apprehending him, and trapping him in an

Infinite Prison Loop—all within a strict 29-day window—is inherently an extreme hurdle. On top of that, Makoto himself poses a massive obstacle.

2. The Katagiri Effect

Makoto possesses an extraordinary aura/presence. Throughout the manga, he attracts three female figures around him. While all of them serve a purpose, Katagiri is unquestionably his most critical asset, providing vital support for tactics like his Tokyo Identity Diversion and Victim Sourcing (WC Farming).

By the end of the manga, Katagiri's raw power is terrifying. Combined with her complete inability to control herself, she places Makoto at the absolute center of her existence. Regardless of any elite protection detail assigned to a detective pursuing Makoto, Katagiri will slaughter the guards and ultimately disembowel the detective.

\[NOTE\]: Despite her frail appearance, Katagiri can exert over 10,000 Newtons of force and remains fully functional even with her face completely shattered.

3. The Fugitive Factor

Makoto always operates on the run while committing his murders. This constantly forces ordinary detectives out of their familiar jurisdictions, dragging them from city to city across the country.

4. Information Pollution & Narrative Deceptions

A detective’s investigation will be severely hindered by widespread misinformation and counter-tactics throughout the narrative:

High Intellectual Baseline: Makoto is far from naive; he actively formulates and executes strategic counter-plans against you.

The Tokyo Alibi: While killing Katagiri’s father, Katagiri drops Makoto's ID in the middle of Tokyo Square, establishing a ironclad alibi for him.

Media Misdirection: When traveling to a small town to kill his mother, Makoto utilizes the "Evisceration/Guts Tactic." The media subsequently misinterprets this supernatural occurrence as the work of an organ-smuggling ring.

Internal Isolation: Your entire investigative team remains convinced of Makoto's innocence; you stand alone in having to prove his guilt.
The Hospital Camera Performance: You must see through his calculated performance in front of the hospital cameras, which he orchestrated to absolve himself of the Daiki murder.

Exponential Growth & CPI Disparity: Neither you nor Makoto fully comprehend the complete limits and alternative applications of the Time Loop curse. However, because Makoto holds direct access to the curse and possesses an immensely high CPI (Cognitive Processing Index) alongside an Exponential Learning Curve, he adapts and masters the system exponentially faster than any investigator ever could.
