I hear that popular characters were banned for a week to encourage discussions on lesser known characters. If so, might I suggest analysing Terminator characters? I think this guy in particular is really good in strategy.

I wouldn't normally make a post about this but even with how relatively small SCD is as a community, I'm somewhat surprised Terminator doesn't get mentioned much because I think its characters are all quite good for it. No super high tiers or anything mind blowing but still quite decent.

I specifically wanted to draw attention though to this guy: Cromartie. A Terminator 888 who serves as one of the main antagonists of the Sarah Connor Chronicles. I think his strategy in S2 to isolate John Connor is actually really damn good.

It goes as follows: his objective is to kill John Connor. John however is protected by his own Terminator and his mom and uncle (which isn't as lame as it sounds lol. Future John is literally humanities top military leader and he stated his mother was the single greatest fighter he ever knew. Sure you could say it might be bias talking there, but I don't think it is, especially given that she taught him everything he knew and his uncle is also one of his top combatants in the future), and Cromartie also doesn't know ow where they are or anything really since they're all living under extensive false aliases.
So, in order to get to them, he follows on his only real lead which is Sarah's ex-fiance Charlie, whom she was engaged to while living under her previous alias. Cromartie previously went undercover as an FBI agent to get Charlie's address and question him, and has kept an eye on him since.
Now, when things start getting more and more heated, Sarah suggested to Charlie to take his wife and leave the area, which is when Cromartie strikes.
Now, his actual strategy here is abducting Charlie's wife, taking her to his hideout where he has a radio scanner allowing him to listen in on all phone calls in the area. This is important for later. While she's there, he builds a fake bomb, giving her the impression that it's real and will detonate should she move as it's strapped to her chair. He also injects her with adrenaline to make this more difficult for her.
While this is happening, he anticipates what Charlie and Sarah are going to do: Charlie is going to plead to Sarah for help, and Sarah will reluctantly accept because she still actually has feelings for him and is just generally a good person. He also anticipates that Sarah is going to leave John out of it as to avoid putting him in danger, and is likely to take Derek (John's Uncle) with her. (I'm assuming he anticipates that last part tbf because I feel like it makes more sense).
He predicts that Sarah is going to call John and either explain to him what's going on,  or lie to keep him away, it doesn't really matter which, what's important is that he hears the conversation on his radio scanner. This is important because he anticipates that they will have some sort of code word. The Connors know that Terminators can look and sound like anyone, even them, so they have a code word to ensure that they are in fact really them. Cromartie anticipates this and uses this trap to initiate a phone call between them and learn the code word.
After this, he allows Charlie's wife to contact her husband and Sarah to help them figure out her location, leading them to believe he had made a mistake when he actually planned this. He then straps actual explosives to the nearby radio tower. He waits for them to arrive, leaving them to assume he left and sabotage their car (I should mention that his hideout is way off in a secluded desert with no civilisation for miles and miles, so sabotaging their car leaves them stranded). He then calls John, mimicking Sarah's voice and now using the code word, and tells him there's been a change of plans and to meet on a particular pier, and then after confirming this, he detonate the explosives on the nearby radio tower, cutting off all phone signals and critically injuring Charlie's wife. (If you're wondering why he doesn't simply walk in and gun them all down, Derek knows the recipe to a futuristic type of ammunition that Terminators are vulnerable to. He certainly must have understood this).

And that's pretty much the bulk of it. I'm certain he also knew the radio tower explosion would have baldy injured at least someone in the hideout, giving them yet another problem in addition to being stranded in the desert with no transportation or phone signal. The strategy doesn't really account for Cameron, the Terminator defending John, but I think he likely just thought he could beat her in a straight fight at the pier given that he was beating her before in their previous fights.
