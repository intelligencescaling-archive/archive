Part 1: After the Tribulation

After Li Qing successfully escaped the three cities, the three Immortals still remained unaware of this fact. Believing he was hiding within, they sealed off the cities, ordering their forces to dig up every inch of land in search of him.

At the same time, the three Rule-tier items extracted from the destroyed Ninth-Tier Beasts were handed over to the Destiny-Bearers of each Immortal's Dao lineage:

Namgong Haoran: Destiny-Bearer of the Confucian School from the Hundred Schools of Thought.

Tang Wanshan: Destiny-Bearer of the Wu Ji Dao Sect.

Huang Wanchao: Destiny-Bearer of the True Treasure Dao Sect.

Through his intricate intelligence network, Li Qing swiftly learned of this event—dubbed the “Immortals’ Bestowal of Treasures”—a clear sign that the battle among Destiny-Bearers was about to begin.

At this moment, Li Qing had already stepped into Level 5—a realm of cultivation marked by two critical characteristics:

The Three Calamities: Wind, Fire, and Thunder—each must be overcome by the cultivator in succession.

Fated Tribulation: Known as Heaven’s Tribulation, but with a deeper twist—this time, the threat lies in the form of a “Heaven-Designated Nemesis”, meaning an individual chosen by fate itself to oppose the cultivator undergoing the trial.

Once the three cities had been thoroughly searched and it was confirmed that Li Qing had transcended the Tribulation and was still cultivating somewhere, the Eight Immortals began to suspect that he had long since fled—and was now peacefully training in some hidden sanctuary.

Thus, a new plan was born—one targeting his Three Calamities.

The stronger a cultivator's potential, the more ferocious the Three Calamities would become. Though the elements of Wind, Fire, and Thunder might appear to occur at random, they could still be monitored.

To advance to Level 6, Li Qing would need something extremely vital: the Primordial Dharma Aspect—a manifestation representing the core cultivation technique of Level 6.

Previously, Li Qing had already obtained three out of the eight Primordial Dharma Aspects. Only those from five major forces remained.

To acquire the remaining five, it was highly likely that Li Qing would attempt to infiltrate the inner circles of each Dao lineage to steal their Dharma Aspects. However, he wouldn’t do so himself—he might instead rely on planted spies.

For example, a Level 5 cultivator loyal to Li Qing, on the cusp of ascending to Level 6, might request access to the Primordial Dharma Aspect as part of their advancement.

Within the Dao lineages of the Destiny-Bearers, there was no shortage of Level 5 cultivators. And due to the concealing power of Destiny, using Heaven’s Secrets to discern who had contact with Li Qing was impossible.

Therefore, the Eight Immortals could only tighten internal security, with special attention paid to this group of Level 5 cultivators.

Moreover, they would increase protection for all Level 6 cultivators, just in case Li Qing, when cornered, resorted to extreme measures—such as kidnapping a Level 6 cultivator from a Dao lineage and extracting their memory of the Primordial Dharma Aspect.

Finally, efforts were made to identify Li Qing’s Heaven-Designated Nemesis. But this task proved nearly impossible, as only the one undergoing the Tribulation would know the identity of their nemesis. Even the nemesis themselves might be unaware of their destined role.

—

Aside from Li Qing, the Eight Immortals also had their own follow-up plans—this time, aimed at the remaining beasts.

Currently, only five Ninth-Tier Beasts remained. Their numbers diminished, their power waning, they were at an absolute disadvantage. Now was the perfect time for the Immortals to begin their hunt.

Five of the Immortals would take on the responsibility of hunting down the beasts, both in the Dark Side and the Bright Realm, while the remaining three would hold their ground at the Four Domains of Jiuzhou, in case the beasts had any other schemes—and to provide support if needed.

Right now, the beasts were in a precarious position. If they wished to overturn the situation, their only path forward was to seek out the Seal of Heaven. To do so, the cultic factions under their command would need to become increasingly active.

The Eight Immortals, seeing this, chose not to stop them. Instead, they would let the beasts do the work—and follow them to the seal.

As for Li Qing—should he wish to ascend to an even higher cultivation realm, he would likely continue to set traps for the beasts. This, too, could be turned to advantage.

After all, if Li Qing can lay traps for the beasts, then so can the Eight Immortals.

The Beasts' Emergency Assembly

On the other side, the surviving beasts convened an emergency assembly.

They did not know the full details of the Six Paths Ascension Plan, only that it would allow the Eight Immortals to rule the world in absolute dominion, forging a new Celestial Court and Netherworld. In this system, even cultivators of Level 8 and 7 would receive Divine Thrones, granting them immortality and eternal youth.

Their top priority now was survival.

The Immortals would be actively hunting them down, and so, for the foreseeable future, they must hide. At the same time, they would mobilize lesser beasts to continually attack the Four Domains of Jiuzhou, forcing the Immortals to hold back a portion of their power for defense—and preventing them from going all-out in the hunt.

Next came the search for the Seal of Heaven.

The downfall of the three beasts before them had come because they were deceived into stepping into traps. All the information they had received had been fed to them by others.

This time, if they were to act, it must be based on information uncovered by themselves, and not from unknown sources—only then could it be trusted.

While the Eight Immortals and the Beasts Continued Their Game of Cat and Mouse...

Li Qing finally discovered the identity of his Destined Calamity Nemesis (Mìngzhōng Dà Dí)—none other than the Nameless One, Xuan Yuan Changlong.

Li Qing pondered the many ways of transcending this tribulation. Killing Xuan Yuan Changlong outright would indeed end the ordeal, but the benefits would be meager. Since this foe was a bearer of Heaven’s Mandate, and even a participant in the Six Paths Ascension, then why not let him and another Mandate-bearer live until the final moment—then defeat him at the height of his power?

The sheer scale of this tribulation tied to the Six Paths Ascension was enough for Li Qing to attain the Supreme Origin Soul—stepping into the Sixth Realm.

This time, however, the Eight Immortals were far more cautious. Their scrutiny and supervision within the sects of Mandate-bearers had become exceedingly strict. Countless cultivators at Levels 6, 7, and 8 had been deployed solely for surveillance.

To overcome this, Li Qing began crafting his grand strategy.

The Six-Step Grand Strategy

Step One:
Quickly unite the three heretical sects—Demonic Beasts (Yao Dao), Ghost Cultivators (Gui Dao), and Buddhist Heretics (Fo Dao). Their unified front would place immediate pressure on the three orthodox Daoist sects, tying down their forces.

Step Two:
Deploy the Six Mandate-bearers of the Hundred Schools of Thought (Chuzǐ Bǎijiā) to invade Longzhou, the central region. Use it as a springboard to launch pincer offensives on the three Daoist sects from both the eastern and western flanks.
However, the goal was not full conquest—only to secure positions, then negotiate ceasefires with the Daoist sects. The advance must not be too aggressive, lest the three sects ally with the heretics and crush the Hundred Schools.

Step Three:
Once peace talks succeeded, shift to collaborating with the three Daoist sects to eradicate the three heretical factions—Yao, Fo, and Gui.

Step Four:
While the three heretical factions were being destroyed, turn on the Daoist sects, launching an all-out war.

Step Five:
After the three Daoist sects were defeated, trigger a civil war within the Hundred Schools of Thought itself.

Step Six:
Ensure that Xuan Yuan Changlong controls half the world, while another Mandate-bearer holds the other half.
At the climax, defeat Xuan Yuan Changlong at the peak of his power—thus concluding the tribulation and bringing about the ultimate breakthrough.

Every contribution made by these Level-5 cultivators would count as merit, granting them access to the Primal Dharma Idols.

Once they achieved enough merit and justification, they would be granted audience with the Original Law Idols of each faction—providing Li Qing the window he needed to seize the remaining five Primal Dharma Idols for himself.

● Part 2: The Six Paths Ascension Begins

Li Qing’s first target was Qingzhou and Dongzhou, the territory of Wuji Dao Sect, where three bearers of Heaven’s Mandate resided: Duong Wanshan, Guo Wushuang, and Chen Shanhe.

He selected three Level-5 cultivators beneath these three to act as undercover agents: Daoist Baiyun, Daoist Yehuo, and Daoist Xuanzhen.

Li Qing knew he could not simply break in and control Daoist Xuanzhen directly—he would have to borrow another's hand.

The Three Borrowed Power Talismans

First, Li Qing used three Borrowed Power Talismans—a type of talisman allowing him to store spells within. He sealed into them three charges of the spell "Human Dao Brainwash".

He then controlled a rat, attaching the three talismans to its body, and sent it stealthily into the military camp.

Under Li Qing’s precise direction, the rat entered the tent of a Level-2 officer, one of the rare middle-ranking officials who was already a covert agent of Li Qing.

The moment the Level-7 cultivator completed his routine scan, the officer took possession of the three talismans. All that remained was to bring them into contact with Daoist Xuanzhen.

To accomplish this, Li Qing manipulated the officer to visit his direct superior. The officer presented a ledger—claiming discrepancies in the military grain supply and reporting that resources had been embezzled.

Recognizing the gravity of the issue, the superior immediately reviewed the ledger. Hidden within its pages, the first talisman activated—brainwashing the superior into Li Qing’s thrall.

This superior, acting perfectly normal, then brought the ledger to his own superior, reporting the grain embezzlement. The second talisman activated—subverting yet another link in the chain of command.

Above that superior was none other than Daoist Xuanzhen himself. But at this point, Li Qing halted the operation.

Three direct meetings—especially involving a closely watched category like Level-5 cultivators—might trigger the suspicions of the Level-7 supervisor. Two encounters could be passed off as routine chain-of-command communication, but a third would risk exposing the entire plan.

---

A Few Days of Patience

Over the next few days, the second superior pretended to investigate the grain embezzlement, following Li Qing’s orders. Once the ruse was complete, he formally submitted the matter to Daoist Xuanzhen.

At that moment, the third talisman activated—Daoist Xuanzhen fell under Li Qing’s control.

Using the same method, Li Qing placed an undercover Level-5 cultivator within each major sect.

At present, the world stood at a critical turning point. Aside from the highest-ranking members—the Eight Immortals and Level-8 cultivators—the Level-7 cultivators stationed across various military camps had already stood on opposing sides of the upcoming war, each one seeking merit and distinction in order to earn a Divine Title once the Six Paths Ascension concluded.

Thus, the likelihood of these cultivators revealing their camp’s secrets to others was extremely low.

Before advancing the unification of the Demonic, Buddhist, and Spirit Sects, Li Qing made contact with Sun Qiankun, assigning him a crucial task: to create disturbances within both the Hundred Schools of Philosophy and the Three Dao Sects, preventing these orthodox forces from interfering with the affairs of the unorthodox paths.

In the armies of Duong Wanshan, Chen Shanhe, and Guo Wushuang, Li Qing’s undercover agents initiated a skirmish along the borders where the three factions converged. After several casualties, both sides retreated.

When news reached the respective headquarters, the tension along the border escalated. Duong Wanshan, receiving the reports, was led to believe the others were plotting against him.

After all, it was Duong Wanshan who had received a Rule Artifact from the Immortals—a symbol that carried heavy implications. Chen Shanhe and Guo Wushuang likely saw him as the greater threat and were conspiring to eliminate him first.

Duong Wanshan immediately summoned a war council.

During the meeting, Daoist Baiyun—Li Qing’s agent—spoke under Li Qing’s subtle manipulation. He suggested that this may have been a probing maneuver, a test before an actual military campaign.

Daoist Baiyun stated that while receiving a reward from the Immortals may not confer real power or authority, it symbolized the Immortals' favor. That in itself was enough to make other Mandated bearers see Duong Wanshan as a thorn in their side.

Thus, this probing action was likely a deliberate provocation—designed to force Duong Wanshan into panic, making poor strategic decisions such as moving his forces to the front lines, leaving the rear vulnerable.

Behind his rear lay the Hundred Schools of Philosophy.

If Chen Shanhe and Guo Wushuang were secretly allied with the Hundred Schools, they could use the tactic of “luring the tiger from the mountain” to draw all of Wanshan’s troops forward, then strike his rear in a surprise attack. Duong Wanshan would be doomed.

This scenario was entirely plausible. The Demonic Sect in the North had already been annihilated, leaving the Hundred Schools free to act, unafraid of being flanked themselves.

To counter this, Daoist Baiyun proposed a strategy of turning the scheme upon its maker—to ally with the Hundred Schools and use their strength against Chen Shanhe and Guo Wushuang.

The Hundred Schools were a chaotic web of interests, with six bearers of Heaven’s Mandate cramped within Beizhou and Yangzhou, densely populated and starved for territory.

Therefore, Duong Wanshan need only offer a promise: once the two rival forces were defeated, he would divide their lands evenly with the Hundred Schools. The reward would far surpass what they could gain from fighting Wanshan alone.

He could even go further—ceding a portion of his own land to ensure that the newly conquered territory would link seamlessly with Beizhou and Yangzhou, forming a contiguous domain.

Such a gesture would greatly increase the likelihood of an alliance.

As for deceiving Chen Shanhe and Guo Wushuang, Duong Wanshan could stage a feint: mobilizing part of his army to the border and deliberately creating the illusion of a large-scale campaign—drums beating, banners flying—enough to mislead both factions into believing a major offensive was imminent.

At the same time, the five remaining Grade Nine Beasts were also plotting a counteroffensive against the Eight Immortals.

They deployed their avatars into the Nine Provinces and Four Territories to personally oversee the scheme, while their true bodies stirred the heavenly mechanisms, disrupting fate itself to prevent the Immortals from foreseeing their plans.

The bait would be none other than the Seal of Heaven. For the trap to function, the beasts considered cooperating with Li Qing, for they were convinced that he, too, desired the fall of the Eight Immortals.

Indeed, from Li Qing’s standpoint, the situation had become too unbalanced—the Immortals held overwhelming dominance. Such imbalance could not be allowed. In his eyes, the Eight Immortals needed an opposing force to keep them in check.

According to the beasts’ plan, their avatars would descend and carry out five seemingly random massacres throughout the mortal realm—each act imbued with encoded clues. They understood that the artifact in Li Qing’s possession would reveal their true intentions to him.

As expected, Li Qing recognized the message hidden within the chaos. He saw this as an opportunity. In return, he simply sent back a message: he would only deliver the “goods” when the time was right.

With Li Qing's tacit support secured, the five Grade Nine Beasts began to lay the trap.

The chosen site: an ancient tomb beneath Bai Meng City, a place that had existed for over one hundred thousand years.

The target: the Ten-Thousand Calamities Demon Lord, the Immortal of the Demonic Path—the most covetous among the Eight Immortals.

The plan was straightforward. They would have a Demonic Path cultivator "accidentally" come into contact with the aura of Heaven and release him back into the northern territories. It would only take a Nascent Soul cultivator or higher to detect the aura—word would then swiftly reach the Demon Lord.

As for the ancient tomb, it had already been reforged into a sealed dimension by the combined efforts of the five beasts. Inside, they carefully constructed a deadly formation.

Li Qing, fully aware of the plan, soon delivered a sealed box to them—inside was a trace of Heavenly Qi. That aura was placed at the center of the tomb.

Because Heavenly influence grew in layers, intensifying over time, the trap needed several months to ferment before it could truly ensnare its prey.

● Part 3: The Unification of the Outer Paths

● Part 3.1: The Demonic Path of Beasts (Yao Dao)

Temporarily setting aside the scheme of the Five Beasts, Li Qing turned his attention to the Southern Region—territory of the Yao Dao (Beast Path)—as the first target in accelerating the pace of unification.

There, three bearers of Heavenly Mandate stood in a tripartite stalemate:
— Great Ape King,
— Qilin Crown Prince,
— True Dragon Young Lord.

At present, none of the three dared to act recklessly, each wary that the other two might join forces should one overstep.

Li Qing’s objective was to shatter this triangular balance, but the only pieces capable of truly influencing the tide of battle were the Grade Five operatives he had buried deep within each faction.

Naturally, had he planted more pieces, it would have made toppling the balance even easier. However, doing so would increase the risk of discovery by the Grade Seven cultivators stationed as battlefield overseers. Once exposed, the entire spy network would be purged, and the intricate web of infiltration he had constructed would collapse entirely.

Hence, Li Qing chose to support the Great Ape King, for among the three, his agents had penetrated deepest into that faction’s ranks.

---

☆ Terrain Layout:

The territories controlled by the Great Ape King, the Qilin Crown Prince, and the True Dragon Young Lord formed a rough triangle.

— The Great Ape King’s domain lay nearest to that of the True Dragon Young Lord.
— The True Dragon Young Lord was closest to the Qilin Crown Prince.
— However, the distance between the Great Ape King and the Qilin Crown Prince was three times greater than the other two.

If Li Qing wanted the Great Ape King to triumph, then the first thorn to pluck was the True Dragon Young Lord.

According to initial intelligence, each faction was limited to a standing army of 30,000. To maintain such a force required immense expenditures. Meanwhile, the larger organizations backing each Heavenly Mandate holder were prohibited from providing aid, lest the unification war of Yao Dao be dragged out unnecessarily. Thus, all costs had to be borne personally by each Heavenly Mandate bearer.

With limited time, funds, and soldiers, victory would require courage, foresight, and cunning.

Currently, the financial lifeblood of the True Dragon Young Lord came from three sources:
— Ore mines,
— Ancient cultivator caves,
— And minor business holdings in Earth Dragon City.

Among these, the ore mines were by far the most lucrative.

Li Qing dug deeper and discovered that the one in charge of the ore mines was Hundred-Eyed Beast King, a Grade Five cultivator. This individual had originally come from a small, weak clan within Earth Dragon City and had clawed his way up to his current post. Yet even after joining the True Dragon Young Lord’s faction, he remained undervalued—cast aside to oversee the mines.

Recognizing this as a lever ripe for pulling, Li Qing swiftly moved his agent embedded in the Great Ape King's court to present a strategic proposal during the king’s war council with his generals.

At this moment, the True Dragon Young Lord and the Qilin Crown Prince were locked in battle at Black Dragon Pass. Meanwhile, the forces of the Great Ape King were being held at bay by a defensive line established by one of the True Dragon Young Lord’s top generals—General Divine Eagle.

This defensive line was stationed in rugged terrain, easy to defend and hard to attack, reinforced with ten thousand troops. To break through, one would need at least triple that number—exactly thirty thousand soldiers. However, if all thirty thousand troops were deployed, it would leave the rear completely undefended, exposing them to ambush and near-certain defeat.

Worse yet, if one waited until the battle between the True Dragon Young Lord and the Qilin Crown Prince reached a conclusion, the victor would absorb the loser’s forces, and the balance of power would tilt irreversibly—leaving the Great Ape King with slim chances of survival.

The Great Ape King had tried dividing his troops before, but every time he did so, General Divine Eagle would launch raids into the Great Ape King’s territory, putting him in a constant bind.

To resolve this deadlock, Centipede Demon King—Li Qing’s spy—offered a plan under his subtle manipulation.

After months of observation, Centipede Demon King had identified a critical weakness in the True Dragon Young Lord’s faction—a vulnerability that all three factions shared, but which was most pronounced in this one: their financial lifeline was a distant ore mine, far removed from the main stronghold.

If that resource stream were cut off, chaos would ensue.

That was precisely why General Divine Eagle was stationed at the outer perimeter, and why the mine itself was heavily fortified. An assault on the mine by force was unfeasible.

A stealth force small enough to slip past General Divine Eagle’s line wouldn’t be enough to conquer the mine. Worse, it might end up caught in a pincer attack between the mine’s defenders and Divine Eagle’s troops. Sending a large force would certainly trigger alarms. The situation was deadlocked.

Thus, Centipede Demon King’s plan was not to attack the mine directly—but to use the mine to set a trap for the True Dragon Young Lord.

His deployments were meticulous, save for one flaw: the man assigned to oversee the mine—Hundred-Eyed Beast King—was a Grade Five cultivator who came from a weak clan in Earth Dragon City. Though he had fought his way up to his current position, he was never truly trusted by the faction, relegated to a rear logistics post. His soldiers were barely more than glorified miners.

Such resentment could be turned into leverage.

By blood, Centipede Demon King was distantly related to Hundred-Eyed Beast King. He would approach him under this pretext and offer him status, reward, and real power—the kind he could never achieve under the True Dragon Young Lord. The chance of swaying him to the side of the Great Ape King was exceedingly high.

Once Hundred-Eyed Beast King had agreed to switch allegiance, the next step was to feed false information to the True Dragon Young Lord—that traces of True Dragon blood had been discovered deep within the mine.

For someone like the True Dragon Young Lord, True Dragon blood was an artifact of immense symbolic and practical value. There was a high chance he would come in person to collect it. If so, then a joint strike by Hundred-Eyed Beast King’s internal troops and a detachment sent by the Great Ape King would be enough to slay him on the spot.

If he didn’t come personally but sent trusted subordinates, fake True Dragon blood would be prepared to deceive them. After all, who could truly recognize what True Dragon blood looked like? As long as greed was stirred in the Young Lord’s heart, the bait had served its purpose.

If the True Dragon Young Lord remained cautious and refused to take the bait, the plan would shift. Ten thousand troops would be left to hold off General Divine Eagle, while the remaining forces would march silently toward the mine.

Even if the Young Lord wasn’t a fool, he would surely realize the Great Ape King intended to seize the blood. He would grow anxious, perhaps believing the blood was genuine. The army led by Divine Eagle would then deliberately slow their pursuit, making it appear as if the Young Lord’s forces would arrive at the mine just in time.

But in reality, ambushers would be lying in wait, and once the Young Lord’s troops took the bait, they would be hit from within by the traitorous soldiers of Hundred-Eyed Beast King, and from without by the Great Ape King’s main force.

At best, it would result in the death of the True Dragon Young Lord. At worst, he would be pinned down long enough for a double-pincer attack to decimate his forces completely.

The Great Ape King accepted the strategy. Immediately, Centipede Demon King set off to persuade Hundred-Eyed Beast King.

Under the conditions of receiving command over his own forces, high military merits, permission for his entire clan to relocate to Divine Ape City, and preferential treatment ten times greater than what he had under the True Dragon Young Lord—with no more oppression or marginalization—Hundred-Eyed Beast King quickly agreed to cooperate.

Soon after, the Great Ape King led five thousand troops on a silent detour around General Divine Eagle’s defensive lines. Despite deploying powerful treasures to conceal their presence, they were nearly discovered thirty times by the aerial surveillance birds under Divine Eagle’s control. Thankfully, Li Qing personally moved ahead, eliminating these scouting threats one by one, ensuring the army passed through without a trace.

Upon arrival at the mine, the Great Ape King was introduced by Hundred-Eyed Beast King to the defensive formation protecting the area. This formation was not only capable of repelling intruders, but could also be reversed into a prison, trapping any enemies within. It was the perfect mechanism to enact the previously designed ambush.

Thus, the Great Ape King formulated a simple plan: hide the troops within the mine, and the moment the True Dragon Young Lord entered, activate the formation to seal him in and unleash a killing strike.

As planned, the True Dragon Young Lord received word from Hundred-Eyed Beast King. The Beast King did not directly mention True Dragon blood, instead describing a strange pool of blood that made his bloodline tremble whenever he approached—as if standing before an ancestor. Though he couldn’t confirm what the blood was, he was certain it wasn’t ordinary.

Though still uncertain about the blood’s origin, the True Dragon Young Lord immediately noticed the inconsistency in the report. He knew the terrain of the mine intimately, and such a substance shouldn’t have existed there.

It was at this moment that Green-Eyed Serpent King—Li Qing’s Grade Five spy—entered the scene.

When the True Dragon Young Lord expressed his doubts, Green-Eyed Serpent King stated that everything described by Hundred-Eyed Beast King closely matched legends of True Dragon blood. He further explained that he had once read a document claiming that a True Dragon had fallen in the Southern Region, though its blood and bones were never found.

Then, Green-Eyed Serpent King hinted that this might be destiny’s gift. After all, the True Dragon Young Lord bore the Mandate of Heaven—it was only natural that such fortune would descend upon him. He warned that since Hundred-Eyed Beast King had only reported it out of ignorance, the moment he realized what it truly was, this opportunity might slip away.

These words gradually eroded the True Dragon Young Lord’s suspicions, replacing them with greed and urgency. However, as one bearing the Heavenly Mandate, he remained cautious—hesitant and conflicted.

Seeing this, Green-Eyed Serpent King volunteered to scout the mine first. The True Dragon Young Lord agreed, bestowing upon him a seal talisman—if anything went wrong, he was to crush it at once and the Young Lord would know.

After receiving his orders, Green-Eyed Serpent King vanished from the main camp, slipping away in secret toward the mine.

Such large-scale observation, however, would require a seventh-rank cultivator to execute. And this played perfectly into Li Qing’s true plan—pulling the seventh-rank cultivator away from the Beast Dao’s ancestral shrine so that he could sneak in and steal their Dharma Statue.

If the observations confirmed a deception, the Beast Dao could seize the border fortress and fortify it. Then, they could wait until the four factions were forced to truly fight. Only once true casualties emerged—weakening all sides—would the Beast Dao intervene.

None of them realized: the “wise plan” they debated over was orchestrated entirely by Li Qing.

He had led them into a trap—manipulating their decision to observe the spiritual battlefield while luring their guardian cultivator away from the ancestral shrine. All so that he could infiltrate it undetected.

Seeing Haoran Saint take the lead, the others nodded, agreeing that it must be a trap.

At the same moment, the four ninth-grade beasts attempted to descend into the Nine Provinces and Four Domains, seemingly in great haste to achieve something. They also commanded all beasts from the Dark Side to launch a simultaneous assault—creating the illusion for the Immortals that this was merely a ploy to stall for time.

They feared the Immortals might think too deeply—so the beasts had to move swiftly.

The beasts’ timing was too coincidental—as if they were headed directly for the very place where the Seal of Heaven had just been exposed. Could it be that the location was genuine?

This was exactly how Li Qing manipulated his target into stepping into the trap.
Through this maneuver, Haoran Saint came to realize just how terrifyingly dangerous Li Qing truly was—and resolved that should the opportunity arise, he must eliminate Li Qing without hesitation.

After the four monstrous beasts had entered the seal, the Demon Emperor, the Buddha, and Zhenbao Dao Lord finally arrived. This forced Li Qing to send an urgent message to Tu Zimou: if the killing formation expended all its power yet still failed to slay Zhenbao Dao Lord, then Tu Zimou must strike personally.

Li Qing knew well that Tu Zimou would never agree to step into a deathtrap entirely controlled by Li Qing. Therefore, it was only by making this request at the very moment the killing formation reached its limit—and the situation demanded immediate action—that Tu Zimou would agree to act.

First, this was a trap orchestrated by the monstrous beasts—Zhenbao Dao Lord had likely been ambushed the moment he stepped through the seal, now besieged by the enemy.

Second, a hidden hand had interfered with the beasts’ plan. Yet even in this case, Zhenbao Dao Lord would still be in extreme danger, caught within the design of an unknown factor. Meanwhile, the true target of the beasts’ plot could very well be the three Immortals still stationed outside, or even the entirety of the Nine Provinces and Four Realms.

As such, they had no choice but to recover what little they could by wreaking as much destruction as possible, attempting to delay the Heavenly Mandate War’s progress and buy time for a new plan.

The beasts, knowing they had no better option, agreed.

With no time to hesitate, the three Immortals summoned forth the origin of their respective worlds, engaging the five Immortal-level entities in an all-out clash.

The goal was to maintain equilibrium between the two opposing forces.

After a brutal, drawn-out confrontation, Zhenbao Dao Lord was at last slain, and the Zhenbao Pagoda, an artifact embodying the laws of reality, fell into Li Qing’s hands.

At this critical juncture, the Netherworld Demon Lord suddenly initiated secret communication with the three Immortals guarding the outside. He proposed an alliance: join forces to slay the four Ninth-Rank Beasts.

The Immortals immediately understood the move—just as one Immortal had perished, the Demon Lord now sought to balance the field by killing a beast. He was no different from Li Qing: a force aligned with neither side, aiming to gradually wear down both camps.

This was an overt, naked stratagem.

If the three Immortals refused, the beasts would continue their onslaught, possibly cutting down another Immortal. If they accepted, the Demon Lord would get exactly what he wanted—one beast dead, one Immortal dead, perfect balance.

Aside from Haoran Saint, the other two Immortals knew little of what was transpiring within the seal. Anxiety grew—they feared the two Immortals inside might fall into peril or emerge too late, in which case defeat would be inevitable. The Demon Lord was continuously feeding in strength from his true body; even with Sun Zimoactively suppressing him, he could not be held back forever.

Faced with this grim reality, the three Immortals agreed to the proposal.
