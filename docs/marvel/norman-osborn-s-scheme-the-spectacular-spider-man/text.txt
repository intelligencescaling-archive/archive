Norman Osborn's Scheme from Spectacular Spiderman

Spectacular Spiderman did so many things well, but my favourite was the reveal of Norman being the Green goblin, which was already an old cliché, but it was done so well and diverted our guess from him so many times that it was actually such a suprise. I'll try to explain his whole plan one by one so you can understand it if you never seen the show:

1. How he got the Goblin's powers

Norman, unlike his son, Harry, got his powers through absorbing the gaseous form of the GREEN in small doses which gives him the powers, doing it slowly so that he doesn't get any black outs or any dissociative disorders and personalities. Doing so made him grow stronger progressively one by one and remaining sane and in control.

2. Plan's start

His plan started first with him turning Dr. Octavious to Doc Ock so he doesn't ruin his plan, making sure he doesn't snitch or tell anyone about the hidden experiments, by trapping him in the electric chamber which caused his mechanical harness to fuse with his spine, creating Doc Ock, and making it so that he's a supervillain that went insane, knowing he won't be able to tell anyone.

In Halloween, he goes and kidnaps Hammerhead to the refinery and tries to make a deal by joining hands and controlling NYC, ofcourse, he refuses, and Goblin leaves him there. He then went to Big Man's (Lincoln) office and tells him that HammerHead has a drive that got enough evidence to get him arrested for life. He knows that Goblin is trying to lure him, but Norman says "That's what makes it fun," and warns him to come somewhere alone before leaving.

He then went to Oscorp to steal the Gopweb Inhibitor which is a really important and experimental asset for Oscorp (that he creates). He made it seem like he was one of the witnesses with his employees and guards so noone would suspect him, not knowing that the Norman with them is actually The Chameleon in disguise (He can disguise as anyone and even copy their personalities and voices), which made him out of view as a suspect for being The Goblin and causing a conflict between the Underworld Gangs like Big man's to become the crime boss of NYC.

Then after, at the same refinery, Hammerhead tells Tombstone and Spider-Man that there is no incriminating evidence of the Big Man and it was all a trap. Then Goblin comes and attacks all three, with Spidey fighting him and the other two escaping. After both of them fight for a while, Spiderman deflects one of Goblin's bombs and causes him to crash, after so limping to his glider and flying to Oscorp with Spidey on his tail.

3. The "main" reveal

When Spidey reached Oscorp, he sees the Goblin waking up and removing his mask showing... Harry? Norman's son?!? Who seemed to have regained consciousness with his leg broken. Osborn asked why would he take in the Green fluid that was really unstable. With Harry saying he wanted to succeed in academics and sports as he was never good in both and wanted to make his father proud by using the Green fluid to enhance his intelligence and strength. But mid-convo, the Goblin Persona takes control again and throws Norman to the he walls of the room and destroying it, but he, for some reason, seemed uninjured?

4. The ACTUAL main reveal

After some cat and mouse stuff, the finally find the goblin and Spiderman, Harry and Norman follow after him with the two latter using a helicopter. After a bit of struggle, Spidey is able to uncover the Goblin's mask to show Norman Osborn, to which a confused Harry looks at his father with Norman saying "he must be impersonating me," which Harry didn't believe and realises that Norman, before going after the Goblin, apologized to Peter, even though as he says "Norman Osborn never apologies" (Which has been the motto for Norman since he first appeared) and removed the realistic face mask, showing the Chameleon who then jumps out of the helicopter after being caught. With this, Norman decided to explain everything. The Green goblin fluid, his plan to control NYC, hiring Chameleon, how Harry was unconscious. Explaining that when he crashed into Oscorp, he saw Harry unconscious on the ground from blackout using the green fluid, and decided to frame him as the Green goblin by dressing him with the time he had from losing Spiderman and even going as far as twisting his ankles to make him limp to frame him. After Peter, enraged after finding out everything, slings a a goblin bomb at Norman's glider which causes him to crash into a containment he prepared to lure Spiderman into and explode it. And that's how the Green Goblin Saga ends.

5. The Truth???

In an airport, the Connor's (Previously the Lizard) were heading to go on a plane to Florida. And incidentally, we see another man named "Mr. Romann" boarding a plane to Cayman islands. The flight attendant apologized to him for the delay and the man says, while lowering his sunglasses, "Do not apologize. I never do." Revealing that Norman Osborn actually survived the explosion and was able to escape Scot-free with a new identity.
