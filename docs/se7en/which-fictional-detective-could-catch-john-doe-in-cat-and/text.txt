Which fictional detective could catch John Doe (Se7en) in Cat & Mouse from scratch?

I’ve been thinking about this for a while now, but John Doe is genuinely so strong in C&M from scratch (without resources). Which has made me curious as to which fictional detective could catch him in such a situation. For brief overview, I’ll supply a summary on why Doe is so elusive here —>

1. **[ TOTAL IDENTITY ERASURE ]**

 - Doe literally has no fingerprints. He has been slicing off the tips of his fingers for upwards of five years, to the point no prints can even be found at his base of operation.

- Doe only purchases items using pure physical cash, not owning a credit card (and thus is transactionally untraceable).

- Not even items such as Doe’s furniture can be traced back to anywhere, showcasing complete concealment from all angles.

- In the comic run, Doe states he had pulled his own teeth out and turned them into a powder to avoid detection via dental records. We can assume this is the same reason he constantly shaved his head, as to avoid any DNA test or match (he also wore a wig whilst out in public, which would contradict him being the killer even if detected).

- In the comic run, we specifically see Doe recalling memories where his own mother refers to him as “John”. Since it’s basically confirmed “John Doe” is only a moniker he’s adopted to remain anonymous (supported by his statement where he displays the fact his identity doesn’t matter and also by Somerset’s statement of “he’s a John Doe by choice”), this means Doe had disciplined himself into replacing memories containing his real name with his falsified alias, where not even he himself would be able to relay information of his true name or identity to any law enforcement entity.

- Throughout his entire life, Doe does not have even a singular employment instance and thus lacks an employment record altogether.

2. **[ PLANNING COVERAGE ]**

- Doe’s main plan had been prepared for upwards of five years and is insanely contingency-dense, where 30+ countable instances of shielding and contingencies are shown in the film. One of the victims was even set up a year in advance, meaning there wouldn’t be any hope to directly halt the plan even towards the start.

- Doe even had a contingency in place in case his base of operations was discovered, overloading law enforcement with information and a lack of dates on said information to buy him time in the event of detection (this type of risk calculation is pretty ludicrous, considering the odds of him being tracked in the first place are basically nonexistent). Doe even wrote a text on one of these notebooks taunting the lead detective for this exact scenario, showcasing just how many variables he had anticipated and accounted for.

- Doe has been shown to be incredibly adaptable, being able to morph his plan around in any form of compromise (such as his literal homebase being uncovered).

- Doe has a vice-grip on law enforcement, commonly using a press disguise to purchase information directly from the precinct. In fact, he has such a tight hold on the entities chasing him he’s capable of forecasting their procedures down to the day they occur.

3. **[ FINAL NOTES ]**

- The ONLY reason Doe was even tracked in the first place during the film’s events was because of an underground illegal FBI-ran system only ONE detective out of the entire precinct knew about and actively broke the law to utilize, having a SPECIFIC connection with a random individual who works in the system. Even then, Doe had a contingency in place for this (despite it being a variable untraceable by normal detection). Doe had STILL outmaneuvered this counteraction, regardless of its impossibility.

- As stated, Doe achieved all of this without any resources. He underwent an autodidact-like process to attain academic knowledge and operated without any immediate influence nor resources.

So, which fictional detective would have the best chance at catching Doe here (C&M from scratch)?
