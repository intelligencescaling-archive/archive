It’s worth noting that there is no universal condition for a microexpression - By definition, a microexpression itself is a variable that changes from subject to subject. Even if it was argued that Doe simply knew the “common” microexpressions one may feel under stress or anger - This sentiment itself isn’t accurate. For example, many people’s breathing may decelerate instead of accelerate whilst under stress. Instead of tightening one’s knuckles they may chew their fingernails instead - The fight or flight response is a perfect example of how multiple individuals have alternating and changing microexpressions as a result of stress or anger. It isn't possible or logical to apply a universal condition to a variable that originates from an individual's unique, internal, and independent emotional state, the physical manifestations (which also alternate, showcasing another layer to the variable itself) of which are known as microexpressions.
