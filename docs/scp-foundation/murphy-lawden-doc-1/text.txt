This part is especially enticing:“Statues that will kill you as fast as you blink.” Humans blink in 1/10th of a second or 100 milliseconds; Murphy here is talking about his own experiences, thus he must experience this on the daily, meaning his reaction time must be faster than 1/10th of a second, much faster in fact. The average reaction time is 250 milliseconds, the average in healthy teens/adults is 180-200 milliseconds, and possibly 160 milliseconds for healthy male teens (light stimuli). Anything below 100 milliseconds is presumably super human reaction time, something not possible by a human, so Murphy has super human reaction time and decision making.
(PSI, Faster thinking, decision making)
