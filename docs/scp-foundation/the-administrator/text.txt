“ Mankind in its present state has been around for a quarter of a million years, yet only the last 4,000 have been of any significance. So, what did we do for nearly 250,000 years? We huddled in caves and around small fires, fearful of the things that we didn't understand. It was more than explaining why the sun came up, it was the mystery of enormous birds with heads of men, and rocks that came to life. So we called them 'gods' and 'demons,' and begged them to spare us and prayed for salvation.

        In time, their numbers dwindled and our numbers rose. The world began to make more sense when there were less things to fear. Yet, the unexplained can never truly go away, as if the universe demands the absurd and impossible.

Mankind must not go back to hiding in fear. No one else will protect us, we must stand up for ourselves.

While the rest of mankind dwells in the light, we must stand in the darkness to combat it, contain it, and shield it from the eyes of the public, so that others may live in a sane, normal world.

We secure.

We contain.

We protect. ”

INTRODUCTION

The Administrator is an enigmatic individual - usually theorized to be the founder, the most powerful man of the SCP Foundation. The Administrator is in charge of governing the distribution of data and Administration of Duties, maybe he also documents SCPs. He is extremely mysterious to the point that most of the anomalous community and staff of SCP Foundation themselves don’t accept his existence although there are few exceptions.

In some canons, The Administrator is a role that others fill in, while there are also canons where The Administrator is a single and specific individual, stated to exist far longer than any member of the Foundation. This doc will scale their intelligence throughout all versions that I have read ( Composite ). However you can use any specific version as you want. The most well-known version is F. William ( Fritz / Frederick / Franz / Francis … ), some other identities could be Aaron Siegel, Kismet, Everett Mann … but this doc will probably not include them. There are several articles which refer to the Administrator by his role only, not using any particular name.

Further Analysis :

Although this detail was just mentioned as an insignificant part of the whole plot-line. A closer look on how SCP Foundation operates makes this feat way more impressive than it seems.

* SCP Foundation consists of the Administrative Department and around 21 departments that manage the research and containment of anomalies in various fields. Besides that, there are site guardians, agents and Mobile Task Forces (MTFs) - elite units that are mobilised to deal with specific threats and situations that exceed the operational capacity or expertise of regular field personnel, they represent “the best of the best” of the Foundation. At the lowest level, we have the D class - personnel. They are death row inmates who become "guinea pigs" for the Foundation.
As the Admin stated, he has replaced all of them and done their job for 3 years. This shows his extraordinary cognitive capability and multitasking skill, since he shared the same hive-mind with his clones or in other words, his mind is processing the workload of the whole Foundation.

   * Next, since the Admin played the role of researchers and scientists of the Foundation, his intelligence must be relative to them. Researchers of the Foundation are selected from the brightest minds globally. Their general ability includes vast knowledge, analytical skills ( analyzing complex entities or situations ), problem-solving skills ( developing effective containment procedures ) and adaptability ( adapting to rapidly-change and unpredictable anomalies ).
Now, we will consider the expertise of some SCP departments which, as I refered, research and contain anomalies in various fields. I’ll take the prime examples of The Department of Miscommunications, specialises in anomalies that manipulate language or communication in ways that make them difficult to accurately describe and contain, and the Department of Mythology and Folkloristics, studying the role of anomalies in human culture, from urban legends to myths. As both’s overall introductions suggest, working in these two requires high linguistic intelligence and deep understanding about human culture and customs.

Finally, this feat also showcases a high level of adversity capacity. Threats that need MTFs to deal with are highly aggressive with the scale of destruction ranging from a city to an entire planet. Although the Admin was technically immortal via cloning, he still had to endure the physical pain and stress from the risk of an apocalyptic scenario that could end humanity. Beside that, this also shows his ability to maintain focus on a variety of work and determination to complete the Foundation’s task for 3 years.

        Description : SCP 5555 is a mass grave, filled with anomalous bodies. When a cataloged SCP willingly enters SCP-5555, it will die. Its skills, personality, and anomalous attributes will be slightly modified and reassigned to a new instance, which will instantaneously manifest near the location of the SCP's initial discovery.

In this game, each of them will be assigned a role - being the leader of a group of interest, and each role will have its own goal. If a leader is the first one to complete their win-con, they will win the game. Every anomaly then will be thrown into SCP 5555 to reset the universe, starting a new round of the game.

Skip through several flashbacks and stuff, Yoshihide was now working on containing SCP 001. In that time, SCP 001 was a different thing from what I stated above.

SCP-001 refers to an anomalous corpse, hanging within a containment chamber at Site-01, entrapped within a cocoon of silk threads. SCP-001's cocoon has thus far proven impenetrable; attempts to damage the exterior have proven futile. Blood continuously flows from between the threads on its body, at a rate of approximately five liters per day.
SCP-001's primary anomalous effect occurs at random. At an average incidence of once per day, a silk thread with identical composition to SCP-001's cocoon will manifest around the middle finger of a member of Foundation personnel. This individual is labelled SCP-001-A.
Over the course of 48–72 hours, the silk will continue to coil around SCP-001-A, extending from the location of manifestation. The speed of the silk's growth varies between subjects, though typically immobilizes them within 24 hours.
Once SCP-001-A has been wholly cocooned within the silk, it will begin to constrict around them. This process lasts approximately three hours, during which blood will begin to seep from between the strands at increasing rates. Once SCP-001-A expires, the cocoon will become inert, and the silk will lose its anomalous properties.
Thus far, over 85 Foundation personnel have been terminated in this manner.

However, the Administrator held him back, he put Yoshi’s morality on the scale. I will put the whole text here so you guys can have a better grasp of this scene.
