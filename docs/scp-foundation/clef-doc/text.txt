Geometric patterns corresponding to thaumaturgical runes have been carved into the structure and filled with a mixture of silver powder, grave dirt, and collagen-based adhesive.4 SCP-8009 instances manifest approximately once per year, on average. Manifestation is accompanied by the aforementioned increase in local ambient Hume levels and Aspect Radiation spike, a side effect of SCP-8009’s physical manifestation in our universe. Over the course of the next 24 hours, the geometric runes carved into the SCP-8009 instance will begin to emit high-energy ionizing radiation, further manifesting as a bright blue glow similar to Cherenkov radiation.5 Upon reaching full charge, SCP-8009 will activate, forming a linkage between our universe and an alternate universe (designated SCP-8009-B). SCP-8009 manifestations are always preceded by a sudden spike in ambient Hume levels in the high 100s to 400s, followed by a burst of Aspect Radiation in the ten-thousand kiloCasper (Blue, Sharp, Tight) range.
