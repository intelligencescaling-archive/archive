Some Nobody Feats

In SCP-5877 Nobody creates a plan to give SCP-5877 instances a proper life. Although not directly stated it might be safe to assume that SCP-5877 instances are potential or past hosts of Nobody or as Nobody stated the nameless. People who have been forgotten by the world. Either way she (in this article Nobody inhabited a female body)(also smash) wishes to grant these nameless proper lives. Keep in mind everything that happens after is entirely anticipated by Nobody. To follow through on her plan she gathers the nameless to a single location. That being the Englewood residence. In "Everyday Is Somebody's Birthday" tale she allows herself to be captured by the SCP Foundation. Why? Well she understands that the Foundation would be able to give the nameless new identities and new lives. When Nobody was interrogated she spoke to Tyler Maliz. Tyler is the son of Hester Maliz, a previous agent who hunted down both Nobody and the Man in the White Suit. Now Nobody was able to lead on Maliz saying that they must stop a “cataclysm” from happening. She says that the way to stop it is to capture the SCP-5877 instances. Now this is a lie to make the situation seem more urgent to the Foundation as it is their goal to protect humanity. Even if they suspected Nobody was lying it would be too much of a risk to ignore it and Nobody knew this. The Foundation sent in MTF Theta-15 to the Englewood residence to capture all of the SCP-5877 instances. It was there that they met the Man in the White Suit. Why was he there? It wasn't directly stated but given that the MITWS goal is to make sure Nobody suffers as much as possible he was there to ruin Nobody's plan and kill all of the nameless. Nobody was able to perfectly anticipate the exact moment that both the MITWS and the MTF arrived so that the MTF could save the nameless all while she was in captivity and having no contact with the outside. After Maliz once again questions Nobody and Nobody says that everything that has happened was a part of her plan. At that moment the MITWS breaks into the Foundation giving Nobody the chance to escape which was also anticipated by her. With a final goodbye Nobody leaves the body they were in and escapes accomplishing their final goal of saving the nameless.

In Gaslight, Gatekeep, Girlboss, Nobody finds himself in the city of Esterburg which is located in a pocket dimension in Poland. Nobody tries to figure out who he is and breaks into a Foundation site and learns that he is Nobody. That's when forms a goal to find other Nobodies and later meets a woman. He reads that this woman knows things unlike the thieves and smugglers that inhabit the room he is in judging from the way she dresses and her body language. He deceives her by saying that he seeks to end the misery of the Nobodies. She believes him and takes him to a place called “the Forest.” There he is met with hundreds to thousands of broken people trapped by their queen in an endless hell. Nobody is able to feel their pain and sorrow as they try to make Nobody one of them. But Nobody is able to resist the thousands of people for days. After he strayed from the path he was on he met a creature that was evil incarnate. The creature tempted Nobody saying that it can tell him who he is in exchange for becoming its host. It told Nobody parts of his real name and the places he's been but Nobody is able to resist it once again showing insane EM and EE as throughout his many iterations one consistant thing about Nobody is that finding who he is has been an important goal for him.

In Nobody's home, Nobody along with some other characters enter an artists gallery . There was an art show and the first people to show off their art were two brothers, Phillip and Lloyd Willis. The art they presented was literally nothing. There was no art piece but Phillip put on an act that shows that he was in the exact place and state he wanted to be in and that tricked the crowd. Nobody was able to instantly see through the act. Nobody was the only person to see that Phillip was trying to come up with anything to save the situation.
