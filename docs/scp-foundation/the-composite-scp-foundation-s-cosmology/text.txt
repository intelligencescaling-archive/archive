The Pataspheres

Also known as a Canon, a Universe Cluster, a Space-Time-Narrative Continuum, a Narrative Dimension (not to be confused with the MetaF stack narrative dimensions, which are a numerical value), the Universal Narrative, the Law of Narrative Causality, and I. H. Pickman’s Proposal.
A Patasphere is the infinitely complicated story that the rest of reality imitates, as well as the set of all possible stories conceivable by humans within a given timeline (may also refer to physical modal realism).

Every canon corresponds to, and if we use Placholder’s model as a whole, then each country branch has its own Universal Narrative, but is not necessarily limited to a it.

A baseline Universal Narrative is stated to encompass the Noosphere and Semiosphere.

The Site-17 Deepwell Patasphere also corresponds to SCP-4755, which exists at Level 5 of the same Conceptual Hierarchy where the Anti-Noosphere is Level 4, adding consistency to the Patasphere being above the Noosphere.

However, some Pataspheres are known to go much beyond this due to having extensive internal cosmologies. It is unknown if all Pataspheres are equal in size and get “filled” to varying extents, or if they start at a baseline and then “expand” to encompass the internal cosmology as needed.
Some canons address the existence of their Patasphere and include extra structures beyond it, however, this is only the case when there’s definitive evidence to suggest this being the case, and if it doesn’t use the MetaF stack in a way that implies a simulated recursion of itself (more on that in later sections).
Contrary to what some believe, this isn’t a case of Placeholder favoritism where any canon not written by him is inherently contained within a Patasphere, since there are several non-Placeholder-affiliated series that do actually acknowledge the existence of and scale to these structures. For example, Yggdrasil’s Surveyor has the Orchard, an infinite multiverse of structures referred to as World Trees, with each one corresponding to its own “fiction” and existing as its own timeline. This Orchard is managed by the Gardeners, a subgroup of Swann Entities whose job it is to prune these World Trees and keep them from becoming entangled, those World Trees should refer to the Universal Narratives.

Additionally, in the Project Palisade series, it’s likely that the "timeline clusters" generated by the Altis Engine from aren’t just regular timelines but entire Pataspheres onto themselves, since some of these clusters are referred to as “canons”, with each cluster also being implied to correspond to its own Wanderer’s Library, a structure consistently depicted as being comparable to or larger than the Noosphere.

The Proxyverse/The Alpha Layer

The Proxyverse is where editors reside.

An interesting property of the Proxyverse is that it has exactly 3 narrative dimensions, whereas the SCP Simulation has 2 narrative dimensions.

The significance of this comes from the fact that everything in the SCP Simulation, from baseline humans to the largest Pataspheres, has a narrative value of exactly 2, implying that the Proxyverse views every part of it as flat, equally fictional, and equally unable to reach it. That is to say, the level of transcendence between a baseline human and the biggest Patasphere is viewed as so insignificant in comparison to the Alpha Layer that it doesn’t even budge the narrative value whatsoever.

The entire SCP Multiverse is stated to have 2 narrative dimensions, being 50% fictional and 50% real, in other words, 50% governed by the will of higher imaginers and 50% governed by its own internal laws of physics.

This would include the Patasphere, which is addressed in the article and treated as being part of the SCP Multiverse, even being referred to as "the local Patasphere".

The Alpha Layer is stated to have 3 or more narrative dimensions, however, there's never any indication it would be anything higher than 3, and that detail doesn't even matter much to the overall scaling, the crucial detail is establishing that the difference between baseline reality and the Patasphere doesn't even register on the scale of narrative dimension value.

The Alpha Layer does in fact refer to the realm of the Proxy Swanns, rather than that of the True Swanns. The place where the term “Alpha Layer” comes from is Operation ÓverMeta, which has this rather confusingly worded paragraph on the subject

On one hand, this kind of implies the Database is among the narrative planes below the Alpha Layer, but on the other hand, it’s unclear what “Alpha Layer” would even be referring to other than the realm of the Proxy Swanns, with Overvoid Lurk even confirming it to be where the readers live, which in context would be referring to the Proxy Swanns since it's known that Editor Swanns and "Reader Swanns" exist on the same plane.

This contradiction can be resolved by interpreting “the Database” here as referring to the phenomenon in which the SCP Simulation is altered by the Swanns, rather than referring to the Swann Entities themselves. This makes sense since the original Database/Swann’s Proposal article mainly focuses on the phenomenon rather than the entities causing it. This is also backed up by the fact that it’s stated in the original quote that the strongest coherent factor between narratives is the presence of Swann instances; obviously these “instances” aren’t referring to the Swanns themselves manifesting in the lower narratives they just created, since that would have to be done through author inserts like Dr. Placeholder McD and SCP-3999, in which case they wouldn’t be Swanns anymore, so it must be instead referring to the imprint left by the Swanns’ influence.

The MetaF Stack

The MetaF stack is a structure introduced in the SCP-6747 article, consisting of an infinite narrative stack where where each higher layer corresponds to an additional “narrative dimension”.

Since we know the tier of transcendence needed to raise the narrative value by any amount is above any repetition of the SCP Simulation’s internal gaps, this means this same tier of transcendence has to be repeated for every higher narrative layer. This makes sense, as it implies that each narrative can hold the same level of internal complexity as the previous, and could potentially contain the previous types of structures extending upward from its own baseline.
It’s worth noting that the higher layers don’t necessarily all have the same internal complexity as the SCP Simulation, as they lack the Noosphere-Semiosphere-Patasphere-Infosphere hierarchy, but rather that they should in theory be able to contain such complex structures.

The bottom of the MetaF stack is a nullspace void with zero narrative potential.

The 1st layer is has 1 narrative dimension and is 100% fictional, being dictated purely by the imagination of higher entities, while the 2nd layer is the SCP Simulation, which is 50% fictional and 50% real due to having 2 narrative dimensions.

The 3rd layer is the Proxyverse, which has 3 narrative dimensions and is 66% real.

The 4th layer is where the True Swanns reside, and is the in-verse representation of where the real people behind the wiki accounts exist. This realm would be 75% real, and is referred to colloquially as the Swann universe of the "real world".
