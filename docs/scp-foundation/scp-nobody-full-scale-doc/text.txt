Who Is Nobody
Nobody is no one.
They aren't simply a person, but a title.
 individuals bestowed with the moniker of “nobody” have shown extreme similarities with each other. Examples being but not limited to;

No more than one nobody being present at the same time (Alreast by the foundation's records)

Anyone can be a Nobody, and can vary in looks.

Anti-Memetic abilities
(Memetics are information that spreads, like an infectious mental illness. Anti-Memetics are the opposite, Anti-memetic information is different as it affects the brain and makes that information extremely hard and near impossible to notice and remember)

Helping the foundation without authorization or request (in other cases, hindering the foundation and lessening its effectiveness)

An Urban Legend To Foundation Rookies

Extremely intelligent and Generally non-hostile towards foundation personal (atleast to their knowledge)

Lack of a conceptual identity (Messiah of non-existence)

Overall, Their goal is similar to the foundations; they want to protect mankind from its dark side and anomalies whilst trying to resist their nature as an anti-memetic force and give their existence meaning

A nobody is impossible to remember for long due to their anti-memetic nature and even down to their clothes are unable to be described.
Almost all nobody's have the same clothes (Black Business Suit/Cloak, Black Fedora, ect), the same face, and the same interests.

By a nobody's known words, “Nobodys can get to any place they want. They're usually very smart and coordinated; they're anti-memetic in nature and can't be known for too long”

Every Nobody has access to “The HUB”, a region of space in an Extradimensional  realm only accessible to Nobody’w across tales and articles.

(Fun info: this takes the form of a long horizontal building. Inside this space is an amalgamation of places. It's an infinite building with wooden walls. There's bar’s, casino's, bowling places, and anything close to these three.
Nobody’s usually uses this space for relaxation, drinking, gambling, ect.)

Every Nobody is the Messiah of non-existence. Reality would collapse without a nobody, a world without a nobody didn't exist.

They're the collective idea of  contradictions and what doesn't exist, the universe punishing them for its own mistakes, leaving a constant nobody.

Nobody made a deal with Marshal, a leading figure of Marshal Carter and Dark, an organization that sells SCPs to the highest bidder. The deal was that if Nobody could procure two artifacts, one from the Chaos Insurgency and one from the Sarkics, Marshal would provide him with coordinates he was seeking. Both items were invaluable to their respective organizations and highly protected. Despite dedicating numerous resources to the task, Marshal failed to obtain either of the items on his own. Nobody agreed to the deal and created a plan spanning over 90 years.

The first stage of the plan involved Nobody gathering information on every operating member of both the Sarkic cults and the Chaos Insurgency. Using his contacts from both the GOC and SCP Foundation, he obtained extensive records of each organization's known members. From these records alone, Nobody identified 1,430 members who would oppose his presence within their respective organisations. He then analysed every past operation from both groups and mapped out every future operation they would perform for the next 20 years. Understanding that each group punished members who repeatedly failed, Nobody ensured that the operations involving the 1,430 operatives in question would result in failure. He did this over 20 years to maintain good diplomatic relations with both groups and to cull the more unwilling members without drawing attention to his involvement.

During these 20 years, Nobody also expanded his information network to encompass multiple informants for multiple groups, spies within organisations, and plants within the Parawatch forums. He specifically expanded into these areas to utilise this network in the future. Additionally, he created multiple false identities with extensive histories and credentials, varying in position and stature, with some even appearing to oppose Nobody himself. These identities were precautions in case an unforeseen event compromised his plans, allowing him to interfere without exposing an ulterior motive by assuming one of these identities and making it appear as if an unaffiliated third party intervened.

After 20 years, having successfully culled the organizations without drawing attention to himself, Nobody began the second phase of the plan: making himself a trusted and reliable asset to both groups. He introduced himself to both groups and provided each with actionable intelligence. He informed the Sarkics of a Chaos Insurgency supply route, which was real. When the Sarkics attacked it, they were easily repelled because Nobody had also informed the Chaos Insurgency of the Sarkics' planned raid. By doing this, Nobody built trust with both organisations. While the Sarkics did not succeed in their mission, they believed they had underestimated the Chaos Insurgency’s forces, and the Chaos Insurgency protected a valuable supply route thanks to Nobody’s information.

Next, Nobody continued to gain favour by freeing captured Chaos Insurgency operatives and retrieving an artefact for the Sarkics. For the prison break, Nobody coordinated with a third-party mercenary group and remotely disabled Foundation security systems. To obtain the artefact, he impersonated a Foundation employee and stole it from a Foundation site.

After all this, Nobody was seen as a valuable asset but needed to further elevate his status to that of a saviour. He spread misinformation to Foundation informants about a possible alliance between the Sarkics and the Chaos Insurgency. Predicting a preemptive Foundation attack, he fortified positions without informing the Sarkics or the Chaos Insurgency. When the Foundation attacked and was repelled with minimal losses, both groups credited Nobody’s intervention, boosting his status. Even if certain individuals within each organization grew suspicious, he had embedded himself in a way that would be detrimental to remove him.

Afterwards, Nobody needed to grow each organization’s power so they would be substantial enough for an alliance. He had planted allies in important positions, such as politicians and CEOs, to subtly manipulate the economy in favor of both groups. He spent the next 50 years destabilizing regions opposing each group and funneling resources into advantageous areas.

Once both organizations reached an acceptable stage, he began diplomatic engagements. He had spent years shifting each organization's philosophy to be more accepting of outside aid. When diplomacy began, he ensured both sides had reasons to cooperate. Starting with weapon and information trades, he staged successful joint operations to build mutual trust.

Finally, Nobody proposed a deal involving both items. Knowing each organisation’s goals, he offered the Sarkic artefact to the Chaos Insurgency to create a rebellion-inducing weapon and the Chaos Insurgency artefact to the Sarkics to create an avatar of their deity. Both groups accepted the deal.

Nobody acted as the middleman, planning the deal and the eventual ambush. He had chosen the island decades prior and built an entrance to a pocket dimension there. Using his information network, he spread word to UIU informants, the Wanderer's Library, and Parawatch forums about the exchange. The Foundation and GOC, believing in an alliance, mobilised their forces. Informants and the Wanderer's Library got the Serpent’s Hand and UIU involved, sowing chaos as he planned for all groups to converge on the location simultaneously.

During the deal, Nobody began the exchange when all groups arrived to intercept. Amid the chaos, he escaped into the pocket dimension. The Chaos Insurgency and Sarkics blamed each other for the raid, resulting in a fallout. The intercepted groups covered up the events, framing them on the Chaos Insurgency and Sarkics. Nobody had anticipated this, reducing attention on him. Both groups believed him dead, further removing suspicion. Nobody successfully acquired both items without major suspicion. It was later stated that Nobody had anticipated and planned every event over the past 90 years.

Simplification of the plan:
1. Initial Information Gathering:
Utilizing contacts in the GOC and SCP Foundation, Nobody obtained records on all known members of the Sarkic cults and the Chaos Insurgency.
Identified 1,430 members from both groups who would likely oppose his infiltration.
Analyzed past operations to predict future missions and ensured these 1,430 members consistently faced failures over 20 years.

2. Expansion of Information Network:
Established a wide-reaching information network with informants, spies, and plants.
Created multiple false identities with detailed backstories to protect his true identity and motives.

3. Building Trust:
Provided each group with actionable intelligence, such as the Chaos Insurgency supply route.
Gained favor by orchestrating a successful prison break for Chaos Insurgency operatives and retrieving a Sarkic artifact.

4. Establishing Himself as a Saviour:
Spread misinformation about an alliance between the Sarkics and Chaos Insurgency.
Predicted and fortified positions for a preemptive Foundation attack, resulting in minimal losses and increased trust in him.

5. Growing Organizational Power:
Planted allies in key positions to subtly manipulate the economy in favor of both groups.
Destabilised opposing regions and funneled resources to strengthen the Sarkics and Chaos Insurgency.

6. Diplomacy:
Shifted organizational philosophies to be more open to outside aid.
Staged successful joint operations to build mutual trust.

7. Deal:
Proposed enticing uses for each artifact to secure the deal.
Acted as a middleman and orchestrated an ambush during the exchange.
Spread information to involve multiple groups, ensuring chaos during the deal.

The narrative jumper is a device that moves an individual up a narrative layer. In simple terms,
Imagine an infinite grilled cheese sandwich. This infinite grilled cheese has infinite stacks of cheese, with each cheese manipulating its lower.
That's a dumbed down version of narrativistics.

Lower narrative beings traveling to higher narrative layers are frequently overwhelmed by what they encounter and may go insane to some extent. The context above is a basic framework that is needed to understand Nobody’s narrative jumper feat.

When Nobody entered the narrative jumper, it is stated that he saw "everything." It is further elaborated that this includes not only all pieces of information, but also platonic concepts such as nature, music, pain, relief, and death. Additionally, Nobody mentions specific events from various articles and tales.
 He witnessed all events that have ever occurred, not only within his own “universe”, but throughout all others.

Not only did he observe all of this, but he also processed and analyzed this vast amount of information. Nobody directly states that he was able to sort through this information and locate Murphy and himself amongst “everything”. It's common knowledge that universes in SCP are infinite in size, holding infinite information. He was able to sort out infinite information upon  infinite information and so on. Infinite sets of infinite knowledge upon knowledge.

this should mean that Nobody was able to process an infinite amount of data in a finite amount of time, efficiently sort through the data, and make use of it. Additionally, ten months later, during his confrontation with Murphy, he was able to retain the information he had processed.

Upon entering the narrative jumper, Nobody briefly experienced a fleeting sense of being "somebody". This momentary feeling of significance served as the catalyst for Nobody's actions outlined in the SCP-7043 article. Given that individuals who take on the identity of Nobody are incapable of creating any concept of identity, this singular instance of feeling like somebody was the equivalent to a blind man to see for only a fraction of a second. Despite its brevity, this sense of joy spurred Nobody to create an elaborate plan involving manipulation of the O5 council and extensive preparation, all in pursuit of replicating that fleeting moment of happiness.

Nobody's aim for this plan was to be caught by Murphy. After entering the Narrative Jumper, he realised that to replicate the sense of identity he felt, he needed to create the most interesting story possible that would attract the most attention from the pataphysical entities on higher narrative layers. He decided a detective story would achieve this. For ten months, Nobody prepared his plan. His aim was to lose to Murphy but maintain complete control over the investigation and Murphy’s actions.

Initially, Nobody presented a proposal to members of the O5 Council, which included the usage of the 110-Montauk procedure as a method in which the council could embezzle money. This initiative was done in order to begin exerting influence over the O5 Council's decisions and ultimately engineering a scenario wherein Murphy would uncover the conspiracy in the future. It was also used to build trust between himself and the specific council members he provided the proposal to.

It was also confirmed by the author and implied in the article, that Nobody had only proposed the embezzling scheme to only around half of the O5 council members, and convinced those who were aware of the embezzlement to keep the information hidden from the council members that were unaware. Nobody purposefully chose to keep O5-13 in the dark as he predicted that O5-13 would be more willing to reach out to Murphy to assist in the investigation. The O5 members selected for the proposal were chosen because they had the highest likelihood of accepting the plan. The O5 Council often used the embezzled funds to finance holidays, and Nobody was able to deceive the unaware members into believing that no malicious activity was involved in obtaining these funds for their vacations.

Subsequently, Nobody killed O5-7, anticipating that O5-13 would seek the aid of Murphy to investigate the incident without the knowledge of the other council members. The O5 council are the leaders of SCP Foundation and possess vast resources and are often composed of the most intelligent individuals on the planet. They have access to vast amounts of anomalies and weaponry of destroying the world and rebuilding it in their own image, and in most canons are protected by the most effective security on the planet. Not only are they well protected, but their locations and identities are often kept hidden through layers of falsified documents and purposeful misinformation campaigns. Nobody being able to successfully contact them is an accomplishment in and of itself, but expertly manipulating them to achieve his own aims and even planning their deaths is an exceedingly impressive act to perform.

Once O5-7 is murdered, Dr. Patra and her team at Site-19 open an investigation into the disappearance of the body that O5-7 stole. Dr. Patra also learns about the recent break-ins that occurred in the pataphysics department.

Following the murder of O5-7, Nobody made sure that the crime scene was devoid of any evidence that could lead to any actual identification, only leaving behind planted pieces of information that would be used to subtly control Murphy's investigation. These clues, including a traceable card connecting to a foundation front company, directed Murphy to Foundation Site-19 and to learn about one of several aliases created by Nobody: Nolan Boddy. Prior to the murder, Nobody retrieved the body from Sasha’s Cleaning Products. A month before this, he ordered various cleaning supplies under the name Nolan Boddy, ensuring his name was on the ledgers. This setup was crucial. When Murphy arrived and learned that O5-7 had checked the ledger, he would request to do the same. Seeing the name Nolan Boddy would later help Murphy deduce that Nobody was behind the events of SCP-7043.

After Murphy departs from Sasha’s Cleaning Products, the now suspicious clerk calls Site-19 to report that an individual with high-level clearance had requested sensitive information. Nobody had predicted this response, intending to draw the attention of O5-5, a member involved in the embezzling of funds and someone Murphy had prior experience with. Nobody ensured this sequence of events, where Murphy meets O5-5, so that Murphy would become suspicious of O5-5 and, by extension, the rest of the O5 Council, which would play into his plan later.

When Murphy arrives at Site-19, he is greeted by O5-5 and immediately becomes suspicious of her, deducing that she is hiding something. Following their encounter, Murphy travels to the investigation division of Site-19 and spots Dr. Patra. He is immediately intrigued by Dr. Patra and is drawn to a photograph of the body bag that O5-7 stole. Through a conversation with her, he learns more information about the case.
By planning these events at the particular time period he did, Nobody ensured that Murphy would encounter Dr. Patra alone and discover the break in of the pataphysics department 10 months prior. Dr. Patra specifically mentions how she was the only member of her team who continued to work despite the holiday, implying that Nobody, in addition to planning the meeting with Dr. Patra, ensured that his plan would take place during a time when only Dr. Patra was present.

It is important to note that there is no indication that Nobody had any influence over O5-7 obtaining the body in question. This means that Nobody predicted O5-7 would eventually take this action and created the entire plan in anticipation of it. He also predicted that O5-7 would steal the body during a Foundation employee holiday and that Dr. Patra would be the only one remaining at the site despite the holiday. Nobody also predicted that Dr. Patra’s team would be assigned the case and that Dr. Patra would have photos of the body bag that O5-7 had stolen. He also anticipated that she would be informed about the break-ins at the pataphysics department and the possible connection to the Children of the Scarlet King. This also remains true for the ledger, where Nobody had predicted that O5-7 would choose to view the ledger and that Murphy would be interested in said ledger.

Through the interaction with Dr. Patra, Nobody deliberately directed Murphy to the pataphysics department, intending for him to discover the existence of the narrative jumper. While there, Nobody also plans for Murphy to uncover another of his aliases when it is mentioned that an individual named "Norridge Bolly" had recently broken into the department. Furthermore, Nobody ensured that Murphy would acquire the files for SCP-271-3 and subsequently travel to Former Site Director August’s mansion.

Once Murphy left the pataphysics department Nobody anticipated that Murphy would travel to Former Site Director August’s Mansion and planted evidence that would lead Murphy to discovering the embezzlement that was occurring through SCP-231-7 and also planting false records of a meeting place between O5-6, O5-7, and O5-8. Nobody had intentionally planted a sheet of expenses and a drawn question note at the bottom of the page that would allow for Murphy to deduce that embezzlement was occurring through SCP-231-7. This was done to manipulate Murphy into confronting the O5s in this fabricated location, who had already been informed by Nobody of Murphy’s actions. Nobody also was the one to have signed off on August's retirement as "Noah Dee," another false name. This means that he had previously infiltrated the foundation and was able to deceive them into believing he was a high ranking member of the foundation.

Nobody engineered the situation so that the O5’s that were present would attempt to use the SRA v7 to contain Murphy. He orchestrated a scenario in which the O5s were led to believe they could effectively contain Murphy, when in reality, their efforts were destined to fail. Nobody did this as he knew that the attempt would fail and would cause the death of said O5 council members while also effectively framing Murphy for all the crimes he committed. The reason behind wanting to frame Murphy for all the crimes was to make the finale more “dramatic.” Nobody successfully predicted the entire outcome of the scenario, including Murphy’s anomalous abilities, and positioned himself strategically to be seen by Murphy after the act, ensuring that Murphy would seek him out.

After Murphy killed O5-6, O5-7, and O5-8, Nobody left behind one of his two gloves in the position from where he was watching the encounter, which possesses the anomalous ability to enhance his luck. He allowed Murphy to acquire one of his gloves as it would cause Murphy to seek assistance from Dr. Patra in tracing its origin. Upon doing so, Murphy discovered that the glove belonged to the organization Marshal, Carter, and Dark, and had been stolen when one of their delivery trucks was overturned. At the location where the truck was found, it is revealed that a notebook belonging to Nobody was at the scene of the crash. After hearing the name, Murphy connected the various aliases Nobody had used and deduced that an individual named Nobody was behind all the events. He then called Sasha’s Cleaning Products and learned from a staff member that Nobody disclosed that he worked at the Aria. Throughout the entire investigation, Nobody had been leaving pseudonyms that sounded like the word “Nobody.” He knew that at this exact moment, when Dr. Patra mentioned his name for the first time in conjunction with the revelation to Murphy that there was an outside force at play, Murphy would realize he was responsible for all the actions. This was the precise outcome Nobody desired.

Nobody had planned for Murphy to uncover his identity at this precise moment, effectively manipulating Murphy throughout the entire investigation. When Murphy first hears the name "Nobody," he calls Sasha’s Cleaning Products and asks to read their ledger. Once he hears Nobody’s alias, he connects all the small hints and clues Nobody deliberately placed throughout the investigation. Nobody planted these clues to ensure Murphy would arrive at that exact outcome and realization at that precise moment, accurately predicting how Murphy would interpret and react to each new clue presented.

When Murphy finally arrived at the hotel where Nobody was staying, Nobody once again anticipated his arrival and had ensured a big win in gambling the previous day before checking in. This was done because Murphy knew that Nobody had a probability-affecting anomaly, and Nobody wanted to lure him to his room in a manner that made it seems as if Murphy was catching Nobody off guard. When Nobody finally meets Murphy, he has successfully manipulated the entirety of Murphy’s investigation and actions to lead to this specific encounter.

A simplified version of Nobody's plan:

 1. Before the events of SCP-7043 took place, O5-7 stole a body linked to SCP-231-7 from Site-19, causing a case to be opened. Dr. Patra and her team are tasked with working on the investigation.

2. Nobody comes into Sasha’s Cleaning Products under the alias “Nolan Boddy” and purchases a few packages of disinfectant and magic eraser sponges. He then discloses to the attendant that he works as a janitor at the Aria.

3. Nobody books a room at the Aria and ensures that he wins big in the lottery beforehand.

4. Nobody breaks into the pataphysics department once again, but this time leaves the pseudonym "Norridge Bolly."

5. O5-7 transported the stolen body to Sasha’s cleaning products and specifically asked to see the order ledger.

6. Sometime between O5-7's death and Murphy's involvement, Nobody deliberately picked the body up from Sasha’s Cleaning Products, causing it to vanish from Foundation records.

7. Nobody leaves a card from Sasha’s Cleaning Products in O5-7’s car, which is later discovered by Murphy.

8. Murphy informs the O5 council members about the embezzlement he is investigating further regarding SCP-231-7 and plants fake evidence within August’s mansion, leading Murphy into a trap. He convinces the O5 council members that they will successfully contain Murphy, all the while knowing they will fail and the present members will die.

9. As Murphy encounters and kills the O5 council members, Nobody watches and ensures he is seen by Murphy. He then leaves one of his two gloves at the position where he had been.

10. Nobody waits for Murphy to arrive within the Aria.

Nobody needed someone to perfectly pretend to be him to fake his death. so he made a “friendship” with a man and He used his knowledge and experience about Emotions to gaslight the man, entertaining and spoiling him to the point he thought they were best friends.
This wasn't the end, intentionally and unintentionally, he wiped memories from the man's mind of his family. It started small with taking pictures, then personal belongings like glasses and phone chargers.
He put amnesiac’s in their food and making them forget each other (after that, he killed them)

After all of this, he gaslight the man into believing his family and everything never existed, that he was becoming a Nobody, ersed his memory of his phone and pictures, swapped his clothes with the classic nobody suits, and drove the man near suicide.

Once all of that was done, he offered the man a new chance. Nobody reconstructed the man's personality, physicality, memories, and made him think he was a nobody.

What's impressive is that not only did this take 3 years (1 for the gaslighting and manipulation, and 2 for turning him into a nobody) but he made the man a perfect replica of himself so nobody didn't have to keep an eye on him.

For a contingency if anything went wrong, He didn't just do this to 1 person, he did this to 5 in that three year period
