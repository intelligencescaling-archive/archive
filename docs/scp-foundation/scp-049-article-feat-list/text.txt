The Original article

SCP 049 is a humanoid with the appearance of a plague doctor who is locked up in the facilities of one of the most powerful fictional organizations called "SCP Foundation", then I will present some of 049's feats recorded in the Foundation's files.

049 displays extensive knowledge of anomalous medicine as he is able to partially "cure" several subjects "affected" by the Plague. Despite being captured by the Foundation, he was able to adapt to the environment and see this as a means to advance the cure of the plague. (Knowledge,EE,Adaptability)

During a conversation he demonstrated that he could speak multiple languages (English, French, a language not yet deciphered, and possibly many more) ​​and maintain a coherent conversation in his secondary language and apparently get along "well" with Dr. Hamm. (VCI, Social skills, Social Awareness,Social Integration)

He was able to perform several experiments on animals with which he had little experience and still manage to apply his medical knowledge and learn. When he was given a bovine, it showed some anger but quickly managed to calm down. (Knowledge,EM, Learning Ability, observation, adaptability)

SCP 049 kills Dr Hamm because he is infected…

When Dr. Sherman corners O49 about Dr. Hamm's death, he diverts attention and tells how he had to endure the death of thousands of living beings at the hands of the plague, and that even so he remains determined to find a cure. (EE, overall AQ)

After several weeks since Dr. Hamm's death, 049 is feeling well emotionally and although he feels sorry for Dr. Hamm, he continues on his normal path. (EM,ER,EA, Empathy)
