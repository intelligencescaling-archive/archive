Extended MITWS Nobody Full Analysis

Description: Nobody is a character in the SCP wiki who is defined by his lack of a real identity. Often, Nobody is not an individual, but rather a role that others fill. Memory and abilities are the same for all Nobodys, although personality traits and behaviors can be influenced by the host. Nobody is incapable of being described, nor can their appearances be remembered. They frequently have no idea who they were before becoming Nobody and are typically dressed in a gray or black suit.

This doc aims to explain the Extended MITWS Nobody canon and explore the feats present within it.

By: XDwasGone

Tales Included: A Place Called Nowhere, Nobody’s That Good at Poker, Nobody Came to Help, Almost a Century in the Making, and A Nice Waffle Dinner.

Note: The non-extended MITWS canon does not have an analysis here, despite being considered one of the cycles within the extended canon. This is because I plan to make a separate analysis of the non-extended MITWS canon on its own eventually.

Context: In the extended MITWS canon (Main MITWS + additional tales), the central focus is on Nobody and his overarching goal to escape the cycle in which he is trapped. To fully understand this, it is important to understand the role of the main antagonist, The Man in the White Suit, who not only plays a central role in the story but also is a key part of the reality collapse plan.

The Man in the White Suit is the manifestation of all the individuals whose identities have been consumed by Nobody over time. In this canon, “Nobody” is not a singular person, but a mantle that passes from one individual to another. Each person who becomes Nobody loses their identity, which fades into nothingness. However, these lost identities don’t vanish entirely—they come together and form the essence of the Man in the White Suit. He is a living byproduct of the fragmented consciousnesses erased by Nobody.

At some point, the Man in the White Suit learns about SCP-2747, an entity referred to as “The Devourer,” which destroys narrative layers that meet certain conditions. Realizing that this force poses an inevitable threat to all realities, the Man in the White Suit makes the decision to abandon the primary branch of reality. He believes that no matter what he does, all universes are doomed to be consumed by SCP-2747. So, in a desperate attempt to survive, he escapes to the void—a realm outside of all known reality and even beyond the reach of the Wanderer’s Library, which itself contains all information that exists, could exist, and will never exist.

Within this void, the Man in the White Suit creates his own separate reality, one he believes can outlive the ultimate collapse of all reality. His intent is to create a world immune to the effects of SCP-2747. However, in the process, he discovers a fundamental rule: every reality he creates must have a Nobody. This is because the role of Nobody is necessary across all realities, functioning as a stabilizer against the inherent contradictions posed by SCP objects. These SCPs defy the natural order of reality, and without a Nobody to counterbalance these contradictions, the reality itself would implode under their weight.

This discovery frustrates the Man in the White Suit. He views Nobody as the source of his own suffering, and now he’s bound to create one in the very reality he intended to control. But instead of merely surrendering himself to this requirement, he turns it into an opportunity for revenge. He decides to spend eternity not just maintaining his reality, but torturing Nobody within it. Although he’s not omnipotent within his self-made world, the Man in the White Suit has significant power, including the ability to manipulate and reshape reality to his will. He doesn’t just want to create a stable world—he wants a world where he can endlessly toy with and punish Nobody for eternity.

In each cycle of this reality, the Man in the White Suit sets a trap for Nobody. He manipulates the environment to plant clues that suggest Nobody can discover his true identity if he finds a particular house. This goal seems just within reach, giving Nobody a sense of purpose. But when Nobody eventually reaches the house, the Man in the White Suit confronts him, briefly revealing the truth of his situation. Before Nobody can fully comprehend or act on this knowledge, the Man in the White Suit erases his memory and resets the cycle in a newly modified world. The process starts all over again, with Nobody unaware of his previous experiences and once again searching for answers to his identity.

To ensure that Nobody can’t ever predict his actions, he also periodically wipes his own memories and implants new, false ones. By altering his own personality and thought process with each cycle, the Man in the White Suit essentially becomes a different person each time, one with new motivations and strategies. This means even if Nobody ever managed to regain his memories, the Man in the White Suit would be unrecognizable in his behavior, making it impossible for Nobody to anticipate his moves.

Explanation: Though not much is covered about this period, the narrative actually begins 5 billion years before the main articles. During one unspecified loop, Nobody entered the Wanderer’s Library in search for information regarding his identity. After making a deal with an unknown entity that granted him the speed required to complete his task, Nobody read every single book in the Wanderer’s Library. The library, as previously stated, contains an infinite number of books, holding every book that has been written, will be written, and will never be written, as well as all information in existence.

It is later referenced that after reading every book, Nobody not only memorized each one but also created a perfect mental recreation of the library’s entire landscape. He could visualize the entire space, down to the individual details of each book. Additionally, Nobody understood every language present in the library, some of which he became the sole entity capable of comprehending.

During his time in the Wanderer’s Library, Nobody encountered an alternate version of himself. Without exchanging a single word, the main Nobody immediately understood something fundamental about his own existence upon seeing this alternate self. Though they never spoke, this encounter caused Nobody to discover a revelation that eventually led him to uncover the truth about his reality.

After finishing all the material in the library, Nobody came to a singular conclusion: he was trapped in a false reality that periodically wiped his memories. From the vast amount of information he had read, combined with his new theory, Nobody deduced that The Man in the White Suit—a being he had read about in the library—was the one responsible for the loop and his torment. He further theorized that The Man in the White Suit had created a separate reality outside of existence to hide from the devourer, SCP-2747.

Nobody then deduced that his goal—to find his identity—had been fabricated by The Man in the White Suit as a way to keep him distracted. The clues that seemed to lead to his goal had been planted by The Man in the White Suit. Understanding this, Nobody decided to tie all his memories to one of the clues that The Man in the White Suit would reuse in each cycle: a small wooden box.

Using his understanding of The Man in the White Suit’s he gained from the books, Nobody believed that the same clues would appear in each cycle, and that eventually, he would reach the wooden box and regain his memories. Nobody was able to make these preparations because the Wanderer’s Library was the one place where The Man in the White Suit had no power. The reason The Man in the White Suit allowed Nobody to enter the library, despite this fact, was because there was no way for Nobody to travel to other realities from within it. This led The Man in the White Suit to believe there was no harm in letting Nobody explore the library.

After completing his preparations, Nobody exits the Wanderer’s Library and continues through the cycle as usual. Nobody manages to keep up the facade that he is unaware of being trapped in the loop, behaving in a way that completely deceives The Man in the White Suit. The Man in the White Suit believes nothing is out of the ordinary, a deception that Nobody maintains constantly from this point onward.

As the cycle progresses, Nobody follows the usual path, eventually entering the house that The Man in the White Suit has set as the endpoint for all loops. However, this time, Nobody has already anticipated everything, using this loop only to confirm his theory. During their conversation, Nobody is able to construct a perfect psychological profile of The Man in the White Suit, gaining a perfect understanding of his thought process and emotional state.

This insight becomes critical to the plan Nobody formulates during the same conversation. Nobody later reveals that his Reality Collapse Plan was devised in that exact moment. Given that the conversation was similar to all their previous encounters, it shows that during this exchange, Nobody both psychologically profiled The Man in the White Suit and created a plan that would span the next several billion years in the span of a few minutes. By the end of this conversation, Nobody had mapped out the next 5 billion years of the cycle, anticipating every single action The Man in the White Suit would take, including how he would alter his mind and the contents of each and every single loop. With this knowledge, Nobody devised a plan that would ultimately lead to the manifestation of SCP-2747 within The Man in the White Suit’s realm, something that would have otherwise been impossible.

Although much of what happened during the period between this moment and the beginning of the main series remains unclear, some information is provided through dialogue between Nobody and The Man in the White Suit. It is revealed that after their initial encounter, Nobody successfully deceived The Man in the White Suit for several billion years, leading him to believe that Nobody remained oblivious to the true nature of the cycle. This feat is impressive, not only because of the immense length of time involved but also because Nobody had to maintain this facade without raising suspicion among the fully sentient beings within each loop. Nobody alludes to the fact that he had put himself into a pseudo-brainwashed state, convincing even himself that everything was normal in order to act as naturally as possible.

Additionally, whenever Nobody and The Man in the White Suit met at the end of each cycle, Nobody used the opportunity to subtly influence him through their dialogue. Because Nobody fully understood The Man in the White Suit’s psyche, he knew exactly how to manipulate him in order to achieve his own goals. Over the course of several billion years, through these brief conversations, Nobody carefully planted seeds in The Man in the White Suit’s mind. These seeds gradually led The Man in the White Suit to believe he was creating the final cycle on his own, when in reality, every aspect of it had been designed by Nobody to execute the final stage of his plan.

Nobody also revealed that he had anticipated every single world created by The Man in the White Suit in each cycle and had formulated a plan for each one. While we do not see every cycle, we know that in one instance, Nobody orchestrated the genocide of an entire planet, and in another, the events of the mainline Man in the White Suit canon occurred.

Following these events, the main sequence of the story takes place, with the final cycle beginning like any other: Nobody's memory is wiped. However, what makes this cycle unique is that every single aspect of the reality has been engineered by Nobody to accomplish his goal—creating the perfect setting for SCP-2747 to manifest. Everything in this reality is carefully planned to be both effective in achieving his objectives and subtle enough that The Man in the White Suit remains oblivious to the true nature of the cycle.

During the early stages of this cycle, while Nobody is still without his memories, he breaks into a Foundation site. This particular site was specifically designed by Nobody with one purpose in mind: to orchestrate an encounter between his mind-wiped self (MW Nobody) and Francis, a Foundation staff member. Francis was psychologically designed by Nobody in such a way that, after this initial encounter, he would become obsessed with finding and capturing MW Nobody.

Nobody knew that his mind-wiped self would indulge in the cat and mouse game with Francis for a time before eventually deciding that Francis needed to be eliminated as a threat. At this point, MW Nobody would look for ways to make Francis useful before killing him, and Nobody, knowing this, had planted that exact value in the situation. The usefulness would come in the form of Francis’ knowledge of Foundation sites. MW Nobody, like his non-mind-wiped self, would realize that he could use Francis to break into a Foundation site and steal some SCP items before ultimately disposing of him.

Anticipating this thought process, Nobody planted information about a small wooden box—one tied to his memories—within the Foundation site when designing the reality. He knew that Francis would eventually download it, and through the sequence of events, it would fall into MW Nobody’s hands, triggering the recovery of his memories.

As predicted, MW Nobody grows bored of the game with Francis and eventually comes to the same conclusion as his non-mind-wiped self: he should use Francis to steal from the Foundation. However, MW Nobody knows that Francis isn’t simply going to comply with his requests, so he formulates a plan to manipulate Francis into carrying out his bidding.

The plan which MW Nobody came up with was to drive Francis insane and make him believe that he was actually going to become the next Nobody and have him try and disappear, stealing foundation artifacts in the process. To do this MW Nobody first finds the location of Francis’s house and upon doing so replaces his flask with one laced with psilocybin, a hallucinogen. After waiting for Francis to take the flask and leave for work, MW Nobody then breaks into Francis’s house once again and plants some anomalous devices and clothing that matched his own.

During this same day, MW Nobody finds the guard to storage locker 17 in the site Francis works at and infects him with something that causes him to call in sick. MW Nobody then hacks into the foundation systems and causes the email to not be received by the site director then logs it as the guard having showed up in the system. Once this is done Nobody begins the main part of his plan.

After midnight, MW Nobody sits calmly in a waffle house, aware that the Foundation will soon locate him and send a team to apprehend him. Given Francis's past and the proximity of the waffle house to the Foundation site, MW Nobody knows that Francis will lead the team. As expected, Francis, along with a sizable group of MTF agents, arrives, surrounding the perimeter of the building. Before any drastic measures are taken, Francis decides to enter the waffle house himself, hoping to persuade MW Nobody to surrender peacefully. But before doing so, Francis takes a swig from his flask—something MW Nobody had anticipated.

When Francis enters, he finds MW Nobody casually eating waffles. He begins by asking MW Nobody to surrender to the Foundation. Instead, MW Nobody convinces Francis to let him finish his meal first, pointing out the obvious: with dozens of agents surrounding the building, escape would be impossible. Reluctantly, Francis agrees, allowing the conversation to occur.

As MW Nobody eats, the conversation remains uneventful until he brings up a proposition—he hints at the idea of making Francis his successor. MW Nobody tries to convince Francis to join him, but as expected, Francis refuses. This refusal is something MW Nobody had already anticipated; in fact, he never truly intended to convince Francis. Instead, he lies, claiming that the process of becoming Nobody is non-consensual. At this precise moment, the psilocybin starts to take effect, distorting Francis's vision and causing him to believe he is physically transforming into Nobody.

MW Nobody had planned this moment perfectly. He knew when Francis would drink from his flask and timed the conversation to pivot toward taking up the Nobody mantle just as the drug’s effects began to kick in. As Francis spirals into hallucinations and confusion, he loses consciousness. Meanwhile, MW Nobody enacts an escape plan he had prearranged, vanishing from his seat just as the Foundation agents begin storming the building.

When Francis regains consciousness, MW Nobody's reason for allowing his encirclement is revealed. Despite the psilocybin’s effects wearing off, Francis continues to hallucinate, convinced that he is still transforming into Nobody. During their brief conversation, MW Nobody had subtly rewired Francis’s brain, causing him to believe that the transformation into Nobody was real. This manipulation causes Francis’s mind to replicate the effects of the psilocybin, further reinforcing the illusion that he is becoming Nobody. This mental alteration drives Francis to take actions MW Nobody had planned all along: he decides to break into the Foundation site where he works and steal as much as he can, all while believing he is fully transitioning into Nobody.

When Francis returns home, the rewiring continues. He discovers clothing identical to Nobody's and several anomalous devices planted there, convincing him even more that he is undergoing a transformation. The hallucinations intensify, and Francis becomes more motivated than ever to fulfill MW Nobody’s plan. Convinced of his new identity, he is now determined to break into the Foundation site and carry out MW Nobody’s orchestrated heist.

The next day, as planned by MW Nobody, Francis heads to the Foundation site where he works. Despite no outward signs of transformation into Nobody, Francis is fully dressed in attire similar to Nobody’s. By this point, MW Nobody has hacked into the Foundation's systems, giving him minor control over the site. When Francis tries to swipe in with his Foundation-issued ID card, he finds that the system doesn’t recognize it. This is actually due to MW Nobody’s interference—he had manipulated the entry system to reject Francis’s card.

Realizing his ID card won't work, Francis pulls out one of the anomalous devices that MW Nobody had left in his home. Using it, he disables the entry booth and cameras for 20 minutes. When the camera systems come back online, they show Francis inside storage locker 17. MW Nobody had already dealt with the guard responsible for that area the day prior, leaving Francis free to take whatever he wanted without interference. Once Francis believed he had gathered a sufficient number of SCP objects—including a Sarkic artifact that would later prove important—he attempted to leave. However, MW Nobody locked the door, trapping him in the storage locker for several minutes.

After that period, MW Nobody unlocked the door, allowing Francis to continue toward the site director’s office. MW Nobody had perfectly predicted the path Francis would take and how it would align with guard rotations. By delaying Francis in the storage locker, MW Nobody ensured that when Francis moved toward the office, he would avoid all guards and arrive just as the site director left to use the bathroom.

Once inside the site director’s office, Francis’s brainwashing is made even more evident; he repeatedly mutters the phrase, "I can’t see my face." Afterward, Francis emerges from the office carrying a tablet. This tablet contains details about the wooden box. At that exact moment, MW Nobody orchestrates a containment breach in the Euclid wing, opening all cells simultaneously and diverting the site's attention toward the chaos, allowing Francis to escape unnoticed.

After Francis leaves the site, MW Nobody tracks him down and kills him, retrieving all of the stolen items in the process. Upon reviewing the tablet’s contents, MW Nobody's interest is piqued by the wooden box.

Following this, the Administrator of the Foundation contacts MW Nobody and extends an invitation to join him and the O5 Council in a poker game. While this event may seem random, it was all part of Nobody's plan. He intended for this encounter to take place after MW Nobody had learned about the wooden box, giving him a unique opportunity to retrieve it afterward.

MW Nobody accepts the invitation and is directed to a secure compound where the game is to be held. Throughout the event, MW Nobody remains hyper-aware of the O5 Council members and the Administrator, carefully observing their behavior. Upon first engaging with the O5s, he quickly deduces from their body language that they doubt his resources and capabilities. To prove them wrong, MW Nobody produces a small vial of SCP-500 from his coat pocket. SCP-500 is an incredibly rare and valuable pill that can cure any disease, including anomalous ones, and the Foundation has only a limited supply. The moment MW Nobody reveals the vial, security immediately raises their firearms in response. Based on their positions and movements, MW Nobody deduces that there are four additional guards just outside his peripheral vision.

However, the Administrator quickly orders the guards to stand down, allowing MW Nobody to showcase the vial as proof of his worth. The fact that he possesses such a valuable object convinces the O5s that he is more than capable of matching the 20 million dollar buy-in required for the poker table.

The game begins, and MW Nobody wastes no time in demonstrating his abilities. In the first round, he successfully reads O5-12’s hand. Having previously studied files on O5-12, MW Nobody combines his knowledge with observations of O5-12’s body language to make accurate deductions. During the dinner prior to the game, MW Nobody had closely analyzed the body language of each of the 13 overseers, identifying patterns in their behavior. O5-12’s posture was typically rigid and tense, likely due to his experiences with SCP-939. However, as the game started, MW Nobody noticed subtle irregularities in O5-12’s body language. His usual stiffness was slightly relaxed, his glare less intense, and there was a small change in his blinking pattern. From this, MW Nobody inferred that O5-12 had a decent hand—one that amused him.

MW Nobody deduced that O5-12’s cards held personal significance, likely relating to his birthday, February 3rd. As someone who frequently worked with numbers, O5-12 would find statistical quirks like a hand reflecting his birthdate and the chronological nature of the cards amusing. From this, MW Nobody guessed that O5-12 was holding a three and a two.

As the game progressed, MW Nobody continued to pick up on O5-12’s behavior. His reactions suggested that his hand was being strengthened by the cards being dealt. After reviewing the cards on the table and studying O5-12’s responses, MW Nobody correctly deduced that O5-12 had a straight, specifically holding a three of hearts, two of spades, five of hearts, king of diamonds, and nine of clubs.

When O5-12 revealed his hand, MW Nobody’s deduction was proven correct. However, MW Nobody had an even stronger hand: a queen of spades, three of hearts, ten of clubs, king of diamonds, and seven of hearts—winning the round. The other overseers had already folded, allowing MW Nobody to take the pot. Although the story focuses on MW Nobody’s analysis of O5-12, it’s implied that he made similar deductions about the other overseers throughout the game, considering O5-12 the only real threat.

In the first round of the poker game, MW Nobody didn't just rely on cold reading the O5 Council members—he went further. He had memorized the tiniest details of each card, down to their molecular differences, allowing him to identify their value just by looking at the backs. This ability extended even to the 23 random cards shuffled into play each round.

MW Nobody had also noticed subtler irregularities in the body language of all the O5 Council members. During dinner, he observed that they were communicating with one another using a coded language based on body movements. At the time, he couldn’t decipher it, as he had only their gestures to work with. However, after seeing the hands of all the overseers in the first round, he managed to crack their code. He realized they were subtly signaling information about their cards to one another. Armed with this knowledge, MW Nobody was always a step ahead, able to outplay the overseers—even though the 23 random cards should have kept the game unpredictable. Their secret communications gave them no advantage over MW Nobody, as he knew exactly what cards they were holding.

After winning the game, MW Nobody requested the wooden box from Foundation custody as his prize. The Administrator, having little interest in the box, agreed to give it to him. This moment was crucial because it was after receiving the box that Nobody began to regain his lost memories.

It’s important to note that all of these events, leading up to his memory recovery, were carefully orchestrated by Nobody. He had not only foreseen every step, but he had also designed every individual within this final cycle to meet his goals. Each person in the loop was created with their own consciousness, thoughts, and ideas. Despite having no memories during most of this cycle, Nobody had created a reality where every character's psychology and behavior would help him toward his ultimate objective. The geography and history of this entire cycle were also carefully shaped to meet his needs, making this plan an extraordinary feat of emotional and psychological manipulation.

After regaining his memories upon receiving the wooden box from the Administrator, Nobody began the final stage of his plan: the cross-organizational alliance. In designing this world, Nobody had deliberately arranged for Marshal, of the group Marshal, Carter, and Dark, to possess the coordinates to The Man in the White Suit’s house. This arrangement would allow Nobody to execute his cross-organizational alliance plan. The plan would not only provide him with the coordinates to the house but would also help create the perfect environment to manifest SCP-2747.

The cross-organizational alliance was a plan spanning over 90 years, during which Nobody orchestrated each action and event. To gain the coordinates, Nobody needed to retrieve artifacts from the Chaos Insurgency and the Sarkic cults for trade with Marshal. First, Nobody tapped into his contacts within the Global Occult Coalition and the SCP Foundation to pull records on every known operative within each of these organizations. Analyzing this information, Nobody profiled each operative, identifying those who would oppose his plan. Over the next 20 years, Nobody orchestrated the deaths of 86,420 of these individuals, avoiding direct involvement to prevent raising suspicion. Instead, he carefully predicted every major operation that each organization would undertake, arranging for those who posed a risk to die either through seemingly natural causes or as part of failed operations.

After the culling, Nobody began approaching each organization as a neutral third party. His first aim was to establish trust. He provided the Sarkics with intelligence on a Chaos Insurgency supply route, knowing they would attempt an ambush. He then covertly informed the Chaos Insurgency of the impending Sarkic attack, enabling them to strengthen their defenses and repel the assault. The defeat did not lead the Sarkics to suspect Nobody of setting them up; instead, they saw it as a lesson on the Chaos Insurgency's strength and appreciated the accuracy of Nobody’s intelligence. This successful operation marked the first step in Nobody’s relationship-building efforts with both organizations.

Nobody then focused on earning the trust of each organization individually. To gain favor with the Sarkics, he returned an artifact that Francis had stolen from an SCP Foundation site years prior. For the Chaos Insurgency, Nobody arranged a prison break to free several high-level operatives from a Foundation site.

Nobody embedded six agents within the Foundation prison ten years ahead of time, having them integrate into the staff and await his orders. When the time came to initiate the prison break, Nobody hacked into the site’s systems, causing the entry booth to malfunction and forcing a staff member named Sam to take over manually. Nobody knew that this would lead to Sam’s colleague, Jeff, stepping in for him; Jeff, however, was one of Nobody’s agents, tasked with taking control of the primary camera systems in the site.

Once Sam arrived at his new post, Nobody approached the site’s entrance. Knowing how frustrated Sam felt about his sudden shift, Nobody played on his emotions. After engaging him in conversation, Nobody appeared sympathetic to Sam’s situation and subtly manipulated him into going directly to the site director’s office to complain. While doing so, Nobody planted a device on Sam, stole his key card, and sidestepped Sam’s request for identification by shifting the conversation to Sam’s annoyance.

The device Nobody planted contained an anomalous virus that disrupted and destroyed nearby electronics, spreading to other systems. En route to the site director’s office, the virus infected multiple devices, causing all site systems to go down simultaneously, sparking panic. At that exact moment, Nobody’s hired paramilitary group began an assault on the site, drawing attention away from his approach to the cells. Before proceeding, Nobody anticipated that, due to the systems’ failure, the site director would instruct Sam to call Jeff in the monitor room to initiate a lockdown. Under Nobody’s orders, however, Sam ignored the call, prompting the director to send Sam to the monitor room himself.

When Sam arrived at the monitor room, Jeff remained hidden. As Sam attempted to lock down the site by entering his credentials, Nobody used Sam’s stolen card to enter and knocked him unconscious. Nobody then freed the prisoners and began framing Sam as a deep-cover spy. First, he instructed Jeff to delete all security footage, then Jeff injured himself to make it appear as if Sam had attacked him in a struggle. Since Sam’s key card was used to release the prisoners and Jeff lay injured on the floor, the setup pointed directly to Sam as the prime suspect.

Nobody proceeded to the site director’s office, perfectly navigating past guards and cameras. Anticipating the exact moment when two guards would round a corner, he preemptively shot them both. Once inside the office, he killed the site director with the same gun and placed it on the director’s desk. With Sam’s fingerprints on the weapon and memories of Sam being the last person seen with the director, suspicions would almost certainly fall on Sam.

Knowing that a guard on his way to the locker room would soon discover the bodies, Nobody made his way to the extraction point. With assistance from his five embedded agents, he exited the site unnoticed.

After establishing goodwill, Nobody solidified his position within each organization. He leaked information suggesting a possible Chaos Insurgency and Sarkic alliance to the SCP Foundation, anticipating they would launch preemptive strikes on known insurgency and Sarkic bases. Nobody had fortified these sites without informing either group. When the Foundation's attacks were repelled with minimal losses, each organization attributed their survival to Nobody, further strengthening his standing with both groups.

With trust established, Nobody needed to position both organizations to seek some form of alliance. To achieve this, he worked to increase their global influence. He planted agents in key roles—CEOs, politicians, and figures within the anomalous community—who would subtly favor each organization’s agenda. Over the next 50 years, Nobody helped bolster both groups while gradually altering their philosophies, making them more open to cooperation. Slowly, he introduced the organizations to each other, leading each to believe he was serving their interests while concealing his true alignment. Eventually, Nobody proposed a device that would help each achieve their ultimate goals. To create this device, however, each group needed the other’s artifact, which Nobody needed to trade with Marshall for the coordinates. Ultimately, both groups agreed to the exchange, which Nobody arranged.

Nobody chose a remote island he had pre-selected, complete with an escape route: a one-way and one-use door to a pocket dimension he had prepared years in advance. Then, using networks of informants and spies he’d built over two decades, Nobody began distributing information throughout the anomalous community. He leaked information on Parawatch, aware that the Foundation and the GOC monitored it, tipped off UIU informants, and spread rumors in the Wanderer's Library. The Foundation was predisposed to believe the leak, having already been convinced of a Chaos Insurgency-Sarkic alliance.

On the day of the exchange, Nobody gathered all necessary artifacts at the designated site. Just as the items were about to be exchanged, as Nobody anticipated, all the organizations arrived, sparking a chaotic firefight. Using the ensuing disorder as cover, Nobody seized both artifacts and escaped, faking his death in the process. He knew the Chaos Insurgency and Sarkics would blame each other for the leak and assume he was dead. Moreover, the ambushing organizations would likely cover up the incident to avoid the embarrassment of an uncoordinated assault resulting in over 120 casualties. Confident he had time to lay low, Nobody traded the artifacts for the coordinates to the house.

Additionally, Nobody created several aliases to step in when needed. If an issue required addressing but Nobody couldn’t be directly involved, he would use one of his many aliases, each with its own history and position across both the anomalous and non-anomalous worlds, to get the task done. Some of these aliases were even framed as working against Nobody, allowing him to control the flow of information surrounding his operations.

Once Nobody obtained the coordinates to the house, he initiated the final step of the final cycle and made his way there. Arriving in a clearing, he saw the house perched atop a hill. As Nobody climbed, space itself seemed to distort; he crossed what felt like a hundred hills and spent what seemed an eternity just traveling to reach the house. Finally, after crossing the hundredth hill, Nobody entered and met the MITWS as usual. Feigning ignorance as always, Nobody remained undetected until the MITWS went to open the door to create a new cycle.

At that moment, instead of finding the expected space beyond, the MITWS found only an empty void. This conversation was the final step needed to manifest SCP-2747, which consumed the reality of the MITWS, leaving only an empty void and fulfilling Nobody's revenge.

After a brief confrontation with the MITWS, both Nobody and the MITWS ultimately accept their fate to live for eternity in the void. In this void, Nobody manages to manifest an item—something that should be impossible in such a place—and attributes this ability to the knowledge he gained from reading every book in the Wanderer’s Library.

Analysis: The Extended MITWS canon presents a series of impressive feats for Nobody, showcasing his incredible intelligence and mastery of manipulation. The following section will examine these feats in detail.

Library Visualization: The Wanderer’s Library, within the specific canon of Extended MITWS, is described as possessing all possible information within reality, containing all books that have been, will be, and will not be written. The singular exception is any event that occurs within the Void, a realm outside of all existence. The only known occurrences in the Void are the creation of The Man in the White Suit’s reality and the main story events within the canon.

It is repeatedly stated that the Library contains all information in existence and thus also contains every language and vocabulary word within its shelves. The size of the Library is infinite, directly proportional to the number of books present within.

The above is essential to understanding Nobody’s abilities, as within the canon, Nobody creates a perfect mental copy of the Library, including every single book within it. His mental visualization is so precise that he can recognize and replicate each book’s individual details and textures. This provides Nobody with an unquantifiable level of VSI and means that he has memorized every vocabulary word and language in existence. This ability is consistent with what the canon portrays, as Nobody demonstrates a deep understanding of complex vocabulary and nuance, as well as knowledge of ancient and forbidden languages known only to him.

False Reality Discovery: Nobody’s discovery that he was within a false reality is a extremely impressive feat given the circumstances he was presented with. Initially, during his time within the Library, Nobody encounters an alternate version of himself. From this singular encounter—despite the absence of any verbal communication—Nobody abducts that something about his reality feels wrong or contradictory. After reading all the books in the Wanderer’s Library, he realizes that not a single book mentions any events from his reality. From this, Nobody deduces that either his reality is false in some manner or that, for some reason, it exists outside the scope of the Library.

From here, Nobody reasons that if his reality seems unique, it would likely have been created by some entity and, by proxy, observed by that very entity. He abducts that the purpose of this reality must be to torment him in some way and concludes that his drive to discover his identity has been fabricated to manipulate him. He further deduces that the clues he has been uncovering were specifically placed to lead him to a predetermined location.

Drawing from all the books he’s read, Nobody hypothesizes that the mastermind is The Man in the White Suit, believing only he would be capable of orchestrating such a reality. Nobody also concludes that this man must have created a reality within the Void to avoid the effects of SCP-2747. Nobody is then able to deduce that the clues across each cycle must remain consistent in some manner, as it would be the most pragmatic decision to make if his objectives are to remain consistent throughout each cycle.

The Man in the White Suit Profiling: As displayed within the canon and the writeup above, Nobody was able to create an incredibly accurate psychological profile of the Man in the White Suit from their very limited interactions. At the time Nobody discovered the true nature of his reality, he would have known nothing about the Man in the White Suit, due to the fact that, as the Man in the White Suit later revealed, he would periodically manipulate his own memories and essentially reprogram himself to act as an entirely different person.

When Nobody discovered the cyclical nature of his reality, many cycles had already passed, meaning the Man in the White Suit would have had the chance to alter his mind many times over. The reason why Nobody was able to conclude that the Man in the White Suit was behind this reality—using only the information within the books in the Library, which lacked details about the current Man in the White Suit—is that Nobody had profiled his state before the loop’s creation. Nobody deduced that a past version of the Man in the White Suit would have had the willingness and capacity to create the reality in the Void and reached conclusions about his possible motives and methods based on this profile.

Later, during his meeting with the Man in the White Suit, in a span of 5-10 minutes, Nobody was able to perceive his emotions perfectly and use this insight to further develop a highly accurate psychological profile. This profile was so precise that Nobody anticipated every action the Man in the White Suit would take over the course of 5 billion years, including how he would alter his mind and the creation of each cycle.

This profile was dynamic rather than static, as Nobody was able to adapt it to each iteration of the Man in the White Suit. He used this understanding to craft the perfect plan for vengeance, subtly manipulating the Man in the White Suit to achieve his own goals through carefully calculated conversations spanning millions of cycles.

Creation of the Final Cycle: The final cycle is where the main events of the canon take place; however, it was carefully designed and planned by Nobody. The final cycle served as the perfect environment for the manifestation of SCP-2747. Despite the fact that the Man in the White Suit was consistently attempting to create realities and worlds that would prevent any manifestation of SCP-2747, Nobody managed to manipulate him into believing that the creation of the final cycle would also remain unaffected by SCP-2747.

As revealed during the encounter between Nobody and the Man in the White Suit, over the course of the 5 billion years he was trapped within the cycle, Nobody had been subtly implanting the concept of the final cycle into the mind of the Man in the White Suit. While the Man in the White Suit believed the final cycle was just another of his creations, in reality, Nobody had designed every aspect of it to align with his plan.

Every single event and detail within the final cycle was orchestrated by Nobody to facilitate his plan, including every individual, piece of architecture, history, and more. This not only demonstrated an exceptional level of visualization—as Nobody would have needed to visualize the entirety of the final cycle in order to shape it accurately as the perfect grounds for an SCP-2747 manifestation—but also showcased immense emotional understanding. Given that Nobody designed each individual in the cycle to behave in a way that furthered his plan, this meant he had to psychologically engineer billions of unique people and tailor the behaviors of key individuals, such as Francis, the O5 Council, and the Administrator, to conform to specific events within his plan. Achieving this required an extraordinary level of insight into the human mind, which Nobody demonstrated through the meticulous creation of the final cycle.

Memory Wiped Self Understanding: A significant part of Nobody’s plan involved accounting for periods when he lacked his memories. Due to the fact that, after each cycle, Nobody’s memories would be wiped and he would be placed in a new reality, he had to develop a plan that could circumvent this obstacle. This was achieved through his profound understanding of himself. Since Nobody already knew how each cycle would play out due to his detailed profiling of the Man in the White Suit, he could anticipate his own actions even when memory-wiped in each cycle and create a plan that would build upon those actions over the span of 5 billion years.

This demonstrates an extraordinary level of emotional insight, as Nobody was able to perfectly understand his memory-wiped self and, even in the final realization, construct a scenario that allowed him to navigate his memory-wiped state to further the SCP-2747 manifestation more effectively.

Alliance Creation: The creation of the alliance between the Sarkics and the Chaos Insurgency may not appear particularly impressive on the surface; however, in reality, this union demonstrates Nobody’s exceptional social intelligence and his ability to manipulate even large groups of individuals. The Sarkic cults and the Chaos Insurgency are effectively polar opposites in terms of ideology and operations. As their name suggests, the Sarkic cults are typically loose collections of cultists who share a common belief system centered around the worship of either the Grand Karcist or Yaldabaoth. Infighting within the faction is not uncommon, as many of its members are fanatics who do not tolerate even slight deviations from what they perceive as the correct interpretation of their religion.

In contrast, the Chaos Insurgency is a group of idealists driven solely by self-interest, functioning as terrorists within the anomalous world. They hold no religious beliefs or ties to the Sarkic cults and operate with the sole objective of serving their own purposes.

Despite these profound differences, Nobody, over the course of years, successfully integrated both organizations into a cooperative alliance and managed to quell the religious extremism that would have otherwise prevented such an outcome. Nobody displayed his social intelligence by understanding precisely how to approach each organization. Despite having no prior ties or ideological similarities with either group—and, in the case of the Sarkics, even finding them repulsive at times—Nobody was able to position himself as a trusted ally.

Additionally, Nobody engineered a situation in which both organizations looked past their differences and naturally engaged in joint operations and trade. His insight was so refined that he understood how influential individuals within each organization shaped broader beliefs, allowing him to identify and eliminate specific figures whose removal would make both groups more amenable to an alliance and his own integration.

His importance and value in the eyes of each organization were further demonstrated when both organizations fell into complete turmoil after his supposed death, showcasing how he was the pivotal force maintaining the alliance between two groups comprising thousands upon thousands of individuals.

Organization Integration: The strategy that Nobody employed to build trust within each respective organization is a brilliant demonstration of his tactical acumen and ability. This strategy revolved around Nobody deliberately taking specific actions to enhance his image in the eyes of each organization while simultaneously setting up an eventual betrayal to retrieve both artifacts he needed from each side.

Initially, before the major part of his strategy began, Nobody identified certain individuals within each organization who would oppose him upon his introduction. Over the course of the first 20 years, Nobody strategically eliminated these threats while building a strong foundation of influence. Recognizing that he would need vast resources to achieve his goal, Nobody constructed an extensive network of informants, spies, and allies across the globe. Once major obstacles were removed, Nobody approached each organization and provided them with information about the movements of their respective counterparts.

To appreciate the brilliance of revealing the location of a Chaos Insurgency supply route and then warning the Chaos Insurgency of a potential attack on that route, it is essential to understand the specific intent behind Nobody’s actions. Nobody revealed vague information about the supply route to the Sarkic cults, knowing he could use this scenario to appear as if he was also operating with limited information. He never compelled the Sarkics to attack the supply route, nor did he provide any additional commentary beyond its existence. As expected, the Sarkics attempted an assault on the supply route, which led to the Chaos Insurgency only fortifying, rather than preparing a counter offensive, their position due to their still-limited trust in Nobody. The Chaos Insurgency successfully repelled the attack. This outcome was part of Nobody’s plan, as he predicted the Sarkics would act regardless of his input, allowing him to warn the Chaos Insurgency and provide accurate intelligence to both sides. This maneuver built rapport with both organizations without any detriment to himself. From both perspectives, Nobody had offered reliable and actionable intelligence; the Sarkics believed Nobody was also operating with limited intelligence, and their failure to overpower the Chaos Insurgency was simply attributed to underestimation of Chaos Insurgency forces on their part.

Subsequently, Nobody further bolstered his reputation by presenting an artifact he already possessed to the Sarkics and orchestrating the breakout of several high-level operatives from the Chaos Insurgency. These actions demonstrated Nobody’s commitment and reliability to both organizations, making them more inclined to cooperate with him and view him as a trusted ally.

Following this, Nobody secretly reinforced specific bases of both organizations and used his informant network to tip off the SCP Foundation about a fabricated alliance between the two groups. This move served two purposes: it enhanced Nobody’s reputation within both organizations when Foundation attacks were repelled due to his supposed goodwill in fortifying key sites, and it misled the Foundation into believing in a potential alliance between the groups. This belief would make it easier for Nobody to manipulate the Foundation during the betrayal that he planned for years into the future.

Afterwards, Nobody began to strengthen the capabilities of both groups. Due to the inherently extremist and contrasting ideologies of each group, Nobody understood that unless both were perceived as valuable to one another, neither would be willing to engage in any form of cooperation or joint action. To address this, Nobody covertly utilized his extensive network of influence—which by this point included CEOs and politicians—to funnel resources into projects that would directly benefit both groups, thereby enhancing their strengths.

During this time, Nobody subtly shifted the core ideologies of each group to be more open to change and cooperation. Both organizations valued strength, and by bolstering the power of each side, Nobody made them more willing to consider the possibility of a truce. The prior encounter where the Sarkics were repelled by the Chaos Insurgency also created an illusion of strength on the Insurgency’s part, reinforcing their image as formidable and making them a more attractive ally to the Sarkics.

Once these conditions were met, Nobody began introducing the groups to each other. Understanding that he needed to start small, he facilitated initial exchanges of information, knowing this would serve as a good foundation for building trust. Over time, the relationship grew to the point where a joint operation was proposed. Nobody specifically orchestrated this operation to ensure its success, as he knew that a successful joint venture would encourage both organizations to consider a more permanent alliance. This strategy highlights Nobody’s understanding of both groups on a micro and macro level and his ability to engineer situations that would make them more willing to cooperate. It also demonstrates Nobody’s skill in influencing large groups and even subtly altering their fundamental beliefs and ideologies to align with his objectives.

This growing cooperation between the two groups was also a key part of Nobody’s strategy to acquire the artifacts. He knew that as their bond strengthened, they would be more willing to trade sensitive items, such as the artifacts, with one another. Nobody presented ideas to both groups, suggesting that each could gain a significant advantage if they possessed the other’s artifact. Once both groups agreed, Nobody facilitated the exchange, further bolstering his image within each group as someone who worked tirelessly to secure advantageous deals.

The exchange was arranged on a remote island, chosen for strategic reasons that aligned with Nobody’s eventual betrayal. The island’s isolation would create uncertainty about what had truly occurred once the betrayal unfolded. Additionally, it provided an ideal escape route due to a pre-placed gateway that Nobody had set up, allowing him to leave during the ensuing chaos. The island’s remoteness also contributed to the confusion when the inevitable firefight broke out.

Once the date and location of the exchange were finalized, Nobody set his information network into action for the purpose he had always envisioned. He started leaking small bits of information about the upcoming exchange throughout the anomalous community, targeting specific groups that opposed either the Chaos Insurgency or the Sarkics in some capacity. Nobody began with Parawatch forums, fully aware that both the SCP Foundation and the GOC would be monitoring them. His choice of these two groups was strategic: the Foundation already suspected collaboration between the Insurgency and the Sarkics, while the GOC, known for its aggressive stance, would likely respond by deploying significant forces to intercept the exchange.

Additionally, Nobody tipped off informants within the UIU and the Serpent’s Hand, hinting that important artifacts were involved. This move was calculated to attract the attention of these groups while exploiting their lack of communication and the secrecy surrounding their motives.

On the day of the meeting, Nobody had carefully orchestrated the timing. He knew exactly when each group would arrive, timing the event so that just as he secured both artifacts, the other factions would converge. This would trigger chaos, giving Nobody the distraction he needed to escape and reach the bunker he had prepared.

This strategy served multiple purposes. Firstly, by leaking information selectively, Nobody guaranteed a large-scale confrontation between the Insurgency and the Sarkics. Their anger at the apparent betrayal and Nobody's supposed death would destroy the alliances they held, causing a major conflict. This conflict would divert attention away from what truly happened on the island, leaving fewer resources dedicated to investigating Nobody himself.

Furthermore, Nobody anticipated that the UIU, GOC, SCP Foundation, and Serpent’s Hand would immediately move to cover up the incident. An attempted raid on anomalous terrorists resulting in a deadly crossfire between major players, with over 120 casualties, would be a bad image for each organization. The subsequent misinformation campaigns and mutual blame would bury the truth even deeper, effectively obscuring Nobody’s role. This strategy was masterful in its design, turning mistrust and secrecy among groups into tools for his own escape, ensuring that Nobody remained beyond suspicion amidst the chaos.

Francis Manipulation: The manipulation performed on Francis demonstrates Nobody’s incredible aptitude within the field of manipulation. Firstly, it is important to establish that during the manipulation, Nobody had his memory wiped, meaning that the profiling and methods he used on Francis were vastly inferior to his true abilities when he possessed all of his memories.

Within the canon, we see Nobody drive Francis to insanity in what appears to be a single conversation between the two characters. In actuality, the manipulation by Nobody is a complex process in which he applied specific choices tailored for Francis.

The first step of the manipulation was to spike Francis’s drink with psilocybin as a baseline for the hallucinations Nobody would induce later. Nobody knew that Francis would take a drink from his flask and, by proxy, ingest psilocybin prior to entering the waffle house. He used this to his advantage to begin manipulating Francis into believing he was becoming the new Nobody.

Initially, Nobody broached the subject of becoming the new Nobody and made it appear as if the process was involuntary. He took advantage of both Francis’s predisposition to believe him—since Francis already knew that Nobody possessed anomalous abilities—and Francis's fatigue by setting the confrontation early in the morning. Since Nobody knew the exact moment the psilocybin would begin to take effect, he used that knowledge in tandem with the conversation to reprogram Francis’s mind into believing he was already becoming Nobody, with the psilocybin reinforcing this belief.

The effectiveness of this is evident, as Francis’s mind, even after the effects of the psilocybin wore off, continued to experience hallucinations. Moments after he regained consciousness, he began to see his fingers flickering in and out of his vision. Following this, when Francis came home, Nobody initiated the next stage of his manipulation.

Nobody had broken into Francis’s home after he had left and replaced his clothes with his own. Known for wearing the same attire consistently, Nobody arranged it so that upon arriving home and seeing his clothes mysteriously replaced with Nobody’s attire, Francis’s belief that he was becoming Nobody would be reinforced, intensifying his paranoia.

This manipulation proves effective, as the next day, Francis has embraced the identity of Nobody. Dressed in Nobody's attire, he decides to break into the foundation site. Nobody was able to engineer a situation in which Francis believed his only choice was to break into the foundation site. Nobody facilitated the following thought process in Francis’s mind: I am becoming Nobody. Since I am becoming Nobody, people will forget who I am. I will forget who I am. I will lose access to my foundation credentials. The foundation and other organizations will begin to hunt me because they believe I am Nobody, which I am. Therefore, before that happens, I will take advantage of the time I have left to steal from the foundation while they are not hunting me and disappear. Nobody specifically wanted to foster this thought process in Francis’s mind, so Francis would believe it was his own decision to break into the foundation site and steal from the foundation.

While in the foundation site, Nobody continues reinforcing the idea in Francis’s mind that he has become Nobody by making it appear that Francis cannot use his foundation credentials, leading him to believe the transformation is already underway. Later, in audio logs, we hear how much Francis has deteriorated; even without the influence of psilocybin, he repeatedly states that he cannot see his own face.

Nobody’s Emotional Engagement: Within the cycles, Nobody displays incredible fortitude, stemming from his emotional engagement. Despite the adversity imposed by the Man in the White Suit, the constant memory wipes, and the immense span of 5 billion years, Nobody consistently remained dedicated and focused on his singular goal. Not once did he show a lapse in his fortitude or composure during this entire period, as he remained solely focused on achieving his one objective: manifesting SCP-2747 to destroy everything the Man in the White Suit had worked for.

Nobody's commitment was so strong that he was willing to alter his own mind, placing himself in hypnotic states for extended periods to accomplish his goal. It's important to note that Nobody could have easily given up upon initially realizing his circumstances. He was trapped in an endless loop controlled by someone who despised him more than anyone else. However, instead of succumbing to despair, he immediately began devising a plan to put an end to it all and exact revenge on the Man in the White Suit. He never wavered at the prospect of spending the next 5 billion years acting on and executing his plan, despite the fact that this essentially meant voluntarily giving up his freedom during this period.

Nobody’s Acting Ability: While not heavily focused on throughout the canon, it is a fact that Nobody deceived the Man in the White Suit for 5 billion years while steadily progressing toward his goal. To put this in perspective, over the span of 5 billion years, every action taken by Nobody was carefully planned to further his goal of manifesting SCP-2747. Every conversation he had with the Man in the White Suit was an opportunity to plant ideas that would ultimately lead to the final cycle. Despite this, the Man in the White Suit never suspected that Nobody was planning anything and genuinely believed that Nobody remained enthralled within the cycle.

During each conversation, Nobody had to feign surprise, carefully crafting all his reactions to each event in the cycle. Already knowing the outcome of every action, he had to convincingly fake his responses to avoid arousing suspicion. Not only was he deceiving the Man in the White Suit, but also the countless individuals within the cycles, each of whom was fully sentient and acting of their own will. Over those 5 billion years, likely involving millions of people, not a single individual noticed anything unusual about Nobody’s actions.

Nobody’s Emotional Perception: While not directly shown in the canon, further analysis reveals that Nobody possesses an extraordinary aptitude for perceiving others' emotions through body language. The first example of this skill can be seen during the poker match between Nobody and the O5 Council. Using a preliminary profile based on O5-12’s service history with the Foundation, Nobody was able to combine this profile with observations of O5-12’s facial expressions and body language to perfectly deduce the cards he held.

Another example is Nobody’s first conversation with the Man in the White Suit. To create the necessary profile, Nobody would have had to reference the Man in the White Suit’s speech patterns alongside his body language. This approach aligns closely with what Nobody is shown to do when facing a new adversary: he observes their facial expressions and body language to draw conclusions about them. The extent of this emotional perception is evident in that the information gathered to create this profile was so accurate that it enabled Nobody to predict the actions of the Man in the White Suit over the next 5 billion years.

The Exchange: The final exchange between the Sarkics and the Chaos Insurgency was an event that Nobody had been building up to throughout the final cycle. The goal of the final cycle was for Nobody to obtain the two necessary artifacts from both the Sarkics and the Chaos Insurgency in a manner that would allow him to avoid heavy scrutiny.

The location of the final exchange had been chosen years before the idea was even broached between the two groups, as Nobody had preemptively constructed a one-use, one-way entrance to a pocket dimension that he could escape to.

The attack that occurred during the exchange was specifically tailored to facilitate Nobody’s escape. Nobody had spread misinformation throughout the anomalous community in a way that ensured key figures would intervene during the exchange, causing enough chaos to allow him to slip away unnoticed.

The specific organizations were chosen carefully because Nobody knew that, following the event, they would attempt to obscure information about the attack to save face. Moreover, with the Chaos Insurgency and Sarkics dissolving their alliance and falling into conflict—each blaming the other for the attack and the presumed death of Nobody—Nobody would be able to operate relatively freely afterward.

The attack itself had been set up by Nobody years in advance, as he had planted the idea of a possible alliance between the Chaos Insurgency and Sarkics within the Foundation years earlier while gaining the trust of both organizations.

Final Cycle: The final cycle within the canon is where most of the content is centered. While not overtly stated, the final cycle was specifically created by Nobody to manifest SCP-2747. Manifesting SCP-2747 requires a very specific set of circumstances, under which SCP-2747 will emerge and destroy the layer. It’s important to note that the Man in the White Suit specifically tailored each reality to make an SCP-2747 manifestation impossible. Nobody was able to bypass this through the subtle brainwashing of the Man in the White Suit over the course of 5 billion years.

The fact that Nobody managed to manipulate the Man in the White Suit into creating the exact reality he needed—with every detail Nobody had planned—while still making him believe it was his own idea, demonstrates Nobody’s incredible ability to logically and directly manipulate others. It also showcases his verbal comprehension, as he navigated conversations in a way that appeared natural, expressing shock and outrage while subliminally planting ideas into the Man in the White Suit’s mind.

Additionally, it’s revealed that every single detail of this cycle—including geography, history, and even the individuals within it—was designed by Nobody to facilitate SCP-2747’s manifestation. This means that Nobody had to create billions of people, specifically engineering their psyches to fit his needs, map out the entire history of the cycle and how it evolved over thousands of years, and visualize each detail of the cycle, including its terrain. Once again, the fact that Nobody could manipulate the Man in the White Suit to create this exact cycle without him ever realizing what was truly happening is a testament to Nobody’s abilities.
