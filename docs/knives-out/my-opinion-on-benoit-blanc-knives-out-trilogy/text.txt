My Opinion On Benoit Blanc From The Knives Out Trilogy

i just finished the 3 movies and they were rlly good. benoit blanc, the protagonist is a detective who solves the 3 movies main mysteries. he’s been mentioned occasionally but pretty rarely.

As of now I believe he is a high-end low tier and i want to discuss is best and worst cats. his best performances feats wise would probably be 1. Wake Up Dead Man, 2. Glass Onion, 3. Knives Out

# Reasoning, Thinking, And Sensory

this, a surprise to no one is his best and he is pretty good. Wake Up Dead Man would probably be his best in thinking and reasoning whereas Knives Out would be his best in sensory.

His best feat in reasoning and thinking would obv be >!deducing the priest had fabricated his death with the assistance of the culprit and another accomplice, then the culprit and accomplice worked together in the murder, then the culprit killed the accomplice!< you could use his scheming in the Glass Onion to buff his thinking as well.

His best sensory feat would be seeing the blood on protagonist’s shoes which seemed to he a pretty small drop, then was able to see 2 ppl sitting in a car from what appears to be at least a mile away in broad daylight. you could also use his visualization of the fake murder room in Glass Onion

# Scheming, Deception, Manipulation And Foresight

I believe this is where he begins to lack feats enough to make it to mid tier. His foresight is good in the Glass Onion, being able to predict a lot of the other peoples movement after only hearing their personalities from someone who i believe didnt even meet them and heard from her sister.

In scheming he is just fine ig, the Glass Onion scheme isnt anything special and has points which can be countered pretty easily. In Deception he kinda sucks, his concealment in that movie wouldn’t have worked if the culprit was smarter, he’s also just okay at acting. his misdirection is fine and fabrication has the same issue as concealment. In manipulation the mf was featless.

# EQ And SQ

His EQ and SQ are pretty good. His best EQ feats is obv understanding the psychology of everyone somewhat involved in a crime. Im not joking when i say he understands these ppl through and through and his EP is good as most of the time he understands these ppl at first encounter. His EE is good since he is willing to sacrifice his life to solve a murder, only issue is he is fairly featless in EM. Just like EQ tho, his SQ is good for the same reason. He understands the motives of everyone involved, their desires, and is shown to be convincing to ppl.

**The Culprit****s**

The culprits of Glass Onion and Knives Out are low tiers, Glass Onion culprit is an idiot even stated as such several times in the movie.

Knives Out culprit is pretty smart and good at scheming, deception, and manipulation. he also had to improvise a lot, but in the same time he got lucky a LOT.

However, a contest in no way shape or form, the smartest Culprit is the Wake Up Dead Man one. Not only are they the smartest culprit, but they are also the smartest in the series, yes above Benoit. They have great scheming, thinking, manipulation, deception, EQ, SQ, and reasoning. In fact I want to scale them in this sub, but i wanted to make this post first to introduce the series to ppl.

# In Conclusion

fun movies, even when not considering scd the movies were fun to watch. If you enjoy scaling low tiers or want more low tiers included then i’d give these movies a shot.
