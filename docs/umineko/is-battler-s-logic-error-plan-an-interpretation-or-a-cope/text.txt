To all Umineko Scalers (especially Battler scalers). Is Battler's Logic Error Plan an Interpretation or actually just a cope theory?

The main topic in hand will be about whether Battler planned everything, or it was just a backup strategy of his which was lucky enough to be successful.

Every single one of Battler scalers I have seen believe in this, like all 8 of them I have seen talked about this like it's a fact.

 Most of the supporting arguments do make sense, but at the same time, they don't feel strong enough to be 100% valid.

>Battler is the Game Master and knew about Beato's identity trick, he must know a way out of Logic Error.

This is true, he most likely knew about the solution for the Logic Error but at the same time, it can be argued by the fact that Battler didn't solve LE because he didn't want to expose Beatrice's heart. (stated in the story)

>if he couldn't solve it for Beatrice's sake, then why would he make a dumb decision to declare red and fall for the Logic Error in the first place? It wouldn't make sense, unless he planned it to revive Beato.

This is probably the most common argument I have seen, however, the story never explicitly stated it, people just somehow accepted this as fact because it made the most logical sense.

>Why did Battler create the Love Trial Gameboard? As well as Shannon Versus Kanon Duel? unless it was to revive Beato

Of course, it is implied that it was to indirectly manipulate Chick Beatrice, leading to her full revival. But again, implied is not the same as 100% truth

>Why did Battler set up the condition for Erika to trap him, with the duct tapes?

Of course, this was to lead to the Kyrie escape plan, which leads to Erika decapitating them, which led to the Logic Error

And people believe this was all in Battler's plan to revive Beatrice.

For this Logic Error Plan to be valid, there are way a lot of coincidences which makes me start to doubt it as a whole.

what if Bern never set up the Wedding, then Chick Beato wouldn't have been pressured to save Batttler, leading to the failure of the plan.

What if Erika wasn't smart enough to cut Kyrie and the rest of the family out as a savior of Battler option? Then of course his plan fails once again.

Or what if Erika didn't even use the duct tapes as Battler supposedly intended her to?

Because there were actually layers to all the traps, in which the outsmarting happened, it was not a simple "A makes Trap and B falls for it"

Battler not only have to anticipate the strategy Erika would use regarding the duct tapes, he would also need to anticipate all the ways that could lead to Logic Error, and narrows it down to the Decapitation part. Yet all of this wasn't explained in the story.

Lastly, the last point people use for this is the only one I cannot deny.

That point is Battler creating the gameboard beforehand, in which the gameboard including the love duel.

This could mean two things.

1. Battler predicted his Logic Error, and prepared Love Duel to revive Beatrice
2. Battler prepared Love Duel just in case something bad happens to him, and Chick Beatrice would be enlightened after seeing the duel

Of course, second interpretation just downscales his plan a lot, because it would mean Battler didn't anticipate everything.

I don't really know how to feel about this, what do you think?
