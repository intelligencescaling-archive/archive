Background info for non-Umineko readers
Before we start with the doc, I will briefly talk about what you need to know to understand the context.

In this Bern's game, there's the gamemaster and the player, the gamemaster creates the gameboard where the murder happens in a world, and the player tries to solve it. It's similar to a typical murder mystery, but there will be some rules.
Battler and Beatrice are the only ones playing, and the Battler in the gameboard is inhrently different from the Battler in the Meta World playing the game. If it's confusing for you or you're non Umineko readers, just approach me, I will try to explain properly to you.

1. Red Truth:
Red Truths are true statements and anything said in red is a fact that can be presented by the gamemaster unquestionably without any evidence. Red can work as hints for the players to solve the mystery. it can also be used to restrict absurd theories which could go against detective fiction rules

2. Blue Truth:
Blue Truths are used by the players, and they are the proposed theories to the murder happening in the gameboard. (eg. how the murder happened) The gamemaster must counter the blue with red or the player would come closer to winning the game. Whatever is in blue must abide by the red truths stated.

3, Purple Statements
Purple Statements are new type of statement used in Bern's game, and they are statements which are considered as truths, unless stated by the murderer. This means the murderer can lie with Purple.
