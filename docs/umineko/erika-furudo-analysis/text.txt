Introduction
Erika Furudo is one of Uminekos main antagonists, striving for the truth and only the truth. She finds enjoyment in uncovering the truth and seeing the peoples reaction and bringing them an unpleasant feeling.  She is extremely sadistic, arrogant, ruthless and cruel.
She herself is a servant and double created by Bernkastel, made in her image to take apart  Beatrice’s gameboard .
She takes the human side and tries to defeat the witches side (Lambdadelta in Episode 5, Battler in Episode 6) in the witches’ gameboard.

Solving the Epitaph
Context:
“The Witch’s Epitaph” is a riddle put up by Kinzo Ushiromiya. To solve it, one needs to use a wide range of different reasoning types.
To solve the riddle one has to know about kanji, the english language and asian geography. The complete solving process is extremely tedious and without extra clues given later in the story, it seems almost impossible. I will not go over the whole epitaph now, so look it up online when you are interested about the specific contents.
I will talk about the most important and impressive feats of Erika. Do note that Erika has in fact solved the Epitaph in cooperation with Battler, so she will have to share some of these credits. Battler has however tried to solve the epitaph before in the story and failed on his own. His progress in the solving process did not come close to the actual solution, leeding to the belief that Erika has in fact done most of the solving at this point.
She did however state: “Also, I wouldn’t have been able to solve this riddle myself. I ask that you praise Battler-san’s achievement as well, everyone.”

-The epitaph should be solved by knowing about Kinzos relation with Taiwan. Erika did NOT have this knowledge.
-With said knowledge, one should connect the metaphor “sweetfish river” with a train rail system.
-Knowing both of these things, one should look for certain kanji in the epitaph which overlap with the names of a certain railway station in the Danshui(=Freshwater) Line in Taiwan.
-Taking the 10 twilights and multiplying them with themself 4 times (indicated through the 4 treasures of the golden land) gets you the number “quadrillion” which is also written on the chapel.
-When taking away the letters of the certain railway station in Taiwan, one can reduce the word “quadrillion” to “U-D-R-L-O”.
-When rearranging these letters on said chapel, one finally opens the secret passage to the gold.

Erika has stated that the epitaph must be solvable for the people in the mansion, since Kinzo did not publish it anywhere else besides showing it openly in the mansion. (Reasoning)
Erika has solved the whole epitaph in a span of roughly 45 minutes (probably even faster since they had to walk to the chapel, 45 is the absolute maximum), without knowing Kinzo’s connection with Taiwan (Reasoning).
To solve it, Erika went straight to the library to look at an atlas after thinking about it for a short while to search for (presumably) train systems in Taiwan without knowledge about Kinzo’s connection with Taiwan. (Intuition)
 Erika has memorized the entire epitaph after looking at it for a few moments in the main hall. (Memory)

 It is confirmed that those thought to be inside the mansion at 24:00 were indeed inside the mansion. None of them left the mansion before 1:00 except Natsuhi.

Erika claims that everyone who wanted to reach the second floor of the guesthouse had to pass through the lounge. At this time, Erika was in the lounge the whole time.

Detective Authority
The Detective Authority is a supernatural power given to Erika in Episode 5, since she is the Detective in this episode.
Here is a list of feats which one could connect with the DA:

With the DA, her hearing is boosted so that she can hear way better than she normally could.
-Erika could hear everything that happened in the cousins room by pressing her ear against the wall. (Perception)

With the DA, she has the right to inspect all crime scenes without anyone getting in her way.
-Erika has entered the room in which the relatives cried about George, Jessica, Rosa and Maria who presumably died. Despite their grief, Erika has managed to calm them down and enter the room to observe the crime scene without anyone attempting to stop her. (Manipulation)

With the DA, every clue needed for the solution of the crime must be given to the detective.
-Erika was given the status of the corpses by Doctor Nanjo, who normally wouldn't share such information to an outsider like Erika. (Manipulation)

With the DA, an assembly which concludes the mystery and tries to bring forth the true culprit of a crime can be called.
-Erika brought all survivors in one place and made them unable to refuse to listen to her. (Manipulation)

The Detective can not be the murderer.
-This part will get really important later on, just thought I’d mention it here.

Later on, Battler reveals that the DA is similar to magic in the sense that the user cannot do something, which they normally couldn’t do. In other words, all of the above mentioned feats are, in fact, only possible with the DA since Erika can also accomplish them on their own.

The Logic Error Strategy

Erika plans to trap Battler in a “Logic Error” so that she can take over the gameboard. By doing so, she must trap Battler in a situation which is impossible to solve.

Erika states that because she has failed to win in the last game, she will have to raise the stakes by giving away her Detective Authority. This is the beginning of Erika’s “Logic Error” Strategy. All of this happened before the first Twilight, so there was no way of knowing what kind of mystery Battler would actually construct.

Erika has made up a psychological profile of Battler, predicting that he might not actually kill the victims due to the victims being his family. (Emotional Understanding/Hot Reading/Foresight)
This is especially useful since Battler has to carry out the murders/plan the murders as the Game-Master. Knowing that the victims will only play dead is a giant advantage in their “fight”.

Erika gave up her DA in order to later on kill the victims herself to trap Battler in the Logic Error. (Strategy)

Erika was the first person to state that they shall raise the stakes, ergo, that she should give up her DA. Bernkastel pushed her even further to this, however, Bernkastel only did so after Erika had originated the idea. This was done so that Battler believes Erika to be a victim of Bernkastel. He believes that Erika does not actually want to give away her DA. (Deception)

After the first twilight, everyone gathered in the guest house. The 3 husbands, equipped with Winchester Rifles obviously mourn the death of their wives and are obviously filled with rage. Rudolf suggests finding the culprit and implies to kill them when finding them.

Erika however calms everyone down through her reasoning and takes complete control of the situation, ordering the characters around. (Manipulation)

Erika talks to Rudolf and Hideyoshi in secret, telling them that Gohda had a chance to kill the victims. Erika, thanks to Battler, knows for a fact that Gohda isn't cooperating with the culprit. (Deception)
She reasons that the killings could only have happened without the victims fighting back, if the culprits acted as a group. (Reasoning)
Erika strengthens this theory by showing a possible way for the servants to carry out the locked-rooms. Erika herself knows about the red truths and that this very theory is fundamentally false. She does this to strengthen the “servants-culprit” theory. (Deception)

She does this, so that the two of them actually suspect the servants, spreading distrust in the group to make it easier to manipulate them. (Strategy)
By using the “Servant Culprit", Erika gets the 3 husbands to lock everyone up in the guesthouse. (Manipulation)

Battler argues with Bernkastel about Erika not using the Detectives Authority. Erika seems to be stressed out about the whole thing, since Bernkastel seems to force her to not fight with the DA.
Lambda claims to take care of Bern, giving both Battler and Erika the opportunity to fight with their whole power.
Battler asks Erika to use the DA. She, however, can't use it due to Bernkastel not allowing her to.
This whole move seems to have been planned and orchestrated by Erika.

When asked if there are any other ways for Dlanor to use red truths, Battler receives the info that the Duct Tape used by Erika can actually hold the same power. Battler trusts Dlanor, who was also used by Erika in being ordered to say what she wanted Battler to believe.

Erika, saying that she wants no pity, tricks Battler, who does in fact feel pity for Erika, into giving her 3 rooms worth of Duct Tape seal. This was possible due to an understanding of Battlers psyche.

Dlanor asks Battler to change the Duct Tape retrospectively, so that Erika did in fact have it before the first Twillights were even discovered. Since Dlanor is being ordered to do so by Erika, this is also part of her plan, more on this later on. Battler trusts in Dlanor and grants her wish. (Manipulation/Deception/Hot Reading/Emotional Understanding/Planning)

When locked up in “the next room over”, she deceives Hideyoshi into letting her leave the room due to her claiming that she has to confirm something with the servants in the “cousins room”. This, of course, is a lie to let her leave. (Deception/Manipulation)

By manipulating the husbands to lock everyone up in the two rooms, Erika was given the opportunity to move around freely on the island. (Manipulation)

After confirming everyone's location, Erika seals everyone supposedly still alive inside either the cousins room or the next room over via Duct Tape. (Strategy)

Erika finds a letter, which talks about Piece-Battler’s corpse being taken away.

From here on, the main part of the Logic Error Strategy starts.
To fully grasp it, we have to look at Battlers intended way to go at this;

-All 6 victims are not actually dead. They are cooperating with each other.
-Piece-Battler placed the letter there and moved away from his own room.

This is Battlers originally intended idea.

Erika immediately figures this out (Reasoning) and starts her counter attack:
Due to Dlanor asking for the retrospectively added seals, Erika now has the opportunity to retrospectively add a seal on a door. She uses this to seal the room in which Piece-Battler was found retrospectively. (Planning)

Battler lets her do this, since he also has another plan which is the following:

-All the people who seemed to be dead, are actually alive
-Piece-Battler hides inside the room in which his “corpse” was found, therefore not breaking the seal.
-One of the other seemingly dead people place the letter there
-Piece-Battler leaves the room once Erika has entered it and therefore broken the seal

Erika figures this out as well (Reasoning), finds a strategy to counter this by setting the chain right after she enters the room, therefore sealing the room again (Strategy) and also predicts Battler’s next plan after this, before he himself thought of it, which is the following; (Foresight)

-Someone of the other people who are still alive comes into the room after Erika has opened it
-Said person changes places with Battler
-Said person is still inside the room, however Battler himself would have the chance to escape the room

Erika pre counters this by decapitating  all of the supposed victims, excluding Battler, herself to confirm their deaths. (Strategy) This was only possible due to her predicting this kind of situation early on (Foresight) and giving away her DA to eliminate any possibility of Battler being saved. (Planning)

Before entering the room (and before letting Battler know that she has already figured out the solution and before countering by setting the chain) she asks Battler if he didn’t want to surrender this room to her by claiming that Battler is still there.
Erika uses this to get Lambda to judge over this fight of the room and therefore trapping Battler into the Logic Error. Having a clear picture of Battler’s psyche now, Erika knows that he will not back down, therefore trapping him in this battle. (Manipulation/Emotional Understanding)
Battler tries to counter Erika’s setting the chain by claiming that the chain was already broken.
Thanks to being allowed to use the Duct Tape for a whole room, thanks to Dlanor convincing Battler to retroactively add the Duct Tape into Erika’s possession, who, mind you, was probably ordered by Erika to say so since Battler has a good relationship with Dlanor, Erika was able to tape the halves of the chain back together, therefore being able to set it yet again. (Reasoning/Planning/Emotional Understanding/Strategy)

Now, Battler uses the second strategy that Erika has already predicted. Here, of course, comes Erika’s move of killing all the victims beside Battler herself. Looking at it retrospectively, this was probably already done by her when she checked each of the bodies shortly after they were discovered, since Bernkastel shortly after discovering the corpses mentions that Erika’s way of inspecting the corpses could as well be called a red truth.
By already killing the victims back then, Erika does not only confirm the victims death (Strategy) but also sets a trap for Battler way before the logic error seems to be important. (Planning/Trap Setting).

Battler is then trapped in the Logic Error.

After trapping Battler in a logic error, Erika begins preparations for her wedding with Battler.
Since Battler is currently trapped inside the Logic Error, he cannot really do anything, making him unable to refuse marrying Erika.

She eliminates herself from being the saviour in order to eliminate the possibility of rescuing him herself by accident. (Strategy, Trap Evasion)

Conclusion
Erika Furudo has shown a long list of feats, highlighting her Reasoning, Strategy, Planning, Perception, Manipulation and Foresight.
Her Reasoning is next to none in her verse, conducting strong solutions with only a small and simple bit of given information.
Her Strategies are extremely complex and often include spontaneous hidden traps to corner her opponents.
Her Planning finds its apex in her Logic Error Strategy, where she sets the necessary pieces to fulfill her Strategy early on.
Her Perception with the DA is next to none, however even without the DA her Perception is extremely reliable and accurate.
Her Manipulation is extremely effective due to her brilliant Emotional Understanding and Hot Reading of other people.
Her Foresight is very long ranging, foreseeing Battler’s plans to eventually solve the Logic Error and taking countermeasures long before it seems relevant.
