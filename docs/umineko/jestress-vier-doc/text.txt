Jestress’s Role in the Story:
Jestress is the court jester of the Three Kings, serving as their agent and right-hand woman in carrying out their plans and objectives. She is one of the main antagonists operating behind the scenes in Ciconia: When They Cry, responsible for orchestrating all the events that unfold throughout the story.
Context:
Ciconia takes place during the A3W era, 100 years after the Third World War, an event that resulted in the annihilation of the entire planet. The 8MS system is the only thing maintaining the façade of stability. However, despite this system and the attempts to erase the causes of the conflict from history, palpable tensions persist among the various global factions, potentially leading to another world war. The Gauntlet Knights are tasked with upholding the walls of peace to prevent the extinction of all life. Since Ciconia is a work for which only 1/4 of the material has been completed so far, and with Phase 2 still awaiting an announcement, this analysis and explanatory piece will serve as a tribute to the series through my personal theories and interpretations.

Therefore, let us perform a calculation.

Calculation
1. Let us interpret the phrase “several centuries” as referring to 600 years.
Therefore:
Approximately 1 year = 31,536,000 seconds.
600 × 31,536,000 = 18,921,600,000 seconds
A supercomputer in the Ciconia universe would take approximately 18,921,600,000 seconds to hack advanced military technology.
2. Users with Level P3 Conscious Parallel Processing can accomplish this task in an extremely short period, approximately 5 seconds.
18,921,600,000 ÷ 5 = 3,784,320,000
In this case, a Conscious Parallel Processing user completes the operation approximately 3,784,320,000 times faster than a supercomputer in the Ciconia universe.
3. We assume that a supercomputer in the Ciconia universe can perform approximately 50 quintillion calculations per second, given the advanced technological development of that era.
50 × 10¹⁸ × 3,784,320,000 = 1.89216 × 10²⁹
The result suggests that CPP users possess an estimated processing capacity of approximately 189.216 octillion calculations per second (Intelligence – Cognitive Proficiency).

Profecía:
A prophecy involves receiving the words of an omnipotent and omniscient God, a being possessing absolute knowledge of everything and nothing within reality, as it transcends such limitations at an ontological level. Therefore, receiving the words of such an entity and subsequently conveying them transforms those words into a yogen (予言), meaning a prophecy or an omen concerning specific events.
In this sense, Miyao’s words are, in fact, a forewarning and an announcement of a future that has already been foretold. Regardless of whether someone attempts to deny what will happen or tries to prevent it, the event has already been determined. Consequently, her words serve as a prediction of an outcome that has already been established.
> It means 'prophecy', to receive words from God.
God is omnipotent, and He also knows everything about the future. So, the words of God are a 'promise' about the future. When you receive those words of God and you speak them, it becomes 'yogen', a prophecy...

Master Plan — Human Eradication:
Everything Jestress does from this point onward will be directed toward achieving her ultimate plan: eradicating anyone who opposes her scheme to destroy the world.
1. She caused the first World Wars.
2. She exploited the aftermath of the First and Second World Wars to manipulate public opinion regarding various organizations.
3. She used infiltrators within the Gauntlet Knights as assets to relay real-time information about the actions of other Gauntlet Knights, taking advantage of the fact that the organization was considered one of the most secure in the world.
4. She used Toujiro as a pawn to initiate the Fourth World War.
5. Toujiro controls the God Program, an existential model capable of influencing society's behavior through the subconscious of certain individuals, enabling them to oppose the prevailing opinion of their own species.
6. The infiltrated Gauntlet Knights provide information about potential opponents of the Divine Plan, allowing them to be identified and their possible actions anticipated.
7. She instigated multiple intercontinental conflicts involving the Gauntlet Knights. Although they attempt to contain these conflicts and maintain order, they are gradually drawn into a confrontation with one another.
8. Toujiro strips Miyao of control over his own body, ultimately causing him to kill Lingji.
9. The Fourth World War begins and eventually culminates in humanity's self-inflicted extinction, with Jestress delivering the final blow to ensure this outcome.

PHASE 1 - World wars and Infiltration
(VN) - In the past, Jestress understood that achieving her goal of eradicating humanity required her to carefully prepare and orchestrate the events necessary to carry out her plan. To accomplish this, she had to begin by instigating the first World Wars and establishing the conditions that would allow subsequent events to unfold.
Her objectives during this phase would be as follows:
1. Establish a false narrative in which the true history behind the wars could never be uncovered, creating multiple conflicting accounts of the same events.
2. Foster hatred between countries seeking to investigate the true history, encouraging mutual criticism and sowing the seeds of future conflicts, including the Fourth World War.
3. Manipulate historical information so that history could never be documented in its entirety and no reliable records of past events would exist. Consequently, countries would lack a clear frame of reference to defend themselves against threats of equal or even greater magnitude.
4. Suppress historical research during the period in which Ciconia takes place, preventing individuals from reconstructing past events and discovering the true origins of the conflicts.
5. Continue manipulating the populations of individual countries, using the nations themselves as instruments of her plan. Jestress would continue influencing people regardless of whether they hated one another or maintained seemingly peaceful relationships, ensuring that events gradually progressed toward the scenario she needed.
6. Maintain infiltrators across every corner of the planet, all of whom would provide her with information.
7. Place some of these infiltrators within the ranks of the Gauntlet Knights. These loyal servants would follow her orders and continuously relay information, allowing Jestress to assess potential adversaries and use a strategic asset at her disposal to counter each threat, ensuring that the resulting countermeasures remained under her control.
There are no indications of how long Jestress took to formulate this phase of her plan. However, given that she is portrayed as an individual capable of exerting absolute influence over the world's chaotic system, this system can be understood as a complex adaptive system whose overall scope is virtually immeasurable due to the countless interactions among its constituent elements. These include the world's economic, political, historical, social, and institutional structures.
For Jestress to interact with a system of this magnitude, she would need to take specific actions and develop a plan consisting of multiple steps directed toward achieving a particular objective. Therefore, we can argue that Jestress would be constantly adapting her plan in response to the numerous variables emerging within the system.
This implies that her planning would not necessarily be static but could instead be continuously adjusted to accommodate changes and developments throughout the world. This would allow her to maintain control over the pursuit of her objective, even as new variables and unforeseen circumstances emerged.

But who are the Three Kings?
(VN) - My interpretation is that the Three Kings somehow embody the three World Wars that occurred throughout the history of Ciconia When They Cry.
At the beginning, it is established that Ciconia takes place 100 years after the Third World War. In this context, there is a particularly striking piece of dialogue concerning the Kings, in which they state: “Even when we try to exterminate them completely, they manage to survive and multiply in the blink of an eye.” This could imply that they have already attempted to eradicate humanity in the past and that their objective remains, in some capacity, to achieve the complete annihilation of the human race by following what they call the “Divine Plan.” Considering that the events responsible for the greatest number of human deaths throughout history have been wars, the World Wars fit particularly well with this interpretation.
The very nature of the gameboard also makes this possibility plausible, especially when taking into account elements previously introduced in Umineko. At no point in the story do the Kings interact directly with any character other than Jestress herself, who acts as the spokesperson for their will. This contrasts with the rest of the cast, whose members interact with multiple characters throughout the narrative.
For this reason, I believe the Three Kings could be manifestations of Jestress's subconscious rather than necessarily being independent entities. The fact that they interact exclusively with her is particularly noteworthy, as Jestress may be projecting these figures as distinct facets of her own mind and using them to represent different aspects of her plan.
This interpretation gains further support from the information provided in Jestress's final data fragment, which expands our understanding of her planning capabilities and the way she manipulates events. This referred to a single work within their world, one that encompassed the entirety of its canon. The canon served as a guide to their religion, which was why some individuals attempted to insert their own interpretations into it, introducing forgeries disguised as the authentic book. These forgeries described how humanity would be destroyed in the future, presenting it as an event foretold in the true canon. This information had been completely erased from history, making it a unique piece of knowledge that only they possessed.

This book was not a prophecy in the strict sense, as it was more akin to a plan devised by Jestress. If it were truly a prophecy, it would have to fit that interpretation perfectly. However, it did not fully align with such an interpretation, as it only achieved a score of 80 out of 100. Even if we assume that the opponent's objective is to reach 100%, they have only achieved 80% of that goal so far.
If these games are meant to provide entertainment rather than simply determine who wins and who loses, then it would be rather unappealing for our opponent to begin with a perfect move, especially if they consider everyone else inferior to them.
This indicates that Jestress did not begin by making perfect moves from the outset when carrying out her World Eradication plan. Instead, she considers her opponents part of a game designed for her own amusement, as they are inferior to her in every respect.
Therefore, it would be reasonable to conclude that she had already established reliable contingencies from the very beginning. She simply chooses to treat the confrontation as a game because the others pose no genuine threat capable of preventing her from achieving her goal of World Eradication.
She is toying with her opponents, as she has been deliberately suppressing herself from the very beginning to give them the opportunity to put up a good fight.

To put it concisely, it can be said that, in some way, “God’s” plan to exterminate humanity was devised and presented as a prophecy against humankind 2,000 years ago. In this case, it is reasonable to assume that, despite Jestress’s youthful appearance, she is far older than she seems, given that this plan has existed for two millennia. This would make little sense if Jestress were genuinely young. A more plausible explanation is that she is considerably older than she appears and formulated this plan long ago, waiting only for the right moment to carry it out, during an era when its execution would be much easier. After all, she has already been responsible for causing the world wars, laying the groundwork for the Fourth World War, and ultimately eradicating humanity as she intended.
It is only thanks to Seshat that this book, containing information that was completely erased from history, has been preserved. This raises the question of whether Jestress knew that Seshat possessed this evidence or had the book in her possession.
However, given Jestress’s position, it is reasonable to conclude that if she is the messenger of an omniscient and omnipotent God, she would undoubtedly be aware of it.

This book is not a prophecy, but rather a plan written by Jestress herself. However, it is worth emphasizing that if it were indeed a prophecy, everyone would be completely doomed. This would suggest that Jestress possessed an extraordinarily precise understanding of what the other key individuals in the world were thinking and how they would act, to the point that she could accurately predict their behavior and use them as mere pawns on her chessboard.
This would also imply that the outcome had already been decided from the very beginning, as everyone ultimately ended up playing right into her hands. Jestress would not need to improvise in response to the actions of others; instead, she would have already anticipated their moves and every possible response, keeping them confined within a scenario she herself had carefully orchestrated.      An additional point worth highlighting is that, although Toujiro and Seshat are familiar with most of the overall structure of Jestress’s plan, this knowledge was insufficient for them to understand its true scope and full extent, as demonstrated in the final chapters of Ciconia.
As mentioned, Jestress knew that Seshat and Toujiro possessed information about her, yet she still had ways to counter any potential moves they might make. This demonstrates that her opponents’ knowledge of part of her plan did not pose a genuine problem for her, as she regarded them as weak opponents on her game board. Consequently, she was able to anticipate and respond to their potential moves.

Basically, it is revealed that Seshat and Toujiro are Jestress’s opponents, as they must win to prevent humanity from becoming nothing more than a piece on the game board of God’s representative.

This brings the data fragment to a close, providing us with sufficient information to infer that Jestress understands every move and action taken by any individual on the planet, given her position as the representative of an omnipotent and omniscient God.
Furthermore, this means that she possesses enough information to formulate her plans with such effectiveness that, from the perspective of others, they could easily be mistaken for prophecies. As a result, she chooses to toy with her opponents, since her optimal moves would guarantee her victory.
