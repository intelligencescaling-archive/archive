Analysing Majora's Mask — Link Suppression Planning

Deku Transformation Strategy:

In his Deku form, Link is only recognised as a child by the guards of Clock Town, so is unable to leave within the First Three Days cycle. Furthermore, in order to confront Majora on top of the Clock Tower, Link would have to use a Deku flower given his small proportions. However, the only one nearby is owned by a Deku businessman, who would only give his place up for a Moon Tear, a very rare item that can only be accessed via the Astral Observatory, which in itself is blocked off on both sides by the Bomber Kids group and and large metal gate surrounding the perimeter.

As such, even within the first cycle, Link is forced to befriend and integrate himself into the Bomber Kids group to gain access to the observatory and reach Majora to retrieve his Ocarina; should Link fail to do so (and also if he fails to recover the Great Fairy and regain his magic, he can't attack Majora anyways), he is trapped with no means to prevent the Moon from crashing into Termina.

Area Isolation Planning:

Majora uses its power to influence the four regions of Termina in distinct ways that would make them more difficult to traverse. Majora's aim is to cause as much internal strife as possible, as seen from Monkey Framing at the swamp (tbf the monkey could speak English so Majora isn't completely a fraud), or the Pirate-Zora conflict at Great Bay that was instigated by Majora.

Majora's core influence over the swamp area is poisoning the waters, making traversal difficult especially for the Link. Furthermore, Majora assaulted one of the Witch sisters that runs the boat cruise for the swamp and drained her energy, leaving her abandoned in the lost woods near the potion shop. As such, it is nigh-impossible for Link to reach the Deku Palace on his own. Majora also anticipated that the Deku Princess would try and enter the Woodfall Temple with the help of the monkeys to try and purify the waters, only for the princess to be abducted, resulting in the Deku King framing the entire situation on the monkey that accompanied the princess instead of helping to access the Temple, concealing the existence of the Sonata of Awakening needed to access the Temple after the monkey is captured.

For the mountains, the entrance to the area is blocked off by ice, requiring Link to possess the bow and arrow to pass through. The Goron Village itself has its movements restricted in that the Gorons struggle to move through the intense cold. An additional effect of the cold weather is the freezing of the weapon's forge, which prevents Link from upgrading his sword until finding spring water from Darmani's grave, which can only be found using the Lense of Truth hidden in an isolated cave away from the village.

In the Great Bay, the murkiness of the waters and infestation of sea snakes makes it impossible for the Zora tribe to contact the rest of Termina. The only reason Link was able to learn about the events in Zora's domain was through Mikau, who by coincidence washed up on the shore of the Great Bay after being attacked by the Pirates. Additionally, Great Bay is only reachable by horseback, and since the beginning of the game Epona has been abducted and placed in Romani Ranch, which in itself is another strategy.

And finally, in Ikana kingdom, the upper village is only accessible via hookshot, which is an item concealed within one of the temples. Should Link get past this obstacle, the route to Ikana Castle is locked behind the labyrinth underneath the well. As a result of Majora's curse on reviving the dead in Ikana, Link must obtain the Gibdo mask from healing Pamela's father, whom Majora has turned insane after drawing him towards research of the Gibdos in the canyon.

Epona Abduction Strategy:

Epona (Link's horse) is abducted by Majora very early on into the game, with him bluffing about killing Epona to get a reaction out of Link. Majora then takes Epona to Romani Ranch and blocks the path to the ranch off with a boulder, one that can only be broken with either a powder keg, or by letting a Goron break is open over the three days. A powder keg is a high power explosive that can only be purchased after receiving a use permit from Biggoron in Goron village after a competency trial, which in itself can't be carried out until Link defeats Goht in Snowhead Temple.

Simply put, there is very little chance for Link to access Romani Ranch within the first three day cycle except for on the Third Day. Next is the involvement of Romani, and her abduction by the 'Aliens' (Hal reference) if Link doesn't protect the ranch sufficiently. In order to retrieve Epona, Link must learn Epona's song from Romani. The problem with this is that, assuming Link cannot access the powder keg, Romani is abducted and has her memories erased, leaving her in a disoriented state on the third day and this being unable to teach Epona's song to Link.

Given Majora's knowledge and overall awareness over Termina, it is very likely that Majora was aware of the 'Aliens' that visit the ranch annually, meaning it predicted the entire Alien invasion and how Clock Town would respond to blocking off the ranch, timing things perfectly such that Link would have wait until defeating Goht before he could access Epona, which severely limits his progress throughout Termina, and prevents access to seashore, linking to Majora's best Plan of Great Bay Temple Concealment, which I'll either make a separate analysis on just dedicate a section to it in the overall feats doc.
