First Part (Kuze, Yuki and Ayano all accomplished)
Volume 5 Pages 162-170 + 176-177:

Analysis of the Quiz Research Game with the Trivia.
Rules: 31 questions, 6 topics with 5 questions on each + 1 additional question, 10 seconds on each answer. You have the option to call your partner once (this game was made for Yuki and Alya, the candidates for the Council President position), and while doing so, you get 10 seconds from the normal time (the other person has to answer), 10 seconds to communicate with your partner and 10 additional seconds to make the answer. Only one of the contestants can make a call at a time, not both. The difficulty of the question determines how many points it's worth, and its difficulty is determined by a statistic of the right answer from non-contestants (Alya and Yuki).

You can't give any hints or do any type of help to your partner nor can anyone else because they'll just kick that person out and ask a different question which makes it useless. What's more, as a last detail, they give you the view to see which of the 2 main contestants answers they picked.

EXTRA DETAILS ABOUT THE DIFFICULTY:

Thanks to the talk with my friend Latemantella, I realized that I didn't clearly state why a lot of the reasoning in the first part was needed and how it's not out of place, that is, the deceptiveness of this Arc.

Firstly you aren't at all given any hints or reason to believe there's something fishy with the game or that there's any pattern, you have to reason it out yourself and notice that the set-up seems weird and there's balancing issues with how the game was made aka you need to properly comprehend it and pay good attention to the whole picture
Secondly it's made to seem like a regular Quiz Game and with decently regular template for topics + evaluation, which was outright stated by the host and assumed by anyone who seen quiz games with how they interpreted pretty regular template as I stated before
Third of all, you get outright lied to and not just in any part but IN THE RULES and the lie is about something as drastic as it gets, evaluation. If you've read the reasoning before and how the evaluation actually works here you understand what I mean, and it's not even being misleading or ambiguous but outright a lie:

As shown here they say this: The host while explaining the rules said outright that they have their own technology that used THEIR OWN PERSONAL surveys. Not only does it say they used their own one's and have the technology for it while acting like everything is already accounted for (which is the misleading and heavily implied part) but it also says surveys aka multiple which just isn't true and outright a lie as they used a single one in the real time.
That shows the importance of the reasoning in the beginning and to literally find enough evidence and logic to reject a rule and direct statement from the host as well as the whole appearance set up of the game.
You who read this, probably know it already, but that's pretty rare to see in a reasoning feat where you don't even know you should use reasoning and then it's outright stated that something is a lie in something that should be established rules.
