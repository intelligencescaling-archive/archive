Natalie's Manipulation Plan Analysis (Memento)

Spoilers for all of Memento, I highly recommend watching the entire movie blind for the first time. The rest of the post assumes you're mostly familiar with the plot and premise of the movie. I'll also be talking about the plot in chronological order.

Natalie's boyfriend Jimmy Grantz leaves the bar towards the beginning of the story to meet "some guy named Teddy" for a drug deal (while carrying a lot of money), but never returns. Instead, Leonard drives up to the bar wearing Jimmy's clothes and driving his car. Natalie recognizes him as "the memory guy" that Jimmy's mentioned in the past. She asks him if he knows Teddy, and if he knows what happened to Jimmy, but Leonard doesn't remember.

Natalie doesn't know if Leonard's memory condition is real or not, so she tests it by having a few people spit in a mug of beer, then serving it to Leonard after a few minutes. Leonard drinks it, unaware of the spit. From this, she knows his condition is real, and knows his memory resets after a couple minutes. She also figures that someone with this condition couldn't plan a murder, so the Teddy that planned the murder must be someone else. After talking a bit more, she also finds out about Leonard's wife, and deduces that he has a strong sense of justice when it comes to women being abused/assaulted by men.

At this point, Jimmy's dead and his money is presumed gone (Natalie probably assumes Teddy took it, even though it's really still in the trunk of Jimmy's car). Natalie knows that Jimmy owes money to a dangerous people (Dodd), so she's on the hook now that Jimmy's dead. She also figures that Dodd will meet Leonard soon since he'd recognize Jimmy's car. She also knows that Leonard's condition, and strong sense of justice, makes him easy to manipulate. Because of this, Natalie decides to manipulate Leonard into killing Dodd for her.

Natalie invites Leonard to her house, then proceeds to collect all the pens and paper she can find and hide them in her purse. She knows Leonard writes notes for himself, so hiding the pens prevents him from calling out her lie in the future. Next, she deliberately goads Leonard into punching her by insulting his wife and calling him a freak. Leonard snaps and bruises her lip, so she quickly leaves the house to wait in the car for a few minutes. Leonard struggles to find a pen, but can't do it in time. Once Natalie comes back, she pretends Dodd just assaulted her, and she needs Leonard's protection. She writes a note describing his appearance, and telling Leonard to get him onto Teddy, or kill Dodd himself. Whatever happens, Natalie's doesn't have to put herself in harms way, can take care of Dodd, and potentially gets revenge on Teddy for killing Jimmy.

It's not the most complicated plan imaginable, but I appreciate how realistic it feels. Natalie's actions make perfect sense given the information she has. Outside of the plan itself, she's also able to stay composed and come up with a plan immediately after finding how her boyfriend's been murdered. On top of that, she was potentially staring at the killer in the face, and had a violent criminal chasing after her trying to collect his money.
