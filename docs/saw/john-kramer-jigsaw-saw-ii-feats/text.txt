It is worth noting that the “nerve gas” being pumped into the house within the film, is NOT a real-world substance. The film portrays a fictional nerve agent, often referred to as "the nerve gas" in the house - That is supposedly a fast-acting, lethal sarin-like toxin delivered as an airborne vapor. Victims inhale it, and it progressively causes symptoms like coughing blood, seizures, organ failure, and death over a period of about 2 hours - With an antidote (in the form of syringes hidden around the house) that can temporarily reverse or slow the effects of such an agent.
No real chemical warfare agent matches Kramer's concoction exactly. Sarin (the most commonly referenced real nerve agent) is real, but it kills in minutes (often under 10–15 minutes via inhalation), not hours, and there is no simple "antidote syringe" you can just inject to fully recover someone who has already absorbed a lethal dose. Real antidotes (atropine, pralidoxime, diazepam) must be given very quickly by trained personnel and are not guaranteed to work if exposure is severe.
Other real nerve agents (VX, tabun, soman, novichok agents, etc) also act extremely rapidly (seconds to minutes) and are not slow-burn toxins you can survive for hours at a time.
The concept of a colorless, odorless gas that gives you exactly two hours of increasingly specific symptoms, with conveniently placed antidote doses that reset such symptoms, is completely unheard of from a scientific and chemical standpoint.
Not only this, Kramer had developed an antidote to match this agent. He had understood the craft so well that he was able to create an antidote for a nerve agent that by all means surpasses any real world discovery or field concerning chemistry, which showcases immaculate comprehension over the subject as well as incredibly acute complex problem solving abilities.
Kramer had bypassed nearly every limitation of real-world chemistry to develop an agent that serves incredibly specific conditions and purposes. These include —>  Introducing perfect temporal control, airborne lethality with delayed systematic collapse, functional reversal of effects, and closed-loop toxin–antidote pairing. To achieve this, he would’ve needed to apply levels of chemical knowledge and overall academic intelligence far beyond our own, so much so that he was able to completely bypass the real-world limitations of chemistry/biology. If such a substance existed, it wouldn’t be an extension of modern chemistry - it would violate fundamental biological variability. He created an entirely new substance that is particularly specific in operation and unheard of in the field as a whole (incomprehensibly ahead of our own advancements), all whilst creating an impossible antidote to match.
At its core, chemistry operates on formal constraints:
* Conservation laws (mass, charge, energy)

* Stoichiometric balance

* Valence rules and orbital occupancy

* Thermodynamic feasibility (ΔG, ΔH, ΔS)

* Kinetic constraints (activation energy, rate laws)

Every chemical conclusion follows the form:
   * Given conditions A + rules R —> outcome B
That is fundamentally deductive logic, supplemented by probabilistic/statistical logic at scale. Due to the microscopic scale at which chemical reactions occur, the amount of reasoning required to engineer specific responses/results (especially in the case of perfect temporal control) would be astronomical. In standardized chemical reactions, there are already dozens to hundreds of variables present. Kramer surpassed the very laws that govern standard chemical reaction and created a substance that operates on a micro-temporal scope - meaning, a vast number of interdependent variables across chemistry, physiology, and behavior were forecasted nigh-perfectly.
Kramer's nerve agent is a creation that implies a level of biochemical mastery far beyond real-world science. Its precise timing, reversible effects, and engineered symptom progression would require not just advanced chemistry, but near-omniscient predictive modeling of biological systems. Within context, this positions Kramer as operating on a level of intelligence that exceeds contemporary scientific capability by orders of magnitude - once again, doing so within a constrained context.

Kramer’s strategy consisted of an in-depth fabrication of a currently running Jigsaw trial, with Eric’s son as one of the subjects as a form of leverage:
   1. Nerve Gas House Events Creation (where Kramer created a constrained chaotic system within a physical space and forecasted its phases into a probabilistic funnel).
   2. Transmission Manipulation + Double Houses (Kramer had created two houses - the true location of the nerve gas events, and a decoy. Kramer had rigged the decoy to generate the signal broadcasted by the immediate monitors, allegedly showing the currently progressing trial).
   3. Gordon Footage (Kramer had an apprentice, Dr. Gordon, be the one to perform the preparations on a subject and film it separate to the nerve gas house trial - the preparations occurring precisely two hours before the miniature game takes place, which is coincidentally the same length of the nerve gas house trial. The footage is then left at the scene, which misdirects law enforcement into believing Jigsaw was preoccupied with the surgery at that point in time, eliminating any plausible premeditation on his end once the actual strategy begins).
   4. Clock Motif/Profile Contradiction (Kramer had strategically placed a timer and a clock at the site of where law enforcement would be spectating the nerve gas house footage and observing Kramer himself. Notably, in all of Jigsaw’s games up to this point, a clock has always ticked down towards loss, never victory. The prime example being the bathroom trial. As such, it contradicts the profile law enforcement as carefully crafted for Jigsaw through past observations. To see the clock as a sign of victory would be to contradict this entirely. Even detective Kerry, who has been on the case for some time now, states Kramer’s actions here fit the profile they’ve constructed perfectly - highlighting the depth of Kramer’s deception).
   5. Rigg Framing (indirectly conditions Rigg into inadvertently acting as a strategic shield for Kramer, which serves the secondary purpose of building Rigg as a possible scapegoat in the future)
   6. Amanda Concealment (concealing the existence of Amanda as a disciple through 1 - Fabricating her as a subject in the nerve gas house trial and 2 - Ensuring even if the Gordon footage is seen through, Amanda doesn’t fit the profile for the apprentice seen in the footage)
Each layer has a multitude of intricacies to ensure solving the deception as a whole is nearly impossible due to the numerous logical contradictions that would be formed when piecing it together (example: There is a second Jigsaw —> This explains the footage —> it CAN’T be Amanda due to profiling).

Kramer had also carefully implemented two main strategic shields to accompany his layers of deception, as to ensure his strategy was fundamentally fortified from all angles:
   1. Rigg Conditioning (again, the indirect manipulation of Rigg acted as a secondary agent that shares Eric’s hostility. This ensures that if any members of law enforcement, such as Kerry, attempt to intervene and half Eric’s assault on Kramer that Rigg will preclude them, shielding Kramer’s strategy inadvertently).
   2. Elevator Tethering (by connecting the traceability of the signal to the elevator’s activation, it ensures that 1 - Upon Eric abducting Kramer, the signal will be traced and thus law enforcement will be forced to abandon Eric and 2 - If the technology team arrives early, it ensures the strategy isn’t hindered as the team won’t be able to trace the signal until Eric falls into Kramer’s trap).

Kramer’s alignment of the footage to real-time occurrences such as the technology team’s arrival implies immaculate predictive ability, being capable of arriving at probabilistic conclusions concerning a constrained chaotic system such as the nerve gas house itself, which would require managing numerous branching variables to a rough temporal level (to generate a specific phase occurring at a forecasted point). The nerve gas house fabrication itself utilizes a chemical that by all means surpasses nearly every limitation currently binding our real-world understanding of chemistry and how it interacts with the human body (introducing perfect temporal control, airborne lethality with delayed systematic collapse, functional reversal of effects, and closed-loop toxin–antidote pairing - all impossible from our current comprehension over the fundamental laws of chemistry). These bypasses (of fundamental laws of nature) place Kramer’s chemical intelligence centuries above our own modern standards in terms of raw cognitive advancements (likely formulated within a sparse timeframe and utilized knowledge he obtained in an extraordinarily shortened window - using resources only existent within our real-world limitations). Not only this, Kramer prepared a contingency (the elevator being linked to the signal traceability) in the event of the original forecast failing, showcasing coverage over nearly every possible outcome. Overall, Kramer’s strategy consisted of six deceptive layers intertwined and two main strategic shields, implementing multiple tactics revolving around misdirection and psychological breakage in the seams.
