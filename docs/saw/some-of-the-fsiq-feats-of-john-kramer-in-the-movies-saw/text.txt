Some Of The FSIQ Feats Of John Kramer In Movies

Hello Friends, I hope you are in a good mood and having a good day. Today I will try to give you some of Kramer's FSIQ feats. Kramer Might Probably Be My Favorite SCD Character (And from what I've seen, there are very few people ( u/FluffyTaste4061 and u/Intelligent_Dog7943 ) posting about Kramer in this community.) And It's Really Sad To See The Underrated Takes He Gets Around. Anyway, I Hope You Guys Enjoy Reading This.

**1)** In the [first movie](https://sawfilms.fandom.com/wiki/Saw), Kramer said that he injected Zep with a poison that only he had the antidote for. This means that Kramer either created a poison for [which he only had the cure](https://youtu.be/sqyk7_8Af0g?si=sA82w_Oojaz-wVSv&t=29), or he created an antidote for a poison for which there was no antidote. This Shows His Incredible Knowledge of **Toxicology**.

**2)** In the [second movie](https://sawfilms.fandom.com/wiki/Saw_II), John hacked the computer systems so methodically and effectively that he created a systematic system for determining when Eric would go crazy and beat John himself so bad, how long it would take for the technical team to decipher his complex system, and when the team would be able to decipher the system and find the game location while Eric and John were driving. In short, **he created a complex system for the technical team that would keep them busy for as long as he wanted**, in line with his own plan and for its systematic progress. (This also includes things like a very high degree of foresight and a very good manipulation of Eric's psychology.)

**3)** In the [third film](https://sawfilms.fandom.com/wiki/Saw_III), before Amanda and Hoffman kidnap Allison, Allison takes the VHS tape from the [Troy's game](https://sawfilms.fandom.com/wiki/Classroom_Trap) to examine it. (And interestingly, the tape was the only one thing not damaged by the huge explosion in the room.) While Allison is examining the tape at home, her television screen suddenly becomes static and distorts, showing a camera inside her closet. Allison tries to use the remote control several times, but to no avail. The important point here is this: **VHS tapes can never normally be hacked or corrupted**. But Amanda and Hoffman **manage to do this**. Kramer himself taught them how to do this. And in fact, Kramer must be superior to them in almost every subject he taught them. (John also 'literally' mocks Lynn Denlon's medical skills and university jargon in the third film. Amanda's nervous prose of medical terms also shows that John is **very good at in Medical Matters**.)

**4)** In the [fourth film](https://sawfilms.fandom.com/wiki/Saw_IV), we see how John makes his audio recordings. The reason FBI And Police haven't been able to convert the corrupted voice from his tapes to the original is because **he does such an excellent job**. If he hadn't willingly told Eric his name in SAW 2 or refused to meet them in person, he would still have remained anonymous. The biggest example of this is Hoffman's tapes in SAW VI. Hoffman's tapes are slightly flawed, and because of this flaw, the original audio can be distinguished from the corrupted audio. (Also in the 4th movie, John shows Jill how **he made a 300-year-old clock work like brand new**.)

**5)** In the [fifth film](https://sawfilms.fandom.com/wiki/Saw_V), when John kidnaps Hoffman, he tells him that the knife Hoffman used in the Pendulum Trap was [truly terrible](https://youtu.be/jL58gsgeU1w?si=uXTkx6yuFyX7ZaRQ&t=187). If he wanted true sharpness, he would have to use tempered steel. Despite Hoffman's creation of such a complex machine that even the human mind couldn't comprehend, **John pays attention to the details of his mistake**. Agent Peter Strahm has repeatedly stated in the films that **Kramer is a perfect and flawless person**. Also in the 5th movie, John says that **he never leaves anything to chance**.

**6)** In the [sixth film](https://sawfilms.fandom.com/wiki/Saw_VI), John, **recognizing at a glance a change Hoffman made to the Rack machine**, offers a quick answer and solution: ''If you changes the gear ratio, you must also change the gear oil to compensate for the increased friction.'' While not quite the same, he also points out that William forgot to include "[Will to Live](https://youtu.be/HjiazF1pijQ?si=_520hVPXvw7wlI6a&t=20)" in his insurance formula. He also **discusses the geography and history** of the Far East during a conversation with William in his office.

**7)** In the [seventh film](https://sawfilms.fandom.com/wiki/Saw_3D), John makes a short conversation with Bobby during his encounter. [He gives information about ancient Egyptian history](https://youtu.be/mrdgpAXvMDI?si=FTRDrvQ3URGJ8jaB&t=81). We've already mentioned that John is very knowledgeable about history and geography. Even planning for his unborn son to be born in the Chinese Year of the Pig zodiac is related to history and myths. While talking to Bobby, he says of history itself, "History is a passion for me." From this, **we can say confidently that he is very intelligent when it comes to history and geography**. Also, towards the end of the film, we see him [treating Dr. Gordon's severed leg](https://youtu.be/q5wZHoRbUeg?si=PvWwjs3eO80KxU_R&t=65). From this, we can say that **he can treat very serious injuries**. (I didn't include the Jigsaw movie in the list, but I should also mention this: John also [treated Logan Nelson](https://youtu.be/TNVGOITF_TA?si=51j0zUDhZbo_fjj4&t=115) when he was seriously injured.)

**8)** And finally in the [tenth movie](https://sawfilms.fandom.com/wiki/Saw_X), John **learned some Spanish** in less than a week. And in a foreign country far from his own, **he also made these traps in less than a week and organized a huge system:** [Pipe Bomb Trap](https://sawfilms.fandom.com/wiki/Pipe_Bomb_Trap),[Wire Saw Trap](https://sawfilms.fandom.com/wiki/Wire_Saw_Trap),[Brain Surgery Trap](https://sawfilms.fandom.com/wiki/Brain_Surgery_Trap),[Radiation Trap](https://sawfilms.fandom.com/wiki/Radiation_Trap),[Bloodboarding Trap](https://sawfilms.fandom.com/wiki/Bloodboarding_Trap),[Gas Chamber Trap](https://sawfilms.fandom.com/wiki/Gas_Chamber_Trap) and [Disembowelment Trap](https://sawfilms.fandom.com/wiki/Disembowelment_Trap). ([Eye Vacuum Trap](https://sawfilms.fandom.com/wiki/Eye_Vacuum_Trap) It's not included in the list because it wasn't maked. But the incredible thing here is that John designed the eye vacuum trap mechanism in his mind within a split second and arranged it according to the crime the [Custodian](https://sawfilms.fandom.com/wiki/Custodian) committed and made it a symbol.)
