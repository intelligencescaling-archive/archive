The Self and the Other

In this post I'll explain how Ririka "becoming" Kirari works and how this impacts both of their scalings

So Ririka is able to "become" Kirari, this was something she was taught to do since birth and was capable of doing is so well as a child that their relatives weren't able to tell them apart

It should be noted that Ririka doesn't just act like Kirari or imitates her, she quite literally believes herself to be Kirari when doing this. This belief is so deep that their thought processes, body language, speech patterns and pretty much anything else you can think of are nearly identical.

This is how they're able to perfectly read each other's hands in Change Poker, since they're both Kirari they can tell exactly which hands they have but picking up on extremely subtle tells. This is also a metacognition feat for Kirari since she'd have to understand her own tells in order to be able to pick up on Ririka's tells when Ririka is Kirari

However the Kirari that Ririka becomes and the actual Kirari do still have subtle differences (as you can see in the image I used in this post) and the real Kirari is able to pick up on them. Interestingly the kid versions of them do not appear to have any differences when they are both Kirari which is probably because Kirari wanted Ririka to be independent so over time she let Ririka to do things on her own, like during the Pop Idol Contest where they were separated and were doing independent things which eventually resulted in these subtle differences. So yeah it is likely that Kirari had been gradually giving Ririka her own identity since they were kids and finally succeeded with Change Poker

So in terms of scaling this obviously an amazing EQ feat for Ririka, while Kirari gets really good metacognition, foresight, manipulation and scheming feats from this. Both also get good STP, thinking, reasoning
