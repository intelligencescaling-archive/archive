The Explaination:

Kakegurui - Compulsive Gambler - Chapter 97:

In Chapter 97, which is Yumeko‘s backstory arc, we are introduced to Yumeko‘s sister and aunt, Souko Jabami (Yumeko’s elder sister) and Tsugiko Jabami (Yumeko and Souko’s Aunt), in chapter 96, we saw that Souko Is very good at gambling, constantly winning, always betting and getting money, she’s very good, she’s even praised by her Aunt, Tsugiko for being a very very skilled and talented gambler, a little too skilled. that she added.

But Although it does lead to the question, Tsugiko in the manga panel below my words said that “truly the blood of the Jabami family flows strong” and praised Souko for her great talent of gambling, so what exactly does the Jabami family do?

https://preview.redd.it/x8dy9pe88sbe1.jpg?width=900&format=pjpg&auto=webp&s=c72ebf4a533259b8010454e231c71452429bb24a

https://preview.redd.it/6l0kpne88sbe1.jpg?width=900&format=pjpg&auto=webp&s=9b40c77e9c3a724b25d9421bb26c59ba47ea2aae

Well to answer that question, the Business of the Jabami Family is GAMBLING. As Tsugiko now explains the Jabami family and it’s cursed bloodline which I will now be explaining.

https://preview.redd.it/tpevcxiy8sbe1.jpg?width=900&format=pjpg&auto=webp&s=265e56cd85c8c1399b927b37999af9be195681d4

Now this will all correlate to Yumeko because she is a Jabami herself. So basically, what Tsugiko explained about the Jabami‘s family cursed bloodline is there is a “deviant” child, deviant meaning departing from normal behaviour, and that “Deviant” child always being a female in the family, however this only happens in rare cases She would be born, smart, good-natured and Pretty, and will always expose herself for danger, basically what we seen in Yumeko when we watched the anime or read the manga, this is called “The Jabami Curse”

and this all started with Yumeko and Souko‘s mother, (we don’t know her name), she embodies the Jabami curse. She was Smart, Pretty, good-natured, likes risks, basically what Was described of the Jabami curse, you may be wondering, what does this have to do with Yumeko, well. You see. Tsugiko Explained that Yumeko and Souko’s mother dominated every gamble, no matter the setting, situation, no matter how smart her opponents may be, she would always come Out victorious.

Does it sound familiar? We also see this in Yumeko as well. we almost never see her loose at all. If you have watched the anime and read the manga, Yumeko in every gamble would come out as the victor. Unless she allows herself to loose or the game is rigged, such as the life and death gamble (not even Baku, Hal and Ayanokoji could beat this tbh), hence, this means the curse was passed down to Yumeko and maybe even Souko when they were born. Yumeko also was stated to have a love for gambling when she was born. (Check chapter 104 and chapter 104.5)

https://preview.redd.it/6p71guu3csbe1.jpg?width=900&format=pjpg&auto=webp&s=9e30591d8d8a298c96795e2b128b776e2060d63d

Tsugiko also mentioned about Yumeko’s Mother winnings being too abnormal which would correlate to Yumeko‘s wins as well as the curse was also passed down to her, Yumeko had some insane and abnormal feats to winning a gamble, defying all logic sometimes, for example, the tower of doors.

Yumeko was able to look up at the moon from the tower and from that view, she was able to analyse the tower of doors and how it works, she used analytical trigomometry (sorry if I spelled that wrong) calculations to find out where exactly the door of the middle tower would be based on the moon’s location, which is a very abnormal thing that the average human wouldn’t be able to do, while doing this she even countered sayaka’s logical reasoning which was one of the most logical ways to beat the tower of doors game. And bested her In doing so. Which is why it’s correct for Tsugiko To say that the victories of The Jabami family in gambles are very abnormal. They are able to adapt to any fixed situations and turn even logic against them and win against them.

and to why the jabami curse even is like that, literally buffing up a woman figure In the family, enhancing their intelligence and skills in gambling, adapting to situations, all leads to something supernatural, a god-given talent in gambling.

https://preview.redd.it/vzq9c93gesbe1.jpg?width=899&format=pjpg&auto=webp&s=b54682abba0a7ef3ed03551776f332a316904b5d

Tsugiko Explained that the reason why Yumeko’s mother and why Souko (and eventually yumeko) was so skilled in gambling and intelligence in general is because they were given a god-given talent for it. Which links to something supernatural, and in fact, the jabami curse is something supernatural Which naturally Makes yumeko a very intelligent person and very skilled gambler, Imo this is a very good narrative that would help yumeko in SCD for normal and narrative scaling. (And no this is not hax)

CONCLUSION:

so in conclusion, this sums up Yumeko’s narrative and why you will see her almost never losing in any gambling match/fixed situation, With the jabami curse, and the god-given talent for gambling, this enhances her intelligence too, which allows her to Never loose a gamble at all, and to turn situations in her favor no matter the opponent, situation and setting, this also helps her defy any logical reasoning and even strategies By coming up with her own strategy first. Imo, this narrative is really good, I would give this atleast a high tier in narrative scaling. still a kirari victim in narrative tho 😔
