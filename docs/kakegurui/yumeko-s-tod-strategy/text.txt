Round 2

After another interval, however, since Yumeko had found out that the middle door wasn’t working, she next tried to observe the outside of the tower, using the only landmark, the moon. And when she does, she figures out everything about the tower, as the moon wasn’t in the same relative location it was, as Yumeko first assumed (like Sayaka) that the outer doors were rotating, she should’ve seen the moon from the door “78”, but it wasn’t.

Yumeko assigns the x-axis to represent time and the y-axis to represent location within the tower. This means that as time progresses (moving along the x-axis), the location within the tower changes (moving along the y-axis). Yumeko had remembered the relative position of the moon in the sky in relation to the tower. The moon's position and angle can serve as a reliable indicator of time and location, especially in relation to the tower. By comparing the calculated position of the moon based on the time-location coordinates she's established with the actual observed angle of the moon, Yumeko can detect any discrepancies. If the moon's angle doesn't correspond with its expected position based on the time and location within the tower, it indicates that something is wrong.

With this, Yumeko realized that the doors weren’t rotating, but rather, the inside of the tower was, as in, they were. This meant that the moon was still in the position f0, but then that made something not make sense, why was the middle door blocked off? Yumeko tackled this by further deducing that the tower was made of 2 different towers, an inner tower that didn’t rotate, and an outer tower that did, she knew this because she realized that, while the floor doors didn’t change, their numbers when rotations happened, which indicated how many floors they lead down to, the middle door did, but because she opened it when it had “12C”, which is 300, it wasn’t going to lead anywhere. And since the middle door was rotating every turn (aka, at the beginning of each interval), on the 6th turn, it’ll be back to “5”. [Abductive Reasoning + Deductive Reasoning + Perception + Prediction + VSI + Abstract Thinking]

Yumeko: F5 (doesn’t move)
Sayaka: F4 -> F2

Round 3

Sayaka gets to the final floor, so far playing a perfect game, however, Yumeko after confirming all her hypotheses and understanding the working mechanism of the tower, decides to get down in 3 turns, so she can use the middle door right on the turn when she’s on the first floor, making her finish the tower in 4 turns, which is the least amount of moves possible from her position. However, a more important reason was for her to drop Sayaka’s guard, as she realized that Sayaka’s purely logical mind would cause her to doubt the simplicity of the game, so she might risk her 4th turn to confirm if the middle door truly doesn’t open, and it won’t, which would give Yumeko the time she needs to move. [Emotional Understanding + Logical Manipulation + Inductive Reasoning + Prediction + Tactics]

Sayaka gets to the first floor.
