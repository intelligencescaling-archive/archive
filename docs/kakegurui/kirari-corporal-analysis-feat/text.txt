During the presidential elections, Kirari was practicing their childhood connection with Ririka, but as Ririka was no longer attached to what she was before and had changed her ideals, she ended up with a failed attempt at "harmonization" with her sister. Ririka says she has no words (or also that she didn't notice the difference) and Kirari tells her that sooner or later she will be able to see the difference.

Kirari was practically able to notice even the smallest difference, this could be argued with the fact that since they were little she and Ririka practiced that connection and applied it to perfection, so much so that even Sumika (Character with great observation and lie detection skills) stated that the differences were imperceptible, so following the logic of this, the 2 sisters during that stage had to be in complete synchrony and to do so they had to eliminate even the smallest difference, differences that were controlled by both, although currently only by Kirari.

So, how can Kirari detect such differences? In order for her to achieve this she must take into account two factors, the first is Ririka's body, and the second is her own body. Based on Ririka's body, she must make multiple observations to notice the differences, such as her posture, hand placement, eye movement, blink size, mouth movement, leg placement, among others. These observations are retained in her memory at all times, plus the subsequent changes in Ririka's body (Kirari is practically constantly recalling every single detail of Ririka's physical appearance as more changes occur). But, to notice these differences, you have to have a referential image to associate these differences, so practically Kirari, in addition to retaining visual information about Ririka's body (plus constant changes), she has to visualize her own body, this based on her own perception and reasoning (especially inductive, since based on what she perceives, she has to perform multiple inductions and calculations to determine a clear image and result), which indicates that Kirari has a clear image about her body. So in short, Kirari has to be constantly retaining visual information about Ririka's body (through observations) and her own body (through her perception), this in a few seconds of difference.

In addition to this visual information, auditory information could be added, since to be completely mixed, their voices must also harmonize perfectly (tone of voice + breathing + pauses + speaking speed + speaking volume). Kirari was surely also taking this factor into account, indicating that there is a greater amount of information for her to retain and analyze.

Total information: Posture + Gestures (positioning of hands, legs, etc.) + Eye movement + Facial expression + mouth movement + Language expression (tone of voice, volume, etc.) + Kirari's body and language expression (reasonings, perceptions, analysis, detailed visual images of his body)

All this base information, in addition to new changes, is stored, analyzed and processed in practically seconds by Kirari.
