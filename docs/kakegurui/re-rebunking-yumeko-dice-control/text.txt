Re-Rebunking Yumeko Dice Control
By SoundStorm
One of the many attempts at debunking this feat was made into a debunk doc. I then rebunked this and then an attempt was made to debunk it once again. If this keeps going it will be ridiculous so if you still have questions after this then please talk to me directly on my discord, soundstorm7, and we can discuss/debate this.

The second debunk completely abandons all the points made in the first, probably because I destroyed them, so I’ll take this as an admission that all of the previous points are invalid. The second debunk focuses on the idea that dice control is too difficult to perform, because Yumeko would have to detect nanometer irregularities in the dice, air currents, temperature, and other very minute details that are impossible to detect without having powers. But if you actually read Kakegurui before trying to debunk a feat from a manga you haven’t read, you’d realise that the Dice Control skill is shown to not be 100% consistent. The only situation where these ridiculously insignificant factors have even the slightest potential of mattering is if it worked perfectly for hundreds of tries in a row - then I admit that it would be weird as to how it could be so consistent. However, while the Dice Control feat is stated to be an impressive skill, and it’s shown to get very good results, it still fails occasionally. It’s not often, but it happens.

So yeah, if the Dice Control feat was 100% consistent, then it would raise some questions. But given the consistency we’re presented with, these barely-present factors actually fit quite well into my interpretation, as they could be minor contributors to why Tsugiko and Souko messed up occasionally. And claiming that Yumeko wouldn’t have been able to grasp the physics behind Dice Control because she’s just not smart enough for it is just desperate. You even admit that if it was Junko Enoshima there, you wouldn’t doubt it, but because it’s Yumeko you automatically class it as invalid because you don’t judge her as capable enough. It’s not like that goes contrary to the narrative, Yumeko is consistently presented as an incredibly intelligent demon of a girl, with inhuman processing ability. She isn’t, however, presented as a girl with inhumane intuition. Speaking of which, you have done nothing to defend your interpretation that Yumeko used intuition against the points in my previous rebunk. Once again, I assert that using calculations remains as the only possible explanation for how she performed this feat.
