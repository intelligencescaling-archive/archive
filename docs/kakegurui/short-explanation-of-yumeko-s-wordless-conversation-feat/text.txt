Short explanation of Yumeko's best EQ feat, the wordless conversation

During the RPS Poker Tournament, Yumeko and Mary have an entire conversation without actually speaking any words, they just read each other's thoughts so well that they're able to hold a conversation

Now this would mean that they're analyzing each other's facial expressions, body language, actions etc. so deeply that they're able to form coherent "words" that the other can understand

Now obviously this might sound absurd, however communication without words is something that was already established to exist previously in the manga, the characters of Miyo Inbami and Miri Yobami are able to communicate through subtle signals between each other that only they would understand. Mary and Yumeko are both vastly superior to Miyo and Miri in terms of intellect and due to Yumeko and Mary already having known each other for some time, it is not too absurd that they would have a solid understanding of each other, which once combined with their incredible intellect, would allow them to have a conversation without words, just like Miyo and Miri

However you can upscale Yumeko's EQ even further than this. You see within the manga a large amount of characters that Yumeko gambles against (Mary included) mentions how they can't read or anticipate Yumeko's thoughts at all. So in order for this feat to not contradict the narrative it would imply that Yumeko is allowing her thoughts to be read by Mary, in other words she understands how Mary would interpret her signals and is deliberately making those signals in order to allow Mary to read her thoughts and have the wordless conversation
