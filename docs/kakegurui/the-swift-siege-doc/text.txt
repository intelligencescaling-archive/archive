Introduction

The Swift Siege plan is a very precise, concise, and calculated plan orchestrated by none other than Yua Shishiba. She is a 3rd year middle school student in Hyakkaou Private Academy, she is also the self-proclaimed little sister of Aoi Mibuomi. Yua is a very intelligent, cunning, and calculating girl. She has a talent for gambling and is overall a very skilled gambler for her age.

What prompted me to make this doc
This doc was made out of pure boredom, and at the same time, amazement. I was in utter disbelief that a very “low” tier SCD character and a mid tier in Kakegurui had a quite intricate and neatly executed plan that a Kakegurui character normally wouldn't have. I would go as far as saying that if all of its potential preparations, contingencies, and coordinated tactical/strategic approach are considered and accepted, this plan would definitely be the best planning feat in the verse, and yes, it easily slams Yumeko/Kirari in terms of overall planning. I just really wanted to analyze it (albeit the feat doesn't really scale absurdly high in planning).

Yua’s Morals, Beliefs, and Reasons
Yua Shishiba is somebody who I believe is morally grey. Yua’s affection towards Aoi Mibuomi (her big brother), is nothing short of obsessive love, as much as I want to say ew. Because of this, in Yua's eyes, Aoi is someone who's always morally right. She was influenced by his beliefs and morals, hence following his lead, similar to Ouri Shimotsukiuri, she is indifferent towards using others for her own objectives. Yua is neither good nor evil, even if she was introduced and presented as an antagonist, she has her own reasons to become antagonistic towards Mary. In fact, she wasn't really evil towards Mary at all, Yua took time to get to know Mary, to understand and see the morality behind her actions. But all she saw was rotten, unfair, and overly-disregarding behavior from a person who was supposed to be “heroic”. Believe it or not, Yua always takes measures to fully see someone for who they actually are. She might be insidious, and yes, she is, but she does take advantage of that in order to analyze her target's ideology. Whether her target is deserving of punishment or torment, that she humbly brings, is all up to her own judgement supported by tactical triggers. Throughout this whole plan, she exploits Mikoto and Shougou in various ways, they are basically just pawns that she uses in order to propel her plan in the most unique ways.

  The Preparation of the Cheat and Major Contingencies

This is the page where Yua intricately set up a cheat, but foresaw that Mary would eventually notice this cheat and try to use that against Mikoto and Shougou. The game is a simple Blackjack game and the cheat she planned to use is the SI Stebbins Stack. Where an entire 52 card deck is arranged in an order wherein the card deck could be read by using mathematical cycles. That way, it wouldn't be hard to memorize the pattern of the cards and be able to discern what numbers/suits are next. The SI Stebbins Stack goes like this:
Club -> Heart -> Spade -> Diamond (the cycle repeats). Then, 3 -> 6 -> 9 -> Queen -> 2 -> 5 -> 8 -> Jack -> Ace -> 4 -> 7 -> 10 -> King (+3 every card). In this exact page, It's narratively confirmed that Yua had already deployed the SI Stebbins order to EVERY card deck that the Literary Club has, that way, it doesn't matter which card deck they choose, and a sneaky intention; If Mary notices the cheat and goes on to check every card deck, Yua will have no choice but to admit it, BUT another tactic will be set off by Yua, which I will discuss soon in the later parts of this doc.
Yua was able to do this without resulting in any suspicion thanks to her acting skills that masked her true objective in joining as a trial member.
The ability to deploy this cheat was the main reason why it was such a definitive event to make it as a trial member for the club.

And here's where her contingency comes to play:
Through the process of perspective change, she predicts that from Mary’s perspective
   * Mary wanted and will use their cheat against them, and like Yua said, it's easy to put into words but really hard to execute, because the SI Stebbins System follows a fixed cycle, and if the cut isn't in Mary and Tsuzura's favor, or if they're not aware of it, they will have near-impossible chances of winning. Additionally, they (Yua and her friends) know exactly what the cheat is, so Yua was able to conclude that Mary and Tsuzura would have to rely on luck and the smallest opportunities of winning that Mary is very likely to notice and take advantage of.
   * So by this way of thinking and taking into account what factors (the SI Stebbins Stack order, the dealer's cut, who is winning/losing, what the next card is, Mary's realization and action) will come into play, Yua had to think of a completely plausible scenario, in which:
   1. They had to be assured of their victory.
   2. If Mary and Tsuzura weren't aware of the cheat, they will always, 100% stand, not risking anything due to the risk of their hand busting if they hit. So by this process, Tsuzura's hand had to be somewhere over 15 (>15), a hand where there's a very high likelihood that the hand would bust if the player hit.
   3. If they do hit, Mary and Tsuzura's hand will beat Mikoto and Shougou's.

And after coming up with those fundamental conditions, she finally found the only plausible combo that satisfies those three that she listed.
But it is not as easy as it seems, Yua had to figure out several situational fundamentals that will act as a groundwork for the predicted situation. It is extremely complex to the point where even somebody as prescient and adaptive as Mary couldn't foresee. Here are the main foundations and the factors that were taken into consideration for the trick, some of it are already mentioned by Yua herself, but some of it aren't:

   1. Tsuzura already lost all of her chips, resulting in a defeat for her.
   2. Mary will notice the trick (that the cards are in a certain order), as anticipated by Yua.
   3. Mary knows that Tsuzura will win if they continue the game.
   4. Mary has to urge or tell Tsuzura to continue playing, whether it's by
directly telling or using some sort of signal doesn't really matter, but let's assume that Yua predicted that Mary will use a sneaky signal in order to avoid detection. The reason for this is already stated by Mary herself in the future panels.
   5. Mary will try to persuade Mikoto and Shougou to keep playing, doubling the amount of the bet, and finally adding in the Literary Club as a bet also. One of the biggest parts of the interpretation in this doc is the fact that Yua forced Mary into a situation where she had to sacrifice potentially losing the Literary Club in order to instigate Mikoto and Shougou to keep playing. Mary did this because she was certain that Tsuzura had a clear chance of winning. This kind of thought process from Mary was instilled by Yua through these:
   * Yua used the SI Stebbins System, an order which is very predictable to players that know it. In this circumstance, Mary was led to believe that the SI Stebbins order is the only trick in the card deck. This is of course, due to her own overconfidence and tunnel vision, but Mary would've never bet the Literary Club if she wasn't sure that Tsuzura will win, and the order of the card deck was incredibly easy to follow and read. The order of the cards being this obvious, allowed Mary to see through it without a problem, and allowed her to think that Tsuzura was clearly winning.
   * Tsuzura values being a winner, that's why she challenged Mikoto and Shougou after seeing Nadeshiko lose in the first place. Mary could've just ignored Tsuzura's defeat, even if it costs 9 million yen, since they do have the means to cover that (as said by Yukimi). But one thing that Mary cannot let go of is Tsuzura, she was saddened by her loss, feeling like her mission of becoming a winner failed. Mary and Tsuzura both have mutual goals; to become a winner. And Mary simply couldn't have abandoned that feeling from Tsuzura, she had to help her. And because she knew that Tsuzura has a chance of winning if she convinced Mikoto and Shougou to another round again, there was no reason for her to not do everything she can and bet almost everything she has, hence betting the Literary Club.
   6. Once the round starts Shougou has to be in a losing position and Tsuzura has to have a hand higher than his.
   7. It is Tsuzura's term, and if she hits, she wins, if she doesn't, she will lose (because Shougou will get the card instead and Shougou relies on the next card to win). Yua was able to conclude and figure out their cards from this through simple deduction:
In the SI Stebbins  Stack, the order does not change unless it's shuffled -> In the whole cycle, the order of “8 -> Jack -> Ace -> 4 -> 7 -> 10 -> King” is the only one which satisfies the conditions, which should start with Shougou getting 8, Tsuzura getting a Jack, and so on with 7, 10, and King as the remaining cards.

After figuring out their cards, Yua switched the 7 and 10 cards, so instead of Tsuzura winning if she hits, she will weaken her position because instead of hitting and getting 7, which makes her hand: 8 + Ace (from 11 to 1) + 7. And his hand 4 + Jack (10) if he stands, and 4 + 10 + 10 if he hits. Their hands would wind up becoming like this:
Tsuzura's hand: 8 + Ace (From 11 to 1) + 10 = 19
Shougou's hand (if he hits, which he will): 4 + Jack (10) + 7 = 21
Which means that Shougou will win and Tsuzura loses, winning the 36 million yen bet and the Literary Club is officially theirs.

The Blackjack Game
Before the game, Mary asks Yua if she knows Mikoto and Shougou. Yua responds by telling a blatant lie that is backed up by the truth. She tells Mary that despite not knowing them personally, as they are from different classes, she is certain that Mikoto and Shougou are strong. The middle school of Hyakkaou Private Academy has the same Housepet System as the high school does, although not the real deal, the housepets there are treated just as horribly. Yua tells them that Mikoto and Shougou used to be housepets, but they were able to make it out because of their skills. Yua told them this because Yua knew that Tsuzura   used to be a housepet too. This fact is what she will exploit later on as a tactic to fully see Mary's beliefs and values, and to be able to judge whether she is deserving of punishment or not.

Mary goes on to tell Yua that Tsuzura used to be a housepet, and that she was able to make it out of that status too. Yua quietly responded with an unimpressed “wow”, which implies that she either already knows this fact, probably from the rumors about the Literary club, or she was bitter and didn't believe in Tsuzura at all.

Mikoto and Shougou finally arrive at the Literary Club's gambling den, which is where Yua had set up all of the tricks and traps for them to utilize. Mikoto announces the game that they will play, and it is Blackjack.

Blackjack is a simple game where two or more players have to get as close to 21 as possible with the sum of the cards in their hands. In this game, you can hit (get a random card from the deck) or stand (refuse to get anything from the deck, this suggests that you are comfortable with your hand). Then, the betting starts from there on and the   game continues until someone runs out of chips.

The game is played between Shougou and Tsuzura. Mikoto tells Yukimi to lend them any deck, this is because Yua had already set each card deck that the Literary club has into the SI Stebbins Stack, and the trap is present in every deck too, so it doesn't really matter whatever deck they choose as long as it's from the Literary Club.

Mikoto says that she's not comfortable with letting high school students deal for them in the game, because they “might” do some sort of cheating. Throughout this minor chaos of picking the dealer, Mikoto uses their stance as middle schoolers in high school students’ lair as an excuse for controlling the dealer, which cannot be argued by the Literary club since they will probably do the same if they were in their shoes.

This is the exact reason why even though Yua had to lie and put up an act to be able to join the Literary Club, she had to primarily capitalize on fixed factors throughout this whole plan too. For example, this whole Blackjack game and the intricacies set up by Yua behind it, all of it are fixed, so fixed in fact that it was almost inevitable in a way, in this case, even if Mary found out that Yua, Mikoto, and Shougou are all conspiring together, Yua still wouldn't lose anything, because the moment Tsuzura asked Mikoto and Shougou to take her on, the trap was already ignited. As you can see, most of these are all idealized concepts, but their positivity is what makes them deceiving, and effective at masking and disguising the core parts of this plan.

After a small argument happened between the two sides, Shougou decided that Kaede (a temporary replacement for Aoi, who is the Student Council Treasurer and the appointed supervisor for the trial students) will be the dealer for the game.
