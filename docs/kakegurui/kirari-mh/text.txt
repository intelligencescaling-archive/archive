The Calculations
So, to understand how much information Kirari was processing and manipulating in her memory, we must first establish the two different types of information she was processing:
1. Auditory information
Kirari is constantly processing auditory information from the game, as the characters are always speaking in the manga panels.
Considering that the game is happening in only 8 rooms at a time (There are 12 rooms and, while all the monitors are on, the game only happens at 8 rooms per turn), we will consider 8 as the value of monitors that auditory information is coming from.
Now we need to establish what is the average number of syllables spoken per second in the Japanese language (since that is the auditory info she will be processing). According to this study: [https://worldcrunch.com/tech-science/syllable-counting-and-the-secret-to-the-39speed39-of-languages] the number is 8.03 syllables per second (It can vary from study to study, but it usually is 7 to 8 syllables).
Calculating the amount of information Kirari is processing would be: 8 * 8.03, which equals 64.24 different auditory information processed per second.
2. Visual information
Kirari is keeping up with and understanding everything that’s happening in the different rooms of the Fasciation Hall. Furthermore, she is visualizing the pentagonal structure of the Hall and each one of the twelve rooms through the monitors to decide which number she should assign to each door to ensure that the game goes in the direction that she wishes.
So, Kirari is visually keeping up with everything that is happening in each room and is also interfering with the game. She is processing and keeping up with far more visual information than auditory information. Unlike the auditory one, which can be exactly calculated, visual information cannot. But assuming that we know that she is processing far more visual information than auditory, we can make estimations:
Low-ball: 2 times more – 64.24 * 2 = 128.48
Mid-ball: 3 times more – 64.24 * 3 = 192.72
High-ball: 4 times more – 64.24 * 4 = 256.96

Conclusion
Kirari is processing and manipulating in her memory: auditory + visual information + performing parallel thoughts to be able to interfere with the game.

Low-ball: 128.48 + 64.24 = 192.72 different information per second
Mid-ball: 192.72 + 64.24 = 256.96 different information per second
High-ball: 256.96 + 64.24 = 321.2 different information per second

So, Kirari is processing and mentally manipulating all that information per second while also performing parallel thoughts to be able to interfere with the game.
For this feat: CPI > VSI
