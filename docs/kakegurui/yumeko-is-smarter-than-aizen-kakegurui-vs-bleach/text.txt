Yumeko is smarter than Aizen.

When you start separating intelligence from hax, Yumeko Jabami starts looking a lot closer to Aizen than a lot of people would like to admit.

And no, I’m not saying Yumeko is literally as intelligent as Aizen in every aspect. That would be forcing the hell out of it.

Aizen has feats of long-term planning, political manipulation, strategy, science, and psychological manipulation that are far beyond what Kakegurui requires from Yumeko. Fair enough.

BUT, the point is something else: when people portray Aizen as some kind of intellectually omniscient entity, they seem to forget just how much Kyōka Suigetsu does the heavy lifting for him.

The ability literally allows him to manipulate his opponent’s senses. Sight, hearing, touch, spatial perception, and even the identification of the target themselves can be completely distorted. This means Aizen doesn’t necessarily have to outsmart someone in every aspect of perception to deceive them. He can simply make the person’s brain receive a false reality.

And that’s where comparing him to Yumeko gets funny.

Because Yumeko doesn’t have a sword that turns her opponent’s perception of reality into a lie. She has to look at the game and figure out what’s actually happening.

She can identify cheating after only a few rounds, notice extremely small details in modified cards, memorize huge amounts of information, and even detect tiny differences in weight.

She also managed to solve the mechanism of the Tower of Doors by observing the environment and realizing that the tower’s own mechanism, rather than the numbers, was the key to the game.

In other words, when Yumeko makes some absurd deduction, there isn’t a “perfect hypnosis” button that explains the result. It’s simply perception, memory, reasoning, and reading her opponent.

That’s why I find it funny when people treat every demonstration of Aizen’s intelligence as automatic proof that he possesses some infinitely superior cognitive ability compared to every character from another verse.

Is Aizen extremely intelligent? Yes.

But he’s also a character whose main ability was literally designed to turn information into an advantage. Kyōka Suigetsu allows him to control what other people believe they are seeing and perceiving. That obviously doesn’t make his feats fake, but it does mean you have to separate pure intelligence from intelligence being heavily amplified by an absolutely broken ability.

And that’s exactly where Yumeko starts looking much closer to him.

She doesn’t need to be a female “Aizen” in terms of planning decades into the future. The argument is that, in certain aspects of perception, deduction, and manipulation within a game, her feats are much more impressive than the way some people portray the character.

Aizen can put someone inside a false reality and then look like a strategic god, while Yumeko looks at a game table, watches a few rounds, thinks “something is wrong here,” and wins the game. Without any crazy magical ability that makes the job significantly easier.

Aizen is described as one of the smartest characters in anime, but he is heavily carried by hax and supernatural abilities to make his plans work.

Bro, give me his tools and I could redo his entire plan myself.

Having an ability that literally manipulates someone’s perception makes his manipulations look “super intelligent” pretty easily, doesn’t it?

When analyzing pure intelligence, he doesn’t actually have that many impressive feats.

Meanwhile, Yumeko doesn’t have a magical ability to rely on. She has to figure things out through pure intelligence, and that’s it.

I seriously doubt the “super genius” over there could pull off all of his plans without using his magical sword, which is basically a walking cheat code.

Aizen is supposedly so intelligent that most, if not all, of his plan wouldn’t have worked without such a ridiculously broken ability. If he were really the super-intelligent character people claim he is, he should be able to make all of his plans work without relying on an ability that is basically a deus ex machina that makes everything go according to plan.
