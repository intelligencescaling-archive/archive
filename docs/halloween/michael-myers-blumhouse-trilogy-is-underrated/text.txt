Michael Myers (Blumhouse trilogy.) is kinda underrated ngl.

To start off you most likely know who Michael Myers is, if you haven't seen a film you've probably heard of the guy.

Or at least know the stereotype of slasher villains. Dumb, lumbering masked (usually.) supernatural beings who don't die and chase a hot final girl?

Yeah, so Michael I'm gonna explain his basic story.

Six year old Mike kills his older sister for basically no reason, gets sent to a mental hospital for 15 years breaks out, goes on a killing spree, gets caught again, locked up for forty years, breaks out goes on another killing spree, doesn't get caught goes into hiding for four years, is old, infected and weak from being infected from untreated wounds, eventually gets killed by his "nemesis."

Alright with that out of the way let's start.

So despite being locked up in a mental asylum since he was six, Michael is a fairly good manipulator despite never saying anything.

The entire state thought he was catatonic, he didn't move or say anything and he just stared at the wall constantly, everyone fell for this until his psychiatrist Dr. Sam Loomis. Noticed 8 years later "That is when I realized, what was living behind that boy's eyes was purely and simply....evil."

They don't go into HOW Loomis could tell Mike was faking it, but everyone else didn't believe Loomis.

He mastered driving just by watching people in the back of cars when he was being transported, and is able to blend in with people around him effortlessly.

Prior to his first murder spree, Mike had made plans and also had ID's of himself (according to the novelizations.) and acquired road maps to drive his way back to Haddonfield.

Michael will also use his environment very well, he displays corpses as very crude Halloween decorations, this is for two reasons.

1: because he is a sadistic asshole who likes doing what he does.

2: to make his victims panic/frightened, because he knows what fear can do to somebody (albeit never feeling fear himself.) and he knows you are acting on emotion and not logic.

Another thing he does is play dead, a LOT.

Michael will either pretend to be incapacitated and can hold and control his breathing very well (in Halloween Kills we see this when Karen stabs him in the back and stomped on his skull.) Michael pretended to play dead until she grabbed his mask.

For some odd reason, Michael gets war flashbacks when you touch that thing, there's many theories but I personally think he considers his mask his "face" in a way, and he hates being seen as human.

Why he will go out of his way just to get it back, or even let somebody get away just to put it back on. Ig you can't kill without looking like bleached Bill Shatner.

Michael has also shown to plan ahead quite often, like cutting the phone and power lines in the end of the first film, using a rake to block the door so Laurie couldn't get out, and using the bodies of his victims as decorations.

It is heavily implied Michael has some sort of influence on the mentally ill, some kind of...I don't want to say mind control but I want to say mind control. **Infecting** is what I i would say.

Now for Michael's battle IQ.

Michael while being able to tank things most human beings would die from, he knows how to fight and parry decently well. He parried attacks from trained firefighters with a tool he had never used before.

Michael also is aware of the limits of his immortality, he can tank beatings and gunshots pretty well but he is still human, obviously there's something inhuman about him but he is still human.

In Halloween Kills when he breaks into the old couples house, we can see him in the bathroom bandaging up his damaged hand and taking what looks like some kind of pain killers? There were pill bottles so I assumed that.

And he will lock the fuck in when there's a gun in the situation.
