Tbags sona escape strategy

I guess we will see eachother again

it must be said before anything else this is the first time im breaking something down so if it seems pretty wtv then thats why i just wanted to give tbag some praise since i never see his strategies getting any

Phase 1: tbags first move in sona is social integration, hes working off of the assumption that michael and co arent in the prison so he takes advantage of his lack of reputation and puts on his "hillbilly" act, this functions as a double bluff to lechero he plays the cunning fool for lechero debasing himself but showing clear intentions of wanting to integrate into the social power structure of the prison, lechero them believes hes seen through bagwell giving him the perceived upper hand in the situation which causes him to let his guard down and bring bagwell in

Phase 2: with the additional of michael and co the plan changes and ill outline that later on, right now with michael, mahone and bellick in the prison he knows michael is going to be planning an escape and wants to get in on it but he also doesnt abandon his own plan. He leverages his newly found position in the prison hierarchy to box michael out from lechero leaving him as michaels only in to the resources lechero has access to, as well as this he uses the social positioning of bellick to use him as a pawn in the most literal sense, he uses bellick as a potential resources should he ever need to use him

Phase 3: him boxing michael out works in part as michael uses bagwells reputation as leverage to access lecheros resources, michael and lechero are now both blind to bagwells intentions and he plays them off eachother to complete his next goal which is disrupting the internal hierarchy of lecheros group. He uses the fact that michael is using him to access these resources under lecheros nose but makes plays sammys suspicions of him off lechero, this discredits sammys perception to lechero who believes he has bagwell figured but there is still obviously shady stuff going on with lecheros resources and thus lechero tries in a very one dimensional way to manipulate bagwell which is exactly what he wants, he makes lechero believe the idea to have bagwell as his "eyes and ears" is his and coupled with him discrediting sammys perception in lecheros eyes hes planted the seeds of division in both of them.

Phase 4: Tbag now wants to establish some sort of foundation for accessing lecheros outside resources, he understands that there is some chain of relation between lechero and his resources on the outside world, whoever is supplying him with all his amenities isnt doing it directly this must mean this is all dependent on some sort of intermediary, there was multiple intermediaries but that doesnt matter at this point, he identifies the girl that comes to visit lechero for sex as a weak link in this chain and seduces her playing off lecheros neglect and disrespect of her, now he has a foot in the door to accessing lecheros outside resources and connections sort of like how people say the front desk workers really run the hospital, this is his desk worker

Phase 5: his aim now is to solidify his position within the hierarchy of the prison, a common thing tbag does when placed in a vunerable position is inserting himself into some sort of important function to turn himself into a necessity, like with him eating the map or getting in on the fox river escape. This time is no different he continues to play lecheros group off of eachother while positioning himself near the prisons drug dealer, he learns the trade under the guise of being at the lowest level of lecheros inner circle then creates the vacancy in the position by killing him, this serves 2 purposes. Firstly hes able to guarantee he'll fill this vacancy due to him already establishing lecheros perception of him underestimating how cunning he can be, secondly hes pushing sammy further away from the group and lechero due to sammys lack of self management and mistrust of tbag, him guaranteeing himself this role leaves him basically immune from sammy for now

Phase 6: now his objective is to get rid of sammy, while sammy is more isolated from the inner circle and has lost credibility to lechero they are still long time friends and tbag will not will with just social violence, he uses michaels dependency on him to gather information on whos in on michaels plan, he identifies mahone and its pretty easy to tell hes a drug addict which is perfect, he then starts trying to make mahone dependant on his supply in hopes of using him to kill sammy with the chicken foot however mahone pretty easily sees through this so he pivots to bellick

Phase 7: at this point hes identified bellick as the "outsider" of the in group and starts working two jobs at once, he becomes closer with bellick to establish a link between himself and the group as michael depends on him less and less as an intermediary and he knows michaels moral character and that he wont just abandon bellick at this point in the plan without a reason, at the same time hes trying to plant the seeds of social division between sammy and lechero, he kind of holds this position for a while cause either A he inserts himself into michaels plan or B he collapses the social bonds within lecheros group.

Phase 8: michael gets caught at the window lecheros power becomes unstable and due to sammy being isolated more and more from the inner circle he pulls a coup and takes power, tbag shifts his focus to getting rid of sammy and as he had maintained bellick as "material" he uses his new found notoriety in the prison to manipulate bellick into getting rid of sammy while this doesn't work he still maintains his relationship with lechero throughout this entire coup which serves as his knew "in" to michaels plan so he pivots back to michael.

Final phase: at this point sammy dies, him bellick lechero get manipulated by michael and he rats out sucre getting him into the prison. This is where his initial end game comes in, his end game for breaking the inner circle was to make lechero dependent on him and thus hold power over him and use the social power lechero holds in the prison to cause a riot and subsequent escape, lechero is broken down and finally realises tbag was manipulting him the whole time and he kills him, he uses the vacuum in power and order in the prison to focus the disorder outward causing a riot leading to the escape of everyone. The initial plan of escape was to leverage the political power lechero has within the prison against the guards, before tbag even got to the prison its understood that sona is structured the way it is due to a former prison riot but its evident inside the prison that those guards are only to deter potential escapes, the real order in the prison comes from lechero. Lechero like any good leader maintains his power by dividing and depriving those he governs he hoards resources and creates systems of violence to uphold that, as long as the prison stays this way there is no threat of a riot, the only real time where a riot can be deterred is at night when the differences in gun power are too grand to overcome, through his proximity to michaels plan tbag understands that the only way out is during the day and thus he immediately gets to leading a day time riot knowing the guards dont have the resources to push back resulting in his escape.
