Raid on Sloth-

Ragebait feats

Anyhow, the (first) raid on Sloth can be broken down rather easily.

Step 1: After finding the general location of the Witch Cultists (using past experiences), Subaru acts as bait to attract Witch Cultists. This is done due to the Witch’s Miasma present on him which is like a bright beacon flaring up in the night sky. Subaru attributes it to finding “-a cockroach in the corner of a quiet room. Perfect cockroach bait.” He then immediately explains that he means to meet ‘their boss’ or Petelgeuse.

Step 2: He finds Petelgeuse and has a dialogue. His main goal was to stall out Petelgeuse and gain the element of surprise. Additionally, after gaining info on Petelgeuse, the ‘gospel’ and observing his reactions he infers that such a book is of incredible importance. The man he is with is mentally deranged and an obsessed lunatic likely prone to provocation.

Step 3: He then provokes Petelgeuse by saying he threw his ‘gospel’ out, and dodges his Unseen Hand. This frustrates Petelgeuse to an unimaginable degree while lowering his guard.

Step 4: He has Mimi and Tivey attack the entrance of the cave, collapsing it and the other Witch Cultists within the area. This reduces the amount of targets and variables within the fight, while also removing an escape option for Petelgeuse (which will be important later). This also kills one of Petelgeuse’s fingers. (Which will be explained soon)

Step 5: While Petelgeuse is rambling like a madman, Subaru interjects into his speech and engineers a state of confusion. This alongside the elimination of several of Petelgeuse's allies, the shock of his Authority being visible and Subaru’s seemingly unfaithful and loveless actions causes him to overlook the nail in the coffin.

Step 6: From behind, Wilhelm jumps down striking Petelgeuse, this is done at (essentially) the same time as step 5 and gives Wilhelm the element of surprise, operating from a covert location before being given ‘the signal’.

Now, Petelgeuse is dead (or at least the main body is). Despite this, Subaru asks for confirmation from Felix (the greatest healer in Luganica) and even checks again with Mimi using magic to essentially explode (possibly vaporize) the body.

(Planning, Strategy, Tactics, Counteraction, Emotional Manipulation, Direct Manipulation, FRI, Deductive Reasoning, Crystallized Intelligence)

What/Who are Petelgeuse’s fingers?

Petelgeuse’s fingers are subordinates of Petelgeuse. Petelgeuse is capable of transferring his ‘soul’ into them, acting as essentially another layer of immortality.

There are 10 fingers in total. However it is to be noted that we don’t know how much more special they are compared to a regular subordinate. We can assume they are more compatible with Sloth or some other X-factor that makes them distinguishable from standard Witch Cultists.

Note: It is possible there are downsides to inhibiting different bodies like a possible loss of some memories (as the possessed fingers are seen to be shocked when Subaru dodges the ‘Unseen Hand’ when Petelgeuse already knows he can see them)
