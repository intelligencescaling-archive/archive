Subaru, even at the beginning of the story had been experiencing death over and over again for “ten or so days” and had adapted remarkably quickly to this given his very casual attitude of going out to attempt to save Emilia again. (Adversity Capacity:Mental Fortitude, Stress Tolerance and Perseverance + Adaptability: Cognitive and Environmental + EQ: Management)

(Foresight: Prediction + Field Skills: Trap Setting, Risk Calculation and Information Gathering + Intelligence: Spatial)

Has fought the three criminals 88 times, something he has apparently been keeping track of mentally ever since they first met. He even already considered himself a “veteran” in fighting with them specifically, which he proved by going on to quickly kill all three of them using a stick he had picked up a few minutes prior. Guessed based on Elsa’s appearance, sadistic tendencies and inclination towards blood and gore that she’s likely a vampire. (Reasoning: Abductive)

He was also slightly implied to have somehow done this anonymously and he positioned himself up in a ward which he knew would be a good place to oversee the fight.
