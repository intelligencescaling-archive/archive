Introducing: Ben 10.

In this post, I plan to list some of the feats of Ben K. Tennyson, wielder of the Omnitrix and the hero who saved the universe. He’s a character whose feats in many categories **can’t be logically explained** because he operates beyond us (i.e., on a narrative scale), so I won’t go into unnecessary explanations. Also, it's important to mention that I'll be scaling Ben based on his feats both as a human and in his various alien forms.

Of course, there are more feats than the ones I've mentioned, and they probably span more subcategories, but this post is just an introduction, so I'm not going to list them all. Without further ado, I hope you enjoy the post.

**General Intelligence (GI):**

* Gs, Gwm: In *"The Vengers"* (Omniverse, season 6, episode 3), Brainstorm quickly observes and analyzes the Vengers and their entire environment, retaining complete information about each of them in his mind and using it to his advantage to devise a scheme against them. The cerebrocrustacean also claims to have an IQ of 1×10\^32.
* Gv, Gwm: In *"A Small Problem"* (Original Series, season 1, episode 11), Grey Matter needs to know where he is, looks out the window, and says there's a mountain peak 13.2 miles to the west, with a vertical angle of 45°, which requires an insane mental ability for spatial reasoning.
* Gf: Basically any episode in which Brainstorm or Grey Matter appears. In *"Rad"* (Omniverse, season 7, episode 3), the Galvan was immediately able to understand the malfunction in his ship's warp engine, the Lovely Duck, even though Ben practically knows nothing about science. He was also able to repair that extremely complex technological system with great ease, evidencing a massive level of pattern abstraction and sequential induction to construct a novel solution.
* Gc, Ga: In the same episode mentioned above, Grey Matter was able to decipher the Pyxi language on the ship's monitor, a language he had never heard before. This demonstrates an aptitude for learning verbal structures, grammatical sensitivity, and a vocabulary repertoire for establishing comparisons, as well as the ability to discriminate sounds, phonemes, and accents never previously perceived.

**Emotional Intelligence (EI):**

* EP: In "*A Knight to Remember"* (Ultimate Alien, season 3, episode 22), Ben hears Driscoll's story about George, the first Forever Knight who lived in the Middle Ages, and concludes that he is still alive. Kevin, who is very perceptive, doesn't understand how he knew; and Ben explains that he noticed it from the emotionality of his account, since Driscoll spoke as if he knew him personally and they had a familiarity, even though there were no markers in his narration that indicated it.
* EC: In *"Paradox"* (Alien Force, season 1, episode 12), Ben infers that the interdimensional beast from Los Soledad is Hugo, the assistant from the 1952 experiment. He manages this by noticing that the creature had a route that went past the phone booth, the police station, and the dormitories, relating it to what a normal person would do if they were lost in a military facility and felt scared, understanding that his feeling of being alone and helpless would become the driver of an anxious search throughout the entire place.
* EF: In *"And There Were None"* (Omniverse, season 5, episode 9), Ben adopts a state of concentrated calm and active resignation, using the feeling to boost his intuition and hand the Omnitrix over to the Ben without a watch, inspiring in him the determination to reboot the multiverse.
* ER: In *"Perfect Day"* (Original Series, season 4, episode 1), Ben, a 10-year-old boy, breaks an artificial induction of hedonism and comfort designed to hijack his dopaminergic reward system, voluntarily detaching himself from a system of perfect euphoria, regulating himself so as not to invalidate his autonomy and moral values.

**Adversity Quontient (AQ):**

* Origin, Ownership: In *"Showdown: Part 2"* (Omniverse, season 2, episode 10), Ben overcomes the childhood trauma he suffered when he lost his favorite alien, Feedback, which also took part of his very being (literally). He makes peace with his past self and takes it upon himself to forgive himself and resolve the current adverse situation.
* Reach, Endurance, Control: In *"The Final Battle: Part 2",* Ben was defeated by Vilgax and had to hand over the Omnitrix, feeling completely without options against the conqueror's army. However, he recovers from this and devises a strategy to turn the situation around, taking control of it and not letting desolation leave him static without fighting.

**Manipulation:**

* Direct, emotional, logical: In *"Alone Together"* (Alien Force, season 1, episode 14), within 24 hours and despite being hating by him because of their past history, Ben manages to turn an extreme speciesist and supremacist like Reinrassig III—a Highbreed who from birth was indoctrinated with ideas of racial superiority—into someone who sees humans and all species as equals, questioning his beliefs and inspiring feelings of equality in him.

**Deception:**

* Misdirection, fabrication, acting skills and concealment: In *"Of Predators and Prey: Part 1"* (Omniverse, season 1, episode 9), Ben, knowing that Khyber is watching him, pretends to be desperate to find him, going with Rook to Undertown to look for him. Rook harasses a citizen supposedly checking that he isn't the hunter's pet. This provokes a fake argument with Ben, who claims to disagree with his police methods and with what they should do. Throughout this entirely public argument, he acts furious and even threatens to start a fight, breaking his working relationship with Rook. Blonko leaves, leaving him alone and crushing with his truck a soda can that Ben had made look like his Plumber badge (a communicator), something Rook reinforces with his words. Thus, with this setup, he makes Khyber—who doesn't know that Ben has already deduced his existence—believe that he is partnerless, paranoid, and out of contact, setting a trap for him and waiting for him to catch him. In reality, Ben keeps the real badge in a pocket, waiting for Khyber to capture him and take him to his base of operations.
* Acting skills, misdirection, fabrication: In *"Birds of a Feather"* (Alien Force, season 1, episode 19), Ben plans to deceive Simian by acting as if he doesn't know that he is lying to him about being the prince of Aracna and about his true intentions. When Simian is knocked out by the crystal's security system, Ben takes an ordinary box and puts a DNAlien resin cleaner inside it to give the impression that he actually has the crystal inside (due to its weight and size). The moment Simian wakes up, he argues that he stored the crystal in that box because it could be radioactive. After that, Ben forces a fight to make Simian believe that he is just now discovering his lie, forcing him to escape quickly without confirming the contents of the box, thinking he had gotten away with it.

**Scheming:**

* Structure, integration, effectiveness, versatility, adaptability: During *"Ben Again"* (Omniverse, season 3, episode 5), Ben swapped minds with his 11-year-old self, but he has to devise a plan to prevent Eon from stealing the Chrononavigator from his time, and he can't time travel without severely altering the timeline, so he's trapped in the past. Since Eon is capable of traveling through time, he designs a treasure map that only he, at 10 years old (in his 16-year-old body), could solve to find the Chrononavigator. The plan consists of systematically leaving a set of clues in various locations that he knows won't be destroyed or affected over the next 5 years, guaranteeing that the trail isn't lost. The clues are objects that only he as a kid would give importance to, catching his attention to start the search. To make sure, he asks Gwen to go find him exactly 5 years in the future, no matter where he is, and take him to Mr. Smoothy, the starting point, which is also a location he goes to regularly. He also tells her not to talk about it with anyone until the right moment, guaranteeing there are no inconsistencies in the chronology. There he leaves his favorite Sumo Slammers sticker on a post, anticipating that the 11-year-old Ben would see it and Rook would investigate the post, finding that coordinates and the name "Armodrillo" are inscribed at the top. Thus the search begins; at each location there's another clue that only Ben can access through his alien forms, such as Goop, who must look inside a faucet, NRG in a nuclear reactor, and Wildmutt, sniffing and digging underground. This way, even if Eon discovers his strategy, he could never follow it, since he ignores the starting point and can't transform into those aliens. Finally they arrive back at Mr. Smoothy, since, as another safeguard of the plan, Ben hid the Chrononavigator at the start of the treasure map, exploiting a psychological blind spot. But in addition, Ben chose Mr. Smoothy because he was aware that Eon had moved through time with Professor Paradox, but not through space, so when Eon arrives in the present and takes the Chrononavigator, his misuse of the device opens cracks in space-time that allow both Bens to communicate with Professor Paradox (at the end of time, still in the same place) and organize together to defeat Eon, thus returning the consciousnesses of both Bens to their own moment in time.

**Foresight:**

* Logical prediction: Also in *"The Vengers",* Ben, transformed into Brainstorm, calculates in seconds all the exact movements of each of the Vengers during their fight, including the order of attack, the form, the timing, and the type. Then, based on their psychological profiles, he predicts that if Ben steps aside, they will no longer have a common goal, and their egos, arrogance, personal differences, and constant pursuit of media attention would start clashing with each other, which would be aggravated by their workload as heroes, inevitably leading to their self-destruction as a team within hours.
* Anticipation: In *"Above and Beyond"* (Alien Force, season 2, episode 15), Ben plans to deceive the Plumbers' Helpers, simulating that he has gone insane and is trying to eliminate Grandpa Max. Ben foresees each of the team's actions, anticipating that at first they will all attack separately, knowing who, where, and how, as well as the outcome of each battle. Later, he declares that he is three steps ahead the whole time, which is demonstrated by his constant traps prepared even before the kids act.
* Anticipation: In *"The Color of Monkey"* (Omniverse, season 7, episode 7), Ben foresees that Argit must be involved in illicit activities and infers that it's about the sale of a Tiffin that was believed lost, since it was stolen. Ben knows that Tiffins are tasty but very expensive, so Argit would have to sell it to a buyer from Argistix Security. Upon observing that Simian works with him, his suspicions grow even more and he connects the dots, predicting with accuracy almost half of the episode: that Simian would betray Argit to keep the payment for the Tiffin, that he would hire Rojo to steal it, and that Argit was going to pin all the responsibility for the corruption in his company on Simian. This is why, when he was at the Plumbers' headquarters, before everything started, he kept an ordinary Argistix Security container, foreseeing that Argit would store the Tiffin in an identical box so as not to draw attention among the products; afterwards, he swaps the containers behind his Tenn-Speed and puts an artificial edible inside it so that Argit thinks he has recovered the Tiffin.

Many of the feats mentioned here **overlap across various categories and subcategories**, as I already mentioned. For example, Ben's plan also involves indirect manipulation and misdirection, his AQ feats imply powerful emotional regulation, and his foresight ability is also intertwined with his developed cognitive and affective theory of mind. As I said, I haven't named all the feats I know of, nor in all categories, but rather in some of the most important ones, so the post could have a continuation or could go deeper into some of the feats already mentioned.

Overall, Ben is regularly established as someone **extremely cunning and insightful**, although, like any young person his age, he does make mistakes and at times, due to his ego or other personality traits, may even seem foolish. However, in the series where he shows the most maturity (such as Alien Force during the first two seasons), he achieves more than impressive feats. In my opinion, he can be considered superior to characters like Light Yagami and L Lawliet from Death Note, Shuntarō Chishiya and Arisu Ryōhei from Alice in Borderland, many characters from Moriarty the Patriot such as Charles Augustus Milverton and even Sherlock Holmes, etc. That said, I hope you enjoyed the post, until next time!
