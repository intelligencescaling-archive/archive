OVERVIEW

BACKGROUND INFO: Frederick Charles Krueger (or Freddy Krueger) is the main antagonist of the Nightmare on Elm Street (NOES) film series, although he appears in several different mediums. Freddy, in lore, is a serial killer known as the “Springwood Slasher”. Freddy would target and kill children, until he was taken into custody. Freddy evaded prosecution due to an error in the defense, so he walked free. The parents of Springwood, upon seeing this, took matters into their own hands. The parents tracked Freddy down and burned him alive, killing him. However, Freddy made a deal with the dream demons and he was granted immortality and dream powers.
