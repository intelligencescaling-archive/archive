Herta feat list

Herta intelligence feats:

**“I'm already perfect, so what else should I do?” — Herta controlling one of her puppets**.

In Silver Wolf’s companion quest, during her battle against SW, she not only predicted every trick and trap that SW would set up and effortlessly navigated around them, but also devised a ploy to deceive SW into believing she was the one doing the deceiving. In reality, she systematically neutralized every remaining option SW had, then stripped her of the satisfaction of continuing the battle by reverse-hacking her devices, locating all 76 of her Interastral Network accounts, and freezing them simultaneously. This feat showcases \[ST (Strategic Thinking) EU (Emotional Understanding) EP (Emotional Perception) PM (Psychological Manipulation) IDM (Indirect Manipulation) Foresight and CT (Creative Thinking)\].

She co-created the Simulated Universe, as mentioned in Screwllum’s dialogue with Herta when he attempts to shut it down. We know what she specifically contributed to — alongside him, she spent hundreds of system hours recreating all the resources of an entire planet and developing the most advanced technology in the universe for the simulation. Together, they also defined the concept of the Simulated Universe as a miniature world designed to discover traces of the Aeons. This feat showcases \[VSI (Visual Spatial Index) CT (Creative Thinking) and ST (Strategic Thinking)\].

She managed to understand Nous’s true intentions and philosophy on such an intricate level that she was the only one to be gazed upon by Nous twice. This feat showcases \[EU (Emotional Understanding) and EP (Emotional Perception)\].

 She solved the solitary waves theory in her childhood through reasoning: If the unstable index < constant → universe = stable and predetermined If the unstable index > constant → universe = chaotic and unpredictable then realising that the constant itself changes depending on the interference of different paths; it can never be fully solved or pinned down. The fourth Cosmic Conundrum is, in short, a paradox: the universe does not follow a single fixed equation because it is path-dependent. This feat showcases \[LR (Logical Reasoning) WMI (Working Memory Index) and LGT (Logical Thinking)\].

She is able to recall every person who has met her twice sorting out Caelus/Stelle as the 345th person in the cosmos who’s been able to meet her twice. This feat showcases \[LTM (Long Term Memory) SA (Self Awareness) and SOA (Social Awareness)\].

She created thousands of ball-jointed puppets scattered throughout the universe, all connected to her mind, allowing her to control whichever puppet she chooses and exert universal influence without ever needing to be physically present. This feat showcases \[ST (Strategic Thinking) VSI (Visual Spatial Index) and CT (Creative Thinking)\].

In the Simulated Universe, during an event where Nous’ presence merges with the replay of the Borderstar Trade War, the members of the Genius Society are “excruciatingly eviscerated,” enduring unbearable pain and complete dismemberment. Yet moments later, as everyone is reloaded elsewhere, she remains unfazed, saying, “It’s not real anyway. It’s just data being dissipated—we just have to gather it again. War doesn’t care about individual deaths. It’s normal that we’ve all died once. Ha, but we can cheat death in the Simulated Universe! How useful is that?” This reveals that the agony they experienced was indistinguishable from true death itself. This feat showcases \[MF (Mental Fortitude) ST (Stress Tolerance) EM (Emotional Management) and AC (Adversity Capacity)\].

In the Simulated Universe, she consistently demonstrates the ability to predict the behavior of Aeons, using this insight to lure and ultimately capture them with precise efficiency. This feat showcases \[EU (Emotional Understanding) EP (Emotional Perception) PM (Psychological Manipulation) IDM (Indirect Manipulation) and Foresight\].

She can identify the serial numbers of each of her thousands of ball-jointed puppets just by quickly glancing at them from afar. This feat showcases \[Sensory\].

She evolved the Herta Space Station into a major scientific research hub it was originally a gifted museum built by the IPC. This feat showcases \[ST (Strategic Thinking) and CT (Creative Thinking)\].

She’s able to recall there are 249 of her ball-jointed puppets in service on the Space Station and another 32 as backups. This feat showcases \[LTM (Long Term Memory) and SOA (Social Awareness)\].
