DIO Lost His Final Fight. Not Because Of Confidence, But Because Of Cautiousness.

Everyone, including Non-JJBA fans have heard it before. “DIO could have won against the Crusaders if he just had played it smart and wasn't being an idiot” And I won't disagree, he could have absolutely won against them, *just not during the final fight*. This might be a controversial take simply due to how hardwired we have all heard that DIO was arrogant and how he played around with his food too much. And while yes, he was absolutely arrogant and overestimated himself, this did not impact his decisions against Jotaro and the fellow Crusaders in fact you could argue that DIO's downfall was not because overconfidence, but rather how **safe** he decided to fight against them.

So lets start by dissecting this point by point. Let's ignore the fact that we know how the fight turned out and let’s analyze how the fight developed ever since [Polnareff encountered him on the stairs.](https://youtu.be/c3lB9NLwhFw?si=jIQdAWsgqQ21UOBo) We are doing this not only because it gives us a chance to take a closer look at DIO's decisions but also because if DIO knew before hand how the fight would turn out since the beginning, the only change in his strategy he would do to win the confrontation is stop time [when he first encountered Jotaro](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/jjba/images/d/d4/TW_SP_clash.png/revision/latest?cb=20150605172739) right after he told him "I need to get closer to beat you up", that's it.

There are three major points where the majority of the people would agree that DIO could have won had he not played around and that's during the coffin scene, when Jotaro first encountered him, and the Road Roller. As an extra we can talk about Polnareff stairs scene because had the Crusaders not appeared Polnareff would have just died, DIO's intent in that scene was to get a last minute minion to do his bidding which unfortunately didn't work, but realistically would have been worth the try. However I mention this not only to explain why DIO did this but because it gives us an insight on what DIO's mindset is in this scene. We had been repeated over and over through the series [that DIO is afraid of the Joestars, he is afraid of them yet seeks to overcome them to finally be without fear.](https://ibb.co/yqW4GJp) The reason he's sending minions is precisely because he doesn't want to face them directly, he knows first hand how tenacious the bloodline is and how they would fight to their last breath to take him down. He's afraid of them and wants to minimize their interactions with them to the minimum.

[Starting with the coffin scene](https://youtu.be/1NuB-Icu_kg?si=KxolSioB9KIUIevu), while it is true that he could have killed a couple Crusaders, he would have needed to quickly decide which one would be the best target, kill said target in about 1-2 seconds of timestop and then get out of dodge in the next 3. Not possible considering that it’s still day so he can’t run and staying leaves him open to a quick retaliation of 3 or 2 angered crusaders, who even if they had not succeeded they would likely run away now with a huge clue of what DIO's power really is. As we can see, at best the same thing happens just like in the series, with the crusaders scattering, at worse they manage to get a clean hit on DIO and puts him at risk, he has no blood supply in his mansion so injuries there are lethal. Compared to what he did with Nukesaku, he minimizes the risk of facing them and still makes them scatter and manages to intimidate them all. Yes he didn't kill any of them but he managed to split them in pairs, which in turn made them easier to kill off.

And the repercussions of this decision were good for DIO as well. Separated, the crusaders were just fodder. [He killed Kakyoin in one shot](https://youtu.be/ZDgS3ho2WlE?si=raeG0kWPKSVMomjR), [he killed Joseph with one knife.](https://youtu.be/o49oS5grMlo?si=LiFQvOEeQTg79GtW) In fact as you can see here he was getting more and more confident as he killed them, after all now he only had to worry about Jotaro and Polnareff. In fact, he could have killed Jotaro as soon as he stopped time... [except he moved.](https://youtu.be/eFrZiTcOMAI?si=gi__RRdjRvmUsvo8) This is the part of the strategy where everything falls apart for DIO. Before diving into this section deeper, we have to remember that up to this point DIO had no way of even deducing the idea of Jotaro having time stop, because honestly, not even the viewers could have predicted that. Jotaro moving in time stop caused DIO's strategy to completely change. Before Jotaro moved his finger, DIO was indeed confident and arrogant, he indulged Jotaro in a fist cuff exchange just to crush him, but as soon as Jotaro managed to land a good punch on him he decided that it was enough of that and stopped time to kill him.

This turned his arrogance into terror. Jotaro moved, *no he could also stop time*, and DIO knew that this was a great threat to him. Now his ace in the hole wasn't an ace any longer, at least not one that only he could pull. Is no coincidence that DIO started to throw knives to Jotaro after he landed that time stop punch. DIO at this point of the fight was doing his absolute best to avoid a direct confrontation with Jotaro because he no longer was confident he could win that way.
[Now beyond that the knives worked!](https://youtu.be/UkFbq8qdE14?si=ksMVYDjS60V7XlFM) He took down Jotaro and even managed to get one on his skull, or at least that's what he thought. Sure he could have thrown some knives to Jotaro to secure the kill [but he even goes one step above and gets a cop to shoot at him. DIO is not taking any risks against Jotaro, and it shows. Jotaro wouldn't have needed to stop his heart if DIO was being arrogant.](https://youtu.be/L7rhPCMSxYM?si=vtZaNkDFmqHm6a7S)
Finally, we can analyze the Blood and Road Roller scene. [For years I thought that DIO lost the moment he absorbed Joseph's blood, this was the moment where his arrogance rose to its zenith](https://youtu.be/uuBrrWuMG80?si=BmzHQtnysRVgzTZL). But let's take a look at what he does. [DIO charges at Jotaro, tests his regeneration and proceeds to break one of Jotaro's arms, his clavicle and his ribs and then kicks him kilometers away into a bridge.](https://youtu.be/KORUZndUQM4?si=y7zQe2cQaL1WXc_z) At this point is a miracle that Jotaro is conscious, and yet not satisfied with this, DIO goes out to bring a roadroller to crush him. Now a lot of people may ask themselves: "Why a Road Roller, why not just go there and crush his skull" Because DIO knew that the moment he got closer to Jotaro he would have lost the confrontation! Remember how DIO tested his regeneration? He got in a direct confrontation against Jotaro and lost! He knows this is not the way to win. Now why the Road Roller? If you see how DIO uses it is to crush Jotaro against the bridge, he basically forces Jotaro to use his time stop not to fight against DIO but to defend himself from the huge vehicle. Not only that, but DIO is placed at a safe distance where Jotaro couldn't have manifested Star Platinum to try and give him a quick one shot. With this information in mind, a road roller makes perfect sense, strategy wise, to both kill Jotaro and not risk getting killed.
