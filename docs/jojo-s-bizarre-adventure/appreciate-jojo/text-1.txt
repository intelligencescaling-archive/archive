PART 2 OF APPRECIATE JOJO!

Joseph Joestar (Part 2).

Joseph vs Esidisi feat:

Brief Summary of the Situation:

A Pillar Man named Esidisi comes in search of the Red Stone. Upon arriving, he encounters Joseph’s instructor. By the time Joseph arrives, the instructor has already been killed by the Pillar Man.

At first, Joseph approaches Esidisi. After a bit of conversation, Esidisi tries to swipe at Joseph with his hand to kill him, but Joseph manages to block it using just one finger — even piercing through Esidisi’s hand. This surprises Esidisi, since Joseph had only been training with Hamon for three weeks, yet was already that strong.

However, Esidisi then twists Joseph’s arm, preventing him from using Hamon. But Joseph jumps up and wraps a string around Esidisi’s arm — a string he had previously wound around the arm of his now-dead instructor while Esidisi was distracted and not paying attention.

=> Setting Traps, Creativity, Tactics, Deception

Scale Joseph 2:

After having his arm cut off, Esidisi suddenly starts crying like a child, which surprises Joseph — he had expected Esidisi to be furious. After a short while of crying, Esidisi suddenly stops and explains that he cries when he's angry because it helps him clear his mind.

Then, Esidisi walks over to the corpse of Joseph’s instructor and cuts off the instructor’s arm to use as his own replacement arm, which terrifies Joseph. When Joseph looks back at the body, he notices it’s simmering. Esidisi explains that the reason the body is heating is because he can control the temperature of his own blood, and he has put some of his blood in it.

Joseph then throws his Clackers at Esidisi’s arm, but Esidisi dodges it by detaching his own arm (Pillar Men can tear off any part of their body and regenerate quickly). Esidisi then tries to attack Joseph using his veins (his power is manipulating his blood vessels and raising his blood’s temperature), but Joseph blocks them using a metal mask.

However, some of the blood still touches Joseph, forcing him to discard the mask. Joseph then tries to distract Esidisi by talking nonsense, but Esidisi sees through the ruse. Even so, Joseph had already come up with a plan — while pretending to dodge Esidisi’s attacks, he was secretly weaving a web of string across the sharp spikes without being noticed. When Esidisi steps into the trap’s range, Joseph will wrap the thread around Esidisi and finish him off using Hamon.

=>PSI, VSI, Formulation Speed, Adaptability, Deception

Scale Joseph 3:After Joseph came up with his plan, Esidisi suddenly started laughing and impaled himself onto several sharp spikes. His goal was to release more of his blood vessels to use in his attack. Then, Esidisi jumped up and attacked Joseph with his blood vessels, but Joseph dodged.

Suddenly, the thread from Joseph’s hat started getting pulled out. Esidisi was surprised — he had fallen into Joseph’s trap. At that moment, Joseph thought he had definitely won. But in reality, Esidisi had figured out Joseph’s plan earlier and had wrapped his blood vessels around the spikes beforehand to cut Joseph’s threads.

Even so, Joseph remained calm because Esidisi had actually been tricked. Joseph had used a sleight-of-hand trick so that the threads would still work even if they were cut — he had secretly tied them back together.

=> Flexibility Strategy, Contingency, Strategic Deception, EQM.

Bonus.

Restaurant feat:

Brief Summary: Joseph, Smokey (a Black kid Joseph recently befriended), and Erina (Joseph’s grandmother) were eating at a restaurant. Suddenly, a nearby customer insulted Smokey in front of many other patrons, which made Erina and Joseph furious.

Joseph stood up, ready to beat the guy up. The customer stood up too and reached into his jacket pocket, trying to grab brass knuckles. Then Joseph said: “Hey mister, if you’re looking for your brass knuckles, they aren’t in your jacket pocket! They’re in the back pocket of your pants!”

The customer, surprised, reached into his back pocket and indeed found the brass knuckles there. Then Joseph said: “Your next line is: ‘How’d you know about my brass knuckles, you bastard?’”

And sure enough, the customer said exactly that right after finding them.

=>EQP.

After that, by examining the marks on the man's dominant hand, Joseph deduced that the man had just been in a fight earlier. The bloodstains on the man's shirt were also from blood being splattered.

Based on the bloodstains on the shirt, Joseph also realized that the man had taken off his jacket during the fight.

In short, when the man removed the brass knuckles from his fingers, since he wasn’t wearing his jacket at the time, he had put the brass knuckles into his pants pocket instead. That was Joseph’s conclusion.

=> Observation, Reasoning.
