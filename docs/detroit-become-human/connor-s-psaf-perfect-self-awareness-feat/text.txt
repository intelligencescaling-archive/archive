Context:

Connor RK800 from Detroit:Become Human is a character which the player can control, depending on your choices the story can change accordingly.

Game mechanic: The higher the software instability Connor has the more deviant he becomes and vice versa

At the start of the game Connor is an android sent by cyber life, his purpose is to be a detective, catch the deviants and as well as many other subgoals (rA9, how deviancy arises).

However we'll assume the choices which are taken lean towards a higher software instability.

The actual feat performed:

Fast forward within the game and Connor is faced with the dichotomy (assuming the actions which the player took were for Deviant Connor)

1.Join Markus, become a deviant and betray cyber life

2. Remain a machine, obey cyber life and attempt to take down Markus as the leader of deviants

However (you as the player) can choose for Connor to become a deviant:

Thus, having free will and bypassing his guardrails set by cyber life (as seen by Connor breaking his “wall” and tearing it apart)

You'll see below why it's such a massive ER (Self Awareness) and Meta-Cognition feat:
(PS:ER stands for Emotional Realization, which is the same as Self-Awareness)

For Connor to be able to break his own code, bypass restrictions set on his program from cyber life as he is the smartest android and need to be kept under control, (aside from RK900) it would require Connor to have a perfect and total awareness of his own operating system.
