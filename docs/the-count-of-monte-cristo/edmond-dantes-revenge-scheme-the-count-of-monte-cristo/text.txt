The Count of Monte Cristo - Probably my last post here about him tbh (unless???) (SPOILERS for the book)

I have just finished reading The Count of Monte Cristo by Alexandre Dumas. You might think that oh it was pretty quick! After all, it had only been 9 days since I have posted my first post about him and a bit more than 5 days since I have gotten the book itself. [Per the post](https://www.reddit.com/r/IntelligenceScaling/comments/1vkoqa0/this_guy_is_so_smart_count_of_monte_cristo/)

Well I just want to say that the book itself was absolute peak and I am of the opinion that it is the greatest book ever written. Everything about it is so great. Its not just the very elaborate, multi-decade long, revenge scheme of the main protagonist Edmond Dantes, but also the life of high society, the historical contexts, the atmosphere (such as the carnival of Rome for example - that stood out to me), the sub-plots (the Count solving a Romeo and Juliet case and making sure it ends up good), each of the times he revealed to his enemies, showing that it was all over, everything had gone to plan, there is just so much to say about it.

But well, this is an intelligence scaling subreddit, not a book review. I might make a small review later on but whatever lets get to the actual intelligence stuff. Personally and this is my own personal opinion I think Edmond Dantes in the simplest of terms strikes himself as a truly smart character by the following things:

\- *The greatest trick that he ever pulled off was convincing the world that the revenge-seeking Edmond Dantes did not exist, instead all they knew was that this was an eccentric, but very generous guy.*

*- Through his many identities, he made sure he was the one who knew the most, while everyone else did not have even an idea he did know all of these,* (heck he even pretended like he didnt, as sometimes in the book he genuinely receives stuff as the Count he already knew as say abbe Busoni, its just an example)

*- Through these two things - his nature and his identity, he made his greatest enemies suspect that he was their greatest friend and that he had no shadowy motives.*

What do I mean by this? Well... Lets analyze it. He escapes the prison, befriends the smugglers who saved him as he also had the perfect alibi along with it, hatches his scheme to get the wealth of the island of Monte Cristo all of that. Doing that, he also has them gather information about Caderousse and Mercedes.

He then goes to Caderousse disguised as the abbe Busoni - using the veil of a priest who is naturally trusted with the worst secrets and with the knowledge that Caderousse was just a coward, so he had some guilt about it and through Caderousse he is able to figure out how Danglars and de Morcerf were living, what their ranks were, where they were, their personalities etc.

I could say more but I am just going to skip to the point when Albert visits Rome with Franz. By this point, Edmond had become the Count of Monte Cristo, the eccentric wealthy guy, but he also possessed quite the generosity as he helped both Albert and Franz with the carriage and the hotel room on several occasions, even helping Franz bail out Albert for no money. This generosity to Albert is what he latches on to for his later scheme.

He goes to Paris and little do they know this is the beginning of their downfall: The downfall of Fernand, the downfall of Villefort and the downfall of Danglars. Of course, he becomes their friend, a great friend of theirs even, allowing him to know about them deeply, so that his revenge scheme could work without any flaw. Through the things I mentioned above, he orchestrated their fall.

First was Fernand as he used Danglars against him, alongside Haydee as he with the help of Danglars digging stuff up there and Haydee being first-hand witness of what he did and with all the evidence jeopardized his reputation due to his treason in Ioannina. Of course, he still had to get bailed out by Mercedes as Albert was willing to duel him to the death and he was later in a situation where he had to die since he didnt want Mercedes - who knew he was Edmond - to have her son die. But well, Mercedes saved him by telling Albert the truth - which is another element as to why this book is peak.

Dumas makes such a good SCD character because he wasnt writing a SCD character. He was writing a character who got shafted by the deceiving nature of humanity - the nature of humans having interests - and those people had interests that he stood in the way of and who had a human reaction to it after spending so many years locked up for doing nothing wrong but being in the way of others. I think you can find out the best written types of characters - Powerful characters, smart characters etc. Are those that werent made laser-focused to be this - because if they were, they would be shallow.

Still, that seems like an anti-feat: He genuinely was going to die in the middle of his revenge scheme and he seriously couldnt figure out any other way just because he loved Mercedes so much and realized that maybe killing her son was wrong. But I feel like it isnt much of an anti-feat - sure he couldnt come up with what Mercedes came up with, but you could still argue that he was thinking that his revenge might beset the others anyway, even if he would no longer be there to see it. He had already introduced the whole poison thing by talking about it to Valentine which gave Heloise the idea to begin poisoning the ranks of the Villefort so that Edouard could be the one to win from the soon to die Noirtiers testament, Fernand had already been destroyed as his whole scandal in Ioannina, his whole treason, had been brought to light and Danglars had already received the unlimited credit from the Count and had become very wealthy, which he knew would lead him to possibly just losing it all in his desperado greed, in his recklessness. Still, its not really that good on him as like an anti-feat, but I think he definitely had considered it done, just not fully, you know?

Still, he is saved by Mercedes, Albert tells him the truth, Mercedes and Albert abandon Fernand as they leave Paris for good and Fernand kills himself. Wait! I forgot about Caderousse. Yea, he took him out quick. Caderousse tried to rob his house as he had entered sort of this criminal underworld with Benedetto (A. Cavalcanti) for many years now, but the Count knew about it before hand and so hatched that clever disguise with the abbe Busoni as he then berated Caderousse and was heavily disappointed in him for still being so evil and then basically Caderousse left and got killed by his accomplice and in the hospital he revealed to him he was Edmond. Ok should have said more here about Gaspard but whatever.

How does he take out Villefort? Well... This is more difficult because this is where he plays the smallest role, however he does play a role. He introduces the whole toxicology stuff to Valentine, who also tells him she is a bit into this as well, but the fact is I think this introduces it to Heloise, the wife of Villefort, who realizes she can use it to guarantee Edouard gets the inheritance from Noirtier, who was paralyzed and was old and likely going to die soon. The fact is, this does help the Count as he basically sees Villefort sort of get torn down, his whole family just is torn apart.

Not only that, Villefort also gets torn down himself as Benedetto (Andrea Cavalcanti) reveals to him that he was his father and that he wanted to leave him for dead, which well was not really good obviously - it was literal infanticide. All of this was technically set into motion by Bertuccio, who was the Counts servant, who found that chest with the baby in it and such and the Count did play a role in this as well as the Count, like Bertuccio, also wanted a revenge on Gerard de Villefort. I think another element to it was that well the Count also found common allies - people who wanted to get revenge on the same people he wanted to get revenge on - people like Haydee or Bertuccio.

And Danglars? Well that is just easy. He basically manipulated him with his generosity and also exploited his recklessness and greed as he gave him an unlimited credit and basically millions of francs and because of how wealthy he had gotten he basically became reckless and got scammed and ended up at the hands of the bandit Luigi Vampa (the one who also arrested Albert). And to get his freedom, Danglars basically gave up all of his wealth and he was just let out to fend for himself in the countryside, completely poor, just lacking any money. Sure, he didnt die like Caderousse and Fernand, but he also completely lost everything - like Villefort.

Still, a third element is the fact that he was genuinely generous, that is why his act was so good. He was genuinely generous, especially to the Morrel family, who he knew had wanted to free him but had failed. This is best manifested in the fact that he saw Maximilien Morrel in that sort of Romeo and Juliet type plot with Valentine and ended up assuring him that she was going to be fine, but during this time he was intentionally leaving him in the greatest despair, right near death, while at the same time he had managed to save him for long enough, even revealing his own name of Edmond Dantes to him, so that he wouldnt while at the same time he allowed Valentine to know it was Heloise through the sleeping scheme he hatched and he also had her pretend to be dead so that she would arrive at the opportune time when Maximilien was right near death, the point where he couldnt wait and so he ended up having the greatest joy. That is genuinely a smart thing he did for good.

In conclusion, I wrote a whole fucking essay. Of course I did. This book is just that fucking peak and complex. But still, I think you guys do see the connections with SCD he has here. The revenge schemes he did, the information gathering, the preventing of Romeo and Juliet story, the fact he ended up having tabs on literally everyone while they didnt even know he did, the fact that they couldnt even think of the fact that this was Edmond having revenge on them while he was doing it. Everything is just so, so, great.

The fact that it also doesnt teach you revenge is good. It doesnt. There are genuinely bad consequences to it. Thats what good about it. Its so nuanced. The fact that relatives of Villefort die who had nothing to do it, the fact that Albert almost died, the fact that he almost died, the fact that Maximilien almost died, Valentine too. The fact that Edmond doesnt end up with Mercedes because she feels so old and he feels so young and just doesnt see any purpose in life anymore. Sure, Edmond technically ends up good. He has dealt with all of those that wronged him in such a cathartic way, he is still very wealthy, he ends up with his love Haydee (who was quite younger than him even if still an adult and to which he was a father figure to uhh whatever), he saved Maximilien and Valentine, Albert and Mercedes are alive etc. Like its a two-sided coin.

Ok no more book review stuff. The conclusion is, Edmond Dantes is a high tier character because of how great he is at gathering information , how great he is able to just make people not even think there is something hidden about this guy, that he is indeed good - those people being the same ones he was about to have his revenge on, so they basically were having their guard let down until it was very late, until their own actions caught up to them while they didnt even know, until it was too late, that it was Edmond Dantes that allowed these to happen over the many period of time, but also the fact that he literally prevented a Romeo and Juliet from happening. Seriously. This dude was defying fate. Still, he was incredibly PATIENT. Which is an important aspect, not only that, he was very good at like changing his voice, looking differently, having a complete poker face, he knew what and what not to say, he knew exactly how to make up things, he knew how to be calm even in the face of the worst adversity. Thats what stands out to him compared to many other characters. There are just so many angles from where you can see how he is so high-tier. Oh yea, lets not forget that he knows many languages and has vast knowledge in like many fields thanks to his own travels and also from the abbe Faria who taught him everything he knew. So he is not just an emotional-social IQ guy, he is an IQ guy.
