Season 2 episode 2

Early into the episode Leif manages to shoot a wild boar with a shotgun while Billy is driving their car, the boar was also quite far away which adds to the feat.  Right after that scene Billy notices a dear in the woods which is visually shown to be blocked by some trees aswell as being about 300 meters away from them which he manages to shoot down. It is later stated that there were at least 8 dead animals when their new neighbor Tony told them about the incident which means there were 6 offscreen kills

(VSI,Stress tolerance,Perserverance,Cognitive discipline)

They get confronted by their new neighbor Tony about the recent killings of animals around the local area where they seems to remain quite calm despite them being the ones responsible. They are then able to act in a convincing enough manner that Tony dosent start to suspect that they are the culprit
(Mental fortitude,Stress tolerance,Cognitive discipline,Acting skills)

The next morning while eating breakfast Billy reads the local newspapers. In the paper he finds out that Tony and 2 other hunters are determined to find the culprit and bring him to justice. The local police have also come forward to say that they view this incident very seriously. Leif almost instantly relaiseses that he is going to have to change his method since they will likely be keeping taps on the road that the animals were shoot at which then leads him to the conclusion to hunt by foot rather then by car since they likely won’t be as focused inside the forest. Althoguh his reasoning isnt explained we can draw do some guessing

(PSI,Mental fortitude,Logical prediction,Anticipation,Trap evasion,Environmental adaptability,Threat assasement)

As they are out hunting in the woods they notice a camera which Leif quickly shoots in order to not get caught, Tony later states that there were six cameras destroyed which means they managed to find six hidden cameras in the forest in order to escape their set up

(Trap evasion)

The 3 hunters after noticing that the unknown culprits strategy seems to be to shoot down the cameras decides to trap them by placing a camera in a tree which is used as bait to force the culprit to shoot it down while having another camera behind it in order to catch the culprit. Leif and Billy fall for this set up and gets exposed to Tony as the people behind the shootings

(Anti trap evasion)

As Tony gets home he starts to watch the recording which proved that Leif and Billy were the culprits of the shootings, they then manage to sneak up to his window meaning they most likely predicted what he would do what he did.  They then shoot Tony with a dart with some kinda sleeping poison in it

(Logical prediction,Resource utilization)

After this we see Tony wake up. He immediately takes the tape which he believes has evidence against Leif and Billy and calls the cops stating that he has proof on who the culprits are. The cops,Leif,Billy and Tony get into a room to watch the tape which is noticeably different since they never shoot the camera in it hinting that they most likely have replaced the old footage with a newer version to frame themselves in a better light. We can also determine that they were confident that Tony would call the cops since he believes he has the evidence and maybe becouse of his expressed hatred they saw him potray towards the unknown person behind everything. This theory is further supported by the fact that they made a second tape tape where they place his unconscious body at the window with a shotgun shooting at them and even adding a fake voice to him in order to frame him further. After this the police officers decide to investigate Tony’s house where they find the meat that Leif and Billy had in their fridge which heavily indicates that they predicted that the officers would investigate Tony’s house afterwards due to the suspicion of him by the fake tape which they used to their advantage by moving the meat from their fridge to his which pretty much proves to the police officers that Tony is the culprit

(Emotional understanding,Indirect manipulation,Logical manipulation,Misleading,Fabrication,Concealment,Logical prediction,Anticipation,Trap evasion,Setting traps,Planning)
