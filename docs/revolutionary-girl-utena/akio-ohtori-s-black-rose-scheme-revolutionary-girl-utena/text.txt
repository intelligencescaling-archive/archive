Akio Ohtori’s Black Rose Scheme(Revolutionary Girl Utena)

This analysis will help going over all of Akio’s actions within the Black Rose Arc and how it is connected to his main plan.

# Goals

firstly it’s important to understand his goals. Akio’s entire scheme is to host an experiment. Akio needs a pawn he could use that would challenge Utena and further prep her for the revolution. His second goal is to successfully achieve “eternity” which is essentially making the school capable of keeping people immortal.

# Nemuro/Shouji Manipulation Strategy

Akio sends his sister Anthy to disguise as a boy named Mamiya. Akio instructs her to reconstruct his personality perfectly to manipulate Professor Nemuro into being obsessed with the idea of saving him. Mamiya dies before the events of the story due to sickness. Nemuro is a young prodigy scientist known to be the best within the experiments they were holding in Nemuro Memorial Hall and known as “the supercomputer”. Anthy, disguising as Mamiya is also instructed to get close to Tokiko, someone implied to be a romantic interest for Nemuro who was also a brilliant scientist who cared for Mamiya.

As for what was happening in Nemuro Memorial Hall, the test being held was a way to obtain “eternity” which was basically immortality in the sense they never age.

Akio manipulates all of the researchers and boys participating in the experiments by giving them this promise, then giving all of them a rose crest ring to symbolize their partnership.

The important of the rose crest has multiple purposes. Firstly, Akio wants Nemuro to notice how almost everyone has one to cause him to become curious and to use Shouiji’s savior complex to entice him into joining his partnership. Secondly, he wants to create a connection to the past events of Nemuro Memorial Hall to the present events of the story. Thirdly, the rose crest rings they have will obtain the ability to create Black Roses which will be very important in the future.

Akio eventually approaches Nemuro with an offer. Knowing “Mamiya” is going to die, he offers Shoiji a chance for him, Tokiko, and Mamiya to stay together for eternity. Akio hands him the rose crest ring to symbolize their allegiance.

To add the final fuel to fire(haha get it) Akio makes out with Tokiko in public to assure that Numero sees. Offscreen Akio has seduced Tokiko for the specific purpose to pushing Numero over the edge. Not only that, but Akio intends on manipulating Tokiko into falling for him so that he could invite her back to the school once he decides to conclude his scheme.

Eventually, as apart of Akio’s plan. The memorial hall gets burned down with the 100 boys with rose crest rings die while stuck under the building.

Nemuro, under Akio’s instructions, kills them all for his promised eternity. When Nemuro confronts Tokiko about how they, and Mamiya can be together for eternity, Tokiko slaps him then leaves him, abandoning her potential eternal life and Nemuro. Tokiko however believes “Mamiya” died in the fire.

At some point after this, Akio begins his gaslighting of Nemuro with the intended goal of fabricating his original memory and believing Mamiya was still around.

Eventually, as time passes and Nemuro suffers from grief, his memories deteriorate drastically. Akio sets up a fake persona for Nemuro, that being “Shouji Mikage” a genius student who sets up seminars.

Akio keeps Shouji inside the repaired memorial hall with the instruction to host seminars within that building. Akio gives Shoiji false promises that Mamiya is trapped within the memorial hall, and that if Shouji follows his instructions and he takes out the Rose Bride(who is Anthy) then he can save Mamiya.

This false persona, the false promise, and being kept in the memorial hall causes Shouji to suffer from severe amnesia and psychosis which Akio planned.

Shouji begins to believe that he truly is a student due to his appearance being young and being around ppl who look as young as him. Shouji replaces the original memory of when he burned down the memorial hall with a completely altered one. This new memory has Mamiya being the one who burned down the memorial hall and Tokiko gets upset at him, which Shouji later intervenes claiming this was for “eternity” before she leaves. Shouji also is completely in belief that Mamiya is in the memorial hall and that they need to kill the Rose Bride together in order to free Mamiya.

Now at present day, Shouji hosts his seminars that delve into the deepest, selfish or resentful ambitions of people who attend. Shouji uses a “Black Rose” these come from the rose crest rings of those who have died, what “black roses” do is take control of anyone with these selfish or resentful ambitions and completely morph them into more open with them. Shouji attempts to create duelists from these students(and one random lady ig) to challenge Utena in a duel so that if they win, they kill Anthy.

Akio predicted that Shouji would target student council members. This is because of his understanding of Shouji’s philosophy and motivations as well as trusting in his intellect(narratively, this mf shouji is a bum in scd).

Shouji genuinely believed that by hosting his seminar and using the black roses to manipulate people, revealing their darkest ambitions, that he was helping them.(i mean in a way he wasnt wrong but a hell of a way to give out therapy ig)

This ambition which Shouji had was likely also engineered by Akio. This because the basis for Akio’s manipulation of Nemuro in the first place was taking advantage of his desire to help those he was close with. Then Akio sends Shouji back to the Nemuro Memorial Hall where he believed he was helping ppl in the first place for the specific purpose of using those with deep negative feelings. Not to mention, his way of controlling his actions is false promises of saving Mamiya who he didn’t actually know in the first place.

All that aside now we go into the experiment’s Akio was conducting, using this scheme.

# Utena Experiment Plan

Akio’s biggest priority is testing Utena. Akio wants to explore her savior complex towards Anthy, and her ambitions of being a prince. He also wants to figure out how to break it down.

The specific methods he is conducting here is seeing how Utena reacts to betrayal. Akio ofc intends to reveal himself to her, as well as having Anthy betray Utena, so experimenting with how she might develop was of interest to him. He also wanted to see how much he could cause Utena to believe in the prince ambition he manipulated into her in the first place(i will analyze that at a later date). Lastly, he wanted to explore how deep her relationship with Anthy could be, since that was the main way he was able to control her and he intended to mess with their relationship at a later date.

Simply put, he wants to develop Utena then discover what might break her down after that.

Lastly, he also wants to experiment with the student council. Akio wants to develop the student council further so they can also pose more of a threat to Utena. Knowing Shouji would target those close to the student council, he examines how each react to their close ones and what their close ones darkest resentments are. This is so he can manipulate them in his scheme right after this.

# Tokiko Return Strategy

After his experiment was done he concludes his scheme by inviting Tokiko back into the academy. Akio predicts him inviting Utena and assures that Shouji would end up seeing Tokiko. Once she returns Shouji sees him and his sense of time and real memories begin to surface after seeing how old she becomes. Tokiko meets up with Akio to warn him that something is wrong with the school, unaware that this mf is behind that.

Shouji once he meets up with Utena begins to project Tokiko onto her, even hallucinating that she is Tokiko. Utena, as Akio expected is pissed after realizing Shouji was “behind everything” then challenges him to a duel.

Shouji loses ofc and during the duel he makes the realization that this entire time he was manipulated by Akio and calls him on the phone to confront him about this while Akio confirms that he used his memory and attachments to bend him into his will. Then we never see Shouji again, which we can only assume he left the school after, letting go of his desire to save Mamiya.

# Conclusion

In conclusion, this scheme is pretty intricate, with lots of steps over several years and the use of several multi-step manipulations at a mass scale. It is also very resistant, with no weak points to actually take advantage of since his manipulation of Numero was done to specifically counter him. For everyone else involved there is no way to determine who is responsible. Akio shows pretty good reasoning and foresight with his deduction of Shouji going to use close ones to student council members as well as how he intended to do so. There was also a lot of EU and SQ involved. Lastly, the manipulation here is obv great along with the deception.

This is one of his lower scaling schemes(which ofc isnt to say its bad), but its also important and will help a lot in the long run to get this out of the way. also its one of the easier schemes to go over with the only issue being how much symbolism and subtle storytelling is packed in this scheme. obv that isnt a bad thing but for a scd scheme it makes it more difficult to scale.
