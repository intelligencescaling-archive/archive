I will try to give some feats and information regarding the kid (MC) from kindergaten 1, 2 and 3 indie-games, because I saw nobody talking about this

I will give context because I would assume nobody knows him, the kid which Im refering to attends to schools in Kindergarten 1, 2, and 3 that are not normal educational institutions. They are deeply corrupt, highly lethal environments masking dark conspiracies.

The adults are unstable sociopaths, ranging from a teacher who actively plots to murder students to hide her secrets, to a Principal running a clandestine underground laboratory conducting bio-weapon testing and genetic experiments on children.

His peers are a chaotic mix of unstable personalities, including a heavily armed bully, a nerd who sells contraband and has lethal technology, a weird kid who can poison you etc etc etc, basically one mistake and the mc is death, he can actually die like 60 different  ways around all 3 games.

So, the main ability of the kid is that he repeats the same day always and he is trapped in a loop, so he decides to carry out numerous plans or routes with other characters; if he ends up completing one of these, the loop does not break, but he obtains an item that can be kept once the reset of the day happens an he use those items to complete other routes. He does this until he has enough objects to carry out his main plan, which is to dismantle the school conspiracy (the final mission)

every route has their feats counting the 3 games there are more than 10, I will explain only 2 feats from each kindergarten 1 and 2. I exclude kindergarten 3 because I hardly remeber things about it.

**from kindergarten 1**

**teachers pet route:**

In the Teacher's Pet route, the Kid demonstrates a cold, mercenary approach to social engineering, weaponizing school authority figures and personal insecurities to systematically eliminate almost the entire school.

He starts before school by psychological baiting: he uses deep personal trauma regarding Buggs' father to provoke Buggs into physical aggression, perfectly timing a call for help so Ms. Applegate removes Buggs. He then immediately purchases a voice recorder to prepare for Cindy's false accusations, baiting her into framing him and using the recorded audio evidence in the Principal's office to lock Cindy away while securing Buggs' release for later use.

During class, the Kid acts as a double-agent. He trades with Monty for a Yo-Yo to trick Jerome into handing over his Principal Pass, then immediately uses that pass to expose Jerome to Ms. Applegate and have him dragged off. At lunch, he plays Nugget and Buggs against each other, using toxic masculinity and peer pressure to trick Buggs into eating a poisoned nugget. Next, he baits the Janitor's insecurity about his literacy to brutally incapacitate Monty, then trades Monty's broken glasses to the Lunch Lady as a pass to enter the hallway. There, he betrays Lily by reporting her eavesdropping directly to the Principal. Finally, at recess, he retrieves a dog corpse from Nugget's hole to provoke Ms. Applegate into throwing Nugget down the cave, completing his contract and escaping with a Lunch Pass.

**(Manipulation, Deception, Social Intelligence (SQ) & Emotional Intelligence (EQ), Scheming, Foresight & Reasoning, Field Skills & Observation)**

**Final route:**

In Lily's route, the Kid acts as a mastermind operative, executing an intricate, high-stakes undercover infiltration to uncover a missing child and shut down a secret underground laboratory.

He begins before school by gathering critical intel. He coordinates with Lily, uses Monty's translation services to decrypt a hidden note, and provides essential security clearance tools (the Teacher's Phone and Office Key). To locate the secret entrance, he intentionally provokes a confrontation with the Principal, navigating a precise lie during interrogation to inspect the office's floor rug and confirm the hidden hatch's location.

During morning time, he executes a stealth reconnaissance mission under the guise of a bathroom break. By manipulating the Janitor into cleaning a stall, he distracts him long enough to steal human remains (a severed finger) and evidence (Billy's shoe) from the Janitor's secret stash. At lunch, he handles field security by bribing the Hall Monitor with cigarettes to clear their path, then uses Lily to create a distraction call that pulls the Principal out of his office. Inside, he solves the security mechanism by finding the hidden safe on the desk, unlocking the hatch, and descending into the lab. Under immediate threat of death from both a mutant monster and the Principal, the Kid operates a complex laboratory terminal under pressure—terminating a hostile bio-weapon, reversing Billy's mutation, and releasing a live creature at the exact right moment to execute the Principal and rescue Billy.

**(Deception, Field Skills, Observation, Manipulation, Reasoning, Thinking, Scheming, AQ, FSIQ)**

**kindergarten 2:**

**flowers for diana:**

in this route the mc is challenged by a professor to find five distinct colored flowers hidden across the school grounds before science class, or face execution. The Kid must maneuver through adult staff, strict hall monitors, student rivalries, and romantic entanglements to collect every flower, then figure out how to safely feed them to Dr. Danner’s giant carnivorous plant, Diana.

Before school starts, the Kid uses Nugget’s sewer predicament to report a emergency, forcing the Principal to pull Ms. Margaret away from her post. He then pays Monty to cover for him with Bob, allowing him to bypass security, reach the handicap ramp to harvest the **Blue Flower**, and manipulate the Hall Monitor into granting him an unrestricted morning hall pass.

In morning time, the Kid plays matchmaker between Cindy and Felix, using his pass to dodge Stevie and deliver love notes to Felix for a $3.00 reward. He then enters the girls' bathroom and manipulates Bob’s crush on Ms. Applegate, instructing him to buy her flowers—effectively planting the source for the **Red Flower** later in the day.

During lunch, the Kid hires Carla for $5.00 to lure Stevie away from his post, allowing him to slip into the restricted Teacher’s Lounge and grab the **Yellow Flower**. He completes Felix’s request for a vegan meal from Ms. Margaret, keeping the trade network active.

at recess, the Kid executes Felix’s request to eliminate Penny. He plants contraband under a tree and shakes it, triggering a bee swarm attack on Penny. This forces her to open the restricted back gate, granting access to the **Green Flower** growing behind the school. He collects the **Purple Flower** from Cindy as a reward, steals a **Red Flower** from Bob's discarded bouquet, sells the leftover contraband back to Monty for $5.00, and pays Stevie his lab fee to enter Science.

Trapped in science class with the lethal plant Diana, the Kid observes three separate environmental clues: the fish tank (Blue, Yellow, Purple), the jars (Yellow, Purple, Red), and the chemistry set (Purple, Red, Green). He overlaps these overlapping 3-color segments to deduce the exact 5-step feeding sequence (Blue, Yellow, Purple, Red, Green), saving his life and earning an A+.

**(Reasoning, Thinking, Knowledge, Observation, Manipulation, Field Skills, Scheming, FSIQ)**

**Final route route**

in the true-ending culmination of Kindergarten 2, the Kid coordinates a grand-scale operation to dismantle the school's leadership, rescue kidnapped students, and destroy the underground bio-lab. Armed with key items collected across previous loops (the Monstermon Plushie, Faculty Remote, and Laser Bomb), the Kid unites a coalition of students—including Lily, Billy, Buggs, Cindy, Carla, Nugget, and Bob—to systematically execute three faculty members, breach the secret lab, and eliminate the mutated Principal in a final high-stakes showdown.

The Kid begins by equipping the stolen Faculty Remote with a battery to make Penny shoot open the sewer grate, freeing Nugget and establishing contact with Lily and Billy. He then builds a full tactical strike team by trading assets and issuing remote commands, returning Buggs' knife for support, manipulating Penny to secure Cindy's help, trading the Laser Bomb with Carla for a wall-breaching explosive, and equipping Nugget with a jackhammer to dig a bypass tunnel.

To eliminate the faculty, the Kid infiltrates the Teachers' Lounge through Nugget's tunnel to plant the bomb that kills Dr. Danner, uses his Lunch Pass to step out into the hallway, and executes a stealth backstab on Ms. Applegate in the bathroom to save Buggs. He clears the pathway to the Secret Lab by using the remote to drive Penny into the Nugget Cave, snapping Bob out of a trance in the weapons closet to serve as combat support, and pacifying Penny with a doll so she uses her retinal scan to unlock the lab elevator.

Inside the lab, the Kid manages three simultaneous monster threats by operating overhead mechanical claws and firing lasers from Penny's detached head, culminating in using a red claw to drop the Principal into a vat of goo. Finally, under extreme action-point constraints, he repositions the green claw to bind the mutated Principal's jaws shut and shifts the red claw to force her gaze upward, setting her up perfectly for Nugget to execute a lethal drill strike.

**(Manipulation, Deception, Social Intelligence (SQ) & Emotional Intelligence (EQ), Scheming, Foresight & Reasoning, Field Skills & Observation, FSIQ, AQ, Thinking)**
