Alastor's Freedom Plan explanation (Hazbin Hotel)

Context:

Alastor has a deal with Rosie to be the Strongest Sinner in Hell in exchange for being her servant. Rosie orders him to work at the Hazbin Hotel and aid its owner, Charlie Morningstar. In aiding them, he has to at one point fight Archangel Adam, and in the process his staff is broken and he’s badly injured. Alastor goes to visit Rosie to ask her to heal him and fix his staff. She refuses. Alastor has a rival Vox, who’s obsessed with him, and wants to get to heaven to conquer it, and also spends the first few episodes constantly slandering the Hotel.

Basically, Alastor’s key way to deal with this is to strengthen Vox’s connections and status, to bring him to the point of being the new Strongest Sinner in Hell. He also manages to accomplish a few other goals by the end of it

False Defeat

–First, Alastor challenges Vox to a fight to get him to stand down from slandering the Hotel. He knows that Vox will bring his allies, Valentino and Velvette, the other Vees, and Alastor also knows that being weakened from the Adam fight, he’ll lose.

–While on the ropes, he offers Vox a deal: Alastor will go with them completely voluntarily as their prisoner and not resist, but Vox is not to lay his hands on Charlie Morningstar.

–The important facts are regarding Alastor and Rosie’s deal are:

1: Alastor is to help the Hotel,

2: Alastor fought Adam for the Hotel and was weakened

3: Rosie didn’t restore Alastor’s strength

4: Alastor fought Vox explicitly to help the Hotel

5: Alastor, seemingly being overpowered *specifically because he was weakened* was forced to make a deal with Vox which, again, benefitted only the Hotel

The facts are that Rosie’s end of the deal was for Alastor to be the strongest sinner, and while fighting Adam was ultimately his choice it was still done as part of holding up his end. Rosie did however, come up short by not restoring his strength, and that weakness is very greatly shown to be why he bombed the fight with Vox, which again, was in service of the Hotel. His deal with Vox especially, was the number one example of Alastor doing everything he can in his power, his limited power from Rosie’s lack of support, in order to help the Hotel. Any rational interpretation even of these circumstances alone by whatever entity overseas Hell deals would see this as Alastor having kept to his side and Rosie having let him down. (Preparation, Overall Strategy)

Building Vox’s Influence

–Alastor is one of the most renowned, and feared beings in Hell. The fact of Vox having defeated him immediately places him in a greater light than previously with other overlords, creating the image of him being stronger and more capable than he truly is. This image led to them being swayed more easily by Vox. This can be seen throughout the song [Bad With Us](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1USxshmIk_A).

–Vox’s main goal in this season was to ascend to, and conquer, Heaven itself. However for pretty much all the first 4 episodes, his progress on this front was nothing. Like all he even did in all that time was just slander the Hotel. However, this is where the deal Alastor made with him comes into play even further as it takes that option from Vox, and seeing as his new victory over Alastor has bolstered his standing with the other Overlords, the best option for Vox has now become to make alliances with all of them in order to strengthen his own influence.

–Of course, basically every chance he gets, Alastor ragebaits Vox mercilessly. He’s largely aided in this because Vox has terrible self-control and obsesses over Alastor, though Alastor himself proclaims, no victory would ever be enough for Vox. His entire mentality is based around attempting to shine brighter than before, and ascend to higher and higher peaks, ensuring that Vox will go along with Alastor’s intentions of influence building throughout the latter half of season 2.

–Additionally, Alastor consistently insults and exploits Vox’s dependence on others. He does this to drive a wedge between him and the other Vees, and this is something that can be seen when Vox is alone and switches from [Once We Get Up There](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nnQfZMbwdt0) to [Once I Get Up There](https://youtu.be/V2JkSLYqiXg?t=43) as he grows more and more obsessed with trying to become independent.

I won’t say that everything Vox did was because of Alastor, the Lucifer confrontation, blackmailing Carmilla and Lucifer trapping were all him. But the general idea that Vox would wind up with some method or weapon strong enough to challenge Heaven was ultimately what Alastor was driving towards. After all, Vox’s entire thing is technology, and sublimating important jobs to those who are more qualified, so this is likely also predicted by Alastor. (Direct Manipulation, Indirect Manipulation, Emotional Manipulation, Logical Manipulation, Mass Manipulation, Individual Manipulation, Social Awareness, Social Understanding, Prediction, Preparation, Overall Strategy.)

The Finale

–Vox ultimately comes to the point Alastor had been egging him onto the entire time, with a giant cannon pointed at Heaven and Charlie there to see it. Alastor uses his one small favor Charlie owes him (previous season thing, doesn’t matter here how he got it) to compel Charlie to announce Vox as the Strongest Sinner in Hell. This was the aim of having Vox up himself again and again because, in a sense, Alastor did believe that Vox could get to this point where his accomplishments merited the title, and now with Charlie, the Princess of Hell having announced it it becomes official (apparently, was never really explained) and Alastor is now free of his deal with Rosie.

–The phrasing of Alastor’s deal with Vox “you are not to lay a hand on Charlie Morningstar” was because he knew Vox for decades, and thus knew him well enough that A: Vox would be too distracted from the prospect of Alastor as his own prisoner to notice the wording even in spite of his own astuteness in deal-making, and B: Vox is a really touchy-feely guy, probably touch-starved in the same way that he’s generally starved for affection and needs approval from people like Alastor or Valentino. This can even be seen once Alastor is a prisoner, and Vox frequently puts his hands over Alastor such as on his shoulders or grabbing Alastor’s face to make him look and generally man-handling him, so Alastor knew that Vox, in such a moment of triumph as having been declared the Strongest Sinner, would likely slip up and do something like touch Charlie’s shoulders patronisingly, freeing Alastor due to the wording of the deal. –Following being freed, Alastor proceeds to beat the shit out of Vox. Furthermore, his exploitation of Vox’s insecurities of depending on others and the wedge he drove between Vox and the other Vees can be seen as coverage to minimise the odds of them interfering. However, this was not necessary as, predictably, the Hotel crew showed up to stop them. I say predictable because they’re the good guys, of course they were going to try to stop Vox, this was basically a given.

–Now, having been publicly, in front of literally all Heaven and Hell brutally outsmarted by and got his ass beaten by his rival of over half a century, Vox is understandably pissed off worse than he likely has ever been before. What’s more, since he’s had the wedge driven between him and the other Vees on account of his own disdain of relying on them, as well as having a cannon strong enough to destroy heaven, there is one thing left for Vox: suicide attack. This is what all the ragebaiting was for, what the isolation of Vox and building his power culminated in. This idiot tried to destroy all Heaven and Hell with a wildly unstable cannon just to kill Alastor, and Al gives him the[ most shit-eating grin](https://youtu.be/JaQzNA8Apw4?t=32) imaginable in response.

–Having been belittled and sidelined by Vox repeatedly over the past few episodes, the other Vees are naturally far from happy. And guess what, this man was literally trying to kill ALL of them just to get rid of Alastor. Of course, they stop him and Val rips Vox’s head off. The damage this has done to their relationship is bound to be damn near irreparable at this point.

–Now, with the canon about to blow and kill everyone, Alastor makes a deal with Rosie. His power is immense and would likely be of a great help in disabling the explosion, and she for whatever reason wants to keep Charlie alive. So, he makes some new deal with her (likely getting her to fix his staff, though what else is unknown). What’s more, returning to the group, to all outside appearances he left to get Rosie and have her help.

–In fact, to Charlie’s perspective, EVERYTHING he’d done so far was for her. He let his own biggest rival beat him, imprison him and parade him around to all Hell restrained for a deal to get Vox to stop slandering the Hotel. He then emerged in the finale to beat Vox (honestly carrying the group) and then got his friend Rosie to help with diffusing the cannon's explosion. Even the likes of Vaggie, who naturally distrusts him, at this point has reason to stand by him now. In fact, Charlie’s dependance on her is at an all time high, especially as now lacking the one favor he held over her, she can basically go about completely without fear of him exploiting her. (Emotional Manipulation, Logical Manipulation, Direct Manipulation, Indirect Manipulation, Relationship Management, Trap Setting, Prediction, Contingencies, Overall Strategy and Overall Planning.)

In short, Alastor freed himself without losing his strength, caused intense, lasting damage between Vox and his allies, and peaked Charlie’s trust in him.
