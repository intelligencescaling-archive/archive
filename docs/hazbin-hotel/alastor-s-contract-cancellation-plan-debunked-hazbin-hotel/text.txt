Alastor's contract cancellation plan debunked

Let's start by covering the steps of this plan.

1-Get Vox to make a contract with Alastor in which the promise will be made that Vox will "Not lay hands on Charlie Morningstar".

2-Get Charlie to admit that Vox is the strongest sinner in Hell which will break Alastor's contract with Rosie.

3-Get Charlie and Vox to meet so that Vox puts his hands on Charlie's shoulders, which will break the deal between Alastor and Vox.

Now the first step is pretty easy to achieve, Alastor just has a fight with Vox and after it he admits that he's tired of constantly fighting Vox so he proposes a deal. He does this after the fight to make it truly seem like he's tired of the fighting and not that he's trying to trick Vox.

The second step and third step are much harder to achieve though.

In order to have Charlie's words of Vox being the strongest break the contract with Rosie, Charlie has to be on the news so that every sinner in Hell can hear her and acknowledge her words, however there is literally no way for Alastor to guarantee this will happen while he's being held captive by Vox. Though he does get around this by simply rage baiting Vox into inviting Charlie.

However a problem arises here, Charlie still has to accept the invite. However she really has no reason to do so. First we must establish why she went there in the show and that is because of 2 reasons. One is to save Angel Dust and the other is to stop Vox's weapon. Alastor wasn't even part of the plan. Now the second problem arises, Alastor has no knowledge of either Angel Dust being a spy or that Vox has a weapon being built, there is no narrative implication he did.

The only reason Vox even started building a weapon was because he had just declared war on Heaven, which is something Alastor had no knowledge would happen beforehand so you can't even say that he predicted Vox's actions because Vox's actions were altered by this.

So Charlie had no reason to visit Vox, well what about Vox visiting Charlie? Not happening, Vox at the current moment would be too focused on the incoming war with Heaven, so he would have no reason to visit Charlie and her hotel.

So the situation where the second and third step of the plan can be fulfilled only appeared cause of unknown variables Alastor had neither knowledge nor control of.

However, the third step was completely reliant on luck. The only reason Vox laid his hands on Charlie was because he felt like he had won, this was because Charlie's entire plan to rescue Angel Dust and stop Vox had failed. As we know already, this plan is both unforeseen by Alastor and he has no way to influence it either.

If the plan had actually succeeded (The only reason it didn't was because Baxter was cheering Nifty on instead of pushing the button that would make the plan succeed, even though he had a ton of time to press the button). This was the reason why Charlie lost and Vox won and subsequently put his hands on her shoulder and broke the deal.

So in conclusion, Alastor's plan mostly succeeded out of sheer luck. The actual steps to achieve it are good, but the execution is awful. If he had just explained his plan to the Hazbin Hotel crew beforehand, they could've helped him achieve it which would make it way more likely to succeed.
