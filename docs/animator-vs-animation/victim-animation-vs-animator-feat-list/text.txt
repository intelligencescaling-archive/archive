Victim (Animation vs Animator) feat list

Was created and killed by Alan seemingly every single day from June 3rd 2006 to March 15th 2007. If we don't count March 15th as that is the day he escaped that means he was killed every day for 285 days and yet he still remained sane after all of this. (Emotional Management, Composure, Adversity Capacity)

Escaped Alan's PC by remaining motionless (His intention was probably to make Alan bored so he would leave and he could escape) until Alan is called by someone to leave the room. Alan then traps victim in a box but Victim managed to leave a small opening and concealed it using his hand (Deception, Strategy)

Grabbed as many tools as he could before escaping into the outernet in his rocket. (Strategy, Foresight (realizing he would need tools in case he runs into danger in the outernet))

Managed to gain the admiration of an entire village despite literally NEVER HAVING A SOCIAL INTERACTION BEFORE (Social Integration, Charisma)

In just 4 years, together with Mitsi, started a company in the village and it eventually grew to be very successful, even featured on front pages of news magazines and having best selling products (Leadership, Charisma, Asset Management, Influence Building)

Managed to form a relationship with Mitsi (Basically every Emotional Intelligence+Charisma)

At this same time he started to study technology to a deep extent, despite not even being 5 year old chronologically (Learning Ability)

In the 9 years following Mitsi's death he starts gathering information about anything related to Alan Becker or the Chosen One. (Intel Gathering)

At the end of Episode 11: Victim, in the box covered with newspapers and headlines, if you actually read through them you will see that he tried to form many theories in order to find the Chosen One and Alan (Reasoning)

Realized he wouldn't be able to sustain the company with the employees leaving so he starts cloning the grey stick figure. (Strategy, Foresight, Problem Solving)

Advanced his company even further, basically making it the stickman equivalent of the CIA or FBI (Leadership, Asset Management, Influence Building)

In just just 2 and a half years he managed to track down and capture the Chosen One, a superpowered being who can destroy entire websites, which seem to be the equivalent to cities in the outernet. (Strategy, Adversity Capacity)

Was able to remember which weapons he used to fight Alan all the way back in the original Animation vs Animator, which chronologically happened almost 17 years ago. (Long-Term Memory)

Was able to spot Yellow's very small head in the Chosen One's memory (Observation)

Set a trap for Alan by making it seem like Yellow and the rest were in trouble, so Alan would unknowingly change his name to H4CK3R. He also predicted that Alan wouldn't remember the name victim and wouldn't grow suspicious, he also added a timer to put Alan into an abnormal state of mind so he would be more likely to fall into the trap. (Trap Setting, Emotional Manipulation, Stress Inducement, Deception, Prediction)

Sent a threatening message to Alan. (Fear Inducement)

Best categories: Influence Building, Charisma, Strategy, Deception, basically all EQ categories, Adversity Capacity
