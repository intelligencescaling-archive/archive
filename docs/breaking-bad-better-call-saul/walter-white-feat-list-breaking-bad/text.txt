Walter White feat list

Walter White Intelligence feats:

***“I did it for me” -*** Walter Hartwell White Sr AKA Heisenberg.

Planning: [During](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GIsMm4sA6eY) a confrontation with Hank while inside the RV he phones Saul and gets his secretary to pretend to be an employee from the hospital then the secretary phones Hank and tells him that Marie has been in a horrible accident hank flees the scene, and he and Jesse escape. He [poisoned](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yqyEYVFiPQk)Brock by turning the lily of the valley plant into a juice box, disguising himself as a regular chemistry teacher at Brock’s school switching the juice box in his lunchbox with his posioned version to get him to drink it he also had Huell lift the ricin cigarette from Jesse during a pat-down so that Jesse would later confront him, allowing him to convince Jesse that it was Gus who poisoned Brock. [Turned](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MybaTnzu9AA) Hector into a discreet Suicide Bomber to kill Gus by building a sophisticated bomb and strapping it discreetly to one of his wheel chairs. [Knowing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NoElfyULO5E) that he would be searched upon entering to Jack's compound, he placed a pivoting machine gun in the trunk of his car. And activates the machine gun with the simple push of a button, it proceeds to spray bullets through the car's trunk *and* the compound walls, killing nearly everyone inside except him and Jesse. [While](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuMALfMuFuk) preparing to be killed by Mike, he offers to call Jesse Pinkman to bring him to the location so he can be executed. In reality, he uses the call to tell Jesse that he can’t kill Gale Boetticher and he will have to be the one to [kill](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_hQHRy4ctjw) Gale Boetticher, then stops Mike from shooting him by revealing Gale Boetticher’s address.

Strategy: [Purchasing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qLDHbhQ_qrs) and [utilising](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PWKT-dQ4QnY) Vamonos Pest the employees of Vamonos Pest were known to burglarise the houses that they fumigated, and he realised that a pest-removal company would make the perfect front for a mobile meth lab they could go in while the tents up bomb the place and leave before the tent get’s pulled down. [Coercing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T3x1tzOuXbQ) Tuco into giving him 50k using fulminated mercury disguised as a bag of meth. After he [realised](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=34bWdiCsJ_0) a large chunk of his meth money is going to Mike's men in prison as a form of "hazard pay" he has all the prisoners [killed](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mR_Spjj7zH4) through Jack's Aryan Brotherhood connections. [Poisoned](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RBb9XEoO32Y) Lydia by slipping some ricin into her stevia she uses for her tea. [Hides](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UyvFiG1K0W0) his money in his floorboards then the [desert](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5QC1fNwCsK0) mentally mapping the coordinates. He [frames](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JpPm2KAcwbQ) Hank as someone who made him cook because of his chemical knowledge as a chemistry teacher and sold the products through his connections to the DEA he then claims Hank became partners with Gustavo Fring, Hank forced him into servitude of Gus and when he tried to quit, Gus wouldn’t let him later, Hank and Gus had a falling out, and Gus ordered a hit on Hank that failed but left him injured he says he paid Hank’s medical bills, and that Hank planned revenge with Hector, forcing him to build a bomb all in a confession video. [Causes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fI52h9YZ-bI) a car to be rigged to explode using a Squeegee and Car Battery. [Does](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y7cgzAmuPUs) it again with folded paper and a lighter.

Manipulation: [While](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jEK5pIfDfzI) held at Gunpoint by Jesse gaslights him into believing he didn’t poison brock Gus did. [Coercing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T3x1tzOuXbQ) Tuco into giving him 50k using fulminated mercury disguised as a bag of meth. He [coerces](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CN6RkaJPAbI) the Schwartzs into giving his $9,720,000 million to his children by giving the $200,000 he did have to two of the best hitmen west of the Mississippi to track down the Schwartzs if they don’t. [Gaslights](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R0b0kEW5eHY) Hank into believing he isn’t W.W but Walt Whitman is.

Chemistry: [Creates](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VTTc9OsGOkw) a Battery by creating six electrochemical cells and explains to Jessie why it works while doing so. [Processes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h2DUMIvMD84) Ricin [using](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ATQjB26jKbU) beans. [Created](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y_UtUG3jJ5s) phosphine gas to poison Emilio and Krazy-8. [Used](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuHfqnnlaLk) Hydrofluoric acid to dissolve evidence and explains how it can melt metal rock glass ceramic but can’t melt plastic. [Explains](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wz8_lCqEVbo) what Chemistry is explains why you shouldn’t cook methamphetamine in a volumetric flask but instead a boiling flask explains chiral and how theide and thalidomide are different explains how powerful thermite really is when it comes to melting which he would later use alongside a blowtorch to break a lock explains what bonds are and makes phenylacetone in a tube furnace using reductive ammunition to yield the methamphetamine all in this one video. [Coercing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T3x1tzOuXbQ) Tuco into giving him 50k using fulminated mercury disguised as a bag of meth. His meth purity is [99.1%](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f31XtQNuF0M) [admired](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5TBbuIanjrI) by even Gale Boetticher who has made 96% purity.

Other: [Made](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6P7XzoTJRa8&t=14s) Skyler White look innocent in the eyes of the law. [Pulls](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f31XtQNuF0M) off a drug distribution. [Deduces](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zfo-Ytr_atM)Jesse Pinkman is alive through logical reasoning. Blue Sky meth is still being distributed, and the only people who know how to make it are him and Jesse. Therefore, Jesse must still be cooking. [Observes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1-BobfHVIc)Jesse Pinkman hiding under a car from a semi-far distance. [Foresees](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Ok1DOLEDrJo) Krazy 8’s plan of using a broken piece of plate to stab him.
