Gus clearing his name feat analysis

I think this is my first time ever doing a proper analysis on a feat, just a heads up, also I have not finished breaking bad but inspired by r/[Reddest\_Velvet6](https://www.reddit.com/user/Reddest_Velvet6/) with his Walter analysis I wanted to do my own. Please no spoilers since I've watched up to S4 EP8 as of writing this.

In S4 EP 8 Gus was called by a detective, we can assume it's not Hank since he didn't recognise the voice. Anyways, as he waits in the lobby he sees a board with a missing wanted poster of Victor, him now being aware they're looking for Victor and his mind now being reminded of Gale's death. Not soon after he sees Hank briefly through the door.

Seeing this Gus knows:

Hank is investigating Heisenberg (Walter) before his gun wounds

His fingerprints are at the crime scene of Gale do to have being there before.

Gale is linked to his business and that Gale has a number to the laundry mat where their meth is made (Gus has shown to be present with Gale in his home and it's fair to assume he's seen the number there).

Gale's death is linked with the Heisenberg case.

Hank likely investigated Gale's death and into his life since it could possibly be connected to the Heisenberg case.

Hank likely has Gus's fingerprints due to Hank visiting Pollos Hermanos and that a Pollos Hermanos napkin with the laundry mat number while Gale is vegan would cause it to link to him.

Gus is likely the prime suspect of Heisenberg, he now needs a story to clear his name.
Some wouldn't fully believe Hank's story if he provides a strong enough alibi since Gus is a prominent civic donor for the DEA and is shown to be kind/warm, also providing food when the officers were at the hospital with Hank/the Salamanca twins.

As he walks in Gus talks as per usual, kind and loving asking questions about their lives and even reminds them that he and Hank met a few days ago. This highlighting his SQ/acting skills and possibly AC since he isn't freaking out.

Gus is asked to have an attorney which he responds with "I can't see why that would be necessary." playing into his profile he's created and helping convince the others that he's innocent since up to this point he's been basically a maytr.

When told his fingerprints were found confirming his thoughts that Hank was investigating him now he acts fine and has his story ready. He even mentions Gale's name which catches some off guard since many would deflect and act as if they don't know the victim, especially when told your fingerprints were found at the crime scene. Gus was probably using reverse psychology where it would be like: "Why would he mention that if he wasn't innocent?"

An agent states he knew the victim and Gus composes himself by sighing and says his alibi.

He mentions that he created a chemistry scholarship 15 years ago, mentioning how he named it after a dead friend of his to gain sympathy/play into his role again, and saying Gale attended. When Hank states when Gale graduates, Gus agrees it confirms the date knowing Hank investigated Gale and that he looked into his life, confirming it also somewhat showing he has nothing to hide.

Gus understood keeping touch ever since Gale graduated seems stupid/odd so he plays it off as only meeting him a few weeks ago where Gale came to 'reestablish contact' and invited Gus for dinner (providing a valid reason as to why his fingerprints were there).

Gus then says a week later he reads about Gale's death and asks why it wasn't mentioned the death was drug related in the paper, and said he assumed it was just a random crime.

Hank presses since the story sounds off as too why Gale came to Gus out of the blue, where Gus (now knowing Hank is sharper than he seems) predicts that someone would mention this and counters stating Gale needed money. This being a good reason because when you're broke/needing of money you'd go to anyone and Gus is known for being helpful.

Gus clarifies that Gale didn't beg however since it doesn't fit the profile Gale has (in the DEA's minds with the evidence they have) and that Gale suggested an investment opportunity, and states he suspected that Gale wanted to ask for money but was being vague about it. Gus shows that he still has average human brain abilities and not acting like an angel so they get suspicious if it's an act.

Gus is aware Gale is the reason as to why he's being investigated due to a fried chicken napkin being at a Vegans house and that he could be passed off as Heisenberg since the laundry mat was shipped to ABQ in his name (stated by Hank in S4 EP7 I think). Gus knowing this states that Gale could've been a great scientist however liked shortcuts (suggesting he was a drug dealer which is fine since he knows it's a drug related murder as the DEA stated).

Hank knows Gale is like an oddball and goofy, not a hard-headed criminal, and profiled him that way due to the video he watched of him singing that karaoke song. Gus knowing that Gale can assume that Hank also knows what he likes and strengthens his story by stating he was a warm gentle person, basically saying how they profiled him. Also adding he thought alot of him showing he's kind, and adding to his own reputation/profile since again he's a friend of the DEA being a prominent civic donor and knows many wouldn't suspect him of being a drug dealer let alone a drug ring boss.

He says he declined Gale's offer and that it was a pleasant meal and left.

When asked where he was on Wednesday 23rd he brings out a book of his dates stating he was at a fundraiser which aligns with his profile of being a helper/kind person where in reality he was talking to Hector (iirc).

With that his story has combated any accusations since Gus has:

Explained how he was connected to Gale
Explained why Gale has a Pollos Hermanos Napkin while being vegan.
Explained why his fingerprints were present at the crime scene.
Pushed them in the direction that Gale is Heisenberg.
Strengthened his profile of being a kind/honest person and a friend of the DEA.

However one thing Gus didn't anticipate was Hank asking is Gustavo was his real name, he remains composed but acts somewhat surprised since it's fair to be surprised when asked is your name your name.

Hank mentions that Gus is a Chilean national but there's no records of him being there and acts surprised, Hank elaborates saying that records showed he immigrated to Mexico in 1986 and later allowed in the US but no record of being in Chile. Gus being able to remain composed while being reminded of his brother's death is a SQ + AC feat (I think)

While Gus acts surprised he probably already knew that perhaps someone looked into his life and found no record and has a reason for this too saying the government (idk the name) was sinful and abused human rights but also notoriously known for not being able to keep records and that if he keeps digging he'll be found.

With that being settled and refuted Gus was excused and acts kind once more further solidifying he's a good person.

After this in the elevator Gus now knows:

Hank is the most prominent member of the DEA about this matter, having the most information and being the only one to have known about the records thing. Also the fact he gathered the fingerprints himself.

He's aware Hank doesn't fully believe him due to being the most prominent (this being confirmed when Walter and him put a tracker on his car)

He's bought time if Hank tries to research/find his Chilean records it would take time since even Mike couldn't find them.
