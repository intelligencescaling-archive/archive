Walter White Feats Document

Walter White is the main protagonist of the iconic AMC crime drama: Breaking Bad. He is portrayed by Brian Cranston and was created by Vince Gilligan. He is widely regarded as one of the best characters in TV history next to figures like Tony Soprano and Scarface. However this is not a document regarding an in depth character analysis. This is a document going into great detail of how intelligent a high school chemistry teacher turned Kingpin can actually be. How smart is Walter White? Well let’s find out!
