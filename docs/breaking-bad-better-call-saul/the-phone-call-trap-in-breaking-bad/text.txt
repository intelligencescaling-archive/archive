Thoughts on the Phone Call trap in Breaking Bad?

When rewatching this clip, I was reminded of the Lind L Tailor trap. It’s one of those traps designed precisely just for one person.

They both relied on the victim not thinking of an alternate possibility and acting on impulse. But I’ll say that this trap is harder to see through because of the stakes involved for Walter and the fabrication of the barrels.

# Analysis of the trap

 **Setting up the trap**

Hank and Steve Gomez keep Huell Babineaux in an unknown location, guarded by a DEA agent, under the pretense that it’s for Huell’s own safety.

They present a story that Walter, after killing Jesse Pinkman, would go after Huell. To break through Huell’s skepticism, they show a photo of Jesse killed, after being shot in the head (fake photo).

They probe Huell on why Walter would want to kill him, causing Huell to spill about buying a van in secret for Walter and loading 7 barrels of cash into it.

The trio (Jesse, Steve Gomez, Hank) deduce that Walter buried the cash in the desert.

**Execution of the trap**

Walter receives a photo of a barrel of money seemingly in the desert.

Jesse calls him shortly after, and claims to have interrogated Huell.

This serves 2 purposes
1. Explains how Jesse found the barrels (interrogated Huell —> led to the van —> van had GPS)
2. Ties in with Saul Goodman telling Walter that Huell went missing, leaving Walter to wonder why

(Just before this scene, Saul suggests to Walter that Jesse is going on a killing spree, because Huell went missing.)

Jesse conveniently provides the explanation for why Huell went missing right as Walter wondered, increasing the likelihood that Walter believes it.

To prevent Walter from calling for help, Jesse threatens to burn all the money if he loses the call for any reason. And to add more pressure, preventing Walter from thinking clearly, Jesse claims he will burn $10,000 every minute Walter isn’t here.

This triggers Walter to justify himself to Jesse. There, he inadvertently admits to numerous crimes:

1. He poisoned Brock
2. He killed Gus
3. He ran over the gangbangers
4. He killed Krazy-8 and Emilio

Walter then drives to where he buried his money.

From this trap, the trio gets
1. Full confession of Walter over numerous crimes
2. The location where he buried his money

Nonetheless, Walter almost immediately figured it out after stepping out of the car, but by that point, it’s too late.

# Thoughts

Imo this is really hard to see through. That money was everything Walter had been working for and it’s all he has to show for, for all the murder, deceit, risk and bodily harm he had committed / endured.

The fabrication of the photo was good and tied in well with Walter wondering why Huell was nowhere to be seen.

I can only think of a few ways Walter could’ve seen through this:
1. He knew the truck didn’t have GPS. Hank and Jesse assumed Walter didn’t research or know the truck didn’t have GPS. Imo, considering how careful Walter is, this is the weakest assumption in their trap
2. He noticed the sand in the photo wasn’t the shade OR recognised it was Hank’s backyard
3. He abducted that Jesse wouldn’t go out into the desert with no plan other than to burn his money, knowing Walter would just kill him (Walter knows Jesse knows he killed Mike and the prisoners) —> more likely that it’s a trap.

Even if Walter considered the possibility that this was a set up, it was still too risky to not treat it like a real threat. Jesse’s threats on top of that completely prevented Walter from thinking clearly. Really good trap.
