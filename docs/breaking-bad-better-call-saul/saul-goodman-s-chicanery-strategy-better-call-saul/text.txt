Saul Goodman’s Chicanery strategy (ANALYSIS)

Hello everyone!!!

Continuing on with my trend of deep tactical analyses - I’d like to cover one of my personal favorite schemes that oddly doesn’t get much analytical attention, despite being very impressive. This analysis will take a deep-dive into Jimmy McGill’s strategy he enacted in Better Call Saul S3 EP5 “Chicanery”. I hope you all enjoy!

**[ CONTEXTUAL ADVERSITY ]**

Notably, Jimmy had been placed in a situation where multiple disadvantages were at play - Jimmy needs to maneuver around these major oppositional advancements and formulate a method to neutralize them —>

1. Jimmy had been secretly recorded by Chuck delivering a confession that he had tampered with material used in court by Chuck, as to humiliate him - which inevitably led to the case discrediting Chuck’s reputation as a whole.

2. Chuck had presented this recording openly to law authorities (however he knew it couldn’t actually be used to press charges) - this caused Jimmy to lash out and destroy the tape, now being entrapped by Chuck into “tampering with evidence” - an actual charge that can be applied.

3. Not only this, Chuck had a copy of the tape created to shield his trap further - placing Jimmy in a near unbeatable position from this point on.

4. Chuck is one of the most renowned lawyers in Albuquerque - at least in terms of legal skill, being seen as a prodigy in the area as a whole. This naturally renders facing him in a court of law difficult, especially with the trap that has been laid out. This extends to other individuals in the trial, such as Howard or the chairmen.

5. Since this altercation will be taking place in a court of law, Jimmy is placed into an extremely fixed scenario where his flexibility is limited - also induced with a very strict timeframe.

6. With all these factors in place, an official court date has been set for Jimmy - where Chuck will finalize the purpose behind his trap and attempt to fully dismantle Jimmy’s image.

**[ THE STRATEGY’S GOALS ]**

As a result of the situational disadvantages, Jimmy’s strategy has three main goals —>

1. Jimmy needs to propagate the idea that the initial confession itself was merely an appeal to Chuck’s mental state, without any real validity behind it.

2. Shift the focus away from Jimmy’s charge (destruction of evidence) onto a more pressing event ensures his reputation maintains strength, despite the fortification of Chuck’s trap.

3. Tying into the previous goals - A key component is to emphasize Chuck’s mental illness, making it known and clear (mainly through inducing a mental collapse).

**[ LOGISTICS ]**

Jimmy’s strategy needs to account for these goals and overcome the situational adversities, which needs to take place in an extremely restrained environment (meaning flexibility is limited), the timeframe is shrunken, and the oppositional agents are likely far more experienced and skilled in this specific fixed field compared to Jimmy himself (Chuck, Howard, etc). Jimmy’s strategy is formulated and enacted with the following logistical approaches —>

1. Jimmy instructs Huell to secretly drop a cellphone battery into Chuck’s breast pocket in the stairwell. This sets the courtroom trap as well as ensuring that the actual electromagnetic emitter cannot be confiscated upon the trial beginning (which actually does occur to every other electronic in the episode, showcasing Jimmy’s anticipation), as it’s attached to the one individual who wouldn’t be carrying any electronics.

2. Jimmy also plans to state (in court) he personally had Huell plant the battery. This ensures that 1) Chuck feels completely outplayed, inducing the mental collapse further - 2) Huell’s involvement isn’t concealed and thus any future investigation into him is precluded - 3) Jimmy places the blame upon himself, once again shifting the focus from Huell to himself legally (which he is shifting back onto Chuck at the same time).

{NOTE — Jimmy framed the events of the courtroom to flow focus/blame through multiple agents, with the final flow-destination being Chuck (who is the main target of the strategy) - Huell (the agent confirmed to have planted the battery) —> Jimmy (admitted to have enlisted Huell) —> Chuck (now proven to be mentally ill and is undergoing a mental collapse in front of every party present)}

3. Jimmy manages to convince Rebecca (Chuck’s wife) into traveling to the courthouse to witness the trial. This acts as a reinforcement to psychologically shatter Chuck - Jimmy knows his relationship with Rebecca is a root behind his mental illness and integrating it into the strategy increases the likelihood of it operating without flaw.

4. Jimmy also instructs Francesca (his personal receptionist) to remain at stand-by during the trial. This doesn’t raise suspicions as she’s deeply linked to his legal history, however she serves the true purpose of acting as an informational contingency - if the strategy is disrupted, she acts as a failsafe to alert Jimmy (which we see her doing in the episode itself when Rebecca’s flight is delayed - to which Jimmy stalls the trial, displaying the in-practice value of this failsafe).

5. Finally, Jimmy keeps an (empty) cellphone in his own pocket - the battery of which is the one Huell planted in Chuck’s breast pocket.

With these logistical details noted and laid out, Jimmy’s strategy is fully mapped in this fashion —>

FIRST LAYER — Empty Cellphone

1. Jimmy reveals the empty cellphone, as to present the idea Chuck’s condition is purely mental - As stated before, this cellphone has no battery in it. As such, this is a false strategy designed to misdirect Chuck (fundamentally a provocation).

{NOTE — This is the cellphone’s main purpose as a first-layer trap for Chuck}

2. If Chuck sees through the cellphone and deduces it’s been disabled (via battery removal), then Chuck is concealed from the trap’s second layer - not only this, it induces the mental state of confidence and justification in Chuck, building his emotional status before the second layer is triggered. This ensures Chuck’s mental collapse is more severe, due to inducing rapid fluctuations in his psychological state.

3. Secondarily, if Chuck doesn’t see through the cellphone or doesn’t make the proper deduction - the cellphone serves the same purpose as the batter. This proves Chuck’s condition is psychological rather than physical, which achieves two of Jimmy’s pre-stated goals.

SECOND LAYER — Planted Battery

1. As stated previously, if Chuck sees through the cellphone - Jimmy reveals the battery in Chuck’s breast pocket, cleanly displaying the fact Chuck’s illness is purely mental.

2. If Jimmy is unable to keep the cellphone on-hand during the trial (the confiscation is instead pressed more heavily) - then the battery acts as a main trap, maneuvering around any confiscation due to the fact the electromagnetic emitter has been planted on the one individual who would be free of any in-depth search (Chuck).

**[ CATHARSIS/SUMMARY ]**

As a result of Jimmy’s strategy, Chuck suffers a mental breakdown - now feeling inferior, having his condition be proven as mental, and all in front of his ex-wife.

{NOTE — This mental breakdown is notably portrayed as incredibly major - Chuck’s breakdown in this scene goes on to be one of the driving forces, if not the main driving force, behind Chuck’s eventual act of taking his own life. A rather dark outcome, and one Jimmy definitely didn’t directly intend, but it is worth noting Jimmy’s goal was indeed to shatter Chuck psychologically as much as he could here - and as we see through Chuck’s later actions, this goal was adequately met. The official transcript for “Chicanery” also states Chuck’s breakdown encompasses “ALL of his rage and hatred”, which had been brewing for decades against Jimmy specifically. Naturally, this is likely an hyperbole - however it stresses the emotional facilitation pretty clearly}

With this, Jimmy achieves every goal he needed to cover with the strategy - he successfully reframed the confession as an appeal to emotion, redirected focus from him to Chuck, and displaying the fact Chuck’s condition is purely cognitive. Jimmy achieved these goals despite the heavy contextual adversity placed upon him - where he was confined to a restrained situation, had a limited amount of time, was facing oppositions with decades more experience compared to himself (including Chuck who is an academic prodigy in law practices), and recovered from an immaculate trap sprung against him. To summarize, Jimmy’s strategy included the following logistical components of fortification —>

1. Rebecca placement (enforces mental collapse)

2. Francesca informational contingency (supplies Jimmy with information if issues arise)

3. Huell’s battery plant maneuvering (negates any confiscation placed upon the court)

4. Jimmy’s cellphone — false strategy (misdirects Chuck and conceals the true trap)

5. Jimmy cellphone — possible main trap (if Chuck doesn’t see through the false strategy, it serves the same purpose as the true trap regardless)

6. Battery reveal (the true trap - proves Chuck’s condition is purely mental)

7. Huell shielding (flows the focus/blame through multiple agents, notably ending in Jimmy’s target — Heull —> Jimmy —> Chuck)
