People who rank Jimmy McGill (Saul Goodman) above Walter White and Gustavo Fring, why?

Yes, Jimmy has great social intelligence. Yes, Chicanery's plan and the Sandpiper strategy are good. Yes, Goodman managed to reduce his 80-year sentence to 7.

But none of that comes close to Walter and Gus's feats.

Let's start with the basics. James is far more impulsive than Walter and has a very low stress tolerance. Walter and Gus are often criticized for having poor antifeats (which I don't consider to be as bad as they're made out to be), but James is on another level. He's genuinely incapable of controlling his own emotions (as we see when Chuck easily rage-baited him), he's bad at thinking about the immediate consequences of his actions, and, most importantly, he chose to work with Walter and Jessie, a criminal who, at the time, was just a meth cook with cancer, lacking any experience or influence, and an undisciplined drug addict. That was a recipe for disaster, and Goodman had no realistic way of reasoning or predicting how intelligent Walter was or that he would become the biggest drug empire in America.

Narratively speaking, he doesn't have much influence and isn't that good a lawyer. In fact, he has to promote himself on television and lacks credibility; we can infer that he doesn't deal with very important people or cases more complicated than Walter's.

Now, in comparison, Walter is a brilliant chemist who, in two years, was able to create the largest cartel in America with a small group of people, with the DEA right by his side and completely unsuspecting, all while battling cancer and without any initial support, in addition to being a drug addict. Walter not only possesses exceptional fluid-crystallized intelligence and superior self-management, but he also has far better intelligence, thought processes, and reasoning than Saul, allowing him to see Gustavo's weakness with Hector, while James couldn't see any use for him. His Lily of the Valley plan is superior and carries less risk than any scheme Jimmy ever devised.

Speaking of Gustavo, there's no fair comparison with him. Gus is completely underrated and is easily the smartest character in Breaking Bad/Better Call Saul. Gus managed to build a perfect reputation, escape all suspicion, build a much more prolific and sustainable empire than Walter, create an unfathomable money laundering and smokescreen, manipulate and destroy the Mexican cartel, and would have been indestructible and impossible to stop if it hadn't been for Walter. Unlike Walter, Gustavo didn't leave a single loose end and didn't make any serious mistakes. What's more, Gustavo is superior to Saul in Social Skills, supposedly his best attribute. Gus definitely deserves better treatment from SCD.
