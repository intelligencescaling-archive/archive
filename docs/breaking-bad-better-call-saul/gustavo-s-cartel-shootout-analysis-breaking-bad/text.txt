Gustavo’s Cartel Shootout Analysis

This is a feat from Breaking Bad S3 EP2 “Caballo sin Nombre” S3 EP6 “Sunset” S3 EP7 “One Minute” and S3 EP8 “I See You”

He wants to ensure the cartel doesn’t overtake his empire in the Southwest so the cartel still needs to rely on him. His plan to make sure it doesn’t happen is to create an active shootout with the twins and the DEA, drawing publicity directly to the DEA so they crack down on the Cartel’s Southwest supply. His second objective within this plan is to have the DEA kill the twins to damage Hector Salamanca psychologically and protect Walter White. His backup plan is to use Mike to eliminate any surviving twin in the hospital.

Part One: V V V

The Salamanca twins, hired by Hector Salamanca, are chasing down Walter White to eliminate him for supposedly eliminating Tuco. Gus can’t have this because Walter White is now working as his chemist, and Gale hasn’t learned Walter’s formula. So he directly confronts them and tells them not to murder W.W, as he would eventually do it himself after Gale learns the formula.

The twins wait. But they eventually get tired of waiting, and they attempt a hit on W.W Gus has Mike watch Walter’s house beforehand, anticipating the Salamanca twins would get tired of waiting. Mike sees the twins arrive beforehand and alerts Gus by telling one of Gus’s henchmen what was happening through a phone call, and Gus texts the twins to go to Pollos, making them call it off.

After this, Gus realises this revenge plot actually works in his favor, and he meets the twins, revealing that W.W didn’t eliminate Tuco—DEA Agent Hank Schrader did—to make the twins target Hank. Gus makes sure they go after him by dismissing their policy of not targeting the DEA, stating that because they are in his territory, he decides who gets permission.

Part Two: V V V

He then, off-screen, has Mike that day keep track of Hank going to the mall and, afterward, the Salamanca twins heading to the mall, and report to him when the Salamanca twins are close to the mall. Gus then has Victor use a burner phone with a voice changer to call Hank and warn him that the Salamanca twins are coming to murder him and are one minute away so he will be prepared, making sure to be vague and straightforward so Hank doesn’t think of it as a joke.

By doing this, he ensured the unknown hit the Salamanca twins were going to perform on Hank now turned into a shootout. He also made sure that nothing gave away that he was the one helping Hank, concealing his identity by using Victor, a burner phone, and a voice changer.

Part Three: V V V

After the successful shootout, he made sure that the surviving twin, Leonel Salamanca, would be eliminated to ensure no loose ends, having Mike administer a lethal injection to Leonel in the hospital where he was being treated via a syringe while no one was watching. Gus most likely had the syringe made incase of this predicament and made sure Mike discarded it in a sharps bin for proper concealment. Meanwhile, Gus acts oblivious to Walter wanting answers, concealing his involvement once more, and has Walt shake his hand and thank him while he walks off, so it appears to the naked eye that Walter is thanking Gus instead of asking about the situation.

By doing all of this, he accomplished his plan, making the DEA crack down on the cartel and psychologically damaging Hector, all while keeping his involvement in the shootout unknown.

Extra Information: V V V

Walter White confronts Gus about all of this afterward, and since he had psychoanalysed Gus long enough to understand how pragmatic he was, he predicted that it was all Gus’s doing.

Conclusion: V V V

He ensured his supremacy in the Southwest remained intact. He removed the threat to Walter White. He used the twins against the DEA to make them crack down on the cartel. And he used the DEA against the twins to psychologically damage Hector Salamanca—all in a single move.
