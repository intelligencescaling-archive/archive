Walter White’s abduction of the One Minute strategy

Many people say WW’s only good at crystalized intelligence and manipulation. Of course, this is a notion I completely disagree with, and to show my point of view today I’ll analyze Walter’s reasoning, an overlooked aspect of his skills within the realm of outsmarting.

Do note that most of this inference is completely explained and shown to us in this scene ( https://youtu.be/eLbE_05rS1A?si=--xJGM5sXri4JG9z , writing this from my phone so I can’t put more than one attachment on a post 💔). I’ve only added premises that Walter must’ve logically handled to come to his conclusions, which is why I’ve added why he thought he would’ve naturally been the prime target of the cousins, amongst some other things.

Context: Tuco’s cousins are trying to kill Walter as revenge for their family. Gus and Juan Bolsa haven’t permitted it, given that the former is in a fruitful business relationship with Heisenberg, yet the cousins still pester. They show up at Los Pollos Hermanos, constantly intimidating clients and putting Gus’ empire in jeopardy as a form of protest.

To protect Walter and get rid of the twins while gaining influence and power(amongst other goals), Gus decides to approach his future victims, goading them into killing Hank Schrader, an action he deliberately decides to take despite Bolsa’s orders.

After the twins approach Hank, Gus sends a call to the agent with the intention to create a gunfight, a struggle which would ultimately make the DEA and police fight back against the Mexican cartel, closing the supply of methamphetamine and information, which would allow Gus to kill Juan Bolsa while simultaneously monopolizing the entire American drug market to himself.

Walter only quite knows about the hit on Hank and how the hitmen are Tuco’s cousins and also see him as a target. This later changes when Hank tells everyone that before the attack he was given an anonymous tip about the attempt on his life. After hearing this, Walter analyzes the information given and comes to a strikingly accurate conclusion. Here is the abductive schema I believed Walter used.

————————————

P1: Hank has been targeted in an assassination attempt by Tuco’s cousins coming from Mexico.

P2: Hank’s only interaction with the Mexican cartel was engaging in a gunfight and killing Tuco Salamanca.

C1: These cartel members were trying to kill Hank as a form of revenge for Tuco’s death.

P3: Tuco kidnapped me and Jesse before dying. While at a safe house in the dessert, he explained to his cousins- Hank’s assasins- that he wanted to be picked up and mentioned me and Jesse.

P4: Before dying, Tuco thought someone had ratted him out due to Gonzo’s death, which he did not know off.

P5: Tuco thought it was Gonzo who did so, but the only proof he had was Gonzo not being incarcerated, a factor that also applied to Walter and Jesse, which Tuco only brushed off due to his “lie detector” tactic.

P6: After Hank killed Tuco, he must’ve followed basic police procedure and went inside of the shack, meaning he talked to Hector and explained he’s from the DEA.

P7: Hector is lucid and able to communicate.

C2: Hector most probably assumed I was a DEA informant and told the cartel, which would explain why Hank appeared.

P8: Despite cartels being extremely intolerant of betrayal and especially regarding the police, I was never targeted. Instead, Hank was.

P9: Hank being targeted is almost improbable, given that going after a DEA agent is extremely counterproductive due to the police and since there was a person involved in Tuco’s death who’s hit would be far easier to execute and would have essentially no negative consequences for the cartel.

C3: Something or someone must’ve diverged the hitmen to Hank.

P10: Before the fight broke out, Hank received a tip from an anonymous caller saying that he’d be attacked by two men in one minute.

C4: This person wanted a gunfight to break out.

P11: After this fight, the DEA and Mexican government retaliated, applying more investigation and border security, cutting the supply of methamphetamine from Mexico to the U.S.

P12: Gus is a kingpin in ABQ who distributes and provides methamphetamine in the U.S.

P13: I am Gus’ top meth cook and my product outclasses any other in the market.

P14: Gus Immediatly approached the DEA and police right after the attack on Hank, offering a 10.000$ reward on any information regarding the case. An action that would, undoubtedly, bring a lot of attention from the public and thus, the police, to the case.

**Final conclusion: Gustavo Firing orchestrated the hit on Hank as part of a scheme to shield me and monopolize the American drug market to himself.**

————————————

Here we clearly see Walter use bits and pieces of information to abduct a well concealed strategy down to its logistics and goals. It’s also noteworthy that on the scene I provided Walter uses this inference to his favor, saying he would’ve done the same in Gus’ situation as a tactic to shape his relationship with him into one more akin to that of two peers, while simultaneously following this tactic up with him asking for more protection to him and his family, a request that’s far more enticing to accept once you see the person asking for it as a peer and as someone with leverage.
