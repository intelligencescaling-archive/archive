# Minoru Tanaka (Death Note) VS Juan Bolsa (BRBA/BCS)

Psychology, social intelligence, mentality, reasoning, manipulation, deception, counteraction, field skills, counteraction: **The Structured Man**

Thinking (>=), scheming, foresight: **A God who backed down**

Cognition, sensory: **Undecided**. (Cognition due to Minoru’s inconsistent scaling, sensory via both having pretty much nothing).

Yes. This is an actual take of mine.

After thoroughly reanalyzing Breaking Bad and especially Better Call Saul, I have come to the conclusion that Juan Bolsa, contrary to popular belief, is not an oblivious idiot but a very cunning and competent individual. I currently have him comfortably on the top 9 in the verse and above some other bottom tiers.

Dare I say, he’s close to low tier, a sentiment I share with Minoru.

I’m unsure of this matchup’s winner, but I do have Minoru steamrolling in categories like scheming and foresight while Bolsa far outclasses the former in psychology or reasoning.

I’m not going to start a Bolsa agenda or anything, but this big buff for him has consequently upscales Lalo and especially Gus’ positions for me, which is why I’m sharing this post.

To start, I’ll ask some simple questions. Ponder on these for a while and ask yourself why Bolsa does these actions.

1. Why did the cartel only strike back against Gustavo’s attempt at monopolization after weeks or even months of Fring’s DEA scheme concluding with Bolsa’s death? The picture in slide four is a screenshot of the S3 E8 script “I See You.” It actually provides us a scrapped line about Bolsa having an important police connection in the form of his cousin.

2. In episode 1 of season 5 in Better Call Saul, Bolsa explains to Lalo that Eladio essentially holds no grudge for minor betrayals and secretive behavior against the cartel’s honor. If he is aware of Eladio’s manipulation and facade, why does he play along with it in episode 10 of season 5, acting annoyed when Eladio brings up the fact that Lalo has brought in more money than Gus?

3. Why is Bolsa the one to tell Hector about Lalo’s supposed death instead of Leonel and Marco or even Joaquin Salamanca?

4. Why is Bolsa’s police chief brother in charge of getting information from Lalo’s autopsy report despite Eladio being the one who put a bounty on Nacho’s head and seemingly the one in charge of the investigation?

5. Why do zero serious consequences arise in Bolsa’s way after the Colombian gang he hired to disrupt Lalo’s bail gets brutally murdered in the dessert?

6. Why does Gus immediately shut down the French engineer in season 4 once he mentions building a tunnel in El Paso?

7. Why does Lalo choose to be smuggled out of Mexico by a group of people unaffiliated to the cartel who don’t know him?

And most importantly,

**8. Why does Lalo, despite the MOUNTAIN of circumstantial evidence he has or could have about Gus’ schemes against the cartel,** >!(Gus shielding the laundromat, one of Werner’s men confessing about the secret underground lab and its logistical work, the very bad coverup story Gus provided for Werner Ziegler)!<** ****NEVER try to bring up the evidence to Eladio simply because Bolsa would back Fring up?**

Here’s my hottest take: Bolsa was more useful to Gus’ disposal of Lalo than quite literally any other of his proxies. Saul, Mike and, dare I even say Nacho didn’t really help Gus as much as Juan did. Plus, the other three had their downsides which were exploited. Lalo never does this with Bolsa, he literally couldn’t find a way to fend him off. Season 5 was Gus and Bolsa vs Lalo.
