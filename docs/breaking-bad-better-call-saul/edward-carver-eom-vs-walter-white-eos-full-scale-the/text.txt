# OVERALL: Walter White LOW-MID DIFFS (+)

Wooooo, I’ve had this take for a WHILE. I love this matchup, Walter being the good guy is always fun because he’d be extremely self righteous about winning like he was with Tuco acting like he isn’t almost as bad.

Anyways, The Poughkeepsie Tapes did not disappoint me in the horror department, but I expected something better from one of *the* “genius mastermind horror villains.” Maybe Kramer has something better in store.

Walt is ultimately just infinitely better. Walter just eclipses Ed in any offensive category. Although Carver’s insane terrain exploitation, concealment and profiling skills would be of trouble for Walter I don’t believe for a second he has enough raw scheming potential to break through the layers of contingencies that Walter applies in his Felina version. That paired up with Heisenberg’s insane  adaptability and I can’t really see Carver getting a good hit on Walt that ends the fight.

On the other hand, Walter has far superior resource allocation, social engineering, proxy management and control under a more contingent field. He could most probably reason out anything Carver tries to set up and then use it agains him.

No matter how many factions or different elements in the field there are, Walter just has better control over them via more potent offensive moves and a more sensible approach. Think back to Felina and how Walter uses the main four factors in his adversity against each other providing around four layers of invincibility to his scheme. Framework is another factor, too. The police in TPT is *ridiculously* incompetent. We’re not given any information about their process and there’s no elaborate traps Edward has to evade. His process is also entirely contingent on a criminal police officer not dying or getting arrested in a whole decade which can be pretty damn sad. And also, his psychological breaking isn’t that impressive when you take into account the whole decade of timeframe he had.

Compare that to what Walter pulled off in Casa Tranquila for example (Gus as his intellectual opposition, having basically 0 resources vs dozens of henchmen, insane info gathering and influence, knowledge about past actions and a tailor made profile of Walter, the DEA has to not find out about him). Despite all of this Walter pulls off an insane scheme with around 7 layers of complex manipulations with multiple holistic connections to each other all in a day’s work.

Also, Walter has two insane aces under his sleeve: crystalized intelligence and unpredictability. His crazy knowledge application can work in so many situations and with how unorthodox Walter’s methods are I can see Edward having a problem with them given how mid his counteraction is.
