-Planning-

Coverage

- First he learnt cell crystallisation.
- To practise this, he bought an ice truck.
- Then he killed a prostitute, cut her into pieces and froze her body.
- He chose a place to display these parts and put them on display.
- Then he did the same with another prostitute.
- Then he did the same to another prostitute and so he killed a total of 3 prostitutes.
- The reason for this is to become a serial killer and attract Dexter's attention.
- A few days after his last victim, he killed another one, dismembered her and froze her body.
- Again he chose a place to display the parts, but this time he kept the victim's head for himself.
- A day later, at night, he followed Dexter in an ice truck.
- When Dexter began to follow him, he made a U-turn, switched on the high beams, so that Dexter could not see him from the driver's seat.
- He then threw the victim's head out the window and fled.
- Then he sneaks into Dexter's apartment.
- He brought a Barbie doll with him. Before he arrived, he painted all of its fingers with different nail polish.
- He took it apart and put the pieces in the fridge.
- So he sent Dexter a friendly message.
- A few days later, he killed another prostitute and cut off her hand.
- He painted her fingers with different nail polish.
- He froze her hand in an ice cube, and put it in his truck.
- Then he left it lying around for the police to find, and the police found it. Brian, of course, cleaned the truck top to bottom and left no evidence.
- Brian again broke into Dexter's apartment and stole parts of a Barbie doll.
- Brian again killed a prostitute and followed the same procedure as the others, but this time he planned to put the pieces in the middle of a hockey stadium.
- Of course, Brian was aware of the cameras, so he used Tony Tucci, a security guard at the stadium, to accomplish what he had planned.
- Brian forcefully got Tucci to place the parts. Brian wasn't on camera when he did this.
- Brian could have broken the cameras directly, but he didn't. because it would have been in his best interest to make Tony Tucci the prime suspect for a little while.
- Brian, of course, didn't pick up this prostitute in his own car. He picked her up in Neil Perry's car, an important person we'll get to later, a wood-panelled station wagon.
- This time Brian left a man's hand on a beach.
- This choice of location is not random. Brian went through his childhood photos in Dexter's apartment, and he chose this beach because he saw a photo of Dexter's foster parents.
- This hand is Tony Tucci's hand. Even if it was only for a few days, Brian was able to frame Tony as the killer.
- Now Brian leaves Tony's foot on a football pitch.
- Again, this choice of location is not random. It's a place Brian found from Dexter's childhood photographs.
- Brian draws a smile on one of the photographs.
- After the last two events, Dexter looked at his photos and saw this smile, went to the place in the photo, but could not find anything.
- The reason for this is that Brian diverted Dexter's attention to another place, making it safe for him to actually go to another place.
- Brian has now put Tony's other foot in a pumpkin patch.
- A place Dexter used to go to when he was a kid.
- Brian has drawn a smile on another photograph. This is a photograph of Dexter with his stepfather in front of a hospital.
- When Dexter arrives at this hospital, he finds Tony in the basement.
- Just then Brian takes a photograph of Dexter and runs away.
- Brian plants this photo in Dexter's apartment, but Dexter burns it.
- Brian starts working as a prosthetist in a hospital under the name Rudy Cooper.
- To do this, Brian has to learn the job beforehand and create a false identity.
- Brian makes and fits Tony with prosthetic legs.
- Brian now manipulates Neil Perry, the man I mentioned earlier, into talking to the police.
- Neil claims he saw someone at the hospital, but the police won't listen to him.
- Brian continues to watch Dexter after that. He witnesses Dexter kill two people and also sees him throw the bodies of the people he killed into the sea.
- Brian pulls the bodies out of the sea and places them back where Dexter killed them.
- Brian then calls the police here, but does not aim to catch Dexter.
- Brian goes to Dexter's house and draws a smiley face on a blood sample he took from one of the people Dexter killed, saying that he dug up the body.
- Seeing this, Dexter realises that this is a clue. Dexter takes the blood of a person he killed, and takes the fingerprints of the other person he killed and places them on a knife, making him look like the killer.
- Brian kills someone again and puts the parts on display. He manipulates Neil into going to the display.
- Neil, who appears at two crime scenes, becomes a suspect for the police.
- The police go to Neil's house and find out that he is doing research on cell crystallisation, etc. It was also well thought out that Neil would be the one to be portrayed as the killer. Brian realised Neil's desire for fame, so he used him to make him look like the killer and both sides got what they wanted. Neil, who was already an aggressive person, seems as fit for the crimes that the Ice Truck Killer committed.
- Finally, Brian manipulates Neil to be caught sleeping with a prostitute and the police are sure that he is the killer.
- During these events, Brian became lovers with Dexter's half-sister Debra.
- Also during these events, Dexter posted a message on the internet when Brian did not contact him for a long time.
- Although this message was only about Barbies, Brian realised that it was from Dexter and responded.
- Brian, who said in the message that they would soon get involved, started to get to work.
- He killed Dexter's biological father, and left the entire inheritance to Dexter.
- Brian accomplished this by going to Dexter's father's house dressed as a mechanic and killing him there.
- Dexter is thus forced to go to his inherited home. Knowing this, Brian tells Debra that they should be with Dexter and Brian goes there.
- When Dexter arrives at the morgue, he learns that his father died of a heart attack, but since he is not sure of this, he secretly takes blood from his father and tells his friend Masuka to test this blood.
- The DNA test results come back and it is revealed that there is a sedative in his father's system.
- Dexter then goes to the hospital at night to examine his father but cannot find him. Brian had realised that Dexter is going to come at night so Brian cremated the body. He then picks up Dexter when he leaves the hospital and drives him home.
- In the next step of his plan, Brian drained the blood of the 5 people he had killed, and dumped all the blood in a hotel room 103.
- Brian then sent the key to this room to the police in a jar full of blood. The police were intrigued by the jar full of blood, so they went to the hotel and saw the room.
- Dexter is called into the room, but he collapses from the blood. There's a reason for this. When Dexter and Brian were young, their mother was murdered in front of them, and they were both trapped in a container full of blood for days. Brian triggered Dexter's trauma and sent him another message. Now you might say that Dexter should have realised that Brian was the killer after that, but no, because Dexter is so young that he only remembers that he and his mother were there.
- When the police examined the room, they kept seeing the number 103. This 103 actually stands for 10.3, which is 3 October. 3 October 1973 is the date on which a newspaper shared that Dexter's mother had died.
- Of course, Neil Perry is released after this.
- After this, a detective named Batista, while in a nightclub, sees a woman with all her fingers painted with different nail polish. The woman says to him "1 year ago a man saw my fake hand and got horny, he started to worship it, and he painted all my fingers with different nail polish, and that's what I've been doing ever since". Batista comes to Brian's office to enquire about prosthetic joints. Realising that Batista is up to something, Brian steals Batista's card.
- As Batista is about to get into his car in a car park, Brian comes from behind and stabs him, but at the same time, he gets a cut on his lip.
- Brian finds the woman who gave Batista this information and kidnaps her. He then videotapes himself killing her and does to her what he does to everyone else. He then displays the pieces and the tape in a place.
- He also makes a clock in the place where he exhibits the pieces 01:03.
- Brian invites Debra to dinner on a private yacht and they go to the yacht.
- While they are on the yacht, Dexter finds blood on Batista and when he compares it to Brian, it comes back positive. Dexter begins efforts to find Debra.
- While on the yacht, Brian knocks Debra out with a drug-laced alcohol and ties her up.
- Before coming here, Brian left a cassette tape called "Born Free" at his house. "Born Free" means born free, and symbolises the rebirth of Dexter and Brian after the deaths of their mothers. Dexter sees this when he arrives at the apartment in an attempt to find Debra, and goes to the container where his mother died. But in fact this is just an attempt to buy time.
- During this time, Brian carries Debra to Dexter's mother's house and ties her to a table there.
- Realising that the meaning of number 103 is not actually connected to the container but directly to his mother, Dexter goes to his mother's house.
- When Dexter arrives, Brian approaches him from behind and knocks him unconscious.

There is no need to write what happens after this because it does not go according to Brian’s plan (because Dexter has plot armour 😭).
Accuracy
Nearly everything Brian had planned had gone exactly right until Dexter arrived at his mum's house (it’s all because of plot armour fr).
Intricacy
This plan is actually a very complicated plan. The plan is designed in such a way that only Dexter can understand it, so it is practically impossible for anyone else to solve it. The fact that there are fake suspects, many different crimes, fake identities, etc. in this plan has made the plan much more complicated.
Preparation Process
He learned how cell crystallisation works. And that's because he was looking for a way to freeze his victims. The other thing he does is he gets an ice truck. This way, he can do cell crystallisation, but he can also be on the move all the time, making it less likely that he will be caught. Later, Brian intentionally exposed the ice truck and built a special ice house in part of his house so that he could do the same thing. A few episodes later, we see Brian working as a prosthetist in a hospital. Brian needs to learn this job for this.
Contingencies
I didn’t write this part where I explained coverage because Brain (who was literally controlling the whole season) failed at the end because of a simple trick (it’s all because of plot armour 😭). Anyways, Brian knew Dexter had studied martial arts, so in the possibility of Dexter trying to fight him when they face each other, Brian developed fighting skills too, and he managed to overpower Dexter when Dexter tried to attack him.
Aim
His goal was to get Dexter to work with him.

-Strategy-
Efficiency
Brian executed his strategy perfectly, even on low resources. He attracted Dexter's attention with a few dead prostitutes and an ice truck even without having an influence, then continued the murders through an icehouse in his house, and finally led Dexter to his mother's house using only random items with the numbers 103 on them.
Shielding
Brian has never left evidence on crime scenes or anything else and so has never been caught. His use of different things instead of communicating directly with Dexter made him untraceable. Instead of just one item, he wrote 103 on many items in many different places, ensuring that Dexter would recognise the trick. In order for his encounter with Dexter to take place, he became lovers with Debra and met Dexter many times by using her. When Batista started to get information from a girl Brian was talking to, he defended his strategy by both injuring Batista and killing the girl.
Foresight
He foresaw that Dexter would understand the meaning of the message sent by the barbie toy and stay in contact with him. After leaving the truck lying around, he foresees that the police will find it. When Dexter realised that the 2 crime scenes were places he had visited in his childhood, he foresaw that Dexter would look at the other photos. He foresaw that Dexter would go back to the hospital after the DNA test result. After the bloodbath in the hotel room, he foresaw that Dexter would think of his mother's death. He foresaw that after writing the number 103 on many items, Dexter would realise that this number led to his mother's house.
Strategy Effectiveness
Although his goal of working with Dexter failed, Brian still got the chance to meet with him even for a short time.
Execution
Brian perfectly planned and executed everything he thought of.
Control
Brian was constantly in control of Debra and Dexter in this strategy, even when he did not directly contact them (bro is Red John), and by controlling them he made Dexter come to his mother’s house and nearly achieved his ultimate goal.
