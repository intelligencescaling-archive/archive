Debunk
The argument clearly contains gaps. First, the proposition falls under the scope of ECREE (Extraordinary Claims Require Extraordinary Evidence). The claim that Dexter performed such a precise count is highly absurd. We've also explained this by pointing out the excessively high Hz value of his brain. So, how does this debunk the feat? Let's explain: Can you prove that Dexter actually made a completely error-free count? According to ECREE, you need to prove this, because the claim you're making is extremely extraordinary, and extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence. There is no narrative or statement at any point in the show that proves Dexter's count is completely accurate. Under normal conditions, if Dexter truly counted second by second or minute by minute, there should be a deviation of approximately 4 days, according to the formulas. So, it should have been 43 days since his brother died, not 39. And what about while sleeping? How did he manage to continuously maintain such a precise measurement while sleeping? This isn't impossible; if the brain continues this count unconsciously, it's theoretically possible for the count to continue during the REM phase. However, the human brain is not constantly in the REM phase, and keep in mind that subconscious processes are not sufficiently correlated with cognitive ability.
