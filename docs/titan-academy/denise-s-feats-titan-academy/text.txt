Denise from Titan Academy

Denise is a character from the Titan Academy Youtube video series. She is a student from class T1-T5. Ofc the series is like semi-satirical, semi-serious, it just depends.

She basically almost always scores 100 on her exams and finishes before anyone else most of the time. The two times she got a 99/100 her mother constantly berated her for it (Asian parent stereotypes)

She got 98% on the Putnam Exam according to a video, which is out of 120, so it would be 118/120 approximately. The average score is anywhere from 8-13/120 and the median score is 0-2/120. Top individual scores often are 80-90/120 with only a very select group of people ever getting a perfect score. Only 5 people got one from 2021-2024 in real life. Btw they intentionally used this exam as a plot point when Mr. Alan from the Organization wanted to send her to the elite classes and she got 98% on this so she clearly would have gone to those but nah Mr. Alan wanted a perfect score on THIS.
