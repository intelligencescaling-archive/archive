Screw it, I want to talk about Michael's Plan in Season 2 of The Good Place-

Firstly, MAJOR SPOILERS FOR THE GOOD PLACE up until the halfway point of Season 2.

Don't let your eyes decieve you. The Good Place is a masterpiece of a show disguised as a sitcom that literally changed my life. It is riddled with plot twists and has a Massive overarching narrative that full completes itself over 4 Seasons where literally Every Episode Matters.

The Season 1 Finale is also famous for having a mind shattering plot twist that completely recontexualizes the whole Show and makes you wonder where they would possibly go from here after literally nuking the entire status quo. (Before Seasons 2, 3, and 4 decide to nuke the status quo once every few Episodes or so).

Anyway, Let's go-

SET UP-

SPOILERS AHEAD-

Michael is a >!Demon!< , part of an immensely powerful alien race that exists beyond the physical realm. For reasons that are never revealed (or perhaps no reason at all beyond simple boredom), these creatures have taken it upon themselves to give us Humans an afterlife.

After creating an immensely flawed system that calculates human morality, humans are judged based on everything they do on Earth. Every action, every word, every thought. The more good you are, the more points you get, and the more bad you are, the more points you Lose.

Once a human dies, if their point total is approaching: "a million or whatever," they go to The Good Place, where all their wishes will be granted.

If they fail to reach this high threshold, they are sent to The Bad Place where they are tortured for their misdeeds for eternity.

Michael has recently been promoted to built his own section of The Bad Place and design his own torture techniques.

Having become bored of the basic: "lava and the bees and the lighting that tears off their flesh," Michael decides to try a new concept- Psycological torture.

Michael constructs a neighborhood based entirely around the hope that the 4 real humans put into it will be perfect for torturing each other psychologically. They won't know they are in The Bad Place, and will Believe it is The Good Place, however the other inhabitants (Demons in disguise) and Michael will think on their feet and come up with scenarios that will push them to their limits of sanity, all while the 4 humans' personalities continue to clash and torture each other on their own.

This is all before Season 1 by the way.

Season 1 covers the first attempt. Things go well for a couple of months until Eleanor (one of the 4 humans) comes to the realization of what's really going on. For a place that's supposed to be heaven, things have just been way too awful for her and the 3 other real humans, and thus, this must all actually be The Bad Place in disguise.

Michael is at first gleefully astonoshed to see his plan has been seen through, and is excited to erase their memories and try again.

However, the head Demon, Shaun, claims that he is only allowed one more attempt, as not a lot of faith and Way too much effort was put in to make this whole neighborhood to begin with, all for the sake of 4 people. If Michael fails again, he will be retired and essentially be burned alive for eternity.

Despite Michael's best attempts, the humans continue to deduce they are in The Bad Place within a year, forcing Michael to reset everything again, and just convince the other Demons not to tell Shaun.

This goes on for almost 300 years and 802 attempts.

After a rebellion from the Demons, threatening to expose Michael's failure to Shaun, and the humans figuring out the ploy yet again within barely a week, Michael decides to team up with the humans. He will reset the neighborhood one more time, but this time, it'll be the Demons in the dark. They will Believe that they have control over the Neighborhood this time, but in reality, the humans will have their memories in tact this time around and only Pretend to be oblivious, all while working with Michael to try and find a way to find safety in the Real Good Place.

This goes well and the humans and Michael end up forming a real bond as they all become genuinely better people, Michael included.

Then, Shaun shows up.

It turns out Shaun truly believes Michael's "second attempt" at the Neighborhood has been a resounding success and wants Michael promoted.

Now, will Michael take up this offer? Or will he remain loyal to his human friends?

THE PLAN-

Michael decides to remain loyal to his human friends.

The trouble is- How can he do this while also pretending to be loyal to Shaun so they don't all get sent to the real hell?

Michael decides to make use of every element we have seen of the Show so far.

Michael brings the humans into his office and, in front of Shaun, "reveals" to them that they are in The Bad Place.

He makes fun of Chidi (one of the humans)'s love for philosophers, including Kierkegaard.

Michael's hope is that mentioning Kierkegaard specifically, who was well known for writing a book called: 'Leap of Faith,' will clue into the humans that they should trust Michael and take a leap of faith themselves.

Later in the day, during a: 'Comedy Roast,' Michael roasts each of the 4 humans, while also leaving them a crucial hint on what to do.

To Jason, he makes fun of his favorite football Player, but deliberately misnames: 'Blake Bortles,' as: 'Derek Bortles.'

You see, the Neighborhood had a hyper intelligent AI like individual called Janet who helped the humans. She however has had metal handcuffs places on her by Shaun, which takes away her abilities and makes her mind act as though she is drunk, so that she can't save the humans.

However, Janet had very recently made Another, less advanced version of her, named Derek, who has been put away recently and Shaun is obvious about.

The hint is for the humans to go find Derek and ask for help.

To Tahani, he makes fun of her live of parties and claims everyone would leave early.

Tahani knows that at her extravagant parties, no one is supposed to leave until the last song of the party plays.

The hint is to wait until the last song of the night plays during the Demons' party, before getting Derek to call an escape train.

To Eleanor, Michael makes fun of her belief that she deserved a: "Medium Place," when in reality, she belongs here.

The hint is that the humans should Call the escape train, but Not board it and instead, stay here, as the Demons will undoubtedly go to search for them once they see the train is called.

To Chidi, he makes fun of his life for the Trolly Problem, and claims his Students probably wishes He would end up under the Trolly.

The hint is for the humans to hide Under the train, so that the Demons don't find them as they take off to search for where they went when the train leaves.

Eleanor figures this all out.

During the Demon party, Michael taunts Vicki (a demon who believes she is better at torturing humans than Michael, and want to expose Michael's lies to Shaun if she doesn't end up promoted for her work on this Neighborhood), by whispering into Janet's ear:

"Something, Something, Vicki, Something, Something."

This is done to comedic effect, so that when Vicki asks Janet what Michael is saying to her, and she, in her drunken state, repeats those 5 exact words, it makes it look as though Janet is incapable of reciting back exactly what Michael is saying, causing her to attempt to take off her handcuffs and ask the other Demons to help. She is unable to get the handcuffs off.

When the last song plays, the train is called and the humans hide underneath it as it is sent away.

Shaun is annoyed, as it looks as though the humans have escaped, and Michael pretends to panic. He calls another train so that the humans remain hidden.

Vicki then attempts to expose Michael to Shaun, but another Demon reveals that she tried to take the handcuffs off of Janet.

Michael uses this to frame Vicki into being a Traitor for trying to sabotage him the whole time.

Shaun, disgusted by this accusation, puts Vicki in a cacoon where she can't speak, just as she is trying to reveal the truth about Michael's constant reboots of the Neighborhood.

Michael then asks a Bad Place version of Janet to scan the Neighborhood for the humans. She does so, scanning the whole Neighborhood (minus the train right behind them), and finds no Humans, leading Shaun and the other Demons to leave on the newly called train, leaving Michael on his own, tasked with taking down the Neighborhood in order to search every last square inch for the humans.

As the train leaves, the humans can finally sit back up, and Michael reveals that he was indeed on their side, and that their leap of faith has paid off.

Michael claims that he had actually left them 1,200 other clues on what to do throughout the day, and did so because he knew humans to be significantly dumber than Demons, and be is glad that, despite them only having picked up on 5 (and All by Eleanor), it was enough to get them to follow the plan.

That's it.

I dunno. I think it's a pretty cool plan. Sounds like something Yuichi would do if he was... A good person.
