Harry Potter feat list

Harry Potter Intelligence feats:

**You're a wizard, Harry** \- **Hagrid to Harry**

(The Philosopher’s Stone) He solved the Mirror of Erised puzzle by understanding that the mirror revealed a person’s deepest desire and that the only way to retrieve the Stone was to want to find it, but not to use it. This feat showcases \[EU (Emotional Understanding) LGT (Logical Thinking) and NLT (Non Linear Thinking)\].

(The Goblet of Fire and The Order of the Phoenix) He resisted the Imperius Curse, overcoming it after initially failing by applying mental discipline and self-awareness. He also overcame his fear of Voldemort, ultimately facing him without hesitation. This feat showcases \[MF (Mental Fortitude) SA (Self Awareness) ST (Stress Tolerance) and Perseverance\].

(The Philosopher’s Stone) He comes up with a lie on the spot about seeing himself winning the House Cup and shaking hands with Dumbledore to Voldemort/Quirrell keeping him distracted long enough for the Stone’s magic to activate. This feat showcases \[EU (Emotional Understanding) PM (Psychological Manipulation) AS (Acting Skills) IM (Individual Manipulation) DIM (Direct Manipulation) SOS (Social Skills) and Adaptability\].

(The Chamber of Secrets) Exploiting the law that house-elves can only be freed when their master gives them clothes, he hides his own sock in a book that Lucius was going to return to the elf, so when Lucius returns the book, it technically counts as him freeing Dobby. This feat showcases \[PS (Problem Solving) CT (Creative Thinking) and ST (Strategic Thinking)\].

(The Deathly Hallows) He figured out he was the true master of the Elder Wand by reasoning, based on Ollivander’s explanation that a wand’s allegiance changes when its master is defeated—not necessarily killed—that Draco Malfoy had disarmed Dumbledore before Dumbledore’s death, making Draco the wand’s master and since he disarmed Draco, he became the wand’s master. This feat showcases \[DR (Deductive Reasoning) PER (Perceptual Reasoning) LGT (Logical Thinking) LT (Linear Thinking) AT (Analytical Thinking) and PR (Pattern Recognition)\].

(The Prisoner of Azkaban) He realised Sirius Black was innocent and that Scabbers (Ron’s rat) was Peter Pettigrew. He pieced together from Lupin’s story, the Marauder’s Map, and Pettigrew’s missing body, recognizing that the map showing “Peter Pettigrew” in Ron’s pocket meant Scabbers was alive. This feat showcases \[DR (Deductive Reasoning) PER (Perceptual Reasoning) LGT (Logical Thinking) AT (Analytical Thinking) NLT (Non Linear Thinking) and PR (Pattern Recognition)\].

(The Goblet of Fire) He solved the Second Task by figuring out that “to recover what was taken” meant his friends would be underwater and used Gillyweed to breathe underwater. He also improvised alternative solutions for other champions under time pressure. This feat showcases \[VCI (Verbal Comprehension Index) PS (Problem Solving) CT (Creative Thinking) and ST (Strategic Thinking)\].

(The Goblet of Fire) During the graveyard duel with Voldemort. When surrounded and wandless allies were dead, He calculated his odds, timed a simultaneous spell Expelliarmus against Avada Kedavra, and used the *Priori Incantatem* effect to his advantage retreating tactically. This feat showcases \[WMI (Working Memory Index) and BI (Battle Intelligence)\].

(The Order of the Phoenix) Leading Dumbledore’s Army, he designed practical lessons to teach Defense Against the Dark Arts spells, tailored to students’ weaknesses, and simulated realistic combat situations while demonstrating solutions to those scenarios. This feat showcases \[EU (Emotional Understanding) and BI (Battle Intelligence)\]

(The Half Blood Prince) He reverse-engineered Snape’s handwritten potion instructions in the Half-Blood Prince’s textbook, correctly following advanced non-standard directions crossed-out notes, shorthand, experimental annotations to brew perfect potions. This feat showcases \[CT (Creative Thinking) NLT (Non Linear Thinking) and PR (Pattern Recognition)\].

(The Half Blood Prince) He Interpreted Snape’s memories after his death coming to the conclusion that Snape had been protecting him all along, that he himself was a Horcrux, and he had to sacrifice himself to destroy Voldemort’s soul fragment. This feat showcases \[DR (Deductive Reasoning) NLT (Non Linear Thinking) and PR (Pattern Recognition)\].

(The Half Blood Prince) He reasoned that Draco Malfoy was using the Room of Requirement and the Vanishing Cabinet. from Draco’s absences, emotional state, and specific use of Crabbe and Goyle as lookouts. This feat showcases \[EU (Emotional Understanding) DR (Deductive Reasoning) NLT (Non Linear Thinking) and PR (Pattern Recognition)\].

(The Deathly Hallows) He allowed himself to be struck by Avada Kedavra, knowing it would only destroy the final fragment of Voldemort’s soul. \[DM (Decision Making) Foresight PS (Problem Solving) CT (Creative Thinking) and ST (Strategic Thinking)\].

(The Deathly Hallows) He realized that the Hallows were real after figuring out Dumbledore had intentionally left him clues in the Snitch, the Sword, and the *Tales of Beedle the Bard*. This feat showcases \[DR (Deductive Reasoning) NLT (Non Linear Thinking) and PR (Pattern Recognition)\].

(The Half Blood Prince) He instinctively knew that Draco Malfoy was up to something secretive and dangerous at Hogwarts, even before gathering any concrete evidence. This feat showcases \[intuition and EU (Emotional Understanding)\].

He learned and mastered multiple subjects at Hogwarts, earning an Outstanding in Defence Against the Dark Arts, an Exceeds Expectations in Charms, Transfiguration, Potions, Care of Magical Creatures, and Herbology, and an Acceptable in Astronomy. This feat showcases \[LC (Learning Capability) EU (Emotional Understanding) BI (Battle Intelligence) LA (Learning Ability) Adaptability LGT (Logical Thinking) AT (Analytical Thinking) and Academics\]
