Factory Reset Theory | A Mr Robot SCD Analysis

**!! This post will contain spoilers for the entire Mr Robot TV show. Do NOT read any further if you want to remain unspoiled !!**

I mentioned yesterday that I was building a theory that makes Elliot Alderson from Mr Robot scale absurdly high. This post will be covering the basic idea/overview of this theory. Thank you all for the nice comments you left on my previous post. Without further ado, let's go over the basics.

First, we all know that Elliot Alderson is a character with Dissociate Identity Disorder also known as having "split personalities". The major twist at the ending of the show is that the "Elliot" we've been following all along is actually yet another personality of the actual Elliot Alderson known as "The Mastermind". The finale of the show features all the personalities of Elliot finally coming together and thus the "real Elliot" awakens at last.

But something has bugged me about Elliot's personalities and it's touched upon very, very briefly in the final episode. The Mastermind is the personality we've been focusing on the entire time but fake-Krista makes an interesting point: That The Mastermind is the only personality "that forgot that it was just another personality"

*The crucial implication here is that all the other personalities are self aware that they are just alternate personalities of the Real Elliot*

This is consistent with how they are portrayed within the show. One of Mr Robot's very first things he says to The Mastermind is "You're in a prison, Elliot. And I'm gonna break you out". This is in clear reference to him wanting to break out the Real Elliot that has been "imprisoned" in an utopian fantasy by The Mastermind shortly before the pilot episode.

So wait a minute, if Mr Robot was already aware of the prison that Real Elliot was "trapped" in and Mr Robot is self aware that he's just another personality, then does that mean that Mr Robot isn't actually acting on his own?

**Theory: The Real Elliot engineered the entire events of the show all the way from the pilot episode to Whiterose's Project being exploded in Washington Township in order to initiate a "Factory Reset" of Elliot's mind all in order to bring his personalities back together again so he can reawaken at last.**

Ok I jumped through a lot of hoops just now but hear me out.

Before I get into evidence of this theory, I want to make one thing clear. By "The Real Elliot", I don't mean the fake version of Elliot that's trapped within the utopian illusion that we see in the second-to-last episode. That version of Elliot is just a hypothetical version of himself if he never had DID or any mental health issues, which obviously the actual, real Elliot still has. So what I mean by "The Real Elliot" is more of an abstract idea, the actual brain/body the The Mastermind operates in where himself and all the other personalities reside. The Real Elliot essentially uses the personalities that *aren't* The Mastermind as sock puppets to talk through to try and get to The Mastermind himself.

Now, I don't mean that the other personalities have no minds of their own (because then they wouldn't be "split personalities"), I instead mean that they essentially have no free will of their own/they all build towards a common purpose facilitated and directed by Real Elliot. Again, this is because according to fake-Krista (aka just Elliot himself), all the other personalities are aware that they are other personalities. This means that they cannot possible have any true autonomy since they're ultimately aware of the purpose they serve as just mere aspects of the "Real Elliot".

 So, to recap:

* The Mastermind is just another personality/aspect of The Real Elliot. The Mastermind eventually grew so strong to the point where it forgot that it was another personality and became a mind of its own. The other personalities of Elliot (Mr Robot, child Elliot and his mother) were still beholden to the Real Elliot, but his mind had been hijacked by The Mastermind who had gain enough control over Elliot to forget Elliot's backstory entirely (forgetting his father, Darlene, his childhood trauma etc.) because those memories did not belong to The Mastermind.
* Whatever was left over from the Real Elliot that wasn't apart of his other split personalities became the Fake-Elliot that The Mastermind had subconsciously imprisoned within a never ending fantasy where he got to live out Elliot's perfect life. Again, this aspect of Elliot is often referred to as the "real Elliot" but this isn't true because again, this is a version of Elliot that has no mental health issues but the Real Elliot will forever and always have DID, making this impossible. Instead, this is the "leftovers" of the Real Elliot so to speak since The Mastermind didn't assimilate Mr Robot, his child self or his mother into his mind.
* The Real Elliot (AKA the will/shared subconscious of all personalities/aspects that aren't The Mastermind) then hitched a plan to take back control from The Mastermind in order to "reset" their shared brain so the Real Elliot can awaken again

Now being honest, you could just scale this plan entirely to Mr Robot as he's pretty much the main enforcer of this plan throughout the show. The only reason why I don't is because it's apparent that the Child-Elliot is also aware of the plan and helps execute it as well during S4E8. His mother personality is also aware of the plan as she refers to The Mastermind as "the other one".
