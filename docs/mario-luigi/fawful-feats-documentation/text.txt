Upon further analysis, I found a potential theory regarding Fawful’s bigger role in Partners in time. Fawful received a vision of a path that opened on the viewing platform, where Fawful described it as: “a new path… of doom!” Which implies Fawful, who had been wandering the sewers for two years knew about the ongoing news of E. Gadd’s time machine, the new alien species coming through from the past, as well as the time portals opening up through differing locations, one that Fawful was able to “envision” with an act akin to that of a mentalist or fortune teller. This would imply that Fawful was either:

  A. Truly psychic and possessed precognition.
 B. Used his technology to plant secret cameras across the castle.

Given Fawful’s character, I concluded that possibility B would have been the most likely scenario, which allowed for Fawful to gather as much information as he needed from Peach’s castle staff, the Mario Brothers, and Professor E. Gadd himself. This would effectively create a network Fawful would have established for both Peach’s side and Bowser’s side given his aforementioned role as a merchant selling badges to Bowser’s army.

This would enable Fawful to:

  1. Keep tabs on the current Mushroom Kingdom news, and what happened even behind what the news would cover, allowing for him to make a proper assessment as to the strange creatures coming through the time portals.
