Fluixon full character intelligence analysis . last post on fluixon

Fluixon, despite being the architect of the Fluixon conspiracy, ironically was himself the victim of another conspiracy—the Sidefall's Island 2 conspiracy of unification against Island 1.

That conspiracy drove Fluixon's decisions, either from paranoia or for the greater good of uniting Island 2.

*"Guys. Guys! Island 1 is uniting. They have hundreds of people all aligned toward the same goal: to kill us and take our resources. And if they decide to attack any single one of our nations, it'll be easy! All they'll need to do is walk ahead. Which is why we either need to merge all of our nations right now—we all choose a spot and combine into one civilization—or we attack Island 1 first."* **\[Strategy\] \[Foresight\]**

This issue caused a rift between Fluixon and Luminara, 3BelowZero (another leader), and other empires and nations, especially those who had reasonable responses about checking the situation, bridging Island 1 and 2, and providing aid. **\[Social Intelligence\]**

His opposition to these ideas damaged his reputation, preventing him from becoming vice leader of Luminara and hurting the generally good reputation he had in Island 1.

In all meetings, his stubbornness and bad communication isolated any potential allies.

Despite all the damage he had done to himself, Fluixon quickly organized himself  after losing all his support. He kept fighting with no consideration for others. **\[Thinking\] \[Adversity Capacity\]**

He gathered his loyalists around his project after being rejected from Next and built a secret bunker for their operation and the conspiracy he planned, which involved his friend. **\[Planning\] \[SQ\]**

Fluixon showed great emotional management by involving his friend in the conspiracy and using Saps, the neutral moderator of every meeting. He understood the implications, but he was convinced it was for the greater good of uniting Island 2 despite knowing the means he was using. **\[EQ\]**

*"If you had to betray one person to save a thousand... would you?"*

He also showed a good ability to carry the emotional pain of using his friend. **\[EQ\]**

So, he orchestrated the conspiracy and convinced Tricolour's third-in-command to kill their leader, Jophiel. **\[Deception\] \[SQ\]**

However, Seraphim misinterpreted Fluixon's message and immediately killed Jophiel, sending Island 1 into chaos, as they weren't used to deaths like those on Island 2.

Despite the setback created by Seraphim, Fluixon and his loyalists quickly adapted to the new situation. Fluixon called Saps for an emergency meeting for tomorrow and messaged Seraphim to sneak into the meeting and accomplish the conspiracy. However, they failed to build the contraption correctly. **\[Adaptability\] \[Planning\]**

*"Is there a conspiracy going on?"*

*"Are the leaders safe?"*

—Saps

Six out of ten leaders were killed via dripstone. **\[Trapping\]**

Seraphim was successful in carrying out Fluixon's command to execute the conspiracy and caused the dripstone assassination of the leaders. However, due to the chaotic situation, the dripstone only killed 6 of the 10 leaders, and Cass didn't attend the meeting.

Saps managed to escape the scene.

However, they were lucky that Bumwealth and other nations were entirely destroyed. Bumwealth specifically was already rivaling being NPCs.

Saps was also stupid enough to think that 3BelowZero was involved, although he was still smarter than the entirety of Bumwealth combined.

The collapse and downfall of Island 1 resulted in Fluixon gaining all the power. **\[Strategy\]**

Fluixon and his loyalists paid the judge for a rigged trial, and combined with Bumwealth's stupidity, as they were the jury, Saps stood no chance. However, Saps managed to escape. **\[Deception\]**

It worked for the conspiracy since Saps became the scapegoat for Island 1, especially for Bumwealth's last leader, who wanted Saps dead. **\[Deception\] \[Subversion\]**

After that, Fluixon commanded his loyalists to assassinate 3BelowZero and Cass. They successfully killed 3BelowZero, but failed to kill Cass, causing Cass to withdraw from any meetings afterward. **\[Strategy\]**

Fluixon then rigged the election against LegacyAN, causing him to win the election and become the strongest head in Island 1 during the chaotic period. He stopped the construction of the bridge and promised aid to Island 2. **\[Deception\] \[Leadership\]**

However, that would eventually turn against him in the future, which he failed to see. He also failed to account for the fact that people would not forget what he had done.

He didn't bother getting rid of the evidence of bribing the judge and bribing the voters because he believed that everyone was too busy dealing with their own problems during this time and wouldn't have the time to stop Fluixon.

Despite Saps managing to escape to Island 2 when the border opened, a huge bounty was placed on Saps, and hunters were assigned to find him. Bumwealth also wanted him dead, as they considered Saps directly responsible for Bumwealth's downfall and collapse.

Fluixon used 3BelowZero's death, which he had organized, to motivate Luminara into completing the bridge, purely to uphold his public image as their president. **\[Manipulation\]**

With all the power, Fluixon did what he wanted to do—rule Island 1 and continue his conspiracy against Island 2. Even after the conspiracy of Island 2 was proven wrong, he still thought he was right about Island 1. **\[Strategy\] \[Thinking\]**

At the international meeting between Island 1 and Island 2—the Blue Cross Summit—he successfully convinced leaders at the Blue Cross summit not to declare war on Infernus and instead pursue a trial. **\[Deception\]**

He successfully influenced major political decisions involving Infernus, establishing a strong relationship with them and seeing Infernus as a safe haven due to its location. **\[Social Intelligence\]**

On the way back from an international Blue Cross meeting, Tricolour leadership confronted Fluixon.

Tricolour accused Fluixon of being behind all the assassinations based on a little video and suggested putting him on trial. However, they were never entirely convinced that Fluixon was behind everything. They simply wanted a trial to determine whether he was innocent.

*"This is an outrage!"*

Fluixon dodged the questions there and flipped the video evidence against Tricolour. However, he agreed to go along with Tricolour as a group. **\[Deception\] \[Countering\]**

The group could not flee because of the number of guards, so NewKids came to assassinate Fluixon and distract them in order to flee and throw Tricolour off their trail.

Fluixon messaged one of his crew members to attack so that he could get away from the trial and look innocent. **\[Planning\] \[Deception\]**

The conspiracy managed to get into communication with BSHSBoreal of Tricolour and convinced him of their innocence. Boreal was unable to convince the rest of Tricolour, but the seed was planted. **\[Deception\]**

On arriving in Luminara, Fluixon threatened LegacyAN.

By threatening to release his knowledge of LegacyAN's ties to the community and possibly the rest of Island 2, Fluixon successfully got LegacyAN to drop out of the election, and he was automatically declared the winner, becoming president of Luminara. **\[Deception\] \[Manipulation\]**

By doing this, Fluixon believed that any future attempts to arrest him would be rendered nearly impossible with the support and backing of Luminara's community. **\[Foresight\]**

However, Saps survived the assassination attempt afterward.

Saps' survival snowballed into a major blunder for Fluixon after Westhelm trusted Saps.

Westhelm informed LegacyAN about Saps' testimony.

Fluixon noticed that the leaders of Island 1 were avoiding him. He became paranoid that the leaders were after him and discussing Fluixon and the conspiracy behind his back. **\[Sensory\]**

He prepared an escape route and traps with mines, as he foresaw his arrest. **\[Foresight\] \[Trapping\]**

Eventually, Cass came to investigate the conspiracy, found the rigged trial evidence, and immediately gathered other nations and ordered a **"kill on sight"** on Fluixon.

Fluixon managed to escape their **"kill on sight"** order by making himself look innocent. **\[Deception\] \[Countering\]**

However, this made him look suspicious to Cass' coalition.

Fluixon used this situation to make himself look good to the people in Luminara who were aware of him. **\[Manipulation\]**

He successfully manipulated enemy positioning to turn pursuit situations into opportunities for counterattack. **\[Countering\] \[Strategy\]**

Fluixon then opened the tunnel he had designed and escaped from everyone to Island 2, specifically Infernus, where Fluixon and his loyalists joined forces with Infernus in exchange for helping them build traps. **\[Strategy\] \[Trapping\]**

However, they also built a secret tunnel for themselves as an escape route. **\[Planning\] \[Foresight\]**

On the last day, Fluixon was able to survive the onslaught from every nation and managed to escape using the tunnel. **\[Adversity Capacity\]**

However, Fluixon accepted Saps' duel.

*"Can't say no to a friend, can I?"*

*"You know, Saps, none of this would've happened if you had died like you were supposed to."*

At the end, Fluixon died in the duel.

Fluixon as a character was charming and calculating, but he was ultimately paranoid. This paranoia made him overlook many critical details because he believed he was right
