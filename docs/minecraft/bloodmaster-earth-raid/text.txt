【 PART 1 - COLORLESS CUBE 】

In spite of being an ambitious builder, Scope isn’t the type to put that mind into his own comfort, but rather he believes in expansion, something that will serve as an objective net-positive for future progress.

This is reflected in that, despite having a Nether hub that spans across The End, as well as all over the continent, all of which is very richly decorated, he still lives in a tiny shelter in the mountain, happy with what he has.

(Quick thing, but the Nether in the story IS elaborated as an actual bottom layer at the Earth’s crust, hence why transportation is so much quicker and why each block in the Nether is 8 blocks in the Overworld. Every few days, Scope likes to inspect what the residents are doing out of genuine curiosity. Scope debates whether he should break the house apart, seeing as how he doesn’t live in it, nor expanding upon it, but ultimately decides he won’t do anything today.

【 PART 2 - NO STRESS 】
Days pass, as the crew have officially sailed to the nearest land. They found a very open, promising place they inspected while the rest were asleep still.

Unlike Colorless Cube, where you had various inactive, petty, or straight up destructive residents, No Stress requires a far more rigorous whitelist for passage. The town doesn’t take donations from its sister locations, and instead requires and prioritizes ingenuity & consistency. A town where true visionaries will preserve, where you don’t have to work for someone else’s needs, so in that sense, all of your “stress” is your own.

As Scope and Roscow started with their base architecture, one of the residents, Graci, woke up from the boat and immediately jumped on his project. Those initial, core foundations were very quick as they carried all of their materials.

* The assassin whose attack was stopped, ran off into the jungle.
* MiniBingo, after being exiled, immediately headed towards the jungle.

Of course, it wasn’t impossible for a regular, innocent citizen to have simply arrived from the jungle, while not being affiliated with raiders, and happened to join the community, but Scope isn’t the kind to just wag his tail to something that he intuitively considers slightly suspicious, it’s just the way he’s wired, and as everyone already saw, most of the time, his hunches are correct.

Roscow is also aware of Scope’s personality, by simply being aware of previous schemes that happened in Colorless Cube, where he can accurately predict that Scope won’t be confrontational about his suspicion, and will instead try to make his suspicion concealed and set a trap for you instead that you will simply happen to walk into if you happen to be guilty.
