Unstable SMP Wraith Analysis SCD

Context for those who haven't seen the Unstable smp:

This takes place on a hardcore minecraft server called unstable smp with thousands of players online at all times and up to tens of thousands more waiting in queue, hence logging back on after logging off takes months.

Characters involved:

Wraith: the main schemer of this feat, a group of player with invisibility and netherite armor and a unique trim that present themselves as one person, presumably has one person planning out their maneuvers with how well they lines up with each other.

Wemmbu: an Elytra macer with powerful weapons like the stab shots (fishing rod that send tnt explosion in a pillar like shape to wherever you’re aiming at, generally starting from build limit down to bedrock) and orbital strike cannons (send circles of tnt spawning on where you’re aiming at)

Egg: Wemmbu’s best friend, pretty bad at pvp and surviving situations by himself. Wemmbu won't stop at anything to make sure he's safe.

Flame: a rival of Wemmbu for the most powerful player on the server, had some rough history with them.

Parrot: a friend(?) of Wemmbu who's running a kingdom and preparing for war, relatively under resourced by the timeframe this happened.

Cindercrest: faction of players that Flame is associated with.

Cave kingdom: one of the factions on the server that’s interested in having Wemmbu work for them.

Statis chamber: a remote controlled floating enderpearl which teleports the person who the pearl belonged to at the pearls location upon activation.

This analysis is going off the mindset that:

Wraith as a group of Assassin has one mastermind making all the important decisions throughout the arc. When there are notable moments which I believe the main Wraith is the one being showcased, I’ll put a (Main) indicator when mentioning them

Wraith(Main)’s goal has always been consistent throughout the arc and their seemingly contradictory goals are simply misdirection and lies which they told others to achieve  a specific outcome. And that mostly every action which lead to their goal being fulfilled in the end are intentional

# Wraith’s Introduction

The Wraith first appeared in Wemmbu’s first S3 video:

In this video, the Wraith(Main) seeing that Wemmbu’s team was missing a player when competing in a tournament in order to get information about a mace hidden away in a vault joined them. They ended up winning and Wemmbu and egg hunted down the vault. As they made their way through the vault and it’s trap, Wraith(Main) followed them and allowed them to trigger the traps up until they found the mace and stole it from them. Wraith(Main) then got into a fight with Wemmbu and egg, after a considerably difficult fight, Wraith gave up the mace to Wemmbu and escaped. Stating there’s “other places to get maces”

Because of the contradictory nature of his actions (Stalking Wemmbu to get a mace to not "waste armor dura" but still fighting him instead of running away with full invisibility, Wraith's fight and overall encounter with Wemmbu was almost 100% to profile him, his fight style and behaviours for later schemes.

End Goal: Wraith wants to Kidnap Eggchan and sell him to other factions as a bargain
