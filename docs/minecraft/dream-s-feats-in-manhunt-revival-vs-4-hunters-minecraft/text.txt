Dream (Manhunt Revival): How smart is Dream specifically from the latest Manhunt revival video with 4 hunters (probably the one with his most impressive feats so far)?

Btw this is Dream from just one specific Manhunt video and it is only just his recent one, it is not a composite Dream manhunt scaling, it is not even a composite Dream manhunt revival scaling, it is just Dream manhunt revival vs 4 hunters scaling. Like thats it.

Dream VS 4 Hunters REVIVAL

Dream at the beginning is surrounded by the 4 hunters and he is able to escape them by parkouring on the bamboo, which keep in mind is very thin and he is under the pressure of being chased by the hunters so it would normally be easy to make a mistake but he just doesnt. He is able to do this without much difficulty. This feat obviously demonstrates WMI, VSI and maybe even PSI.

Dream was on top of the trees and bamboo, very low on health and without food as he was escaping the hunters. He towers up and when the hunters are down, he crafts a wooden spear and decides to jump off, using the spear he kills BadBoyHalo and Antfrost while they were completely unprepared, landing on just one water block below. This demonstrates WMI, PSI, FRI, VSI, Deception and also Emotional Regulation (ER) and likely Creativity too.

Dream also does a 180 and he lands a boat clutch which yea normally is not that impressive but if they were playing on the newest version of Minecraft the boat clutch becomes like harder now due to some stupid thing they have done or whatever, ofc Dream learned it so it necessitates WMI, PSI, VSI and FRI, as he was being ambushed and had to figure out a way to escape.

While at the nether fortress, he places some beds on the ground, mining the nether brick to place them. he then uses the lava to cover up the beds so that when the hunters would come they would unknowingly explode the beds as they naturally begin placing blocks on top of the lava to escape. This demonstrates WMI, FRI, Creativity, Deception, Theory of Mind, Trap Setting, Planning and Strategy. This obviously worked as Dream did exactly what they thought they would as George just placed a block and immediately died.

When he was in a very narrow space in the nether fortress and the hunters were about to kill him, he is able to escape with just a half a heart remaining by placing a bunch of blocks while under a lot of pressure, demonstrating FRI and PSI.

The hunters put pressure on Dream while he comes back in the Overworld to begin his search for the stronghold using eyes of ender. He has to tower up in order to escape them, which obviously pushes the hunters towards him as their goal is to try to catch him, obviously. Dream towering up really high results in all 4 hunters being in the same space as they try to reach Dream. He tried bowing them but it doesnt quite work how he wanted it to since they were still after him.

So to solve this Dream crafts a diamond spear, remembering the earlier thing he had done with the wooden spear while he was low on health and the hunters were after him at the beginning of the manhunt. He jumps down and is able to hit BadBoyHalo, George and Antfrost while falling down, one shotting them in the process. He is not only able to time his hits perfectly but he is also able to turn around in time to get each one of them, landing on the water they placed to try and get him. He kills 3/4 of the hunters, freeing himself up.

This play requires WMI, PSI, FRI, VSI, Theory of Mind (knowing they would come after him like this) and Creativity.

When Dream reaches the portal room, he decides to set up what I would like to call the 3 worlds trap. He throws an ender pearl towards the nether portal while at the same time taking one foot step to get into the End, triggering the advancement achievement that he got to the End. This makes the hunters assume he is in the End, because not even their compass is showing that he is in the Overworld, so he must actually be in the End, but they dont know that Dream went to the nether until they go to the End and realize he is not there, but its too late since they are stuck because they cant kill the Ender Dragon. This gives Dream time to stack up with a lot more equipment to go into the End, while they are more limited in what they can do cuz they cant get new stuff, only use the stuff they have already or can get from the End, which is like almost nothing. Ok I call it the 3 worlds trap because Dream is in the overworld, he throws an ender pearl to the nether while going to the end, so this play basically involves all 3 Minecraft worlds. After they go into the End, Dream comes back and just destroys their bed, meaning if they die they will literally be far from the End.

This requires WMI, FRI, VSI, Theory of Mind, Trap Setting, Planning, Strategy, Deception, Manipulation and probably some other categories. Probably the most impressive feat thus far.

Dream anticipates that because of the huge time he spent gathering loot and everything that the hunters probably built some sort of trap in the End when he gets there, so he places down water alongside the End portal, which would sort of be used as an ender pearl stasis chamber. So just before he enters the portal he throws the ender pearl into the water and he sees they have made a dripstone trap but the ender pearl saves him just before that was going to land on him, then he just goes back to the End, evading their trap.

WMI, FRI, VSI, Foresight, Trap Evasion, Planning, Strategy, Counter-Manipulation (probably some others too idk)
