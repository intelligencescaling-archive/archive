Ukai Zero (Chapter 1) – Feat Introduction: Rescuing Teammates

BRIEF INTRODUCTION TO ZERO AND HIS TEAMMATES:

Zero is the leader of a righteous heist group consisting of three teammates — individuals who were considered failures in society — who came together to help victims of bank transfer scams. During a mission to recover money stolen by a criminal organization, Zero's teammates were ambushed and kidnapped due to their greed when they tried to withdraw some cash from the bank as a "self-reward."

Scale 1: Recovering money for the victims of a scam organization

To infiltrate the head office of the criminal ring, Zero’s three teammates disguised themselves as food delivery workers and stealthily swapped the office leader’s key with an identical duplicate (Pic 1), allowing the team to quietly break into the organization at any time.

However, to access the organization’s bank account, both the physical card and the passcode were needed. So, immediately after swapping the key, they also installed several hidden cameras and audio bugs (Pic 2). Thanks to this, Zero was able to find out that the card was stored in the desk drawer.

Finally, for the bank account passcode: with the help of intercepted conversations, when the criminals discussed withdrawing the money, Zero swiftly acted ahead of them. The passcode was revealed to be based on the second-to-last digit of the bank card number, so it only took a little thinking to figure it out. (Account number: 0492-090-2712643 → 4621 are the 4 digits starting from the left of the second-to-last digit, which is 3.) (Pic 3)

Scale 2: Grasping the Situation of His Teammates

First, we need to understand that Zero’s three teammates were people who had previously contemplated suicide. Because of that, Zero had given each of them a piece of gum that supposedly contained poison — a last resort in case they ever found themselves with no way out (Pic 4). In reality, the gum wasn’t poisoned at all; instead, it contained a hidden tracking device, just in case something happened later.

The three teammates had once attempted group suicide together in the past, so if they were to try again, Zero predicted it would likely be as a group again. If any of them had wanted to do it alone, they would’ve already done so long ago, and Zero wouldn’t have been able to stop them. So, if the three of them stayed apart for a long enough time, it would serve as a warning to Zero that they might be attempting suicide again (Pic 5).

To make matters worse, they gave him a false location — saying they were at a restaurant, when in reality they were somewhere else entirely (Pic 6).

With all these clues, Zero was able to deduce that his friends were in trouble — possibly even kidnapped by the same criminal organization as before. Because of this, he chose not to maintain contact or go to the restaurant as planned (since he could be walking into a trap), and instead went straight to rescue his teammates.

=>Prediction, Creativity, Planning, Abductive Reasoning, Trap Evasion

Scale 3: Planning the Rescue of His Teammates

Zero had called the police beforehand, then threatened the scam group to release his teammates if they didn’t want to end up in jail (Pic 7).

In reality, the sound of police sirens was just a recording played through a portable speaker that Zero had placed on the opposite side of the criminals’ emergency exit (Pic 8).

This allowed Zero’s team to make a successful retreat — until they were suddenly caught off guard. Zero had predicted that in an emergency situation, none of the criminals would dare approach what they thought was a real police presence — the actual source of the sound being just a decoy speaker.

=>Creativity, Acting Skills, Bluffing, Fabrication, Tactics
