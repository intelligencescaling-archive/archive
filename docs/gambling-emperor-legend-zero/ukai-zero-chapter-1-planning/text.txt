The complete doc of part 1 and 2 will take more time. I put some manga panels to support my explanation at the end, you can check the manga too (this all comes from literally one single chapter lol).
Enjoy and please share your opinion in the comments of my community post.

Zero was the leader of the righteous thieves, let's start with how Ukai managed to recruit them two weeks before the events of chapter 1.
After meeting on a website, four depressed people decided to end their lives in a remote place. Based on the picture, they were inside a car and they connected a cable to send exhaust fumes inside and make them suffocate to death. The last person, Ukai, observed them from a distance and saved them by cutting the cable with an axe. Logically, the other three were initially angry arguing on how much they were suffering everyday, etc. Zero responded that he understood their feelings and how determined they were to die but that he had a little request: to give him his lifes. He offered to let them breathe this “sweet, wonderful, refreshing air” instead of the exhaust fumes. He then  explained that their lives, compared to a candle, aren’t running out of wax or fuel to burn but rather the lack of oxygen extinguishing their flames and that's why they need fresh air. Instead of the stagnant air deprived of oxygen they needed a nice cool breeze, so he also offered to open up that cave and persuaded them by saying he would still guarantee their deaths. Zero then gave them “poisoned gums” (bluff) and said they had a fatal dosage of hydrocyanic acid within them. He included himself saying they were all humans who had already died before and are ready to face death again anytime, there are many things they can do, so that would be their strength. The rest were convinced they had nothing to fear and that they would die peacefully, that's how he recruited them.

It’s implied that during these two weeks Zero and his group robbed money from con organizations, related with yakuzas, and returned it to the victims, applying a similar method in their acts. However, this made the organizations to be more cautious and wary and ended up with Ukai’s teammates being caught after disobeying his orders.
This happened two weeks later, when zero executed his plan to rob money of a con organization called “Rats of Heisei” to return it to the victims. They returned all the transfers and announced it on TV by sending the following not handwritten message to the press : “We stole the money back. They will be returned on your side” -Zero-.  Ukai was already known as the leader of the righteous thieves, probably because they also indirectly announced on TV their previous thefts. It was speculated Zero was someone deep within the organization who had a grudge, most likely because it's hard to imagine someone from the outside stealing money from lots of these types of groups constantly.

Now, how did Ukai send these messages to the press while keeping their identities unknown?
It isn’t explained but we can assume he did something similar to this: Printing the letters after typing them, covering them with a lining, using gloves to not leave fingerprints, sending them on different postal mail agencies at different hours so police/yakuzas don't find a pattern. It's also likely he instructed someone else to do this. We can perfectly assume something similar to this process since it is highlighted that the letters were not handwritten in order to hide their identities.

Now, let’s focus on how zero specifically did this last theft and why their teammates were caught. From the pictures, we can assume the “Rats of Heisei” disguised as a normal company, this means they would have a working time of between 9am and 5pm approximately and a lunch break at 12pm or 1pm that lasts one hour, this is very important. Ukai Zero instructed his three teammates to dress up as delivery boys and go to deliver food during this lunch break. During this time most workers usually go to eat outside, so only a few of the workers would be eating inside with home-prepared food or delivery. This gave even more space for the righteous thieves to follow zero instructions:
First, to steal the master key to their office and make a copy of it, making key backups don't take longer than 10 mins so it’s perfectly possible to do before the hour ends. Then when they returned to take back the food plates, they quickly switched the original key with an identical copy which also had the exact same keychain.
They did more than this, when they delivered the food, they put some listening devices and hidden cameras around the place, the reason was to see where the card was kept (it was on a desk, they saw it through the cameras when the boss gave it to some of his men to get money) and to hear the password (they heard when the boss was telling his men the password was backwards from the second last number). With all of this, they got the key to the office where the card was hidden and the information to use it.
How were the teammates caught? Despite Ukai warning them that the yakuzas began to notice what was going on, they still wanted a little bit of money for themselves and when they went to withdraw money again, they were already surrounded by the “Rats of Heisei” and got caught, even one of them got stabbed.

Now, Zero predicted a possible outcome like this, so he made even more preparations. Ukai’s teammates resisted the solitude of calling Zero but after being threatened with torture, they gave in and started calling with orders from the cons to fabricate the call saying they were in a restaurant to celebrate to make Zero come join them. Ukai answered the call and said he was coming and quickly cut the call.
Ukai already knew where they were and was heading toward there, I'll explain this later. His teammates initially were scared and some tried to blame Zero for all  the burden, but they remembered the words Zero said and feelings he made them experience during those two weeks and felt grateful toward him, also remembering they still had the gums which could make them also fulfill their previous desire of dying if things got worse (they didnt want to suffer and torture was likely). Though, the boss, advised by the boss of other faction, already decided not to torture them as there was the possibility of Zero coming to negotiate and they needed to be in good state. After an hour, the boss got desperate and offered freedom to another teammate if he lied to zero saying they needed money to pay for the food, but when he grabbed him, the gums fell into the floor and the boss stomped on them. Then everyone noticed something more, Zero had put a homing device inside all the gums given to all the teammates. The boss then thought there wasn't any point in fabricating as Ukai had been monitoring their locations, so decided to just kill them. At that exact moment, Ukai, who was since long before outside the door (he didn't just arrived, because he wasn't sweating and was breathing normally, i will backup this later), entered the room smiling and revealing the trick of the gums to his teammates, the gum was intended to do the opposite effect from what they were expecting: it was to prevent them from commiting suicide. Also Zero reasoned that if they were to try it again, they would do it together as a group as they didn’t have the courage to do it alone, otherwise they would have done it long ago. This is also my backup for Ukai being outside the door for a while, he went to their location the moment he saw they were together and he could deduce through the fake calls that something was wrong.

Ukai then told the boss nonchalantly to let his teammates go, the rats got angry and tried to attack him. Zero shut them up and asked them if they could hear the sound of their greatest enemy: a police car siren that kept getting closer. Ukai said he already told the police the situation and showed a GPS gum on his hand. Then he mentioned their trouble: 3 brutally beaten people and one stabbed that is dying, saying this could be enough evidence for capital punishment. Continued getting the rats angrier explaining that they prison sentence would get longer if they caused further injuries and also that the one who stabbed his teammate would be in prison the longest, this finally made them panic and try to run away, which Ukai took advantage of and started scaping with his team, taking especial care of the stabbed one. Ukai had left a radio with the sound of the police siren reproducing outside before entering, all he said was a bluff/fabrication. By the way, Ukai managed his emotions greatly here too, since he just noticed one of his teammates was stabbed, and he still executed his bluff perfectly and took care of his friend while being well composed.

For page 63+:

The fact the righteous thieves were blocked by Zaizen and his lackeys isn’t Zero mistake, because his plan would have worked perfectly if they just managed to leave the room, they just needed to run and it wouldn’t really matter if the rats noticed the radio then. Also Ukai was outside the door until the very last moment, so him not perceiving someone was close is extremely unlikely given his insane sensory feats three days later. We don’t know how Zaizen’s right hand managed to know they were there at the time, but he was sure of it since he called Zaizen himself too. Well, this guy is literally the richest person of Japan and has control of all the underworld so pulling off some resources wouldn’t be hard, the thing is Ukai was cautious till the last moment, he just didn’t expect someone that powerful to follow him.

Ukai calling the police can be argued to be strange because he himself is escaping from it but it can be understood given this was a life or death situation for his teammates, and because their public image was pretty positive and therefore people would try to help them leave prison for sure, so this trick was pretty strong too.
Also police would have no way of knowing that they are the righteous thieves.

What if Zero’s teammates eat the gums? Homing devices can be extremely small microchips that wouldn’t be enough to harm someone if eaten. For instance a dog GPS has the size of 72 x 29 x 16 millimeters and weighs 30 grams, Zero could have bought this or something similar. This risk was well calculated by Ukai too.

Why did Ukai take some time before entering the door to rescue his teammates? The main reason was making sure his teammates suffered injuries from the rats, to guarantee the police car siren bluff was effective.

Finally, remember that the chivalrous thieves were being searched by the police all the time, that’s one additional adversity to take into account, and it’s possible there is more unexplained coverage in this planning.
