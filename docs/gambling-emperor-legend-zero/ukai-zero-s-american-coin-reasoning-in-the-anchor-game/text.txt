Ukai Zero American Coin Reasoning

Ukai is easily one of the best characters in Reasoning,especially his process which makes it even more impressive,not to mention that he's doing it while maintaining his composure in dangerous situations,i'll explain here one of the best reasoning he did in the Anchor Game.

**Context**: in the Anchor game round,Ukai Zero and 2 other intellectually average friends were tied firmly to an anchor floor where there would be a very sharp anchor dangling on top rotating like a pendulum,Zero and his friends were asked trivial quiz questions and some of them got unreasonably difficult and arbitrary. Zero and his 2 friends should take turns to answer each question and they were not allowed to tell each other the correct answers, if they got the answer right, the anchor would go up until they win officially after finishing the quiz questions, if they answer the question wrong the anchor will go down bit by bit until it reaches low enough to swing the anchor blade that cuts the 3 men’s head off like a guillotine.

So after getting humilitaed by the Kanji Reasoning,Kotarou(the game master) is angry and thus he will do his best to find a hard question that Zero can't solve since it worth 4 points,but the questions he had in the monitor relies on general knowledge which most likely Zero would be able to solve(In Kotarou's perspective),which forced him to make a question himself.

After thinking for some moments,he brought some coins from his wallet,put them in his table and turned the monitor on to show them:

After this,he grabbed one of these coins in his hand and here's the question:

Zero friends argue that this is not a quiz but just a random chance,Kotarou said that leaving everything to luck isn't bad,after all the representative of Zaizen will gamble later in the global competition,meaning he also thinks that this is an impossible question for Zero.

After this Kotarou gives a hint for Zero by reminding him that there are 6 kinds of coins in Japan**: 500 Yen,100 Yen,50 Yen,10 Yen,5 Yen and 1 Yen.**

**Reasoning:**

*P1 → His mountain of coins didn't seem like something that was randomly split from his wallet,but stacked and placed them perfectly as if to hide something.*

*P2 → He has a bad personality and habits,he isn't the nice guy who helps people by giving them hints.*

*and*..

*His resentment and desire for revenge grew after the humiliation he suffered after revealing the meaning behind the kanji in his head.*

***C1*** *→ He wants to instill the idea that he holds a Japanese coin in his hand by subconsciously eliminating the possibility that it's not.*

*P3 → His full name is Kotarou Hill Mountain Williams Harrison Jagasatake James Shiroyama,he has American names in the middle of his name.*

***C2*** *→ At least one of his parents,are probably American citizens.*

*P4 → He visited America multiple times.*

*P5 → The coin in his hands isn't a Japanese coin.*

***C3*** *→ The coin is likely American.*

*P6 → He looks surprised after it was revealed that it's American.*

***C4*** *→ The coin is American : a Quarter,Penny,Dime,Nickle,Penny or Dollar coin.*

*P7 → When he hid the coin in the mountain of the coins,there was enough space only for it.*

*P8 → if he got the coin after paying for something,he would probably at least have 2-3 coins after the payment.*

*and..*

*He has only one coin in his hand.*

***C5*** *→ it was a single coin that he kept inside his wallet on purpose.*

*P8 → people usually keep a single coin with them because it symbolizes something good,or because it's new and rare.*

*P9 → Penny is considered a symbol for good luck.*

*P10 → Dollar coin was created in 2007 recently.*

***C6*** *→ The coin he holds would be a lucky penny or Dollar coin.*

Having reached this far,Zero had no choice but to choose one of the two possibilities. He chose the penny, but unfortunately the correct answer was the dollar coin.

(Abductive Reasoning,PSI,WMI,FRI,Crystallized Intelligence,Lateral Thinking,Linear Thinking,Critical Thinking,Emotional Understanding,Emotional Perception,Psychoanalysis,Trap Evasion).

The fact that Zero completed this reasoning feat quickly is really impressive,also his high contextual adversity here makes it even better.
