I believe this guy has the highest IQ in human history based on his feats and statements

This is John von Neumann he was a 20th-century polymath and mathematician

I believe he has the highest IQ in terms of logical reasoning, abstract thinking, problem-solving speed, working memory, and spatial reasoning  in human history. He was essentially a human calculator with a eidetic memory.

(There are probably several people in history who were smarter but never reached their potential due to timing or circumstances)

Intellectual Feats:

* **Age 6:** He could mentally divide 8-digit numbers, converse jokingly in classical Greek with his father, and amaze guests by memorizing phone books.
* **Age 15:** He began studying advanced calculus under the renowned analyst **Gábor Szegő**. Upon their first meeting, Szegő was reportedly so moved by von Neumann's mathematical speed and talent that he was moved to tears.
* **Age 19:** Published two major mathematical papers. The second paper provided the modern definition of ordinal numbers
* **Age 39:** Recruited by the Manhattan Project, he designed the mathematical 'lenses' for the Fat Man bomb and solved the complex fluid dynamics equations that predicted exactly how the blast would behave.
* He formulated the mathematical foundations of quantum mechanics, described the logic of genetic self-replication before the discovery of DNA, and founded the field of game theory.

He had Contributed foundational work towards

* Game theory
* Functional analysis
* Set theory
* Quantum mechanics
* Computer architecture

He also has an entire [Wikipedia page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_things_named_after_John_von_Neumann) listing things named after him(most are things he discovered, influenced, or was honored with)
