Akemi Murder

Context: Masami Hirota (real name: Akemi Miyano) made a deal with the Organisation, presumably even Gin & Vodka themselves, that she and two other guys she’ll hire will steal 1 Billion Yen and in exchange she and her sister can leave the organisation.

(I might make a seperate doc on the heist itself as I have no idea whether Gin or Akemi made the plan like it played out.)

Prior to meeting Gin and Vodka at the end of the case, Gin gave Akemi some “sleeping” pills to put the giant, Akira, to sleep. However, when he drank the drink with the pills he coughed up blood and died. It turns out that Gin fooled Akemi and instead of sleeping pills he gave her potassium cyanide.

It also turns out that Gin never intended to let Akemi’s sister (which we later in the story find out is Haibara) leave the organisation. He simply used Akemi to get the money for the organisation and he says that unlike Akemi her sister is an indispensable member.

This means that from the start Gin manipulated Akemi using her sister to get the money from the heist. He knew that Akemi would do anything to get her sister out of the organisation.

(EU + Influence + Negotiation + Direct Manipulation + Emotional Manipulation + Sophistry + Bluffing + Info Control + Linear Thinking + Systematic Thinking)

Afterwards Gin threatens Akemi with a Gun forcing her to tell him where the money is. When she doesn't tell them he doesn't hesitate to shoot her.

When Conan and Ran arrive at the scene Akemi just falls to the ground from the shot, but Gin and Vodka are already gone. Keep in mind that after they heard the shot they immediately found Akemi. Gin and Vodka had only little time to escape (judging from the panel) and yet they did it without leaving a single trace behind.

Before they escaped, Gin let it seem like Akemi killed herself by leaving behind her fingerprints on the gun which everyone besides Conan (who had time to talk to the dying Akemi) believed.

This goes to show how careful the BO is as to not leaving anything behind that might indicate their involvement. A better example of this will be shown in the Haibara/Pisco Arc later on.

Plan to murder the Politician

Alrighty, sooo. I'm just gonna count this as a Planning feat for Gin since he was the one to give the order to Pisco. All the things Pisco did I'll count for Pisco and Gin together because…. why not 🤷.

The goal of this plan is to kill the politician Shigehiko Nomiguchi, who's supposed to be arrested on this exact day. The reason why is because he seems to have some sort of connection to the Black Organisation and since he's about to be arrested they decided to eliminate him.

In that party everyone is wearing black, making it hard for Conan to identify who Pisco is (though that wasn't the exact intention I still wanted to call that out lol).

Something important to note is that, whilst every guest got a colored handkerchief, only seven people had a purple handkerchief, Pisco and Vermouth (who was also present) included. It's unknown whether the BO knew that or not. The plan was to be executed once the slideshow starts and the light goes out.

The plan went something like this:

Pisco would go and tell Nomiguchi to stand beneath the chandelier after the lights went out. That way no one will know where he's standing during the slideshow. He most likely persuaded him by saying he'll help him against the police.

Pisco will then put his purple handkerchief over his silencer in order to hide the flash created when firing the gun.

Prior to everything, the floor beneath the chandelier and a part of the chandelier’s hook were painted by florescent paint so that Pisco knows where Nomiguchi is supposed to stand and what part of the chandelier he's supposed to shoot so that everything works out perfectly. The way they did it was done so well it wouldn't have been noticed had it not been for Conan finding the painted part and the handkerchief.

When the police began to investigate the seven people with a purple handkerchief, Vermouth lent Pisco her own so that he wouldn't get caught during the interrogation.

Overall, the plan is not all too bad, but there are some errors to it.

1. Pisco was caught by a cameraman where he was shown aiming at the chandelier with his gun. He didn't kill him, which resulted in Gin later killing Pisco.
2. Conan was present. While it's not Pisco's or Gin's fault, Conan kinda ruined the whole plan by finding the burned handkerchief and the painted chandelier part.

Rescuing Kir

(This one is gonna be long as I'll also include the van chase, so be prepared for lots of text and a lot of scans via pictures.)

Now that the Black Organisation knows where Kir is they have to get her back. Since they can't just waltz in Gin created this Strategy/Plan (kinda a mix of both tbh) to get her back. However, there are 3 things that make this hard:

The first is obvious: the FBI is guarding her. The second is that the hospital is huge. And the third: since the hospital is huge there are lots of rooms they'd have to look through.

In order to bypass all these Gin orchestrated all of the things I'll now list:

1st: He and the others caused 3 accidents simultaneously in places that are near the Haido Central Hospital. One was at the “Family Restaurant Haido” where guests were complaining about stomach aches which looked like food poisoning, although it could've been a drug that causes the same symptoms.
The second was in the Haido Station where a nasty odor was released that causes peoples eyes & throat to hurt/become itchy etc.
The third was a fire outbreak in the Haido Cinema. Gin predicted that the victims of these accidents will go to the nearest Hospital to get treated as fast as possible, which is the Haido Central Hospital.

2nd: He sent in a flowerpot under the name of Rikumichi Kusuda to James Black (you could say he's sorta like a boss to the FBI). The flower he sent is a columbine, or “Odamaki” as it's called in japan. Conan points out that the language of this flower means something like “We will obtain without fail.” and “Winning Decisively.”, which is a way of showing that they declared war with them that they're confident they'll win. Another thing is that they planted a bomb inside the flowerpot with a timer of about 30 minutes.
