(Copied from the other doc) Context: Conan coincidentally finds out that the news reporter Rena Mizunashi is a Black Organisation. How? He placed a transmitter at her door for a case that Kogoro was ordered to solve and forgot to remove if. Here he finds out about an Assassination that they have planned.

The person they want to kill is referred to as “DJ” and he's supposed to be killed in a place called “Eddie P”. Vermouth calls it a suitable place to hunt with Kir later on saying that with the rain gone it's a perfect hunting weather.

Conan then bumps into Jodie Starling, a FBI agent that's pursuing Kir. From her he finds out about the 3 people Kir wants to interview.

* Teito University professor and inventor of numerous drugs, Eisuke Tokiwa
* 2nd heir of a wealthy family & popular actor Junji Sendou
* and finally son of a former japanese defence agency officer and he himself being a officer of the self defense force, Yasuteru Domon

Conan now has to find out who out of these 3 is DJ and where Eddie P is.

When all of them try to brainstorm on what DJ could mean Haibara points out that DJ normally stands for “Disc Jockey”. When Jodie says that she hasn’t heard any info about any of them being interested in music or gambling, Conan is stuck on the gambling part and immediately figures out who DJ is.

Firstly, he figures out that DJ stands for “Jack of Diamonds” and that the BO uses cards to nickname their target.

He figures that it can't be Sendou since the card stands for the the courtier or the knight, not prince or son (which Haibara also points out).

His Reasoning process here would have looked like this.

P1: DJ is the person they want to kill.
P2: DJ is one of the 3 people Rena wants to interview.
P3: Putting DJ in comparison with “Gambling”, you get “Jack of Diamonds”.
P4: The BO uses cards, which the japanese call trumps, to systematically nickname their targets.

P5: The Jack in the cards stands for the courtier or the knight and not for the prince or the son.
P6: Junji Sendou is the son of a wealthy politician and he'll be a second-generational diet member with a legacy.
C1: It can't be Sendou since the Jack in the cards doesn't mean son.

P7: Domon Yasuteru’s name starts with a “Do”.
P8: The character “Do” (kanji = 土) expresses “earth”.
P9: Astrologically, diamonds have properties of earth.

C2: Domon Yasuteru is “DJ” and Gin plans to assassinate him.

After being unsuccessful in stopping Domon from going to the interview Conan sorta panics as they have less than 30 minutes left to safe him. After talking with Ran over the phone Jodie comments that Conan acts like he has a split personality, and here's where Conan connects the last dots.

P1: Haibara has mentioned earlier that the “P” could stand for “Park”.
C1: Eddie P is a Park.

P2: Vermouth called Eddie P a “perfect hunting ground”.
P3: Jodie comments on Conan’s “Split Personality”.
P4: The Eddie in “Eddie P” is short for “Edward”.
P5: Edward Hyde is the name of a character in Stevenson’s horror story “Jekyll and Hyde”.
P6: There's a park called “Hyde Park” in their city.
P7: The story originated in London.
P8: Deers and Boars lived in the Hyde Park in London.
P9: The Hyde Park served as the loyal hunting ground until the 16th century.

C2: Eddie P is the Hyde Park.

Overall: Gin wants to assassinate Domon Yasuteru in the Hyde Park using Kir to interview him in order for Chianti & Korn to kill him.
