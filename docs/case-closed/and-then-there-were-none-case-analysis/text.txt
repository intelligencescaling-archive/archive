And Then There Were No Mermaids Case analysis (one of Conan's top 5 feats)

This is one of Conan/Kudo's best reasoning feats. If I included every clue in the context, it would be way to long to read, so I'll put them into the actual reasoning chain and you can all read it that way.

CONTEXT: Conan and Heiji go to Bikuni Island, also called 'The Island Where the Mermaid Lives'. They go there because of a letter sent by a person called Saori but when they arrive, they discover Saori missing from the island. There, they encounter a few murders and then have to solve the murders. The three murders:

1. Toshimi by strangulation and hanging from a waterfall

2. Naoko by strangulation found tangled in a net with fish scales on the beach next to the wake (a sort of funeral event)

3. Kimie by burning, body found in a burned down warehouse

Other than this, the only thing you need to know is that there are 7 main people in this case: Toshimi, Naoko, Kimie, Saori (who's gone missing), Rokuro, Lady Mikoto (the dugong arrow giver) and Mr Benzo (Saori's father).

REASONING:

PART 1: THE SECOND MURDER

P1: There is a murder victim tangled in a net

P2: This murder was done on a beach

P3: This beach is right next to a wake

P4: There are bootprints but the victim was not wearing bootprints and no one else was at the scene except the murderer and the victim so the bootprints belong to the murderer

P5: The bootprints leading to the murder scene and into the ocean but not back to the wake

P6: There should be bootprints leading back to wake since the bootprints indicate that the murder went from the wake to the murder scene then to the ocean and it is impossible to fly back there without leaving footprints

P7: The tide was lower during the time of murder

P8: If the tide was to rise, it would erase footprints

P9: There were fish (?) scales scattered in the same direction as the footprints

P10: There is a mermaid legend on the island

**C1**: The murderer went from the wake, killed the victim, then walked along the shore, erasing their footprints using the tide and making everyone think a mermaid was the cause of the death by scattering scales

P11: There is a dugong arrow on the ground, trampled into the sand

P12: The dugong arrow is known to be valuable

P13: The victim's jacket was searched

P14: The area was dark, meaning the arrow would be hard to see on the ground so it makes sense that the murderer didn't take it after searching through the jacket

**C2**: The arrow was the motive for this kill (Naoko's death or murder 2) and the murderer didn't take the arrow because it was too dark to see

PART 2: THE FIRST MURDER

P15: The victim (Toshimi) was strangled to death by a rope that was hanging by a fence, effectively turning into a noose that hung her from the waterfall

P16: There's a 'mermaid's grave' that Toshimi was obsessed about so it'd make sense for her to be lurking around in the dark to try and find the grave

**C3**: It could be an accident

P17: There is a pool tube found in the vicinity of the murder

P18: The pool tube could definitely support Toshimi's weight if she were to be dropped into the river next to the waterfall

P19: Toshimi was gone for 2 hours

P20: It only takes 1 hour to get to the top of the waterfall where she was hanged

P21: The pool tube was placed there quite recently so it wasn't there by accident

**C4**: **C3** was a red herring and it was not an accident but a murder. P was just an excuse to cover up the murder

PART 3: THE THIRD MURDER

P22: There is a body in the burnt warehouse

P23: The body has brown hair and a blue sweater, which was identical to Saori's appearance

P24: Saori is the only person on the island with that appearance

**C5**: Saori is the one who was killed in the burned warehouse

P25: The dental records, however, match Kimie's dental records

**C6**: The victim is not Saori like it says in **C** but actually Kimie. The appearance was just another red herring

PART 4: NARROWING DOWN THE SUSPECTS

P26: Kimie, Mr Benzo, and Rokuro's names were entered into the wake's guest list before Naoko's

P27: If they're listed after Naoko, it means they arrived after her, because even if they wrote their names in a row before, it'd be suspicious

P28: If they arrived after her, they could sneak out and not be detected by anyone else there

**C7**: During the wake, the only ones that could've snuck out of the wake and killed Naoko were Kimie, Rokuro and Mr Benzo

PART 5: THE MOTIVES BEHIND THE FIRST TWO MURDERS

P29: Toshimi won an arrow, which could've been the target of the murderer

P30: Naoko also won an arrow, which the murderer tried to find

**C8**: The dugong arrows was the motive of the first two murders

PART 6: THE THIRD MURDER MOTIVE AND SUSPECTING MR BENZO

P31: Mr Benzo sold a dugong arrow for a hefty price after his daughter won it a year ago

P32: He is known to be greedy and spends a lotta money on alcohol

P33: Dugong arrows are all worth a lot of money no matter whose it is

**C9**: Combined with **C8**, we can see that Mr Benzo had a very good motive to kill the first two victims for their arrows

P34: The winners of the arrows are written down in a notebook

P35: Benzo had tried to steal the notebook. His fingerprints were on the notebook, which means he definitely did try (solid evidence)

P36: Kimie was at the scene when Mr Benzo was stealing the book

P37: If Kimie exposed him, Mr Benzo would definitely be punished

**C10**: Kimie was killed by Benzo in order to hide his theft

PART 7: SEEING THROUGH THE RED HERRINGS

P38: The murders were carried out very carefully with little evidence left behind

P39: Benzo left his fingerprints in a very clumsy manner, contradicting P38

P40: All the locals knew where to find the notebook which means he could've done it on a better day without these detectives snooping around

P41: Naoko was suspicious of Benzo which reduces the possibility of the 2nd murder

P42: Benzo's fingerprints were on the float tube at the 1st murder but a dugong arrow ticket (to win the dugong arrow) was also on it, which meant he wanted to steal the ticket but got his fingerprints on the tube by accident

P43: Benzo stole the notebook because he was afraid everyone would think he was the murderer if they found out he had Toshimi's ticket, which meant he wanted to destroy it in order to hide the fact he had a dead person's ticket

P44: Benzo could've just burnt the notebook if he was the murderer during the 3rd murder with the warehouse fire

**C11**: Benzo couldn't be the murderer

P45: The murders were done by someone who could easily lure the three victims out

P46: Rokuro is close to all three victims

**C12**: Rokuro is the only one left of the three in **C7** (Kimie couldn't have because she's dead)

P47: Rokuro showed genuine grief at Kimie's death

P48: The murders were all pre-mediated since the tricks were prepared beforehand (the first murder was prepared with a tube and the second needed to lay out the footprints beforehand)

P49: To prepare for these murders, the murderer must be someone who knew who would win the arrows, otherwise they wouldn't know who to kill for the arrows

**C13**: Rokuro does not know any of this stuff so he is cleared

P50: Only the old lady Mikoto knew who would receive the arrows

**C14**: Old lady Mikoto is the murderer

P51: Mikoto is 130 years old, making it physically impossible for her to kill them

P52: The 3rd victim is her own great granddaughter

**C15**: Although contradicting, Mikoto is the only one left who could be the murderer, yet cannot be the murderer due to her physical weakness

PART 8: "WHAT IF THE VICTIM IN THE WAREHOUSE WASN'T KIMIE?"

P53: Kimie said she'd gone to the dentist with Saori

P54: The dental records matched Kimie's

P55: If Saori lost her national health insurance certificate and borrowed Kimie's, Saori would be listed as Kimie

P56: Kimie could easily disguise herself as Saori due to her make up skills

**C16**: Saori could be the one that took the appointment (but there is no evidence yet)

P57: There were painkillers in Saori's bag for toothaches

P58: The dentist claimed the patient had bleached hair and glasses (Saori's description)

**C17**: Saori is the one with the appointment and not Kimie

**C18**: The body then at the 3rd murder scene is not Kimie but Saori

**C19**: Kimie and Lady Mikoto were the ones who conspired and killed the three (Lady Mikoto as well because of P49 and P50)

P59: Lady Mikoto only appeared outside during the festivals, meaning she appeared to a few people a few times in her life

P60: It wouldn't be hard for Kimie to imitate Mikoto (see P56)

P61: Because of P59, even if her disguise isn't perfect, the chances of people seeing through her disguise is low

P62: In a previous fire incident, the burnt corpse had its legs destroyed by crushing, meaning its legs were tied up and crushed and this is known to Kimie

**C20**: Kimie is Mikoto and disguised herself by tying up her feet

So yeah this is pretty much it. In this case there are several layers of misdirection: the identity of the burnt body, Mr Benzo being the killer, the footsteps leading to the ocean, and so on.
