Conan's Wedding Hall Panic feat

So I was just watching through this a few days ago, and I thought it might be interesting to put it on here (you know, to show how cracked Kudo is)

**CONTEXT: Conan, Ran, and Kogoro go to a wedding hall and happen to meet the police there. They are then informed that a couple (groom: Masateru Taira, bride: Rei Masudo) had been threatened with death by a serial killer thief who broke into the bride's (Rei's) home before and tried to steal some stuff.**

**During the attempted robbery (this is very important), Rei just happened to stumble upon the thief after hearing weird noises, and then ran away in the dark (the lights were unable to be turned on). Right before the thief caught her and was about to stab her, the groom (Taira) arrived, due to a bad feeling that something was gonna happen, and fought off the thief, but just before that, Rei claimed she saw a sty (swollen lump) on the thief's eye. Taira then claimed that his left arm was crushed during the struggle and had to keep it in a sling until very recently, a week before the wedding. The knife left behind had a fingerprint on it (a left hand fingerprint). So if they check the fingerprints of the guests at the wedding, they should be able to find the thief's fingerprints. (Or at least that's what they assume. The fingerprints on there doesn't necessarily mean it's the thief; although it does match with a killer thief. The police are unsure if the killer thief fingerprints on the knife belong to the same killer thief who robbed Rei. This is very important.)**

**Also before the wedding, someone broke into Taira's apartment while he was out, but nothing was stolen. Instead, the computer that he turned off was on. In the computer was a false wedding date, so even if the thief saw it, he couldn't have gone to the wedding by looking at that date because it was a fake date. Instead, there was a list of the guests and their addresses.**

**During the wedding, a dead burnt man was found in a warehouse next to the Temuzu River with a slit throat. The man was wearing a suit meant for weddings.**

**At the actual wedding, there are three suspicious men (who could potentially be the killer thief). All three of them were hiding their hands (suspicious because it looks like they're purposely trying to avoid leaving fingerprints to be checked) during the whole time. Each of the three men greeted the bride (actually a police stand-in but this doesn't really matter) in turn.**

**After a while, a package is delivered to the couple, which Taira opens without hesitation. Inside is a camera with a video they'll be playing at the wedding.**

The Reasoning (I'll provide the clues along the way because I didn't mention much of them during the context part):

P1: Taira opened up the package carelessly

P2: It is known that he is being threatened by the killer thief

P3: There could've been a possibility of the package being sent by the killer thief, meaning it would be dangerous

**C1**: Taira might've already known what was in there so he is suspicious for now

P4: Rei saw that the thief had a sty under his eye

P5: The sty is near impossible to see in the dark

**C2**: There must've been light in the room

P6: Neither the thief nor Rei had a flashlight

**C3**: Light must've come from another source from the outside

P7: When Taira came to rescue her, she could see the sty, even though it should've been too dark to see

**C4**: Taira brought a flashlight

**C5**: Taira must've known the lights would be out, otherwise he wouldn't have brought a flashlight

**C6**: Taira was working with the thief, because otherwise he couldn't have known that the lights would stop functioning at that time

*Taira has been pinned down as the culprit by Conan, but there still isn't enough evidence to go against him at this point, so...*

P8: The three suspicious men were all hiding their hands

P9: It is known that the knife had the fingerprints of the killer thief

**C7**: It is possible that they are trying to avoid fingerprints to be tested

P10: All three of the men greeted the bride (although she was just a police look-alike but that doesn't really matter here)

P11: The thief knew that his voice was heard by Rei (bride) but doesn't know how clear it was

P12: The thief would want to avoid being detected by Rei too early on for his crime to be carried out

**C8**: The three men are not the killer thief even though their hand-hiding is suspicious

P13: Taira was wearing a cast on his left hand not too long ago

P14: Taira's left hand would be unable to be tested for fingerprints

P15: In the wedding video on the camera, it shows that Taira could change clothes even with his cast extremely quickly (suspiciously quickly)

P16: The prints for *a* killer thief, not necessarily the one that robbed Rei, was from the left hand

**C9**: The left hand fingerprints were from Taira, who tried to hide it by faking a crushed hand and a cast, which means he is a killer thief

P17: A guy broke into Taira's apartment and saw a list of names and addresses of the wedding guests as well as the date of the wedding

P18: The wedding date was a fake one, the true wedding was one day after the fake

P19: The dead man was dressed formally as for a wedding

P20: Taira knew he wasn't in any danger, as demonstrated in **C1**

**C10**: The dead man most likely went to the wedding hall before the actual wedding, dressed formally, met Taira, and was led away and killed in the warehouse (the motive was that his accomplice, the thief, was too dumb to understand his plans for stealing more money, so he just killed him there. Conan didn't figure this part out though)

*Conan sends a trap to further incriminate the culprit, Taira, and push him into revealing himself as well as confessing. (He could've just taken his fingerprints and sent him to jail like that but idk...) He sends Chiba, an officer who wasn't present during this entire time, and dresses him up as the thief. He then presents this 'fake' thief to threaten Taira in front of everyone. Taira then confidently says he isn't the real thief.*

They then know that Taira knows the thief very well because:

* The build of the thief would be impossible to determine on a first encounter due to the darkness
* Taira claimed that the voice was one of the police officers in the wedding hall so it couldn't be the thief. This is false, however, because the police officer as the 'fake' thief wasn't even present in the wedding hall, so Taira couldn't possibly have known his voice.

Taira is then arrested, after confessing dramatically, trying to escape, and kicked down by Ran.

PS: There are two killer thieves in case anyone is confused. One of them is the one that robbed Rei, the other is Taira

This feat is basically Conan solving 4 cases at the same time, which in my opinion, might not even be in his top 5 feats. It's definitely harder than the 12 Million Hostages Case imo, but I'd like to know what other people think
