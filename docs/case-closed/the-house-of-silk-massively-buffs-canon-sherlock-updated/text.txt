The House of Silk massively buffs canon Sherlock (and updating an old take)

Couldn't find many good pictures of him that I haven't used so I resorted to this fanart

After reading THoS, I can confidently say that he has gone up considerably in my scaling. Although I was still somewhat dissatisfied with his scheming (my expectations were too high ngl), his deception, manipulation and strategy have gotten upscaled, especially methodologically.

Anyways, here's an updated version of my Kudo vs canon Sherlock take (my old one was a month ago):

FSIQ: Kudo extreme diffs (old take didn't change)

EQ: Sherlock extreme diffs (old take was Sherlock high diffs)

I had Sherlock taking EU pretty easily before but now, I think Kudo probably catches up with his feat during the Murderer, Shinichi Kudo Case. In the case, he is able to deduce the entire mental state and the changes within the mental state of a person by examining a few clues and without even meeting the guy

SQ: Sherlock very high diffs (old take didn't change)

AC: Sherlock extreme diffs (old take didn't change)

Adaptability: Kudo very high diffs (old take was Kudo mid diffs)

Kudo's performance in the Clash of Red & Black adaptability > Sherlock's prison escape adaptability by a bit; LA remains the same

Thinking: Sherlock extreme diffs (old take didn't change)

Reasoning: Sherlock very high diffs (old take was Sherlock extreme diffs)

The entire HoS case involved a couple of misdirections like with a lot of Kudo's best feats, which I think lowered the diff for him. Kudo's reasoning is still arguably more complex (seeing through the creative tricks and connecting them together) but equal in rigour

Deception: Kudo very high diffs

Some people might disagree with this but imo, Fake Akai plan > Sherlock's prison escape strategy in overall deception. Concealment goes to Kudo and misdirection to Sherlock though

Manipulation: Kudo very high diffs

Also very similar to deception. Sherlock might take logical manipulation maybe

Scheming: Kudo very high diffs (old take was Kudo mid diffs)

THoS massively buffed Sherlock's strategy with the prison escape scheme, which to my knowledge is the only notable scheme Sherlock has methodologically. I honestly don't interpret the Final Problem as him planning the whole thing, at least not yet (maybe someone can convince me)

ToM: Sherlock high diffs (old take remains the same)

Nothing to say here

Field Skills: Kudo extreme diffs

Now it gets pretty close here; Sherlock takes trap evasion and info gathering, Kudo takes trap setting>=, info control, and knowledge application>====. Kudo also takes countering but again, it's really close

Situational Adversities: Sherlock extreme diff or CGEW

Moriarty hunting him down is harder than Kudo's feats imo because a lot of the time, he (as Conan) was hidden away while performing his feats except for a few times especially in the 12MH case. The reason why I put Sherlock a tiny tiny bit higher is because although the danger was considerably less than a bomb in your face, his opponents had much more resources than him while Sherlock only had Watson and a few others, making him go against them with a massive disadvantage (talking about THoS here)

Intellectual Adversities: Kudo high diffs

Gin and Vermouth > Sherlock's best opponents by a bit

OVERALL: Kudo wins extreme diff

Last time I had Kudo high diffing Sherlock, so this is a massive improvement for him. I did do a lot more analysises on Kudo though, so it might change again, who knows?
