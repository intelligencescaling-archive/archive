Story:

Heiji and Kazuha are visiting Tokyo because the son of one of Heiji’s mother friends is getting married. Originally, his mom was supposed to go but she broke a bone and had to stay home, which is why she sent Heiji and Kazuha to attend the wedding. Both of them met Kogoro, Ran and Conan when they arrived and invited them to come with them.

They met the butler and the son's fiancée in a shrine and arrived at the mansion together with them, where they met the family’s servant and the rest of the fiancée’s family, including her fiance himself.

When night arrived, the Mori’s planned to go home and the Osaka duo (Heiji,Kazuha) decided to accompany them to say goodbye. They then heard glass break and all but Ran ran back to the mansion, where they discovered the butler's corpse.

Before the murder

When the 5 of them were at the Meiji shrine, they met the butler Akio Shigematsu and the fiancée of the family's son, Kaede Katagiri.

When Shigematsu asked what wish Kaede made at the shrine, she answered with “To have beautiful children.”.

Conan and Heiji look a bit confused as Heiji mentions that when she was making the wish it didn't look like she was wishing for children earlier as she was tearing up and making a gloomy face. This thought gets immediately swayed away by Kazuha who says that women before marriage are always like that with Ran agreeing on this.

(Observation + EP)

They were then invited to come to the mansion of the family where they met the family's servant Yuji Sakuraba, Kaede’s fiancée and the eldest son Kikuhiko Morizono, the family head Mikio Morizono, the eldest daughter Yurie Morizono and the family's cat Rebecca. We get a bit of Background info on them as Mikio tells that his wife died due to an accident some years ago. He himself was in there aswell, but survived and now has a damaged leg.

When Conan, Kogoro and Ran were planning to go they + Kazuha and Heiji heard a glass break, which came from a window. Everyone but Ran, who was told to observe everything outside, ran back inside, where they then found Akio’s corpse.

(Fast Thinking)

Conan's & Heiji's Investigation

After seeing the corpse Kazuha screamed, which attracted the other family members, who were shocked to see Shigematsu’s corpse with Kaede even fainting from the shock.

When Kogoro said that he called the police, Conan and Heiji immediately went to check out the entire room. They opened the window to see Ran still in her position. When she was asked if she saw anyone escaping through the window she denied it.

When Heiji and Conan heard this they instantly went to the body where Heiji found the keys to all rooms. Sakuraba even confirms that Shigematsu has all the keys of the house and that there's no copy of them, besides the keys of the peoples bedrooms.

Heiji then went to check with the key if the door was locked, and it was. Taking in everything from the locked windows, to the locked door and to the fact that only Shigematsu had keys to a room like that Heiji abduces that this is a murder in a locked room case they're dealing with.

Kikuhiko then said something about the dead Shigematsu, that he isn't a family member and that he won't postpone his honeymoon for him, which caused Sakuraba to punch him. This punch caused Kikuhiko to be sent flying, which in turn destroyed the formerly closed window. Before anything escalated the family head intervened.

Strangely enough, the family cat was already in the locked room for some reason.

Note: Whilst touching everything Heiji used a cloth to grab the keys and to open the window so he doesn't leave any of his fingerprints behind. He and Conan made sure to completely investigate the entire room.

Another important thing to note is that whilst Heiji looked through Shigematsu’s corpse, he never flinched or showed signs of any emotional disturbance. Kaede faints by only looking at the body and Heiji is touching it without giving any reaction except when he found the keys.

This happens many times throughout the series and Conan is on the same page here. Both are mentally strong enough (and already used to) to withstand the emotional trauma of constantly seeing a dead human being. + They never showed that it affects their mental health which is wild considering that constantly seeing people being murdered should normally traumatize you.

(VSI + Info Gathering + Mental Fortitude + Impulse Control + Knowledge + Lateral Thinking + Abductive Reasoning)

After the police arrived and started their own investigation. There isn't much to say here except that Megure sent out his people to search for the missing murder weapon.

When Conan and Heiji were talking while the police were searching for the weapon, they started to talk about 4 things that were bothering them.

1st: Why didn't the murderer leave the weapon next to the body? And why is the weapon missing entirely?

2nd: The blood marks on the floor. Why was the victim into the room?

3rd: The only room that was lit was the room where the victim was dragged into.

4th: The cat.

Heiji and Conan both have a feeling that they're missing out something, but don't know what. When they witnessed the interaction between Kikuhiko, Sakuraba, Ran & Kazuha they got another mystery to solve after Kazuha mentions that Sakuraba & Kaede have the exact same pendant.

(Intuition + Observation + Info Gathering)

The police Investigation also ended and none of the police found the weapon. Megure orders them to search some more.

Heiji notes that they should also search for some clothes since there's a lot of bleeding. The murderers clothes must've been splashed with blood after taking the knife out.

Megure later on talks about the alibis of the suspects:

Mikio: Waiting for Shigematsu in his room to talk.

Yurie: Taking a bath.

Kikuhiko: Reading magazines in his room whilst listening to music.

Sakuraba: Looking for the cat per Mikio’s request.

Kaede: Asleep in her room.

Kaede's alibi got Megure’s attention because Yurie went to knock on Kaede's door and nobody answered. According to Kaede she was so tired that she didn't hear a thing.

(Info Gathering)

A police officer then shows Megure and Heiji that there's a blood stain behind the window lock. Conan then spots a piece of glass that was on the balcony floor that had a little hole, which Heiji didn't notice when the window wasn't broken. Conan says that it's logical he didn't notice. He guesses that the piece of glass was placed at the edge of the wooden frame judging from its shape.

When the family head asks if Shigematsu can be put in a grave next to his wifes grave since they were close friends, Heiji knows that the story his mom told him was true:

According to his mom the dead wife of the family head Mikio was in love with Shigematsu. Mikio thought that everytime she came to his house it was to see him so he asked her to marry him (even though it was to see Shigematsu). After seeing Mikio say that he knows that the story must be correct and he notices a pattern since something similar is going on with Kaede and Sakuraba with the same pendant.

Conan then wants to test if they can see the thing they're looking for from the lower balcony and says that Heiji should go. Heiji then climbs down to the lower balcony as if it was nothing, even though he could very well slip and fall to his demise.

Heiji then observes something in the tree right in front of the room where they found the body, which is a bit wild considering it's literally pitch black outside.

(Crystallized Intelligence + EU + Observation + VSI + FRI + Cold Reading + Risk Taking)

A bit of trouble occurred here when Kikuhiko suddenly called out to Conan, who then lost his hold and nearly fell down if it wasn't for Sakuraba saving him.

The reason Kikuhiko didn't go to help him was because of his fear of heights.

Immediately after this scene Conan and Heiji went to the tree to search for the thing that Heiji observed.

There they found the knife and gloves wrapped in a blood stained shirt with a string around it. While they figured out the identity of the culprits, there are still some key details missing.

That doesn't matter though since it started raining. The rain made both of them think of the blinds and they immediately figured everything out. And since they have no proof for anything, they want to make the culprit give them the proof. How?

By making a Strategy.

(Formulation Speed)

Catching Shigematsu’s killer Strategy

Conan and Heiji have now figured out who the culprit is. But, to catch him with proof they had to come up with a Strategy to make him present the proof.

To catch him, they've had to go through these steps:

1st: Deducing the wrong culprit.

-Heiji “exposed” the completely wrong person and put all the blame of the crime on him without making it seem like he only wants to use him as bait. Heiji was talking and acting like this person was the real culprit.

Whilst at it, Heiji showed a trick on how the window was closed from outside via the string and the hole in the piece of glass. Together with this he counted out the people that couldn't have done this trick due to some condition they have. In this, he also counted out the actual culprit to make him feel like he's not being suspected. While he showed that this trick works, it was never actually used by the real culprit.

He then talks about the motive the “puppet culprit” had for killing Shigematsu, which included the puppet culprit's connection to Kaede, since the puppet culprit was outside with Kaede. With this he indirectly made Kaede expose this fact so that she can't protect the puppet culprit. The puppet culprit gets “arrested” and all are supposed to go to bed. Heiji also says that the weapon hasn’t been found. This is a lie he said to manipulate the real culprit into thinking that he can still put more blame on the puppet culprit.

Conan also plays a part in this step and convinces Ran & Kogoro that they should sleep there aswell.

2nd: The bedroom Trap.

-Heiji (and most likely Conan) anticipated that the real culprit would get the shirt, gloves and knife from the tree and put all of that in the puppet culprit’s bag to further make him seem like he's actually the culprit. Heiji is aware that the real culprit knows the house very well. He uses this to his advantage and switches the name of his room with the name of the puppet culprit's room, so that the real culprit would believe that he's in the puppet culprit's room, while in reality he's in Heiji’s room trying to hide the evidence in Heiji's bag.

Heiji also informed the police of their strategy and told them to be in position so that the culprit doesn't have a way to escape.

3rd: Catching the culprit.

-Heiji and Conan both catch the real culprit in Heiji's room and start telling their deduction of the case. Heiji also points out that they're in his room. Here the culprit gives up and admits that he did it.

1st Step: False culprit

(I'm just gonna put every picture here of his “false” deduction or however you want to call it)

Heiji called everyone into the room where the crime happened. Here, he starts his deduction and “exposes” Sakuraba as the culprit. Of course, Sakuraba denies this.

Heiji then goes to show the “trick” that “Sakuraba used”:

For this he uses a string with a loop and a bit of cellotape. He fastens the loop around the window’s lock, puts the broken piece of glass back and pulls the string through it. He then goes outside on the balcony, closes the window and pulls the string, which causes the window to be locked. When the string is pulled harder it gets removed from the tape. A perfect locked room murder.

Everything I've just written is supposed to be what Sakuraba did, and as Heiji showed it actually works.

Heiji says that the reason Sakuraba hit Kikuhiko was so that he'd hit the window and break the glass, so that this piece of evidence would disappear. He then goes on to say that Sakuraba is the only one that's able to do that because:

1.Yurie & Kaede wouldn't be able to do that with the clothes they're wearing.

2.Mikio has a damaged leg, therefore it would be impossible for him aswell.

3.Kikuhiko has a fear of heights, so he can't be the culprit either.

“Conclusion”: Therefore Sakuraba must be the killer, since he's able to climb trees to catch the family's cat. With all of the things Heiji said so far, Kaede cuts in and says that it's impossible for Sakuraba to be the killer, since the both of them were outside searching for Rebecca (the cat).

Heiji used this to point out that there's more going on between the two of them because they wear the same pendant. He uses this to say that Kaede's statement can't really be trusted because of their relationship. He then also says that one of Sakuraba’s Shirts was found covered in blood.

All of this results in Sakuraba getting “arrested”, just as Conan and Heiji wanted.

All while Heiji was explaining this he remained in his act as if Sakuraba really did the crime. He made everyone (besides Kaede & Kikuhiko) question Sakuraba whilst maintaining his act.

(FRI + Deductive Reasoning + Inductive Reasoning + Logical Reasoning + Analytical Thinking + Linear Thinking + Direct Manipulation + Indirect Manipulation + Scapegoating + Acting Skills + Sophistry + Psychological Warfare)

After Sakuraba gets arrested Heiji says that the weapon hasn't been found yet, which is a bluff considering we saw him and Conan finding the knife before, but the others don't know that.

Conan then innocently asks if they could stay there to sleep aswell. Considering that the real culprit is exposed by both later on we can assume that he successfully persuaded them.

(Persuasion + Acting Skills + Bluffing)

2nd & 3rd Step: Catching the real culprit

When everyone went to sleep, a figure went to the tree where all the evidence was. He then walked through the dark corridor and found “Sakuraba's Room” and went inside to hide the knife there. Heiji then turned on the light and exposed the figure as the real criminal, Kikuhiko Morizono.

Kikuhiko immediately denies being the culprit saying that he couldn't have done the trick because of his fear of heights. Heiji then tells him that there was no trick. He reasons that because there's only a blood print on the lock, but the balcony is clear. When Kikuhiko questions how he could've locked the window and door, Conan cuts in from behind and says that there weren’t any tricks at all and that Kikuhiko was in the room from the start.

Conan continues and says that he was most likely hiding behind the left curtain in the room next to the one where the body was found. That's also the reason why one of the blinds was closed, so that no one could see that he was standing there. That's also the reason why he switched off the light and dragged the body to the other room. Everyone would follow the trail and would be too distracted by the body, which gave him the chance to join the others from behind the curtain, pretending to have rushed here after Kazuha screamed.

As for why the cat was there: Kikuhiko placed the cat there so that Sakuraba's alibi would become fragile since his dad, the family head, would ask Sakuraba to search for the cat. Conan then continues that Kikuhiko had the scissors and string prepared and had already drilled a hole in the glass.

He called Shigematsu into the room, killed him and dragged his body to the other room. He then rolled the shirt, gloves and knife together and threw them out the window into the tree, switched the lights off in the next room and hid behind the curtain. He saw Conan, Heiji etc. through the window, so he smashed the window with the chair to get them to find the body and then appeared when everyone joined in.

Conan then says that Kikuhiko is the only one that could've done it and the shirt that was wrapped around the knife and clothes proves it. Because of his fear of heights, Kikuhiko had no choice but to throw the evidence into the tree. The hole and the provocation of Sakuraba was only done so that the blame would shift onto Sakuraba and conceal the true intent behind it.

Heiji mentions that the police are surrounding the building and waiting for his signal, indicating that he included them in this Strategy. Kikuhiko then tries to say that he only went into Sakuraba's room to help the police find the murder weapon, which “he found in Sakuraba's bag”.

Heiji boredly points out that this is his (Heiji's) bag and not Sakuraba's, immensely shocking Kikuhiko. Conan then says that Sakuraba's room is next to Heiji's room. They anticipated that something like this would happen. The room and the bag is a trap that Heiji set alongside the police to lure Kikuhiko out into giving them crucial evidence.

Seeing that there's no way out, Kikuhiko confesses that he did it. His motive was that he thought that Shigematsu was blackmailing him to drop the marriage or else he'd tell his father about his company's bad payments. He says that he had to do something or he'd be blackmailed forever. That's also the reason why he wanted to put the blame “on the one who stole his fiancée”, aka. Sakuraba.

Heiji & Conan then go on to say that Shigematsu never blackmailed him. Infact, Sakuraba never stole Kaede from him, she always came to see Sakuraba. Shigematsu arranged the marriage because he thought Kaede liked Kikuhiko, but he was wrong, like his dad back then.

The reason why Shigematsu didn't tell Kikuhiko anything is because of the shock Kikuhiko would feel. Shigematsu didn't blackmail him, he only wanted the wedding to be renounced so that Sakuraba and Kaede could be together. Two young people who share the same tragic fate that he did back then.

Afterwards Kikuhiko was arrested.
