New potential character's feat analysis - Yuri/Joeri (Yu-Gi-Oh Arc-V)

Yuri is one of the four counterparts of Zarc in Yugioh arc-V. Zarc was a powerful duelist, having the four dimension dragons in his deck, and later got divided into four different people: Yuri (fusion dimension), Yugo (Synchro dimension), Yuto (xyz dimension) and Yuya (pendulum dimension).

To explain Yuri's main feat, we need to understand his main goal: power. As one of Zarc's counterparts, Yuri was the only one who inherited his bad side. Sadistic, ruthless, and an enormous lust for power. That drove Yuri to later plan the most plot changing action that could be compared to Furuta's dragon plan in a lower scale.

The plan had a single goal: merge all Yu's into one and revive zarc, giving Yuri tremendous power that he urged for his whole life.

Yuri first went after Yuya's father: Yusho, cornering him and his son, making Yusho challenge Yuri for a duel. Yuri, having the power to imprison Yusho in a card, didn't miss the chance to do it right in front of Yuya, knowing fully well the damage it would cause, making Yuya's resistance to his darkness more fragile, and more susceptible to Zarc merge.

Yuri makes sure to use his trump card "starving venom dragon", one of the four dimensional dragons to finish Yusho off and imprison him in a card, knowing that this action would lead to Yuya asking for a duel with him, which was exactly what he wanted.

Yuya challenges Yuri, and says that if he wins, Yuri must free Yusho. Yuri also said his conditions: if Yuya loses, Yuri will kill Yusho right in front of Yuya. Here, Yuri deliberately puts pressure on Yuya, making him more emotional and more susceptible to mistakes, which is the opposite of the usual showmanship behavior Yuya has.

During the battle, Yuya deliberately disturbs Yuya by using "starving venom dragon", the same dragon that basically sealed Yusho's fate, disturbing Yuya (and Yuto, who was fused with Yuya),  whose heart was already being taken by darkness, slowly opening the gates for Zarc's merge.

As the battle progresses, Yuya finds himself more and more cornered, he was on verge of losing this duel, and it would be Yuri's turn soon. If he lost, Yusho would die, and that made Yuya sets a spell card that would negate any damage he would take in Yuri's turn, with the condition of discarding a card, but, the only card he had was "Smiling world", the only memory he had of his father, and if he discarded it, he would lose the most important thing for him.

Yuya, to avoid using the spell card and discarding the only card he has, decides to look for action cards that negate damage (cards that appear randomly in the arena). But everytime Yuya finds and uses one, Yuri immediately answers with a card that negates Yuya's efforts.

Feeling pressured and about to lose from Yuri's final attack, and consequently losing his father, Yuya instinctively discarded Smiling World, negating the damage he would take, but at the cost of the only memory of his father Yusho, which hurt him deeply, driving him to insanity.

Yuya, now in pure rage, plays more aggressively, he has to win, he at least needs to save his father's body. That leads to Yuya summoning rebelious dragon and odd eyes dragon, both dimensional dragons that were belonging to zarc. Yuri had also expected that, and part of the pressure he was putting on Yuya was for the summoning of that two dragons.

Yuri, with his starving venom on field, summons his other dragon, clear wing dragon. All the dimensional dragons were on the field, and as the duel progressed, Yuri saw himself more and more cornered, but it didn't matter, since his plan was a win-win situation now.

Yuya deals the final blow on Yuri, at the cost of his own sanity. Yuri says that Yuya can absorb him now, Yuya's friends say that he wouldn't do such a thing. But now, Yuya was susceptible to darkness, since he lost his essence: the showmanship, replaced by an urgent desire to win, triggered by the immediate threat of his dad's death.

Yuya, who had episodes ago absorbed Yuto, absorbs Yuri, who previously absorbed Yugo. And now, with the four dragons and the four counterparts together, the ultimate symbol of power, the very thing Yuri craved for, was reborn:  Zarc.
