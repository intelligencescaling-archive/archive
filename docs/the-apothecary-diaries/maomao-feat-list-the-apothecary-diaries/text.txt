Maomao feat list

Maomao Intelligence feats:

**“If I should die, I'd want to die of poison” - Maomao**

(S2E14) She solved the Mystery of the Dancing Ghost. After Jinshi brings her along to investigate, she examines the storeroom adjacent to Lishu’s bathroom and quickly discovers black mold. From this, she reasons that there is an underground drainage system beneath the consort’s bath, and that when Lishu released the water, the rising steam caused the curtains to ruffle in a way that looked like dancing. To explain the appearance of a ghostly face, she also finds a mirror Lishu had been gifted by her late mother. The special bronze mirror was etched with markings that cast an image when light struck it from a certain angle, and as the moonlight streamed through the bathroom window, it projected the face of a woman—leading Lishu to believe she was seeing a ghost. This feat showcases \[DR (Deductive Reasoning) PER (Perceptual Reasoning) AT (Analytical Thinking) LGT (Logical Thinking) LT (Linear Thinking) and LR (Logical Reasoning)\].

(S1E4) She solved the case of gradual lead poisoning that affected Lady Lihua and killed her infant son. She first confirmed the illness was chronic lead poisoning by observing Lady Lihua’s pallid complexion low vitality and face powder. Since the infant had died while Lady Lihua survived, she reasoned—based on her understanding of how the poison was absorbed—that inhaling the powder dust was harmful primarily to the mother, whereas the infant’s poisoning was much more severe because it had been ingested orally with honey, a faster and far more lethal route than inhalation. She then connected this to the palace’s history, recalling stories of the former emperor using high-grade lead ink on the walls, which suggested the banned powder came from an old, potent stock. Finally, she realised that the head lady-in-waiting had been administering the powder in honey, believing it to be a “precious” cure, which explained the direct and fatal ingestion by the infant. This feat showcases \[DR (Deductive Reasoning) LR (Logical Reasoning) LGT (Logical Thinking) LTM (Long Term Memory) NLT (Non Linear Thinking) PER (Perceptual Reasoning) and AT (Analytical Thinking)\].

(S2E9) She uncovered the true cause of the Late Emperor’s death. When Empress Dowager Anshi visited the inner court, she sought her help, wanting to know whether she had somehow cursed her late husband. After the Emperor died, his body did not decay for nearly a year while it was on display in the palace, and Anshi feared she might have been responsible. Upon learning that the Emperor had spent his final days in seclusion painting, she deduced that he had used orpiment—a toxic substance used to produce yellow pigment. Her theory was confirmed in Jinshi’s old toy box. She then explained to the Empress Dowager that orpiment behaves similarly to arsenic, causing a body to take much longer to begin decomposing. This feat showcases \[DR (Deductive Reasoning) LR (Logical Reasoning) LGT (Logical Thinking) and LT (Linear Thinking)\].

(S1E13) She solved how the bureaucrat ended up in a coma by reasoning that the highly poisonous but perfectly prepared puffer fish was a red herring, and that the true problem lay in an improperly prepared garnish. She traced the seaweed’s origin to a southern distributor and uncovered that the bureaucrat’s angry brother-in-law was behind both the seaweed and the poisoning. This feat showcases \[DR (Deductive Reasoning) AT (Analytical Thinking) PS (Problem Solving) TE (Trap Evasion) and NLT (Non Linear Thinking)\].

(S1E22) After Lakan challenges Jinshi to find a blue rose to please the Emperor, Jinshi turns to her for help. She works day and night, cultivating healthy roses in a hothouse, then dyes the rose water to temporarily turn them blue for the Emperor’s spring party. This feat showcases \[PM (Psychological Manipulation) IDM (Indirect Manipulation) ST (Strategic Thinking) PS (Problem Solving) TE (Trap Evasion) and CT (Creative Thinking)\].

(S1E6) She solved the attempted Garden Party poisoning by being the taste tester, sampling each meal to ensure its safety. She then noticed that Lishu’s meal had been switched and realised that something was amiss. From this, and dusted fingerprints, she reasoned that Fengming was responsible, noting that Lishu’s dishes had been prepared differently from the others. This feat showcases \[PER (Perceptual Reasoning) LGT (Logical Thinking) LT (Linear Thinking) PS (Problem Solving) and AT (Analytical Thinking)\]

(S2E15) She realizes that Consort Gyokuyou might have a dangerous breech birth. After Gyokuyou tells her that the baby is kicking downwards, she feels the baby and confirms that a breech delivery is imminent. She then calls her adoptive father to assist with the delivery knowing that he was the doctor who performed a successful c-section on Empress Dowager Anshi. This feat showcases \[VCI (Verbal Comprehension Index) LGT (Logical Thinking) LT (Linear Thinking) and PS (Problem Solving)\].

(S1E19) She unknowingly saved Jinshi by first studying the scene of a warehouse explosion in earlier episodes, where ceremonial tools had disappeared in the fire. She then examined the items found at the scenes of a poisoning and another explosion, which both traced back to the warehouse incident and pointed toward the next planned catastrophe: a sabotaged beam set to fall during a purification ceremony. Realising the beam was meant to kill she rushed unknowingly to his aid and unknowingly saved him. This feat showcases \[TE (Trap Evasion) LT (Linear Thinking) DR (Deductive Reasoning) PER (Perceptual Reasoning) PS (Problem Solving) Foresight and AT (Analytical Thinking)\].

(S1E19) She sustained getting hit and falling down a flight of stairs while trying to enter and unknowingly save Jinshi, ignoring her pain with only adrenaline to keep her going—a surge that subsided a few minutes later when her leg was severely injured by the rubble—yet she still managed to control her emotions throughout. This feat showcases \[MF (Mental Fortitude) ST (Stress Tolerance) EM (Emotional Management) and Perseverance\].

(S2E10) She saves herself and others from carbon monoxide poisoning. After agreeing to join Yinghua on a night out with a group of ladies gathering to tell ghost stories, everyone begins to feel sleepy as they sit around the fire, including her. But when she starts to feel herself losing consciousness, she realises something is wrong. Even through the haze clouding her thoughts, she snaps to attention and recognizes that the very air is poisoning them all. With the windows and doors closed and a fire burning without any ventilation, everyone in the room is succumbing to carbon monoxide poisoning. She then gets Yinghua and Shisui to help bring everyone out into the open air. This feat showcases \[LGT (Logical Thinking) LT (Linear Thinking) PER (Perceptual Reasoning) PS (Problem Solving) SOA (Social Awareness) and AT (Analytical Thinking)\].

She was trained by Luomen, a genius physician, through whom she acquired extraordinary knowledge of poisons, diseases, and medicines, as well as exceptional deductive skills, further honing her abilities in the inner palace for two years, though her training was occasionally interrupted by disruptions. This feat showcases \[CI (Crystallised Intelligence) LA (Learning Ability) and LC (Learning Capability)\].

She became a poison tester, experimenting with a variety of poisons on herself to build resistance and immunity to most of them. This feat showcases \[ST (Strategic Thinking) LGT (Logical Thinking) LT (Linear Thinking) and PS (Problem Solving)\].
