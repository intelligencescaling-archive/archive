Hannibal's "Team Transformation Strategy" is extremely superficial, but such superficiality doesn't exist anywhere else in the world.

Jack Crawford: By kidnapping his first assistant, Hannibal instills a sense of "failure to protect" in Jack, giving him a profound feeling of guilt. In the following process, during a dinner with Hannibal, he mentions that Will Graham is overly fragile, presenting Will as "new and in need of protection." Consequently, Jack would push Will even harder, which would play a crucial role in Will Graham's eventual breaking.

Additionally, since it becomes officially certain that Hannibal is the killer but they still lack the evidence to catch him, they are forced into the plan seen in the Season 2 finale. Due to the pain Jack suffered because of his wife and the events of that season finale, Jack is consumed by an overwhelming desire for revenge.

In Season 3, Hannibal allows himself to be beaten by Jack for this reason; because if Hannibal is beaten by Jack, Jack will be freed from his vengeful feelings, purified of his weaknesses and pains, and Hannibal will teach him the "occasional necessity of shedding blood."

Alana Bloom: The Alana Bloom situation is even more tragic. Hannibal, who is both her colleague and an old acquaintance, claims Abigail Hobbs before Alana (who also wanted to protect her) can. Using certain methods and drugging Abigail, he makes her say, "I see a family." Before this, Hannibal provides exactly the environment Abigail Hobbs’ psychology craved; he cooks sausage and eggs, the last meal she had with her father and family. Naturally, someone like Abigail Hobbs, suffering from family loss, sees Hannibal next to Alana Bloom and perceives it as a family moment due to the influence of the drugs.

After this, everything moves very fast; anyone who has watched the series has seen this relationship. In the ensuing process, Hannibal uses Alana Bloom both in his strategy to surround Will and as a constant witness to cover up his own murders. She essentially reaches a point where we can call her a "shield."
Of course, this changes after the Season 2 finale.

Quite unexpectedly, Alana Bloom becomes gay and enters a relationship with Margot that develops at incredible speed. However, Hannibal's goal here was for Alana to work with Verger to catch him, while also protecting the baby he wanted in line with the baby plan. But what he wanted most was for someone like Alana Bloom to finally take responsibility.

Margot Verger and Her Brother: Margot Verger actually had a similar past to Alana; she was someone who had been put in difficult situations by her brother and realized she couldn't claim the family inheritance without a baby. Previously, Hannibal had Mason's face eaten by dogs for this reason; as a result, her brother would lose his physical abilities and she, along with Alana Bloom, could pursue him.

Margot had become gay because, in line with the baby plan, she was already someone with many traumas caused by men and now she had a baby. Besides, who could say no to a beautiful woman like Alana Bloom? :D

Anyway, I didn't explain the Will Graham and Chilton characters because they are both more layered and are some of the main subjects of the series.
