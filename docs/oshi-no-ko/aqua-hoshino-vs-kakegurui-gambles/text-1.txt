INTRODUCTION ARC:

GAMBLE 1: VOTE ROCK PAPER SCISSORS.

Opponents: Mary Saotome

Result: Fails ❌ , Low Diff

Reason: Aqua’s analytical skills are not that good in a fixed situation, he wouldn’t be able to spot out the cheat that Mary had planned by having others manipulate the votes and having the others letting see which card aqua has picked, and Mary could emotionally break him too.

GAMBLE 2: DOUBLE MEMORY

Opponents: Itsuki Sumeragi

Result: Fails ❌ , Low diff

Reason: For this game, you need to require memory skills and decent CPI and even analytical skills, which aqua isn’t good at from what I remember, and getting it all In A row is even harder, he wouldn’t be able to outsmart sumeragi in this match too.

GAMBLE 3: LIFE OR DEATH

Opponents: Yuriko Nishinotouin

Result: Fails ❌, No Diff

Reason: like said before, his analytical skills isn’t good in a fixed situation, he wouldn’t be able to spot out the cheat that the dealer and yuriko has been using and furthermore, the game was rigged, and it’s impossible to win against yuriko in this case, imo not even Baku would clear lol.

**All-out Battle Against the Student Council Arc:**

GAMBLE 6: Top Idol Championship

Opponent: Yumemi Yumemite

Allies: Itsuki Sumeragi, Mary Saotome, Ryota Suzui

Result: FAILS ❌ , Mid Diff

Reason: The Gamble is divided into 9 mini games, he will be able to pass a few, like the lung power check, because as an actor, he has really good stamina, so he should be able to Clear this one, and as well as the fan appreciation one if he knows Mary Saotome‘s birthday or is close to it. And he could also pass the “make a poker face” as yumemi cannot take spicy food (idk if aqua can take it, but I will give it to him) Overall, he does fail because yumemi has more experience than him in this gamble.

GAMBLE 7: Choice Poker

Opponent: Kaede Manyuda

Allies: Itsuki Sumeragi

Result: FAILS ❌ , Mid Diff

Reason: this really depends on luck honestly, on who has the better hand and weaker hand and they are able to choose, even with itsuki getting in, kaede would be able to overcome in as shown in the anime, and aqua wouldn’t be able to manipulate itsuki to the extent of Yumeko, so therefore he would lose against kaede.

GAMBLE 8: Tarot

Opponent: Kirari Momobami

Allies: Ryota Suzui

Result: FAILS ❌ , No Diff

Reason: alright idk if I even have to explain this bruh, even the opponent is overkill for aqua already 😭 but anyways, since the gamble really comed down to Ryota, Aqua wouldn’t be able to manipulate to the extent of Yumeko to Ryota to activate his “gambling freak on” meaning he wouldn’t be able to get his good intuition to tie the game, meaning aqua would lose to Kirari.
