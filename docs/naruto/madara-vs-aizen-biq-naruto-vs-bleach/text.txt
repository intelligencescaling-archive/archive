Madara vs Aizen BIQ

This is made in reference to a Twitter thread I made awhile back. Despite the fact I deleted my account, I always wanted to go back and redo this as I got more knowledgeable, so as a result, here we are. I’ll be going for certain categories of SCD because pretty sure most would agree in any other situation, Aizen STOMPS hard.

I wanna say no abilities will be used and stats will be equalized.

Strategy: I’ll start with Aizen since people claim that he is primarily carried by Kyoka Suigetsu, much like claims around Light with his Death Note and Lelouch with Geass, but that isn’t as seen on many occasions of Aizen actually performing his feats. We see in his solo vs Gotei 13 and Vizards fight he had a tactic to take them down, by rialing up the team in that he brought to the forefront Toshiro’s hatred for him, which then when Toshiro stabbed Momo in a moment of vulnerability, Aizen played some mind games to trick them he activated Kyoka Suigetsu from the start, and so when Toshiro charges head first, Aizen proceeds to cut down the whole team.

It should be noted that unlike some claims, Aizen avoided using Kyoka Suigetsu from the start, because we see much of the fight from Ichigo’s POV, and he doesn’t call out anything weird to suggest Aizen is using it (remember he is the only one not affected by Aizen’s hypnosis) and to add to it, when he has a flashback about when Aizen used KS, he remembers he used it around the time Soi Fon tried to stab him (makes sense as the only wound on Momo as it seems is the one Toshiro gave her, not the stabs from Shunsui and Soi Fon).

Something to add is that in order for Kyoka Suigetsu to work fully, Aizen needs to observe vocal tones and patterns of his opponents to then create the convincing illusion after observing for an extensive amount of time, so what if we looked at a situation where Aizen is at a disadvantage? When he fought against Yamamoto, the old man exposed a weakness in that Aizen didn’t cover up the faint spiritual pressure from Kyoka Suigetsu, so Yamamoto could tell he is holding onto the real Aizen, and whilst Aizen was busy, Yamamoto engineered a technique that effectively wipes out everyone, with very little options, Aizen allowed Wonderweiss to enter the picture and fight. Or how when he fought against Yhwach he was sealed and imprisoned, so he tested to see if his Kyoka Suigetsu works and it did, so then when he fought Yhwach, he was able to cover his perception of the Almighty with his illusions, and then when Yhwach seemingly saw through it at first, it was actually another illusion Aizen created to throw him off, allowing Ichigo enough of a split second time to read the situation and act on Aizen’s tactics.

Yhwach’s Almighty allows him to foresee an infinite amount of futures at a higher plane and it allows him to see through everything and once those powers take his side, they can’t be used against him again, this means Aizen needed to create layers of infinite amounts future illusions all at once in his mind to trick Yhwach, on top of him counting on Ichigo, all whilst he is at a disadvantage and has no choice but to use his illusions.

As for Madara, there is a point in which he actually strategies, when he activated the Infinite Tsukuyomi, knowing that Naruto and Sasuke are big points of concern, he launches meteors and creates Limbo clones to distract the both of them, allowing him for long enough to activate the Jutsu and put everyone in the dream. Another would be how when he first got the 10 Tails, he was able to apply battle tactics of removing the intelligence gathering of the Leaf and cripple their forces.

This should be a tie, right? Well no, because the difference lies in how they proceed with their strategies, Madara's just tend to focus on overpowering the opponents, meteors and limbo are overwhelming forces yes, but that's all he did, and removing the intelligence of the opponent is a pretty simple tactic, especially with how he had the 10 Tails with him, Aizen’s plans meanwhile introduced enough moving parts to disrupt his targets, including emotional manipulation, contingencies, introduce elements of chaos to take down team work, all complex aspects that Aizen handles with efficiency.

So I would say Aizen takes strategy as a result.

Observation: Both characters tend to be incredibly observant, noticing patterns within opponents' attacks, anticipating what attack will happen at what time, and commenting on strengths and weaknesses. What separates the two is that Aizen makes more complex and profound observations, he takes mental notes of all those aspects at the same time during fights, such as how he anticipated Toshiro charging head first and readjust his reflexes when under Shinji’s Shikai when noticing it was being taken in effect, on top of consistently seeing through his opponent’s thought process to then either manipulate them or taunt them, as seen with how he reconstructed the situation in Hueco Mundo and found out Ichigo’s thought process in his hesitation whilst fighting him or how he taunted Hiyori during a fight, and of course, the fact Kyoka Suigetsu requires Aizen to visualize the illusion before creating everything would require him to observe everything during a fight. Madara is observant sure, but his more so come off as passive, like how he observed Sasuke’s Rinnegan ability after it was used, meanwhile Aizen was actively analyzing situations, thought process, abilities and more during fights, an example like how he adapted to Shinji’s Shikai. Even when his arrogance was taking over his analytical mind, Aizen still had his tendency to make observations and speculate on opponent’s power-ups.

It was harder to judge for Madara as unlike Aizen, he has the Sharingan, which shortens the information gathering process dramatically, and unlike the claims for Aizen, a case can be made Madara would be carried by Sharingan, as shown in the Sasuke vs Deidera fight, it shortened the process of information gathering, with Sasuke pointing out he can see Chakra as different colors and see at a cellular level. This isn't to say Madara isn't observant, but his could be due to Sharingan, and whenever he does make an observation of his own, its usually a comment like "man this Lava style ain't worth absorbing".

Emotional Management: This one is hard, much like other Uchiha, Madara keeps up a very dead eyed expression despite being called emotional by Tobirama, and Aizen keeps up his composure for like 90% of the time (that last part going to when he allowed his arrogance to consume him during his Hogyoku transformations to where he abandoned his analytical side) rarely losing face or panicking. However, Madara gets more consumed by his arrogance more times than Aizen does, like how Madara lost to Hashirama because he let his guard down and not expecting a back stab, or how the Kage often times got free hits because Madara was more so just focused on having fun instead of getting done with it, and how he arrogantly proclaimed that he has both Naruto and Sasuke’s powers and that it will be the final battle only to get proven wrong immediately, in fact we see him in his mind having wishing thoughts about wanting to have Sasuke on his side instead of Obito. Aizen meanwhile rarely lets his arrogance consume him, I would say the fact Aizen can make more effective observations and strategies during moments of where most would overthinking shows he has better control of his emotions, sure he got caught by surprise with Yamamoto, but who would have expected Yamamoto still having some fight in him after he used his whole body to condense an explosion that would have wiped out everyone and laid on the ground for some time, Aizen even criticizes himself afterwards and won’t allow a mistake like that to happen again.

So yeah, I would say this category easily goes to Aizen, with him rarely allowing his emotions to get the better of him.

Critical thinking: Both have displayed moments of critical thinking that they need to maneuver around in some way, Madara used Wood Clone to misdirect the Kage and get a clean hit on Tsunade, Aizen has used Kyoka Suigetsu at the right time to make Toshiro stab Momo. Who has it better? It would have to be Aizen again. On many occasions, we see Aizen take routes and not put himself in unnecessary situations, when Yamamoto sealed him away, he let the Espada fight and bid his time, which they did, softening the enemy, so that when he broke free and recognized the Espada weren’t living up to his expectations, he immediately was able to take over and performed better and had an easier time, or how when he fought the Gotei 13 and it was pointed out they are focused on not letting him use Kyoka Suigetsu, Aizen primarily uses Zanjutsu, Hakuda, Hoho and spiritual energy when needed to overpower them, only using Kyoka Suigetsu at the last second and played mind games with Shinji to misdirect him to make the Vizard believe he used Kyoka Suigetsu from the start, meanwhile Madara, he tends to overpower his opponents when put in a corner, when he was being sealed, he jumped to Perfect Susanoo, or how when the Kage proclaimed they made him use a clone, Madara just revealed more to overpower them. So in total, Madara tends to prefer raw power, meanwhile Aizen knows how to play his cards right.

Improvisation: It was hard to judge this point for Aizen, as he isn’t in many situations that require improvisation, sure his tactic against Yhwach in a disadvantageous situation could prove it, and he adapted to Shinji’s Shikai, but I wouldn’t say it’s true improvisation, both could just be a form of adaptation that don't need to encompass improvisation. Madara meanwhile constantly shifted his battle tactics when needed, half the time he performs an ability just because he wants to test it out and can use it for great lengths, like the Majestic Attire Susanoo, or how when he noticed he was being overpowered by Naruto and Sasuke, he was able to substitute himself with Limbo and delayed Naruto and Sasuke just enough so he can grab his second Rinnegan. Aizen could improvise, but he tends to lean more in planning and manipulating his opponents, Madara revels in the joy of battle and does it more consistently than Aizen, so he takes this category.

This isn’t to say Aizen can’t do it, via scaling and his FRI he should be capable of it, but he never really performs it in a lot of his battles because most of the time he doesn't need to

So with all the categories done, we see that while Madara takes improvisation, Aizen takes everything else when it comes to a fight, I can see the fight going that Aizen sees Madara’s arrogance and his tendency to be consumed by the fight and plays along with it for his plan, and so when Madara’s guard is down after Aizen lets his arrogance consume him, Aizen takes him down with a surprise attack when the time comes.
