Rick Prime's Diane Massacre Strategy - Superficial Analysis

When Rick Prime meets Rick Sanchez for the first time and Sanchez rejects his offer to become a "God of the Multiverse," Prime says, "Dude, Ricks don't reject this. Who do you think you are?" to which Rick replies, "I guess I'm just a different kind of Rick."

The real core issue here is that Rick Prime had never faced such a rejection before. Because he hadn't encountered this kind of pushback, it’s highly probable he failed to see the full extent of the influence Diane and Beth held over other Ricks.

This is precisely why even Prime despises his own variants—because they are all the same idiots who believe they are better than him and think their relationship with Diane is eternal. Yet, in reality, relationships are fueled by learning limits and discovering unknown secrets. Even though Rick’s bond with Diane would naturally unravel over time through arguments or death, driving him out into the multiverse anyway, this process was being delayed.

That’s why Rick Prime killed every single Diane across the infinite multiverse: by removing this limiting shield, every Rick—instead of Rick Prime having to go universe by universe handing out portal devices—would now be forced to develop this technology on their own.

However, he murdered Rick Sanchez's wife right in front of his eyes, at his happiest moment while going out for ice cream. The reason was to crush Rick Sanchez’s smug, potential-wasting, emotional nature—both to punish him and to construct an irreplaceable "Flawless Wife/Companion" picture in the minds of Ricks who hadn't yet grown tired of their relationship with Diane.

From that moment on, Rick began to sanctify and worship a woman in his mind who might never have truly existed that way, who might not have been like that at all, and who perhaps had trillions of flaws.

Rick Farming Theory: Why don’t all the Ricks realize that they’re actually in an even worse position while making fun of Jerry?

They themselves interfere with Beth and Jerry’s lives across an infinite number of universes to ensure that they end up together, and then they kidnap the Mortys that are born from those relationships. But isn’t their own life basically built around the same dynamic?

I don’t know why, but so far, every Rick we’ve seen seems to follow the same path when it comes to Diane and Beth. We have never seen a Rick who exists outside of this relationship dynamic.

If this multiverse works according to quantum fluctuations, with a separate universe being created for every possible outcome, then in roughly 80% of the multiverse, Diane should have been replaced by another woman, and Beth should have been replaced by another daughter—or even a son.

And we must not forget that Rick isn't even the smartest person in the entire multiverse; he simply lives behind the fence he's built between the multiverse, in a dimension where only he is the smartest, and that actually reveals his hypocrisy.

I think there is an organization above even the Citadel that controls the lives of every Rick, and Rick Prime erased every Diane in order to save the Ricks from it.
