Evil Morty & Rick Sanchez C-137’s Citadel Feat

To avoid making this complicated, I’ll give a description of C-137 and Evil Morty’s actions separately.

(Close Rick Counters of the Rick Kind Season 1 Episode 10 Season 3 Episode 7 The Ricklantis Mixup & Rickmurai Jack Season 5 Episode 10)

EM: He needed a way to breach the Central Finite Curve to reach a universe outside the CFC where Rick was not the smartest being in existence. The only way to do that was to obtain the schematics of the Citadel to find a way to weaponize it to breach the CFC, and those schematics were in the mind of Rick Sanchez from Dimension C-137—the architect behind the Citadel. So, in advance, he used Evil Rick—his original Rick—controlling him via a transmitter hidden in his eyepatch, since he had forced Evil Rick to have a receiver implanted in his brain, allowing him to control Evil Rick’s actions. He then had Evil Rick kill multiple Ricks from other dimensions and hacked C-137’s portal gun to upload the travel data from Evil Rick onto C-137’s portal gun, making it appear as though C-137 was the culprit. He specifically chose to frame C-137 because he was the most believable suspect due to his murderous history, his noncooperation with the Council, and his rogue nature. He also deliberately murdered the second most likely suspect—the Scientist Formerly Known as Rick—through Evil Rick. He did this because he needed C-137, and only C-137, to trace the hacking of his portal gun back to Evil Rick and confront him at a base trap designed to capture him. There, he strapped him down and hooked him up to a mind-reading device to scan his brain for the dimensional data drive through Evil Rick. For the base trap, he kidnapped hundreds of other Mortys beforehand to use them as a cloaking device to mask his brainwaves from the Citadel so the Council of Ricks couldn’t locate the base. After Rick killed Evil Rick and reported to the Council of Ricks that Evil Rick was the true culprit, Evil Morty was already gone—destroying the transmitter, with the dimensional database now in his mind. He then knew he had to blow a hole in the Central Finite Curve using the dimensional drive, but he also needed a plan to escape afterward. So he became President of the Citadel to gain the power, resources, and direct access to the Mortys and Ricks necessary for his escape. He won the election by staging a failed assassination attempt to gain sympathy votes. He hired Investigator Rick to give incriminating files about himself to his former campaign manager, ensuring the former campaign manager would show up at his rally and shoot him. He then deliberately held the former campaign manager’s left hand during their handshake so he would be forced to fire with his non-dominant left hand, causing him to miss any vital organs. Afterward, he had the former campaign manager ejected into space. He also had Investigator Rick killed afterward via ejection into space, using his newfound presidential authority to order both executions. Afterward, he attempted to indirectly murder C-137 following a dinner with him and his Morty by hacking his portal gun again, this time making it disintegrate instead of travel—meaning that if C-137 tried to leave through a portal, he would be killed. After that plan failed, it was revealed that he hadn’t just hacked Rick’s portal gun—he had hacked the entire city’s portal supply to redirect Ricks and Mortys to dimensions that would kill or disintegrate them, ensuring no one could leave the Citadel through portal travel. He then overcharged the dimensional drive and used it to punch a hole in the Central Finite Curve, breaking the barrier. He got into his ship in a spacesuit and had it automatically push him through the breach, escaping the blast radius and entering the new universe.

C-137: Before all of this, he had conceptualised the Citadel and founded the Council of Ricks. After he and his Morty are brought before the Council of Ricks because he was supposedly guilty of murdering multiple Ricks, he realises that his portal gun was hacked, as he would remember if he had used it to murder multiple Ricks. He then escapes from the Council of Ricks, evading their Rick soldiers by exploiting his portal gun to send them to dimensions with creatures that kill them. While on the run, before going through the Furniture Universe, he creates multiple portals that he and his Morty could have gone through, forcing the Council to search each one and buying himself time. At an unnamed restaurant in the Furniture Universe, he repairs his portal gun and traces the remote hacking signal back to the original hacker. He meets Evil Rick and Evil Morty and, assuming Evil Rick was the hacker, gets captured and strapped down, having his memories scanned to download the dimensional database. He then kills Evil Rick, believing he has stopped the hacker, and reports to the Council that Evil Rick was the true culprit behind the crimes. Later, he and his Morty are invited to dinner with President Morty. Despite not having met him before, he suspects he is evil because usually all politicians are. During the dinner, he notices how calm and nonchalant President Morty is about letting them leave despite openly revealing he is evil. From this, he deduces that his portal gun may have been hacked again. He tests it by forcing a Rick soldier into a portal and, upon confirming it was tampered with, he connects the dots President Morty isn’t just evil—he’s Evil Morty. The only ones who had ever hacked his portal gun were Evil Rick or Evil Morty, and since Evil Rick was dead, it had to be Evil Morty. He escapes the dinner and realises that Evil Morty is hacking the entire city’s portal supply based on what is happening to the other Ricks and Mortys attempting to flee the Citadel. As he makes his way down to escape Citadel Street, he deduces that Evil Morty needed the schematics for the dimensional drive to blow a hole in the Curve—something President Morty revealed during dinner. As for needing the dimensional drive itself, he concludes that Evil Morty must have had Evil Rick scan his brain for it. He now understands that when he first met Evil Rick, Evil Rick was merely taking credit for what Evil Morty had orchestrated in order to deceive him. To escape the impending destruction, he detaches Mortyburg from the rest of the Citadel by remotely controlling it and uses it to escape the blast radius.
