Evil Morty feat list

Backstory: He exploited Evil Rick’s alcoholism by offering him a drink pulling out a package of alcohol from the pop-up freezer before leaving to make his black eyepatch, so he could return to the garage when Evil Rick was drunk, render him unconscious with a red-controlled shock, and implant a brain chip receiver inside him without struggle. He also made sure the garage was shut beforehand so no one would hear his screams before the red-controlled shock was administered.

Rick Framing: He needed a way to breach the Central Finite Curve to reach a universe outside the CFC where Rick was not the smartest being in existence. The only way to do that was to obtain the schematics of the Citadel to find a way to weaponize it to breach the CFC, and those schematics were in the mind of Rick Sanchez from Dimension C-137—the architect behind the Citadel. So, in advance, he used Evil Rick controlling him via a transmitter hidden in his eyepatch, since he had forced Evil Rick to have a receiver implanted in his brain, allowing him to control Evil Rick’s actions. He then had Evil Rick kill multiple Ricks from other dimensions and hacked C-137’s portal gun to upload the travel data from Evil Rick onto C-137’s portal gun, making it appear as though C-137 was the culprit. He specifically chose to frame C-137 because he was the most believable suspect due to his murderous history, his noncooperation with the Council, and his rogue nature. He also deliberately murdered the second most likely suspect—the Scientist Formerly Known as Rick—through Evil Rick. He did this because he needed C-137, and only C-137, to trace the hacking of his portal gun back to Evil Rick and confront him at his base. There, he strapped him down and hooked him up to a mind-reading device to scan his brain for the dimensional data drive through Evil Rick; however, he couldn’t get it because Evil Rick was killed before the data drive could be transferred to him. For the base he kidnapped 827 other Mortys beforehand alongside Evil Rick killing Ricks to use them as a cloaking device to mask his brainwaves from the Citadel so the Council of Ricks couldn’t locate the base. After the Mortys killed Evil Rick Rick reported to the Council of Ricks that Evil Rick was the true culprit, Evil Morty was already gone—destroying the transmitter. He anticipated the Council of Ricks looking into him after figuring out Evil Rick was controlled to frame C-137 and broke into the Morty Agency, killing Ricks through Evil Rick, and deleted his Morty history from the Morty Dictionary beforehand so they couldn’t look into him. He anticipated the Council of Ricks would look into the second most likely suspect—the Scientist Formerly Known as Rick so he had him killed beforehand through Evil Rick. He anticipated Evil Rick trying to commit suicide in the base trap and beforehand programmed his black eyepatch to administer a control shock to Evil Rick so he would be stopped before he could do so, also programming his eyepatch to alert him when Evil Rick attempts to do so, so he can confront Evil Rick while he is shocked. He also beforehand kidnapped Ricks, bringing them to the base through Evil Rick to get information on the Citadel, then killed them afterward, which led to him learning about the Morty Agency and the Central Finite Curve.

Election Speech: He then knew he had to blow a hole in the Central Finite Curve using the dimensional drive, but he also needed a plan to escape afterward. So he would become President of the Citadel to gain the power, resources, and direct access to the Mortys and Ricks necessary for his escape. He made sure he would be a top contender by entering the election and making a speech about how Ricks and Mortys aren’t divided, but the Ricks and Mortys who like the Citadel divided are a problem within the Citadel—how the Citadel is the problem because it doesn’t believe in the uniqueness of Mortys, that they don’t believe Mortys should fight real injustice and instead just start wars between themselves, that they don’t believe a Rick should have equal pay from their Rick bosses despite all having the same IQ, that the Ricks and Mortys who believe in the Citadel are just feeding on the Citadel before its eventual downfall, and that those Ricks and Mortys are outnumbered by those who want change—highlighting flaws of the Citadel to get the votes from those who want it to change and to make those who don’t want it to change want change because of fear.

Staged Assassination Theory: He won the election by staging a failed assassination attempt to gain sympathy votes. He turned off his blast shield and implanted a new receiver into another Rick and controlled the Rick into becoming Investigator Rick, then controlled the new Investigator Rick into giving incriminating files about himself to his former campaign manager, with the mission to assassinate him, ensuring the former campaign manager would show up at his rally and shoot him. He then deliberately held the former campaign manager’s left hand during their handshake so he would be forced to fire with his non-dominant left hand, causing him to miss any vital organs. Afterward, he had the former campaign manager ejected into space. He also had Investigator Rick killed afterward via ejection into space, alongside the incriminating files using his newfound presidential authority to order both executions.

Central Finite Curve Breach: Afterward, he attempted to indirectly murder Rick C-137 following a dinner with him and his Morty, where he had secretly scanned his mind through a built-in mind-scanning device within the dinner table by hacking his portal gun again, this time making it disintegrate instead of travel—meaning that if C-137 tried to leave through a portal, he would be killed. After that plan failed, it was revealed that he hadn’t just hacked Rick C-137’s portal gun—he had hacked the entire city’s portal supply, including 5 million portal guns, to redirect Ricks and Mortys to dimensions that would kill or disintegrate them, ensuring no one could leave the Citadel through portal travel. He then overcharged the dimensional drive and used it to punch a hole in the Central Finite Curve, breaking the barrier. He got into his ship in his spacesuit and had it automatically push him through the breach, escaping the blast radius and entering the new universe. He scanned Rick C-137’s mind beforehand to get the dimensional drive he couldn’t get before. The mind-scanning device also linked to his own mind, meaning he processed the entire mind of Rick C-137 in roughy a span of a minute.

Operation Phoenix Sabotage: During the Central Finite Curve Breach, he deliberately made an announcement for Ricks and Mortys to stay in the Citadel in a Rick voice so all Ricks and Mortys would disobey due to their desire to deny authority, resulting in them being killed when trying to leave through portal travel. But he also knew about their immortality plan through Operation Phoenix, where their corpse’s minds get transported into an identical body in a chamber to escape death, so he had built 10 million Operation Phoenix chambers that would reroute the initial chambers for all 10 million Ricks and Mortys, with these new chambers having identical bodies to Ricks and Mortys, meaning he recreated 300,000,000,000,000,000,000 cells, making it so as soon as they come back to life in their new body, the new chamber they are in acts as a blender, grinding them into blood and redirecting the blood to the dimensional drive to fuel it, so he could keep getting power to punch a hole into the CFC.

Omega Device Obtainment: He feels shockwaves from Rick C-137 fracking the CFC and portals to him to investigate, immediately realising what Rick C-137 is doing to try and kill Rick Prime. He is also unfazed by the prospect of Rick C-137 having designed a rope specifically made to deal with him and calls out how his strategy of fracking the CFC to flush out Rick Prime is flawed because he’s using the scanner ray to hit infinite targets instead of speeding up the process by filtering for probability stasis. After killing all the Ricks Rick Prime threw at him and Rick C-137 in a Diane Tournament, they escape by creating portal fluid, and he follows C-137 to Rick Prime’s lair, getting into a fight with Rick Prime. During this fight, as Rick Prime is regenerating, he gives his eyepatch to Morty Prime and pretends to be Morty Prime, even saying Oh jeez, so he can get close enough to Rick Prime and catch him off guard with a finger gun that renders him unconscious. He then drags Rick Prime into another room, restrains him to a chair, and scans his mind, erasing any of Rick Prime’s backup clones to prevent him from coming back before finding and downloading the schematics for the Omega Device. He wanted the Omega Device as a way of showing he could end all surviving Ricks at any time so C-137 doesn’t try to find him, which is why he chose to follow C-137 to Rick Prime’s lair instead of leaving through portal travel after seeing what the interruption was. He then left the room, allowing C-137 to kill Rick Prime, knowing C-137 wouldn’t change emotionally after doing so and would just feel empty. He then reveals he has the schematics and could remake the Omega Device at any time, but that he won’t for now, as he anticipated that he would have to deal with vengeful Summers if he did so before leaving through portal travel.

Extra Information: He built his eyepatch to quickly scan his opponent’s brain so he can see incoming attacks before they happen, which is what he does against Rick Prime in their fight. That fight also reveals he seems to know Taekwondo, and he built a chip he can just throw onto robotics to control them through his eyepatch. He usually keeps a blast shield on him as a safety feature from Ricks and general enemies but can lower it for strategic moves, as seen in his assassination attempt.
