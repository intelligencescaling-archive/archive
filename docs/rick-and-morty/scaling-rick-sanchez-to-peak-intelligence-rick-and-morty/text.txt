Scaling Rick Sanchez to Peak Intelligence

I saw quite a few people say Rick is overrated in terms of intelligence, I completely disagree. By the way you may realise I use the same feats as [vsbattles](https://vsbattles.fandom.com/wiki/Rick_Sanchez) but don't worry thats because I made Rick's profile, all the research was done by me.

Rick is exceptionally intelligent to the point where he can manipulate events and have control over entire episodes and seasons (Season 3), often regarded as the smartest human in the universe (S3 Ep1). He constructed a space cruiser capable of traveling between galaxies in a short time using random items and objects he found in his garage (S1 Ep1). He outsmarted Snuffles, a super-intelligent dog, by pretending to be dead and then using dream inceptors to ensure Snuffles left humans alone (S1 Ep2). He built [Anatomy Park inside the body of Ruben](https://imgur.com/fZL3d8u), though he got help from the Dr. Xenon Bloom (S1 Ep3). He discerned that he was ensconced within a simulation, which was within another simulation, itself nestled within a larger simulation. The final layer was nearly indistinguishable from reality in every conceivable aspect. Later, he manipulated and tricked the aliens into self-destructing. (S1 Ep4)

He implied he could repair his portal gun one-handed using materials from sex dolls (S1 Ep5), created a robot with artificial intelligence [solely to pass him the butter](https://giphy.com/gifs/aMaDZCkqkL1OPItyhM). He was able to recognize the devil instantly with little information, then devised a way to measure the "evil" in objects and invented a device that could detect and counteract their curses. It was stated that Rick was able to steal time crystals from the Time Cops and [also manipulate one of them](https://giphy.com/gifs/MBknMCEglS4JFe1GfA), even though their race is Nigh-Omniscient. Toxic Rick [discovered the toxic equivalent of electricity and a completely new element in a world made of toxins, figured out a way to go back to the real world and tricked the healthy Rick who is just as smart as him](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hetUen28Qok)

[Rick placed a spatially tessellated void inside a modified temporal field until a planet developed intelligent life, creating a infinite sized universe.](https://giphy.com/gifs/BuHyQZr309MkaurKfr) He managed to [trick the Galactic Government even while they were using the Brainalyzer](https://youtu.be/ffuUF9v8yOQ?si=XF2VehMwkCK0bt_k&t=51), a device that lets them enter your mind and access all your thoughts and memories. Somehow, Rick was able to hide the fact that he had altered his backstory, ensuring that instead of writing his portal gun formula, he wrote a virus that gave him full control over the system. Created a mech suit for himself using the corpses of rats and bugs then took down an entire mafia organization all while he was a pickle.
