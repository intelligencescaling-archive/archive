Okay for some context he is introduced when he is in a room full of monitors and is watching another Jerry and mushroom head Rick, at which point he commands a henchman or something to bring back that Jerry, he swiftly made this decision since as the Rick of that universe states “trans-dimensional Jerry travel hasn't been discovered yet” so seeing himself at a theme part might hint to alternate worlds. As such a powerful man with a desire to expand he decides to meet up with this other Jerry in order to learn more about this opportunity and offer the alternative Jerry his status in this world for the trans-dimensional technology that has brought them there, he persuades Jerry using many manipulative tactics in the following conversation before presenting this offer to Jerry. For some context from the camera at the amusement park and their first confrontation in the monitoring room he gleaned that Jerry was not like him whatsoever, a pushover, possibly even a failure
(Decision-making, Psychological profiling, Tactics, Linear thinking, Analytical thinking, Abductive Reasoning, Emotional Perception, Intuition)

He states that he is the richest man on earth and owns “pretty much everything” making the offer he is going to present even sweeter before doing so, surrounding himself with women while he's doing it.
(Influence, Emotional manipulation)

He states that his desire for greatness is tireless which I suppose would explain why he's so rich, this also sets up the reason for his offer making it more natural. He sells the idea of a great man who believes that the other Jerry, since he is like him, is great too. By bragging so much he also sells a narcissistic persona this would implant the idea in Jerry's mind that he could not see himself as being not quite as great in other universes
(Emotional Engagement, Direct Manipulation)

This speaks for itself, he's the definition of success which again Jerry so desires, and he sells the idea I brought up before even further by suggesting that Jerry too is that successful.
(Tactics, Persuasion, Indirect Manipulation)
He then naturally Segways into the offer and sells the idea even further in one move by asking Jerry what to do when you've already conquered so much? Jerry, entirely unfamiliar with such a scenario, cannot exactly answer that questions and then Doofus Jerry answers that question himself which he then turns into a way to start negotiating the deal

He starts taking a more direct approach to counteract Rick's advice recommending that by accepting this deal Jerry would practically become a god among men, with large amounts of money and authority, insulting Rick directly and stating that Jerry will have absolute freedom.
(Social Intelligence, Direct Manipulation, Emotional Manipulation)

Once all of this fails he decides to instead just pull out his gun, very simple but effective
(Decision-making, Intimidation)

He threatened Morty so he would remain silent by subtly implying he can just kill him.
(Coercion, Emotional Manipulation)

He bested every single attempt Rick, the smartest guy in the world/universe, could throw at him to the point he just kinda gave up and in anticipation of Rick's final measure he disarmed a very dangerous bomb in advance.
(Anticipation, Trap Evasion)

Him winning is literally against the natural order of things
(Narrative)

He provoked Morty by suggesting he was uhhhhhhhh you know what with his mother
(Provocation)

He understands that the status quo is that Rick's are generally very far above the Jerry's. As such, when the Rick council was called in for Doofus Jerry, the one they mistakenly perceived as the weakest of the universe's due to their prejudice against Jerry's, they believe him to be the strongest. Weakest of the weak. They don't even suspect a thing when he wanders into the genetic restructure. Essentially turning himself into a god, but you can't turn yourself into a god genetically, rather he used it to alter his pheromones into something that is fatally poisonous to Rick's, he did this to essentially conquer the Rick's and use their minds and technology to conquer even further
(Social Awareness, EU, Strategy, Strategy Building, Strategy Adaptability, Strategy Vision, Strategy Efficiency, Strategy Efficiency, Strategy Execution, Strategy Shielding, Strategy Alignment, Planning, Long Term, Planning Aim, Planning Intricacies, Process)

Prepared a really large Jerry robot much thing in preparation of anything Rick might throw at him
(Contingencies)

Note that it is possible Doofus Jerry had this end game in mind from the start as the moment he had begun to suspect trans-dimensional travel as a thing. If there are multiple Jerry's, there are multiple Rick's, Rick's are incredibly smart so it's not a massive stretch they have a meeting spot someone given a Rick came up with the trans-dimensional gun in the first place, so he could have then reasoned there would be a Rick council or council adjacent thing and as such provoked Rick by dismantling all his attempts and beating him in order for the other Rick's to be called in at which point he would find a way to dominate them and conquer everything.
(All Strategy, All Planning, Abductive Reasoning, Inductive Reasoning)
