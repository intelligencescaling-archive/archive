Mexican Standoff explanation (rocketjump featuring Key and Peele)

So first of all, let's identify the characters before we get any further:

Jordan Peele's character is Cortez, let's call Keegan Michael Key's character Chuck E. Cheese (nothing else to call him) and the other one's character is Blueberries Johnson.

As best I can decipher, Cortez, the real Cortez, is in fact a cop, dead-set on eliminating dangerous criminals, and has been since, apparently, before even entering a police academy. Chuck E. Cheese and Blueberries Johnson are both seemingly such criminals. Hence, Cortez created an elaborate plan to eliminate both of them at once, with both of them (mostly Chuck E. Cheese) attempting to counter it several times over.

**Cortez' Plan:**

–Have Blueberries Johnson brainwashed to be a sleeper-agent triggered by the code-phrase "blueberry pie"

–Have Blueberries Johnson imitate himself (Cortez), and shall thus be referred to as C. B. J (Cortez Blueberries Johnson)

–Take on the disguise of Blueberries Johnson, and from henceforth become known in this post as D. Cortez (disguised Cortez)

–Have C. B. J make an alliance with another criminal, Chuck E. Cheese

–Have C. B. J send Chuck E. Cheese to police academy

–Join police academy and formally meet Chuck E. Cheese

–Perceives that Chuck E. Cheese is aware that he (D. Cortez) us aware that Chuck E. Cheese is a spy (Emotional Perception)

–Even so, manages to build up a relationship that spans years, even attending each other's big family events, such as Cortez' family's wedding

–Corner C. B. J with Chuck E. Cheese

–Have a sniper trained on C. B. J's position

–Anticipate that C. B. J will have an unmanned drone to take out the sniper

–Have a hacker to take out unmanned drone

–Anticipate that C. B. J will have the hacker's wife kidnapped

–Have Chuck E. Cheese have an affair with the hacker's wife for 6 months leading up to the standoff, prompting her to file for divorce, insulating the hacker from caring about her being kidnapped

–Have a secret second sniper trained on Chuck E. Cheese's position for when Cheese inevitably reveals himself to be working with C. B. J

–Trigger Blueberries Johnson and then reveal that the two of them were secretly each other in order to confuse Chuck E. Cheese

–Have a bullet-proof vest for the ensuing gunfight while the other two kill each other

–Implant Blueberries Johnson, sleeper-agent programmed by Cortez with the secret mission to investigate Cortez for corruption

–Despite he and D. Cortez mutually knowing each other are aware of each other's secrets, manages to build up a relationship that spans years, even attending each other's big family events, such as Cortez' family's wedding

–Corner C. B. J with D. Cortez

–C. B. J recruits other criminal Chuck E. Cheese

–Chuck E. Cheese and D. Cortez both join the police academy, meet each other and perceive each other as aware of each other's secrets
