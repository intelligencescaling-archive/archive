Anyway, the Ink Demon actually do have some really good Manipulation feats since by the end of chapter 5 of Dark Revival, he managed to manipulate tricked and persuaded Audrey to join the black puddle with him as a pawn through promised during her desperate moment after fighting Wilson and use the analogy that her road and path is over, with her legs being cut off, really saying that her path and road is over with that, thus making her desperate and join him and give up her suffering. Is really underrated cause people's immediately see it as a anti feats after it cause of... Well what happened after it but I won't blame them tbh.

Is described to be extremely charismatic and cunning (Via, The Voice In All Of Us), to the point that he can make people worship him just by talking to them; https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/character-stats-and-profiles/images/9/92/Whathisfollowersays.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20241013091547&format=original

Throughout his living time, Ink Demon terrorized and reigned over The Cycle, converting the any inhabintants of it to his cult and heavily implied to have put civilizations or other communities under his own influence (Via The Dark Monarch). With him also previously succeed in making Henry believe himself is still alive and indirectly dragged his soul into The Cycle through faking Joey Drew in a telegram during Chapter 1 and making him active the ink machine.

Exists inside the mind of all beings in The Cycle as a disembodied consciousness; https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bendy-and-the-ink-machine/images/4/4a/The_Voice_in_All_of_Us.png/revision/latest?cb=20241007010557&format=original which means that the Ink Demon have to multitask talking to literally everyone in the series via this with telepathy and manipulating them, via *"A voice that calls us to a purpose. We know is a lie, but his intoxicating pull is strong. He is calling, always calling. Can you not hear him?"*

Endured hourly sessions of physical totures and surgical invasions, without any pain reactions in Keeper Log 44. Doesn't possess a soul. Soul in one being is the core which all emotions, personality along with memories stemmed from, directly connecting to their non-physical mind. Meaning that the Ink Demon basically have absolute EM via this.

Implied to be aware of himself being in a time loop.

He is aware of soul existence which no one else could do. Can see and hear everything that has been said or happening across space and time. Predicting the fate of Audrey at the end of the journey and seemingly sees through her true past despite him existing in a parallel to the Real World, on an entirely seperated temporal dimension. Able to foreshadow and predict via pure Perception on his cycle and subconsciousness. And what will happen in the future, and perceive the past of Audrey regardless of herself having no actual memories about it.  Can seal souls even though they are normally unperceivable. Making his Perception and Foresight basically... Unrealistic since he was able to predict the fate of Audrey from his perception alone.

Can observe, processed and hear everything throughout The Multiverse at once via his perception, in which there are infinite universes existing in infinite amounts of dimensions; https://character-stats-and-profiles.fandom.com/wiki/User_blog:Polastournecis/Bendy:_Cosmolgy_Analyzation#The_Multiverse

Built a far more ultra-modern version of the Ink Machine, a supernatural machinery that helped Joey Drew create The Cycle and using its interior as a fortress, with him initially were even able to ultilize the Ink Machine to turn humans into Ink Creatures in Chapter 5.
