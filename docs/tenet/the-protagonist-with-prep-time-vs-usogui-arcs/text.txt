**Protoporos Island:** Clears High Diff (+)
\-Explanation:

**Air Poker:** Clears Extreme Diff-CGEW
\-Explanation:
TP would definitely figure out the rule of the cards during his 24 hours of prep time but the issue comes down to if his PSI and CPI is good enough to handle forming the hands together. IMO his FSIQ is good but likely not good enough to handle the game at the same level Baku and Lalo could. The Protagonist wouldn't deliberately tell Lalo the rule but would still try and employ the same strategy as him by conserving his air and just mainly relying on luck/intuition rather than stress about what cards are left remaining. Lalo having superior cards and better FSIQ combined with the fact that he'd likely figure out the rule on his own eventually unprompted means that he has a stronger chance of winning but it all comes down to if The Protagonist could plan out a route using the info he has on what cards there are available to just know what cards to play ahead of time under every situation

But The Protagonist certainly has good enough Strategy and Fluid Intelligence in order to adapt to Souichi's mind reading as well
