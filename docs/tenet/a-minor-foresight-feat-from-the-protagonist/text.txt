A minor Foresight feat from The Protagonist because I’m bored [Doc Revamp sneakpeak]

During TP’s introduction to Kat where he adapts to the knowledge of Kat and Sator's relationship, he sets up her manipulation by implanting the idea in her head that he could be her chance at getting revenge on her husband Sator via getting rid of the faked drawing that Sator uses to blackmail Kat.

In the diner, before TP is sent to the kitchen to get beaten, TP slides his phone number into Kat's coat pocket and says to call him later. Kat literally says to his face "you won't be taking my call" to which TP responds with "I might surprise you"

Later on, after Kat witnesses TP emerging from the kitchen after having beaten all of Sator's men, Kat is outside her sons school and then reaches into her bag to call TP, having changed her mind. The moment she dials his number, TP shows up right behind her, saying “I told you I’d surprise you.”

This means that The Protagonist had anticipated the exact moment that Kat would make up her mind about The Protagonist and decide to call him for help, why else would he be there in that moment?

It would’ve been completely pointless for him to have been there for any other reason. He already gave her his phone number and said to call him specifically and only on the condition that she accepts his help. If she didn’t call him, then she didn’t want his help. So clearly he wasn’t there to simply ask her again and she just so happened to decide to ring him at that moment.

To be even more clear, this means that TP essentially predicted Kats exact thought process down to the second she’d decide to call him a full day in advanced, if not outright predicting all of it the second she said “you won’t be taking my call”, hence the response from TP being “I might surprise you” and echoing that line when he showed up the day after. He foresaw her emotional development from accepting that she can’t escape Sators hold over her to trying to seize her opportunity to escape.
