**Overview:**

During the preparation for the Stalsk-12 battle, Priya informs The Protagonist of a rally point at Trondheim where the paramilitary of Tenet + TP and co will board an inverted ship, traveling to Stalsk-12 and backwards through time to May 14th. On this ship, we get a glance at the gigantic Turnstile that was built into the ship itself that allowed for the entire Tenet paramilitary to invert quickly and seamlessly \[Image 1\]. We don't see TP himself go through this Turnstile, but we know that he would've had to at some point because he was uninverted during the Stalsk-12 battle but inverted while on the ship.

The feat in question arises when you ask yourself: How exactly did Tenet build this Turnstile? As we know, The Protagonist's own future self is the founder and leader of the Tenet organization, so that means the construction of this Turnstile would've been his responsibility and ultimately, it was him and no one else who constructed this machine. But...how?

Previously, I was under the impression that The Protagonist's possibly just stole some blueprints for Turnstile designs. Priya confirms in an earlier scene that such blueprints exist, although we don't know where they are, we just know Sator was given them by Posterity. However, this means my previous assertion that "TP went back after sliming Priya and stole the blueprints off Sator's yacht" makes no sense because.

1: There's literally no reason for the aforementioned blueprints to be on the yacht since Sator had already built every Turnstile well before that point
2: After the ending of the film, TP had exactly 0 resources. He'd have no way of even getting to the boat and it's also highly unlikely that they just left it sitting there after Sator's "disappearance"

So with that, all evidence points towards an extremely unrealistic interpretation: **TP built the Boat Turnstile using nothing but sheer Learning Ability and Reasoning.**

**How he did it:**

Throughout the film, TP gets a good look and even goes inside multiple Turnstiles himself. In order, his experience with Turnstiles is:

1: Seeing the Oslo Turnstile and fighting his inverted counterpart that came out of it \[Image 2\]
2: Seeing the Tallinn Turnstile, going through the inverted interrogation and then going through it himself, getting a look at the inside of a Turnstile for the first time \[Image 3\] (the inside of a turnstile is just an empty room btw, nothing interesting)
3: Going through the Oslo Turnstile again, this time *as* the inverted counterpart his previous self was fighting.
4: Seeing and then going through the Boat Turnstile \[Image 1\].

And that's it. The total amount of information that TP knows about Turnstiles is literally the exact same as we the viewers get to know about them. If you were to watch Tenet a single time, you'd know equally as much about Turnstiles as TP did.

Meaning, just via a few glances at a couple of turnstiles, the majority of which looked and operated VASTLY differently from his own boat Turnstile. **TP was able to deduce the method of constructing a Turnstile based solely on that**.

**What this means:**

Turnstiles are a device for inverting people and objects entropy's, thereby making them move backwards in time. Turnstiles were invented by Posterity, to which I estimated that, at a lower bound, they're 150 years ahead 2019. Somewhere around the year 2169+ (could be a lot higher).

This means that TP, armed with only basic knowledge of inversion, turnstiles and a very limiting background in any scientific field (his role in the CIA involved nuclear containment)**, TP was able to completely break the modern understanding of physics, thermodynamics, entropy and time itself 150+ years ahead of time. Using nothing but his own brain power and logical reasoning.**

Imagine someone from 1876 being able to design and construct a rocket ship, the first iPhone and invent the modern understanding of quantum mechanics all at the same time using nothing but brain power while having next to zero scientific background. That's essentially what TP did, if not even better than that.

**Timeframe:**

The only real downside to this feat is that The Protagonist would've had quite some time in order to perform it. After the end of the film, TP inverts back from 2019 to 2008, spending 11 years while inverted before finally making it to 2008 where the Tenet operation truly began.

This means that TP effectively had 11 years in order to learn what was required and perform this feat. It's still an insane feat even with 11 years worth of prep time, but still, 11 years is quite a long time for an LA feat.

The thing is though, TP did not have 11 full years in order to build this. There was still a great number of things he necessarily needed to do before he could even *start* to perform this feat. In this section, I'll try to build a conservative estimate of the timeframe TP had to break science entirely.

Step 1: Use his paramilitary to overtake a Russian nuclear test site and steal the Plutonium 241 (the last piece of The Algorithm).
Estimated time frame this would take: **1 month of prep + 1 week to execute it** (TP: "The station was held for a week")

Step 2: After the paramilitary that helped him steal the Plutonium is gone in order to keep the secret, TP would've needed to build up Tenet's considerable wealth all in secret while leaving no trace. Tenet is quite rich, being able to afford it's own large paramilitary, scientists, medical staff, intelligence agents, arms dealers, and their own large vehicles/buildings such as the Boat that the Boat Turnstile is on. At a rough estimate, 1 billion dollars of wealth should be enough to cover the cost of operating Tenet and TP had to generate this all in secrecy.
Estimated time frame this would take: **2 years**, 1 billion dollars is a lot of money

Step 3: During Step 2 after even after it's done, TP would've been recruiting each and every single member of Tenet, including those that would eventually help him build the Boat Turnstile. This means that every single member of Tenet we see in the film (Neil, Priya, Ives, Crosby, the paramilitary etc etc) would've had to be recruited during this time. As well as setting up their qualification for the mission in the case of Neil (getting him a Masters in Physics, weapons training etc)
Estimated time frame this would take: **1 year** at the lowest balling of it.

Then after this, TP would be left with nothing but time. Waiting for all the pieces to come together all in order to finally begin the mission on May 14th 2019, where he recruits his past self into the organization. It is only right here that TP could've begun building the Boat Turnstile.

**Final amount of time TP would've had in order to start, build and finish the Boat Turnstile: 7.9 years at the most conservative estimate possible.**

Yes, it's still quite a lot of time. But don't be mistaken, pran (yes pran I'm talking to YOU in particular). TP did NOT have 11 full years just to build one device. Even during these 7.9 years, TP's focus would've been split among a SHIT ton of other tasks while he set up everything so the mission goes perfectly. This isn't even accounting for the "stuff" TP and Neil got up to according to Neil at the end of the film, which would've also eaten up the time TP had in order to build this.

**Conclusion:**

The Protagonist, using nothing but his Learning Ability, Logical Reasoning and ungodly levels of General Intelligence, was able to build a Turnstile remarkably superior to Sator's and possibly even Posterity itself 150 years before humanity as a whole discovered how to make them. This involved completely breaking the modern understanding of several fields of science while having next to no knowledge on any of it prior to this himself. Yes Neil using his Masters degree in physics could've theoretically helped but we have no idea when exactly Neil was recruited and also, a modern degree in physics would not help AT ALL in understanding physics that's 150+ years in the future from when they are now.

Basically, The Protagonist >>> CTW L, WJM, Ayanokoji and the Usogui Duo in Intelligence, Reasoning and Learning Ability.
