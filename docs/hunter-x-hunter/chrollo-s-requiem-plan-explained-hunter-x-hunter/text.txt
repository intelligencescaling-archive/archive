Chrollo's requiem plan explained . short analysis

It starts in September 1st, at the Yorknew arc. The Spider gather around the outskirts of Yorknew City to carry out their heist of Yorknew's underground auction.

All members of the Spider/Phantom Troupe are allowed to think and carry out their own actions independently, as long as it doesn't harm the group. This allows the Spider to have versatility in their operations and exploits.

Seven Spiders were sent to commit the heist at one of the auctions. However, the seven found that the vault was completely empty.

They informed the leader, Chrollo, that the vault was empty and perhaps someone knew that the Phantom Troupe were coming. This raised the possibility that there was an insider/traitor.

Chrollo reasoned that there couldn't be a traitor inside the Phantom Troupe since no Troupe member really cares about money, fame, etc.

If the traitor informed the Mafia, then why didn't they strengthen the security? Their prudence doesn't make sense.

There is an informant, but the informant can't give certain information.

Chrollo later connects this to the Ten Dons and their Shadow Beasts, which do their bidding.

One of the Shadow Beasts changed the plan after receiving information from the informant. This Shadow Beast must have some type of Nen ability similar to Shizuku's.

Now, the Mafia realizes that the Phantom Troupe have Nen, so they're going to send the Shadow Beasts after them.

Chrollo orders Uvogin to cause a disturbance to gather the attention of the Mafia and the Shadow Beasts.

\[Foresight\]

The Spiders were able to find the "Owl." However, Uvogin was kidnapped by the Chain User, Kurapika.

One of the Spiders, Machi, was able to track Uvogin using her Nen. This led them to the Nostrade HQ, where they rescued Uvogin.

Kurapika was out since he had been contacted by Hisoka.

Uvogin and the other Spiders informed the group that the Chain User was playing the role of a bodyguard for the Nostrade family.

Uvogin leaves the Spiders temporarily to fight the Chain User and hold him captive.

However, Chrollo and the Spiders notice that Uvogin didn't return.

Chrollo orders the Spiders that if Uvogin didn't return by dawn, their plans would change.

Chrollo's requiem

1.first, Chrollo would disguise himself and investigate the Nostrade family and Mafia, whom he suspects either gave information to the Shadow Beasts or that the Shadow Beasts told the Nostrade family and gave them information prior to the heist.

Chrollo then figures out that the bodyguards in the Nostrade family were there to protect the daughter of Nostrade. The daughter of Nostrade is Neon. She is a fortune teller and likely has a Nen Hatsu that gives prior information about future events.

At the same time, Chrollo likely hires Illumi to deal with the 10 Dons for his plans. He foresaw that after the disturbance caused by the Phantom Troupe, there would be an assassination attempt against them, likely involving the Zoldycks. Chrollo would then try to steal their abilities using Bandit's Secret, his Nen Hatsu.

2.after finding the daughter of Nostrade, Neon, Chrollo asks her to give a fortune about Chrollo's fate. At the same time, Chrollo activates his Nen Hatsu and steals her ability, so Nostrade can't cause more problems for the Phantom Troupe.

From the fortune, Chrollo learns that Uvogin is dead and also learns the activation conditions for this Nen Hatsu.

Chrollo begins escorting Neon out of the restaurant at Nostrade's place. Chrollo causes a diversion and Neon suddenly falls ill. Chrollo escapes while the guards carry Neon.

However, one assassin hired to deal with Chrollo notices that Chrollo knocked Neon out.

Exactly what Chrollo wanted. Chrollo kills the assassin.

3.after reading his fortune, Chrollo orders the Spider to wreak havoc for Uvogin's death.

This leads to the whole Mafia knowing that the Phantom Troupe is in the city and is after the auction.

The Zoldycks know and inform the other bodyguards that they don't stand a chance against the assassin in the building, so it's better that they leave.

4.Chrollo fights the two Zoldycks on his own, trying to steal their abilities. Both Zoldycks notice that Chrollo is after their abilities rather than trying to kill them.

While fighting Chrollo, the Zoldycks receive a message from the other Zoldyck, Illumi, that the 10 Dons were assassinated by order of Chrollo.

Since the 10 Dons who hired Zeno and Silva Zoldyck are dead, they are not going to get paid, so they leave Chrollo.

At the same time, the 10 Dons who were controlled by Illumi's Nen Hatsu broadcast that the leader and the Troupe are both dead.

After that, at the auction, the Spiders disguise themselves and steal the original artwork and antiques, as well as the bidding money.

TLDR:

Phantom Troupe is in the city -> 10 Dons broadcast + assassin Zoldycks leaving + bodies of the Phantom Troupe and their leader -> Phantom Troupe are gone for good, now we can begin the auction.

Later + Phantom Troupe don't have any registry -> means they are from Meteor City, where registries are never recorded.

major feats - foresight, deception, planning, thinking
