Pariston Hill (maybe even Beyond Netero) feat

Pariston Hill, Beyond Netero (Hunter x Hunter)

It's best to read the 13th Hunter Chairman Election arc and Succession Contest arc to understand this more clearly. This explanation focuses on how Pariston and Beyond managed to enter the Dark Continent so easily without being stopped by the V5 or the Zodiacs.

1. The Goals and Actions of the V5:

The V5 group absolutely did not want Kakin to independently launch an expedition to the Dark Continent and attempt to claim the benefits for itself.

Kakin—being a country not part of the V5—was not bound by the group’s treaties and resolutions regarding restrictions on entering the New World. Therefore, Kakin organized a massive public expedition that anyone could join into the New World.

The V5, fully aware of Kakin’s intentions and being the organization that issued the ban on free access to the new world, had to find a way to stop them and gain their own share of the spoils.

So, the V5 released statistics on failed expeditions and extremely low survival rates, emphasizing the disastrous consequences. This allowed them to frame the issue as a global threat, one that could impact national security.

With that, the V5 invoked clauses in the international charter to legally mobilize military forces and force Kakin to join, thus turning the V5 into the V6.

The V5 wasn’t aiming for war with Kakin—they simply wanted Kakin to join them. Once Kakin became part of V6, they would have to follow the restrictions on access to the Dark Continent.

V6 would then act as a unified front; Kakin's king would be honored publicly, but in exchange, the spoils of exploration would be divided equally among V6 members.

Afterward, V5 wouldn’t interfere in Kakin’s actions and would quietly pursue their own gains behind the scenes.

The Hunter Association was given the role of capturing Beyond to halt the expedition and serve as a negotiation tool with Kakin (an extra “weapon” beyond direct military threats).

This way, the V5 could exploit the Association without having to deploy its own expensive forces—avoiding a financial crisis.

In short: all of this was for the sake of self-interest.

2. Beyond’s Move:

The moves of the V5 and the Hunter Association were already within Beyond’s (and Pariston’s) calculations.

First, Beyond and Pariston had anticipated this outcome, so they proposed a policy of unrestricted applicant recruitment for the expedition. Thanks to this, the V5 could invoke the reasoning that civilians would be endangered, giving them a justification to interfere.

Otherwise, the V5 would only have one option: use military force against Kakin — but without any legitimate excuse. They would be relying solely on old V5 treaties, which were only binding among V5 nations — something no country wanted to deal with.

This reckless open recruitment policy was a trap laid by Beyond, giving the V5 the evidence they needed to act. Now, V5 just needed to arrest the mastermind causing chaos — Beyond — and apply political pressure by accusing Kakin’s policies of endangering its people.

Thus, Beyond handed V5 both tools (public danger + an arrestable figure) to force Kakin to share benefits of the expedition — while still being able to say they were acting “lawfully and morally” under long-standing global charters.

Next move: Beyond turned himself in to the Hunter Association. At this point, Beyond became the ticket for the Association to enter the Dark Continent.

 In exchange, certain demands from Beyond had to be accepted:

First, the original expedition plan by Kakin must proceed, and the King of Kakin must be credited publicly.

Second, if Beyond escapes after reaching the Dark Continent, the Association must not pursue or act against him anymore.

Third move: Beyond sought to weaken the power of the Hunter Association — his greatest rival in the expedition.

If the Association were to follow Netero’s orders, they could have sent their full strength into the Dark Continent.

 However, by accepting Beyond’s proposal, they were now forced to split their resources:

Protect civilians (to uphold V5’s public justification),

Keep constant watch on Beyond — All while still participating in the expedition.

This mission was almost impossible to balance.

Of course, if the Association succeeded, they would gain more fame, retain their reputation with clients, enter the Dark Continent freely, and possibly bring huge benefits to humanity.

Why didn’t the Association just imprison or kill Beyond, if he was such a threat?

Because by this point, the Association had already been checkmated.

Reason:

If the Hunter Association refuses Beyond’s proposal and he manages to escape and enter the Dark Continent, the Association would lose their clients.

If they simply capture Beyond without agreeing to his demands, 5,000 Chimera Ants will be released into the outside world. The Association would then be fully responsible for the consequences:

First, the Association would have to mobilize all its forces to deal with the Chimera Ants, leaving no resources to handle Beyond.

Second, the Association’s reputation would be severely damaged. Pariston could easily shift all the blame onto them, using his former position as Vice Chairman of the Association to manipulate public trust and pin all responsibility on both the Association and the Zodiacs.

Therefore, the Hunter Association is forced to secretly cooperate with Beyond and follow his terms.

(This cooperation is kept hidden from the V5, who believe the Association is simply detaining Beyond and complying with their orders.)

Ultimately, Beyond’s goal is to exploit the power of the Zodiacs once they’ve entered the Dark Continent.

What’s the benefit? As explained above, if the Zodiacs accept Beyond’s request, they must also protect civilians to maintain trust with the V5 (their core client).

Beyond is likely to exploit this weakness, along with other strategic plays not yet revealed, to achieve his ultimate goal.

From this, we can conclude that Beyond and Pariston are exceptionally gifted, both in strategy and intellect.

Beyond’s only real goal is to reach the New World — and secure the agreement with Kakin to have the King of Kakin’s name recorded in history.

All the other issues — open recruitment, the V5’s reaction, the Association’s actions — were merely pawns in the grand plan that he and Pariston had already calculated.

Only Ging actually saw through their plan and intentionally joined the expedition to destroy it.

Everything above was predicted, orchestrated, and manipulated by Pariston Hill, who understood the inner workings of both the V5 and Kakin.

Every move was planned well in advance, and no one noticed — except Ging.
