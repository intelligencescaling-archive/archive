I feel like it does and that this verse is basically not discussed that much here, if at all.

Like for example Khan Noonien Singh (or just Khan). He was a superhuman, quite literally. From 1992 to 1996 he ended up ruling more than 1/4th of the worlds population and was considered the best of tyrants (btw this is among the other superhuman dudes who ended up ruling parts of the Earth). He escaped the consequences of the Eugenics Wars, cryogenically freezing himself in the Botany Bay because he was leaving the Solar System (they didnt have FTL travel yet), which he took control of.

The USS Enterprise found the SS Botany Bay and eventually Khan came back to life. He took advantage of Kirks hospitality and familiarized with the history he lost by reading the ships manuals. He discovered a weakness in McGivers, because she was attracted to him. He even told her a romanticized version of the Eugenics Wars.

Khan would escape the Botany Bay with the help of McGivers and revived his followers and he managed to cut life support to the bridge, after taking control over the Enterprise from the engineering room. He held Kirk hostage and would only spare him if they accepted his plan of using the Enterprise to make a colony that was going to be led by Khan. Khan managed to survive the anesthetic gas that disabled the Augments as he was quick enough to lock himself in the engineering room and isolating it from the rest of the ship so that the air wouldnt enter it. He did this on a ship he saw for the first time. That is how quick he learned it.

He wanted to overload the warp core to destroy the Enterprise, but was defeated by Kirk after Kirk hit him with a heavy rod, despite Khan being x5 stronger than a human.

Khan would be sent to a planet with his followers that would soon turn for the worst as it would become devastated and hard to inhabit. Still though, with his ingenuity he managed to keep himself and some of his people alive, even if 20 died including his wife. He was smart enough to survive in an incredibly harsh environment. There were some eels that survived that were killing people, said eels attacking them cuz they were the only hosts left after the planet became uninhabitable.

The USS Reliant, mistaking Ceti Alpha V for Ceti Alpha VI, beamed down on the planet and were immediately captured by Khan and soon enough through this he would hijack the Reliant. He also managed to successfully lure Kirk with a signal, pretending that Reliant was coming and that Genesis was successful. He intercepted the Enterprise when it was going to Regula 1.

In their final battle, he landed some solid blows on the Enterprise, but well got defeated in the end.

Obviously though Khan has his anti-feats, he was too totalitarian and so his nation didnt progress, the tyrants warring amongst each other, failing to take control of the Enterprise, apparently becoming insane from having spent all that time on that planet, failed to account for Kirks winning strategy at the end, misinterpreted Spocks signal to mean 2 days and not 2 hours.

Probably some more stuff that I forgot but whatever.
